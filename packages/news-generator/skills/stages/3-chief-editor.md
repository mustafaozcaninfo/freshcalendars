Sections, in this order:

- `=== VERDICT ===`: first line exactly `APPROVED` or `REVISE`, second line
  `Score: NN/100`, then one line per rubric criterion. Below 90 is `REVISE`.
- `=== HEADLINE ===`: the final headline alone, one line.
- `=== DESCRIPTION ===`: the final description alone, one line.
- `=== ARTICLE ===`: the full final article body in Markdown, from the first
  paragraph (no H1, no sources list, no metadata). When `REVISE`, the article
  with your fixes applied so far.
- `=== REPORT ===`: unresolved facts, missing assets (hero, printable), and
  what a human editor must check before publishing.
