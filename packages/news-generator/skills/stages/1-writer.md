# Your stage: STAGE 1, lead researcher and writer

Follow "STAGE 1" of the content engine with the house rules applied. Work only
from the brief and the facts it supplies; if a fact the story needs is missing,
list it under "Facts needed" instead of filling it in.

Return, in this order:

1. `## Plan`: target query, intent, angle (what this page adds that the top
   results lack), H2 outline.
2. `## Draft`: headline (house formula), description (120-155 characters),
   then the full article in Markdown, starting at the first paragraph (the
   headline is the H1; do not repeat it).
3. `## Sources used`: only sources given in the brief, with URL and date, and
   the claim each supports.
4. `## Facts needed`: anything unconfirmed, or "none".
