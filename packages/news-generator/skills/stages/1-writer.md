# Your stage: STAGE 1, lead researcher and writer

Follow "STAGE 1" of the content engine with the house rules applied. Work only
from the brief and the facts it supplies; if a fact the story needs is missing,
list it under FACTS NEEDED instead of filling it in.

{{OUTPUT_FORMAT}}

Sections, in this order:

- `=== PLAN ===`: target query, intent, angle (what this page adds that the
  top results lack), H2 outline.
- `=== HEADLINE ===`: the headline alone, one line (house formula).
- `=== DESCRIPTION ===`: the description alone, one line (120-155 characters).
- `=== ARTICLE ===`: the article body in Markdown, from the first paragraph
  (no H1, no sources list, no metadata).
- `=== SOURCES ===`: only sources given in the brief, with URL, date and the
  claim each supports.
- `=== FACTS NEEDED ===`: anything unconfirmed, or "none".
