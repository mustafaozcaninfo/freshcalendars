6. PW: the prize honors "the best English-language novel published in the past year". The livestream is on "the Booker Prizes' socials" (PW).
7. Time math (computed): on Nov. 9, 2026, London is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), 5 hours ahead of ET and 8 hours ahead of PT (U.S. clocks fell back Nov. 1, 2026). Example: last year's 9:30 p.m. GMT start would be 4:30 p.m. ET / 1:30 p.m. PT; do not present it as this year's time.
