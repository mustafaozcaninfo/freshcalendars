Fresh Calendars News is published by Fresh Calendars.

## News desk

Stories are written and edited by the [Fresh Calendars News Desk](/news/author/news-desk/), the editorial team of Fresh Calendars. The desk is responsible for choosing stories, checking dates against primary sources and publishing corrections.
