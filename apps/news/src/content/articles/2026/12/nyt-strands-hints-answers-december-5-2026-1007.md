**Strands #1007 from The New York Times (NYT) is the Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026 puzzle, and its clue is "Carry-all".** Christina Iverson constructed it and Tracy Bennett edited it. Every hint below points to a start cell on the 8-row, 6-column board without giving up a word. Today the spangram is a 14-letter phrase that spans the board sideways; our [Strands page](/news/topic/nyt-strands/#how-it-works) explains how spangrams work.

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: A three-word phrase for carrying on cheerfully when the going is slow.
- Hint 2: The words have 4, 2 and 8 letters. It begins with K and ends with G, and K, E and N each appear twice.
- Hint 3: Its first tile is row 5, column 2. It heads left, climbs, and crosses to column 6 before it ends in row 4, column 4.

## Theme word hints

Seven words answer the clue, listed shortest first and alphabetical within a length. Rows count from the top and columns from the left.

- **4 letters, starts with D, ends with P:** the kind of vehicle that tips its bed to unload gravel. It starts at row 1, column 2, and its letters are all different.
- **4 letters, starts with F, ends with E:** the kind of vehicle that races to a blaze. It starts at row 1, column 4.
- **4 letters, starts with F, ends with D:** the kind of vehicle that serves lunch from a window. It starts at row 3, column 4, and one letter repeats.
- **4 letters, starts with S, ends with I:** the kind of vehicle that pulls a trailer down the highway. It starts at row 2, column 2, and ends at row 3, column 1.
- **5 letters, starts with P, ends with L:** a van built with sides and no rear windows. It starts at row 6, column 6.
- **6 letters, starts with P, ends with P:** the kind with an open cargo bed behind the cab. It starts at row 6, column 2.
- **7 letters, starts with G, ends with E:** the kind that makes the rounds on collection day. It starts at row 8, column 4, and both G and A appear twice.

## How a sideways spangram walls off the Dec. 5 board

The spangram takes 13 steps, all straight and none diagonal: five to the right, three left, three up and two down. From row 5, column 2 it goes left to the edge, up to row 4, right to column 2, up to row 3 and right to column 3. It then drops to row 5, runs right to column 6, climbs to row 4 and finishes by stepping left to column 4.

Rows 4 and 5 are made only of spangram tiles, and row 3 has two more. The board is therefore cut into two separate pockets:

| Row | Spangram tiles | Theme tiles | Theme words in the row |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 6 | 3 |
| 2 | 0 | 6 | 4 |
| 3 | 2 | 4 | 2 |
| 4 | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| 5 | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| 6 | 0 | 6 | 3 |
| 7 | 0 | 6 | 3 |
| 8 | 0 | 6 | 3 |

What that means for the solve:

- The top pocket has 16 tiles and holds four words of 4 letters each. Any 4-letter find there leaves a neat gap for the next one.
- The bottom pocket has 18 tiles in three rows, and three words fill it: 5, 6 and 7 letters long.
- Columns 1 and 2 of rows 6 to 8 are a single six-letter word, a good place to start from the bottom edge.

## Solving tips

Skip the middle band at first. Rows 4 and 5 look like a pile of jumbled letters, but none of them belongs to a theme word, so a word you spot there is a dead end. Find the theme words in the top and bottom blocks and the middle clears itself. ## Today's Strands answers (spoilers)

- **Spangram:** Keep On Trucking
- **Theme words:** Dump, Fire, Food, Garbage, Panel, Pickup, Semi

All seven theme words are kinds of the vehicle the spangram's phrase refers to. Start and end cells, counted from row 1 and column 1:

| Word | Letters | Start cell | End cell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep On Trucking | 14 | row 5, column 2 | row 4, column 4 |
| Dump | 4 | row 1, column 2 | row 1, column 3 |
| Fire | 4 | row 1, column 4 | row 2, column 5 |
| Food | 4 | row 3, column 4 | row 2, column 6 |
| Semi | 4 | row 2, column 2 | row 3, column 1 |
| Panel | 5 | row 6, column 6 | row 7, column 5 |
| Pickup | 6 | row 6, column 2 | row 6, column 1 |

Semi holds the top-left corner by bending around row 1, column 1. Dump and Fire share row 1 side by side, and Food comes down from the right edge. Pickup fills the bottom-left corner, Garbage sits in the bottom middle and Panel takes the bottom-right.
