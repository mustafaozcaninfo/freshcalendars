The top row of the opening board holds four tiles from four different groups, so the first row tells you nothing about how they pair up. Four tiles on the board can pass for parts of a plant, which is the trap to keep in mind while you solve.

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: What you do to a book's pages when you want to find your spot quickly and not read every line.
- Hint 2: The entries run 4, 4, 4 and 5 letters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Think of what pushes up from damp ground in a forest after rain and can end up on a pizza.
- Hint 2: The entries run 3, 5, 6 and 4 letters.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: One shared word could follow each entry, and that word is something a lamp or a signal gives off.
- Hint 2: The entries run 3, 5, 7 and 2 letters, and the 2-letter one is a pair of capitals.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Each entry is a word with its first letter chopped off, and the missing letters belong to hunters with whiskers.
- Hint 2: The entries run 6, 4, 3 and 3 letters, and two of the missing letters are the same.

## Solving tips

Read the 4-letter tiles as a mixed bag rather than a set; they land in three different places. Settle the group with the odd-looking fragments first, since a fragment that is not a real word on its own has only one job.

## Why Leaf, Stem, Bud and Anther mislead on Dec. 7

Leaf, Stem, Bud and Anther all name parts of a plant, so a solver who spots one will hunt for the other three. No plant group exists. Each of the four goes to a different color, and the starting spots (1, 3, 8 and 6) keep them scattered, so the eye never lands on all four together.

| Tile | Letters | Position | Tempting pull | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leaf | 4 | 1 | A plant part, paired with Stem | Riffle (Through), since you leaf through a book |
| Stem | 4 | 3 | A plant part, paired with Leaf | Parts of a Mushroom, the stalk under the cap |
| Bud | 3 | 8 | A plant part, paired with Anther | ___ Light, as in Bud Light |
| Anther | 6 | 6 | A flower part, paired with Bud | Big Cats Minus First Letter, Panther without the P |
| Thumb | 5 | 4 | A body part, not a plant part | Riffle (Through), thumb through a book |
| Cap | 3 | 5 | A hat or bottle top | Parts of a Mushroom, the top of the fungus |

Length confuses things further. Five tiles have four letters: Flip, Leaf and Skim sit in Riffle (Through), Stem in Parts of a Mushroom and Iger in Big Cats Minus First Letter. The Big Cats group is the only one with a shape rule, since each entry is a real cat missing its first letter (P, T, L and P).

A safe route:

1. **Big Cats Minus First Letter.** Anther, Iger, Ion and Uma are not words until a letter goes on the front.
2. **___ Light.** Bud, Pilot, Traffic and UV all take the same word after them.
3. **Parts of a Mushroom.** Cap, Gills, Spores and Stem are what is left when the plant words are cleared away.
4. **Riffle (Through).** Flip, Leaf, Skim and Thumb are the four ways to move through pages.
