## Solving tips

Two of the four groups are close cousins, so a tile that feels certain may belong to the other one. Start with the group whose label is a phrase with a gap, since it does not overlap with anything else on the board.

## Why each decoy nearly fits on Dec. 6

The pull comes from two directions: the mail-related words and the words for a signature quirk lean on each other, and the gossip words lean toward the written ones. Here is each near-fit, entry by entry.

| Tile | Tempting group | Where it lands | Why it does not fit the other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stamp | Written Message, since a stamp goes on mail | Identifying Trait | A stamp is a mark that shows who made something, not the message |
| Signature | Written Message, since a signature ends a note | Identifying Trait | It is the sender's own mark, like Hallmark, not text that carries news |
| Calling Card | Written Message, since a card is left for someone | Identifying Trait | The label asks for a trait of a person, and a calling card is a signature touch |
| Address | Written Message, since it is written on an envelope or means a speech | Street ___ | Street address is a phrase, and the other three entries (Fighter, Legal, Smarts) take the same front word |
| Dispatch | Places TO Get The Tea, since newspapers use the word in their names | Written Message | A dispatch is a sent report, the same job as Missive |
| Tabloid | Written Message, since it is printed | Places TO Get The Tea | A tabloid is read for gossip, not sent to one person |
| Grapevine | Written Message, since it passes information along | Places TO Get The Tea | It spreads news by mouth, so it is a spot for gossip |
| Communication | Places TO Get The Tea, since it is a broad word for passing news | Written Message | Nothing in the gossip group is a general word, while Letter, Dispatch and Missive are all sent messages |

Two groups are loose: Written Message and Identifying Trait can both claim Stamp, Signature or Calling Card, so settle them together. Teapot is the one tile with no pull in either direction, since it is the literal source of tea, which makes it the first tile to place.

A safe route:

1. **Places TO Get The Tea.** Teapot is the anchor, and Grapevine, Tabloid and Water Cooler are where the gossip travels.
2. **Street ___.** Address, Fighter, Legal and Smarts all follow the same front word.
3. **Identifying Trait.** Calling Card, Hallmark, Signature and Stamp are what a person or a maker leaves behind.
4. **Written Message.** Communication, Dispatch, Letter and Missive are what remains.
