## Guessing with a double letter in mind

A word with one repeated letter has only four different letters, so a first guess of five distinct letters can learn less than it seems. With a single vowel letter in play, a starter heavy in vowels will turn up mostly yellow and gray. Aim your second guess at the consonants.
