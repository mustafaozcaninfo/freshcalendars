## One-cell regions on the Dec. 4 Pips boards

Hard asks for a sum in 12 of its 17 regions and has no empty cell. Of those sums, eight are one-cell, and one of them totals 0. Hard also holds four one-cell sums of 4, and the set of 16 tiles carries exactly four 4s, so every 4 on that board lands on one of those four cells. No board uses the unequal rule.

## The forced first move on each Dec. 4 board

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left.

**Easy.** R1C3 and R1C6 each add up to 3, and exactly two tiles in the set carry a 3, 3-1 and 3-6. Row 1 holds no other cells, so those two tiles stand upright, one under each of the two cells. The pairs R2C3 with R2C2 and R2C5 with R2C6 are equal, which then settles what sits beside them. A search of the saved data finds a single arrangement for the board.

**Medium.** R5C4 is the only cell in row 5 and touches only R4C4, and the two sit in a region of three equal cells. A tile with the same number on both halves is needed, and 3-3 is the only double in Medium's set, so it covers R4C4 and R5C4 before anything else is decided. R4C3 then also holds a 3.

**Hard.** R6C2 adds up to 0, so it holds a 0. Its left neighbor R6C1 is less than 2, which would need a 0-0 or 0-1 tile, and its right neighbor R6C3 adds up to 5, which would need 0-5. The set has none of those three, so the 0 tile runs up or down column 2. In all eight arrangements the tile on R6C2 is 0-3, with the 3 below it.

## Pips hints for Dec. 4, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 3-1, 5-2, 0-1, 2-6, 3-6)**

- Hint 1: R1C3 and R1C6 both add up to 3, and only two tiles in the list carry a 3.
- Hint 2: R2C1 has a single neighbor, and that neighbor must equal R2C3.

**Medium (eight dominoes: 2-1, 4-3, 6-4, 0-4, 6-3, 3-3, 2-6, 3-5)**

- Hint 1: R5C4 has one neighbor and sits in a region of equal cells, so look for the one double in the list.
- Hint 2: R4C1 is less than 2, so it holds a 0 or a 1, and only 2-1 and 0-4 carry one.

**Hard (sixteen dominoes: 0-3, 2-1, 2-2, 6-3, 6-2, 4-3, 5-5, 1-4, 2-5, 2-0, 1-1, 4-5, 3-3, 2-4, 3-1, 6-0)**

- Hint 1: R6C2 adds up to 0, so it holds a 0, and three tiles in the list carry one.
- Hint 2: R1C3 adds up to 4 and touches only R2C3, which together with R3C3 adds up to 5.
