## Which letter pairs make Tuesday's box hard?

The box allows 108 ordered steps between letters on different sides. In the 531 words, 29 of them never happen, and 4 more happen in exactly one word each: T to P, U to Z, O to F and O to Z.

The Z is the trouble. Eight of its nine possible exits are dead, so a Z inside a word is always followed by an O. Getting in is no easier: T leads to it in 9 words, L in 3, and U and O in one each. The X is friendlier but still narrow, entered only from O (14 words) or I (9 words).

The S runs the other way. It sits in 339 words and ends 242 of them, nearly half the list. Only 3 words end in an F.

The thinnest link is L/T/F to X/U/P at 183 words, and X/U/P to O/S/R is the widest at 316.

## How many two-word solutions does the dictionary allow?

One. A pair counts when the second word starts on the last letter of the first and the two together use all 12 letters. In this list only the Times' own pair does that, at 13 letters in all.

Par is 6, so the Times' route finishes four words under it. A route of three words has more room: 137 chains of three words in the list cover every letter. Nothing in the list gives a second pair to pick from, so a hunt for a different two-word answer ends where it started.

The list has 531 words, and the 3-letter and 4-letter words make up 247 of them. That is the dictionary the Times supplied with the puzzle; any word outside it is refused by the game.

## What is the longest word in the list?

Three words reach 10 letters and 12 more have 9. Length does not help, though. The best any word does is touch 7 of the 12 letters, and 13 words get there. No word reaches 8, so a one-word answer cannot exist today.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, four fewer than the par of 6.
2. The first word has 7 letters and the second has 6. The list holds 47 words of 7 letters and 76 of 6.
3. Each word visits all four sides. The second word opens on the side where the first one ended.
4. The first word leaves out P, R, I, T and Z. The second word brings all five.
5. The first word starts with B and ends in S. The second starts with S and ends in Z.
