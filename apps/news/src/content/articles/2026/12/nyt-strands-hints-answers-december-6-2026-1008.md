**Strands #1008 from The New York Times (NYT) is the Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026 puzzle, and its clue is "Power boost".** Tracy Bennett built and edited it. The hints below name a start cell on the 8-row, 6-column board and never a word. Today's spangram is a two-word phrase of 14 letters laid out as a staircase; the rules sit on our [Strands page](/news/topic/nyt-strands/#how-it-works).

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: A two-word phrase for climbing the social or economic ladder.
- Hint 2: The words have 6 and 8 letters. It begins with U and ends with Y, and I appears twice.
- Hint 3: Its first tile is row 7, column 1. It alternates a step right with a step higher until it reaches the top-right corner, then turns left and drops one to end in row 2, column 5.

## Theme word hints

Five words answer the clue, listed shortest first and alphabetical within a length. Rows count from the top and columns from the left.

- **4 letters, starts with L, ends with T:** a free ride offered to someone waiting at a bus stop, or what a crane does to a steel beam. It starts at row 1, column 4, and its letters are all different.
- **7 letters, starts with A, ends with E:** to go forward. It starts at row 8, column 1, and A appears twice.
- **7 letters, starts with E, ends with E:** to bring to a higher level. It starts at row 3, column 2, and E appears three times.
- **7 letters, starts with P, ends with E:** to give a bigger title at work, or to publicize a product. It starts at row 4, column 3, and O appears twice.
- **9 letters, starts with R, ends with D:** to put in a good word for a candidate. It starts at row 8, column 4, and both E and M appear twice.

## A staircase spangram across the Dec. 6 board

The spangram takes 13 steps, all straight: five to the right, six higher, one to the left and one down. From row 7, column 1 it goes right to column 2, then alternates higher and right until it reaches column 6 in row 3. It climbs two more rows to row 1, turns left to column 5 and drops to row 2, column 5.

| Row | Spangram tiles | Spangram columns | Theme tiles |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 5, 6 | 4 |
| 2 | 2 | 5, 6 | 4 |
| 3 | 2 | 5, 6 | 4 |
| 4 | 2 | 4, 5 | 4 |
| 5 | 2 | 3, 4 | 4 |
| 6 | 2 | 2, 3 | 4 |
| 7 | 2 | 1, 2 | 4 |
| 8 | 0 | none | 6 |

What that means for the solve:

- The staircase cuts the board on a diagonal. The upper left holds 18 tiles in three words, and the lower right holds 16 tiles in two.
- The spangram occupies the top-right corner, so the corner your eye goes to first is not a theme word.
- Column 1 has seven theme tiles and one spangram tile. Column 6 is split three to five, and column 5 holds four spangram tiles, more than any other column.
- Row 8 is clear of the spangram. Its six tiles belong to two words, three each, so the bottom edge is a good place to begin.

## Solving tips

Work from the two corners the spangram leaves alone, the bottom edge and the left edge. The 4-letter word sits in the top row to the left of the spangram's top, and it is the quickest to confirm. - **Spangram:** Upward Mobility
- **Theme words:** Advance, "Elevate", Lift, Promote, Recommend

Every theme word is a way to boost someone or something. Start and end cells, counted from row 1 and column 1:

| Word | Letters | Start cell | End cell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upward Mobility | 14 | row 7, column 1 | row 2, column 5 |
| Lift | 4 | row 1, column 4 | row 1, column 3 |
| Advance | 7 | row 8, column 1 | row 6, column 5 |
| "Elevate" | 7 | row 3, column 2 | row 3, column 1 |
| Promote | 7 | row 4, column 3 | row 2, column 1 |
| Recommend | 9 | row 8, column 4 | row 5, column 5 |

Lift fills the top row beside the spangram, and Promote wraps the top-left corner. "Elevate" runs down column 2, then turns at row 6 and climbs column 1. Advance holds the bottom-left corner and Recommend takes the bottom-right.
