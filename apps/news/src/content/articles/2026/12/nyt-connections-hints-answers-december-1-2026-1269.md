**Connections #1269 from The New York Times (NYT) is the Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026 puzzle, edited by Wyna Liu.** Eleven of today's tiles have three letters or fewer, among them one for a digital video recorder (DVR) and one printed as the letters (NME), and the rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works). Two hints per color come first, then the solution below a spoiler line.

## Today's 16 words

Bean, CD, Chi, DVR, LP, Mo, NME, NV, PC, QT, Sequin, Tablet, Tinsel, TV, Washer, XL

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Think of gadgets that get plugged in or charged somewhere around a house.
- Hint 2: The entries have three, two, six and two letters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Picture each one lying flat on a table with an empty gap through its center.
- Hint 2: The entries have two, two, six and six letters.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Read each tile out loud one letter at a time, and listen for a plain word.
- Hint 2: The entries have three, two, two and two letters.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Put the same short word after each tile and you get a city's nickname.
- Hint 2: The entries have four, three, two and six letters.

## Red herrings on the Dec. 1 board

Seven of the 16 tiles point toward a group they do not belong to. Three of them pull by meaning and four pull by looks, because nine tiles are short letter strings and several sit next to each other. The tile-by-tile explanation is below the answers, so nothing is given away here.

## Solving tips

With nine short letter strings, spelling is a weak guide. Sort by what each tile is or does before you sort by how it looks, and start with the tiles that have only one sensible home.
