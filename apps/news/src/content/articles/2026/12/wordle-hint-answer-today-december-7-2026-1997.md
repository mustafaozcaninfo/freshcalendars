1. **Part of speech:** a noun.
2. **Meaning:** a small electronic part that lets current run in one direction and blocks it in the other.
3. **Vowels:** three vowel letters, and they are not all together.
4. **Repeated letters:** one letter shows twice, in the first tile and the fourth.
5. **First letter:** D. The last letter is E.

## Planning around the split pair

Three vowels is a lot for five tiles, so a guess made of consonants will leave most of the board gray. A repeat with a gap is easier to overlook than a side-by-side pair: a gray tile can mean you found the first copy and the second is still hidden.
