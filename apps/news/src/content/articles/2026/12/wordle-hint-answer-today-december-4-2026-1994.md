## Solving notes for a crowded ending

Once the last four tiles turn green, you can be left with one open tile and a long list of words that fit. A guess that cannot be the answer is sometimes the better move.

Count your remaining tries before you commit. With three or more left, spend one on a word that covers several candidates at once. With one or two left, pick the likeliest word.
