## Which tiles nearly fit a second group on Dec. 3

Only three tiles have a convincing pull toward a group that is not theirs, and each is undone by one detail.

| Tile | Where it tempts you | Where it lands | What rules the other group out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Washington | State Anagrams, because a state name sits on the board | Seen on a U.S. Dollar Bill | The anagram group holds scrambled states. Washington is spelled plainly, and its scrambled twin is Saw Nothing |
| Scramble | State Anagrams, because scrambling is what an anagram is | Ways to Prepare an Egg | It is spelled as a normal word, so nothing in it rearranges into a state |
| Taxes | Seen on a U.S. Dollar Bill, because money brings taxes to mind | State Anagrams | Taxes is not on a bill, and its letters rearrange into Texas |

Washington is the one that looks odd once solved. Its letters make Saw Nothing, so the board holds a state in plain form and the same state in disguise, and each sits in a different group.

A safe route:

1. **Ways to Prepare an Egg.** Boil, Devil, Fry and Scramble all describe a way to cook an egg, and Scramble can be set aside as the one that only looks like an anagram clue.
2. **Kinds of Dogs, Informally.** Chow, Doodle, Lab and Pit are the short names for dog types.
3. **Seen on a U.S. Dollar Bill.** Eagle, Eye, Pyramid and Washington remain once the dog and egg tiles are gone.
4. **State Anagrams.** Anime, Ran Madly, Saw Nothing and Taxes are what is left, and each rearranges into a state.
