**Connections #1270 from The New York Times (NYT) is the Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026 puzzle, edited by Wyna Liu.** Four tiles stretch to eight letters, none is shorter than four, and the rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works). Two hints per color come first, then the solution below a spoiler line.

## Today's 16 words

Again, Anew, Birdbath, Balloon, Basket, Burner, Hammock, Hose, Lotion, Once More, Over, Parcells, Plotz, Sandbags, Shed, Tractor

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Each entry is a way to ask for one more attempt.
- Hint 2: The entries have five, four, eight and four letters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Look out a back window and picture what stands on the lawn.
- Hint 2: The entries have eight, seven, four and four letters.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Every entry belongs to something that floats above the ground.
- Hint 2: The entries have seven, six, six and eight letters.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Each tile opens with a word you would find on a property survey.
- Hint 2: The land word at the start has three, six, four or five letters.

## Solving tips

Start from the tile with the fewest possible homes, which is the one that reads as a plain request to redo something. Then ask of each remaining tile whether the whole word or only its first few letters carries the meaning.

## Why each trap on Dec. 2 nearly fits

Five tiles outside the green group can pass for something seen in a yard, so the yard pool holds nine tiles.

| Tile | What pulls you | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| Burner | A gas burner sits on many a patio grill | Parts of a Hot Air Balloon |
| Sandbags | Stacked sandbags can line a lawn or a garden bed | Parts of a Hot Air Balloon |
| Balloon | A party balloon can float over a lawn | Parts of a Hot Air Balloon |
| Tractor | A tractor can be parked in a farmyard | Starting With Pieces of Land |
| Plotz | A plot is also a patch of garden, so it nudges you toward the yard | Starting With Pieces of Land |

Balloon, the word that names the group itself, is the hardest to trust: it matches the group title, so it feels too easy to be in it.

### The arithmetic of a wrong guess

Sixteen tiles allow 1,820 different picks of four, and exactly 4 of them are real groups. Take the nine yard-flavored tiles alone: they allow 126 picks of four, and only 1 is the real yard group. Twenty of those picks hold three yard tiles plus one intruder, which is the "one away" result in the game, and the other 105 are further off. The tester above counts none of them as a mistake.

A safe route:

1. **From the Top.** Again, Anew, Once More and Over share one meaning and no other group wants them.
2. **Starting With Pieces of Land.** Lotion, Parcells, Plotz and Tractor each open with Lot, Parcel, Plot or Tract, which removes Tractor and Plotz from the yard pool.
3. **Parts of a Hot Air Balloon.** Balloon, Basket, Burner and Sandbags remain, all pieces of one craft.
4. **Things Seen in a Yard.** Birdbath, Hammock, Hose and Shed finish the board.
