Only one tile is as short as 3 letters, the automated teller machine (ATM), and our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works) covers the rules for anyone new. ## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Picture someone on tiptoe going for a high shelf; each entry is a way of pushing past a comfortable limit.
- Hint 2: The entries run 5, 5, 6 and 7 letters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Press a digit on any of these and something happens: cash, a total, a floor or a call.
- Hint 2: The entries run 3, 10, 8 and 9 letters, and the 3-letter one is an abbreviation.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: These are steps you take at the counter before the first sip of the morning.
- Hint 2: The entries run 4, 5, 7 and 4 letters, and the two 4-letter tiles on the board both belong here.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Each entry opens with something that sits on a dinner table, while the rest of the word has nothing to do with eating.
- Hint 2: The entries run 5, 8, 7 and 10 letters, and the longest one ties for the biggest tile on the board.

## Solving tips

Start with the group that has the most letters per tile: the long tiles are rarely generic words, and a 10-letter tile usually has a narrow job. Then deal with any word that works as both a noun and a verb, since those tiles are where one-away mistakes pile up. If you hold a trio and the fourth will not come, test the group with the tester above before spending a try.

Every red herring on this board has a second reading that lands in a group it does not join. The table checks each against the actual groups.

Elevator, Crane and Forklift are the strongest decoy on the board: a machine trio with no group behind it. Cupid and Measure pull on the coffee theme from the outside, which tempts a solver to build a mug-themed set that does not exist.

A safe route:

1. **Starting with Tableware.** Cupid, Forklift, Plateau and Spoonerism are the only tiles that open with a utensil or dish.
2. **Things with Numbered Buttons.** ATM, Calculator, Elevator and Telephone are the objects with a keypad.
3. **Coffee-Making Verbs.** Brew, Grind, Measure and Pour are the steps left once the keypad items are gone.
4. **Extend with Effort.** Crane, Reach, Strain and Stretch are the four leftover verbs.
