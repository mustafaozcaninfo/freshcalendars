## How far are the four-letter words from Genius?

The 15 four-letter answers are worth 15 points, under 8% of the 198 needed. The other 183 points must come from the 43 answers of five letters or more, which hold 268 between them. Fifteen five-letter answers lift the count to 90, the sixes to 168, and then the seven-letter words do the rest: five of them cover the missing 30. Wednesday's list had 20 four-letter answers and a bar of 106; Thursday has 5 fewer short answers and a bar 92 points higher.
