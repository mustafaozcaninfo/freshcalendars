## Solving tips

Animal words crowd this grid, so sort them before you commit to anything. Ask what each one could be besides an animal: a body part, a verb, a nickname, a toy. Then look for the group that has a single obvious tile, since one fixed point shrinks the rest. If three tiles agree and a fourth is only a guess, run the group through the tester above before it costs a try.
