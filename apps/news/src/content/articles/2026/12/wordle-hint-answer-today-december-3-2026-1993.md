## Solving notes for a repeat-heavy word

With only three different letters in five tiles, a guess built from five new letters will waste tries. Fill the tiles you already know and let the repeats do the work.

A gray tile can mislead when a letter repeats, because the game grays the extra copy of a letter that sits in the word fewer times than you typed it.
