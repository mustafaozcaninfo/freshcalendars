## Solving tips

Band names are the loudest idea on this board, so count how many tiles can be one before you place any. If more than four can, the extra ones are guarding another group. Also read each tile backward from its end, a habit that pays off when a group depends on how a word finishes. When two ideas collide, run four tiles through the tester above before they cost a mistake.

Fruit endings are the hardest trap: Peplum, Minifig, Disappear and Sublime look unrelated until you read their last letters. Sublime is the double bluff, since it fits the band idea and the fruit idea, but only one of those is a group.

A safe route:

1. **Expressions of Agreement.** Indeed, No Doubt, Sure and Totally each answer a question with yes.
