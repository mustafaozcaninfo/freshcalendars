## Why the decoys pull on Dec. 4

The board is built so that a few tiles can sit in two places, and every one of them is settled by a single detail.

| Tile | Where it tempts you | Where it lands | What settles it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rank | Taxonomic Ranks, since a rank is a level in biological classification | Stinky | Here it means smelling bad, the same sense as Foul |
| Rating | Taxonomic Ranks, because it reads as a grade or standing | Movie Listing Info | A rating is a line on a film listing, like Cast and Runtime |
| Class | Movie Listing Info, next to Rating, since both read as a grade or a standing | Taxonomic Ranks | It is a level of classification, with Order and Kingdom |
| Order | Same pull toward grade or standing | Taxonomic Ranks | Same level of classification as Class and Family |
| Family | Movie Listing Info, since "family film" is a common label | Taxonomic Ranks | It is a level of classification between order and genus |
| Cast | Homophones, since to cast is to throw and Thrown is on the board | Movie Listing Info | Cast is the actors on a listing, and Thrown only works by sound |
| Ripe | Funky, since both can describe a strong smell | Stinky | Both belong to the same group, so the pull is real but harmless |

Rank is the tile that causes the most trouble: it is the one entry that could fill a slot in the blue group with its own meaning and still has a second life in the yellow one. Four tiles can read as a standing (Rating, Class, Order, Rank), and they split across three different groups.

A safe route:

1. **Homophones of Slang for Toilet.** Cannes (can), Jawn (john), Lieu (loo) and Thrown (throne) are the only tiles that need to be said aloud, so lock them in first.
2. **Movie Listing Info.** Cast, Rating, Runtime and Synopsis are what a listing prints under a title.
3. **Stinky.** Foul, Funky, Rank and Ripe remain among the smell words once the film tiles are gone.
4. **Taxonomic Ranks.** Class, Family, Kingdom and Order are what is left.
