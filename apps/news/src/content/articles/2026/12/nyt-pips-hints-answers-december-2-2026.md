## Where to place the first domino on each board

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left.

**Easy.** R4C3 and R5C3 add up to 8, and no tile in the set totals 8 (the sums run 0, 5, 9, 2 and 4), so each cell carries its own 4. R5C3 touches R4C3 and R5C4 only, and R4C3 shares its region, which leaves R5C4. The first move is a sideways tile on R5C3 and R5C4, a 4 on the left and something under 5 on the right.

**Medium.** R4C6 has one neighbor, R3C6, and the two cells must be equal. A tile with the same number twice is a double, and the set has only one, 0-0. That tile goes on R3C6 and R4C6, and a search finds every other cell settled too: Medium has a single arrangement.

**Hard.** R8C4 touches only R7C4, and both sit in the four-cell equal region. A double must cover them, and the set holds three: 3-3, 6-6 and 2-2. R7C5 then has only R6C5 left to pair with, and R6C5 adds up to 5, so a 5 sits next to whichever number the double carries.

## Pips hints for Dec. 2, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 0-0, 4-1, 4-5, 0-2, 0-4)**

- Hint 1: R4C3 and R5C3 add up to 8 across two tiles, so look for the 4s.
- Hint 2: R1C4 is more than 1, so the 0-0 double never reaches it.

**Medium (eight dominoes: 2-4, 4-5, 4-0, 0-0, 6-1, 5-3, 2-6, 1-2)**

- Hint 1: R1C3, R2C5 and R3C3 each add up to 4 alone, and exactly three tiles carry a 4.
- Hint 2: R3C4 and R3C5 total more than 9, so at least one of them holds a 5 or a 6.

**Hard (sixteen dominoes: 2-0, 5-1, 4-2, 3-3, 6-4, 0-1, 1-3, 2-3, 2-6, 5-4, 6-6, 5-6, 3-5, 5-2, 2-2, 2-1)**

- Hint 1: R1C4 is a dead end, so its tile runs down to R2C4, and the pair totals more than 10, which only 5-6 or 6-6 can do.
- Hint 2: R5C4, R5C5 and R6C4 total less than 3, so no cell in that region holds more than 2.

## Which Pips dominoes turn around on Dec. 2

Easy and Medium have no tile that can turn end for end alone; Easy's three arrangements come from tiles taking different spots. Hard has six that can reverse without breaking a region: 5-1, 0-1, 5-4, 5-6, 3-5 and 2-1. The placements below print the Times' own data for each tile.
