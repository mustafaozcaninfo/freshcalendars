**Paramount Press Express, the official Paramount press site, says the fourth and final season of "Yellowjackets" returns to Paramount+ with the Premium Plan on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, three weeks after "Dexter: Resurrection" season 2 returned on Friday, Oct. 30.** The table below lists each dated November title with the plan it needs, and a free printable calendar follows it.

<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 28, 2026.</b> Paramount has not published a full November 2026 list, so this covers only the titles with a date in its releases. Paramount's releases give no start time for any streaming drop, so none appear here.</p>

## What is new on Paramount+ in November 2026?

Every row comes from a Paramount Press Express release, with the weekday computed from the date.

| Date | Title | Episodes at launch | Plan stated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 30 | Dexter: Resurrection, season 2 | Not stated | Not stated |
| Fri., Nov. 6 | It Gets Worse | All six | Premium Plan only in the U.S. |
| Tue., Nov. 17 | He Said, She Said: Till Death Do Us Part | All three | Not stated |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | Yellowjackets, final season | Not announced | Premium Plan |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | The JLo Show | One special on CBS | Premium to stream live |

## When does Yellowjackets season 4 come out?

In its Oct. 14, 2026 release, "Paramount+ Releases Final Season Teaser for Emmy-Nominated Yellowjackets," Paramount Press Express says: "The series returns to Paramount+ with the Premium Plan on Friday, November 20." This is the fourth and final season. Paramount has not announced the episode count or whether episodes arrive weekly or all at once, so this story does not guess a finale date.

## When is the Dexter: Resurrection season 2 release date?

The release headline reads: "He's Back and Sharper Than Ever: The Official Dexter: Resurrection Season 2 Trailer Is Here; Series Returns Friday, Oct. 30 on Paramount+." The release does not state an episode count or release pattern, and it names no plan, so none is listed here.

## What else is dated in November?

"It Gets Worse" is a new comedy series created by and starring Leo Reich. Paramount's Oct. 8, 2026 release says it "will be available to stream on November 6 on Paramount+ only on the Premium Plan in the U.S." and calls it "a new six-episode comedy series." All six episodes are available Nov. 6.

"He Said, She Said: Till Death Do Us Part" is a three-part documentary from See It Now Studios. Paramount's Oct. 20, 2026 release says it "premieres on Tuesday, November 17, exclusively on Paramount+ in the U.S. and Canada" and that "All 3 episodes will be available to stream at launch." The release does not name a plan.

## When is The JLo Show on Thanksgiving?

Paramount's Sept. 17, 2026 release says "The JLo Show," a Jennifer Lopez Las Vegas residency concert special, "will premiere Thanksgiving evening, Thursday, Nov. 26 (8:00-10:00 PM, ET/PT)" on CBS. That is 8 to 10 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and Pacific Time (PT). "Paramount+ Premium plan subscribers will have access to stream live," while "Paramount+ Essential subscribers will not have the option to stream live." An extended version is available exclusively on Paramount+. For the holiday date itself, see [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

## Which Paramount+ plan do you need?

Three of the five titles name the Premium Plan in the releases: "It Gets Worse," "Yellowjackets" and the live stream of "The JLo Show." The Dexter and "He Said, She Said" releases name no plan, so check the show page before you count on Essential. The paramountplus.com homepage, checked Oct. 28, 2026, lists Paramount+ Essential at $8.99 a month and Paramount+ Premium at $13.99 a month. No annual prices were stated.

## What is not announced yet?

Paramount has not published a full November 2026 list. Not yet announced: other November titles, the Yellowjackets episode count and release pattern, start times for the streaming drops, and the live sports schedule on Paramount+. One date falls in the next month: Paramount's release dated Oct. 15, 2026 says "Frisco King" premieres Dec. 18.
