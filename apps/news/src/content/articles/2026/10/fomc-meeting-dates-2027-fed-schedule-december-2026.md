**The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) has eight tentative meetings on its revised 2027 calendar, from Jan. 26-27 to Nov. 16-17**, according to the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, which announced the change on Nov. 12, 2026. The December 2027 meeting (Dec. 7-8) that the Fed listed before is gone. Next up is the Dec. 8-9, 2026 meeting, which is associated with a Summary of Economic Projections (SEP).

The FOMC statement released at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Oct. 28, 2026 kept the federal funds target range at 3-3/4 to 4 percent. Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack voted against the action; the Fed says both preferred to raise the range by 1/4 percentage point. The next decision is Wednesday, Dec. 9, 2026.

## What are the FOMC meeting dates for 2027?

The Fed first published the 2027 schedule on Sept. 5, 2025, then revised it on Nov. 12, 2026. The revision adds a Nov. 16-17 meeting, drops Dec. 7-8 and sets aside Oct. 26-27 for broad monetary policy strategy issues. The Fed says it will announce its policy decision as usual on the second day of each meeting, except Oct. 26-27. It also lists Jan. 25-26, 2028. The Board's calendar states that "each meeting date is tentative until confirmed at the meeting immediately preceding it." In 2027 each decision day is a Wednesday. The last column is our calculation from the Fed's three-week rule for minutes: the decision day plus 21 days.

| Meeting | Decision day | Minutes expected (our calculation) |
|---|---|---|
| Jan. 26-27 | Wed., Jan. 27 | Wed., Feb. 17 |
| March 16-17 | Wed., March 17 | Wed., April 7 |
| April 27-28 | Wed., April 28 | Wed., May 19 |
| June 8-9 | Wed., June 9 | Wed., June 30 |
| July 27-28 | Wed., July 28 | Wed., Aug. 18 |
| Sept. 14-15 | Wed., Sept. 15 | Wed., Oct. 6 |
| Oct. 26-27 | None, strategy meeting | Not announced |
| Nov. 16-17 | Wed., Nov. 17 | Wed., Dec. 8 |

Source for meeting dates: Federal Reserve Board press release of Nov. 12, 2026 and FOMC meeting calendar. The revised schedule does not say which 2027 meetings go with a Summary of Economic Projections, and the Fed's calendar shows no asterisk on any 2027 meeting yet, so we give none. The printable at the end of the story lists the seven decision days on one page, and you can also mark them on a [2027 printable yearly calendar](/printable-yearly-calendar.html).

## When is the next FOMC meeting after October 2026?

The Oct. 27-28, 2026 meeting, which had no SEP, is over. The Fed's calendar shows **Dec. 8-9, 2026 with one**. The most recent meeting with an SEP was Sept. 15-16, 2026, and its minutes were released on Oct. 7, 2026.

| 2026 meeting | Decision day | SEP | Minutes expected (our calculation) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct. 27-28 | Wed., Oct. 28 | No | Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Dec. 8-9 | Wed., Dec. 9 | Yes | Wed., Dec. 30 |

The December minutes land in the last week of the year. Put the decision day on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) or in a [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) if you plan around it.

## What time is an FOMC decision in other time zones?

FOMC statements carry the line "For release at 2:00 p.m. EDT", as the [Sept. 16, 2026 statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm) does. So statements have been released at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the meeting's second day. We computed each conversion below with a time zone calculation; the conversions are ours, not the Fed's.

| Decision day | 2:00 p.m. Eastern | Pacific | London |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wed., Oct. 28, 2026 | EDT | 11 a.m. PDT | 6 p.m. GMT |
| Wed., Dec. 9, 2026 | EST | 11 a.m. PST | 7 p.m. GMT |
| Wed., Jan. 27, 2027 | EST | 11 a.m. PST | 7 p.m. GMT |
| Wed., March 17, 2027 | EDT | 11 a.m. PDT | 6 p.m. GMT |
| Wed., April 28, 2027 | EDT | 11 a.m. PDT | 7 p.m. BST |
| Wed., June 9, 2027 | EDT | 11 a.m. PDT | 7 p.m. BST |
| Wed., July 28, 2027 | EDT | 11 a.m. PDT | 7 p.m. BST |
| Wed., Sept. 15, 2027 | EDT | 11 a.m. PDT | 7 p.m. BST |
| Wed., Nov. 17, 2027 | EST | 11 a.m. PST | 7 p.m. GMT |

Two dates fall in weeks when the United States and the United Kingdom are on different clocks. The U.S. is on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) from March 14, 2027, while the UK stays on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) until March 28, so 2:00 p.m. EDT on March 17 is 6 p.m. in London, not 7 p.m. Oct. 28, 2026 shows the same gap in the table: the UK is already on GMT while the U.S. is still on EDT.

## How are FOMC minutes timed?

The Fed's calendar says: "The minutes of regularly scheduled meetings are released three weeks after the date of the policy decision." That puts every minutes release on a Wednesday as well in 2027, since 21 days is exactly three weeks. The dates in the tables are our arithmetic, not dates the Fed has announced.
