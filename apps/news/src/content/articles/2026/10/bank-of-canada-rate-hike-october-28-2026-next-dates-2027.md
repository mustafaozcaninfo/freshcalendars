**On Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, at 9:45 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), the Bank of Canada raised its target for the overnight rate by 25 basis points to 2.5%**, according to its press release datelined Ottawa. The Bank Rate is 2.75% and the deposit rate is 2.45%. The next scheduled announcement is Wednesday, Dec. 9, 2026. This story adds a table of every 2026 and 2027 decision, local times for Canadian time zones (our conversion) and a one-page printable.

## What did the Bank of Canada decide on Oct. 28, 2026?

The release says the Bank "today increased its target for the overnight rate by 25 basis points to 2.5%, with the Bank Rate at 2.75% and the deposit rate at 2.45%." The Bank's key interest rate page says a change takes effect the day after its announcement, so the new rate applies from Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026.

On the reason, the release says Governing Council "decided to increase the policy rate to reduce the risk that continuing high energy prices spread broadly to the prices of other goods and services." It adds: "There could be a need to increase the policy rate further, but given uncertainties, Governing Council agreed to proceed carefully."

The October 2026 Monetary Policy Report (MPR) was released with the decision. Governor Tiff Macklem and Senior Deputy Governor Carolyn Rogers held a press conference at about 10:30 a.m. ET, according to the Bank.

## What did the Bank say about inflation and growth?

| Measure | What the Bank said on Oct. 28, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Consumer price index (CPI) inflation | Around 3% since April; expected to rise to about 3½% by the end of the year |
| CPI inflation excluding gasoline | 2.2% in September |
| CPI inflation outlook | Expected to average 3% in 2027, then come down to 2% in the middle of 2028 |
| Gross domestic product (GDP) growth | 3.3% in the second quarter |
| Economic growth outlook | Expected to grow 1% in 2026, and 2% in 2027 and 2028 |

Source: Bank of Canada, press release of Oct. 28, 2026.

The Bank said growth is expected to slow in the fourth quarter of 2026 owing to new US tariffs.
