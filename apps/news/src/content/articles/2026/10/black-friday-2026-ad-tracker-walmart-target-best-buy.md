Walmart+ members get the early access to the "hot Deal drops" in each of the first two events; the rest of the deals open to all shoppers a day later. The release mentions 25 toys under $25 and thousands of gifts under $20 but does not list doorbuster prices for televisions or laptops.

In its Oct. 7 announcement, Walmart confirmed that its stores will be closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas, so the Black Friday Deals week skips store shopping on Thursday, Nov. 26. Compared with 2025, when Walmart announced its events on Oct. 27 and ran them Nov. 14 to 16 and Nov. 25 to 30, this year's main event starts two days earlier, on the Monday of Thanksgiving week, and runs a full seven days.

## Target Black Friday 2026: the early deals

Target has given the clearest calendar. From Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, it will post a Deal of the Day, and it will run Early Black Friday deals from Nov. 5 to 7. The Oct. 21 release named starting prices on holiday gifts: cashmere from $25, pajamas from $10, decor from $5, faux fur throws for $30, and gift ideas at $5 and under or $20 or less. Target has not announced its Black Friday day-of event or a Cyber Monday plan for 2026.

Last year (2025), Target ran a Holiday Price Match Guarantee from Nov. 1 to Dec. 24 and extended its return window for electronics and entertainment bought in that period. Those 2025 policies have not been restated for 2026. Target's standard policy matches a lower price at Target.com within 14 days of purchase.

### How to tell a Black Friday deal is real

- Check the price history. A number is only a deal against what the item recently sold for, not the sticker beside it.
- Read the price-match and price-adjustment window. Target matches a lower Target.com price within 14 days of purchase; last year it ran a longer holiday guarantee, which it has not yet restated for 2026.
- Check the return policy before you buy, including the deadline and who pays return shipping. The FTC notes that return and exchange policies vary by store.
- Pay with a credit card. The FTC points out that credit cards give you the right to dispute a charge if something goes wrong.

## Shopping-safety checklist from the FTC

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) publishes holiday guidance every year. Its main points:

- Pay by credit card for the dispute rights it carries. A seller that will take only a gift card, wire transfer, payment app or cryptocurrency is probably running a scam, the FTC says.
- Check out an unfamiliar seller before you buy by searching its name and website address with words like "review," "complaint" or "scam."
- Know the return policy first: the window and who pays to ship a return.
- Keep receipts and original packaging. Without a receipt, the FTC says to expect store credit, often at the lowest sale price.
- If an online order is late, the FTC says the seller has to ship on time or give you the chance to get your money back.
