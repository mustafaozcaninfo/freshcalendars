**Orthodox Christmas 2027 falls on Thursday, Jan. 7, and Three Kings Day falls the day before, on Wednesday, Jan. 6.** The Jan. 7 date comes from calendar arithmetic: Dec. 25 on the Julian calendar is Jan. 7 on the Gregorian calendar used in the United States. The tables below give the weekdays through 2030 and show when the date will shift.

## Why is Orthodox Christmas on January 7?

Churches that keep the Julian calendar celebrate Christmas on Dec. 25 by that calendar. From March 1, 1900 through Feb. 28, 2100 the Julian calendar runs 13 days behind the Gregorian calendar. Add 13 days to Dec. 25 and you reach Jan. 7 of the next Gregorian year. We checked this with a Julian-to-Gregorian conversion of Dec. 25 for each year in the table below.

| Julian Dec. 25 of | Gregorian date | Gap in days |
|---|---|---|
| 1900 | Jan. 7, 1901 | 13 |
| 2026 | Jan. 7, 2027 | 13 |
| 2099 | Jan. 7, 2100 | 13 |
| 2100 | Jan. 8, 2101 | 14 |

The 2100 row is the first change. Feb. 29, 2100 does not exist in the Gregorian calendar but does in the Julian one, so the gap widens to 14 days and Julian Christmas moves to Jan. 8. Nobody reading this will see it, but it explains why "Jan. 7" is a rule of the current century and not a fixed fact of the church year.

## What weekday is Orthodox Christmas from 2027 to 2030?

Three Kings Day (Epiphany) is Jan. 6 on the Gregorian calendar. The Julian calendar's Christmas Eve is Dec. 24, which is Jan. 6 on the Gregorian calendar, so in 2027 the two share a Wednesday. Weekdays below are computed from the calendar.

| Year | Three Kings Day and Orthodox Christmas Eve (Jan. 6) | Orthodox Christmas (Jan. 7) |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Wednesday | Thursday |
| 2028 | Thursday | Friday |
| 2029 | Saturday | Sunday |
| 2030 | Sunday | Monday |

## Which other January 2027 dates fall nearby?

The U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists New Year's Day and Martin Luther King Jr. Day as federal holidays. In 2027 New Year's Day is Friday, Jan. 1, and Martin Luther King Jr. Day is Monday, Jan. 18, the third Monday of the month.
