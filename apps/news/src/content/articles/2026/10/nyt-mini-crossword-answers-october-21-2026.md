**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues and a small hidden trick.** The first two Across answers combine into one phrase. Inside them, circled letters spell a word that fits the theme, which is a neat trick for a puzzle this small. Below is a hint for every clue, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *With 5-Across, words whispered in a game of telephone*: the first word of a request to hand something along.
- **5-Across** (4 letters), *See 1-Across*: two short words that finish the phrase.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Modern way of creating images and text, for short*: chatbots and image tools, abbreviated.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *Some fuzzy brown boots*: a sheepskin brand from Australia.
- **9-Across** (4 letters), *Whisper appropriately spelled (in order!) by the circled letters of 1-/5-Across*: the sound you make to get a friend's attention quietly.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (3 letters), *Wilbur in "Charlotte's Web," e.g.*: a farm animal.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Devoured completely*: two words, past tense of eat plus a direction.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Spotify playlist makeup*: tracks.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *Unexpected problems*: also what a sweater does on a nail.
- **7-Down** (3 letters), *Suffix with "novel" or "capital"*: turns a word into a person.

## Solving tips

Start short. The three-letter entries at 1-Down and 7-Down are gimmes, and between them they give you a letter in the top row and two in the bottom half, which is often enough to guess the boot brand at 8-Across and the five-letter verbs at 2-Down and 4-Down. Then the theme answer at 9-Across confirms the whole top half.
