**Eight video games have a November 2026 release date on their publishers' own pages, and the last is Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI on Thursday, Nov. 19.** Nintendo, Blizzard Entertainment, Sega, Sports Interactive and Aspyr have posted the rest. ## When is Grand Theft Auto VI out, and what has Rockstar not said?

Rockstar's game page reads "Coming November 19, 2026," and its newswire article is titled "Grand Theft Auto VI is Now Set to Launch November 19, 2026." The platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The Rockstar pages we read give no launch time and no price. Our [GTA 6 countdown by time zone](/news/gta-6-countdown-release-time-by-time-zone/) tracks the days left, and [what is confirmed for the game](/news/gta-6-october-22-2026-what-is-confirmed-release-countdown/) covers the rest.

## Which November 2026 releases share a day?

Three games share Thursday, Nov. 12: Metaphor: ReFantazio and Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, both on Switch 2, and Sonic Pico Park on several platforms. Football Manager 27 and DreamWorks Shrek 2 Remastered both land on Tuesday, Nov. 10. Sega's June 10, 2026 release says Metaphor: ReFantazio on Switch 2 "will launch on November 12, 2026, at a lower price." It lists 3,990 yen including tax for Japan and does not state a US price.

Aspyr lists a pre-order price of $26.99 for DreamWorks Shrek 2 Remastered, down from $29.99 before the discount. We do not give prices for the other games, because the pages we read do not state them.

## Reported dates that publishers have not confirmed

Gematsu reported on Aug. 18, 2026 that The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ Online arrives Nov. 19. We found no publisher page for it, so it is not in the table or on the printable. Other rumored November titles are left out for the same reason.
