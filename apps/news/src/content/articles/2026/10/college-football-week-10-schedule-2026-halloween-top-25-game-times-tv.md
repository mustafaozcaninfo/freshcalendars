## Where was Florida vs. Georgia played in 2026?

In Atlanta. Georgia Athletics listed the game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium this year, and ESPN had it at 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC; Georgia won 27-8. Florida is ranked 17th and Georgia third in the AP Top 25 of Oct. 25.

## Which game ran past midnight into the clock change?

Texas State at Boise State kicked off at 10 p.m. ET on The CW and ended after midnight, when Sunday, Nov. 1 had begun; Boise State won 46-45. Clocks fell back at 2 a.m. that morning, which is why every Saturday kickoff was still on Eastern Daylight Time.
