**There were 11 National Hockey League (NHL) games on Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026, with the first puck drops at 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the last three at 10:00 p.m. ET, per the NHL's own schedule data.** Two of them streamed nationally on ESPN+, Hulu and Disney+: the Chicago Blackhawks at the Detroit Red Wings and the Buffalo Sabres at the Anaheim Ducks.

We took the games, start times, arenas and US TV listings from the league's schedule feed, and the final scores from the same feed on Nov. 14. The [NHL schedule](https://www.nhl.com/schedule) shows every result.

## Where the NHL games were shown

Two games were open to fans anywhere in the country: Blackhawks at Red Wings at 7:30 p.m. ET and Sabres at Ducks at 10:00 p.m. ET, both on ESPN+, Hulu and Disney+. Canadiens at Stars was listed on Prime Video for viewers in the Stars' home market.

For the other eight, the listed channels were the teams' local partners, which are blacked out or simply not carried outside their regions.
