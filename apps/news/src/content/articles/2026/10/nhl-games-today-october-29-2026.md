## Where to watch NHL games tonight

Two games are open to fans anywhere in the country: Blackhawks at Red Wings at 7:30 p.m. ET and Sabres at Ducks at 10:00 p.m. ET, both on ESPN+, Hulu and Disney+. Canadiens at Stars is listed on Prime Video for viewers in the Stars' home market.

For the other eight, the listed channels are the teams' local partners, which are blacked out or simply not carried outside their regions. Check your provider's guide for out-of-market options. Unlicensed free streams are not legal ways to watch; use the league's partners or your TV provider.
