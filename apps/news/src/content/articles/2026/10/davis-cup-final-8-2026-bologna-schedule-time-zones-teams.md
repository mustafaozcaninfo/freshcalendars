**The Davis Cup by Generali Final 8 takes place Nov. 24-29, 2026 at the BolognaFiere in Bologna, Italy, and the first quarterfinal, Czechia v Canada, starts Tue., Nov. 24 at 16:00 Central European Time (CET), which is 10 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the schedule daviscup.com confirmed on Sept. 23, 2026.** "All times are CET," the organizer says. The table below converts every session to Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), ET and Pacific Time (PT). Television and streaming are not yet announced.

## What is the Davis Cup Finals 2026 schedule?

The Final 8 has seven sessions over six days, according to daviscup.com. Each session "includes two singles matches and a possible doubles match," says the official hospitality page. The semifinal pairings depend on the quarterfinal results, so they are shown as winners.

| Round | Date | Match | CET |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quarterfinal | Tue., Nov. 24 | Czechia v Canada | 16:00 |
| Quarterfinal | Wed., Nov. 25 | Italy v Korea, Rep. | 16:00 |
| Quarterfinal | Thu., Nov. 26 | Great Britain v Germany (day session) | 10:00 |
| Quarterfinal | Thu., Nov. 26 | Austria v Spain (evening session) | Not before 17:00 |
| Semifinal 1 | Fri., Nov. 27 | Winner Italy/Korea v winner Czechia/Canada | 16:00 |
| Semifinal 2 | Sat., Nov. 28 | Winner Great Britain/Germany v winner Austria/Spain | 12:00 |
| Final | Sun., Nov. 29 | Winners of the two semifinals | 15:00 |

Mark the week on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html); the final falls on the last Sunday of the month. Nov. 26 is also U.S. Thanksgiving, so the Great Britain v Germany and Austria v Spain sessions fall on the holiday ([when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/)).

## What time is each Davis Cup match in the U.S., U.K. and Canada?

In late November Bologna is on CET, which is 1 hour ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). The U.K. is on GMT (UTC). U.S. clocks are on standard time, so ET is UTC minus 5 hours and PT is UTC minus 8 hours. Toronto is on ET and Vancouver on PT.

| Match | CET (Bologna) | GMT (U.K.) | ET (U.S. East, Toronto) | PT (U.S. West, Vancouver) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Czechia v Canada, Tue., Nov. 24 | 16:00 | 3 p.m. | 10 a.m. | 7 a.m. |
| Italy v Korea, Rep., Wed., Nov. 25 | 16:00 | 3 p.m. | 10 a.m. | 7 a.m. |
| Great Britain v Germany, Thu., Nov. 26 | 10:00 | 9 a.m. | 4 a.m. | 1 a.m. |
| Austria v Spain, Thu., Nov. 26 | Not before 17:00 | Not before 4 p.m. | Not before 11 a.m. | Not before 8 a.m. |
| Semifinal 1, Fri., Nov. 27 | 16:00 | 3 p.m. | 10 a.m. | 7 a.m. |
| Semifinal 2, Sat., Nov. 28 | 12:00 | 11 a.m. | 6 a.m. | 3 a.m. |
| Final, Sun., Nov. 29 | 15:00 | 2 p.m. | 9 a.m. | 6 a.m. |

Only the Austria v Spain start is open-ended: the organizer lists it as "not before" 17:00 CET, so it begins after the day session ends. The other start times are fixed on the schedule page. The conversions are ours; the CET times are the organizer's.

## Which teams are in the Davis Cup Final 8?

Italy are the hosts and reigning champions. The 2025 final page lists "Italy (d. Spain 2-0)," and Italy are bidding for a fourth consecutive title, according to the organizer's nominations article of Oct. 26, 2026. Korea, Rep. are Final 8 debutants. These are the nominated squads from that article:

| Team | Nominated players | Captain |
|---|---|---|
| Czechia | Jakub Mensik, Jiri Lehecka, Patrik Rikl, Adam Pavlasek | Tomas Berdych |
| Canada | Felix Auger-Aliassime, Denis Shapovalov, Gabriel Diallo, Liam Draxl, Duncan Chan | Frank Dancevic |
| Italy | Including Flavio Cobolli, Luciano Darderi, Lorenzo Musetti | Filippo Volandri |
| Korea, Rep. | Kwon Soonwoo, Chung Hyeon, Park Uisung, Nam Jisung, Hong Seongchan | Chung Jongsam |
| Great Britain | Arthur Fery, Cameron Norrie, Henry Patten, Neal Skupski | Leon Smith |
| Germany | Alexander Zverev, Jan-Lennard Struff, Kevin Krawietz, Tim Puetz | Michael Kohlmann |
| Austria | Jurij Rodionov, Lukas Neumayer, Joel Schwaerzler, Neil Oberleitner, Lucas Miedler | Jurgen Melzer |
| Spain | Carlos Alcaraz, Rafael Jodar, Alejandro Davidovich Fokina, Pedro Martinez, Marcel Granollers | Not listed here |

The article notes that Carlos Alcaraz returns for the first time since the 2024 Final 8; injury kept him out in 2025. It calls Flavio Cobolli Italy's team No. 1 and says he won all three of his matches at the 2025 Final 8, and it lists Felix Auger-Aliassime as world No. 7. Germany reached the semifinals in 2025. The Italian list above is partial: we did not capture the full squad.

## Where can I watch the Davis Cup Final 8 2026?

Not yet announced by the organizer for the United States, United Kingdom or Canada. We have not seen a broadcaster listed on the schedule or nominations pages, so check local listings and daviscup.com closer to the event. The same goes for the exact arena layout inside the BolognaFiere, which the pages we read do not describe.

On the spot, daviscup.com says tickets are on sale, and that over 60,000 supporters attended last year's Final 8. The official hospitality page lists packages from 549 to 1,220 euros per session. General ticket prices are not published on the pages we read.
