**Carabao Cup Round Four was played Oct. 27 to 29, 2026, and Fleetwood Town knocked out Arsenal, winning 5-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw, according to British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC) Sport.** Liverpool and Manchester City also advanced from penalty shootouts. The quarter-final draw gave Crystal Palace v Sunderland, Fleetwood Town v Everton, Liverpool v Bournemouth and Manchester City v Bradford City, to be played Dec. 15 to 17.

The table below has every Round Four result, both time zones for the kick-off, the UK channel and the U.S. platform in one place, and a printable version is further down.

## What were the Carabao Cup Round Four results?

Eight ties were played over three days. BBC Sport's League Cup scores and fixtures pages carry the results; the UK and other U.S. zone kick-off times are worked out from the English Football League (EFL) viewing guide.

## What was the Fleetwood vs Arsenal result?

Fleetwood Town drew 1-1 with Arsenal on Tue., Oct. 27, and won 5-3 on penalties, BBC Sport says. The tie kicked off at 8pm in the UK, 4 p.m. ET, on Sky Sports Football, and on Paramount+ in the US.

## What was the Liverpool vs Chelsea result?

Liverpool drew 1-1 with Chelsea at Anfield on Wednesday, Oct. 28, and won 6-5 on penalties, per BBC Sport. The tie kicked off at 8pm GMT, 4 p.m. ET, on Sky Sports Football and free-to-air on ITV's streaming service in the UK, and on Paramount+ in the US.

## How could you watch the Carabao Cup in the US?

The EFL says 2024/25 began a new broadcast partnership with CBS Sports. Games were shown "across the CBS Sports family of platforms and channels, including on Paramount+ and the Golazo Network - check local listings." The EFL's U.S. table put every Round Four tie on Paramount+.

In the UK, the EFL says "every Round Four tie" was available live on Sky Sports. Bradford City against Peterborough United and Everton against Newcastle United were on Sky Sports+. For the other four Wednesday ties the EFL did not name a channel.

## Why was the UK four hours ahead of the US during Round Four?

The UK went back to GMT on Oct. 25, while the U.S. stayed on daylight time until Nov. 1. For that week the gap was 4 hours, so 4 p.m. ET was 8pm in the UK. After the U.S. clocks went back on Nov. 1 the gap returned to 5 hours; our [guide to the UK and Europe clock change](/news/uk-europe-clocks-go-back-october-25-2026-time-difference/) explains the week in between. The same gap affects other UK events, such as the [Ballon d'Or 2026 ceremony in London](/news/ballon-dor-2026-date-time-by-time-zone-nominees-london/).

## Who is in the Carabao Cup quarter-finals?

The draw gave four ties, per BBC Sport: Crystal Palace v Sunderland, Fleetwood Town v Everton, Liverpool v Bournemouth and Manchester City v Bradford City. The EFL had said all quarter-finals are played in the week commencing 14 December, and the dates and kick-off times are now set. Crystal Palace, Liverpool and Everton confirmed theirs on their own sites; Manchester City v Bradford City is from BBC Sport and Sky Sports. UK times are Greenwich Mean Time (GMT); ET is five hours behind from Nov. 1.

| Date | Tie | UK (GMT) | ET | UK channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue., Dec. 15 | Manchester City v Bradford City | 8pm | 3 p.m. | Sky Sports |
| Wed., Dec. 16 | Crystal Palace v Sunderland | 7:30pm | 2:30 p.m. | Sky Sports |
| Wed., Dec. 16 | Liverpool v Bournemouth | 8pm | 3 p.m. | Sky Sports |
| Thu., Dec. 17 | Fleetwood Town v Everton | 8pm | 3 p.m. | Sky Sports and ITV |

Sky Sports lists the first three on Sky Sports; Everton say the Fleetwood tie is also on Independent Television (ITV). Dates for later rounds are not covered in these announcements.
