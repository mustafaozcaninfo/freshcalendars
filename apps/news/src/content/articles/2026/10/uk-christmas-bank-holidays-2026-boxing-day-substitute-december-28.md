**Boxing Day 2026 is a bank holiday on Monday, Dec. 28, because Dec. 26 is a Saturday**, according to the GOV.UK list of United Kingdom (UK) bank holidays. Christmas Day is Friday, Dec. 25, 2026, and New Year's Day is Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. GOV.UK lists all three for England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

The leave planner below shows each day from Dec. 24 to Jan. 4 and which days our computation says can be bridged with three days of annual leave. A one-page version is attached as a printable.

## Is Dec. 28, 2026, a bank holiday?

Yes. GOV.UK's bank holidays page and its data file list "Boxing Day (substitute day)" on Monday, Dec. 28, 2026, in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Dec. 26 falls on a Saturday this year, so the holiday moves to a weekday. For the date question on its own, see [when is Boxing Day in the UK](/when-is/uk-boxing-day/) and [when is Christmas Day](/when-is/christmas-day/).

## What are the Christmas and New Year bank holidays in 2026 and 2027?

| Holiday | Date | Where (GOV.UK) |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas Day | Friday, Dec. 25, 2026 | England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland |
| Boxing Day (substitute day) | Monday, Dec. 28, 2026 | England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland |
| New Year's Day | Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 | England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland |
| 2nd January (substitute day) | Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 | Scotland only |

Jan. 2, 2027, is a Saturday, which is why Scotland's extra holiday moves to Monday, Jan. 4. England, Wales and Northern Ireland have no bank holiday on Jan. 4. Our [UK bank holidays 2027 story](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/) lists the full 2027 year.

## How can I plan leave around Christmas and New Year?

This table is our computation from the GOV.UK dates, for a standard Monday-to-Friday week.

| Date | Weekday | England and Wales | Scotland | Northern Ireland | Leave planning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dec. 24, 2026 | Thursday | No | No | No | Christmas Eve |
| Dec. 25, 2026 | Friday | Bank holiday | Bank holiday | Bank holiday | Christmas Day |
| Dec. 26, 2026 | Saturday | Weekend | Weekend | Weekend | Boxing Day date |
| Dec. 27, 2026 | Sunday | Weekend | Weekend | Weekend | |
| Dec. 28, 2026 | Monday | Bank holiday | Bank holiday | Bank holiday | Boxing Day substitute |
| Dec. 29, 2026 | Tuesday | No | No | No | Leave day to bridge |
| Dec. 30, 2026 | Wednesday | No | No | No | Leave day to bridge |
| Dec. 31, 2026 | Thursday | No | No | No | Leave day to bridge |
| Jan. 1, 2027 | Friday | Bank holiday | Bank holiday | Bank holiday | New Year's Day |
| Jan. 2, 2027 | Saturday | Weekend | Weekend | Weekend | |
| Jan. 3, 2027 | Sunday | Weekend | Weekend | Weekend | |
| Jan. 4, 2027 | Monday | No | Bank holiday | No | Scotland's 2nd January substitute |

From Friday, Dec. 25, to Monday, Dec. 28, a Monday-to-Friday worker gets four days off in a row. New Year gives a three-day weekend, Friday, Jan. 1, to Sunday, Jan. 3, in England and Wales and in Northern Ireland, and four days in Scotland, Friday, Jan. 1, to Monday, Jan. 4.

Taking annual leave on Dec. 29, 30 and 31 joins the two blocks. In England and Wales that gives 10 days off, Dec. 25 to Jan. 3. In Scotland the same three days give 11 days, Dec. 25 to Jan. 4. The same dates apply in Northern Ireland as in England and Wales.

## Do I get the bank holidays off?

Check your contract or ask your employer. This story lists the days GOV.UK names as bank holidays and does not set out what any worker is owed.

## What happens in 2027?

Christmas Day 2027 falls on a Saturday. GOV.UK's data lists Monday, Dec. 27, 2027, as the Christmas Day substitute day and Tuesday, Dec. 28, 2027, as the Boxing Day substitute day in England and Wales and in Northern Ireland.
