**The 53rd Village Halloween Parade stepped off at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026, on Sixth Avenue in Manhattan**, according to the parade's organizers, who said it would end around 11 p.m. The parade site said Spectrum News NY1 presented it live at 8 p.m. The line-up, step-off, television and subway times are in one table below, with a one-page night plan to print as a record.

The 2026 theme was "Body & Soul". The site said the parade would be "led by a spectacular new performance: Firehorse!", a puppet commissioned from puppeteer Basil Twist and choreographed by performance artist Julie Atlas Muz. The Grand Marshal was performance artist Joey Arias. ## Where is the Village Halloween Parade route?

The watch page said the parade ran straight up Sixth Avenue from King Street to West 15th Street. Spectators could stand on the east or west side of Sixth Avenue between King Street and West 15th Street. The organizers said the streets were most crowded between Bleecker Street and 14th Street.

The participate page gave a different starting point: marchers lined up at Canal Street and Sixth Avenue, and the parade went up Sixth Avenue to 15th Street. Read together, the line-up area was at Canal, and the stretch the organizers described for watching ran from King Street to West 15th Street.

## How to watch the Village Halloween Parade on NY1

The parade site said the parade was presented live on Spectrum News NY1 at 8 p.m. on Oct. 31. The in-person step-off was an hour earlier, at 7 p.m. The site did not list another broadcast or stream.

## Is the Village Halloween Parade free to watch?

Yes. The watch page called the parade a free public event, and the site said everyone was invited to watch. Ticketed viewing areas existed, but the tickets page listed standing ($250) and seating ($450) as sold out. Its $125 Skip the Line marcher ticket was also sold out. The organizers described that ticket as a way to avoid the wait with "the 80,000+ General Public Participants", a figure from the parade site.

## How do you march in the parade?

Only people in costume could march. The participate page said to join the line-up area at the corner of Canal Street and Sixth Avenue on Saturday, Oct. 31 between 6:30 and 9 p.m. It listed these entry rules:

1. Enter the line-up area only from the east and south, meaning Sullivan, East Broome and Canal streets.
2. If you come from the west, enter only from Canal Street.
3. Skip the Line ticket holders arrive between 5:30 and 8:30 p.m.

The site said marchers shared the street with "Hundreds of Giant Puppets", "More than 50 bands" and dancers. It advised against bringing a car and said groups should meet away from the line-up and walk over together.

## Which subway lines change for the parade?

The parade site's subway notice listed two changes. The Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA), not the parade site, is the transit agency.

| When | Change on the parade site's notice |
|---|---|
| Friday, Oct. 30 at 10 p.m. to Monday, Nov. 2 at 5 a.m. | A and C trains rerouted in Manhattan and Brooklyn |
| Saturday, Oct. 31, 5 to 10 p.m. | Downtown C trains bypass Spring Street station |

The notice also said uptown A trains ran via the F from Jay St-MetroTech to W 4 St-Wash Sq, and stopped at 23 St and 50 St. The parade site called the subway the best way to reach the line-up.

The parade site did not list street closures or a rain plan beyond saying it would go on in rain or shine, and we do not give them here. The only end time it gave was "around 11:00 pm".
