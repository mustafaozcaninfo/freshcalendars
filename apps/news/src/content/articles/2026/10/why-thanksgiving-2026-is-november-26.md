Thanksgiving Day 2026 is **Thursday, Nov. 26**. The date is not chosen each year; it follows a rule written into federal law.

## The rule

Title 5 of the US Code lists Thanksgiving Day as "the fourth Thursday in November." Because November 2026 starts on a Sunday, its Thursdays fall on the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th, so the fourth one is Nov. 26.

## Earliest and latest dates

The fourth Thursday of any November falls between Nov. 22 and Nov. 28. Black Friday is always the next day, Nov. 27 in 2026.

## For your calendar

Federal offices, banks and the US Postal Service close on Thanksgiving Day. Many schools also close on the Wednesday and Friday around it, so check your district calendar.
