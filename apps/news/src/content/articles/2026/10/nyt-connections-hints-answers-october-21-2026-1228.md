## Why the traps worked

**The food words.** Big Cheese, Top Banana, Hot Potato, Sweetbread and Grape-Nuts all sound like a menu, but they land in three different groups: two are slang for a boss, one is a party game and two are misnomers.

**The animals.** Alpha Dog, Leapfrog, Duck Duck Goose and Mantis Shrimp look like a zoo group. Only Mantis Shrimp is a real animal name, and it is wrong twice: the creature is neither a mantis nor a shrimp.

**The misnomers.** Grape-Nuts cereal contains neither grapes nor nuts. Sweetbread is neither sweet nor bread; it is the thymus or pancreas of a calf or lamb. The English horn is a woodwind in the oboe family, neither English nor a horn.

**Heavyweight.** It suggests boxing, but here it means an important, influential person.
