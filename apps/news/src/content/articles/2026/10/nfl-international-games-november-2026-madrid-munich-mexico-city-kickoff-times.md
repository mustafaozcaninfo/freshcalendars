**Bengals at Falcons in Madrid is Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, at 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), which is 3:30 p.m. in Madrid, on NFL Network, according to NFL.com.** Two more National Football League (NFL) international games follow: Patriots at Lions in Munich on Nov. 15 and Vikings at 49ers in Mexico City on Nov. 22. Below: every kickoff in U.S. zones plus the United Kingdom, central Europe and Mexico City, and the clock gap that changes between Europe and the United States on Nov. 1.

## When are the NFL international games in November 2026?

NFL.com lists the three games below. The team named second is the designated home team. NFL Football Operations says the 2026 season has "a record-breaking nine games played across four continents." The other six were Melbourne (Sept. 10), Rio de Janeiro (Sept. 27), London (Oct. 4, Oct. 11 and Oct. 18) and Paris (Oct. 25, Steelers and Saints at the Stade de France).

| Week | Date | Game | Stadium | Kickoff (ET) | TV and streaming |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Sun., Nov. 8 | Bengals at Falcons | Bernabéu Stadium (Estadio Santiago Bernabéu), Madrid | 9:30 a.m. | NFL Network, NFL+ |
| 10 | Sun., Nov. 15 | Patriots at Lions | FC Bayern Munich Stadium, Munich | 9:30 a.m. | FOX, FOX One, NFL+ |
| 11 | Sun., Nov. 22 | Vikings at 49ers | Estadio Banorte, Mexico City | 8:20 p.m. | NBC, Peacock, NFL+ |

The Patriots' own announcement on May 14, 2026, gives the Munich start as "9:30 a.m. ET (3:30 p.m. Munich time)." For the full season, see our [printable NFL schedule for 2026-2027](/news/printable-nfl-schedule-2026-2027-pdf/).

## What time do the games start in your time zone?

The Eastern times are NFL.com's. Every other column is our conversion, using the standard-time offsets in force on all three dates: Eastern Standard Time (EST) is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) minus 5 hours, Central (CST) minus 6, Mountain (MST) minus 7, Pacific (PST) minus 8, Alaska minus 9 and Hawaii minus 10. The United Kingdom is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, UTC+0), Madrid and Munich on Central European Time (CET, UTC+1), and Mexico City on UTC-6.

Arizona does not observe daylight saving time and is on UTC-7, so by our conversion its times match the Mountain column in November.

## Why is Europe 6 hours ahead of Eastern Time in November?

Europe and the United States change clocks on different dates. Europe moved back on Sunday, Oct. 25, and the United States moves back on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. Between those dates the gap was 5 hours for Madrid and Munich and 4 hours for London, by our conversion. From Nov. 1 the gap is 6 hours for Madrid and Munich and 5 hours for London, which is why 9:30 a.m. ET is 3:30 p.m. in Madrid.

The clock change does not move the kickoff; the Eastern times above are the ones NFL.com lists. It only changes the hours between you and the stadium.
