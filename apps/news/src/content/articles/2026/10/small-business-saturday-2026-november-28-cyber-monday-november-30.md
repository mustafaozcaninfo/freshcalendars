**Small Business Saturday 2026 is Saturday, Nov. 28, and Cyber Monday is Monday, Nov. 30.** Both are counted from Thanksgiving, which falls on Thursday, Nov. 26 this year (see [why Thanksgiving is Nov. 26](/news/why-thanksgiving-2026-is-november-26/)). Black Friday sits between them and Small Business Saturday on Friday, Nov. 27.

## Thanksgiving weekend 2026 at a glance

| Date | Day |
|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving Day |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Black Friday |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | Small Business Saturday |
| Mon., Nov. 30 | Cyber Monday |

Our [Black Friday date page](/when-is/black-friday/) and [Cyber Monday date page](/when-is/cyber-monday/) cover those two days on their own.

## What Small Business Saturday is

Small Business Saturday is a "shop small" day for independent local businesses. American Express created it in 2010, and the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) has co-sponsored it since 2011, according to the SBA. Black Friday, by comparison, is about broad retail deals.

## What Cyber Monday is

Cyber Monday is the Monday after Thanksgiving. It is a retail marketing term, not a federal holiday.

## Which of these days is a holiday

Of the four days, only Thanksgiving on Nov. 26 is a closed day for the U.S. Postal Service (USPS). For mail and package cutoffs around the holiday, see our [USPS holiday shipping deadlines](/news/usps-holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026/). For store hours on Black Friday, see our [Black Friday store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/).

## How the dates are worked out

All of these days are counted from Thanksgiving, the fourth Thursday of November, which is Nov. 26 in 2026. Black Friday is the next day, Small Business Saturday is the Saturday after Thanksgiving, and Cyber Monday is the Monday after it. That puts them on Nov. 27, Nov. 28 and Nov. 30. Nov. 29 is the Sunday in between, so the weekend runs Thursday to Monday with one quiet day before the Cyber Monday date.

## Shop small or shop broad

The two days differ in focus. Small Business Saturday asks shoppers to spend at independent local businesses, and Black Friday is known for broad retail deals. This story does not cover store hours or deals; for those, use the guides linked below once retailers publish their plans.
