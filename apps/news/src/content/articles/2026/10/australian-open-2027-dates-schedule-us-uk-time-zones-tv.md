**The Australian Open 2027 runs Jan. 10-31, 2027 at Melbourne Park, according to the What's On page on ausopen.com, the tournament's official site.** The page lists Opening Week from Sunday, Jan. 10 to Saturday, Jan. 16, Main Draw Week One from Sunday, Jan. 17 and Main Draw Week Two through Sunday, Jan. 31. The Mar. 23, 2026 announcement gave Jan. 11 as the start and said the finals "will be held on 30 and 31 January." The page adds that all dates and event details are subject to change. Melbourne is far ahead of the United States and the United Kingdom (UK), so much of the play lands overnight or on the previous evening for viewers there. The time table below shows the shift.

## What are the Australian Open 2027 dates?

The rows below come from ausopen.com: the What's On page, the Mar. 23, 2026 announcement and an Aug. 3, 2026 article on qualifying. Weekdays are computed from the 2027 calendar.

| Stage | Date | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| Opening Week begins | Jan. 10 | Sunday |
| Qualifying begins | Jan. 11 | Monday |
| Qualifying ends | Jan. 16 | Saturday |
| Opening Week ends | Jan. 16 | Saturday |
| Main-draw action begins | Jan. 17 | Sunday |
| Main Draw Week One ends | Jan. 24 | Sunday |
| Main Draw Week Two begins | Jan. 25 | Monday |
| Finals | Jan. 30 and Jan. 31 | Saturday and Sunday |

The ausopen.com provisional schedule, a one-page PDF with the footer "Match dates and schedule are subject to change. Version current as of August 2026," lists the women's singles final for Saturday, Jan. 30 and the men's singles final for Sunday, Jan. 31, and qualifying for Jan. 11 to Jan. 16. It has no session times or court order. The Kids Tennis Day date and the draw ceremony are not yet published as of Nov. 12, 2026. Opening Week includes qualifying, player practice, Kids Tennis Day and live entertainment across the precinct. The Lawn Tennis Association (LTA), the UK tennis body, lists qualifying for Monday, Jan. 11 to Saturday, Jan. 16. Mark the three weeks on our [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html) or use the planner in the downloads box.

## What time do Australian Open matches start in the U.S. and UK?

The 2027 order of play is not yet published. For 2026, Ticketmaster's session listings showed night sessions at 7 p.m. local time on Rod Laver Arena. The Associated Press (AP), through the Lawrence Journal-World, reported that the first Rod Laver match on opening day started at 11:30 a.m. Day sessions on Rod Laver were usually 11:30 a.m. that year, and outside courts started at 11 a.m. local time. The Association of Tennis Professionals (ATP) Tour reported in January 2026 that extreme heat can move start times.

In January, Melbourne uses Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT), which is UTC+11. The UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (UTC+0), Eastern Time (ET) is UTC-5 and Pacific Time (PT) is UTC-8. The table applies 2026 start times to a sample 2027 day, Monday, Jan. 18, in Melbourne.

| Melbourne start (2026 time) | UK | ET | PT |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 a.m. Monday, outside courts | Midnight Monday | 7 p.m. Sunday | 4 p.m. Sunday |
| 11:30 a.m. Monday, Rod Laver day session | 12:30 a.m. Monday | 7:30 p.m. Sunday | 4:30 p.m. Sunday |
| 7 p.m. Monday, Rod Laver night session | 8 a.m. Monday | 3 a.m. Monday | Midnight Monday |

A morning start in Melbourne falls on the previous evening in the United States, and a Melbourne night session falls in the early morning of the same date in ET. These are 2026 times; check the 2027 order of play once it is out.

## Where can you watch the Australian Open 2027 in the U.S. and UK?

The tournament's own Broadcast Partners page on ausopen.com, checked Nov. 4, 2026, lists HBO Max for the UK and Ireland and ESPN and Tennis Channel for the United States. The page gives no year for these deals, so it does not confirm 2027 rights by itself.

The LTA says something different for the UK: "Watch and stream all of the 2027 Australian Open live on Turner Network Television (TNT) Sports and discovery+." The two pages differ, so check the broadcaster closer to January.

For the United States, ausopen.com lists ESPN and Tennis Channel. ESPN holds Australian Open rights for the U.S. and Canada through 2031, according to a May 19, 2022 Ministry of Sport report that cited Tennis Australia. The 2027 U.S. channel list by round is not yet published.
