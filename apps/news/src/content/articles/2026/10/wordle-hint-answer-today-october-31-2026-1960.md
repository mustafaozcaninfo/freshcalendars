## How Wordle works

You get six tries to find a five-letter word. After each guess, a green tile means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means the letter is in the word but in another spot, and gray means it is not in the word at all. A letter that appears twice is only marked twice if you guess it twice.

## Six clues, gentle to strong

1. **Letters used:** four different letters make up the five-letter word.
2. **Vowels:** A and O. The A comes second, and the O fills both the fourth and fifth spots.
3. **Repeated letter:** yes, the letter O appears twice, side by side at the end.
4. **Third letter:** H.
5. **First letter:** Y.
6. **Strong nudge:** a word you might shout when you are delighted, and also the name of a well-known internet brand.

## Guessing tips for today

The word ends in two Os, so a guess that puts O in the last two spots can turn two tiles green at once. A starter with A and a common consonant, such as "raise" or "crane," shows whether the A lands in the second spot. Because one letter repeats, remember that a double O only turns both tiles colored if you guess it twice.
