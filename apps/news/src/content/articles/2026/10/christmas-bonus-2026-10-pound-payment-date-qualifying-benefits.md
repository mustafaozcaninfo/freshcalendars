The Christmas Bonus is a one-off tax-free £10 payment made "before Christmas", according to GOV.UK, the official UK government site. You do not need to claim it: GOV.UK says "you should get paid automatically". The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has not published a 2026 payment date or a 2026 qualifying week, so this page gives the rule, the dates it implies and the 24 benefits that count. A printable checklist of the 24 benefits comes with this story.

## When is the Christmas Bonus paid in 2026?

GOV.UK says the bonus is paid before Christmas. No exact 2026 date has been announced, and this page does not guess one. Christmas Day falls on Friday 25 December 2026; the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and [when Christmas Day falls](/when-is/christmas-day/) show the days around it.

The same wording applies in Northern Ireland, where nidirect says the bonus is a one-off tax-free £10 payment that you do not need to claim.

## What is the qualifying week in 2026?

Whether you get the bonus depends on the qualifying week, which GOV.UK says is "normally the first full week of December". You must be present or ordinarily resident in the UK, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man or Gibraltar during that week, and you must get a qualifying benefit.

DWP has not confirmed the 2026 week. Two readings exist, and they differ by a week.

| Source | How it reads | 2026 qualifying week | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| GOV.UK rule, worked out here | First full Monday-to-Sunday week wholly in December. 1 December 2026 is a Tuesday, so the first Monday is 7 December | Monday 7 to Sunday 13 December 2026 | Our calculation, not a DWP announcement |
| Retirement Expert, 4 September 2026 | Reported dates | Monday 30 November to Sunday 6 December 2026 | Reported by a third party, not confirmed by DWP |

Until DWP publishes the week, treat both as unconfirmed. Anyone whose benefit starts or stops around the turn of the month should check GOV.UK for the confirmed dates.

## Who misses out even though they get money from the state?

Two cases catch people out, and both come straight from the GOV.UK rules.

- **Universal Credit alone does not qualify.** It is not on the list above. A person on Universal Credit gets the bonus only if they also get one of the 24 listed benefits.
- **An unclaimed State Pension means no bonus.** GOV.UK says: "If you have not claimed your State Pension and are not entitled to one of the other qualifying benefits, you will not get a Christmas Bonus."

Because the bonus is paid automatically, there is no form to fill in. The route to the £10 is the qualifying benefit itself. The State Pension is one of the qualifying benefits; the next rise is covered in [the April 2027 State Pension increase](/news/state-pension-increase-april-2027-triple-lock-budget/).

## What else does winter bring from DWP?

The Christmas Bonus is separate from winter heating help. The [Winter Fuel Payment for 2026](/news/winter-fuel-payment-2026-dates-amounts-35000-threshold/) and the [Cold Weather Payment for 2026 to 2027](/news/cold-weather-payment-2026-27-dates-25-pounds-who-gets-it/) have their own rules and dates, covered in those stories.
