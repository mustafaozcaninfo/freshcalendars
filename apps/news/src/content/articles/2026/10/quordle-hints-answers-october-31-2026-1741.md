## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**The word that starts with A**

- Letters: starts with A, ends with E, and has an N, an I and an M inside.
- Meaning: a style of animation that comes from Japan.

**The word that starts with S**

- Letters: starts with S, ends with M, and has a W, an A and an R inside.
- Meaning: a large group of insects, such as bees, moving together; also a crowd moving in a mass.

**The word that starts with C**

- Letters: starts with C, ends with K, and has an H, an A and an L inside.
- Meaning: a soft white limestone, or a stick of it used for writing on a blackboard.

**The word that starts with R**

- Letters: starts with R, ends with H, and has two A's and a J inside; it is the only word today that repeats a letter.
- Meaning: a historical title for an Indian king or prince.

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess such as Slate, with S, L, A, T and E, puts at least one letter into all four boards, and its A lands on every board. A second guess such as Chimp, which adds C, H, I, M and P, also touches all four boards: I and M on one, M on another, C and H on a third, and H on the fourth. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
