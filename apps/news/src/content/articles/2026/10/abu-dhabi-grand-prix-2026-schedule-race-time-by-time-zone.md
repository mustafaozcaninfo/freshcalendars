**The Formula 1 (F1) Abu Dhabi Grand Prix starts Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, at 5 p.m. Gulf Standard Time (GST), which is 8 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), 5 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) and 1 p.m. in the UK, at Yas Marina Circuit, according to Formula 1's official timetable.** It is the final round, Round 23 of 23, of the 2026 season. Below: all five sessions in eight time zones, the race length, last year's result and how to watch in the U.S.

## What is the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix 2026 schedule?

F1's race page for the meeting of Dec. 4 to 6 gives session starts in GST, the circuit's offset of UTC+04:00 (Coordinated Universal Time, UTC). The other columns are our conversion, not an official schedule. In December the U.S. is on standard time: ET is UTC-5, Central Time (CT) is UTC-6, Mountain Time (MT) is UTC-7 and PT is UTC-8. The UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), equal to UTC, and Central European Time (CET) is UTC+1.

## What time does the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix start on Sunday?

The race starts at 5 p.m. in Abu Dhabi on Sunday, Dec. 6. By our conversion that is 8 a.m. ET, 7 a.m. CT, 6 a.m. MT and 5 a.m. PT. In the UK it is 1 p.m. GMT, and in Central Europe it is 2 p.m. CET. F1's race page lists 58 laps and a race distance of 306.188 km at Yas Marina Circuit.

## What time is Abu Dhabi qualifying at Yas Marina?

Qualifying is on Saturday, Dec. 5, at 6 p.m. GST, which is 9 a.m. ET and 6 a.m. PT. Practice 3 starts earlier that day at 2:30 p.m. GST, 5:30 a.m. ET. On Friday, Dec. 4, Practice 1 starts at 1:30 p.m. GST (4:30 a.m. ET) and Practice 2 at 5 p.m. GST (8 a.m. ET).

## How did the 2025 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix end?

In 2025, the Federation Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) reported on Dec. 7 that Max Verstappen won the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, his 71st career win, and that Lando Norris was crowned the 2025 drivers' world champion. This story does not cover the 2026 title situation.

## Where can you watch the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix in the U.S.?

Formula 1 announced on Oct. 17, 2025 a five-year partnership with Apple to become the sport's exclusive U.S. broadcast partner from 2026, with Apple TV hosting all Free Practice, Qualifying, Sprint sessions and Grands Prix. Coverage details outside the U.S. were not confirmed on an official page for this story.

F1's 2026 calendar lists Abu Dhabi last, as Round 23 of 23.
