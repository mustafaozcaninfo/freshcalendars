**LEGO has five advent calendars for 2026, priced from $34.99 to $44.99** on LEGO.com: City and Friends at the lower price, and Star Wars, Marvel and Disney Princess at the higher one. All five went on sale Sept. 1, and each has 24 doors to open from Tuesday, Dec. 1 to Christmas Eve. Harry Potter fans will be disappointed: there is no Harry Potter calendar this year.

## LEGO advent calendars 2026 at a glance

| Calendar | Set number | Pieces | Price (U.S.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| LEGO City | 60510 | 252 | $34.99 |
| LEGO Friends | 42698 | 211 | $34.99 |
| LEGO Star Wars | 75456 | 293 | $44.99 |
| LEGO Marvel | 76340 | 317 | $44.99 |
| LEGO Disney Princess | 43298 | 290 | $44.99 |

Prices and piece counts are from LEGO.com. The licensed themes cost $10 more than LEGO's own City and Friends lines, the usual pattern for LEGO calendars.

## What's inside each one

**City (60510), $34.99.** LEGO's classic Christmas calendar, for ages 5 and up. Brick Fanatics lists minifigures of Santa, Mrs. Claus and elves, plus a snowman and a baby reindeer among the 24 mini builds. The box folds out into a Santa's workshop play mat.

**Friends (42698), $34.99.** The smallest set at 211 pieces, built around animals. It comes with three mini-dolls, Liann, Nova and Aliya, and mini animals including a seal, a pony, a polar bear and a penguin, according to Brickset.

**Star Wars (75456), $44.99.** This year's calendar follows The Mandalorian. Brick Fanatics reports the Mandalorian and Grogu in holiday sweaters, Moff Gideon and Carson Teva, alongside micro builds such as the Razor Crest, Mando's N-1 starfighter, an X-wing, an Imperial shuttle and a Jawa sandcrawler.

**Marvel (76340), $44.99.** The biggest set, at 317 pieces, with six minifigures. The headline figures are Wolverine in a Christmas sweater and a comic-style Doctor Doom, his first LEGO appearance since 2013, according to Brick Fanatics.

**Disney Princess (43298), $44.99.** LEGO describes it as advent surprises "and a game": the box turns into a board game. BrickEconomy lists micro-dolls of Ariel, Cinderella, Moana and Mulan with Gus, Mushu, Pua and Sebastian.

## Which one to buy

| If you want | Pick |
|---|---|
| The most pieces for the money | Marvel, 317 pieces |
| The lowest price | City or Friends, $34.99 |
| A calendar for a 5-year-old | City (ages 5+) |
| Something to play after Christmas | Disney Princess (board game) or City (play mat) |
| Collectible minifigures | Marvel or Star Wars |

Per door, the calendars cost about $1.46 (City and Friends) or $1.87 (Star Wars, Marvel, Disney Princess) a day.

## No Harry Potter, no Minecraft

Brick Fanatics describes this year's lineup of five calendars as "back to normal." The Minecraft calendar was not repeated, and there is no Harry Potter calendar for 2026. LEGO has not announced any additional calendars.
