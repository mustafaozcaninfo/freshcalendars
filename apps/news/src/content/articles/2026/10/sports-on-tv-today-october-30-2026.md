## Which sports are on national TV tonight?

- **Prime Video:** Knicks at 76ers (7:30 p.m. ET) and Lakers at Warriors (10 p.m. ET), the two NBA games on a national channel, per NBA.com. Prime Video is also the U.S. listing for Kraken at Flames (8 p.m. ET) in the NHL's schedule data, which that data does not mark as national.
- **NHL Network:** Devils at Golden Knights (6 p.m. ET).
- **ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPNU:** four college football games, per NCAA.com.
- **USA Network and Peacock:** Breeders' Cup, 1 to 6 p.m. ET.
- **Apple TV:** both F1 practice sessions, free without a subscription.

The other eight NBA games are on local TV and NBA League Pass.
