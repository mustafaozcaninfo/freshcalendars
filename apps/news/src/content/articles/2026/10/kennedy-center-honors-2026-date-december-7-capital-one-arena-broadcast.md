The 49th annual Kennedy Center Honors are set for Monday, Dec. 7, 2026, at Capital One Arena in downtown Washington, D.C., according to a Kennedy Center spokesperson quoted by the Associated Press (AP) and CBS News on Oct. 2, 2026. The center named the five honorees on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, the AP and AARP reported. The host, the broadcast network and the air date have not been announced.

## What is set and what is not yet announced?

| Item | Status for 2026 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Event | 49th annual Kennedy Center Honors | AP, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| Date | Monday, Dec. 7, 2026 | AP, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| Venue | Capital One Arena, downtown Washington, D.C. | AP and CBS News, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| Honorees | Paul Anka, David Foster, Andy Garcia, David Mamet and Barbara Mandrell, announced Monday, Nov. 2, 2026 | AP and AARP, Nov. 2, 2026 |
| Host | Not yet announced | The A.V. Club, Nov. 2, 2026 |
| Broadcast network | Not yet announced; expected "later this fall" | CBS News, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| TV air date | Not yet announced | Not reported yet |

The date comes from the AP report, which said "the event will take place on Dec. 7," and the AP's Nov. 2 story repeats that this year's honors program "will be held on Dec. 7 at Washington's Capital One Arena." Dec. 7, 2026 is a Monday. Mark it on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and treat the other lines as open until the center says more.

## Who are the 2026 Kennedy Center honorees?

The Kennedy Center announced the honorees on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, according to the AP and AARP. The kennedy-center.org site blocks automated access, so these facts rest on those independent newsrooms. The fields below are as the AP describes them.

| Honoree | Field, per the AP |
|---|---|
| Paul Anka | Songwriter ("My Way") |
| David Foster | Producer |
| Andy Garcia | Actor ("The Untouchables," "The Godfather Part III") |
| David Mamet | Playwright |
| Barbara Mandrell | Country Music Hall of Fame member |

AARP says the five will be honored on Dec. 7 at Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C. The AP reported that this is the first time the program is held away from the Kennedy Center. The A.V. Club reported that the host had not been announced.

## Why is the event at Capital One Arena?

CBS News reported that the Kennedy Center's main building is closed for what the administration calls safety assessments. The AP said the 49th awards are set for the downtown arena, as a Kennedy Center spokesperson said. The AP also reported that a federal judge has twice said the center's name cannot be changed without Congress.

## When will the 2026 Kennedy Center Honors be on TV?

No network or air date has been announced. CBS News reported that the center is expected to announce its broadcast partner for the 2026 program later this fall. CBS aired the Honors every year from 1978 to 2025, CBS News reported, and the contract ended after the 2025 program, according to a source familiar with the contract.

Last year's schedule shows how far apart the steps fell. This is 2025 only, not a forecast for 2026:

| Step | 2025 (48th Honors) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Honorees announced | Wednesday, Aug. 13, 2025 | AP |
| Ceremony taped | Sunday, Dec. 7, 2025, Kennedy Center Opera House | Paramount Press Express |
| Broadcast | Tuesday, Dec. 23, 2025, 8 to 10:30 p.m. ET/PT on CBS, streaming on Paramount+ | Paramount Press Express |

In 2025 the honorees were named 116 days before the ceremony; in 2026 the announcement came on Nov. 2, 35 days before Dec. 7. In 2025, the broadcast followed the taping by 16 days. The honorees were George Strait, the rock band Kiss, Gloria Gaynor, Michael Crawford and Sylvester Stallone, according to the AP. The Paramount Press Express page lists President Donald J. Trump as the 2025 host.
