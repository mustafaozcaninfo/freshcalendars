The 49th annual Kennedy Center Honors are set for Monday, Dec. 7, 2026, at Capital One Arena in downtown Washington, D.C., according to a Kennedy Center spokesperson quoted by the Associated Press (AP) and CBS News on Oct. 2, 2026. The honorees, the host, the broadcast network and the air date have not been announced.

## What is set and what is not yet announced?

| Item | Status for 2026 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Event | 49th annual Kennedy Center Honors | AP, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| Date | Monday, Dec. 7, 2026 | AP, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| Venue | Capital One Arena, downtown Washington, D.C. | AP and CBS News, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| Honorees | Not yet announced; to be named "at a later day," per the center's spokesperson | AP, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| Host | Not yet announced | Not reported yet |
| Broadcast network | Not yet announced; expected "later this fall" | CBS News, Oct. 2, 2026 |
| TV air date | Not yet announced | Not reported yet |

The date comes from the AP report, which said "the event will take place on Dec. 7." Dec. 7, 2026 is a Monday. Mark it on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and treat the other lines as open until the center says more.

## Why is the event at Capital One Arena?

CBS News reported that the Kennedy Center's main building is closed for what the administration calls safety assessments. The AP said the 49th awards are set for the downtown arena, as a Kennedy Center spokesperson said. The AP also reported that a federal judge has twice said the center's name cannot be changed without Congress.

## When will the 2026 Kennedy Center Honors be on TV?

No network or air date has been announced. CBS News reported that the center is expected to announce its broadcast partner for the 2026 program later this fall. CBS aired the Honors every year from 1978 to 2025, CBS News reported, and the contract ended after the 2025 program, according to a source familiar with the contract.

Last year's schedule shows how far apart the steps fell. This is 2025 only, not a forecast for 2026:

| Step | 2025 (48th Honors) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Honorees announced | Wednesday, Aug. 13, 2025 | AP |
| Ceremony taped | Sunday, Dec. 7, 2025, Kennedy Center Opera House | Paramount Press Express |
| Broadcast | Tuesday, Dec. 23, 2025, 8 to 10:30 p.m. ET/PT on CBS, streaming on Paramount+ | Paramount Press Express |

In 2025 the honorees were named 116 days before the ceremony, and the broadcast followed the taping by 16 days. The honorees were George Strait, KISS, Gloria Gaynor, Michael Crawford and Sylvester Stallone, according to the AP. The Paramount Press Express page lists President Donald J. Trump as the 2025 host.

A [weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) is handy for noting the air week once a network and date are named.

## What should you check next?

- The honoree announcement, which the center has said will come at a later day.
- The broadcast partner, expected later this fall according to CBS News.
- The air date and any streaming service, which no report has given yet.
