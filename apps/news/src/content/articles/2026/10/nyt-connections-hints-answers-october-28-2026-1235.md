## Today's 16 words

Curiosity, Rout, Subject, Attraction, Attachment, Spirit, Body, Coarse, Crush, Weigh, Signature, Interest, Rode, Opportunity, Feelings, Perseverance

## How the game works

Sort the 16 entries into four groups of four. You can make four mistakes. The groups run from yellow to green, blue and purple, with purple the group that depends most on wordplay.

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Words for being drawn to another person.
- Hint 2: Think of a schoolyard word for a first romantic interest, a spark between two people, and the plural noun for what you have for someone.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Pieces you fill in or add when you write a message on a computer.
- Hint 2: One is the line that says what the message is about, one is the main text, one is a file you add, and one is the sign-off block at the bottom.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Each word is the name of a robotic vehicle sent to another planet by the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).
- Hint 2: The planet is Mars, and the names are all words that could describe a person's qualities.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Say each word aloud and it sounds like another word.
- Hint 2: The sound-alikes are course, road, route and way, all ways to get from one place to another.

## Solving tips

Start with purple, because the four sound-alikes share almost no meaning with the other twelve words. Then take green, where the four email parts are concrete things you can picture on a screen. Blue and yellow are the ones that blur: several words name a quality of mind or heart, so sort by who gave the name, a space agency or a feeling for another person.
