**World Vegan Day is tomorrow, Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, and it opens World Vegan Month,** according to The Vegan Society, the UK charity whose founders coined the word "vegan." Below are the weekdays of World Vegan Day through 2030, a short timeline of how the day began, and a free one-page planner for the 30 days of November.

## When is World Vegan Day in 2026?

Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. The Vegan Society says: "Every November, World Vegan Month is celebrated worldwide as a time to shine a light on the vegan movement." It is an awareness day, so schools and offices keep their own schedules. The month runs Nov. 1-30, which is 30 days with four full weekends (Nov. 7-8, 14-15, 21-22 and 28-29); Sunday, Nov. 1 and Monday, Nov. 30 sit on either side. Put it on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## How did World Vegan Day begin?

The Vegan Society's World Vegan Month page says the society "started in November 1944 when our founding members coined the word 'vegan.'" Its history page says Donald Watson called a meeting that November with five other non-dairy vegetarians, including Elsie Shrigley. Words considered and dropped included "dairyban," "vitan" and "benevore." Watson said "vegan" holds the first three and last two letters of "vegetarian."

| Year | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 1944 | Founding members coin the word "vegan" | The Vegan Society |
| 1 Nov. 1994 | First World Vegan Day, for the society's 50th anniversary | The Vegan Society |
| Later | The day is extended to World Vegan Week, then World Vegan Month | The Vegan Society |
| Nov. 1, 2026 | 33rd World Vegan Day; the society turns 82 in November | Our count from the 1944 and 1994 dates |

The society says its former President, Louise Wallis, created World Vegan Day in 1994, and that November was chosen because it is the month the society was founded.

## How can you plan World Vegan Month 2026?

Our planner splits Nov. 1-30 into four weekly plans with a tick box for each step. The ideas are ours and are about cooking and sharing meals, not health or nutrition. The society has not confirmed 2026 events or campaigns that we could list.

- **Week 1 (Nov. 1-7):** mark World Vegan Day, pick three recipes and shop for them.
- **Week 2 (Nov. 8-14):** cook one recipe and look for a cookbook at your library.
- **Week 3 (Nov. 15-21):** invite a friend to share a meal and note what you liked.
- **Week 4 (Nov. 22-30):** save your best recipes and pick what to keep after the month ends.
