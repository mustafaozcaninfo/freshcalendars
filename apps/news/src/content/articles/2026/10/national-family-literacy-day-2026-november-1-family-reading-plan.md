National Family Literacy Day is Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, a day the National Center for Families Learning (NCFL) says it established "more than 30 years ago, in collaboration with Congress." NCFL also recognizes all of November as National Family Literacy Month. This story lists the November dates, including the group's 2026 conference, and gives a free 7-day family reading plan for Nov. 1 to Nov. 7.

## What is National Family Literacy Day?

NCFL's blog post, "Celebrate National Family Literacy Month with NCFL this November!", is the source for the day. It names no founding year and no 2026 theme, so we give neither. The day is an awareness day. It is not in the federal holiday list kept by the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM), so there is no day off.

## National Family Literacy Month dates in 2026

| Date | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday, Nov. 1 | National Family Literacy Day | NCFL |
| Sunday, Nov. 1 to Monday, Nov. 30 | National Family Literacy Month | NCFL |
| Sunday, Nov. 8 to Wednesday, Nov. 11 | Families Learning Conference, San Antonio, Texas | NCFL |

Weekdays are our computation. NCFL says the 2026 Families Learning Conference "takes place November 8-11, 2026," at the Marriott Rivercenter on the River Walk in San Antonio. The conference overlaps Veterans Day on Wednesday, Nov. 11; our [Veterans Day 2026 story](/news/veterans-day-2026-wednesday-november-11/) has that date. We did not check registration or agenda details, so confirm them with NCFL.

Because the month runs Nov. 1 to Nov. 30, you can start the plan below on the day or on any later week. The [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) shows all 30 days.

## A 7-day family reading plan for Nov. 1-7

The plan below is our suggestion, not an NCFL program. NCFL's post does not prescribe activities, and nothing here is a claim about results. Each day has one small step.

| Day | Idea |
|---|---|
| Sunday, Nov. 1 | National Family Literacy Day: everyone picks one book from home |
| Monday, Nov. 2 | Read aloud together before bed |
| Tuesday, Nov. 3 | Let a child read a page to an adult |
| Wednesday, Nov. 4 | Talk about a favorite character |
| Thursday, Nov. 5 | Visit or check the website of your local library |
| Friday, Nov. 6 | Read a recipe, sign or map together |
| Saturday, Nov. 7 | Each person tells the family one thing they read this week |

The printable version has tick boxes, two groups of days and a notes area labelled "Books we read together." Print the colour sheet or the ink saver copy. Put it next to the [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) so reading time sits beside practices, lessons and other plans.
