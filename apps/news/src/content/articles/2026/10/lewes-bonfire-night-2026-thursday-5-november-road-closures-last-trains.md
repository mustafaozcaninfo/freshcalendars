**Lewes Bonfire was on Thursday, 5 November 2026; roads into Lewes closed from 4.45 p.m. and no trains were due to stop at Lewes or nearby stations from 5 p.m.**, according to a joint release from East Sussex County Council and Govia Thameslink Railway (GTR), dated 18 September 2026. All times here are UK time, Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). US Eastern Time (ET) is 5 hours behind, so processions starting around 5 p.m. were around noon ET, the United Grand Procession at about 9 p.m. was about 4 p.m. ET, and a 1 a.m. finish was 8 p.m. ET on Thursday.

The release was issued on behalf of the council, East Sussex Fire & Rescue Service, Sussex Police, Southern Railway and partners. The timeline below keeps its rail and road times next to the procession times from the Lewes Bonfire Celebrations site, as a record of the plan. A one-page printable version is in the download box.

## Which roads closed from 4.45 p.m.?

The joint release listed five closures from 4.45 p.m. on Thursday 5 November 2026:

- A27 junction with A277 Brighton Road (Ashcombe Roundabout)
- A275 Offham Road with B2116 Plumpton Road
- C7 Kingston Road junction with C324 Wellgreen Lane
- A26 junction with A2029 Malling Street
- A26 Church Lane junction with Mayhew Way

It advised Lewes residents to get vehicles home before 4 p.m. to avoid the closures and told visitors not to bring vehicles into the town. The Lewes Bonfire Celebrations site said road closures started "from about 2pm", earlier than the official 4.45 p.m.

## What are the last trains from Lewes?

The release said trains were not expected to stop at Lewes or the surrounding stations of Falmer, Cooksbridge, Glynde and Southease from 5 p.m. on Thursday 5 November until the start of service on Friday 6 November.

| Last train from Lewes | Destination | Stops, per the release |
|---|---|---|
| 16:44 | Brighton | All stations to Brighton |
| 16:55 | London Victoria | Cooksbridge, Wivelsfield, then the normal route to London |
| 16:59 | Ore | Polegate, Eastbourne, then all stations to Ore |
| 17:02 | Seaford | All stations to Seaford except Southease |

The release added that at local stations such as Berwick and Southease the last train would be earlier. It gave no time for those.

For people coming from London, the last trains back from Victoria to these stations were due at 3.24 p.m. (to Eastbourne) and 3.54 p.m. (to Ore), the release said.

## What time are the processions?

The Lewes Bonfire Celebrations site said the processions started around 5 p.m. and finished about 1 a.m. The United Grand Procession was around 9 p.m., when the societies marched together through the town for about an hour. Exact start times per society were not published on the official pages we could read.

## Do you need a ticket, and where are the fire sites?

The Lewes Bonfire Celebrations site said all bonfire society fire sites were "ticket only", with the line "No Ticket No Entry". Tickets came from Society Headquarters and the Lewes Tourist Information Centre. Cliffe Bonfire Society invited visitors to its fire site on Thursday 5 November 2026. Fire site locations, times and prices for 2026 were not published on the official pages we could read, and neither were bus diversions.

## Did the release advise people from outside Lewes to travel?

No. The release urged people who do not live in Lewes not to travel. It said the event was not suitable for children, young families or dogs, and that with 5 November falling on a Thursday, higher attendance was anticipated. It advised going to a local organised display instead. The release's other advice:

- Do not pick up spent fireworks or torches.
- Report anything suspicious to a steward or police officer.
- St John Ambulance provides first aid.
