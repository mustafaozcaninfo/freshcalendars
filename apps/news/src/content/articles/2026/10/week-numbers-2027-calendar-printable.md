**Week 1 of 2027 starts on Monday, Jan. 4**, and the year has **52 weeks** under the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) date standard, ISO 8601. The first three days of January are left over from 2026: Friday, Jan. 1 through Sunday, Jan. 3 are part of week 53 of 2026. The final week, week 52, starts on Monday, Dec. 27, 2027 and ends on Sunday, Jan. 2, 2028.

## What week is it today?

Sunday, Oct. 18, 2026, is the last day of **ISO week 42** (Oct. 12 to 18). Tomorrow starts week 43. If your office counts weeks from Sunday, the U.S. way, today is already the first day of week 43.

## How ISO week numbers work

The rule fits on one line: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week that contains the year's first Thursday. Put another way, week 1 always contains Jan. 4.

In 2027, the first Thursday is Jan. 7, so week 1 runs Jan. 4 to 10. Most years end up with 52 weeks. A year gets a 53rd week when it starts on a Thursday, or when it is a leap year that starts on a Wednesday. 2026 starts on a Thursday, which is why it has 53. After 2026, the next 53-week year is 2032.

| Year | Starts on | ISO weeks | Week 1 begins |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Thursday | 53 | Monday, Dec. 29, 2025 |
| 2027 | Friday | 52 | Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 |
| 2028 | Saturday | 52 | Monday, Jan. 3, 2028 |

Europe uses this system, and so does most business and scheduling software outside the United States. It is also what calendar apps show when you turn on week numbers with a Monday start.

## The U.S. system gives different numbers

Many American calendars, and Excel's default WEEKNUM function, count weeks differently. Weeks start on Sunday, and week 1 is simply the week that contains Jan. 1, however short it is. In 2027 that first week is just two days long (Friday, Jan. 1 and Saturday, Jan. 2). Week 2 starts Sunday, Jan. 3.

So the two systems disagree for nearly the whole year. Thursday, Nov. 25, 2027 (Thanksgiving) is ISO week 47 but U.S. week 48. The U.S. count also runs to week 53, ending with the six days from Sunday, Dec. 26 to Friday, Dec. 31. When a colleague in another country mentions "week 12," check which system they mean.

## Week numbers in Excel and Google Sheets

| Formula | System | Result for Jan. 1, 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| =ISOWEEKNUM(A1) | ISO 8601 | 53 |
| =WEEKNUM(A1,21) | ISO 8601 (Excel only) | 53 |
| =WEEKNUM(A1) | U.S., Sunday start | 1 |
| =WEEKNUM(A1,2) | Monday start, week of Jan. 1 is week 1 | 1 |

Microsoft documents ISOWEEKNUM and the WEEKNUM return types on its support site, and Google Sheets supports the same ISOWEEKNUM function. If a report shows a week number that is one higher than your wall calendar, the formula is probably the plain WEEKNUM.
