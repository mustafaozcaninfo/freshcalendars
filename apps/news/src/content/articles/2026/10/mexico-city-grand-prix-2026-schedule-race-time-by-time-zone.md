**The Mexico City Grand Prix race is Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, at 14:00 local time, which is 3 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), according to Formula 1 (F1).** F1 lists the race as 71 laps or 120 minutes, and says its timetable is subject to change. Below: every session in U.S. time zones, and the clock change that moves Sunday's start.

## What is the Mexico City Grand Prix 2026 schedule?

F1 gives these times in Mexico City local time, Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) minus 6 hours (UTC-6). The U.S. columns are our conversion, not an official schedule.

| Session | Mexico City (UTC-6) | Eastern | Central | Mountain | Pacific |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| First practice, Fri., Oct. 30 | 12:30 p.m. | 2:30 p.m. EDT | 1:30 p.m. CDT | 12:30 p.m. MDT | 11:30 a.m. PDT |
| Second practice, Fri., Oct. 30 | 4 p.m. | 6 p.m. EDT | 5 p.m. CDT | 4 p.m. MDT | 3 p.m. PDT |
| Third practice, Sat., Oct. 31 | 11:30 a.m. | 1:30 p.m. EDT | 12:30 p.m. CDT | 11:30 a.m. MDT | 10:30 a.m. PDT |
| Qualifying, Sat., Oct. 31 | 3 p.m. | 5 p.m. EDT | 4 p.m. CDT | 3 p.m. MDT | 2 p.m. PDT |
| Grand Prix, Sun., Nov. 1 | 2 p.m. | 3 p.m. EST | 2 p.m. CST | 1 p.m. MST | 12 p.m. PST |

The abbreviations are Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), Central Daylight Time (CDT), Mountain Daylight Time (MDT), Pacific Daylight Time (PDT), Central Standard Time (CST), Mountain Standard Time (MST) and Pacific Standard Time (PST).

## What time is the F1 race on Sunday?

The race starts at 2 p.m. in Mexico City on Sunday, Nov. 1. By our conversion that is 3 p.m. EST, 2 p.m. CST, 1 p.m. MST and 12 p.m. PST. Elsewhere, it is 20:00 Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in the United Kingdom and 21:00 Central European Time (CET) in central Europe.

Arizona is on MST, UTC-7, which our conversion puts one hour behind Mexico City: first practice at 11:30 a.m. MST on Friday and the race at 1 p.m. MST on Sunday.

## Why does the clock change matter for race day?

U.S. clocks fall back at 2 a.m. on Sunday, Nov. 1, the same morning as the race. Friday and Saturday sessions are still on U.S. daylight time, so Eastern viewers are 2 hours ahead of Mexico City for practice and qualifying. On Sunday the United States is on standard time, so the gap is 1 hour. That is why practice runs at 2:30 p.m. EDT on Friday for a 12:30 p.m. local start, while the race at 2 p.m. local is 3 p.m. EST, not 4 p.m.
