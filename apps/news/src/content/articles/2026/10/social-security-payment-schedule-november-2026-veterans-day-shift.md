**Social Security pays people with birthdays on the 1st through the 10th on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, one day early, because the usual second-Wednesday date, Nov. 11, is Veterans Day.** The Social Security Administration (SSA) publishes the 2026 schedule as Publication No. 05-10031, and its rule is that a payment due on a weekend or federal holiday is paid the business day before. The other November dates are Tuesday, Nov. 3, Wednesday, Nov. 18 and Wednesday, Nov. 25. The Nov. 10 and December rows below apply that rule to the 2026 weekdays; check them against SSA's PDF.

## Which Social Security payments come in November 2026?

| Date | Who is paid |
|---|---|
| Friday, Oct. 30 | Supplemental Security Income (SSI) for November (Nov. 1 is a Sunday) |
| Tuesday, Nov. 3 | Benefits started before May 1997, or Social Security plus SSI |
| **Tuesday, Nov. 10** | **Birthdays on the 1st to the 10th (moved up from Wednesday, Nov. 11, Veterans Day)** |
| Wednesday, Nov. 18 | Birthdays on the 11th to the 20th |
| Wednesday, Nov. 25 | Birthdays on the 21st to the 31st |

No SSI payment is dated in November: November's went out Friday, Oct. 30, and the next is Tuesday, Dec. 1. Our [October payment schedule story](/news/social-security-payment-schedule-october-2026-who-gets-paid-today/) explains the Oct. 30 payment in detail.

## Why does Veterans Day change one payment date?

Social Security pays by birthday group on the second, third and fourth Wednesdays of the month. In November 2026 the second Wednesday is the 11th, which is Veterans Day, a federal holiday. Because SSA pays the business day before a holiday, the 1st-to-10th group is paid Tuesday, Nov. 10. The other two Wednesday groups keep their dates. Mark Tuesday, Nov. 10 on your [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## What are the December 2026 payment dates?

| Date | Who is paid |
|---|---|
| Tuesday, Dec. 1 | SSI for December |
| Thursday, Dec. 3 | Benefits started before May 1997, or Social Security plus SSI |
| Wednesday, Dec. 9 | Birthdays on the 1st to the 10th |
| Wednesday, Dec. 16 | Birthdays on the 11th to the 20th |
| Wednesday, Dec. 23 | Birthdays on the 21st to the 31st |
| **Thursday, Dec. 31** | **SSI for January 2027** |

Two SSI payments are dated in December. SSI is normally paid on the 1st, and Jan. 1, 2027 is New Year's Day, a Friday and a federal holiday, so January's payment is moved to Thursday, Dec. 31. Put both on your [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When does the 3.6% COLA start?

American Broadcasting Company (ABC) News reported on Oct. 14, 2026 that Social Security benefits will increase 3.6% beginning in January, the cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) for 2027. SSI recipients get the higher amount in the Dec. 31 payment. The December Social Security payments in the table above are November benefits, so they use 2026 amounts.

The January 2027 dates are Wednesdays, Jan. 13, 20 and 27. For the 3rd-of-the-month group, Jan. 3, 2027 is a Sunday, and the payment has been reported as moving to Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026; check SSA's schedule for that date. Amounts and the full January table are in [Social Security COLA 2027 is 3.6%](/news/social-security-cola-2027-3-6-percent-payment-dates/).

## How is your payment date set?

- **Birthday on the 1st to the 10th:** second Wednesday of the month.
- **Birthday on the 11th to the 20th:** third Wednesday.
- **Birthday on the 21st to the 31st:** fourth Wednesday.
- **Benefits started before May 1997, or you get both Social Security and SSI:** Social Security on the 3rd of the month, SSI on the 1st.
- **Weekends and federal holidays:** the payment moves to the business day before.
