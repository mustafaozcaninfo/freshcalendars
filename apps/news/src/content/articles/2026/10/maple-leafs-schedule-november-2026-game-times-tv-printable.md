**The Toronto Maple Leafs host the Utah Mammoth on Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) at Scotiabank Arena, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) schedule.** The NHL lists 13 Leafs games in November, including the Hockey Hall of Fame Game on Sunday, Nov. 8, and a one-page printable of the month is further down.

The source is the NHL's club schedule for Toronto, accessed Oct. 31, 2026 and subject to change. U.S. clocks fall back on Sunday, Nov. 1, so every November start below is Eastern Standard Time, which is also Toronto local time. One game, in Chicago, also gets a Central Time (CT) time. Weekdays are our own calculation. The Leafs play at Vancouver on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 7 p.m. ET and have no game on Nov. 1 or Nov. 2. As of Oct. 30, the NHL standings showed Toronto at 7-6-2 with 16 points after 15 games, in the Atlantic Division.

## Maple Leafs game times and TV channels for every November game

Nine of the 13 games are at home and four are on the road. Channels are as listed in the NHL schedule: Sportsnet (SN), NHL Network (NHLN) and New England Sports Network (NESN), plus TSN4, truTV, HBO Max and Sportsnet+ (SN+). ## When is the Hockey Hall of Fame Game in 2026?

The Hockey Hall of Fame lists the Hockey Hall of Fame Game for Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. at Scotiabank Arena in Toronto, with the Maple Leafs against the New Jersey Devils. It is part of Induction Weekend, which the Hall lists as Saturday, Nov. 7 to Monday, Nov. 9. The Induction Ceremony is Monday, Nov. 9, at 7:30 p.m. at Brookfield Place in Toronto.

The Class of 2026, announced by NHL.com on June 22, 2026, is players Patrice Bergeron, Carey Price, Pekka Rinne, Keith Tkachuk and Cindy Curley, and builder Brian Burke. The Leafs' home game against Montreal on Saturday, Nov. 7 opens the weekend.

## Back-to-backs, road games and the Thanksgiving weekend

These counts come from reading the dates in the NHL schedule:

- **Back-to-backs:** Sat., Nov. 7 and Sun., Nov. 8 (both at home), and Fri., Nov. 27 and Sat., Nov. 28 (both on the road).
- **Home stand:** eight of the first nine November games are at Scotiabank Arena, from Nov. 3 to Nov. 21, with Chicago on Nov. 17 the only road game in that stretch.
- **Three straight road games:** at the Islanders on Nov. 23, at Boston on Nov. 27 and at Pittsburgh on Nov. 28, before the Nov. 30 home game against Philadelphia.
- **Nov. 27:** the 1 p.m. ET game in Boston falls the day after U.S. Thanksgiving, which is Thu., Nov. 26, 2026. Our [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has the holiday.
