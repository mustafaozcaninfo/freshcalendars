**Amazon's own announcements put "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power" season 3 on Prime Video on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, and all eight episodes of "Blade Runner 2099" on Wednesday, Nov. 25.** Every dated Prime Video title for November is listed here, with a free printable calendar.

<p class="callout"><b>Checked Nov. 10, 2026.</b> Amazon has no single November list in what we found, so this list is built from its individual announcements on aboutamazon.com and The Chosen's official site. Dates can change, so confirm on Prime Video before you plan a watch night.</p>

## What is new on Prime Video in November 2026?

Five titles have an Amazon date. The Chosen has a date on its official site, and one more title is a theater-only release. Each row names its source.

| Day and date | Title | Type | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wednesday, Nov. 4 | The Greatest | Series about Muhammad Ali, all eight episodes, streaming now | Amazon, updated Nov. 4, 2026 |
| Wednesday, Nov. 11 | The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season 3) | Series, first four episodes (episodes 5 and 6 on Nov. 18, final two on Nov. 25) | Amazon, updated Oct. 21, 2026 |
| Sunday, Nov. 15 | The Chosen (Season 6) | Series, three episodes, then weekly through Dec. 6 | thechosen.tv and 5&2 Studios, Apr. 3, 2026 |
| Wednesday, Nov. 18 | Madden | Film with Nicolas Cage, directed by David O. Russell | Amazon, updated Sept. 24, 2026 |
| Friday, Nov. 20 | Perfect Liars | Film directed by Jesús Colmenar | Amazon, updated Oct. 20, 2026 |
| Wednesday, Nov. 25 | Blade Runner 2099 | Series, all eight episodes | Amazon, updated Nov. 9, 2026 |

Amazon says The Greatest will reach "more than 240 countries and territories worldwide." The dates here are U.S. dates and does not check other regions for the other titles.

## When does Rings of Power season 3 come out?

Amazon says the first four episodes of season 3 "will premiere November 11, 2026," on Prime Video, on a page it updated Oct. 21, 2026. The page says "the fifth and sixth episodes will be available to stream on November 18," with "the final two episodes of the season releasing on November 25." That makes eight episodes in three drops (our tally). Amazon gives no release time.

## When does Blade Runner 2099 come out?

Amazon says "all eight episodes of Blade Runner 2099 will premiere November 25, 2026." Amazon first published its page on July 24, 2026 and updated it Oct. 12, 2026. Nov. 25 is the day before Thanksgiving; see [when Thanksgiving falls](/when-is/thanksgiving/). For football and parade times on Nov. 26, see the [Thanksgiving 2026 TV schedule](/news/thanksgiving-2026-tv-schedule-macys-parade-nfl-games/).

## Which Prime Video dates fall on Wednesdays?

Four of the five Amazon title dates are Wednesdays: Nov. 4, 11, 18 and 25. The fifth, Perfect Liars on Nov. 20, is a Friday. We checked each weekday against the 2026 calendar. The pattern is a count of these five announcements, not a statement about how Amazon schedules its releases.

## What are the two films?

Amazon says "Madden will be available to stream globally starting November 18 on Prime Video." The film stars Nicolas Cage and is directed by David O. Russell. Amazon says "Perfect Liars premieres exclusively on Prime Video on November 20 worldwide." Jesús Colmenar directed it. Black Friday is the following Friday, Nov. 27; see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/).

## What is not on Prime Video in November?

Amazon says "I Play Rocky is out now in select theaters" and "will be in theaters everywhere later in November," on a page it updated Nov. 6, 2026. Amazon has announced no Prime Video date for it, so it is not in the table.

## Is The Chosen season 6 on Prime Video?

Yes. The official site thechosen.tv says "Season 6 will stream on Prime Video starting November 15th, 2026." A 5&2 Studios press release dated Apr. 3, 2026 says the season "will debut three episodes on November 15, followed by the release of a weekly episode through December 6." Weekly episodes therefore fall on Sundays, Nov. 22 and 29 and Dec. 6 (our weekday check). BroadwayWorld reported on Oct. 15, 2026 that the season has six episodes.
