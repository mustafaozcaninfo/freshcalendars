**Amazon's own announcements put "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power" season 3 on Prime Video on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, and all eight episodes of "Blade Runner 2099" on Wednesday, Nov. 25.** The table below lists every dated Prime Video title for November, and a free printable calendar follows it.

<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 26, 2026.</b> Amazon has no single November list in what we found, so this list is built from its individual announcements on aboutamazon.com. Dates can change, so confirm on Prime Video before you plan a watch night.</p>

## What is new on Prime Video in November 2026?

Five titles have an Amazon date. One more is reported by a news outlet, and one is a theater-only release. Each row names its source.

| Day and date | Title | Type | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wednesday, Nov. 4 | The Greatest | Series about Muhammad Ali, all eight episodes | Amazon |
| Wednesday, Nov. 11 | The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season 3) | Series, first four episodes | Amazon, updated Oct. 21, 2026 |
| Sunday, Nov. 15 | The Chosen (Season 6) | Series, reported date | Catholic World Report, Apr. 3, 2026 |
| Wednesday, Nov. 18 | Madden | Film with Nicolas Cage, directed by David O. Russell | Amazon, updated Sept. 24, 2026 |
| Friday, Nov. 20 | Perfect Liars | Film directed by Jesús Colmenar | Amazon, updated Oct. 20, 2026 |
| Wednesday, Nov. 25 | Blade Runner 2099 | Series, all eight episodes | Amazon, updated Oct. 12, 2026 |

Amazon says The Greatest will reach "more than 240 countries and territories worldwide." This story uses U.S. dates and does not check other regions for the other titles.

## When does Rings of Power season 3 come out?

Amazon says the first four episodes of season 3 "will premiere November 11, 2026," on Prime Video, on a page it updated Oct. 21, 2026. The page lists further episodes weekly through Nov. 25; check the page for the episode count, which this story does not state.

## When does Blade Runner 2099 come out?

Amazon says "all eight episodes of Blade Runner 2099 will premiere November 25, 2026." Amazon first published its page on July 24, 2026 and updated it Oct. 12, 2026. Nov. 25 is the day before Thanksgiving; see [when Thanksgiving falls](/when-is/thanksgiving/). For football and parade times on Nov. 26, see the [Thanksgiving 2026 TV schedule](/news/thanksgiving-2026-tv-schedule-macys-parade-nfl-games/).

## Which Prime Video dates fall on Wednesdays?

Four of the five Amazon dates are Wednesdays: Nov. 4, 11, 18 and 25. The fifth, Perfect Liars on Nov. 20, is a Friday. We checked each weekday against the 2026 calendar. The pattern is a count of these five announcements, not a statement about how Amazon schedules its releases.

## What are the two films?

Amazon says "Madden will be available to stream globally starting November 18 on Prime Video." The film stars Nicolas Cage and is directed by David O. Russell. Amazon says "Perfect Liars premieres exclusively on Prime Video on November 20 worldwide." Jesús Colmenar directed it. Black Friday is the following Friday, Nov. 27; see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/).

## What is not on Prime Video in November?

Amazon says "I Play Rocky" opens "in select theaters on November 6, 2026." Amazon has announced no Prime Video date for it, so it is not in the table.

## Is The Chosen season 6 on Prime Video?

Catholic World Report reported on Apr. 3, 2026 that season 6 of "The Chosen" premieres Nov. 15, 2026, with three episodes, then weekly through Dec. 6. We did not find an Amazon page for it, so the date is labeled as reported and is in the printable the same way.
