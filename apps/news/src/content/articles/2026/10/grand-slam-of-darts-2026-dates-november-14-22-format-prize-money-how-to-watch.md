**The Grand Slam of Darts 2026 runs from Saturday, Nov. 14 to Sunday, Nov. 22 at WV Active Aldersley in Wolverhampton, England, according to Sky Sports, and in the U.S. NBC Sports says Peacock streams Sky Sports' coverage.** The Professional Darts Corporation (PDC) organizes the event, sponsored as the Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts. Sky Sports lists sessions at 1 p.m. and 7 p.m. GMT, which is 8 a.m. and 2 p.m. ET, and the final at 7:30 p.m. GMT on Sunday, Nov. 22 (2:30 p.m. ET).

## How does the 48-player format work?

Sky Sports says the field expands to 48 players for the first time, in 16 groups of three. Only the group winner moves on, so each player has two group matches. A win is worth two points and a loss none. The 16 group winners go into a knockout bracket from round two (the last 16): Group A winner against Group B winner, and so on, then quarter-finals, semi-finals and the final.

## How did players qualify for the Grand Slam of Darts 2026?

Sky Sports and Dartsnews both report two routes: the top 16 were seeded from the PDC Order of Merit, the rankings list, and ten places went through a Tour Card Holder Qualifier on Friday, Nov. 6, 2026.

The PDC page published on Nov. 5, 2026 said 91 players were entered in the qualifier for ten places. It was played at the Mattioli Arena in Leicester.

## How much is the Grand Slam of Darts prize money?

Sky Sports says the prize fund rises from £650,000 in 2025 to £1,000,000 in 2026 with the larger field.

## What time is the Grand Slam of Darts in the US?

The UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) for the whole event. U.S. clocks went back on Nov. 1, so Eastern Time (ET) is GMT minus 5 hours, Central Time (CT) minus 6, Mountain Time (MT) minus 7 and Pacific Time (PT) minus 8. The GMT times are Sky Sports' session times; the other columns are computed.

| Session (GMT) | ET | CT | MT | PT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 p.m. | 8 a.m. | 7 a.m. | 6 a.m. | 5 a.m. |
| 7 p.m. | 2 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. |
