**The 84th Golden Globes air Sunday, Jan. 10, 2027, on CBS, and the 99th Oscars follow on Sunday, March 14, 2027, live on ABC at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) / 4 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), according to the Golden Globes and the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences.** Golden Globe nominations come first, on Monday, Dec. 7, 2026, and Oscar nominations on Thursday, Jan. 21, 2027. The table below puts every confirmed date of the season in one place, and a printable version is further down.

## Awards season 2027 calendar: dates, networks and times

Each row comes from the organizer's own announcement. "None listed" and "Not listed" mean the organizer's release we used does not give that detail.

| Date | Event | Network | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon., Nov. 16, 2026 | Grammy nominations announced | None listed | Not listed |
| Mon., Dec. 7, 2026 | Golden Globe nominations announced | None listed | Not listed |
| Sun., Jan. 3, 2027 | 32nd Critics Choice Awards, The Barker Hangar | USA Network | 7 to 10 p.m. ET / 4 to 7 p.m. PT |
| Sun., Jan. 10, 2027 | 84th Golden Globes | CBS, Paramount+ | Not listed |
| Thu., Jan. 21, 2027 | Oscar nominations announced | None listed | Not listed |
| Sun., Feb. 7, 2027 | 69th Grammy Awards, Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles | ABC, Disney+, Hulu | Not listed |
| Sun., March 14, 2027 | 99th Oscars, Dolby Theatre at Ovation Hollywood | ABC | 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT |

The date and weekday for each row come from the organizers' pages cited under Sources. We have no confirmed dates for the British Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA) Film Awards or the Actor Awards, so they are left out until the organizers post them.

## Golden Globes 2027: ceremony, nominations and ballot deadline

The Golden Globes announced its 84th ceremony timetable on May 7, 2026. The show is Sunday, Jan. 10, 2027, on CBS, with streaming on Paramount+ in the U.S. Nominations are announced Monday, Dec. 7, 2026. Final ballots are due Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027, at 5 p.m. PT.

## Oscars 2027: show time, nominations and voting dates

The Academy announced the 99th Oscars on April 7, 2026, with ABC. The release says the show "will air live at 7 p.m. EST / 4 p.m. PST on ABC" from the Dolby Theatre at Ovation Hollywood. We write ET and PT because U.S. daylight saving time starts at 2 a.m. that same Sunday, so clocks spring forward that morning; see [our 2027 clock-change dates](/news/clock-changes-2027-when-does-daylight-saving-time-start-again-march-14/) for the full list.

The Academy's key dates page lists the steps toward the show. It adds that "all dates are subject to change."

| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Nominations voting | Mon., Jan. 11, 2027, 9 a.m. PT, to Fri., Jan. 15, 2027, 5 p.m. PT |
| Nominations announcement | Thu., Jan. 21, 2027 |
| Nominees luncheon | Tue., Feb. 16, 2027 |
| Final voting | Thu., Feb. 25, to Thu., March 4, 2027 |
| 99th Oscars | Sun., March 14, 2027 |

## How to watch the 2027 awards shows

For the Golden Globes, the Golden Globes name CBS and Paramount+ in the U.S. The Oscars are on ABC. The Recording Academy says the Grammys on Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027, are simulcast on ABC, Disney+ and Hulu; our [Grammy nominations story](/news/grammy-nominations-2027-date-69th-grammys-february-7/) covers that season in detail. The Critics Choice Association lists USA Network for its Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027, show. A start time for the Golden Globes and the Grammys was not in the releases we used.

For game fans, [The Game Awards 2026 story](/news/the-game-awards-2026-tickets-october-29-date-december-10-nominations/) has its own date. Another big date in the same stretch is [Super Bowl 61 (LXI)](/news/super-bowl-lxi-2027-date-sofi-stadium-channel-halftime/).
