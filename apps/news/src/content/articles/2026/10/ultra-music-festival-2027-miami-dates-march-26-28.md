**Ultra Music Festival returns to Bayfront Park in downtown Miami on March 26-28, 2027**, according to the festival's official site, which lists the event as its 27th edition. Tickets are on sale, and the festival is open only to people 21 and older. This time the three days line up with Easter weekend: Friday, March 26 is Good Friday, and Sunday, March 28 is Easter.

## Ultra 2027 at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Dates | Friday-Sunday, March 26-28, 2027 |
| Place | Bayfront Park, downtown Miami, on Biscayne Bay |
| Edition | 27th |
| Age | 21 and over |
| Tickets | On sale at ultramusicfestival.com |
| Lineup | Phase 1 announced |

## The Phase 1 lineup

Ultra released a first wave of artists in October 2026. EDM Identity, an electronic dance music news site, reported on Oct. 14 that it includes Armin van Buuren, Dom Dolla, Steve Aoki, Michael Bibi, and a back-to-back set by Marshmello and Subtronics. More names are typically announced in later phases. The official site has the current list and set times once they are published.

## Why the timing matters

Ultra lands at the end of South Florida's shared spring break. Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach county schools are all off March 22-26, 2027, so the festival weekend overlaps with families heading home and with Easter church services and gatherings. Expect heavy traffic downtown and on the causeways, and book hotels early. See the school calendars for [Miami-Dade](/news/miami-dade-school-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), [Broward](/news/broward-county-school-calendar-2026-27/) and [Palm Beach](/news/palm-beach-county-school-calendar-2026-27/).

Travelers flying in from abroad should note that Good Friday is a public holiday in Canada, the UK and much of Europe and Latin America, which can raise airfares for the long weekend. U.S. stock markets are closed on Good Friday too: see the [2027 stock market holidays](/news/stock-market-holidays-2027-nyse-closed-dates/).

## Before you go

Bring a valid photo ID that shows your age; the 21-and-over rule applies to everyone. Buy tickets only from the official site or its authorized partners, since resold wristbands may be invalid. Check the festival's list of prohibited items before packing, because the rules on bags and other items are strict and change from year to year.

Easter falls early in 2027. See [when Easter falls](/when-is/easter/) and [when Good Friday falls](/when-is/good-friday/) in other years, and mark the weekend on a [March 2027 printable calendar](/march-calendars.html).
