**Premier League Gameweek 10 opens Friday, Nov. 6, 2026 with Everton v Coventry City at 8pm UK time, which is 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), on Sky Sports, according to the Premier League.** Nine more matches follow on Saturday, Nov. 7 and Sunday, Nov. 8, and the league says all fixtures are Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and 15:00 kick-offs unless otherwise stated.

The table below gives every match in UK time (GMT), ET and Pacific Time (PT), with the UK channel. The Premier League published the November broadcast selections on Sept. 21, 2026, and a printable version is further down.

## Gameweek 10 kick-off times and UK TV

From this weekend the UK is 5 hours ahead of ET, because the Premier League lists times in GMT from Sunday, Oct. 25 and U.S. clocks fall back on Sunday, Nov. 1. ET times match those NBC Sports published on Oct. 31, 2026; PT is ET minus 3 hours, worked out by Fresh Calendars.

## Which Gameweek 10 matches have no UK TV listing?

Arsenal v Hull City and Fulham v Newcastle United, both Saturday, Nov. 7 at 3pm UK time (10 a.m. ET), have no UK TV broadcaster listed by the Premier League. In the UK the league lists Sky Sports for seven matches and TNT Sports for one, Leeds United v Tottenham Hotspur.

## Why did three Sunday matches move?

The Premier League says Crystal Palace v Liverpool, Ipswich Town v Bournemouth and Sunderland v Chelsea were moved because of the teams' participation in UEFA Europa/Conference League. UEFA competitions displace the matches; the league's Sept. 21 notice lists all three at 2pm UK time on Sunday, Nov. 8, alongside Brighton & Hove Albion v Brentford at the same time, which makes four Sunday matches at 2pm, or 9 a.m. ET. Manchester United v Aston Villa follows at 4:30pm.

## What time is Crystal Palace vs Liverpool?

Crystal Palace host Liverpool at Selhurst Park on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 2pm UK time, on Sky Sports, per the Premier League. That is 9 a.m. ET and 6 a.m. PT. NBC Sports lists the match at 9 a.m. ET but has not yet listed which U.S. network shows it.

## What time is Man Utd vs Aston Villa?

Manchester United v Aston Villa closes the round at Old Trafford on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 4:30pm UK time, on Sky Sports, per the Premier League. That is 11:30 a.m. ET and 8:30 a.m. PT.

## How can you watch Premier League matches in the US?

NBC Sports says that in the USA you can watch all 380 Premier League games during the 2026-27 season across NBC, USA and Peacock. It has not yet listed a network for each Gameweek 10 match, so check its listings closer to the date.
