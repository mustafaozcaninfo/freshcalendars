**Premier League Gameweek 10 opened Friday, Nov. 6, 2026 with Everton v Coventry City at 8pm UK time, which was 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), on Sky Sports, according to the Premier League.** Nine more matches followed on Saturday, Nov. 7 and Sunday, Nov. 8, and the league said all fixtures were Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and 15:00 kick-offs unless otherwise stated. The table below adds each result, which ESPN and Sky Sports agree on.

The table gives every match in UK time (GMT), ET and Pacific Time (PT), with the UK channel and the result. The Premier League published the November broadcast selections on Sept. 21, 2026, and a printable version is further down.

## Which Gameweek 10 matches have no UK TV listing?

Arsenal v Hull City and Fulham v Newcastle United, both Saturday, Nov. 7 at 3pm UK time (10 a.m. ET), had no UK TV broadcaster listed by the Premier League. In the UK the league listed Sky Sports for seven matches and TNT Sports for one, Leeds United v Tottenham Hotspur.

## Why did three Sunday matches move?

The Premier League said Crystal Palace v Liverpool, Ipswich Town v Bournemouth and Sunderland v Chelsea were moved because of the teams' participation in UEFA Europa/Conference League. UEFA competitions displace the matches; the league's Sept. 21 notice listed all three at 2pm UK time on Sunday, Nov. 8, alongside Brighton & Hove Albion v Brentford at the same time, which made four Sunday matches at 2pm, or 9 a.m. ET. Manchester United v Aston Villa followed at 4:30pm.

## What time was Crystal Palace vs Liverpool?

Crystal Palace hosted Liverpool at Selhurst Park on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 2pm UK time, on Sky Sports, per the Premier League. That was 9 a.m. ET and 6 a.m. PT. Liverpool won 3-1, according to ESPN and Sky Sports.

## What time was Man Utd vs Aston Villa?

Manchester United v Aston Villa closed the round at Old Trafford on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 4:30pm UK time, on Sky Sports, per the Premier League. That was 11:30 a.m. ET and 8:30 a.m. PT. The match ended 4-4, according to ESPN and Sky Sports.
