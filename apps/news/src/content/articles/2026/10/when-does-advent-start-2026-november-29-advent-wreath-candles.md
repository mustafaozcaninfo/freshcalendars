**Advent 2026 starts on Sunday, Nov. 29**, the First Sunday of Advent in the Liturgical Calendar 2026 of the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB). The season ends on Thursday, Dec. 24, which makes Advent 26 days long in 2026 (our count, first Sunday through Dec. 24, both days included). Christmas follows on Friday, Dec. 25.

This story covers the church season. For the countdown-style calendars that begin on Dec. 1, see our story on [Advent calendar 2026 dates](/news/advent-calendar-2026-dates-free-printable/).

## What are the Advent 2026 dates and candle colors?

The USCCB describes Advent as the four Sundays, and the weekdays, leading up to Christmas. Its Advent page says the season begins with Evening Prayer I of the Sunday that falls on or closest to Nov. 30. The table gives each Sunday for 2026, with the color from the USCCB's wreath description.

| Sunday | Date | Candle color | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Sunday of Advent | Sun., Nov. 29, 2026 | Purple | Advent begins |
| Second Sunday of Advent | Sun., Dec. 6, 2026 | Purple | |
| Third Sunday of Advent | Sun., Dec. 13, 2026 | Rose | Gaudete Sunday |
| Fourth Sunday of Advent | Sun., Dec. 20, 2026 | Purple | |
| Last day of Advent | Thu., Dec. 24, 2026 | None | Christmas is Fri., Dec. 25 |

Weekdays are computed for 2026 and match the USCCB calendar for the first Sunday and the season's end. The Sunday before, 22 November, is Stir-up Sunday in Britain; see [Stir-up Sunday 2026 dates and the pudding tradition](/news/stir-up-sunday-2026-november-22-christmas-pudding/).

## What order are the Advent wreath candles lit in?

The USCCB describes the wreath as "a circle of evergreen branches into which four candles are inserted, representing the four weeks of Advent." Three of the candles are purple. According to the USCCB, the purple candles symbolize prayer, penance and preparation. The rose candle is lit on the third Sunday, which the USCCB calls "the Sunday of rejoicing."

That puts purple on Nov. 29 and Dec. 6, rose on Dec. 13 and purple again on Dec. 20. The [printable tracker](#downloads) lists the same order, so you can tick each Sunday as you go.

## When is Gaudete Sunday 2026?

Gaudete Sunday is the third Sunday of Advent, which is **Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026**. The USCCB says the liturgical color for Advent is purple, "just like Lent," and that on Gaudete Sunday the color may be rose instead of purple.

## Why does Advent last 26 days in 2026?

Advent ends on Christmas Eve, but its first day moves with the calendar, so the season is not the same length each year. The count below runs from the first Sunday through Dec. 24, both days included.

| Year | First Sunday of Advent | Days through Dec. 24 | Source for start date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Sun., Nov. 29 | 26 | USCCB Liturgical Calendar 2026 |
| 2027 | Sun., Nov. 28 | 27 | USCCB Liturgical Calendar 2027 |

The day counts are our computation from those dates. The USCCB 2026 calendar also shows that the Sunday cycle of readings, Year B, runs from Nov. 29, 2026, to Nov. 21, 2027. Its December 2026 pages are headed "Year B, Weekdays I", so the weekday readings follow cycle I.

## Which feast days fall between Nov. 29 and Dec. 24, 2026?

The December 2026 pages of the USCCB "Liturgical Calendar for the Dioceses of the United States of America 2026" list these days between the first Sunday of Advent and Christmas.

| Date | USCCB listing | Color listed |
|---|---|---|
| Sun., Dec. 6 | Second Sunday of Advent | Violet |
| Tue., Dec. 8 | The Immaculate Conception of the Blessed Virgin Mary (Patronal Feastday of the United States of America), Solemnity, Holyday of Obligation | White |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | Our Lady of Guadalupe, Feast (USA) | White |
| Sun., Dec. 13 | Third Sunday of Advent | Violet or rose |
| Sun., Dec. 20 | Fourth Sunday of Advent | Violet |
| Fri., Dec. 25 | The Nativity of the Lord (Christmas), Solemnity, Holyday of Obligation | White |

The optional memorial of Saint Nicholas is not listed for 2026 because Dec. 6 is a Sunday; in the USCCB's December 2025 page it appeared as "[Saint Nicholas, Bishop]". ## Do other churches start Advent on the same Sunday?

Several do, though the color and the calendar can differ. The table gives each church's own published 2026 dates.

| Church | 2026 Advent dates | Color |
|---|---|---|
| Roman Catholic (USCCB) | Sun., Nov. 29 to Thu., Dec. 24 | Violet; rose on Dec. 13 |
| Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA) | Nov. 29 through Dec. 24 | Blue |
| Revised Common Lectionary (RCL) | Sundays Nov. 29 to Dec. 20 (Year B, 2026-27) | Not stated on the page |
| Orthodox Church in America (OCA) | Nativity (St. Philip's) Fast, Nov. 15 through Dec. 24 | Not stated on the page |

The ELCA "Year B 2026/2027" Church Year Calendar lists "Advent Nov. 29, 2026 through Dec. 24, 2026" and gives the color for Sunday, Nov. 29, the First Sunday of Advent, as blue. United Methodist Communications says "the color for Advent has traditionally been purple, although some churches have adopted blue." The Revised Common Lectionary at the Vanderbilt Divinity Library lists Year B (2026-27) with Advent Sundays from Nov. 29 to Dec. 20.

The Orthodox Church in America lists the Nativity Fast, also called St. Philip's Fast, as Nov. 15 through Dec. 24. Nov. 15, 2026 is a Sunday. That is a fast, not the Western Advent season, so it starts two weeks earlier. Check your own church for its calendar and customs.
