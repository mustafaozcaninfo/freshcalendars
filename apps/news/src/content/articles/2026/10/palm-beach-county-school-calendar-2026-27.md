**Students in the School District of Palm Beach County get the full week of Thanksgiving off, Nov. 23-27, 2026**, and a winter break that runs from Dec. 21 to Jan. 1, according to the 2026-27 calendar the School Board approved on April 15, 2026. The school year, which began Aug. 10, ends for students on **Thursday, May 27, 2027**.

## Palm Beach County 2026-27 breaks

| Break | Dates | Students return |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Tuesday, Jan. 5 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027 | Tuesday, March 30 |

Two breaks are effectively longer than they look. Monday, Jan. 4 and Monday, March 29 are teacher duty days with no students, so winter break stretches to 17 days counting weekends and spring break to 10 days.

Sept. 21 falls on Yom Kippur this year, and Nov. 11 is Veterans Day. Election Day, Nov. 3, is a student holiday, and many schools serve as polling places.

## Grading periods

Elementary schools run on trimesters: Aug. 10 to Nov. 2 (60 days), Nov. 4 to Feb. 12 (55 days) and Feb. 16 to May 27 (66 days). Middle and high schools use quarters: Aug. 10 to Oct. 9, Oct. 13 to Dec. 18, Jan. 5 to March 19 and March 30 to May 27. The first semester has 85 student days and the second 94.

## District offices in summer

The district says its offices will close on Fridays in June 2027 and on Friday, March 26, 2027, Good Friday.

## Neighboring counties

Palm Beach County shares its Thanksgiving week, winter break and spring break with Broward County, whose last day is Friday, May 28. Miami-Dade starts and ends later, finishing on June 3. Compare the [Broward County school calendar](/news/broward-county-school-calendar-2026-27/) and the [Miami-Dade school calendar](/news/miami-dade-school-calendar-2026-27-breaks/). Spring break overlaps the [Ultra Music Festival on March 26-28](/news/ultra-music-festival-2027-miami-dates-march-26-28/), so expect busy roads toward Miami that weekend.
