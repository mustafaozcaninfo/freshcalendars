**The 2026 National Football League (NFL) trade deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10, at 1 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 4 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the league's important dates as posted by Seahawks.com.** The post, "NFL Announces Important Dates For 2026-2027" (July 7, 2026), says "The trade deadline for the 2026 season is November 10 at 1 p.m."

<p class="callout"><b>Last updated Oct. 29, 2026, 11:50 a.m. ET.</b> The 4 p.m. ET time now comes straight from the league calendar quoted on Seahawks.com, which says "All trading ends for 2026 at 4:00 p.m., New York time." Week 10 starts two days after the deadline, on Thu., Nov. 12.</p>

This page adds a time-zone table and a one-page countdown printable that runs from the deadline to the Super Bowl. Both are below.

## What time is the NFL trade deadline in my time zone?

Chargers.com ("What You Need to Know About the Chargers Ahead of 2026 NFL Trade Deadline," Oct. 21, 2026) gives the time as "1 p.m. (PT)" and says "all trades must be submitted to the league by 1 p.m. (PT) on that day." Daylight saving time ends on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, so on Nov. 10 the clocks are already on standard time. The conversions below are our calculation, not a league figure.

| Time zone | Deadline on Tue., Nov. 10, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Eastern Time (ET) | 4 p.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | 3 p.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 2 p.m. |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 1 p.m. |
| Alaska Standard Time (AKST) | 12 p.m. |
| Hawaii-Aleutian Standard Time (HST) | 11 a.m. |
| United Kingdom (UK), Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | 9 p.m. |

Our [daylight saving time page](/when-is/daylight-savings/) has the Nov. 1 clock change.

## How does the 2026 deadline compare with 2025?

NFL.com's trade tracker gives the 2025 cutoff as "Nov. 4's 4 p.m. ET," a Tuesday. The 2026 deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10, so it falls six days later in the calendar. The 4 p.m. ET time is the same in both years by our calculation.

| Season | Deadline | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Tue., Nov. 4, 4 p.m. ET | NFL.com tracker |
| 2026 | Tue., Nov. 10, 4 p.m. ET (1 p.m. PT) | Seahawks.com and Chargers.com |

## What comes after the deadline?

The Seahawks.com post also lists the season's key dates. The regular season began on Wednesday, Sept. 9, 2026. Week 18 is Saturday, Jan. 9 and Sunday, Jan. 10, 2027, Wild Card weekend is Jan. 16 to 18, 2027, and Super Bowl 61 (LXI) is Feb. 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. Our [Super Bowl LXI date story](/news/super-bowl-lxi-2027-date-sofi-stadium-channel-halftime/) covers that game.

Fox Sports reports that bye weeks run from Week 5 to Week 14. That is a reported schedule detail, not one we confirmed with the league.

On Thanksgiving, NFL.com's Bills schedule page lists "Chiefs at Bills, Thursday, November 26th, 8:20 PM, NBC," with NBC being the National Broadcasting Company. We did not verify the other Thanksgiving games for this page. Our [Thanksgiving football schedule](/news/thanksgiving-football-schedule-2026-nfl-college-games/) has more, and the [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) gives the holiday itself.
