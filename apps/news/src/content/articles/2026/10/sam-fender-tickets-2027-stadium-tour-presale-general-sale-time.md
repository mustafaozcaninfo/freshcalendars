**Sam Fender's general sale for the Proper Massive Stadium Tour 2027 was set for Friday, Oct. 30, 2026 at 9:30 a.m. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), 5:30 a.m. Eastern Time (ET)**, according to Ticketmaster UK and samfender.com. A day earlier, on Thursday, Oct. 29, the Newcastle United presale for the St. James' Park shows was set to start at 9:30 a.m. UK time, the club said. Both sale days have passed; this story keeps the sale times for the record.

The United Kingdom (UK) went back to GMT on Oct. 25 and US clocks changed on Nov. 1, so on both sale days GMT was 4 hours ahead of ET. Our table below converts the time for every US zone, and a one-page checklist of the UK, Irish and Belgian show dates is in the downloads. North American dates are further down.

## What time did Sam Fender tickets go on sale?

| Sale | Date | UK and Ireland (GMT) | ET | Central Time (CT) | Mountain Time (MT) | PT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newcastle United presale (St. James' Park shows) | Thu., Oct. 29, 2026 | 9:30 a.m. | 5:30 a.m. | 4:30 a.m. | 3:30 a.m. | 2:30 a.m. |
| General sale | Fri., Oct. 30, 2026 | 9:30 a.m. | 5:30 a.m. | 4:30 a.m. | 3:30 a.m. | 2:30 a.m. |

The time conversions are our calculation. Ireland uses the same clock as the UK. We did not find a separate Irish sale time; samfender.com says general sale is Friday, Oct. 30. Our [UK and Europe clocks story](/news/uk-europe-clocks-go-back-october-25-2026-time-difference/) explains the clock change.

## Who can use the Newcastle United presale?

Newcastle United said eligible supporters could access the presale from 9:30 a.m. on Oct. 29, 2026. The club named Season Ticket Holders and Mags Members as eligible. New members had to sign up by 5 p.m. on Oct. 27. It covers the St. James' Park shows on Thursday, June 3 and Saturday, June 5, 2027, presented by Kilimanjaro Live. The club said all remaining tickets would go on general sale at 9:30 a.m. on Oct. 30 via samfender.com.

samfender.com also described an album pre-order presale on Wednesday, Oct. 28 at 9:30 a.m., and its page said access was closed when we checked on Oct. 28. It adds that entry to the presale does not guarantee tickets.

## What are the ticket limits and prices?

samfender.com says the maximum is 6 tickets per person. No official ticket prices were on the pages we checked on Oct. 28, 2026, so this story does not quote any.

## What else has been reported?

The music magazine New Musical Express (NME) and the station Radio X reported that general sale was at 9:30 a.m. GMT, matching the official pages. NME also reported a Rock Werchter festival headline slot in Belgium on July 3, 2027, which samfender.com now lists too, as Rock Werchter in Brussels on its tour page.
