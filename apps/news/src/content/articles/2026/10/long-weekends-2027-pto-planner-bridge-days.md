**Ten paid time off (PTO) days can become 27 days off in 2027**, if you work Monday to Friday and get the 12 federal holiday days off that the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists for the year. We checked every combination of bridge days on that schedule, and four blocks beat the rest: two single Fridays in November, the Christmas and New Year's stretch, and a nine-day Memorial Day block.

This is a computed plan, not an OPM product. It assumes the federal schedule from our [2027 federal holidays list](/news/federal-holidays-2027-observed-dates-list/), including the Friday, Dec. 31, 2027 day off for New Year's Day 2028. Private employers are not required to give federal holidays, so move the dates to match your own holiday list.

## Which 2027 holidays already make long weekends?

Eight of the 12 days off give a three-day weekend with no PTO at all: New Year's Day (Fri., Jan. 1), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Mon., Jan. 18), Washington's Birthday (Mon., Feb. 15), Memorial Day (Mon., May 31), Juneteenth (observed Fri., June 18), Independence Day (observed Mon., July 5), Labor Day (Mon., Sept. 6) and Columbus Day (Mon., Oct. 11). Our [Memorial Day](/when-is/memorial-day/) and [Juneteenth](/when-is/juneteenth/) pages show those dates year by year.

Veterans Day (Thu., Nov. 11) and Thanksgiving (Thu., Nov. 25) are different. Each stands alone in the middle of the week, so each is one day off unless you bridge it.

## Bridge plan for each 2027 federal holiday

The table gives, for each holiday, the days off in a row without PTO, the weekdays to request and the block you get. A bridge day is a weekday between a holiday and a weekend.

| Holiday (observed) | Off without PTO | PTO days to request | Days off in a row |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day, Fri., Jan. 1 | Jan. 1-3 (3 days) | Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 (1 day) | Dec. 31, 2026 to Jan. 3 (4 days) |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Mon., Jan. 18 | Jan. 16-18 (3) | Tue., Jan. 19 to Fri., Jan. 22 (4) | Jan. 16-24 (9) |
| Washington's Birthday, Mon., Feb. 15 | Feb. 13-15 (3) | Tue., Feb. 16 to Fri., Feb. 19 (4) | Feb. 13-21 (9) |
| Memorial Day, Mon., May 31 | May 29-31 (3) | Tue., June 1 to Fri., June 4 (4) | May 29 to June 6 (9) |
| Juneteenth, Fri., June 18 | June 18-20 (3) | Mon., June 14 to Thu., June 17 (4) | June 12-20 (9) |
| Independence Day, Mon., July 5 | July 3-5 (3) | Tue., July 6 to Fri., July 9 (4) | July 3-11 (9) |
| Labor Day, Mon., Sept. 6 | Sept. 4-6 (3) | Tue., Sept. 7 to Fri., Sept. 10 (4) | Sept. 4-12 (9) |
| Columbus Day, Mon., Oct. 11 | Oct. 9-11 (3) | Tue., Oct. 12 to Fri., Oct. 15 (4) | Oct. 9-17 (9) |
| Veterans Day, Thu., Nov. 11 | Nov. 11 (1) | Fri., Nov. 12 (1) | Nov. 11-14 (4) |
| Thanksgiving, Thu., Nov. 25 | Nov. 25 (1) | Fri., Nov. 26 (1) | Nov. 25-28 (4) |
| Christmas, Fri., Dec. 24, and New Year's Day 2028, Fri., Dec. 31 | Dec. 24-26 and Dec. 31 to Jan. 2 (3 each) | Mon., Dec. 27 to Thu., Dec. 30 (4) | Dec. 24 to Jan. 2, 2028 (10) |

The Friday and Monday holidays all work the same way: four PTO days fill the week next to the long weekend and give nine days off. The two Thursdays are the cheap ones, at one PTO day for four days off. The December block joins two three-day weekends, so four PTO days give 10 days.

## The 10-day PTO plan for 2027

Spending the 10 days on the best four blocks gives this plan. We counted every set of non-overlapping blocks that fits in 10 PTO days on this schedule, and 27 is the highest total.

| Request off | PTO days | Days off in a row | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tue., June 1 to Fri., June 4 | 4 | May 29 to June 6 (9) | Memorial Day, Mon., May 31 |
| Fri., Nov. 12 | 1 | Nov. 11-14 (4) | Veterans Day, Thu., Nov. 11 |
| Fri., Nov. 26 | 1 | Nov. 25-28 (4) | Thanksgiving, Thu., Nov. 25 |
| Mon., Dec. 27 to Thu., Dec. 30 | 4 | Dec. 24 to Jan. 2, 2028 (10) | Christmas, Fri., Dec. 24, and New Year's Day 2028, Fri., Dec. 31 |
| **Total** | **10** | **27 days** | |

The Memorial Day block can swap places with Columbus Day: Tue., Oct. 12 to Fri., Oct. 15 also costs 4 PTO days and gives nine days off, Oct. 9-17. Either way the total is 27.

If your 10 days must cover Juneteenth and Independence Day, taking every weekday from Mon., June 21 to Fri., July 2 gives 18 days off in a row, June 18 to July 5. That is fewer days off than the four-block plan.
