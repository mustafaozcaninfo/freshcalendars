**Spotify has not announced the 2026 Wrapped date**, and its newsroom, "Spotify - For the Record," had no launch day as of Oct. 26, 2026. The Spotify for Artists page "Submitting Your Artist Messages for 2026 Wrapped" says "Until November 6, all artists can submit a short video message in Spotify for Artists to thank their fans for listening." It gives no launch date either.

Spotify's own posts do show when past Wrapped launches landed. This story lists all six, adds how many days each came after U.S. Thanksgiving, and gives a [printable Wrapped watch timeline](#downloads).

## When does Spotify Wrapped 2026 come out?

Not yet announced. If Spotify follows the pattern of 2021 to 2025, the matching day is **Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026**. That is our estimate from past dates, not a Spotify date, and Spotify could pick any day. We will update this page when the company announces it.

## Is there a pattern to the Wrapped launch day?

Five launches in a row, 2021 to 2025, fell on a Wednesday between Nov. 29 and Dec. 4. Each was the Wednesday after Thanksgiving, which sits on the fourth Thursday of November. The 2020 launch was a Tuesday, five days after Thanksgiving.

Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, Nov. 26 (see [when Thanksgiving falls](/when-is/thanksgiving/)). Six days later is Wednesday, Dec. 2. A pattern is not a promise, so treat Dec. 2 as a planning guess and wait for Spotify's post.

## What listening period will Wrapped 2026 cover?

Spotify has not stated the 2026 window. In its Dec. 5, 2025 newsroom post, Spotify said "Wrapped captures your listening from January 1 until mid November." It added: "We do this to ensure we have enough time to check the data and get everything ready for launch day."

## Who gets Wrapped, and how many songs do you need?

The 2026 rules are not yet published. In 2025, Spotify said Wrapped was "available for both Free and Premium users in nearly all markets where Spotify is live" and that "you need to have streamed at least 30 songs for more than 30 seconds each." Spotify also said that listening in Private Mode, and songs or playlists you excluded, do not count.

The Spotify Wrapped help page says Wrapped "may not be available to all users." In its 2024 post, Spotify advised keeping the iOS or Android app up to date.

## What can you do before Wrapped 2026 launches?

- Mark the dates on the [printable timeline](#downloads) and check Spotify's newsroom for the announcement.
- Artists have until Nov. 6, 2026 to submit a video message in Spotify for Artists.
