## How the game works

You sort the 16 words into four groups of four that share a link. You can make four mistakes before the game ends. The groups are color-coded by difficulty: yellow is the most straightforward, then green and blue, and purple is usually the trickiest, often built on wordplay.

## Why the traps worked

**Mini and Minion.** They look like a pair, and Minions are famous cartoon characters. But Minions come from Illumination's Despicable Me films for Universal, not from Disney. Minion is a word for an underling here, while Mini is the one that sounds like a Disney name.

**Wrap and Dock.** Wrap looks like it could be food or clothing, and Dock could be a boat word or mean to cut short. Both are decoys pulling you away from their real groups.

**Poodle and Puppet.** Poodle seems to be the odd dog out, but in British usage it is slang for someone who slavishly follows a more powerful person. Puppet carries the same meaning: someone whose strings are pulled by another.

**Arial.** It is best known as a typeface, which might send you looking for fonts. Spell it differently and it is a character from an animated film.

**Pencil, Chevron, Horseshoe, Walrus.** None of these looks related to the others until you think of facial hair.
