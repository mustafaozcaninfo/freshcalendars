Veterans Day 2026 falls on **Wednesday, Nov. 11**. It is a federal holiday, so federal offices, the Federal Reserve, most banks and the U.S. Postal Service close for the day, while the stock market stays open. Unlike most federal holidays, Veterans Day is tied to its date rather than a weekday, which is why it lands midweek this year instead of creating a long weekend.

## What is open and closed on Nov. 11, 2026

| Service | Status on Wednesday, Nov. 11 |
|---|---|
| Federal government offices | Closed |
| Federal Reserve | Closed |
| Most banks | Closed (ATMs, cards and online banking work) |
| U.S. Postal Service | Post offices closed, no regular mail delivery |
| Bond market | Closed (industry recommendation) |
| New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq | Open, 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET |
| Public schools | Varies by district |
| Most stores and restaurants | Open |

The federal closures come from the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) 2026 holiday table, which lists Veterans Day on Wednesday, Nov. 11, and from the Federal Reserve's 2026 holiday schedule. Because the Federal Reserve is closed, bank transfers and direct deposits started on Nov. 11 generally settle on Thursday, Nov. 12.

Stock trading continues because the New York Stock Exchange does not close for Veterans Day. The bond market follows a different calendar: the Securities Industry and Financial Markets Association (SIFMA) recommends a full close on Veterans Day, as it does on Columbus Day.

Schools are the biggest variable. Some districts close for the day, while many stay open and hold Veterans Day assemblies. Check your district calendar before planning around it.

## Why Veterans Day stays on Nov. 11

The date marks the armistice that halted fighting in World War I at 11 a.m. on Nov. 11, 1918, the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month. President Woodrow Wilson proclaimed the first Armistice Day in 1919, Congress called for an annual observance in 1926, and a 1938 law made Nov. 11 a legal federal holiday.

In 1954, after World War II and the Korean War, Congress renamed it Veterans Day to honor all American veterans. A later change did not last: under the Uniform Monday Holiday Act of 1968, Veterans Day moved to the fourth Monday of October starting in 1971. Many states kept Nov. 11 anyway, and in 1975 Congress passed a law returning the federal observance to Nov. 11, beginning in 1978. It has stayed there since.

## When Nov. 11 falls on a weekend

Because the holiday keeps its date, it sometimes lands on a Saturday or Sunday. OPM's rule is that a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before and a Sunday holiday on the Monday after, for federal pay and leave purposes.

| Year | Nov. 11 falls on | Federal holiday observed |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Wednesday | Wednesday, Nov. 11 |
| 2027 | Thursday | Thursday, Nov. 11 |
| 2028 | Saturday | Friday, Nov. 10 |
| 2029 | Sunday | Monday, Nov. 12 |
| 2030 | Monday | Monday, Nov. 11 |
| 2031 | Tuesday | Tuesday, Nov. 11 |

Ceremonies, including the national observance at Arlington National Cemetery, are traditionally held on Nov. 11 itself even in weekend years.

## Veterans Day vs. Memorial Day

The two holidays are often confused. Veterans Day honors everyone who has served in the U.S. armed forces, living or dead. Memorial Day, observed on the last Monday in May, honors those who died while serving. In 2027, Memorial Day falls on Monday, May 31.

## Planning tips for a midweek holiday

- Send time-sensitive mail and bank transfers by Tuesday, Nov. 10.
- If you have children in school, confirm whether your district is open; closures differ even between neighboring districts.
- Many restaurants and retailers offer discounts to veterans and service members on Nov. 11; most require a military ID or proof of service.
- Thanksgiving follows two weeks later, on Thursday, Nov. 26, so November 2026 has two federal holidays in the same month.
