Veterans Day 2026 falls on **Wednesday, Nov. 11**. It is one of the federal holidays listed by the Office of Personnel Management.

## Fixed date

Unlike Memorial Day or Labor Day, Veterans Day is tied to the calendar date, not a weekday. When Nov. 11 falls on a Saturday or Sunday, federal employees get the nearest Friday or Monday off, but in 2026 it is a Wednesday, so there is no shift.

## What is closed

Federal offices and the US Postal Service close. Many banks follow the Federal Reserve schedule and close as well. Schools vary by district.
