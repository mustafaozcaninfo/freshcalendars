**Miami-Dade County Public Schools (M-DCPS) students get the full week of Thanksgiving off, Nov. 23-27, 2026**, and winter recess runs from Dec. 21 to Jan. 1, according to the district's 2026-27 calendar for elementary and secondary schools. The 180-day year began Thursday, Aug. 13, and ends **Thursday, June 3, 2027**, about a week later than in neighboring Broward and Palm Beach counties.

## Miami-Dade 2026-27 recesses

| Break | Dates | Students return |
|---|---|---|
| Fall recess and Thanksgiving | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Winter recess | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Monday, Jan. 4 |
| Spring recess | Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027 | Tuesday, March 30 |

Teacher planning days stretch two of these. Friday, Dec. 18 has no students, so winter recess starts a day early for children, and Monday, March 29 adds a day to spring recess.

## Every day students are off

| Date | Day | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7 | Monday | Labor Day |
| Sept. 21 | Monday | Teacher planning day |
| Nov. 3 | Tuesday | Districtwide professional development day |
| Nov. 11 | Wednesday | Veterans Day |
| Nov. 23-25 | Mon.-Wed. | Fall recess |
| Nov. 26-27 | Thu.-Fri. | Thanksgiving and recess |
| Dec. 18 | Friday | Teacher planning day |
| Dec. 21-Jan. 1 | Mon.-Fri. | Winter recess |
| Jan. 15 | Friday | Teacher planning day |
| Jan. 18 | Monday | Martin Luther King, Jr. Day |
| Feb. 15 | Monday | Presidents' Day |
| March 10 | Wednesday | Teacher planning day |
| March 22-26 | Mon.-Fri. | Spring recess |
| March 29 | Monday | Teacher planning day |
| May 31 | Monday | Memorial Day |

The teacher planning day on Jan. 15 and the King holiday on Jan. 18 combine into a four-day weekend. Friday, June 4 is a teacher planning day after the last day for students.

## Grading periods and early release

The four grading periods end on Oct. 16, Jan. 14, March 19 and June 3.

Students in grades 2-5, and in grades 2-8 at K-8 centers, are released one hour early every Wednesday. Check your school's bell schedule for the exact time.

## How Miami-Dade compares

Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties all take Thanksgiving week, Dec. 21-Jan. 1 and March 22-26 off. Miami-Dade starts three days later (Aug. 13 instead of Aug. 10) and finishes six days later. See the [Broward County school calendar](/news/broward-county-school-calendar-2026-27/) and the [Palm Beach County school calendar](/news/palm-beach-county-school-calendar-2026-27/). Spring recess ends with the [Ultra Music Festival, March 26-28](/news/ultra-music-festival-2027-miami-dates-march-26-28/), at Bayfront Park in downtown Miami.
