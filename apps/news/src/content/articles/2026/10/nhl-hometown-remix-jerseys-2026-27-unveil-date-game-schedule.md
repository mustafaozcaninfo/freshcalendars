**The National Hockey League (NHL) and Fanatics unveiled the Hometown Remix jerseys for all 32 teams on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, and the jerseys went on sale that day.** The league counts 261 games in 2026-27 with a team in Hometown Remix, 19 of them with both teams wearing it, according to NHL.com. The Florida Panthers, Chicago Blackhawks and San Jose Sharks wore theirs first, that night.

Four teams had posted full game dates by Oct. 27, 2026: Buffalo, the Islanders, Nashville and St. Louis. The table below merges their lists into 37 games, and a one-page tracker of the same games is further down. Dates from January to April fall in 2027, and dates through Nov. 7 are now past.

## When were the NHL Hometown Remix jerseys unveiled?

On Nov. 2, 2026. NHL.com said Fanatics and the league "unveiled Hometown Remix, a Leaguewide special edition uniform, apparel and headwear program," and that each club wears its jersey several times this season. Before that, the Nashville Predators had written on nhl.com on Sept. 25 that "the full unveiling of all 32 jerseys and corresponding merchandise will be in November." The Islanders had said they "will unveil their Hometown Remix jersey in early November," and the Sabres had said "at a later date."

NHL.com has a page with design details for all 32 jerseys: [Hometown Remix: A look at all 32 NHL jerseys](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-fanatics-hometown-remix-jersey-descriptions).

## What is the Hometown Remix program?

NHL.com announced it on Aug. 19, 2026 as "special edition uniforms that uniquely and authentically celebrate the fans, city, and local culture of each team." It calls this Fanatics' "first 32-team special edition uniform, apparel and headwear program." NHL.com added that "more information on the Hometown Remix program will follow shortly after the season begins."

Team pages give some themes. Nashville's draws on the city's music, artists and fashion trends and honors Dolly Parton. The Islanders say each home game "will spotlight a different element of Long Island culture."

## Which dates did two teams list?

Two games appear on two teams' lists, so both sides are scheduled to wear Hometown Remix. On Wed., Nov. 25 the Blues play at the Islanders, and on Wed., Jan. 20, 2027 the Sabres play at the Islanders. We counted each once, which turns 39 team entries into 37 games.

Nov. 25 is also the one date on which all four teams wear the design: Nashville at Dallas, Buffalo vs. the New York Rangers and the Blues at the Islanders. By month, our count of the table is 10 games in November, 9 in December, 6 in January, 5 in February, 6 in March and 1 on April 1.

## When did Hometown Remix jerseys go on sale, and for how much?

They went on sale Nov. 2, 2026. NHL.com said the on-ice Authentic Pro and Premium jerseys and a line of headwear and men's and women's fan gear were "available beginning today" across the Fanatics network, including NHLShop.com, NHLShop.ca and Fanatics.com, plus team stores and the NHL Shop NYC flagship store. The release gives no prices. For comparison only, the 2022 Reverse Retro program was a different release: NHL.com said on Oct. 20, 2022 those jerseys were "available Nov. 15 for $190-$240 in the U.S. and $210-$260 in Canada." Do not read that as a Hometown Remix price.
