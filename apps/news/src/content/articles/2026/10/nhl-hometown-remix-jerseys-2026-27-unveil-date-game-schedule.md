**The first Hometown Remix games announced by teams are Wednesday, Nov. 4, when the Buffalo Sabres host Ottawa at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), and Thursday, Nov. 5, when the New York Islanders host Carolina; a Nashville Predators post on the National Hockey League (NHL) site says all 32 jerseys are unveiled in November.** Those dates come from team pages on nhl.com checked on Oct. 27, 2026. No team page gives an exact unveil day.

Only four teams have posted game dates so far: Buffalo, the Islanders, Nashville and St. Louis. This page merges their lists into one dated table of 37 games, and a one-page tracker of the same games is further down. Dates from January to April fall in 2027.

## When are the NHL Hometown Remix jerseys unveiled?

The league has not named a day. The Nashville Predators wrote on nhl.com on Sept. 25, 2026: "The full unveiling of all 32 jerseys and corresponding merchandise will be in November." The Islanders said on Oct. 1 they "will unveil their Hometown Remix jersey in early November", and the Sabres said on Sept. 23 the jerseys "will be unveiled at a later date."

The Hockey News reported on Oct. 17, 2026 that the Philadelphia Flyers will debut theirs on Nov. 3. We have not confirmed that with the team or the league, so it is reported only.

## What is the Hometown Remix program?

NHL.com announced it on Aug. 19, 2026 as "special edition uniforms that uniquely and authentically celebrate the fans, city, and local culture of each team." It calls this Fanatics' "first 32-team special edition uniform, apparel and headwear program." NHL.com added that "more information on the Hometown Remix program will follow shortly after the season begins."

Team pages give some themes. Nashville's is "inspired by rich history of music, artists and fashion trends in Nashville" and honors Dolly Parton. The Islanders say each home game "will spotlight a different element of Long Island culture."

The Blues' release says the Nov. 17 game is the first time fans "will have the opportunity to see the Hometown Remix jerseys at Enterprise Center." Edmonton's Sept. 28 post says its dates will be "announced at a later date." We found no posted game dates from the other teams.

## Which dates did two teams list?

Two games appear on two teams' lists, so both sides are scheduled to wear Hometown Remix. On Wed., Nov. 25 the Blues play at the Islanders, and on Wed., Jan. 20, 2027 the Sabres play at the Islanders. We counted each once, which turns 39 team entries into 37 games.

Nov. 25 is also the one date on which all four teams wear the design: Nashville at Dallas, Buffalo vs. the New York Rangers and the Blues at the Islanders. By month, our count of the table is 10 games in November, 9 in December, 6 in January, 5 in February, 6 in March and 1 on April 1.

## When do Hometown Remix jerseys go on sale, and for how much?

Not announced. NHL.com has given no on-sale date or price for Hometown Remix. For comparison only, the 2022 Reverse Retro program was a different release: NHL.com said on Oct. 20, 2022 those jerseys were "available Nov. 15 for $190-$240 in the U.S. and $210-$260 in Canada." Do not read that as a Hometown Remix price.
