Kiritimati in the Kiribati Line Islands (UTC+14) reaches midnight on Jan. 1, 2027 at 10:00 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026. That is 5:00 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) and 19 hours before New York, according to the Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA) time zone database. The table below converts local midnight for 32 UTC offsets into ET and UTC.

The last midnight in the table, American Samoa and Niue at UTC-11, arrives at 6:00 a.m. ET on Friday, Jan. 1. From the first midnight to the last is 25 hours.

## Which places changed their New Year moment in 2026?

Several Canadian, Cuban and Moroccan rules changed in 2026, so older "same time as" comparisons no longer hold. The IANA release notes record each change.

|---|---|---|---|
| Alberta (Calgary, Edmonton) | Permanent UTC-6 since June 18, 2026 | 2026c (July 8, 2026) | Midnight at 1:00 a.m. ET, with Chicago |
| British Columbia (Vancouver) | Permanent UTC-7 since March 9, 2026 | 2026b (April 22, 2026) | Midnight at 2:00 a.m. ET, with Denver and Phoenix |

## When does New York ring in 2027?

New York, Toronto and Winnipeg share UTC-5, so midnight there falls at 12:00 a.m. ET on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, which is 5:00 UTC. ## Which places are on daylight time on Dec. 31?

Daylight time in the southern hemisphere shifts several of the earliest midnights. New Zealand’s daylight saving started on Sept. 27, 2026 at 2 a.m. and ends on April 4, 2027 at 3 a.m., per the New Zealand Government, which puts Auckland and Wellington at UTC+13. In Australia, New South Wales daylight saving started Sunday, Oct. 4, 2026 and ends Sunday, April 4, 2027. New South Wales, Victoria, South Australia, Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory observe it, while Queensland, Western Australia and the Northern Territory do not. That is why Sydney (UTC+11), Brisbane (UTC+10) and Perth (UTC+8) reach midnight at three different hours.
