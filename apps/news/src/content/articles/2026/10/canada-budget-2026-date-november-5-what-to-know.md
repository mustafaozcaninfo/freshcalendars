The Department of Finance Canada released Budget 2026 on Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, in Ottawa, tabled by Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne. The Canadian Press reported it was tabled Thursday afternoon in the House of Commons. The measures below are proposals until Parliament passes them. A one-page timeline is in the downloads for printing.

## What was announced on budget day?

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026 | Department of Finance Canada, Nov. 5, 2026 |
| Tabled by | The Honourable François-Philippe Champagne, Minister of Finance and National Revenue | Department of Finance Canada, Nov. 5, 2026 |
| Time of day | Thursday afternoon; the department's release gives no hour | The Canadian Press, Nov. 5, 2026 |
| Measures | Proposed, see the dated list below | Budget 2026 documents |
| Budget Implementation Act | Date not yet announced | Not in the sources used here |

CP24 described it, before the budget, as the second budget under Prime Minister Mark Carney.

## Which Budget 2026 proposals have dates?

| Proposal | Proposed date or timing | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Single tax due date | "Setting May 15 as the single due date for filing returns and paying taxes owing"; "would apply for the 2027 and subsequent taxation years." The first May 15 deadline would fall in 2028, when 2027 returns are filed, as The Canadian Press noted | Budget 2026, chapter 6 |
| Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit | "A one-time 50% increase for the 2026-27 benefit year delivered in early 2027" | Budget 2026: Key Measures |
| Refundable Canada Caregiver Credit | "Up to $1,260 annually (for 2027, indexed thereafter)" | Budget 2026: Key Measures |
| Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP) | "As of January 1, 2027, permanently allowing a qualifying family member to open" one | Budget 2026, chapter 6 |
| Intergenerational farm transfers | "Would apply to transfers that occur on or after January 1, 2027" | Budget 2026, chapter 6 |
| Flat-rate claim methods for certain deductions and credits | "For the 2027 and subsequent taxation years" | Budget 2026, chapter 6 |
| Common cannabis excise stamp | Provinces "may indicate their desire to do so by January 31, 2027"; stamp available to licensed producers "on January 1, 2028" | Budget 2026, chapter 6 |

The [Budget 2026 documents](https://budget.canada.ca/2026/home-accueil-en.html) and the [Key Measures backgrounder](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/11/budget-2026-key-measures.html) have the full text. For related Canadian payroll and benefit dates, see the [Employment Insurance (EI) premium rate for 2027](/news/ei-premium-rate-2027-maximum-insurable-earnings-canada/) and the [November and December 2026 benefit payment dates](/news/canada-benefit-payment-dates-november-december-2026-cpp-oas-ccb/).

## Why is the federal budget in the fall now?

The Department of Finance Canada said on Oct. 6, 2025 that, "starting with Budget 2025, the federal budget will be tabled in the fall," with "an economic and fiscal update released in the spring." Budget 2025 was released on Tuesday, Nov. 4, 2025. Budget 2026 is the next one in that fall cycle.

## What is the Budget 2025 to Budget 2026 timeline?

| Date | Step | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Oct. 6, 2025 | Finance Canada announces the fall budget and spring update | Department of Finance Canada |
| Tue., Nov. 4, 2025 | Budget 2025 released | Department of Finance Canada |
| Spring 2026 | Economic and fiscal update, as the new cycle provides | Department of Finance Canada (Oct. 6, 2025 plan) |
| Mon., July 6, 2026 | Consultations ahead of Budget 2026 launched | Department of Finance Canada |
| Tue., Sept. 8, 2026 | Consultations end | Department of Finance Canada |
| Tue., Oct. 20, 2026 | Budget date announced in question period | The Canadian Press |
| Thu., Nov. 5, 2026 | Budget 2026 tabled | Department of Finance Canada |
| Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Proposed: RDSP family-member change; farm transfer rule | Budget 2026, chapter 6 |
| Sun., Jan. 31, 2027 | Proposed: provinces may opt in to a cannabis stamp | Budget 2026, chapter 6 |
| Mon., May 15, 2028 | Proposed single tax due date, first for 2027 returns | Budget 2026, chapter 6; The Canadian Press |

In its July 6, 2026 release, the department said: "This fall, the Government of Canada will deliver Budget 2026." The date of the spring update and its figures are not covered in the sources used for this story.

## What did the Parliamentary Budget Officer forecast before the budget?

The Parliamentary Budget Officer (PBO) expected a $63.3-billion deficit in 2026-27 and a balanced operating budget in 2028-29, a year later than promised, The Canadian Press reported via Advisor.ca on Oct. 20, 2026. The same report says the finance minister's office disputed the PBO's assumptions. This story gives no Budget 2026 deficit figure.
