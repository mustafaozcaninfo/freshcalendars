The federal Liberal government will table its 2026 federal budget on Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, according to The Canadian Press, which reported the date on Oct. 20, 2026. Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne announced it in question period that Tuesday. Counted from Wednesday, Oct. 28, the budget is eight days away. A one-page timeline is in the downloads for printing.

## What is the date and time of Canada's Budget 2026?

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026 | The Canadian Press, Oct. 20, 2026 |
| Announced by | Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne, in question period on Tuesday, Oct. 20, 2026 | The Canadian Press, Oct. 20, 2026 |
| Time of day | Not yet announced | No hour in the sources used here |
| Measures | Announced when the budget is tabled | None confirmed |

CP24 describes it as the second budget under Prime Minister Mark Carney. Champagne's official title is Minister of Finance and National Revenue.

## Why is the federal budget in the fall now?

The Department of Finance Canada said on Oct. 6, 2025 that, "starting with Budget 2025, the federal budget will be tabled in the fall," with "an economic and fiscal update released in the spring." Budget 2025 was released on Tuesday, Nov. 4, 2025. Budget 2026 is the next one in that fall cycle.

## What is the Budget 2025 to Budget 2026 timeline?

| Date | Step | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Oct. 6, 2025 | Finance Canada announces the fall budget and spring update | Department of Finance Canada |
| Tue., Nov. 4, 2025 | Budget 2025 released | Department of Finance Canada |
| Spring 2026 | Economic and fiscal update, as the new cycle provides | Department of Finance Canada (Oct. 6, 2025 plan) |
| Mon., July 6, 2026 | Consultations ahead of Budget 2026 launched | Department of Finance Canada |
| Tue., Sept. 8, 2026 | Consultations end | Department of Finance Canada |
| Tue., Oct. 20, 2026 | Budget date announced | The Canadian Press |
| Thu., Nov. 5, 2026 | Budget 2026 tabled | The Canadian Press |

In its July 6, 2026 release, the department said: "This fall, the Government of Canada will deliver Budget 2026." The date of the spring update and its figures are not covered in the sources used for this story.

## What is the Parliamentary Budget Officer forecasting?

The Parliamentary Budget Officer (PBO) expects a $63.3-billion deficit in 2026-27 and a balanced operating budget in 2028-29, a year later than promised, The Canadian Press reported via Advisor.ca. The same report says the finance minister's office disputed the PBO's assumptions. The government's own figures come with the budget.
