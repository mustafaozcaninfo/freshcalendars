**There were 7 National Basketball Association (NBA) games on Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026 (Halloween), with the first tip-off at 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the last at 8:30 p.m. ET, per the NBA.com games page.** NBA.com listed all seven as regular-season games, and none of them was an NBA Cup game. NBA TV had two of them: 76ers at Hornets and Thunder at Rockets.

The game list and TV details come from NBA.com, fetched Oct. 29, 2026. We cross-checked the arenas against the CBS Sports schedule, which lists "all times US/Eastern." Final scores are not recorded here; the [CBS Sports schedule](https://www.cbssports.com/nba/schedule/20261031/) has the results.

NBA.com showed no national TV for the 3:00 p.m. game between the Trail Blazers and the Jazz. It listed radio for that game: 97.5 FM for Utah and Rip City Radio 620 for Portland. NBA.com did not list local TV for any of the seven games.
