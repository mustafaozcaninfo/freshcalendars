**There are 7 National Basketball Association (NBA) games today, Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026 (Halloween), with the first tip-off at 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the last at 8:30 p.m. ET, per the NBA.com games page.** NBA.com lists all seven as regular-season games, and none of them is an NBA Cup game. NBA TV has two of them: 76ers at Hornets and Thunder at Rockets.

The game list and TV details come from NBA.com, fetched Oct. 29, 2026. We cross-checked the arenas against the CBS Sports schedule, which lists "all times US/Eastern." This page does not carry live scores. Once a game has started, the [CBS Sports schedule](https://www.cbssports.com/nba/schedule/20261031/) shows the score and then the result.
