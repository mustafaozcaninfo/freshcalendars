## What time is Bengals vs. Falcons in Madrid?

NFL.com lists Bengals vs. Falcons on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 9:30 a.m. ET on NFL Network, with a mobile stream on NFL+; the Bengals won 34-17. The Atlanta Falcons listed the game on NFL Network nationally and on Atlanta News First locally, with NFL+ and ESPN streaming platforms. The Cincinnati Bengals listed NFL Network nationally and a local Cincinnati station. Our [Bengals vs. Falcons Madrid guide](/news/bengals-falcons-madrid-2026-kickoff-time-tv-streaming/) has the full viewing details. In Madrid, on Central European Time (CET, UTC+1), that was 3:30 p.m. In the United Kingdom it was 2:30 p.m. Our [NFL international games story](/news/nfl-international-games-november-2026-madrid-munich-mexico-city-kickoff-times/) has the stadium, Bernabéu Stadium, and the Munich and Mexico City games that follow.

## How many Week 9 games kick off in primetime?

Three Week 9 games were alone in national primetime: Jaguars at Ravens on Thursday at 8:15 p.m. ET on Prime Video, Buccaneers at Bears on Sunday at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC, and Bills at Vikings on Monday at 8:15 p.m. ET. The earliest kickoff of the week sits at the opposite end of the clock, with Bengals vs. Falcons from Madrid at 9:30 a.m. ET on Sunday, so the slate runs from mid-morning to late at night.

## Week 9 rest days: Jaguars and Ravens play on four days, four teams return from bye

Counting calendar days between each team's previous game and its Week 9 game in the NFL.com schedule data, the shortest turnaround belongs to the Jacksonville Jaguars and Baltimore Ravens. Both played on Sunday, Nov. 1 and met on Thursday, Nov. 5, four days later. The Seattle Seahawks and Chicago Bears played the Monday game of Week 8 on Nov. 2 and got six days. The New Orleans Saints, New York Giants, Houston Texans and San Francisco 49ers last played on Oct. 25 and came back after 14 days. The Steelers and Titans were on bye in Week 9.
