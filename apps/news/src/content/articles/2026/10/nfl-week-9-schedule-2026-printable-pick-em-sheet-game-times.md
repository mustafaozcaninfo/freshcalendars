**The National Football League (NFL) plays 15 Week 9 games from Thursday, Nov. 5 to Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, including Bengals vs. Falcons in Madrid at 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Sunday, according to NFL.com.** Below: every game with its network, the kickoff in each U.S. time zone and the United Kingdom, and a one-page pick'em sheet with blanks for your picks, the final scores and a Monday tiebreaker.

## What is the NFL Week 9 schedule for 2026?

NFL.com lists these games, all times ET. Clocks fall back on Sunday, Nov. 1, so every Week 9 kickoff is on Eastern Standard Time (EST), as the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) says daylight saving time in 2026 ends "November 1 at 2 a.m. (local time)". The Steelers and Titans are on bye, and Monday's game is on ESPN, the American Broadcasting Company (ABC), ESPN2 and NFL Network. The schedule matches our [printable NFL schedule for 2026-2027](/news/printable-nfl-schedule-2026-2027-pdf/), which was reconciled with NFL.com on Oct. 23, 2026.

## What time is Bengals vs. Falcons in Madrid?

NFL.com lists Bengals vs. Falcons on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 9:30 a.m. ET on NFL Network. In Madrid, on Central European Time (CET, UTC+1), that is 3:30 p.m. In the United Kingdom it is 2:30 p.m. Our [NFL international games story](/news/nfl-international-games-november-2026-madrid-munich-mexico-city-kickoff-times/) has the stadium, Bernabéu Stadium, and the Munich and Mexico City games that follow.

## Which games are on Sunday, Nov. 8?

NFL.com lists five Sunday start times, all ET.

- 9:30 a.m.: Bengals vs. Falcons in Madrid on NFL Network.
- 1 p.m.: seven games, three on CBS and four on FOX.
- 4:05 p.m.: Texans at Chargers and Raiders at 49ers, both on CBS.
- 4:25 p.m.: Cardinals at Seahawks and Packers at Patriots, both on FOX.
- 8:20 p.m.: Buccaneers at Bears on NBC.
