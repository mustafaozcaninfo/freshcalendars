**Tonight, Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, the Indiana High School Athletic Association (IHSAA) plays its football sectional semifinals, with a 7 p.m. local-time kickoff unless the host school sets another time; the state finals are Nov. 27-28 at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis.** The IHSAA's 2026-27 State Tournament Series page lists every round from Oct. 23 to Nov. 28. Below: the round-by-round dates, state final kickoff times in Eastern and Central Time, ticket prices and where to stream.

## When are the IHSAA football tournament rounds in 2026?

The IHSAA runs the tournament across six Fridays and one Saturday. Class 6A starts at the semifinals tonight, while Classes 5A, 4A, 3A, 2A and 1A also played a first round on Oct. 23. Sectionals, regionals and semi-states kick off at "7 pm local time or as determined by host school," the IHSAA says. Indiana has counties in both the Eastern and Central time zones, so local time depends on the school.

| Date | Round | Kickoff | Ticket (per person) | Stream on IHSAAtv |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 23 | Sectional first round (Classes 5A, 4A, 3A, 2A, 1A) | 7 p.m. local | $8 | Included |
| Fri., Oct. 30 (tonight) | Sectional semifinals (all six classes) | 7 p.m. local | $8 | Included |
| Fri., Nov. 6 | Sectional championships | 7 p.m. local | $8 | Included |
| Fri., Nov. 13 | Regionals | 7 p.m. local | $10 | Included |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | Semi-states | 7 p.m. local | $12 | Pay-per-view, $12 a game or $20 for all games |
| Fri.-Sat., Nov. 27-28 | State finals, Lucas Oil Stadium | See the next table | $20 per day | Pay-per-view, $20 a game or $25 for all games |

The IHSAA lists 48 sectionals, 8 in each class, with 4 to 8 teams in each sectional. Pairings and brackets are on the [IHSAA tournament page](https://www.ihsaa.org/sports/boys/football/2026-27-tournament) under the round tabs; the pairings show was Oct. 11.

## What time are the IHSAA football state finals on Nov. 27-28?

The state finals are at Lucas Oil Stadium, 500 S. Capitol Avenue, Indianapolis. The IHSAA gives the times in ET. The Central Time (CT) column is our conversion, one hour earlier.

| Day | Class | Kickoff (ET) | Kickoff (CT, our conversion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 2A | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 4A | 3 p.m. | 2 p.m. |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 6A | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 1A | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 3A | 3 p.m. | 2 p.m. |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 5A | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. |

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, so the finals are the Friday and Saturday right after it. Check the [Thanksgiving date](/when-is/thanksgiving/) and our [Thanksgiving football schedule](/news/thanksgiving-football-schedule-2026-nfl-college-games/) if the holiday weekend changes your plans.

## How much are IHSAA football tournament tickets?

Sectional, regional and semi-state tickets are digital and sold through Eventlink at ihsaa.eventlink.com; show the ticket on your phone at the gate. State finals tickets are sold by Ticketmaster or at the Lucas Oil Stadium box office. Children age 5 and younger are free at every round.

| Round | Price per person |
|---|---|
| Sectionals | $8 |
| Regionals | $10 |
| Semi-states | $12 |
| State finals | $20 per day |

## How can you watch IHSAA football games?

The IHSAA streams games on IHSAAtv (IHSAAtv.org), with apps for Firestick, Apple TV and Android TV. Sectionals and regionals are on IHSAAtv; search by school. Semi-states are pay-per-view at $12 a game or $20 for all games, and state finals are $20 a game or $25 for all games. The IHSAA page names no TV network.
