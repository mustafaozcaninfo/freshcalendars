## How often was the purple group wordplay?

In 28 of the 31 puzzles. We sorted each purple title by a fixed rule: a title with a blank (___) is a fill-in-the-blank group; "Homophones" in the title is a homophone group; "Letter Changed" or "Minus a Letter" is a letter change; a title beginning "Starting", "Starts", "Ending" or "Ends" is a hidden start or end; the rest were checked by hand.

| Purple type | Puzzles | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden start or end of the words | #1208, #1210, #1212, #1213, #1215, #1218, #1221, #1223, #1225, #1226, #1229, #1238 | 12 |
| Fill in the blank (___ X or X ___) | #1209, #1211, #1214, #1216, #1217, #1220, #1224, #1230, #1231, #1234 | 10 |
| Letter changed or removed | #1222, #1233, #1237 | 3 |
| Homophones | #1227, #1235 | 2 |
| Words built from postal abbreviations | #1219 | 1 |
| Plain category, no wordplay | #1228, #1232, #1236 | 3 |

The three plain purple groups were Double Misnomers (#1228), Things That Can Be Snapped (#1232) and Things That Feature Three Colors (#1236). Hidden starts and ends were the most common single type, ahead of fill-in-the-blank by two groups.

## How many entries had more than one word, and which was longest?

Across the month, the 31 puzzles held 496 entries, of which 479 were different; 17 words appeared twice, such as Chest, Ring and Power. Of the 496, 46 had more than one word (44 with a space and two with a hyphen, Grape-Nuts and Pop-Up). Eleven puzzles had at least one.

| Puzzle | Multi-word entries |
|---|---|
| #1210, Sat., Oct. 3 | 16 |
| #1228, Wed., Oct. 21 | 12 |
| #1236, Thu., Oct. 29 | 5 |
| #1232, Sun., Oct. 25 | 4 |
| #1231 and #1233 | 2 each |
| #1209, #1214, #1218, #1226 and #1238 | 1 each |

Every one of the 16 entries in #1210 had more than one word. The longest entry overall was Campbell's Soup Can (#1228), at 19 characters with spaces and the apostrophe. The longest single word was Orchestrations (#1214), 14 letters.

## What this tells you for November

These are patterns in last month's puzzles, not a forecast of any puzzle. They can still shape how you play.

- **Check purple for wordplay first.** It was wordplay in 28 of 31 puzzles.
- **Look at the first and last letters.** Hidden starts or ends made up 12 of the 31 purple groups.
- **Test a shared blank.** A fill-in-the-blank group was purple in 10 puzzles and blue in three more, 13 in all.
- **Expect straight categories in yellow and green.** 60 of 62 were.
- **Do not split a phrase.** In 11 puzzles an entry held more than one word, so a pair of words on the board can be one entry.
