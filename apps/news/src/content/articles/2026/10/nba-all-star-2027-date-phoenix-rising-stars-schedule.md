**The 76th National Basketball Association (NBA) All-Star Game tips off Sunday, Feb. 21, 2027, at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, according to NBA Events.** All-Star 2027 runs Feb. 19-21. NBA.com says Castrol Rising Stars opens the weekend on Friday, Feb. 19, at 7 p.m. Mountain Time (MT), which is 9 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) by our conversion.

## When is NBA All-Star Weekend 2027?

NBA Events gives the dates as Feb. 19-21, 2027. The table below puts each event on its day. The start times for Saturday and Sunday have not been announced in the sources we read, so the table says so.

| Day | Event | Start | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Feb. 19, 2027 | Castrol Rising Stars | 7 p.m. MT (9 p.m. ET, our conversion) | NBA.com, Oct. 8, 2026 |
| Sat., Feb. 20, 2027 | State Farm All-Star Saturday Night, including the Kia Skills Challenge | Not announced | NBA Communications, Mar. 7, 2024 |
| Sun., Feb. 21, 2027 | 76th NBA All-Star Game | Not announced | NBA Events |

## What time is Rising Stars in each time zone?

NBA.com gives the start as 7 p.m. MT. Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so in February Phoenix is on Mountain Standard Time, the same clock as Denver. Every other row is our conversion, checked with a time zone database.

## What channel is the NBA All-Star Game on?

NBC Sports said on Aug. 13, 2026, that it will present the NBA All-Star Game on Feb. 21, 2027. The sources we read do not name a network for Rising Stars or All-Star Saturday Night, and they do not list streaming services for the weekend. Check the listings closer to the date.

## Where is NBA All-Star 2027 played?

At the Mortgage Matchup Center, home of the Phoenix Suns. The NBA's March 7, 2024 release announcing Phoenix as host called the arena Footprint Center. The Suns and Phoenix Mercury announced the new name in a naming rights release on Oct. 2, 2025.

## What else is on the 2026-27 NBA calendar around All-Star?

The NBA's Aug. 13, 2026 schedule release says the 81st regular season began Tuesday, Oct. 20, 2026, and will conclude on Sunday, April 11, 2027, with all 30 teams in action. NBC Sports lists a Presidents' Day quadrupleheader on Feb. 15, 2027, six days before the All-Star Game. ## Not yet announced

The sources we read do not give the All-Star Game tip time, the start of All-Star Saturday Night or the game format, so we do not list them. We will update this story when the NBA publishes them.
