**The standard Medicare Part B premium is $209.40 a month for 2027, up $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026, and the annual deductible is $292, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) said in its fact sheet dated Nov. 17, 2026.** CMS says the increase is "mainly due to projected increases in healthcare spending." The 2026 Medicare Trustees Report had projected $209.50, so the official premium is $0.10 lower.

## What is the 2027 Part B premium and deductible?

The table sets the 2027 amounts beside the two earlier years.

| Item | 2025 (CMS) | 2026 (CMS) | 2027 (CMS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard monthly Part B premium | $185.00 | $202.90 | $209.40 |
| Part B annual deductible | $257 | $283 | $292 |

The 2027 premium is $6.50 higher than 2026, and the deductible is $9 higher. The 2026 premium was $17.90 higher than the 2025 premium. The Medicare website's Part B costs page now shows "($209.40 in 2027)" and "($292 in 2027)". The 2026 Medicare Trustees Report had projected $209.50, as AARP and the Military Officers Association of America (MOAA) reported.

## What do higher-income members pay in 2027?

People with higher incomes pay an income-related monthly adjustment amount (IRMAA) on top of the standard premium. These are the 2027 amounts from the CMS fact sheet.

| Income bracket, individual | Income bracket, joint | IRMAA surcharge | Total monthly Part B premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| $112,000 or less | $224,000 or less | $0.00 | $209.40 |
| Over $112,000 to $141,000 | Over $224,000 to $282,000 | $83.70 | $293.10 |
| Over $141,000 to $177,000 | Over $282,000 to $354,000 | $209.40 | $418.80 |
| Over $177,000 to $212,000 | Over $354,000 to $424,000 | $335.00 | $544.40 |
| Over $212,000 and under $500,000 | Over $424,000 and under $750,000 | $460.60 | $670.00 |
| $500,000 or more | $750,000 or more | $502.40 | $711.80 |

For married people who file separately, the premium is $209.40 at $112,000 or less, $670.00 above $112,000 and under $388,000, and $711.80 at $388,000 or more.

## What are the 2027 Part A amounts?

| Part A item | 2027 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Inpatient hospital deductible | $1,776 | $1,736 |
| Monthly premium for people who buy Part A, 30 to 39 quarters of coverage | $328 | $311 |
| Monthly premium for people who buy Part A, fewer than 30 quarters | $597 | $565 |

CMS also lists 2027 coinsurance of $444 a day for hospital days 61 to 90, $888 a day for lifetime reserve days and $222 a day for skilled nursing facility days 21 to 100.
