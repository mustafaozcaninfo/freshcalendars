**The Medicare Trustees Report projects a standard Part B premium of $209.50 a month for 2027, up from $202.90 in 2026, but the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) had not announced the official amount as of Oct. 28, 2026.** AARP reported the projection on Oct. 5, 2026, and the Military Officers Association of America (MOAA) wrote in June that "the annual trustee report projects a $209.50 monthly Part B premium for 2027, up from $202.90 this year." Treat $209.50 as a projection until CMS publishes its fact sheet.

## What is the 2027 Part B premium?

Not yet known. AARP says the official amounts "will be announced later this fall." The same Trustees Report projection puts the 2027 Part B deductible at $292, according to AARP. The table sets the projection beside the amounts CMS has already published.

| Item | 2025 (CMS) | 2026 (CMS) | 2027 (Trustees projection) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard monthly Part B premium | $185.00 | $202.90 | $209.50 |
| Part B annual deductible | $257 | $283 | $292 |

The projected 2027 premium is $6.60 higher than 2026, and the projected deductible is $9 higher. The 2026 premium was $17.90 higher than the 2025 premium. The 2025 and 2026 columns come from the CMS fact sheets; the 2027 column comes from the Trustees Report as reported by AARP and MOAA.

## When will CMS announce the 2027 Part B premium?

CMS has not given a date. It released the 2025 figures on Nov. 8, 2024 and the 2026 figures on Nov. 14, 2025, so the last two announcements both came in November. That is a record of the past, not a schedule for this year. Medicare open enrollment runs from Oct. 15 to Dec. 7, 2026, as covered in our [Medicare open enrollment story](/news/medicare-open-enrollment-2026-december-7-90-dollar-rebate/), so the official number may land before the window closes. Mark the days on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and check the CMS newsroom.

## What do higher-income members pay in 2026?

People with higher incomes pay an income-related monthly adjustment amount (IRMAA) on top of the standard premium. These are the 2026 amounts from the CMS fact sheet released Nov. 14, 2025. The 2027 brackets had not been published when this story was written.

| Income bracket, individual | Income bracket, joint | IRMAA surcharge | Total monthly Part B premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| $109,000 or less | $218,000 or less | $0 | $202.90 |
| $109,001 to $137,000 | $218,001 to $274,000 | $81.20 | $284.10 |
| $137,001 to $171,000 | $274,001 to $342,000 | $202.90 | $405.80 |
| $171,001 to $205,000 | $342,001 to $410,000 | $324.60 | $527.50 |
| $205,001 to under $500,000 | $410,001 to under $750,000 | $446.30 | $649.20 |
| $500,000 or more | $750,000 or more | $487.00 | $689.90 |

## Where does the Social Security increase fit?

The 2027 Social Security cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) of 3.6% starts in January, as ABC News reported on Oct. 14, 2026. Our [Social Security COLA story](/news/social-security-cola-2027-3-6-percent-payment-dates/) has the payment dates, and the [COLA scams story](/news/social-security-cola-2027-scams-increase-chart/) covers what to watch for in the coming weeks.

## What to do until CMS announces it

- Use $209.50 only as a planning estimate, and label it that way in any budget.
- Note the official figure on the printable when CMS publishes it.
- Compare the 2026 amounts above with the CMS fact sheet when it arrives; it lists the new IRMAA brackets.
- Finish any plan change by Monday, Dec. 7.
