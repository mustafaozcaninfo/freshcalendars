**Today's Waffle (wafflegame.net) is puzzle #1739, for Monday, Oct. 26, 2026, and it hides six five-letter words in one waffle-shaped grid.** Three words read across and three read down, and every word is an ordinary English word. Use the clues below one word at a time; the answers, and a swap-by-swap route to the five-star score, are in a marked spoiler section at the end.

## How Waffle works

The board is a five-by-five grid with four holes, so 21 letters are in play. Three words run across (rows 1, 3 and 5) and three run down (columns 1, 3 and 5). You swap any two letters, and you have 15 swaps to finish. Per the game's own rules page:

| Tile color | What it means |
|---|---|
| Green | The letter is in the right place. |
| Yellow | The letter is in that row or column, but in a different spot. |
| Gray | The letter is not in that row or column. |

A letter on a corner can be yellow because of either of the two words that meet there. You earn a star for every swap left once the Waffle is solved, and the game says every Waffle can be solved in a minimum of 10 swaps.

- **Column 5:** starts with E and ends with T. A verb meaning to put into vigorous action, as in exert yourself.

## A solving tip for today

Start with the corners, because each one serves two words. Four of today's six words end in E, so an E is a safe bet for the last spot of many lines. Once you have a green corner, fix the rest of that row or column before moving on, since a swap that turns two tiles green is worth more than one that turns one tile green. Count your swaps as you go: with 15 available and 10 as the minimum, you can afford five detours before stars are at risk.
