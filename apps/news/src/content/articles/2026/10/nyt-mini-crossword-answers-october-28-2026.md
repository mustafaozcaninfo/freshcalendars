**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Tracy Bennett.** Black squares sit in the top left and bottom right corners, so 1-Across and 8-Across have four letters each. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *Work benefit*: an extra such as free lunch or a gym pass.
- **5-Across** (5 letters), *Delay airing, as a news story*: a two-word phrase meaning to hold something back for now.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *⏸, on a remote*: the button that stops the show for a moment.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Back-of-book search feature*: the alphabetical list of topics with page numbers.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *They get socked*: a pun. Think of what goes inside a sock.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Chopin's instrument*: it has 88 keys.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Chopin practice piece*: a French word for "study," the name of a short piece built around one technique.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Pinkish wines*: the plural of a French wine name that also names a flower.
- **4-Down** (4 letters), *Lego competitor*: a construction toy of rods and connectors, spelled with a K.
- **5-Down** (4 letters), *___ and polish*: a phrase for a thorough shine, from soldiers cleaning their boots.

## Solving tips

Start with 6-Across, the button on a remote, and 1-Down, the instrument. They cross in the first column of the third row. The second Chopin clue, 2-Down, runs next to the piano and starts with an E. Leave 5-Across for later: it is a two-word phrase written as one entry, and the Down answers fill most of its letters.
