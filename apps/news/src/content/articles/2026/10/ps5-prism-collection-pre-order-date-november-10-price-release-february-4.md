**PlayStation 5 (PS5) Prism Collection pre-orders begin Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time, which is 7:00 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) and 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) for U.S. buyers on direct.playstation.com.** PlayStation Blog announced the collection on Oct. 28, 2026, and says it launches starting Feb. 4, 2027.

The collection adds "three striking transparent designs for DualSense wireless controllers and PS5 console covers," in Flare Orange, Emerald Green and Graphite Gray. Each color has a controller and a matching cover.

## What time do Prism Collection pre-orders start?

PlayStation says pre-orders start on Nov. 10, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time, directly from PlayStation where available and at participating retailers. For the U.S., it gives 7:00 a.m. PT and 10:00 a.m. ET. U.S. clocks move back on Sunday, Nov. 1, so Nov. 10 falls on standard time and the conversions below use it.

| Where | Pre-orders open on Tue., Nov. 10 |
|---|---|
| Eastern Time (ET) | 10:00 a.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | 9:00 a.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 8:00 a.m. |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 7:00 a.m. |
| Alaska | 6:00 a.m. |
| Hawaii | 5:00 a.m. |
| U.K. (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT) | 10:00 a.m. |
| France, Germany, Austria, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg (Central European Time, CET) | 10:00 a.m. local time |
| Portugal | 10:00 a.m. local time |

The Central, Mountain, Alaska and Hawaii rows are our conversions from PlayStation's 10:00 a.m. ET. PlayStation lists direct orders in the U.S., U.K., France, Germany, Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Spain, Portugal and Italy. Canada and Japan are not on that list.

Not yet announced: the Canada pre-order channel and time, the Japan pre-order time, and start times at retailers such as Amazon, Best Buy and GameStop.

## How much does the transparent DualSense cost?

PlayStation Blog lists these recommended prices; only the yen prices are stated as including tax. The last row is our addition of one controller and one cover in the same color.

| Item | U.S. | U.K. | Euro | Japan |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DualSense wireless controller | $84.99 | 74.99 pounds | 84.99 euros | 12,480 yen |
| PS5 console cover | $74.99 | 64.99 pounds | 74.99 euros | 9,980 yen |
| Controller plus cover | $159.98 | 139.98 pounds | 159.98 euros | 22,460 yen |

iPhone in Canada reports Canadian prices of $109.99 for a controller and $94.99 for a cover. Those figures are not on an official page we could confirm.

## When is the Prism Collection release date?

PlayStation says: "The Prism Collection launches starting on February 4, 2027." It adds that the exact launch date and availability may vary by country or region. Feb. 4, 2027 is a Thursday, 86 days after the Tuesday, Nov. 10 pre-order start. Mark both on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html).

## Which PS5 consoles do the covers fit?

PlayStation's post names no console models for the covers; it says only "PS5." PlayStation has not said which PS5 models the covers fit; Forbes reports base and Slim only. The post says the covers will be available exclusively directly from PlayStation where available, and in other regions in limited quantities through select retailers.

## Pre-order checklist

1. Sign in to your PlayStation account on direct.playstation.com before 10 a.m. ET on Nov. 10.
2. Choose your color: Flare Orange, Emerald Green or Graphite Gray.
3. Decide between a controller, a cover or both.
4. Check the pre-order time for your zone in the table above.
