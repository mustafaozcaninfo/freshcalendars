**Friday's Connections: Sports Edition from The Athletic and New York Times (NYT) Games is puzzle #767, for Oct. 30, 2026, and only one of its 16 tiles is a number.** Hints for each group come first, then the answers in a spoiler section.

## How the Sports Edition works

It is the sports version of Connections, built by The Athletic with New York Times Games. You sort 16 sports terms into four groups of four and can make four mistakes. Yellow is usually the easiest group, purple the trickiest. It launched on Feb. 9, 2025, with a football puzzle, and new puzzles post at midnight local time, according to The Athletic's announcement.

## Solving tips

Look for a tile that fits two groups and settle the group with no decoy first. When a name could sit in a team list or a roster, check whether the other three entries of that list really match it. Friday's board has a few of those, so hold back on your first guess.

## Friday's hints, #767, Oct. 30, 2026

Friday's board mixes names of teams, people and one number.

- **Yellow:** Four names of teams, pro and college, that all share one home state.
- **Green:** Four quarterbacks (QB) who have thrown passes for the same franchise.
- **Blue:** Four things linked to one famous quarterback, including a nickname and a number.
- **Purple:** Four first names that all go in front of the same surname.

Watch for the overlap: one of the first names also belongs to a coach tied to the yellow state, and another is a quarterback who is not part of the green group.

## Friday's answers, #767, Oct. 30, 2026 (spoilers)

The 16 tiles, spelled out: Marc Bulger, Jared Goff, Matthew Stafford and Kurt Warner, all quarterbacks (QB) for the Los Angeles or St. Louis Rams; Wisconsin, the University of Wisconsin Badgers, with the Brewers, Bucks and Packers; for Joe Montana, No. 16, the San Francisco 49ers, his nickname Joe Cool and his college, Notre Dame; and Bo Ryan, the Wisconsin basketball coach, Buddy Ryan, a football coach, Matt Ryan, a quarterback, and Nolan Ryan, a pitcher.

- **Yellow, Wisconsin Teams:** Brewers, Bucks, Packers, Wisconsin
- **Green, Rams QBs, Past and Present:** Bulger, Goff, Stafford, Warner
- **Blue, Associated With Joe Montana:** 16, 49ers, Joe Cool, Notre Dame
- **Purple, ___ Ryan:** Bo, Buddy, Matt, Nolan

Who they are, group by group:

- **Yellow:** The Milwaukee Brewers play baseball, the Milwaukee Bucks basketball and the Green Bay Packers football, and Wisconsin stands for the Wisconsin Badgers, the state university's teams.
- **Green:** Kurt Warner (1998 to 2003), Marc Bulger (2002 to 2009) and Jared Goff (2016 to 2020) played quarterback for the Rams, and Matthew Stafford has since 2021. Warner led the Rams to their first Super Bowl title, a 23-16 Super Bowl win over the Tennessee Titans that capped the 1999 season.
- **Blue:** Joe Montana played quarterback for the 49ers from 1979 to 1992 in No. 16, came to them from Notre Dame, was called "Joe Cool" for staying calm late in games, and won four Super Bowls with them.
- **Purple:** Bo Ryan coached Wisconsin's men's basketball team and entered the Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame in 2024. Buddy Ryan ran the defense of the 1985 Super Bowl champion Chicago Bears, then coached the Philadelphia Eagles (1986 to 1990) and Arizona Cardinals (1994 to 1995). Matt Ryan was the Atlanta Falcons' quarterback from 2008 to 2021 and was voted the league's best player for 2016. Nolan Ryan is a Hall of Fame pitcher whose 5,714 strikeouts are the all-time record.

## Why Friday's traps worked

Bo Ryan coached at Wisconsin, so Bo pulls toward the yellow group. Matt Ryan is a quarterback, so Matt pulls toward the green group, but he never played for the Rams. The 49ers look like one more team for a list of teams, yet here they belong with Joe Montana, and 16 is the only number on the board.
