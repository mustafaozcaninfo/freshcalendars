**U.S. viewers can stream the 2026 Ballon d'Or on Dazn, which the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) names as the ceremony's global broadcaster, starting at 4 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Monday, Oct. 26.** The ceremony begins at 20:00 Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) at the London Palladium. Thierry Henry and Kate Scott host it, UEFA says.

Some guides online list 3 p.m. ET. That is one hour early for this year: Europe changed its clocks on Sunday, Oct. 25, and the United States keeps daylight saving time until Nov. 1, so New York sits 4 hours behind London this week.

## How to watch the Ballon d'Or 2026 in the U.S.

UEFA's broadcaster list reads "Global: DAZN (excluding all other territories listed below)." The 20 territories it lists are in Europe and Central Asia. The United States is not among them, so Dazn is the official U.S. option.

1. Open the Dazn app on a phone, smart TV or streaming device, or go to dazn.com.
2. Sign in, or create an account if the app asks for one.
3. Search for "Ballon d'Or" or open the live and upcoming events row.
4. Start the stream a few minutes before 4 p.m. ET.

Dazn says it will make the ceremony available free to fans in 200 markets. Its announcement does not name the United States or say whether you need an account, so check the event page in the app before the start.

## Is the Ballon d'Or on U.S. TV?

No U.S. cable or broadcast channel is named in UEFA's list or in Dazn's announcement. To watch on a television, open the Dazn app on the TV or on a streaming device plugged into it. We have not found an official full live stream on YouTube.

## What time does the Ballon d'Or 2026 start in the U.S.?

UEFA says the ceremony will commence at 20:00 GMT, which is 21:00 Central European Time (CET). The U.S. times below are our conversion from that start.

| U.S. time zone | Start on Monday, Oct. 26 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (New York, Miami, Atlanta) | 4 p.m. EDT |
| Central (Chicago, Dallas, Houston) | 3 p.m. CDT |
| Mountain (Denver, Salt Lake City) | 2 p.m. MDT |
| Arizona (Phoenix) | 1 p.m. MST |
| Pacific (Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle) | 1 p.m. PDT |
| Alaska (Anchorage) | Noon AKDT |
| Hawaii (Honolulu) | 10 a.m. Hawaii-Aleutian Standard Time (HST) |

Red carpet and press conference times are posted on the official Ballon d'Or site, which blocked our automated check. We left them out rather than guess. ## Which awards are handed out?

UEFA lists 13 prizes: the men's and women's Ballon d'Or, plus men's and women's awards for young talent, goalkeeper, coach, club and striker of the year, and the Sócrates Award. UEFA revealed the nominees on Sept. 8, with 30 men and 30 women up for the main trophy. This story does not predict winners.
