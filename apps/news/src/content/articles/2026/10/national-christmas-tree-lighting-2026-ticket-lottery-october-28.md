The National Park Service (NPS) says the 2026 National Christmas Tree lighting takes place **Thursday, Dec. 3** on the Ellipse in Washington, D.C., and its free ticket lottery is open now and closes at 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Wednesday, Nov. 4. Below is the lottery timeline with weekdays and times, the separate U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree, and a one-page printable.

## When does the National Christmas Tree ticket lottery run?

The NPS release of Oct. 19, 2026 gives these dates. The weekdays are ours, computed from the calendar. Mark them on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

| Step | Day and time | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Lottery opens | Wed., Oct. 28, 10 a.m. ET | NPS |
| Lottery closes | Wed., Nov. 4, 3 p.m. ET | NPS |
| Winners notified | Thu., Nov. 12 | NPS |
| Lighting ceremony | Thu., Dec. 3, on the Ellipse | NPS |

The NPS says each winner may receive up to five tickets. It also says there is no charge to enter the lottery or receive tickets. Entry is online at Recreation.gov, under the name National Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony. A reminder set for 10 a.m. ET on Oct. 28 will help you be there when it opens.

## What time is the ceremony and how can I watch?

The ceremony start time is not yet announced. The National Park Foundation says the Dec. 3 ceremony "will premiere nationwide on December 5," a Saturday, on the National Park Foundation's YouTube channel and on other national and local media channels, and it tells viewers to check local listings. A 2026 television broadcaster is not yet named. Check [thenationaltree.org](https://www.thenationaltree.org/) for updates.

## Is the U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree the same event?

No. The U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree is a separate event with its own website; see our [Capitol Christmas Tree 2026 tour schedule and stops](/news/us-capitol-christmas-tree-2026-tour-schedule-stops-dates/). In a release dated Mar. 17, 2026, it said the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests will provide the 2026 tree. The site says the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Forest Service provides a tree each year from one of 154 national forests. The 2026 tree is an Engelmann spruce from the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests in Idaho.

| Capitol tree item | 2026 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tree | Engelmann spruce, Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests, Idaho | U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree |
| Whistle-stop tour in Idaho | Thu., Oct. 29 (Grangeville) to Thu., Nov. 5 (Arco) | U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree |
| Lighting date | Not yet announced | U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree |

For comparison, the Architect of the Capitol listed last year's ceremony for Tuesday, Dec. 2, 2025 at 5 p.m. on the West Front Lawn. That is a 2025 detail, not a 2026 date.
