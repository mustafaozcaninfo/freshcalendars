## Which letters showed up most often?

We counted every letter in the 31 answers, counting a repeated letter each time it appears. The count covers 155 letters in all.

| Rank | Letter | Times used | Answers containing it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | E | 18 | 14 |
| 2 | R | 13 | 13 |
| 3 | S | 12 | 11 |
| 4 (tie) | O | 11 | 9 |
| 4 (tie) | Y | 11 | 11 |
| 6 (four-way tie) | A, D, I and T | 8 each | 8 each |

E led by a wide margin, in 14 of the 31 answers. R was in 13 answers and Y in 11, and no answer used Y twice.

## Which answers repeated a letter?

Ten of the 31 answers had at least one letter twice, about one in three:

- Usury (U)
- Peeve (E three times)
- Gaffe (F)
- Penny (N)
- Reset (E)
- Trill (L)
- Dress (S)
- Geeky (E)
- Motor (O)
- Yahoo (O)

Peeve is the only answer with a letter three times, three Es in a five-letter word.

## Which answers had only one vowel, or none?

Psych (#1943, Wed., Oct. 14) has no A, E, I, O or U, so a guess built around vowels would have found nothing. Only Y does the work of a vowel. Twelve more answers had exactly one of the five vowels: Ritzy, Shack, Dimly, Strew, Bumpy, Shady, Stink, Wrest, Penny, Trill, Dress and Grist. Peeve and Yahoo had three vowels each, the most of the month.

## What this tells you for November

These are patterns in last month's words, not a forecast of any answer. They can still shape how you play.

- **Spend early guesses on common letters.** E, R and S together made up 43 of the 155 letters in October's answers.
- **Try Y at the end.** Eight of 31 answers ended in Y, more than any other final letter.
- **Allow a repeated letter.** Ten of 31 answers repeated one, so ruling out doubles early would have been wrong about once in three days.
- **Do not count on vowels.** Psych had none, and 12 more answers had only one, so a vowel-heavy opener can come back mostly gray.
