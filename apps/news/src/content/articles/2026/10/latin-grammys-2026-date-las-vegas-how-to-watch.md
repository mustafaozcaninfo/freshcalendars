Oct. 27, 2026, 3:30 a.m. ET.</b> The Latin Recording Academy's Oct. 20 press release confirms the venue, the Univision broadcast and the start time: 8 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST) on Thursday, Nov. 12. The Latin Grammy Premiere time is still to be announced.</p>

**The 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards air live from the Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (MGM) Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas on Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026, beginning at 8 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST) on Univision, the Latin Recording Academy says.** The three-hour telecast follows a one-hour pre-show at 7 p.m. EST. Roselyn Sánchez returns as host for the tenth time.

The Academy announced the first performers on Oct. 20: Anitta, Banda Los Recoditos, Daddy Yankee, Jorge Drexler, Silvana Estrada, Kany García, Juanes, Carín León, Mon Laferte, Vivir Quintana, Rawayana, Rosalía, Elena Rose and Xavi. Daddy Yankee is the Latin Recording Academy Person of the Year.

## What time do the Latin Grammys start?

Univision's three-hour telecast begins at 8 p.m. EST on Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026, with a one-hour pre-show at 7 p.m. EST. Because the ceremony falls after the Nov. 1 clock change, the Eastern zone is on standard time. The Academy lists the telecast at 8 p.m. Eastern and Pacific and 7 p.m. Central.

| Time zone | Pre-show | Telecast |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern Standard Time (EST) | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. |
| Pacific Standard Time (PST) | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. |
| Central Standard Time (CST) | 6 p.m. | 7 p.m. |
| Mountain | Check local Univision listings | Check local Univision listings |

For Mountain time, check local Univision listings; the Academy's release does not give a Mountain start time.

## Who performs at the Latin Grammys 2026?

The Latin Recording Academy announced the first performers on Oct. 20, 2026: Anitta, Banda Los Recoditos, Daddy Yankee, Jorge Drexler, Silvana Estrada, Kany García, Juanes, Carín León, Mon Laferte, Vivir Quintana, Rawayana, Rosalía, Elena Rose and Xavi. Daddy Yankee is also the Latin Recording Academy Person of the Year.

## What is the Latin Grammy Premiere?

The Latin Grammy Premiere, where most of the categories are awarded, takes place before the main telecast. The Academy said its details will be announced soon, so its start time is not yet announced.

## How to watch the Latin Grammys 2026

In the U.S., the show airs on Univision. Agenzia Nazionale Stampa Associata (ANSA) reported on July 3, 2026 that Radiotelevisione Italiana (RAI) carries the show in Italy. We did not find a streaming service or replay plan in our sources, so we do not name one. Check Univision's listings closer to Nov. 12.

## What happens during Latin Grammy week?

ANSA reported that Daddy Yankee, the Latin Recording Academy Person of the Year, is honored at a gala the evening before the ceremony.
