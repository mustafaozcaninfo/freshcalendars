<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 25, 2026.</b> The Latin Recording Academy lists the date; venue, broadcaster and start time below are as reported and will be confirmed.</p>

**The 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards fall on Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026, according to the Latin Recording Academy, whose site lists the "27.a Entrega Anual" (27th annual ceremony) for "12 de Noviembre."** Agenzia Nazionale Stampa Associata (ANSA) reported on July 3 that the show is held at the Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas and airs on Univision in the U.S. The start time has not been announced.

## What is confirmed and what is only reported?

| Detail | Status | What we have |
|---|---|---|
| Ceremony date | Confirmed by the Academy | Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026 |
| Nominations | Confirmed by the Academy | Announced Sept. 16, 2026 |
| First performers | Confirmed by the Academy | Announced Oct. 20, 2026 |
| Venue | Reported by ANSA, July 3, 2026 | Grand Garden Arena, Las Vegas |
| U.S. broadcaster | Reported by ANSA, July 3, 2026 | Univision |
| Italy broadcaster | Reported by ANSA, July 3, 2026 | Radiotelevisione Italiana (RAI) |
| Start time | Not yet announced | Last year: 8 p.m. ET and PT |

The weekday is our computation from the Academy's date. We will confirm the venue, broadcaster and time against the Academy's own announcements when they appear.

## What happens during Latin Grammy week?

ANSA also reported that Daddy Yankee is honored as Person of the Year the evening before the ceremony.

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Wed., Sept. 16 | Nominations announced | Latin Recording Academy |
| Tue., Oct. 20 | First performers announced | Latin Recording Academy |
| Wed., Nov. 11 | Person of the Year gala, Daddy Yankee honored | ANSA, reported |
| Thu., Nov. 12 | 27th Latin Grammy Awards | Latin Recording Academy |

## How to watch the Latin Grammys 2026

In the U.S., ANSA reported the show is broadcast on Univision. In Italy, the same report said RAI is the broadcaster, which was the point of the July 3 article. We did not find a streaming service or replay plan in our sources, so we do not name one. Check Univision's listings closer to Nov. 12.

## What time do the Latin Grammys start?

Not yet announced for 2026. For comparison only, the Academy's 2025 announcement said the telecast started at 8 p.m. ET and PT. Treat that as last year's time and wait for the 2026 confirmation.
