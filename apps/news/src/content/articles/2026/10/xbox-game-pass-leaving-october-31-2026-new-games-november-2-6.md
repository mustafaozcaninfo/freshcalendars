**Nine games leave Xbox Game Pass today, Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026, according to Xbox Wire's Game Pass post of Oct. 20.** Each is listed for Cloud, Console and PC. Football Manager 26 follows on Tuesday, Nov. 10, and Xbox Wire's Oct. 30 post lists five arrivals for Nov. 2 to 5. One table below puts both lists in date order, and a free printable follows.

<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 31, 2026.</b> Xbox Wire gives dates only. Microsoft states no time of day for removals, and this story does not state one.</p>

## Which games leave Xbox Game Pass on Oct. 31, 2026?

Xbox Wire's "Leaving October 31" list has nine titles, and all nine are marked "Cloud, Console, and PC".

| Game | Leaves | Platforms listed |
|---|---|---|
| Day of the Tentacle Remastered | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Dead Static Drive | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Deep Rock Galactic | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Full Throttle Remastered | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Grim Fandango Remastered | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Lara Croft and the Temple of Osiris | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Lego Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Wartales | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Wildgate | Sat., Oct. 31 | Cloud, Console, PC |

## What is coming to Xbox Game Pass on Nov. 2 to 6?

Xbox Wire editor Will Fulton's "Next Week on Xbox" post of Oct. 30 lists five games with a Game Pass label. The labels below are copied from that post; the post does not say which tier carries Gravhounds or Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School, so this story does not either.

| Game | Date | Xbox Wire label |
|---|---|---|
| Gravhounds (Game Preview) | Mon., Nov. 2 | Game Pass / Xbox Play Anywhere |
| Pit of Goblin | Tue., Nov. 3 | Game Pass / Xbox Play Anywhere |
| Football Manager 27 (PC Early Access) | Tue., Nov. 3 | Game Pass |
| Void/Breaker (1.0) | Wed., Nov. 4 | PC Game Pass |
| Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School | Thu., Nov. 5 | Game Pass |

The Oct. 20 post also lists Pit of Goblin for Cloud, Xbox Series X|S and PC on Nov. 3. For Football Manager 27, Xbox Wire says: "Pre-orders on PC, Game Pass Ultimate, and PC Game Pass will grant early access starting on November 3."

## What leaves and arrives from Oct. 31 to Nov. 10?

The Oct. 20 post also lists Football Manager 26 (PC) and Football Manager 26 - Console Edition (Cloud, Console and PC) as leaving Nov. 10. Weekdays below are computed from the dates.

| Date | Leaves | Arrives |
|---|---|---|
| Sat., Oct. 31 | The nine games above | none listed |
| Mon., Nov. 2 | none listed | Gravhounds (Game Preview) |
| Tue., Nov. 3 | none listed | Pit of Goblin; Football Manager 27 (PC Early Access) |
| Wed., Nov. 4 | none listed | Void/Breaker (1.0) |
| Thu., Nov. 5 | none listed | Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School |
| Tue., Nov. 10 | Football Manager 26 (PC); Football Manager 26 - Console Edition | none listed |

"None listed" means the two Xbox Wire posts above name nothing for that date. The Football Manager 26 exit comes seven days after Football Manager 27 starts on Nov. 3, counted from the two dates.

## What does Xbox Game Pass cost?

Xbox Wire said on April 21, 2026 that Game Pass Ultimate drops from $29.99 to $22.99 a month and that PC Game Pass drops from $16.49 to $13.99 a month. The tier names on xbox.com are Essential, Premium, Ultimate and PC Game Pass. This story has no Essential or Premium prices because the sources above do not give them.
