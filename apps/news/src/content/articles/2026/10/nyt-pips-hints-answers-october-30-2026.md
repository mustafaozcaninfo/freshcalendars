## How Pips works

You place every domino so that each colored region meets its condition. A number means the pips in the region add up to it; = means every half shows the same value; ≠ means all are different; > and < set a total above or below a number; a region with no condition takes anything. Rows are counted from the top and columns from the left, so R2C5 is the second row, fifth column.

## Solving tips

Look for single cells with a sum target: a cell that must add up to 0, 3 or 6 can only hold that value. An "equal" region shrinks your options fast once one value is known; with three cells, as in Friday's Hard board, you need three matching halves, and a double can supply two of them. On the Hard board, count the blanks: there are two 0 halves among the 13 dominoes.
