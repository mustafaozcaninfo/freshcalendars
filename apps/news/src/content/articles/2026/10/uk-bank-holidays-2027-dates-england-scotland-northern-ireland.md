**The UK has eight bank holidays in England and Wales in 2027, nine in Scotland and 10 in Northern Ireland**, according to GOV.UK. Two things stand out. Easter is early, with Good Friday on March 26. And Christmas Day falls on a Saturday, so the Christmas and Boxing Day bank holidays move to **Monday, Dec. 27 and Tuesday, Dec. 28, 2027**.

|---|---|---|---|

| Dec. 28 | Tuesday | Boxing Day (substitute day) | All UK nations |

## Substitute days in 2027

When a bank holiday falls on a weekend, a weekday is taken instead. GOV.UK lists three substitute days in 2027:

- **Monday, Jan. 4** in Scotland, because Jan. 2 is a Saturday.
- **Monday, Dec. 27**, for Christmas Day on Saturday, Dec. 25.
- **Tuesday, Dec. 28**, for Boxing Day on Sunday, Dec. 26.

That gives most workers a four-day Christmas weekend, from Saturday, Dec. 25 to Tuesday, Dec. 28. New Year's Day 2028 also falls on a Saturday, and GOV.UK already lists its substitute day: Monday, Jan. 3, 2028 (with Scotland's 2nd January moving to Tuesday, Jan. 4).

## Planning around the long weekends

Five Monday bank holidays in England and Wales make 2027 easy to plan around: Easter Monday, early May, late May, the August bank holiday and the Christmas substitute day on Dec. 27. Taking four days of leave from Tuesday, March 30 to Friday, April 2 turns Easter into a 10-day break, from Good Friday to Sunday, April 4. Four days after the late-May bank holiday, June 1-4, give a nine-day break.

Bank holidays are not automatically paid days off. Whether you get them depends on your employment contract, and many shops and services open on some of them.
