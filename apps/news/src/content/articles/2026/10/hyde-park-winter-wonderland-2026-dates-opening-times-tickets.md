**Hyde Park Winter Wonderland 2026 opens on Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026 and runs to Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027**, according to the official Winter Wonderland website and The Royal Parks. The site says it is open "everyday" in that period, "apart from Christmas Day." Opening times in this story are Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), the time in London.

On Nov. 19 the gates open at 12 p.m. (7 a.m. Eastern Time, ET, by our calculation; both sides of the Atlantic are on standard time by then). Our day-by-day table below covers all 45 open days, and a one-page printable of it is in the downloads.

## What are the Winter Wonderland 2026 dates?

| Item | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Preview Night | Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026 (tickets sold out) | The Royal Parks |
| Opening day | Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026 | Winter Wonderland; The Royal Parks |
| Closed | Friday, Dec. 25, 2026 | Winter Wonderland |
| Final day | Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027 (a Family Fun Day, details to come) | Winter Wonderland |

From Nov. 19 to Jan. 3 inclusive there are 46 calendar days. With Christmas Day closed, that leaves 45 open days, by our count. The site lists no special hours for Dec. 24.

## How does 2026 compare with last year?

In 2025, The Royal Parks listed the run as Friday, Nov. 14, 2025 to Thursday, Jan. 1, 2026 (published Nov. 1, 2025). The 2026 run opens 5 days later and ends 2 days later, by our calculation. Treat the 2025 dates as last year's pattern only.

## How do Winter Wonderland tickets work?

The Tickets Guide describes entry tickets in one-hour slots from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m., with last entry at 9:30 p.m. Slots come in three types: Off-Peak, Standard (gold) and Peak (orange). The guide says Peak slots are "mostly later in the day and over weekends." We did not find a list of 2026 Peak dates.

- **Off-Peak:** "always free to enter (you must still book an entry ticket)," the Tickets Guide says.
- **Free entry with spend:** spending £25 in one transaction when pre-booking gives free entry.
- **Advance prices:** available until 23:59 the day before the visit.

A standard entry price is not given clearly enough on the page to quote, and a ticket on-sale date was not stated when we checked on Oct. 27, 2026. We have not found 2025 prices either.

## What do attractions cost?

The home page shows "from" prices: Coaster Pass £43.10, Explorer Pass £27.45, Santa Land Unlimited Ride Pass £27.50 and Santa Land Package (child) from £36.50. It names Gandeys K-Pop Dragon Circus, The Giant Wheel, The Ice Rink (age 3 and over), Bar Ice (new for 2026) and Ice Sculpting Workshops (age 12 and over).

Magical Ice Kingdom presents Neverland runs Nov. 19 to Jan. 3, 10:00 to 21:00, and is for under-12s with an adult aged 18 or over. Advance prices from its page:

## How do you get to Hyde Park Winter Wonderland?

The Getting Here page names Hyde Park Corner (Piccadilly line), Knightsbridge (Piccadilly line) and Marble Arch (Central line) as the closest stations, and expects them to be busy. For step-free access it points to Green Park (about a 17-minute walk) or Bond Street (about a 14-minute walk). There is no Elizabeth line service on Dec. 26, which runs a Boxing Day timetable.
