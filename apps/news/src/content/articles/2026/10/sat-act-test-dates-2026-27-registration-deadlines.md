The regular registration deadline for the Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026 ACT passed Nov. 9, according to ACT. Late registration stays open until Sunday, Nov. 29 at 11:59 p.m. Central Time, with a $42 late fee. For the Dec. 5 SAT, College Board's deadline is Friday, Nov. 20 (late by Tuesday, Nov. 24).

**The Dec. 12, 2026 ACT is now in its late-registration window, which ends Sunday, Nov. 29, and the Dec. 5 SAT deadline is Friday, Nov. 20**, according to ACT and College Board. The next regular ACT deadline is Friday, Jan. 22 for the Feb. 27, 2027 test. The Nov. 7 SAT has already taken place.

## SAT test dates 2026-27

| Test date | Registration deadline | Late registration |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026 | Nov. 20 | Nov. 24 |
| Saturday, March 6, 2027 | Feb. 19 | Feb. 23 |
| Saturday, May 1, 2027 | April 16 | April 20 |
| Saturday, June 5, 2027 | May 21 | May 25 |

SAT deadlines are 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. The SAT is digital, taken in the Bluebook app on a laptop or tablet. Students who need to borrow a testing device from College Board must register at least 30 days before test day. College Board also runs in-school SAT testing on weekdays during two windows, Oct. 1-30, 2026 and March 1-April 30, 2027; ask your school whether it takes part.

There is no SAT in January or February 2027, so the gap between the Dec. 5 and March 6 tests is three months.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026 | Nov. 9 (passed) | Nov. 29 | Dec. 7 | Dec. 22 |
| Saturday, Feb. 27, 2027 | Jan. 22 | Feb. 9 | Feb. 22 | March 16 |

| Saturday, June 12, 2027 | May 7 | May 25 | June 7 | June 23 |
| Saturday, July 10, 2027 | June 4 | June 22 | July 5 | July 20 |

ACT deadlines are 11:59 p.m. Central Time. Standby testing means showing up without a confirmed seat; it costs extra and is not guaranteed.

## What the ACT costs

The base ACT costs $70. Adding the writing test costs $25 more, and the optional science section $5, according to ACT. Late registration adds $42 to the test fee, which makes $112 for the base ACT in the late window. Standby carries an additional fee. Students who qualify can request fee waivers from their school counselor for both the SAT and the ACT.

## Anticipated SAT dates for 2027-28

College Board lists anticipated SAT weekend dates for the next testing year. It has not yet posted registration deadlines for them.

| Season | Anticipated SAT dates |
|---|---|
| Fall 2027 | Aug. 28, Sept. 18, Oct. 9, Nov. 6, Dec. 4 |
| Spring 2028 | March 4, May 6, June 3 |

The SAT costs $68 and late registration adds $38 through June 2027, according to College Board.

## Picking a date

Juniors often take a first test in the spring and keep a summer or fall date for a retake. Seniors applying early should check each college's score deadline before counting on the Dec. 5 SAT or [the Dec. 12 ACT](/news/act-december-12-2026-registration-deadline-november-6/), since scores arrive weeks after test day. Watch for school breaks too. The March 6 SAT comes two weeks before spring break in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties (March 22-26), and the May 1 SAT falls the day after [NYC public schools](/news/nyc-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks-holidays/) end spring recess on April 30.
