**The next SAT is Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, and the registration deadline is this Friday, Oct. 23**, according to College Board. Students who miss it can still register until Tuesday, Oct. 27 with a late fee. The next ACT is Saturday, Dec. 12, with a regular deadline of Nov. 6. Between them, the two tests offer 10 more Saturdays this school year.

## SAT test dates 2026-27

| Test date | Registration deadline | Late registration |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026 | Oct. 23 | Oct. 27 |
| Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026 | Nov. 20 | Nov. 24 |
| Saturday, March 6, 2027 | Feb. 19 | Feb. 23 |
| Saturday, May 1, 2027 | April 16 | April 20 |
| Saturday, June 5, 2027 | May 21 | May 25 |

SAT deadlines are 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. The SAT is digital, taken in the Bluebook app on a laptop or tablet. Students who need to borrow a testing device from College Board must register at least 30 days before test day, which for the Nov. 7 exam has already passed. College Board also runs in-school SAT testing on weekdays during two windows, Oct. 1-30, 2026 and March 1-April 30, 2027; ask your school whether it takes part.

There is no SAT in January or February 2027, so the gap between the Dec. 5 and March 6 tests is three months.

## ACT test dates 2026-27

| Test date | Regular deadline | Late deadline | Standby deadline | Scores (target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026 | Nov. 6 | Nov. 29 | Dec. 7 | Dec. 22 |
| Saturday, Feb. 27, 2027 | Jan. 22 | Feb. 9 | Feb. 22 | March 16 |
| Saturday, April 10, 2027 | March 5 | March 23 | April 5 | April 20 |
| Saturday, June 12, 2027 | May 7 | May 25 | June 7 | June 23 |
| Saturday, July 10, 2027 | June 4 | June 22 | July 5 | July 20 |

ACT deadlines are 11:59 p.m. Central Time. Students who sat the Oct. 17 ACT have a target score release of Oct. 27. Standby testing means showing up without a confirmed seat; it costs extra and is not guaranteed.

## What the ACT costs

The base ACT costs $70. Adding the writing test costs $25 more, and the optional science section $5, according to ACT. Late registration and standby carry additional fees. Students who qualify can request fee waivers from their school counselor for both the SAT and the ACT.

## Picking a date

Juniors often take a first test in the spring and keep a summer or fall date for a retake. Seniors applying early should check each college's score deadline before counting on the Nov. 7 SAT or the Dec. 12 ACT, since scores arrive weeks after test day. Watch for school breaks too. The March 6 SAT comes two weeks before spring break in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties (March 22-26), and the May 1 SAT falls the day after [NYC public schools](/news/nyc-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks-holidays/) end spring recess on April 30.
