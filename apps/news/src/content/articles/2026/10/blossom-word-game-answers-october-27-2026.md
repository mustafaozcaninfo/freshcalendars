**Today's Blossom from Merriam-Webster, for Tuesday, Oct. 27, 2026, puts I in the center with the petals A, C, D, N, O and R.** Two everyday words use all seven letters, one a musical instrument and one a word for a harsh rule. Below are the rules, words to try and hints for the pangrams, which are revealed in a marked spoiler section at the end.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** I (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** A, C, D, N, O, R

## How Blossom works

You get 12 words to score as many points as you can. Each word must be at least four letters long and include the center letter, and letters can be used more than once. Proper nouns, hyphenated words and offensive words do not count. Longer words score more, a pangram, a word that uses all seven letters, earns 7 extra points, and each use of the highlighted bonus petal earns 5 more, so check which petal is lit before each word.

## Words to try

With I in the middle and C, D, N and R around it, plenty of short words fall out quickly: Acid, Arid, Iron, Icon, Coin, Raid and Radio. Longer ones score more, so look for Nadir, Cairn, Ironic, Rancid, Ordain and Candid. Words with repeated letters, such as Candid, Carrion and Iconic, collect the petal bonus twice when the repeated letter is the one highlighted. Save a pangram for a turn when a common petal such as A or O is lit.

## Pangram hints

- **Pangram 1** (9 letters): a squeezebox, the instrument played by pushing and pulling its bellows.
- **Pangram 2** (9 letters): very harsh or severe, said of a rule or punishment, after an ancient Athenian lawmaker.
