**Groundhog Day 2027 falls on Tuesday, Feb. 2, and the Punxsutawney Groundhog Club, which runs the event at Gobbler's Knob, has not yet posted its 2027 schedule or the time of Phil's prediction.** The club's [schedule page](https://groundhog.org/groundhog-day/groundhog-day-events/schedule-info/) links to its events listing, so that is where the official time will appear. This story gives the date, last year's call, Phil's record from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) and a count of what "six more weeks" means on the calendar.

## What day and time is Groundhog Day 2027?

The date is fixed at Feb. 2. The weekday changes from year to year, as the table shows.

| Year | Groundhog Day |
|---|---|
| 2027 | Tuesday, Feb. 2 |
| 2028 | Wednesday, Feb. 2 |
| 2029 | Friday, Feb. 2 |
| 2030 | Saturday, Feb. 2 |
| 2031 | Sunday, Feb. 2 |

The 2027 prediction time is not yet announced. Check the club's schedule page closer to the day; this story will carry the time once the club posts it. Our [February printable calendars](/february-calendars.html) have a grid with Tuesday, Feb. 2 ready to circle.

## What did Punxsutawney Phil predict in 2026?

The Associated Press (AP) reported on Feb. 2, 2026 that Phil saw his shadow, which forecasts six more weeks of winter. The Washington Times ran a report with the same result. The 2027 forecast has not happened yet, and nothing here predicts it.

## How accurate is Punxsutawney Phil?

NOAA's National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI) says: "On average, Phil has gotten it right 30% of the time over the past 10 years." That figure comes from NCEI's page "Groundhog Day Forecasts and Climate History," updated on Jan. 30, 2026, so it covers the 10 years up to that update and will shift as new forecasts are scored.

## When do the six more weeks end in 2027?

Tradition says a shadow means six more weeks of winter. Counting 42 days from Tuesday, Feb. 2, 2027 lands on Tuesday, March 16, 2027. If Phil sees his shadow in 2027, that is the date the forecast would run to. Our [March printable calendars](/march-calendars.html) show it in the month grid. The tracker below has a row for it.

| Date | What it marks |
|---|---|
| Tuesday, Feb. 2, 2027 | Groundhog Day and the forecast |
| Tuesday, March 16, 2027 | Six weeks after Groundhog Day |
