## How Pips works

You place every domino so that each colored region meets its condition. A number means the pips in the region add up to it; = means every half shows the same value; ≠ means all are different; > and < set a total above or below a number; a region with no condition takes anything. Rows are counted from the top and columns from the left, so R2C5 is the second row, fifth column.

## Solving tips

Look for sums that only one pair can make: 11 needs a 5 and a 6, 12 needs the 6-6, 0 needs blanks. An "equal" region shrinks your options fast once one value is known; with four cells, as in Monday's column 7, you need four matching halves, and a double can supply two of them. On the Hard board, count the blanks: there are six 0 halves among the 16 dominoes.
