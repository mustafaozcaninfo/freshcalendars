**Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving, is not a federal holiday, but at least 22 states list it as a paid holiday for state employees, according to each state's official holiday schedule.** The U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) federal holidays list names Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, Nov. 26, and no Friday. Each state decides for its own employees. The table below uses each state's own page and its name for the day.

## Is the day after Thanksgiving a federal holiday?

No. The [OPM federal holidays page](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/) does not list the Friday after Thanksgiving. For when Thanksgiving falls, see [When is Thanksgiving?](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

## Which states close state offices on Friday, Nov. 27, 2026?

Twenty-two states list the day as a paid holiday or closure. Several use a different name, such as Family Day in Nevada, American Indian Heritage Day in Maryland and Native American Heritage Day in Washington. Indiana moves Lincoln's Birthday to the Friday and observes Washington's Birthday on Thursday, Dec. 24.

Louisiana's Acadian Day is a governor's proclamation, and Missouri's closure is an executive order, so both are set for 2026 only. The Louisiana proclamation text is read from a copy hosted outside a state domain.

## Which states could not be confirmed?

This story could not confirm the day from a readable official page for 18 states: Alabama, Alaska, Arkansas, Colorado, Maine, Massachusetts, Michigan, Mississippi, New Hampshire, New Mexico, North Dakota, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Tennessee, Utah, West Virginia and Wisconsin. They are not marked yes or no. Check with your agency.

## Are motor vehicle offices open on Friday, Nov. 27, 2026?

Four state motor vehicle agencies, including the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) in California, say they are closed on the day, based on their official pages read Nov. 8, 2026. Other states' motor vehicle agencies did not list Nov. 27 on pages we could read, so check your state's agency before going.

| State | Motor vehicle agency status on Nov. 27, 2026 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| California | Closed. The DMV lists "Thursday, November 26 and Friday, November 27 - Thanksgiving" on its 2026 holiday list, which runs from New Year's Day 2026 to New Year's Day 2027. | [California DMV holidays](https://qr.dmv.ca.gov/portal/?p=5335) |
| Florida | State offices closed. The Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV) lists "Friday after Thanksgiving," "Friday, November 27, 2026." County tax collector offices set their own hours. | [FLHSMV office closures](https://www.flhsmv.gov/locations/temporary-office-closures/) |
| Oregon | Closed. All DMV offices are closed for "Day after Thanksgiving - Friday, Nov. 27, 2026." | [Oregon DMV statewide holiday closures](https://www.oregon.gov/ODOT/DMV/pages/offices/closures.aspx) |
| Pennsylvania | Closed. Driver and Vehicle Services lists "November 27, 2026 - Day after Thanksgiving." | [Pennsylvania Driver and Vehicle Services](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dmv/alerts/holiday-closings) |

## What about union contracts and private employers?

Union contracts can differ from a state's published list. Private employers set their own policy; for retail see [Black Friday 2026 store hours](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/), and for grocery stores see [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/).
