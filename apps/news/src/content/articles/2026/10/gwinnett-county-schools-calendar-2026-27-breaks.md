**Gwinnett County Public Schools (GCPS) has no school from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 for Thanksgiving break**, according to its 2026-27 School Year Calendar, the approved calendar linked on the district's calendar page. Winter break follows from **Monday, Dec. 21 through Friday, Jan. 1**, and spring break is **Monday, Apr. 5 through Friday, Apr. 9, 2027**. The last day of school is Wednesday, May 26, 2027.

## Gwinnett County 2026-27 breaks

| Break | Dates | Students return |
|---|---|---|
| Fall break (already past) | Mon.-Fri., Oct. 12-16, 2026 | Monday, Oct. 19 |
| Thanksgiving break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Tuesday, Jan. 5 (Jan. 4 is a student holiday) |
| Spring break | Mon.-Fri., Apr. 5-9, 2027 | Monday, Apr. 12 |

The school year began Wednesday, Aug. 5, 2026. The first semester ends Friday, Dec. 18 (Day 87), and the second semester begins Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027 (Day 88). The last day, Wednesday, May 26, 2027, is Day 180.

## Which days are no school in Gwinnett County?

The table lists every weekday the calendar marks as a school holiday, student holiday or systemwide holiday, with the weekday worked out from the date.

| Date | Day | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day (Systemwide Holiday), past |
| Oct. 12-16, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Fall break, past |
| Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Thanksgiving break |
| Dec. 21, 2026-Jan. 1, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Winter break |
| Jan. 4, 2027 | Monday | Teacher Planning/Staff Development (Student Holiday) |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) Day (Systemwide Holiday) |
| Feb. 12, 2027 | Friday | Student/Teacher Holiday |
| Feb. 15-16, 2027 | Monday-Tuesday | Student/Teacher Holidays |
| Apr. 5-9, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Spring break |

Added up, that is 31 weekdays without students before the last day: 1 for Labor Day, 5 for fall break, 5 for Thanksgiving, 10 for winter break, 1 on Jan. 4, 1 for MLK Day, 3 in February and 5 for spring break. Six are behind us, so 25 remain. Memorial Day, Monday, May 31, 2027, falls after the last day of school.

## Early release and digital learning days

These days are on the calendar but are not full days off.

| Date | What the calendar lists |
|---|---|
| Fri., Sept. 18, 2026 | Digital Learning Day #1 |
| Tue., Nov. 3, 2026 | Digital Learning Day #2 |
| Wed.-Thu., Oct. 28-29, 2026 | Early release, elementary and middle school |
| Wed.-Fri., Dec. 16-18, 2026 | Early release for high school exams |
| Wed.-Thu., Mar. 3-4, 2027 | Early release, elementary and middle school |
| Fri., Mar. 19, 2027 | Digital Learning Day #3 |
| Mon.-Wed., May 24-26, 2027 | Early release for high school exams |
