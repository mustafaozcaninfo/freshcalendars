Halloween is Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026, so shoppers who start on Thursday, Oct. 29 have two days left, and no official nationwide list of Halloween store hours exists in the sources we checked on Oct. 29. This page covers where to look for a costume, what changed at Party City, and a warning from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) about decorative contact lenses. For the date itself, see [when is Halloween](/when-is/halloween/) and the [October 2026 printable calendar](/october-calendars.html).

## Where can I find a Halloween costume in the last two days?

The table shows what we could confirm for three retailers and where to check the rest.

| Retailer | What we confirmed | Where to check hours or stock |
|---|---|---|
| Spirit Halloween | Seasonal stores; an official locator searches by city or ZIP code. Hours, store count and closing dates were not confirmed. | [Spirit Halloween store locator](https://stores.spirithalloween.com/), or call the store |
| Party City | Closed its stores in early 2025, as widely reported. In April 2026 Staples announced Party City shops inside more than 700 Staples locations and on Staples.com. | The Staples store page |
| Target | Its Aug. 12, 2026 newsroom story says Drive Up and Order Pickup make it easy to bring home Halloween finds. No Halloween hours were published. | Target's store page or app |

Walmart, Walgreens, CVS and Amazon published no Halloween 2026 hours or order-by dates that we could find. Check the store page or app rather than relying on a guess.

## What is Spirit Halloween's schedule?

Spirit Halloween runs seasonal stores, and its official locator lets you search by city or ZIP code. Its website returned errors to our check on Oct. 29, so we cannot give hours, a store count or a closing date. Hours and seasonal closing dates vary by store. Open the locator page for the store nearest you, or call it, before you make the trip.

## What happened to Party City?

Party City announced in December 2024 that it would close its stores, and most had closed by early 2025, as widely reported. In April 2026, Staples announced Party City shops inside more than 700 Staples locations and on Staples.com, with plans to expand by the end of 2026, according to [Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/party-city-finds-a-new-home-in-700-staples-stores/619044/) and [FOX 7 Austin](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/party-city-staples).

An old "Party City near me" listing may point to a closed store. Check the Staples store page instead.

## Can I use Target pickup for a costume?

Target's Aug. 12, 2026 newsroom story, "Target's Halloween Assortment Brings Together Style, Fandom and Family Fun at Incredible Value," says that "using Drive Up or choosing Order Pickup, Target makes it easy to bring home stylish Halloween finds." Target published no Halloween hours in that story, so confirm your store's hours and an item's stock on its page or in the app.

## Why should I skip decorative contact lenses?

The FDA's page "Decorative Contact Lenses for Halloween and More" (content current as of Oct. 22, 2024) says: "All contact lenses must be prescribed by a doctor, including decorative lenses." It also says you should never buy contact lenses from a street vendor, beauty supply store, flea market, novelty store or Halloween store.
