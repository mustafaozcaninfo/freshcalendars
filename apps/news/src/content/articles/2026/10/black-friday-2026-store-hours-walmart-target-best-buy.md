**Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, and most big chains will open their doors between 5 and 6 a.m. after closing for Thanksgiving.** Walmart and Home Depot open at 6 a.m. on Black Friday, and Kohl's and JCPenney at 5 a.m.; Best Buy, Dick's, Costco and Lowe's have confirmed Thanksgiving closures but no Black Friday hour. Target, Macy's and Sam's Club have not announced 2026 hours, so last year's hours are the guide for them. Below: the opening times, who closes on Thanksgiving, when the online sales start, and a free planner in three styles.

The 2025 hours come from The Associated Press's Black Friday roundup. The 2026 Thanksgiving closures and openings come from each company's own announcement: Walmart (Oct. 7 and Oct. 26), Best Buy (Oct. 27), Dick's (Oct. 15), Kohl's (Oct. 30), JCPenney (Nov. 2), Costco (Nov. 3), Lowe's (Oct. 8) and Home Depot's newsroom. Hours can differ by location, especially in malls, so check your store's page the night before.

## Which stores close on Thanksgiving

Most national chains now stay closed on Thanksgiving Day. For 2026, Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, Best Buy, Kohl's, JCPenney, Dick's and Costco have confirmed it. Kohl's stores reopen at 5 a.m. local time on Friday, Nov. 27, and Costco said it will be closed on Thursday, Nov. 26. Walmart said stores "will be closed again on Thanksgiving and Christmas this year," and Lowe's said its stores "will remain closed on Thanksgiving Day" while online deals continue. Target and Macy's closed on Thanksgiving in 2025 and have not announced 2026 hours. Grocery stores and pharmacies are the main exception: many open on Thanksgiving morning with shorter hours, so check before you go. Our [Costco holiday hours list](/news/costco-holiday-hours-closures-2026-2027/) has every day Costco closes through 2027.

## When the deals start

Black Friday sales now run for weeks before the day itself. Lowe's started "Four Weeks of Black Friday Deals" on Oct. 29, followed by 12 Days of Deals beginning Nov. 16, with early online access for MyLowe's Rewards members. This year Walmart runs Early Black Friday Deals from Nov. 12 to 15 and Black Friday Deals from Nov. 23 to 29, with online access at 12:01 a.m. ET and stores at 6 a.m. local time, it announced on Oct. 26. JCPenney's Black Friday Deals run Nov. 20 to 29, with in-store giveaways at 5 a.m., 9 a.m., 1 p.m. and 5 p.m. on Nov. 27. Best Buy said on Oct. 27: "The holiday savings pick up Nov. 6 with the arrival of Early Black Friday Deals and Doorbusters," and "Starting Nov. 19, Best Buy will offer 10 days of Black Friday doorbusters and deals." Last year Walmart's events ran Nov. 14 to 16 and Nov. 25 to 30, and Amazon's Black Friday Week ran Nov. 20 to Dec. 1. For 2026, Amazon's page confirms Black Friday on Nov. 27 and Cyber Monday on Nov. 30, but Amazon has not announced when its 2026 Black Friday Week starts.
