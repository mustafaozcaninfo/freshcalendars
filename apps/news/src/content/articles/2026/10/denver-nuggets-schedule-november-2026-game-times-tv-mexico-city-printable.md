**The Denver Nuggets' next game is Monday, Nov. 2, 2026: the Portland Trail Blazers at Denver at 9 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), which is 7 p.m. Mountain Time (MT), according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule. Denver plays 14 games in November, including the NBA Mexico City Game against Indiana on Saturday, Nov. 7.**

The NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF is dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." It gives times in ET. The table below adds a Mountain column, Denver's time, for every Nuggets game in November, with the home or away site, national TV and NBA Cup games. A one-page printable of the same 14 games is further down.

## When and where is the Nuggets Mexico City game?

Denver and Indiana play on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, at an arena in Mexico City. The NBA schedule lists it at 5 p.m. ET and 3 p.m. MT, which is 4 p.m. local time in Mexico City. In its April 16, 2026 release, the NBA said the game will air live in Mexico on ESPN, "across Latin America and around the world." The NBA schedule lists no U.S. national TV for it. The PDF writes the matchup as "Denver vs Indiana," and this story does not say which team is the designated home team.

## Which Nuggets games are in the Emirates NBA Cup?

Denver's Cup group is Houston, Phoenix, Dallas and Utah, per the NBA's Cup groups announcement, updated Oct. 28, 2026. The NBA says group play runs every Friday from Oct. 30 through Nov. 27, plus "Cup Nights" on Tuesday, Nov. 24 and Wednesday, Nov. 25. Three Nuggets games in November carry the Cup mark in the schedule: Houston at Denver on Fri., Nov. 20 (Prime Video), Dallas at Denver on Tue., Nov. 24 (NBC/Peacock) and Denver at Utah on Fri., Nov. 27. The Oct. 30 game at Phoenix was a Cup group game too. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) lists every round, and the [Lakers' November schedule](/news/lakers-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-nba-cup-printable/) has the same layout for Los Angeles.

## Where to watch the Nuggets in November 2026

The NBA's schedule lists national TV only: Prime Video on Nov. 20 and NBC/Peacock on Nov. 24. For the rest, the team's How to Watch page says "The local TV home for the Denver Nuggets continues to be Altitude Sports" and that Altitude partners with 9News to broadcast 20 Nuggets games over the air. That page does not say which November games those are, so check it before tip-off. For the games on a given night, see [NBA games today on Nov. 1](/news/nba-games-today-november-1-2026/).
