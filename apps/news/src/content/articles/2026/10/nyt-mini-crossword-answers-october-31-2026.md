**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026, is a seven-by-seven grid with 16 clues by Joel Fagliano.** On Halloween, three of the Across clues ask for half of one candy and half of another. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (3 letters), *Candy holder for a trick-or-treater*: the sack or pail a child holds open at each door.
- **4-Across** (4 letters), *Org. interested in Mars and the Milky Way*: the American space agency, written as a four-letter acronym.
- **5-Across** (7 letters), *Half Starburst, half Skittle?*: the first four letters of one candy name plus the last three of the other; it means to give someone a sudden fright.
- **8-Across** (3 letters), *Golfer's goal*: the number of strokes a hole is rated at.
- **9-Across** (3 letters), *Iconic Australian animal, for short*: a clipped form of kangaroo.
- **10-Across** (7 letters), *Half Airhead, half candy corn?*: the first three letters come from Airhead; the last four are candy corn with one letter changed, and together they name a loud noisemaker.
- **12-Across** (6 letters), *Half Mounds, half Reese's?*: the first three letters come from Mounds and the last three from Reese's.
- **13-Across** (3 letters), *Female sheep*: an adult one, which sounds like the word "you".

**Down**

- **1-Down** (3 letters), *Candy ___*: a slab of chocolate; it also names a place with stools.
- **2-Down** (6 letters), *Houston baseball team*: a plural nickname that ends in S.
- **3-Down** (6 letters), *Aplenty*: it follows a noun to mean "in great supply".
- **4-Down** (6 letters), *Opposite of wide*: how a lane looks when two cars can barely pass.
- **5-Down** (3 letters), *Health resort*: a place for massages and facials.
- **6-Down** (5 letters), *"Je ___" (French "I love you")*: the answer is the second word with its apostrophe removed, so it starts with T.
- **7-Down** (4 letters), *Many, many millennia*: very long spans of time, plural.
- **11-Down** (3 letters), *Shade of a color*: another word for a tint or tone, starting with H.

## Solving tips

Start with the short fill-ins: 1-Down, 8-Across, 9-Across and 13-Across have three letters each. 1-Across and 1-Down share their first square, so one guess checks the other. Then take the three "Half ..., half ...?" clues, 5-Across, 10-Across and 12-Across, which each need letters from two candy names. Once 5-Across and 10-Across are in, each one supplies a letter to the long Down answers that cross it.
