**Lifetime's "It's a Wonderful Lifetime" Christmas movie event starts Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, with 11 new movies on Saturdays and Sundays through Sunday, Dec. 20, according to Lifetime's schedule page.** TVLine, which first reported the lineup on Oct. 15, 2026, writes that the network "is unwrapping nearly a dozen new Christmas movies in 2026." The weekend-by-weekend table below lists every date and time, and a free printable watch list follows it.

## What is the Lifetime Christmas movies 2026 schedule?

Lifetime puts the first premieres on Saturday, Nov. 28. Each movie airs at 8 p.m. or 10 p.m. ET (7 p.m. or 9 p.m. CT). Lifetime says new movies are available on TV Everywhere and video on demand the day after they premiere. Titles marked "working title" are labeled that way by Lifetime and may change.

## Which Lifetime Christmas movies are on the app first?

Lifetime says six vertical-format microdramas start Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, on the Lifetime app and website. Two are originals, "Casting Christmas" and "Arrives Before Christmas." The other four are library films: "Dancing Through the Snow," "Ghosts of Christmas Past," "Match Made in Mistletoe" and "Planes, Trains, and Christmas Trees." Lifetime's Oct. 15 schedule page listed "Christmas, Delivered" as the second original; its Nov. 12 update names "Arrives Before Christmas." None of the microdramas are part of the 11 movies counted above. The two originals are on the printable.

## When does the run end?

The last movie on Lifetime's schedule, "Christmas at Mistletoe Hall," premieres Sunday, Dec. 20, at 8 p.m. ET. Some coverage gives the end of the run as Saturday, Dec. 20, but Dec. 20, 2026 is a Sunday, so we use Sunday here.
