**Lifetime's "It's a Wonderful Lifetime" Christmas movie event starts Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, with 11 new movies on Saturdays and Sundays through Sunday, Dec. 20, according to TVLine's Oct. 15, 2026 report.** TVLine writes that the network "is unwrapping nearly a dozen new Christmas movies in 2026." The weekend-by-weekend table below lists every date and time, and a free printable watch list follows it.

Counting the table above, the first three weekends carry three movies each and the last carries two, for 11 in all.

## Which Lifetime Christmas movies are on the app first?

TVLine and TV Guide report that two vertical-format films, "Casting Christmas" and "Christmas, Delivered," debut on the Lifetime app on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026. They are not part of the 11 movies counted above. Both are marked as reported on the printable.

## When does the run end?

The last movie on TVLine's list, "Christmas at Mistletoe Hall," is set for Sunday, Dec. 20. Some coverage gives the end of the run as Saturday, Dec. 20, but Dec. 20, 2026 is a Sunday, so we use Sunday here.
