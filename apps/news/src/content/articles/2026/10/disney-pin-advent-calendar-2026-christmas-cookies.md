**Disney's 2026 pin advent calendar is the Christmas Cookies 24-Day Advent Calendar Mystery Pin Set**, a $275 limited edition of 5,750 worldwide. Its oven-shaped box holds 24 cookie-themed cloisonné pins, one for each day from Dec. 1 to 24. The set went on sale in mid-October at the Disney parks and on DisneyStore.com, where it was still available when we checked on the morning of Monday, Oct. 19.

## The 12 known pins

Disney has shown 12 of the 24 designs. Each is a "Christmas cookie" version of a character, made of metal with polyvinyl chloride (PVC) details and paired with a prop on its own baking pan:

1. Santa Mickey Mouse with Minnie Mouse
2. Goofy
3. Meeko
4. Pumbaa
5. White Rabbit
6. Simba
7. Pascal
8. Cheshire Cat
9. Jiminy Cricket
10. Dale
11. Lucky
12. Figaro

The other 12 stay hidden until their compartments are opened. Every pin carries a Disney Pin Trading 2026 backstamp, and the set includes a pinboard display tray. Disney Store says the item cannot be refunded, exchanged or returned.

## Is it worth $275?

At $275 for 24 pins, each one works out to about $11.46, and half of the designs are surprises, which is the main appeal for collectors. Disney's 2020 pin advent calendar cost $245, according to Disney Food Blog, so the price has risen $30 in six years.

The catch is the edition size. With 5,750 sets worldwide and a two-per-guest limit, stock may not last until December. If you end up buying from a reseller, check that the listing shows the limited-edition backstamp and sealed, unopened perforated doors.

## Where to buy it

- **DisneyStore.com.** Available at $275 on Oct. 19, two per guest.
- **The parks.** Disney says the set is also sold at the Disneyland Resort and Walt Disney World Resort while supplies last. Stock in the parks is separate from the online store.
- **Disney Store Europe.** The European store lists its own version at 250 euros, shipping within Europe.
