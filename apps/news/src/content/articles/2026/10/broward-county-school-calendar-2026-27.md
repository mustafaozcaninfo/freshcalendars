**Broward County Public Schools students get the full week of Thanksgiving off, Nov. 23-27, 2026**, followed by a two-week winter break from Dec. 21 to Jan. 1. The district announced on Dec. 18, 2025 that its School Board had approved calendars for both 2026-27 and 2027-28. This year began Monday, Aug. 10, 2026, and ends on **Friday, May 28, 2027**.

## Broward County 2026-27 key dates

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| Monday, Aug. 10, 2026 | First day of school |
| Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Thanksgiving break |
| Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Winter break |
| Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027 | Spring break |
| Friday, May 28, 2027 | Last day of school |

With weekends, Thanksgiving break runs nine days, from Saturday, Nov. 21 through Sunday, Nov. 29, and winter break 16 days, from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Sunday, Jan. 3. Spring break runs Saturday, March 20 through Sunday, March 28, which takes in Good Friday on March 26 and Easter Sunday on March 28.

The last day falls on the Friday before Memorial Day, May 31. Summer starts with a three-day weekend.

## Single days off

Not every day off is a break. Broward's full calendar also lists federal holidays, early release days and teacher planning days, and some schools, including charter schools and those with special programs, may follow schedules of their own, so check the calendar posted on the district website or your school's site before booking travel around a Monday holiday.

## Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach side by side

| | Broward | Miami-Dade | Palm Beach |
|---|---|---|---|
| First day | Aug. 10 | Aug. 13 | Aug. 10 |
| Thanksgiving week | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21-Jan. 1 | Dec. 21-Jan. 1 | Dec. 21-Jan. 1 |
| Spring break | March 22-26 | March 22-26 | March 22-26 |
| Last day | May 28 | June 3 | May 27 |

The three largest South Florida districts line up on every major break. That helps families with children in more than one county, and parents who commute from Broward to jobs in Miami-Dade or Palm Beach, plan a single set of vacations. Palm Beach and Miami-Dade also give students a no-school Monday after spring break, March 29.
