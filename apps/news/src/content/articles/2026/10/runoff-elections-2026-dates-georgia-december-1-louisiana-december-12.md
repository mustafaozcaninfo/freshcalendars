**Georgia's General Election Runoff, if one is required, is Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026, according to georgia.gov, and Louisiana's second round is Saturday, Dec. 12, according to the Louisiana Secretary of State.** Mississippi also lists Dec. 1 as a runoff day for judge and school board races. Every deadline below comes from those state calendars, and a runoff happens only if no race on the ballot is decided on Nov. 3 under the state's rules.

## When is the Georgia runoff and what are the deadlines?

georgia.gov lists the General Election Runoff for Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026, if needed. That is four weeks after the Nov. 3 election. The state's page does not give poll hours for the runoff, so check with your county election office.

- **Register:** georgia.gov says Monday, Nov. 2 "is the last day eligible Georgians can register to vote in the Runoff Election, if one is required." Confirm your status with your county registrar.
- **Absentee ballot:** the last day to request one is Friday, Nov. 20. Absentee ballots must be received by 7 p.m. on Dec. 1.
- **Early voting:** Monday, Nov. 23 to Wednesday, Nov. 25. georgia.gov calls it "a very short early voting window, abbreviated due to the Thanksgiving holiday."

For this election, the Georgia Election Guide on georgia.gov lists an Oct. 5 registration deadline and early voting from Oct. 13 to Oct. 30. Our [Georgia ballot and advance voting guide](/news/georgia-2026-ballot-amendments-advance-voting/) covers the Nov. 3 ballot.

## When does Georgia certify the Nov. 3 results?

The Brennan Center for Justice, in an analysis updated Oct. 13, 2026, cites Official Code of Georgia Annotated (O.C.G.A.) sections 21-2-493(k) and 21-2-499(b). It says counties certify by 5 p.m. on the Monday after the election, Nov. 9, and the Secretary of State certifies by 5 p.m. on the 17th day, Nov. 20. Our [midterm results timeline](/news/when-will-we-know-election-results-2026-midterms/) explains how counting works across states.

## What is Louisiana's Dec. 12 election?

The Louisiana Secretary of State's calendar names Nov. 3 the "Open U.S. Representative Primary/Open Primary." It names Saturday, Dec. 12 the "Open U.S. Representative General/Open General/Special Primary (14th State Senatorial District Only)." Dec. 12 is the second round in Louisiana's open system. The calendar adds a note: "Per Act 7 of the 2026 Regular Session, U.S. Representative is no longer considered a closed party office."

| Louisiana deadline for Dec. 12 | Date |
|---|---|
| Register in person or by mail | Thu., Nov. 12 |
| Register online (GeauxVote) | Sat., Nov. 21 |
| Early voting, 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28-Sat., Dec. 5, except Sun., Nov. 29 |
| Request a mail ballot | Tue., Dec. 8 |
| Mail ballot must be received | Fri., Dec. 11 |

Louisiana readers can also look at our [Louisiana sample ballot guide](/news/louisiana-sample-ballot-2026-senate-amendments/) for the Nov. 3 ballot.

## What is on the Mississippi runoff calendar for Dec. 1?

The Mississippi Secretary of State lists Tuesday, Dec. 1 as "General Election/Special Election Runoff Day (if required) for Mississippi Supreme Court, Court of Appeals, Chancery, Circuit, and County Court Judges; School Board Members/Trustees," with polls open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. The runoff registration deadline is Nov. 2. In-person absentee voting ends Saturday, Nov. 28 at noon. Mail ballots must be postmarked by runoff day and received by Dec. 8 at 5 p.m.

## Do Texas and Alabama have general election runoffs?

Texas local runoffs, if any, follow dates set by each city or district; check the Texas Secretary of State. Our [Texas election calendar](/news/texas-election-calendar-2026-early-voting-dates-deadlines/) covers the state's other dates. Alabama has runoffs only in primaries, and the Alabama Secretary of State lists the 2026 primary runoff as June 16. No Alabama general election runoff appears in that calendar.
