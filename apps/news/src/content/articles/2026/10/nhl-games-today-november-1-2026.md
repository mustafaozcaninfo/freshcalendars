**Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026 has 5 National Hockey League (NHL) games, the first at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the last at 8:00 p.m. ET, per the NHL's official schedule data.** They are the first NHL games after U.S. clocks fall back. The Winnipeg Jets are at the Carolina Hurricanes at 1:00 p.m. ET, and NHL Network carries that game in the U.S.

## Times after the clock change

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) says: "During 2026, daylight saving time is in effect from March 8 at 2 a.m. (local time) to November 1 at 2 a.m. (local time)." Every start on Sunday comes after that switch, so every time on this page is Eastern Standard Time (EST). Our story on [daylight saving time ending Nov. 1](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) has the full details.

Most of Arizona (the Navajo Nation excepted) and Hawaii do not change their clocks. From Nov. 1, Arizona is 2 hours behind Eastern Time instead of 3, and Hawaii is 5 hours behind instead of 6.
