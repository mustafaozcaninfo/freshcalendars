**Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026 had 5 National Hockey League (NHL) games, the first at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the last at 8:00 p.m. ET, per the NHL's official schedule data.** They were the first NHL games after U.S. clocks fell back. The Carolina Hurricanes beat the Winnipeg Jets 7-0 in the 1:00 p.m. ET opener, which NHL Network carried in the U.S.

We took the games, start times, arenas and TV listings from the league's schedule feed, and the final scores from the same feed on Nov. 14. The [CBS Sports NHL schedule](https://www.cbssports.com/nhl/schedule/20261101/) shows the same slate.

## Times after the clock change

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) says: "During 2026, daylight saving time is in effect from March 8 at 2 a.m. (local time) to November 1 at 2 a.m. (local time)." Every start on Sunday came after that switch, so every time on this page is Eastern Standard Time (EST). Our story on [daylight saving time ending Nov. 1](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) has the full details.

Most of Arizona (the Navajo Nation excepted) and Hawaii do not change their clocks. From Nov. 1, Arizona is 2 hours behind Eastern Time instead of 3, and Hawaii is 5 hours behind instead of 6.
