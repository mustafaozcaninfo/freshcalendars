**Harry Kane and Alexia Putellas won the 2026 Ballon d'Or at the London Palladium on Monday, Oct. 26, the Ballon d'Or organizers said.** Kane, of Bayern Munich, won the men's award and Putellas won the women's. The ceremony was held at 4 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), 9 p.m. in London, hosted by Thierry Henry and Kate Scott. Below: the full winners list by award, how the voting finished, and both nominee lists.

## Who won the Ballon d'Or 2026?

The Ballon d'Or organizers published the winners on ballondor.com after the ceremony. These are the awards we could confirm.

| Award | Winner |
|---|---|
| Men's Ballon d'Or | Harry Kane (Bayern Munich, England) |
| Women's Ballon d'Or | Alexia Putellas (London City, Spain) |
| Men's Young Talent (Kopa Trophy) | Lamine Yamal |
| Women's Young Talent (Kopa Trophy) | Vicky López |
| Men's Goalkeeper (Yashin Trophy) | Unai Simón |
| Women's Goalkeeper | Cata Coll |
| Men's Striker of the Year (Gerd Müller Trophy) | Harry Kane |
| Women's Striker of the Year | Charlyn Corral |
| Men's Coach of the Year (Johan Cruyff Trophy) | Luis de la Fuente |
| Women's Coach of the Year | Pere Romeu |
| Sócrates Award | Bukayo Saka |

The Striker of the Year is Kane's second, the organizers said. The organizers also list men's and women's club of the year awards; this story leaves those out because we could not confirm the winners.

## How did the men's and women's votes finish?

Kane is the first English men's winner in 25 years, ESPN reported, and he finished ahead of Kylian Mbappé in second place and Lamine Yamal in third. Putellas won her third Ballon d'Or, ESPN reported, ahead of Ewa Pajor and Melchie Dumornay. Yamal, who placed third in the men's vote, also won the men's young talent award (Kopa Trophy) for the third year in a row, per ESPN.

## Where was the ceremony and what time was it?

The 2026 Ballon d'Or was staged at the London Palladium Theatre in London, the first time the ceremony was held in London, according to the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA). It began at 20:00 Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), which was 4 p.m. ET. Thierry Henry and Kate Scott hosted. Dazn streamed the ceremony live and free in 200 markets with a free Dazn account.

The table below converts the 20:00 GMT start into local times; it is our conversion, not an official schedule.

Europe left summer time on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, and the United States ends daylight saving time on Nov. 1. ## How does it compare with last year?

In 2025, the 69th Ballon d'Or ceremony took place at the Théâtre du Châtelet in Paris on Monday, Sept. 22, and began at 21:00 Paris time, UEFA said. Counting from that edition, 2026 is the 70th. The ceremony moved from September to late October and from Paris to London.
