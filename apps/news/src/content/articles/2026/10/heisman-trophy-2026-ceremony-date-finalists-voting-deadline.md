The 2026 Heisman Trophy winner is announced at the Heisman ceremony on Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, according to the key dates on heisman.com, the website of the Heisman Trophy Trust. Ballots are due Monday, Dec. 7, by 5 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), and the finalists are announced that evening. A free printable timeline with all four dates is below.

## What are the 2026 Heisman Trophy dates?

The Heisman Trophy Trust lists four dates for the 2026 award. We checked the weekdays against the 2026 calendar.

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026 | Heisman ballots distributed | heisman.com key dates |
| Monday, Dec. 7, 2026 | Voting deadline: ballots due by 5 p.m. ET | heisman.com key dates and balloting page |
| Monday, Dec. 7, 2026 | Finalists announced, in the evening | heisman.com key dates and balloting page |
| Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026 | Winner announced at the Heisman ceremony | heisman.com key dates |

Voters have four days between the day ballots go out and the deadline, a count from the dates above. Put the dates on your own sheet with the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), or use the Heisman printable on this page.

## What time is the Heisman voting deadline in each time zone?

The Heisman balloting page says ballots are due by 5 p.m. on Monday, Dec. 7. The page writes the zone as "EST"; we treat it as Eastern Time. By our conversion:

| Zone | Deadline on Mon., Dec. 7 |
|---|---|
| Eastern | 5 p.m. |
| Central | 4 p.m. |
| Mountain | 3 p.m. |
| Pacific | 2 p.m. |
| Alaska | 1 p.m. |
| Hawaii | noon |

## When will the Heisman finalists be announced?

The balloting page says the 2026 finalists are announced in the evening of Monday, Dec. 7. The exact time is not yet announced. In a December 2025 press release, ESPN said it continues to exclusively announce the finalists each year under a multi-year agreement with the Heisman Trophy Trust.

## What time and channel is the 2026 ceremony?

Not yet announced. The Heisman Trophy Trust has confirmed the date, and an October 20, 2026, release about hospitality packages refers to "the televised Heisman Trophy Ceremony presented by Nissan." The start time, the network and the venue are not in the sources we read.

The ESPN release of Dec. 19, 2025, says ESPN remains the ceremony's home. Whether the show airs on the American Broadcasting Company (ABC) or ESPN this year is not stated.

## How did the 2025 ceremony compare?

In 2025, ESPN said the ceremony was Saturday, Dec. 13, at 7 p.m. ET on ABC, from the Appel Room at Jazz at Lincoln Center in New York. Treat those details as last year's, not as 2026 plans.

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Ceremony date | Saturday, Dec. 13 | Saturday, Dec. 12 |
| Start time | 7 p.m. ET | Not yet announced |
| Network | ABC | Not yet announced |
| Venue | Jazz at Lincoln Center's Appel Room, New York | Not yet announced |

The 2025 finalists were Jeremiyah Love (Notre Dame), Fernando Mendoza (Indiana), Diego Pavia (Vanderbilt) and Julian Sayin (Ohio State). Mendoza, Indiana's quarterback, won with 2,362 points, according to heisman.com.

## Who votes for the Heisman Trophy?

The Heisman balloting page lists 145 media votes per section, 870 media votes in all. Every living Heisman winner also votes. We did not find a total voter count in the sources we read, so we do not give one.
