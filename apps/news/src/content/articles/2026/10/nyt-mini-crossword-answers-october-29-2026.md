**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Joel Fagliano.** Three of the Across clues rhyme with each other, and one answer is a three-letter acronym. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *Gummy ___*: a candy shaped like a woodland animal that hibernates.
- **5-Across** (5 letters), *Dummy*: a word for a foolish person.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Tummy*: the soft part of the body below the chest.
- **8-Across** (5 letters), *Mean facial expression*: a scornful look with a curled lip.
- **9-Across** (3 letters), *Left-wing political org. that includes Zohran Mamdani and A.O.C.*: an abbreviation of the group's name, three letters; the group's first word is "Democratic."

**Down**

- **1-Down** (4 letters), *Garments worn in high chairs*: what a baby wears to keep food off a shirt.
- **2-Down** (4 letters), *Garden for Adam and Eve*: a biblical paradise, starting with E.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Felt sick*: the past tense of a verb meaning to be unwell, ending in -ed.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *Willy Wonka and Bob Dylan, for Timothée Chalamet*: parts an actor plays on screen.
- **6-Down** (4 letters), *Banks of "America's Next Top Model"*: the model and host's first name, four letters.

## Solving tips

Start with the three rhyming Across clues, 1-Across, 5-Across and 7-Across: "Gummy," "Dummy" and "Tummy." Each crosses the Down answers. Then use 2-Down, a famous garden, to confirm the second letter of 1-Across. Leave 9-Across until last: it sits in the bottom row, and its letters come from 3-Down, 4-Down and 6-Down.
