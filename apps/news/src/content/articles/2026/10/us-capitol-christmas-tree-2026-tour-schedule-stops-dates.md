The U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree is on the road: the official tour began Oct. 29, 2026, in Grangeville, Idaho, stops in Moscow, Idaho, today (Saturday, Oct. 31) and in Orofino and Kamiah, Idaho, on Sunday, Nov. 1, and ends at Joint Base Andrews, Maryland, on Thursday, Nov. 19, according to the project's route announcement of Oct. 1, 2026. Below is the full stop list by date, with weekdays and a count of days from Oct. 31, plus a one-page printable tracker.

## What time does the tree reach my town?

Not yet announced. The project says "Partners and communities will finalize event details over the next several weeks." It also says "Festivities are free and open to the public." Because the route page lists no times or venues, check the host town's own page or the [project's website](https://www.uscapitolchristmastree.com/) before you go. The Joint Base Andrews stop on Nov. 19 is limited to people with military ID.

## Where did the 2026 Capitol Christmas Tree come from?

The Architect of the Capitol (AOC) lists the 2026 tree as an Engelmann spruce, 76 feet tall, from the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests in Idaho. The project homepage says it was harvested from the Lochsa-Powell Ranger District and that companion trees and handmade ornaments will make the journey too. The number of ornaments is not announced. In an Aug. 13, 2026 release, the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Forest Service said the tree would be harvested in late October, ahead of a lighting "in early December."

## When will the Capitol tree arrive and be lit?

The 2026 arrival date and the lighting date and time are not yet announced. The AOC says specific 2026 ceremony dates "will be announced in the fall." The route page says the tree is traveling to the West Lawn of the U.S. Capitol Building "this holiday season."

For comparison only, the 2025 tree arrived at the Capitol on Friday, Nov. 21, 2025, at 10 a.m., and the 2025 lighting began at 5 p.m. on Tuesday, Dec. 2, 2025, on the West Front Lawn, the AOC reported. Those are last year's details, not 2026 dates. When a date is set, mark it on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html); the day it falls near is also on our [When is Christmas?](/when-is/christmas/) page.

## How is the Capitol tree different from the National Christmas Tree?

They are two separate trees and two separate events. The Capitol tree travels from a national forest to the West Lawn of the U.S. Capitol Building. The National Christmas Tree is lit on the Ellipse near the White House, and its free ticket lottery is run by the National Park Service. Our story on the [National Christmas Tree lighting, ticket lottery and Dec. 3 ceremony](/news/national-christmas-tree-lighting-2026-ticket-lottery-october-28/) has those dates. For other trees and lights, see the [New York holiday calendar with the Rockefeller tree](/news/nyc-holiday-calendar-2026-rockefeller-tree-macys-parade/) and [U.S. Christmas market opening dates](/news/us-christmas-markets-2026-opening-dates-philadelphia-dc-detroit-nyc/).
