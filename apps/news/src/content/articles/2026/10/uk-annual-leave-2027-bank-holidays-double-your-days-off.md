**Gov.uk's 2027 bank holiday list for England and Wales puts Good Friday on 26 March and Easter Monday on 29 March, so 4 days of leave from Tuesday 30 March to Friday 2 April give 10 days off in a row.** The same trick works around the early May, late May, August and Christmas holidays. This story works out the leave days for a Monday-to-Friday worker in each nation, from the dates on the GOV.UK [bank holidays page](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays) (last updated 24 November 2025). For the full list of dates, see our [2027 bank holidays story](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/).

## How do you count the days off?

Each row below books the working days next to a bank holiday and counts every day from the first free day to the last, weekends included. All weekdays were computed for 2027. Gov.uk also says that if a bank holiday falls on a weekend, a substitute weekday becomes a bank holiday, normally the following Monday. That is why Christmas 2027, which lands on a Saturday, gives Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December.

## England and Wales: where to book leave in 2027

Gov.uk lists eight bank holidays for England and Wales in 2027: 1 January, 26 March, 29 March, 3 May, 31 May, 30 August, 27 December and 28 December.

| Bank holiday | Book | Leave days | Time off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good Friday 26 Mar, Easter Monday 29 Mar | Tue 30 Mar to Fri 2 Apr | 4 | 10 days, Fri 26 Mar to Sun 4 Apr |
| Easter, the week before | Mon 22 to Thu 25 Mar | 4 | 10 days, Sat 20 to Mon 29 Mar |
| Easter, both weeks | Mon 22 to Thu 25 Mar and Tue 30 Mar to Fri 2 Apr | 8 | 16 days, Sat 20 Mar to Sun 4 Apr |
| Mon 3 May | Tue 4 to Fri 7 May | 4 | 9 days, Sat 1 to Sun 9 May |
| Mon 31 May | Tue 1 to Fri 4 Jun | 4 | 9 days, Sat 29 May to Sun 6 Jun |
| Mon 30 Aug | Tue 31 Aug to Fri 3 Sep | 4 | 9 days, Sat 28 Aug to Sun 5 Sep |
| Mon 27 and Tue 28 Dec | Wed 29 to Fri 31 Dec | 3 | 9 days, Sat 25 Dec to Sun 2 Jan 2028 |

## Scotland: a different Easter and an August holiday

Scotland's 2027 list on gov.uk has 1 January, 4 January (the substitute for 2 January), 26 March, 3 May, 31 May, 2 August, 30 November (St Andrew's Day), 27 December and 28 December. There is no Easter Monday, so the Easter bridge is one day longer.

| Bank holiday | Book | Leave days | Time off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Jan and Mon 4 Jan | Tue 5 to Fri 8 Jan | 4 | 10 days, Fri 1 to Sun 10 Jan |
| Good Friday 26 Mar | Mon 29 Mar to Fri 2 Apr | 5 | 10 days, Fri 26 Mar to Sun 4 Apr |
| Mon 3 May | Tue 4 to Fri 7 May | 4 | 9 days, Sat 1 to Sun 9 May |
| Mon 31 May | Tue 1 to Fri 4 Jun | 4 | 9 days, Sat 29 May to Sun 6 Jun |
| Mon 2 Aug | Tue 3 to Fri 6 Aug | 4 | 9 days, Sat 31 Jul to Sun 8 Aug |

| Mon 27 and Tue 28 Dec | Wed 29 to Fri 31 Dec | 3 | 9 days, Sat 25 Dec to Sun 2 Jan 2028 |

## Northern Ireland: St Patrick's Day and the Battle of the Boyne

Northern Ireland's 2027 list has 1 January, 17 March (St Patrick's Day), 26 March, 29 March, 3 May, 31 May, 12 July (Battle of the Boyne), 30 August, 27 December and 28 December. It shares the English Easter, May and August dates and adds two midweek and Monday holidays of its own.

## What does a combined 2027 plan look like?

Taking only the biggest bridges in each nation, the totals below come from the tables above.

| Nation | Bridges used | Leave days | Days off |
|---|---|---|---|
| England and Wales | Easter, 3 May, 30 August, Christmas | 15 | 37 |
| Scotland | Easter, 3 May, 2 August, Christmas | 16 | 37 |
| Northern Ireland | St Patrick's Day (18 and 19 March), Easter, Battle of the Boyne, Christmas | 13 | 33 |

In England and Wales, 15 leave days buy 37 days off, about two and a half days off for every day of leave. Gov.uk says most workers on a 5-day week must receive at least 28 days' paid annual leave a year, and that an employer can choose to include bank holidays as part of that statutory leave. If your employer does, the eight bank holidays count toward your 28 days, so check your contract before you book. The late May bridge (1 to 4 June) is an extra 4 days if your allowance stretches.

## Does the Christmas 2027 bridge carry into the new year?

Christmas Day 2027 is a Saturday and Boxing Day a Sunday, so Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December are the bank holidays. Leave on Wednesday 29, Thursday 30 and Friday 31 December gives 9 days off to Sunday 2 January 2028. New Year's Day 2028 is a Saturday; the 2028 bank holiday list is not part of this story's source, so check gov.uk before you plan anything for 3 January 2028.
