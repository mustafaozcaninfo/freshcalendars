Kwanzaa 2026 starts on **Saturday, Dec. 26**, and ends on **Friday, Jan. 1, 2027**, according to Maulana Karenga's site, which says the holiday "is celebrated from December 26 to January 1." From Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, the first day is 62 days away. This page lists the principle for each day and offers a one-page printable calendar.

## When is Kwanzaa 2026?

Each day has one of the seven principles, called the Nguzo Saba. The table follows the order in which the Official Kwanzaa Website lists them. Its greeting page confirms Umoja on the first day and Kujichagulia on the second. The weekdays are computed for 2026.

| Day | Date | Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sat., Dec. 26 | Umoja | Unity |
| 2 | Sun., Dec. 27 | Kujichagulia | Self-Determination |
| 3 | Mon., Dec. 28 | Ujima | Collective Work and Responsibility |
| 4 | Tue., Dec. 29 | Ujamaa | Cooperative Economics |
| 5 | Wed., Dec. 30 | Nia | Purpose |
| 6 | Thu., Dec. 31 | Kuumba | Creativity |
| 7 | Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Imani | Faith |

Day 7 falls on New Year's Day, which the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists as a federal holiday on Jan. 1, 2027. Kwanzaa itself is not a federal holiday. ## What is Kwanzaa and where does it come from?

The Official Kwanzaa Website says Kwanzaa "was established in 1966 in the midst of the Black Freedom Movement." Karenga's site says he "created Kwanzaa in 1966 as an act of cultural recovery and reconstruction." The official site's homepage calls him Dr. Maulana Karenga. The name comes from Swahili and means "first fruits."

## What do you say on each day of Kwanzaa?

The greeting is "Habari gani?" The answer is that day's principle, "such as 'Umoja' on the first day and 'Kujichagulia' on the second," according to the Official Kwanzaa Website. On day 3 the answer would follow the same pattern with the third principle, Ujima.

## What are the seven symbols and the candle colors?

The Official Kwanzaa Website lists seven symbols.

| Symbol | Meaning on the official site |
|---|---|
| Mkeka (The Mat) | Tradition and history, "the foundation on which we build" |
| Kinara (The Candle Holder) | "Our roots, our parent people -- continental Africans" |
| Mishumaa Saba (The Seven Candles) | The Seven Principles |
| Mazao (The Crops) | "African harvest celebrations and of the rewards of productive and collective labor" |
| Muhindi (The Corn) | "Our children and our future" |
| Kikombe cha Umoja (The Unity Cup) | Unity |
| Zawadi (The Gifts) | "The labor and love of parents and the commitments made and kept by the children" |

The same site says "the colors of Kwanzaa are black, red and green": black for the people, red for their struggle, and green for the future and hope that comes from their struggle. The black candle is lit first on the first day, and the remaining candles are lit afterwards from left to right on the following days.

## What is the Kwanzaa 2026 theme?

The 2026 theme is not yet announced. In 2025, the theme was "Practicing the Seven Principles in Dimly-Lit Times: Lifting Up the Light, Hurrying the Dawn."
