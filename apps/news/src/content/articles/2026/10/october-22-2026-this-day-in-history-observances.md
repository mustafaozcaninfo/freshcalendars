**Thursday, Oct. 22, 2026, is the 295th day of the year, and the date has a long list of firsts: the first recorded parachute jump, the first xerographic copy and the first photograph from the surface of another planet.** It is also the day Sam Houston became president of the Republic of Texas and the day John F. Kennedy told Americans about Soviet missiles in Cuba. Below: what happened on Oct. 22 through the years, the day's observance, and what is on the calendar this week.

## This day in history: October 22

| Year | What happened | Years ago |
|---|---|---|
| 1797 | André-Jacques Garnerin makes the first recorded parachute jump, dropping from a balloon over Paris with a silk parachute | 229 |
| 1836 | Sam Houston is inaugurated as the first elected president of the Republic of Texas | 190 |

## Three stories behind the dates

**Texas, 1836.** Houston, who had led the Texan army at San Jacinto that April, served as president from Oct. 22, 1836 to Dec. 10, 1838, according to the Texas State Historical Association's Handbook of Texas. He later served a second term as president, then as a U.S. senator and governor after Texas joined the Union.

**Queens, 1938.** Carlson's first image, made with static electricity and powder, recorded the date and place: "10-22-38 ASTORIA." The process he named xerography became the basis of the office copier, as The Henry Ford museum and the American Physical Society describe.

**Washington, 1962.** Kennedy's televised address that evening opened the public phase of the Cuban Missile Crisis. The full text is preserved by the American Presidency Project at UC Santa Barbara.

## The observance on Oct. 22

International Stuttering Awareness Day falls on Oct. 22 every year. It has been observed since 1998 and is organized by the International Stuttering Association together with the International Fluency Association and the European League of Stuttering Associations. No United Nations day falls on Oct. 22; UN Day follows on Saturday, Oct. 24.

Oct. 22 is not a federal or state holiday, so banks, schools, post offices and federal offices keep their usual hours. The next federal holiday is Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Born on this day: Doris Lessing, the British novelist and 2007 Nobel Prize in Literature winner, in 1919, according to the Nobel Prize's biography.
