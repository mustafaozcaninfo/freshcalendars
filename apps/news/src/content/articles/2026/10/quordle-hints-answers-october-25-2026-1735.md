## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**Top left**

- Letters: starts with R, ends with Y.
- Meaning: an adjective for weather with showers.

**Top right**

- Letters: starts with B.
- Meaning: a verb, past tense, for carried people on a large passenger vehicle.

**Bottom left**

- Letters: starts with P, ends with Y.
- Meaning: a person authorized to act for someone else, as in a vote or a legal matter.

**Bottom right**

- Letters: starts with U, ends with D.
- Meaning: past tense of "undo."

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess with R, A, I, N and S touches at least three of the four boards. A second guess with U, D, E, O and Y fills in most of the rest. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
