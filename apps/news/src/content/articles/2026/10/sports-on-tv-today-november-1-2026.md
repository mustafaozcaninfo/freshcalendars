## NFL on Sunday: which games are on TV where you live?

NFL.com lists eight games at 1 p.m. ET, four on CBS and four on FOX, then Chargers at Rams at 4:05 p.m. ET on FOX and Chiefs at Broncos and Patriots at Dolphins at 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS. The afternoon games are regional, so your CBS and FOX stations show the ones picked for your market. The Texans, Saints, Giants and 49ers are on bye. Kickoffs in seven time zones are in our [NFL Week 8 schedule](/news/nfl-week-8-schedule-2026-game-times-tv-clock-change/).

The night game is the Eagles at the Commanders at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock. Time zones, streaming options and the November schedule are in [Sunday Night Football tonight](/news/sunday-night-football-tonight-eagles-commanders-snf-november-1-2026/). NFL+ streams local and primetime games on phones and tablets, per NFL.com.

## F1 Mexico City Grand Prix: 3 p.m. ET on Apple TV

F1 lists the race at 2 p.m. in Mexico City, which is 3 p.m. EST now that the U.S. has left daylight time; the gap to Mexico City shrinks from two hours on Saturday to one on Sunday. Apple TV is "the U.S. home of Formula 1," according to Apple, and the race needs an Apple TV subscription. Every time zone is in our [Mexico City Grand Prix 2026 schedule](/news/mexico-city-grand-prix-2026-schedule-race-time-by-time-zone/).

## NYC Marathon on TV

NYRR, the organizer, lists ESPN2 nationally from 8 to 11:30 a.m. ET, Channel 7 in New York from 7 a.m., ESPN3 in Spanish and a finish-line show on ABC from 3 to 5 p.m. ET. The professional women start at 8:35 a.m. and the men at 9:05 a.m. Waves, road closures and viewing spots are in our [NYC Marathon 2026](/news/nyc-marathon-2026-start-times-road-closures-where-to-watch/) guide.

## College basketball starts in Rome

Notre Dame plays Villanova in Rome at 9:30 a.m. ET on FOX, the first game of the 2026-27 men's season. Notre Dame Athletics says it is believed to be the first men's college basketball season opener played outside the United States. The rest of the season's dates are in our [college basketball 2026-27 key dates](/news/college-basketball-2026-27-key-dates-season-start-march-madness-printable/).

## NBA and NHL on Sunday
