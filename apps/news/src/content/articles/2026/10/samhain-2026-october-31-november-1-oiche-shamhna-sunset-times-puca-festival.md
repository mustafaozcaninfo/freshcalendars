Samhain eve, Oíche Shamhna, falls tomorrow, **Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026**, and Samhain itself is **Sunday, Nov. 1**; sunset in Dublin tomorrow is 4:55 p.m., per the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO). The National Museum of Ireland (NMI) says "the festival of Samhain, the first day of winter, was marked on the 1 November," and that the eve, Oíche Shamhna, "is still celebrated all over Ireland with feasting and games."

## When is Samhain 2026?

Samhain is Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, and its eve is Saturday, Oct. 31. Many people who mark Samhain today begin on the evening of Oct. 31, in line with the NMI's word "eve." The Library of Congress American Folklife Center says Halloween "originated with the Celtic festival known as Samhain in Ireland." The same post says it is "not as clear today that Samhain marked the beginning of the Celtic year," so this story does not call it a Celtic New Year.

## What time is sunset on Oíche Shamhna in 2026?

Irish Standard Time ended Oct. 25, so Dublin is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) tomorrow. These are USNO sunrise and sunset times for Oct. 31, 2026, in local clock time.

U.S. clocks fall back at 2 a.m. on Sunday, Nov. 1, so U.S. sunsets that day come earlier on the clock. The Library of Congress says Hallowmas includes Halloween, All Saints Day (Nov. 1) and All Souls Day (Nov. 2). Weekdays below are computed for 2026.

## What is the astronomical midpoint after Samhain?

USNO lists the September equinox for Sept. 23, 2026, at 00:05 Universal Time (UT) and the December solstice for Dec. 21, 2026, at 20:50 UT. The astronomical midpoint between the two falls at about 22:27 UT on Friday, Nov. 6, 2026. That is our calculation from USNO times, and it comes about a week after the calendar date of Samhain.

## What is on at the Púca Festival 2026?

The festival's official site lists Oct. 29 to Nov. 1, 2026, and Discover Ireland places it in Trim and Athboy, County Meath. Discover Ireland lists the Púca Procession in Trim for Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, and says to see the festival website for the time. The festival lists a "Lighting of the Samhain Fire," but its date and time are not listed on the official page, so check pucafestival.com before you travel.
