South Park season 29 episode 4 aired Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026 at 10 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and Pacific Time (PT) on Comedy Central and streams on Paramount+ the next day, Thursday, Oct. 29. The next new episode is Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, according to Comedy Central's May 19, 2026 press release.

The release lists six premiere dates for the season, one every other Wednesday. We counted the episode numbers and the off weeks from those dates; both are in the table below and on a one-page printable.

## When is the next South Park episode?

Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, at 10 p.m. ET/PT on Comedy Central. The press release says "additional new episodes will premiere on Wednesdays: Sept. 30, Oct. 14, Oct. 28, Nov. 11, and Nov. 25." The South Park Studios homepage repeats the weekly slot: "New episodes of South Park premiere Wednesday nights at 10p ET on Comedy Central."

## What is the South Park season 29 schedule?

The first date, Sept. 16, comes from the release's line that the 29th season "will premiere Wednesday, Sept. 16 at 10:00 PM, ET/PT on Comedy Central." Episode numbers and the Paramount+ column are our count from the announced dates. Every date is a Wednesday, checked against the 2026 calendar.

| Episode | Comedy Central, 10 p.m. ET/PT | Paramount+ next day | Status on Oct. 29 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wed., Sept. 16, 2026 | Thu., Sept. 17 | Aired |
| 2 | Wed., Sept. 30, 2026 | Thu., Oct. 1 | Aired |
| 3 | Wed., Oct. 14, 2026 | Thu., Oct. 15 | Aired |
| 4 | Wed., Oct. 28, 2026 | Thu., Oct. 29 | Aired |
| 5 | Wed., Nov. 11, 2026 | Thu., Nov. 12 | Next |
| 6 | Wed., Nov. 25, 2026 | Thu., Nov. 26 | Last date announced |

Comedy Central has not named a season finale. Nov. 25 is the last date in its announced schedule, and it falls the night before Thanksgiving. See [When is Thanksgiving?](/when-is/thanksgiving/) for that holiday's date.

## Which weeks have no new episode?

Five Wednesdays between premieres have no new episode, going by the six dates in the release: Sept. 23, Oct. 7, Oct. 21, Nov. 4 and Nov. 18. Nov. 4 and Nov. 18 are still ahead. Mark episode nights on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) or look back at the [October 2026 printable calendar](/october-calendars.html), or keep the South Park season 29 episode tracker printable on the fridge.

## When can you stream new South Park episodes?

The release says "new episodes will be available to stream on Paramount+ around the world, with next-day availability in the U.S., Canada and Australia." That puts episode 5 on Paramount+ on Thursday, Nov. 12, and episode 6 on Thursday, Nov. 26 in those three countries. The release gives no streaming hour, so check the Paramount+ app.

## What time does South Park air in each time zone?

Comedy Central lists 10 p.m. ET/PT, and the homepage says 10 p.m. ET. The release gives ET and PT only. For Central and Mountain Time, check local listings. The release names no Canadian channel, so Canadian viewers should look at their provider's guide or Paramount+ the next day.

## Are episode titles announced?

Not in the sources we checked. The release and the homepage give dates and times only, so the table leaves titles out.
