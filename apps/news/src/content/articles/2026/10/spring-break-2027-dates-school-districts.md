**Eight of 19 large U.S. school districts are off Monday, Mar. 22 through Friday, Mar. 26, 2027 for spring break**, according to the 2026-27 calendars the districts publish. The rest are scattered from Monday, Mar. 8 in Houston to Friday, Apr. 30 in New York City.

We read each calendar on the district's own website or in its official PDF. Easter Sunday is Mar. 28, 2027 and Passover begins at sundown on Apr. 21, 2027, dates listed on the official Fairfax County and Dallas calendars. For the holiday itself, see [when is Easter](/when-is/easter/).

## CMS spring break 2027 and the other districts that break after Easter

The Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (CMS) calendar lists spring break from Monday, Mar. 29 to Friday, Apr. 2, 2027, the week after Easter. Students are out earlier than that: Thursday, Mar. 25 is a teacher workday and Friday, Mar. 26 is Good Friday, so CMS students are off seven weekdays, Mar. 25 through Apr. 2, by our count. See our [Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools calendar](/news/charlotte-mecklenburg-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) story for the full year. Wake County, the other North Carolina district here, has the same Mar. 29 to Apr. 2 week, with Good Friday as a holiday before it and Monday, Apr. 5 as a teacher workday.

## Which week is spring break most often?

Counting the 19 districts above, the week of Mar. 22 is the most common, with eight. It is the week before Easter, and it ends on Good Friday, Mar. 26.

| Week of | Districts | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Mar. 8 | Houston | 1 |
| Mar. 15 | Clark County, Dallas, Orange County, Austin | 4 |
| Mar. 22 | Chicago, Los Angeles Unified, Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County, Hillsborough County, Fairfax County, Philadelphia | 8 |
| Mar. 29 | Charlotte-Mecklenburg (CMS), Wake County, Montgomery County (starts Fri., Mar. 26) | 3 |
| Apr. 5 | Gwinnett County, Cobb County | 2 |
| Apr. 22 | New York City | 1 |

That count covers only these 19 districts, not all U.S. schools. Montgomery County is counted in the week of Mar. 29 because five of its six weekdays fall there. No district in the table breaks the weeks of Apr. 12 and Apr. 19 in full.

## Notes on specific districts

- **New York City:** Spring recess runs Thursday, Apr. 22 through Friday, Apr. 30, seven weekdays, the same count as the seven weekdays CMS students are out. Passover begins at sundown on Wednesday, Apr. 21, the evening before it starts. Good Friday, Mar. 26, is a separate closure on the NYC calendar. Our [NYC public schools calendar](/news/nyc-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks-holidays/) story has every holiday.
- **Chicago:** The 2026-2027 CPS Family Calendar lists Mar. 22-26. See our [Chicago Public Schools calendar](/news/chicago-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) story for the full year.
- **Los Angeles Unified:** The district calendar lists Spring Recess Monday, Mar. 22 to Friday, Mar. 26, and Wednesday, Mar. 31 is Farm Workers Day with no school.
- **Miami-Dade:** Monday, Mar. 29 is a teacher planning day, so students return Tuesday, Mar. 30.
- **Hillsborough County:** Monday, Mar. 29 is a non-student day, so students return Tuesday, Mar. 30. Orange County, also in Florida, breaks a week earlier, Mar. 15-19, and its fourth marking period begins Monday, Mar. 22.
- **Clark County:** The district marks its calendar "Subject to change". Classes resume Monday, Mar. 22.
- **Dallas:** Friday, Mar. 26 is also a student holiday, a week after the Mar. 15-19 break. Austin ISD has the same Mar. 15-19 week.
- **Palm Beach County:** The same Key Dates page lists Wednesday, Mar. 10, 2027 as a separate "Spring Holiday", two weeks before the Mar. 22-26 break.
- **Philadelphia:** The break is four weekdays, Monday through Thursday.
- **Montgomery County, Md.:** The calendar lists Spring Break Holiday on Friday, Mar. 26 and Monday, Mar. 29, Non-Instructional Days on Mar. 30 and 31 and Systemwide Closures on Apr. 1 and 2. Classes are back Monday, Apr. 5.
- **Cobb County, Ga.:** Spring break is the week of Apr. 5-9, the same as Gwinnett County.

Before booking, confirm the dates on your own district's calendar, because a district can change them. Clark County says so on its calendar.
