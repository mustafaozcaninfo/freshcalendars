**Five of 10 large U.S. school districts are off Monday, Mar. 22 through Friday, Mar. 26, 2027 for spring break**, according to the 2026-27 calendars the districts publish. The rest are scattered from Monday, Mar. 8 in Houston to Friday, Apr. 30 in New York City.

We read each calendar on the district's own website or in its official PDF. Easter Sunday is Mar. 28, 2027 and Passover begins at sundown on Apr. 21, 2027, dates listed on the official Fairfax County and Dallas calendars. For the holiday itself, see [when is Easter](/when-is/easter/).

## Spring break 2027, district by district

| District | Spring break 2027 | Weekdays off | Number of weekdays |
|---|---|---|---|
| Houston Independent School District (ISD) | Mon., Mar. 8 to Fri., Mar. 12 | Mar. 8-12 | 5 |
| Clark County School District (CCSD), Las Vegas | Mon., Mar. 15 to Fri., Mar. 19 | Mar. 15-19 | 5 |
| Dallas Independent School District (ISD) | Mon., Mar. 15 to Fri., Mar. 19 | Mar. 15-19 | 5 |
| Chicago Public Schools (CPS) | Mon., Mar. 22 to Fri., Mar. 26 | Mar. 22-26 | 5 |
| Broward County Public Schools | Mon., Mar. 22 to Fri., Mar. 26 | Mar. 22-26 | 5 |
| Palm Beach County | Mon., Mar. 22 to Fri., Mar. 26 | Mar. 22-26 | 5 |
| Fairfax County Public Schools (FCPS), Va. | Mon., Mar. 22 to Fri., Mar. 26 | Mar. 22-26 | 5 |
| School District of Philadelphia | Mon., Mar. 22 to Thu., Mar. 25 | Mar. 22-25 | 4 |
| Gwinnett County Public Schools, Ga. | Mon., Apr. 5 to Fri., Apr. 9 | Apr. 5-9 | 5 |
| New York City Public Schools | Thu., Apr. 22 to Fri., Apr. 30 | Apr. 22-23 and Apr. 26-30 | 7 |

The table is sorted by start date. Houston's calendar lists its week as "Recess (no classes)", Palm Beach County's as "Spring Break; No School", Philadelphia's as "Spring Recess; Schools Closed" and New York City's as "Spring Recess, schools closed". Houston's calendar was updated Apr. 6, 2026, and Philadelphia's board approved its calendar on Feb. 26, 2026.

## Which week is spring break most often?

Counting the 10 districts above, the week of Mar. 22 is the most common, with five. It is the week before Easter, and it ends on Good Friday, Mar. 26.

| Week of | Districts | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Mar. 8 | Houston | 1 |
| Mar. 15 | Clark County, Dallas | 2 |
| Mar. 22 | Chicago, Broward County, Palm Beach County, Fairfax County, Philadelphia | 5 |
| Apr. 5 | Gwinnett County | 1 |
| Apr. 22 | New York City | 1 |

That count covers only these 10 districts, not all U.S. schools. No district in the table breaks the week of Mar. 29, the week after Easter, or the weeks of Apr. 12 and Apr. 19 in full.

## Notes on specific districts

- **New York City:** Spring recess runs Thursday, Apr. 22 through Friday, Apr. 30, seven weekdays, the longest break in the table. Passover begins at sundown on Wednesday, Apr. 21, the evening before it starts. Good Friday, Mar. 26, is a separate closure on the NYC calendar. Our [NYC public schools calendar](/news/nyc-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks-holidays/) story has every holiday.
- **Chicago:** The 2026-2027 CPS Family Calendar lists Mar. 22-26. See our [Chicago Public Schools calendar](/news/chicago-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) story for the full year.
- **Clark County:** The district marks its calendar "Subject to change". Classes resume Monday, Mar. 22.
- **Dallas:** Friday, Mar. 26 is also a student holiday, a week after the Mar. 15-19 break.
- **Palm Beach County:** The same Key Dates page lists Wednesday, Mar. 10, 2027 as a separate "Spring Holiday", two weeks before the Mar. 22-26 break.
- **Philadelphia:** The break is four weekdays, Monday through Thursday.
