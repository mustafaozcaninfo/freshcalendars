summary:
  - "25 of 80 school districts are off Monday, Mar. 15 through Friday, Mar. 19, 2027, and 23 are off the week of Mar. 22, according to their 2026-27 calendars; Chicago, Los Angeles Unified, Miami-Dade County, Broward County, Palm Beach County, Hillsborough County and Fairfax County are off Mar. 22-26 (Thursday, Mar. 25 for Philadelphia)."
  - "Six districts start first, Mar. 8-12: Houston, Clear Creek, Katy, Knox County, North East and Northside; ten are off Apr. 5-9, including Gwinnett County, Cobb County, Fulton County and Minneapolis."
  - "CMS spring break 2027 is Monday, Mar. 29 to Friday, Apr. 2 at Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (CMS), the same week as Wake County and 11 other districts; Montgomery County, Md., is off Friday, Mar. 26 through Friday, Apr. 2."
  - "New York City is last, with spring recess from Thursday, Apr. 22 through Friday, Apr. 30, seven weekdays."
faq:
  - q: "When is spring break 2027?"
    a: "It depends on the district. In the 80 districts we checked, the dates run from Mar. 8-12 in Houston to Apr. 22-30 in New York City. 25 districts are off the week of Mar. 15 and 23 the week of Mar. 22, 2027."
  - q: "When is spring break 2027 in NYC?"
    a: "Thursday, Apr. 22 through Friday, Apr. 30, 2027, listed as Spring Recess with schools closed on the NYC Public Schools 2026-27 calendar. Good Friday, Mar. 26, is a separate closure."
  - q: "When is spring break 2027 for Chicago Public Schools?"
    a: "Monday, Mar. 22 through Friday, Mar. 26, 2027, according to the 2026-2027 CPS Family Calendar."
  - q: "When is CMS spring break 2027?"
    a: "Monday, Mar. 29 to Friday, Apr. 2, 2027, on the Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (CMS) 2026-2027 Calendar Key Dates. A teacher workday on Thursday, Mar. 25 and Good Friday, Mar. 26 come right before it."
  - q: "When is spring break 2027 for Los Angeles Unified and Miami-Dade?"
    a: "Both are off Monday, Mar. 22 through Friday, Mar. 26, 2027. Miami-Dade has a teacher planning day on Monday, Mar. 29, so its students return Tuesday, Mar. 30."
  - q: "Is spring break always the week before Easter?"
    a: "No. Easter Sunday is Mar. 28, 2027, and 23 of the 80 districts here are off the week before it, the week of Mar. 22. Six districts break the week of Mar. 8, 25 the week of Mar. 15, 13 the week after Easter (Mar. 29), ten the week of Apr. 5, and Seattle, Boston and New York City in the weeks of Apr. 12, Apr. 19 and Apr. 26."

**25 of 80 school districts are off Monday, Mar. 15 through Friday, Mar. 19, 2027 for spring break**, according to the 2026-27 calendars the districts publish. Another 23 are off the week of Mar. 22. The rest run from Monday, Mar. 8 in Houston and five other districts to Friday, Apr. 30 in New York City.

We read each calendar on the district's own website or in its official PDF. Easter Sunday is Mar. 28, 2027 and Passover begins at sundown on Apr. 21, 2027, dates listed on the official Fairfax County and Dallas calendars. For the holiday itself, see [when is Easter](/when-is/easter/).

## CMS spring break 2027 and the other districts that break after Easter

The Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (CMS) calendar lists spring break from Monday, Mar. 29 to Friday, Apr. 2, 2027, the week after Easter. Students are out earlier than that: Thursday, Mar. 25 is a teacher workday and Friday, Mar. 26 is Good Friday, so CMS students are off seven weekdays, Mar. 25 through Apr. 2, by our count. See our [Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools calendar](/news/charlotte-mecklenburg-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) story for the full year. ## Which week is spring break most often?

Counting the 80 districts above, the week of Mar. 15 is the most common, with 25, and the week of Mar. 22 follows with 23. The week of Mar. 22 is the week before Easter, and it ends on Good Friday, Mar. 26.
