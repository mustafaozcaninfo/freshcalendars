**There are 10 National Basketball Association (NBA) games today, Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, with the first tip-off at 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), per the CBS Sports schedule and NBA.com.** It is opening night of Emirates NBA Cup group play, and NBA.com labels all 10 games as group-stage games. ## What is the NBA Cup, and why does tonight matter?

NBA.com calls the Emirates NBA Cup a fourth-annual in-season competition. It tips off on Oct. 30 with the first of five straight Friday Group Play doubleheaders on Prime Video. Group play has 60 games from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, per the NBA's Aug. 12, 2026 schedule announcement. Only 15 of those 60 games are on Prime Video, NBC or ESPN; the rest are on NBA League Pass and local TV.

Tonight's national game in the East is Knicks at 76ers, listed as East Group B, at 7:30 p.m. ET. The NBA's announcement lists Lakers at Warriors as the second half of the Prime Video doubleheader.

| Cup stage | Date (2026) | Source detail |
|---|---|---|

| Cup Nights | Nov. 24 (NBC), Nov. 25 (ESPN) | NBA.com key dates |
