**There were 10 National Basketball Association (NBA) games on Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, with the first tip-off at 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), per the CBS Sports schedule and NBA.com.** It was opening night of Emirates NBA Cup group play, and NBA.com labels all 10 games as group-stage games. Prime Video carried a doubleheader: New York Knicks at Philadelphia 76ers and Los Angeles Lakers at Golden State Warriors. NBA.com lists all ten as final.

## What is the NBA Cup?

NBA.com calls the Emirates NBA Cup a fourth-annual in-season competition. It tipped off on Oct. 30 with the first of five straight Friday Group Play doubleheaders on Prime Video. Group play has 60 games from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, per the NBA's Aug. 12, 2026 schedule announcement. Only 15 of those 60 games are on Prime Video, NBC or ESPN; the rest are on NBA League Pass and local TV.

The national game in the East was Knicks at 76ers, listed as East Group B, at 7:30 p.m. ET. The NBA's announcement lists Lakers at Warriors as the second half of the Prime Video doubleheader.

| Cup stage | Date (2026) | Source detail |
|---|---|---|
| Group play | Oct. 30 to Nov. 27 | 60 games, five Friday doubleheaders on Prime Video started Oct. 30 |
| Cup Nights | Nov. 24 (NBC), Nov. 25 (ESPN) | NBA.com key dates |
| Quarterfinals | Dec. 4-5 | NBA.com key dates |
| Semifinals | Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9 | NBA.com key dates |
| Championship | Dec. 11 | Hinkle Fieldhouse, Indianapolis |
