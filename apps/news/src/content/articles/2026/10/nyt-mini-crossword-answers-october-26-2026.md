**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Monday, Oct. 26, 2026, is a six-by-five grid with 11 clues, constructed by Joel Fagliano, and its first four rows are filled edge to edge with six-letter answers.** The last row starts with two black squares and holds a four-letter entry. Below is a hint for every clue, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (6 letters), *Sound of fall leaves underfoot*: what dry leaves do when you step on them.
- **7-Across** (6 letters), *Santa's laugh*: three repeats of the same two letters.
- **8-Across** (6 letters), *Hit film franchise with blue-skinned aliens known as Na'vi*: a one-word title.
- **9-Across** (6 letters), *Brain nerve cell*: the cell that sends electrical signals.
- **10-Across** (4 letters), *"Say ___!" ("Alright, I get it")*: the opposite of more.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (4 letters), *Action star Jackie*: his surname, four letters.
- **2-Down** (4 letters), *Wander this way and that*: to roam without a plan.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Big name in moving vans*: a rental truck brand written as one word.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *___-Dame Cathedral*: the word before Dame in the cathedral's name.
- **5-Down** (5 letters), *Complete pandemonium*: total disorder.
- **6-Down** (5 letters), *"Hook 'em ___" (battle cry of the University of Texas)*: what a bull has on its head, in plural.

## Solving tips

Start with the Down entries, since each column is short and several are direct. Once 1-Down gives you the first letters of 1-Across and 7-Across, the long rows come quickly. The bottom row is the only one with black squares, so 10-Across fits under the last four columns, and 3-Down, 4-Down, 5-Down and 6-Down each end on its letters.
