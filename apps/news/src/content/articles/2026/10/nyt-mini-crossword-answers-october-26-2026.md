**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Monday, Oct. 26, 2026, is six squares wide and five deep, with 11 clues by Joel Fagliano.** Four Across answers run the full six letters, so the grid takes a little longer than a usual Monday Mini. Below is a hint for every clue, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (6 letters), *Sound of fall leaves underfoot*: the noise of stepping on something dry and brittle.
- **7-Across** (6 letters), *Santa's laugh*: one short syllable, said three times.
- **8-Across** (6 letters), *Hit film franchise with blue-skinned aliens known as Na'vi*: James Cameron's series set on Pandora.
- **9-Across** (6 letters), *Brain nerve cell*: it fires signals to other cells.
- **10-Across** (4 letters), *"Say ___!" ("Alright, I get it")*: the opposite of more.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (4 letters), *Action star Jackie*: famous for doing his own stunts.
- **2-Down** (4 letters), *Wander this way and that*: a verb that rhymes with stove.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Big name in moving vans*: the orange-and-white rental trucks.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *___-Dame Cathedral*: the Paris landmark.
- **5-Down** (5 letters), *Complete pandemonium*: total disorder.
- **6-Down** (5 letters), *"Hook 'em ___" (battle cry of the University of Texas)*: what a longhorn has on its head.

## Solving tips

Start with the three gimmes: Santa's laugh at 7-Across, the film franchise at 8-Across and Jackie at 1-Down. With those in, the six-letter rows fill from the left, and the Paris clue at 4-Down confirms the middle columns. 10-Across is the only short Across entry, so leave it for last.
