## What day is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday. Because Dec. 25 lands at the end of the week, most stores close early on Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, and reopen on Saturday, Dec. 26. Christmas Day is a federal holiday by law, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM), so federal offices, banks, post offices and the stock market are closed. Christmas Eve is not a federal holiday. For closing times and what stays open, see our [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 store hours](/news/christmas-eve-christmas-day-2026-store-hours/).

## Christmas shipping deadlines for 2026

If you are mailing gifts, the U.S. Postal Service (USPS) recommends send-by dates for delivery before Dec. 25 in the lower 48 states. The agency announced them on Sept. 22, and it calls them recommendations, not guarantees.

| Service | Recommended send-by date |
|---|---|
| USPS Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail | Thursday, Dec. 17 |
| Priority Mail | Friday, Dec. 18 |
| Priority Mail Express | Saturday, Dec. 19 |
| First-Class Package International and Priority Mail International | Wednesday, Dec. 9 |

Our [USPS holiday shipping deadlines](/news/usps-holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026/) story has the military and international tables and the service-commitment tool for Alaska, Hawaii and the territories.

## December deadlines beyond gifts

Two health-coverage deadlines fall in the same month. Medicare open enrollment ends Monday, Dec. 7, according to Medicare.gov. For Affordable Care Act (ACA) Marketplace plans on HealthCare.gov, choosing a plan by Tuesday, Dec. 15 starts coverage on Jan. 1, 2027; plans picked later start Feb. 1.

| Deadline | Date | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Medicare open enrollment ends | Dec. 7 | Monday |
| ACA deadline for Jan. 1 coverage | Dec. 15 | Tuesday |

## New Year's Day 2027

The next federal holiday after Christmas is New Year's Day, which falls on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, according to OPM. The countdown clock above has an option for it. From Saturday, Oct. 24, 2026, that is 69 days away.
