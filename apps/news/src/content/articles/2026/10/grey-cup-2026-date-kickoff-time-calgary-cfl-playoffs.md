**The 113th Grey Cup is scheduled for Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, at McMahon Stadium in Calgary, with a 6 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) start, according to the Canadian Football League (CFL).** The Montreal Alouettes and the Edmonton Elks will play in it, the CFL said on Nov. 7, 2026. Below: the playoff results, the time in every Canadian zone and where to watch.

## What is the Grey Cup 2026 date and time?

The CFL's Oct. 24 release gave every playoff game before the Grey Cup; its Nov. 7 release names the finalists. Times are in ET. Canadian coverage is on The Sports Network (TSN), Bell Media's broadcast network (CTV), the French-language Réseau des sports (RDS) and Crave.

| Date | Game | Time (CFL) | Canadian TV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday, Oct. 31 | Eastern Semi-Final: Winnipeg Blue Bombers at Toronto Argonauts, Toronto | 1 p.m. ET (noon CT) | TSN1, TSN4, CTV, RDS |
| Saturday, Oct. 31 | Western Semi-Final: Saskatchewan Roughriders at BC Lions, BC Place | 4:30 p.m. ET (1:30 p.m. PT) | TSN1, TSN4, RDS |
| Saturday, Nov. 7 | Eastern Final: Winnipeg Blue Bombers at Montreal Alouettes, Percival Molson Stadium. Montreal won. | 3 p.m. ET | TSN1, TSN3, CTV, Crave, RDS |
| Saturday, Nov. 7 | Western Final: BC Lions at Edmonton Elks, Commonwealth Stadium. Edmonton won 28-16. | 6:30 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. PT) | TSN1, TSN3, TSN4, RDS |
| Sunday, Nov. 15 | 113th Grey Cup: Montreal Alouettes vs. Edmonton Elks, McMahon Stadium, Calgary | 6 p.m. ET | TSN, CTV, RDS, Crave (outside Canada, CFL+) |

Winnipeg and BC advanced from the Oct. 31 semi-finals, the CFL's Oct. 31 release shows. Montreal beat Winnipeg in the Eastern Final, the CFL said. TSN and Global News both reported Edmonton's 28-16 win over BC in the Western Final. The semi-finals fall on Saturday daylight time; clocks fall back on Sunday, Nov. 1, so the Nov. 7 finals and the Grey Cup are on standard time.

The Sports Network (TSN) listed the Grey Cup on Nov. 15 at 6 p.m. too, with the teams to be determined and the note "Schedule subject to change." The CFL's Nov. 7 release now names Montreal and Edmonton and keeps the Sunday, Nov. 15, 6 p.m. ET start.

## Why did the semi-final times change?

The CFL's Dec. 9, 2025 schedule listed the semi-finals at 3 and 6:30 p.m. ET. The TSN broadcast schedule later showed 1 and 4:30 p.m. The league's Oct. 24 release settles it on TSN's times: 1 p.m. ET in Toronto and 4:30 p.m. ET in Vancouver. The division finals keep the 3 and 6:30 p.m. ET slots.

## What time is the Grey Cup in each time zone?

Clocks fall back on Nov. 1, so on Nov. 15 the 6 p.m. ET start falls on standard time everywhere in this table. The CFL gives only the ET time. Every other row is our conversion.

| Time zone | Example place | Grey Cup start, Sun., Nov. 15 |
|---|---|---|
| Pacific Standard Time | Pacific zone, Canada and the U.S. | 3 p.m. |
| Mountain Standard Time | Calgary | 4 p.m. |
| Central Standard Time | Winnipeg | 5 p.m. |
| Central Standard Time, no clock change | Regina, Saskatchewan | 5 p.m. |
| Eastern Standard Time | Eastern zone, Canada and the U.S. | 6 p.m. (CFL time) |
| Atlantic Standard Time | Atlantic Canada | 7 p.m. |
| Newfoundland Standard Time | Newfoundland | 7:30 p.m. |
| Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) and Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | United Kingdom | 11 p.m. |

Saskatchewan does not change its clocks, so Regina is on Central Standard Time all year and also sees a 5 p.m. start. For the date the change happens, see our [daylight saving time guide](/when-is/daylight-savings/) and the [daylight saving time ends Nov. 1, 2026](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) story.

## Who is performing at the Grey Cup halftime show?

Riley Green performs in the Bud Light Grey Cup Halftime Show, and Sarah McLachlan sings the national anthem, the CFL said on Nov. 7.

## Where can you watch the Grey Cup?

In Canada, the CFL's Nov. 7 release lists TSN, CTV, RDS and Crave for the Grey Cup, and CFL+ internationally. Tickets are available at Ticketmaster, the CFL says.

In the United States, the CFL says select games air on CBS Sports Network and the rest stream on CFL+. The CFL's Nov. 7 release lists CFL+ for the Grey Cup internationally and names no U.S. TV channel for it.

## Who won the Grey Cup last year?

As reported by The Canadian Press, the Saskatchewan Roughriders beat the Montreal Alouettes 25-17 in the 112th Grey Cup on Nov. 16, 2025, in Winnipeg.
