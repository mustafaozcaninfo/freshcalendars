**The Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) next announces a rate decision on Thursday 17 December 2026**, according to the Bank's Upcoming MPC dates page. On 5 November 2026 the MPC raised Bank Rate by 0.25 percentage points to 4%, by an 8-1 vote. For 2027 the Bank published eight dates in a news release on 17 September 2026. This story adds a weekday table, the number of days between decisions, a side-by-side with the US Federal Reserve's 2027 dates, and a one-page printable.

## When is the next Bank of England interest rate decision?

One announcement remains in 2026, on a Thursday. The Bank lists 2026 Report months as February, April, July and November, so the December decision comes without a Monetary Policy Report; the November Report came with the 5 November decision.

| Date | Weekday | Monetary Policy Report | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 November 2026 | Thursday | Yes | Decided: Bank Rate raised to 4% |
| 17 December 2026 | Thursday | No | Next decision |

Source: Bank of England, Upcoming MPC dates and the November 2026 Monetary Policy Summary and Minutes (published 5 November 2026).

In the November summary, eight members voted to raise Bank Rate by 0.25 percentage points to 4%, and one member voted for a 0.5-point rise to 4.25%. The Bank's dates pages do not give a time of day, so we do not state one. The [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) shows the month of the last decision.

## What are the Bank of England MPC dates for 2027?

The Bank's release "Monetary Policy Committee dates for 2027", published 17 September 2026, lists eight confirmed dates. It lists the MPC announcement and the publication of the Minutes on the same dates.

| Date | Weekday (our calculation) | Monetary Policy Report | Days since previous decision (our calculation) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 February 2027 | Thursday | Yes | 49 |
| 18 March 2027 | Thursday | No | 42 |
| 29 April 2027 | Thursday | Yes | 42 |
| 17 June 2027 | Thursday | No | 49 |
| 29 July 2027 | Thursday | Yes | 42 |
| 16 September 2027 | Thursday | No | 49 |
| 4 November 2027 | Thursday | Yes | 49 |
| 16 December 2027 | Thursday | No | 42 |

Source: Bank of England, Monetary Policy Committee dates for 2027 (17 September 2026). The first gap counts from 17 December 2026.

Four of the eight decisions come with a Monetary Policy Report: February, April, July and November. The gaps are all either 42 days (six weeks) or 49 days (seven weeks), and 2027 has four six-week gaps and four seven-week gaps counting from 17 December 2026.

## Were the 2027 dates provisional?

They were. A Bank of England release of 18 December 2025 called the same dates provisional, and the release of 17 September 2026 confirmed them. The [Self Assessment story](/news/self-assessment-deadline-31-january-2027-tax-return-penalties/) covers another set of UK dates that fall early in 2027.

## How do the 2027 dates compare with the US Federal Reserve?

The Federal Reserve's Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) calendar page lists tentative 2027 decision days, all Wednesdays, and the page itself says they are tentative. We matched them against the Bank's dates.

| FOMC decision | Bank of England decision | Days apart (our calculation) |
|---|---|---|
| 27 January 2027 | 4 February 2027 | 8 |
| 17 March 2027 | 18 March 2027 | 1 |
| 28 April 2027 | 29 April 2027 | 1 |
| 9 June 2027 | 17 June 2027 | 8 |
| 28 July 2027 | 29 July 2027 | 1 |
| 15 September 2027 | 16 September 2027 | 1 |
| 27 October 2027 | 4 November 2027 | 8 |
| 8 December 2027 | 16 December 2027 | 8 |

Source: Federal Reserve, FOMC meeting calendars (tentative 2027 dates); Bank of England, MPC dates for 2027.

In four weeks of 2027 both banks decide (the [Fed 2027 schedule story](/news/fomc-meeting-dates-2027-fed-schedule-december-2026/) has the full US list), with the Fed on the Wednesday and the Bank the next day: 17 and 18 March, 28 and 29 April, 28 and 29 July, and 15 and 16 September. In the other four pairs the Bank decides eight days after the Fed. The [Bank of Canada rate decisions](/news/bank-of-canada-rate-hike-october-28-2026-next-dates-2027/) story covers another central bank's 2027 schedule.
