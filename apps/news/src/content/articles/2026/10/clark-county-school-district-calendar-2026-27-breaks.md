**Clark County School District (CCSD) students have no school on Friday, Oct. 30, 2026**, listed as Nevada Day Observed on the "2026-2027 School Calendar for Students" (footer: "Subject to change"). It is the next day off on the calendar. **Winter break runs Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027**, and spring break is Monday through Friday, March 15-19, 2027.

## CCSD 2026-27 breaks

| Break | Dates | Weekdays with no school |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving | Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27, 2026 | 3 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 |
| Spring break | Mon.-Fri., March 15-19, 2027 | 5 |

The weekday counts are ours, computed from the printed dates. Winter break is the longest of the three. Add the Staff Development Day on Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 and students have 11 weekdays in a row without school, from Dec. 21 to Jan. 4. Classes resume Tuesday, Jan. 5, when the calendar lists the start of the second semester. The first semester ends Friday, Dec. 18, with winter break beginning at the end of that day.

Spring break begins at the end of the day on Friday, March 12, the end of the third grading period. Classes resume Monday, March 22. For how other districts set theirs, see our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) and the [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

| Date | Day | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day |
| Sept. 21, 2026 | Monday | Staff Development Day (students only) |
| Oct. 12, 2026 | Monday | Parent-teacher conferences (elementary students only) |
| Oct. 30, 2026 | Friday | Nevada Day Observed |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Election Day, Staff Development Day (students only) |
| Nov. 11, 2026 | Wednesday | Veterans Day |
| Nov. 25, 2026 | Wednesday | No school (First Contingency Day, if needed) |
| Nov. 26-27, 2026 | Thursday-Friday | Thanksgiving Day and Family Day |
| Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 | Monday-Friday, two weeks | Winter break |
| Jan. 4, 2027 | Monday | Staff Development Day (students only) |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Martin Luther King, Jr. Day |
| Feb. 15, 2027 | Monday | President's Day |
| March 15-19, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Spring break |
| March 26, 2027 | Friday | No school (Second Contingency Day, if needed) |
| March 29, 2027 | Monday | Staff Development Day (students only) |

The first day of classes is Monday, Aug. 10, 2026. The first grading period ends Friday, Oct. 9. On the three days marked "students only" or "elementary only", the calendar says school is out for students in that group; it does not say what other school staff do.

## Is there school on Nevada Day?

No. Friday, Oct. 30, 2026 is Nevada Day Observed on the CCSD calendar, so students have a three-day weekend. The district uses the word "observed" and the calendar prints no other detail about the date. The same stretch of the fall has a second day off soon after: Tuesday, Nov. 3 is Election Day and a Staff Development Day with no school for students, and Wednesday, Nov. 11 is Veterans Day. Plan around both on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## How long is Thanksgiving break?

The calendar lists no school on Wednesday, Nov. 25, and on Thursday and Friday, Nov. 26-27, which it labels Thanksgiving Day and Family Day. That is three weekdays. Nov. 25 is also named the First Contingency Day, so it is a day the district could use for make-up classes if needed.

## What are contingency days?

The calendar marks three contingency days "if needed": Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, Friday, March 26, 2027 and Friday, May 21, 2027. A contingency day is a make-up day the district can call on instead of a day off. The calendar shows Nov. 25 and March 26 as no-school days, so treat them as days off unless CCSD announces a change. May 21 falls after the last day of school.

## Is there school on President's Day and Martin Luther King, Jr. Day?

No. Monday, Jan. 18, 2027 is Martin Luther King, Jr. Day and Monday, Feb. 15, 2027 is listed as "President's Day", both with no school.

## When does the school year end?

The last day of school is Thursday, May 20, 2027, listed as the end of the school year (180 days). Friday, May 21 is the Third Contingency Day, to be used only if needed.
