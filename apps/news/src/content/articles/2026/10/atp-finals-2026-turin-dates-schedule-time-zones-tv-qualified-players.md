**The Nitto ATP Finals will take place from Nov. 15 to 22, 2026 at the Inalpi Arena in Turin, Italy, according to the event's official site, and its TV page, checked Nov. 13, 2026, lists Tennis Channel for the United States.** Nov. 15 and Nov. 22 are both Sundays. The event's 2026 tournament schedule puts the first singles match of each round-robin day not before 2 p.m. in Turin, 8 a.m. Eastern Time (ET). The draw was made on Thursday, Nov. 12, and Sunday opens with world No. 1 Alexander Zverev against Rafael Jodar. The Association of Tennis Professionals (ATP) runs the tour.

## What are the ATP Finals 2026 dates?

The Nitto ATP Finals site says: "This year, the Nitto ATP Finals will take place from 15 to 22 November at the Inalpi Arena in Turin." The event's long-standing format is round-robin groups, then semifinals and a final. The event's schedule runs round-robin matches Nov. 15 to 20, semifinals on Saturday, Nov. 21, and finals on Sunday, Nov. 22.

The singles field is complete. The event's Nov. 12 draw report names eight players in two round-robin groups; the top two in each group reach the semifinals. Seeds are in brackets.

|---|---|
| [1] Alexander Zverev | [2] Carlos Alcaraz |
| [3] Ben Shelton | [4] Daniil Medvedev |

## What time do ATP Finals matches start?

The Nitto ATP Finals tournament schedule for 2026, checked Nov. 3, gives these start times in local time in Turin, which is on Central European Time (CET). Each day has a doubles match first, then a singles match "NB" (not before) the listed time. The event's ticket site calls the times indicative and subject to change.

**Order of play, first two days (Inalpi Arena, Turin time)**

| Time | Sun., Nov. 15 | Mon., Nov. 16 |
|---|---|---|
| 11:30 a.m. (doubles) | [1] Heliovaara/Patten vs [8] Luz/Matos | [3] Granollers/Zeballos vs [5] Bolelli/Vavassori |
| Not before 2 p.m. (singles) | [1] Zverev vs [8] Jodar | [2] Alcaraz vs [7] Tiafoe |
| Not before 6 p.m. (doubles) | [4] Arevalo/Pavic vs [6] Arribage/Olivetti | [2] Harrison/Skupski vs [7] Krawietz/Puetz |
| Not before 8:30 p.m. (singles) | [3] Shelton vs [6] Fils | [4] Medvedev vs [5] Cobolli |

The order of play for Nov. 17 onward is not yet announced.

In November 2026 Turin is on CET (Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus 1 hour). U.S. clocks fall back on Nov. 1, so Eastern Time is UTC minus 5 hours, six hours behind Turin. Central Time (CT) is 7 hours behind, Mountain Time (MT) 8 hours and Pacific Time (PT) 9 hours. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in the U.K. is 1 hour behind.
