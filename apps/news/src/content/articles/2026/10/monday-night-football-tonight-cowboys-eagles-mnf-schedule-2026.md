**The Chicago Bears beat the Seattle Seahawks 34-17 on Monday Night Football (MNF) on Mon., Nov. 2, 2026, at Lumen Field in Seattle, per the National Football League (NFL) schedule and the Bears' game recap.** Kickoff was at 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on ESPN. The win moved the Bears to 6-2, and the Seahawks are 4-4, per NFL.com standings.

According to the Bears' recap, quarterback Caleb Williams went 24 of 34 for 247 yards and 1 touchdown, D'Andre Swift ran 27 times for 69 yards and 3 touchdowns, and Rome Odunze caught 4 passes for a team-high 87 yards. The recap says Lynch intercepted a Darnold pass that Booker deflected and returned it 16 yards to the Seahawks' 6, which set up Swift's touchdown that made it 34-17. Chicago plays next in Week 9 against Tampa Bay on Sun., Nov. 8, at 8:20 p.m. ET at Soldier Field on NBC, per the Bears' schedule.

The next Monday game is the Buffalo Bills at the Minnesota Vikings on Mon., Nov. 9, at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN, per NFL.com; see our [Bills at Vikings Monday Night Football page](/news/monday-night-football-tonight-bills-vikings-mnf-november-9-2026/). The previous Monday game ended with the Dallas Cowboys beating the Philadelphia Eagles 38-21 on Oct. 26, according to the FOX Sports box score. This page keeps the kickoff for the Nov. 2 game in every U.S. time zone, the channel, and the full list of the remaining dated Monday games. A one-page printable of that list is further down.

## What time was Bears at Seahawks in my time zone?

Bears at Seahawks on Mon., Nov. 2, kicked off at 8:15 p.m. ET. Clocks fell back on Sunday, Nov. 1, so Nov. 2 was on standard time: that is 8:15 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST). The NFL keeps Monday games at 8:15 p.m. ET on NFL.com. Our [guide to when daylight saving time ends](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) covers the change itself.

| Time zone | Mon., Nov. 2 (Bears at Seahawks) |
|---|---|
| Eastern Standard Time (EST) | 8:15 p.m. |
| Central Standard Time (CST) | 7:15 p.m. |
| Mountain Standard Time (MST) | 6:15 p.m. |
| Pacific Standard Time (PST) | 5:15 p.m. |
| Arizona (no clock change) | 6:15 p.m. |
| Alaska Standard Time (AKST) | 4:15 p.m. |
| Hawaii-Aleutian Standard Time (HST) | 3:15 p.m. |

We worked these out from the NFL's 8:15 p.m. ET listing. Arizona stays on Mountain Standard Time all year, and Hawaii does not change its clocks either.

## What channel is Monday Night Football on?

NFL.com listed ESPN for Bears at Seahawks and lists it for every other Monday game on this page. In its May 11, 2026, press release, ESPN said its games often air on the American Broadcasting Company (ABC) too: "the majority of ESPN's games also simulcast on ABC." NFL.com does not list ABC per game, so check your local listings if you want ABC.
