**Louisiana voters can see their exact Nov. 3, 2026 ballot by name or address in the Louisiana Voter Portal or the GeauxVote app.** The statewide ballot has the U.S. Senate general election, open primaries for all six U.S. House seats and 10 proposed constitutional amendments. Parish and local races vary by precinct.

## How to find your Louisiana sample ballot

1. Go to the Louisiana Voter Portal at voterportal.sos.la.gov, or open the free GeauxVote app.
2. Choose "Search by Voter" and enter your name and address details, or search by precinct if you know your ward and precinct.
3. Open "Sample Ballot." It lists every race and amendment for your precinct.
4. Mark your choices and print them. The Secretary of State says the marked sample ballot is for preparation only and is not an official ballot.

The portal can also search by candidate or amendment. Ballots appear about three weeks before an election, once qualifying closes. Not in Louisiana? Our [sample ballot lookup for every state](/news/sample-ballot-by-zip-code-2026-texas-ballot/) lists each official tool.

## Key dates for Nov. 3

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| Mon., Oct. 5 | Registration deadline in person or by mail (passed) |
| Tue., Oct. 13 | Online registration deadline (passed) |
| Tue., Oct. 20 to Tue., Oct. 27 | Early voting, 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., closed Sun., Oct. 25 |
| Fri., Oct. 30, 4:30 p.m. | Last day to request a mail ballot (other than military and overseas) |
| Mon., Nov. 2, 4:30 p.m. | Registrar must receive voted mail ballots |
| Tue., Nov. 3 | Election Day, polls open 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | U.S. House general election, where needed |

Anyone in line at 8 p.m. can still vote. Bring photo ID with your name and signature, such as a driver's license, LA Wallet or a special ID card. Without one, you can vote by signing a voter affidavit.

## What is on the ballot

### U.S. Senate

Louisiana used closed party primaries for the Senate for the first time this year. The Secretary of State's candidate list for Nov. 3 shows two candidates:

- **Jamie Davis**, Democrat
- **Julia Letlow**, Republican

### U.S. House

Under Act 7 of the 2026 session, House races moved to the fall and are open primaries on Nov. 3, with candidates of any party on one ballot. If nobody wins a majority, the top two meet on Saturday, Dec. 12. Your sample ballot shows the candidates in your district.

### 10 constitutional amendments

Each amendment is on every ballot statewide. The short descriptions below follow the official ballot questions in the Secretary of State's guide.

| No. | The ballot asks whether to |
|---|---|
| 1 | Let a disabled veteran's surviving spouse transfer the extra property tax exemption once to a new home |
| 2 | Let local taxing bodies keep a lower millage rate without losing the right to return to the maximum |
| 3 | Bar post-conviction bail for anyone convicted of an aggravated offense against a minor child |
| 4 | Bar anyone who has served more than one and a half terms as governor from being elected again |
| 5 | Let state retirement systems put one-time state money toward any unfunded liability, not only the oldest |
| 6 | Let parishes and cities give an extra homestead exemption to owners 65 and older in the special assessment level |
| 7 | Allow public money to find and replace lead drinking water lines on customers' property |
| 8 | Bar a foreign adversary or its agent from expropriating property |
| 9 | Raise the income limit for the senior special assessment level on homesteads |
| 10 | Allow property tax exemptions for rehabilitated blighted or derelict property |

The guide has the full text and the current constitutional language for each one.
