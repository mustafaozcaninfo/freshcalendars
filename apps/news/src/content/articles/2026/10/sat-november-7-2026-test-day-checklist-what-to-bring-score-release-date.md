**The SAT was on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, and College Board's student scores arrive Friday, Nov. 20.** Free score sends can be used or changed until Monday, Nov. 16. Students who want another try can register for the Saturday, Dec. 5 test by Friday, Nov. 20. Print the [score and next-test checklist](#printable-checklist) and tick it off. All SAT Weekend deadlines run to 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), College Board says.

## What is the countdown from today to SAT scores?

The table runs from the free score send deadline to the backup test.

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Nov. 16 | Last day to use or change free score sends (computed) | College Board, sending scores |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | Scores to students | College Board, score release dates |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | Registration deadline for the Dec. 5 SAT | College Board, test dates and deadlines |
| Mon., Nov. 23 | Scores to the K-12 Score Reporting Portal | College Board, score release dates |
| Tue., Nov. 24 | Late registration deadline for Dec. 5, with additional fees | College Board, test dates and deadlines |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | Backup SAT; scores to students Fri., Dec. 18 | College Board, test dates and score release dates |

The Nov. 16 date is our calculation from College Board's rule that you have "until nine days after the test" to use or change your free score sends. Weekdays are our addition.

## What do I need to bring to the SAT?

For the Dec. 5 test, College Board lists these as required:

- A fully charged testing device with Bluebook installed and exam setup completed.
- An up-to-date admission ticket from Bluebook. Printing is preferred, and emailing the ticket is allowed.
- Acceptable photo identification. It must be a physical document, not an electronic one.
- Pencils or pens for scratch work.
- Your College Board username and password.
- An acceptable calculator for the Math section.

College Board also suggests a drink or snacks for the break and a watch without an audible alarm. We left out a list of prohibited items because we did not check one.

## What time should I arrive for the SAT?

College Board's test-day page says centers open at 7:45 a.m. and doors close at 8 a.m., "unless otherwise noted on your admission ticket." Testing starts between 8:15 and 8:30 a.m., and standard time ends between 10:45 and 11 a.m. There is one 10-minute break between sections. Students who borrow a device must arrive 30 minutes early.

Bluebook's own page tells students to arrive 15 minutes before the test begins. The two pages differ, so go by the arrival time and test center address on your ticket.

## When do exam setup and the admission ticket happen?

College Board says that "1-5 days before your test, you'll complete a quick exam setup and generate your admission ticket." For the Dec. 5 test that is Monday, Nov. 30 through Friday, Dec. 4. The ticket becomes available five days before the test, which is Nov. 30. Doing setup early leaves time to fix a Bluebook problem before the weekend.

## When do November SAT scores come out?

In its Fall 2026 table for SAT Weekend, College Board lists the Nov. 7 test with student scores on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026 and the K-12 Score Reporting Portal on Monday, Nov. 23. The Dec. 5 test follows on Dec. 18 for students and Dec. 21 for the portal.

Up to four score sends are free with each weekend registration. If you want to change who gets them, do it by Monday, Nov. 16.

## What if I want to test again?

The next SAT is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026: register by Friday, Nov. 20, or by Tuesday, Nov. 24 with additional late fees. If you need to borrow a testing device from College Board, it asks for requests at least 30 days before test day, and a request does not guarantee a device. For Dec. 5 that mark was Thursday, Nov. 5 (our calculation). The full 2026-27 list is in our [SAT and ACT test dates story](/news/sat-act-test-dates-2026-27-registration-deadlines/).
