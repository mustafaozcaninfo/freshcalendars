**November 2026 starts on Sunday, Nov. 1 and has 30 days, two federal holidays and 19 working days.** The holidays are Veterans Day on Wednesday, Nov. 11 and Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, Nov. 26, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM). The month opens with the clock change: daylight saving time ends at 2 a.m. on Nov. 1, per the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Below are 14 free November 2026 calendar templates, each drawn by Fresh Calendars and saved as a one-page PDF with real, selectable text. Click any picture to open its PDF.

## November 2026 at a glance

A Sunday start makes November 2026 a tidy month on paper: five full rows, Nov. 1 in the first box and Nov. 30 on the last Monday. Sundays and Mondays each come five times.

| | November 2026 |
|---|---|
| First day | Sunday, Nov. 1 (clocks fall back 1 hour at 2 a.m.) |
| Last day | Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Days | 30 (21 weekdays, 9 weekend days) |
| Federal holidays | Veterans Day, Wed., Nov. 11 · Thanksgiving Day, Thu., Nov. 26 |
| Working days | 19 (18 if you also get the Friday after Thanksgiving) |
| Election Day | Tuesday, Nov. 3 |
| Thanksgiving weekend | Thu., Nov. 26 to Sun., Nov. 29 |
| Friday the 13th | Nov. 13 |
| Week numbers | Weeks 44 to 49, under International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 8601 |
| Full moon | Tue., Nov. 24, 9:53 a.m. EST (Beaver Moon) |
| Same layout as | November 2020, 2015 and 2009 |

Any November that starts on a Sunday has the same grid, so a November 2020 or 2015 page lines up day for day. November is also Native American Heritage Month, according to the Library of Congress.

## November 2026 holidays and key dates

Only Veterans Day and Thanksgiving are federal holidays. The rest of the table lists the dates that most often go on a November calendar, each from the agency or organization that sets it.

| Date | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 30 | November Supplemental Security Income (SSI) paid early, because Nov. 1 is a Sunday | Social Security Administration (SSA) |
| Sun., Nov. 1, 2 a.m. | Daylight saving time ends; clocks fall back 1 hour | NIST |
| Sun., Nov. 1 | HealthCare.gov open enrollment for 2027 plans begins | HealthCare.gov |
| Sun., Nov. 1, 3:28 p.m. EST | Last quarter Moon | U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) |
| Tue., Nov. 3 | Election Day; Social Security for people paid on the 3rd | State election offices; SSA |
| Night of Nov. 4-5 | Southern Taurid meteor shower peak | American Meteor Society |
| Sun., Nov. 8 | Diwali | See our [Diwali 2026](/news/diwali-2026-date-lakshmi-puja-time-usa/) story |
| Mon., Nov. 9, 2:02 a.m. EST | New moon | USNO |
| Tue., Nov. 10 | Social Security payment, birthdays on the 1st to 10th | SSA |
| Wed., Nov. 11 | Veterans Day, federal holiday | OPM |
| Night of Nov. 11-12 | Northern Taurid meteor shower peak | American Meteor Society |
| Night of Nov. 16-17 | Leonid meteor shower peak | American Meteor Society |
| Tue., Nov. 17, 6:48 a.m. EST | First quarter Moon | USNO |
| Wed., Nov. 18 | Social Security payment, birthdays on the 11th to 20th | SSA |
| Tue., Nov. 24, 9:53 a.m. EST | Full moon, often called the Beaver Moon | USNO |
| Wed., Nov. 25 | Social Security payment, birthdays on the 21st to 31st | SSA |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving Day, federal holiday | OPM |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Black Friday | Retail date, not a holiday |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | First Sunday of Advent | U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops |
| Mon., Nov. 30 | Cyber Monday | Retail date, not a holiday |

Medicare open enrollment runs from Oct. 15 to Dec. 7, so it covers all of November. Poll hours on Election Day vary by state; check your state election office.

The Social Security date is the one most calendars miss. SSA normally pays people born on the 1st to 10th on the second Wednesday of the month, which is Nov. 11. That is Veterans Day, so the 2026 schedule moves the payment up a day to **Tuesday, Nov. 10**. For everything that opens and closes on the holiday itself, see [Veterans Day 2026](/news/veterans-day-2026-wednesday-november-11/), and for the countdown to each date, our [November 2026 holidays and important dates](/news/november-2026-important-dates-days-until/) story.

## Thanksgiving week 2026 on the calendar

Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November, which in 2026 is Nov. 26. The latest it can ever fall is Nov. 28. The week around it fills up fast: the full moon on Tuesday, Nov. 24, the last Social Security payday of the month on Wednesday, Nov. 25, Black Friday on Nov. 27 and Small Business Saturday on Nov. 28. The Thanksgiving calendar above gives each of those eight days its own lined box. Why the holiday lands on Nov. 26 this year is in [why Thanksgiving 2026 is Nov. 26](/news/why-thanksgiving-2026-is-november-26/), and the date itself is on [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

## The clock change on Nov. 1

The first day of the month is also 25 hours long in most of the United States. At 2 a.m. local time on Sunday, Nov. 1, clocks go back to 1 a.m., so evenings get dark about an hour earlier from that day on. Arizona (outside the Navajo Nation) and Hawaii do not change. Details by state are in [daylight saving time ends Nov. 1, 2026](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/).
