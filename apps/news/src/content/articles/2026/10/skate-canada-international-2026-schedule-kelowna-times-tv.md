**Kevin Aymoz of France won the men's title and Lia Pereira and Trennt Michaud of Canada won pairs at Skate Canada International 2026 in Kelowna, British Columbia, according to the International Skating Union (ISU) results; the women's free skate and the free dance close the event on Sunday, Nov. 1, at 3 p.m. and 5:15 p.m. Eastern Time (ET).** In Kelowna those are 1 p.m. and 3:15 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), and Peacock streams both, NBC Sports says.

## Skate Canada International 2026 results so far

Scores are from the ISU results pages for the event, checked on Sunday afternoon.

| Event | Gold | Silver | Bronze |
|---|---|---|---|
| Men | Kevin Aymoz, France, 258.43 | Rio Nakata, Japan, 249.25 | Mihhail Selevko, Estonia, 248.66 |
| Pairs | Lia Pereira and Trennt Michaud, Canada, 202.17 | Alisa Efimova and Misha Mitrofanov, United States, 191.90 | Oxana Vouillamoz and Tom Bouvart, Switzerland, 183.12 |

After Saturday's short program the women's leader is Mao Shimada of Japan with 82.11, ahead of Isabeau Levito of the United States (78.50) and Niina Petrõkina of Estonia (77.24). In ice dance, Lilah Fear and Lewis Gibson of Great Britain lead after the rhythm dance with 81.37, ahead of Evgeniia Lopareva and Geoffrey Brissaud of France (77.90) and Caroline Green and Michael Parsons of the United States (74.24). The table below converts every session to Mountain, Central, Eastern and Atlantic time, and a second table lists the rest of the 2026-27 International Skating Union (ISU) Grand Prix season.

## Skate Canada International 2026 start times by time zone

Skate Canada says that "all times are in Pacific Time (PT) and are subject to change." The Kelowna times below are Skate Canada's; the other columns are our conversion. Mountain and Central mean U.S. Mountain Time (MT) and U.S. Central Time (CT), such as Denver and Chicago.

One detail changes the math on Sunday. The U.S. and Atlantic Canada set clocks back at 2 a.m. on Nov. 1, but British Columbia does not: its government said that on Nov. 1, 2026 "no change will be made" and that Pacific time is set at UTC-7 (see our [Canada clock change story](/news/canada-clocks-fall-back-november-1-2026-which-provinces-no-time-change/)). Kelowna therefore stays one hour ahead of Seattle on Sunday, and the gap to Eastern Time shrinks from three hours to two.

## Who is entered in Skate Canada International 2026?

Skate Canada announced the entry list on June 16, 2026. It includes, among women, Madeline Schizas and Fée Ann Landry of Canada, Isabeau Levito of the United States, Mao Shimada of Japan and Niina Petrõkina of Estonia. Men include Stephen Gogolev and Wesley Chiu of Canada, Kevin Aymoz of France, Kazuki Tomono of Japan and Liam Kapeikis of the United States.

In pairs, entries include Lia Pereira and Trennt Michaud of Canada and Alisa Efimova and Misha Mitrofanov of the United States. In ice dance they include Marie-Jade Lauriault and Romain Le Gac of Canada, Lilah Fear and Lewis Gibson of Great Britain, Evgeniia Lopareva and Geoffrey Brissaud of France, and Caroline Green and Michael Parsons of the United States. The list was announced in the summer, so check Skate Canada for withdrawals.
