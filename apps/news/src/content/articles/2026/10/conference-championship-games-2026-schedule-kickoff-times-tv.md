**The Big Ten Conference lists its 2026 football championship game for Saturday, Dec. 5, at 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on FOX at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis.** The table below puts the Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) title games on Friday, Dec. 4 and Saturday, Dec. 5 in kickoff order, with Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT) and Pacific Time (PT) and the source behind each row. One conference, the American, had not posted its game when we checked on Oct. 26.

## What are the conference championship game times in 2026?

Both days fall on standard time in the United States. Only the ET or CT time comes from a source; the other zones are our conversion.

| Kickoff (ET) | CT / MT / PT | Game | Network | Venue | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Dec. 4, 7 p.m. | 6 / 5 / 4 p.m. | Sun Belt | ESPN | Home field of the division winner with the better record | Listed in the Sun Belt's May 27 release |
| Fri., Dec. 4, 7 p.m. | 6 / 5 / 4 p.m. | Conference USA | CBS Sports Network | Not in our sources | Date from Conference USA; kickoff from Western Kentucky athletics |
| Fri., Dec. 4, 8 p.m. | 7 p.m. CT / 6 / 5 p.m. | Big 12 | American Broadcasting Company (ABC) | AT&T Stadium, Arlington, Texas | Big 12's championship page |
| Fri., Dec. 4, 9 p.m. | 8 / 7 / 6 p.m. | Mountain West | FOX | Higher team hosts; site not set | Mountain West Conference |
| Sat., Dec. 5, noon | 11 a.m. / 10 a.m. / 9 a.m. | Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) | ABC | Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte, N.C. | ACC |
| Sat., Dec. 5, noon | 11 a.m. / 10 a.m. / 9 a.m. | Mid-American Conference (MAC) | ESPN | Ford Field, Detroit | MAC schedule, as published by Central Michigan athletics |
| Sat., Dec. 5, 4 p.m. | 3 / 2 / 1 p.m. | Southeastern Conference (SEC) | ABC | Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta | Time as reported by National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) site NCAA.com; the SEC page lists only the date and stadium |
| Sat., Dec. 5, 8 p.m. | 7 / 6 / 5 p.m. | Big Ten | FOX | Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis | Big Ten Conference |
| Not yet posted | Not yet posted | American Conference | Not yet posted | Not yet posted | The American's site |

The Mountain West lists its game as "8 p.m. CT/7 p.m. MT/6 p.m. PT/4 p.m. HT" (Hawaii Time), which is 9 p.m. ET by our conversion. The Big 12 time is from the Big 12's championship page, which we could not load directly, so recheck it there.

## What time is the Big Ten championship game in 2026?

The Big Ten says "Game time is set for 8:00 p.m. ET" on FOX on Sat., Dec. 5, in its Aug. 19, 2026 championship information post. That is 7 p.m. CT, 6 p.m. MT and 5 p.m. PT, by our conversion. It is the last of the Saturday games in the table.

## What time are the ACC and SEC championship games?

The ACC says its 22nd annual game kicks off at noon ET on ABC at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina, home of the Carolina Panthers. Its Jan. 26, 2026 announcement calls it "the first game to kick off Championship Saturday."

The SEC's page lists the 35th SEC Football Championship Game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta on Dec. 5, between the top two teams by winning percentage. It gives no kickoff. NCAA.com reports 4 p.m. ET on ABC, so treat that time as unconfirmed by the conference until its page adds it.

## Which championship games are on Friday, Dec. 4?

Four. The Big 12 game is at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, at 7 p.m. CT on ABC, according to the Big 12's championship page. The Mountain West's 14th championship game is at 9 p.m. ET on FOX; the host is the higher team by conference win percentage among the top two, so the venue is not set. The Sun Belt's release lists a 7 p.m. ET game on ESPN at the home field of the division winner with the better record; we could not read the body of that release, so we say "listed in" it. Conference USA's schedule release gives the date and CBS Sports Network; the 7 p.m. ET kickoff comes from Western Kentucky athletics, not the conference.

## What is still not announced?

The American Conference had not posted a date, site or time on its site. The Mountain West and Sun Belt venues depend on which team earns the home game. Check each conference page near the date, because the tables above rest on conference releases and pages dated January to August 2026, and the Big 12 page is undated.

## What happens after championship weekend?

The College Football Playoff (CFP) says the final Selection Committee rankings of the 2026 season are released on Selection Day, Sunday, Dec. 6, from noon to 3 p.m. ET on ESPN; see our [CFP rankings schedule](/news/college-football-playoff-rankings-2026-schedule-dates-times/). The CFP broadcast schedule lists the first round on Friday, Dec. 18 at 8 p.m. ET on ESPN, and on Saturday, Dec. 19 at noon ET (ABC and ESPN), 3:30 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. ET (Turner Network Television (TNT), truTV and Home Box Office (HBO) Max), with campus sites set on Dec. 6. The title game is Monday, Jan. 25, 2027, at 7:30 p.m. ET at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas.

| Date | Event | Time and network |
|---|---|---|
| Sun., Dec. 6 | CFP Selection Day | Noon to 3 p.m. ET, ESPN |
| Fri., Dec. 18 | CFP first round | 8 p.m. ET, ESPN |
| Sat., Dec. 19 | CFP first round | Noon ET (ABC/ESPN); 3:30 and 7:30 p.m. ET (TNT/truTV/HBO Max) |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | Army-Navy Game, MetLife Stadium | 3 p.m. ET, CBS |
| Mon., Jan. 25, 2027 | CFP title game, Las Vegas | 7:30 p.m. ET |

The [Army-Navy Game](/news/army-navy-game-2026-date-kickoff-time-metlife-stadium-tv/) is a week after title weekend. For earlier games, see the [Thanksgiving football schedule](/news/thanksgiving-football-schedule-2026-nfl-college-games/).
