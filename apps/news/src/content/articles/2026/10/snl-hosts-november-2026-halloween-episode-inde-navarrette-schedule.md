**Inde Navarrette hosts Saturday Night Live (SNL) this Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026 (Halloween), at 11:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on NBC, with Gracie Abrams as musical guest, according to NBC Insider.** The tables below list every season 52 host announced so far, with weekday dates, and a free printable tracker follows them.

<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 29, 2026.</b> NBC has not yet announced the hosts or musical guests for Nov. 21 and Nov. 28. We will update this story when it does.</p>

## Who is hosting SNL on Oct. 31, 2026?

NBC Insider says Inde Navarrette is a first-time host. Its Oct. 27, 2026 story says: "After breaking through in the massive horror hit Obsession, the actress is set to star in the upcoming X-Men film." The same story says "Gracie Abrams is the Musical Guest." In NBC Insider's musical guest guide, Abrams "marks her second-ever Musical Guest stint on Halloween night."

## What time is SNL on Halloween?

NBC lists the show for "Saturdays at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC and next day on Peacock." The Oct. 31 episode airs live on NBC, and Peacock carries it the following day. NBC gives no local times for other time zones, so check your local NBC listings.

U.S. clocks fall back at 2 a.m. Sunday, Nov. 1, a few hours after the Oct. 31 episode ends. See [when daylight saving time ends in 2026](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/). Trick-or-treaters can check [Halloween 2026 sunset and trick-or-treat times](/news/halloween-2026-saturday-sunset-trick-or-treat-times/) for earlier in the evening.

NBC's Aug. 11, 2026 season guide says season 52 premiered on Sept. 26, 2026 with host Jalen Brunson and musical guest Katseye. NBC has also not said whether Nov. 21 and Nov. 28 are new episodes.

## Who hosts SNL in November 2026?

Druski hosts on Nov. 7 with The Strokes, and John Mulaney hosts on Nov. 14 with Phoebe Bridgers. NBC Insider says The Strokes "will perform as Musical Guests for their fifth time during the November 7 episode hosted by Druski." It calls Bridgers' Nov. 14 slot her "second solo Musical Guest appearance."
