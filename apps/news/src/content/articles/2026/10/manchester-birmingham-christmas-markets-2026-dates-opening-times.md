**Birmingham's Frankfurt Christmas Market opens first, on Thursday 5 November 2026, and Manchester Christmas Markets open the next day, Friday 6 November 2026**, according to Visit Birmingham and Visit Manchester, the cities' official tourism sites. Our side-by-side table and a one-page printable are below.

Visit Manchester has not yet confirmed Manchester's opening hours for 2026. Birmingham's hours are published, so the table gives them in full.

## What are the Manchester and Birmingham Christmas market dates?

| | Manchester Christmas Markets | Birmingham Frankfurt Christmas Market |
|---|---|---|
| Opens | Friday 6 November 2026 | Thursday 5 November 2026 |
| Main sites close | Tuesday 22 December 2026 | Thursday 24 December 2026 |
| Longer-running sites | Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens to Sunday 3 January 2027 | None listed |
| Days open, by our count | 47 (main sites); 59 (Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens) | 50 |
| Hours | Not yet confirmed | Published (see below) |
| Source | Visit Manchester; Transport for Greater Manchester | Visit Birmingham |

The day counts are our calculation, counting both the first and last date. They assume the markets trade on every date in the range; neither source lists closed days.

## What are the Manchester Christmas Markets 2026 dates?

Visit Manchester says the markets "will open on Friday 6 November 2026 and run until Tuesday 22 December 2026," with 210 stalls "spread throughout some of the city's most popular locations." It adds that Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens "will remain open until Sunday 3 January 2027."

Transport for Greater Manchester (TfGM), in its stakeholder advice last updated on 19 October 2026, gives the same pattern. Markets open across the city centre from Friday 6 November and "primarily run until Tuesday 22 December," with smaller sites at Cathedral Gardens and Albert Square staying open longer.

Visit Manchester says "opening hours for 2026 have not yet been confirmed" and that further details of all market locations will be announced closer to the event. We have not listed individual sites for that reason.

## What are the Birmingham Frankfurt Christmas Market opening times?

Visit Birmingham lists the 2026 market as running from 5 November to 24 December 2026, from Victoria Square down New Street in the city centre. Its weekly hours are:

| Day | Opens | Closes |
|---|---|---|
| Monday to Thursday | 11am | 9pm |
| Friday | 11am | 9.30pm |
| Saturday | 10am | 9.30pm |
| Sunday | 10am | 9pm |

On Remembrance Sunday, 8 November, the market opens later, at 1pm. The page does not state Christmas Eve hours, so check Visit Birmingham before travelling on Thursday 24 December.
