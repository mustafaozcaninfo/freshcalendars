**St Andrew's Day is a bank holiday in Scotland on Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, and there is no substitute day this year**, according to the GOV.UK list of United Kingdom (UK) bank holidays. From Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, that is 36 days away. St Andrew is Scotland's patron saint.

The table below runs from 2023 to 2028 and shows when the date moved. A one-page version is attached to this story as a printable list.

## Is St Andrew's Day a bank holiday in 2026?

Yes, in Scotland. GOV.UK's bank holidays page and its machine-readable data file for Scotland both list St Andrew's Day on 2026-11-30, a Monday, with no substitute day. Our [UK bank holidays 2027 story](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/) covers next year's list for England, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## What are the St Andrew's Day bank holiday dates for 2023 to 2028?

| Year | Nov. 30 falls on | Bank holiday in Scotland (GOV.UK) |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | Thursday | Nov. 30, 2023 |
| 2024 | Saturday | Monday, Dec. 2, 2024 (substitute day) |
| 2025 | Sunday | Monday, Dec. 1, 2025 (substitute day) |
| 2026 | Monday | Nov. 30, 2026, no substitute day |
| 2027 | Tuesday | Nov. 30, 2027 |
| 2028 | Thursday | Nov. 30, 2028 |

The weekdays in the second column are computed from the calendar. The holiday dates come from GOV.UK's Scotland data.

## What happens when Nov. 30 falls on a weekend?

The St Andrew's Day Bank Holiday (Scotland) Act 2007 adds "30th November, if it is not a Saturday or Sunday or, if it is a Saturday or Sunday, the first Monday following that day." That is the rule behind the two substitute days in the table. Nov. 30, 2024, was a Saturday, so the holiday moved to Monday, Dec. 2. Nov. 30, 2025, was a Sunday, so it moved to Monday, Dec. 1.

GOV.UK states the general principle for all bank holidays: "If a bank holiday is on a weekend, a 'substitute' weekday becomes a bank holiday, normally the following Monday." No weekend date appears in the 2026 to 2028 rows, so none of those years needs one.

## Do I get the day off or paid?

That depends on your employer. GOV.UK's holiday entitlement guidance, which applies across the UK, says "Bank or public holidays do not have to be given as paid leave." It also says "An employer can choose to include bank holidays as part of a worker's statutory annual leave." Check with your employer whether you get the day off.
