**Nevada voters decided two statewide ballot questions on Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026: Question 6, on a constitutional right to abortion, and Question 7, on voter identification, according to the Clark County Election Department's 2026 General Election Guide.** Both passed, the Nevada Independent and the Las Vegas Review-Journal reported; final figures come from the state's certified results. News 3 Las Vegas (KSNV) reported on July 6, 2026 that they were the only two statewide measures to qualify. Both are constitutional initiatives that voters approved in 2024, so this was the second vote on each.

## What Questions 6 and 7 change in Nevada

| Question | Petition | Topic | A yes vote | A no vote |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Initiative Petition C-05-2023 | Abortion | Creates a new section of the Nevada Constitution to establish a person's constitutional right to abortion | Keeps the constitution in its current form; does not affect abortion as a statutory right under Nevada law |
| 7 | Initiative Petition C-02-2023 | Voter identification | Requires in-person voters to present certain identification and mail ballot voters to provide certain information | Keeps the constitution in its current form |

The table follows the yes and no statements in the Clark County guide. The guide's wording for Question 6 begins: "Should the Nevada Constitution be amended to create an individual's fundamental right to an abortion, without interference by state or local governments…" Its no statement says a "No" vote "would not impact the availability of abortion as a statutory right under Nevada law."

## Question 7: what voters will show, and when it starts

The guide's question asks whether the constitution should require voters to present photo identification when voting in person, or to provide certain personal information when voting by mail ballot. For mail voters, that information would be the last four digits of their Nevada driver's license number, or of their Social Security number if they have no license.

The guide notes: "Under current law, Nevada voters must only show identification in certain situations." If Question 7 is approved, the Legislature could pass related laws in its 2027 session, and the changes would take effect for the 2028 election cycle.

## Why Nevada votes on these questions twice

A constitutional initiative in Nevada has to pass in two general elections. Voters approved Question 6 and Question 7 in 2024, which was the first vote. With the second yes vote on Nov. 3, 2026, each becomes part of the Nevada Constitution, per the newsroom reports above. Fox5 Vegas (KVVU) reported on Sept. 29, 2026 that Question 6 had to be approved a second time this year.

The guide's no statements for both questions said a no vote would keep the constitution in its current form. Because the 2024 result was only the first step, the 2026 ballot was where each question was decided.

## Nevada voting dates and deadlines for Nov. 3, 2026

| Date | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sat., Oct. 17 to Fri., Oct. 30 | Early voting in Clark County (ended) | Clark County Notice of Early Voting, Sept. 24, 2026 |
| Tue., Oct. 20 | Online registrants after this date did not get a mail ballot | Clark County Notice of Close of Registration |
| Tue., Nov. 3, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Pacific Time (PT) | Polls were open; 10 p.m. Eastern when they closed | Nevada Revised Statutes (NRS) 293.273 |
| Tue., Nov. 3 | Same-day registration in person at vote centers until polls closed; online registration ran through this day | Clark County Notice of Close of Registration |
| Tue., Nov. 3 | Mail ballots had to be postmarked on or before Election Day | NRS 293.269921 |
| Sat., Nov. 7, 5 p.m. | Mail ballots had to be received by 5 p.m. on the fourth day after Election Day | NRS 293.269921 |

The Nov. 7 date is computed from the statute: counting four days after Tuesday, Nov. 3 gives Saturday, Nov. 7. The early voting dates are for Clark County. For other states' rules, see our [mail ballot deadlines by state](/news/mail-ballot-deadlines-2026-by-state/) and [when polls close on Election Day](/news/what-time-do-polls-close-election-day-2026/).

## What else is on the Nevada ballot

The Clark County list also included statewide races for governor, lieutenant governor, secretary of state, state treasurer, state controller and attorney general, along with U.S. House seats. No U.S. Senate race appeared on the Clark County list.
