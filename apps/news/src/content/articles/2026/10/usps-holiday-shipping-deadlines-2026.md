## USPS holiday shipping deadlines 2026

| Service | Lower 48 states | Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, territories |
|---|---|---|
| USPS Ground Advantage | Thursday, Dec. 17 | Check your ZIP Codes (10 days or more) |
| First-Class Mail (cards, letters) | Thursday, Dec. 17 | Thursday, Dec. 17 |
| Priority Mail | Friday, Dec. 18 | Friday, Dec. 18 |
| Priority Mail Express | Saturday, Dec. 19 | Saturday, Dec. 19 |

USPS says Ground Advantage packages between the lower 48 and Alaska, Hawaii, American Samoa, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands and the Freely Associated States have a service expectation of 10 days or more. Use the [USPS Service Commitments tool](https://postcalc.usps.com/ServiceCommitments) for your origin and destination ZIP Codes instead of the lower-48 date.

If you use Ground Advantage or First-Class Mail, aim to mail before Dec. 17 rather than on it. A few days of cushion helps with weather and heavy volume, the two most common reasons holiday mail runs late.

## International mail: Dec. 9 and Dec. 16

USPS lists the same dates for every region it names: Africa, Asia and the Pacific Rim, Australia and New Zealand, Canada, the Caribbean, Central and South America, Europe, Mexico and the Middle East.

| Service | Recommended send-by date |
|---|---|
| First-Class Package International | Wednesday, Dec. 9 |
| Priority Mail International | Wednesday, Dec. 9 |
| Priority Mail Express International | Wednesday, Dec. 16 |

Customs checks and destination rules can slow international parcels, so treat these as the latest dates, not the target.

## Military and diplomatic addresses

Mail to an Army Post Office (APO), Fleet Post Office (FPO) or Diplomatic Post Office (DPO) address has its own dates, and they are earlier. USPS lists them by ZIP Code group. For the AE groups it shows (090 to 099, 340 and 962 to 966):

| Service | Recommended send-by date |
|---|---|
| USPS Ground Advantage | Wednesday, Nov. 4 |
| First-Class Mail | Wednesday, Dec. 9 |
| Priority Mail | Wednesday, Dec. 9 |
| Priority Mail Express Military Service | Wednesday, Dec. 16 |

Priority Mail Express Military Service is not offered for ZIP Codes 093 and is available only at selected military and diplomatic Post Offices, so confirm with your local Post Office. The full table, including other ZIP groups, is on the USPS holiday page.

## Post office closures

Post offices close on federal holidays. The ones that matter for holiday shipping:

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 11 | Wednesday | Veterans Day |
| Nov. 26 | Thursday | Thanksgiving Day |
| Dec. 25 | Friday | Christmas Day |
| Jan. 1, 2027 | Friday | New Year's Day |

Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, is not a federal holiday, but retail hours vary by branch. Our [Veterans Day story](/news/veterans-day-2026-wednesday-november-11/) lists what else is closed on Nov. 11.
