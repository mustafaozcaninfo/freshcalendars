## When does Black Doves Season 2 come out?

Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, per Netflix Tudum's What to Watch guide of Oct. 30. The same guide lists The Neighborhood Seasons 1-8 and North of North Season 2 for that day. Netflix does not state an episode count for Black Doves Season 2 on the page, so none is given here.

## Is The Diplomat Season 4 a November show?

No. Netflix Tudum said in August that "The Diplomat Season 4 arrives on Oct. 15." On Oct. 20, Tudum wrote: "You can watch all 8 episodes of Season 4 now." The season has been out since Thursday, Oct. 15, 2026. The Netflix pages checked do not announce a Season 5.

## When does The Hunting Wives Season 2 come out?

Netflix Tudum says the season "debuts on Netflix this Nov. 26." That is Thanksgiving Day in the United States; the [Thanksgiving 2026 date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has the holiday itself. Netflix does not state an episode count on that page.

## What are the other Netflix series dates after Nov. 5?

Netflix's Oct. 27 Tudum story on the main trailer gives Tuesday, Nov. 10 for the premiere of The Devil's Plan: Dead Again worldwide. Netflix's title is "Dead Again," so it is not called Season 3 here. The Netflix Newsroom announced on Sept. 23 that "The Empress Season 3, premiering November 12, exclusively on Netflix worldwide." It is the third and final season. For The Altruists, Tudum says: "All eight episodes of the series will stream on Netflix starting Nov. 19."

## What time do the new Netflix series start?

The Netflix pages checked on Oct. 31 give no release time for any title in the table. Check the title's page on the day.
