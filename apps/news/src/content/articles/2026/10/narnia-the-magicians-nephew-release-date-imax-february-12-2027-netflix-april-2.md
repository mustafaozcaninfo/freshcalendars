**Netflix says Greta Gerwig's Narnia: The Magician's Nephew will be released in theaters on Friday, Feb. 12, 2027, and on Netflix on Friday, April 2, 2027, according to a Netflix Tudum article dated Oct. 15, 2026.** Sneak previews only in IMAX begin Wednesday, Feb. 10, 2027. Below are the dates side by side, what Netflix has not announced and a free one-page planner.

## When is the Narnia release date?

Tudum's article says: "Narnia: The Magician's Nephew will be released in theaters Feb. 12, 2027, and on Netflix April 2, 2027. Sneak previews only in IMAX will begin on Feb. 10, 2027." It also calls the release "a truly global cinematic event in IMAX in theaters on Feb. 12, 2027, and on Netflix on April 2, 2027."

| Milestone | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| First trailer | Tue., Oct. 13, 2026 | Reported by NarniaWeb and TV Insider |
| IMAX-only sneak previews | Wed., Feb. 10, 2027 | Netflix Tudum |
| In theaters | Fri., Feb. 12, 2027 | Netflix Tudum |
| Netflix | Fri., April 2, 2027 | Netflix Tudum |

From Fri., Feb. 12 to Fri., April 2, 2027 is 49 days, or seven weeks. From Fri., Oct. 30, 2026 to the theatrical release is 105 days.

## Is opening weekend a three-day weekend?

Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027 is Valentine's Day, and Monday, Feb. 15, 2027 is Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day), a federal holiday on the third Monday of February. That makes the opening weekend a three-day weekend for many U.S. schools and offices. Whether yours is closed is up to your school or employer, so check its calendar. See [when Presidents' Day falls](/when-is/presidents-day/) or mark the dates on a [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html).

## Who is in the Narnia cast and what is the book?

Netflix Tudum lists these cast members, without naming roles in the article: David McKenna, Beatrice Campbell, Emma Mackey, Carey Mulligan, Ciarán Hinds, Daniel Craig, Meryl Streep, Kobna Holdbrook-Smith, Denise Gough and Susan Wokoma. Tudum says Gerwig unveiled the first trailer at a sneak peek event in New York City.

The film is based on The Magician's Nephew by C.S. Lewis, first published in 1955, according to Wikipedia. It was the sixth Narnia book published and is the first in story order.

## Did the Narnia release plan change?

NarniaWeb reported in May 2026 that the film had earlier been planned for a two-week IMAX run at Thanksgiving 2026 before moving to a full theatrical release in 2027. That is NarniaWeb's report; the Tudum article gives only the current dates.
