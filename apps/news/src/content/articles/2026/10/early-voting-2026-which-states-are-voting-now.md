**Early in-person voting is open now in many states ahead of Election Day on Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026.** Connecticut, Texas, North Carolina, Massachusetts, Tennessee and New Mexico are already voting, and Maryland and Delaware start today, Thursday, Oct. 22. This is a guide to selected states with confirmed dates, not a complete list. The Center for Election Innovation & Research (CEIR) says 47 states plus the District of Columbia offer early in-person voting in 2026, and most start in mid-October.

## Early voting dates by state

| State | Dates | Hours and notes |
|---|---|---|
| Connecticut | Mon., Oct. 19-Sun., Nov. 1, every day | 10 a.m.-6 p.m.; 8 a.m.-8 p.m. on Tue., Oct. 27 and Thu., Oct. 29 |
| Texas | Mon., Oct. 19-Fri., Oct. 30 | Hours and locations set by county; no excuse needed |
| North Carolina | Thu., Oct. 15-Sat., Oct. 31 | Hours vary by county; sites open at least during county board office business hours on weekdays; ends 3 p.m. on Oct. 31 |
| Massachusetts | Sat., Oct. 17-Fri., Oct. 30 | Two weekends included; cities and towns set schedules, and some limit Friday hours |
| Tennessee | Wed., Oct. 14-Thu., Oct. 29 | Confirm your county schedule |
| Maryland | Thu., Oct. 22-Thu., Oct. 29 | 7 a.m.-8 p.m. daily, weekends included; vote at an early voting center in your county of residence |
| Delaware | Oct. 22-24 and Oct. 26-27, then Oct. 28-Nov. 1 | 7 a.m.-7 p.m. on the first five voting days, 11 a.m.-7 p.m. on the last five; no early voting on Sun., Oct. 25 |
| New Mexico | Tue., Oct. 6-Sat., Oct. 31 | Starts at county clerk offices; expanded alternate sites open Sat., Oct. 17 |

Hours are local time. For Massachusetts and Tennessee, the official pages were not reachable by automated tools when we checked. The Massachusetts dates are from the Secretary of the Commonwealth as reported, so confirm with your town clerk. The Tennessee dates are from [a local news report from Oct. 14](https://www.wsmv.com/2026/10/14/election-2026-early-voting-tennessees-november-3-general-election-is-underway/); confirm your county schedule on the [Tennessee Secretary of State's elections page](https://sos.tn.gov/elections).

## When early voting ends

End dates are close together. Tennessee finishes first among these states on Thursday, Oct. 29, followed by Texas and Massachusetts on Friday, Oct. 30. North Carolina and New Mexico end Saturday, Oct. 31, and Connecticut ends Sunday, Nov. 1. CEIR lists end dates from Oct. 27 in Louisiana and Pennsylvania to the day before Election Day in 14 states. If you live elsewhere, start with your state's election office.

Michigan and New York open Saturday, Oct. 24, and have their own guide: [early voting starts Oct. 24 in Michigan and New York](/news/early-voting-starts-october-24-michigan-new-york-2026/). Texas voters can find county-level detail in the [Texas election calendar](/news/texas-election-calendar-2026-early-voting-dates-deadlines/).

## When is Election Day 2026?

Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026. Early voting is separate from voting by mail. If you plan to return a mail ballot, see the [mail ballot deadlines by state](/news/mail-ballot-deadlines-2026-by-state/). Wondering whether you get the day off? ## How to find your early-voting location

1. **Start with your official state election website.** Use the voter lookup or polling place finder run by your state, such as Connecticut's Secretary of the State portal, VoteTexas.gov or the North Carolina State Board of Elections.
2. **Confirm the county or municipality location and hours.** Many states let counties or towns set schedules, so the site near you may differ from one across the county line.
3. **Check identification and eligibility rules.** Requirements differ by state. Look up what to bring and whether you are registered at your current address.
4. **Recheck before you leave.** Hours can change, and some sites shorten days. Confirm the schedule the same day.
