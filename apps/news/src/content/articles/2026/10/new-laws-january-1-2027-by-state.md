**Laws in six states are set to take effect on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, according to the official pages of each state's legislature and, for Illinois, the governor's July 10, 2026 announcement.** This story lists 21 of them by state, plus seven state minimum wage rates and the federal Social Security cost-of-living adjustment. It covers only laws confirmed on official pages, so it is not a full count of every law with that date. ## What do the Illinois laws cover?

Gov. JB Pritzker's office listed the Illinois bills in its July 10, 2026 announcement, "Gov. Pritzker Takes Bill Action." Twelve of the bills on that list carry the date Jan. 1, 2027. They touch schools (HB 4535, HB 4577, SB 2909), health care and long-term care (HB 5393, SB 3341, SB 3967), courts and victims (HB 4659, SB 0118), guardianship (HB 3811), state finance (SB 2968) and oil and gas wells (SB 3113). HB 4757 is a technical change to the Health Facilities Planning Act.

## What do the Florida laws cover?

The Florida Senate's bill pages list five 2026 laws with a Jan. 1, 2027 date. HB 991, approved April 1, 2026, states that "this act shall take effect January 1, 2027," except for sections that took effect on becoming law. HB 505, on virtual currency kiosks, takes effect Jan. 1, 2027 except as otherwise provided in the act. The other three, SB 1134, HB 1329 and HB 967, deal with local governments: their official actions, their finances and electronic payments made to them.

## Which laws in Washington, Colorado, New York and Louisiana start in 2027?

Washington's legislature shows an effective date of 1/1/2027 for HB 2225 and SB 6228. Colorado's HB26-1139, signed June 2, 2026, says that on and after Jan. 1, 2027, the rule applies "when determining coverage for health-care services." New York's S8828, signed March 27, 2026, states that the act takes effect on Jan. 1, 2027. Louisiana's SB 275, signed May 15, 2026, has an effective date of 1/1/2027.

## What changes at the federal level in January 2027?

The Social Security cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) for 2027 is 3.6%, and benefits rise beginning in January. The Social Security Administration site blocks automated access, so this figure rests on two newsrooms that agree: Fox Business and ABC News, both reporting on Oct. 14, 2026. Payment dates are in our [Social Security COLA 2027 story](/news/social-security-cola-2027-3-6-percent-payment-dates/). For the federal calendar, see [federal holidays in 2027](/news/federal-holidays-2027-observed-dates-list/).
