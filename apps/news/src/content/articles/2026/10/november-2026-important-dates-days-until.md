**November 2026 opens with the clock change on Sunday, Nov. 1 and closes with Cyber Monday on Nov. 30.** In between come Election Day on Nov. 3, Diwali on Nov. 8, Veterans Day on Nov. 11, the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI on Nov. 19 and Thanksgiving on Thursday, Nov. 26. The countdowns below are counted from Monday, Oct. 19, 2026, the day this story was published.

## November 2026 calendar at a glance

November 2026 starts on a Sunday and ends on a Monday, so it fills exactly five weeks on a Sunday-start calendar. Federal holidays are in bold.

The two federal holidays are Veterans Day on Wednesday, Nov. 11 and Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, Nov. 26, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management. ## Which November 2026 dates are federal holidays?

Only two of the month's best-known dates give federal employees a day off. The U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists Veterans Day and Thanksgiving Day for 2026; the rest are election, observance or shopping dates.

| Date | Event | Federal holiday? |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday, Nov. 3 | Election Day | No |
| Wednesday, Nov. 11 | Veterans Day | Yes |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving Day | Yes |
| Friday, Nov. 27 | Black Friday | No, a retail shopping day |
| Monday, Nov. 30 | Cyber Monday | No, an online shopping event |

A federal holiday does not close every business or school. Banks, stores, transit systems and districts set their own schedules, so check the organization directly. Black Friday and Cyber Monday hours and deals are set by each retailer; our [Black Friday 2026 store hours](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/) list shows what chains have announced.

## The first week: clocks, ballots and Diwali

**Nov. 1, clocks fall back.** At 2 a.m. on Sunday, Nov. 1, most of the U.S. returns to standard time. Sunsets jump about an hour earlier, to 4:44 p.m. in New York by Nov. 8. Our [daylight saving time report](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) covers the change and the stalled bill to make daylight time permanent.

**Nov. 3, Election Day.** Polls open across the country for federal, state and local races. Texas early voting runs through Friday, Oct. 30. Our guide to finding your [sample ballot by address](/news/sample-ballot-by-zip-code-2026-texas-ballot/) explains why a ZIP code alone can show the wrong races.

**Nov. 5.** Searches for this date are rising, but no single U.S. event explains them. It is Guy Fawkes Night in Britain, and several video games are scheduled for release that day.

**Nov. 6 to 10, Diwali.** The five days run from Dhanteras on Friday to Bhai Dooj on Tuesday, with Lakshmi Puja on Sunday evening, Nov. 8. Our [Diwali 2026 guide](/news/diwali-2026-date-lakshmi-puja-time-usa/) lists evening puja times for 12 U.S. cities.

## Mid-month: Veterans Day and a big game launch

Veterans Day falls on Wednesday, Nov. 11. Federal offices, banks and post offices close, while most stores stay open. Our [Veterans Day story](/news/veterans-day-2026-wednesday-november-11/) lists what is open.

Rockstar Games says Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on Thursday, Nov. 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after two delays. Players who pre-order a digital copy can start pre-loading on Nov. 12, the company says.

## Thanksgiving week

The full Beaver Moon peaks on Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 9:53 a.m. Eastern, according to the U.S. Naval Observatory, so the evenings of Nov. 23 and 24 are best for viewing. Wednesday, Nov. 25 is the day before Thanksgiving and one of the heaviest travel days of the year.

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26. It is always the fourth Thursday of November, which can fall anywhere from Nov. 22 to Nov. 28; our [Thanksgiving explainer](/news/why-thanksgiving-2026-is-november-26/) explains the rule. Black Friday follows on Nov. 27, and Cyber Monday on Nov. 30. Advent begins on Sunday, Nov. 29, four Sundays before Christmas. A frozen 16 to 20 lb turkey needs to go in the fridge on Nov. 21 or 22; our [turkey thawing timeline](/news/when-to-thaw-thanksgiving-turkey-2026/) covers other weights.
