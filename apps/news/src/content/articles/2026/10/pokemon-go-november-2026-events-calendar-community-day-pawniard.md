## What is Pawniard Community Day on Nov. 21, 2026?

Pokémon GO says the event runs Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. Bonuses and the spawn window run 2 to 9 p.m. local time, and the evolution requirement returns to normal at 9 p.m. that day. A shiny Pokémon and a special background are possible, not guaranteed.

The page, updated Oct. 28, 2026, says an Ultra Ticket Box ($1.99) is "available soon". The Special Research ticket is marked "stay tuned", so its price is not yet announced.

The same site has a save-the-date for the next season: the Community Day above and a Super Mega Raid Day on Sat., Nov. 28, 2026, with no times or featured Pokémon yet.

## What does Halloween 2026 Part II include?

Part II runs from Sunday, Nov. 1, at 10 a.m. to Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, at 8 p.m. local time. The official page names Mega Sableye and the debut of Shiny Greavard. Part I ran from Tuesday, Oct. 27, at 10 a.m. to Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, at 10 a.m.

GO Pass: Halloween 2026 is available from Tue., Oct. 27 at 10 a.m. to Thu., Nov. 5 at 8 p.m. local time. Its rewards expire Saturday, Nov. 7, at 8 p.m. local time.

Trainers in Mexico get a separate Día de Muertos event from Tue., Oct. 27, 10 a.m. to Thu., Nov. 5, 8 p.m. local time. The official Spanish page also lists Paseo de la Reforma in Mexico City from Sat., Oct. 31, 10 a.m. to Thu., Nov. 5, 8 p.m., and Mexicráneos from Oct. 17 to Nov. 16, 2026.

## When is the Chandelure Super Mega Raid Day?

Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. Mega Chandelure debuts in Super Mega Raids, and a shiny is possible. Tyranitar comes through Timed Research. The ticket is sold until Saturday, Oct. 31, at 5 p.m. local time.

The Global Challenge and Remote Raid bonus runs "Friday, October 30, at 5:00 p.m. PDT to Saturday, October 31, 2026, at 8:00 p.m. PDT." In Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) that is 8 p.m. Friday to 11 p.m. Saturday.

## When is Wild Area: Global 2026?

The save-the-date page says citywide events are in Sendai, Japan, and Mexico City "from November 6 to November 8, 2026," and Wild Area: Global is "on November 14 and 15, 2026."

For Europe, the official page lists in-person events in Karlsruhe on Saturday, Nov. 14, from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. local time, and in Stockholm on Sunday, Nov. 15, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. local time. Tickets and details for the Global event are "available next Season", so they are not yet announced.

## What else runs in mid-November?

Minior Leonids runs from Nov. 14 at 5 p.m. to Nov. 19 at 11:59 p.m. local time, and the page puts the sky effect at 5 to 9 p.m. local time every day. For the real meteor shower, see our [November 2026 meteor showers guide](/news/november-2026-meteor-showers-taurids-leonids/).

Dynamax Trails, featuring Eternamax Eternatus, has early access on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 10 a.m. local time. The event runs Monday, Nov. 16, at 10 a.m. to Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at 8 p.m. local time, and its GO Pass rewards expire Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 8 p.m. local time.

## What does GO Pass: November cover?

GO Pass: November runs "From Tuesday, November 3, at 10:00 a.m. to Tuesday, December 1, at 10:00 a.m. local time." A window with no daily cap on GO Points runs Saturday, Nov. 28, at 12:00 a.m. to Sunday, Nov. 29, at 11:59 p.m. local time. Rewards expire Thursday, Dec. 3, at 10 a.m. local time.

## When is GO Tour 2027?

The official save-the-date puts Kaohsiung, Taiwan and Los Angeles from Friday, Feb. 19, to Sunday, Feb. 21, 2027. The Global event is Saturday, Feb. 27, and Sunday, Feb. 28, 2027. Tickets are "available next Season". GO Fest 2027 dates are not yet announced on the official site.
