## What is Pawniard Community Day on Nov. 21, 2026?

Pokémon GO says the event runs Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. A limited-time evolution lets Bisharp turn into Kingambit, which knows the Charged Attack Kowtow Cleave, after you defeat 1 Dark- or Steel-type Raid Boss with Bisharp as your buddy. It applies from 2 to 9 p.m. local time that day, and the page says it covers all of your Bisharp whether or not they are your buddy. Eternamax Eternatus is at most Power Spots from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. local time the same day, so that window ends as Community Day starts. A shiny Pokémon and a special background are possible, not guaranteed.

The official page lists the Community Day Special Research at US$1.99 (or the local equivalent) with 3 Special Background Pawniard encounters, 1 Premium Battle Pass and 1 Rare Candy XL, and says to "stay tuned" for when tickets go live. The Pawniard Community Day Ultra Ticket Box ($1.99, with five Ultra Balls) is listed without a release date.

The next event after it is the Clefable Super Mega Raid Day on Sat., Nov. 28, 2026, described below.

## What does Halloween 2026 Part II include?

Part II ran from Sunday, Nov. 1, at 10 a.m. to Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, at 8 p.m. local time. The official page names Mega Sableye and the debut of Shiny Greavard. Part I ran from Tuesday, Oct. 27, at 10 a.m. to Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, at 10 a.m.

GO Pass: Halloween 2026 ran from Tue., Oct. 27 at 10 a.m. to Thu., Nov. 5 at 8 p.m. local time. Its rewards expired Saturday, Nov. 7, at 8 p.m. local time.

Trainers in Mexico had a separate Día de Muertos event from Tue., Oct. 27, 10 a.m. to Thu., Nov. 5, 8 p.m. local time. The official Spanish page also lists Paseo de la Reforma in Mexico City from Sat., Oct. 31, 10 a.m. to Thu., Nov. 5, 8 p.m., and Mexicráneos from Oct. 17 to Nov. 16, 2026.

## When is the Chandelure Super Mega Raid Day?

It took place Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. Mega Chandelure debuted in Super Mega Raids, with a shiny possible, and Tyranitar came through Timed Research.

The Global Challenge and Remote Raid bonus ran "Friday, October 30, at 5:00 p.m. PDT to Saturday, October 31, 2026, at 8:00 p.m. PDT." In Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) that is 8 p.m. Friday to 11 p.m. Saturday.

## When is Wild Area: Global 2026?

The save-the-date page said citywide events were in Sendai, Japan, and Mexico City "from November 6 to November 8, 2026," and Wild Area: Global is "on November 14 and 15, 2026." A separate official notice says gameplay features around the Namba area of Osaka may be temporarily adjusted on Nov. 14 and 15 because another mobile game is holding an in-person event there.

For Europe, the official page lists in-person events in Karlsruhe on Saturday, Nov. 14, from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. local time, and in Stockholm on Sunday, Nov. 15, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. local time. Wild Area: Global Special Research begins Saturday, Nov. 14, at 10 a.m. local time, and ticket add-ons were active from Friday, Nov. 6 through Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026.

## What are the Into the Wild events from Nov. 9 to 15, 2026?

Into the Wild ran Monday, Nov. 9, at 10 a.m. to Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, at 8 p.m. local time. Raid Hours were 6 to 7 p.m. local time: Crowned Sword Zacian on Monday, Nov. 9, and Crowned Shield Zamazenta on Tuesday, Nov. 10. Hour of Max Battles was 6 to 7 p.m. local time: Registeel on Wednesday, Nov. 11, Regidrago on Thursday, Nov. 12, and Latias and Latios on Friday, Nov. 13.

The Remote Raid daily limit was 30 from Nov. 9 to 13, with no limit on Saturday, Nov. 14, and Sunday, Nov. 15. Daily Discoveries does not take place from Nov. 9 through Nov. 15, 2026. GO Pass: Into the Wild runs from Monday, Nov. 9, at 10 a.m. to Sunday, Nov. 15, at 6 p.m. local time, and rewards can be claimed until Tuesday, Nov. 17, at 11:59 p.m. local time.

## What else runs in mid-November?

Minior Leonids runs from Nov. 14 at 5 p.m. to Nov. 19 at 11:59 p.m. local time, and the page puts the sky effect at 5 to 9 p.m. local time every day. For the real meteor shower, see our [November 2026 meteor showers guide](/news/november-2026-meteor-showers-taurids-leonids/).

Dynamax Trails, featuring Eternamax Eternatus, has early access on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 10 a.m. local time for Trainers who claim Rank 1 rewards on GO Pass: Into the Wild. The event runs Monday, Nov. 16, at 10 a.m. to Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at 8 p.m. local time, and its GO Pass rewards expire Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 8 p.m. local time.

Spotlight Hour is Thursday, Nov. 19, from 6 to 7 p.m. local time, with Grookey, Scorbunny and Sobble (shiny possible) and bonus 2x Catch Candy. Eternamax Eternatus takes over most Power Spots on Saturday, Nov. 21, from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. local time. The Remote Raid daily limit rises from 10 to 30 from Nov. 20 at 1 p.m. to Nov. 21 at 6 p.m. Pacific Standard Time (PST).

## What is Twilight Wishes?

Twilight Wishes runs Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 10 a.m. to Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, at 8 p.m. local time, with an increased chance of Shiny Staryu and Shiny Elgyem.

## When is the Clefable Super Mega Raid Day?

Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. Mega Clefable debuts in Super Mega Raids, a shiny Clefable is possible, and it can reach Super Max Level for the first time. Timed Research gives a Premium Battle Pass and a Metagross encounter. The US$4.99 event ticket is sold in the in-game shop until Saturday, Nov. 28, at 5 p.m. local time.

The Global Challenge runs from Friday, Nov. 27, at 5 p.m. Pacific Standard Time (PST) to Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, at 8 p.m. PST. In Eastern Standard Time (EST) that is 8 p.m. Friday to 11 p.m. Saturday. The Remote Raid limit rises to 20 for the same window.

## What is announced for December 2026?

The official save-the-date for the next season lists Max Battle Day on Saturday, Dec. 5, December Community Day on Saturday and Sunday, Dec. 12 and 13, Raid Day on Saturday, Dec. 19 and Hatch Day on Saturday, Dec. 26, 2026. The Pokémon and times for those days are not yet announced. Minior Geminids runs Dec. 11 at 5 p.m. to Dec. 16 at 11:59 p.m. local time.

## What does GO Pass: November cover?

GO Pass: November runs "From Tuesday, November 3, at 10:00 a.m. to Tuesday, December 1, at 10:00 a.m. local time." A window with no daily cap on GO Points runs Saturday, Nov. 28, at 12:00 a.m. to Sunday, Nov. 29, at 11:59 p.m. local time. Rewards expire Thursday, Dec. 3, at 10 a.m. local time.

## When is GO Tour 2027?

The official save-the-date puts Kaohsiung, Taiwan and Los Angeles from Friday, Feb. 19, to Sunday, Feb. 21, 2027. The Global event is Saturday, Feb. 27, and Sunday, Feb. 28, 2027. Tickets are "available next Season". GO Fest 2027 dates are not yet announced on the official site.
