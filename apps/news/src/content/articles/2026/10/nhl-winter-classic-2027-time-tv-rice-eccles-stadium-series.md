**The 2027 Winter Classic is Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, at 6 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), with the Utah Mammoth hosting the Colorado Avalanche at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City, according to the National Hockey League (NHL).** The NHL event page lists Turner Network Television (TNT), truTV and Home Box Office (HBO) Max in the United States. Below: the faceoff in every U.S. zone, the 2026-27 outdoor and international games and last year's result.

## What is the date and time of the 2027 Winter Classic?

The game carries the 2027 name but is played on New Year's Eve 2026, a Thursday. NHL Commissioner Gary Bettman announced that "the date of the game will be Dec. 31, 2026," NHL.com reported on April 25, 2026. He described the time as "late afternoon, early evening, prime time in the East." The NHL event page lists a 6 p.m. ET start, and the official name is the 2027 Discover NHL Winter Classic.

The Mammoth are the home team. The game is on the University of Utah campus. For the holiday itself, see [when New Year's Eve falls](/when-is/new-years-eve/) and plan the day on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## What channel is the Winter Classic 2027 on?

The NHL event page lists TNT, truTV and HBO Max in the United States, and Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada.

## Where do tickets stand for the Winter Classic?

NHL.com said tickets went on sale Tuesday, June 16, 2026, at 10 a.m. MT. The event page now reads "Individual Tickets Are Now on Sale!" We did not find prices in the sources we read.

## When is the 2027 NHL Stadium Series?

The 2027 Navy Federal Credit Union NHL Stadium Series is Saturday, Feb. 20, 2027, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, home of the National Football League (NFL) Dallas Cowboys. The Dallas Stars play the Vegas Golden Knights. In the NHL's Jan. 29, 2026 release, the game "will begin at 7 p.m. CT and be broadcast on American Broadcasting Company (ABC)." Ticket information "will be announced when available."

## Which NHL outdoor and international games are in 2026-27?

The NHL's 2026-27 regular-season schedule release, published July 16, 2026, lists these games. It said complete broadcast details will be announced when available, so we give no Global Series times or channels.

| Date | Game | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026 | Heritage Classic (played) | Winnipeg |
| Nov. 12 and 14, 2026 | Global Series: Carolina Hurricanes vs. Seattle Kraken | Helsinki, Finland |
| Dec. 18 and 20, 2026 | Global Series: Ottawa Senators vs. Chicago Blackhawks | Düsseldorf, Germany |

The Heritage Classic has already happened. The NHL lists it at Princess Auto Stadium in Winnipeg on Oct. 25, 2026, where the Montreal Canadiens beat the Winnipeg Jets 2-1.

## Who won the last Winter Classic?

The New York Rangers beat the Florida Panthers 5-1 on Friday, Jan. 2, 2026, at loanDepot park in Miami, before a sellout crowd of 36,153, NHL.com reported.
