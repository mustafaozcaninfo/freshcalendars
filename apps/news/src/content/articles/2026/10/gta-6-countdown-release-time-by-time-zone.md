## What time does GTA 6 come out?

PlayStation says midnight local time on PS5, so the moment moves with the time zone. The PlayStation Store lists a release time in UTC on each of the five product pages we read, and the times differ by country, which matches that sentence. Rockstar's own regional release page could not be read, so these are the store listings, converted.

| PlayStation Store | Listing shows | Local time | In Eastern Time | In Pacific Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | Nov. 19, 05:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. ET, Thu., Nov. 19 | 12:00 a.m., Thu., Nov. 19 | 9 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Canada | Nov. 19, 05:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. ET, Thu., Nov. 19 | 12:00 a.m., Thu., Nov. 19 | 9 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| UK | Nov. 19, 00:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | 7 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 | 4 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Germany | Nov. 18, 23:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. Central European Time (CET), Thu., Nov. 19 | 6 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 | 3 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| India | Nov. 18, 18:30 UTC | 12:00 a.m. India Standard Time (IST), Thu., Nov. 19 | 1:30 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 | 10:30 a.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |

Nov. 19 falls after U.S. clocks go back on Nov. 1, so Eastern Time is standard time (UTC minus 5 hours) on launch day. The Canadian listing carries the same timestamp as the US one.

### GTA 6 release time in the US time zones

The US listing's 05:00 UTC converts like this on Wednesday, Nov. 18 and Thursday, Nov. 19:

| US time zone | Local time |
|---|---|
| Eastern Time (ET) | 12:00 a.m., Thu., Nov. 19 |
| Central Time (CT) | 11 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 10 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |

If the US store's moment applies to you, the West Coast gets the game on Wednesday evening, not at midnight. The listing is for the storefront, so the one to read is the one that matches your console account's store region. Whether changing a console's region unlocks the game early is not confirmed by Rockstar, Sony or Microsoft; our [New Zealand release time story](/news/gta-6-new-zealand-release-time-nz-trick/) covers it.

### What we could not confirm

- **Xbox consoles.** The Xbox store page lists a release date of 11/19/2026 and no time or time zone. Microsoft gives 1 a.m. ET for cloud streaming only, so an official release hour for Xbox consoles is not announced.
- **A single worldwide moment.** None is announced; PlayStation's wording is midnight local time.
- **Australia and Japan store listings.** We could not load them, so the midnight times for those places come from PlayStation's sentence and our conversion.
- **Other countries.** We checked five stores. Mexico, Brazil and the rest of the list are not covered by a listing.
