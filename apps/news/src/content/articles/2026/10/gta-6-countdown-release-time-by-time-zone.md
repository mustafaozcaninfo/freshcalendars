## What time does GTA 6 come out?

The PlayStation Store lists a release time in UTC on every product page we could load, and the times differ by country. That points to a local-midnight release in each store, not one worldwide moment. Rockstar Games has not published a separate release schedule, so these are the store listings, converted.

| PlayStation Store | Listing shows | Local time | In Eastern Time | In Pacific Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | Nov. 19, 05:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. ET, Thu., Nov. 19 | 12:00 a.m., Thu., Nov. 19 | 9 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Canada | Nov. 19, 05:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. ET, Thu., Nov. 19 | 12:00 a.m., Thu., Nov. 19 | 9 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| UK | Nov. 19, 00:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | 7 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 | 4 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Germany | Nov. 18, 23:00 UTC | 12:00 a.m. Central European Time (CET), Thu., Nov. 19 | 6 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 | 3 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| India | Nov. 18, 18:30 UTC | 12:00 a.m. India Standard Time (IST), Thu., Nov. 19 | 1:30 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 | 10:30 a.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |

Nov. 19 falls after U.S. clocks go back on Nov. 1, so Eastern Time is standard time (UTC minus 5 hours) on launch day. The Canadian listing carries the same timestamp as the US one.

### GTA 6 release time in the US time zones

The US listing's 05:00 UTC converts like this on Wednesday, Nov. 18 and Thursday, Nov. 19:

| US time zone | Local time |
|---|---|
| Eastern Time (ET) | 12:00 a.m., Thu., Nov. 19 |
| Central Time (CT) | 11 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 10 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 9 p.m., Wed., Nov. 18 |

If the US store's moment applies to you, the West Coast gets the game on Wednesday evening, not at midnight. The listing is for the storefront, so the one to read is the one that matches your console account's store region. Neither Sony nor Rockstar has said what happens when that region differs from where you live.

### What we could not confirm

- **Xbox.** The Xbox store page lists a release date of 11/19/2026 and no time or time zone.
- **Australia and Japan.** We could not load those PlayStation Store listings, so there is no time for them here.
- **Other countries.** We checked five stores. Mexico, Brazil and the rest of the list are not in this story.
- **Pre-load time.** Take-Two Interactive says digital pre-orders can pre-load from Nov. 12, and the PlayStation listing shows a "7-Day Pre-load." Neither gives an hour.
