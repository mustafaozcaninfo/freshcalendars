## How the game works

Sort the 16 entries into four groups of four. You can make four mistakes. The groups run from yellow to green, blue and purple, with purple the group that depends most on wordplay.

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Ways of handling something without taking it seriously.
- Hint 2: Four verbs that often come before "with," as in playing around with a thing you are not meant to touch.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Pieces of one part of the body, the part you hear with.
- Hint 2: One is a tube-like passage, one shares its name with an instrument, and two share names with objects from a workshop or a saddle.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Each word can come right before the word bell.
- Hint 2: Think of a holiday song, a restaurant chain, a symbol of freedom and gear used underwater.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Each word can come right after the word bell.
- Hint 2: Flared trousers, a spicy or sweet vegetable, a tall structure that holds a bell, and a glass container with a lid.

## Solving tips

Start with green, because the ear theme is clear and it holds the two words most likely to mislead you. Treat the two bell groups as one problem: decide for each word whether bell goes before it or after it. Leave yellow for last, since its four verbs are close in meaning and easy to mix up.
