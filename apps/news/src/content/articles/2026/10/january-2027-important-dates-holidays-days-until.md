**January 2027 begins on Friday, Jan. 1 and has two federal holidays, New Year's Day and the Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. on Monday, Jan. 18, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** The month also holds the Quadrantid meteor shower on Jan. 3-4, the Golden Globes on Jan. 10, the first three National Football League (NFL) playoff rounds and a fourth-quarter estimated tax date on Jan. 15. The countdowns below are counted from Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026.

## Federal holidays in January 2027

OPM lists two federal holidays for January 2027. New Year's Day is Friday, Jan. 1, and the Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. is Monday, Jan. 18, the third Monday of the month. What is closed on the second one, from federal offices to the New York Stock Exchange, is in our story on [what is closed on Martin Luther King Jr. Day 2027](/news/martin-luther-king-jr-day-2027-january-18-whats-closed/).

## The sky: Quadrantids, perihelion and moon phases

The American Meteor Society (AMS) lists the Quadrantid peak night as Jan. 3-4 and predicts the peak near 10:30 p.m. EST on Sunday, Jan. 3. Our [Quadrantid meteor shower 2027 guide](/news/quadrantid-meteor-shower-2027-peak-wolf-moon/) gives the peak time in each U.S. time zone. The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) puts Earth's perihelion, the point of its orbit nearest the Sun, at 9:33 p.m. EST on Saturday, Jan. 2.

## Awards and football in January 2027

The Critics Choice Awards come first, on Sunday, Jan. 3, on USA Network from 7 to 10 p.m. Eastern Time (ET). The 84th Golden Globes follow on Sunday, Jan. 10, on CBS, and Oscar nominations are announced Thursday, Jan. 21. The full season, through the Oscars on March 14, is in our [awards calendar for 2027](/news/golden-globes-oscars-2027-dates-nominations-how-to-watch-awards-calendar/).

The NFL's important dates list puts the end of the regular season in Week 18 on Jan. 9-10, Wild Card Weekend on Jan. 16-18, the Divisional Playoffs on Jan. 23-24 and the Championship Games on Sunday, Jan. 31. Wild Card Weekend ends on Martin Luther King Jr. Day. Matchups are set after Week 18; see the [NFL playoff schedule for 2027](/news/nfl-playoff-schedule-2027-wild-card-divisional-championship-super-bowl-lxi/).

## Deadlines in January 2027

- **Jan. 1 to 5, college applications:** Harvard lists Jan. 1 for Regular Decision, and Yale, Cornell and Johns Hopkins list Jan. 2. Duke and Notre Dame list Jan. 4, and Stanford, Penn and Brown list Jan. 5. These are the dates on each college's admissions page on Oct. 31, 2026; the [Regular Decision deadlines story](/news/regular-decision-deadlines-2027-january-1-january-5-colleges/) has the wording and the weekday of each.
- **Jan. 15, estimated tax:** the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) lists Friday, Jan. 15, 2027 as the due date for the fourth-quarter 2026 estimated tax payment.

## Orthodox Christmas, Three Kings Day and Burns Night

Orthodox Christmas falls on Thursday, Jan. 7, and Three Kings Day on Wednesday, Jan. 6. The Jan. 7 date is Fresh Calendars' calendar arithmetic: Dec. 25 on the Julian calendar is Jan. 7 on the Gregorian calendar until 2100. The reasoning is in our [Orthodox Christmas 2027 story](/news/orthodox-christmas-2027-january-7-three-kings-day-january-6/). Burns Night, the birthday of the poet Robert Burns, is Monday, Jan. 25, according to VisitScotland, and our [Burns Night 2027 story](/news/burns-night-2027-january-25-burns-supper-order-of-events/) sets out the order of a Burns Supper.
