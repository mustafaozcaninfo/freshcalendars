**Today's Quordle from Merriam-Webster is puzzle #1731, for Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026.** All four words are common, but two start with the same letter and two have a repeated letter. Work through the clues below one board at a time; the answers are in a marked spoiler section at the end.

## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**Top left**

- Vowels: two, O and I, next to each other.
- Meaning: slightly wet, the way a good cake should be.

**Top right**

- Vowels: A and E. One consonant appears twice.
- Meaning: a part in a pipe or an engine that opens and closes to control flow.

**Bottom left**

- Vowels: two E's. Starts with the same letter as the top-right word.
- Meaning: a stanza of a poem or song, or a line from scripture.

**Bottom right**

- Vowels: one, O.
- Meaning: a compass direction, toward the top of the map.

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess with R, S, T and E finds at least one letter in all four words. A second guess with O, I, V and N fills in most of the rest. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
