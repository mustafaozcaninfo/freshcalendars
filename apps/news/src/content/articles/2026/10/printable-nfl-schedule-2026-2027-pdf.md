**The 2026 National Football League (NFL) regular season runs 18 weeks, and a free one-page PDF below lists every week with its Sunday date, the Thursday-to-Monday span and the key games we could confirm.** No account or email is needed. It is a plain PDF (Portable Document Format) with an ink-saver version.

Read this first: the printable lists weeks and verified key games, not all 272 matchups. Every game, kickoff time and TV channel is on [NFL.com/schedules](https://www.nfl.com/schedules), and [the team-by-team view](https://www.nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-team) shows one club at a time.

## The season in numbers

According to NFL Football Operations, the season has 18 weeks and 272 games. Each team plays 17 games and has one bye week. The league released the schedule on Thursday, May 14, 2026, the Cleveland Browns' site reported.

The opener was Patriots at Seahawks on Wednesday, Sept. 9, 2026, at 8:20 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to NFL.com. Week 1 also includes a game in Melbourne, Australia. Today, Fri., Oct. 23, falls in Week 7, which runs Thu., Oct. 22 to Mon., Oct. 26.

## Bye weeks we have confirmed

NFL.com's Week 7 and Week 8 pages name the teams on bye. The full range of bye weeks is on NFL.com.

- **Week 7 (Oct. 22 to 26):** Bills, Jaguars, Chargers, Commanders.
- **Week 8 (Oct. 29 to Nov. 2):** Texans, Saints, Giants, 49ers.

Week 8 is next: Panthers at Packers on Thursday, Oct. 29, at 8:15 p.m. ET; Eagles at Commanders on Sunday night, Nov. 1, at 8:20 p.m. ET; Bears at Seahawks on Monday, Nov. 2, at 8:15 p.m. ET.

## Thanksgiving week and the finish

CBS Sports reported the Thanksgiving week games: Packers at Rams on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 8 p.m. ET on Netflix; Bears at Lions at 1 p.m. on CBS, Eagles at Cowboys at 4:30 p.m. on FOX and Chiefs at Bills at 8:20 p.m. on NBC on Thursday, Nov. 26; and Broncos at Steelers on Friday, Nov. 27, on Prime Video. Our [Thanksgiving 2026 TV schedule](/news/thanksgiving-2026-tv-schedule-macys-parade-nfl-games/) has the full week. Christmas Day games are not yet announced here; check NFL.com.

Week 18 is flexible. NFL.com says matchups and days are set after Week 17. The format includes three Saturday games on Jan. 9, 2027 (1 p.m. ET on Netflix, 4:30 p.m. and 8 p.m. ET on ESPN and the American Broadcasting Company (ABC)), with the rest on Sunday, Jan. 10. Super Bowl 61 (LXI) is Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, according to the NFL. Playoff start dates for the 2026 season are not yet announced.
