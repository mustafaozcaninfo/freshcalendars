**Election Day in Illinois was Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, and polling places were open from 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.** Early voting began statewide on Sept. 24 and in-person early voting ended on Monday, Nov. 2. The State Board of Elections lists Tuesday, Nov. 17 as the last day to count vote-by-mail and provisional ballots, and the next election, a consolidated primary, for Tuesday, Feb. 23, 2027. The dates below come from the board and county and city election authorities.

## Illinois polling hours on Nov. 3, 2026

Polls opened at 6 a.m. and closed at 7 p.m. on Election Day, the Illinois State Board of Elections says. That was 13 hours. Voters in line when the polls closed were allowed to stay and vote. For future elections, the board's [Polling Place Lookup](https://ova.elections.il.gov/PollingPlaceLookup.aspx) finds your assigned polling place.

## Illinois voting dates at a glance

| Date | Day | What happened or happens |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 29 | Thursday | Last day to apply for a mail ballot by mail |
| Nov. 2 | Monday | Last day of in-person early voting |
| Nov. 3 | Tuesday | Election Day, polls open 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. |
| Nov. 17 | Tuesday | Last day to count vote-by-mail and provisional ballots, per the State Board of Elections |

## Illinois early voting in 2026

Early voting began across Illinois on Sept. 24, 2026 at election authority offices, according to the state board. Local authorities then opened more sites:

- **Chicago.** In-person early voting was open at all 50 ward sites from Monday, Oct. 19 through Monday, Nov. 2, the Chicago Board of Election Commissioners says.
- **Suburban Cook County.** Early voting began at six locations on Wednesday, Oct. 7 and expanded to 56 sites from Oct. 19, running through Nov. 2, the Cook County Clerk says.

Outside Cook County, check your county clerk or election authority for site locations and hours, because start dates and the number of open sites are set locally and differ from place to place. The last day to vote early in person statewide was Monday, Nov. 2.

## Illinois vote by mail: deadlines and counting

The first day to file a vote-by-mail application for the Nov. 3, 2026 general election was Aug. 5. All requests sent by mail had to be received by your election authority by Thursday, Oct. 29, the state board says.

A completed mail ballot had to be postmarked by Election Day or returned in person by 7 p.m. on Nov. 3. The State Board of Elections lists Tuesday, Nov. 17 as the last day for vote-by-mail ballot counting and for provisional ballot counting. Ask your election authority about your own ballot. Illinois also offers accessible vote by mail for voters with print disabilities, using the OmniBallot system in Cook County, the City of Chicago, DuPage, Lake, Marshall and Sangamon counties.

## When did polls close in Illinois?

Illinois polls closed at 7 p.m. Central Time (CT) on Tuesday, Nov. 3. For when results were reported across the country, see [what time polls close on Election Day 2026](/news/what-time-do-polls-close-election-day-2026/).

The Illinois State Board of Elections 2026 Ballot Preparation Guide listed these offices on the general election ballot. This story names offices only, not candidates.

| Office | On the 2026 ballot |
|---|---|
| U.S. Senator | Yes |
| U.S. Representatives | All 17 districts |
| Governor and Lieutenant Governor | Yes, elected as a ticket |
| Attorney General | Yes |

Your county or city election authority published the races and questions in each precinct. Our guide to [finding a sample ballot by address](/news/sample-ballot-by-zip-code-2026-texas-ballot/) explains why a ZIP code alone can show the wrong races.

## Is Election Day a school or work holiday in Illinois?

Election Day, Nov. 3, was not a federal holiday; see [what is open on Election Day 2026](/news/is-election-day-a-holiday-2026-whats-open/). Chicago Public Schools had no classes on Nov. 2 and Nov. 3, as its [2026-27 calendar](/news/chicago-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) shows, which freed many school buildings to serve as polling places.
