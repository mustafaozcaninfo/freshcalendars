## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**The word that starts with D**

- Letters: starts with D, ends with T, and has an E and an A inside.
- Meaning: the past tense of "deal", as in cards or a blow.

**The word that starts with F**

- Letters: starts with F, ends with E, and has an L and a U inside.
- Meaning: a narrow channel carrying water, or a ravine with a stream.

**The word that starts with S**

- Letters: starts with S, ends with K, and has two E's side by side.
- Meaning: smooth and glossy, as a polished car or a well-groomed cat.

**The word that starts with R**

- Letters: starts with R, ends with C, and has an E and an I inside.
- Meaning: something old kept from the past, such as an antique.

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess such as Stale, with S, T, A, L and E, puts at least one letter into every board. A second guess such as Round, which adds R, O, U, N and D, covers most of the remaining common letters. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
