**Lyft's VOTE26 code takes 50% off one ride to the polls, up to $10, on Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, and Lime's HEADCOUNT code gives two free bike or scooter rides of up to 30 minutes each through Nov. 3.** Sixteen transit agencies have posted fare-free service for Nov. 3 on their own websites, from LA Metro and the Metropolitan Transit Authority of Harris County (METRO) in Houston to the Suburban Mobility Authority for Regional Transportation (SMART) in the Detroit suburbs and the Birmingham-Jefferson County Transit Authority (MAX) in Alabama, each with its own rules. As of Oct. 30, Uber had announced no 2026 Election Day ride discount. This guide lists only programs we confirmed in official sources and is not a complete nationwide list.

| Code | Who | What you get | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| VOTE26 | Lyft app | 50% off one ride to a polling place, up to $10 | Tue., Nov. 3, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. local time |
| HEADCOUNT | Lime app | Two free rides, up to 30 minutes each | Oct. 24 to Nov. 3 |
| 110326 | LA Metro Bike Share (choose "1-Ride") | One free 30-minute ride | Tue., Nov. 3 |

| VOTE26 | MAX Connect app (Birmingham On-Demand) | Two free rides, one there and one back | Tue., Nov. 3 |

Three different programs use the code VOTE26, so enter it in the right app. The free printable on this page puts every code and free-fare rule on one page.

## Lyft: code VOTE26

| Detail | What Lyft says |
|---|---|
| Discount | 50% off, up to $10, one ride |
| When | Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. local time |
| Rides covered | One Lyft car ride, or one Lyft scooter ride or Lyft-run bikeshare ride in the markets where Lyft offers them |
| Not covered | Lyft Black rides, including the larger Black vehicles, and tips |
| Where to | A polling place where Lyft operates in the U.S.; a registered ballot drop box only in vote-by-mail states or states that allow ballots to be returned to drop boxes |
| How | Add VOTE26 to the Lyft app before Nov. 3; one code and one ride per person, limited quantity, not combined with other credits |

Lyft also works with nonprofit partners that give out their own ride codes: the League of Women Voters, VoteRiders, Asian and Pacific Islander American Vote (APIAVote), the National Council on Aging, the National Down Syndrome Society, the National Coalition for Homeless Veterans, the Students Learn Students Vote Coalition, When We All Vote, Civic Alliance and Power the Polls. Lyft says the APIAVote and National Council on Aging programs reach early voting as well; those codes follow each partner's own terms, while VOTE26 itself is good only on Nov. 3. The offer is not tied to voting or to how you vote, Lyft's terms say.

## Lime: code HEADCOUNT

Lime's "Lime to the Polls" page says the HEADCOUNT code is valid from Oct. 24 to Nov. 3, 2026, wherever there is a Lime bike or scooter nearby. Its terms give two free rides of up to 30 minutes each, one offer per rider, and say the rides can go to a polling place, work or anywhere else in the service area. No voter registration, voting or proof of voting is required. The code cannot be combined with LimePrime, Lime Access, Group Rides or other discounts, and unused value does not carry over. Lime's terms page does not list the eligible cities or a dollar cap per ride, so check the amount in the app before you start a ride. Because the code started Oct. 24, it also covers early voting.

## Free rides during early voting

Trinity Metro in Fort Worth and Tarrant County, Texas, is free during early voting, Oct. 19-30, 2026, in addition to Election Day. Enter code VOTE26 in the GoPass app for one free Local Day Pass, or ask for a paper day pass at the customer service kiosk at Fort Worth Central Station. On-Demand riders going to an early voting site in their zone get a free round trip, and Trinity Metro Bikes riders can use VOTE26 in the bikes app. No ID or proof of voting is needed.

Lime's HEADCOUNT code, valid from Oct. 24, also works on early voting days. Which states are still voting early, and until when, is in [early voting 2026: which states are voting now](/news/early-voting-2026-which-states-are-voting-now/).

## Uber

As of Oct. 30, 2026, Uber had posted no Election Day ride discount for 2026 in its official newsroom; its most recent post, dated Oct. 22, is not about voting. For the 2024 election, Uber's Go Vote! tile in the app took 50% off, up to $10, one ride to a polling place on Election Day, Nov. 5, 2024, excluding Reserve rides, the company said at the time. That offer does not mean a 2026 offer exists. If Uber posts one before Nov. 3, we will add it here.

## Before you ride

- **Confirm where you vote.** Your polling place is set by your home address and can change from one election to the next, USAGov says, so check your state or local election office's official lookup before the trip. Our [sample ballot by ZIP code guide](/news/sample-ballot-by-zip-code-2026-texas-ballot/) links every state's lookup.
- **Check the hours.** Polls close as early as 6 p.m. local time in some states; see [what time polls close](/news/what-time-do-polls-close-election-day-2026/). The Lyft code stops at 10 p.m., and Mountain Line's free rides run only while polls are open.
- **Know your state's ID rules.** A voter registration card is generally not required to vote, USAGov notes, though states set their own ID rules. In Dallas the card is what gets you the free DART fare.
- **Book paratransit early.** Free paratransit still needs the usual reservation; Birmingham's deadline is 5 p.m. Monday, Nov. 2, and DART asks riders to call ahead.

Whether you get time off to vote, and what is open on Nov. 3, is in [is Election Day a holiday](/news/is-election-day-a-holiday-2026-whats-open/). Plan the day on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) or a [weekly planner](/printable-weekly-calendar.html).
