**Westminster City Council and Leeds City Council both list Monday 15 to Friday 19 February 2027 as February half term, but their Easter 2027 holidays do not match.** Westminster's dates run from Good Friday, 26 March, to Friday 9 April, while Leeds City Council lists Monday 5 to Friday 16 April. The tables below set both councils side by side for 2026-27 and 2027-28, and the printable at the end keeps the 2026-27 year on one page.

## School term dates 2026-27 in Westminster and Leeds

The Westminster dates come from the bi-borough document it shares with the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea. Its notes call the dates "proposed", and the council's term dates page was updated on 29 October 2026. The document counts 195 school days for the year. Leeds publishes its dates on its own school holidays page.

| Break | Westminster | Leeds |
|---|---|---|
| Autumn half term | Mon 26 to Fri 30 October 2026 | Term ends Fri 23 October; back Mon 2 November |
| End of autumn term | Fri 18 December 2026 | Fri 18 December 2026 |
| Christmas holiday | Mon 21 December 2026 to Fri 1 January 2027 | Mon 21 December 2026 to Fri 1 January 2027 |
| February half term | Mon 15 to Fri 19 February 2027 | Mon 15 to Fri 19 February 2027 |
| Easter holiday | Fri 26 March to Fri 9 April 2027 | Mon 5 to Fri 16 April 2027 |
| May half term | Mon 31 May to Fri 4 June 2027 | Mon 31 May to Fri 4 June 2027 |
| Last day of summer term | Thu 22 July 2027 | Thu 22 July 2027 |

Leeds starts its autumn term on Tuesday 1 September 2026. Its summer holidays run from Friday 23 July to Friday 3 September 2027. Westminster's summer term runs from Monday 7 June to Thursday 22 July 2027.

## Why do Easter 2027 school holidays differ?

The two councils agree on every 2026-27 break except Easter. Westminster's holiday starts on Good Friday, 26 March 2027, and ends on Friday 9 April. Leeds starts on Monday 5 April, ten days later, and ends on Friday 16 April. A family with children in both areas would see one break end a week before the other does.

The May Day bank holiday falls on Monday 3 May 2027, per Westminster's document. ## School term dates 2027-28: where the councils split

The 2027-28 year is published too, and the dates run into 2028. Here the February half terms fall a week apart.

| Break | Westminster | Leeds |
|---|---|---|
| Autumn term starts | Wed 1 September 2027 | Mon 6 September 2027 |
| Autumn half term | Mon 25 to Fri 29 October 2027 (term ends Fri 22 October) | Term ends Fri 22 October; back Mon 1 November |
| Christmas holiday | Mon 20 December 2027 to Mon 3 January 2028 | Mon 20 December 2027 to Mon 3 January 2028 (term ends Fri 17 December) |
| February half term | Mon 14 to Fri 18 February 2028 | Mon 21 to Fri 25 February 2028 |
| Easter holiday | Mon 3 to Mon 17 April 2028 | Mon 3 to Mon 17 April 2028 |
| May half term | Mon 29 May to Fri 2 June 2028 | Mon 29 May to Fri 2 June 2028 |
| End of summer term | Fri 21 July 2028 | Wed 26 July 2028 |

Easter lines up in 2028, which is the reverse of 2027. Westminster's 2027-28 summer term starts on Monday 5 June 2028.

## What to check for other councils and schools

Westminster says academies and voluntary-aided schools may set different dates from the council's. Councils such as Birmingham, Manchester, Kent and Glasgow publish their own calendars; their official pages could not be read automatically for this story, so no dates for them are given here. Check your council's page and your school's own letter before booking travel.
