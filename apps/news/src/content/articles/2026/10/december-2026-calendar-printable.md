**December 2026 starts on Tuesday, Dec. 1 and has 31 days, one federal holiday and 22 working days.** The holiday is Christmas Day on Friday, Dec. 25, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM); Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, is not a federal holiday. Hanukkah runs from sundown Friday, Dec. 4 to nightfall Saturday, Dec. 12, per Hebcal and Chabad.org. ## December 2026 at a glance

Because the month starts on a Tuesday and ends on a Thursday, those two days and every Wednesday come five times. A Sunday-start grid fits it in five rows, and so does a Monday-start grid.

| | December 2026 |
|---|---|
| First day | Tuesday, Dec. 1 |
| Last day | Thursday, Dec. 31 (New Year's Eve) |
| Days | 31 (23 weekdays, 8 weekend days) |
| Federal holiday | Christmas Day, Fri., Dec. 25 |
| Working days | 22 |
| Christmas weekend | Fri., Dec. 25 to Sun., Dec. 27 |
| Hanukkah | Sundown Fri., Dec. 4 to nightfall Sat., Dec. 12 |
| Winter solstice | Mon., Dec. 21, 3:50 p.m. EST |
| Week numbers | Weeks 49 to 53, under International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 8601 |
| Full moon | Wed., Dec. 23, 8:28 p.m. EST (Cold Moon) |
| Same layout as | December 2020, 2015 and 2009 |

Two details set this December apart. The year ends in ISO week 53, which only some years have: week 53 runs from Monday, Dec. 28 to Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027. And the month has five primary Moon phases instead of four, with a last quarter on both Dec. 1 and Dec. 30, according to the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO).

## Christmas week 2026 on the calendar

Christmas Day on a Friday makes a three-day weekend, Dec. 25 to 27, for anyone who gets the holiday. The week before it carries most of the deadlines: the last USPS send-by dates on Dec. 17, 18 and 19, the solstice on Monday, Dec. 21, the last Social Security payday of the year and the full moon on Wednesday, Dec. 23, and the early stock market close on Christmas Eve. The countdown sheet above puts each of those on its own day. Store hours for both days are in [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 store hours](/news/christmas-eve-christmas-day-2026-store-hours/), and the live countdown is in [Christmas 2026: days until Dec. 25](/news/christmas-2026-calendar-printable-countdown/).

## Week 53 and the end of 2026

ISO 8601 gives a year 53 weeks when it starts on a Thursday, as 2026 did. Its last week, week 53, runs from Monday, Dec. 28, 2026 to Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027, so the first three days of January still belong to 2026 for anyone who plans by week number. Week 1 of 2027 starts Monday, Jan. 4. Our [2027 week numbers](/news/week-numbers-2027-calendar-printable/) story has the full table.
