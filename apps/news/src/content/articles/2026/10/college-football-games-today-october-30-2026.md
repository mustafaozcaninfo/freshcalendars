**There are 4 college football games on Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, and the first two kick off at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) TV schedule published Oct. 29.** Tulane plays at Charlotte on ESPN2 and Harvard plays at Dartmouth on ESPNU, both at 7 p.m. ET. Baylor visits the University of Central Florida (UCF) at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN, and Kent State is at Sacramento State at 10:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2.

The four games are the only Friday games on that NCAA.com page. For the rest of the weekend, see [Saturday's Week 10 schedule](/news/college-football-week-10-schedule-2026-halloween-top-25-game-times-tv/). This page has no live scores.

## College football schedule today: 4 games, times and TV

| Start (ET) | Game | TV |
|---|---|---|
| 7 p.m. | Tulane at Charlotte | ESPN2 |
| 7 p.m. | Harvard at Dartmouth | ESPNU |
| 7:30 p.m. | Baylor at UCF | ESPN |

UCF Athletics lists the Baylor game as "Fri, Oct 30" at "7:30 PM EDT" in Orlando, Florida, at the Acrisure Bounce House. Sacramento State is the home team against Kent State. We did not confirm venues for the other games, so the table does not name them.

## What time are the games in my time zone?

Oct. 30 is before the U.S. clock change on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, so Eastern Time is four hours behind Coordinated Universal Time (UTC-4). We converted the NCAA.com start times ourselves.

| Game | ET | Central Time (CT) | Mountain Time (MT) | Pacific Time (PT) | TV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tulane at Charlotte | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | 5 p.m. | 4 p.m. | ESPN2 |
| Harvard at Dartmouth | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | 5 p.m. | 4 p.m. | ESPNU |
| Baylor at UCF | 7:30 p.m. | 6:30 p.m. | 5:30 p.m. | 4:30 p.m. | ESPN |
| Kent State at Sacramento State | 10:30 p.m. | 9:30 p.m. | 8:30 p.m. | 7:30 p.m. | ESPN2 |

Arizona does not change its clocks, so until Nov. 1 it matches the Pacific Time column. Sacramento State Athletics announced on May 27, 2026 that its home game against Kent State would be on ESPN2 at 7:30 p.m. Pacific, which matches the table.

## Which game is on which channel?

| Channel | Game and start |
|---|---|
| ESPN | Baylor at UCF, 7:30 p.m. ET |
| ESPN2 | Tulane at Charlotte, 7 p.m. ET; Kent State at Sacramento State, 10:30 p.m. ET |
| ESPNU | Harvard at Dartmouth, 7 p.m. ET |

ESPN2 carries both the first and the last game of the night, 3 hours 30 minutes apart by start time. The 7 p.m. ET window has two games on different channels, ESPN2 and ESPNU. Dartmouth Athletics confirmed the ESPNU broadcast when it announced on June 10, 2026 that the Harvard game had been rescheduled.

We did not confirm streaming options, so check your TV provider's guide for how to watch each channel.

## What is the late game on Friday night?

Kent State at Sacramento State starts at 10:30 p.m. ET, which is 7:30 p.m. PT. It is the last of the four games on the NCAA.com list. For viewers on Pacific Time, Baylor at UCF starts at 4:30 p.m. and the late game follows at 7:30 p.m.
