**The Women's Tennis Association (WTA) says the 2026 WTA Finals will be held at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden in Indian Wells, California, from Nov. 8-15, and the WTA's draws page, checked Nov. 4, 2026, splits the eight qualified singles players into two groups of four.** Both Nov. 8 and Nov. 15 are Sundays. The WTA's order-of-play page headline on Nov. 4 names Rybakina vs. Noskova as the opener, but session start times were not readable. The tables below give the dates, the groups and a time conversion you can reuse once the schedule is posted.

## What are the WTA Finals 2026 dates?

The WTA tournament page lists Nov. 8 to Nov. 15, 2026. In its July 1, 2026 ticket guide, the WTA lists Round Robin 1 on Nov. 8-9, Round Robin 2 on Nov. 10-11, Round Robin 3 on Nov. 12-13 and Championship Weekend on Nov. 14-15. The WTA has not posted which day is the semifinal or the final. The event ends Sunday, Nov. 15.

| Date | Weekday | WTA stage |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 8 | Sunday | Round Robin 1 |
| Nov. 9 | Monday | Round Robin 1 |
| Nov. 10 | Tuesday | Round Robin 2 |
| Nov. 11 | Wednesday | Round Robin 2 |
| Nov. 12 | Thursday | Round Robin 3 |
| Nov. 13 | Friday | Round Robin 3 |
| Nov. 14 | Saturday | Championship Weekend |
| Nov. 15 | Sunday | Championship Weekend |

## Who has qualified for the WTA Finals 2026?

The WTA says the top eight singles players and doubles teams on the Race to the WTA Finals qualify. Its singles standings page, checked Nov. 4, 2026, marks these eight players Qualified:

| Race rank | Player | Race points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elena Rybakina (KAZ) | 8,099 |
| 2 | Aryna Sabalenka | 6,485 |
| 3 | Mirra Andreeva | 6,306 |
| 4 | Coco Gauff (USA) | 5,763 |
| 5 | Jessica Pegula (USA) | 5,760 |
| 6 | Elina Svitolina (UKR) | 5,121 |
| 7 | Karolina Muchova (CZE) | 4,665 |
| 8 | Linda Noskova (CZE) | 4,343 |

Iga Swiatek is ninth with 4,188 points and did not qualify. The WTA's draws page, checked Nov. 4, splits the singles field into two groups of four, with the seed in brackets:

| Group | Singles players |
|---|---|
| Group 1 | Elena Rybakina (1), Coco Gauff (4), Jessica Pegula (5), Linda Noskova (8) |
| Group 2 | Aryna Sabalenka (2), Mirra Andreeva (3), Elina Svitolina (6), Karolina Muchova (7) |

The same page lists the doubles groups, with teams as the WTA prints them and the seed in brackets:

| Group | Doubles teams |
|---|---|
| Group 1 | Gabriela Dabrowski / Luisa Stefani (1), H. Guo / K. Mladenovic (3), S. Hsieh / J. Ostapenko (5), E. Mertens / S. Zhang (7) |
| Group 2 | K. Siniakova / T. Townsend (2), C. Bucsa / N. Melichar-Martinez (4), E. Perez / D. Schuurs (6), S. Errani / J. Paolini (8) |

The 2025 edition was in Riyadh, and the WTA says the 2026 Finals are at Indian Wells.

## What time do WTA Finals matches start?

Not yet announced. Session start times are not posted, and the WTA's order-of-play page headline on Nov. 4 ("Rybakina to face Noskova in opener") came without readable times. The table below is a set of conversion examples, not the schedule. U.S. daylight saving time ended Nov. 1, 2026, so Indian Wells is on Pacific Standard Time (PST), Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) minus 8 hours, for the whole event. Eastern Standard Time (EST) is 3 hours ahead, Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in the U.K. is 8 hours ahead, Central European Time (CET) is 9 hours ahead, and Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT) is 19 hours ahead, at UTC plus 11 hours.

| If a match starts at (Indian Wells, PST) | EST | GMT | CET | AEDT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 a.m. | 2 p.m. | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 6 a.m. next day |
| 1 p.m. | 4 p.m. | 9 p.m. | 10 p.m. | 8 a.m. next day |
| 4 p.m. | 7 p.m. | 12 a.m. next day | 1 a.m. next day | 11 a.m. next day |
| 6 p.m. | 9 p.m. | 2 a.m. next day | 3 a.m. next day | 1 p.m. next day |
| 7 p.m. | 10 p.m. | 3 a.m. next day | 4 a.m. next day | 2 p.m. next day |

The clock change matters for planning: the same gap applies to every day of the event. Our [daylight saving time ends Nov. 1, 2026](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) story covers the change, and the [daylight saving time page](/when-is/daylight-savings/) has the rule.

## What else has the WTA not announced?

The WTA's pages we read do not give a 2026 prize money figure, and do not show semifinal or final times. The announcement describes the event only as having "one of the largest prize pools in women's sport." The WTA has not posted broadcast details for the United States or United Kingdom on the tournament page, so check your local listings.
