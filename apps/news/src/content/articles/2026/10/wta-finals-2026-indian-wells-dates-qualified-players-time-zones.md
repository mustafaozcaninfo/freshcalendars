**The Women's Tennis Association (WTA) says the 2026 WTA Finals run Nov. 8-15 at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden in Indian Wells, California, with the semifinals on Saturday, Nov. 14 and the championships on Sunday, Nov. 15.** The round robin ended Nov. 13. On the WTA's draws page, Linda Noskova and Jessica Pegula (Group 1) and Mirra Andreeva (Group 2) are in the singles semifinals, and Aryna Sabalenka or Elina Svitolina takes the last spot. The WTA lists the first singles match at 12 p.m. Pacific, which is 3 p.m. Eastern.

## What are the WTA Finals 2026 dates?

The WTA tournament page lists Nov. 8 to Nov. 15, 2026. The WTA's schedule guide puts round-robin play on Nov. 8-13, the semifinals on Nov. 14 and the championships on Nov. 15. Nov. 8 and Nov. 15 are both Sundays.

| Date | Weekday | WTA stage |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 8 | Sunday | Round robin, Group 1 singles and Group 2 doubles |
| Nov. 9 | Monday | Round robin, Group 2 singles and Group 1 doubles |
| Nov. 10 | Tuesday | Round robin, Group 1 singles and Group 2 doubles |
| Nov. 11 | Wednesday | Round robin, Group 2 singles and Group 1 doubles |
| Nov. 12 | Thursday | Round robin, Group 1 singles and Group 2 doubles |
| Nov. 13 | Friday | Round robin, Group 2 singles and Group 1 doubles |
| Nov. 14 | Saturday | Semifinals |
| Nov. 15 | Sunday | Championships (singles and doubles finals) |

## Who has reached the WTA Finals 2026 semifinals?

The WTA's field was the top eight singles players on the Race to the WTA Finals. Elena Rybakina, the No. 1 seed, withdrew with an illness, according to the WTA, and Marta Kostyuk, the alternate, replaced her in Group 1. The WTA also named Diana Shnaider to replace Karolina Muchova in the group-stage match against Andreeva. Each group plays a round robin, and the top two advance, with the winner of each group meeting the runner-up from the other.

| Group | Singles players | Group record on the WTA draws page (Nov. 13) |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Linda Noskova (8), Jessica Pegula (5), Coco Gauff (4), Marta Kostyuk (9) | Noskova 2-1, Pegula 2-1, Gauff 2-1, Kostyuk 0-3 |
| Group 2 | Mirra Andreeva (3), Aryna Sabalenka (2), Elina Svitolina (6), Karolina Muchova (7) | Andreeva 2-0, Sabalenka 1-1, Svitolina 1-1, Muchova 0-2 |

In Group 1, Noskova and Pegula advanced, and the WTA's profile says Noskova won the group. Pegula beat Noskova 7-6(2), 4-6, 7-5 after Noskova was one point from the win, and Gauff was left out. Andreeva clinched Group 2 and the semifinals by beating Sabalenka. The Sabalenka vs. Svitolina match on Nov. 13 decides the other semifinalist, and the order of play for Nov. 14 had not been posted when we checked. Under the WTA's format, Andreeva meets the Group 1 runner-up, and the Group 1 winner meets the Group 2 runner-up.

The WTA draws page shows the doubles groups, with the seed in brackets and the group record on Nov. 13:

| Group | Doubles teams | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Gabriela Dabrowski / Luisa Stefani (1), H. Guo / K. Mladenovic (3), S. Hsieh / J. Ostapenko (5), E. Mertens / S. Zhang (7) | Dabrowski/Stefani 2-0, Hsieh/Ostapenko 1-1, Guo/Mladenovic 1-1, Mertens/Zhang 0-2 (two matches still to play Nov. 13) |
| Group 2 | K. Siniakova / T. Townsend (2), C. Bucsa / N. Melichar-Martinez (4), E. Perez / D. Schuurs (6), S. Errani / J. Paolini (8) | Errani/Paolini 3-0, Siniakova/Townsend 2-1, Bucsa/Melichar-Martinez 1-2, Perez/Schuurs 0-3 |

Errani/Paolini and Siniakova/Townsend are in the doubles semifinals. The two Group 1 doubles spots were still open on the WTA's draws page.

## What time do WTA Finals matches start?

The WTA's schedule guide lists the first singles match at 12 p.m. local time on Saturday, Nov. 14 and Sunday, Nov. 15. The other singles match and the doubles matches are listed as followed by the match before, with no fixed time, and the doubles final and both trophy presentations follow the singles final. U.S. daylight saving time ended Nov. 1, 2026, so Indian Wells is on Pacific Standard Time (PST), Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) minus 8 hours, for the whole event. Eastern Standard Time (EST) is 3 hours ahead, Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in the U.K. is 8 hours ahead, Central European Time (CET) is 9 hours ahead, and Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT) is 19 hours ahead, at UTC plus 11 hours.

## How much is the WTA Finals 2026 prize money?

The WTA says a total of $10 million is available, with $8 million for singles and $2 million for doubles. An undefeated singles champion would earn $3.37 million, including participation fees, and an undefeated doubles pair $725,000. Ranking points per win are 200 in the round robin, 400 in a semifinal and 500 in the final, so an undefeated title is worth 1,500 points.
