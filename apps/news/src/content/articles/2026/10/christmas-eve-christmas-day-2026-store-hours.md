**Christmas Eve falls on Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026, and Christmas Day on Friday, Dec. 25, so most stores will close early on a weekday and stay shut on Friday.** Home Depot has already posted its 2026 hours: open until 5 p.m. on Christmas Eve and closed on Christmas Day. Lowe's says its stores will be closed on Christmas Day. For other chains, last year's hours are the best guide until each one confirms. Below: Christmas Eve closing times, what is open on Christmas Day, banks, mail and the stock market, and a free checklist in three styles.

## What is closed on Christmas Day

Last year Aldi, Costco, Harris Teeter, Home Depot, IKEA, Jewel-Osco, Kroger, Lowe's, Macy's, Meijer, Publix, Sam's Club, Sprouts, Target, Trader Joe's, Walmart and Whole Foods all closed on Christmas Day, Scripps News reported. Walmart has confirmed it will close again on Christmas Day 2026; expect the rest of the list to repeat: the stores reopen on Saturday, Dec. 26, which also starts the busy week of returns and gift-card shopping.

## What is usually open on Christmas Day

CVS and Walgreens keep a share of their stores open on Christmas Day, though pharmacy counters may close or keep shorter hours. Selected Albertsons and Safeway stores, 7-Eleven and other convenience stores, and McDonald's restaurants whose owners choose to open are the other usual options. Starbucks cafes inside hotels, airports and grocery stores are more likely to open than standalone stores. Movie theaters run Christmas Day showings. Call ahead or check the store's page before you go.

## Banks, mail, markets and government

| Service | Thursday, Dec. 24 | Friday, Dec. 25 |
|---|---|---|
| Stock market (NYSE, Nasdaq) | Open, closes early at 1 p.m. ET | Closed |
| Banks | Open; branches may close early | Closed (federal holiday) |
| U.S. Postal Service | Open, normal delivery | Closed, no regular delivery |
| UPS and FedEx | Operating | No regular pickup or delivery |
| Federal offices | Open unless a new executive order closes them | Closed |

Christmas Day is a federal holiday by law. Christmas Eve is not; in 2024 and 2025, presidents gave federal workers the day off by executive order, but each order covered only that year. The New York Stock Exchange's 2026 calendar sets the 1 p.m. close on Dec. 24.
