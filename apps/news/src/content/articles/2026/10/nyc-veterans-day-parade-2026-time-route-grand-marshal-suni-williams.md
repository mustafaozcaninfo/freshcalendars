**The 107th New York City Veterans Day Parade marched up Fifth Avenue on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, with National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) astronaut and U.S. Navy veteran Sunita "Suni" Williams as Grand Marshal**, according to the United War Veterans Council (UWVC), the parade's organizer. ABC7 New York reported on Oct. 24 that it would step off at 12:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) at Fifth Avenue and 26th Street. UWVC calls it "the largest Veterans Day parade in the country."

Nov. 11, 2026 falls on a Wednesday, and Veterans Day is a federal holiday. See [When is Veterans Day?](/when-is/veterans-day/) for the date and our [Veterans Day 2026 story](/news/veterans-day-2026-wednesday-november-11/) for what is closed. The times are in one table below, with a one-page day plan you can print. UWVC says details of the 2027 parade are still to come.

## What time does the NYC Veterans Day Parade start?

| Time (ET) | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 10:55 a.m. | Wreath-laying in Madison Square Park | Reported by ABC7 New York, Oct. 24, 2026 |
| 12:30 p.m. | Parade stepped off at Fifth Avenue and 26th Street | Reported by ABC7 New York, Oct. 24, 2026 |
| 2 p.m. | Birmingham, Ala., parade begins (1:00 p.m. CT) | National Veterans Day Foundation |

ABC7 wrote that the parade "will step off from Fifth Avenue at 26th Street at 12:30 p.m." and that the wreath-laying comes "prior to the parade at 10:55 a.m. in Madison Square Park." We did not find these times on the UWVC parade page when we checked on Oct. 29, so treat them as reported, not confirmed by the organizer. UWVC did not publish an end time.

## Where does the parade route start?

The reported start was Fifth Avenue at 26th Street. UWVC said the parade "returns to Fifth Avenue on November 11, 2026." The city's construction embargo list gave the route as "5th Avenue between 25th Street and 48th Street," with marchers forming up on Broadway and the side streets between West 23rd and West 29th Streets and dispersing on East and West 45th Street between Sixth and Madison Avenues. Non-emergency street work in those blocks was suspended from 12:01 a.m. Sunday, Nov. 8 through 11:59 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 11. That embargo was not a list of street closures.

## Who is the 2026 Grand Marshal?

UWVC says Sunita "Suni" Williams, a NASA astronaut and U.S. Navy veteran, served as Grand Marshal for the 107th parade. The Honorary Marshal was Vicki Bello, a U.S. Navy veteran and Manager of Community and Veterans Programs for the Theatre Development Fund (TDF).

## How to watch the NYC Veterans Day Parade

UWVC's watch page says the full recording will be posted with the parade recap, and as of Nov. 14 it listed the coverage time as not yet available. ABC7 New York wrote that "Channel 7 is proud to be a partner of the event." The same page links to recordings of the 2025 and 2024 parades.

## Other Veterans Day parades on Nov. 11: Birmingham

The National Veterans Day Foundation listed its Birmingham, Ala., parade for Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026. Its page said: "The parade begins at 1:00pm, rain or shine!" That is Central Time (CT), so 2 p.m. ET. The event was listed from 1:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. CT. Times and routes for eight more cities are in our [Veterans Day parades by city](/news/veterans-day-parades-2026-by-city/) table.

## Arlington National Cemetery observance

We did not find the 2026 Arlington National Cemetery observance time in the sources we checked. The national observance is traditionally held at the cemetery's Memorial Amphitheater. Last year's (2025) listing was 11 a.m. ET.
