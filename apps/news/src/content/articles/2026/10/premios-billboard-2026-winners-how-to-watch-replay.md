<p class="callout"><b>Updated Oct. 25, 2026:</b> the full official winners list was not yet posted in a form we could read; we list winners Telemundo has announced and will add the rest.</p>

**Fuerza Regida, Peso Pluma and Tito Double P, Rosalía and Wisin y Yandel are among the winners of the 2026 Billboard Latin Music Awards (Premios Billboard), according to Telemundo, which aired the show live on Thursday, Oct. 22, at 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) from the James L. Knight Center in Miami.** NBCUniversal said Carmen Villalobos and Javier Poza hosted the night, which included 16 musical performances. Below are the winners announced so far, by category, plus when and where the show aired and where to look for it now.

## Premios Billboard 2026 winners announced so far

The table lists winners as headlined on Telemundo's [Premios Billboard page](https://www.telemundo.com/PremiosBillboard). English glosses are in parentheses where the meaning is plain. This is not the full list of categories.

| Category | Winner |
|---|---|
| Canción del Año, Streaming (Song of the Year, Streaming) | Fuerza Regida |
| Global 200 Canción Latina del Año | Fuerza Regida |
| Hot Latin Song | Peso Pluma and Tito Double P |
| Colaboración Vocal del Año (Vocal Collaboration of the Year) | Peso Pluma and Tito Double P |
| Álbum Top Regional Mexicano (Top Regional Mexican Album) | Peso Pluma and Tito Double P |
| Top Latin Album del Año (Top Latin Album of the Year) | Peso Pluma and Tito Double P |
| Artista "Latin Pop" del Año, Solista (Latin Pop Artist of the Year, Solo) | Rosalía |
| Top Latin Pop del Año | Rosalía |
| Artista Latin Rhythm del Año, Dúo o Grupo (Latin Rhythm Artist of the Year, Duo or Group) | Wisin y Yandel |

Peso Pluma and Tito Double P appear in four rows, and Rosalía and Fuerza Regida in two each. Telemundo's finalists article, published Sept. 9 and updated Sept. 21, 2026, said Peso Pluma, Tito Double P, Fuerza Regida, Karol G, Prince Royce and Bad Bunny led the nominations. It did not give counts, and we do not add winners we cannot confirm.

## When, where and how the show aired

| Detail | What NBCUniversal and Telemundo said |
|---|---|
| Date | Thursday, Oct. 22, 2026 |
| Venue | James L. Knight Center, Miami |
| Start time | 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. Central Time, CT) |
| Live on | Telemundo, Peacock, Universo, the Telemundo app and Telemundo Internacional |
| YouTube livestream | From 6 p.m. ET on @TelemundoEntretenimiento |
| Hosts | Carmen Villalobos and Javier Poza |
| Performances | 16 |

Source: NBCUniversal, "How to Watch the 2026 Billboard Latin Music Awards," Oct. 19, 2026. Telemundo's July 21, 2026 announcement also said the show would be available live on Telemundo and Peacock.

## How to watch Premios Billboard now

The show streamed on Peacock when it aired. We could not confirm whether a replay is available, so we do not promise one. Check Peacock and the Telemundo app for the 2026 Billboard Latin Music Awards, and the Telemundo [Premios Billboard page](https://www.telemundo.com/PremiosBillboard) for winners and any video it posts.
