**Dunkin' starts its 2026 holiday season and its "Avengers: Doomsday" menu on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, at participating U.S. restaurants, the company says in newsroom releases dated Oct. 6 and Oct. 28.** The Marvel Studios lineup is limited-time and runs while supplies last. Below are every item Dunkin' named, a table of 2026 holiday menu start dates by chain, and a one-page checklist to print.

## When does the Dunkin' holiday menu start in 2026?

Wednesday, Nov. 4. In its Oct. 28, 2026 release, Dunkin' says it is "unveiling its 2026 holiday season this November with the debut of its very own Donutcracker, a playful new design appearing across holiday cups, donut and Munchkins boxes nationwide beginning Nov. 4."

The release headline says Cookie Butter and Toasted White Chocolate return. The page we read showed only the headline and first paragraph, so the full holiday drink list is not confirmed here, and we are not naming individual drinks. We will add them if Dunkin' publishes the list.

## Holiday menu start dates 2026, by chain

Dunkin' starts two days after Caribou Coffee and one day before Starbucks. Other chains had not announced 2026 dates on their newsrooms as of Oct. 29, 2026.

| Chain | 2026 start | Day | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caribou Coffee | Nov. 2 | Monday | Caribou Coffee release, Oct. 13, 2026 |
| Dunkin' | Nov. 4 | Wednesday | Dunkin' releases, Oct. 6 and Oct. 28, 2026 |
| Starbucks | Nov. 5 | Thursday | Starbucks, see our [Starbucks holiday menu story](/news/starbucks-holiday-menu-2026-red-cup-day-date/) |
| Dutch Bros | Not yet announced | n/a | 2025 menu started Nov. 1, 2025 |
| McDonald's, Krispy Kreme, Panera, Tim Hortons | Not yet announced | n/a | Newsrooms checked Oct. 29, 2026 |

Dutch Bros' 2025 holiday menu started Nov. 1, 2025, according to its newsroom. That is last year's date, not a 2026 one.

## What is on the Dunkin' Avengers: Doomsday menu?

Dunkin' says the lineup starts Nov. 4 at participating restaurants nationwide, while supplies last. Each description below is Dunkin's.

| Item | Type | Dunkin's description |
|---|---|---|
| Iced Captain'ccino | Drink | Espresso, caramel flavor and coffee milk, lightly blended and served over ice |
| Clobbered Cloud Latte | Drink | Espresso, whole milk and toasted white chocolate flavor topped with Cookie Butter Cold Foam and crumbles inspired by The Thing |
| Wakandan Daydream Refresher | Drink | Berry Clementine Refresher, raspberry flavor and oatmilk topped with Purple Marshmallow Cold Foam, inspired by Black Panther |
| Optic Blast Lemonade | Drink | Sweet-tart lemonade charged with blue raspberry flavor, inspired by Cyclops |
| Doom's Orders Energy | Drink | Green Apple Dunkin' Zero, sparkling water and Purple Marshmallow Cold Foam, inspired by Doctor Doom |
| Doom Cloud Latte | Drink | Espresso, whole milk, mocha swirl and vanilla flavors topped with Sweet Cold Foam and cocoa powder |
| Doomsday Donut | Donut | The Brownie Batter Donut returns with chocolate icing and green and chocolate sprinkles |
| Avenging Donut | Donut | A cotton candy-filled donut with vanilla-flavored icing and red, yellow and blue sprinkles |
| Doomsday & Avenging Munchkins Donut Hole Treats | Munchkins | Glazed Chocolate and Buttermilk Munchkins get villain- and hero-inspired treatments |

## How much is the Doctor Doom Munchkins Tin, and what else is collectible?

From Nov. 4, the Doctor Doom 25-Count Munchkins Tin, inspired by Doom's mask and green hood, is available with any 25-count Munchkins purchase for $14.99 at participating restaurants, while supplies last. Dunkin' says availability and quantities may vary by restaurant.

The limited-edition collection also includes Avengers: Doomsday-themed tumblers, a color-changing mug, glove cup sleeves and character cup sleeves. The glove sleeves feature Doctor Doom, Black Panther, Mister Fantastic and Captain America. Dunkin' lists specific items and quantities as varying by restaurant.

## What else has Dunkin' said is coming?

Dunkin' says "more surprises" will be announced at the top of November. The film, directed by Anthony and Joe Russo, releases in theaters Friday, Dec. 18, 2026, per the same release.

For the film's runtime, ticket details and a dated countdown, see our [Avengers: Doomsday release date story](/news/avengers-doomsday-release-date-december-18-countdown-tickets-runtime/).
