**Sam's Club's 2026 Member's Mark Thanksgiving meal went on sale Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026: 11 items for about $160, serving up to 16 people, for less than $10 per person, in clubs and online, according to Sam's Club.** Five more deals are in play. Walmart's $35 Holiday Meal Basket for 10 and Amazon Grocery's $25 meal are available now. Aldi's meal for 10 at under $39 is also on, Target's $30 meal for six starts Sunday, Nov. 8, and Lidl's $35.86 basket starts Wednesday, Nov. 11. Below: all six side by side, the dates each deal is live, and a printable calendar. For the holiday date itself, see [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/); it falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026.

## How do the six Thanksgiving meals compare?

| Retailer | Price (before tax) | Feeds | Cost per person (our math) | On sale | Where | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sam's Club | About $160 | Up to 16 | About $10 | From Oct. 28 | In club and online | Sam's Club members |
| Aldi | Under $39 | 10 | Under $3.90 | Oct. 14 to Nov. 25 | In store, Aldi's Thanksgiving page, DoorDash, Instacart, Uber Eats | No coupons, loyalty cards, minimum purchases or memberships |
| Lidl | $35.86 | Up to 10 | $3.59 | Nov. 11 to Nov. 25 | Not stated in the release | Lidl Plus members: deal runs Nov. 11 to 25 |
| Target | $30 | 6 | $5 | Nov. 8 to Nov. 29 | Target | Not valid in Alaska or Hawaii |
| Walmart | $35 | 10 | $3.50 | Oct. 28 to Dec. 24 | In stores and online (pickup and delivery) | Excludes Alaska, Hawaii, Oklahoma, Puerto Rico and Wisconsin; fees of $0 to $19.95 may apply |
| Amazon Grocery | $25 | Not stated by Amazon | Not available | Through Nov. 26, while supplies last | Amazon.com/ThanksgivingMeal | Free Same-Day Delivery on orders of $25 or more, Amazon says |

Each price comes from the retailer's own 2026 press release. The cost-per-person column is our division of the announced price by the number of people served. Aldi's "under $39" is a ceiling, so its per-person figure is a ceiling too. Sam's Club itself says "less than $10 per person." Aldi notes that fees may apply on its delivery partners. Amazon does not say how many people its meal serves, so we do not give a per-person price for it.

## What is in each meal?

The items differ, so the per-person price is a guide and not a like-for-like comparison.

- **Sam's Club:** 11 Member's Mark items, including two 12-inch pumpkin pies. Sam's Club said the meal can be prepared in about two hours. Members can add it to their cart with a single click.
- **Aldi:** a 16-lb. Farmer's Choice whole turkey, three boxes of Chef's Cupboard Stuffing Mix, 3 lbs. of sweet potatoes, L'oven Fresh Hawaiian Sweet Rolls, 12 oz. of cranberries, two bags of Season's Choice Steamable Frozen Peas, baby peeled carrots and celery, Baker's Corner Pumpkin Pie Mix with Bake House Creations Pie Crusts, and Friendly Farms Whole Milk and Heavy Whipping Cream. In stores, the items are marked with a turkey logo.
- **Target:** a Butterball Premium Whole Young Turkey of up to 10 lbs., a 3-lb. bag of Good & Gather Russet Potatoes, a 14-oz. can of Ocean Spray Jellied Cranberry Sauce, two 14.5-oz. cans of Del Monte French Style Green Beans, a 10.5-oz. can of Campbell's Condensed Cream of Mushroom Soup, 6 oz. of Good & Gather French Fried Onions, a 6-oz. box of Stove Top Stuffing Mix for Turkey, 12 oz. of Heinz Homestyle Turkey Gravy and two Favorite Day Take & Bake Baguettes. Target also lists Good & Gather turkey at 79 cents per pound.
- **Walmart:** a Holiday Meal Basket with 26 items, which Walmart says is 18% more food items than last year. Walmart's own question-and-answer page calls it its 2026 Thanksgiving Meal Basket. Free Express Delivery on the basket runs through Nov. 30, 2026, subject to availability, and fees and restrictions may apply.
- **Amazon Grocery:** an 8 lb. Butterball frozen turkey plus ingredients for classic sides and dessert: cranberry sauce, gravy mix, stuffing, potatoes and pie crusts. Same-Day Delivery is offered in more than 2,300 U.S. cities and towns where Amazon offers fresh groceries, and it is free on orders of $25 or more, Amazon says. Amazon also lists frozen turkey at $0.79 per pound from Nov. 1 to 26 and fresh turkey at $0.99 per pound from Nov. 14 to 26.
- **Lidl:** a 14-lb. Shady Brook Farms frozen whole turkey is part of the basket for up to 10 people. Lidl said it will match the comparable total price of the basket at major national retailers, naming Walmart and Aldi.

## Which stores are covered, and which have not announced?

Walmart announced its 2026 meal on Oct. 28 and Amazon on Nov. 2. Kroger has not announced a 2026 meal; we could not find an official 2026 release.

For context, here is what three retailers said last year, labeled as 2025:

- In 2025, Walmart's meal served 10 for $39.93, or under $4.00 per person, according to its Oct. 21, 2025 release. The 2026 basket is $35 for 10, or $3.50 per person.
- In 2025, Target offered a Thanksgiving meal for four for "under $20," according to its Nov. 5, 2025 release. The 2026 meal feeds six for $30, which Target describes as "maintaining last year's $5-per-person value."
- In 2025, Kroger's "Freshgiving" meal was "a complete holiday dinner for ten at an industry-leading price of less than $4.75 per person," with prices valid beginning Nov. 12, 2025, it said on Nov. 6, 2025.

Aldi also made a savings claim: over the last five years, its shoppers have saved an average of 26% on a classic Thanksgiving meal compared to the national average reported by the American Farm Bureau Federation (AFBF). That is Aldi's statement, not our finding.
