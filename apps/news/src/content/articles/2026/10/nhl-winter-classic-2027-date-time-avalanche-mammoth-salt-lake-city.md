**The 2027 Discover NHL Winter Classic is Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. Mountain Time (MT), when the Colorado Avalanche visit the Utah Mammoth at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City, according to the National Hockey League (NHL).** That is 6 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) by our conversion. The NHL's Winter Classic page of frequently asked questions (FAQ) names Utah as the home team.

## Why is the 2027 Winter Classic played in 2026?

The game carries the 2027 label because it belongs to the 2026-27 season. It is played on New Year's Eve, Dec. 31, 2026, not on Jan. 1. The NHL's FAQ lists the Thursday, Dec. 31 date, and the league's July 16, 2026 schedule release repeats it. For the holiday itself, see [New Year's Eve](/when-is/new-years-eve/).

The NHL gives the start as 4:00 p.m. MT. Dec. 31 falls on standard time, so that is Mountain Standard Time. Every other row is our conversion, checked with a time zone database.

## What channel is the Winter Classic on?

The Utah Mammoth announcement, as published on the Delta Center site, says the game is televised exclusively on Turner Network Television (TNT) in the U.S. and on Sportsnet in Canada. Check both networks closer to the date.

## Where is the Winter Classic played?

At Rice-Eccles Stadium, on the University of Utah campus in Salt Lake City, according to the NHL's FAQ. Utah is the home team.
