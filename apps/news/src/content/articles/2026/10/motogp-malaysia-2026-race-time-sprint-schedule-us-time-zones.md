**Marc Marquez won the Malaysian Grand Prix (GP) at Sepang on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, by 4.1 seconds from Pedro Acosta, with polesitter Alex Marquez third, MotoGP reports.** It was Marc Marquez's seventh win of 2026. The race started at 2 a.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), 15:00 local time in Malaysia, Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus 8 hours (UTC+8). Below: the results, the championship gap, and every session of the weekend in U.S. time zones.

## MotoGP Malaysia 2026 results

| Race | Winner | Second | Third |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tissot Sprint, Sat., Oct. 31 | Pedro Acosta | Marc Marquez | Marco Bezzecchi |
| Malaysian GP, Sun., Nov. 1 | Marc Marquez | Pedro Acosta | Alex Marquez |

MotoGP says Acosta won the Sprint by just over half a second. After the race Marc Marquez leads Jorge Martin by 44 points and Bezzecchi by 67. Martin withdrew from the Malaysia round injured, MotoGP said on Oct. 31. MotoGP's race report calls the result "match point" for the 2026 title; it does not say the title is decided. Finishing times are in MotoGP's official classification.

## What time was the Malaysian GP on Sunday?

MotoGP said: "The Malaysian GP begins at 15:00, with 20 laps deciding our winner." That is 07:00 UTC. In the United States it was 2 a.m. EST, 1 a.m. CST, 1 a.m. MDT and 12 a.m. (midnight) PDT on Sunday, Nov. 1, by our conversion. The race distance is 110.86 km (68.89 miles), MotoGP's Malaysia event page says.

The Moto3 race is 15 laps and the Moto2 race is 17 laps, per the same page. MotoGP also lists a post-race press conference at 16:45 local time.

## What time was the MotoGP Sprint at Sepang?

MotoGP said "the Tissot Sprint starting at 15:00" on Saturday, Oct. 31. That is also 07:00 UTC, so it was 3 a.m. EDT, 2 a.m. CDT, 1 a.m. MDT and 12 a.m. (midnight) PDT on Saturday, by our conversion. MotoGP qualifying is at 10:50 local time the same day, which is Friday night, 10:50 p.m. EDT, in the East.

## Why did the clock change matter for a 2 a.m. race?

U.S. clocks fell back at 2 a.m. local time on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, the same morning as the race. Saturday's Sprint at 07:00 UTC is still on daylight time, so Eastern viewers are 12 hours behind Sepang and the Sprint is 3 a.m. EDT. By race time on Sunday, which is also 07:00 UTC, the East is on standard time and 13 hours behind, so the same 15:00 start is 2 a.m. EST. Pacific time is still on daylight time at 07:00 UTC, so the race is 12 a.m. PDT. Central has already changed by then, so it is 1 a.m. CST, a time that occurs twice that night in the Central zone.
