Martin Luther King Jr. Day 2027 falls on **Monday, Jan. 18**, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM), which lists the federal holiday under its official name, "Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr." From Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, the day is 85 days away. Federal offices and the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) close, and the page offers a one-page printable planner.

## Is MLK Day a federal holiday?

Yes. Title 5 of the U.S. Code, section 6103, names "Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr., the third Monday in January" among the federal holidays. Congress added the day by Public Law 98-144 in 1983. The National Archives' Reagan Library says President Ronald Reagan signed the bill, H.R. 3706, into law on Nov. 2, 1983, and that the first official Martin Luther King, Jr. Day was celebrated on Jan. 20, 1986.

OPM sets the federal schedule, and the NYSE says all of its markets observe the U.S. holidays on its list for 2025, 2026 and 2027. The Federal Reserve Board publishes its own holiday dates. Local services decide their own hours, so confirm them with your bank, the United States Postal Service (USPS), your school and your employer.

## Are national parks free on MLK Day?

The National Park Service (NPS) did not list MLK Day as a fee-free day in 2026. Its 2026 list, on a passes page last updated Oct. 9, 2026, is Feb. 16, May 25, June 14, July 3 to 5, Aug. 25, Sept. 17, Oct. 27 and Nov. 11. The 2027 list has not been posted, so 2027 dates are not yet announced. The NPS also says that beginning in 2026, free entrance on those days is for U.S. citizens and residents only. Our story on [national parks free days from Oct. 27](/news/national-parks-free-days-october-27-veterans-day-2026/) tracks the 2026 list.

## When is MLK Day from 2026 to 2030?

The holiday is always the third Monday in January, so the date moves between Jan. 15 and Jan. 21. These are the Federal Reserve Board's dates.

| Year | MLK Day |
|---|---|
| 2026 | Monday, Jan. 19 |
| 2027 | Monday, Jan. 18 |
| 2028 | Monday, Jan. 17 |
| 2029 | Monday, Jan. 15 |
| 2030 | Monday, Jan. 21 |
