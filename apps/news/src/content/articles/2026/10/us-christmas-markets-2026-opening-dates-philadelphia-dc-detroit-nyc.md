**Detroit's Downtown Winter Markets open on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, the District of Columbia (DC) Holiday Market opens Thursday, Nov. 12 and the Christmas Village in Philadelphia opens Saturday, Nov. 21**, according to each market's official website. Leavenworth, Washington, starts its Christmastown festivities Friday, Nov. 27, and Bryant Park's Winter Village in New York City (NYC) is already open. The opening-day calendar, the Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve hours and the closed days are below, with a one-page printable.

## When do US Christmas markets open in 2026?

| Opens | Market | Closes | Days from opening to closing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open now | Bank of America Winter Village at Bryant Park, NYC | Not stated on the site | Not stated |
| Wed., Nov. 11, 2026 | Downtown Detroit Winter Markets, 60 Cadillac Square | Sun., Jan. 3, 2027 | 54 |
| Thu., Nov. 12, 2026 | DC Holiday Market, 1500 block of New Hampshire Avenue NW | Wed., Dec. 23, 2026 | 42 |
| Sat., Nov. 21, 2026 | Christmas Village in Philadelphia, City Hall, 1500 Arch Street | Sun., Dec. 27, 2026 | 37 |
| Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | Leavenworth, Washington, Christmastown festivities | Thu., Dec. 24, 2026 | 28 |

The day counts are our calculation and include both the first and last date. They count closed days inside each run, so they are the length of the season, not the number of open days.

## What are the Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve hours?

| Market | Thanksgiving, Thu., Nov. 26 | Christmas Eve, Thu., Dec. 24 | Christmas Day, Fri., Dec. 25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Philadelphia | 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. | Noon to 5 p.m. | Closed |
| Detroit | 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. | Closed |
| DC | Closed | No special hours listed | Market ended Dec. 23 |
| Leavenworth lights | 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. | Festivities end this day | Lights daily through February, 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. |
| Bryant Park | Not stated | Not stated | Not stated |

Philadelphia's regular hours are Sunday to Thursday noon to 8 p.m. and Friday and Saturday noon to 9 p.m., according to philachristmas.com. Detroit also lists New Year's Eve and New Year's Day as 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. For the wider holiday picture, see [when is Christmas Day](/when-is/christmas-day/).

## Which Christmas market days are closed in 2026?

Closed days are the detail the headline dates hide.

- **Philadelphia:** closed Monday, Nov. 23 to Wednesday, Nov. 25 and on Friday, Dec. 25. The market opens Saturday, Nov. 21, shuts for those three days and reopens on Thanksgiving.
- **DC:** closed Thursday, Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving Day) and Monday, Nov. 30 for exhibitor turnover.
- **Detroit:** closed every Monday and Tuesday, and on Christmas Day.

## What are the Detroit winter market hours?

Decked Out Detroit, operated by Bedrock, lists the Downtown Detroit Winter Markets as open from Nov. 11, 2026 through Jan. 3, 2027 at 60 Cadillac Square. The site does not state an admission price.

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Wednesday and Thursday | 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. |
| Friday and Saturday | 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. |
| Sunday | 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. |
| Monday and Tuesday | Closed |

## What are the DC Holiday Market hours?

The DC Holiday Market, formerly the Downtown Holiday Market, runs Thursday, Nov. 12 through Wednesday, Dec. 23, according to dcholidaymarket.net. It is on the 1500 block of New Hampshire Avenue NW, just north of Dupont Circle. Monday to Friday hours are noon to 8 p.m., and Saturday and Sunday hours are 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. The site does not state an admission price.

## What does the Christmas Village in Philadelphia charge, and when is it open?

Philachristmas.com lists no admission charge. The village is at City Hall, 1500 Arch Street, from Nov. 21 to Dec. 27, 2026.

## What do we know about Bryant Park and Leavenworth?

Bryant Park's site says the Rink, Holiday Shops and The Lodge are open for the 2026-2027 Winter Village season, and Cozy Igloos return on Nov. 5, 2026. It says The Rink is open 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. The page gives no closing date or admission price. Our [NYC holiday calendar for 2026](/news/nyc-holiday-calendar-2026-rockefeller-tree-macys-parade/) covers the other city events.

The Leavenworth Chamber of Commerce lists 2026 Christmastown festivities from Nov. 27 to Dec. 24. The village lights are on from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily, Thanksgiving through February, and are free to enjoy. Concerts at the Festhalle are ticketed at $5 to $15.
