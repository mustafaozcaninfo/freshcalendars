**February 2027 begins on Monday, Feb. 1, with W-2 forms due to employees and the Social Security Administration (SSA), according to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS), and its one federal holiday, Washington's Birthday, falls on Monday, Feb. 15, the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) says.** The month's surprise is the weekend before that holiday: Super Bowl 61 (LXI) and Valentine's Day are both on Sunday, Feb. 14. The countdowns below are counted from Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026. For plain monthly layouts, see the [February 2027 printable calendars](/february-calendars.html).

## February 2027 dates and days until each

February 2027 has 28 days, from a Monday to a Sunday. Religious and lunar dates sit next to the holidays so the month reads in order.

To count from another day, add the days left in the current month to the days in each month before your date. From Oct. 31 there are 0 days left in October, 30 in November, 31 in December and 31 in January, so Feb. 1 is 0 + 30 + 31 + 31 + 1 = 93 days away.

## Super Bowl LXI, Valentine's Day and Presidents' Day in one weekend

Sunday, Feb. 14 holds two fixed dates. Super Bowl LXI is on that day at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, according to the National Football League (NFL), and Valentine's Day is always Feb. 14. The Walt Disney Company says ESPN and ABC carry the traditional presentation and Disney+ livestreams it. Presidents' Day, which OPM lists as Washington's Birthday, follows on Monday, Feb. 15, so federal workers have a three-day weekend. Our guides cover the [Super Bowl LXI date, stadium and channel](/news/super-bowl-lxi-2027-date-sofi-stadium-channel-halftime/), the [NFL playoff schedule](/news/nfl-playoff-schedule-2027-wild-card-divisional-championship-super-bowl-lxi/), [Valentine's Day 2027](/news/valentines-day-2027-sunday-february-14-dates-printables/) and [what is open on Presidents' Day](/news/presidents-day-2027-february-15-whats-open-closed/).

In Canada, Feb. 15 is Family Day in Ontario and Alberta, and Nova Scotia calls the same Monday Heritage Day; see [Family Day 2027](/news/family-day-2027-canada-february-15-provinces-whats-closed/).

## Lunar New Year, Ramadan and Lent in the same week

Three observances start within five days of each other. Lunar New Year's Day is Saturday, Feb. 6, on the Hong Kong Labour Department's 2027 holiday list, and the Lantern Festival closes the season on Saturday, Feb. 20, by the Hong Kong Observatory's conversion table (see [Lunar New Year 2027](/news/lunar-new-year-2027-year-of-the-goat/)). Ramadan is expected to begin on Monday, Feb. 8, as our [Ramadan 2027 calendar](/news/ramadan-2027-calendar-dates-timetable/) explains. Mardi Gras is Tuesday, Feb. 9 and Ash Wednesday, the first day of Lent, is Feb. 10, 46 days before Easter on March 28; the [Mardi Gras and Lent calendar](/news/mardi-gras-2027-february-9-ash-wednesday-february-10-lent-calendar/) has the dates to Easter.

## Moon phases in February 2027

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) lists the new moon on Saturday, Feb. 6 at 10:56 a.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), the same date as Lunar New Year's Day. The full moon follows on Saturday, Feb. 20 at 6:23 p.m. EST.

## Tax forms, Groundhog Day and awards dates

The IRS Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 put the deadline for sending W-2 forms to employees and to the SSA on Monday, Feb. 1, 2027. Groundhog Day is Tuesday, Feb. 2; the Punxsutawney Groundhog Club had not yet posted its 2027 schedule, as our [Groundhog Day 2027 story](/news/groundhog-day-2027-tuesday-february-2-time-where-to-watch/) notes. The 69th Grammy Awards are Sunday, Feb. 7, and final voting for the Oscars begins Thursday, Feb. 25, with the show on March 14, according to the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences. Our [Golden Globes and Oscars 2027 calendar](/news/golden-globes-oscars-2027-dates-nominations-how-to-watch-awards-calendar/) lists every awards date, and the [Grammys 2027 story](/news/grammy-nominations-2027-date-69th-grammys-february-7/) covers Feb. 7.
