New York is **13 hours behind Philippine Standard Time (PHT)** right now, the gap in place since US clocks fell back one hour to standard time at 2 a.m. local time on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026; before that it was 12 hours. The Philippines does not move its clocks, so the whole shift lands on the US side, under the rule from the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). A one-page [printable US-Philippines time converter](/news/downloads/time-change-2026/us-philippines-time-converter-november-2026.pdf) is on this page for your desk.

The Philippines' own abbreviation for its standard time is PhST, also written PST. We use PHT here to avoid confusion with Pacific Standard Time. All the times below come from our own arithmetic on the Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) offsets.

## Does the Philippines change its clocks on Nov. 1, 2026?

No. The Philippines keeps UTC+8 all year. The time zone database that phones and computers use, kept by the Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA), lists the zone Asia/Manila and shows daylight saving time in the Philippines last in 1990, from May 21 to July 28. The Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration (PAGASA) describes itself on its home page as disseminating the Official Philippine Standard Time.

The US side does change. NIST says that "during 2026, daylight saving time is in effect from March 8 at 2 a.m. (local time) to November 1 at 2 a.m. (local time)." Eastern Time (ET) goes from Eastern Daylight Time (EDT, UTC-4) to Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC-5), and the other zones move the same way. See our [report on the US change](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) for the full rule.

## How far ahead of each US zone is Manila?

| US zone | UTC offset until Oct. 31 / from Nov. 1 | Manila ahead until Oct. 31 | Manila ahead from Nov. 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eastern (New York) | UTC-4 / UTC-5 | 12 hours | 13 hours |
| Central (Chicago) | UTC-5 / UTC-6 | 13 hours | 14 hours |
| Mountain (Denver) | UTC-6 / UTC-7 | 14 hours | 15 hours |
| Pacific (Los Angeles) | UTC-7 / UTC-8 | 15 hours | 16 hours |
| Alaska (Anchorage) | UTC-8 / UTC-9 | 16 hours | 17 hours |
| Arizona (Mountain Standard Time all year) | UTC-7 / UTC-7 | 15 hours | 15 hours |
| Hawaii | UTC-10 / UTC-10 | 18 hours | 18 hours |

NIST says that "DST is not observed in Hawaii, American Samoa, Guam, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands and the state of Arizona" with the exception of the Navajo Indian Reservation, which does observe it. So Arizona and Hawaii keep their gap with Manila, apart from the Navajo Nation in Arizona, which follows the Nov. 1 change.

## What time is it in the Philippines when it is 9 a.m. in California?

From Nov. 1, 2026, 9 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) is 1 a.m. PHT on the next day. For New York, 9 a.m. ET is 10 p.m. PHT on the same day. The table converts common times and shows where the date flips (each row checked against the UTC offsets, valid from Nov. 1).

| US time | Philippines time (PHT) |
|---|---|
| 7 a.m. ET | 8 p.m., same day |
| 9 a.m. ET | 10 p.m., same day |
| 12 p.m. ET | 1 a.m., next day |
| 6 p.m. ET | 7 a.m., next day |
| 8 p.m. ET | 9 a.m., next day |
| 10 p.m. ET | 11 a.m., next day |
| 7 a.m. PT | 11 p.m., same day |
| 9 a.m. PT | 1 a.m., next day |

Going the other way, 8 a.m. PHT is 7 p.m. ET on the evening before and 4 p.m. PT the day before. A call at 8 p.m. PHT is 7 a.m. ET and 4 a.m. PT on the same calendar day.

## Which hours work for a call with the Philippines?

Manila's business week mostly runs Monday to Friday, so we take 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. PHT as the window to reach the Philippines (our assumption for the guide, not an official rule). These are the US hours inside it now:

| US zone | Hours inside the window |
|---|---|
| Eastern | 8 p.m. ET to 8 a.m. ET the next morning |
| Pacific | 5 p.m. PT to 5 a.m. PT the next morning |

Through the summer this Manila window sat one hour later on US clocks, so a Pacific or Eastern team's usual slot has moved an hour earlier for the standard-time stretch.

A meeting pinned to Manila time slides on the US calendar. A standing call at 8 p.m. PHT sat at 8 a.m. ET until Oct. 31 and is at 7 a.m. ET from Nov. 1. A phone in Manila with automatic time zone on keeps up by itself, yet a US-side invite saved to a fixed hour wants a second look. For another zone with no clock change, see the [US-India time-difference story](/news/us-india-time-difference-after-november-1-2026-ist-10-5-hours/).

## A full Manila workday in US clock times

A 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Manila business day (Monday to Friday, our assumption for the guide, not an official rule) reads off the four main US zones as below. The US times come from the offsets in the first table, and "day before" is the previous US calendar day.

| Manila business day (PHT) | New York (ET) | Chicago (CT) | Denver (MT) | Los Angeles (PT) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 a.m. (start) | 8 p.m., day before | 7 p.m., day before | 6 p.m., day before | 5 p.m., day before |
| 1 p.m. (midday) | 12 a.m. (midnight) | 11 p.m., day before | 10 p.m., day before | 9 p.m., day before |
| 5 p.m. (close) | 4 a.m. | 3 a.m. | 2 a.m. | 1 a.m. |

A Manila morning therefore falls on the US evening before, which is why many US-Philippines teams meet near the start or end of one side's day.

## When does the gap with Manila shrink again?

Under 15 U.S.C. 260a, daylight saving time starts on the second Sunday of March, which is Sunday, March 14, 2027 (our calculation). New York will then be 12 hours behind PHT again. Australia moves its clocks in the other direction, as the [US-Australia time-difference story](/news/us-australia-time-difference-after-daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/) explains.

## Philippine holidays before March 14, 2027: when Manila is off
Two presidential proclamations set the dates, as published by the Lawphil Project: Proclamation No. 1006 of Sept. 3, 2025 for 2026, and Proclamation No. 1427 of Sept. 8, 2026 for 2027. Between now and the U.S. return to daylight saving time on Sunday, March 14, 2027, they list:

| Date | Holiday in the Philippines | Type | Day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 30, 2026 | Bonifacio Day | Regular holiday | Monday |
| Dec. 8, 2026 | Feast of the Immaculate Conception | Special non-working day | Tuesday |
| Dec. 24, 2026 | Christmas Eve | Special non-working day | Thursday |
| Dec. 25, 2026 | Christmas Day | Regular holiday | Friday |
| Dec. 30, 2026 | Rizal Day | Regular holiday | Wednesday |
| Dec. 31, 2026 | Last Day of the Year | Special non-working day | Thursday |
| Jan. 1, 2027 | New Year's Day | Regular holiday | Friday |

Chinese New Year 2027 and the Eid holidays of 2027 are not yet proclaimed, so they are not on the list.

- Skip Manila on the seven dates above when you plan a late-year call.
- Late December is crowded: Manila is off on Thu., Dec. 24, Fri., Dec. 25, Wed., Dec. 30 and Thu., Dec. 31, then Fri., Jan. 1, 2027.
- Mark Sun., March 14, 2027 as the day the gap with New York shrinks to 12 hours.
