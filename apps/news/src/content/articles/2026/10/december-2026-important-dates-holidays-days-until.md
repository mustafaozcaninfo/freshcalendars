**December 2026 begins on Tuesday, Dec. 1, and its one federal holiday, Christmas Day, falls on Friday, Dec. 25, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** Hanukkah starts at sundown on Friday, Dec. 4, the winter solstice arrives on Monday, Dec. 21, and the month closes with New Year's Eve on a Thursday. It is also a month of deadlines: Medicare open enrollment ends Dec. 7, federal funding runs out after Dec. 11, and the Postal Service's last recommended Christmas shipping dates fall between Dec. 9 and Dec. 19. The countdowns below are counted from Saturday, Oct. 24, 2026.

## Hanukkah, Dec. 4 to 12

Hanukkah 2026 runs from sundown on Friday, Dec. 4 to nightfall on Saturday, Dec. 12, on 25 Kislev to 2 Tevet in the Hebrew calendar. The first and last candle-lighting nights both fall on a Friday, so the menorah is lit before the Shabbat candles on those evenings. Our [Hanukkah 2026 calendar](/news/hanukkah-2026-dates-calendar/) has the nightly schedule and four printables.

## The sky: Geminids, the solstice and a supermoon

The Geminid meteor shower peaks the night of Dec. 13-14 with the Moon only about 21% lit, a good year to watch. The winter solstice, the shortest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere, comes on Monday, Dec. 21, at 3:50 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), the U.S. Naval Observatory says. Two days later, on Wednesday, Dec. 23, the Cold Moon is full at 8:28 p.m. EST; it is the closest full moon of 2026, a supermoon. Our [supermoon and solstice guide](/news/supermoon-2026-beaver-moon-cold-moon-geminids-solstice/) has viewing times.

## Deadlines that land in December

- **Dec. 7, Medicare:** open enrollment, which began Oct. 15, ends on Monday; changes take effect Jan. 1, 2027. Details are in our [Medicare open enrollment story](/news/medicare-open-enrollment-2026-december-7-90-dollar-rebate/).
- **Dec. 11, federal funding:** agencies are funded through Friday, Dec. 11, under the stopgap law signed Sept. 2. Our report on the [December funding deadline](/news/government-funding-deadline-december-11-2026-shutdown/) explains what lapses if Congress does not act.
- **Dec. 15, HealthCare.gov:** pick a Marketplace plan by Tuesday for coverage that starts Jan. 1. Open enrollment continues to Jan. 15, with coverage from Feb. 1; see our [Affordable Care Act (ACA) open enrollment guide](/news/aca-open-enrollment-2027-dates-november-1-january-15/).
- **Dec. 9 to 19, holiday mail:** the Postal Service recommends Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail by Dec. 17, Priority Mail by Dec. 18 and Priority Mail Express by Dec. 19 for delivery by Christmas in the lower 48 states. Every date is in our [USPS holiday shipping deadlines](/news/usps-holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026/) story.

## Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve

Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, is a federal holiday, so federal offices, banks and post offices close and many workers get a three-day weekend. Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, is not a federal holiday, though presidents closed most federal offices that day by executive order in five recent years; none had been issued for 2026 as of Oct. 23, as our report on whether [Christmas Eve is a federal holiday](/news/is-christmas-eve-a-federal-holiday-2026/) explains. The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) closes early at 1 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Christmas Eve and stays shut on Christmas Day. New Year's Eve falls on Thursday, Dec. 31, and New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, is the next federal holiday.
