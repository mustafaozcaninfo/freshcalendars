<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 26, 2026.</b> The Army athletics page blocked automated access, so we read its kickoff and CBS listing through a search summary. We will confirm it against the page itself.</p>

**The 127th Army-Navy Game is set for Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, at 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on CBS at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, according to Army West Point Athletics.** The department lists the game, presented by United Services Automobile Association (USAA), in its May 27, 2026 kickoff and television announcement. Below: the time in each zone, including overseas bases, and what the sources leave open.

## What is the Army-Navy Game 2026 date and time?

Two sources give the date. Only one gives a kickoff.

| Item | What the source lists | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026 | Army West Point Athletics |
| Kickoff | 3 p.m. ET | Army West Point Athletics |
| Channel | CBS | Army West Point Athletics |
| Place | MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, New Jersey | Army West Point Athletics |
| Event window | Dec. 12, 3 to 7 p.m., East Rutherford, N.J. | U.S. Naval Academy (USNA) |

The USNA Public Affairs Office lists the game as an event from 3 to 7 p.m. That is a window, not a stated kickoff. The 3 p.m. kickoff comes from Army's list.

## What time is the Army-Navy Game in each time zone?

Dec. 12 falls on standard time in the United States, so the 3 p.m. ET start falls on Eastern Standard Time. The athletics department gives only the ET time. Every other row is our conversion.

| Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | United Kingdom | 8 p.m. |
| Central European Time (CET) | Germany | 9 p.m. |
| Japan Standard Time (JST) | Japan | 5 a.m., Sun., Dec. 13 |
| Korea Standard Time (KST) | South Korea | 5 a.m., Sun., Dec. 13 |

The last four rows are for service members abroad. In Japan and South Korea the start falls on the next calendar day, Sunday, Dec. 13.

## What channel is the Army-Navy Game on?

Army West Point Athletics lists CBS. Its announcement is titled "Army Football Announces 2026 Kickoff Times and TV Networks; Features Four National Broadcasts," and it lists the Army-Navy Game among them. A streaming option was not announced in the sources we read, so we do not name one. Check CBS before game day.

## Where is the 2026 Army-Navy Game?

At MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Both the Army athletics announcement and the USNA event page name East Rutherford.

## Who won the Army-Navy Game last year?

As reported by The American Legion, Navy beat Army 17-16 in the 126th game on Saturday, Dec. 13, 2025, at M&T Bank Stadium in Baltimore. The Legion also reports that Navy kept the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy.
