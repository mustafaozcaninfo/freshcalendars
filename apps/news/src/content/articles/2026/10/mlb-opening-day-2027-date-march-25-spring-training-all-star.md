**Traditional Major League Baseball (MLB) Opening Day 2027 is Thursday, Mar. 25, with 14 games on the schedule, according to MLB.com's July 16, 2026 schedule release.** The season is slated to begin a day earlier, on Wednesday, Mar. 24, with an Opening Night game streamed live on Netflix. MLB.com lists that matchup as to be determined.

## Is Opening Day on March 24 or March 25?

Both dates appear in the release, and they are different events. Opening Night is Wednesday, Mar. 24. Traditional Opening Day is Thursday, Mar. 25. If you are planning a day off or a watch party around the full slate of games, Mar. 25 is the date MLB.com gives for 14 games.

## What are the key MLB dates for 2027?

All dates below come from MLB.com's July 16, 2026 schedule release and MLB's Sept. 4, 2026 Spring Training release. The All-Star location also matches MLB's All-Star page.

| Date | Event | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Feb. 19, 2027 | Spring Training begins | All 30 clubs in action |
| Sat., Mar. 20 to Tue., Mar. 23, 2027 | Exhibition games | A number of games will be played, per MLB |
| Wed., Mar. 24, 2027 | Opening Night | One game, live on Netflix; matchup to be determined |
| Thu., Mar. 25, 2027 | Traditional Opening Day | 14 games |
| Mon., July 12, 2027 | T-Mobile Home Run Derby | Streamed exclusively on Netflix |
| Tue., July 13, 2027 | 97th MLB All-Star Game | Wrigley Field, Chicago; exclusively on FOX |

## When does Spring Training 2027 start?

MLB's Sept. 4, 2026 press release says 2027 Spring Training will officially begin on Friday, Feb. 19, with all 30 clubs in action. The release describes games across Arizona and Florida. It also says a number of exhibition games will be played from Saturday, Mar. 20, through Tuesday, Mar. 23, the days before Opening Night.

## When and where is the 2027 All-Star Game?

The 97th MLB All-Star Game, presented by Mastercard, is set for Tuesday, July 13, 2027, at Wrigley Field in Chicago, and it airs exclusively on FOX, according to MLB.com. The MLB All-Star page also lists Wrigley Field and July 13, 2027. The T-Mobile Home Run Derby is the night before, Monday, July 12, streamed exclusively on Netflix.

## What has MLB not announced yet?

The sources we read do not give the Opening Night matchup, the date the regular season ends or when the postseason starts. They also do not name an international opener for 2027 or give report dates for pitchers and catchers. We will update this story when MLB publishes them.
