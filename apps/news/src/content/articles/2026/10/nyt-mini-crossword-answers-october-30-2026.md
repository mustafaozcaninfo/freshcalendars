**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Sam Ezersky.** With Halloween one day away, three of the Across clues begin with a vampire, a ghost and a skeleton. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (3 letters), *Vampire ___ (flying mammal)*: a winged animal that sleeps hanging upside down; the same word names a baseball tool.
- **4-Across** (4 letters), *Ghost ___ (abandoned place)*: a settlement bigger than a village, here one everybody has left.
- **5-Across** (5 letters), *Land on the northern edge of Mount Everest*: a high plateau region of China whose capital is Lhasa.
- **6-Across** (4 letters), *Shapes of Whopper candies*: round balls, plural; a fancier word for spheres.
- **7-Across** (3 letters), *Skeleton ___ (super's item)*: a building superintendent carries one that opens many locks.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Celebrity chef Flay*: his first name, the familiar form of Robert.
- **2-Down** (4 letters), *Amazes*: fills with wonder; the verb ends in S.
- **3-Down** (3 letters), *Explosive material*: three capital letters that stand for a long chemical name.
- **4-Down** (4 letters), *Something attached to a rope for a tree swing*: an old rubber wheel cover from a car.
- **5-Down** (3 letters), *Tik___*: the end of a short-video app's name, which repeats its first half with one letter changed.

## Solving tips

Start with the three Halloween clues, 1-Across, 4-Across and 7-Across, since each is a fill-in-the-blank phrase. 5-Across, the middle row, crosses every Down answer, so a geography guess there checks the whole grid. Leave 1-Down for last: it is the only five-letter Down answer, and the Across words already give you most of its letters.
