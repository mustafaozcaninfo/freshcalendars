**Valentine's Day 2027 falls on Sunday, Feb. 14, and the next day, Monday, Feb. 15, is Presidents' Day**, which the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists as Washington's Birthday. For federal workers that makes a three-day weekend. This story gives the weekday for each year through 2032, the dates around the holiday and a free printable to plan the week. For the holiday itself, see the [February printable calendars](/february-calendars.html).

## Valentine's Day weekdays from 2027 to 2032

Valentine's Day is always Feb. 14, so only the weekday changes. These weekdays are computed from the calendar.

| Year | Valentine's Day | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Feb. 14 | Sunday |
| 2028 | Feb. 14 | Monday |
| 2029 | Feb. 14 | Wednesday |
| 2030 | Feb. 14 | Thursday |
| 2031 | Feb. 14 | Friday |
| 2032 | Feb. 14 | Saturday |

## Dates around Valentine's Day 2027

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| Wed., Feb. 10 | Ash Wednesday, 46 days before Easter Sunday (March 28, 2027) |
| Sat., Feb. 13 | The day before Valentine's Day |
| Sun., Feb. 14 | Valentine's Day |
| Mon., Feb. 15 | Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day), a federal holiday per OPM |

Ash Wednesday is calendar arithmetic: Easter Sunday falls on March 28, 2027, and Ash Wednesday comes 46 days earlier. The Presidents' Day date is from the OPM federal holiday list. Our [Presidents' Day 2027 story](/news/presidents-day-2027-february-15-whats-open-closed/) covers what is open and closed that Monday.

## Valentine's weekend 2027 plan

The printable below turns those dates into a checklist: order flowers and cards by Wednesday, Feb. 10, book or plan dinner for Saturday, Feb. 13 or Sunday, Feb. 14, and note the Monday, Feb. 15 holiday. A [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) works for the rest of the week.

## What the National Retail Federation said last year

These are 2026 figures, not 2027. In its Jan. 27, 2026 release, the National Retail Federation (NRF) said consumer spending on Valentine's Day was expected to reach a record $29.1 billion. Shoppers were budgeting a record $199.78 on average for gifts, and 55% of consumers planned to celebrate. Pet gifts were projected at $2.1 billion, up from $1.7 billion in 2025. The NRF survey was conducted Jan. 2-8, 2026.

The NRF has not yet released a 2027 survey. Last year it came out Jan. 27.
