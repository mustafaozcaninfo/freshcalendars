**Super Bowl LXI (61) will be played at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, in February 2027, according to the National Football League (NFL) on NFL.com.** ESPN and the American Broadcasting Company (ABC) will air it. The league has not yet announced the day, the kickoff time or the halftime headliner.

This page sorts what is confirmed from what is still open, and a one-page planner at the end lets you keep both lists.

## What has the NFL confirmed about Super Bowl 2027?

NFL.com says Super Bowl LXI in February 2027 "will be played at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood." It will be the second Super Bowl at SoFi Stadium. The table separates confirmed facts from open ones, as of Oct. 25, 2026.

## When is the Super Bowl LXI date?

The NFL pages we checked say only "February 2027." Many schedules list Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027, but we could not confirm that on the league's own pages, so treat it as a planning date until the NFL posts it.

## What channel is the Super Bowl 2027 on?

In a May 12, 2026, release, ESPN Press Room said: "ESPN and ABC will air the English-language telecast." The same release says ESPN Deportes and Univision each produce a Spanish-language telecast. The NFL has not announced a kickoff time.

## Who is the Super Bowl 2027 halftime performer?

The NFL has not announced a headliner. Puck and Deadline reported on Oct. 20, 2026, that Jay-Z is in line to headline. Neither the NFL nor Roc Nation had confirmed it when we checked on Oct. 25, 2026. Treat the report as unconfirmed until the league says otherwise.

## What happened to the Pro Bowl game?

No. In August 2026 the NFL said the American Football Conference (AFC) versus National Football Conference (NFC) Pro Bowl game will no longer be played. The league said the 2027 Pro Bowl class will be recognized "during the traditional Pro Bowl weekend in Los Angeles," and that the roster reveal comes in "late December." The NFL's page does not give a day for either.
