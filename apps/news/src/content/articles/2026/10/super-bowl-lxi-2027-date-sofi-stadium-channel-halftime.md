**Super Bowl LXI (61) will be played at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, on Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027, according to the National Football League (NFL) on NFL.com.** ESPN and the American Broadcasting Company (ABC) will air it. The league has not yet announced the kickoff time or the halftime headliner.

This page sorts what is confirmed from what is still open, and a one-page planner at the end lets you keep both lists.

NFL.com says Super Bowl LXI "will be played at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood." It will be the second Super Bowl at SoFi Stadium. The table separates confirmed facts from open ones, as of Oct. 31, 2026.

The NFL's "2026-27 National Football League important dates" lists "February 14 - Super Bowl LXI at SoFi Stadium (Inglewood, California)." Feb. 14, 2027, is a Sunday.

## What channel is the Super Bowl 2027 on?

In a May 12, 2026, release, ESPN Press Room said: "ESPN and ABC will air the English-language telecast." The same release says ESPN Deportes and Univision each produce a Spanish-language telecast. In an Oct. 7, 2026, release, The Walt Disney Company said Disney+ will livestream Super Bowl LXI on Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027, with "the traditional game presentation airing on ESPN and ABC." The NFL has not announced a kickoff time.

## Who is the Super Bowl 2027 halftime performer?

The NFL has not announced a headliner. Puck and Deadline reported on Oct. 20, 2026, that Jay-Z is in line to headline. Neither the NFL nor Roc Nation had confirmed it when we checked on Oct. 25, 2026. Treat the report as unconfirmed until the league says otherwise.

## What happened to the Pro Bowl game?

No. In August 2026 the NFL said the American Football Conference (AFC) versus National Football Conference (NFC) Pro Bowl game will no longer be played. The league said the 2027 Pro Bowl class will be recognized "during the traditional Pro Bowl weekend in Los Angeles," and that the roster reveal comes in "late December." The NFL's page does not give a day for either.
