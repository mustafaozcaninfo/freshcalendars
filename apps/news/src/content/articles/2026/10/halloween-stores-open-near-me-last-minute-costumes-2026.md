Spirit's main site did not respond to our check on Oct. 29 (it returned a server error), so we could not confirm 2026 Halloween-day hours, a closing date or a shipping cutoff. This story gives you a four-day plan, the one big 2026 change in party-store shopping, and the costume safety checks from the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). A one-page checklist follows the same plan.

## What should I do each day before Halloween?

Thursday, Oct. 29 through Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026 is the whole window. Use each day for one job.

| Day | What to check |
|---|---|
| Thu., Oct. 29 | Find stores on the locator. Open the exact store page for hours. Call to confirm your size and whether the item is on the shelf. Test makeup on a small patch of skin: the FDA says to test at least 24 to 48 hours ahead, and Thursday is the last test that fits 48 hours before Saturday. |
| Fri., Oct. 30 | Buy. Confirm any pickup ready time. Check what is in the package (wig, props). Look for "flame resistant" on the label of the costume, wig and accessories. |
| Sat., Oct. 31 (Halloween) | Check the store's Saturday hours on its own page before leaving. Confirm trick-or-treat times with your town; our [sunset and dark times](/news/halloween-2026-saturday-sunset-trick-or-treat-times/) cover 16 cities. |
| Sun., Nov. 1 | Ask the store about seasonal return terms if something did not fit or was missing. |

## Is Party City open, and what is inside Staples?

After its December 2024 bankruptcy filing, Party City wound down its stores. In 2026 it came back inside another retailer. In a joint release on Apr. 21, 2026, distributed by Business Wire, Staples and Party City announced a partnership "bringing Party City inside more than 700 Staples locations nationwide and on Staples.com."

Not every Staples has it. Check the store before you go, and confirm the costume you want is on its shelf, not only on its website.

## What about Halloween City, Target and Walmart?

We could not confirm Halloween City's 2026 status. Target and Walmart have not published Halloween hours in a source we could check, so look at each store's own page for Saturday, Oct. 31. We give no hours for them.

## What should I check when buying in a hurry?

- An online "in stock" label does not guarantee the item is on the shelf. Call.
- If you order for pickup, confirm the ready time.
- Check what is included: confirm whether the wig, props and accessories come with the costume.
- Ask about return terms for seasonal items.
- A costume from clothes you already own is a fast option when stores are out of your size.

## Which costume safety checks apply?

The AAP, in "Halloween Fun & Safety Tips for Kids of All Ages" on HealthyChildren.org (last updated Sept. 29, 2026), says:

- "Costumes should be short enough to prevent tripping and avoid catching on objects."
- "Look for 'flame resistant' on costume labels, and check wigs and accessories too."
- "Test makeup ahead of time on a small patch of your child's skin."
- "Consider non-toxic makeup and decorative hats as safer alternatives to masks."
- "Do not use decorative contact lenses without an eye exam and a prescription."

The FDA's Halloween page (content current as of Oct. 19, 2023) adds that decorative contact lenses sold without a prescription in costume or other retail stores are illegal. It also says: "Test the makeup you plan to use at least 24-48 hours in advance."
