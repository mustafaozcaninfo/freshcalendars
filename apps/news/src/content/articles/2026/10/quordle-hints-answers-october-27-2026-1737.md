## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**The word that starts with A**

- Letters: starts with A, ends with E, and has a K in the middle.
- Meaning: the joint between your foot and your leg.

**The word that starts with H**

- Letters: starts with H, ends with C.
- Meaning: widespread chaos or damage, as in "wreak ___."

**The word that starts with S**

- Letters: starts with S, ends with Y, and U is its only vowel.
- Meaning: quietly bad-tempered, the mood of someone who did not get their way.

**The word that starts with W**

- Letters: starts with W, ends with E.
- Meaning: the color of fresh snow.

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess such as Whale, with W, H, A, L and E, puts at least one letter into every board. A second guess such as Snuck, with S, N, U, C and K, fills in much of the rest. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
