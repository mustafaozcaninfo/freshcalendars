**Qatar (round 20) is over, and two rounds remain in the 2026 MotoGP season, according to MotoGP's official calendar: Portugal on Nov. 20-22 and the Valencia finale on Nov. 27-29.** Marc Marquez leads the championship by 60 points with 74 left, so the title is not yet decided. The table below converts every remaining MotoGP session to Eastern Time (ET), Pacific Time (PT) and UK time. Our [MotoGP Malaysia 2026 race time story](/news/motogp-malaysia-2026-race-time-sprint-schedule-us-time-zones/) has the full Sepang schedule.

## Which MotoGP rounds are left in 2026?

MotoGP's calendar, published by Dorna Sports, ends with 22 rounds. After Malaysia (round 19, Petronas Sepang International Circuit, Oct. 30-Nov. 1) the season continued with Qatar and finishes as follows.

| Round | Grand Prix | Circuit | Dates, 2026 | Local time zone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | Qatar Airways Grand Prix of Qatar (run) | Lusail International Circuit | Nov. 6-8 | UTC+3 |
| 21 | Repsol Grand Prix of Portugal | Autódromo Internacional do Algarve (Portimão) | Nov. 20-22 | UTC+0 |
| 22 | Motul Grand Prix of Valencia | Circuit Ricardo Tormo | Nov. 27-29 | UTC+1 |

UTC is Coordinated Universal Time. Portimão runs on UK time in late November, so Portugal's local times are the UK times.

## What happened at the MotoGP Qatar round?

Marco Bezzecchi (Aprilia Racing) won the Qatar Airways Grand Prix of Qatar at Lusail on Sunday, Nov. 8, MotoGP reported. Marc Marquez (Ducati Lenovo Team) was second and Raul Fernandez (SuperFile Trackhouse MotoGP Team) third. Jorge Martin (Aprilia Racing) finished seventh, two weeks after breaking his wrist in Australia. Marquez won the Sprint on Saturday, Nov. 7, with Fabio Di Giannantonio (Pertamina Enduro VR46 Racing Team) second and Martin third; Bezzecchi crashed out of the Sprint after contact with Marquez.

| Qatar result | Sprint, Nov. 7 | Race, Nov. 8 |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Marc Marquez | Marco Bezzecchi |
| 2nd | Fabio Di Giannantonio | Marc Marquez |
| 3rd | Jorge Martin | Raul Fernandez |

Qatar was postponed from earlier in 2026. Crash.net reported on Mar. 16, 2026, that the round was "postponed due to the ongoing geopolitical situation in the Middle East," and that the Portuguese round moved to Nov. 20-22, "the original Valencia date," with Valencia shifting to Nov. 27-29.

## What time is the MotoGP Portugal race?

The Repsol Grand Prix of Portugal at Portimão starts at 13:00 local time on Sunday, Nov. 22, per MotoGP. That is 8 a.m. ET, 5 a.m. PT and 1 p.m. UK time. The Sprint is at 15:00 on Saturday, Nov. 21, which is 10 a.m. ET, and Q2 is at 11:15 local time, 6:15 a.m. ET.

## What time is the Valencia finale?

The Motul Grand Prix of Valencia at Circuit Ricardo Tormo starts at 14:00 local time (UTC+1) on Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026, the last race of the 22-round calendar. That is 8 a.m. ET, 5 a.m. PT and 1 p.m. UK time. The Sprint is Saturday, Nov. 28, at 3 p.m. in Spain, or 9 a.m. ET.

In 2025, the Valencia round was run as the Motul Grand Prix of the Valencian Community, with the race on Nov. 16, according to MotoGP's video archive. This year's finale falls on Nov. 29, the Sunday after Thanksgiving in the U.S.

The Valencia weekend is Thanksgiving weekend in the United States. Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, and Free Practice 1 is the next morning, Black Friday, Nov. 27, at 4:45 a.m. ET. Our [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) page has the holiday date.

## Is the 2026 MotoGP title still open?

Yes, but Marc Marquez can end it in Portugal. MotoGP reports he leads Jorge Martin by 60 points and Marco Bezzecchi by 74, with 74 points left: 37 at each of the two remaining rounds. MotoGP's report on the Qatar race says that if Marquez wins the Sprint in Portimão on Saturday, Nov. 21, he is crowned 2026 World Champion.

Martin's route, per MotoGP: he needs to outscore Marquez by 24 points over the Portugal weekend to keep the fight alive for Valencia, and countback does not help him, because Marquez has seven Grand Prix wins to his one. If Marquez does not clinch in the Sprint, Sunday's race on Nov. 22 is the next chance.

## Where can you watch the last two MotoGP rounds?

MotoGP's own VideoPass streams every session, according to motogp.com. On Nov. 12 MotoGP announced that ESPN becomes its exclusive U.S. rights holder from the 2027 season. A 2026 U.S. or UK broadcaster for Portugal and Valencia is not named in the pages we read, so we name no channel. Times can also change, so check motogp.com before you set an alarm for a 4 a.m. start.
