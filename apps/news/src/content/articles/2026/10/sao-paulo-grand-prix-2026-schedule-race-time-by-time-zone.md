<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 26, 2026.</b> Times below come from the Formula 1 (F1) timetable as of that date. F1 says the timetable is subject to change.</p>

**The São Paulo Grand Prix race is Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, at 14:00 local time, which is 12 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), according to Formula 1 (F1).** F1 lists the race as 71 laps or 120 minutes. This is the Brazil round of the 2026 season, Round 20 on F1's calendar. Below: every session in U.S. and other time zones, and what comes next.

## What is the São Paulo Grand Prix 2026 schedule?

F1 gives these times in São Paulo local time, Brasília Time, which is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) minus 3 hours (UTC-3). F1's page says São Paulo is 3 hours behind UTC. The other columns are our conversion, not an official schedule. U.S. clocks fall back on Nov. 1, so all five sessions fall on U.S. standard time: EST, Central Standard Time (CST), Mountain Standard Time (MST) and Pacific Standard Time (PST).

| Session | São Paulo (UTC-3) | Eastern (EST) | Central (CST) | Mountain (MST) | Pacific (PST) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| First practice, Fri., Nov. 6 | 12:30 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. | 10:30 a.m. | 9:30 a.m. | 8:30 a.m. | 7:30 a.m. |
| Second practice, Fri., Nov. 6 | 4 p.m. to 5 p.m. | 2 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. |
| Third practice, Sat., Nov. 7 | 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. | 9:30 a.m. | 8:30 a.m. | 7:30 a.m. | 6:30 a.m. |
| Qualifying, Sat., Nov. 7 | 3 p.m. to 4 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Grand Prix, Sun., Nov. 8 | 2 p.m. | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. | 9 a.m. |

The U.S. columns show the start time. Each practice session and qualifying runs one hour, so add one hour for the end.

## What time is the Brazil Grand Prix race on Sunday?

The race starts at 2 p.m. in São Paulo on Sunday, Nov. 8. By our conversion that is 12 p.m. EST, 11 a.m. CST, 10 a.m. MST and 9 a.m. PST. Elsewhere, it is 17:00 Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in the United Kingdom and 18:00 Central European Time (CET) in central Europe. In Sydney the race falls on Monday, Nov. 9, at 4 a.m. Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT).

F1 lists the race as 71 laps or 120 minutes, on a lap of 4.309 km.

## Is there a sprint race at the São Paulo Grand Prix?

No. F1's timetable for the weekend lists three practice sessions, qualifying and the Grand Prix, with no F1 sprint. The only other races listed are support races: the Porsche Sprint Challenge Brazil and the Porsche Carrera Cup.

## Where can you watch the São Paulo Grand Prix in the U.S.?

On Oct. 21, 2026, formula1.com published a streaming guide that said: "Apple – Formula 1's new Official US Broadcaster – will host F1 TV included for free within its Apple TV subscription." By that account, U.S. viewers stream F1 sessions through Apple TV, which needs a subscription. Check the app for the start of coverage.
