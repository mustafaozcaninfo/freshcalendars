## Why is the time gap different on Saturday, Sunday and Monday?

The UK went back to GMT on Oct. 25, 2026, according to GOV.UK. U.S. clocks went back on Sunday, Nov. 1 at 2 a.m., when daylight saving time ended (see [when daylight saving time ends on Nov. 1, 2026](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/)). So the UK was 4 hours ahead of ET on Saturday and 5 hours ahead on Sunday and Monday.

That is why the same 8pm UK start is 4 p.m. ET on Saturday (Aston Villa v Fulham) and 3 p.m. ET on Monday (Newcastle United v Everton). Our [guide to the UK and Europe clock change](/news/uk-europe-clocks-go-back-october-25-2026-time-difference/) explains the week in between.

## What time was Chelsea vs Man United?

Chelsea v Manchester United was the first match of the weekend, on Saturday, Oct. 31 at 12:30pm UK time, per the Premier League. Chelsea won 2-0. That was 8:30 a.m. ET and 5:30 a.m. PT. The league listed TNT Sports in the UK; NBC Sports listed USA Network in the US.

## What time was Liverpool vs Arsenal?

Liverpool v Arsenal was played on Sunday, Nov. 1 at 4:30pm UK time on Sky Sports, per the Premier League. Arsenal won 1-0. U.S. clocks went back at 2 a.m. that day, so the kick-off was 11:30 a.m. ET and 8:30 a.m. PT. NBC Sports listed the match on Peacock.

## What time was Newcastle vs Everton, and what channel?

Newcastle United hosted Everton at St James' Park on Monday, Nov. 2 at 8pm UK time, on Sky Sports, per the Premier League. Everton won 1-0. That was 3 p.m. ET and 12 p.m. PT, and NBC Sports listed USA Network. The sides had last met on Saturday, Feb. 28, 2026 at St James' Park, where Everton won 3-2, according to Newcastle United's match report.

## What was on TV in the UK this weekend?

The Premier League listed five matches with a UK broadcaster: Chelsea v Manchester United on TNT Sports, and Tottenham Hotspur v Crystal Palace, Liverpool v Arsenal, Aston Villa v Fulham and Newcastle United v Everton on Sky Sports. That was one TNT Sports match and four Sky Sports matches. The next round opened on Friday, Nov. 6 at 8pm UK time with Everton v Coventry City on Sky Sports, 3 p.m. ET; Everton won 3-1, per the Premier League. The round after that starts on Saturday, Nov. 21 with Manchester City v Fulham at 12:30pm UK time, according to the league's fixture list.
