## When do the College Football Playoff rankings come out?

The committee reveals its rankings six times. The first five land on Tuesday nights; Selection Day moves to a Sunday afternoon. All of them air on ESPN in Eastern Time, per NCAA.com.

<p class="days-live" data-days-until="2026" hidden></p>

| Date | Day | CFP rankings show | Time (Eastern) | Days from Oct. 24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 3 | Tuesday | First rankings | 7 to 8 p.m. | 10 |
| Nov. 10 | Tuesday | Weekly rankings | 9 to 9:30 p.m. | 17 |
| Nov. 17 | Tuesday | Weekly rankings | 9 to 9:30 p.m. | 24 |
| Nov. 24 | Tuesday | Weekly rankings | 7:30 to 8 p.m. | 31 |
| Dec. 1 | Tuesday | Weekly rankings | 7 to 7:30 p.m. | 38 |
| Dec. 6 | Sunday | Selection Day: final rankings and bracket | Noon to 3 p.m. | 43 |

The first show carries the most suspense because it is the committee's opening look, which can differ from the polls. Last season the committee's first No. 1 was Ohio State, released Nov. 4, 2025, while Indiana ended up No. 1 on Selection Day, Dec. 7, 2025, according to NCAA.com.

## How the 12-team field is chosen

This is the third year of the 12-team playoff. The bracket takes the champions of the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC), Big Ten, Big 12 and Southeastern Conference (SEC), plus the highest-ranked champion among the Group of Five leagues, which are the American, Conference USA, the Mid-American Conference (MAC), the Mountain West, the Pac-12 and the Sun Belt. The next seven highest-ranked teams fill the rest of the field, per fbschedules.com.

The 13-member selection committee, chaired this season by Arkansas athletic director Hunter Yurachek, weighs results against ranked opponents, head-to-head games, conference championships and strength of schedule. The weekly shows explain the thinking before the bracket is locked on Selection Day.

## College Football Playoff schedule 2026-27

Selection Day on Dec. 6 seeds the bracket. First-round games are played at the home stadiums of the higher seeds; the quarterfinals and semifinals rotate through the New Year's Six bowls, and the title game is at a neutral site.

The quarterfinal and semifinal bowls listed here are the designated playoff sites; kickoff times for each game depend on the matchups set on Selection Day.
