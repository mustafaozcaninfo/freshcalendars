**The Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payment dated Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, is November's payment moved early because Nov. 1 is a Sunday. It is not an extra check.** No SSI payment is dated in November; the next one after Oct. 30 is Tuesday, Dec. 1. The month's Social Security dates are Nov. 3, Nov. 10 (moved up from Veterans Day on Nov. 11), Nov. 18 and Nov. 25. Before then, the next regular Social Security payday is Wednesday, Oct. 28, for people born on the 21st through the 31st. Every date below is from the Social Security Administration (SSA) published 2026 schedule.

## Who gets paid next

If your birthday is on the 21st through the 31st, your October payment is dated Wednesday, Oct. 28, the fourth Wednesday of the month. That applies to retirement, survivor and disability benefits for people who started receiving Social Security after April 1997 and do not also get SSI.

The October payment is your benefit for September: Social Security pays each month's benefit in the following month.

## October 2026 payment schedule

| Date | Who is paid |
|---|---|
| Thursday, Oct. 1 | SSI for October |
| Friday, Oct. 2 | Benefits started before May 1997, or Social Security plus SSI (the 3rd is a Saturday) |
| Wednesday, Oct. 14 | Birthdays on the 1st to the 10th |
| Wednesday, Oct. 21 | Birthdays on the 11th to the 20th |
| **Wednesday, Oct. 28** | **Birthdays on the 21st to the 31st** |
| Friday, Oct. 30 | SSI for November (Nov. 1 is a Sunday) |

## November 2026 payment schedule

| Date | Who is paid |
|---|---|
| Tuesday, Nov. 3 | Benefits started before May 1997, or Social Security plus SSI |
| Tuesday, Nov. 10 | Birthdays on the 1st to the 10th (moved up from Wednesday, Nov. 11, Veterans Day) |
| Wednesday, Nov. 18 | Birthdays on the 11th to the 20th |
| Wednesday, Nov. 25 | Birthdays on the 21st to the 31st |

There is no SSI payment dated in November: the November payment goes out Oct. 30, and December's comes on Tuesday, Dec. 1. Nov. 3 is also Election Day; it is not a federal holiday, so that payment keeps its date. See [whether Election Day is a holiday](/news/is-election-day-a-holiday-2026-whats-open/) for what else is open.

## December and the 3.6% raise

December's Wednesdays are Dec. 9, 16 and 23, and the early-filer payment is Thursday, Dec. 3. These December payments are November benefits, so they still use the 2026 amounts. The 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment that SSA announced on Oct. 14 starts with the payments made in January 2027. SSI recipients get the higher amount sooner: January's SSI payment is dated Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, because Jan. 1 is New Year's Day. The new amounts and January dates are in [Social Security COLA 2027 is 3.6%](/news/social-security-cola-2027-3-6-percent-payment-dates/).

## How your payment date is set

- **Birthday on the 1st to the 10th:** second Wednesday of the month.
- **Birthday on the 11th to the 20th:** third Wednesday.
- **Birthday on the 21st to the 31st:** fourth Wednesday.
- **Benefits started before May 1997, or you get both Social Security and SSI:** Social Security on the 3rd of the month, SSI on the 1st.
- **Weekends and federal holidays:** the payment moves to the business day before.

The birthday that counts is that of the worker whose record the benefit is based on: if you are paid as a spouse, for example, your date follows your spouse's birthday, and everyone paid on one record shares the same day.

## If your payment is late

SSA asks you to wait three more mailing days after the scheduled date before contacting it. If someone calls, texts or emails about a payment problem, check our [COLA scam guide](/news/social-security-cola-2027-scams-increase-chart/) before you share anything.
