**Minimum wages rise on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, in California, Washington, New Jersey, Minnesota, Virginia, Michigan, South Dakota, Seattle, Denver, Connecticut, Montana, Rhode Island and Ohio, according to official pages, state law and news reports.** Colorado and Arizona have 2027 rates set too. Michigan's rate goes to $15.00 an hour, and Seattle's city rate goes to $22.14. New York stays put, and the federal minimum stays at $7.25.

Every rate in the table comes from a source listed under the story: an official page, state law or, for Colorado, Ohio and Arizona, two news reports each. The dollar and percent changes are our own arithmetic on those rates, rounded to one decimal. A printable one-page list of the same rates is in the downloads box on this page, and the changes sit alongside other [new laws that start on Jan. 1, 2027](/news/new-laws-january-1-2027-by-state/).

## What changes in each state

**California.** The Department of Industrial Relations says the statewide minimum wage will rise to $17.40 an hour on Jan. 1, 2027, up $0.50 from $16.90 in 2026.

**Washington.** The Department of Labor & Industries announced $17.73, a 3.5% increase from $17.13.

**New Jersey.** The labor department set $16.48 for most employees, up $0.56 from $15.92.

**Minnesota.** The Department of Labor and Industry adjusted the rate for inflation to $11.87, up from $11.41. The training wage for workers under 20 rises from $9.31 to $9.68.

**Virginia.** State law, Va. Code 40.1-28.10, moves the rate from $12.77 to $13.75 on Jan. 1, 2027, and to $15.00 on Jan. 1, 2028.

**Michigan.** The Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity states: "Effective January 1, 2027, the minimum wage rate is $15.00 per hour." The 2026 rate was $13.73.

**South Dakota.** The Department of Labor and Regulation lists $12.30 for non-tipped workers, up from $11.85. The tipped rate goes from $5.925 to $6.15.

**Connecticut.** The governor's office says the minimum wage will adjust to $17.48 an hour beginning Jan. 1, 2027, up from the 2026 rate of $16.94.

**Montana.** The Department of Labor & Industry lists $11.20 effective Jan. 1, 2027, against a current $10.85. It states that no tip credit, meal credit or training wage is allowed under Montana's wage and hour laws.

**Denver.** Denver Labor lists $19.84 from Jan. 1 to Dec. 31, 2027, up from $19.29 in 2026. The 2027 tipped rate is not given on the page.

**Seattle.** The Office of Labor Standards says the city's minimum wage increases every year on Jan. 1. The 2027 city rate is $22.14, up from $21.30.

**Rhode Island.** H 5029, as passed by the General Assembly, reads: "Commencing January 1, 2027, the minimum wage is seventeen dollars ($17.00) per hour." The 2026 rate is $16.00. Bloomberg Tax reported the two-year schedule on June 27, 2025.

**Colorado.** The Colorado Department of Labor and Employment announced the 2027 rate on Aug. 13, 2026, and its release was not readable for us. Bloomberg Tax reported on Aug. 14, 2026, that the hourly minimum wage for 2027 "will increase by 55 cents to $15.71," with a tipped rate of $12.69 based on the state's $3.02 tip credit. ColoradoBiz reported the same day that the rate is up 3.6% from $15.16 in 2026. Neither report gives a start day, so the table says "for 2027."

**Arizona.** ktar.com reported on Sept. 12, 2026, that workers must be paid at least $15.65 an hour at the beginning of 2027, up from $15.15 in 2026. A Phoenix Business Journal report carried by ABC15 puts the adjustment at 3.4%; our arithmetic on the two rates gives 3.3%. No tipped cash wage is confirmed.

**Ohio.** The Ohio Department of Commerce announced the rate on Sept. 30, 2026. Local 12 (WKRC) reported that the minimum wage rises by 40 cents for most non-tipped workers beginning Jan. 1, 2027, and that the tipped rate goes from $5.50 to $5.70. Cleveland 19 reported $11.40 an hour from the same date.

## Which minimum wages do not change on January 1, 2027?

New York's rates stay the same. The governor's office says the state minimum wage "will not change on January 1, 2027": $17.00 in New York City, Long Island and Westchester, and $16.00 in the rest of the state. The office adds that a proposed increase on March 1, 2027, needs legislation.

The federal minimum wage stays at $7.25 an hour, per the U.S. Department of Labor Wage and Hour Division.

## Other places with changes to confirm

Maine, Nebraska and Vermont are still unconfirmed, so we list no 2027 rates for them. Maine's official pages were not readable for us. For Nebraska, one newsroom has reported a figure, and we need a second source or the Nebraska Department of Labor page. Vermont lists $14.42 for 2026 (tipped $7.21), and no 2027 figure is posted. We will add each rate here once it is confirmed.
