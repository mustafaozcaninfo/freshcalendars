**The Nations Championship November series starts Friday, Nov. 6, 2026, with Ireland v Argentina at 8:10pm Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), 3:10 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to Rugby Australia's fixture list.** England v New Zealand is Saturday, Nov. 21, at 2:10pm GMT, 9:10 a.m. ET and 6:10 a.m. Pacific Time (PT). Finals Weekend follows on Nov. 27 to 29 at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham, which Scottish Rugby also lists.

Rugby Australia prints its times in Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT). We converted them to GMT, the UK time after clocks went back on Oct. 25, and to U.S. Eastern and Pacific time. The U.S. clocks go back on Sunday, Nov. 1, so for every match below Eastern Time is 5 hours behind GMT and Pacific Time is 8 hours behind.

## When are the Nations Championship finals at Twickenham?

Finals Weekend is Friday, Nov. 27, to Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026, at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham, per Rugby Australia and Scottish Rugby. Each North team plays the South team that finished in the same place, so 1st v 1st is the final. Which teams fill each slot is not decided in the sources we used.

| Date | Match | UK (GMT) | ET | PT |

Rugby Australia's page prints the finals days with a typo, "Saturday 29 November". The days here come from converting its AEDT times and match Scottish Rugby's 27, 28 and 29 November.

## How does the Nations Championship work?

Rugby Australia describes 12 nations and six weeks of fixtures across two periods and two hemispheres. The July Southern Hemisphere series was Rounds 1 to 3, the November Northern Hemisphere series is Rounds 4 to 6, and Finals Weekend ends the tournament.

| Group | Nations |
|---|---|
| North | England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland, Wales |
| South | New Zealand, South Africa, Australia, Argentina, Japan, Fiji |

## Where can I watch the Nations Championship?

Rugby Australia says Stan Sport shows every game in Australia. In the UK, BroadcastNow reported that Independent Television (ITV) and Scottish Television (STV) hold free-to-air rights; that is a trade report, not a broadcaster listing. A U.S. broadcaster was not yet announced in the sources we checked, so check your local listings.
