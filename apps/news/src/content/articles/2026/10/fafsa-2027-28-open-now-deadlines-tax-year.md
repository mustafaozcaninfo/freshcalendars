The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) for 2027-28 opened to all students on Wednesday, Sept. 23, 2026, the earliest release in the program's history, according to [Fox 5 New York](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/2027-28-fafsa-opens-record-time-heres-what-you-need-know.amp) on Sept. 24, 2026. The official pages on StudentAid.gov and ed.gov were blocked to our automated checks, so every figure below is attributed to news reports. Confirm dates with the Department of Education and each college.

A free printable family checklist for this story puts the steps and the reported deadlines on one page.

## Who is the 2027-28 FAFSA for?

The form is for students starting classes on or after July 1, 2027, The National News Desk reported through WJLA on Sept. 24, 2026. It said the U.S. Department of Education announced the launch, with Under Secretary Nicholas Kent and Secretary Linda McMahon named in the report. The same report said the FAFSA Simplification Act requires the form to be available by Oct. 1, so the Sept. 23 opening came ahead of that date.

For comparison, secondary reports put the 2026-27 opening at Sept. 24, 2025.

## What changed in the 2027-28 form?

The National News Desk listed these changes for 2027-28:

- Clearer language on the form.
- Easier renewal.
- Contributors, such as a parent, can be invited by text or email.
- Earnings outcomes shown for schools.
- Fraud detection.

## What are the FAFSA dates and deadlines?

| Date | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Wed., Sept. 23, 2026 | 2027-28 FAFSA opened to all students | Reported by Fox 5 New York and The National News Desk |
| Tue., March 2, 2027 | California Cal Grant deadline | Reported by secondary sources |
| Texas | Priority date varies by source | Check with the college |
| Illinois | File as soon as possible; aid is first-come | Reported by secondary sources |
| Fri., June 30, 2028 | Federal deadline, 11:59 p.m. Central Time | Reported by student-aid guides, not confirmed on an official page |

Student-aid guides report that the 2027-28 form uses 2025 tax information. Confirm that on StudentAid.gov before you gather documents.

## What should families do first?

1. Create or check your StudentAid.gov account.
2. Invite contributors by text or email, one of the changes The National News Desk listed for 2027-28.
3. List the colleges that should receive your FAFSA.
4. Check your state's deadline and each college's own aid date. Dates differ, and Texas sources conflict.
