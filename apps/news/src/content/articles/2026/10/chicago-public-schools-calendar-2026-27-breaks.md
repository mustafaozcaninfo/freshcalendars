**Chicago Public Schools (CPS) students have no school on Monday, Nov. 2 or Tuesday, Nov. 3**, the next days off on the district's 2026-27 calendar: Monday is a parent-teacher conference day and Tuesday is the General Election holiday. After that come Thanksgiving on Nov. 26-27, **winter vacation from Monday, Dec. 21 through Friday, Jan. 1**, and spring vacation from March 22 to 26, 2027.

## CPS 2026-27 breaks

| Break | Dates | Students return |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving holiday | Thu.-Fri., Nov. 26-27, 2026 | Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Winter vacation | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Tuesday, Jan. 5 (Jan. 4 is a professional development day) |
| Spring vacation | Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027 | Monday, March 29 |

The school year started Monday, Aug. 24, 2026 for kindergarten through 12th grade and Tuesday, Aug. 25 for pre-K. It ends Friday, June 11, 2027 for K-12 and Thursday, June 10 for pre-K, with 176 student attendance days.

## Election Day replaces Veterans Day

The CPS calendar lists Nov. 3 as "General Election observed in lieu of Veterans Day." Illinois treats the general election as a school holiday, and CPS uses that day off instead of closing for Veterans Day. On Wednesday, Nov. 11, students still stay home, but as a professional development day for staff. Families get Monday, Nov. 2 off as well, for parent-teacher conferences, so many children are home Monday and Tuesday that week.

Because Labor Day was late this year, the first semester ends after winter vacation, on Jan. 22, rather than before it, Chalkbeat Chicago reported when the calendar was proposed.

## Graduation and summer

High school graduations cannot be held before Friday, May 28, 2027, and elementary graduations not before Monday, June 7. Summer programs can start as early as July 6, 2027. June 15-17 and 21-22 are emergency days, added only if snow or other closures push student days below the state minimum.
