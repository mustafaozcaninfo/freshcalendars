**Lunar New Year 2027 falls on Saturday, Feb. 6**, the first day of the Year of the Goat. New Year's Eve, with its reunion dinner, is Friday, Feb. 5, and the 15-day season ends with the Lantern Festival on Saturday, Feb. 20. Korea marks Seollal one day later, on Sunday, Feb. 7. The dates come from the Hong Kong Observatory (HKO), which publishes the official conversion between the Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian one.

## Why the date changes every year

The Chinese calendar is lunisolar: months follow the Moon, while the year is kept in step with the seasons. New Year's Day is usually the second new moon after the winter solstice, so it always lands between Jan. 21 and Feb. 20.

In 2027 that new moon comes at 15:56 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) on Feb. 6, according to the U.S. Naval Observatory. In Beijing it is 11:56 p.m., just four minutes before midnight, so Feb. 6 is New Year's Day in China. Four minutes decide it. In Seoul the clock already reads 12:56 a.m. on Feb. 7, which is why Korea's Seollal comes a day later. Vietnam's Tet follows the same calculation for Hanoi time, where the new moon falls at 10:56 p.m. on Feb. 6.

The same new moon also matters for [Ramadan 2027](/news/ramadan-2027-calendar-dates-timetable/), which begins two days later, and it brings an annular solar eclipse over South America and West Africa.

## Year of the Goat: who is a Goat?

2027 is a Fire Goat year, called ding-wei in Chinese. The Goat (also translated Sheep or Ram) is the eighth of the 12 animals, after the Horse and before the Monkey.

| Goat year | Started on | Element |
|---|---|---|
| 1955 | Jan. 24, 1955 | Wood |
| 1967 | Feb. 9, 1967 | Fire |
| 1979 | Jan. 28, 1979 | Earth |
| 1991 | Feb. 15, 1991 | Metal |
| 2003 | Feb. 1, 2003 | Water |
| 2015 | Feb. 19, 2015 | Wood |
| 2027 | Feb. 6, 2027 | Fire |

Zodiac years start at Lunar New Year, not Jan. 1. That trips people up. Someone born on Jan. 20, 1979, for example, is a Horse, because the Goat year began eight days later.
