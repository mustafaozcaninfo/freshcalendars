**February 2027 starts on Monday, Feb. 1 and has 28 days, one federal holiday and 19 working days.** The holiday is Washington's Birthday, commonly called Presidents' Day, on Monday, Feb. 15, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM). The free one-page printable at the bottom of this story marks that day and 13 other dates from official sources, including the Moon phases with their times.

## February 2027 at a glance

2027 is not a leap year, so February has 28 days: exactly four full weeks. The month begins on a Monday and ends on a Sunday, which gives it four Mondays, four Tuesdays and so on. A Monday-start calendar fits it in four tidy rows. A Sunday-start calendar needs five, with Sunday, Feb. 28 alone on the last one.

| | February 2027 |
|---|---|
| First day | Monday, Feb. 1 |
| Last day | Sunday, Feb. 28 |
| Days | 28 (20 weekdays, 8 weekend days) |
| Federal holiday | Washington's Birthday, Mon., Feb. 15 |
| Working days | 19 |
| Three-day weekend | Sat., Feb. 13 to Mon., Feb. 15 |
| Full moon | Sat., Feb. 20, 6:23 p.m. EST |

The 19 working days count weekdays minus the one federal holiday. The calendar of days off for the whole year is in our [federal holidays 2027](/news/federal-holidays-2027-observed-dates-list/) story, and what stays open on the day is in [Presidents' Day 2027: what is open and closed](/news/presidents-day-2027-february-15-whats-open-closed/).

## February 2027 holidays, Moon phases and key dates

Only the Feb. 15 row is a federal holiday. The rest are dates that go on a February calendar, each from the organization that sets it. Moon times are in Eastern Standard Time (EST), converted from the Universal Time (UT) the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes.
