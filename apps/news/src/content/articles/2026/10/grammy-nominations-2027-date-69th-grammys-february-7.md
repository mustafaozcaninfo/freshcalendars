<p class="callout"><b>Checked Nov. 13, 2026.</b> The Recording Academy has posted the nominations time, the venue and the final-round voting dates; the host and the ceremony start time are not announced yet.</p>

**The Recording Academy begins the 2027 Grammy nominations presentation Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, at 7:45 a.m. ET (4:45 a.m. PT), and Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027, is Grammy night for the 69th Annual Grammy Awards.** Only works released within the eligibility period qualify. This page puts the season in one timeline, from the release window to the ceremony, and shows which details each source confirms.

## When are the Grammy nominations announced?

On Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. The Recording Academy's schedule lists "7:45 a.m. ET / 4:45 a.m. PT: Nominations Special Presentation Begins" and adds that all times are approximate and subject to change. The complete list of nominees is published on Grammy.com at 9 a.m. ET (6 a.m. PT). ABC News also reports a start at 7:45 a.m. EST, live from New York City. Mark the day on your [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## How to watch the Grammy nominations 2027

The Recording Academy says nominees are revealed in a special presentation on Grammy.com and the Grammys YouTube channel, with live coverage on Good Morning America. Its list of presenters includes Alan Menken, Amy Allen, Carly Pearce, David Guetta, Herbie Hancock, Juanes, Kehlani, Olivia Dean, Pharrell Williams, Renée Fleming and Ziggy Marley, among others; no single host is named.

## What is the date of the 2027 Grammys?

Grammy night is Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027, live from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, on ABC, Disney+ and Hulu, according to the Recording Academy. It names the ceremony the 69th Annual Grammy Awards. The start time is not announced yet. The Premiere Ceremony streams live exclusively on Disney+ the same day at 11:30 a.m. PT / 2:30 p.m. ET. If you plan your February around it, put it on the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html). Super Bowl 61 (LXI) also falls in February 2027, and we cover it in our [Super Bowl LXI date and channel story](/news/super-bowl-lxi-2027-date-sofi-stadium-channel-halftime/).
