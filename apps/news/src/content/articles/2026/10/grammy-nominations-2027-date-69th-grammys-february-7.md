**The Recording Academy's Grammy site lists Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, as the day nominees for the 2027 Grammys are announced, and Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027, as Grammy night for the 69th Annual Grammy Awards.** The same site says only works released within the eligibility period qualify. This page puts the season in one timeline, from the release window to the ceremony, and separates what the Academy confirms from what news summaries report.

## When are the Grammy nominations announced?

On Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, according to the Recording Academy. Its site lists "Nominees Announced" for that date and says all nominees at the 2027 Grammys will be announced. We could not confirm the time of day, so we do not give one. Mark the day on your [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and check back for the time.

## What is the date of the 2027 Grammys?

Grammy night is Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027. The Recording Academy names the ceremony the 69th Annual Grammy Awards. If you plan your February around it, put it on the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html). Super Bowl 61 (LXI) also falls in February 2027, and we cover it in our [Super Bowl LXI date and channel story](/news/super-bowl-lxi-2027-date-sofi-stadium-channel-halftime/).
