**The 2026-27 National Football League (NFL) playoffs begin with Wild Card Weekend on Saturday, Jan. 16 through Monday, Jan. 18, 2027, and finish with Super Bowl LXI on Sunday, Feb. 14, according to the league's important dates list.** The regular season ends in Week 18 on Jan. 9-10. This page gives each round with its weekday and the days between them, and links a printable with blank lines for the matchups.

## NFL playoff dates by round, 2027

| Round | Dates | Weekdays | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 18 (regular season ends) | Jan. 9-10, 2027 | Saturday and Sunday | Three Saturday games have times and TV; Sunday times to be announced after Week 17 |
| Wild Card Weekend | Jan. 16-18, 2027 | Saturday to Monday | Matchups set after Week 18 |
| Divisional Playoffs | Jan. 23-24, 2027 | Saturday and Sunday | Matchups set after Week 18 |
| American Football Conference (AFC) and National Football Conference (NFC) Championship Games | Jan. 31, 2027 | Sunday | Matchups set after Week 18 |
| Super Bowl LXI | Feb. 14, 2027 | Sunday | SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, California |

*Dates: NFL, 2026-27 National Football League important dates. Weekdays computed by Fresh Calendars.*

The NFL's schedules page lists times for three Week 18 games on Saturday, Jan. 9: 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Netflix, then 4:30 p.m. ET and 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN and ABC. The other Week 18 games are set for Sunday, Jan. 10 at 1:00 p.m., 4:25 p.m. or 8:20 p.m. ET, with times to be announced after Week 17. The league lists no kickoff times or TV windows for any playoff game yet. Those are not yet announced and are set after Week 18, so they are not in this table.

## How many days are there between the playoff rounds?

Counting from the league's dates, the playoffs span 35 days from the end of Week 18 on Sunday, Jan. 10 to Super Bowl LXI on Feb. 14. The gaps between rounds, computed from the NFL's dates:

- Wild Card Weekend (last day Monday, Jan. 18) to the Championship Games on Jan. 31: 13 days.
- Championship Games on Jan. 31 to Super Bowl LXI on Feb. 14: 14 days.

Wild Card Weekend ends on a Monday because the weekend runs Jan. 16-18, and Monday, Jan. 18 is also Martin Luther King Jr. Day. Mark them on a [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html), and the Super Bowl falls on the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html).

## Where and how is Super Bowl LXI shown?

The NFL schedules Super Bowl LXI at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, on Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027. The Walt Disney Company said on Oct. 7, 2026 that Disney+ will livestream the game on Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027, with the traditional game presentation airing on ESPN and ABC. Our [Super Bowl LXI date, channel and halftime story](/news/super-bowl-lxi-2027-date-sofi-stadium-channel-halftime/) has the rest of the game-day details.

## What about the Pro Bowl?

The NFL says the 2027 Pro Bowl class will be honored during the traditional Pro Bowl weekend in Los Angeles, with the roster revealed in late December. No date for that weekend has been announced, so it is not on the schedule above.

The format comes from two newsrooms that agree; we did not check it against an official NFL page. NBC Sports (Jan. 7, 2026) describes a 14-team format with seven teams from each conference, where division winners take the top four seeds and only the No. 1 seed gets a first-round bye. CBS Sports (Jan. 6, 2025) also lists the division winners as seeds 1-4 and the three wild cards as seeds 5-7. Both give the Wild Card games as No. 2 against No. 7, No. 3 against No. 6 and No. 4 against No. 5. In the Divisional round, CBS Sports says the lowest remaining seeds travel to the highest remaining seeds, so the No. 1 seed hosts the lowest remaining seed. Both newsroom pieces describe earlier seasons, so check the league's own pages once the seeds are set.
