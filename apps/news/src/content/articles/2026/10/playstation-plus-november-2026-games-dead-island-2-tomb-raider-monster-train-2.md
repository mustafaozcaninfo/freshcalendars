**Sony announced on the PlayStation Blog on Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, that Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered and Monster Train 2 are the PlayStation Plus (PS Plus) Monthly Games for November, playable from Tuesday, Nov. 3 to Monday, Nov. 30.** The blog post says all three are available to play across that window. The tables below add the dates around it, and a free printable calendar follows.

<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 28, 2026.</b> Sony has not given a time of day for the Nov. 3 start, the Nov. 30 end or the Nov. 2 cutoff for the October games. This story does not state one.</p>

## Which games are the PS Plus November 2026 Monthly Games?

The PlayStation Blog lists three games. Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered is the remastered collection of the "Darkness Trilogy." Dead Island 2 is a role-playing game (RPG) set in a zombie-overrun Los Angeles the post calls "Hell-A," with online three-player co-op. Monster Train 2 is a single-player deckbuilding roguelite.

| Game | Platforms | Playable from | Add by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dead Island 2 | PS5, PS4 | Tue., Nov. 3 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered | PS5, PS4 | Tue., Nov. 3 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Monster Train 2 | PS5 only | Tue., Nov. 3 | Mon., Nov. 30 |

The "Add by" column follows the blog's window, which runs to Monday, Nov. 30.

## When do the PS Plus November games come out?

The blog post says all three are available "from Tuesday November 3 to Monday, November 30." That is a 28-day window, counted from the two dates. Since Sony gives no time of day, check the PlayStation Store on Nov. 3 itself instead of planning around a clock time.

## What happens to the October PS Plus games?

The PlayStation Blog says members have until Monday, Nov. 2 to add the October Monthly Games to their library: F1 25, Hunt: Showdown 1896 and Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2. Sony has not given a time of day, so add them before Monday, Nov. 2 ends in your time zone.

## What does PS Plus cost in November 2026?

The Monthly Games need an active PS Plus membership, and PlayStation Plus says games you add stay playable while your membership stays active. These are the U.S. prices listed on playstation.com on Oct. 28, 2026.

| Tier | 1 month | 3 months | 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | $10.99 | $27.99 | $79.99 |
| Extra | $16.99 | $43.99 | $134.99 |
| Premium | $19.99 | $54.99 | $159.99 |

If you play on PS5, our [PS5 price and crossplay guide](/news/peak-ps5-price-crossplay-guide-2026/) covers the console side.
