## When does Percy Jackson season 3 come out?

Season 3 starts Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, with a two-episode premiere on Disney+ and Hulu, according to the Oct. 15 Disney+ Press list. A Disney+ Press release from July 23, 2026 says season three, "The Titan's Curse," premieres Nov. 20. The "Percy Jackson and the Olympians Official Podcast" season 3 premieres the same day, and two new episodes of the series follow on Friday, Nov. 27: a Nov. 2, 2026 Disney+ Press release says season 3 debuts with a two-episode premiere, followed by two episodes released weekly on Disney+ and Hulu. Disney+ Press does not give a drop time for either Friday, so none is given here.

## Which special events are on Disney+ in November?

Disney+ Press lists "The 60th Annual CMA Awards" streaming live at 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Wednesday, Nov. 18. For the host, channel and nominees, see our [CMA Awards 2026 guide](/news/cma-awards-2026-date-time-channel-host-nominees/). On Oct. 22, 2026, Disney+ Press also announced "The Pursuit: F1 Academy," a documentary special, for Thursday, Nov. 19. The "F1" in that title stands for Formula 1 (F1).

## What is on Disney+ around Thanksgiving?

Disney+ Press lists a holiday special episode of "Sofia the First: Royal Magic" on Thursday, Nov. 26, which is Thanksgiving Day; see [when Thanksgiving falls](/when-is/thanksgiving/). "Mickey's Home Alone," a holiday special, premieres Thursday, Nov. 26, at 6 p.m. ET/PT on Disney Jr. and streams the next day, Friday, Nov. 27, on Disney+, according to a Nov. 3, 2026 Disney+ Press release. "Dasher" premieres Saturday, Nov. 28; the page calls it a premiere and does not say whether it is a film or a series. The Nov. 6 "SuperKitties" Thanksgiving special and the Nov. 23 "Mickey Mouse Clubhouse: Mickey's Adventures in Wonderland" listing are also on the page. For football and parade times on Nov. 26, see the [Thanksgiving 2026 TV schedule](/news/thanksgiving-2026-tv-schedule-macys-parade-nfl-games/). Christmas is later; see [when Christmas Day is](/when-is/christmas-day/).

## What are the Hulu-only highlights?

The same Disney+ Press page lists titles labeled Hulu only, which are not in the main table.

- Wednesday, Nov. 4: "All's Fair" (Season 2), three-episode premiere.
- Wednesday, Nov. 11: "Rivals" (Season 2, Part 2).
- Friday, Nov. 20: the holiday films "A Christmas Story" (1983), "Four Christmases" (2008), "National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation" (1989) and "The Polar Express" (2004).
