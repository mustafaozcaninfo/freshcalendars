**Passover 2027 begins at sundown on Wednesday, April 21, 2027, and ends at nightfall on Thursday, April 29 outside Israel, according to Hebcal and Chabad.** India's central government holiday list for 2027 gives Holi as Tuesday, March 23, as reported by Staffnews and People Matters. This story gives the Passover days in a table, explains the Seder nights and notes where Holi dates can differ. For your own planning, use the [April printable calendars](/april-calendars.html) and the [March printable calendars](/march-calendars.html).

## When does Passover 2027 begin and end?

Hebcal says Passover begins at sundown on Wednesday, April 21, 2027. Its entry for Pesach I is Thursday, April 22, 2027, which is 15 Nisan 5787. Outside Israel the holiday ends at nightfall on Thursday, April 29, 2027, per Hebcal. Chabad lists the span as April 21 to 29.

The Union for Reform Judaism (URJ) says Passover starts on Wednesday, April 21, in 2027 and lists April 21 to April 28. It describes the holiday as lasting seven days in Israel and for most Reform Jews, and eight days for many other Jews.

## Passover 2027 day by day

| Date | Weekday | What it marks |
|---|---|---|
| April 21, 2027 | Wednesday | Passover begins at sundown; first Seder that night |
| April 22, 2027 | Thursday | First day, 15 Nisan 5787 (Hebcal); second Seder that night outside Israel |
| April 23 to 27, 2027 | Friday to Tuesday | Days between |
| April 28, 2027 | Wednesday | End of the seven-day span listed by URJ |
| April 29, 2027 | Thursday | Passover ends at nightfall outside Israel (Hebcal) |

Weekdays are computed by Fresh Calendars from the dates the three sources give.

## Which nights are the Seders?

Chabad lists the Seders for the nights of April 21 and 22, 2027. Outside Israel there are two Seders, on the first two nights, while in Israel there is one. The first Seder is the evening before the first day: Wednesday, April 21.

## What time does Passover start on April 21?

It depends on where you live. Sundown differs by place, and local communities set their own candle lighting times, so check with your synagogue or community for the exact time before the first Seder on Wednesday evening.

## Holi 2027: what does India's holiday list say?

India's central government holiday list for 2027 comes from a Department of Personnel and Training office memorandum dated July 16, 2026. Staffnews and People Matters (October 26, 2026) both give Holi as Tuesday, March 23, 2027. It is a gazetted holiday for central government offices in Delhi and New Delhi. Staffnews's list of restricted holidays shows "Holika Dahan, Dolyatra" on Monday, March 22, 2027.

## Can Holi fall on a different day for your community?

Yes. A government list only sets days off for central offices, and religious practice does not follow it. Many Hindu calendars (panchang) and communities may mark Holi on a different day, often a day earlier. Check with your temple or community before you plan. This story does not give a panchang date.
