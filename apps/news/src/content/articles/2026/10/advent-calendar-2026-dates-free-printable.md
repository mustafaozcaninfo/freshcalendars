**Advent 2026 begins on Sunday, Nov. 29**, four Sundays before Christmas, which falls on a Friday this year. Most advent calendars, though, follow the simpler countdown: 24 doors from **Tuesday, Dec. 1 to Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24**. Searches for 2026 advent calendars are climbing fast this week, from LEGO and Disney pins to luxury beauty and golf sets. Here are the dates, which brand calendars are actually confirmed for 2026, and four free calendars you can print today.

## Advent 2026 dates

| Date | Day | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 29 | Sunday | First Sunday of Advent |
| Dec. 1 | Tuesday | Door 1 on most advent calendars |
| Dec. 6 | Sunday | Second Sunday of Advent; St. Nicholas Day |
| Dec. 13 | Sunday | Third Sunday of Advent (Gaudete Sunday) |
| Dec. 20 | Sunday | Fourth Sunday of Advent |
| Dec. 24 | Thursday | Christmas Eve, the last door |
| Dec. 25 | Friday | Christmas Day |

Because Christmas falls on a Friday, the season of Advent lasts 26 days in 2026, from Nov. 29 through Christmas Eve. It can be as short as 22 days or as long as 28, depending on the weekday of Christmas. The church season and the shop-bought calendar are different things: the first starts on a Sunday, the second almost always on Dec. 1.

## Four free printable advent calendars

Not every countdown needs a box of chocolates. The four templates below are free to print, with no sign-up:

- **Advent Calendar 2026 countdown.** 24 shuffled doors with the weekday under each number. Children color in or cross off a door each morning; the Advent Sundays are highlighted in gold.
- **Activity advent calendar.** One easy family activity per day, from putting up the tree on Dec. 1 to setting out cookies on Christmas Eve. Weekend days are matched to longer activities such as baking.
- **Kindness advent calendar.** A "reverse" advent calendar: 24 small acts of giving, such as donating canned food, writing a thank-you note or calling someone who lives alone.
- **Do-it-yourself (DIY) advent calendar.** Blank doors with the date and weekday, for your own treats, Bible verses or plans. Print it on card stock and cut out the doors to hide small surprises behind them.

All four are sized for US Letter paper. For a month-at-a-glance view of the season, pair them with a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## How to choose an advent calendar

**Count the doors.** Most calendars have 24 doors, but beauty and golf brands often sell 12-day sets, and some include a 25th door. A 26-door calendar matches the 2026 church season exactly.

**Buy early, or plan to print.** LEGO's sets went on sale Sept. 1, and TechRadar has reported some selling out in some regions; Disney's limited pin calendar, released in mid-October, is capped at 5,750 sets worldwide. Printable calendars cost nothing and never sell out.

**Check the price per door.** Disney's $275 pin set works out to about $11.46 per pin; LEGO's $44.99 calendars to under $2 per day.

**Mind the start date.** If you buy a 24-door calendar, the first door is Dec. 1. If your church or family follows the liturgical season, start on Sunday, Nov. 29.
