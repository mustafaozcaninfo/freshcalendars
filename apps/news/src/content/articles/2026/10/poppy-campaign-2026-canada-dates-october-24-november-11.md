**The Royal Canadian Legion's National Poppy Campaign is under way today, Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026, and runs from Saturday, Oct. 24, to Wednesday, Nov. 11**, according to the Legion's National Poppy Campaign page, last updated Oct. 20, 2026. From today, 13 days remain until the campaign ends on Remembrance Day.

This is the first year of a new rule. The Legion's page says: "Beginning in 2026, the National Poppy Campaign will run annually October 24–November 11." The printable timeline attached to this story keeps the key dates on one page.

## When does the poppy campaign run in 2026?

The Legion says its Dominion Executive Council "has approved a standardized annual period for the National Poppy Campaign." From 2026 the campaign runs each year from Oct. 24 through Nov. 11. The Legion gives its reason: the consistent period "will ensure Branches have enough time to carry out a successful campaign and will provide greater certainty for Branches, partners and the public." The same rule appears on a second Legion page titled "National Poppy Campaign dates changing."

| Date in 2026 | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sat., Oct. 24 | National Poppy Campaign starts | Royal Canadian Legion |
| Sun., Nov. 8 | Indigenous Veterans Day in Canada | Veterans Affairs Canada |
| Wed., Nov. 11 | Remembrance Day; campaign ends | Royal Canadian Legion |

Counting both end dates, the 2026 campaign spans 19 days. Veterans Affairs Canada's Veterans' Week page says "November 8 is Indigenous Veterans Day" and "On November 11, our job is to remember." It also says: "Wear a poppy." The official 2026 dates for Veterans' Week were not confirmed on that page, so we do not list them.

## Why do some guides say the last Friday of October?

Many guides and older Legion pages still describe a start on the last Friday of October. That is out of date. Under the old last-Friday pattern, the 2026 campaign would have started on Friday, Oct. 30, which is tomorrow. The fixed period moved the start up to Saturday, Oct. 24.

The fixed dates also mean the weekdays shift from year to year. We computed them for the next five years:

| Year | Campaign starts (Oct. 24) | Campaign ends (Nov. 11) | Last Friday of October (old pattern) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Saturday | Wednesday | Oct. 30 |
| 2027 | Sunday | Thursday | Oct. 29 |
| 2028 | Tuesday | Saturday | Oct. 27 |
| 2029 | Wednesday | Sunday | Oct. 26 |
| 2030 | Thursday | Monday | Oct. 25 |

The weekdays come from the calendar for each year, applied to the Legion's fixed Oct. 24 and Nov. 11 dates. The last column is the date the old last-Friday pattern would have given.

## What is the UK poppy appeal date?

The Royal British Legion runs its own Poppy Appeal in the United Kingdom (UK). Its Poppy Appeal 2026 page lists a national launch on Oct. 22, which has passed, and a March Past the Cenotaph in Whitehall on Nov. 8. That is Remembrance Sunday, Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. Our [Remembrance Day 2026 story](/news/remembrance-day-2026-canada-statutory-holiday-remembrance-sunday-uk/) has the Canadian province-by-province holiday table and the UK service details.

## When is the National Remembrance Day Ceremony in Ottawa?

The 2026 start time is not yet announced. Last year, the Governor General of Canada's page, dated Nov. 10, 2025, listed the ceremony for Tuesday, Nov. 11, 2025, at 10:30 a.m. at the National War Memorial in Ottawa. Treat that as the 2025 time only. We do not name a 2026 Silver Cross Mother because none has been announced.

## How should a poppy be worn?

The Legion's public poppy guidance page was unavailable when we checked on Oct. 29, so this story does not give wearing, removal or disposal rules or say what donations fund. Check legion.ca or ask your local Legion branch for its guidance.
