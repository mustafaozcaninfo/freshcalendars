**The Toronto Maple Leafs play 13 games in November 2026, starting Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against the Utah Mammoth at Scotiabank Arena, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) schedule.** The full month with TV in Canada and the United States is below, and a one-page printable is further down.

The source is the NHL's club schedule for Toronto, first fetched Oct. 31, 2026 and checked again Nov. 14, when the start times and channels matched. We converted its start times to ET, which is Toronto time, and calculated the weekdays. Nine games are at home. Four are on the road. The Leafs opened the month with six straight home games, Nov. 3 to Nov. 14, before the first road game of the month, at Chicago on Tuesday, Nov. 17. Through Nov. 12 they had played five of them and lost all five in regulation, by the scores in the table. ## What time was Leafs vs. Canadiens on Nov. 7, 2026?

Toronto hosted the Montreal Canadiens on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7 p.m. ET at Scotiabank Arena, and Montreal won 4-2. The NHL listed Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada and no U.S. broadcaster for that game. It was the first game of a home back-to-back, followed by New Jersey on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 7 p.m. ET, a 4-0 New Jersey win.

## Back-to-backs, road games and Thanksgiving weekend

These are our own counts from the NHL's schedule:

- **Back-to-back:** Sat., Nov. 7 and Sun., Nov. 8, both at Scotiabank Arena (played).
- **Away games:** at Chicago on Tue., Nov. 17, at the New York Islanders on Mon., Nov. 23, at Boston on Fri., Nov. 27 and at Pittsburgh on Sat., Nov. 28.
- **Thanksgiving weekend:** the Boston game is at 1 p.m. ET on Fri., Nov. 27, the day after U.S. Thanksgiving on Thu., Nov. 26, 2026, and the Leafs play at Pittsburgh the next night. The Leafs have no game on Nov. 26 in the NHL's schedule.
