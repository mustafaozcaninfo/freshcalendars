**The Toronto Maple Leafs play 13 games in November 2026, starting Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against the Utah Mammoth at Scotiabank Arena, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) schedule.** The full month with TV in Canada and the United States is below, and a one-page printable is further down.

The source is the NHL's club schedule for Toronto, fetched Oct. 31, 2026. We converted its start times to ET, which is Toronto time, and calculated the weekdays. Nine games are at home. Four are on the road. The Leafs have no game on Nov. 1 or Nov. 2, so the first chance to see them this month is Tuesday night against Utah, the first of six straight home games before the first road game of the month, at Chicago on Tuesday, Nov. 17. For the other games around the league on Nov. 1, see our [NHL games today for Nov. 1](/news/nhl-games-today-november-1-2026/) page.

## What time is Leafs vs. Canadiens on Nov. 7, 2026?

Toronto hosts the Montreal Canadiens on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7 p.m. ET at Scotiabank Arena. The NHL lists Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada and no U.S. broadcaster for that game. It is the first game of a home back-to-back, followed by New Jersey on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 7 p.m. ET.

## Back-to-backs, road games and Thanksgiving weekend

These are our own counts from the NHL's schedule:

- **Back-to-back:** Sat., Nov. 7 and Sun., Nov. 8, both at Scotiabank Arena.
- **Away games:** at Chicago on Tue., Nov. 17, at the New York Islanders on Mon., Nov. 23, at Boston on Fri., Nov. 27 and at Pittsburgh on Sat., Nov. 28.
- **Thanksgiving weekend:** the Boston game is at 1 p.m. ET on Fri., Nov. 27, the day after U.S. Thanksgiving on Thu., Nov. 26, 2026, and the Leafs play at Pittsburgh the next night. The Leafs have no game on Nov. 26 in the NHL's schedule.
- **Month end:** Philadelphia visits on Mon., Nov. 30, at 7:30 p.m. ET, the last game of the month.
