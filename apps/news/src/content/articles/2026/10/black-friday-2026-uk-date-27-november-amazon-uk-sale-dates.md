**Black Friday 2026 is Friday, 27 November 2026 in the United Kingdom (UK), the day after United States (US) Thanksgiving (Thursday, 26 November), and Cyber Monday is Monday, 30 November; Amazon UK says its Black Friday Week runs 19-30 November 2026.** Its Black Friday guide, updated on 10 November 2026, says: "Amazon's UK Black Friday Week is returning from 19–30 November." For the plain date answer, see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/). A free [UK shopping planner](#downloads) is below.

## When does Amazon UK Black Friday 2026 start?

Thursday, 19 November 2026, and it runs until Monday, 30 November: 12 days counting both ends (our computation). Amazon UK says its Black Friday Week "is returning from 19–30 November". Amazon Devices deals come earlier: "From 16 November, enjoy savings on a selection of Amazon Devices, including Echo, Fire TV Stick, Ember smart TVs, Kindle, Ring and Blink, with Amazon Alexa Tablets from 18 November." The UK page gives no start or end clock times, so none are listed here.

Argos, Currys, John Lewis, Very, Boots, Tesco, Asda and M&S had not announced 2026 Black Friday dates as of 12 November 2026, and none had announced a 2026 Christmas return extension. We will update this page as they do.

Amazon UK's own releases show what it did in the last two years. In 2025 it said it was "kicking off 20 November and running until 1 December." In 2024 its Black Friday Week ran from "21st November 00.00am GMT" until "23.59pm GMT on 2nd December" (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT).

## Did Amazon UK keep its Thursday start in 2026?

Yes. In 2024 and 2025 Amazon UK began Black Friday Week on the Thursday eight days before Black Friday and ended it on Cyber Monday (our observation from its releases). The 2026 dates follow the same shape: Thursday, 19 November to Monday, 30 November. Amazon UK announced them on 10 November, against 8 November in 2024 and 13 November in 2025. The US sale follows a similar rhythm; our [Amazon Black Friday 2026 story for the US](/news/amazon-black-friday-2026-sale-dates-when-does-it-start/) has those dates and the US return policy.

## How long can you cancel an online order in the UK?

The Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 set a 14-day cancellation period. For goods bought online, regulation 30 says it ends 14 days after the day the goods come into your physical possession. Which? (15 September 2026) puts it this way: "your right to cancel an order starts the moment you place it and ends 14 days after your goods are delivered to you."

Example: goods delivered on Saturday, 28 November 2026 can be cancelled until Saturday, 12 December 2026. Check the regulation and the retailer's terms for your own order, as exceptions are not covered here.
