**Black Friday 2026 is Friday, 27 November 2026 in the United Kingdom (UK), the day after United States (US) Thanksgiving (Thursday, 26 November), and Cyber Monday is Monday, 30 November; no major UK retailer had published 2026 Black Friday sale dates as of 28 October 2026.** Amazon UK's newsroom had no Black Friday 2026 announcement when we checked on 28 October; its latest item was a Prime Day item dated 22 October 2026. For the plain date answer, see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/). A free [UK shopping planner](#downloads) is below.

## When does Amazon UK Black Friday 2026 start?

Not yet announced. Amazon UK has not published 2026 dates. Argos, Currys, John Lewis, Very, Boots, Tesco, Asda and M&S have not announced 2026 Black Friday dates either, and none has announced a 2026 Christmas return extension. We will update this page as they do.

Amazon UK's own releases show what it did in the last two years. In 2025 it said it was "kicking off 20 November and running until 1 December." In 2024 its Black Friday Week ran from "21st November 00.00am GMT" until "23.59pm GMT on 2nd December" (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT).

## Is there a pattern for the 2026 start date?

This is our observation from two releases, not an Amazon statement. Amazon UK announced its dates between 8 and 13 November, and its Black Friday Week began on the Thursday eight days before Black Friday and ended on Cyber Monday, in both 2024 and 2025.

If Amazon repeats that pattern, the start would be Thursday, 19 November 2026 and the end Monday, 30 November. Two years is a short record. Amazon has not announced 2026 dates, so treat 19 November as a planning guess and wait for its own release. ## How long can you cancel an online order in the UK?

The Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 set a 14-day cancellation period. For goods bought online, regulation 30 says it ends 14 days after the day the goods come into your physical possession. Which? (15 September 2026) puts it this way: "your right to cancel an order starts the moment you place it and ends 14 days after your goods are delivered to you."

Example: goods delivered on Saturday, 28 November 2026 can be cancelled until Saturday, 12 December 2026. Check the regulation and the retailer's terms for your own order, as this story does not cover exceptions.
