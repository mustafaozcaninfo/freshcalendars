**The Los Angeles Lakers' next game is tomorrow, Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, at the Golden State Warriors at 7 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 10 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), on Prime Video, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule. ## Which Lakers games are NBA Cup games?

The NBA schedule flags four Lakers games through Nov. 30 as Emirates NBA Cup group play: Fri., Oct. 30 at the Warriors, Fri., Nov. 6 against Portland, Fri., Nov. 20 against Sacramento and Fri., Nov. 27 at San Antonio. Prime Video carries three of them. All four fall on Fridays, which the NBA calls "Cup Nights" in its release on the schedule; our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round, with the quarterfinals on Dec. 4 and 5 and the final on Dec. 11 in Indianapolis.
