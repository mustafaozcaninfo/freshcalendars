**The Budget on Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026 kept the State Pension triple lock, and MoneySavingExpert reports the April 2027 increase at 3.9%, which would take the full new State Pension to £250.70 a week.** HM Treasury's Budget 2026 document says the State Pension "will continue to rise by the highest of 2.5%, inflation or earnings growth each year through to April 2029." The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) publishes the official 2027-28 rates later, usually in late November. A one-page printable tracker is in the downloads.

## What did the Budget change for the State Pension?

HM Treasury's Budget 2026 document says "the State Pension will continue to rise by the highest of 2.5%, inflation or earnings growth each year through to April 2029." From April 2030, the document says the triple lock is adjusted "so that the State Pension rises each year by the highest of 2.5%, inflation, or the amount required to maintain its value relative to earnings as reached in April 2029." HM Treasury estimates that change cuts State Pension spending by £15 billion a year by the late 2030s and £50 billion a year by the late 2040s.

The Budget also sets a new Pension Tax Allowance from 6 April 2027. HM Treasury says people above State Pension age with total income up to and including the value of the full new State Pension will not pay Income Tax. It says around 500,000 pensioners benefit in 2027-28, around 290,000 paying no tax at all and the rest receiving a tapered allowance.

For wider context, the Budget raises the National Living Wage by 3.9% to £13.21 an hour on Thursday, April 1, 2027.

## What would the State Pension be in 2027-28?

The DWP has not yet published the official 2027-28 figures. MoneySavingExpert reported on Oct. 28, 2026 that the State Pension will rise 3.9% in April 2027. The table pairs the 2026-27 weekly rates from the DWP with those reported April 2027 amounts.

| Rate | 2026-27 weekly | April 2027 weekly (reported) | April 2027 yearly |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Full new State Pension | £241.30 | £250.70 | £13,036.40 |
| Full basic (old) State Pension | £184.90 | £192.10 | £9,989.20 |

Source: 2026-27 rates from the DWP, "Benefit and pension rates 2026/2027"; April 2027 weekly rates and the full new yearly figure reported by MoneySavingExpert (Oct. 28, 2026); the basic yearly figure is Fresh Calendars arithmetic (£192.10 × 52). Pension Credit is set separately; the 2026-27 standard minimum guarantee is £238.00 a week for a single person and £363.25 for a couple, and the 2027-28 amounts are not yet announced.

## How does the triple lock work?

The Government Actuary's Department report on the draft up-rating order 2026 (Jan. 13, 2026) says: "Triple Lock increases are determined as the highest of the annual growth in average May-July earnings (AWE), September prices (CPI) and 2.5%." AWE is average weekly earnings, and CPI is the Consumer Prices Index.

The same report says April 2026's rise was "4.8% in line with the May-July 2025 AWE earnings growth". The DWP said in an April 4, 2026 press release that both State Pensions rose 4.8% under the triple lock from Monday, April 6, 2026. That took the full new State Pension from £230.25 to £241.30 a week, and the basic State Pension from £176.45 to £184.90, per the DWP rates publication.

## What did the earnings and inflation figures say for 2027?

The Office for National Statistics (ONS) published two of the three triple lock inputs this autumn.

| Input | Figure | ONS release |
|---|---|---|
| Earnings, May to July 2026, total pay (including bonuses) | 3.9% | Average weekly earnings in Great Britain: September 2026 (Sept. 15, 2026) |
| Earnings, May to July 2026, regular pay (excluding bonuses) | 3.5% | Same release |
| CPI inflation, 12 months to September 2026 | 3.5%, up from 3.1% the previous month | Consumer price inflation, UK: September 2026 (Oct. 21, 2026) |
| Triple lock floor | 2.5% | Government Actuary's Department, Jan. 13, 2026 |

The highest of these inputs is 3.9% on total pay, which matches the 3.9% rise MoneySavingExpert reported for April 2027. See our [UK Budget 2026 story](/news/uk-budget-2026-date-wednesday-28-october-what-time/) for the time it was delivered.

## Is the State Pension age changing?

GOV.UK says the State Pension age is rising from 66 to 67 between April 2026 and March 2028. That is separate from the weekly rate, which is what the triple lock sets.
