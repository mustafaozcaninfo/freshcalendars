**The 2027 Women's World Cup opens on Thursday, June 24, 2027, and ends with the final on Sunday, July 25, 2027, at the Estádio do Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro, according to the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) schedule released Aug. 20, 2026.** Reuters reported the same dates the same day. Match kickoff times are not yet published, so this page lists what FIFA has confirmed and will add the times in Eastern Time (ET), Pacific Time (PT), UK and Canadian time once they are out. The final draw, which sets the eight groups, starts Friday, Dec. 11 at 1:30 p.m. in Rio, FIFA says, which is 11:30 a.m. ET.

## Women's World Cup 2027 dates to mark

FIFA said the release confirms "when and where all 64 fixtures will be played" for "all 32 teams." Hosts Brazil begin their campaign at the Maracanã on Thursday, June 24, then play at Arena Itaquera in São Paulo on Tuesday, June 29 and at the Estádio Nacional in Brasília on Sunday, July 4. From June 24 to July 25 inclusive the tournament spans 32 days, a count by Fresh Calendars. Mark both dates on the [June 2027 printable calendars](/june-calendars.html) and the [July 2027 printable calendars](/july-calendars.html).

| Date | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 10, 2026 | Draw for the final phase of the play-off tournament, 11:00 a.m. in Rio (9:00 a.m. ET) | Time confirmed by FIFA |
| Fri., Dec. 11, 2026 | Final draw at the Museum of Modern Art Rio de Janeiro, 1:30 p.m. in Rio | Time confirmed by FIFA; broadcaster not named |
| Thu., June 24, 2027 | Opener: Brazil at the Estádio do Maracanã | Date confirmed; kickoff not yet published |
| Sun., July 25, 2027 | Final at the Estádio do Maracanã | Date confirmed; kickoff not yet published |

FIFA also says that one semi-final and the final take place at the "iconic Estádio do Maracanã."

## Which host cities and stadiums will stage matches?

FIFA named eight host cities on May 7, 2025. The Maracanã is the only venue the schedule release ties to specific dates so far; group-stage fixtures are still to be allocated to the other cities.

| Host city | Stadium |
|---|---|
| Belo Horizonte | Estádio Mineirão |
| Brasília | Estádio Nacional |
| Fortaleza | Arena Castelão |
| Porto Alegre | Estádio Beira-Rio |
| Recife | Arena de Pernambuco |
| Rio de Janeiro | Estádio do Maracanã |
| Salvador | Arena Fonte Nova |
| São Paulo | Arena Itaquera |

## What time is the Women's World Cup 2027 draw?

FIFA's Nov. 11, 2026 release gives the final draw as Friday, Dec. 11 at 13:30 local time in Rio de Janeiro, which FIFA lists as 17:30 CET. Brazil does not change its clocks, so that is 4:30 p.m. UK time, 11:30 a.m. ET and 8:30 a.m. PT. The play-off draw is a day earlier, Thursday, Dec. 10 at 11:00 local time (9:00 a.m. ET). The conversions are ours. The 32 teams are split into four pots of eight, ranked by the FIFA/Coca-Cola Women's World Ranking published Monday, Dec. 7. Brazil is in Pot 1 and is placed in position A1 automatically. The three last places go to the winners of the play-off tournament, played from Feb. 23 to March 6, 2027.

## When will Women's World Cup 2027 kickoff times be announced?

After the draw on Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, FIFA "will finalise and publish an updated version of the match schedule, with group-stage fixtures allocated to Host Cities and kick-off times confirmed." Until then there is no official kickoff time, so no conversion to ET, PT, UK or Canadian time is possible, and we are not guessing one.
