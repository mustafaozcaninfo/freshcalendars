## Is The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth on Netflix in November?

No. Netflix's guide lists "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth" for Dec. 23, 2026 on Netflix, with an IMAX release on Nov. 25. That makes it a December Netflix title, so it is not on the November list or the printable.

## What time do new Netflix movies come out?

Netflix's Help Center says Netflix originals are "typically released globally at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time (PT)." That is 3 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), our calculation from the Pacific time; the Help Center does not give an Eastern time. The U.S. is on standard time after Nov. 1, 2026, and the 3 a.m. ET conversion holds for every date in the table. The guide does not give a release time for any single movie, so check the title's page on the day.
