<p class="callout"><b>Checked Oct. 25, 2026.</b> Post times below are from the Breeders' Cup's Oct. 21 announcement of the final race order; the full race-by-race list will be added as we confirm it. Post times can change.</p>

**The Breeders' Cup Classic 2026 has a post time of 6:16 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Saturday, Oct. 31, at Keeneland Race Course in Lexington, Ky., according to the Breeders' Cup's announcement of Oct. 21.** The two-day Championships start Friday, Oct. 30. Below: the post times we have confirmed, the TV windows, and the Classic in each U.S. time zone.

## When is the Breeders' Cup 2026?

The Breeders' Cup lists the World Championships for Oct. 30-31, 2026, a Friday and a Saturday. NBC Sports says they take place at Keeneland Race Course in Lexington, Ky.

## What are the Breeders' Cup 2026 post times?

These are the post times in the Breeders' Cup's announcement of the final race order. We have not confirmed the rest of the card, so races not listed are left out.

| Day | Event | Post time (ET) |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 30 | First race of the day | 11:55 a.m. |
| Fri., Oct. 30 | Juvenile Turf Sprint (first Championship race) | 2:55 p.m. |
| Fri., Oct. 30 | FanDuel Breeders' Cup Juvenile (Race 9) | 4:55 p.m. |
| Sat., Oct. 31 | Filly & Mare Sprint (Race 4) | 1:10 p.m. |
| Sat., Oct. 31 | Distaff | 4:58 p.m. |
| Sat., Oct. 31 | Turf | 5:36 p.m. |
| Sat., Oct. 31 | $7 million Longines Breeders' Cup Classic (Race 12) | 6:16 p.m. |

Third-party guides may list a different Classic time. We use the Breeders' Cup's 6:16 p.m. ET.

## What time is the Breeders' Cup Classic in my time zone?

Saturday, Oct. 31 is still on U.S. daylight time, so the table uses Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), Central Daylight Time (CDT), Mountain Daylight Time (MDT) and Pacific Daylight Time (PDT). The United Kingdom has been on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) since Oct. 25. The conversions are ours, not the Breeders' Cup's.

| Place | Classic post time, Sat., Oct. 31 |
|---|---|
| Eastern | 6:16 p.m. EDT |
| Central | 5:16 p.m. CDT |
| Mountain | 4:16 p.m. MDT |
| Pacific | 3:16 p.m. PDT |
| United Kingdom | 10:16 p.m. GMT |

## What TV channel is the Breeders' Cup on?

NBC Sports said on Apr. 8, 2026 that the Championships "will be televised live by NBC Sports and FanDuel TV," and that NBC will present the World Championships on NBC and Peacock. The Breeders' Cup's broadcast announcement gives the windows below.

| Day | Time (ET) | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 30 | 1 to 6 p.m. | USA Network and Peacock |
| Sat., Oct. 31 | 1 to 3:30 p.m. | USA Network |
| Sat., Oct. 31 | 3:30 to 6:30 p.m. | NBC and Peacock |

The Breeders' Cup says FanDuel TV shows the undercard and the Championship races except the Classic. By these windows, the 6:16 p.m. ET Classic falls in the NBC and Peacock block.
