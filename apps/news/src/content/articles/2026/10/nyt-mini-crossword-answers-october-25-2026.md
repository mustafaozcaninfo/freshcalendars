**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Christina Iverson, and two of its Across answers are music genres that start and end with the same letters.** Joel Fagliano edited it. Below is a hint for every clue, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *Music genre for Joni Mitchell or Woody Guthrie*: acoustic guitars and storytelling.
- **5-Across** (5 letters), *Flood embankment*: a raised bank along a river.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *"That is to say ..."*: two words you say before rephrasing yourself.
- **7-Across** (4 letters), *Music genre with the same first and last letters as 1-Across*: think James Brown and a heavy bass groove.
- **8-Across** (3 letters), *Hosp. areas that are usually on the ground floor*: where ambulances pull up, in plural shorthand.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Strongest bone in the human body*: the thigh bone.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Array in a culinary classroom*: what students bake in.
- **3-Down** (4 letters), *Plumbing problem*: a slow drip.
- **4-Down** (3 letters), *He's a doll!*: Barbie's companion.
- **5-Down** (4 letters), *Magazine whose cover featured Elizabeth Taylor a record 14 times*: a classic photo weekly with a one-word name.

## Solving tips

Start with the bone at 1-Down: once its first letter is in, 1-Across follows, and 7-Across shares that genre's first and last letters, so two answers come almost at once. The three-letter entries at 4-Down and 8-Across fill the right side, and the plumbing clue at 3-Down confirms the middle.
