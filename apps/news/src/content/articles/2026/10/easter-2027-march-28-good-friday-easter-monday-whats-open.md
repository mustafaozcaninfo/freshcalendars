**Easter Sunday 2027 is March 28, and the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) lists Good Friday, Friday, March 26, 2027, as a market holiday** on its [holidays and trading hours page](https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars). Good Friday is not a federal holiday under the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) list, and it is not on the Federal Reserve's holiday list either. Below: what that means, the United Kingdom (UK) bank holidays, Holy Week 2027 and a table of Easter dates through 2035. For how the Easter date is set, see our [when is Easter page](/when-is/easter/).

## Which Easter 2027 days close the stock market and federal offices?

The two lists differ. Here is what each official source says for 2027.

| Organization | Good Friday, March 26, 2027 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) | Listed as a holiday: "Good Friday: Friday, March 26" | NYSE holidays and trading hours |
| Federal Reserve | Not on the list of Federal Reserve holidays | Federal Reserve K.8 page |
| Federal government | Not on the list of 2027 federal holidays | OPM federal holidays page |

So the stock market takes the day off while the Federal Reserve and federal government do not list it as a holiday. Our [NYSE 2027 closed dates story](/news/stock-market-holidays-2027-nyse-closed-dates/) covers the other market holidays in the year. Easter Sunday itself is a Sunday, and Easter Monday, March 29, is not on the NYSE, Federal Reserve or OPM lists for 2027 that we checked.

If you need a particular office, school or business, check with it directly. This story only reports the three lists above. Our [Good Friday page](/when-is/good-friday/) has the date in other years.

## Which UK bank holidays fall at Easter 2027?

GOV.UK, the United Kingdom government's website, lists these 2027 bank holidays.

| Nation | Good Friday, March 26 | Easter Monday, March 29 |
|---|---|---|
| England and Wales | Bank holiday | Bank holiday |
| Scotland | Bank holiday | No bank holiday |
| Northern Ireland | Bank holiday | Bank holiday |

The full list for the year is in our [UK bank holidays 2027 story](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/).

## What are the dates of Holy Week 2027?

Holy Week is the week before Easter. We computed these days from Easter Sunday, March 28, 2027.

| Day | Date in 2027 |
|---|---|
| Palm Sunday | Sunday, March 21 |
| Holy Thursday | Thursday, March 25 |
| Good Friday | Friday, March 26 |
| Easter Sunday | Sunday, March 28 |
| Easter Monday | Monday, March 29 |

Lent leads up to this week. Our [Mardi Gras and Ash Wednesday 2027 story](/news/mardi-gras-2027-february-9-ash-wednesday-february-10-lent-calendar/) has the start of the season.

## When is Easter from 2027 to 2035?

We computed the date of Western Easter Sunday with the Anonymous Gregorian algorithm, then counted two days back for Good Friday and one day forward for Easter Monday. The result for 2027 matches the March 28 date implied by the GOV.UK bank holidays.

| Year | Good Friday | Easter Sunday | Easter Monday |

| 2029 | Fri., March 30 | Sun., April 1 | Mon., April 2 |
