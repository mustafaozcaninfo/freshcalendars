<p class="callout"><b>Checked Friday, Nov. 6, 2026.</b> The Internal Revenue Service released the 2027 tax brackets on Nov. 5. We will add the 2027 401(k) limits the day it publishes them.</p>

**On Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) released the tax-year 2027 inflation adjustments in news release IR-2026-133 and Revenue Procedure 2026-35.** The 2027 standard deduction is $16,600 for single filers and $33,200 for married couples filing jointly, and the top rate stays 37%. The release says the 2027 figures generally apply to tax returns filed in 2028, so the return due in April 2027 still uses the 2026 numbers below. The 2027 401(k), 403(b) and individual retirement account (IRA) limits are not yet announced; IR-2026-133 does not contain them.

We use only official figures here. The amounts below come from IRS revenue procedures and notices and from the Social Security Administration (SSA). We do not publish third-party projections.

Last year the IRS published the 2026 retirement-plan limits in a news release and Notice 2025-67 on Nov. 13, 2025. The IRS has not said when the 2027 retirement-plan limits will follow.

## 2027 tax brackets

These rates are from Revenue Procedure 2026-35 for tax year 2027. Taxable income above each threshold is taxed at that rate.

| Rate | Single, over | Married filing jointly, over | Head of household, over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| 12% | $12,800 | $25,600 | $18,250 |
| 22% | $52,025 | $104,050 | $69,650 |
| 24% | $109,125 | $218,250 | $109,100 |
| 32% | $208,325 | $416,650 | $208,300 |
| 35% | $264,550 | $529,100 | $264,550 |
| 37% | $661,375 | $793,650 | $661,350 |

The 10% rate applies up to $12,800 for single filers, up to $25,600 for married couples filing jointly and up to $18,250 for heads of household.

## 2027 Health Savings Account limits (official)

The IRS sets Health Savings Account limits earlier than the rest, in a spring revenue procedure. Revenue Procedure 2026-24, published in Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-25 on June 15, 2026, sets the 2027 amounts.

| Health Savings Account item | 2027 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Contribution limit, self-only | $4,500 | $4,400 |
| Contribution limit, family | $9,000 | $8,750 |
| Catch-up, age 55 and older | $1,000 | $1,000 |
| High-deductible plan out-of-pocket max, self-only | $8,700 | not listed here |
| High-deductible plan out-of-pocket max, family | $17,400 | not listed here |
| Excepted-benefit health reimbursement arrangement max | $2,250 | not listed here |

The age-55 catch-up amount is fixed by statute at $1,000, so it does not change with inflation. An out-of-pocket maximum applies to a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) that pairs with a Health Savings Account.

## 2027 Social Security changes (official)

The Social Security Administration released its 2027 fact sheet on Oct. 14, 2026.

| Social Security item | 2027 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) | 3.6% | n/a |
| Taxable maximum (wages subject to the tax) | $192,300 | $184,500 |
| Earnings test, under full retirement age | $25,560 per year | n/a |
| Earnings test, year reaching full retirement age | $67,920 | n/a |

Below full retirement age, the Social Security Administration withholds $1 in benefits for every $2 earned above $25,560 in 2027. In the year a worker reaches full retirement age (FRA), it withholds $1 for every $3 above $67,920, counting only earnings before the birthday month. The Social Security payroll tax rate stays at 6.2% each for the employee and the employer. Our [Social Security cost-of-living adjustment report](/news/social-security-cola-2027-3-6-percent-payment-dates/) has the 2027 payment dates.

## 2026 additional standard deduction

The additional standard deduction for taxpayers who are 65 or older or blind is $1,650 each for married filers and $2,050 each for single filers and heads of household in 2026. The 2027 versions of these amounts are not in the figures we have confirmed.
