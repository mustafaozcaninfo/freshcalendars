**Major League Soccer's (MLS) regular season ended on Decision Day, Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026 (Eastern Conference at 4 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), Western Conference at 7 p.m. ET), the playoffs start with Wild Card matches on Thursday, Nov. 19, and MLS Cup presented by Audi is on Friday, Dec. 18, according to the league's official playoff key dates.** MLS announced the schedule on Mar. 16, 2026, and says the dates are subject to change.

This page lays out each round by date with its weekday, lists the final field and the matchups, and says where the games air. A one-page printable of the timeline is below.

## What is the MLS Cup playoffs 2026 schedule?

MLS publishes one date or range for each stage. Round One is a best-of-3 series. MLS's matchup page says it begins Saturday, Nov. 21, while its key-dates page gives a window that opens Friday, Nov. 20; the posted games run from Sat., Nov. 21 to Wed., Dec. 2.

| Stage | Dates |
|---|---|
| Decision Day | Sat., Nov. 7 |
| Wild Card | Thu., Nov. 19 |
| Round One, Game One | Sat., Nov. 21 to Tue., Nov. 24 |
| Round One, Game Two | Fri., Nov. 27 to Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Round One, Game Three (if necessary) | Wed., Dec. 2 |
| Conference Semifinals | Sat., Dec. 5 to Sun., Dec. 6 |
| Conference Finals | Fri., Dec. 11 to Sat., Dec. 12 |
| MLS Cup presented by Audi | Fri., Dec. 18 |

## When is MLS Decision Day 2026?

Decision Day was Sat., Nov. 7, 2026, per MLS, with the East at 4 p.m. ET and the West at 7 p.m. ET. MLS says the field is complete, with all 18 spots confirmed following Decision Day. Nashville SC won their first-ever Supporters' Shield, announced by the club on Oct. 24, 2026. After Decision Day, MLS says it will observe the November Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) international window, and the Wild Card does not start until Thu., Nov. 19.

## How does the MLS playoffs 2026 format work?

MLS says 18 teams qualify, the top nine in the East and the top nine in the West. Seeds 1 to 7 in each conference go to Round One. Seeds 8 and 9 play single-game Wild Card matches, hosted by the higher seed.

## Who hosts each MLS playoff round?

MLS names the host rule for every round. MLS says the Round One dates were added on Oct. 7 and are subject to change.

| Round | Dates | Who hosts |
|---|---|---|
| Wild Card (No. 8 vs. No. 9 seed, single game) | Thu., Nov. 19 | The higher seed; winners face their conference's No. 1 seed |
| Round One, Game One (best-of-3) | Sat., Nov. 21 to Tue., Nov. 24 | The higher seed |
| Round One, Game Two | Fri., Nov. 27 to Sat., Nov. 28 | The lower seed |
| Round One, Game Three (if necessary) | Wed., Dec. 2 | The higher seed |
| Conference Semifinals (single game) | Sat., Dec. 5 to Sun., Dec. 6 | The higher seed (single-elimination match) |
| Conference Finals | Fri., Dec. 11 to Sat., Dec. 12 | The higher seed (single-elimination match) |
| MLS Cup presented by Audi (primetime) | Fri., Dec. 18 | The finalist with the best regular-season record |

Source: Major League Soccer, 2026. MLS says all 16 Round One teams play at least one home game.

## Where can I watch the MLS playoffs?

MLS says every playoff match is broadcast live on Apple TV. MLS's Nov. 7 release says MLS Cup airs on FOX and FOX Deportes in the U.S. and on The Sports Network (TSN) and Réseau des sports (RDS) in Canada, "while select additional playoff matches will air on FS1, FOX Deportes, TSN and RDS." The two Wild Card matches are on Apple TV. In the Round One schedule MLS posted, every game is on Apple TV, and some games add FS1, FOX Deportes, TSN and RDS; the Round One table above lists the channels game by game.

## Who won MLS Cup last year?

In 2025, Inter Miami CF beat Vancouver Whitecaps FC 3-1 in MLS Cup on Saturday, Dec. 6, 2025, at Chase Stadium in Fort Lauderdale, Fla., per MLS. MLS says the 2026 final is the last MLS Cup in December before the league's shift to a summer-spring calendar.
