**The Emirates NBA Cup 2026 begins on Fri., Oct. 30 and concludes on Fri., Dec. 11 with the Championship at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA).** NBA.com says the competition "begins on Oct. 30 and concludes Dec. 11 with the Emirates NBA Cup 2026 Championship." Opening night is a Prime Video doubleheader: the New York Knicks at the Philadelphia 76ers at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the Los Angeles Lakers at the Golden State Warriors at 10 p.m. ET.

This page puts every round on one dated table with its weekday and the network facts the league has confirmed. A one-page printable of the same dates is further down.

## What are the NBA Cup 2026 dates?

NBA.com lists Group Play on Oct. 30 and on Nov. 6, 13, 20, 24, 25 and 27. The Quarterfinals are Dec. 4 and 5, the semifinals are Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9, and the Championship is on Friday, Dec. 11. The weekdays below are our own calculation from those dates.

| Round | Date | Weekday | What the NBA says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group Play | Oct. 30 | Friday | Competition begins; a Cup Night |
| Group Play | Nov. 6 | Friday | A Cup Night |
| Group Play | Nov. 13 | Friday | A Cup Night |
| Group Play | Nov. 20 | Friday | A Cup Night |
| Group Play | Nov. 24 | Tuesday | NBC and Peacock air two Group Play games |
| Group Play | Nov. 25 | Wednesday | Three Group Play games, the day before Thanksgiving |
| Group Play | Nov. 27 | Friday | A Cup Night; last Group Play date |
| Quarterfinals | Dec. 4 and 5 | Friday and Saturday | Played in NBA team markets |
| Semifinals | Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9 | Tuesday and/or Wednesday | On Prime Video |
| Championship | Dec. 11 | Friday | Hinkle Fieldhouse, Indianapolis; on Prime Video |

NBA.com gives the semifinal date as "Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9", so the split between the two nights is not in the sources we used.

## What are Cup Nights?

In its Aug. 13, 2026 release on the 2026-27 regular season schedule, NBA Communications describes "60 games on 'Cup Nights' every Friday from Oct. 30 through Nov. 27." Those Fridays are Oct. 30 and Nov. 6, 13, 20 and 27. Nov. 24 and Nov. 25 are the two Group Play dates that fall outside the Friday pattern.

The Nov. 25 date sits on the day before Thanksgiving, which falls on Thursday, Nov. 26. Our [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has that holiday's details.

## How are the groups set?

NBA.com says all 30 teams "have been randomly drawn into groups of five within their conference," based on 2025-26 records. This page does not list the groups, since we did not copy them from the league's page. Group Play has 60 games in total, according to NBA.com's schedule article.

## Where can I watch the NBA Cup games?

The networks that the league has confirmed so far:

- **Group Play:** NBA.com says that of the 60 total Group Play games, 15 will be broadcast across Prime Video, NBC and ESPN. NBA Communications adds that NBC and Peacock will air two Group Play games on Tuesday, Nov. 24.
- **Knockout Rounds:** Prime Video will present all seven games, including the two semifinals and the Championship, according to NBA Communications.

NBA.com's Aug. 12 Group Play release names the national network for each Cup Night:

| Cup Night | National TV |
|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 30 | Prime Video: Knicks at 76ers, 7:30 p.m. ET; Lakers at Warriors, 10 p.m. ET |
| Fri., Nov. 6, 13 and 20 | Prime Video |
| Tue., Nov. 24 | NBC and Peacock |
| Wed., Nov. 25 | ESPN |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Prime Video |

Other Group Play games air on local and regional channels and NBA League Pass. Check NBA.com for their tip times.
