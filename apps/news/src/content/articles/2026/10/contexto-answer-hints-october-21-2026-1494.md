## How Contexto works

Contexto ranks every guess by how close its meaning is to the secret word, which sits at number 1. It does not care about spelling or letters, only about how words are used together. A guess in the top 300 or so shows green, a middling one yellow, and a distant one red. Use your best-ranked words to steer your next guesses.

## Hints, broad to strong

1. It is a noun of five letters, and it is also a verb.
2. It happens very fast, in an instant.
3. It is connected with light: cameras, storms and fireworks all make one.
4. A superhero who runs at super speed shares its name.
