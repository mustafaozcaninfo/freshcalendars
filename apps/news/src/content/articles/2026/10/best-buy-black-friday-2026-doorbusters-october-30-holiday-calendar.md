**Best Buy's Black Friday 2026 hours are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Friday, Nov. 27, and stores are closed Thursday, Nov. 26, according to its Nov. 17 announcement.** Its 10 days of Black Friday doorbusters and deals began Thursday, Nov. 19. DoorBOOsters began Friday, Oct. 30, and Early Black Friday Deals and Doorbusters began Nov. 6. Best Buy Corporate News says new Doorbusters drop every week and that shoppers get major savings events through December, including Cyber Week Deals on Dec. 1-6. Extended store hours started Sunday, Nov. 1. Best Buy's Nov. 17 announcement lists a sneak peek of sale prices (quoted in the prices section), and the rest of this story covers dates, hours and rules. Every date from Oct. 30 to Jan. 31, 2027 is in the table below, and a one-page printable lists those from Nov. 1.

## What sale prices has Best Buy listed?

Best Buy's Nov. 17 release gives a "sneak peek" of the Black Friday Sale. These are the lines it lists, with Best Buy's own wording for each price:

- Apple MacBook Air 13-inch Laptop: save $250 (sale price $1049.00).
- iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max: save $100 (sale price 1099.99).
- Sony Wireless Noise Cancelling Over-the-Ear Headphones: save $229 (sale price $169.00).
- Samsung Galaxy S26 FE: save $200 (sale price $499.99).
- Shark PowerDetect Speed Cordless Stick Vacuum Cleaner: save $300 (sale price $199.99).
- Oura Ring 5: as low as $299.99.
- Select trading cards and select major appliances: save up to 50%.

Best Buy says the Cyber Sunday and Monday Sale Event offers savings of up to 50%. Prices are valid during, but may start before and end after, each event, with limited quantities and no rainchecks. Store hours, end times by U.S. time zone, the return window and price match terms for that weekend are in our [Best Buy Cyber Monday 2026 story](/news/best-buy-cyber-monday-2026-dates-return-window-price-match/).

## When can I return what I buy?

Best Buy says "most purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 31 can be returned through Jan. 15, 2027, and Jan. 31, 2027 for Plus and Total members." The announcement says "most," so check the product page for exceptions.

| Bought | Standard return deadline | My Best Buy Plus and Total members |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026 | Fri., Jan. 15, 2027 | Sun., Jan. 31, 2027 |

Deadlines at other chains are in our [holiday return deadlines by store](/news/holiday-return-policies-2026-return-deadlines-by-store/) guide.

## What do double rewards and price match cover?

From Nov. 19 to Dec. 6, My Best Buy Plus and Total members earn double rewards on purchases. A footnote explains that 1 point per $1 equals 2% back in rewards, inclusive of the 1% members already get. Points post 2-3 weeks after purchase, and the offer is valid through Dec. 6, 2026.

Best Buy's Holiday Price Match Guarantee says: "If the price of a product sold by Best Buy drops during the holiday season, we'll match it." It excludes Marketplace products.

## Is Best Buy open on Thanksgiving and Black Friday?

Best Buy's Nov. 17 announcement lists Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26, as closed. Its Black Friday hours are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Best Buy gives no time zone, so these are local store times, and it adds "Hours may vary by location." Check your local store page for up-to-date hours.

| Date (2026) | Day | Store hours |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 19 | Thursday | 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. |
| Nov. 20-22 | Friday to Sunday | 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. |
| Nov. 23-25 | Monday to Wednesday | 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. |
| Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving Day) | Thursday | Closed |
| Nov. 27 (Black Friday) | Friday | 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. |
| Nov. 28 | Saturday | 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. |
| Nov. 29 (Cyber Sunday) | Sunday | 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. |
| Nov. 30 (Cyber Monday) | Monday | 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. |

Black Friday also has an in-store-only offer on Nov. 27: My Best Buy members (free, Plus and Total) "can purchase a $100 Best Buy Gift Card for $60 on Black Friday." Best Buy says at least 50 gift cards will be available at each store while supplies last, limited to 1 gift card offer per person. For other chains, see our [Black Friday 2026 store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/).

## What has not been announced?

- The start time of the Thanksgiving 24-Hour Flash Sale and of the Cyber Monday Flash Sale.
- An end date for the 10-day Black Friday sale.

## How does Best Buy compare with Walmart and Target?

Walmart announced Early Black Friday Deals Nov. 12-15 and Black Friday Deals Nov. 23-29 on Oct. 26, 2026, and Target set Early Black Friday deals for Nov. 5-7 with a Deal of the Day from Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, in its Oct. 21 release. Our [Black Friday 2026 ad tracker](/news/black-friday-2026-ad-tracker-walmart-target-best-buy/) follows all three, and the [Small Business Saturday and Cyber Monday guide](/news/small-business-saturday-2026-participating-stores-events/) covers the weekend after.

Patrick McGinnis, chief revenue officer at Best Buy, said in the announcement: "Everyone does their holiday shopping a little differently. Some start in October and are done by Thanksgiving, and some don't start until the last minute."
