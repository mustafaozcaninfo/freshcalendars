**Best Buy's holiday deals start Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, with the return of DoorBOOsters, the retailer said in its holiday calendar announcement on Oct. 27.** Best Buy Corporate News says "Halloween marks the official start of holiday savings at Best Buy," that new Doorbusters will drop every week and that shoppers get major savings events through December. Early Black Friday Deals and Doorbusters begin Nov. 6, and Cyber Week Deals run Dec. 1-6. Best Buy has not published doorbuster products or prices, so this story lists dates and rules only. Every date from Oct. 30 to Jan. 31, 2027 is in the table below and on a one-page printable.

## What are Best Buy's 2026 holiday dates?

| Date (2026) | Day | What Best Buy announced |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 30 | Friday | DoorBOOsters kick off holiday savings |
| Nov. 1 | Sunday | Extended store hours start (Best Buy also mentions 24/7 Pickup Lockers at select stores) |
| Nov. 6 | Friday | Early Black Friday Deals and Doorbusters; new deals drop every Friday |
| Nov. 13, 20, 27 | Fridays | Weekly Friday drops (our weekdays from the "every Friday" line) |
| Nov. 19 | Thursday | 10 days of Black Friday doorbusters and deals start; double rewards for My Best Buy Plus and Total members begin |
| Nov. 26 | Thursday | Thanksgiving Day: stores closed; deals continue in the app and online |
| Nov. 27 | Friday | Black Friday (computed) |
| Nov. 28 | Saturday | 10th day of the Black Friday sale by our count |
| Cyber Sunday and Monday | Dates not given | Best Buy gave no exact dates; Cyber Monday 2026 is Nov. 30 and the Sunday before is Nov. 29 (computed) |
| Dec. 1-6 | Tuesday to Sunday | Cyber Week Deals; double rewards end Dec. 6 |
| Dec. 18-24 | Friday to Thursday | Last-Minute Savings Event |
| Dec. 31 | Thursday | Last day of the holiday return-eligible purchase window |
| Jan. 15, 2027 | Friday | Return deadline for most holiday purchases |
| Jan. 31, 2027 | Sunday | Return deadline for My Best Buy Plus and Total members |

Best Buy did not publish an end date for its 10-day Black Friday sale. Ten days from Thursday, Nov. 19, runs through Saturday, Nov. 28 by our count. Our [Black Friday 2026 date page](/when-is/black-friday/) and [Cyber Monday page](/when-is/cyber-monday/) confirm the weekdays, and you can mark everything on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When can I return what I buy?

Best Buy says "most purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 31 can be returned through Jan. 15, 2027, and Jan. 31, 2027 for Plus and Total members." The announcement says "most," so check the product page for exceptions.

| Bought | Standard return deadline | My Best Buy Plus and Total members |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026 | Fri., Jan. 15, 2027 | Sun., Jan. 31, 2027 |

## What do double rewards and price match cover?

From Nov. 19 to Dec. 6, My Best Buy Plus and Total members earn double rewards on purchases. A footnote explains that 1 point per $1 equals 2% back in rewards, inclusive of the 1% members already get. Points post 2-3 weeks after purchase, and the offer is valid through Dec. 6, 2026.

Best Buy's Holiday Price Match Guarantee says: "If the price of a product sold by Best Buy drops during the holiday season, we'll match it." It excludes Marketplace products.

## Is Best Buy open on Thanksgiving and Black Friday?

Best Buy says stores will be closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, with deals continuing in the app and online. From Nov. 1 it offers extended store hours, and customers can find updated hours on their local store page. Best Buy did not give a Black Friday opening time in the announcement. For other chains, see our [Black Friday 2026 store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/).

## What has not been announced?

- Specific doorbuster products and prices.
- Store opening time on Black Friday.
- Exact dates for the Cyber Sunday and Monday Sale Event.
- An end date for the 10-day Black Friday sale.

## How does Best Buy compare with Walmart and Target?

Walmart announced Early Black Friday Deals Nov. 12-15 and Black Friday Deals Nov. 23-29 on Oct. 26, 2026, and Target set Early Black Friday deals for Nov. 5-7 with a Deal of the Day from Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, in its Oct. 21 release. Our [Black Friday 2026 ad tracker](/news/black-friday-2026-ad-tracker-walmart-target-best-buy/) follows all three, and the [Small Business Saturday and Cyber Monday guide](/news/small-business-saturday-2026-november-28-cyber-monday-november-30/) covers the weekend after.

Patrick McGinnis, chief revenue officer at Best Buy, said in the announcement: "Everyone does their holiday shopping a little differently. Some start in October and are done by Thanksgiving, and some don't start until the last minute."
