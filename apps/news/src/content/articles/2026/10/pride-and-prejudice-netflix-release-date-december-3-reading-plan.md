**Netflix's six-part Pride and Prejudice series, written by Dolly Alderton and starring Emma Corrin and Jack Lowden, premieres Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026, Netflix Tudum said on Oct. 28, 2026, the day it released the trailer article.** Netflix's title page labels it a "Limited Series." Below are the cast, what Netflix has not yet announced, and a free 35-day plan to read the novel before the premiere.

## When does Pride and Prejudice come out on Netflix?

Netflix Tudum's article "Pride and Prejudice: Teaser, Cast, Photos, Release Date" says the series "debuts on Dec. 3." Dec. 3, 2026 is a Thursday. Alderton announced the date at the Jane Austen Festival in Bath, England, according to a second Tudum article from Oct. 28, 2026.

Netflix has not announced these details, so none are given here:

| Detail | Status |
|---|---|
| Release time of day | Not announced |
| Whether all six episodes arrive at once | Not announced; Netflix says only "debuts on Dec. 3" |
| Episode runtime | Not announced |
| Filming locations | Not announced |

## Who is in the Netflix Pride and Prejudice cast?

Netflix Tudum's trailer article names the cast below. It says Alderton wrote all six episodes and that Euros Lyn directs.

| Character | Actor |
|---|---|
| Elizabeth "Lizzie" Bennet | Emma Corrin |
| Mr. Darcy | Jack Lowden |
| Mrs. Bennet | Olivia Colman |
| Mr. Bennet | Rufus Sewell |
| Jane | Freya Mavor |
| Mary | Hopey Parish |
| Kitty | Hollie Avery |
| Lydia | Rhea Norwood |
| Mr. Collins | Jamie Demetriou |
| Mr. Bingley | Daryl McCormack |
| Mr. Wickham | Louis Partridge |
| Lady Catherine de Bourgh | Fiona Shaw |

Alderton and Lyn are also executive producers, "alongside Laura Lankester, Will Johnston, and Louise Mutter for Lookout Point," Tudum says. It adds that "the six episodes will hew closely to the 1813 novel."

## Can you read Pride and Prejudice before the premiere?

Dec. 3 is 35 days after Thursday, Oct. 29, so the days from Oct. 29 through Wednesday, Dec. 2 make five full weeks. The Project Gutenberg edition (eBook #1342), a free public-domain text, has 61 chapters. The plan splits them into four weeks of 12 chapters and a last week of 13.

| Week | Dates | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thu., Oct. 29 to Wed., Nov. 4 | 1 to 12 |
| 2 | Thu., Nov. 5 to Wed., Nov. 11 | 13 to 24 |
| 3 | Thu., Nov. 12 to Wed., Nov. 18 | 25 to 36 |
| 4 | Thu., Nov. 19 to Wed., Nov. 25 | 37 to 48 |
| 5 | Thu., Nov. 26 to Wed., Dec. 2 | 49 to 61 (13 chapters) |
| Premiere | Thu., Dec. 3 | Series debuts on Netflix |

Week 5 begins on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, so it is the week to plan around. Print the table as a one-page checklist and tick off each week as you finish it.
