## Which pick'em sheet should you print?

All six sheets are original designs with team names in plain text and no logos. Pick the format that matches how your group plays.

| Format | What players fill in | Scoring | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic pick'em sheet | A tick box for the away or home team in each game, the final score blanks and a tiebreaker | Your group's rule; one point per correct pick is the simplest | Family, friends and one-sheet-per-person pools |

## How do you run a pick'em pool with paper sheets?

1. Print one sheet per player, or one office pool grid for the group. Use the classic sheet for a simple pool and the confidence sheet if players rank their picks.
2. Hand them out early in the week and set the deadline at the first kickoff: Thursday at 8:15 p.m. ET in both weeks.
3. Collect the sheets before that time and check that each confidence number from 1 to 14 (Week 8) or 1 to 15 (Week 9) is used once.
4. After each game, write the final score and tick the winner on the classic sheet, or fill the "Won?" box on the confidence sheet.
5. Add up each player's correct picks, or confidence points, after Monday's game.
6. If players tie, use the total points guess for the Monday tiebreaker game, and agree on how it breaks a tie before play starts.
