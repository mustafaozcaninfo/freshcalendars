## What this tells you for November

These are patterns in last month's words, not a forecast of any answer. They can still shape how you play.

- **Try short everyday words early.** Fourteen of 31 answers had 3 to 5 letters, and the four answers from Oct. 27 to Oct. 30 had 15 letters between them.
- **Start with a broad category word.** For Grape, the game ranked Fruit second and Apple sixth, and for Pear it ranked Apple second and Peach third.
- **Test other forms of a near word.** Six of 31 answers had a form of their own word among the five closest, so a guess like Criticism or Declare can point straight at the answer.
- **Keep general words in your guesses.** Quality, Product, Knowledge and Prevent each appeared near two different answers.
