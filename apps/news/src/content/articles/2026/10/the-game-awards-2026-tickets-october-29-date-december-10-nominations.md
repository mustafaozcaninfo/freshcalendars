**Public tickets for The Game Awards 2026 go on sale Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026, at 10 a.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 1 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), through AXS, and the show is Thursday, Dec. 10, 2026 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, according to The Game Awards.** Its announcement says "a limited number of public tickets" will be available, so buy "while available." Below: the sale time in each time zone, what is still not announced, and a dated countdown.

## What time do The Game Awards 2026 tickets go on sale?

The Game Awards gives the time as 10 a.m. PT / 1 p.m. ET on Thursday, Oct. 29, and points buyers to AXS. The other columns are our conversion. U.S. and Canadian clocks are still on daylight time on Oct. 29, because U.S. daylight time ends Sunday, Nov. 1. The United Kingdom already moved to Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) on Oct. 25.

| Where | Time zone | Sale opens on Thu., Oct. 29 |
|---|---|---|
| Hawaii | Hawaii Standard Time (HST) | 7 a.m. |
| Alaska | Alaska Daylight Time (AKDT) | 9 a.m. |
| Pacific (Los Angeles, Vancouver) | Pacific Daylight Time (PDT) | 10 a.m. |
| Mountain (Denver, Calgary) | Mountain Daylight Time (MDT) | 11 a.m. |
| Central (Chicago, Winnipeg) | Central Daylight Time (CDT) | 12 p.m. |
| Eastern (New York, Toronto) | Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) | 1 p.m. |
| United Kingdom | GMT | 5 p.m. |

Prices are not listed on The Game Awards announcement. The [AXS event page](https://www.axs.com/events/1554271/the-game-awards-tickets) is the place to see what is on offer.

## When is The Game Awards 2026?

The Game Awards 2026 takes place on Thursday, Dec. 10, 2026, "streaming live from Peacock Theater in Los Angeles," according to its Aug. 10, 2026 announcement. From the ticket sale on Oct. 29 to show night is 42 days. Last year the show was on Thursday, Dec. 11, 2025, per The Game Awards nominees page.

## When are the 2026 Game Awards nominations announced?

The Game Awards homepage lists the 2026 nominees as "coming mid-November." No exact day is announced yet, so treat any specific date you see elsewhere as unconfirmed. The site says nominations are selected by a voting jury of over 100 leading media and influencer outlets across the globe.
