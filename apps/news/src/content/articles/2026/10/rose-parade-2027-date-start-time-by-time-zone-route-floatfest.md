**The 138th Rose Parade presented by Honda begins at 8 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, which is 11 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the Tournament of Roses.** The organization lists the parade from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. in Pasadena, California. Below: the start time in each U.S. time zone, the route, and one week of dates from four separate official pages.

## What time does the Rose Parade start in each time zone?

The Tournament of Roses gives the start in Pacific Time. The other rows are our conversion, not an official schedule.

| Time zone | Parade start | Parade end |
|---|---|---|
| Pacific Standard Time (PST), Pasadena | 8 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Alaska Standard Time (AKST) | 7 a.m. | 9 a.m. |
| Hawaii Standard Time (HST), UTC-10 | 6 a.m. | 8 a.m. |
| Mountain Standard Time (MST) | 9 a.m. | 11 a.m. |
| Central Standard Time (CST) | 10 a.m. | 12 p.m. |
| Eastern Standard Time (EST) | 11 a.m. | 1 p.m. |
| Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) | 16:00 | 18:00 |
| London, Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | 4 p.m. | 6 p.m. |

The 10:00 a.m. end is the Tournament of Roses listing for the event. See also [when New Year's Day falls](/when-is/new-years-day/) on the calendar.

## What is the Rose Parade route?

The Tournament of Roses describes a 5 1/2-mile route. It begins at the corner of Green Street and Orange Grove Boulevard in Pasadena and runs north on Orange Grove at 2 1/2 miles per hour. It then turns east onto Colorado Boulevard, "where the majority of the parade viewing takes place." Near the end, the parade turns north onto Sierra Madre Boulevard and concludes at Villa Street.

Overnight parking on Pasadena streets begins at noon the day before the parade, which is Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026. The Tournament of Roses points to reserved parking through Sharp Seating Company and the City of Pasadena; use the official page for contact details.

## What are Bandfest and Floatfest?

Bandfest is at 1570 E. Colorado Blvd. in Pasadena, and the Tournament of Roses says the marching bands chosen for the Rose Parade also perform there. It is ticketed.

Floatfest, the Tournament of Roses float viewing event, is at East Sierra Madre Boulevard and East Washington Boulevard in Pasadena. It is also ticketed. The Saturday 7:00 to 9:00 a.m. block is reserved for seniors and disabled visitors.

## What time is the Rose Bowl Game in 2027?

The Rose Bowl Game presented by Prudential is a College Football Playoff (CFP) quarterfinal on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, according to a Feb. 3, 2026 release on rosebowlgame.com.
