## Why Italy is five hours ahead this week

Florence is usually six hours ahead of New York. For one week each fall the gap is five. Europe set its clocks back on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, moving Italy to Central European Time (CET), while the US keeps daylight saving time until Sunday, Nov. 1. Our story on [the European clock change](/news/uk-europe-clocks-go-back-october-25-2026-time-difference/) has the full table, and [daylight saving time ends in the US on Nov. 1](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/).

That matters for anyone who set a reminder from an Italian listing: 8:45 p.m. in Florence is 3:45 p.m. ET this Thursday, but the same Italian kickoff time after Nov. 1 is 2:45 p.m. ET.
