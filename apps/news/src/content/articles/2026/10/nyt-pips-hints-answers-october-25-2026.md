## How Pips works

Pips is a domino logic puzzle the Times launched on Aug. 18, 2025, its first original logic game. You place every domino on the board so that each colored region meets its condition, printed at the region's corner:

- **A number:** the pips in the region add up to it.
- **= (equal):** every half-domino in the region shows the same value.
- **≠ (not equal):** every half shows a different value.
- **> or <:** the region's total is more or less than the number.
- **No condition:** any value fits.

There are three boards a day, Easy, Medium and Hard. When every domino is placed and every condition holds, the game shows a congratulations page.

## Solving tips

Start with the most restrictive region. A one-cell region that must add up to 0 can only take a blank half, and a two-cell region that adds up to 12 needs the 6-6 double. Regions marked = narrow fast once one value is known. On the Hard board, count how often each number appears across the dominoes; a value with only one or two copies left decides where its domino can go.

## How to read the solutions

Rows are numbered from the top and columns from the left, so R1C1 is the top-left cell and R2C5 is the second row, fifth column. Each line gives a domino and the value on each of its two cells.
