## Which Maine races use ranked-choice voting on Nov. 3?

Public Law 2025, chapter 539 (LD 1083) amends 21-A M.R.S. §1 to apply ranked-choice voting (RCV) to "general and special elections for the offices of United States Senator and United States Representative to Congress." It applies only when "3 or more candidates have qualified" or at least two such candidates plus one or more declared write-in candidates have qualified.

| Race | Ranked-choice on Nov. 3? | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. Senate | Yes | Six write-ins filed, per Spectrum News (Aug. 26, 2026) |
| U.S. House | Yes, when the candidate rule above is met | Public Law 2025, chapter 539 |
| Governor (general election) | No, decided by plurality | Public Law 2025, chapter 539 lists only Senate and House for general elections |

The same law also applies ranked-choice voting to primaries for U.S. Senator, U.S. Representative, Governor, State Senator and State Representative. That covers primaries only; the governor's race in November is decided by plurality. Governor and U.S. House candidates are on your sample ballot; this story does not name them because no authoritative list was available to us.

## Who is in the Maine Senate race?

Spectrum News reported on Aug. 26, 2026 that "six candidates met the filing deadline to run as write-ins in the U.S. Senate race," which triggers ranked-choice voting. In the same coverage, the candidates on the ballot are Republican Susan Collins and Democrat Troy Jackson. Secretary of State Shenna Bellows said voters on Nov. 3 can rank "officially declared write-ins," according to Spectrum News.

## How does ranked-choice counting work?

You rank candidates in order of preference. If no candidate has a majority of first choices, the last-place candidate is eliminated and those ballots move to their voters' next choice. The count repeats until one candidate has a majority. If a candidate wins a majority of first choices, no further rounds are needed.

A ranked count can take longer than a single tally. For how that affects results night in every state, read [when will we know the 2026 midterm results](/news/when-will-we-know-election-results-2026-midterms/).
