**The 2027 NASCAR Cup Series runs from Saturday, Feb. 13, to Sunday, Nov. 14, with the 69th Daytona 500 on Sunday, Feb. 21 on FOX, according to FOX Sports' 2027 schedule tracker.** The full schedule came out Wednesday, Aug. 26, 2026, and holds 36 points races plus three non-points events, as reported by Racing News and Yahoo Sports. NASCAR is the National Association for Stock Car Auto Racing. Its own site blocked automated access when this story was checked, so every 2027 detail here comes from those three outlets. Race start times have not been announced.

The table below puts each date with its weekday (computed from the calendar), the event, the track and the TV window: FOX, Prime Video, Turner Network Television (TNT) Sports, then USA Network and NBC. ## When does the 2027 NASCAR season start?

The season opens with the Clash on Saturday, Feb. 13, a non-points event at Daytona. The Duel at Daytona, non-points qualifying races, follows on Thursday, Feb. 18. The Daytona 500 is Sunday, Feb. 21, six days later than the 2026 race, which was Sunday, Feb. 15, 2026, according to NASCAR's 2026 schedule. Atlanta and Circuit of the Americas in Austin follow on Feb. 28 and Mar. 7. For the month view of that stretch, see the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html).

## What changed from the 2026 schedule?

The 2026 dates come from NASCAR's official 2026 schedule and the 2027 dates from its 2027 schedule, which match FOX Sports' tracker.

| Track or event | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Clash | Feb. 4 at Bowman Gray Stadium | Feb. 13 at Daytona |
| Daytona 500 | Sun., Feb. 15 | Sun., Feb. 21 |
| Watkins Glen | May 10 | Sep. 19 |
| All-Star Race | May 17 at Dover | May 23 at North Wilkesboro |
| Naval Base Coronado | Jun. 21 | Aug. 1 |
| Iowa | Aug. 9 | Jul. 4 |
| Richmond | Aug. 15 | Sep. 11 |
| Bristol night race | Sep. 19 | Aug. 14 |
| New Hampshire | Aug. 23 | Sep. 26 |
| World Wide Technology Raceway | Sep. 13 | Aug. 22 |

Two track changes stand out. Dover, which hosted the 2026 All-Star Race, has a points race on May 16, 2027, per FOX Sports. North Wilkesboro had a points race on Jul. 19, 2026, and in 2027 it hosts only the All-Star Race, a non-points event.

## When is the 2027 season finale?

Homestead-Miami closes the year on Sunday, Nov. 14, 2027, according to FOX Sports. Before it come Martinsville on Nov. 7, Talladega on Oct. 31 and Phoenix on Oct. 24. This story does not describe the playoff format for 2027, because the sources used here do not confirm it.
