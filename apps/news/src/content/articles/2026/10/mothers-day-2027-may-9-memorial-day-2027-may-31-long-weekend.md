Mother's Day 2027 is Sunday, May 9, and Memorial Day 2027 is Monday, May 31, based on 36 U.S.C. 117(a) ("The second Sunday in May is Mother's Day") and 5 U.S.C. 6103(a) ("Memorial Day, the last Monday in May"). The U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists "Monday, May 31, Memorial Day" for 2027. Below are the whole month in one table, the long weekend and the weekdays through 2031.

If you only need the Memorial Day date, the [when is Memorial Day page](/when-is/memorial-day/) has it. This story puts both May holidays side by side and adds the other dated events of the month. A free printable is at the end.

## What is the Memorial Day 2027 long weekend?

Memorial Day falls on Monday, May 31, so the long weekend is three days: Saturday, May 29, Sunday, May 30 and Monday, May 31, 2027. Mother's Day comes 22 days earlier, on Sunday, May 9.

Several institutions close on the holiday itself:

- Federal offices: OPM lists Memorial Day on May 31, 2027.
- The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) lists Monday, May 31, 2027 as a full market closure.
- The Federal Reserve lists Memorial Day on May 31, 2027 in its K.8 holiday schedule.

## Mother's Day and Memorial Day dates, 2027 to 2031

Both dates follow a weekday rule, so the day of the month moves. These dates are computed from the two federal rules above.

| Year | Mother's Day (second Sunday) | Memorial Day (last Monday) |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Sunday, May 9 | Monday, May 31 |
| 2028 | Sunday, May 14 | Monday, May 29 |
| 2029 | Sunday, May 13 | Monday, May 28 |
| 2030 | Sunday, May 12 | Monday, May 27 |
| 2031 | Sunday, May 11 | Monday, May 26 |

## Is Memorial Day also a UK holiday in 2027?

GOV.UK lists the late May bank holiday as Monday, May 31, 2027, the same date as the U.S. holiday. They are separate holidays that share a date.
