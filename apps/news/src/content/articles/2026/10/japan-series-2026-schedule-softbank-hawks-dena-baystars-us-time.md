**Game 4 is today, Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, at 6 p.m. Japan Standard Time (JST), which was 5 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), at Mizuho PayPay Dome Fukuoka, according to Nippon Professional Baseball (NPB).** The Fukuoka SoftBank Hawks lead the Yokohama DeNA BayStars 3-0 in the best-of-seven Japan Series, so one more win would end it. Below: every remaining game in U.S. time zones, including the clock change that moves Game 7.

## What is the Japan Series 2026 schedule?

NPB lists the games below on its page for the series, which Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC) sponsors. Games 5 to 7 are played only if needed, and the series ends when a team wins four. There are no games on Oct. 26 or Oct. 30. NPB does not list start times for Games 1 to 3 on the page we used.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## What time are the Japan Series games in U.S. time zones?

JST is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus 9 hours (UTC+9). The columns below are our conversion, not an official schedule. Games 4 to 6 fall while U.S. daylight time is still in effect, so the zones are Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), Central Daylight Time (CDT), Mountain Daylight Time (MDT) and Pacific Daylight Time (PDT). The United Kingdom has been on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) since Oct. 25.

| Game | Japan (JST) | Eastern | Central | Mountain | Pacific | UK (GMT) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4, Wed., Oct. 28 | 6 p.m. | 5 a.m. EDT | 4 a.m. CDT | 3 a.m. MDT | 2 a.m. PDT | 9 a.m. |
| 5, Thu., Oct. 29 | 6 p.m. | 5 a.m. EDT | 4 a.m. CDT | 3 a.m. MDT | 2 a.m. PDT | 9 a.m. |
| 6, Sat., Oct. 31 | 6:30 p.m. | 5:30 a.m. EDT | 4:30 a.m. CDT | 3:30 a.m. MDT | 2:30 a.m. PDT | 9:30 a.m. |
| 7, Sun., Nov. 1 | 6 p.m. | 4 a.m. EST | 3 a.m. CST | 2 a.m. MST | 1 a.m. PST | 9 a.m. |

Game 7 uses Eastern Standard Time (EST), Central Standard Time (CST), Mountain Standard Time (MST) and Pacific Standard Time (PST). Arizona does not change clocks and is on MST all year, which our conversion puts at 2 a.m. for Games 4 and 5, 2:30 a.m. for Game 6 and 2 a.m. for Game 7.

## Why is Game 7 different in U.S. time?

U.S. clocks fall back at 2 a.m. local time on Sunday, Nov. 1. Game 7 starts at 6 p.m. JST that day, which is 09:00 UTC. By then Eastern time has already gone back, so the game is at 4 a.m. EST. Pacific time falls back at that same moment, when 2 a.m. PDT becomes 1 a.m. PST, so the game is at 1 a.m. PST. Game 7 is the only game on the schedule shown in standard time.

## Who is playing, and where does the series stand?

The Pacific League's SoftBank Hawks are in their 23rd Japan Series, NPB says. The Central League's DeNA BayStars are in their fifth. The Fukuoka SoftBank Hawks said on Oct. 16, 2026, that it had won the Pacific League Climax Series.

NPB's results show SoftBank winning all three games so far:

- Game 1: SoftBank 3, DeNA 1. Osuna got the win and Iwata took the loss.
- Game 2: SoftBank 4, DeNA 3. Hernandez got the win and Ise took the loss.
- Game 3: SoftBank 2, DeNA 1, on a walk-off home run by Masaki, in NPB's headline.

In 2025, SoftBank won the Japan Series 4-1 over the Hanshin Tigers, clinching Game 5 on Oct. 30, 2025, 3-2 at Hanshin Koshien Stadium. NPB lists it as SoftBank's 12th title and the first in five years, so the Hawks are going for back-to-back titles.
