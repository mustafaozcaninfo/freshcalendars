**Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, is the Early Decision (ED) and Early Action (EA) deadline at 10 of the 12 schools below, according to each admissions office.** Duke is the exception at Monday, Nov. 2, and the University of California (UC) takes applications for the whole month. The deadline night is also when U.S. clocks fall back one hour at 2 a.m.

All dates come from each college's own admissions page, checked Oct. 30, 2026.

## Which colleges have a Nov. 1 early deadline?

| College | Early plan | Early deadline | Early decision release | Regular deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harvard | Restrictive Early Action (REA) | Sun., Nov. 1 (11:59 p.m. local time) | By mid-December | Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 |
| Yale | Single-Choice Early Action | Sun., Nov. 1 | Mid-December | Sat., Jan. 2, 2027 |
| Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) | Early Action | Sun., Nov. 1 | Mid-December | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| University of Pennsylvania | Early Decision | Sun., Nov. 1 | December | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Brown | Early Decision | Sun., Nov. 1 (11:59 p.m. local time) | Mid-December | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (11:59 p.m. local time) |
| Cornell | Early Decision | Sun., Nov. 1 | Mid-December | Sat., Jan. 2, 2027 |
| Johns Hopkins | Early Decision I | Sun., Nov. 1 | Dec. 11, 2026 | Sat., Jan. 2, 2027 |
| Rice | Early Decision I | Sun., Nov. 1 | By mid-December | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Vanderbilt | Early Decision I | Sun., Nov. 1 | Mid-December | Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 |
| Georgetown | Early Action | Sun., Nov. 1 | By Dec. 15 | Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 |
| Duke | Early Decision | Mon., Nov. 2 | Mid-December | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| UC | Single application | Nov. 1 to 30 (filing period) | March | Not applicable |

Regular Decision (RD) is Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)'s "Regular Action" and Harvard's regular round. Weekdays are computed from the calendar. Where the table gives no time, the page we checked did not state one.

## Why is Duke's deadline Nov. 2?

Duke's Early Decision deadline is Monday, Nov. 2, not Nov. 1, on its checklist page, which says the dates "represent the 2026-2027 admission cycle deadlines." Duke's Regular Decision deadline is Monday, Jan. 4, 2027. A student who plans around "Nov. 1 everywhere" gets one extra day at Duke, so check the date before relying on it.

## Which later rounds and release dates matter?

- **Johns Hopkins:** Early Decision II and Regular Decision are due Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027. Early Decision II decisions come Feb. 12, 2027 and Regular Decision decisions March 24, 2027.
- **Rice:** Early Decision II and Regular Decision are due Monday, Jan. 4, 2027; Regular Decision decisions come by April 1.
- **Vanderbilt:** Early Decision II and Regular Decision are due Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, with Early Decision II decisions in mid-February 2027.
- **Penn:** Regular Decision decisions come in April; **Cornell:** late March.
- **Georgetown:** Regular Decision decisions come by April 1.

## Does Georgetown have Early Decision?

No. Georgetown offers Early Action only. Its site says, "Early Action applicants to Georgetown may not apply to any binding Early Decision programs." Applying to Georgetown early therefore rules out a binding Early Decision application elsewhere.

## What is the UC application deadline for fall 2027?

UC Admissions lists "November 1 - 30: Fall 2027 admission application filing period" on its 2026-27 dates page. The application opened Aug. 1, decisions come in March, and the Statement of Intent to Register (SIR) is due May 1. Nov. 30 is a Monday.

## Do the clocks change on deadline night?

Yes. Daylight Saving Time (DST) ends at 2 a.m. on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, and clocks fall back one hour, so an 11:59 p.m. deadline that night is in standard time. For a student whose deadline is in Eastern Time (ET), nothing changes. Check the clock on your device and the time the portal shows. See [when daylight saving time ends in 2026](/news/daylight-saving-time-ends-november-1-2026/).

## What could not be confirmed?

We could not check the admissions sites of Princeton, Columbia, Northwestern, Michigan, New York University (NYU), University of Virginia (UVA), Dartmouth, UChicago and Stanford, or the California State University dates, so no dates for them appear here. Confirm those on each college's site.
