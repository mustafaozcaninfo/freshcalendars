**St. Patrick's Day 2027 falls on Wednesday, March 17, and the New York City parade is set for 11:00 a.m. that day on Fifth Avenue in Manhattan, according to the parade's official website.** Dublin's St. Patrick's Festival runs from Saturday, March 13 to Wednesday, March 17, 2027, per the festival. Chicago, Savannah and Boston have not posted 2027 dates, so this page marks them as pending and gives last year's dates only where a source has them.

|---|---|---|---|
| New York | Wednesday, March 17, 2027, along Fifth Avenue in Manhattan | 11:00 a.m. | NYC St. Patrick's Day Parade |
| Dublin | Festival programme from Saturday, March 13 to Wednesday, March 17, 2027 | Full schedule due January 2027 | St. Patrick's Festival |
| Chicago | Not yet announced. Last year: parade on Saturday, March 14, 2026 | Not yet announced | ABC7 Chicago (2026) |

The Chicago row comes from one newsroom: ABC7 Chicago reported the 2026 parade date, and the city's official site could not be reached when this was written. Treat the 2026 date as last year's pattern, not a 2027 schedule.

## What day of the week is March 17 from 2027 to 2031?

March 17 never moves, but its weekday does. Whether a city marches on the day itself or on a nearby weekend changes your plans, so check the weekday first.

| Year | March 17 falls on |
|---|---|
| 2027 | Wednesday |
| 2028 | Friday |
| 2029 | Saturday |
| 2030 | Sunday |
| 2031 | Monday |

Weekdays computed from the calendar. In 2026 March 17 was a Tuesday, and Chicago's parade was held on the Saturday before it, March 14, ABC7 Chicago reported. In 2027 the day is a Wednesday, so a weekend parade, if a city chooses one, would be a different date from the 17th. Our [March printable calendars](/march-calendars.html) show the week at a glance.

## New York parade date and time

The parade's official site states that it "will take place along Fifth Avenue in Manhattan on Wednesday, March 17, 2027 at 11:00 AM." That is the only 2027 parade with a confirmed date and time in our sources. For another New York holiday date list, see our [NYC holiday calendar](/news/nyc-holiday-calendar-2026-rockefeller-tree-macys-parade/).

## Dublin's St. Patrick's Festival in 2027

The festival's website says its 2027 programme "runs from Saturday 13th March to Wednesday 17th March" and that the full events schedule "will be available in January 2027." Until then, only the five-day span is confirmed. Northern Ireland also observes March 17 as a bank holiday; the Wednesday date is in our [2027 UK bank holidays table](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/).
