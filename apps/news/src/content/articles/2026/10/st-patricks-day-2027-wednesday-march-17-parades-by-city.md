**St. Patrick's Day 2027 falls on Wednesday, March 17, and the New York City parade is set for 11:00 a.m. that day on Fifth Avenue in Manhattan, according to the parade's official website.** Dublin's St. Patrick's Festival runs from Saturday, March 13 to Wednesday, March 17, 2027, per the festival. Chicago's parade is set for Saturday, March 13, and South Boston's for Sunday, March 14, according to their organizers. Savannah has not posted 2027 details, so this page marks it as pending and gives last year's date, labeled as last year.

## St. Patrick's Day 2027 parade dates by city

| City | 2027 status | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York | Wednesday, March 17, 2027, along Fifth Avenue in Manhattan; Grand Marshal John Samuelsen | 11:00 a.m. | NYC St. Patrick's Day Parade |
| Dublin | Festival programme from Saturday, March 13 to Wednesday, March 17, 2027 | Full schedule due January 2027 | St. Patrick's Festival |
| Chicago | Parade and river dyeing on Saturday, March 13, 2027 | Not yet announced | Chicago St. Patrick's Day Parade |
| Savannah | 2027 not yet posted. Last year: Tuesday, March 17, 2026 | 10:15 a.m. in 2026 | City of Savannah (2026) |
| Boston | South Boston parade on Sunday, March 14, 2027 | Not yet announced | Allied War Veterans Council of South Boston |

The Chicago and Boston dates come from the organizers' own websites. Neither lists a start time yet. Last year Chicago's parade was held on Saturday, March 14, 2026, ABC7 Chicago reported.

## What day of the week is March 17 from 2027 to 2031?

March 17 never moves, but its weekday does. Whether a city marches on the day itself or on a nearby weekend changes your plans, so check the weekday first.

| Year | March 17 falls on |
|---|---|
| 2027 | Wednesday |
| 2028 | Friday |
| 2029 | Saturday |
| 2030 | Sunday |
| 2031 | Monday |

Weekdays computed from the calendar. In 2026 March 17 was a Tuesday, and Chicago's parade was held on the Saturday before it, March 14, ABC7 Chicago reported. In 2027 the day is a Wednesday, and both Chicago (March 13) and South Boston (March 14) are marching on the weekend before it. ## New York parade date and time

The parade's official site states that it "will take place along Fifth Avenue in Manhattan on Wednesday, March 17, 2027 at 11:00 AM." It is the only 2027 parade with both a date and a start time confirmed in our sources. The site names John Samuelsen, International President of the Transport Workers Union of America (TWU), as the 2027 Grand Marshal. For another New York holiday date list, see our [NYC holiday calendar](/news/nyc-holiday-calendar-2026-rockefeller-tree-macys-parade/).

## Dublin's St. Patrick's Festival in 2027

The festival's website says its 2027 programme "runs from Saturday 13th March to Wednesday 17th March" and that the full events schedule "will be available in January 2027." Until then, only the five-day span is confirmed. Northern Ireland also observes March 17 as a bank holiday; the Wednesday date is in our [2027 UK bank holidays table](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/). If the holiday week also touches Easter planning, see [Easter 2027](/news/easter-2027-march-28-good-friday-easter-monday-whats-open/).

## Chicago River dyeing, Boston and Savannah

The Chicago St. Patrick's Day Parade's website shows "Saturday, March 13 2027" as the save-the-date and describes the river dyeing, along Wacker Drive, as the start of parade day. It lists the river-dyeing time and the parade start time as still to be determined, so both are not yet announced. The 2027 Grand Marshal is Rev. L. Scott Donahue, president and chief executive officer of Mercy Home for Boys & Girls, per the same site.

The South Boston Allied War Veterans Council says its parade "returns to take place on Sunday March 14th 2027" and has not posted a start time. Savannah's city page still shows only the 2026 parade, Tuesday, March 17, 2026, starting at 10:15 a.m. The Savannah parade committee's own site says it marks the Feast of Saint Patrick on March 17 but does not state a 2027 schedule, so Savannah stays pending until it does.
