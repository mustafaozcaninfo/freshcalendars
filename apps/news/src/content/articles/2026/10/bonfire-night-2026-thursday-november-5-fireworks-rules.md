**Bonfire Night is tonight, Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, and fireworks may be set off until midnight**, according to GOV.UK's "Fireworks: the law" guidance. On other nights, the page says, you must not set off fireworks between 11 p.m. and 7 a.m. The autumn buying period has five days left: it ends on Tuesday, Nov. 10.

This story sets out the cut-off times, the buying period and the history behind Guy Fawkes Night, with a one-page timeline you can print.

## What time do fireworks have to stop on Bonfire Night?

The normal rule is simple. No fireworks between 11 p.m. and 7 a.m., GOV.UK says, except on four nights of the year when the limit is pushed later: Bonfire Night until midnight, and New Year's Eve, Diwali and Chinese New Year until 1 a.m.

| Night | Fireworks cut-off (GOV.UK) |
|---|---|
| Most nights | 11 p.m. |
| Bonfire Night, Thu., Nov. 5, 2026 | Midnight |
| Diwali, Sun., Nov. 8, 2026 | 1 a.m. |
| New Year's Eve, Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | 1 a.m. |

Diwali falls on Sunday, Nov. 8 this year, according to our [Diwali 2026 story](/news/diwali-2026-date-lakshmi-puja-time-usa/). That puts a 1 a.m. cut-off three days after Bonfire Night.

## When can you buy fireworks?

Only at set times. GOV.UK says private buyers can get fireworks, sparklers included, from registered sellers during fixed periods, and the autumn one runs from 15 October to 10 November. That window closes on Tuesday, Nov. 10, five days after Bonfire Night itself.

GOV.UK also says you cannot buy "adult" fireworks if you are under 18.

## What is the fine for breaking the fireworks rules?

It can be heavy. GOV.UK says anyone who breaks the rules can be fined an unlimited amount and imprisoned for up to 6 months, and can also get an on-the-spot fine of £90.

The rules for tonight are the same as last year. The Department for Business and Trade and the Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS) ran a consultation, "Fireworks and pyrotechnics in the UK," from July 16 to Oct. 7, 2026. It proposed keeping louder fireworks for professional users only and adding more products to the banned list. The consultation page says the proposals are not yet law.

## Why is Bonfire Night on Nov. 5?

Royal Museums Greenwich says the Gunpowder Plot itself was foiled in the early hours of 5 November 1605. It describes how, in the early hours of Tuesday 5 November, Fawkes was found dressed in a cloak and hat. A later Act provided for "a Public Thanksgiving to Almighty God every Year on the Fifth Day of November."

## Is Bonfire Night a bank holiday?

No. GOV.UK's bank holidays page does not list Nov. 5 for England and Wales, so it is a normal working Thursday. The next bank holidays there are Christmas Day, Dec. 25, and the substitute Boxing Day on Monday, Dec. 28, 2026.

## When do displays take place?

Often on the weekend. With Nov. 5 on a Thursday this year, many organized displays move to the Friday or Saturday nearest to it. Times vary by town, so check with the council or club running yours. ## How does the UK clock change fit in?

UK clocks went back one hour on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, 11 days before Bonfire Night.
