**U2's general sale for the 2027 UK and Europe dates of The Greatest Show On Earth World Tour was Friday, Nov. 6, 2026 at 10 a.m. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), 5 a.m. Eastern Time (ET)**, according to U2.com. The band announced the dates on Oct. 29, 2026 under the name "Carnaval De Luz presents: The Greatest Show On Earth World Tour." A subscriber presale began four days earlier, on Monday, Nov. 2. U2.com's tour page has since added nights and Spain, bringing the UK and Europe leg to 26 shows.

The catch was in the Artist Presale. U2.com said you had to sign up by Sunday, Nov. 1 at 10:59 p.m. GMT, which was before any sale began, and the Lisbon date was not part of that presale. US clocks went back on Nov. 1, so GMT was 5 hours ahead of ET for the whole sale week. A one-page timeline is in the downloads.

## What time did U2 tickets go on sale?

| Step | Date | Time (U2.com) | ET | Central European Time (CET) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Artist Presale sign-up closed | Sun., Nov. 1, 2026 | 10:59 p.m. GMT | 5:59 p.m. | 11:59 p.m. |
| Subscriber presale (Feedback Group) | Mon., Nov. 2, 2026 | 10 a.m. GMT | 5 a.m. | 11 a.m. |
| Subscriber presale (Hype Group) | Tue., Nov. 3, 2026 | Day two of the presale | Not stated | Not stated |
| Visa cardholder presale | Wed., Nov. 4, 2026 | 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. local time | 5 a.m. for the UK start (10 a.m. GMT) | 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. in CET countries |
| Artist Presale | Thu., Nov. 5, 2026 | 10 a.m. local time | 5 a.m. for the UK start (10 a.m. GMT) | 10 a.m. in CET countries |
| General sale at U2.com | Fri., Nov. 6, 2026 | 10 a.m. GMT | 5 a.m. | 11 a.m. |

The GMT, ET and CET conversions are our calculation. U2.com gave the Visa and Artist Presale times as local time: 10 a.m. in the United Kingdom (UK) is 10 a.m. GMT, and 10 a.m. in Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin, Milan or Stockholm is 10 a.m. CET. Lisbon is on Western European Time (WET), which matches GMT, so 10 a.m. there is also 10 a.m. GMT. For US readers, 10 a.m. GMT is 5 a.m. ET and 10 a.m. CET is 4 a.m. ET.

## Who can use the U2 presales?

- **Subscriber presale.** It began Monday, Nov. 2 at 10 a.m. GMT for U2.com subscribers. The Feedback Group entered on the Monday and The Hype Group on Tuesday. Subscribers are emailed a unique presale code in advance, and new subscribers join The Hype Group with access on day two, U2.com says.
- **Visa cardholders.** They had presale access in the European Union (EU) and UK on Wednesday, Nov. 4 from 10 a.m. local time until 10 p.m., while supply lasted.
- **Artist Presale.** It ran Thursday, Nov. 5 at 10 a.m. local time for anyone who signed up by Sunday, Nov. 1 at 10:59 p.m. GMT (11:59 p.m. CET). On Ticketmaster no code is needed because access is tied to your account; sales hosted on other sites may need a code. Some fans may be asked for a selfie check or, in rare cases, an ID via Persona.

## Is this the first U2 UK stadium show in years?

U2.com's tour archive lists Twickenham Stadium in London on 8 July 2017 as the band's last UK stadium date on the Joshua Tree Tour 2017. We have not checked for other UK shows since, so we make no claim beyond the archive.

## What are the ticket limits and prices?

Ticketmaster's article of Oct. 29, 2026 says there is a limit of 4 tickets per show. U2.com's announcement, as we read it, does not state a limit. No ticket prices have been announced in the sources we read, so this story does not quote any.

## What is still to be announced?

U2.com said on Oct. 29 that 2027 dates for North America are set to be announced in the coming weeks; its tour page lists none yet. The Edge said the band will be "wrapping it up in Ireland," with plans for the tour already in the works for 2028; no Irish date is listed. A Latin America leg for 2027 with support from Beck also exists, and its Mexico City shows have sold out, according to U2.com.
