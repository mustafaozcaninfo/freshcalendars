**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Tuesday, Oct. 27, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Joel Fagliano.** Two of them are wordplay, and two black squares in the bottom right corner shorten the last answers. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (5 letters), *Say "C-H-E-E-S-E"?*: the question mark means wordplay. You are saying the word one letter at a time.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Number of stripes on many world flags*: count the bands on the flags of France, Italy or Germany.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Places visited on a Clue board*: the Kitchen, the Ballroom and the Library are three of them.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *"Go back" command*: the opposite of redo, often Ctrl+Z on a keyboard.
- **9-Across** (4 letters), *Follower of six or seven, but not five*: add it to six or seven to make a number word; five takes a different spelling.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Walk with swagger*: what a model does down a runway.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *What gets texts (and also scrolls?)*: the device in your pocket.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Wear away slowly*: what water does to rock over centuries.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *Kind of juice in a French 75 cocktail*: a yellow citrus fruit.
- **5-Down** (3 letters), *"___ Misérables"*: the French word for "the" before a plural noun, from Victor Hugo's novel.

## Solving tips

Start with the gimmes. 6-Across is a number, 4-Down is a fruit and 5-Down comes from a famous title. Those three cross the top half of the grid. Then try 1-Down, which shares its first letter with 1-Across. Leave the two wordplay clues, 1-Across and 9-Across, until a few letters are in place. The bottom row ends at the fourth column.
