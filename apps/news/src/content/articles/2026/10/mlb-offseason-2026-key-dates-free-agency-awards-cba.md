**Major League Baseball (MLB) free agency opens Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026, the day after the World Series ended, according to MLB.com's offseason rules guide.** MLB.com's awards guide (Oct. 26, 2026) then lists the Gold Glove Awards on Sunday, Nov. 1 through the Silver Slugger Awards on Tuesday, Nov. 17. The World Series ended on Wednesday, Oct. 28, when Milwaukee beat Tampa Bay 4-1 in Game 5, so free agency opens Thursday, Oct. 29, with signings with new clubs allowed from 5 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Monday, Nov. 2 by our count. The details are in our [2026 World Series schedule](/news/world-series-2026-schedule-game-times-tv-channel/).

## When does free agency start, by the day the World Series ends?

MLB.com's guide says: "Officially, players become free agents the day after the conclusion of the World Series." Teams must make qualifying offers "within five days of the conclusion of the World Series," and players "cannot sign with a new team until 5 p.m. ET on the fifth day after the conclusion of the World Series." That guide is from Oct. 23, 2025. We applied its rules to this year's possible final games and counted the weekdays ourselves.

| If the Series ends in | Final game | Players become free agents | Qualifying offer deadline (five days after) | Free agents can sign with new clubs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Game 4 | Tue., Oct. 27 | Wed., Oct. 28 | Sun., Nov. 1 | Sun., Nov. 1, 5 p.m. ET |
| **Game 5 (actual)** | **Wed., Oct. 28** | **Thu., Oct. 29** | **Mon., Nov. 2** | **Mon., Nov. 2, 5 p.m. ET** |
| Game 6 | Fri., Oct. 30 | Sat., Oct. 31 | Wed., Nov. 4 | Wed., Nov. 4, 5 p.m. ET |
| Game 7 | Sat., Oct. 31 | Sun., Nov. 1 | Thu., Nov. 5 | Thu., Nov. 5, 5 p.m. ET |

The game dates come from the MLB Stats application programming interface (API) schedule. The Series ended in Game 5, so that row is the one that applies. The qualifying offer column is our count of the five-day window; MLB has not yet announced the 2026 deadline.

## What is the 2026 qualifying offer?

The 2026-27 qualifying offer is $23.15 million, The Sports Network (TSN) reported on Oct. 19, 2026, up 5.1% from $22.025 million last offseason; Front Office Sports reported the same figure on Oct. 21. MLB.com (Aug. 8, 2026) describes the offer as "set at the average salary of MLB's top 125 highest-paid players." A player who receives the offer has a set window to accept the one-year deal; in 2025 that deadline was 4 p.m. ET on Nov. 18, and the 2026 accept date is not yet announced.

## When are the 2026 MLB awards announced?

Every date below is from MLB.com's awards guide of Oct. 26, 2026. The Baseball Writers' Association of America (BBWAA) votes on four of them.

| Date | Award |
|---|---|
| Sun., Nov. 1 | Rawlings Gold Glove Awards, 8:30 p.m. ET on ESPN ([all 60 finalists](/news/gold-glove-awards-2026-tonight-time-espn-finalists-platinum-glove-vote/)) |
| Fri., Nov. 6 | Platinum Glove Awards; Heart & Hustle Awards |
| Mon., Nov. 9 | BBWAA Rookie of the Year |
| Tue., Nov. 10 | BBWAA Manager of the Year |
| Wed., Nov. 11 | BBWAA Cy Young; BBWAA Relief Pitcher of the Year; All-MLB Teams; Hank Aaron Awards; Comeback Player of the Year; Rivera/Hoffman Reliever of the Year; Edgar Martinez Outstanding Designated Hitter |
| Thu., Nov. 12 | BBWAA M.V.P. Awards |
| Tue., Nov. 17 | Silver Slugger Awards |

This is the first season the BBWAA gives a Relief Pitcher of the Year award. Several of the Nov. 11 honors are announced in a show that begins at 9 p.m. ET. The Executive of the Year award is announced during the General Manager (GM) Meetings in Las Vegas. Show times for the other days are not yet announced.

## What happens when the CBA expires on Dec. 1?

Just Baseball (Oct. 13, 2026) reports the Collective Bargaining Agreement "will expire on December 1, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET." The Associated Press reported the same expiration when the deal was signed in 2022. MLB and the MLB Players Association have not published a 2026 calendar of these dates, so the Winter Meetings and non-tender dates above should be rechecked. We do not speculate beyond that: the current agreement expires on that date.

## What has MLB not announced yet?

The 2026 qualifying offer accept deadline, the Rule 5 Draft date, the date the BBWAA names its finalists, award show times beyond those above and the 2027 salary arbitration exchange date are all not yet announced in the sources we used. We will update this story when they appear.
