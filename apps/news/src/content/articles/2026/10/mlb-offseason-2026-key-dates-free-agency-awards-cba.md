**Major League Baseball (MLB) free agency opened Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026, the day after the World Series ended, according to MLB.com's offseason rules guide.** MLB.com's awards guide then listed the Gold Glove Awards on Sunday, Nov. 1 through the Silver Slugger Awards on Tuesday, Nov. 17. The World Series ended on Wednesday, Oct. 28, when Milwaukee beat Tampa Bay 4-1 in Game 5, so free agency opened Thursday, Oct. 29, with signings with new clubs allowed from 5 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Monday, Nov. 2 by our count. The awards ran from Nov. 1; the Silver Slugger Awards on Nov. 17 are the last. The details are in our [2026 World Series schedule](/news/world-series-2026-schedule-game-times-tv-channel/).

## When does free agency start, by the day the World Series ends?

MLB.com's guide says: "Officially, players become free agents the day after the conclusion of the World Series." Teams must make qualifying offers "within five days of the conclusion of the World Series," and players "cannot sign with a new team until 5 p.m. ET on the fifth day after the conclusion of the World Series." That guide is from Oct. 23, 2025. We applied its rules to this year's possible final games and counted the weekdays ourselves.

| If the Series ends in | Final game | Players become free agents | Qualifying offer deadline (five days after) | Free agents can sign with new clubs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Game 4 | Tue., Oct. 27 | Wed., Oct. 28 | Sun., Nov. 1 | Sun., Nov. 1, 5 p.m. ET |
| **Game 5 (actual)** | **Wed., Oct. 28** | **Thu., Oct. 29** | **Mon., Nov. 2** | **Mon., Nov. 2, 5 p.m. ET** |
| Game 6 | Fri., Oct. 30 | Sat., Oct. 31 | Wed., Nov. 4 | Wed., Nov. 4, 5 p.m. ET |
| Game 7 | Sat., Oct. 31 | Sun., Nov. 1 | Thu., Nov. 5 | Thu., Nov. 5, 5 p.m. ET |

The game dates come from the MLB Stats application programming interface (API) schedule. The Series ended in Game 5, so that row is the one that applies. The qualifying offer column is our count of the five-day window; MLB has not yet announced the 2026 deadline.

## What is the 2026 qualifying offer?

The 2026-27 qualifying offer is $23.15 million, The Sports Network (TSN) reported on Oct. 19, 2026, up 5.1% from $22.025 million last offseason; Front Office Sports reported the same figure on Oct. 21. MLB.com (Aug. 8, 2026) describes the offer as "set at the average salary of MLB's top 125 highest-paid players." A player who receives the offer has a set window to accept the one-year deal; MLB.com's offseason guide says players have until 4 p.m. ET on Nov. 17, 2026 to accept; in 2025 the deadline was 4 p.m. ET on Nov. 18.

## What happens when the CBA expires on Dec. 1?

Just Baseball (Oct. 13, 2026) reports the Collective Bargaining Agreement "will expire on December 1, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET." The Associated Press reported the same expiration when the deal was signed in 2022. MLB and the MLB Players Association have not published a 2026 calendar of these dates, so the Winter Meetings dates above should be rechecked. We do not speculate beyond that: the current agreement expires on that date.

## What has MLB not announced yet?

The Silver Slugger Award winners (due Nov. 17), the Winter Meetings confirmation from MLB and the 2027 salary arbitration exchange date are not yet announced in the sources we used.
