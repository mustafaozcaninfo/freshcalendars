**WWE lists Survivor Series: WarGames for Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, at Daikin Park in Houston, streaming live on the ESPN App at 6 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to WWE's event page.** That is 5 p.m. in Houston and 3 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), by our conversion. Below: the start time in every U.S. zone, the UK and Japan, how to watch, and the other WWE events on the calendar before the end of the year.

## What time does Survivor Series 2026 start?

WWE's event page says the show "streams live on the ESPN App on Saturday Nov. 28 at 6 ET/3 PT" and lists the date as "Saturday, Nov 28 | 6/5 PM C." WWE has not announced a Kickoff or pre-show time. Every other zone below is our conversion of WWE's 6 p.m. ET. U.S. clocks are on standard time by Nov. 28, so these are standard-time hours.

| Eastern (ET) | 6 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Central (CT), Houston | 5 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Mountain (MT) | 4 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Pacific (PT) | 3 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Alaska | 2 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Hawaii | 1 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28 |
| United Kingdom, Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | 11 p.m. | Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Japan, Japan Standard Time (JST) | 8 a.m. | Sun., Nov. 29 |

The Japan row is also Netflix's own listing: "Streaming live at 8:00 a.m. JST on Sunday, November 29," from its Sept. 29, 2026 "WWE in Japan" post, which matches 6 p.m. ET.

## Where is Survivor Series 2026?

WWE announced on July 13, 2026, with Houston First Corporation and the Houston Astros, that Survivor Series: WarGames will be held at Daikin Park in Houston. WWE calls it the 40th annual Survivor Series and says it marks the event's return to the city for the first time since 2017.

Tickets went on sale through Ticketmaster.com. WWE's release listed the Club WWE presale on Wednesday, Aug. 5, the general presale on Thursday, Aug. 6, and the public sale on Friday, Aug. 7, each at 11 a.m. ET.

## How can you watch Survivor Series 2026?

In the United States, on the ESPN App. WWE's event page says the show streams live there. A WWE release dated Aug. 6, 2025 said ESPN platforms would become the exclusive U.S. home of all WWE premium live events starting in 2026.

WWE's official pages we checked do not name a UK broadcaster for 2026. Netflix streamed the 2025 event in the UK and Ireland, Advanced Television reported on Nov. 28, 2025; that is last year's arrangement, and 2026 is not confirmed.

## Which matches are on the Survivor Series 2026 card?

As of Oct. 30, no matches are listed on WWE's event page for Survivor Series: WarGames 2026. This story will add the card once WWE posts it.

## How does this compare with Survivor Series 2025?

In 2025, Survivor Series was on Saturday, Nov. 29, at Petco Park in San Diego. WWE called Petco Park the first-ever stadium to host the event.
