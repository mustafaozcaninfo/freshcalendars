**The New York Knicks host Brooklyn on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) at Madison Square Garden, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The NBA lists 14 Knicks games in November, and a one-page printable of all of them is further down.

The source is the NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF from NBA Communications, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." Its times are in ET, so the table needs no conversion except for one game in Minnesota, which also gets a Central Time (CT) time. Weekdays are our own calculation. The Knicks' previous game was at Philadelphia on Friday, Oct. 30.

## Which Knicks games count in the NBA Cup?

The schedule flags four Knicks games as Emirates NBA Cup group play: at Philadelphia on Fri., Oct. 30, at Indiana on Fri., Nov. 13, against Cleveland on Wed., Nov. 25 and against Miami on Fri., Nov. 27. NBA.com puts the Knicks in East Group B. Group play is 60 games from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, per NBA.com's Aug. 12, 2026 release, and the championship is on Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round, including the Dec. 4 and 5 quarterfinals.

## Back-to-backs and road trips in the Knicks' November

Reading the dates in the table shows how the month is built. These are our own counts from the NBA's schedule:

- **Back-to-backs:** Tue., Nov. 10 and Wed., Nov. 11 (both at home), and Sat., Nov. 21 and Sun., Nov. 22 (both at home).
- **Toronto twice:** the Knicks play at Toronto on Sun., Nov. 15 and again on Tue., Nov. 17, two games in three days.
- **Road trip from Nov. 13 to Nov. 19:** at Indiana, at Toronto twice, then at Minnesota, four road games in a row between the Nov. 11 and Nov. 21 home games.
- **Nov. 27:** the Miami game falls on the day after Thanksgiving, which is Thu., Nov. 26, 2026. Our [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has the holiday. The Knicks have no game on Thanksgiving Day itself in the NBA's schedule.
