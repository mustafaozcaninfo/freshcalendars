## A clue for the pangram

- **Pangram** (8 letters, starts with P): a coin-operated telephone once fixed to street corners and station walls; it is the one eight-letter answer.

The quickest handholds are the two four-word openings, AN and NA. Clear those, then chase the seven PA words, which are where most of the points sit.

## What makes Oct. 27 unusual

With no S in the ring, this puzzle leans on repeated letters instead of plurals. Twelve of the 29 answers double a letter back to back, among them Nanny, Poppa, Pappy, Yappy and Henna, and six words end in -y. None end in -ed or -ing.
