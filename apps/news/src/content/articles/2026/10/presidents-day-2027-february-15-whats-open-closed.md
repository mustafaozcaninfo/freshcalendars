**Presidents' Day 2027 falls on Monday, Feb. 15, and the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists it as the federal holiday "Washington's Birthday."** The U.S. Postal Service (USPS) lists the same date among its 2027 holidays, and the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) is closed that day. The table below sorts out who is closed, and the date itself is covered on [When is Presidents' Day?](/when-is/presidents-day/).

## What is closed on Presidents' Day 2027?

| Who | Monday, Feb. 15, 2027 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Federal offices | Holiday, listed as Washington's Birthday | OPM federal holidays |
| USPS | Listed as "Presidents Day" among 2027 holidays | USPS events and holidays |
| Federal Reserve Banks | Closed for Washington's Birthday | Federal Reserve K.8 schedule |
| NYSE | Closed for Washington's Birthday; no early close is listed for February 2027 | NYSE hours and calendars |

The legal name of the holiday is Washington's Birthday, under 5 U.S.C. 6103(a), which is the name OPM, the Federal Reserve and the NYSE use. Presidents' Day is the popular name, and USPS uses it.

## Is the stock market open on Presidents' Day 2027?

No. The NYSE lists Monday, Feb. 15, 2027 as a closed day. Its calendar shows no early close in February 2027.  The full list of exchange closures is in our [2027 stock market holidays story](/news/stock-market-holidays-2027-nyse-closed-dates/).

## Is mail delivered on Presidents' Day 2027?

USPS includes "Monday February 15 Presidents Day" in its list of 2027 holidays, so it is a USPS holiday.

## Why is February 2027 a holiday weekend with Valentine's Day in the middle?

Valentine's Day lands on Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027. That puts it between Saturday, Feb. 13 and Presidents' Day on Monday, so federal workers have a three-day weekend with Valentine's Day in the middle. Our [Valentine's Day 2027 story](/news/valentines-day-2027-sunday-february-14-dates-printables/) has the dates for that Sunday.

## What about stores and other businesses?

Store hours for Presidents' Day 2027 have not been announced, and this story lists none. The sources above cover offices, mail, the Federal Reserve and the NYSE only.
