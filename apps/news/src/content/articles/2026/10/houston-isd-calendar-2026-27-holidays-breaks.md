**Houston Independent School District (ISD) students have no classes on Friday, Nov. 6, 2026**, the next day off on the district's Yearly Calendar 2026-2027 (footer "Updated 4/6/2026"). It is a Staff professional development (PD) day. Thanksgiving recess follows on Nov. 23-27, and **winter recess runs Monday, Dec. 21 through Thursday, Dec. 31**.

## Houston ISD 2026-27 breaks

| Break | Dates | Weekdays with no classes |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving recess | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 |
| Winter recess | Mon., Dec. 21 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | 9 |
| Spring recess | Mon.-Fri., March 8-12, 2027 | 5 |

The weekday counts are ours, computed from the printed dates. Winter recess is followed by New Year's Day (Friday, Jan. 1, 2027) and a Staff PD day with no classes on Monday, Jan. 4. Counting those two days, Houston ISD has 11 weekdays in a row without classes, from Dec. 21 to Jan. 4. The calendar does not print a return day; the next grading cycle begins Tuesday, Jan. 5.

The district's March break is not labeled spring break. The calendar calls it a recess, and Houston ISD lists Monday, March 8 through Friday, March 12, 2027. For how other districts set theirs, see our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

The first day of school is Monday, Aug. 10, 2026. Counting the weekdays above from Aug. 10 through the last student day, there are 29 weekdays without classes. That is our count from the printed calendar, not a district figure.

## Is there school on Presidents' Day?

No. The calendar prints no Presidents' Day holiday, but Monday, Feb. 15, 2027 is a Staff PD day with no classes, so students are home either way. Our [Thanksgiving break roundup](/news/thanksgiving-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) and [winter break roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) show how other large districts set the same weeks.

## What is the March 26 recess?

Friday, March 26, 2027 is listed as a recess with no classes. The PDF does not name the reason. The date coincides with Good Friday: Easter Sunday is March 28, 2027, as covered in our spring break story.

## When does the school year end?

The last day for students is Friday, May 28, 2027. Memorial Day is Monday, May 31, and the last day for teachers is Tuesday, June 1. Report cards for the final grading cycle are dated Wednesday, June 2.

The district can change its calendar, so check the [Houston ISD district calendar](https://www.houstonisd.org/our-district/district-calendar) before you book travel. The calendar prints no early dismissal days and no board approval note.
