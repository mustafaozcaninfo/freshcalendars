**The Golden State Warriors host the LA Clippers on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, at 5:30 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 8:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), at Chase Center in San Francisco, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) official schedule.** The NBA lists no national TV for that game.

The NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change," gives each game's ET time and the local time at the home arena. It lists 14 Warriors games in November. The table below converts every tip-off to PT and ET, and to Central Time (CT) for road games in the Central zone, and a printable of the same rows is further down.

## Where do the Warriors play at home and on the road in November?

By our count, 8 of the 14 games are at Chase Center and 6 are away. The schedule falls into two long blocks and one pair of back-to-back nights on the road:

| Stretch | Dates | Games |
|---|---|---|
| Home stand | Nov. 1 to Nov. 9 | 5 home games |
| Road trip | Nov. 11 to Nov. 19 | 5 road games: Houston, San Antonio, New Orleans, Dallas, Memphis |
| Back-to-back | Nov. 1 and Nov. 2 | Both at home: Clippers, then Phoenix |
| Back-to-back | Nov. 18 and Nov. 19 | Dallas, then Memphis |

After the road trip the Warriors host Boston on Nov. 22 and Sacramento on Nov. 25, play at Portland on Nov. 27 and host the Clippers again on Nov. 29.

## Which Warriors games are NBA Cup games in November?

The NBA's schedule marks three Warriors games in November as Emirates NBA Cup games: Fri., Nov. 13 at San Antonio, Wed., Nov. 25 against Sacramento and Fri., Nov. 27 at Portland. NBA.com places the Warriors in West Group C with the San Antonio Spurs, Los Angeles Lakers, Portland Trail Blazers and Sacramento Kings. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) lists the group play and knockout dates, and the [Lakers November schedule](/news/lakers-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-nba-cup-printable/) covers the other Los Angeles team in the group.

## What is the Warriors TV schedule for November 2026?

The NBA's PDF lists national TV only: ESPN for the Nov. 11 game at Houston and the Nov. 18 game at Dallas, and Prime Video for the Nov. 13 Cup game at San Antonio. The other 11 November games show no national network there. Local broadcast details are on the Warriors' official schedule page at [nba.com/warriors/schedule](https://www.nba.com/warriors/schedule).
