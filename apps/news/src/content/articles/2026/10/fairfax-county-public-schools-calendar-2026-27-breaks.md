**Fairfax County Public Schools (FCPS) has winter break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027, and spring break from Monday to Friday, March 22-26, 2027**, according to its "2026-2027 Standard School Year Calendar" (PDF). The first day of school is Monday, Aug. 24, 2026, and the last day for regular classes is Wednesday, June 16, 2027. The calendar does not print the date the board approved it. The next two dates are Friday, Oct. 30, a two-hour early release for the end of quarter 1, and Tuesday, Nov. 3, Election Day, a Teacher Workday (TW) that is a student holiday.

## When are FCPS schools closed in 2026-27?

The grid marks no-school days with H (holiday), SP (School Planning Day, a student holiday) or TW (Teacher Workday, a student holiday). The table lists each one with its weekday.

| Date | Day | What the calendar says |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 4-7, 2026 | Friday-Monday | Labor Day Break |
| Sept. 21, 2026 | Monday | Yom Kippur (H) |
| Oct. 12, 2026 | Monday | Indigenous Peoples' Day (SP, student holiday) |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Election Day (TW, student holiday) |
| Nov. 25-27, 2026 | Wednesday-Friday | Thanksgiving Break |
| Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 3, 2027 | Monday-Sunday | Winter Break |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Martin Luther King (MLK) Jr.'s Birthday |
| Jan. 29, 2027 | Friday | TW, student holiday |
| Feb. 15, 2027 | Monday | Washington's Birthday and Presidents' Day |
| March 22-26, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Spring Break |
| May 31, 2027 | Monday | Memorial Day |

## How many school days off does the calendar count?

The weekday counts below are ours, computed from the dates in the table. They add up to 27 weekdays.

| Type | Days | Weekdays |
|---|---|---|
| Breaks | Labor Day Break (2), Thanksgiving Break (3), Winter Break (10), Spring Break (5) | 20 |
| Holidays | Yom Kippur, MLK Jr.'s Birthday, Washington's Birthday and Presidents' Day, Memorial Day | 4 |
| Student holidays for planning or teacher workdays | Oct. 12, Nov. 3, Jan. 29 | 3 |

Winter break is the longest stretch, with 10 weekdays from Monday, Dec. 21 to Friday, Jan. 1. The PDF reads "21- January 3 Winter Break" and does not print a return day, so the break ends Sunday, Jan. 3, and the next weekday is Monday, Jan. 4. Plan the holiday weeks on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Which days are early release?

Quarter-end days are a two-hour early release for all schools. The footer also lists "Limited Early Release Wednesdays," three hours early for elementary schools. The calendar does not print those Wednesday dates; the [FCPS page on Limited Early Release Wednesdays](https://www.fcps.edu/ler-wednesdays) has them.

| Date | Day | Early release |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 30, 2026 | Friday | End of quarter 1, two hours early |
| Jan. 28, 2027 | Thursday | End of quarter 2, two hours early |
| April 16, 2027 | Friday | End of quarter 3, two hours early |
| June 16, 2027 | Wednesday | Last day for regular classes, early release |

Friday, Jan. 29, the day after quarter 2 ends, is a Teacher Workday and a student holiday. Lay out the quarter ends on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html) if you track the fall.

## Are FCPS schools closed for religious observances and other dates in the footer?

Yom Kippur, Monday, Sept. 21, 2026, is marked H, a holiday. Other dates appear in the footer list of holidays and observances, and an observance there does not by itself mean no school.

- Rosh Hashanah, Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026, falls on a weekend and appears only in the footer.
- Diwali, Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, is a footer observance.
- Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, is listed among observances; the calendar does not mark it as a day off.
- Lunar New Year, Saturday, Feb. 6, 2027, falls on a weekend.
- Eid al-Fitr, Tuesday, March 9, 2027, is footer-listed as "begins sundown 3/8 - 3/10." The calendar notes that some holidays "may shift due to anticipated lunar sightings."
- Eid al-Adha, Monday, May 17, 2027, is a footer observance. For both Eid dates, confirm with FCPS or your school whether classes meet.
- Juneteenth (Observed), Friday, June 18, 2027, falls after the last day for regular classes.

## When is FCPS spring break 2027?

Spring Break is Monday, March 22 to Friday, March 26, 2027, five weekdays, and Good Friday, March 26, falls inside it. For how other districts line up that week, see our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/). ## When does the school year start and end?

Monday, Aug. 24, 2026 is the first day of school. The last day for regular classes is Wednesday, June 16, 2027, an early-release day.
