**The Cobb County School District in Georgia lists its 2026-27 holiday break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Jan. 4, 2027, a February break Feb. 15-19, and spring break Monday, Apr. 5 through Friday, Apr. 9, 2027**, according to its Important Dates page. School started Monday, Aug. 3, 2026, per the district's Back to School Info page dated July 16, 2026. The district's calendar PDFs show breaks only as shaded cells, so this story puts them in text.

## Cobb County 2026-27 breaks

| Break | Dates | Status on Oct. 28, 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Fall Break | Week of Mon.-Fri., Sept. 21-25, 2026 | Past |
| Thanksgiving week | Not on the Important Dates list; Thanksgiving Day is Thu., Nov. 26 | Check the PDF |
| Holiday Break and staff workdays | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027 | Ahead |
| February break (listed as "Winter Break") | Week of Mon.-Fri., Feb. 15-19, 2027 | Ahead |
| Spring Break | Week of Mon.-Fri., Apr. 5-9, 2027 | Ahead |

The Important Dates page prints the end of the holiday break as "Friday January 4," but Jan. 4, 2027 is a Monday. We give the dates as listed and do not state a return day, because the page does not name one. The year-at-a-glance PDFs for 2026-27 and 2027-28 are on the district's [calendar page](https://www.cobbk12.org/calendar).

## What are Digital Learning Days and early release days?

On a Digital Learning Day, the district's page says students do not report. The page lists four: Mon., Aug. 17, Mon., Oct. 12, Dec. 1 and Mon., Mar. 1, 2027. The page calls Dec. 1 a Monday, but Dec. 1, 2026 is a Tuesday, so confirm that date with your school.

Early release days are at 1:25 p.m.

| Dates | What the district lists |
|---|---|
| Tue.-Fri., Oct. 13-16, 2026 | Conference week, early release 1:25 p.m. (past) |
| Thu.-Fri., Dec. 17-18, 2026 | Early release, 1:25 p.m. |
| Mon.-Wed., May 17-19, 2027 | Early release, 1:25 p.m. |

The page prints the May dates with the year 2026, but the weekdays match 2027, so we list them as 2027.

## When is the last day of school in Cobb County?

We could not confirm a last day from the district's text pages. The Important Dates list shows early release from May 17 to May 19, 2027, and has no last-day entry. Look for it on the year-at-a-glance PDF before you book a trip.
