## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**The two words that start with B**

- First B word, letters: starts with B, ends with N, and has an R, an A and an I inside.
- First B word, meaning: the organ in the head that controls thinking.
- Second B word, letters: starts with B, ends with N, and has an E, a G and a U inside.
- Second B word, meaning: the past participle of "begin", meaning started.

**The word that starts with D**

- Letters: starts with D, ends with Y, and has an I, an M and an L inside.
- Meaning: in a way that is not bright or clear, as in a room lit faintly.

**The word that starts with U**

- Letters: starts with U, ends with R, and has an L, a C and an E inside.
- Meaning: an open sore on the skin or inside the body, such as one in the stomach.

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess such as Crane, with C, R, A, N and E, puts at least one letter into three of the four boards. A second guess such as Doubt, which adds D, O, U, B and T, reaches the fourth board and adds a letter to each of the other three. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
