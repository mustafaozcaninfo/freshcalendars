**Edinburgh's Christmas 2026 runs from Saturday 14 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**, according to the official Edinburgh's Christmas website (edwinterfest.com), created and produced by Unique Assembly. Our day-by-day Big Wheel table and a one-page printable are below.

The official site publishes daily hours for the Big Wheel and for the sites that hold the markets, funfair and bars. It does not list hours for individual stalls, so this story does not give them.

## What are Edinburgh's Christmas 2026 dates?

| Event | Date and time | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Edinburgh's Christmas | Saturday 14 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 | Edinburgh's Christmas website |
| Big Wheel | 14 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, 10:00 to 22:00 | Big Wheel page |
| Family Ice Experience | 21 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, 30-minute sessions | Family Ice Experience page |
| Torchlight Procession | Tuesday 29 December 2026, 18:30, from £5.75 | Torchlight Procession page |
| Night Afore Party with The Fratellis | Wednesday 30 December 2026, 18:00, from £42 | Night Afore Party page |
| Hogmanay Street Party | Thursday 31 December 2026, from 20:00, from £10 | Street Party page |
| Hogmanay in the Gardens with Underworld | 31 December 2026, doors 20:00, stage live from 20:50, from £79 | Underworld event page |

By our count the season lasts 51 days, counting both 14 November and 3 January. That assumes the attractions run on every date; the Big Wheel, for one, is closed on Christmas Day.

## What are the Edinburgh Big Wheel opening times?

The official Big Wheel page lists 10:00 to 22:00, with last admission at 21:30, from 14 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. Prices start from £12, and tickets are date-specific. Residents with an EH postcode get 20% off, up to four tickets per event, according to the same page.

Seven dates have different hours. We added the weekdays:

| Date | Big Wheel hours |
|---|---|
| Tuesday 17 November 2026 | 17:00 to 22:00 |
| Thursday 24 December 2026 | 10:00 to 20:00 |
| Friday 25 December 2026 | Closed |
| Saturday 26 December 2026 | 12:00 to 20:00 |
| Thursday 31 December 2026 | 10:00 to 17:00 |
| Friday 1 January 2027 | 12:00 to 20:00 |
| Sunday 3 January 2027 | 10:00 to 20:00 |

## When is the Edinburgh Hogmanay 2026 Torchlight Procession?

The Torchlight Procession is on Tuesday 29 December 2026 at 18:30, according to the official Edinburgh's Hogmanay website. The event page says prices start from £5.75, and the meeting point is The Meadows (Melville Dr).

The Night Afore Party with The Fratellis is on Wednesday 30 December 2026 at 18:00 in West Princes Street Gardens, billed as a party beneath Edinburgh Castle with Scots rockers The Fratellis. The official listing says prices start from £42.

The Hogmanay Street Party is on Thursday 31 December 2026 from 20:00 on Princes Street, billed on the official site as "Ring in 2027". The official listing gives prices from £10, and a 50p charity donation is collected with each Street Party ticket purchase.

The same evening, "Hogmanay in the Gardens with Underworld" is in West Princes Street Gardens: doors open at 20:00 and the stage is live from 20:50. The listing says prices start from £79 and names "Underworld + Special Guests" without naming the guests. The Hogmanay page says Underworld make their Edinburgh's Hogmanay debut.

Early-bird tickets for the 2026 Street Party and Torchlight Procession went on sale on 9 January 2026 at 2025 prices, according to the festival's news page of that date.

## What are the Edinburgh Christmas market opening hours?

The official Your Visit page puts the Traditional Christmas Markets in East Princes Street Gardens and the fairground rides in West Princes Street Gardens. Entry to all Edinburgh's Christmas sites is free and non-ticketed, according to the official FAQs; attractions inside are booked online or at the point of entry.

| Site | Usual hours, 14 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 | Changed hours |
|---|---|---|
| East Princes Street Gardens | Daily 10:00 to 22:00 | 17 Nov. 16:00 to 22:00; 24 Dec. 10:00 to 20:00; 25 Dec. closed; 26 Dec. 12:00 to 20:00; 31 Dec. 10:00 to 17:00; 1 Jan. 12:00 to 20:00; 3 Jan. 10:00 to 20:00 |
| West Princes Street Gardens | Monday to Wednesday 12:00 to 20:00; Thursday and Friday 12:00 to 21:00; Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 21:00 | 17 Nov. 13:00 to 20:00; 19 to 23 Dec. 11:00 to 21:00; 24 Dec. 11:00 to 19:00; 25 Dec. closed; 26 Dec. 12:00 to 20:00; 27 to 30 Dec. 12:00 to 21:00; 31 Dec. 12:00 to 17:00; 1 Jan. 12:00 to 20:00; 3 Jan. 10:00 to 20:00 |

The page says opening times are subject to change and points to the @edinburghschristmas social channels for updates. It gives site hours, not hours for individual stalls, and we have not guessed those. Check edwinterfest.com before you travel.
