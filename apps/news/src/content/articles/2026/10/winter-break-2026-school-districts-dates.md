**Seventy of the 82 U.S. school districts in our table close for winter break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027**, according to the 2026-27 calendars the districts publish. The bigger difference is the return date: many districts add a staff day on Monday, Jan. 4, so students do not go back until Tuesday, Jan. 5, and Los Angeles Unified and Fresno Unified stay off another week.

We read each calendar on the district's own website or in its official PDF. Here are 82 districts: 24 of the largest and 58 more that each have their own story on our site. Each name links to our story on that district's calendar where we have one.

"Plus" days are teacher planning, professional development or staff days with no students, listed separately on the district calendar. Rows after Lee County come from our own district stories, each built from that district's calendar. Return days that a district does not print are the first weekday its calendar does not list as off, as our story on that district reads it. Prince George's, Baltimore County, Jefferson County, Seattle and DC Public Schools return days are read from the calendar's closure marks, and Anne Arundel County, Columbus, Newark and Salem-Keizer calendars give no return day we can read.

## Three patterns

**The two-week break.** Seventy of the 82 districts give students at least the full two weeks of Dec. 21 to Jan. 1; Douglas County, Colo., lists Dec. 21-31. Dec. 24 is a Thursday this year, so Christmas Eve and Christmas Day fall at the end of the first week.

**The late start.** New York City, Montgomery County, Baltimore County, Anne Arundel County, Md., Boston, Pittsburgh, Newark and Howard County, Md., close from Thursday, Dec. 24. Montgomery County's Dec. 23 is an early-release day. Philadelphia closes from Wednesday, Dec. 23; its calendar lists the recess through Dec. 31 and New Year's Day as a separate closure. Elk Grove Unified and Saint Paul Public Schools also start Dec. 23.

**The long tail.** Los Angeles Unified's winter recess runs through Friday, Jan. 8, and the second semester starts Monday, Jan. 11. The district notes that a school choosing Jan. 11 as a pupil-free day would bring students back Jan. 12 instead. In Dallas, Jan. 4 is still part of winter break and Jan. 5 is a professional development day, so classes resume Wednesday, Jan. 6. Duval County also returns Jan. 6 after two staff days, and so do Polk County, Fla., where Jan. 4-5 are no school for students, Seminole County, Fla., Henry County, Ga., Oakland Unified (Jan. 5 is a professional development day) and Arlington ISD in Texas. Fresno Unified's winter break also runs through Jan. 8, and its return day, Monday, Jan. 11, is our reading of the calendar.
