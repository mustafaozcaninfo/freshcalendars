**Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026 is the date most year-end money moves are due, according to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).** That covers a Required Minimum Distribution (RMD) for the year and cash gifts to charity. Other items, such as Individual Retirement Account (IRA) contributions, wait until April 2027. The dated table and a printable checklist below separate the two groups.

## Year-end money deadlines in date order

| Date | What | Who it applies to | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon., Dec. 7, 2026 | Medicare Open Enrollment ends (it began Oct. 15); changes take effect Jan. 1 | People changing Medicare health or drug coverage | Medicare.gov |
| Tue., Dec. 15, 2026 | Enroll in a new Marketplace plan for coverage starting Jan. 1 | HealthCare.gov states; state-run exchanges may differ | HealthCare.gov |
| Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | Take the 2026 RMD | Account owners required to take one | IRS RMD FAQs |
| Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | Make cash gifts that count for 2026 | Donors | IRS Topic 506 |
| Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | Check the health Flexible Spending Account (FSA) rules | FSA holders | IRS Publication 969 |
| Fri., Jan. 15, 2027 | Pay 2026 estimated tax (fourth payment); last day to enroll in a Marketplace plan for Feb. 1 coverage | People who pay estimated tax; Marketplace shoppers | IRS; HealthCare.gov |
| Mon., Mar. 15, 2027 | End of an FSA grace period, if the plan has one | FSA holders with a grace-period plan | IRS Publication 969 |
| Thu., Apr. 1, 2027 | Latest date for a first RMD that was delayed | People who turned 73 in 2026 | IRS RMD FAQs |
| Thu., Apr. 15, 2027 | Last day to make 2026 IRA contributions | IRA savers | Draft 2026 Form 8606 instructions |

The March 15 row is the end of a 2 1/2 month grace period for a calendar-year plan. The grace-period length comes from Publication 969; the March date is our count.

## What is the RMD deadline for 2026?

The IRS RMD FAQs, reviewed Jan. 29, 2026, say you must take your first RMD for the year in which you reach age 73. You can delay that first one until April 1 of the following year. Later RMDs are due by Dec. 31 each year, which makes Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026 the date for anyone past the first-year RMD.

Delaying the first RMD has a catch. If you take it on April 1, 2027, you will also owe the 2027 RMD by Dec. 31, 2027, so two RMDs land in the same tax year.

A missed RMD "may be subject to an excise tax of 25%," the IRS says, reduced to 10% if the amount is corrected in time.

## Which deadlines wait until 2027?

Several do. The IRS's draft 2026 Form 8606 instructions say 2026 IRA contributions can be made until the filing deadline, Thursday, April 15, 2027. The IRS estimated tax page says to pay 2026 estimated tax by Jan. 15, 2027, a Friday.

Workplace plans are different. Elective deferrals to a 401(k) come out of pay, so changes need to be made with your employer before the final 2026 paycheck.

## What are the 2026 contribution limits?

The IRS set these in Notice 2025-67. The 2027 limits have not yet been announced by the IRS; our story on [2027 tax brackets and 401(k) and Health Savings Account (HSA) limits](/news/2027-tax-brackets-401k-hsa-limits-irs/) tracks them.

| Account | 2026 limit |
|---|---|
| 401(k), 403(b), most 457 plans and the Thrift Savings Plan (TSP): elective deferral | $24,500 (up from $23,500) |
| Catch-up, age 50 and over | $8,000 (up from $7,500) |
| Catch-up, ages 60 to 63 | $11,250 |
| IRA | $7,500 (up from $7,000) |
| IRA catch-up, age 50 and over | $1,100 ($8,600 in total) |

## How do health FSA rules work at year end?

Revenue Procedure 2025-32 sets the 2026 health FSA limit at $3,400 and the maximum carryover at $680 if the plan allows carryover. Publication 969 adds that a plan may offer a grace period of up to 2 1/2 months after the plan year, and a plan with carryover cannot also have a grace period. Which one your plan uses is in your plan documents, so check before Dec. 31.

## Which charity gifts count for 2026?

A gift counts for 2026 when it is made by Dec. 31. IRS Topic 506, updated Sept. 24, 2026, says that beginning with tax year 2026, if you do not itemize, you may deduct up to $1,000 ($2,000 if filing jointly) of cash contributions to certain qualified organizations.

The IRS Working Families Tax Cuts page, updated Oct. 22, 2026, says donations to donor-advised funds and supporting organizations do not qualify. Our [Giving Tuesday story](/news/giving-tuesday-2026-december-1-tax-deduction-charity-check/) covers how to check a charity.

The gift tax annual exclusion for 2026 is $19,000 per recipient, according to Revenue Procedure 2025-32.

## Which health coverage dates fall in December?

Medicare Open Enrollment runs Oct. 15 to Dec. 7, with changes effective Jan. 1, per Medicare.gov. HealthCare.gov says to enroll by Dec. 15 in a new plan for coverage to start Jan. 1. Open Enrollment for 2027 runs Nov. 1 to Jan. 15, and enrolling by Jan. 15 gives coverage starting Feb. 1. Details are in our [Affordable Care Act (ACA) open enrollment 2027 story](/news/aca-open-enrollment-2027-dates-november-1-january-15/); state-run exchanges may differ.
