Each perfect pangram earns 14 points: 7 for its length and 7 for the bonus.

## Which letters showed up most?

H was the center letter on four days, more than any other. I, C, A and L were each in the center three times. Sixteen different letters took the center during the month.

Across all seven cells, N and I were the most common, in 21 of the 31 sets each, followed by A (19), L (18) and O (16). S never appeared in any of the 31 sets. Thirty of the 31 sets had two or three of the five vowels A, E, I, O and U; Oct. 26 (center L; G, H, I, N, T, Y) had only I, plus Y.

## What was the longest word of the month?

Intelligently, the pangram on Fri., Oct. 9, had 13 letters, the longest word in any October puzzle. Bacchanalian (Oct. 3, also a pangram) and Highlighting (Oct. 26) followed at 12 letters each. Four-letter words made up 483 of the 1,499 answers, about 32%, yet they were worth only 483 of the 7,150 points, under 7%.

## What this tells you for November

These are patterns in last month's puzzles, not a forecast of any letter set or answer. They can still shape how you play.

- **Skip the plurals.** None of the 31 October sets contained S, so adding S to a word was never an option.
- **Test seven- and eight-letter words for a pangram.** Of the 47 pangrams, 11 had seven letters and 16 had eight, so 27 had one of those two lengths.
- **Keep looking after the first pangram.** Twelve of 31 days had two or more, and those days averaged 280 points.
- **Aim for longer words.** The 483 four-letter words were worth under 7% of October's points, and Genius takes 70%.
- **Expect a smaller list early in the week.** Monday and Tuesday puzzles averaged 38 and 40 words, against 57 on Sundays, though each weekday had only four or five puzzles.
