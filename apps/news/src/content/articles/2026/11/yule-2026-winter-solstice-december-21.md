**Yule 2026 is tied to the winter solstice on Monday, Dec. 21, which the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) puts at 20:50 Universal Time (UT): 3:50 p.m. ET in the United States and 8:50 p.m. in the UK.** The Pagan Federation, a UK Pagan organization, says Pagans celebrate Yule from Dec. 21 to Jan. 1. Solstice times for every U.S. zone and the UK, the dates through 2030, day lengths in four cities and a printable activity sheet follow.

## What time is the Yule 2026 solstice in each U.S. zone and the UK?

USNO lists the December solstice at 20:50 UT on Dec. 21, 2026. The National Weather Service's Astronomical Seasons page gives the same moment as "December 21st @ 1:50 pm MST," and Royal Museums Greenwich says it falls at 8.50pm Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in the UK. The other zones are computed by Fresh Calendars from the USNO time.

|---|---|---|
| Hawaii | 10:50 a.m. | Monday, Dec. 21 |
| Alaska | 11:50 a.m. | Monday, Dec. 21 |

| Eastern (ET) | 3:50 p.m. | Monday, Dec. 21 |

## What is Yule and where does the name come from?

Merriam-Webster gives the word's origin as "Middle English yol, from Old English geōl; akin to Old Norse jōl, a pagan midwinter festival."

Royal Museums Greenwich writes that "Present-day Christmas customs and traditions such as the Yule log, Yule boar, Yule singing, and others stem from pagan Juul." According to Royal Museums Greenwich, a Yule log was gathered and burnt in the hearth as a tribute to the Norse god Thor. No source used here dates the custom, so no century is given.

## When does Yule start and end in 2026?

In its December 2025 interfaith blog, the Pagan Federation wrote: "We Pagans celebrate Winter solstice on 21st December and Yule from 21st December to 1st January," describing it as "celebrating the rebirth of the sun and a return of longer days of sunlight." On its Heathenry page the federation describes "Yule – a twelve-day festival that begins around the time of the winter solstice," and says Heathen festivals do not follow the "Eight-Fold Wheel of the Year" based on solstices and equinoxes. Practice differs between traditions, so these are the federation's descriptions and not a single rule.

The window from Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 falls on these days in 2026 and 2027:

| Mon. | Tue. | Wed. | Thu. | Fri. | Sat. | Sun. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dec. 21 (solstice) | Dec. 22 | Dec. 23 | Dec. 24 | Dec. 25 (Christmas Day) | Dec. 26 | Dec. 27 |
| Dec. 28 | Dec. 29 | Dec. 30 | Dec. 31 | Jan. 1, 2027 | | |

| City | Sunrise | Sunset | Daylight |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York | 7:17 a.m. | 4:32 p.m. | 9 h 15 min |
| Chicago | 7:15 a.m. | 4:23 p.m. | 9 h 8 min |
| Los Angeles | 6:55 a.m. | 4:48 p.m. | 9 h 53 min |

Los Angeles has the longest day of the four and London the shortest.

## Is Yule a public holiday in the U.S., UK or Canada?

No. The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists "Friday, December 25 | Christmas Day" as the December 2026 federal holiday. GOV.UK lists 25 December (Friday) as Christmas Day and 28 December (Monday) as the substitute day for Boxing Day. The Canada Revenue Agency lists Christmas Day on Friday, Dec. 25, 2026, and Boxing Day on Saturday, Dec. 26, 2026. Yule is on none of the three lists.

## Can you watch the solstice at Stonehenge in 2026?

English Heritage gives free access to Stonehenge on Tuesday 22nd December, and says "Sunrise will take place at 8:09am on Tuesday 22nd December (approximate timings)." As of Nov. 19, its page says "Parking is now sold out." Details are in our [Stonehenge winter solstice story](/news/stonehenge-winter-solstice-2026-december-22-free-access/).
