**The Country Club Plaza lighting ceremony in Kansas City, Mo., is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, from 5 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. Central Time (CT)**, according to the KC Streetcar Authority. Kansas City television station (KSHB) 41 reports the switch is flipped at approximately 6:25 p.m. CT, which is 7:25 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), and that the event is free.

The Plaza's own site could not be read when we checked, so these times rest on the transit authority, Visit KC and two newsrooms, KSHB 41 and KCTV5. Pair the evening with the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html), and see the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) for the weeks the lights stay on.

## What is the Plaza lighting 2026 schedule?

| Time (Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026) | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 5 p.m. CT | Event kicks off; free and open to the public | KSHB 41, KC Streetcar Authority |
| About 6:25 p.m. CT (7:25 p.m. ET) | Switch is flipped | KSHB 41, Visit KC |
| 7:30 p.m. CT | Ceremony ends | KC Streetcar Authority |
| 5 p.m. to 3 a.m. CT, daily after Thanksgiving | Lights on until mid-January | Visit KC |

Visit KC lists fireworks after the switch and calls it the 97th annual ceremony, with a live broadcast on Kansas City station (KMBC). KSHB names Vocalocity, Miller Marley Dance Company, Quixotic and DJ Kirby Hamm among the entertainment. Set times for performers were not published in the sources we found. Street closures and rideshare points were not found either.

## Who flips the switch at the Plaza lighting?

KSHB 41 reports that Royals catcher Carter Jensen, a Park Hill High School graduate, will flip the switch at approximately 6:25 p.m. CT.

## Is the 2026 Plaza lighting earlier than last year?

The switch is about 90 minutes earlier than in 2025. Last year the lighting was on Thanksgiving night, Nov. 27, 2025, with the switch at 7:55 p.m., which KSHB called "roughly an hour later than normal." In 2025 the lighters came from the KC Current, KSHB reported.

KCTV5 reported on Nov. 12, 2026 that the ceremony runs from 5 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. on Thanksgiving Day, and that organizers moved the start earlier to avoid the Chiefs' Thanksgiving game. The Chiefs play at Bills at 8:20 p.m. ET that night, according to our [Thanksgiving football schedule](/news/thanksgiving-football-schedule-2026-nfl-college-games/).

## What are the KC Streetcar hours over Thanksgiving weekend 2026?

The KC Streetcar Authority lists these hours. KCTV5 reports that parking is available near the 6.5-mile streetcar route.

| Day | Streetcar hours | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26 | 7 a.m. to midnight | Two-hour late start, normal end time |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. | Mayor's Christmas Tree lighting at Crown Center, 6 p.m. |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. | |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | 5 a.m. to midnight | |

The streetcar's page lists the Crown Center tree lighting at 6 p.m. on Friday, Nov. 27, followed by a fireworks finale. We did not confirm which stops serve the Plaza, so check kcstreetcar.org.

## How long are the Plaza lights on, and where do you park?

Visit KC says the lights are on daily from 5 p.m. to 3 a.m. from Thanksgiving to mid-January. KSHB, citing the Plaza's press release, says they run through Jan. 10, 2027. Visit KC says all of the Plaza's parking garages are free and open to the public.

The display began in 1925 with a single strand of lights over a store entrance, and the first lighting ceremony was in 1930, Visit KC says.

Mark Thursday, Nov. 26 for the 5 p.m. start and the 6:25 p.m. switch, and Friday, Nov. 27 for the Crown Center tree.
