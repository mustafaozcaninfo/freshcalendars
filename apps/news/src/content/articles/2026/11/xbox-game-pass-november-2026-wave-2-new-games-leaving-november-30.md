**Xbox Wire's Nov. 17, 2026 Game Pass post lists 10 games arriving from Nov. 17 to Dec. 1 and eight games leaving the library on Nov. 30.** Paw Patrol: Dino World was listed as available Nov. 17, and The Outlast Trials and Hela: of Mice & Magic close the list on Dec. 1. Xbox Wire gives dates only, with no time of day. A day-by-day table, a tier matrix and a free printable follow.

## Which games come to Xbox Game Pass from Nov. 17 to Dec. 1?

Weekdays are computed from the dates. "Game Preview" is Xbox Wire's label for a game that is not finished.

| Date | Game | Platforms listed |
|---|---|---|
| Tue., Nov. 17 | Paw Patrol: Dino World | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Wed., Nov. 18 | Call of the Elder Gods | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, Handheld, PC |
| Wed., Nov. 18 | High on Life 2 | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, Handheld, PC |
| Wed., Nov. 18 | Roadside Research (Game Preview) | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, Handheld, PC |
| Thu., Nov. 19 | Black Jacket | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, Handheld, PC |
| Tue., Nov. 24 | Motorslice | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, Handheld, PC |
| Tue., Nov. 24 | Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 | Cloud, Console, Handheld, PC |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Arkheron (Game Preview) | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, PC |
| Tue., Dec. 1 | The Outlast Trials | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, PC |
| Tue., Dec. 1 | Hela: of Mice & Magic | Cloud, Xbox Series X\|S, PC |

Three games land on Wed., Nov. 18 and two on Tue., Nov. 24, counted from the table. Handheld is listed for six of the 10 games.

## Which Game Pass tier includes each new game?

Xbox Wire prints a tier line under every title. For Call of the Elder Gods, High on Life 2, Roadside Research, Black Jacket and Motorslice it reads "Now with Game Pass Premium; joining Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass". The matrix turns those lines into a grid; "Yes" means the tier is on the line.

| Game | Ultimate | Premium | PC Game Pass | Essential | Day one label |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paw Patrol: Dino World | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Call of the Elder Gods | Yes | Yes (new) | Yes | No | No |
| High on Life 2 | Yes | Yes (new) | Yes | No | No |
| Roadside Research | Yes | Yes (new) | Yes | No | No |
| Black Jacket | Yes | Yes (new) | Yes | No | No |
| Motorslice | Yes | Yes (new) | Yes | No | No |
| Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Arkheron | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes |
| The Outlast Trials | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Hela: of Mice & Magic | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes |

Eight of the 10 games list Premium, and Arkheron and Hela are the two that do not. Xbox Wire marks those two as available on day one. "No" means Xbox Wire did not list the tier, not that it is ruled out.

## Which Game Pass perks arrive on Dec. 1, 2026?

Xbox Wire's In-Game Benefits section lists two bonuses for Tue., Dec. 1:

| Benefit | Platform | Tiers listed |
|---|---|---|
| World of Tanks Blitz, Monthly Bonus | PC | Ultimate, Premium, Essential, PC Game Pass |
| World of Tanks Modern Armor, XP Stock | Console | Ultimate, Premium, Essential |

## Which games leave Xbox Game Pass on Nov. 30, 2026?

Xbox Wire's "Leaving November 30" list has eight titles, each marked "Cloud, Console, and PC". Xbox Wire says the games are leaving the Game Pass library and suggests saving on them before they go. The post names no hour, so Monday, Nov. 30 is the last day to count on playing them.

| Game | Leaves | Platforms listed |
|---|---|---|
| Aliens: Dark Descent | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Banishers: Ghosts of New Eden | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| DreamWorks Gabby's Dollhouse: Ready to Party | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Generation Zero | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Kill It With Fire! 2 | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Moving Out 2 | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Star Trucker | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |
| Totally Reliable Delivery Service | Mon., Nov. 30 | Cloud, Console, PC |

The earlier removals are in our [Game Pass leaving Oct. 31 story](/news/xbox-game-pass-leaving-october-31-2026-new-games-november-2-6/). Xbox's store sale dates are in the [Xbox Black Friday sale 2026 story](/news/xbox-black-friday-sale-2026-november-19-december-2/), and PC sale dates are in the [Steam Winter Sale 2026 calendar](/news/steam-winter-sale-2026-dates-december-17-steam-sale-calendar-2027/).

## When is the next Xbox Game Pass list for December 2026?

No December wave 1 list has been announced. Xbox Wire usually posts the next list in early December, and this story will be updated with the first dates when it appears.
