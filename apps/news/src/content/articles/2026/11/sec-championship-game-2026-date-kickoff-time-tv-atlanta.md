**The 2026 Southeastern Conference (SEC) Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5, at 4 p.m. ET on ABC at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, according to ESPN's schedule of June 10, 2026.** The stadium's events page lists "SEC Championship (2026)" on "December 5, 2026" with a "Kickoff time: 4:00 PM."

It is also the last one. The news station (WTOC) reported on Nov. 9, 2026 that "the 2026 edition of the SEC Championship Game will be the final," and Saturday Down South carried the statement from SEC Commissioner Greg Sankey that it "will become the final SEC Championship Game" and "the 35th edition of the SEC title game."

## What time is the SEC Championship Game in your time zone?

The 4 p.m. start is Eastern Time, the local time in Atlanta. Other zones are computed from it.

| Where | Kickoff on Sat., Dec. 5 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET, Atlanta) | 4 p.m. |
| Central (CT) | 3 p.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 2 p.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | 1 p.m. |
| Alaska | noon |
| Hawaii | 11 a.m. |
| United Kingdom (GMT) | 9 p.m. |

The UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in December, so the offset from ET is five hours. The sources give no halftime or end time.

## Why is 2026 the last SEC title game?

The College Football Playoff (CFP) goes "from 12 to 24 teams next season," WTOC reported, and the SEC said the championship game ends with that expansion. Sankey's statement, as Saturday Down South published it, says: "Expanding the College Football Playoff is about more than adding teams." Baton Rouge's Channel 9 (WAFB) also reported the game's end on Nov. 9.

For this season the field is still 12 teams. The National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) site says the champions of the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC), Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC "are in automatically." The first round is Dec. 18-19, 2026 and the championship game is Jan. 25, 2027 in Las Vegas, per ESPN's CFP broadcast schedule. Selection Day is Sunday, Dec. 6, covered in the [conference championship weekend schedule](/news/college-football-conference-championship-games-2026-schedule-times-tv/) and the [CFP rankings and dates guide](/news/college-football-playoff-rankings-2026-schedule-dates-times/).

## Which teams play in the 2026 SEC Championship Game?

Not known yet. The matchup is set after the last regular-season games, and none of the sources names a team. The rivalry games that close the regular season are in the [rivalry week guide](/news/college-football-rivalry-week-2026-iron-bowl-the-game-egg-bowl-times-tv/).

## Who won the SEC Championship Game last year?

In 2025, Georgia beat Alabama 28-7 on Sat., Dec. 6, 2025 at Mercedes-Benz Stadium, per Georgia athletics ("Bulldogs Blitz Tide 28-7 for SEC Championship") and Fox10 TV. That is last year's result.
