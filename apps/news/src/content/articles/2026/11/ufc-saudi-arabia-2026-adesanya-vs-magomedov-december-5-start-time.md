**UFC Fight Night: Adesanya vs Magomedov, also billed as UFC Saudi Arabia, is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, with the main card at 1 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the prelims at 10 a.m. ET, according to the UFC event page.** The card is at The Venue in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and UFC lists Paramount+ as the US stream. We read the page on Nov. 19; below are the times in nine zones and every bout listed.

## Adesanya vs. Magomedov times in nine time zones

UFC gives times in ET. The other zones are our conversion for Dec. 5, when US clocks are on standard time. Riyadh is three hours ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) all year, and in eastern Australia the card falls on Sunday, Dec. 6.

| Zone, Sat., Dec. 5 | Prelims | Main card |
|---|---|---|
| ET | 10:00 a.m. | 1:00 p.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | 9:00 a.m. | 12:00 p.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 8:00 a.m. | 11:00 a.m. |
| PT | 7:00 a.m. | 10:00 a.m. |
| Alaska | 6:00 a.m. | 9:00 a.m. |
| Hawaii | 5:00 a.m. | 8:00 a.m. |
| United Kingdom (GMT) | 3:00 p.m. | 6:00 p.m. |
| Riyadh | 6:00 p.m. | 9:00 p.m. |
| Sydney (Sun., Dec. 6) | 2:00 a.m. | 5:00 a.m. |

UFC lists the prelims as "10:00 AM" and the main card as "1:00 PM EST". For the Nov. 21 card in Doha, Paramount+ once listed a different main-card time than UFC (see our [UFC 334 story](/news/ufc-334-madison-square-garden-november-14-start-time-gane-hokit-ufc-schedule/)), so these times come from UFC's event page. Sydney is on Australian Eastern Daylight Time, 11 hours ahead of Coordinated Universal Time.

## Which fights are on the Dec. 5 UFC Saudi Arabia card?

UFC lists six bouts on the main card, with Israel Adesanya vs. Shara Magomedov first. The page does not label a co-main event; the second bout listed is Ian Machado Garry vs. Yaroslav Amosov. There is no title fight. The order below is UFC's listed order, not a confirmed walkout order.
