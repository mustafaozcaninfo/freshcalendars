**Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua will fight on Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, at Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales, and Netflix says the event will stream live on Netflix globally to all members at no additional cost.** The Press Association (PA), in a report carried by ESPN on Oct. 7, said the ring walk is planned to start at around 1 a.m. in Cardiff, which is about 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Friday by our conversion. Netflix has not yet announced the start time.

## Fury vs Joshua date, venue and who has confirmed it

Netflix's Tudum page, updated Nov. 2, 2026, calls the fight official for Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, at Principality Stadium. Matchroom Boxing's events list shows "Fury vs Joshua: 11 Dec, Principality Stadium, Cardiff, UK". The stadium's own event page says tickets and hospitality packages are on sale now. Mark the night on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Fury vs Joshua ring walk time in each time zone

The planned ring walk is a report, not a final schedule. PA said an application to Cardiff Council confirmed the ring walk is planned to start at around 1 a.m., after a short live music performance, and that the stadium will remain open until 3 a.m. That puts it early on Saturday, Dec. 12 in Cardiff. The United Kingdom is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in December, so the other columns are our conversion from 1 a.m. GMT. Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT) is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus 11 hours, and Central European Time (CET) is UTC plus 1 hour.

| Place | Planned ring walk, about |
|---|---|
| Cardiff (GMT) | 1 a.m. Sat., Dec. 12 |
| Eastern Time (ET) | 8 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Central Time (CT) | 7 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 6 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 5 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Eastern Australia (AEDT) | 12 noon Sat., Dec. 12 |
| Central Europe (CET) | 2 a.m. Sat., Dec. 12 |

Netflix has not announced when the card starts. Treat the table as a planning time for the ring walk and check Netflix for the official start.

## How to watch Fury vs Joshua on Netflix

Netflix says the fight will stream live on Netflix globally to all members at no additional cost. That means it is not pay-per-view, and the same platform carries it in the United States and the United Kingdom. Netflix says to check back for the full fight card and start time.
