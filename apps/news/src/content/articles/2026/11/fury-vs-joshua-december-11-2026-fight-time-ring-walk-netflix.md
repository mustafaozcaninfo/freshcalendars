**Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua will fight on Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, at Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales, and Netflix says the event will stream live on Netflix globally to all members at no additional cost.** The Press Association (PA), in a report carried by ESPN on Oct. 7, said the ring walk is planned to start at around 1 a.m. in Cardiff, which is about 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Friday by our conversion. Netflix has not yet announced the start time.

## Fury vs Joshua date, venue and who has confirmed it

Netflix's Tudum page, updated Nov. 11, 2026, calls the fight official for Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, at Principality Stadium. Matchroom Boxing's events list shows "Fury vs Joshua: 11 Dec, Principality Stadium, Cardiff, UK". The stadium's own event page lists the night of Dec. 11 and says the bout is scheduled for the early hours of the morning, after a full night of boxing.

## Fury vs Joshua fight card

Netflix's Nov. 11 card page lists four fights: Tyson Fury vs. Anthony Joshua, Deontay Wilder vs. Jai Opetaia, Martin Bakole vs. Solomon Adebayo and Da'Mazion Vanhouter vs. Michael Essomba. Netflix says more fights will be announced. A separate seven-fight show at Cardiff's Vale Arena on Thursday, Dec. 10 is not part of this card. Mark the night on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Fury vs Joshua ring walk time in each time zone

The planned ring walk is a report, not a final schedule. ESPN reported on Nov. 10 that Cardiff Council's licensing subcommittee decides on Nov. 25 whether the stadium may run past its usual 1 a.m. finish, after residents objected. PA said an application to Cardiff Council confirmed the ring walk is planned to start at around 1 a.m., after a short live music performance, and that the stadium will remain open until 3 a.m. That puts it early on Saturday, Dec. 12 in Cardiff. The United Kingdom is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in December, so the other columns are our conversion from 1 a.m. GMT. Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT) is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus 11 hours, and Central European Time (CET) is UTC plus 1 hour.

| Place | Planned ring walk, about |
|---|---|
| Cardiff (GMT) | 1 a.m. Sat., Dec. 12 |
| Eastern Time (ET) | 8 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Central Time (CT) | 7 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 6 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 5 p.m. Fri., Dec. 11 |
| Eastern Australia (AEDT) | 12 noon Sat., Dec. 12 |
| Central Europe (CET) | 2 a.m. Sat., Dec. 12 |

Netflix has not announced when the card starts, and the Nov. 25 license decision could affect the plan. Treat the table as a planning time for the ring walk and check Netflix for the official start.

## How to watch Fury vs Joshua on Netflix

Netflix says the fight will stream live on Netflix globally to all members at no additional cost. That means it is not pay-per-view, and the same platform carries it in the United States and the United Kingdom. Netflix says to check back for more card news and the start time.

## What is not yet announced for Fury vs Joshua

- The start time of the card: Netflix has not announced it as of Nov. 14, 2026.
- The rest of the undercard: Netflix says more fights will be announced after the three it lists.
- The license decision: Cardiff Council's licensing subcommittee rules on Nov. 25.
- Titles at stake: there is no official announcement in the sources used here.
- The number of rounds: not announced.
