**Epic Games says to keep Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026 open for the Fortnite Chapter 7 end event, but it has not announced a start time.** In its Oct. 30, 2026 Treasure Hunters post, Epic wrote: "keep Saturday, December 5, open. Time is on your side… for now. Don't miss an unforgettable Chapter 7 end event." That is 29 days from Nov. 6. Below is the full list of Treasure Hunters dates, the Dec. 5 changes Epic confirmed in a separate Nov. 4 post, and what Epic has and has not said about the time and Chapter 8.

## Fortnite Chapter 7 end event date and what Epic has confirmed

Epic has confirmed the day, Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, in two separate posts. The Oct. 30 Treasure Hunters article asks players to keep Dec. 5 open for "an unforgettable Chapter 7 end event," and the Nov. 4 article, titled "Big Changes to Fortnite Crew Launching December 5," ties the same date to the end of the OG Pass "with a dramatic conclusion at the end of Chapter 1: OG Season X."

What Epic has not posted: the start time, an event name or a Fortnite Chapter 8 release date. Shacknews reports that the Chapter 7 end event plays out once, live, with no replay, so the start time matters for anyone who wants to watch it in game.

## Fortnite Treasure Hunters dates from Nov. 6 to Dec. 5, 2026

The Treasure Hunters season began Nov. 1, 2026 and brings a new captain and crew to Battle Royale each week, Epic says, led by the Straw Hats and A$AP Rocky, then Lil Uzi Vert, Cap'n Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto. ## What time was the A$AP Rocky Shop drop in each U.S. time zone?

The one Treasure Hunters drop Epic gave an hour for, A$AP Rocky on Nov. 5, was at 7 p.m. ET. Because the United States returned to standard time on Nov. 1, Eastern Time is five hours behind Coordinated Universal Time. The table converts 7 p.m. ET across the time zones.

| Time zone | 7 p.m. Eastern Time |
|---|---|
| Eastern Time (ET) | 7 p.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | 6 p.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 5 p.m. |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 4 p.m. |
| Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) | Midnight, the next day |

Epic's last live event with a posted time, in June 2026, also began at 7 p.m. ET, and in 2025 the Chapter 6 finale "Zero Hour" ran at 2 p.m. ET on Nov. 29, per esports.gg and Beebom. Those are past events, labeled as such; none of them sets the Dec. 5, 2026 time.

## What changes in Fortnite on Dec. 5, 2026

Epic's Nov. 4, 2026 post, "Big Changes to Fortnite Crew Launching December 5," lists four changes on the same day as the end event.

| Change | What Epic says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fortnite Crew update | "Starting December 5, Fortnite Crew is getting an update"; the price stays $11.99 United States dollar (USD) | [Epic](https://www.fortnite.com/news/big-changes-to-fortnite-crew-launching-december-5): "Price for Fortnite Crew will stay $11.99 USD" |
| OG Pass ends | Ends "with a dramatic conclusion at the end of Chapter 1: OG Season X"; "The Next Era of Fortnite OG drops in late 2027" | [Epic](https://www.fortnite.com/news/big-changes-to-fortnite-crew-launching-december-5): "OG Pass will end on December 5" |
| Crew Pack Outfits end | "Crew Pack Outfits are ending, and the final one will be December's pack" | [Epic](https://www.fortnite.com/news/big-changes-to-fortnite-crew-launching-december-5) |
| Resubscribe window | Resubscribe between Dec. 6 and Dec. 31, 2026 11:59 p.m. ET to keep your starting tier | [Epic](https://www.fortnite.com/news/big-changes-to-fortnite-crew-launching-december-5): "Resubscribe between December 6 and December 31, 2026 11:59 PM ET" |

Epic says a previously subscribed player who resubscribes in that window gets a starting tier that reflects their most recent continuous subscription period over the past 12 months.

Leaked dates circulate online, but leaks are not confirmed, so we do not print them. We will update this story when Epic posts the end event time or a Chapter 8 date.
