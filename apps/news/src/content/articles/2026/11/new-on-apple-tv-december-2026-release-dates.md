## When are the Apple TV finales in December 2026?

Two series end on Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25: "12 12 12" and "Nocturne." Apple TV Press says "12 12 12" runs "one episode weekly until December 25, 2026" after a two-episode premiere on Nov. 13, and "Nocturne" runs "every Friday through December 25, 2026" after its Oct. 30 debut with two episodes. Our [Nocturne episode schedule](/news/nocturne-apple-tv-release-date-episode-schedule-october-30/) covers that series date by date.

"Stick" season 2 ends five days later. Apple TV Press said the 10-episode season premiered Wednesday, Nov. 4 with two episodes, "followed by one episode weekly until December 30, 2026." Our [November 2026 Apple TV list](/news/new-on-apple-tv-november-2026-release-dates/) has the earlier episode dates.

## When is Bad Monkey season 2 on Apple TV?

Apple TV Press, in its Aug. 19, 2026 release, said the season "will make its global debut on Apple TV with one episode on Wednesday, December 2, 2026, followed by one new episode every Wednesday through February 3, 2027." Counting Dec. 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30, then Jan. 6, 13, 20, 27 and Feb. 3, gives ten Wednesdays, which matches the 10 episodes Apple states.

## What are the Apple TV holiday and sports dates in December?

"Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift" is "debuting Friday, December 4," according to the Sept. 24, 2026 Apple TV Press release "There's no place like Apple TV for the holidays." The same release says "A Charlie Brown Christmas" will "stream for free Saturday, December 12 to Sunday, December 13." Our [Charlie Brown Christmas guide](/news/charlie-brown-christmas-2026-free-apple-tv-december-12-13/) has the details.

Apple's Major League Soccer page says Apple TV has "the MLS Cup Final on December 18th." The kickoff time is in our [MLS Cup schedule story](/news/mls-cup-playoffs-2026-schedule-dates-mls-cup-december-18/), which cites the league source.

## What is coming to Apple TV after December 2026?

"Neuromancer" premieres Friday, Jan. 22, 2027 with two episodes, then streams every Friday through Mar. 19, 2027, according to the July 25, 2026 Apple TV Press release.

## How much is Apple TV in December 2026?

Apple's Apple TV page, read Nov. 20, 2026, says "A monthly subscription is just $14.99 per month." The page also showed a limited-time offer of six months at $6.99 per month that ends Nov. 30.
