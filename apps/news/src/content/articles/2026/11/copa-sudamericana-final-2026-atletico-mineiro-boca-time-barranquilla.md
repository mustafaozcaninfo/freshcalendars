**The Copa Sudamericana final is Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, with kickoff at 5:30 p.m. ET (5:30 p.m. Colombia time) at the Estadio Metropolitano Roberto Meléndez in Barranquilla, according to the South American Football Confederation (CONMEBOL).** Brazil's Atlético Mineiro and Argentina's Boca Juniors are the finalists, as CONMEBOL's news pages describe them. CONMEBOL's Barranquilla page gives the time as "17:30 hrs. (UTC -5)".

Colombia does not change its clocks, and the United States is back on standard time, so Eastern Time (ET) and Colombia are both UTC-5 on Nov. 21. The final is a single match in Barranquilla, Colombia, per the same page. The page does not list a TV or streaming channel, and none had been announced on the broadcasters' pages when this story was written, so the printable shows the channel as to be announced (TBA).

## Who plays in the 2026 Copa Sudamericana final?

CONMEBOL's news pages name Atlético Mineiro (Brazil) and Boca Juniors (Argentina) as the finalists: one headline calls Atlético Mineiro "finalista", and its interview with Boca's Leandro Paredes says Boca faces Atlético Mineiro in the final on Nov. 21 in Barranquilla ("enfrente a Atlético Mineiro por la Final"). CONMEBOL's Barranquilla page covers ticket categories, the stadium and the date. Ticket sales run through CONMEBOL's official platform.
