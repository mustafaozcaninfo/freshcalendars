Below are the organizer's preparation calls, last year's theme, a live countdown and a free planner sheet.

## When is World Pneumonia Day and who sponsors it?

The Every Breath Counts Coalition's page describes the day as "A global day of action: 12 November." The Bahrain Ministry of Health's event page says "Every year on November 12th, the world observes World Pneumonia Day" and that it is "sponsored by the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Global Coalition Against Child Pneumonia." That page lists Nov. 12, 2026 as both start and end date. Nov. 12, 2026 is a Thursday. For the rest of the month, see the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## What is the 2026 World Pneumonia Day theme?

The coalition's page says: "In 2026, the theme is A New Era of Pneumonia Control." Last year's theme was different, according to two outlets that covered the 2025 day.

| Year | Theme | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | A New Era of Pneumonia Control | Every Breath Counts Coalition |
| 2025 | Child Survival | Business Standard; a hospital group's page |

Business Standard is a news outlet and the other page is a hospital group's, so the 2025 row is background and not an official statement. No government or WHO page checked gives a 2026 theme.

## Which organizer calls are listed for World Pneumonia Day 2026?

The coalition's page lists three preparation calls, with times in Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) and no year printed next to them. The Nov. 10 call is the last on the list.

| Call date | Time listed |
|---|---|
| Oct. 13 | 10:00 EDT |
| Oct. 27 | 6:00 EDT |
| Nov. 10 | 10:00 EDT |

The page does not list other events. A local event may exist that the organizer does not show, so a hospital or health department near you is the place to ask.
