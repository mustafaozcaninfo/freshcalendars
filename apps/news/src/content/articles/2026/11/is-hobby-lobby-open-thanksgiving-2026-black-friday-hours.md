**Hobby Lobby is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and opens the next morning at 8 a.m., according to the Hobby Lobby Newsroom's "Holiday Hours" list dated Nov. 2, 2026.** The list covers all stores. Friday and Saturday run 8 a.m. to 9 p.m., and the stores close at 5:30 p.m. on Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day. Christmas Day is closed, and so is every Sunday.

## Hobby Lobby holiday hours 2026, Nov. 26 to Jan. 1

The newsroom list gives the day names; the weekdays and the comparison with regular hours are computed here. The regular hours come from Hobby Lobby's Our Story page: "Store hours are Monday through Saturday from 9:00am to 8:00pm." The newsroom page does not say "local time" or use the name Black Friday; the label for Friday is ours.

| Date | Day | Hobby Lobby hours | Against regular hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving Day | Closed | Closed on a regular weekday |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Friday after Thanksgiving (Black Friday) | 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. | Opens 1 hour earlier, closes 1 hour later |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | Saturday after Thanksgiving | 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. | Opens 1 hour earlier, closes 1 hour later |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Regular Sunday | Closed | Closed every Sunday |
| Sun., Dec. 6, 13, 20 and 27 | Regular Sundays | Closed | Closed every Sunday |
| Thu., Dec. 24 | Christmas Eve | 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. | Same opening, closes 2.5 hours early |
| Fri., Dec. 25 | Christmas Day | Closed | Closed on a regular weekday |
| Thu., Dec. 31 | New Year's Eve | 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. | Same opening, closes 2.5 hours early |
| Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | New Year's Day | 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. | Same opening, closes 2.5 hours early |

Hobby Lobby's page words these as "Friday after Thanksgiving – 8 a.m. to 9 p.m." and "Christmas Eve – 9 a.m. – 5:30 p.m." The company's Our Story page says it has more than 1,000 stores in 48 states, and the holiday list applies to all of them.

## Is Hobby Lobby open on Sundays before Christmas?

No. Our Story says: "All Hobby Lobby stores are closed on Sunday." That puts the last Sundays before Christmas, Dec. 6, 13 and 20, outside the shopping calendar, along with Nov. 29 and Dec. 27. The last full days before Christmas Eve are Mon., Dec. 21 through Wed., Dec. 23, and those days run on the regular Monday through Saturday hours. Plan craft and gift-wrapping supply trips for Saturday, Dec. 19, or the three weekdays after it.

## Hobby Lobby Black Friday week ad

The Hobby Lobby weekly ad page shows an ad that runs only through Nov. 14-15, 2026. The Black Friday week ad is not yet posted, so no sale prices are listed here. Check the [Hobby Lobby weekly ad](https://www.hobbylobby.com/weekly-ad) before Friday, Nov. 27.

## Are the 2026 hours different from 2025?

No. In its Nov. 3, 2025 newsroom post, "Hobby Lobby 2025 Holiday Hours for all stores are:" Thanksgiving closed, Friday and Saturday after Thanksgiving 8 a.m. to 9 p.m., Christmas Eve 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Christmas Day closed, and New Year's Eve and New Year's Day 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. The 2026 list repeats each entry.
