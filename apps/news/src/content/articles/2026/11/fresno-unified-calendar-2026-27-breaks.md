**Fresno Unified School District (FUSD) students are off Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 for Thanksgiving, from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Friday, Jan. 8, 2027 for winter break, and from Monday, March 22 to Monday, March 29, 2027 for spring break**, according to the "Academic Calendar 2026 – 2027" PDF linked from the district's "Academic Calendar" page. School ends Thursday, June 10, 2027.

The PDF says the board approved it on Feb. 25, 2026 and that it was revised and approved on March 11, 2026. This story adds our arithmetic from the district's dates: breaks at a glance with weekdays off, every student day off, student days by month, the stretches between days off, and how Fresno compares with Los Angeles and San Diego. A printable school-year calendar is at the bottom of the page.

## Fresno Unified breaks at a glance

Weekday counts are our arithmetic from the district's dates. The district prints no return days, so those are our reading: the next weekday the calendar does not list as off.

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | Students return (our reading) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 8, 2027 | 15 | Mon., Jan. 11, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon., March 22 to Mon., March 29, 2027 | 6 | Tue., March 30, 2027 |

Winter break spans three school weeks and includes Dec. 25 and Jan. 1. Spring break is Monday to the next Monday, so students are out 10 days in a row, from Saturday, March 20 through Monday, March 29. Our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) compares other districts, and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the three weeks.

## How many Fresno Unified student days fall in each month?

Counting every weekday between Aug. 17, 2026 and June 10, 2027, December has the most weekdays off, 9, because winter break takes 9 of its weekdays (Dec. 21-25 and Dec. 28-31). March has 7 and November has 6. In all, 34 weekdays are off and 180 are student days, the number the district prints.

| Month | Weekdays off | Student days |
|---|---|---|
| August 2026 (from Aug. 17) | 0 | 11 |
| September 2026 | 1 | 21 |
| October 2026 | 1 | 21 |
| November 2026 | 6 | 15 |
| December 2026 | 9 | 14 |
| January 2027 | 7 | 14 |
| February 2027 | 2 | 18 |
| March 2027 | 7 | 16 |
| April 2027 | 0 | 22 |
| May 2027 | 1 | 20 |
| June 2027 (to June 10) | 0 | 8 |
| Total | 34 | 180 |

January also has 7: Jan. 1 and Jan. 4-8 from winter break, plus Jan. 18. The [March 2027 printable calendar](/march-calendars.html) shows the March 8 buyback day and spring break.

Our count also matches the district's printed figures. The calendar prints reporting periods that end Oct. 16 (43 days), Dec. 18 (39 days), March 19 (46 days) and June 10 (52 days). Counting the student days between those dates gives 43, 39, 46 and 52. The first semester ends Dec. 18 at 82 days, and the second ends June 10 at 98.

## Does Fresno Unified close for Cesar Chavez Day?

The calendar does not list Cesar Chavez Day, Wednesday, March 31, 2027, as a holiday, and it shows no day off that day. The district's vacation list names Labor Day, Veteran's Day, Thanksgiving, Winter Break, M.L. King, Jr. Day, Lincoln's Birthday, Presidents' Day, Spring Break and Memorial Day for the school year. Our count supports the reading: the 180 student days work out only when March 31 is a school day. Ask the district if you need to confirm it.

## How does the Fresno Unified calendar compare with Los Angeles and San Diego?

Los Angeles Unified School District (LAUSD) dates come from our [LAUSD calendar story](/news/lausd-calendar-2026-27-winter-break-spring-break/), which cites the district's own 2026-27 instructional calendar. San Diego dates come from the district's "2026-27 Academic Calendar," adopted June 24, 2025, and our [San Diego Unified calendar story](/news/san-diego-unified-calendar-2026-27-breaks/).

| | Fresno Unified | Los Angeles Unified | San Diego Unified |
|---|---|---|---|
| First day | Mon., Aug. 17, 2026 | Wed., Aug. 12, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 10, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21 to Jan. 8 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 8 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 4 |

| Spring break | March 22-29 | March 22-26 | March 29 to April 2 |
| Last day | Thu., June 10, 2027 | Fri., June 4, 2027 | Wed., May 26, 2027 |

Fresno starts latest and ends latest of the three: its last day is 6 days after Los Angeles's and 15 days after San Diego's. Its winter break matches Los Angeles's dates and runs four weekdays longer than San Diego's. San Diego's spring week starts a week after the Fresno and Los Angeles weeks. Fresno's spring break is the only one of the three with six weekdays.
