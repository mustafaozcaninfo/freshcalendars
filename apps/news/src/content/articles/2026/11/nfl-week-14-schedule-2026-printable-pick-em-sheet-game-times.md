## Which teams are on bye in NFL Week 14?

NFL.com lists two teams on bye: the Arizona Cardinals and the Dallas Cowboys. The other 30 teams play, in 15 games.

## Which networks show the NFL Week 14 games?

Prime Video has Thursday's Vikings at Patriots. At 1 p.m. on Sunday, NFL.com lists CBS for Saints at Panthers, Falcons at Browns, Bears at Dolphins, Broncos at Jets and Texans at Commanders, and FOX for Buccaneers at Ravens, Titans at Lions and Colts at Eagles. In the late afternoon, CBS has Chargers at Raiders at 4:05 p.m., and FOX has Chiefs at Bengals, Giants at Seahawks and Rams at 49ers at 4:25 p.m. Bills at Packers is on NBC on Sunday night, and Steelers at Jaguars is on ESPN on Monday night.

## How many days of NFL games are in Week 14?

Three. NFL.com lists games on Thursday, Sunday and Monday, with none on Friday, Dec. 11 or Saturday, Dec. 12. Sunday carries 13 of the 15 games.

## Can the NFL Week 14 times change?

NFL.com says dates and times for the designated Week 16 matchups will be announced no later than immediately after Week 14. Under the league's [flexible scheduling procedures](https://www.nfl.com/legal/flexible-scheduling-procedures), Sunday games can be moved, so check NFL.com the week of the game before you plan around a kickoff.
