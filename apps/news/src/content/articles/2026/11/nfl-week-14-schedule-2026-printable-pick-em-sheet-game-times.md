## Which teams are on bye in NFL Week 14?

The Arizona Cardinals and Dallas Cowboys sit out Week 14, per NFL.com. That leaves 30 teams in 15 games.

## Which networks show the NFL Week 14 games?

Thursday's Vikings at Patriots is on Prime Video. Sunday's 1 p.m. window has CBS showing Saints at Panthers, Falcons at Browns, Bears at Dolphins, Broncos at Jets and Texans at Commanders, while FOX shows Buccaneers at Ravens, Titans at Lions and Colts at Eagles. After that, CBS has Chargers at Raiders at 4:05 p.m., and FOX has Chiefs at Bengals, Giants at Seahawks and Rams at 49ers at 4:25 p.m. NBC has Bills at Packers on Sunday night and ESPN has Steelers at Jaguars on Monday night.

## How many days of NFL games are in Week 14?

Three days have games: Thursday, Sunday and Monday, per NFL.com. Friday, Dec. 11 and Saturday, Dec. 12 are empty, and 13 of the 15 games are on Sunday.

## Can the NFL Week 14 times change?

NFL.com says dates and times for the designated Week 16 matchups will be announced no later than immediately after Week 14. Under the league's [flexible scheduling procedures](https://www.nfl.com/legal/flexible-scheduling-procedures), Sunday games can be moved, so check NFL.com the week of the game before you plan around a kickoff.

## Week 14 rest days: Vikings and Patriots on four days, four teams back from bye

The Vikings at Patriots game on Thursday, Dec. 10 has the week's shortest gap. Both teams played on Sunday, Dec. 6, so they have four days. The Seattle Seahawks played Monday, Dec. 7 and have six before their Dec. 13 game. The Kansas City Chiefs and Los Angeles Rams played Thursday, Dec. 3 and wait 10 days. The New York Jets, Indianapolis Colts, Baltimore Ravens and Las Vegas Raiders were on bye in Week 13 and last played on Nov. 29, 14 days before Dec. 13.

## How many Week 14 games share the 1 p.m. window?

Eight of the 15 games open together at 1 p.m. ET on Sunday, Dec. 13. No other window that week holds as many: the afternoon then thins to Chargers at Raiders at 4:05 p.m. ET and three games at 4:25 p.m. ET, and Bills at Packers at 8:20 p.m. ET closes Sunday.
