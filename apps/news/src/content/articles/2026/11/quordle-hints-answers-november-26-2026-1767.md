## Which letters Sharp, Copse, Rebut and Fleck share

Each word uses five different letters, so none doubles a tile. The starting letters are S, C, R and F and the endings are P, E, T and K, all different. E is the one letter in three words: Copse, Rebut and Fleck. Sharp is the only word without it.

| Letter | Slots used | Words |
|---|---|---|
| E | 3 | Copse, Rebut, Fleck |
| S, P, R, C | 2 each | S and P in Sharp and Copse; R in Sharp and Rebut; C in Copse and Fleck |
| H, A, O, B, U, T, F, L, K | 1 each | one word each |

Those rows add up to 20 slots and 14 distinct letters. Copse and Rebut hold two vowels each, Sharp and Fleck one each, so the vowel count is six. Sharp and Copse are the closest pair, sharing S and P, and Copse and Fleck share C and E. Sharp and Fleck share nothing at all.

That is why Tares scores best overall without one green: S, R and E all sit in two or three words, but never in the spot Tares tries.
