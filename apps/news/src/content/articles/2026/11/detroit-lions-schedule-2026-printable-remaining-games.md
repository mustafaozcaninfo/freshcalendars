## When could a Lions game time still change?

The NFL's flexible scheduling procedures let it move games into Sunday Night Football and Monday Night Football (MNF) slots late in the season. Per the procedures, the Sunday night flex "may be used up to twice between Weeks 5-10, and any week during Weeks 11-17." Monday night flex applies "any week in Weeks 12-17," and Thursday flex "up to twice between Weeks 13-17."

Notice for Sunday games in Weeks 5-13 and Monday games in Weeks 12-17 comes "no later than 12 days in advance of the game." For Sunday games in Weeks 14-17 the NFL says "generally ... no later than 6 days prior to the game." We subtracted those windows from each Lions date. These are the latest notice dates, not dates a change will happen:

| Week | Game date | Latest flex notice | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Sun., Nov. 22 | Tue., Nov. 10 (passed) | 12 days |
| 13 | Sun., Dec. 6 | Tue., Nov. 24 | 12 days |
| 14 | Sun., Dec. 13 | Mon., Dec. 7 | 6 days, generally |
| 15 | Sun., Dec. 20 | Mon., Dec. 14 | 6 days, generally |
| 16 | Mon., Dec. 28 | Wed., Dec. 16 | 12 days |
| 17 | Sun., Jan. 3, 2027 | Mon., Dec. 28 | 6 days, generally |

Week 11's deadline has passed, so the Buccaneers game stays at 1 p.m. ET on FOX as posted. The NFL's Thursday flex covers Weeks 13 to 17, so the Week 12 Thanksgiving game sits outside it. The schedule above is as posted on Nov. 16, 2026.
