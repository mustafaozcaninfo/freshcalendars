**The New York Times (NYT) Crossword for Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, is by Rafael Musa and edited by Will Shortz: 70 clues, 36 Across and 34 Down, in a 15-by-15 grid.** The day after Thanksgiving brings a roomier grid than Thursday's, with ten long entries and two clues that play on a sport. Every clue has a hint behind a tap; the answers close the page.

## Two sports puns and a clue that counts its own letters

Two of the 70 clues end in "?", the usual flag for wordplay. Each trick is explained here without its answer.

- **23-Across, "Contact sport?" (4 letters).** The sport is not the answer. In this sport a point comes from touching the opponent, so "contact" is the scoring act, and the answer is the thing held to make it. It begins with E.
- **40-Across, "Basketball time-outs?" (10 letters).** A time-out usually means a short stop in play. Here it means a long break, the months when no games are played. The answer is plural and two words run together.
- **44-Across, "One is seen four times in this clue" (3 letters).** No question mark, but the clue points at itself. Count one small letter in the clue's own words, and the mark that sits above it is the answer.

## Reading the grid: lengths, black squares and Thursday compared

The grid is 225 squares: 31 black, a share of 13.8 percent, and 194 holding a letter. Thursday's [Nov. 26 Crossword page](/news/nyt-crossword-answers-november-26-2026/) described 43 black squares (19.1 percent), 182 letter squares and 76 clues, so Friday has 12 fewer black squares, 12 more letter squares and 6 fewer clues. The ten entries of 9 or 10 letters hold 84 of the 194 letter squares once the crossings are counted a single time, 43.3 percent, against 46 squares and 25.3 percent for Thursday's four long entries. They come in half-turn pairs:

- 19-Across (9 letters, row 4, columns 1 to 9) mirrors 54-Across (row 12, columns 7 to 15).
- 31-Across (10 letters, row 7, columns 1 to 10) mirrors 40-Across (row 9, columns 6 to 15).
- 5-Down (10 letters, column 6, rows 1 to 10) mirrors 28-Down (column 10, rows 6 to 15).

- 33-Down (9 letters, column 3, rows 7 to 15) mirrors 11-Down (column 13, rows 1 to 9).

On the right edge, 11-Down and 12-Down stand side by side from row 1 to row 9. Both answer clues printed as spoken remarks, a smug reply and a casual tag after an opinion, and each answer is written the way it is said. They are the conversational entries of the day. Their mirror images, 32-Down and 33-Down, are in the left columns and start in row 7.
