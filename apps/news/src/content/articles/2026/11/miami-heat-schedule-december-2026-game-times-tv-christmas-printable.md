**The Miami Heat open December 2026 at home against the Atlanta Hawks on Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The date to mark is Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, when the Heat play at the Boston Celtics at 2:30 p.m. ET on ABC and ESPN.

The NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF is dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." It gives each game's local time and its ET time. The table below lists every Heat game in December, adds Central Time (CT) for the road games played in that zone, and a printable of the same games is at the end.

## Heat games in December 2026, by date

The schedule lists 13 Heat games in December: eight at home at Kaseya Center in Miami, per NBA.com's arena list, and five on the road. Weekdays are as printed in the NBA's PDF.

## Heat Christmas game 2026 at Boston

The Heat play at the Boston Celtics on Friday, Dec. 25, at 2:30 p.m. ET, on ABC and ESPN, according to the NBA's schedule. NBA.com's Christmas Day release lists it as "Miami Heat vs. Boston Celtics" at the same time on the same networks. Because Boston is in the Eastern time zone, ET is also the local time at TD Garden. ## Heat schedule gap from Dec. 4 to Dec. 11

The NBA's August schedule lists no regular-season games for any team from Friday, Dec. 4, through Friday, Dec. 11. For Miami, that means a nine-day gap between the Dec. 3 game against Washington and the Dec. 12 game against New York, which is on ABC. The window is the Emirates NBA Cup knockout stage.

NBA.com's Aug. 12, 2026 Group Play release sets the knockout rounds: the quarterfinals on Friday, Dec. 4, and Saturday, Dec. 5, the semifinals on Tuesday, Dec. 8, and/or Wednesday, Dec. 9, and the final on Friday, Dec. 11, at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Miami is in East Group B, and its last group games are Nov. 25 at Philadelphia (5 p.m. ET) and Nov. 27 at New York (7 p.m. ET). Whether Miami reaches the knockouts is not known yet, so the Dec. 4 to Dec. 11 window may hold no Heat game or a Cup game that the regular-season schedule does not list. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round.

## Back-to-back nights and the Chicago stretch

Counting from the NBA's table, the Heat play on consecutive nights once in December: Sunday, Dec. 20, against Sacramento at 6 p.m. ET and Monday, Dec. 21, against Chicago at 7:30 p.m. ET, both at home.

Chicago appears three times in the month. The Heat visit the Bulls on Monday, Dec. 14, at 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. CT), then host Chicago on Dec. 21 and again on Wednesday, Dec. 23, both at 7:30 p.m. ET. The two home games are two nights apart, so they are not back to back. The Christmas game follows two days after the second one, on Friday, Dec. 25, and the road games are not grouped in one trip: the Heat go to Chicago and Memphis on Dec. 14 and 16, then to Boston, Dallas and Oklahoma City on Dec. 25, 29 and 31, with a home game against Charlotte on Dec. 27 between.

## National TV for Heat games in December 2026

Four December games carry a national network in the NBA's schedule: ABC against New York on Dec. 12, Prime Video against the Los Angeles Clippers on Dec. 18, ABC and ESPN at Boston on Dec. 25, and NBC and Peacock at Dallas on Dec. 29. The other nine have none listed. The Heat close the year on the road at Oklahoma City on New Year's Eve, Thursday, Dec. 31, at 8 p.m. ET.
