**Waffle (wafflegame.net) puzzle #1764, for Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, starts with 7 of 21 tiles green, 5 yellow and 9 gray.** A clue for each word comes first, then the color count, and the answers wait in the marked last section.

Three of the nine tiles where an across word meets a down word start out of place, which makes them the ones to chase first; the colors and swap rules are on our [Waffle page](/news/topic/waffle/#how-it-works).

## Clues for each of the six words

**Across**

- **Row 1:** starts with W and ends with K. A hard, noisy hit, or to deliver one.
- **Row 3:** starts with I and ends with E. A picture, or the impression a brand leaves with people.
- **Row 5:** starts with T and ends with T. Taking what belongs to someone else.

**Down**

- **Column 1:** starts with W and ends with T. The joint where the hand meets the forearm.
- **Column 3:** starts with A and ends with E. To ease off, the way a storm or a fever does.
- **Column 5:** starts with K and ends with T. Past tense of lowering yourself onto bent legs.

With 7 tiles already home, 14 are left. Nine of them are gray: their letters appear in no word that runs through their square, or only in a spot already filled by a green tile. The remaining five are yellow.

Two words start with no yellow tile at all, and column 1 is one of them. Row 5 is the only word with two. Column 3 is the best placed, with three of its five tiles green.

### The swap route, move by move

The minimum is 10 swaps, found by search and replayed on the starting grid to reach the solved one. Play them in order, because each assumes the earlier ones are done. Swaps 1, 3, 9 and 10 put two tiles home at once; the other six fix one each.

| Swap | Squares | Letters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Row 1, column 2 and row 4, column 1 | S and H |
| 2 | Row 1, column 4 and row 3, column 1 | I and M |
| 3 | Row 1, column 4 and row 3, column 2 | M and C |
| 4 | Row 2, column 1 and row 4, column 3 | T and E |
| 5 | Row 2, column 1 and row 4, column 5 | E and R |
| 6 | Row 2, column 5 and row 3, column 4 | G and F |
| 7 | Row 2, column 5 and row 3, column 5 | F and N |
| 8 | Row 3, column 5 and row 5, column 2 | F and E |
| 9 | Row 4, column 5 and row 5, column 3 | E and L |
| 10 | Row 5, column 2 and row 5, column 4 | F and H |

- **Swap 1:** S drops into row 4, column 1 for Wrist, and H rises to row 1, column 2 for Whack.
- **Swap 2:** I lands at row 3, column 1, starting Image. M takes its place at the top as a temporary stop.
- **Swap 3:** M slides into row 3, column 2 and C goes up to row 1, column 4, which finishes Whack.
- **Swap 4:** T reaches row 4, column 3, the fourth letter of Abate. E steps into row 2, column 1 for a moment.
- **Swap 5:** R takes row 2, column 1 and Wrist is complete. E parks at row 4, column 5.
- **Swap 6:** G goes to row 3, column 4 for Image. F moves up to row 2, column 5.
- **Swap 7:** N takes row 2, column 5 and F moves down to row 3, column 5.
- **Swap 8:** E reaches row 3, column 5, the last letter of Image. F lands in row 5, column 2.
- **Swap 9:** E and L trade, and both are home: L closes the fourth spot of Knelt and E fills the end of Abate.
- **Swap 10:** F and H trade in row 5, and Theft is finished.

### Why the count is 10

Fourteen tiles are out of place, and a loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops. Ten swaps means 4 loops.
