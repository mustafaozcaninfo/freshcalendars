**Washington's Legislature convenes Monday, Jan. 11, 2027, and members can prefile bills from Monday, Dec. 7, 2026, under the state's own statute and chamber rules on the Washington State Legislature site.** Revised Code of Washington (RCW) 44.04.010 says regular sessions are held annually, "commencing on the second Monday of January," read Nov. 12, 2026. A free printable tracker puts the dates in order, and a table below turns last year's cutoffs into 2027 dates.

The 2027 cutoff calendar, the State of the State date and the first-day schedule are not yet announced. Special sessions can follow a regular session; none is announced for 2027.

## Washington legislative session 2027 dates

| Date | Weekday | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dec. 7, 2026 | Monday | Prefiling opens | [House Rule 10](https://leg.wa.gov/about-the-legislature/legislative-procedures/house-rules/?showall=true), [Senate Rule 55](https://leg.wa.gov/about-the-legislature/legislative-procedures/senate-rules/?showall=true) |
| Dec. 20, 2026 | Sunday | Statute deadline for the governor's budget | [RCW 43.88.060](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=43.88.060) |
| Jan. 11, 2027 | Monday | Session convenes | [RCW 44.04.010](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=44.04.010) |
| April 25, 2027 | Sunday | Scheduled last day (105 days), not yet official | [History of session dates](https://leg.wa.gov/media/zabdgzzy/history-of-session-dates_2026.pdf) |

The weekdays are our computation. The Legislature's session-date history lists 2027 as "Convened Jan. 11, 2027, Adjourned April 25, 2027," length 105, and marks both dates "not yet official." Length is "the total number of calendar days from the convening date to the adjournment date, inclusive."

## How long is the 2027 Washington session?

Article II, Section 12 of the Washington Constitution says: "During each odd-numbered year, the regular session shall not be more than one hundred five consecutive days." In even-numbered years the limit is "not more than sixty consecutive days." The history lists the 2025 session as Jan. 13 to April 27, 2025, also 105 days, and a 2028 session of Jan. 10 to March 10, 2028, 60 days, also marked not yet official.

## When can Washington bills be prefiled for 2027?

House Rule 10 lets members prefile "commencing the first Monday in December preceding any regular session." Senate Rule 55 lets holdover members and members-elect prefile bills with the secretary of the Senate "on any day commencing with the first Monday in December preceding any session year." For 2027 that is Monday, Dec. 7, 2026. These are the current rules, the House's adopted Jan. 24, 2025 and the Senate's for the 2025 session; each chamber adopts its rules for 2027 when the session starts.

By statute, the governor's budget is submitted "no later than the twentieth day of December in the year preceding the session," RCW 43.88.060: Dec. 20, 2026, a Sunday.

## Washington cutoff dates by day number: 2025 and 2027

Washington sets its cutoffs by resolution each session. House Concurrent Resolution (HCR) 4401 set the 2025 cutoffs, and session day 1 in 2025 was Jan. 13. The 2027 resolution is not yet adopted, and the Legislature's cutoff-calendar archive lists 2026 as its newest. If 2027 follows the 2025 day numbers, with Jan. 11 as day 1, the dates fall two days earlier in the calendar. This is our computation, not an announcement.

| 2025 cutoff (HCR 4401) | 2025 date | Day | Same day number in 2027 (if it follows 2025) |
|---|---|---|---|
| House-of-origin policy committee reports | Fri., Feb. 21, 2025 | 40 | Fri., Feb. 19, 2027 |
| House-of-origin fiscal committee reports | Fri., Feb. 28, 2025 | 47 | Fri., Feb. 26, 2027 |
| Last day to consider bills in house of origin, 5 p.m. | Wed., March 12, 2025 | 59 | Wed., March 10, 2027 |
| Opposite-house policy committee reports | Wed., April 2, 2025 | 80 | Wed., March 31, 2027 |
| Opposite-house fiscal committee reports | Tue., April 8, 2025 | 86 | Tue., April 6, 2027 |
| Most bills may not be considered after 5 p.m. (exceptions in the resolution) | Wed., April 16, 2025 | 94 | Wed., April 14, 2027 |

Each 2027 date falls on the same weekday as its 2025 cutoff. Day 105 in 2027 is April 25, the scheduled last day.

Committee schedules for 2027 are not yet announced.
