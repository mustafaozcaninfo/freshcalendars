**The Washington Capitals play 14 games in December 2026, five at Capital One Arena and nine on the road, with the first on Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** Vancouver is the opponent in that opener.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Washington, read Nov. 11, 2026. It gives each start in UTC; we converted the times to ET, which is Washington time, and calculated the weekdays. Broadcasters come from the same feed and can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Capitals game times and TV for all 14 December games

Monumental Sports Network is the Capitals' U.S. channel in the feed (the feed lists a second channel, MNMT2, for two games). Sportsnet and Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports are Canadian networks. "Regional channel" is a local outlet the feed lists under a short code, and for the Colorado and Islanders games it includes the home team's broadcasters.

## Which Capitals games are on national TV in December?

The Capitals' national TV schedule, published Aug. 26, 2026, lists Thursday, Dec. 10, at the Seattle Kraken (9:30 p.m., ESPN) and Tuesday, Dec. 15, at the Dallas Stars (9:30 p.m., TNT). It says the season's broadcast schedule "includes eight games on TNT, four games on ESPN and two games on ABC." The Kraken's release the same day moved the Dec. 10 start from 6 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. Pacific Time, which is 9:30 p.m. ET. The Dec. 15 game is also on truTV and HBO Max in the feed.

## The six-game western road trip

The feed shows the Capitals away for six games in a row, Dec. 3 to Dec. 15, with one stop in each of six cities:

- Thu., Dec. 3: Edmonton, 8 p.m. ET
- Sat., Dec. 5: Calgary, 3:30 p.m. ET
- Mon., Dec. 7: Vancouver, 9 p.m. ET
- Thu., Dec. 10: Seattle, 9:30 p.m. ET
- Sat., Dec. 12: Denver, 9 p.m. ET
- Tue., Dec. 15: Dallas, 9:30 p.m. ET

Five of the six starts are at 8 p.m. ET or later. The only afternoon start is Calgary. Washington returns home Thursday, Dec. 17, against Detroit. Our counts from the feed also show the Capitals play Edmonton twice in the month, away on Dec. 3 and at home on Dec. 28, and play the Maple Leafs on Dec. 21, covered in our [Maple Leafs December schedule](/news/toronto-maple-leafs-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/) and the [Oilers December schedule](/news/edmonton-oilers-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## Capitals dates around Christmas and New Year's Eve

The Capitals have no game from Dec. 22 to Dec. 25. They play at home Saturday, Dec. 26, at 8 p.m. ET and Monday, Dec. 28, at 7 p.m. ET, then travel for two afternoon games: Wednesday, Dec. 30, at the New York Islanders at 4 p.m. and Thursday, Dec. 31, at the Columbus Blue Jackets at 2 p.m. The Capitals' July release confirms both of those times. Another U.S. team's month is in our [Penguins December schedule](/news/pittsburgh-penguins-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## When do the Capitals play after December?

The first January game is Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027, Ottawa at Washington, 7 p.m. ET at Capital One Arena, per the NHL feed for January. Tickets are listed on the team's [NHL.com tickets page](https://www.nhl.com/capitals/tickets); prices are not part of the schedule feed.
