## When could a Giants game time still change?

Under the NFL's flexible scheduling procedures, Sunday Night Football (SNF) can be flexed in any week of Weeks 11 to 17, and Monday Night Football in Weeks 12 to 17. Thursday games can be flexed up to twice between Weeks 13 and 17. A Sunday game in Weeks 5 to 13 and a Monday game in Weeks 12 to 17 are decided no later than 12 days before the game. For Sunday games in Weeks 14 to 17 the notice is generally no later than 6 days before.
