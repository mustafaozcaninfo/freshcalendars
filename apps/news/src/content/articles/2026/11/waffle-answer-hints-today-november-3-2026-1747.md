## How Waffle works

The board is a five-by-five grid with four holes, so 21 letters are in play. Three words run across (rows 1, 3 and 5) and three run down (columns 1, 3 and 5). You swap any two letters, and you have 15 swaps to finish. Per the game's own rules page:

| Tile color | What it means |
|---|---|
| Green | The letter is in the right place. |
| Yellow | The letter is in that row or column, but in a different spot. |
| Gray | The letter is not in that row or column. |

A letter on a corner can be yellow because of either of the two words that meet there. You earn a star for every swap left once the Waffle is solved, and the game says every Waffle can be solved in a minimum of 10 swaps.

## A solving tip for today

Start with the corners, because each one serves two words. In today's starting grid all four corners are already in the right place, and so is the center letter, along with two more tiles on the edges, so seven tiles start green and the other 14 have to move. Count the letters in the solution: one letter appears three times and five letters appear twice, so a tile that looks wrong may belong in a different spot on the same line. Once a row or column is fully green, move on; a swap that turns two tiles green is worth more than one that turns one. Count your swaps as you go: with 15 available and 10 as the minimum, you can afford five detours before stars are at risk.
