**Cyber Monday 2026 falls on Monday, Nov. 30, Amazon says, and Walmart's Cyber Monday event opens on Walmart.com at 12:01 a.m. ET that day and runs through Wednesday, Dec. 2, according to Walmart's Oct. 26, 2026 release.** Amazon's event started earlier and ends the same day; Kohl's Cyber Deals run Nov. 29 and 30; Best Buy's calendar lists Cyber Week Deals Dec. 1 to 6. Below are the start times in Eastern Time (ET) and Pacific Time (PT), what Target and other stores have not said, and a free sale-window printable. The date itself is on [when is Cyber Monday](/when-is/cyber-monday/).

The ET and PT columns are converted from each store's own time; Amazon's 12:01 a.m. on Nov. 19 is Pacific Standard Time (PST) in its frequently asked questions page. Walmart's 12:01 a.m. ET start on Nov. 30 is 9:01 p.m. PT on Sunday, Nov. 29, so West Coast shoppers see it the evening before.

## What does Amazon say about Cyber Monday 2026?

Amazon's frequently asked questions page, published Nov. 4 and updated Nov. 10, 2026, says "Cyber Monday 2026 falls on November 30" and that Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday "kick off at 12:01 a.m. PST on November 19 and run through November 30." Its Nov. 9 release says deals drop twice on Black Friday and twice on Cyber Monday, at midnight and 8 a.m. PT, which is 3 a.m. and 11 a.m. ET. Amazon gives no hour for the end of the event on Nov. 30. Amazon's earlier Black Friday dates are in our [Amazon Black Friday 2026 guide](/news/amazon-black-friday-2026-sale-dates-when-does-it-start/).

## Walmart.com Cyber Monday 2026: Nov. 30 to Dec. 2

Walmart's release, "Walmart Unwraps Three Major Holiday Shopping Events," lists "November 30 - December 2: Cyber Monday only on Walmart.com beginning at 12:01 a.m. ET." The other two events are Early Black Friday, Nov. 12 to 15, and Black Friday, Nov. 23 to 29. Both open on Walmart.com at 12:01 a.m. ET, with stores following at 6 a.m. local time.

Walmart+ early access is named only for the first two events, from 12:01 a.m. ET on Nov. 12 and Nov. 23. The release names no Walmart+ early access for Cyber Monday 2026.

| Walmart Cyber Monday | Start | Walmart+ early access |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Mon., Nov. 30, 12:01 a.m. ET | Not given |
| 2025 | Mon., Dec. 1, 2025, 12:01 a.m. ET | From 7 p.m. ET on Sun., Nov. 30, 2025 |

Walmart's 2025 early access is last year's, per its Nov. 17, 2025 release. Walmart has not said whether it will repeat it. Store hours and openings are in our [Black Friday 2026 store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/).

## Kohl's and Best Buy Cyber Monday dates

Kohl's Oct. 30 release says its "Cyber Deals start Sun., Nov. 29, through Mon., Nov. 30." It also starts Black Friday doorbusters online on Thanksgiving Day, Thu., Nov. 26, at 12:01 a.m. CT. That is 1:01 a.m. ET and 10:01 p.m. PT on Wednesday, Nov. 25.

Best Buy's holiday calendar, published Oct. 27, lists Black Friday deals from Nov. 19 (10 days), then a "Cyber Sunday and Monday Sale Event" and Cyber Week Deals Dec. 1 to 6. The calendar gives no dates for the Sunday and Monday event and no start hour for either. The full list of retailer events is in our [Black Friday 2026 ad tracker](/news/black-friday-2026-ad-tracker-walmart-target-best-buy/).

## Target and other stores: not yet announced

Target's holiday fact sheet, updated Nov. 5, 2026, lists Deal of the Day Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, Early Black Friday deals Nov. 5 to 7, stores open at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday and a Holiday Price Match Guarantee from Nov. 1 to Dec. 24. It has no Cyber Monday 2026 dates.

For comparison, Target's Nov. 28, 2025 release said: "Starting Sunday, Nov. 30 at 2 a.m. CT, Target's Cyber Monday event will run through Monday, Dec. 1," exclusively on Target.com and in the Target app, with select offers available in stores. That is 3 a.m. ET and 1 a.m. PT, and it is a 2025 schedule.

## How big was Cyber Monday last year?

Adobe said consumers spent a total of $14.25 billion online on Cyber Monday 2025, up 7.1% year over year, in its Dec. 2, 2025 report. Cyber Monday 2025 was Dec. 1. That is a 2025 result.
