**Gilbert Public Schools (GPS) in Gilbert, Arizona, has winter break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and spring break from Monday, March 15 through Friday, March 26, 2027**, according to the district's "District Calendar" page and its 2026-2027 School Calendar PDF. Fall break (Oct. 5-16) was also two school weeks, and so is spring break: 10 weekdays each. The first day was Tuesday, July 28, 2026, so what is left of the year is Thanksgiving, winter break, spring break and the last day.

## How many days off does each Gilbert break give?

Counting weekdays from Tuesday, July 28, 2026 to Thursday, May 27, 2027, the days with no school for all students add up to 37, before half days. The count is Labor Day, the two-week breaks, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, winter break, Martin Luther King Jr. Day and Presidents' Day, each counted by weekday.

| Break or holiday | Dates | Weekdays off |
|---|---|---|
| Labor Day | Mon., Sept. 7 | 1 |
| Fall break | Mon., Oct. 5 to Fri., Oct. 16 | 10 |
| Veterans Day | Wed., Nov. 11 | 1 |
| Thanksgiving break | Wed., Nov. 25 to Fri., Nov. 27 | 3 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21 to Fri., Jan. 1 | 10 |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | Mon., Jan. 18 | 1 |
| Presidents' Day | Mon., Feb. 15 | 1 |
| Spring break | Mon., March 15 to Fri., March 26 | 10 |
| Total | | 37 |

Fall and spring breaks run two school weeks, which is five more weekdays than a one-week break. The Thanksgiving break is Wednesday through Friday.

## When is Gilbert Public Schools winter break 2026?

Winter break is "December 21 - January 1, 2027" on the district page. The PDF marks Dec. 21-31 and Jan. 1 as "Winter Break (No School)," which makes 10 weekdays. "School Resumes" on Monday, Jan. 4, 2027. The first semester ends just before the break: a half day for grades 7-12 on Thursday, Dec. 17 and a half day for K-12 on Friday, Dec. 18. See the weeks on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html). Other districts are in the [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

## When is Gilbert Public Schools spring break 2027?

"Spring Break: March 15 - 26, 2027" is two weeks, and the PDF labels it "Spring Holiday." School resumes Monday, March 29. A half-day "Grading Day" falls on Friday, March 12, just before it. Mark the break on the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html). The [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) lists other districts, and [Clark County School District](/news/clark-county-school-district-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) in Las Vegas also starts spring break March 15.

## Last day of school and semester days

The district page lists "Last Day of School: May 27, 2027," and the PDF shows "Last Day for Students, 1/2 day K-12" on Thursday, May 27, then "Last Day for Teachers" on May 28. "Memorial Day (Holiday)" on Monday, May 31 falls after the last day. The PDF lists "Summer School" from June 7 to July 2, 2027. The first day was confirmed in a July 15, 2026 district post: "The first day of school is Tuesday, July 28, 2026."

| Semester | Days (PDF footer) |
