## How does Target's Black Friday 2026 giveaway work?

Target hands out prize redemption tickets to the first 100 individuals in line at each store. The tickets go out beginning approximately one hour before opening and end one hour after opening, or while supplies last. Guests redeem the ticket within the first hour after opening at the in-store event cart, where the GiftCard value ($25, $50 or $100) is revealed. One lucky guest at each store wins a $1,000 GiftCard.

Target stores reopen at 6 a.m. local time on Nov. 27. Computed from Target's wording, ticket handout starts at about 5 a.m. local time and ends at about 7 a.m., and redemption ends at about 7 a.m. as well. Target Circle 360 members also get 24 hours of early access to select deals starting Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 2 a.m. Central Time (CT); that is not a condition of the giveaway.

## How does Target's 2026 giveaway compare with 2025?

In 2025, Target's Nov. 20 release said the first 100 guests in line at stores on Nov. 28 would receive a free limited-edition iridescent holiday tote filled with giveaways. In 2026 the first 100 eligible guests get a GiftCard instead, with a $1,000 prize at each store and a minimum age of 13.

## What does Kohl's give away on Nov. 27 and Nov. 28?

Kohl's stores are closed Thanksgiving Day and reopen at 5 a.m. local time on Friday, Nov. 27. The first 200 shoppers at every Kohl's store nationwide that day receive $5 to $100 in instant Kohl's Cash. On Saturday, Nov. 28, the first 200 shoppers at every store receive a Sephora at Kohl's gift; see our [Kohl's Black Friday guide](/news/kohls-black-friday-2026-dates-early-access-november-6-store-hours/) for its terms.

## What do Academy, JCPenney, Michaels and Lowe's hand out?

Academy's first 100 customers in line at each location get a bonus card valued from $20 to $500 for in-store shopping from Nov. 27 through Nov. 29; its site says stores are closed Thanksgiving Day and reopen on Black Friday at 5 a.m. until 11 p.m. JCPenney opens at 5 a.m. local time and runs "5, 9, 1, 5" surprise giveaways at 5 a.m., 9 a.m., 1 p.m. and 5 p.m. on Nov. 27; a select number of fans entering at opening get a free mini Hogwarts Express snow globe. Michaels starts handing out free advent calendars at checkout on Nov. 27, with 500 per store; one calendar in every store includes a $100 gift card, and one customer wins a $10,000 grand prize. Lowe's Bucket of Gifts Giveaway starts at each store's opening time on Nov. 27 and ends at closing or when the prizes are gone: the first 50 MyLowe's Rewards or MyLowe's Pro Rewards members at each store get a Bucket Prize, the next 100 get a Mini Bucket Prize, and one prize goes to each account. Four of the 50 buckets at each store hold a prize certificate.
