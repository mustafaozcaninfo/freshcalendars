Calendar 2027 splits into four quarters of 90, 91, 92 and 92 days, by Fresh Calendars' count, and the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and the federal government each use their own quarter dates. Quarter 1 (Q1) runs Friday, Jan. 1 to Wednesday, March 31. Below are the start and end of Q1 to Q4, the weekday counts, and where the tax and fiscal-year quarters differ.

## What are the start and end dates of each 2027 quarter?

The table is computed by Fresh Calendars from the Gregorian calendar. "Weekdays" means Monday to Friday and does not remove holidays. The 2027 total is 365 days.

| Quarter | Starts | Ends | Days | Weekdays | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Fri., Jan. 1 | Wed., March 31 | 90 | 64 | 12 weeks and 6 days |
| Q2 | Thu., April 1 | Wed., June 30 | 91 | 65 | 13 weeks exactly |
| Q3 | Thu., July 1 | Thu., Sept. 30 | 92 | 66 | |
| Q4 | Fri., Oct. 1 | Fri., Dec. 31 | 92 | 66 | |

The first half (H1) of the year, Jan. 1 to June 30, has 181 days. The second half (H2), July 1 to Dec. 31, has 184. Q2 is the only quarter of exactly 13 weeks. See the [2027 printable yearly calendar](/printable-yearly-calendar.html) for the full year, or the [April 2027](/april-calendars.html) and [October 2027](/october-calendars.html) pages for the starts of Q2 and Q4.

## When are the IRS quarterly tax dates for 2027?

They are not the calendar quarters. The IRS "About Form 1040-ES" page, last reviewed Sept. 17, 2026, still lists the 2026 form, so the IRS has not yet announced 2027 estimated tax dates. Last year's schedule is the guide. For tax year 2026, IRS Publication 505 gave four payment periods:

| Tax year 2026 period | Due date |
|---|---|
| Jan. 1 to March 31 | April 15, 2026 |
| April 1 to May 31 | June 15, 2026 |
| June 1 to Aug. 31 | Sept. 15, 2026 |
| Sept. 1 to Dec. 31 | Jan. 15, 2027 |

Those periods are 3, 2, 3 and 4 months long. If the IRS keeps the same pattern for 2027, our computation from the weekend-and-holiday rule gives Thursday, April 15, Tuesday, June 15 and Wednesday, Sept. 15, 2027. Jan. 15, 2028 is a Saturday and Monday, Jan. 17, 2028 is Martin Luther King Jr. Day, so the last date would move to Tuesday, Jan. 18, 2028. Treat those as unconfirmed until the 2027 form is posted. Our [2027 IRS tax calendar](/news/tax-calendar-2027-irs-deadlines/) tracks the dates the IRS has published.

## When is the 2027 Form 941 due for employers?

Form 941 is the employer's quarterly return, and it follows calendar quarters. The Instructions for Form 941 say: "Your Form 941 is due by the last day of the month that follows the end of the quarter." The instructions also say that when a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, you may file on the next business day. Our computation for 2027:

| Quarter | Form 941 due |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Fri., April 30 |
| Q2 | Mon., Aug. 2 (July 31 is a Saturday) |
| Q3 | Mon., Nov. 1 (Oct. 31 is a Sunday) |
| Q4 | Mon., Jan. 31, 2028 |

## What quarter is the federal fiscal year in?

The U.S. Code, 31 U.S.C. 1102, says: "The fiscal year of the Treasury begins on October 1 of each year and ends on September 30 of the following year." So federal fiscal year (FY) 2027 runs Oct. 1, 2026 to Sept. 30, 2027, and its first quarter is October to December 2026. By that count, the calendar Q4 of 2027 is the first quarter of FY 2028.
