**The New York Times (NYT) Crossword for Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, was constructed by Peggy Sue Marlin and edited by Will Shortz: a 15-by-15 grid with 74 clues.** Five of those clues point at other entries, so this page explains how the long answers fit together before the hints begin. The answers wait at the end.

## How Wednesday's theme entries connect

Four entries stand out by length: two with 15 letters and two with 12, and all four run Across. They sit in rows 3, 6, 10 and 13 of the grid. The 15-letter entry in row 3 is 17-Across, and the other 15-letter entry, in row 13, is 56-Across. The 12-letter entries are 26-Across in row 6, flush with the right edge, and 43-Across in row 10, flush with the left edge.

The clues show how they relate. The clue for 17-Across reads "56-Across on a store", the clue for 26-Across reads "56-Across on an elevator" and the clue for 43-Across reads "56-Across on an interstate". Each one asks for a message you could find posted in one of those places, and each message bodes ill. The clue for 56-Across, "That spells trouble!", is the revealer: it is the comment that sums up all three.

One more pair is separate from that theme. The clue for 45-Across starts with "With 51-Down", and 51-Down says only "See 45-Across". Both entries have four letters, so a single clue covers an Across and a Down answer that belong together. No other clue in the grid works that way.
