**The Day of the Covenant is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and the Ascension of 'Abdu'l-Bahá is Saturday, Nov. 28, according to the Bahá'í calendar of the Bahá'í International Community at bahai.org.** Each Bahá'í day begins at sunset on the evening before, so the Covenant day starts at about 4:20 to 4:45 p.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 25 and the Ascension at the same hour on Friday, Nov. 27, in the six U.S. and Canadian cities in the table below. A free printable year card for 2027 lists the Fast and every Holy Day.

## When do the Day of the Covenant and the Ascension begin?

bahai.org lists the two days under "Significant Dates" for 183 Bahá'í Era (B.E.), the year that spans 2026 and 2027: Day of the Covenant on 26 November 2026 and Ascension of 'Abdu'l-Bahá on 28 November 2026. It adds: "The day begins at sunset on the eve of each corresponding Gregorian calendar date shown below." The Bahá'ís of Canada say the same: "The Bahá'í day ends and a new one begins at sunset."

We computed sunset for each city from the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) rise, set and transit application programming interface (API), in local standard time. The Covenant day runs from sunset on Wednesday, Nov. 25 to sunset on Thursday, Nov. 26; the Ascension from sunset on Friday, Nov. 27 to sunset on Saturday, Nov. 28.

| City | Covenant day begins, Wed., Nov. 25 | Ascension begins, Fri., Nov. 27 |
|---|---|---|
| New York | 4:31 p.m. ET | 4:31 p.m. ET |
| Toronto | 4:45 p.m. ET | 4:43 p.m. ET |
| Chicago | 4:23 p.m. CT | 4:22 p.m. CT |
| Denver | 4:38 p.m. MT | 4:37 p.m. MT |
| Los Angeles | 4:45 p.m. PT | 4:44 p.m. PT |
| Vancouver | 4:20 p.m. PT | 4:19 p.m. PT |

Local communities set their own gathering times; this page gives the sunset the day starts at, not an event schedule.

## What do the two days commemorate?

The Bahá'ís of Canada describe the Day of the Covenant as "commemorating Bahá'u'lláh's appointment of His son, 'Abdu'l-Bahá." The Bahá'ís of the United States word it as the day that "Commemorates Bahá'u'lláh's appointment of His Son, 'Abdu'l-Bahá, as the Center of His Covenant."

The Ascension marks the passing of 'Abdu'l-Bahá in 1921. The Canadian community's centenary page says he "passed away in Haifa at about 1:00 am on November 28, 1921." That is the hour of his passing; the sources we read do not set an hour for observing the day.

## Is work suspended on the Day of the Covenant and the Ascension?

No, and the two national communities word the rule differently. The Bahá'ís of Canada mark both days with an asterisk: "Work not suspended; Bahá'ís are enjoined to suspend work and attendance at school on all but these Holy Days." The Bahá'ís of the United States write that "Bahá'ís are encouraged to suspend work and school attendance on all Holy Days except the Day of the Covenant" and the Ascension of 'Abdu'l-Bahá. We did not find a UK rule, because the UK community's site was not readable for us.

## Bahá'í holy days from November 2026 through 2027

The dates below come from the Bahá'ís of Canada table for 2026 and 2027; the Fast dates also appear on bahai.org, which lists 183 B.E. and 184 B.E. (2027/28). Weekdays are our calculation. Every day begins at sunset on the evening before the date shown. The last column follows the Canadian note quoted above.

| Holy Day | 2026 | 2027 | Work suspended |
|---|---|---|---|
| Birth of the Báb | Tue., Nov. 10 (past) | Sat., Oct. 30 | Yes |
| Birth of Bahá'u'lláh | Wed., Nov. 11 (past) | Sun., Oct. 31 | Yes |
| Day of the Covenant | Thu., Nov. 26 | Fri., Nov. 26 | No |
| Ascension of 'Abdu'l-Bahá | Sat., Nov. 28 | Sun., Nov. 28 | No |
| Naw-Rúz | Mar. 21 (past) | Sun., Mar. 21 | Yes |
| First Day of Ridván | Apr. 21 (past) | Wed., Apr. 21 | Yes |
| Ninth Day of Ridván | Apr. 29 (past) | Thu., Apr. 29 | Yes |
| Twelfth Day of Ridván | May 2 (past) | Sun., May 2 | Yes |
| Declaration of the Báb | May 24 (past) | Mon., May 24 | Yes |
| Ascension of Bahá'u'lláh | May 29 (past) | Sat., May 29 | Yes |
| Martyrdom of the Báb | July 10 (past) | Sat., July 10 | Yes |

The Fast, which the Bahá'ís of Canada list as 2 to 20 March in both years, runs Tuesday, March 2 to Saturday, March 20, 2027, ending the day before Naw-Rúz.

The two birthdays move because bahai.org places them "on the first and the second day following the occurrence of the eighth new moon after Naw-Rúz." That is why they fell on Nov. 10 and 11 in 2026 and fall on Oct. 30 and 31 in 2027, while the Covenant day and the Ascension keep Nov. 26 and 28.
