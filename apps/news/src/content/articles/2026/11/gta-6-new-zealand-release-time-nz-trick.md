**PlayStation says Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI "releases at midnight local time on November 19, 2026," on PlayStation 5 consoles, and New Zealand's midnight is 6 a.m. ET (3 a.m. PT) on Wednesday, Nov. 18 by our conversion.** That is 18 hours before the 12 a.m. ET Thursday release time that Vice reported on the U.S. PlayStation Store. PlayStation's page prints no single worldwide hour; a midnight that follows the clock gives each place its own moment.

## GTA 6 midnight by place, in ET and PT

New Zealand Daylight Time is UTC+13 and U.S. Eastern Standard Time is UTC-5 (daylight saving time ended Nov. 1, 2026). Make Tech Easier lists New Zealand's midnight as 11:00 UTC on Nov. 18, which matches. Every row below is our conversion of midnight local time on Thursday, Nov. 19.

Vice wrote that the U.S. PlayStation Store lists a release time of "9:00 PM PT on November 18 / 12:00 AM ET on November 19." For the countdown to your own clock, see the [GTA 6 release time by time zone](/news/gta-6-countdown-release-time-by-time-zone/) story.

## What is the GTA 6 New Zealand trick?

Radio New Zealand (RNZ) wrote on Nov. 18 that GTA 6 "will be available at midnight local time on November 19, 2026." It added that for Xbox players elsewhere, "changing their Xbox region could be enough to start playing hours early." That idea is the New Zealand trick: because New Zealand's midnight comes first, a console set to New Zealand might see the game sooner.

Nobody with authority says it works:

- Vice wrote that "the GTA 6 New Zealand method has not been confirmed to work at the time of writing."
- Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft have not confirmed it works.
- Vice also reports that "Sony confirms that an account's country or region cannot be changed after it has been created," which bears on any PlayStation version of the idea.

No steps for changing a region are given here, and the trick is not recommended. Rockstar's own regional release-time page could not be read when this was checked, so a company statement on region changes is not part of this report.

## What is Microsoft's official early hour for Xbox?

Xbox Wire, Microsoft's news site, said on Nov. 10 that GTA VI will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, "starting at 1am ET." Streaming for the game begins at 1 a.m. ET on Nov. 19, which is 12 a.m. CT, 11 p.m. MT and 10 p.m. PT on Wednesday, Nov. 18 by our conversion. Microsoft adds that cloud availability and account eligibility "may vary by market and device" and that the game "must be purchased separately."

Microsoft prints no release hour for Xbox consoles. The cloud time is the only Xbox hour in its post. For platform details, read [GTA 6 on Xbox One and Xbox Cloud Gaming](/news/gta-6-xbox-one-cloud-gaming-ps4-platforms/).

## What is still not announced?

- A single worldwide moment for the release.
- An official release hour for Xbox consoles beyond the 1 a.m. ET cloud streaming start.
- Any confirmation from Rockstar, Sony or Microsoft that a region change unlocks the game early.
