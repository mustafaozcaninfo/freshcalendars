**Ollie's Bargain Outlet closes its stores on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and opens from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Black Friday, Nov. 27, according to the chain's 2026 holiday store hours calendar.** The calendar covers Nov. 26 through Dec. 31, and Ollie's posts it as an image. The full list is written out below, with the Daily Deal Reveals that start Nov. 22.

## Ollie's holiday store hours, Nov. 26 to Dec. 31

Ollie's holiday calendar lists these hours in local store time. Ollie's tells shoppers to use its [store locator](https://www.ollies.com/pages/store-locator) for their own store, so an individual store may differ.

| Date | Store hours |
|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving) | Closed |
| Fri., Nov. 27 (Black Friday) | 5 a.m.-10 p.m. |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 8 a.m.-10 p.m. |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | 9 a.m.-9 p.m. |
| Mon., Nov. 30 to Sat., Dec. 5 | 8 a.m.-10 p.m. daily |
| Sun., Dec. 6 | 9 a.m.-9 p.m. |
| Mon., Dec. 7 to Sat., Dec. 12 | 8 a.m.-10 p.m. daily |
| Sun., Dec. 13 | 9 a.m.-4:30 p.m., then Ollie's Army Night 5-9 p.m. |
| Mon., Dec. 14 to Thu., Dec. 17 | 8 a.m.-10 p.m. daily |
| Fri., Dec. 18 and Sat., Dec. 19 | 8 a.m.-11 p.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 20 | 9 a.m.-9 p.m. |
| Mon., Dec. 21 to Wed., Dec. 23 | 8 a.m.-11 p.m. daily |
| Thu., Dec. 24 (Christmas Eve) | 8 a.m.-6 p.m. |
| Fri., Dec. 25 (Christmas Day) | Closed |
| Sat., Dec. 26 | 8 a.m.-9 p.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 27 | 10 a.m.-7 p.m. |
| Mon., Dec. 28 to Wed., Dec. 30 | 9 a.m.-9 p.m. daily |
| Thu., Dec. 31 (New Year's Eve) | 9 a.m.-6 p.m. |

Regular store hours return Jan. 1, the calendar says: Monday to Saturday 9 a.m.-9 p.m. and Sunday 10 a.m.-7 p.m. The calendar shows no special Jan. 1 hours.

## Is Ollie's open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. The calendar marks Thursday, Nov. 26 "Happy Thanksgiving" and closed. Thanksgiving falls on [Thursday, Nov. 26](/when-is/thanksgiving/), and the stores are open again the next morning. For grocery chains, see [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/).

## What are Ollie's Black Friday 2026 hours?

Black Friday, Nov. 27, runs 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. on the calendar. The next two days are shorter: 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 28 and 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Sunday, Nov. 29. For other chains, compare [Black Friday 2026 store hours at Walmart, Target and Best Buy](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/).

## What are Ollie's Christmas Eve 2026 hours?

Stores open at 8 a.m. on Thursday, Dec. 24 and close at 6 p.m. They are closed on Friday, Dec. 25 ("Merry Christmas") and open 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 26. New Year's Eve, Thursday, Dec. 31, is 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. More chains are in [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 store hours](/news/christmas-eve-christmas-day-2026-store-hours/).

## Ollie's Daily Deal Reveals and Black Friday deals

Ollie's release dated Nov. 16, 2026 from Harrisburg, Pa., says the chain is "featuring a new brand-name bargain each day from November 22nd through November 25th." That is Sunday through Wednesday. It also promises "Ollie's strongest selection of doorbusters ever on Black Friday, November 27th," which is the company's own wording, and "more Daily Deal Reveals leading into Christmas."

| Date | What Ollie's says |
|---|---|
| Sun., Nov. 22 to Wed., Nov. 25 | A new brand-name bargain each day (Daily Deal Reveals) |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Stores closed |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Black Friday doorbusters, stores open 5 a.m.-10 p.m. |

The release says gifts, toys, décor and holiday essentials are "up to 70% off," a comparison Ollie's makes with "the fancy stores' prices." Ollie's has not announced which items are the daily deals or doorbusters, or any prices.

Ollie's also cites its 2026 Holiday Shopper Survey: 85% of respondents say bargains are very or extremely important to stretching holiday budgets, and 78% want deep discounts earlier in the season or shop for bargains before Black Friday. The release does not give the survey's sample size, dates or firm. As of Aug. 1, 2026, Ollie's operated 686 stores in 36 states.

## What is Ollie's Army Night on Dec. 13?

The calendar lists "Ollie's Army Night" from 5 p.m. to 9 p.m. on Sunday, Dec. 13, after the store's 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. daytime hours. Ollie's has not announced what the night offers. Ollie's Army is the chain's free loyalty program: the company's page says joining in store is free and earns 15% off a future purchase, $1 spent earns 1 point and rewards come for every 250 points earned.

## What has Ollie's not announced?

- Which items are the Daily Deal Reveals Nov. 22 to Nov. 25
- Doorbuster items and prices for Nov. 27
- Ollie's Army Night offers on Dec. 13
- Hours beyond "regular hours resume" from Jan. 1
