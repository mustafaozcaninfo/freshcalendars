**This week's pro golf starts Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, with the Professional Golfers' Association (PGA) Tour's World Wide Technology Championship at El Cardonal at Diamante in Los Cabos, Mexico, and the Ladies Professional Golf Association (LPGA) TOTO Japan Classic (Nov. 5-8), according to the tours' schedules.** Both run Thursday through Sunday. The tables below put the two tours side by side through mid-December, and a one-page printable follows.

Tee times and television windows had not been published when we read both schedules on Nov. 1, 2026, so this page lists neither. The tours post TV times in event week.

## What is the PGA Tour and LPGA golf schedule for November 2026?

The PGA Tour's schedule page and the LPGA's tournaments page give these dates. The weekdays are our own calculation.

| Week | PGA Tour event and venue | LPGA event and venue | LPGA purse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 5-8 (Thu-Sun) | World Wide Technology Championship, El Cardonal at Diamante, Los Cabos, Mexico | TOTO Japan Classic, Taiheiyo Club Minori Course, Omitama, Japan | $2.10 million |
| Nov. 12-15 (Thu-Sun) | Austin Championship, Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa (Fazio Canyons Course), Austin, Texas | The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican, Belleair, Florida | $3.25 million |
| Nov. 19-22 (Thu-Sun) | The RSM Classic, Sea Island Golf Club, St. Simons Island, Georgia | CME Group Tour Championship, Naples, Florida | $11 million |
| Dec. 11-13 (Fri-Sun) | Grant Thornton Invitational, Naples, Florida | Grant Thornton Invitational, Naples, Florida | $2.05 million |

The PGA Tour's own purses: $6 million for the World Wide Technology Championship and $6 million for the Nov. 12-15 event, per its Jan. 9, 2026 announcement, and $7.4 million for The RSM Classic. The tours do not list a PGA Tour purse for the Grant Thornton Invitational in the pages we read.

## When is the Austin Championship, also called the Good Good Championship?

Nov. 12-15. The event is listed on the PGA Tour schedule as the Austin Championship and was announced in January as the Good Good Championship, played at Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa (Fazio Canyons Course) in Austin, Texas, with a $6 million purse, according to the PGA Tour's Jan. 9, 2026 article. We use the schedule's name in the table because it is the current listing.

## When is the RSM Classic 2026?

The RSM Classic is Nov. 19-22 at Sea Island Golf Club on St. Simons Island, Georgia, with a $7.4 million purse, according to the PGA Tour. The tour's January announcement says: "The FedExCup Fall then concludes with The RSM Classic (Nov. 16-22), where the FedExCup Fall standings are finalized." The Nov. 16-22 span is the tournament week; play is Nov. 19-22.

## What is at stake in the FedExCup Fall?

The PGA Tour's FedExCup Fall page says "Top 100 Earn PGA Tour Cards" and "Top 125 Earn Conditional PGA Tour Status." It adds that "Nos. 51-60 qualify for the AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am and The Genesis Invitational via the Aon Next 10." The page shows no year, so confirm the cutoffs on the page before relying on them. The standings are finalized at The RSM Classic.

## When is the LPGA CME Group Tour Championship 2026?

Nov. 19-22 in Naples, Florida, with an $11 million purse and a "$4 million first-place prize," according to the LPGA. In 2025 the champion was Jeeno Thitikul. Earlier in November, The ANNIKA driven by Gainbridge at Pelican (Nov. 12-15, Belleair, Florida, $3.25 million) had Linn Grant as its 2025 champion, and Nasa Hataoka won the TOTO Japan Classic in 2025.

## When is the Hero World Challenge 2026?

Dec. 3-6 at Albany GC in Albany, Bahamas, according to the PGA Tour. The purse is not stated on the pages we read. Hideki Matsuyama is the defending champion, "a two-time winner at Albany in 2016 and 2025," per the PGA Tour's Oct. 19, 2026 "Inside the Field" article.

## What golf comes after the CME Group Tour Championship?

The PGA Tour's schedule lists PGA Tour Q-School (qualifying school) presented by Korn Ferry for Dec. 10-13. The Grant Thornton Invitational is Dec. 11-13 in Naples, Florida, and appears on both the PGA Tour and LPGA schedules; the LPGA lists a $2.05 million purse.
