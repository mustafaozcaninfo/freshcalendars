**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, puts D in the center, with C, E, N, P, R and T on the petals.** Beebom and Connections Solver agree on those letters. Below you get hints and a count of the long words, and every answer waits in a marked spoiler section at the end.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** D (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** C, E, N, P, R, T

Every word today needs the D in the center; the rules and the scoring are on our [Blossom page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works).

E is the single vowel letter on the board. Words have to lean on it, and the lists we checked show that long words repeat it again and again.

## Long words built on one vowel

We did not see a full official word list, so these counts cover words confirmed by two answer lists we checked. They agree on 16 words of 9 letters or more, and the longest has 10 letters.

| Length | Confirmed words |
|---|---|
| 10 letters | 4 |
| 9 letters | 12 |

Every one of the 16 uses E at least three times. In 8 of them E shows up four times. Six start with "pre", eight end in "ed", and three more end in "ent". Four finish with "ence", which is why the 10-letter group looks so alike.

Because only one vowel is on offer, the long words stack prefixes and endings around the same few consonants: "re-", "pre-", "-ed", "-er", "-ent" and "-ence". If you are stuck, try adding one of those pieces to a short word that already holds the D.

## Pangram hints

- **How many:** two pangrams are confirmed by the lists we checked.
- **Length:** both have 9 letters.
- **Relationship:** they are anagrams of each other, built from the same nine letters.
- **Shared start:** the first five letters are identical in both.
- **First one:** a noun for an earlier case that is used as a guide for a later one.
- **Second one:** a past-tense verb tied to the job of leading a choir in song.

Once you have one, keep its first five letters, rearrange the last four and test the result against the other hint.

## Today's Blossom pangrams and long words (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it yourself.

- **Precedent** (9 letters)
- **Precented** (9 letters)

Both use C, D, E, N, P, R and T at least once. Both start with the same five letters; only the order of the last four changes, with the D moving from the sixth spot to the ninth. The game may not accept every word that solver sites list.

Two lists agree on these words of 9 letters or more. The two pangrams are included, since they are long enough.

| Ending | Words |
|---|---|
