## When could a Bengals game time still change?

The NFL's flexible scheduling procedures let it move games into Sunday Night Football and Monday Night Football slots late in the season. Per the procedures, Sunday night flex notice for Weeks 5-13 comes "no later than 12 days in advance of the game," and for Weeks 14-17 the NFL says "generally ... no later than 6 days prior to the game." Thursday Night Football "may be used up to twice between Weeks 13-17," with notice "generally ... no later than 21 days prior to the game." We subtracted those windows from each Bengals date. These are the latest notice dates, not dates a change will happen:

| Week | Game date | Latest flex notice | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Sun., Nov. 29 | Tue., Nov. 17 | 12 days |
| 13 | Sun., Dec. 6 | Tue., Nov. 24 | 12 days |
| 14 | Sun., Dec. 13 | Mon., Dec. 7 | 6 days, generally |
| 15 | Sun., Dec. 20 | Mon., Dec. 14 | 6 days, generally |
| 17 | Thu., Dec. 31 | Thu., Dec. 10 | 21 days, Thursday flex |

The Week 17 row applies the Thursday rule because NFL.com lists that game as a Thursday night game on Prime Video; the procedures page does not say whether a game already on Thursday can move. The Week 11 Monday night game and the Week 12 to 15 Sunday afternoon times are as posted on Nov. 16, 2026.
