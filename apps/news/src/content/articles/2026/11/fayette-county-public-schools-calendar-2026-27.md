**The Fayette County Public Schools (FCPS) 2026-27 calendar lists winter break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, spring break from Monday through Friday, April 5-9, 2027, and a last day of classes on Wednesday, May 26, 2027, "pending make-up days"**, according to the district's "2026-27 FCPS Instructional Calendar for Students & Families." The Fayette County Board of Education approved it on June 23, 2025, and the cover says it is "subject to change." Tables below give the weekdays off for each break, the four possible weather make-up days and a comparison with 2025-26.

The [printable school-year calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html) at the bottom of the page puts the district's dates on one sheet.

## Fayette County school breaks at a glance

The district's wording is in the first column. Return days are our reading of the printed dates: the district prints "Students return to school" for Jan. 5 only, and the April 13 return follows from April 12 being listed as "No classes for students".

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Break | Mon.-Fri., Sept. 28-Oct. 2, 2026, then Mon., Oct. 5 (no classes) | 6 | Tue., Oct. 6 |
| Thanksgiving Break | Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27, 2026 | 3 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027, then Mon., Jan. 4 (no classes) | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., April 5-9, 2027, then Mon., April 12 (no classes) | 6 | Tue., April 13, 2027 |

Counting weekends, students are out from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Monday, Jan. 4, 17 days in a row. Spring break runs from Saturday, April 3 through Monday, April 12, 10 days. Fall break covers Saturday, Sept. 26 through Monday, Oct. 5, also 10 days.

## When is Fayette County winter break 2026?

Winter break runs Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, 10 weekdays. The district lists Monday, Jan. 4 as "No classes for students" and Tuesday, Jan. 5 as "Students return to school". The last school day before the break is Friday, Dec. 18, since no earlier day off is listed that week. The [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the weeks, and our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) lists other districts. For Thanksgiving, see the [Thanksgiving break 2026 roundup](/news/thanksgiving-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

## Every FCPS day off in 2026-27

These are the days the calendar lists as closed or without classes, between the first day on Aug. 12, 2026 and the May 26, 2027 last day.

| Date | What the district lists |
|---|---|
| Mon., Sept. 7, 2026 | Labor Day; schools & offices closed |
| Mon.-Fri., Sept. 28-Oct. 2, 2026 | Fall Break |
| Mon., Oct. 5, 2026 | No classes for students |
| Tue., Nov. 3, 2026 | Election Day; no classes for students |
| Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27, 2026 | Thanksgiving Break; schools & offices closed |
| Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Winter Break |
| Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | No classes for students |
| Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | Martin Luther King Jr. Day; schools & offices closed |
| Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | Presidents Day; schools & offices closed |
| Fri., March 19, 2027 | No classes; possible weather make-up day |
| Mon.-Fri., April 5-9, 2027 | Spring Break |
| Mon., April 12, 2027 | No classes for students |
| Tue., May 18, 2027 | Election primaries; no classes for students |

Without March 19, that is 31 weekdays off by the district's dates. Labor Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day and Presidents Day fall on Mondays and make three-day weekends. The calendar shows no early-release days and no grading-period end dates. William Wells Brown Elementary, a Promise Academy school, "follows a slightly different calendar."

## How do FCPS weather make-up days work?

The calendar says FCPS "schedules weather make-up days at the superintendent's discretion" and updates its online calendars and PDFs after each official announcement. The district's calendar page adds that "The PDFs will be updated as weather make-up days are confirmed." Four dates on the 2026-27 calendar are marked as possible make-up days.

| Date | What the district lists | Effect on the last day |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., March 19, 2027 | No classes; possible weather make-up day | None on its own |
| Wed., May 26, 2027 | Last day of classes for students (pending make-up days) | The listed last day |
| Thu., May 27, 2027 | Possible weather make-up day | Moves the last day to May 27 if used |
| Fri., May 28, 2027 | Possible weather make-up day | Moves the last day to May 28 if used |
| Mon., May 31, 2027 | Memorial Day; schools & offices closed | No school |
| Tue., June 1, 2027 | Possible weather make-up day | Moves the last day to June 1 if used |

The calendar does not say in what order the days are used, so the effect column is our reading of the printed dates. If every make-up day in late May were needed, the latest possible last day would be Tuesday, June 1, 2027, after Memorial Day. The [May 2027 printable calendar](/may-calendars.html) shows how those days fall.

## How does FCPS 2026-27 compare with 2025-26?

The district's 2025-26 calendar, which it approved on July 22, 2024 and amended on Dec. 19, 2024, gives last year's dates for the same breaks.

| Item | 2025-26 (last year) | 2026-27 |
|---|---|---|
| First day for students | Wed., Aug. 13, 2025 | Wed., Aug. 12, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 22, 2025 to Fri., Jan. 2, 2026 | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 |
| No classes after winter break | Mon., Jan. 5, 2026 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Students return | Tue., Jan. 6, 2026 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., April 6-10, 2026 | Mon.-Fri., April 5-9, 2027 |
| No classes after spring break | Mon., April 13, 2026 | Mon., April 12, 2027 |

Each date in this table falls one day earlier on the calendar than last year's. Winter break is 10 weekdays off in both years.

## What the FCPS calendar does not say

The calendar does not give the order for make-up days, and it says it is subject to change. The district's page links the "2026-27 Instructional Calendar (K-12)" and notes that "All calendars are subject to change." Check [fcps.net](https://www.fcps.net/calendar) before booking travel around the make-up days in March and late May.
