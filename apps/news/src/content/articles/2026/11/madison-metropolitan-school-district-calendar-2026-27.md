**The Madison Metropolitan School District (MMSD) 2026-27 calendar lists winter break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Thursday, Dec. 31 and Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, spring break from Monday through Friday, March 22-26, 2027, and a last day of school on Thursday, June 10, 2027**, according to the district's "2026-2027 School Year Calendar" page. School resumes Monday, Jan. 4. The page calls these the dates "confirmed thus far" and says "The full calendar of dates will be released at a later time." Tables below split the days off for all students from the ones that apply to one level, and compare the breaks with 2025-26.

The [printable school-year calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html) at the bottom of the page puts the district's dates on one sheet.

## MMSD breaks at a glance

The district's wording for each break is in the first column. Return days are our reading: the district prints "School Resumes" for Jan. 4 only, and the other return days are the next weekday it does not list as a no-school day.

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-Fall Break | Fri., Oct. 16, 2026 | 1 | Mon., Oct. 19 |
| Fall Break | Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27, 2026, then Mon., Nov. 30 (staff only day) | 4 | Tue., Dec. 1 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026, and Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 29, 2027 |

The district does not call Nov. 25-27 Thanksgiving break. Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, falls inside Fall Break. With Saturday and Sunday, students are out six days in a row, from Wednesday, Nov. 25 through Monday, Nov. 30.

## When does MMSD winter break 2026 start?

Winter break starts Monday, Dec. 21, 2026. The last day before it is Friday, Dec. 18, by our reading, since no earlier day off is listed that week. Counting weekends, students are out from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Sunday, Jan. 3: 16 days. Nine weekdays are the district's "No School - Winter Break" plus Friday, Jan. 1, which it lists with the break, for 10 weekdays off. The [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the weeks, and our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) lists other districts.

Four of the Fridays and Mondays make three-day weekends: Jan. 18 (Jan. 16-18), Jan. 29 (Jan. 29-31), Feb. 19 (Feb. 19-21) and April 9 (April 9-11). Including weekends, spring break runs from Saturday, March 20 to Sunday, March 28, nine days.

## Which MMSD days off apply to one grade level?

The district lists conference and professional learning days for one level at a time, which a single "no school" list hides. These come on top of the days above.

| Date | Who has no school | What the district lists |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Sept. 28, 2026 | High school | Staff Professional Learning Day |
| Thu., Oct. 15, 2026 | Elementary and high school | Staff-Only Day; middle school in session |
| Mon., Nov. 2, 2026 | Elementary (no 4K/EC either) | Evening elementary conferences |
| Tue., Nov. 3, 2026 | Elementary | Family-Teacher Conferences |
| Fri., Nov. 6, 2026 | High school | Family-Teacher Conferences |
| Mon., Nov. 9, 2026 | High school | Staff Professional Learning Day |
| Fri., Nov. 20, 2026 | Middle school | Family-Teacher Conferences |
| Thu., Feb. 18, 2027 | Middle school | Family-Teacher Conferences |
| Fri., March 5, 2027 | High school | Family-Teacher Conferences |
| Mon., March 8, 2027 | High school | Staff Professional Learning Day |
| Fri., May 14, 2027 | Elementary and high school | Staff-Only Day; middle school in session |

By this table, high school students have seven extra days off, elementary students four and middle school students two. Two pairs sit next to a weekend: high school has no school Friday, Nov. 6 through Monday, Nov. 9, and Friday, March 5 through Monday, March 8. Mid-Fall Break on Friday, Oct. 16 follows the Thursday, Oct. 15 staff-only day, so elementary and high school students are out from Thursday, Oct. 15 through Sunday, Oct. 18. "4K" is four-year-old kindergarten and "EC" is Early Childhood. The district lists no early-release days.

## When do MMSD quarters and semesters end?

| Date | What the district lists |
|---|---|
| Wed., Nov. 4, 2026 | End of 1st Quarter |
| Thu., Jan. 28, 2027 | End of 2nd Quarter, End of 1st Semester |
| Mon., Feb. 1, 2027 | Beginning of 3rd Quarter, Beginning of 2nd Semester |
| Thu., April 8, 2027 | End of 3rd Quarter |
| Mon., April 12, 2027 | Beginning of 4th Quarter |
| Thu., June 10, 2027 | End of 4th Quarter, End of 2nd Semester |

Friday, Jan. 29 is a staff grading day between the semesters, so the first semester ends Thursday and students are back Monday, Feb. 1.

## When does the MMSD school year start?

The district lists staff days first: New Educator Days Aug. 19-21, Staff Welcome Back/Professional Development Days Aug. 25-28 and a Staff Voluntary Day on Aug. 31. Students start in two steps. Tuesday, Sept. 1 is the "First Day for 4K, K, 6, 9 and Early Childhood (EC)", and on Wednesday, Sept. 2 all students attend a regular schedule.

## How does MMSD 2026-27 compare with 2025-26?

The district's 2025-2026 calendar gives last year's dates for the same breaks.

| Break | 2025-26 (last year) | 2026-27 |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-Fall Break | Fri., Oct. 17, 2025 | Fri., Oct. 16, 2026 |
| Fall Break | Wed.-Fri., Nov. 26-28, 2025 | Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 22 to Wed., Dec. 31, 2025, and Thu.-Fri., Jan. 1-2, 2026 | Mon., Dec. 21 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026, and Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 |
| School resumes | Mon., Jan. 5, 2026 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 23-27, 2026 | Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027 |
| Last day | Wed., June 10, 2026 | Thu., June 10, 2027 |

Each 2026-27 break starts one day earlier on the calendar than last year's, and the last day is again June 10. Winter break is 10 weekdays off both years. | Date | Graduation |
|---|---|
