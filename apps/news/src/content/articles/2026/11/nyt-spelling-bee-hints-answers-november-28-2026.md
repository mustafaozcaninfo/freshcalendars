## The hardest words on Saturday's list

- **Longest answers:** one of 11 letters, four of 9 and five of 8.
- **Rare pairs:** EI, EC and DD each appear in a single answer, and DJ in two.
- **Doubled letters:** 16 answers repeat a letter back to back, 8 with NN, 4 with EE, 3 with OO and 1 with DD.

## The gap from four-letter words to Genius

The 17 four-letter answers give 17 points, about 8% of the 221 needed. That leaves 204 points to find among the 46 answers of five letters or more, which hold 299 in all. Drop the pangram and the other 62 words are worth 300, still 79 over the bar.
