**The Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL) Prep Bowl on Friday, Nov. 27, and Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, puts seven football state title games at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis, according to 5 Eyewitness News (KSTP) and Bring Me The Sports.** Kickoffs are 10 a.m., 1 p.m. and 4 p.m. Central Time (CT) on both days, plus 7 p.m. CT on Friday. The table lists every matchup with Eastern Time (ET) kickoffs, and a printable watch guide follows it.

## Prep Bowl 2026 schedule: matchups and kickoff times

The MSHSL site (mshsl.org) blocks automated access, so the times come from two independent newsrooms that agree. KSTP's Prep Bowl preview lists the games under the headings "Friday, November 27" and "Saturday, November 28", and Bring Me The Sports (via Yahoo Sports, Nov. 22, 2026) covers the same matchups. The Post-Bulletin (via Yahoo Sports, Nov. 7, 2026) gave times for six of the games. The U.S. Bank Stadium event page lists the event on Nov. 27 and Nov. 28, 2026, from 10:00 AM. ET adds one hour to each CT kickoff. Records are the ones the newsrooms list.

| Day | Kickoff (CT) | Kickoff (ET) | Class | Matchup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 10 a.m. | 11 a.m. | Class A | Minneota (12-0) vs. Springfield (12-0) |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | Class 4A | Orono (12-0) vs. Byron (11-1) |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | Class AA (2A) | Caledonia (12-0) vs. Jackson County Central (11-1) |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Class 6A | Champlin Park (12-0) vs. Minnetonka (10-2) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 10 a.m. | 11 a.m. | Nine-Player | Hills-Beaver Creek (12-0) vs. Border West (12-0) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | Class AAA (3A) | Pierz (12-0) vs. Waseca (12-1) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | Class 5A | Cretin-Derham Hall (12-0) vs. Mankato West (12-0) |

Friday's games fall the day after Thanksgiving; the [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) confirms the holiday is Thursday, Nov. 26.

## What to watch in the Prep Bowl title games

Bring Me The Sports points to several storylines:

- Minneota is on a 58-game win streak and seeks a fifth straight title.
- Jackson County Central seeks a third straight title.
- Hills-Beaver Creek seeks a second straight title.
- Byron's only loss was to Orono, 37-14, in Week 2. The two meet in the Class 4A game.

## How to watch the Prep Bowl on TV

According to KSTP, the games air live on 45TV (KSTC) and stream on the KSTP apps. No other broadcaster or stream is confirmed here.

## Game day at U.S. Bank Stadium

The stadium's event page for the Prep Bowl sets these rules:

- Bags and purses do not need to be clear for this event but must not exceed 12 x 6 x 12 inches. Backpacks are prohibited.
- The Legacy Gates are on the west side of the stadium, and the ticket office is on the northwest side.
- Parking lots open six hours before the event, including the Factory Motor Parts Parking Garage.

Ticket prices, gate opening times and parking prices were not on the pages read, so none are listed.
