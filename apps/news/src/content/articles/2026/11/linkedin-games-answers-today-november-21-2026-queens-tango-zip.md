## Pinpoint and Crossclimb nudges for Saturday, Nov. 21

- **Pinpoint #935, first hint:** four of the five clues are one word each, and the fifth is a short phrase.
- **Pinpoint #935, second hint:** the phrase is unfinished; the missing word is the one the whole set points to.
- **Pinpoint #935, third hint:** picture the sky after dark, and add the missing word to each clue in turn.
- **Crossclimb #935, the ladder:** seven words of four letters each, joined by six one-letter changes.
- **Crossclimb #935, the locked ends:** both share one clue, and together they make a single hyphenated phrase about escaping a penalty.
- **Crossclimb #935, the middle:** the first middle word is a narrow gap you could post something through, and the first move changes the second letter.

## How many Pinpoint #935 clues to open

Four single words and one open phrase give a guess plenty to check against. The first two clues alone fit many lists, so a third is a fair point to commit. That is our reading, not a LinkedIn rule. Since the fifth clue is the odd one out, a guess that cannot finish it is worth dropping.

## Where the Crossclimb #935 letters move

Seven words, six moves, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word of the ladder appears here.

| Position | Times it changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |

The six moves run through positions 2, 4, 1, 4, 3, 2. The last letter is touched in the second and fourth moves, and the second letter opens and closes the climb.

## Why the Crossclimb #935 ends sit six moves apart

The locked words differ in all four positions, so four changes would be the least possible. The ladder spends six, because the middle words reuse positions 2 and 4 on the way.

## Saturday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 21, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Crossclimb #935:** Scot, Slot, Slow, Flow, Floe, Flee, Free. Slot, Slow, Flow, Floe and Flee fill the middle in that order; Scot and Free are the locked ends.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
