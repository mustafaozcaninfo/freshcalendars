**Spotify's Premium page offers Individual at "$0 for 3 months, then $12.99 per month after," with the offer ending Dec. 31, 2026, and Audible's home page offers "$0.99/mo for 3 months + $20 Audible bonus" through Dec. 28.** Both pages were read on Nov. 21, 2026, and neither shows a time of day for the end. Spotify's offer is only for people who have not tried Premium before.

## Spotify and Audible offers and end dates

Neither page uses the words "Black Friday." Audible's banner reads "This Season's Biggest Deal."

| Service | Offer on its page | After the offer | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Audible](https://www.audible.com/) | $0.99 a month for 3 months + $20 Audible bonus | $8.99 a month, "terms apply" | Dec. 28, 2026 |
| [Spotify Premium Individual](https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/) | $0 for 3 months | $12.99 a month | Dec. 31, 2026 |
| [Spotify Premium Student](https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/) | $0 for 1 month | $6.99 a month | No end date shown |
| [Audible Premium, a year](https://www.audible.com/) | $99 for a year, "like $8.25/mo" | Renewal not shown | No end date shown |

Spotify's Student plan is "available only to students at an eligible accredited higher education institution." Spotify's page lists Duo at $18.99 a month and Family at $21.99 a month with no trial shown.

Audible's home page does not name the plan behind the $0.99 offer or explain the bonus. The $8.99 renewal price matches the Standard plan on [Audible's membership page](https://www.audible.com/membership), which says it "auto-renews at $8.99/mo after 30 days." That page lists Premium at $14.95 a month and Premium annual at $149.50 a year, and says a membership "is free for 30 days."
