## Where each Pips board starts, Nov. 23

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left. A search of every legal placement against the saved data finds one arrangement on Easy, three on Medium and 64 on Hard.

**Easy.** R1C2 and R3C6 each add up to 2 by themselves, and only two halves in the set show a 2, one on the 2-3 and one on the 4-2. Each of those cells touches a single neighbor, so the domino is decided by what that neighbor must do. R1C1 has to match R2C1, and R2C1 can only join R3C1, which stays below 4. Put the 2-3 on R1C2 and the 3 beside it forces a 3 onto R2C1, whose domino then leaves a 4 on R3C1, breaking the cap. So the 4-2 takes R1C2 and the 2-3 takes R3C6.

**Medium.** R1C3 must be below 2. The only halves under 2 in the set are the 0 and the 1 of the 0-1, and R1C2 must be more than 3, so that domino cannot run sideways and stands across R1C3 and R2C3. All three arrangements agree on a second fact: R3C3 and R4C3 both hold a 5, and the 5-2 closes the bottom row with its 2 on R4C4.

**Hard.** R7C3 adds up to 0 and touches only R7C2, which must be more than 1. Two dominoes carry a 0, the 6-0 and the 0-1. The 0-1 would put a 1 on R7C2, so the 6-0 goes down first, with the 6 on R7C2. Eighteen of Hard's 30 cells hold the same number in all 64 arrangements.

## Pips hints for Nov. 23, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 2-3, 4-0, 0-1, 4-2, 4-3)**

- Hint 1: R1C2 and R3C6 each add up to 2 alone, and the set has only two halves showing a 2.
- Hint 2: R3C1 must be less than 4, and R3C1 shares a domino with R2C1, which must match R1C1.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 5-5, 0-1, 4-6, 3-6, 6-5, 5-3, 5-2)**

- Hint 1: R1C3 must be less than 2, and one domino holds both halves that qualify.
- Hint 2: R3C3 and R4C3 add up to 10, and five halves in the set show a 5.

**Hard (15 dominoes on a nine-column board)**

- Hint 1: R7C3 adds up to 0 and touches only R7C2, which must be more than 1; two halves in the set show a 0.
- Hint 2: R4C7 must be more than 4, and R3C7, R3C8 and R3C9 add up to 11.

## Why Hard's two zero halves matter on Nov. 23

Only 2 of Hard's 30 halves show a 0, and one of them is consumed by R7C3 before anything else is placed. The other 0 sits on the 0-1, which the data lays across R3C1 and R4C1, two cells of the all-different trio with R3C2. Medium has a single 0, and the 0-1 is the only domino that meets the cap on R1C3. Easy is the quiet board of the day: 6 of its 8 regions cover one cell, and one arrangement is all it allows.
