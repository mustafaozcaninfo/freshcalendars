**Comal Independent School District (ISD) lists Nov. 23-27, 2026 as the Thanksgiving student and staff holiday and Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 as the holiday break**, according to its Academic Calendar 2026 - 2027. The PDF's footer reads "Board Approved: January 22,2026 Revised: November 13, 2025." Spring break is March 8-12, 2027, and the last day of school is Thursday, May 27, 2027.

The dates below come from the district's PDF, which its [calendars page](https://www.comalisd.org/apps/pages/calendars) links; both were read Nov. 13, 2026. Weekdays and return days are our calendar check. The district serves New Braunfels, Texas, and is a different district from New Braunfels ISD.

## Comal ISD breaks in 2026-27

| Break | Dates | Students return (our reading) |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving student/staff holiday | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Holiday Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon., March 8 to Fri., March 12, 2027 | Mon., March 15 |

Neighboring San Antonio-area districts are in the [Northside ISD](/news/northside-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) and [North East ISD](/news/north-east-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) calendars.

## When is Comal ISD's winter break in 2026?

The calendar lists a Holiday Break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, marked as a student and staff holiday. That is ten weekdays.

Monday, Jan. 4 is listed as "Student Holiday/Staff Development," so students return Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027. That follows from the calendar entries; the PDF does not print a separate "classes resume" line.

## When does the Comal ISD school year start and end?

Teachers return Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026, and the first day of school is Tuesday, Aug. 25, 2026. The last day of school is Thursday, May 27, 2027, followed by a teacher work day Friday, May 28. Memorial Day is Monday, May 31.

## What are Comal ISD's school hours?

The calendar lists elementary school 7:35 a.m. to 3:10 p.m., middle school 8:15 a.m. to 3:50 p.m. and high school 8:55 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
