**The Chicago Bears host the New Orleans Saints on Sun., Nov. 22, 2026, at 1 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on FOX at Soldier Field, and visit the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving, Thu., Nov. 26, at 1 p.m. ET on CBS, according to NFL.com.** Eight games remain, and a one-page printable with a pick column is further down.

The Bears are 7-2-0 in the National Football Conference (NFC) North, tied with Detroit and Minnesota, per the [NFL standings](https://www.nfl.com/standings/) checked Nov. 16. Their bye was Week 10, and they have won four straight, the latest 27-24 over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Soldier Field on Sun., Nov. 8, per NFL.com. The tables use the National Football League (NFL) schedule and the [Bears' own schedule page](https://www.chicagobears.com/schedule/), which lists the Saints game as "12:00 PM CST". Times are in ET; Central (CT), Mountain (MT) and Pacific (PT) are computed by us.

## When could a Bears game time still change?

The NFL's flexible scheduling procedures let it move games into Sunday Night Football and Monday Night Football slots late in the season. Notice for Sunday games in Weeks 5-13 and Monday games in Weeks 12-17 comes "no later than 12 days in advance of the game." For Sunday games in Weeks 14-17 the NFL says "generally ... no later than 6 days prior to the game." Thursday Night Football "may be used up to twice between Weeks 13-17," with notice "generally ... no later than 21 days prior to the game." We subtracted those windows from each Bears Sunday date. These are the latest notice dates, not dates a change will happen:

| Week | Game date | Latest flex notice | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Sun., Nov. 22 | Tue., Nov. 10 (passed) | 12 days |
| 13 | Sun., Dec. 6 | Tue., Nov. 24 | 12 days |
| 14 | Sun., Dec. 13 | Mon., Dec. 7 | 6 days, generally |
| 17 | Sun., Jan. 3, 2027 | Mon., Dec. 28 | 6 days, generally |

Week 11's deadline has passed, so the Saints game stays at 1 p.m. ET on FOX as posted. The procedures page does not say how the Saturday game in Week 15 or the Christmas Day game in Week 16 are treated, so no notice date is computed for them. The schedule above is as posted on Nov. 16, 2026.
