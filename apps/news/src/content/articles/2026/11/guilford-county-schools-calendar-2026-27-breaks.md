**Guilford County Schools (GCS) in North Carolina has no school for students Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, and Spring Break is March 22-26, 2027, by the district's Traditional Academic Calendar 2026-2027.** The last day of school is Thursday, June 10, 2027. This story covers the traditional calendar; the district's Early and Middle College schools follow separate calendars and differ.

The calendar PDF's footer reads "Revised by the Board of Education 8/11/2026" and it does not say what that revision changed. The next day with no school for students is Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026.

## Guilford County school breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates. The January teacher workday is included because the district's handbook marks teacher workdays as "no school for students."

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Next school day on the calendar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving Break for Students | Wed., Nov. 25 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 3 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break for Students | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027, plus Mon., Jan. 4 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon., March 22 to Fri., March 26, 2027 | 5 | Tue., March 30, 2027 |

Next school days are inferred from the calendar, which shows no closure on those dates. Monday, April 5 is a mandatory teacher workday, so students are out that day as well.

## Guilford County Schools winter break 2026

Winter Break for Students runs Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027. Jan. 4 is a teacher workday with no school for students, and the calendar's next school day is Tuesday, Jan. 5. The calendar does not print a return date, so check with your school for the first day back.

## Guilford County Schools spring break 2027

Spring Break is Monday-Friday, March 22-26, 2027. Friday, March 26 is Good Friday by our count, and Monday, April 5, the day after Easter by our count, is a mandatory teacher workday with no school for students. For other North Carolina districts, see the [Wake County calendar story](/news/wake-county-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-traditional/), the [Charlotte-Mecklenburg calendar story](/news/charlotte-mecklenburg-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) and the [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

The district's Board of Education post of Jan. 13, 2026 adds that the Early and Middle College schools at Greensboro College, Guilford College and the University of North Carolina (UNC) Greensboro share a spring break the week of March 8-12, 2027. Those schools follow separate non-traditional calendars (Extended Year, Early and Middle Colleges and Academies), so check their own calendars.

## Do Guilford County schools list early release or make-up days?

The traditional calendar lists no early release days and no inclement-weather make-up days. The same January 2026 post says the district's revision moves the end of the first quarter grading period to Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026, and sets the fall optional teacher workday for Friday, Oct. 30, 2026.

## When does the 2027-28 Guilford County school year start?

The district has also posted a [2027-2028 traditional calendar](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1778256049/gcsnccom/ri8wtnhqjxyia00ninwq/FINALTRADITIONAL2027-28GCSAcademicCalendar.pdf), last revised 1/13/2026. Students start Monday, Aug. 23, 2027, and the last day is Thursday, June 8, 2028.
