The offers below are as listed on Costco.com on Nov. 28, 2026, with the purchase limit for each, and a printable checklist follows.

## How do Costco's Cyber Monday and Black Friday pages differ?

| | [Black Friday page](https://www.costco.com/o/-/black-friday) | [Cyber Monday page](https://www.costco.com/o/-/cyber-monday) |
|---|---|---|
| Header | "Holiday Savings Valid Nov. 27 - Nov. 30" | "Holiday Savings Valid Nov. 30" |
| Item dates | "Valid 11/27/26 - 11/30/26" | "Valid 11/30/2026" |
| Channel | Items marked Warehouse & Online, Warehouse Only or Online Only | Every item marked Online Only |
| Where valid | Warehouses and Costco.com, by item | Costco.com |

On Monday, Nov. 30, both pages are valid: Black Friday offers, some of them sold in warehouses, end that day, and the Cyber Monday offers run that one day online. The Black Friday page's banner also says "Savings Valid Oct. 30 - Nov. 30, 2026. Valid dates vary by item", so check the date line on each item. Our [Costco Black Friday story](/news/costco-black-friday-2026-dates-november-27-30-pre-thanksgiving-savings-november-16/) lists the warehouse and online items.

## Can you buy Costco Cyber Monday offers in a warehouse?

Costco's page marks all 10 as Online Only, so the promotions are valid only for purchases on Costco.com. Warehouse hours are separate: Costco has not posted chain-wide Monday hours. The Seattle warehouse page, the one warehouse checked, lists Monday to Friday hours of 9 a.m. to 8:30 p.m. for Executive members and 10 a.m. to 8:30 p.m. for Gold Star and Business members, gas from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., and the pharmacy from 9 a.m. to 8:30 p.m. Hours vary by warehouse, so check your own warehouse page. Cyber Monday is not a holiday closure; our [Costco holiday hours and closures story](/news/costco-holiday-hours-closures-2026-2027/) has the closure dates.

## What is Costco's return rule for Cyber Monday purchases?

Costco's customer service page says: "With few exceptions, Costco has a 100% satisfaction guarantee." For major appliances it says: "Costco will accept returns within 90 days from the date you receive the following Major Appliances." That applies to the Whirlpool dishwasher if it is listed there, so check the page for the model. Costco's page, as read on Nov. 28, gives no day count for other returns, so the printable does not list one.

## What has Costco not said about Cyber Monday 2026?

- The hour the Cyber Monday offers end on Nov. 30.
- Delivery times and a Christmas order-by date.
