**The Montreal Canadiens play 14 games in December 2026, nine of them at Centre Bell, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Wednesday, Dec. 2, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against Tampa Bay and ends Thursday, Dec. 31, at 2 p.m. ET in Tampa.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Montreal, read Nov. 10, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to ET, which is Montreal time, and worked out the weekdays. The table puts French and English Canadian TV side by side. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

Réseau des sports (RDS) and Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports are the French-language networks the feed lists. ## How many Canadiens games are at home in December 2026?

Nine, all at Centre Bell: Dec. 2, 5, 6, 8, 10, 14, 26, 27 and 29. The first five are in a row, so Montreal does not leave home until Saturday, Dec. 12, at Columbus. The five road games are Columbus (Dec. 12 and Dec. 22), Buffalo (Dec. 18), Minnesota (Dec. 19) and Tampa Bay (Dec. 31).

Columbus is the one repeat opponent: two road games and one home game on Tuesday, Dec. 29. The Maple Leafs visit Centre Bell on Saturday, Dec. 26, a game that is also covered from Toronto's side in our [Maple Leafs December schedule](/news/toronto-maple-leafs-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/). Boxing Day itself is explained in our [Canada Boxing Day date guide](/when-is/ca-boxing-day/).

## Afternoon starts and back-to-back nights

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Afternoon starts:** Sat., Dec. 5, vs. Florida, 1 p.m. ET; Sat., Dec. 12, at Columbus, 1 p.m. ET; Thu., Dec. 31, at Tampa Bay, 2 p.m. ET.
- **Back-to-backs:** Fri., Dec. 18, at Buffalo and Sat., Dec. 19, at Minnesota; Sat., Dec. 26, and Sun., Dec. 27, both at home.
- **Three road games in a row:** Buffalo, Minnesota and Columbus, Dec. 18, 19 and 22.
- **No game:** Dec. 23, 24 and 25, and Dec. 30.

## When do the Canadiens play after December?

The first 2027 game is Saturday, Jan. 2, at Florida, 1 p.m. ET. Chicago visits Centre Bell on Monday, Jan. 4, at 7:30 p.m. ET.
