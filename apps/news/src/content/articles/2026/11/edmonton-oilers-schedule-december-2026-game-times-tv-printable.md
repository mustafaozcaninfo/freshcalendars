**The Edmonton Oilers play 13 games in December 2026, eight of them away from Rogers Place, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 6 p.m. Mountain Time (MT), which is 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), against Buffalo, and ends Wednesday, Dec. 30, at Boston.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Edmonton, read Nov. 11, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to MT, which is Edmonton time, and ET, and worked out the weekdays. Canadian TV is the first channel column and the U.S. listing the second. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

Sportsnet and its channels (Sportsnet One, Sportsnet+, Sportsnet Oilers) and Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports carry most games in Canada, and Prime Video has the Dec. 30 game. ## How many Oilers games are at Rogers Place in December 2026?

Five: Dec. 1, 3, 17, 19 and 20. The Oilers open with two home games, Buffalo and Washington, and play both again on the road: at KeyBank Center on Dec. 10 and at Capital One Arena on Dec. 28. Our own count from the feed: eight away games.

The Dec. 17 game against Dallas, the Dec. 19 game against the Utah Mammoth and the Dec. 20 game against Anaheim close the home stand, and Dec. 19 is the one afternoon start, at 1:30 p.m. MT.

## Oilers road trips in December 2026

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **First Eastern trip, Dec. 6 to Dec. 14:** five games at Chicago, Minnesota, Buffalo, Ottawa and Montreal.
- **Second trip, Dec. 27 to Dec. 30:** three games at Columbus, Washington and Boston.
- **Back-to-back at home:** Sat., Dec. 19, against Utah and Sun., Dec. 20, against Anaheim.
- **No game:** Dec. 21 to Dec. 26, and Dec. 31.

The first 2027 game is Saturday, Jan. 2, at the Detroit Red Wings, at Little Caesars Arena, 3:30 p.m. ET (1:30 p.m. MT).
