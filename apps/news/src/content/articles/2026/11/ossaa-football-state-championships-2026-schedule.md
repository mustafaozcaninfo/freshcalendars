**The Oklahoma Secondary School Activities Association (OSSAA) lists 12 football state championship games at the University of Central Oklahoma (UCO) in Edmond: six classes on Thursday to Saturday, Dec. 3-5, 2026, and six on Thursday to Saturday, Dec. 10-12.** Each day has two games, at noon and 7 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 1 p.m. and 8 p.m. ET. The table puts both weekends in one place, followed by the semifinal sites and what OSSAA has and has not posted about tickets and video.

## OSSAA football state championship 2026 schedule by class

Six classes are split into parts I and II, among them Class B part I (B-I), Class B part II (B-II), Class A part I (A-I) and Class A part II (A-II); 6A and 2A split the same way. OSSAA's football page puts the finals for classes 6A-I, 6A-II, 4A, B-I, B-II and C on Dec. 3-5, 2026, and the finals for 5A, 3A, 2A-I, 2A-II, A-I and A-II on Dec. 10-12, 2026. The class brackets give the day and time of each final, and each one lists the University of Central Oklahoma as the site. High School On SI reported on Nov. 16, 2026 that the 11-player finals are scheduled for Chad Richison Stadium there. The brackets give Central Time; the Eastern column adds one hour.

| Day | Kickoff (CT) | Kickoff (ET) | Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 12 p.m. | 1 p.m. | C |
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | B-II |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 12 p.m. | 1 p.m. | B-I |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 6A-I |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 12 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 4A |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 6A-II |
| Thu., Dec. 10 | 12 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 3A |
| Thu., Dec. 10 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 5A |
| Fri., Dec. 11 | 12 p.m. | 1 p.m. | A-I |
| Fri., Dec. 11 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | A-II |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | 12 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 2A-I |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 2A-II |

Matchups for the finals are set by the semifinals and are not listed here. The brackets give no score.

## Which classes play semifinals on Nov. 27 and which on Dec. 4?

Every bracket shows the quarterfinals on Nov. 20. The semifinal date splits the classes in two, and it matches the order of the finals.

| Semifinal date | Classes | Finals |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 27 | 6A-I, 6A-II, 4A, B-I, B-II, C | Dec. 3-5 |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 5A, 3A, 2A-I, 2A-II, A-I, A-II | Dec. 10-12 |

OSSAA's football page says the state playoffs started Nov. 13, 2026. The Dec. 4 semifinal matchups depend on the Nov. 20 quarterfinals and are on those classes' brackets.

## Who plays in the Nov. 27 semifinals?

The Class 6A-I semifinals on Friday, Nov. 27 are Broken Arrow vs. Owasso at 1 p.m. and Jenks vs. Bixby at 7 p.m., both at Sand Springs, per the bracket. The other five classes on that date are below as the brackets list them. The sites and times appear on the brackets and are not matched to games here.

| Class | Semifinal matchups | Sites listed |
|---|---|---|
| 6A-II | Choctaw vs. Norman; Stillwater vs. Piedmont | Putnam City at 1 p.m., Edmond North at 7 p.m. |
| 4A | Broken Bow vs. Fort Gibson; Tuttle vs. Sallisaw | East Central University in Ada at 7 p.m., Booker T. Washington at 1 p.m. |
| B-I | Quinton vs. Dewar; Hollis vs. Caddo | Bristow and Pauls Valley, 7 p.m. |
| B-II | Texhoma vs. Pond Creek-Hunter; Oklahoma Bible vs. Davenport | Northwestern Oklahoma State University in Alva and Noble, 7 p.m. |
| C | Sharon-Mutual vs. Medford; Tipton vs. Sasakwa | Northwestern Oklahoma State University in Alva and Pauls Valley, 1 p.m. |

## How to watch and get tickets for the OSSAA finals

OSSAA's football page says to watch all of the football state semifinals on a pay-per-view from the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network on Friday, Nov. 27, and adds that "no other video live streams will be allowed." OSSAA has not posted how the finals will be shown.

The brackets say game tickets are available at the OSSAA website through GoFan, and the football page links GoFan for the playoffs. No ticket price is posted.
