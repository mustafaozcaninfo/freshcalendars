**Wendy's added two peppermint Frostys on Nov. 16, 2026, the Peppermint Frosty Swirl and the Peppermint Brownie Frosty Fusion, according to a release from The Wendy's Company.** They arrived with a new Holiday Box at participating U.S. restaurants, "for a limited time." Wendy's has not posted a price or an end date for the peppermint items. The fine print on the $3 Frosty Key Tag adds a limit the release does not stress: the free Jr. Frosty is "not valid for Frosty with mix-ins, swirls or toppings."

## What are the Wendy's Peppermint Frosty Swirl and Brownie Fusion?

The release, headed "Wendy's Invites Fans to 'Unfreeze the Season' With Its New Holiday Box" and dated Nov. 16, 2026 in Dublin, Ohio, describes two items. The Peppermint Frosty Swirl "combines Wendy's classic Vanilla or Chocolate Frosty with a swirl of peppermint sauce." The Peppermint Brownie Frosty Fusion "takes things up a notch, adding rich brownie pieces," in the company's words. Wendy's home page, read Nov. 18, 2026, points to a new "Peppermint Frosty® lineup."

Wendy's gives no size, price, calorie count or end date for either one. Our earlier [Wendy's Frosty Day story](/news/wendys-frosty-day-2026-november-15-small-frosty-app-offer/) noted that the flavors page listed no Peppermint Frosty as of Nov. 14, 2026, so Nov. 16 is the first date Wendy's gives.

## What does the Wendy's Holiday Box 2026 include?

The Holiday Box has a Dave's Double® "made with fresh, never frozen beef" or 10-piece Crispy or Spicy Chicken Nuggets, small Hot & Crispy Fries, a small soft drink and one of four collectible retro knit hats. The release says the hat comes in a holiday-themed blind bag, and the designs celebrate "Frosty," "Where's the Beef?," "Biggie" and Wendy's. The box is "starting at $11.99" and comes in Solarium-inspired packaging.

The footnote sets the limits: "Limited time only while supplies last. U.S. price and participation may vary. Prices may be higher for delivery. Knit hats may vary by restaurant, cannot be selected and are not available for individual sale." Wendy's does not give an end date or a list of restaurants.

## Does the $3 Frosty Key Tag cover the peppermint Frostys?

No. Wendy's sells the Frosty Key Tag for "only $3," which gives "exclusive access to a free Jr. Frosty with purchase on every visit to Wendy's throughout 2027." The footnote reads: "One (1) free Jr. Classic Frosty per transaction per visit per key tag with any in-restaurant purchase. Not valid for Frosty with mix-ins, swirls or toppings." A Peppermint Frosty Swirl has a swirl and the Brownie Fusion has brownie pieces, so neither is the plain Jr. Classic Frosty the tag covers. The home page describes the tag as a daily free Classic Jr. Frosty with purchase.

Three dates sit in that footnote and the release. Key tags are valid 11/11/2026 through 12/31/2027. Wendy's says 90% of every tag sold from 11/16/2026 to 1/31/2027 benefits the Dave Thomas Foundation for Adoption. The tag is sold "while supplies last" at participating U.S. restaurants. The 2026 tag we covered in the Frosty Day story ran to Dec. 31, 2026; this 2027 tag is a new one.

## Is the Wendy's New York City pop-up still open?

Wendy's built a Solarium at Flatiron Plaza, 5th Ave & W 23rd Street in New York City. It opened to the public on Tuesday, Nov. 17 from 4 to 8 p.m. and on Wednesday, Nov. 18 from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., giving away "one (1) free Holiday Box per guest while supplies last." Nov. 18 is the last day listed.

Tariq Hassan, Chief Marketing & Customer Growth Officer at Wendy's, said: "We're bringing that feeling back, from a real Solarium in New York City to a Holiday Meal fans can enjoy anywhere."

## Is there a Wendy's holiday value meal in 2026?

The release lists the $7.99 Dave's Single® Combo Meal "for a limited time" at participating U.S. restaurants: a made-to-order Dave's Single, small Hot & Crispy Fries and a small soft drink. Its footnote says price and participation may vary and the deal is not valid with any other offer or discount.
