The Stifel Killington Cup, a women's World Cup giant slalom on Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, and a slalom on Sunday, Nov. 29, is live on NBC Sports Network and NBC in the windows Killington Resort lists, with the second runs at 12:30 p.m. and 1:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET). The resort says the race "returns Thanksgiving weekend, November 27-29, 2026," and calls it "live on Peacock." The International Ski and Snowboard Federation (FIS) calendar lists the women's races at Killington, USA, on "28-29 Nov 2026" as "GS SL," giant slalom and slalom. This guide has the TV windows in four time zones, the weekend's events, tickets and parking, plus a printable watch sheet.

## Killington Cup TV times by time zone

Killington's broadcast table gives windows, not race start times. Its page shows no time zone. Sports Video Group (SVG) states the second runs in ET, so the table below uses ET, and the Central Time (CT), Pacific Time (PT) and Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) columns are converted from it. The GMT column is for fans in Europe; the printable adds a Mountain Time (MT) column instead.

| Day | Race and run | Network | ET | CT | PT | GMT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sat., Nov. 28 | Giant slalom, run 1 | NBC Sports Network | 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. | 8:30 to 11:30 a.m. | 6:30 to 9:30 a.m. | 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | Giant slalom, run 2 | NBC | 12:30 to 2 p.m. | 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. | 9:30 to 11 a.m. | 5:30 to 7 p.m. |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Slalom, run 1 | NBC Sports Network | 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. | 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. | 7:30 to 9:30 a.m. | 3:30 to 5:30 p.m. |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Slalom, run 2 | NBC | 1:30 to 2 p.m. | 12:30 to 1 p.m. | 10:30 to 11 a.m. | 6:30 to 7 p.m. |

All four are marked "Live" on Killington's page. The first runs fall inside their windows. Neither Killington nor FIS has posted start times for the runs, so check the FIS race schedule closer to the weekend. SVG's report on Oct. 22, 2026 gives the second run of the women's giant slalom at 12:30 p.m. ET and the second run of the women's slalom at 1:30 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock. It also says coverage of U.S. World Cups "begins with the Stifel Killington Cup on Nov. 28."

## How to watch the Killington World Cup

- **Peacock:** Killington says the race is live on Peacock.
- **NBC:** both second runs, 12:30 p.m. ET Saturday and 1:30 p.m. ET Sunday.
- **NBC Sports Network:** both first runs, 9:30 a.m. ET Saturday and 10:30 a.m. ET Sunday.

NBC Sports reported in May 2026 that the 2026-27 season has "three consecutive weekends of racing in the U.S. starting Thanksgiving weekend with Killington's return to hosting a women's giant slalom and slalom." Boston.com reported in February 2025 that there was no World Cup race there that year because of a chairlift installation, and that the race was "expected to return to Killington Thanksgiving Weekend 2026." So there are no 2025 results to look back on. No athlete names are listed here because no start list has been published.

## What happens Friday to Sunday at the Killington Cup

| Day | What Killington lists |
|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Free admission with live music from Congress The Band, the athlete bib draw and a fireworks display |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | Women's giant slalom; DJ Aluna "between runs of the giant slalom race" |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Women's slalom; Dillon Francis "between runs of the slalom race" |

The music is in the Festival Village behind the K-1 Lodge. Times for the bib draw and for opening the gates are not announced.

## Killington Cup tickets and parking

Killington's ticket page, checked Nov. 4, 2026, says "a valid ticket is required for all attendees, including infants and children." Admission is free on Friday, Nov. 27. Saturday and Sunday prices:

| Ticket | Saturday, Nov. 28 | Sunday, Nov. 29 |
|---|---|---|
| General Admission | $10 | $10 |
| Grandstand | $50 | $45 |
| Premier Grandstand | $110 | $100 |

Free parking and shuttles are available around Killington Resort, and "overnight parking is not permitted during Killington Cup weekend."
