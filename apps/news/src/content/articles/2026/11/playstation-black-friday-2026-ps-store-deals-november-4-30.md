**The PlayStation Store Black Friday sale runs through Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, and some items leave before then, according to PlayStation.Blog's Black Friday post by Sony Interactive Entertainment, revised Nov. 18.** The playstation.com deals page says the same end date: offers run until November 30, and products may have different sales periods. The revised post gives no clock time for the end, so the table below keeps the time from the Nov. 3 version and marks it as that.

PlayStation.Blog says the promotion offers up to 75% off on hundreds of PS5 and PS4 digital games. The post names titles in the sale, among them Madden NFL 27, Marvel's Wolverine, Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced and Resident Evil Requiem Deluxe Edition, but gives no game prices. It also lists accessory savings on direct.playstation.com where available, such as $20 off the DualSense wireless controller and $100 off PlayStation VR2. Game discounts appear on the store's Black Friday promotion page.

## What are the PlayStation Store Black Friday 2026 dates?

The revised post says "the sale runs through to Monday, November 30, and some items leave before the promotion's end." It says new titles joined the promotion on November 16, "starting midnight local time."

The version of the post we read on Nov. 14 labeled every time with Pacific Standard Time (PST), Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and Japan Standard Time (JST), written "PST/GMT/JST." It also gave a Nov. 4 start and a Nov. 20 end for certain discounts. The revised post no longer prints those lines.

| Step | Date | What the post said |
|---|---|---|
| Sale started | Wed., Nov. 4 | Nov. 3 version: began at 00:00 AM PST/GMT/JST |
| Promotion refreshed, new titles joined | Mon., Nov. 16 | Nov. 3 version: 00:00 AM PST/GMT/JST; revised post: midnight local time |
| Certain discounts were set to end | Fri., Nov. 20 | Nov. 3 version: ends at 23:59 PM PST/GMT/JST |
| Sale ends | Mon., Nov. 30 | Revised post: runs through Monday, November 30; Nov. 3 version: ends at 23:59 PM PST/GMT/JST |

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, and the sale's last day, Nov. 30, is also Cyber Monday. Our [when is Black Friday](/when-is/black-friday/) page has the retail date.

## What time does the PlayStation Black Friday sale end in ET, PT and GMT?

The revised post gives no clock time. The Nov. 3 version read 23:59 PST/GMT/JST for the end. Our reading is that each store uses its own local clock at 23:59: Pacific Time in the U.S. store, GMT in the UK store and JST in the Japan store. Neither version spells that out, so confirm the time on your own PlayStation Store.

Pacific Standard Time (UTC-8) is in effect, so Eastern Time (ET, UTC-5) is three hours ahead of Pacific Time (PT). Conversions below are ours and apply the Nov. 3 version's 23:59.

| Step | PT (U.S. store) | Mountain Time (MT) | Central Time (CT) | ET | GMT (UK store) | JST (Japan store) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sale ends | Mon., Nov. 30, 11:59 p.m. | 12:59 a.m. Tue., Dec. 1 | 1:59 a.m. Tue., Dec. 1 | 2:59 a.m. Tue., Dec. 1 | Mon., Nov. 30, 23:59 | Mon., Nov. 30, 23:59 |

For East Coast shoppers the deadline would fall after midnight, so the last full evening to buy is Monday, Nov. 30.

## Which PlayStation Store discounts leave before Nov. 30?

The post says only that "some items leave before the promotion's end." The Nov. 3 version said certain discounts would end on November 20 at 23:59 PM PST/GMT/JST and that some deals would be replaced with new offers, without saying which. On the PlayStation Store, check the end date shown on each game before you count on the Nov. 30 finish. The printable has a column to mark each wish-list item that leaves early.

## What do the deals page and other stores say?

The playstation.com Black Friday page at playstation.com/en-us/deals/black-friday/ now says "until November 30" and that products may have different sales periods. On Nov. 14, 2026, it still carried a placeholder message with no dates.

Other platforms, as read on their official pages on Nov. 25:

- Xbox: Xbox Wire says the sale started Nov. 19 at 9 p.m. PT and runs through Dec. 2.
- Nintendo: its Nov. 12 release lists a sale on Nintendo eShop and the Nintendo Store website from Nov. 19 through Dec. 2.
- Steam: Valve's Steamworks page says Steam is not hosting an official Black Friday fest, and its Discounts and Events page acts as a hub from Nov. 26 at 10 a.m. PT through Nov. 30 at 10 a.m. PT. Valve's dated sales are in our [Steam Winter Sale 2026 dates](/news/steam-winter-sale-2026-dates-december-17-steam-sale-calendar-2027/) story.

For other games coming this month, see the [November 2026 video game release dates](/news/video-game-release-dates-november-2026-gta-6-ocarina-of-time/) and the [PlayStation Plus November 2026 games](/news/playstation-plus-november-2026-games-dead-island-2-tomb-raider-monster-train-2/).
