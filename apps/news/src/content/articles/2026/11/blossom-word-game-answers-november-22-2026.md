**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, puts B in the center with A, E, I, L, R and Y around it.** Three published solution pages agree on eight pangrams. Lengths, a letter check and graded hints come first.

## Sunday's letters and the center B

- **Center:** B
- **Petals:** A, E, I, L, R, Y

Three vowel letters, A, E and I, plus Y give the flower plenty of ways to join B to L and R. Saturday's letters, with center S, are on the [Nov. 21 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-21-2026/), and with eight pangrams in play today, the rules for finding them are on our [Blossom page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works).

## Eight pangrams at 7, 8, 9 and 11 letters

Merriam-Webster shows no official word list, so these counts come from the three solution pages that agree. Only one word reaches 11 letters and one reaches 9. The 7-letter word is the floor, and five words share the 8-letter length.

| Letters | Pangrams | Spare slots beyond seven |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 1 | 0 |
| 8 | 5 | 1 |
| 9 | 1 | 2 |
| 11 | 1 | 4 |

Counting each word's endings gives the day's pattern. Five of the eight end in the same two letters: three of the 8-letter words, the 9-letter word and the 11-letter word. Of those five, the 9-letter and 11-letter words are built on the same stem, the longer one with a two-letter piece added at the front.

## Check a find against the seven letters

Tick B first, since a word without it never counts, then the six petals.

| Tick | Letter | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | B | Center |
| 2 | A | Petal |
| 3 | E | Petal |
| 4 | I | Petal |
| 5 | L | Petal |
| 6 | R | Petal |
| 7 | Y | Petal |

A find with seven ticks is a pangram. A word with six petal ticks and no B is rejected, however long it is.

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- **Hint 1:** Eight pangrams are listed, and one is eleven letters long.
- **Hint 2:** Five of them have exactly eight letters.
- **Hint 3:** Five pangrams finish with the same two letters, and Y is the last letter of each.
- **Hint 4:** The petal that repeats most often is the one just before that Y.
- **Hint 5:** Five of the eight words open with the center letter.

## Odd words and plain ones among the eight

Two of the eight are science words, one for a double layer and one a chemistry term built on a metal's name. For those, the ending pattern is the faster route. Of the other six, five work as adverbs or adjectives and one is an old Scottish noun for an official's district; the longest is the negative form of a shorter one on the list.

### Pangram

The solution pages list these eight. Each holds B, A, E, I, L, R and Y at least once.

- **Illiberally** (11 letters)
- **Liberally** (9 letters)
- **Bailiery** (8 letters)
- **Beryllia** (8 letters)
- **Biyearly** (8 letters)
- **Blearily** (8 letters)
- **Reliably** (8 letters)
- **Bilayer** (7 letters)

Liberally sits inside Illiberally, which adds IL at the front. Blearily, Reliably, Biyearly, Liberally and Illiberally all end in LY. Bilayer is a two-layer sheet, as in a cell membrane, and Beryllia is beryllium oxide. Bilayer uses each letter once, and Illiberally uses L four times.
