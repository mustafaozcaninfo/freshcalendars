**The Illinois High School Association (IHSA) bracket pages, last updated at 8:20 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, list all eight football state finals at Hancock Stadium in Normal: Classes 1A to 4A on Friday, Nov. 27, and 5A to 8A on Saturday, Nov. 28.** Kickoffs on both days are at 10 a.m., 1 p.m., 4 p.m. and 7 p.m. Central Time (CT). Every matchup, with Eastern Time (ET) kickoffs, is in the table, and a printable schedule follows it.

## IHSA football state finals 2026: matchups and kickoff times

Each IHSA class page prints the day, the site ("Normal (Hancock Stadium)") and the hour, for example "Fri 11/27 Normal (Hancock Stadium) 10 am", without a time zone. Sports Illustrated's Oct. 27, 2026 playoff guide lists the same days and hours and says "All kickoff times below are Central." The ET column adds one hour to each kickoff. Numbers in parentheses are playoff seeds from the IHSA bracket. Teams are named as IHSA lists them, town first with the school in parentheses where the bracket gives one.

| Class | Day | Kickoff (CT) | Kickoff (ET) | Matchup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | Fri., Nov. 27 | 10 a.m. | 11 a.m. | (1) Heyworth vs. (1) Mt. Sterling (Brown County) |
| 2A | Fri., Nov. 27 | 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | (5) Wilmington vs. (2) West Frankfort (Frankfort) |
| 3A | Fri., Nov. 27 | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | (3) Elmhurst (IC Catholic) vs. (2) St. Joseph (Ogden) |
| 4A | Fri., Nov. 27 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | (1) Morris vs. (4) Springfield (Sacred Heart-Griffin) |
| 5A | Sat., Nov. 28 | 10 a.m. | 11 a.m. | (6) Cary (Cary-Grove) vs. (11) New Lenox (Providence Catholic) |
| 6A | Sat., Nov. 28 | 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | (6) Oak Park (Fenwick) vs. (20) LaGrange Park (Nazareth Academy) |
| 7A | Sat., Nov. 28 | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | (8) Chicago (Brother Rice) vs. (2) Arlington Heights (Hersey) |
| 8A | Sat., Nov. 28 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | (8) Chicago (Mt. Carmel) vs. (7) Winnetka (New Trier) |

The bracket does not show which team is the home team, and it does not print records, so neither is listed.

## Which day is your school playing?

Fans of Classes 1A to 4A plan for Friday, Nov. 27, the day after Thanksgiving. The [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) confirms the holiday is Thursday, Nov. 26. Classes 5A to 8A play Saturday, Nov. 28. Each day has the same four kickoff hours, from 10 a.m. CT to 7 p.m. CT.

## Who won the IHSA finals in 2025?

Two 2026 finalists have a 2025 final behind them. The FOX 32 Chicago report from Nov. 29, 2025 lists these results for Classes 1A to 4A. The 2025 champions of Classes 5A to 8A are not listed here.

| Class | 2025 result |
|---|---|
| 1A | Lena-Winslow 58, Brown County 13 |
| 2A | Wilmington 42, Maroa-Forsyth 6 |
| 3A | Byron 56, Tolono Unity 50 |
| 4A | Montini 47, Rochester 33 |

Wilmington, the 2025 Class 2A champion, is back in the 2A final against West Frankfort. Brown County, the school behind Mt. Sterling in the 1A bracket, returns to the 1A final after losing it in 2025.

## How to watch the IHSA football finals

IHSA has not named a 2026 broadcaster on its site. No channel or stream is confirmed in this story, and 2026 ticket prices, gate times and parking were not on the pages read.
