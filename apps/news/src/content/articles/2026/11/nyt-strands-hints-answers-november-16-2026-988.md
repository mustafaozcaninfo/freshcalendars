**Strands #988 from The New York Times (NYT) is the puzzle for Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, and its theme clue is "What's the big idea?"** Today's board hides six theme words and a 14-letter spangram, and Christina Iverson constructed it with Tracy Bennett as editor. New to the game? Read [our Strands page](/news/topic/nyt-strands/#how-it-works) for the rules. Hints come first, and the answers wait in a marked spoiler section at the end.

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: It is a spoken phrase of three words, a cheerful remark that plays on the theme.
- Hint 2: Fourteen letters, starting with T and ending with L.
- Hint 3: Its first letter sits in the bottom-left corner of the board.

## Theme word hints

Read the clue's word "big" literally. There are six theme words, listed here from shortest to longest:

- **4 letters, starts with G:** what a seedling does in spring.
- **5 letters, starts with B:** the puffy, overfull feeling after a heavy meal.
- **5 letters, starts with S:** a sudden rush upward, like a wave of electricity.
- **6 letters, starts with E:** what a company does when it opens new branches.
- **7 letters, starts with E:** to make a photo or a print bigger.
- **7 letters, starts with I:** what you do to a tire or a balloon with a pump.

## Where the spangram runs on Monday's board

Rows below count from the top and columns from the left. The spangram starts in the bottom-left corner (row 8, column 1) and runs along the bottom row for three tiles. It then climbs the third column from row 8 up to row 2, turns right along row 2 to column 6, and ends in row 1, column 5. That path touches the bottom and top edges of the board.

The theme words sit on either side of that vertical line. Three of them touch the left edge between row 1 and row 7, and the longest of that trio also stretches along the top row as far as column 4. The other three fill columns 4 to 6 from row 3 down, and the shortest, at four letters, takes the last two rows of that side. The six theme words use 34 letters, which with the 14-letter spangram fills all 48 tiles.

## Solving tips

Start with the spangram, since its path splits the board in two. Any ordinary words you spell, three at a time, earn a hint that lights up the letters of a theme word. Work the left side and the right side separately: below row 1, no theme word crosses the spangram's column.

## Today's Strands answers (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it yourself.

- **Spangram:** That's Just Swell
- **Theme words:** Bloat, Enlarge, Expand, Grow, Inflate, Surge

All six answers fit the clue: each theme word means to get bigger, and the spangram puns on the theme, since swell also means to get bigger. The spangram is a single string of 14 letters on the board, with no spaces or apostrophe.
