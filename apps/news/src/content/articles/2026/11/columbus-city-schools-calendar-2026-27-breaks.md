**Columbus City Schools (CCS) has no school from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027**, and spring break runs **Friday, March 26 through Friday, April 2, 2027**, according to the "26-27 CCS Traditional Calendar" on the district's website. Classes start Thursday, Aug. 20, 2026 for Grades 1-12, and the last day for students is Thursday, May 27, 2027. Other listings of this calendar differ; this story follows the district's own PDF, a PDF created on Oct. 7, 2026 and linked from the district's calendar page.

## Columbus City Schools break dates for 2026-27

The weekday counts and return days below are our reading of the PDF. All of these are labeled "All Schools Closed".

| Break | Dates | Weekdays off | Back in class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wellness Week | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | After the break; no closure is listed for Mon., Jan. 4 |
| Spring break | Fri., March 26 to Fri., April 2, 2027 | 6 | Mon., April 5 |

The district's PDF calls the Nov. 23-27 closure "Wellness Week" and does not say "Thanksgiving"; Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, falls inside it. The same week also lists Wednesday, Nov. 25 as "Compensatory Day for Parent Conferences". See the [Thanksgiving break 2026 roundup](/news/thanksgiving-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) for other districts.

The [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) and the [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) compare other districts.

## When do students return after Columbus City Schools winter break?

The calendar does not state a return date. It lists no closure for Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, so classes resume after the break. Counting weekends, students are out from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Sunday, Jan. 3, which is 16 days. Friday, Dec. 18 is a "30 Minute Early Dismissal". The district has not posted a board approval date on its calendar page, so none is given here.

## Which Columbus City Schools days off do the named breaks miss?

The calendar lists more no-student days than its three breaks. Some are full closures and some are teacher or records days with "Students not in attendance". Pre-K has its own extra days.

| Date | Weekday | What the calendar lists | Who is off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | All Schools Closed - Labor Day | All students |
| Oct. 16, 2026 | Friday | Teacher Workday & Professional Development (PD) Day | All students |
| Oct. 19, 2026 | Monday | Records Day | All students |
| Nov. 2, 2026 | Monday | No Pre-K - Professional Development | Pre-K only |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Teacher Workday & PD Day | All students |
| Jan. 15, 2027 | Friday | Records Day | All students |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | All Schools Closed - Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) Day | All students |
| Jan. 19, 2027 | Tuesday | No Pre-K - Professional Development | Pre-K only |
| Feb. 15, 2027 | Monday | Compensatory Day for Parent Conferences (Presidents' Day is not named) | All students |
| March 25, 2027 | Thursday | Records Day | All students |
| May 7, 2027 | Friday | No Pre-K - Professional Development | Pre-K only |
| May 28, 2027 | Friday | Records Day | All students |
| May 31, 2027 | Monday | All Schools Closed - Memorial Day | All students |
| June 18, 2027 | Friday | All Schools Closed - Juneteenth (Observed) | All students |

Pre-Kindergarten (Pre-K) is the one group the calendar treats separately; the other rows apply to every student. Our [printable school-year calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html) page and the printable below mark the all-student days.

## How long is Columbus City Schools spring break 2027?

Six weekdays, from Friday, March 26 through Friday, April 2. Students are also out on Thursday, March 25, a Records Day, after a 30-minute early dismissal on Wednesday, March 24 that the calendar pairs with the end of the third quarter. Counting weekends, that is 11 days away from classes, Thursday, March 25 through Sunday, April 4.

## What are the first and last days of the Columbus City Schools year?

Professional development days are Aug. 17-19, 2026. Students in Grades 1-12 start Thursday, Aug. 20; kindergarten starts Tuesday, Aug. 25; Pre-K starts Tuesday, Sept. 8. The last day for students is Thursday, May 27, 2027, followed by a Records Day on Friday, May 28. The calendar lists quarter ends on Oct. 15, Jan. 14 and March 24.

## How does Woodcrest's year-round calendar differ?

Woodcrest Elementary is the district's only year-round school, with its own "26-27 CCS Woodcrest Calendar". It shares the winter and spring break, Memorial Day and Juneteenth closures, but it starts earlier, adds four intersessions and ends later.

| | Traditional calendar | Woodcrest |
|---|---|---|
| First day, Grades 1-12 or 1-5 | Aug. 20, 2026 | July 23, 2026 (Grades 1-5) |
| First day, kindergarten | Aug. 25, 2026 | July 28, 2026 (with Pre-K) |
| Intersessions | None on the PDF | Sep. 28-Oct. 9, Jan. 4-8, Mar. 22-25, Apr. 5-9 |
| Last day for students | May 27, 2027 | June 2, 2027 |

For a Woodcrest family, Monday, Jan. 4 is the first day of an intersession, not a return to class. Check which calendar your child's school follows.

## Can the Columbus City Schools calendar change?

Yes. The calendar's footer says it is "subject to revision due to emergencies created by severe weather, energy problems, or other public calamities that force the cancellation of classes." No make-up days are listed, so check the [district calendar page](https://www.ccsoh.us/calendar) for changes.
