**The Country Music Association (CMA) special "CMA Country Christmas," hosted by Lady A, airs Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026 at 9/8c on American Broadcasting Company (ABC), according to a CMA and ABC release dated Nov. 4, 2026.** That is 9 p.m. Eastern Time (ET). The special streams the next day on Disney+ and Hulu, and ABC's show page lists the show on Tuesday, Dec. 1 at 9/8c as of Nov. 13.

## What time is CMA Country Christmas 2026 in each time zone?

"9/8c" is television shorthand for 9 p.m. in the Eastern and Pacific zones and 8 p.m. Central Time (CT).

| Time zone | Time on Tue., Dec. 1, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | 9 p.m. |
| Central (CT) | 8 p.m. |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 9 p.m. |
| Mountain, Alaska, Hawaii | Check your local ABC station |

TV Insider and Laughing Place, both citing ABC's holiday schedule on Oct. 27, 2026, list the slot as 9 to 10:01 p.m. ET. The release gives no runtime.

## What channel is CMA Country Christmas on, and can you stream it?

ABC carries it live. The release says it is "streaming next day on Disney+ and Hulu," which is Wednesday, Dec. 2. The release says the special is filmed in front of a live audience in Nashville and is the 17th annual holiday television special.

## Who performs on CMA Country Christmas 2026?

Lady A hosts and is also on the list. The release names ten performers.

| Performer | Role |
|---|---|
| BeBe Winans | Performer |
| Dan + Shay | Performer |
| Debbie Winans | Performer |
| Grace Bowers | Performer |
| Jordan Davis | Performer |
| Lady A | Host and performer |
| Lindsey Stirling | Performer |
| Russell Dickerson | Performer |
| Trisha Yearwood | Performer |
| Zach Top | Performer |

Songs for each performer are not yet announced. The release credits Robert Deaton as executive producer, Alan Carter as director and Jon Macks as writer.
