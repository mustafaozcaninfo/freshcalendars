**Sunday's Pips from The New York Times (NYT), Nov. 8, 2026, has four dead-end cells on the Easy board, enough to fix every domino's position.** Ian Livengood made Easy and Medium; Rodolfo Kurchan made Hard. Sunday's Hard asks four cells to add up to 0, a rule [our Pips page](/news/topic/nyt-pips/#how-it-works) explains with the rest. Each placement in the answer section matches the Times' data.

## Where each Pips board starts on Nov. 8

Read cell names as row then column: R3C2 is the third row down, second column.

**Easy.** R1C1, R3C2, R1C6 and R3C5 each touch a single cell, so their dominoes are set, and R1C3 with R1C4 is the pair left over. R2C2 and R3C2 share a domino inside an equal region, so it must be a double: 6-6 or 2-2. With the 6-6 there, R1C2 would need the 6 of the 6-2, pushing its 2 into R1C1, which must be more than 4. So the 2-2 fills that column.

**Medium.** R1C3 and R1C4 must total 11, which takes a 5 and a 6, and the 5-2 holds the board's only 5. That domino can't lie inside the pair (it totals 7), so its 5 goes in R1C3 or R1C4 and its 2 spills into R1C2 or R1C5. Each of those sits in an equal pair with the cell below, which then needs a second 2, and only the 2-1 has one.

**Hard.** R4C3 touches only R3C3, and together they add up to 6. Two dominoes total 6 by themselves: the 1-5 and the 0-6. Using the 0-6 there would leave a single 6 for R1C4 and R2C1, which both must be 6. So the 1-5 covers R3C3 and R4C3.

## Sunday's Pips hints, Nov. 8, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 6-6, 2-3, 6-2, 2-2, 3-5)**

- Hint 1: R1C1 and R1C6 must each be more than 4, and the only values that high are 5 and 6.
- Hint 2: R1C3 and R1C4 add up to 8 and share a domino. Two dominoes total 8 on their own: the 6-2 and the 3-5.

**Medium (eight dominoes: 5-2, 4-0, 6-3, 6-1, 2-1, 1-1, 6-6, 1-4)**

- Hint 1: R2C1 and R4C6 must each be exactly 4. The 4-0 and the 1-4 are the only dominoes with a 4.
- Hint 2: R3C1 and R4C1 total less than 3, and the board's single 0 is on the 4-0.

**Hard (fifteen dominoes on a six-row board)**

- Hint 1: R2C4, R3C4, R3C5 and R3C6 add up to 0, so all four are 0s. Eight halves show a 0, two of them on the 0-0.
- Hint 2: R5C1, R6C1, R6C2 and R6C3 must match in the bottom-left corner. Six halves show a 2, including both on the 2-2.

## Reading the boards before you check

Medium has five 1s and two pairs that add up to 2, so most of the 1s go there; the 1-1 double fits either pair. On Hard, R2C5 and R2C6 must reach 10 between them, and R6C4 with R6C5 must stay under 6. Easy has just one blank cell, R3C5.
