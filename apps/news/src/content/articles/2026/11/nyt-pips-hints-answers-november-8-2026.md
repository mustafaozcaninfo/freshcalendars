## How Pips works

You place every domino so that each colored region meets its condition. A number means the pips in the region add up to it; = means every half shows the same value; ≠ means all are different; > and < set a total above or below a number; a region with no condition takes anything. Rows are counted from the top and columns from the left, so R2C5 is the second row, fifth column.

## Sunday's Pips hints, Nov. 8, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 6-6, 2-3, 6-2, 2-2, 3-5)**

- Hint 1: R1C1 and R1C6 must each be more than 4, so each holds a 5 or a 6. Four halves are that big: the 5 on the 3-5 domino and three 6s, two of them on the 6-6 double.
- Hint 2: R1C2, R2C2 and R3C2 are equal, so you need three matching halves. Only the 2 (four halves) and the 6 (three halves) show up that often.

**Medium (eight dominoes: 5-2, 4-0, 6-3, 6-1, 2-1, 1-1, 6-6, 1-4)**

- Hint 1: R2C1 and R4C6 must each add up to 4, so each holds a 4. Only two halves show a 4, on the 4-0 and 1-4 dominoes.
- Hint 2: R1C3 and R1C4 add up to 11, which takes a 5 and a 6. The only 5 is on the 5-2 domino.

**Hard (fifteen dominoes on a six-row board)**

- Hint 1: R2C4, R3C4, R3C5 and R3C6 must add up to 0, so all four hold a 0. Eight halves show a 0, and the 0-0 double supplies two of them at once.
- Hint 2: R1C4 and R2C1 must each add up to 6. Only two halves show a 6, on the 0-6 and 4-6 dominoes, so these two cells use both.

## Solving tips

Look for single cells with a limit: a cell that must be more than 4, as R1C1 is on Sunday's Easy board, can only hold a 5 or a 6, and few halves are that big. An "equal" region shrinks your options fast once you count the halves: on the Easy board, three matching halves are needed, and only the 2 and the 6 appear that often. On the Medium board, two cells need a 4 and only two 4s exist. On the Hard board, 19 of the 30 cells have a fixed total, seven sit in the two equal regions and four sit under a less-than limit. The 0 appears on eight halves, more than any other value, and the 2 follows with six. The 3 and the 6 each appear twice, so place them early.
