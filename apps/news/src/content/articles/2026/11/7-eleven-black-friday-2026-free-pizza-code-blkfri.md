**7-Eleven's code BLKFRI gives a free large pizza on delivery orders of $30 or more on Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, 7-Eleven, Inc. said in a Nov. 17 release distributed by PR Newswire.** The same release, datelined Irving, Texas, adds a Thanksgiving code (THANKFUL) and a Cyber Monday code (LOADUP). The codes are for 7NOW delivery, and THANKFUL also covers pickup. The brands named are 7-Eleven, Speedway and Stripes.

## 7-Eleven Black Friday, Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday codes

| Date | Code | Offer | Minimum order | Order type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26 | THANKFUL | $20 off | $30 | Delivery or pickup |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | BLKFRI | Free large pizza | $30 | Delivery |
| Mon., Nov. 30 | LOADUP | $25 off | $50 | Delivery |

Source: 7-Eleven release, Nov. 17, 2026; Convenience.org (National Association of Convenience Stores), Nov. 18, 2026, lists the same codes. Each offer is "valid only" on its date. For Black Friday the release says "Small basket fees may apply" and "Convenience fees will apply." The release's fine print for the pizza offer also says Nov. 27. A Friendsgiving code (FRIENDS) was valid only Nov. 21 and is over.

| Date | Code or offer | Minimum order | Who can use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26 | THANKFUL, $20 off | $30 | 7NOW delivery or pickup |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | BLKFRI, free large pizza | $30 | 7NOW delivery |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | 7Collection.com 25% off sitewide ends (started Nov. 23) | None given | Online shoppers |
| Mon., Nov. 30 | LOADUP, $25 off | $50 | 7NOW delivery |

| Tue., Dec. 1 to Fri., Dec. 25 | 7NOW digital advent calendar | $20 basket | Gold Pass members, limit 1 per order |
| Thu., Dec. 17 to Wed., Dec. 30 | $1.25 snacks | None given | Participating stores |
| Through Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | $1 Snack Pies; two Iced Cappuccinos for $6; two Red Bull Winter Edition for $6.50 | None given | Participating stores |

Source: 7-Eleven releases, Nov. 17 and Nov. 24, 2026. The snack pie, Iced Cappuccino and Red Bull deals started Oct. 28. 7Collection.com is "25% off sitewide, plus additional markdowns of up to 50% on select items." The Iced Cappuccino deal reads "Plus tax."

In 2025, 7-Eleven's Thanksgiving code (THANKS) was also $20 off $30, valid Nov. 27, 2025, per its Nov. 17, 2025 release. The 2026 code is THANKFUL, and the date is Nov. 26.

## What else is on 7-Eleven's holiday menu?

The Nov. 24 release adds a limited-time Cinnamon Churro 7-Select Iced Cappuccino. Two 7-Select Iced Cappuccinos cost $6, and two 12-ounce Red Bull Winter Edition drinks cost $6.50, both valid Oct. 28 to Jan. 5, 2027. The $1.25 snacks are 7-Select chocolate bars, Rice Krispies Treats and Quaker Chewy Bars, valid only Dec. 17-30.

7Rewards and Speedy Rewards members can get a large coffee and a bakery item for $3, with peppermint mocha creamer, or brown sugar cinnamon cappuccino syrup or powder, as add-ons. The Nov. 24 release lists the $3 deal as valid Oct. 28, 2026 to Jan. 5, 2027, plus tax, for members only, and does not name the bakery item.
