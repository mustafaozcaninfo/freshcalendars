**Joel Fagliano is the constructor of the New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026: a 5-by-5 grid with 10 clues and black squares in two opposite corners.** Every clue has a hint that opens on a tap, and the filled grid waits in the marked spoiler section at the bottom.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *Eye color of white tigers*: a primary color, the same one as a clear daytime sky; it starts with B and ends with E.
- **5-Across** (5 letters), *Home to the largest urban zoo in the United States, with "the"*: one of New York City's boroughs; it starts with B, in the same square where 5-Down begins, and ends with X.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Mayonnaise-like condiment*: a garlic-flavored sauce; four of its five letters are vowels.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Not suited for leadership*: a negative prefix joined to a word for being in good shape; it starts with U and ends with T, the letter that closes 4-Down.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *What Chik'n Nuggets and Beyond Burgers lack*: the animal product that plant-based foods leave out; it starts with M and ends with T.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Pickle juice*: the salty liquid a cucumber soaks in; it starts with B and ends with E.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Shower scrubber*: a rough bath sponge made from the fiber of a gourd; its second and third letters are the same.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Dark, as a room*: the state of a space with no lights on; it begins with the same two letters as 7-Across and ends with T.
- **4-Down** (4 letters), *Door to the outside*: the way you leave a building; it starts with E and ends with T.
- **5-Down** (4 letters), *L. Frank ___, author of "The Wonderful Wizard of Oz"*: the author's surname; it starts with B and ends with M.

## The "with the" clue, a fill-in-the-blank and a new corner layout

Start with 5-Across if you want the day's one trick. The clue does not ask for the zoo; it asks for the place that hosts it, and the closing words "with the" say that place is normally named with an article in front. The article is not part of the entry. Once you read it that way, every crossing letter confirms it, since the entry crosses all five Down entries.

Sorted by what they ask for, the 10 clues fall into these groups:

| Clue type | Count | Clues |
|---|---|---|
| Plain definition or description | 7 | 1-Across, 6-Across, 7-Across, 1-Down, 2-Down, 3-Down, 4-Down |
| Wordplay on the printed words | 1 | 5-Across |
| Brand-name clue | 1 | 8-Across |
| Fill-in-the-blank | 1 | 5-Down |
| Question mark | 0 | none |

5-Down is the only clue with a dotted gap, and it supplies the author's first initial and the book title. Wednesday's Mini had no blank at all.

The constructor changes again: Wednesday's grid was by Ian Livengood, today's is by Joel Fagliano. Counted from the saved data, the two grids share a square count but not a shape:

Both grids have the same 23 white squares, yet today's has no three-letter entry at all, and the corners sit on a diagonal. The four short entries (1-Across, 8-Across, 4-Down and 5-Down) each cross four others, while each of the six five-letter entries crosses five.
