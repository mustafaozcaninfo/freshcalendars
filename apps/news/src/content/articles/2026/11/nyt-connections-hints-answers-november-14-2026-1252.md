## Today's 16 words

Complex, Chess, Battle, Advanced, Marriage, Progressive, Center, Mottle, Go, Facility, Modern, Othello, Trib, Stratego, Campus, Foremost

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Each means ahead of the times, the newest of its kind.
- Hint 2: One also means first in rank; one is a political label for reform-minded voters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Each names the grounds or building where a school, hospital or company is based.
- Hint 2: One is the land and buildings of a university; one can also mean complicated.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Each is a game for two players on a board, won by thinking several moves ahead.
- Hint 2: One shares its name with a Shakespeare tragedy; one is played with black and white stones on a grid of lines.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Change the first letter of each one to get something a new parent buys.
- Hint 2: One turns into where a baby sleeps; one turns into what a baby drinks from.

## Solving tips

Find the two-letter tile first and ask what it could be besides a verb. That opens the tabletop group quickly.

Battle words will distract you, and the fighting theme is not a group. A tile that looks wrong on every reading may need a single new letter at its front.

## Today's Connections answers (spoilers)

Look away now if you want to finish on your own.

- **Yellow, Cutting-Edge:** Advanced, Foremost, Modern, Progressive

- **Blue, Strategy Board Games:** Chess, Go, Othello, Stratego

## Why the traps worked

Battle sits beside Chess and Stratego and looks like the theme of a war game. Swap its B for an R and you get Rattle, a baby toy.

Complex can mean complicated, which makes it a fit for Advanced, yet here it is a building, like Campus. Marriage might seem to pair with Othello, a tragedy about a marriage, until a new first letter turns it into Carriage. Center is also the part of a chessboard both players fight over.
