**Trader Joe's is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, according to Yahoo Shopping, FOX 4 Dallas-Fort Worth and Voz.** The site traderjoes.com blocked automated access, so no Trader Joe's page was read for this story, and the company has not announced hours for the other holidays.

## What do the 2026 reports say about Trader Joe's on Thanksgiving?

Three newsrooms agree on Thanksgiving Day.

- Yahoo Shopping, in a story updated Nov. 18, 2026: "Trader Joe's: Closed on Thanksgiving Day."
- FOX 4 Dallas-Fort Worth, Nov. 19, 2026: "Dallas and Fort Worth locations list Thanksgiving Day as closed on their store pages." That covers Dallas-Fort Worth stores only.
- Voz, Nov. 19, 2026: "Publix, Aldi and Trader Joe's: will remain closed on Thanksgiving."

Hours vary by store, so check your store's page on traderjoes.com before you go. [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26.

## Was Trader Joe's open on Thanksgiving 2025?

No. FOX 4 wrote on Nov. 22, 2025: "Trader Joe's will be closed on Thanksgiving Day at all locations." The same story said stores "will observe normal operating hours in the days leading up to the holiday." CBS News, Nov. 27, 2025, also listed it as closed all day Thursday. One report, from FOX 2 on Nov. 26, 2025, gave Black Friday 2025 hours of 8 a.m. to 9 p.m.

## Is Trader Joe's open on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day?

Hours for Christmas Eve (Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026) and Christmas Day (Friday, Dec. 25) are not announced. In 2025, CBS News wrote "Trader Joe's is open, but will close at 5 p.m. on Christmas Eve," and AOL (Parade) said it "will close early at 5 p.m." AOL and FOX 2 reported stores closed on Christmas Day 2025. Treat those as last year's pattern and confirm with your store. Other chains are in the [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 store hours](/news/christmas-eve-christmas-day-2026-store-hours/) list, and the dates sit on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Is Trader Joe's open on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day?

Not announced for 2026. One report, CBS News on Dec. 31, 2025, said Trader Joe's was "open until 5 p.m." on New Year's Eve 2025. No newsroom read for this story confirmed New Year's Day hours, so Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 stays unconfirmed. For other grocers, see [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/) and [Is Publix open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/is-publix-open-thanksgiving-2026-christmas-eve-hours/). Restaurants are in [restaurants open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/restaurants-open-thanksgiving-2026-hours-by-chain/). The [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) marks the week.

## What Trader Joe's has not announced

Not yet announced for 2026: hours for Wednesday, Nov. 25, Black Friday (Nov. 27), the weekend of Nov. 28 and 29, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day. Standard daily hours are left out because no official page was read.
