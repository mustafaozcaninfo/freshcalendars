**Burger King's Peanuts holiday menu starts Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026, at participating U.S. restaurants while supplies last, according to a Burger King release dated Thursday, Nov. 19.** Burger King lists an adult Peanuts Pack, a King Jr. meal for kids, two Snoopy beanies and eight collectible toys. Guests can order on BK.com, through the BK App or at select participating U.S. restaurants. Dexerto and The Takeout gave the same Dec. 1 start on Nov. 19.

For how other chains compare, see the [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/).

## What is in the Burger King Peanuts Pack and King Jr. meal?

| Part | Peanuts Pack | King Jr. Peanuts meal |
|---|---|---|
| Entree | Whopper with Cheese or 10-piece Chicken Nuggets with Woodstock Golden Ranch | Not listed in the release |
| Side | Charlie Brown Loaded Potato Tots: coin-shaped tots with cheese, bacon bits, sour cream and chives | Not listed in the release |
| Dessert | An all-new Snoopy pie with a chocolate cookie crust and vanilla flavored creme filling | Not listed in the release |
| Drink | Medium fountain drink | Not listed in the release |
| Collectible | One of two Snoopy holiday beanies | One of 8 Peanuts toys |
| Packaging | Collectible Snoopy Doghouse Box | Not listed in the release |

Source: Burger King release, Nov. 19, 2026. The release gives no tot count.

## Which Burger King Snoopy beanies and toys can you collect?

The two beanies come one per Peanuts Pack. The King Jr. meal has one toy per meal while supplies last.

| Collectible | Description |
|---|---|
| Brown beanie | A cozy brown knit beanie with Snoopy resting atop his red doghouse |
| Red beanie | A bright red knit beanie with Snoopy in his Santa hat |
| Peanuts toys | Charlie Brown, Lucy, Linus, Sally and three festive Snoopy designs in his holiday best; the release counts 8 toys in all |

The release says each King Jr. meal has "one of 8 collectible holiday Peanuts toys." The release names Charlie Brown, Lucy, Linus, Sally and three Snoopy designs.

## How much does the Burger King Peanuts menu cost?

Burger King has not announced prices, Dexerto reported on Nov. 19. The Takeout, after a media tasting, reported $14.99 for the Peanuts Pack with a Whopper and $12.99 with nuggets, plus $2 for the beanie as an add-on. Those are The Takeout's figures, not Burger King's, and prices vary by restaurant.

## Burger King Peanuts menu dates to mark

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| Thursday, Nov. 19 | Burger King announces the menu |
