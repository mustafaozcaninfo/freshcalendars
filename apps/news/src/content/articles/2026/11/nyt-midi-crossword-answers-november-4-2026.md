## The trickiest wording: 11-Down

Two clues end in a question mark: 11-Down and the long 27-Across. The 27-Across clue is the theme clue, covered above. The other one is "Walking stick?", and it is the better test of wordplay. Read plainly, it suggests a cane or a hiking staff. The question mark says the plain reading is wrong. Ask what kind of pole a person could stand on while moving about, rather than lean on. The answer has five letters.
