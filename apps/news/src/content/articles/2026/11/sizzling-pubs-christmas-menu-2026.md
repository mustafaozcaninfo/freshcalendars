**Sizzling Pubs lists its Festive Set Menu 2026 at "3 courses from £18.99 per person" and a Christmas Day menu from £60.99 per adult, according to the chain's own pages read on 23 November 2026.** Christmas Day is Friday 25 December, and full payment with pre-order is due by 1 December. Both menus must be pre-booked, and selected sites are excluded.

## What does the Sizzling Pubs Christmas menu cost in 2026?

Two menus, two price lines. The pages do not state a 2-course price, a children's price for the set menu or start and end dates for the Festive Set Menu.

| Menu | Price on the page | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Festive Set Menu | 3 courses from £18.99 per person | Festive Set Menu page |
| Christmas Day, Friday 25 December | 3 courses from £60.99 per adult, £30.99 per child | Christmas Day Menu page |
| Christmas Day, as worded on the hub | from £60.99 per adult, £30.49 per child | Christmas page |

The child price differs by 50p between the Christmas Day menu page and the Christmas page. Confirm it with your pub when booking. Mark the dates on a [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and see [when Christmas falls](/when-is/christmas/).

## What are the Sizzling Pubs Christmas deposits and deadlines?

The Christmas Deposits page sets a different rule for each event. The page prints no year for the 1st December deadline; it is on the 2026 season's pages, so the table reads it as Tuesday 1 December 2026.

| Event | Deposit | Payment rule | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Festive Set Menu | £5 per person when booking | "full pre-payment on day of visit" | Day of the visit |
| Christmas Day | £10 per person, every guest | Full payment and pre-order by 1st December | Tue. 1 Dec 2026 |
| Festive Family Experience | £5 per person when joining | Balance payable 14 days prior to the event | Varies by pub |

Deposits are "non-refundable within 14 days of dining". For Christmas Day that means from Friday 11 December 2026, our count back from Friday 25 December. A party of 6 adults pays £60 in Christmas Day deposits, our multiplication of the £10 rate.

## What else does Sizzling Pubs offer around Christmas?

The Festive Family Experience costs "£11.95 per child" and includes a Kids' Breakfast; dates and times vary by pub. After Christmas, the New Year page lists a Buy One Get One Free burger offer from 26 December to 3 January 2027 (Saturday to Sunday), through the Sizzling app. It says New Year's Eve plans and opening hours vary by pub, and that New Year's Day quiz details are not yet announced. The pages state no Boxing Day opening hours or prices.
