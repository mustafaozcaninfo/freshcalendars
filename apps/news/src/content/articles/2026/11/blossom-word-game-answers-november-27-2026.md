**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, has D in the center with C, E, F, N, O and S on the petals, and the solver lists agree on two pangrams.** One has 7 letters and the other 9. Hints come first, with no word given away.

## The day-after-Thanksgiving flower: center D

- **Center:** D
- **Petals:** C, E, F, N, O, S

Minute Cryptic, Word.Tips and Connections Solver print this letter set, and Lady Puzzle and Beebom agree on the letters. Only E and O are vowels, and every find has to hold the D. Yesterday's center was A, on the [Nov. 26 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-26-2026/). With D in the middle today, the scoring rules on the [Blossom rules page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works) apply as usual.

## Two pangrams, 7 and 9 letters long

A pangram uses all seven letters. Merriam-Webster's Blossom page prints no word list, so the count comes from the lists that agree: two pangrams, 7 and 9 letters long. The gap of two letters comes from repeats. The shorter word uses C, D, E, F, N, O and S once each, with nothing left over. The longer word uses all seven and then repeats E and S, two times each, which accounts for its extra two letters.

| Pangram length | Letters used once | Letters repeated |
|---|---|---|
| 7 letters | all seven | none |
| 9 letters | C, D, F, N, O | E twice, S twice |

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- **Hint 1:** The agreeing lists hold 2 pangrams.
- **Hint 2:** One has 7 letters and the other has 9.
- **Hint 3:** The 7-letter word begins with the center letter, D.
- **Hint 4:** The 9-letter word begins with a petal letter, C, and ends with the center, D.
- **Hint 5:** The 7-letter word ends with the petal S.
- **Hint 6:** The 9-letter word holds two E's and two S's, and the two S's sit side by side.

## A seven-tick check for every find

A word with six of the seven letters can look like a pangram and still fall short. Tick each letter off before you count a find.

| Tick | Letter | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | D | Center |
| 2 | C | Petal |
| 3 | E | Petal |
| 4 | F | Petal |
| 5 | N | Petal |
| 6 | O | Petal |
| 7 | S | Petal |

Seven ticks make a pangram; six do not. Keep a tally on the [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) or the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## Today's Blossom pangrams (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it yourself.

### Pangram

The agreeing lists give these two, each holding D, C, E, F, N, O and S at least once.

- **Defcons** (7 letters)
- **Confessed** (9 letters)

Defcons uses each of the seven letters once. Confessed repeats E and S.

### The near miss

Beebom lists Defcon as a pangram. It has no S, so it is not one; the plural with the S is the pangram, and Defcons is accepted on the solver lists.

| Word | Letter it lacks | Pangram? |
|---|---|---|
| Defcon | S | No |
| Defcons | none | Yes |
