**Shake Shack's Hazelnut Chocolate Shake, made with Ferrero Rocher chocolate, launched Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, and costs around $10, Nation's Restaurant News and Foodbeast reported.** It is one of three holiday shakes. Shake Shack's own site blocked our automated read, so every 2026 fact here comes from news reports that agree, and each is named below. Nobody we read gives an end date.

## What are the Shake Shack holiday shakes for 2026?

Nation's Restaurant News (NRN) names three shakes. Foodbeast and Sporked describe the Hazelnut Chocolate Shake the same way, and Bleeding Cool says "The Holiday Shake Trio is available now."

| Shake | What is in it, as reported | New or returning |
|---|---|---|
| Hazelnut Chocolate Shake | Vanilla frozen custard with chocolate hazelnut spread in a crackable dark chocolate shell, topped with whipped cream, chopped hazelnuts, crunchy wafer flakes and a Ferrero Rocher chocolate square | New this year, made with Ferrero Rocher (NRN) |
| Christmas Cookie Shake | Sugar cookie frozen custard topped with whipped cream and holiday sprinkles | Also on the 2025 list |
| Butterscotch Crème Brûlée Shake | Butterscotch crème brûlée frozen custard topped with whipped cream and honeycomb pieces | New this year, per NRN and Foodbeast |

Foodbeast calls the shell a return of Shake Shack's crackable chocolate shell with a holiday twist. Nick Wuest, Shake Shack's director of culinary innovation, said in NRN's report: "The holidays are one of our favorite times of the year at Shake Shack and some of our favorite flavors we work on all year."

## When did the Shake Shack Ferrero Rocher shake launch, and how much is it?

NRN said the Hazelnut Chocolate Shake "launched Tuesday," Nov. 17, and is "available for a limited time." Sporked wrote that it is "available nationwide on November 17th at participating Shake Shack locations." Foodbeast's wording is "available now for a limited time at participating Shake Shack locations nationwide." NRN says it is "priced around $10," and Foodbeast also says "around $10."

What is missing: an end date, an exact menu price, the start dates of the other two shakes beyond "available now" on Nov. 17, and any 2026 gift card bonus. Check your Shack's menu before you go. For the dates around the launch, see the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Which Shake Shack holiday shakes came back from 2025?

Only the Christmas Cookie Shake appears in both years in the reports we read. In 2025, Food Network ("I Tried All of Shake Shack's Holiday Shakes," Nov. 18, 2025) and The Takeout (Nov. 18, 2025) covered a lineup with a Peppermint Bark Chocolate Shake, a Sticky Toffee Pudding Shake and a Christmas Cookie Shake. Both dated the Peppermint Bark Chocolate Shake from Nov. 18, 2025, and Food Network dated the Sticky Toffee Pudding and Christmas Cookie shakes from Nov. 4, 2025.

| Shake | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas Cookie | Yes, from Nov. 4, 2025 (Food Network) | Yes, reported available on Nov. 17 |
| Peppermint Bark Chocolate | Yes, from Nov. 18, 2025 | Not in the 2026 trio as reported |
| Sticky Toffee Pudding | Yes, from Nov. 4, 2025 (Food Network) | Not in the 2026 trio as reported |
| Hazelnut Chocolate with Ferrero Rocher | Not listed | New, launched Nov. 17, 2026 |
| Butterscotch Crème Brûlée | Not listed | New, per NRN and Foodbeast |

The reports say the Christmas Cookie Shake is available now but give no start date for 2026. The 2025 dates are last year's, not this year's.
