**Visit St. Pete-Clearwater, Pinellas County's official tourism office, lists the Tarpon Springs Epiphany celebration for Wednesday, January 6, 2027, "returning for its 121st year" at St. Nicholas Greek Orthodox Cathedral.** Orthros and Hierarchical Divine Liturgy start at 8 a.m. The time of the cross dive at Spring Bayou is not yet announced; the listing says "Exact times are still being finalized." The celebration's own site was not reachable when we checked, so the 2027 schedule here rests on the tourism office's listing.

## What is the Tarpon Springs Epiphany schedule for Jan. 2 to 6, 2027?

Visit St. Pete-Clearwater lists four timed events. The 2026 column is Q105's schedule published Jan. 2, 2026, before that year's event, which fell on a Tuesday.

| Day | 2027 event (Visit St. Pete-Clearwater) | 2026 (Q105, pre-event) |
|---|---|---|
| Sat., Jan. 2 | 7 p.m., Epiphany Ball, St. Nicholas Community Center, 348 N. Pinellas Ave. | Not in our sources |
| Tue., Jan. 5 | 1 p.m., Blessing of the Fleet, Historic Sponge Docks, along Dodecanese Boulevard | Not in our sources |
| Wed., Jan. 6 | 8 a.m., Orthros and Hierarchical Divine Liturgy, St. Nicholas Greek Orthodox Cathedral | Orthros 8 a.m., Divine Liturgy 9 a.m. |
| Wed., Jan. 6 | Procession to Spring Bayou "following services"; exact times still being finalized | Not in our sources |
| Wed., Jan. 6 | Cross dive: not yet announced | Cross toss at 1 p.m. |
| Wed., Jan. 6 | Epiphany Glendi at Craig Park: hours not announced | 2 p.m. to 6 p.m., St. Nicholas Community Center |
| Wed., Jan. 6 | 8 p.m., Divers Dance, St. Nicholas Community Center | Not in our sources |

For 2026, Q105 wrote that "Archbishop Elpidophoros tosses the cross into the bayou at 1 p.m." The 2027 presiding hierarch is not announced. Do not plan around a 1 p.m. dive until the cross dive time is published; 2026 times are a reference only.

## What is still not announced for 2027?

The listing gives no cross dive time, procession start or route, Glendi hours, presiding hierarch, road closures, parking lots or livestream. This page will carry them once an official source publishes them.

## Is it free, and how crowded is it?

Visit St. Pete-Clearwater says admission is free and open to the public, with food and beverages available for purchase. It adds: "Thousands attend each year, and parking around the cathedral and Spring Bayou is limited, so plan to arrive early."

## Who dives for the cross, and how many dove in 2026?

Fr. James Rousakis, dean of St. Nicholas Cathedral, told the Florida Historical Society in January 2016 that the divers "are boys ages 16 through 18." Orthodox Times described them in 2025 as "Greek Orthodox boys aged 16 to 18" and called that year the 119th celebration.

In 2026, the 120th, the University of South Florida public media station (WUSF) reported that Athos Karistinos, 18, found the cross before 73 other boys, so 74 divers took part. FOX 13 also reported 74 divers. Tampa Bay 28 said Karistinos, 18, of Tarpon Springs retrieved the cross and described "more than 70 young men."
