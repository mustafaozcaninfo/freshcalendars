**The Denver Broncos play the Pittsburgh Steelers on Black Friday Football, Fri., Nov. 27, 2026, at 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Prime Video, according to Amazon and both teams' schedule pages.** The Steelers list the game as "Fri 11/27 · 3:00 PM EST" and the Broncos as 1:00 p.m. MST, with Prime Video as the network. It is Week 12 and it is played at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh.

Below are kickoff and the pregame in every U.S. zone and in the United Kingdom, how the teams arrive, every Black Friday game so far with its score and viewers, and a one-page watch guide.

Amazon says "Black Friday Football features the Denver Broncos at the Pittsburgh Steelers, with kickoff at 3 p.m. ET." Sports Video Group, in a Nov. 17, 2026 report, lists the show as "1:30 p.m. ET pregame, 3 p.m. kickoff". We worked out the other zones from those times.

## What channel is the Black Friday game on?

Prime Video, per the Broncos' schedule page and Amazon. Charissa Thompson hosts the 90-minute pregame, and Al Michaels and Kirk Herbstreit call the game with Kaylee Hartung on the sideline, per Amazon and Sports Video Group.

Amazon's page says all four of its Black Friday sporting events "will be available even if you don't have a Prime Video subscription or Prime membership." Sports Video Group adds that in the U.S. the game is also on mobile and tablet with a NFL+ subscription. The sources we reached do not name a local over-the-air station.

## How do the Broncos and Steelers arrive?

NFL.com's standings page lists the Steelers at 6-4. The Broncos are 8-2 by the results on the Broncos' schedule page.

| Team | Last game |
|---|---|
| Steelers | Sun., Nov. 22: lost 19-16 at Philadelphia, their second straight loss after an 18-38 defeat at Cincinnati on Sun., Nov. 15 |
| Broncos | Sun., Nov. 22: beat the Las Vegas Raiders 38-24, per NFL.com |

The last meeting was in 2024 at Denver, when the Steelers won 13-6, per the Steelers' 2026 opponent preview, published Jul. 2, 2026.
