**Ontario's Bullying Awareness and Prevention Week 2026 runs from Sunday, Nov. 15 to Saturday, Nov. 21, because the Government of Ontario says the week "begins on the third Sunday of November each year."** The province's page, "Bullying: we can all help stop it" (updated Aug. 12, 2026), prints that rule and no 2026 date, so the dates here are worked out from it. A live day count, the dates in other years and a free printable follow.

## How the 2026 dates are worked out

Nov. 1, 2026 is a Sunday, so the Sundays of the month are Nov. 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29. The third is Nov. 15. A week that begins that Sunday ends on Saturday, Nov. 21.

Monday, Nov. 16 to Friday, Nov. 20 are the five weekdays. The province does not say how each board marks them, and no board-level 2026 dates were checked, so boards may set their own events.

## Which date does the third-Sunday rule give in other years?

Because the week starts on the third Sunday, its start can fall only between Nov. 15 and Nov. 21. These dates come from applying the rule, not from a printed calendar.

| Year | Week begins | Week ends |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Sunday, Nov. 16 | Saturday, Nov. 22 |
| 2026 | Sunday, Nov. 15 | Saturday, Nov. 21 |
| 2027 | Sunday, Nov. 21 | Saturday, Nov. 27 |
| 2028 | Sunday, Nov. 19 | Saturday, Nov. 25 |

## What did the province do for the week in 2024?

In 2024, a provincial investment of $4.6 million was reported for the week, according to Sydenham Current and Today's Northumberland. Both are 2024 reports. No 2026 announcement or funding was found, so none is described here.

## Where does Ontario send families for resources?

The same government page lists three places to start:

- Creating safe and accepting schools, on ontario.ca
- School Mental Health Ontario (smho-smso.ca)
- The Healthy Relationships in a Digital World Toolkit (principals.ca), listed with Victim Services Toronto

## Ontario's week and the UK's Anti-Bullying Week

The two are separate. Ontario sets its week by a rule in its own government guidance. The UK campaign has its own organizer and dates, which our [Anti-Bullying Week 2026 UK story](/news/anti-bullying-week-2026-uk-november-16-20-odd-socks-day/) covers. Do not carry the Ontario dates or the third-Sunday rule over to other places.
