**Beaverton School District in Oregon starts students on Tuesday, Aug. 25, 2026 and ends the year for students on Thursday, June 10, 2027**, according to its "2026-2027 District Calendar," a PDF linked from the district's calendar page. The PDF lists "174 Total student days." Below are every weekday students are off, the June 14-18 makeup window and a printable school-year calendar.

## Beaverton first and last days of school

The calendar lists "First day for students" on Tuesday, Aug. 25, 2026, with a note: "First day dates may be modified by grade level." It prints no grade-by-grade start dates, so families should check their school. Staff have "Pre-service/staff development/workday - no students" on Aug. 18-24, before the first day.

The year ends in two steps. "Seniors' last day" is Thursday, June 3, 2027, and "Last day for students" is Thursday, June 10, which is also marked "End of semester." The first semester ends Friday, Jan. 22, 2027, which is not a closure. The 174 student days are split "Q1:45; Q2:42; Q3:45; Q4:42." The calendar footer reads "Calendar approved 01/21/2025."

## Beaverton no-school weekdays in 2026-27

The count adds every weekday between Aug. 25, 2026 and June 10, 2027 that the calendar lists as a closure or a no-student day. Weekends are not counted, and Christmas and New Year's Day fall inside winter break, so they add nothing extra. There are 208 weekdays in that span, and 208 minus 34 leaves 174, the district's own total.

The district's legend separates "Schools closed - no staff/students" from "Schools closed - no students." Veterans Day is today, Wednesday, Nov. 11. The next day off after it is "Fall break" the week of Nov. 23.

## When is Beaverton fall break 2026?

The district uses "Fall break - schools closed" for Nov. 23-27, 2026, which is Thanksgiving week, Monday to Friday, five weekdays. Thanksgiving Day is Thursday, Nov. 26.

## When is Beaverton winter break 2026?

"Winter break - schools closed" runs Dec. 21, 2026 through Jan. 1, 2027, Monday of one week to Friday of the next, 10 weekdays. The calendar then lists Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 as "Staff development/workday - no students." It prints no return date. See the weeks on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When is Beaverton spring break 2027?

"Spring break - schools closed" is Monday, March 22 through Friday, March 26, 2027, five weekdays. Mark it on the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html). For other Oregon and Pacific Northwest dates, see the [Salem-Keizer calendar](/news/salem-keizer-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) and the [Seattle Public Schools calendar](/news/seattle-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), or the [spring break 2027 dates by district](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

## Are there long weekends in the Beaverton calendar?

Two stretches stand out. "Staff development/workday - no students" on Friday, Feb. 12 and "Presidents Day - schools closed" on Monday, Feb. 15 make a four-day weekend, with two weekdays off. "Grading day - no students" on Monday, Jan. 25 and a staff workday on Tuesday, Jan. 26 are two weekdays off in a row, after "End of semester" on Friday, Jan. 22.

## What are the June 14-18 makeup days in Beaverton?

The calendar marks June 14-18, 2027 as "Potential makeup school days." They are not scheduled days. The required hours are set by the Oregon Department of Education (ODE). The district's calendar page explains: "In the event inclement weather or other emergency closures prevent the District from meeting required instructional hours set by ODE, the days will be made up in June." The calendar does not say in advance whether any of those days will be used. Friday, June 11 is a "Grading day - no students."

## Which cultural and religious dates are on the Beaverton calendar?

The PDF marks Rosh Hashanah (Sept. 11-13), Yom Kippur (Sept. 20-21), Diwali (Nov. 8), Lunar New Year (Feb. 6), Eid al-Fitr (March 9-10), Easter (March 28) and Eid al-Adha (May 16-17), with the two Eid dates marked "** Estimated dates." They are marked on the calendar and not listed as closures. The calendar prints no early release or late start days. The district warns that the calendar is "subject to change based on contract negotiations and resource availability."
