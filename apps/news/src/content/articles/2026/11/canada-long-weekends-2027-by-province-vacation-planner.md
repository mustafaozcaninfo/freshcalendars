**Canada's first 2027 long weekend is New Year's Day on Friday, Jan. 1, and the federal government's "Vacations and holidays" list gives five three-day weekends on Mondays and Fridays plus three Thursday holidays.** The Thursdays are Canada Day (July 1), the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation (Sept. 30) and Remembrance Day (Nov. 11); one vacation day on the Friday after each makes four days off where the holiday applies. The tables below show every bridge day for a Monday-to-Friday worker, and our printable keeps the dates on one page.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Family Day, Mon., Feb. 15 | Feb. 13-15 (3) | Tue., Feb. 16 to Fri., Feb. 19 | 4 | Feb. 13-21 (9) |

| Victoria Day, Mon., May 24 | May 22-24 (3) | Fri., May 21 | 1 | May 21-24 (4) |

Easter Monday, Mar. 29, is computed by us: Easter Sunday is Mar. 28 by the Gregorian rule, and no official page we read lists Easter Monday as a 2027 holiday. Good Friday, Mar. 26, is in Manitoba's official 2027 table.

The Thursday holidays are not weekends until you bridge them: Canada Day (Thu., July 1) is on the lists of the federal government, BC, Ontario, Alberta, Manitoba, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and the Northwest Territories; Truth and Reconciliation Day (Thu., Sept. 30) is on the federal, BC, Manitoba (as Orange Shirt Day) and Northwest Territories lists; Remembrance Day (Thu., Nov. 11) is on the federal, BC, Alberta, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and Northwest Territories lists. Nova Scotia's chart says Remembrance Day is governed by its Remembrance Day Act.

Manitoba and Alberta print some 2027 dates themselves (Manitoba: Feb. 15, Mar. 26, May 24, July 1, Sept. 6, Sept. 30, Oct. 11 and Dec. 25; Alberta: Sept. 6 and Oct. 11). ## How many vacation days does a province's list need for 2027?

Counting only the holidays that fall on a weekday in 2027, and one bridge day each (the Friday before a Monday holiday, the Friday after a Thursday holiday, Easter Monday after Good Friday), each bridge makes a four-day weekend. New Year's Day is left out because its bridge day, Thu., Dec. 31, 2026, is in 2026.

| Jurisdiction | Holidays used (2027 weekday holidays) | Vacation days | Four-day weekends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ontario | Feb. 15, Mar. 26, May 24, July 1, Sept. 6, Oct. 11 | 6 | 6 |
| Alberta | Feb. 15, Mar. 26, May 24, July 1, Sept. 6, Oct. 11, Nov. 11 | 7 | 7 |
| Manitoba | Feb. 15, Mar. 26, May 24, July 1, Sept. 6, Sept. 30, Oct. 11 | 7 | 7 |
| British Columbia | Feb. 15, Mar. 26, May 24, July 1, Aug. 2, Sept. 6, Sept. 30, Oct. 11, Nov. 11 | 9 | 9 |
| Federal (Canada Labour Code) | Mar. 26, May 24, July 1, Sept. 6, Sept. 30, Oct. 11, Nov. 11 | 7 | 7 |

Ontario's public holidays are New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Labour Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day and Boxing Day: "Ontario has nine public holidays", says the province's Employment Standards Act (ESA) guide (updated July 8, 2024). Easter Monday and the Civic Holiday are not on it, and for them "the employer is not required to do so under the ESA." BC says "Easter Sunday, Easter Monday and Boxing Day are not statutory holidays in B.C.", so a BC worker takes Mon., Mar. 29 as a vacation day. Alberta's nine general holidays do not include Heritage Day in August on the list we read.

Other lists in our sources: New Brunswick has eight holidays (New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Canada Day, New Brunswick Day, Labour Day, Remembrance Day and Christmas Day), and Victoria Day and Thanksgiving are not on its list. Nova Scotia's paid general holidays are New Year's Day, Nova Scotia Heritage Day, Good Friday, Canada Day, Labour Day and Christmas Day. Newfoundland and Labrador's Labour Standards Act names New Year's Day, Good Friday, Remembrance Day, Memorial Day, Labour Day and Christmas Day, plus other proclaimed days; we do not date Memorial Day. The Northwest Territories lists eleven. Manitoba has nine general holidays; its page says Terry Fox Day is not a general holiday. Saskatchewan has ten public holidays, so check its list for the day each one falls.

## What happens when Christmas and Boxing Day land on a weekend in 2027?

Christmas Day is Saturday, Dec. 25, 2027, and Boxing Day is Sunday, Dec. 26. Each government words its rule differently:

| Jurisdiction | Rule for a holiday on a non-working day | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Federal | "you are entitled to a holiday with pay on the scheduled work day immediately before or after the general holiday." | Government of Canada |
| Manitoba | "the employee must get the next regular workday off with general holiday pay." | Government of Manitoba |
| New Brunswick | "another working day off with pay in lieu of the holiday." | Government of New Brunswick |
| Nova Scotia | "the employer must give the employee a different day off with pay." | Government of Nova Scotia |
| Saskatchewan | "When a public holiday falls on a Saturday, it is not observed on a different day." | Government of Saskatchewan |

For a federally regulated worker, both Christmas Day and Boxing Day are on the list, so each needs a paid day on a scheduled work day before or after: Fri., Dec. 24 and Mon., Dec. 27 are the nearest. With those two paid days, taking Tue., Dec. 28 to Fri., Dec. 31 (4 vacation days) gives Fri., Dec. 24 to Sun., Jan. 2, 2028: 10 days off in a row, with Jan. 1, 2028 on a Saturday. Which work day gets the paid holiday is set by the rule and your schedule; these counts assume Dec. 24 and Dec. 27. We did not find a weekend rule in our sources for British Columbia, Ontario, Alberta, Newfoundland and Labrador or the Northwest Territories, so check those with the government pages linked below.

## Where to check Quebec, Prince Edward Island, Yukon and Nunavut

These four official pages refused automated reads on Nov. 6, 2026, so we list no holidays or dates for them. Each sets its own list: check the Commission des normes, de l'équité, de la santé et de la sécurité du travail (CNESST) site at cnesst.gouv.qc.ca for Quebec, the employee services pages at princeedwardisland.ca for Prince Edward Island, yukon.ca for Yukon and gov.nu.ca for Nunavut. Use the same bridge-day method from the first table once you know which weekdays your list contains.
