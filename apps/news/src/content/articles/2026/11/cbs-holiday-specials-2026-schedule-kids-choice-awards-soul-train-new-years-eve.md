**CBS's holiday special run starts Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026 with the Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Awards (KCAs) at 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), per a CBS Entertainment release dated Oct. 29, and runs to New Year's Eve.** CBS lists 11 dates in the release, which says the programming "airs between Nov. 14 and Dec. 31." The table below puts them in one place, and a printable sheet follows.

Times are as CBS lists them. Most specials show the same clock time in Eastern Time and Pacific Time (PT); the KCAs are the exception at 5 p.m. PT. The release gives no Central or Mountain times, so check local listings for those zones.

## When are the Kids' Choice Awards 2026 on CBS?

The Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Awards air Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026, from 8 to 9:30 p.m. ET and 5 to 6:30 p.m. PT. CBS says it is the first time the show airs on the CBS Television Network and Nickelodeon. Alex Warren hosts, and the show celebrates fans' favorites across film, TV, music, gaming and sports.

## Which CBS specials air in Thanksgiving week 2026?

Four fall in the days around [Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26](/when-is/thanksgiving/), all at 8 p.m. ET and PT.

- **Monday, Nov. 23:** the Pillsbury Bake-Off Classic, 8 to 10 p.m., hosted by Drew Barrymore. CBS describes it as returning to primetime "just in time for Thanksgiving."
- **Thursday, Nov. 26:** The JLo Show, 8 to 10:01 p.m., a Jennifer Lopez concert special from the Las Vegas stage.
- **Friday, Nov. 27:** the Soul Train Awards, 8 to 10 p.m.
- **Saturday, Nov. 28:** A Paw Patrol Christmas, 8 to 9 p.m., a repeat.

For the parade and football side of the holiday, see our [Thanksgiving 2026 TV schedule](/news/thanksgiving-2026-tv-schedule-macys-parade-nfl-games/).

## What are the December CBS specials, from The Price Is Right to Voices of Hope?

Reindeer in Here, a repeat about a young reindeer named Blizzard (Blizz) with one antler smaller than the other, airs Saturday, Dec. 5 and again Wednesday, Dec. 23, both at 8 p.m. ET and PT.

The Price Is Right at Night: Live Holiday Special airs Sunday, Dec. 13 at 8 p.m. CBS calls it the first fully live holiday-themed special in the show's 54-year run. Two nights later, Tuesday, Dec. 15, "The Price Is Right: 20 Seasons of Drew Carey" airs at 8 p.m.

Saturday, Dec. 26 carries two hours. The Family Film & TV Awards run 8 to 9 p.m. ET and PT. "Voices of Hope" follows from 9 to 10 p.m., hosted by Kevin Frazier and Nischelle Turner. It is The Tabernacle Choir's benefit concert, filmed live at the Hollywood Bowl 100 years after the choir first sang there in 1926. Guests include Donny Osmond and Katharine McPhee, and CBS says 100% of ticket revenue benefits global charities.

## What time is New Year's Eve Live: Nashville's Big Bash on CBS?

New Year's Eve Live: Nashville's Big Bash airs Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, in two parts: 8 to 10 p.m. and 10:30 p.m. to 1:05 a.m., which CBS lists for both ET and PT. It is live from downtown Nashville. Hardy, Little Big Town and The Red Clay Strays are the first artists CBS announced; the release names no others.
