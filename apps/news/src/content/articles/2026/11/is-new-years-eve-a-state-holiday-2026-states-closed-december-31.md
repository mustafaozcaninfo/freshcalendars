**Five states close state offices on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, though New Year's Eve is not a federal holiday: Kentucky, Tennessee, Wisconsin, Louisiana and Florida.** The U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) [federal holidays page](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/) lists "Friday, January 01 | New Year's Day" for 2027 and has no Dec. 31 entry, so federal offices are open Dec. 31 and closed Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. Each state sets its own calendar for state workers. The tables use each state's own page, read Nov. 24, 2026, and the state names link to it.

## Which states close state offices on New Year's Eve 2026?

| State | Dec. 31, 2026 | Jan. 1, 2027 | Basis | How the state words it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Kentucky](https://extranet.personnel.ky.gov/Pages/Leave.aspx) | Closed | Closed | Standing holiday list | "New Year's – Thursday, December 31, 2026 & Friday, January 1, 2027" |
| [Tennessee](https://www.tn.gov/hr/employees1/state-holidays.html) | Closed | Closed | Standing holiday list | "Thursday, December 31, 2026, and Friday, January 1, 2027 – New Year's Day" |
| [Wisconsin](https://dpm.wi.gov/Pages/How_Do_I/seeStateHolidays.aspx) | Closed | Not part of what we read | Standing holiday list | "New Year's Eve Day \| December 31" |
| [Louisiana](https://lpsc.louisiana.gov/DNC_Holidays) | Closed | Closed | Governor's proclamation | "New Years Eve* \| Governor Proclamation \| Thursday, December 31, 2026" |
| [Florida](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2026/governor-ron-desantis-announces-additional-state-office-closures-holiday-season) | Closed | Not part of what we read | Governor's order | "state offices will be closed on November 25, December 23, December 24, and December 31" |

Louisiana's row is from the state Public Service Commission's holiday schedule, which marks the day as a governor's proclamation; we did not read the proclamation itself. Wisconsin's page is undated and names the day as a standing rule, and Dec. 31, 2026 is a Thursday. Florida's closures come from a governor's release of Nov. 16, 2026, and are in addition to its regular state holidays. Tennessee's page says the governor approved an added closure day for some lines; it does not say here whether that is Dec. 31, so the table uses the dates on its list.

"Not listed" means the page names no closure for Dec. 31, 2026. It does not rule out an order issued later or a union contract that differs, so check with your agency.

## How long is the weekend for state workers in the five states?

Dec. 31, 2026 is a Thursday and Jan. 1, 2027 is a Friday. In Kentucky, Tennessee and Louisiana, whose lists name both days, state offices on the standard list are closed Thursday and Friday, which gives four days off from Thu., Dec. 31 to Sun., Jan. 3. Wisconsin's page names Dec. 31 only, and Florida's order names Dec. 31 only. For those two, the weekend run depends on the state's own Jan. 1 rule, which we did not read.

## Which states could not be confirmed?

Michigan, Colorado, South Carolina, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Ohio, Minnesota, Mississippi, Oklahoma, New Jersey, Arizona, Alabama and Massachusetts are not confirmed on the state's own page as of Nov. 24, 2026. Michigan's state site returned an access error to our check. They are not marked closed or open. Check your agency's human resources page.

Federal offices are open Dec. 31 and closed Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. This page covers state offices only.
