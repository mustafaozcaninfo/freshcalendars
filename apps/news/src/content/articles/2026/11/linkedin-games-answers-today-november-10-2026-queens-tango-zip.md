- **Crossclimb #924, rung 4:** jeers from a crowd.
- **Crossclimb #924, rung 5:** marshes, or wet ground.

## Step map for the Crossclimb #924 ladder

Seven words, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word appears in this table.

| Move | Letter position that changes | Kind of letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 3 | vowel |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 4 | consonant |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 3 | consonant |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 2 | vowel |

The first letter changes twice and so does the third, while the second and fourth change once each. Today's two vowel moves are far apart: one at the second move and one at the very end. Between them, rungs 3, 4 and 5 are all consonant swaps, so a rung that will not fit with a new vowel is likely waiting on a different consonant.

## Tuesday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 10, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #924:** Things that are white.
- **Crossclimb #924:** Golf, Wolf, Woof, Woos, Boos, Bogs, Bags. Wolf, Woof, Woos, Boos and Bogs fill the middle in that order; Golf and Bags are the locked ends.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row | Golf | none |
| Rung 1 | Wolf | W for G |
| Rung 2 | Woof | O for L |
| Rung 3 | Woos | S for F |
| Rung 4 | Boos | B for W |
| Rung 5 | Bogs | G for O |
| Bottom row | Bags | A for O |

Wolf is the pack animal, woof the bark, woos means courts, boos are the crowd's jeers and bogs are wet ground.

Golf and Bags join up as golf bags. Woof turns into Woos by changing only its last letter, and Woos into Boos by changing only its first, so two of the middle steps are quick to spot once the bark is in place.
