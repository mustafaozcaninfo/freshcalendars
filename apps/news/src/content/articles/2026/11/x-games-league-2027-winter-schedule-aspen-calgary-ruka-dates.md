**X Games Aspen 2027 runs Jan. 29-31 at Buttermilk in Aspen, Colorado, according to Aspen Snowmass, the first of three stops in the MoonPay X Games League (XGL) Winter Season.** Calgary follows Feb. 19-21 and the championship finale is Apr. 9-11 in Ruka-Kuusamo, Finland. The table below puts all three stops side by side, and a free printable season calendar follows it.

## Winter X Games 2027 dates and venues

The X Games site lists "MoonPay X Games Calgary 2027" for "February 19-21, 2027", WinSport's calendar shows Feb. 19 to Feb. 21, 2027, and Ruka-Kuusamo's events page gives "9.4.2027 - 11.4.2027". SnowBrains quoted the Sept. 14, 2026 announcement of the "three-stop schedule" and the Jan. 29-31 opening in Aspen.

| Stop | Dates | Venue | Local time zone |
|---|---|---|---|
| X Games Aspen | Fri., Jan. 29, to Sun., Jan. 31, 2027 | Buttermilk, Aspen, Colorado | Mountain Time (MT) |
| X Games Calgary | Fri., Feb. 19, to Sun., Feb. 21, 2027 | WinSport's Canada Olympic Park, Calgary, Alberta | Mountain Time (MT) |
| X Games Ruka (championship finale) | Fri., Apr. 9, to Sun., Apr. 11, 2027 | Ruka-Kuusamo, northern Finland | Eastern European Summer Time (EEST) |

Every stop runs Friday to Sunday. Weekdays are our calculation.

## Why is it called the X Games League?

People still search for "Winter X Games", and the Aspen event is still the one with the long history: Aspen Snowmass calls 2027 the 26th consecutive year at Buttermilk. What changed is the setting. The X Games says the MoonPay X Games League launches in 2026 as "the newest professional competition arm of the X Games", with eight inaugural clubs, four summer and four winter. The winter draft was held Wednesday, Sept. 16, 2026, at Cosm Los Angeles, per an X Games release dated June 9, 2026. Aspen is now the first stop of a league season rather than a stand-alone event.

## How far apart are the stops, and what time is it there?

Aspen opens 21 days before Calgary does, and Calgary opens 49 days before the Ruka finale. The gap from the end of one stop to the start of the next is 19 days, then 47 days.

| Stop | Opens | Days from the previous opening | Hours ahead of ET | Hours ahead of PT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aspen | Jan. 29 | First stop | 2 (MT is UTC-7, ET is UTC-5) | 1 |
| Calgary | Feb. 19 | 21 | 2 | 1 |
| Ruka | Apr. 9 | 49 | 7 (EEST is UTC+3, ET is UTC-4) | 10 |

The offsets are our calculation. U.S. clocks change on March 14, 2027, and Finland's change to summer time falls on the last Sunday of March, Mar. 28, so Ruka is on EEST in April. Daily start times are not yet posted for any stop, so no session time is converted here. An evening session in Finland would land in the U.S. morning or afternoon.

## Is X Games Aspen 2027 free?

Aspen Snowmass says "X Games is free before 5PM each day while Buttermilk is open for skiing." Last year, X Games Aspen 2026 ran Jan. 23-25, 2026, as the "25th consecutive event", and was free until 4 p.m., per xgames.com. A general admission ticket covers competition, "access to X Fest, and nightly concerts featuring Zhu and Marshmello", the resort says, and a Gold Lounge is offered. Aspen Snowmass describes "three days of nonstop ski and snowboard competition".

Calgary's general admission tickets show as sold out on the X Games home page, which lists music-only tickets as on sale. Ruka-Kuusamo's events page carries no ticket information.

## Where can you watch Winter X Games 2027?

Not yet announced. The Sept. 14 release said details, including "competition dates, disciplines, athlete rosters, broadcast and streaming information," come later. Sports Video Group reported on Mar. 11, 2026 that the league would "stream free to fans nationwide on Roku Sports Channel", starting with summer 2026, "followed by the first winter season in 2027". That is a news report, not a 2027 schedule, and per-event times and channels are not yet announced.
