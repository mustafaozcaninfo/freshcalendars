## What kinds of clues fill Saturday's Mini

Sorting all 17 clues by the work they ask of you shows a grid with no pun in it. The one clue closest to wordplay is 11-Down: nothing in it names the answer, and you have to picture a stop sign swinging out from the side of a road vehicle. The clue never says "school" or "yellow", so the image comes entirely from the crossing letters and your own memory of a street scene. Everything else is direct.

The size does the rest. The grid has 42 white squares, 7 black squares, six 3-letter entries, two 4-letter entries, one 5-letter entry, three 6-letter entries and five 7-letter entries. The black squares sit in the top-left corner, in a run of three in the middle of the right edge, and in an L shape at the bottom-left. Rows two, three and five run the full seven squares, so three long Across answers stack on top of one another, and the three short Downs on the right hang from the top of that block or rise from the bottom of it. Fill one of the long Downs and you get a letter in every long Across at once.
