**The New York Times (NYT) Midi Crossword for Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, is a 10-by-10 grid with 34 clues by Adam Aaronson, edited by Ian Livengood.** Its published title is Going Back for Seconds. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## The Going Back for Seconds theme

The title is the one the puzzle carries as published. Read it as a nudge: three clues say the answer has a repetitive name, and each answer is a food whose name is one chunk of letters said twice. Once you spot the first, the other two follow the same pattern.

## Solving tips

Start with the eleven three-letter entries: 1-Across, 20-Across, 22-Across, 26-Across and 28-Across, plus 1-Down, 2-Down, 15-Down, 23-Down, 24-Down and 25-Down. 1-Across and 1-Down share their first square. The two 10-letter Down entries, 3-Down and 4-Down, sit in the third and seventh columns, and each crosses ten Across answers, so every letter you place in one of them is checked by a second entry.
