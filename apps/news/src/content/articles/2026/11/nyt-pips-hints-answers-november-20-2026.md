## The forced first move on each Pips board, Nov. 20

Cells are named by row, then column, counted from the top left, so R1C1 is the top-left cell. A search of every legal placement against the saved data backs each line.

**Easy.** R4C1 has one neighbor, R4C2, so whatever sits on R4C1 shares a domino with R4C2. R3C2 and R4C2 must add up to 2, and the set has no half showing a 1, so the pair is a 0 and a 2. Putting the 2 on R4C2 would leave a 0 on R3C2 whose only free neighbor is R3C3, and R2C3 would then need a 9. So R4C2 takes the 0, and the double 0-0 fills R4C1 and R4C2.

**Medium.** R2C1 adds up to 5 on its own, and only two dominoes carry a 5: the double 5-5 and the 2-5. Trying 5-5 on R2C1 leaves no valid board, so 2-5 covers R2C1 with the 2 on R1C1.

**Hard.** R2C1 adds up to 6 by itself, and the set shows a 6 exactly once, on 1-6. That uses up the only 6. R3C1, R5C2 and R7C2 each have to be more than 4, so all three hold a 5, and the set has four halves showing a 5. The 1 from that domino lands on R1C1 or R2C2, since R3C1 needs more than 4.

## Pips hints for Nov. 20, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 5-6, 3-3, 0-0, 2-6, 4-6)**

- Hint 1: R4C1 and R1C7 are the two cells with no rule, and each touches a single cell.
- Hint 2: R1C5 and R1C6 must add up to 11, and the set shows a 5 only once.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 5-5, 1-3, 4-0, 1-0, 2-5, 6-0, 0-3)**

- Hint 1: R2C1 adds up to 5 by itself, and only two dominoes show a 5.
- Hint 2: Four regions ask for equal values, and four halves in the set show a 0.

**Hard (ten dominoes on a ten-row board)**

- Hint 1: R2C1 adds up to 6 by itself, and the set shows a 6 on one half only.
- Hint 2: R3C1, R5C2 and R7C2 each have to be more than 4, and the set has four halves showing a 5.

## Reading Friday's boards before you look

One-cell regions carry most of Hard: nine of its 14 regions cover a single cell, against two on Easy and two on Medium. Easy's two single-cell regions are both free cells, R1C7 and R4C1, while Medium's are R2C1 and R2C6. Hard has just one free cell, R4C2.
