**The Leavenworth Chamber of Commerce says the Village of Lights is on from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily, Nov. 26, 2026, through Feb. 28, 2027, and Christmastown festivities run Nov. 27 to Dec. 24.** Times are Pacific Time (PT), local to Leavenworth, Washington. Plan the dates against the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Is there a tree lighting ceremony in Leavenworth in 2026?

No set ceremony is listed. The chamber's December page of frequently asked questions says "We no longer “flip the switch” at a certain time on weekends" and that "the lights are on everyday from Thanksgiving through the entire month of February." The chamber's lights guide says they run "from 6 AM to 11 PM, and they're free to enjoy." If you search for a weekend lighting time, none is posted for 2026.

## What are the Leavenworth Christmas lights hours in 2026?

The guide gives the season as "November 26, 2026, through February 28, 2027." That is Thursday, Thanksgiving, to Sunday, Feb. 28. The chamber's Christmas page markets "over half a million lights from 6 AM–11 PM daily, Thanksgiving through February."

## Leavenworth Christmastown 2026 events calendar

The chamber's page headlines the festivities as "November 27-December 24." Festhalle shows are ticketed, and the chamber lists "Tickets at the door." Babes in arms are free.

## What runs on which days in Leavenworth?

| Feature | When the chamber lists it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Village of Lights | 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily, Nov. 26 to Feb. 28, 2027 | Free to enjoy |
| Festhalle (kids' crafts, letters to Santa, gingerbread house exhibition) | Sunday-Thursday noon to 4 p.m.; Friday and Saturday noon to 6 p.m. | Ticketed shows are separate |
| Christmas carolers roaming downtown | 5 to 7 p.m., Dec. 1-23 (Mondays to Thursdays) | Strolling |
| Gazebo | Throughout the season | Free live music, strolling carolers and performances |
| Santa | Through Dec. 24 | Daily hours not listed |
| Christmas Eve, Thu., Dec. 24 | Festhalle closes 3 p.m.; gazebo music ends 4 p.m. | No strolling carolers that evening |

The chamber adds: "Schedule subject to change without notice due to weather, etc."

## How do you get to Leavenworth and park for Christmastown?

The chamber's parking page says most downtown lots are pay to park, the Enchantments lot is free with a 3-hour limit and the Freund lot is paid, with a free Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) placard option. Lots run 7 a.m. to 3 a.m. The Willkommen Park & Ride sits "behind Safeway with a shuttle that heads right into downtown Leavenworth!" Link Transit Route 22 is "fare-free service seven days a week between Leavenworth and Wenatchee," and the Glacier lot is a stop for the free Route 32 shuttle.

Christmastown-specific 2026 parking rules, road closures and shuttle hours are not posted in the sources we could read. Amtrak's Empire Builder lists a stop at Leavenworth, WA - Icicle Station (LWA), at 11645 North Road; check Amtrak for schedules.
