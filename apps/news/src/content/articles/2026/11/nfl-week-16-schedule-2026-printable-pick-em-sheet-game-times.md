**The National Football League (NFL) lists 16 Week 16 games from Thursday, Dec. 24 to Monday, Dec. 28, 2026, with three on Christmas Day and no team on bye.** NFL.com is the source for every game below, and Sports Illustrated agrees on the Christmas Day games. Four matchups still have no date or time, so the one-page pick'em sheet leaves a blank kickoff line for each of them. Times are Eastern Time (ET), with Pacific Time (PT) added for the holiday games.

## What NFL games are on Saturday, Dec. 26, 2026?

Two, but NFL.com has not said which. Its Week 16 page says two of the four designated matchups "will be played on Saturday at 4:30 PM ET and 8:00 PM ET on NFL Network." That is 1:30 p.m. and 5:00 p.m. PT by our conversion. The page says the remainder will be played either on Thursday night (8:15 p.m. ET), Sunday afternoon (1:00 p.m. and 4:25 p.m. ET), Sunday night (8:20 p.m. ET) or Monday night (8:15 p.m. ET).

## Which four NFL Week 16 games still have no date?

The four designated matchups are Panthers at Steelers, Bengals at Colts, Buccaneers at Falcons and Commanders at Vikings. NFL.com says their dates and times "will be determined and announced at a later date," no later than immediately after Week 14. NFL.com lists ESPN for Panthers at Steelers and no network for the other three. Until the league announces them, write the date and time in by hand on the sheet.

## Can Week 16 Sunday and Monday night games change?

Yes. The league's [2026 Flexible Scheduling Procedures](https://www.nfl.com/legal/flexible-scheduling-procedures) say Sunday Night Football flexing may be used in any week from Week 11 to Week 17, and Monday Night Football flexing in any week from Week 12 to Week 17. Decisions are generally made no later than 6 days before the game. That applies to Jaguars at Cowboys on Dec. 27 and Giants at Lions on Dec. 28, so check NFL.com before you plan around those kickoffs.

## How do you use the NFL Week 16 pick'em sheet?

The one-page sheet lists all 16 games in kickoff order with the network, a blank for your pick and a blank for the final score. The four undated games sit at the bottom with a "Date/time to be determined (TBD)" line to fill in once the league announces them. The tiebreaker is total points in Monday's Giants at Lions game. The first pick is due before 8:15 p.m. ET on Thursday, Dec. 24, when Texans at Eagles kicks off.
