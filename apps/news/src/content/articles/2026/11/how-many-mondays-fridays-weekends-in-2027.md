There are 53 Fridays in 2027 and 52 of every other weekday, by our count from the Gregorian calendar, because the year has 365 days and starts on Friday, Jan. 1. That makes 261 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 104 weekend days, which is 52 full Saturday-Sunday weekends. Below are the counts, the months with five of each weekday and a free printable.

## How many of each weekday are in 2027?

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) states the rule: "years evenly divisible by 4 are leap years, with the exception of centurial years that are not evenly divisible by 400." 2027 is not divisible by 4, so it is a 365-day common year. Fresh Calendars computed everything below from that rule and the Gregorian calendar; no official agency publishes these counts.

365 days is 52 full weeks (364 days) plus 1 day. That extra day is Friday, Dec. 31, so Friday gets a 53rd occurrence.

| Weekday | Days in 2027 | First date | Last date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 52 | Jan. 4 | Dec. 27 |
| Tuesday | 52 | Jan. 5 | Dec. 28 |
| Wednesday | 52 | Jan. 6 | Dec. 29 |
| Thursday | 52 | Jan. 7 | Dec. 30 |
| Friday | 53 | Jan. 1 | Dec. 31 |
| Saturday | 52 | Jan. 2 | Dec. 25 |
| Sunday | 52 | Jan. 3 | Dec. 26 |

## How many weekdays and weekends are in 2027?

Adding the rows gives 261 weekdays (Monday to Friday: 52 + 52 + 52 + 52 + 53) and 104 weekend days (52 Saturdays and 52 Sundays). Because Saturdays and Sundays both occur 52 times, there are 52 full weekends. These are calendar counts before any holiday. For the working days left after federal and stock market holidays, see our [2027 working days by month](/news/working-days-2027-business-days-by-month-federal-nyse/).

## Which months have five Mondays, five Fridays or five weekends in 2027?

A 31-day month has three days that occur five times, a 30-day month has two, and February 2027 (28 days) has none. The table shows each weekday's five-times months.

| Weekday | Months with five in 2027 |
|---|---|
| Monday | March, May, August, November |
| Tuesday | March, June, August, November |
| Wednesday | March, June, September, December |
| Thursday | April, July, September, December |
| Friday | January, April, July, October, December |
| Saturday | January, May, July, October |
| Sunday | January, May, August, October |

A month has five full Saturday-Sunday weekends when it has five Saturdays and five Sundays. That happens in January, May and October 2027. In the other nine months the count is four.

## How many weekdays are in each month of 2027?

Count of Monday-to-Friday days before holidays, with the weekday each month starts on.

| Month | Starts on | Weekdays (Mon. to Fri.) |
|---|---|---|
| January | Friday | 21 |
| February | Monday | 20 |
| March | Monday | 23 |
| April | Thursday | 22 |
| May | Saturday | 21 |
| June | Tuesday | 22 |
| July | Thursday | 22 |
| August | Sunday | 22 |
| September | Wednesday | 22 |
| October | Friday | 21 |
| November | Monday | 22 |

Those two 23-weekday months, March and December, are the ones with five Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays or five Wednesdays, Thursdays and Fridays. ## What changes in 2028?

2028 is a leap year with 366 days, and it starts on a Saturday. Feb. 29, 2028 is a Tuesday. Because 2027 ends on a Friday, the first day of 2028 is the day after. To find years that repeat the 2027 calendar, see [what calendar year is the same as 2027](/news/what-calendar-year-is-the-same-as-2027/).
