**The Vancouver Canucks play 13 games in December 2026, eight of them at Rogers Arena, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 4 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), at Washington, and ends Wednesday, Dec. 30, at home against Seattle.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Vancouver, read Nov. 12, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to PT, which is Vancouver time, and ET, and worked out the weekdays. Canadian TV is the first channel column and the U.S. listing the second. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Canucks December 2026 game times with Sportsnet and U.S. TV

Sportsnet and its channels (Sportsnet Pacific, Sportsnet+) carry 12 of the 13 games in Canada, by our count from the feed. Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports shares three home games, and Prime Video has the Dec. 16 game at Anaheim. In the U.S. column, the market is the one the feed names, "Regional channel" is a local or team outlet the feed lists under a short code, and "None listed" means the feed shows no U.S. broadcaster.

## How many Canucks games are at Rogers Arena in December 2026?

Eight: Dec. 4, 5, 7, 10, 19, 21, 28 and 30. The month's first four home games come in a row, with Buffalo and Dallas on consecutive nights, Fri., Dec. 4, and Sat., Dec. 5. Our own count from the feed: five away games.

Washington appears twice, at Capital One Arena on Dec. 1 and at Rogers Arena on Dec. 7, and the other side of that pairing is in our [Capitals December schedule](/news/washington-capitals-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/). The Kings are the other repeat opponent: they host on Dec. 12 and visit on Dec. 28.

## Canucks road games in December 2026

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **California trip, Dec. 12 to Dec. 16:** three games at the Kings (Crypto.com Arena), the Sharks (SAP Center at San Jose) and the Ducks (Honda Center).
- **Opening game, Dec. 1:** at Washington, the only start before 6 p.m. PT (4 p.m.).
- **Calgary, Sat., Dec. 26:** the Flames at Scotiabank Saddledome, 7 p.m. PT (10 p.m. ET), on Sportsnet, with no U.S. broadcaster listed.
- **No game:** Dec. 22 to Dec. 25, and Dec. 31.

## When do the Canucks play after December?

The first 2027 game is Saturday, Jan. 2, at the Utah Mammoth, at Delta Center, 7 p.m. PT (8 p.m. Mountain Time, 10 p.m. ET).
