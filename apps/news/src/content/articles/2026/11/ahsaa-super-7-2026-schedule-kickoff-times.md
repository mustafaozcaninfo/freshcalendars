**The Alabama High School Athletic Association (AHSAA) state football championships run Tuesday through Saturday, Dec. 1-5, 2026, at Hancock Whitney Stadium in Mobile, with 12 title games on five days, according to the Mobile Sports Authority, the event's host organization.** AHSAA's web addresses for the event still use the name Super 7. The boys' finals for 1A to 6A are Dec. 3-5 at 11 a.m. and 7 p.m. CT, and the schedule table below adds Eastern Time for every game.

## Super 7 2026 schedule: all 12 games in CT and ET

The Mobile Sports Authority links a schedule PDF headed "Hancock Whitney Stadium on USA Campus - Dec 1-5, 2026" (USA is the University of South Alabama). The PDF prints no time zone. Mobile is on Central Time (CT), so the times are CT, and the Eastern Time (ET) column is our addition of one hour.

| Day | CT | ET | Game |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tue., Dec. 1 | 10 a.m. | 11 a.m. | Flag football, private school (Single A and Double AA combined) |
| Tue., Dec. 1 | 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | Flag football, 1A-4A |
| Tue., Dec. 1 | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | Flag football, 5A |
| Tue., Dec. 1 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Flag football, 6A |
| Wed., Dec. 2 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | Single A (private school boys) |
| Wed., Dec. 2 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Double A (private school boys) |
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | 1A |
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 4A |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | 2A |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 5A |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | 3A |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 6A |

Yahoo Sports reported the same times on Apr. 15, 2026, when AHSAA released the schedule. Finalists are not listed on the host's page or the PDF.

## Which day is my class playing?

The eight boys' games each have their own day and time. The Mobile Sports Authority describes the format as six public-school classifications (1A-6A), two private-school classifications (Single A and Double AA) and four girls' flag football divisions.

| Class | Date | Kickoff (CT) |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | Thu., Dec. 3 | 11 a.m. |
| 2A | Fri., Dec. 4 | 11 a.m. |
| 3A | Sat., Dec. 5 | 11 a.m. |
| 4A | Thu., Dec. 3 | 7 p.m. |
| 5A | Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. |
| 6A | Sat., Dec. 5 | 7 p.m. |
| Single A | Wed., Dec. 2 | 11 a.m. |
| Double AA | Wed., Dec. 2 | 7 p.m. |

The 5A and 6A classes also have flag football finals on Dec. 1, at 4 p.m. and 7 p.m. CT. One flag football game covers classes 1A through 4A, and the private-school flag football game combines Single A and Double AA.

## What is new in 2026?

The Mobile Sports Authority calls it an expanded format: 24 teams in 12 championship games over five days. Yahoo Sports reported that the scene switches from Protective Stadium in Birmingham to Hancock Whitney Stadium in Mobile in 2026, and Sports Illustrated placed the stadium on the University of South Alabama campus. The girls' flag football finals open the week on Dec. 1.

## How do the finals fit after the playoff rounds?

AHSAA's bracket page lists the football playoff rounds 1 to 4 on Nov. 6, 13, 20 and 27. For 1A to 6A, the fourth round on Friday, Nov. 27 is the last round before the finals in Mobile, which start Tuesday, Dec. 1.

## Tickets and gates at Hancock Whitney Stadium

| Item | AHSAA's ticket page |
|---|---|
| Digital tickets | Through GoFan at gofan.co/AHSAA |
| Cash tickets | South Box Office, between Gate 2 and Gate 3 |
| Gates | Open 2 hours before each championship game |
| Tuesday, Dec. 1 | Gates open at 9 a.m. and stay open through the 3rd quarter of the 7 p.m. game |

AHSAA's page does not state a ticket price, and no parking details are given on the pages read.

## How to watch the AHSAA state finals

AHSAA says all 12 championship games of the 2026 state football championships will be broadcast live. Video is on the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network, and radio is on the AHSAA Radio Network, produced by R&B Media. The pages read do not give a subscription price.
