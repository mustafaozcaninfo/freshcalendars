**Cherokee County School District (CCSD) in Canton, Georgia, closes schools and the district Nov. 23-27, 2026 for Thanksgiving week, then Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 for winter break and April 5-9, 2027 for spring break**, according to its "2026-27 School Year Calendar - text" page. The last day of school is May 25, 2027. This is the Georgia district, not a Cherokee County in North Carolina or South Carolina.

The district's board changed the 2026-27 calendar on April 23, 2026, and the print PDF carries that footnote, so the dates here are the ones the district posted on Nov. 5, 2026. This story adds a quick guide to the district's "Holiday Break" labels, a table of every day off, weekdays off by month, school days between breaks, and a side-by-side with Cobb and Fulton counties. All counts are our arithmetic from the district's dates.

## Cherokee County School District breaks at a glance

The district calls every break "Holiday Break." Our labels in the first column say which week is the usual fall, Thanksgiving, winter, February and spring break; the district does not use those names.

| Our label | District's dates and wording | Weekdays off | Students return (our reading) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break | Sept. 21-25, 2026, "Holiday Break - Schools Closed" (past) | 5 | Mon., Sept. 28, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving break | Nov. 23-27, 2026, "Holiday Break- Schools & District Closed" | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, "Holiday Break - Schools & District Closed" | 10 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (printed) |
| February break | Feb. 15-19, 2027, "Holiday Break - Schools Closed" | 5 | Mon., Feb. 22, 2027 |
| Spring break | April 5-9, 2027, "Holiday Break - Schools & District Closed" | 5 | Mon., April 12, 2027 |

That is six full Monday-to-Friday weeks off, because winter break covers two. The page prints only one return day, Jan. 5, as "First Day, Second Semester." The others are the next weekday the calendar does not list as off. Jan. 4 is a student holiday and teacher planning day, so students are out 11 weekdays in a row from Dec. 21. The stretch from Sept. 28 to Nov. 20 is the longest at 39 weekdays, about eight weeks with Oct. 12 as the only day off. The page does not give an official instructional-day count, so we do not compare the 178 to one.

## How does Cherokee County compare with Cobb and Fulton?

All three districts share the Nov. 23-27 and April 5-9 weeks, per each district's own calendar. Their other dates differ. Our [Cobb County calendar story](/news/cobb-county-school-calendar-2026-27-breaks-holidays/), [Fulton County calendar story](/news/fulton-county-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) and [Gwinnett County calendar story](/news/gwinnett-county-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) have each in more detail.

| | Cherokee | Cobb | Fulton |
|---|---|---|---|
| First day | Aug. 3 | Aug. 3 | Aug. 3 |
| Fall break | Sept. 21-25 | Sept. 21-25 | Sept. 21-23, plus professional development Sept. 24-25 |
| Thanksgiving | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 |
| Winter | Dec. 21-Jan. 1 | Dec. 21-31 | Dec. 21-Jan. 1 |
| February | Feb. 15-19 | Feb. 15-19 | Feb. 15-17, plus professional development Feb. 18-19 |
| Spring | April 5-9 | April 5-9, "Spring Holidays" | April 5-9 |
| Last day (2027) | May 25 | May 19 | May 27 |

Cherokee's last day is six days after Cobb's and two days before Fulton's, by the dates above. Cobb lists Election Day, Nov. 3, as a student holiday, where Cherokee uses a remote learning day. ## What happens to the Cherokee County calendar when the weather turns?

The district's inclement weather page says Digital Learning Days are used for "intermittent school closures." It lists no makeup days, so none are in the printable. The calendar itself already uses two remote learning days, Nov. 3 and March 9.

## Did the Cherokee County school board change the 2026-27 calendar?

Yes. In a post dated April 24, 2026, the district said the board "unanimously approved changes to the school calendars for the 2026-27 and 2027-28 school years" at its Thursday, April 23, 2026 meeting. The print PDF is footnoted "Amended at the April 23, 2026 Board Meeting." Our sources do not say which dates changed. The board first approved the calendars on a post dated July 19, 2024: "The school board approved calendars for the 2025-26, 2026-27 and 2027-28 school years."

## What are Cherokee County's 2027-28 dates?

As listed on the district's 2027-28 page, the first day is Aug. 2, 2027 and the last day, "Last Day of School/End of Semester," is May 24, 2028. That page lists holiday breaks Nov. 22-26, Feb. 21-25 ("Holiday Break - Schools Closed") and April 3-7. Because the board changed both years in April 2026, check the district page before booking travel that far out.
