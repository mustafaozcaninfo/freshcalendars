**July 2027 begins on Thursday, July 1 and has one federal holiday: Independence Day, observed on Monday, July 5, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** July 4 itself is a Sunday. The full moon on Sunday, July 18 coincides with a penumbral lunar eclipse, and the Major League Baseball (MLB) All-Star Game is Tuesday, July 13. The countdowns below are counted from Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026.

## Independence Day 2027: observed on Monday, July 5

OPM's federal holiday list shows "Monday, July 05" for Independence Day in 2027. Its footnote reads "If a holiday falls on a Sunday, for most Federal employees, the following Monday will be treated as a holiday". The long weekend therefore runs from Saturday, July 3 to Monday, July 5. Independence Day is the only federal holiday in July. Who is closed that day is in [4th of July 2027 observed on Monday, July 5](/news/july-4-2027-observed-monday-july-5-weekend-holidays/), and the date itself is on [When is Independence Day?](/when-is/independence-day/). The month before is in [June 2027 important dates](/news/june-2027-important-dates-holidays-days-until/), which covers Juneteenth.

Canada's Holidays Act says "July 1, not being a Sunday, is a legal holiday," and July 1, 2027 is a Thursday, so the day is not moved. GOV.UK lists "12 July", a Monday, for the Battle of the Boyne in Northern Ireland only.

## Moon phase times in Eastern Time for July 2027

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes times in Universal Time (UT). Daylight time is in effect all month, so New York is four hours behind UT on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). The new moon is at 03:02 UT on July 4, which is 11:02 p.m. on Saturday, July 3 in New York. Our [2027 Moon phase calendar](/news/moon-phases-2027-full-moon-calendar/) has the other months.

## Aphelion and the July 18 penumbral eclipse

USNO lists aphelion, Earth's farthest point from the Sun in its orbit, on July 5, 2027 at 05:06 UT, which is 1:06 a.m. EDT. NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center lists a penumbral lunar eclipse on July 18, 2027, the same day as the full moon. The pages used here do not say where it is visible, so check NASA's eclipse site before planning to watch. NASA lists no solar eclipse in July; the total solar eclipse is on Aug. 2, 2027, the month after.

## Sports and events in July 2027

MLB's site lists the 2027 All-Star Game on Tuesday, July 13 at Wrigley Field in Chicago. The Federation Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) says the Women's World Cup in Brazil is "played from 24 June to 25 July 2027", so the tournament ends on Sunday, July 25. The Open's site says it "returns to St Andrews. 11-18 July 2027", a range that includes practice days, so no round dates are given here.

Formula 1's 2027 calendar shows the British Grand Prix on July 2-4, the Austrian on July 9-11, the Belgian on July 23-25 and the Hungarian on July 30 to Aug. 1. Comic-Con International in San Diego lists the show for July 22-25 with a preview night on July 21.

## Parents' Day, Armistice Day and dates not yet set

The Parents' Day law, 36 U.S.C. 135, says "The fourth Sunday in July is Parents' Day." In 2027 that is Sunday, July 25. National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day on July 27 was set by 36 U.S.C. 127 "of each year until 2003"; since then it has been marked by presidential proclamation.

Several July events have no confirmed 2027 date here. Wimbledon's 2027 dates are not yet confirmed on wimbledon.com, the Tour de France dates are not yet confirmed on letour.fr and Amazon Prime Day 2027 is not yet announced.
