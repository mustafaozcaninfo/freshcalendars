**World Science Day for Peace and Development is Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026.** The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) says the day is "Celebrated every 10 November" and gives the 2026 theme as "Common ground through science diplomacy." Below are the day's four aims, our own classroom and home activity ideas for each, and a free one-page sheet (UNESCO's page checked Nov. 6, 2026).

## What is the World Science Day 2026 theme?

UNESCO's page gives the 2026 theme as "Common ground through science diplomacy." UNESCO is the organization behind the day, so its wording is the one we use.

## When is World Science Day in 2026 and 2027?

The date is Nov. 10 every year, so only the weekday changes. The weekdays are our calculation.

| Year | Nov. 10 falls on |
|---|---|
| 2026 | Tuesday |
| 2027 | Wednesday |

The United Nations (UN) also observes an International Week of Science and Peace each year in the week in which 11 November falls, and its list of international days shows the week starting "09 Nov." In 2026 that is Monday, Nov. 9, to Sunday, Nov. 15; the end date and the Monday start are our calculation from that rule, not UN text.

Mark it on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) or see the other observances in [November 2026 important dates and days until](/news/november-2026-important-dates-days-until/).

## What are the four aims of the day?

UNESCO lists these aims:

1. "Strengthen public awareness of the role of science for peaceful and sustainable societies"
2. "Promote national and international solidarity for shared knowledge and scientific cooperation"
3. "Renew national and international commitment for the use of science for the benefit of societies"
4. "Draw attention to the challenges faced by science and raising support for scientific endeavours"

## Which science activities match each aim?

These ideas are our suggestions, not UNESCO's. The ages are our rough guide for a classroom or a home.

| UNESCO aim | Our activity idea | Age |
|---|---|---|
| Public awareness of science for peaceful and sustainable societies | List five ways science helps your street or school, then share the list | 6 to 10 |
| Solidarity for shared knowledge and scientific cooperation | Shared-knowledge swap: each person teaches the group one fact, then the group builds one poster | 8 to 12 |
| Commitment to use science for the benefit of societies | Pick a small local problem and plan a test that could help | 11 to 14 |
| Attention to the challenges science faces | Write a question to a local scientist or teacher about what makes their work hard | 13 and up |

## Does UNESCO list a 2026 event?

UNESCO's page does not give a 2026 event date or place, so we list none. UNESCO also covers learning in a separate observance; see [International Day of Education on Sunday, Jan. 24, 2027](/news/international-day-of-education-2027-sunday-january-24/).
