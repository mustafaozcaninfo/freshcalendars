Bank of America cardholders get free general admission at partner museums on **Saturday, Dec. 5, and Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026**, the next Museums on Us weekend on the bank's museum list. The program covers "the first full weekend of every month," Bank of America says, and four listed museums limit it to a single day.

December 1, 2026, is a Tuesday, so the first full weekend is Dec. 5-6. The bank's page says the offer is for "our cardholders" and sends visitors to each museum for its own procedures. The city table below lists partner museums in 15 metro areas with those day limits, and a second table computes the 2027 weekends.

The Children's Museum of Houston's page says Museums on Us reaches "more than 225 cultural institutions," so the full list is longer than this table.

## What to bring to a Museums on Us museum

Partner pages such as the Children's Museum of Houston's and the Phillips Collection's set out the same basics. Check your museum's own page, because these two pages do not speak for every partner.

- An active Bank of America, Merrill or Bank of America Private Bank (U.S. Trust) credit or debit card. The Phillips Collection says physical or digital cards are accepted and expired cards are not.
- A photo ID, such as a driver's license, passport or other government-issued ID, which the Phillips Collection says it requires.
- Ticket plans for the exhibits. The Children's Museum of Houston says the offer excludes ticketed and special exhibitions and fundraising events, and describes the program as "Cardholder only."
