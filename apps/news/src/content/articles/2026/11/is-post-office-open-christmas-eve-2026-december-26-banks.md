**The Postal Service did not list Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026, as a holiday, but it did list Friday, Dec. 25.** The U.S. Postal Service (USPS) "Holidays and Events" page shows "Friday December 25 Christmas Day" for 2026 and has no entry for Dec. 24 or Saturday, Dec. 26. The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and Nasdaq close early on Dec. 24 and are closed on Dec. 25. USPS has not posted 2026 hours for Christmas Eve, so confirm your own Post Office before you go.

"Not on the list" means the source names no holiday for that day. As of Nov. 22, no 2026 executive order closing federal agencies on Dec. 24 or Dec. 26 had been announced. ## Is the post office open on Christmas Eve 2026?

Dec. 24 is not a postal holiday: the USPS 2026 section lists only Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25. USPS has not posted 2026 Christmas Eve hours. In 2025, a USPS release dated Dec. 24 said that "others may have limited hours on Christmas Eve, Wednesday, Dec. 24," so hours at some locations can differ from the usual. Look up your Post Office on the USPS locator.

For 2027, USPS's holiday page carries a note that "Post Offices will be open on Friday, June 18, and Friday, Dec. 24, and deliveries will occur as normal." That note covers 2027 only.

## Is mail delivered on Dec. 26, 2026?

Saturday, Dec. 26, is not on the USPS 2026 holiday list, and USPS has not posted a 2026 note for it. Last year's pattern is not a rule for this year, but it is the latest USPS wording: for 2025, USPS said "All Post Office locations will be open, and regular mail delivery will resume, on Friday, Dec. 26, 2025."

USPS recommended 2026 mailing dates in a Sept. 22, 2026, release and urged customers to "plan to mail and ship packages early to ensure arrival by Dec. 25."

## Are banks open on Christmas Eve 2026?

The Federal Reserve's K.8 page, last updated July 8, 2026, lists "Christmas Day | December 25" and no entry for Dec. 24 or Dec. 26. Banks set their own branch hours, and we could not read any bank's 2026 page, so check yours for Thursday.

## Is the stock market open on Christmas Eve 2026?

Yes, for a shortened session. The NYSE says: "Each market will close early at 1:00 p.m. (1:15 p.m. for eligible options) on Thursday, December 24, 2026." Nasdaq Trader lists "December 24, 2026 | Early Close* - U.S. | 1:00 p.m." and "December 25, 2026 | Christmas Holiday - U.S. | Closed." Stock markets do not trade on Saturday, Dec. 26.

SIFMA keeps the bond market on its own schedule: "Early Close (2:00 p.m. Eastern Time): Thursday, December 24, 2026," with Friday, Dec. 25, as "Christmas Day," a recommended close.

## Are federal offices closed on Christmas Eve 2026?

OPM's list shows "Friday, December 25 | Christmas Day" for 2026 and nothing for Dec. 24 or Dec. 26.
