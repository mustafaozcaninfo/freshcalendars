## Pip budget of Wednesday's boards

Add every half of every domino, then subtract what the sum regions demand. The remainder lands in the equal, less-than, more-than and no-rule regions.

| Level | Pips on the dominoes | Sum regions | Pips they require | Left for other regions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 30 | 2 | 20 | 10 |
| Medium | 44 | 4 | 16 | 28 |
| Hard | 89 | 11 | 34 | 55 |

On Easy, the two sum regions soak up 20 of the 30 pips. Hard's 11 sum regions take 34 of 89. Easy's equal region needs two halves of one value, and only the values 1, 3 and 5 show twice in its set.

## The forced first move on each board

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left. A search of every legal placement against the saved data finds one arrangement on Easy, two on Medium and 32 on Hard.

**Easy.** R1C3 and R1C4 must total less than 2, which takes a 0 and a 1 or two 0s, and the set holds a single 0 half. That 0 therefore goes into the R1C3 and R1C4 pair, because nothing else can satisfy it. R4C2 must also be less than 2, and with the 0 spoken for it can only be a 1. Start at R4C2.

**Medium.** R3C1 stands alone and adds up to 4, so it holds a 4, the one value that region allows. R1C4 is more than 4, and the only halves above 4 are the three 6s. Start with R3C1, then R1C4 takes a 6.

**Hard.** R1C5 adds up to 0 and touches only R1C4, which adds up to 3. The one domino that pairs a 0 with a 3 is the 0-3, so its place is settled before any other cell. Start at R1C4 and R1C5.

**Hard (16 dominoes on a frame 9 rows by 9 columns)**

- Hint 2: R3C7, R3C8 and R8C5 carry no rule at all.

## What the free cells and turnable dominoes mean for Hard

Hard looks wide, but 18 of its 25 regions are single cells, which fixes most values outright. The looseness sits in a few spots: the no-rule pair, one sum pair, and the "more than" and "less than" pairs let five dominoes swap ends, which is why the search counts 32 arrangements where Easy has one. Take the forced domino first, work through the single-cell sums, and leave the pairs for last.
