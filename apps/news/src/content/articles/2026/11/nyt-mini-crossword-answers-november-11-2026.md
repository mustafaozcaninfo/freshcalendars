**Ian Livengood built the New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026: a 5-by-5 grid with 10 clues and just two black squares.** Each clue below has a hint that opens on a tap, and the filled grid sits in the marked spoiler section at the end.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (5 letters), *Animal whose prominent feature makes it an unofficial mascot of Wednesday*: a desert animal known for the bumps on its back; it starts with C and ends with L.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Black pizza topping*: the small fruit of a Mediterranean tree, sliced into rings; it starts with O and ends with E.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Mischievous sprite*: a tiny fairy-tale creature with pointed ears and a taste for pranks; it starts with P and ends with E.
- **8-Across** (5 letters), *In a pickle*: unable to move forward or get free; it starts with S and ends with K.
- **9-Across** (3 letters), *Make a choice*: to pick one option over another; it starts with O and ends with T.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (4 letters), *Halloween costumes with badges and handcuffs*: the plural of a slang word for police officers; it starts with C and ends with S.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Supreme Court justice Samuel*: the justice's surname; it starts with A and ends with O, and the last letter is also the first letter of 9-Across.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Minor confusion*: a small slip in communication, such as two guests told different start times; it starts with M and ends with P.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *Force out, as from an apartment*: to remove a tenant by order of a landlord or court; it starts with E and ends with T.
- **5-Down** (4 letters), *Vegetable that resembles a large scallion*: a long white-and-green stalk that flavors soups; it starts with L and ends with K.

## Wednesday's animal clue, the clue types and Tuesday's grid

The clue to try last is 1-Across. It never defines the animal; it gives a joke. Wednesday carries the nickname "hump day", because the week climbs to Wednesday and then slopes down toward the weekend. The clue asks for the animal that carries the matching body part on its back and treats it as the day's mascot. Once you see the nickname, the five letters follow, and the answer also supplies the first letters of 1-Down, 2-Down, 3-Down, 4-Down and 5-Down.
