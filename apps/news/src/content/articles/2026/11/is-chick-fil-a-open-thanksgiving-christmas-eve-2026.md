## Is Chick-fil-A open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, and Chick-fil-A says all U.S. restaurants are closed. For the Thanksgiving meal, the page says to "order Chick-fil-A Catering and schedule pickup or delivery before the holiday," and adds that "Catering menus and availability differ by location." For other chains, see [which restaurants are open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/restaurants-open-thanksgiving-2026-hours-by-chain/).

Friday, Nov. 27 is Black Friday. Chick-fil-A has posted no Black Friday hours page, and the Sunday and holiday closures do not cover that day (computed), so each restaurant keeps its own hours. Check the Chick-fil-A app for your location; no hours are given here because none are posted.

## Is Chick-fil-A open on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day?

Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, is an open day with possible limited hours. Chick-fil-A's rule is that restaurants are open on Christmas Eve unless it falls on a Sunday, and this year it falls on a Thursday (computed). Restaurants are closed on Christmas, Friday, Dec. 25. The [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 store hours](/news/christmas-eve-christmas-day-2026-store-hours/) list covers other retailers, and [when Christmas falls](/when-is/christmas/) is on the calendar site.

## Is Chick-fil-A open on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day?

Yes, with possible limited hours. Chick-fil-A says U.S. restaurants are open on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day unless the holiday falls on a Sunday. New Year's Eve is Thursday, Dec. 31, and New Year's Day is Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 (computed), so neither is a Sunday.

## How many days is Chick-fil-A closed from Thanksgiving to New Year's?

Seven days from Nov. 26, 2026 to Jan. 2, 2027 (computed). Chick-fil-A says its restaurants are closed on Sundays "to give Team Members a day of rest before reopening on Monday morning." Add Thanksgiving and Christmas to the Sundays of Nov. 29, Dec. 6, 13, 20 and 27, and the count is seven. The Sunday page is about delivery, but its closure sentence covers the restaurants. Mark them on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When to order Chick-fil-A catering for Thanksgiving

Chick-fil-A says you can place a Catering order "24 hours before your event at all Restaurants, and many Restaurants offer lower lead times," and that "lead times vary by Restaurant." For a Wednesday, Nov. 25 pickup, the 24-hour rule means ordering by the same time on Tuesday, Nov. 24 (computed from the rule). Chick-fil-A has posted no separate holiday deadline.

## Peppermint drinks run past the holidays

Chick-fil-A says its Peppermint seasonal beverages are available at participating U.S. restaurants from Nov. 16, 2026 "through January 2, 2027, while supplies last." Details are in the [Chick-fil-A peppermint milkshake story](/news/chick-fil-a-peppermint-milkshake-2026/).

## What Chick-fil-A has not posted

Not yet announced: opening and closing times for Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day, hours for Black Friday, and any holiday catering deadline beyond the 24-hour rule. The pages cover U.S. restaurants only.
