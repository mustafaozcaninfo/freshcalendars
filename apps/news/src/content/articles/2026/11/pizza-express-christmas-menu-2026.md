**Pizza Express sells any starter and any main for £20 every day from Tuesday 3 November 2026 to Monday 4 January 2027, according to its "Christmas 2 for 20" page.** The offer is for dining in at its pizzerias only, and seven named pizzerias do not take part. Our research desk read the Pizza Express pages on 21 November; the dishes and rules below follow their wording.

## What is on the Pizza Express Christmas menu 2026?

The restaurant menu page lists these festive dishes. It shows no prices: it says "Select restaurant to see prices". The last column applies the rules of the £20 deal, which is a starter plus a main.

| Course | Dish | What the page says | Counts for the £20 deal? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Three Cheese Fondue Wreath | To share; a wreath of Dough Balls filled with cheese fondue | No, a sharing starter |
| Starter | Dough Balls in Blankets | Dough Balls wrapped in crisp pancetta, with rosemary, Gran Milano cheese and caramelised red onion chutney | Yes, a starter not marked to share |
| Romana pizza | The Festive Five Cheese | Gluten-free version listed | Yes, a Romana pizza |
| Romana pizza | The Christmas Crackler | Gluten-free version listed | Yes, a Romana pizza |
| Side | Sprout & Pancetta Slaw | Named on the page | Not part of the deal |
| Dessert | Loaded Snowball Dough Balls | Named on the page | Not part of the deal |
| Dessert | Vegan Loaded Snowball Dough Balls | Named on the page | Not part of the deal |
| Dessert | Cracking Christmas Brownie | Belgian chocolate brownie served warm with vanilla gelato, a chocolate shell and white chocolate treats | Not part of the deal |
| Dessert | Snowball Dough Ball Sharer | Serves 2 | Not part of the deal |
| Drink | Let It Sloe, White Christmas, So-ho-ho Spritz, Strawberry Daiquiri The Halls, Yule Love Our Strawberry Mimosa, I'm Dreaming of a Passion Fruit Martini, Aperol Spritzmas | Seven festive drinks | Not part of the deal |

Retail Times reported on 27 October that Caviar Dough Balls are available at selected restaurants only. They are not on the Pizza Express menu page, so no price or rule is given here.

## What counts for the Pizza Express £20 deal?

One starter and one main cost £20, and two starters and two mains come to £40. The rules on the deal page:

- **Starters:** any starter or a portion of Dough Balls. Sharing starters are not included.
- **Mains:** any Classic, Leggera or Romana pizza, Al Forno or salad.
- **Alternatives:** gluten-free, vegan and dairy-free alternatives are included.
- **Not included:** sharing starters, Large Classic, Duo Large Classic and Duo Romana pizzas, and the children's menu.
- **Starter only:** a starter without a main stays full price.
- **Splitting:** bills cannot be split to use more than one discount.
- **Dessert add-on:** the Christmas page offers a dessert and a glass of Prosecco for £5 each.
- **Groups:** the page asks you to say if there are 7 or more in your group.

## Which Pizza Express pizzerias do not offer the £20 deal?

The deal page names Barratt Street, Dean Street, Inverness, Greenwich The O2, Brixton, Earls Court and Finsbury Park. It is also not available at airport pizzerias, Welcome Break sites or PizzaExpress Live venues, or at supermarket concessions. It is not for takeaway, collection or delivery, or on Just Eat, Uber Eats or Deliveroo. The page names restaurants, not regions, so check a pizzeria that is not on the list when you book.

## When does the Pizza Express Christmas menu end?

The deal page gives Monday 4 January 2027 as the last day. Retail Times reported the same window on 27 October: "from Tuesday 3rd November 2026 until Monday 4th January 2027". The booking page calls the festive menu "here for a limited time".

## How do you book a Pizza Express Christmas table?

The booking page says: "Pick a restaurant, date and time below." Choosing a restaurant is also how the menu page shows prices. Prices can differ by restaurant, and none are printed here except the £20 deal and the £5 dessert and Prosecco offer. Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year opening hours are not announced on the pages we read.
