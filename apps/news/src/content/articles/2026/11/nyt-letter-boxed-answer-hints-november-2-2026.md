## How Letter Boxed works

You get twelve letters, three on each side of a square. Make words of at least three letters by connecting letters, and never use two letters from the same side one after the other. Each new word must start with the last letter of the word before it. Every letter has to be used at least once, and the goal is to finish within par. A new puzzle comes out each day.
