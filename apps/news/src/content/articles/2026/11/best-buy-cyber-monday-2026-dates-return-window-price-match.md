**Best Buy's Cyber Monday 2026 stores are open 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Monday, Nov. 30, according to Best Buy Corporate News' Nov. 17 holiday hours.** Cyber Sunday, Nov. 29, runs 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Best Buy calls the weekend its Cyber Sunday and Monday Sale Event, says shoppers can save up to 50%, and adds a limited-time Cyber Monday Flash Sale. It says hours may vary by location, so check your store page. The Black Friday side of the calendar is in our [Best Buy Black Friday story](/news/best-buy-black-friday-2026-doorbusters-october-30-holiday-calendar/); the dates below cover the weekend after it.

## What are Best Buy's Cyber Monday 2026 hours?

| Date (2026) | Day | Store hours (local time) | Hours open (our count) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 27 | Friday | 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. | 16 |
| Nov. 28 | Saturday | 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. | 13 |
| Nov. 29 | Sunday (Cyber Sunday) | 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. | 12 |
| Nov. 30 | Monday (Cyber Monday) | 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. | 12 |

Cyber Monday opens an hour later than Cyber Sunday and closes an hour later. Best Buy gives these hours without a time zone, so they are local store time.

## When do Best Buy's Cyber Monday deals end?

Best Buy states its end times in Central Time (CT). Its fine print says the Cyber Sunday and Monday event ends Nov. 30 at 11:59 p.m. CT, and the doorbusters end a day earlier, on Nov. 29 at 11:59 p.m. CT. Prices are valid during the event but may start before it and end after it, with limited quantities and no rainchecks.

| Sale window | Ends (Best Buy) | ET | MT | PT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving 24-Hour Flash Sale | Nov. 26, 11:59 p.m. CT | Nov. 27, 12:59 a.m. | Nov. 26, 10:59 p.m. | Nov. 26, 9:59 p.m. |
| Cyber Sunday and Monday doorbusters | Nov. 29, 11:59 p.m. CT | Nov. 30, 12:59 a.m. | Nov. 29, 10:59 p.m. | Nov. 29, 9:59 p.m. |
| Cyber Sunday and Monday event | Nov. 30, 11:59 p.m. CT | Dec. 1, 12:59 a.m. | Nov. 30, 10:59 p.m. | Nov. 30, 9:59 p.m. |
| Cyber Week Deals | Dec. 6, 11:59 p.m. CT | Dec. 7, 12:59 a.m. | Dec. 6, 10:59 p.m. | Dec. 6, 9:59 p.m. |

The Eastern Time (ET), Mountain Time (MT) and Pacific Time (PT) columns are our conversion. For an East Coast shopper, the doorbusters are gone an hour into Monday morning, before the stores open at 10 a.m.

## When does the Best Buy Cyber Monday sale start online?

Best Buy has not said. The Nov. 17 release names the weekend, the 50% ceiling and the Cyber Monday Flash Sale but gives no hour for any of them. Cyber Week Deals follow on Dec. 1-6, with more deals available throughout the week.

## What is Best Buy's holiday return window?

Best Buy's extended policy covers most purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 31. They can be returned through Jan. 15, 2027. My Best Buy Plus and My Best Buy Total members have through Jan. 31, 2027. Best Buy points shoppers to BestBuy.com/Returns for exceptions.

A purchase on Cyber Monday, Nov. 30, therefore has 46 days to the Friday, Jan. 15 deadline and 62 days to the Sunday, Jan. 31 member deadline, by our count.

## Does Best Buy price match during Cyber Monday?

Best Buy's Holiday Price Match Guarantee says that if the price of an available product sold by Best Buy drops during the holiday season, it will match it. The release excludes Marketplace products and says limitations apply, with details at BestBuy.com/PMG. Neither Best Buy announcement gives an end date for the price-match period, so check that page before relying on it into January.

## What else runs on the same dates?

Plus and Total members earn double rewards on purchases from Nov. 19 through Dec. 6. Best Buy's Last-Minute Savings Event runs Dec. 18-24. Our [Cyber Monday date page](/when-is/cyber-monday/) and the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html) show where these fall on the month.

What Best Buy has not published: Cyber Monday doorbuster prices, the Flash Sale's start time and length, and any Cyber Monday online start time.
