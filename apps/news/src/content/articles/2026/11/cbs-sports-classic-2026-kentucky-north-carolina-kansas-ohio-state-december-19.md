**The CBS Sports Classic 2026 is Saturday, Dec. 19 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, with Kansas vs. Ohio State at 12 p.m. ET and North Carolina vs. Kentucky at 2:30 p.m. ET, according to the event's official frequently asked questions (FAQ) page.** The organizer's Aug. 25, 2026 release calls the second start "approximately" 2:30 p.m. ET, since that game follows the first. Below: the tip times in four U.S. time zones, the matchups set through 2029, last year's results and a one-page viewing card.

Kentucky Athletics says the event "is broadcast each year on The CBS Television Network and streamed live on Paramount+." Add the date to your [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## What time are the CBS Sports Classic games on Dec. 19?

Times are from the organizer's FAQ (last updated October 2026) and its Aug. 25, 2026 release. The Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT) and Pacific Time (PT) columns are converted from ET.

| Game | ET | CT | MT | PT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kansas vs. Ohio State | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. | 9 a.m. |
| North Carolina vs. Kentucky (approximate start) | 2:30 p.m. | 1:30 p.m. | 12:30 p.m. | 11:30 a.m. |

Kentucky's own schedule lists the game as "Sat. Dec 19 vs. North Carolina" at Madison Square Garden in New York, 2:30 pm. The first game's length decides when the second one starts, so check the broadcast on the day.

## How do you get CBS Sports Classic tickets?

The organizer's FAQ says "Tickets are on sale now," and its tickets page links to [Ticketmaster's listing for the Dec. 19, 2026 event](https://www.ticketmaster.com/cbs-sports-classic-new-york-new-york-12-19-2026/event/3B00643BE4CF9B65). The Aug. 25 release said general public tickets would go on sale Oct. 7, with a one-day presale on Oct. 6.

Official travel packages "include tickets to both games and hotel accommodations in downtown NYC," per the FAQ. The organizer's pages list no ticket prices, and they do not say whether a single ticket covers both games.

## Which matchups are set through 2029?

The organizer's Jan. 28, 2026 release says the CBS Sports Classic added Kansas and "has been extended through 2029 with all four schools." It says the matchups and dates for 2027, 2028 and 2029 "have also been set." Kentucky Athletics gives Kentucky's games, and the Lawrence Journal-World, a newspaper, gave Kansas's; the two agree. Both say the sites are not yet determined.

| Date | Game one | Game two |
|---|---|---|
| Sat., Dec. 19, 2026 | Kansas vs. Ohio State (12 p.m. ET) | North Carolina vs. Kentucky (2:30 p.m. ET) |
| Sat., Dec. 18, 2027 | Kentucky vs. Ohio State | Kansas vs. North Carolina |
| Sat., Dec. 16, 2028 | Kentucky vs. Kansas | Not announced |
| Sat., Dec. 15, 2029 | Kentucky vs. North Carolina | Kansas vs. Ohio State |

Kentucky Athletics wrote: "Kentucky will take on Ohio State on Dec. 18, 2027, Kansas on Dec. 16, 2028 and North Carolina again on Dec. 15, 2029." The Journal-World reported that Kansas will face North Carolina on Dec. 18, 2027, Kentucky on Dec. 16, 2028 and the Buckeyes again on Dec. 15, 2029, "all at locations yet to be determined." Neither source states the other 2028 game, and neither gives game times for those years. Weekdays are computed from the calendar.

## What happened at the 2025 CBS Sports Classic?

The 2025 edition was Saturday, Dec. 20 at State Farm Arena in Atlanta, with both games on CBS and Paramount+, the organizer said on May 1, 2025. The results, per the organizer on Dec. 21, 2025:

| 2025 game | Result |
|---|---|
| Kentucky vs. St. John's | Kentucky won 78-66 |
| North Carolina vs. Ohio State | North Carolina rallied to win 71-70 |

The organizer's Jan. 28, 2026 release says the 2026 event will return to Madison Square Garden.
