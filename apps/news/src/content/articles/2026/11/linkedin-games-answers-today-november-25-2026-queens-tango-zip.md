## Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints for Wednesday, Nov. 25

- **Pinpoint #939, first hint:** four of the five clue words are single words; the fifth is a short phrase of two.
- **Pinpoint #939, second hint:** after the second clue, stop thinking about what the words mean alone and ask what each could be attached to.
- **Pinpoint #939, third hint:** the hidden category is a pattern in how the words are used, not a topic such as food, sport or travel.
- **Crossclimb #939, the ladder:** seven words of four letters, joined by six one-letter swaps.
- **Crossclimb #939, the locked ends:** the top and bottom rows share one clue, so they are synonyms of each other.
- **Crossclimb #939, the middle clues:** one rung is a Renaissance Faire performer, one is a bare head, and one is a unit of length.

## What to try after the second Pinpoint #939 clue

The first two clue words name physical objects, which tempts you toward clothing or hardware. Keep that idea loose. By clue three the words stop being objects: one describes an absence and one describes a rank among others. A category that covers both objects and ideas has to be about grammar, not things. Test each guess by asking whether it would still work for the third and fourth clues; if it cannot take an abstract word, drop it. The fifth clue ends in a word that tells you where the pattern points. This is our reading of the clue words, not a LinkedIn rule.

## Where the Crossclimb #939 letters move

Six moves, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word of the ladder appears here.

| Position | Times it changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 3 |

The moves run through positions 4, 1, 4, 3, 1, 4. The second letter never moves, so every word shares the same middle vowel. The fourth position is the busiest, which means most neighbors differ only in their last letter; a pair that differs anywhere else is one of the three moves at positions 1 and 3.

**Tango #779.** Check for a pair of equal symbols with a gap between them, since the gap must take the opposite one.

**Zip #618.** Trace from the highest number backward as well as from 1; corners with one open neighbor settle the path early.

## Wednesday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 25, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #939:** Words that come before "link". The five clue words in order are Chain, Cuff, Missing, Weakest and Click the.
- **Crossclimb #939:** Tale, Talk, Balk, Bald, Bard, Yard, Yarn. Talk, Balk, Bald, Bard and Yard fill the middle in that order; Tale and Yarn are the locked ends.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top row | Tale | none | none |
| Rung 1 | Talk | K for E | 4 |
| Rung 2 | Balk | B for T | 1 |
| Rung 3 | Bald | D for K | 4 |
| Rung 4 | Bard | R for L | 3 |
| Rung 5 | Yard | Y for B | 1 |
| Bottom row | Yarn | N for D | 4 |

Clues as printed by both sources: Talk, "Have a conversation"; Balk, "Stop or hesitate when doing something"; Bald, "Without hair on the head"; Bard, "Renaissance Faire entertainer"; Yard, "Imperial measurement that's just short of a meter". Tale and Yarn share "Two words for a story to be told". Sportsdunia lists the same ladder in reverse, starting at Yarn.

Why each Pinpoint clue fits: a chain link is one ring of a chain; a cuff link fastens a shirt cuff; a missing link is a gap in a sequence; the weakest link is the part that fails first; and Click the link is the instruction that opens a web address. The first two are objects, the next two are idioms, and the last is a command, so the category is the word that follows, not a shared topic.
