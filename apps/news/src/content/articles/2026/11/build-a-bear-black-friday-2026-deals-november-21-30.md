**Build-A-Bear's website lists 30% off select furry friends and free economy shipping on orders of $100 or more through 11:59 p.m. Central Standard Time (CST) on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026.** Other offers end Nov. 28, Nov. 30 and Dec. 8, and the last day to order economy shipping for Christmas Day is Dec. 14. Every end date and the order-by dates by shipping method follow, with a printable.

## Build-A-Bear sale end dates, Nov. 21 to Dec. 8

The site banner reads "Early Black Friday Starts Now!" These are the offers on buildabear.com as read on Nov. 21, 2026. The weekdays are our computation.

| Offer | Starts | Ends | Where | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30% off select furry friends | Not listed | Wed., Nov. 25, 11:59 p.m. CST | Online only | "Exclusions may apply"; cannot be combined with any other offer |
| Free economy shipping | Not listed | Wed., Nov. 25, 11:59 p.m. CST | Online | Qualifying orders of $100 or more, U.S. addresses only; excludes giant furry friends, gift cards, e-gift cards, bonus gift cards and charitable donations; not combinable |
| Clothing & Accessories Sale | Sat., Nov. 21 | Sat., Nov. 28, or while supplies last | Online only | No end time listed |
| Advent Calendar Sale | Not listed | Mon., Nov. 30 | Online and participating stores | While supplies last; not combinable |
| Pajama Shop Sale | Not listed | Tue., Dec. 8, 11:59 p.m. CST | Online only | Tile advertises 50% off matching pajamas; free pajama bag with a qualifying purchase of $40 or more including one Pajama Shop item |
| Beary Goods plush for $6 | Not listed | No end date given | Online and at a Build-A-Bear Workshop | With any purchase, both items in one transaction; participating U.S. locations; "Limited time only. While supplies last." |

Mark the dates on the [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars. The 30% off end time, 11:59 p.m. CST on Nov. 25, is 12:59 a.m. Eastern on Thursday, Nov. 26, Thanksgiving Day.

## How much is the Build-A-Bear advent calendar?

The 25-piece Advent Calendar is $29.00, reduced from $39.00, a $10.00 difference. Build-A-Bear says the Advent Calendar Sale is available online and in participating stores only through Nov. 30, 2026, while supplies last, and cannot be combined with other offers. It is the only offer listed that also runs in stores. For other calendars, see [Michaels' free advent calendars from Nov. 27](/news/michaels-black-friday-2026-free-advent-calendar-november-27/).

## What are the Build-A-Bear Christmas order-by dates for 2026?

Build-A-Bear's "2026 Holiday Shipping Deadlines" table lists the last day to order for delivery by Christmas Day. The site's banner also says "Order by Monday, Dec. 14 for Christmas Delivery!", which is the economy date.

| Shipping method | Last day to order for Christmas Day |
|---|---|
| Economy via FedEx SmartPost | Monday, Dec. 14, 2026 |
| Standard via FedEx Home Delivery | Tuesday, Dec. 15, 2026 |
| FedEx International Economy (Puerto Rico and U.S. territories) | Tuesday, Dec. 15, 2026 |
| Global-E (Canada) | Tuesday, Dec. 15, 2026 |
| FedEx 2-Day (Express) | Friday, Dec. 18, 2026, 11 a.m. CST |
| FedEx Overnight (Express) | Monday, Dec. 21, 2026, 12 p.m. CST |
| Buy Online, Pick Up in Stores | Wednesday, Dec. 23, 2026, for Christmas Eve pickup |

The same page is the source for the table, and the weekdays match Build-A-Bear's. The banner for Hanukkah delivery read "Order by Saturday, November 21 for Hanukkah Delivery!" For carrier dates beyond Build-A-Bear, see [FedEx and UPS Christmas shipping deadlines](/news/fedex-ups-holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026-christmas/).

## Is Build-A-Bear open on Thanksgiving and Black Friday 2026?

Build-A-Bear has not posted Thanksgiving Day or Black Friday store hours on the pages we read. Workshops are mostly in malls, which set their own hours; see [mall hours for Thanksgiving and Black Friday 2026](/news/are-malls-open-thanksgiving-2026-simon-mall-black-friday-hours/). The site banner also advertises free Buy Online, Pick Up in Store.

## What has Build-A-Bear not announced?

- A separate Black Friday (Nov. 27) or Cyber Monday (Nov. 30) sale; the site calls the current sales "Early Black Friday."
- Thanksgiving Day and Black Friday store hours.
- End times for the Clothing & Accessories and Advent Calendar sales.
- An end date for the $6 Beary Goods offer.
