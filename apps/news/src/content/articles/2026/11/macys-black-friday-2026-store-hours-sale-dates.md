**Macy's stores are closed on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and reopen at 6 a.m. on Black Friday, Nov. 27, staying open until 10 p.m., a Macy's spokesperson told TODAY.com on Nov. 25.** Yahoo Shopping's store-hours roundup, updated the same day, lists the same Macy's hours: 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Both say hours may vary among stores.

That is 16 hours on Friday. It also means no Macy's store is open on the day of the Thanksgiving parade; the [Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade guide](/news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-2026-start-time-route-tv-balloons/) has its start time, route and TV channels.

## Macy's hours, Nov. 26 and 27

| Day | Macy's hours | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving) | Closed, all locations | Macy's spokesperson to TODAY.com |
| Fri., Nov. 27 (Black Friday) | 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. | Macy's spokesperson to TODAY.com; Yahoo Shopping |

Macy's has not said when stores close on Thursday evening or whether any location differs. Because hours may vary among stores, confirm yours in the Macy's store directory before you drive.

## Opening time by time zone

Store hours are local, so the same 6 a.m. opening lands at different clock times across the country. If a store keeps the chain-wide 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. schedule, the opening and closing times look like this in Eastern Time (ET), by our conversion:

| Store's time zone | Opens (local) | Opens (ET) | Closes (local) | Closes (ET) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | 6 a.m. | 6 a.m. | 10 p.m. | 10 p.m. |
| Central (CT) | 6 a.m. | 7 a.m. | 10 p.m. | 11 p.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 6 a.m. | 8 a.m. | 10 p.m. | Midnight, Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Pacific (PT) | 6 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 10 p.m. | 1 a.m., Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Hawaii | 6 a.m. | 11 a.m. | 10 p.m. | 3 a.m., Sat., Nov. 28 |

Use the local column to plan your trip. The ET column is for readers who follow Black Friday news on an Eastern clock.

## How Macy's compares with other chains

Yahoo Shopping's roundup, updated Nov. 25, shows where 6 a.m. sits in the Black Friday lineup:

| Opens Black Friday | Stores |
|---|---|
| 5 a.m. | Kohl's, JCPenney |
| 6 a.m. | Macy's, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Dick's Sporting Goods |

Yahoo lists Macy's closing time as 10 p.m., the same as Best Buy and Dick's at many stores. Our [Black Friday store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/) covers Walmart, Target, Best Buy and the rest in more detail.

## What Macy's has not confirmed

- **Sale end date.** We could not read Macy's own Black Friday pages: macys.com returned an access-denied page to an automated request on Nov. 25, and we did not work around it. The newsrooms above gave store hours only. Check macys.com or the Macy's app for when the sale ends; our [sale end dates by store](/news/when-does-black-friday-end-2026-sale-end-dates-by-store/) page lists the chains that have published them.
- **Cyber Monday.** The calendar date is Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, but we have no Macy's start date or offer list from a source we could read.
- **Doorbusters and giveaways.** Neither source lists in-store doorbusters or free gifts, so none are included here.
- **Individual stores.** Both sources say hours may vary among stores.
