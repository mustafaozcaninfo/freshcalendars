**The U.S. Postal Service (USPS) said on Nov. 12, 2026 that "No changes are made to the price of stamps" in its filing for Jan. 17, 2027, so the Forever stamp stays at 82 cents.** The changes that day are to shipping. In Canada, Budget 2026 proposes raising the stamp from C$1.44 to C$2.00 in 2027, with no start date. Canadian dollars are written C$ below. This story puts both countries in one table and lists the USPS shipping changes.

## Stamp prices in the U.S. and Canada, now and in 2027

| Item | USPS now | USPS Jan. 17, 2027 | Canada Post now | Canada Post 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic letter stamp (USPS 1 oz, Canada Post up to 30 g) | $0.82 | No change, per USPS | C$1.44 single, C$1.24 per stamp in a booklet | C$2.00 proposed; date and booklet price not announced |
| Postcard stamp (USPS) | $0.65 | No change, per USPS | Standard-size mail covers letters and postcards at the same rate | Not announced |
| Letter to the other country | USPS Global Forever from $1.75 | Not among the services the release lists for international increases | C$1.75 to the U.S. (up to 30 g); C$3.65 international | Not announced |

Sources: USPS Notice 123 (effective Oct. 4, 2026) and USPS stamps page; USPS release of Nov. 12, 2026; Canada Post stamp prices page; Budget 2026, chapter 6.

USPS lists the Global Forever stamp "From $1.75" on its stamps page, and the Nov. 12 release names four international services for increases (Priority Mail Express International, Priority Mail International, First-Class Package International Service and International Priority Airmail). It does not name a Global Forever change. Canada Post's C$1.75 for the U.S. is in Canadian dollars.

## Is the price of stamps going up in 2027 in the U.S.?

Not on Jan. 17, 2027, according to USPS. Its release says: "No changes are made to the price of stamps: First-Class Mail remains one of the best mailing bargains in the world." The Postal Service filed notice that day with the Postal Regulatory Commission (PRC), which "will review the changes before they are scheduled to take effect."

The 82-cent price is the July 2026 one. The National Newspaper Association and CBS News both reported that the Forever stamp rose from 78 cents to 82 cents on July 12, 2026. USPS has not announced any stamp price after January 2027.

USPS Notice 123, effective Oct. 4, 2026, lists other current letter prices:

| Item (USPS Notice 123, effective Oct. 4, 2026) | Price |
|---|---|
| Stamped letter, up to 1 oz | $0.82 |
| Stamped letter, up to 2 oz | $1.11 |
| Metered letter, up to 1 oz | $0.78 |
| Postcard | $0.65 |
| Large envelope (flat), up to 1 oz | $1.69 |
| Certified Mail fee, added to postage | $5.55 |

Source: USPS Notice 123. The Nov. 12 release states no change for stamps only; it lists no 2027 price for the other rows.

The USPS Postal Explorer index shows two price changes with dates: a "Time-Limited 2026 Price Change" running "Starting October 4, 2026 through January 17, 2027" and the "January 2027 Price Change", "Effective January 17, 2027". Both appear in the printable below, with the July 12 stamp change.

## What changes at USPS on Jan. 17, 2027?

The release covers shipping, not stamps. The USPS Board of Governors approved the adjustments that week, per USPS, and the PRC reviews them first. The Competitive Products filing is Docket No. CP2027-1.

| Change on Jan. 17, 2027 | What USPS says |
|---|---|
| Domestic shipping | Prices rise 5.9 percent on average for Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express, USPS Ground Advantage and Parcel Select |
| Fuel surcharge | The 8 percent transportation-related price change from April 2026 becomes a permanent 8 percent fuel surcharge, plus 3 percent, for a total of 11 percent; not applied to international products now; reevaluated in July 2027 |
| Competitive special services | Prices change on some, including address enhancement services, premium forwarding services and several competitive ancillary services; competitive PO Box prices do not change in January |
| International | Prices rise 7.9 percent on average for Priority Mail Express International, Priority Mail International, First-Class Package International Service and International Priority Airmail; country rate groups and assignments also change |
| New and expanded services | A new optional Outbound Proof of Delivery for Priority Mail International; Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) expanded to International Priority Airmail |

Source: USPS release, Nov. 12, 2026. USPS says mailing services prices are based on the consumer price index, while shipping prices are "primarily adjusted according to market conditions."

## What does Canada's budget say about the C$2 stamp?

Budget 2026 says the measure would "Help bring Canada Post's stamp pricing more closely in line with Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) peers by raising stamp rates, beginning in 2027, to $2.00 for single stamps and 7% per year continuously." It estimates "only approximately $6 per year for consumers and $9 per year for small businesses."

The Canadian Press, in a story carried by CP24 on Nov. 5, 2026, wrote that "The federal budget did not say when the cost of a stamp would increase, just saying it will be in 2027." Global News reported the same $2 figure and the seven per cent yearly rise after it. Canada Post's own stamp page still lists C$1.44 for a single stamp and C$1.24 per stamp in a booklet.

By our calculation from the proposal, C$2.00 less C$1.44 is C$0.56, a rise of about 38.9%. It is a proposal in the budget, not an approved rate with a start date, and nothing in the sources gives the booklet price at the new rate. For readers who send cards across the border, see the [Canada Post holiday shipping deadlines for 2026](/news/canada-post-holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026-christmas/).

Canada Post's stamp page says that for a permanent stamp, "you can use it at any time now or in the future, even if the rates have increased." Its permanent stamps carry the letter P instead of a value and are accepted at the current domestic rate.

## What USPS and Canada Post have not announced on stamps

- Canada Post's start date for the C$2 stamp, and the booklet price at the new rate.
- New Canada Post rates for mail to the U.S. and other countries.
- Any USPS stamp price change after January 2027.
