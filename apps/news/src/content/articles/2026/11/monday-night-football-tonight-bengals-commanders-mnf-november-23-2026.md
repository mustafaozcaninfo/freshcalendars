**The Cincinnati Bengals play the Washington Commanders on Monday Night Football (MNF), Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on ESPN, according to the National Football League (NFL) schedule on NFL.com.** The Bengals' and Commanders' schedule pages list the same time and network. The game is at Northwest Stadium in Landover, Maryland, and it is the only Monday game of Week 11.

Below are the kickoff in every U.S. zone and in the United Kingdom, how the two teams arrive, the parking rule, the last meeting and the next Monday game. A one-page watch guide is at the end.

## What time is Bengals at Commanders in my time zone?

Clocks are on standard time, so the 8:15 p.m. ET kickoff is 8:15 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), as the Bengals' page prints it. We worked out the other zones from that listing.

## What channel is Bengals at Commanders on?

NFL.com, the Bengals and the Commanders list ESPN. The Commanders' schedule page gives radio as 100.3 FM. The pages we reached do not name a streaming service, TV announcers or a Bengals radio station, so check your local listings for those.

## How do the Bengals and Commanders arrive?

NFL.com's standings page lists the Bengals at 7-2 and the Commanders at 3-5-1 going into the game.

| Team | Record | Last game |
|---|---|---|
| Bengals | 7-2 | Sun., Nov. 15: beat the Pittsburgh Steelers 38-18 at Paycor Stadium |
| Commanders | 3-5-1 | Thu., Nov. 12: beat the New York Giants 24-21 at MetLife Stadium |

The Commanders' preview calls Cincinnati an American Football Conference (AFC) contender in its headline and says Washington's last home game against the Bengals was in 2020, a 20-9 Commanders win in Week 11. The Bengals' next game is Sunday, Nov. 29, against the New Orleans Saints at 1 p.m. ET on CBS, per the Bengals.

## Do you need a parking pass at Northwest Stadium?

Yes. The Commanders' ticket page for the game says "A pre-purchased parking pass is required in order to park in the Northwest Stadium parking lots." The stadium's address is 1600 Ring Road, Landover, MD 20785, per the stadium's event page, which lists the game as "Mon Nov 23 | 8:15PM".

## When did the Bengals and Commanders last play?

The most recent meeting was Monday Night Football on Sept. 23, 2024, when the Commanders won 38-33 at Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati, per the Bengals' opponent preview. Washington leads the series 6-5, according to the same preview.
