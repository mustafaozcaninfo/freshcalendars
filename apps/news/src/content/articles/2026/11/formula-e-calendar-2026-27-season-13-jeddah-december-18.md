**The 2026-27 Formula E calendar has 21 rounds at 13 events, opening with a double-header in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, on Fri., Dec. 18 and Sat., Dec. 19, 2026, and ending in Tokyo on Sat., July 24 and Sun., July 25, 2027, according to Formula E.** The series announced the Season 13 calendar on June 23, 2026, and called it the start of the GEN4 era. Race start times and broadcasters are not yet announced.

The table below adds what the official list leaves out: the weekday of each race day, which events are double-headers and which races are in the United States. ## Formula E 2026-27 race dates by city

Formula E called its list the first provisional calendar for the season, following validation from the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) World Motor Sport Council. Monaco's date comes from Formula E's Aug. 5, 2026 update.

| Rounds | Race days | City | Circuit | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Fri., Dec. 18 and Sat., Dec. 19, 2026 | Jeddah, Saudi Arabia | Jeddah Corniche Circuit | Double-header |
| 3 | Sat., Jan. 16, 2027 | Mexico City, Mexico | Not named on the list | Single race |
| 4 | Sat., Feb. 6, 2027 | Austin, United States | Circuit of The Americas (COTA) | Single race, U.S. |
| 5 | Sat., Feb. 20, 2027 | Miami, United States | Not named on the list | Single race, U.S. |
| 6 | Sat., Mar. 13, 2027 | São Paulo, Brazil | Anhembi Sambadrome Circuit | Single race |
| 7 | Sat., Apr. 17, 2027 | Sanya, China | Haitang Bay Circuit | Single race |
| 8-9 | Sat., May 1 and Sun., May 2, 2027 | Monaco | Circuit de Monaco | Double-header |
| 10-11 | Sat., May 8 and Sun., May 9, 2027 | Berlin, Germany | Tempelhof Airport Street Circuit | Double-header |
| 12-13 | Sat., May 29 and Sun., May 30, 2027 | London, United Kingdom | Brands Hatch | Double-header |
| 14-15 | Fri., June 18 and Sat., June 19, 2027 | Zandvoort, Netherlands | Zandvoort circuit | Double-header |
| 16-17 | Sat., June 26 and Sun., June 27, 2027 | Madrid, Spain | Circuito de Madrid Jarama | Double-header |
| 18-19 | Sat., July 10 and Sun., July 11, 2027 | Shanghai, China | Shanghai International Circuit | Double-header |
| 20-21 | Sat., July 24 and Sun., July 25, 2027 | Tokyo, Japan | Tokyo Street Circuit | Double-header |

Weekdays are computed from the dates on Formula E's calendar. The eight double-headers hold 16 races and the five single races add the other five. Jeddah and Zandvoort are the two events that fall on a Friday and Saturday; the other double-headers are on a Saturday and Sunday.

## When is Formula E testing before Jeddah?

Formula E said on June 2, 2026 that Circuito de Madrid Jarama will host pre-season testing from Nov. 16 to 20, 2026. That is Monday to Friday. From the last test day to the Jeddah opener is 28 days, and from the first test day it is 32.

## What changed on the 2026-27 calendar?

Formula E moved Monaco from the originally scheduled May 15 and 16 to May 1 and 2, 2027, to remove a scheduling clash with the World Endurance Championship (WEC). The same Aug. 5 announcement extended the Monaco deal until 2031. Formula E calls Austin the brand new Austin E-Prix at COTA and Zandvoort a new double-header. The June 23 announcement said Tokyo will host the season finale for the first time.

## How far apart are the Formula E events?

These gaps are computed from the first race day of each event, not published by Formula E.

| From | To | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Jeddah, Dec. 18 | Mexico City, Jan. 16 | 29 |
| São Paulo, Mar. 13 | Sanya, Apr. 17 | 35 |
| Monaco, May 1 | Berlin, May 8 | 7 |
| Zandvoort, June 18 | Madrid, June 26 | 8 |
| Shanghai, July 10 | Tokyo, July 24 | 14 |

The 35 days between São Paulo and Sanya are the longest gap between events. Monaco and Berlin fall on consecutive weekends, as do Zandvoort and Madrid. Of the 21 races, 2 are in the United States: Austin on Feb. 6 and Miami on Feb. 20, 2027.

## What is not yet announced for Formula E Season 13?

Race start times are not yet announced for any round, including Jeddah. Formula E's June 23 announcement links to a "ways to watch" page, and no U.S. or U.K. broadcaster is named in the sources used here. This story will be updated when Formula E publishes them.
