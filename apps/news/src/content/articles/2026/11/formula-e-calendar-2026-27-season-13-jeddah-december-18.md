**The 2026-27 Formula E calendar has 21 rounds at 13 events, opening in Mexico City on Sat., Jan. 16, 2027, and ending in Tokyo on Sat., July 24 and Sun., July 25, 2027, according to Formula E's calendar page.** Formula E announced the Season 13 calendar on June 23, 2026, and called it the start of the GEN4 era. Jeddah, listed for Dec. 18-19, 2026 in that announcement, now shows Apr. 16-17, 2027. Race start times and a U.K. broadcaster are not yet announced.

The table below adds what the official list leaves out: the weekday of each race day, which events are double-headers and which races are in the United States. Formula E called its June list the first provisional calendar for the season, following validation from the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) World Motor Sport Council. The dates and round numbers below are from the Formula E calendar page as read on Nov. 27, 2026, which has no date of its own. Formula E's Aug. 5, 2026 news page still says Jeddah "18 & 19 December 2026", so the calendar page is the source here. Monaco's date comes from Formula E's Aug. 5 update.

Weekdays are computed from the dates on Formula E's calendar. The eight double-headers hold 16 races and the five single races add the other five. Jeddah and Zandvoort are the two events that fall on a Friday and Saturday; the other double-headers are on a Saturday and Sunday.

## When was Formula E testing before the Mexico City opener?

Formula E said on June 2, 2026 that Circuito de Madrid Jarama would host pre-season testing from Nov. 16 to 20, 2026. That is Monday to Friday, and it is now past. From the last test day to the Mexico City opener is 57 days, and from the first test day it is 61.

## What changed on the 2026-27 calendar?

Formula E's calendar page now has no round in December 2026. Jeddah moved from Fri., Dec. 18 and Sat., Dec. 19, 2026 to Fri., Apr. 16 and Sat., Apr. 17, 2027, and Sanya moved from Sat., Apr. 17 to Sat., Apr. 3, 2027. Rounds are renumbered, so Mexico City is round 1 and Jeddah is rounds 6 and 7. Formula E also moved Monaco from the originally scheduled May 15 and 16 to May 1 and 2, 2027, to remove a scheduling clash with the World Endurance Championship (WEC). The same Aug. 5 announcement extended the Monaco deal until 2031. Formula E calls Austin the brand new Austin E-Prix at COTA and Zandvoort a new double-header. The June 23 announcement said Tokyo will host the season finale for the first time.

## How far apart are the Formula E events?

These gaps are computed from the first race day of each event, not published by Formula E.

| From | To | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Mexico City, Jan. 16 | Austin, Feb. 6 | 21 |
| Sanya, Apr. 3 | Jeddah, Apr. 16 | 13 |
| Monaco, May 1 | Berlin, May 8 | 7 |
| Zandvoort, June 18 | Madrid, June 26 | 8 |
| Shanghai, July 10 | Tokyo, July 24 | 14 |

Four gaps of 21 days are the longest between events: Mexico City to Austin, Miami to São Paulo, São Paulo to Sanya and Berlin to London. Monaco and Berlin fall on consecutive weekends, as do Zandvoort and Madrid. Of the 21 races, 2 are in the United States: Austin on Feb. 6 and Miami on Feb. 20, 2027.

## Where can you watch Formula E Season 13?

Formula E's Ways to Watch page says: "In the United States, Formula E will be available on Disney+ and ESPN+." Formula E's Aug. 11, 2026 announcement said Disney+ becomes the global streaming home of Formula E, including the U.S., where it streams alongside ESPN+. A U.K. broadcaster is not yet announced; the page says partners "will be updated when confirmed".

## What is not yet announced for Formula E Season 13?

Race start times are not yet announced for any round. A U.K. broadcaster is not yet named. This story will be updated when Formula E publishes them.
