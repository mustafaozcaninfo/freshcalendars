**Quordle #1769 is the Merriam-Webster puzzle for Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, the weekend slot after Thanksgiving.** Every word gets three graded hints, then three openers are scored tile by tile, and the solutions close the page.

Friday's words are on the [Quordle #1768 page](/news/quordle-hints-answers-november-27-2026-1768/). Today's four words reuse letters so often that a first guess can color tiles on several boards at once; the rules are on the [Quordle guide](/news/topic/quordle/#how-it-works).

## Hints for the four Quordle words on Nov. 28

The hints follow no board order.

**Word 1**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 5th letter of the alphabet and ends with the 12th.
- Hint 2: Three vowels, and one of them is used twice.
- Hint 3: The three-legged stand that holds a painter's canvas upright.

**Word 2**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 2nd letter and ends with the 4th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels sit side by side, in the 3rd and 4th places.
- Hint 3: Sliced for sandwiches, toasted at breakfast, made from flour and yeast.

**Word 3**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 2nd letter and ends with the 25th.
- Hint 2: The 3rd and 4th places hold the same consonant.
- Hint 3: The soft front of the body below the ribs.

**Word 4**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 7th letter and ends with the 4th.
- Hint 2: One vowel, in the 3rd place, and no letter used twice.
- Hint 3: An organ such as the thyroid, which releases hormones or sweat.

## Which of three openers colors the most tiles

Each cell counts green (G) and yellow (Y) tiles, with repeated letters scored the way the game does it. Columns follow the word numbers above, since no board order is confirmed.

| Opener | Word 1 | Word 2 | Word 3 | Word 4 | Colored tiles in all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stare | 0G 3Y | 0G 3Y | 0G 1Y | 1G 0Y | 8 |
| Dealt | 0G 3Y | 0G 3Y | 2G 0Y | 1G 2Y | 11 |
| Bagel | 3G 0Y | 1G 2Y | 1G 2Y | 0G 3Y | 12 |

Bagel comes out on top, with 12 colored tiles and three greens on Word 1 alone. Stare scores the fewest tiles and only one green. Dealt lands between them, but it gives Word 3 two greens and nothing else. No opener of the three leaves a word without color, so the choice is about how many greens you want on the first turn.

## The letter tally behind today's four words

Ten different letters fill the 20 slots. Easel has two Es and Belly has two Ls, while Bread and Gland repeat nothing. E and L each take four slots, A takes three, B and D take two each, and S, R, Y, G and N take one each.

| Letter | Slots used | Words |
|---|---|---|
| E | 4 | Easel twice, Bread, Belly |
| L | 4 | Easel, Belly twice, Gland |
| A | 3 | Easel, Bread, Gland |
| B | 2 | Bread, Belly |
| D | 2 | Bread, Gland |
| S, R, Y, G, N | 1 each | one word each |

Two words start with B, and two end in D. Y shows up only in Belly. Each of E, A and L skips exactly one word, which is why Bagel scored on all four.
