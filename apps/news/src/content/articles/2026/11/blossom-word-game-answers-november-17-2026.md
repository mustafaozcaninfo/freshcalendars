**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, puts A in the center, with C, I, L, S, T and Y on the petals.** Beebom and Connections Solver print the same seven letters, and Word.Tips lists the same set. Hints come first, and the pangrams wait in a marked section at the bottom.

## Today's petals: A in the center

- **Center:** A (every word needs it)
- **Petals:** C, I, L, S, T, Y

Two of the seven letters are vowel letters (A and I), and Y can act as a third. That is thin. C, L, S and T are consonants. With A in the middle, long words ending in -ally or -ity are possible, and these letters can spell both. The [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works) explains how a center letter shapes the words, and yesterday's flower is on the [Nov. 16 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-16-2026/).

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- Hint 1: The three pangrams named here are the ones Beebom, Connections Solver and Word.Tips all list.
- Hint 2: Two of them have the same length, 13 letters, and the third has 12.
- Hint 3: Two end in the same six letters; the third ends in -ity.
- Hint 4: All three contain the same four-letter run, in the same order.
- Hint 5: Two begin with the same two letters; the third begins with C.

## What the three pangrams share

Solver pages disagree on the total number of pangrams for this board. Their word lists differ too. The three below are the ones every page we checked lists. Counts of each letter were computed from the words themselves.

| Pangram | Letters | A | C | I | L | S | T | Y |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| First 13-letter one | 13 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 |
| Second 13-letter one | 13 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 12-letter one | 12 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

S and I appear twice in every row, so no pangram is built on a single S or I, and a solver who stops after placing one S has more of the word left to find than it seems. T is different. It drops by one from row to row (3, 2, 1), and L is the only letter that reaches three.

### Pangrams

Beebom, Connections Solver and Word.Tips all list these three. Each uses A, C, I, L, S, T and Y at least once.

- **Statistically** (13 letters)
- **Stylistically** (13 letters)
- **Classicality** (12 letters)

Statistically and Stylistically both end in -ically, and Classicality shares the ical run before ending in -ity. Statistically starts with ST, as does Stylistically, while Classicality starts with CL. Y appears once in Statistically and Classicality and twice in Stylistically, and C appears twice only in Classicality.
