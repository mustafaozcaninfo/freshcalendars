**The Tennessee Secondary School Athletic Association (TSSAA) lists all nine BlueCross Bowl state football championships at W. Max Finley Stadium in Chattanooga on Thursday through Saturday, Dec. 3-5, 2026, with kickoffs at 11 a.m., 3 p.m. and 7 p.m. ET each day.** The three Division II matchups are set, and two of them are rematches of the 2025 finals. The six Division I finals are listed as to be determined (TBD).

## BlueCross Bowl 2026 kickoff times in ET and CT

TSSAA's BlueCross Bowl page is headed "December 3-5, 2026 · All Times Eastern", and its football playoff page says all BlueCross Bowl games are played at Finley Stadium. Much of Tennessee, including Nashville and Memphis, is on Central Time (CT), so the CT column below is our subtraction of one hour.

| Day | ET | CT | Championship | Matchup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. | Division II AA | Lausanne vs. Battle Ground Academy (BGA) |
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 3 p.m. | 2 p.m. | Division II A | Nashville Christian vs. University School of Jackson (USJ) |
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | Division II AAA | Baylor vs. Brentwood Academy |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. | Class 3A | TBD |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 3 p.m. | 2 p.m. | Class 1A | TBD |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | Class 5A | TBD |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 11 a.m. | 10 a.m. | Class 4A | TBD |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 3 p.m. | 2 p.m. | Class 2A | TBD |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | Class 6A | TBD |

Yahoo Sports reported on Nov. 21, 2026 that the Division II games are on Dec. 3 at Finley Stadium, which matches TSSAA's page. The Division I finalists are decided in the semifinals; TSSAA's page lists those finals as TBD.

## Are the Division II finals rematches of 2025?

Two of the three are. Nashville Christian vs. University School of Jackson and Baylor vs. Brentwood Academy met in the 2025 finals, according to a Yahoo Sports article from Dec. 6, 2025. Last year's scores:

| Division II class | 2025 result |
|---|---|
| II AA | Battle Ground Academy 61, Franklin Road Academy 27 |
| II A | Nashville Christian 59, University School of Jackson 7 |
| II AAA | Baylor 28, Brentwood Academy 24 |

Battle Ground Academy, Nashville Christian and Baylor won last year's games. In II AA, Battle Ground Academy meets Lausanne in 2026, not Franklin Road Academy, its 2025 opponent.

## Tickets, parking and gates at Finley Stadium

| Item | Rule on TSSAA's page |
|---|---|
| Day-of cash sales | $20 per day |
| Who needs a ticket | All spectators school-age and older |
| TSSAA ID cards | Not accepted for admission |
| GoFan School Administrator's Pass | Honored at any gate |
| Gates | Open one hour before the first game |
| Parking | $15 per day, cash only; first come, first served |

TSSAA says Finley Stadium opened in 1997 and seats 20,421. The City of Chattanooga hosts the event. Advance online ticket sales are not announced on the page.

## How to watch the BlueCross Bowl

TSSAA says live and on-demand broadcasts of its championship events are on the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network. No TV channel is named. In 2025, Yahoo Sports also reported all nine title games on the NFHS Network, with the same split of three games a day.
