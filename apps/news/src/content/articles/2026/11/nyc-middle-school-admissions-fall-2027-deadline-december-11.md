**New York City (NYC) middle school applications for fall 2027 close Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, and offers are scheduled for Wednesday, April 7, 2027, according to the NYC Public Schools Middle School page.** Dec. 11 is 27 days after Nov. 14, and April 7 comes 117 days after the deadline. The application opened Wednesday, Oct. 14.

Morning Bell, the NYC Public Schools news page, says families have "until 11:59 PM on Friday, December 11, 2026, to update and submit again," and that "only your last and final application will be used." The one-page printable, "NYC Middle School Admissions Dates 2026-27," is below the story.

## NYC middle school admissions timeline for families

| Date | Day | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct. 14, 2026 | Wednesday | Application opens | Middle School page |
| Nov. 2, 2026 | Monday | Deadline to register to test for Mark Twain I.S. 239 (21K239) and District 21 Talent Tests (passed) | Middle School page |
| December 2026 | Not announced | Mark Twain talent test, in person; no day given | Morning Bell |
| Dec. 11, 2026 | Friday | Application closes, 11:59 PM | Middle School page, Morning Bell |
| April 7, 2027 | Wednesday | Offer release | Middle School page |
| After offers | Not announced | Waitlists; the opening date is not listed | Middle School page |

Weekdays and day counts are computed from the calendar. The Middle School page shows no date of its own, so the table reflects what it listed on Nov. 14.

## Who can apply to NYC middle school?

NYC lists two requirements: the student is a NYC resident, and is "a current fifth grade student, or a current sixth grade student if their elementary school ends in sixth grade." Three programs take other grades. District 3 fourth graders may apply to the Center School (03M243), Queens fourth graders to Louis Armstrong Middle School (30Q227), and all current sixth graders to the Baccalaureate School for Global Education (30Q580).

## How do I submit the NYC middle school application?

There are three routes, all listed by NYC Public Schools:

- Online in MySchools.
- Through your current school counselor, for public Department of Education (DOE) and charter school students.
- Through a Family Welcome Center (FWC), for private or parochial school students.

The Middle School page says, "The most important factor in determining where your child goes to middle school is the choices you list on the application." It also says "there is no limit to the number of choices you list." Morning Bell repeats that students can list any number of program choices.

## How are NYC middle school choices ranked?

NYC explains that "a program's admissions priorities determine the order in which applicants are considered for offers to each program." The page names these rules:

- Zoned schools prioritize zoned students for seats.
- Most programs have a district priority.
- For screened programs, students are ranked "based on a composite of their course grades on a 1-4 scale from fourth grade."
- Some schools use Diversity in Admissions (DIA) priorities.
- Audition, language and talent-test programs have their own rules.

## How does the middle school deadline compare with high school?

The [NYC high school application](/news/nyc-high-school-admissions-fall-2027-deadline-december-4/) closes a week earlier. The high school figures come from the NYC Public Schools High School page as read on Nov. 7.

| | High school | Middle school |
|---|---|---|
| Application opened | Tuesday, Oct. 6, 2026 | Wednesday, Oct. 14, 2026 |
| Application closes | Friday, Dec. 4, 2026 | Friday, Dec. 11, 2026 |
| Closing time | Not listed | 11:59 PM |
| Offers | Wednesday, March 3, 2027, after 3 p.m. | Wednesday, April 7, 2027 |
| Number of choices | Recommends at least 12; any number allowed | No limit |

The deadline is 7 days later for middle school, and offers come 35 days after high school offers.

## What happens after offers on April 7?

The offer letter "also includes a list of programs where they've been waitlisted." After initial offers are made, and while waitlists are open, families can add their child to more waitlists. NYC says "schools will be in touch directly if a seat opens up." The waitlist opening date, the deadline to accept an offer and appeal dates are not yet announced.

## Where can NYC families get help?

The NYC Public Schools help line is 718-935-2009, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Family Welcome Centers help with "exploring schools, applying, and checking waitlist status in MySchools." Their hours are Monday to Thursday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Friday, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.; the phone number is 718-935-2828.
