**Polar Express train rides start Friday, Nov. 20, 2026 at Durango & Silverton in Colorado, Mt. Hood Railroad in Oregon and Maine Narrow Gauge Railroad in Portland, and Western Maryland Scenic Railroad follows on Saturday, Nov. 21**, according to each railroad's own website, checked Nov. 18. [Rail Events Inc.](https://www.raileventsinc.com/polar-express-train-ride/locations/), which licenses The Polar Express Train Ride, lists 40 locations: 38 in the U.S. and 2 in Alberta, Canada. A printable sheet carries the confirmed dates and the locations by state.

## Which Polar Express trains run first in 2026?

Three railroads post a Nov. 20 start. Durango & Silverton lists "November 20 – January 2" on its Polar Express page; the line prints no year, so the dates are the season the page sells now. Mt. Hood Railroad says "The available dates are Nov 20 through Jan 2 for the 2026 Polar Express Train Ride season" and that it runs on select days. Maine Narrow Gauge Railroad lists "Fri, Nov 20 - mon, Dec 28" under November 2026.

| Start | Railroad | Season dates | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 20 | [Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad](https://www.durangotrain.com/polar-express/) | Nov. 20 to Jan. 2, per its Polar Express page | Durango, CO |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | [Mt. Hood Railroad](https://mthoodrr.com/train-rides/polar-express-christmas-train/) | Nov. 20 to Jan. 2, select days | 110 Railroad Avenue, Hood River, OR |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | [Maine Narrow Gauge Railroad](https://www.mainenarrowgauge.org/) | Fri., Nov. 20 to Mon., Dec. 28 | 65 Thames Street, Portland, ME |
| Sat., Nov. 21 | [Western Maryland Scenic Railroad](https://www.wmsr.com/) | Nov. 21 to Christmas Eve | Cumberland, MD |

Western Maryland's banner reads "November 21st - Christmas Eve", and its phone number is (301) 759-4400. Mt. Hood asks riders to arrive 45 to 60 minutes early. Maine's departures are at 1:30, 2:45, 4, 5:15, 6:30 and 7:45 p.m., and the times vary by day.

## What does a Polar Express train ticket cost in 2026?

Only three of the railroads we read showed prices. Fares vary by seat class and, at Mt. Hood, by day.

| Railroad | Adult fares listed | Child fares listed |
|---|---|---|
| Mt. Hood Railroad (adult 13+, child 2-12) | Standard $59-$79, Parlor $69-$89, Premium $79-$99, Very Important Person (VIP) $89-$109 | Standard $49-$69, Parlor $59-$79, Premium $69-$89, VIP $79-$99 |
| Texas State Railroad (child 2-12) | Presidential Car 1511 $95, Coach $50, Value Coach $40 | Presidential Car 1511 $75, Coach $30, Value Coach $20 |
| Maine Narrow Gauge Railroad | $37 - $111 across all tickets | $37 - $111 across all tickets |

Texas State's train leaves the Palestine depot at 789 Park Road 70, and its page shows dates only in calendar images. Maine's page showed Nov. 28 at 2:45 and 6:30 p.m., Dec. 12 at 5:15 p.m. and Dec. 18 at 5:15 p.m. as sold out on Nov. 18; Mt. Hood's page said tickets were available now. Durango & Silverton says "Certain dates and car types will sell quickly" and lists no prices. Grand Canyon Railway says its 2026 dates are available and Polar Express is "Now Booking", and Great Smoky Mountains Railroad says "Tickets are selling quickly"; neither page gave us dates or fares we could confirm. Grand Canyon's Polar Express questions page places the station "directly behind the Grand Canyon Railway Hotel".

## Santa trains that are not the Polar Express

Several railroads sell holiday rides without the Polar Express license, and they are not on the Rail Events list.

- [Grapevine Vintage Railroad](https://www.grapevinetexasusa.com/gvrr), 705 S. Main St., Grapevine, TX: Christmas Wine Train "November 20, 2026 - December 17, 2026" and Santa's North Pole Express "through December 30".
- [Wilmington & Western](https://www.wwrr.com/): "The 2026 Santa Claus Special and Holiday Lights Express trains are now scheduled and available for booking."
- [Strasburg Rail Road](https://www.strasburgrailroad.com/): closed for track replacement until Nov. 21, 2026; its holiday trains are Santa's Paradise Express, Night Before Christmas and Santa Claus Limited, with no dates posted.
- [Conway Scenic](https://www.conwayscenic.com/), North Conway, NH: Santa's Holiday Special "From $27"; a New Year's Eve Late Special on Dec. 31, 2026 boards at 8:00 p.m. and departs at 8:15 p.m., for adults over 21.
- [Essex Steam Train](https://www.essexsteamtrain.com/): "North Pole Express Tickets On Sale Now!", with no dates posted.

For free holiday trains, see the [Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) Holiday Train 2026 schedule](/news/cpkc-holiday-train-2026-schedule-stops-times/), and for other holiday events, [zoo lights in 2026](/news/zoo-lights-2026-dates-national-zoo-bronx-lincoln-park-la-denver-houston/) and [Christmas parades in 2026](/news/christmas-parades-2026-us-cities-dates-times-routes/).
