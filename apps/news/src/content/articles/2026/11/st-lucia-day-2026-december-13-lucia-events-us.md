**St. Lucia Day 2026 is Sunday, Dec. 13, and four U.S. organizers have posted Lucia events between Dec. 5 and Dec. 13.** Visit Sweden, the Swedish tourism site, says the feast day "commemorates the martyr Lucia of Syracuse." The American Swedish Historical Museum in Philadelphia, the Swedish American Museum in Chicago, Visit Lindsborg in Kansas and Svenska kyrkan i New York list the dates below, which no single organizer page puts together.

## What is St. Lucia Day?

Dec. 13, 2026 is a Sunday; we computed the weekday. The Nordic Museum in Stockholm (Nordiska museet) says Lucia "is associated with a white-clad figure wearing a crown of candles," and that her followers "carry candles and sing Lucia and Christmas songs." Visit Sweden adds that the lit wreath on her head is the defining feature, that processions are "led by Lucia herself," and that battery candles are now common for safety.

Saffron buns (lussekatter) are the treat. Visit Sweden says they are "traditionally reserved for the weeks leading up to Christmas," and the Nordic Museum notes that the S-shape is now the most common, while saffron began to be used at the end of the 19th century.

## Which weekend has a Lucia event near you?

- **Dec. 5-6 (Saturday and Sunday):** Philadelphia. The American Swedish Historical Museum says that at 10:30 am and 2:00 pm "the lights will dim and the doors will close for the duration of a performance." Chicago's Svenska Skolan (St. Lucia) is at noon on Sunday, Dec. 6.
- **Dec. 12 (Saturday):** Lindsborg, Kansas. Visit Lindsborg calls it "a favorite and beautiful Swedish holiday tradition," held "in the historic Bethany Lutheran Church," and says Saint Lucia is celebrated there on the second Saturday of December. The time is not yet posted.
- **Dec. 13 (Sunday):** New York and Chicago. New York has two celebrations; Chicago has the crowning, a procession and a performance in the afternoon, then the 7 p.m. listing at Ebenezer Lutheran Church.

In Chicago, the 4:45 p.m. Central procession (5:45 p.m. ET) starts at the museum and goes to Catalpa Ave., according to the museum's schedule.

## Tickets and prices

The Philadelphia and Chicago pages list no admission price. Svenska kyrkan i New York says tickets will come with "More information to come." (page changed Nov. 9, 2026), and Visit Lindsborg has not posted a time or price. Check each organizer before you plan a trip. We have not confirmed 2026 dates for other U.S. venues, including the American Swedish Institute in Minneapolis, so none are listed.

## Is there a Lucia celebration outside the US?

Svenska kyrkan i Berlin lists Lucia concerts on Sunday, Dec. 13 at 18:00 and 20:00 in the Kaiser-Wilhelm-Gedächtnis-Kirche.
