## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (5 letters), *Hair problem due to high humidity*: what damp air does to smooth strands, as a noun; it starts with the letter that opens 1-Down and ends in a double letter.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Flood blocker*: an embankment built along a river; it starts with L, the second letter of 1-Down, and ends in a double E.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Nut for a squirrel*: the fruit of an oak; it starts with A, the third letter of 1-Down, and ends with N, the last letter of 5-Down.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *Purple flavor of bubble tea*: a starchy root that gives the drink its lilac color; it starts with T, the fourth letter of 1-Down, and ends with O.
- **9-Across** (3 letters), *"The ___ Who Loved Me" (Bond film)*: the word for a secret agent, which is the missing piece of the title; it starts with S, the last letter of 1-Down, and ends with Y.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Heelless shoes*: footwear with no lift at the back, in the plural; it starts with F and ends with S, the first letter of 9-Across.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Postgame wrap-up*: a short summary of what just happened; it starts with R, the second letter of 1-Across, and ends with P, the middle letter of 9-Across.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *What high-end billiard balls were once made from*: the tusk material of an elephant; it starts with I, the third letter of 1-Across, and ends with Y, the last letter of 9-Across.
- **4-Down** (4 letters), *It's nothing*: the number that stands for none; it starts with the fourth letter of 1-Across and ends with O, the last letter of 8-Across.
- **5-Down** (3 letters), *Chill and peaceful*: a calm, meditative state; it starts with the last letter of 1-Across and ends with N, the last letter of 7-Across.

## How today's clues are built

Sorting the 10 clues by what the solver has to do gives this count, from the clue text in the saved data:

| Clue type | Clues | Entries |
|---|---|---|
| Fill-in-the-blank | 1 | 9-Across |
| Plain definition or synonym | 6 | 6-Across, 7-Across, 8-Across, 1-Down, 2-Down, 5-Down |
| Descriptive or trivia | 2 | 1-Across, 3-Down |
| Indirect remark | 1 | 4-Down |

Most of the grid asks for a direct match, so wordplay is thin. The clue that stretches furthest is 4-Down, *It's nothing*. Read as a definition, the "it" is the answer, and the sentence is a joke on itself: the thing being described is the thing that means no quantity at all. No pun or abbreviation hides in the wording, which is why solvers hunt for a trick that is not there. The crossings settle it quickly, since its first letter is the fourth square of 1-Across and its last is the final square of 8-Across.
