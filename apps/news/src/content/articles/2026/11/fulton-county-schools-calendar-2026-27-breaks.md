**Fulton County Schools (FCS) in Georgia has spring break Monday, April 5 through Friday, April 9, 2027, and the last day of school is Thursday, May 27, 2027**, according to the district's 2026-27 School Year Calendar PDF. FCS is the suburban district around Atlanta, not Atlanta Public Schools, which publishes its own calendar. The board approved the 2026-27 instructional calendar on Dec. 19, 2024, per the Board Bulletin dated Dec. 20, 2024, and the calendar page says the board approves the calendar bi-annually.

## Fulton County Schools 2026-27 breaks at a glance

The PDF gives dates for each break. Weekdays and the count of weekdays students are off are our computation from those dates.

| Break | Dates on the calendar | Students off |
|---|---|---|
| Fall Break | Sept. 21-23, 2026, plus teacher workdays Sept. 24-25 | Mon.-Fri., Sept. 21-25 (5 weekdays), past |
| Thanksgiving | Nov. 23-27, 2026, schools closed | Mon.-Fri. (5 weekdays) |
| Winter Break | Dec. 21-31 and Jan. 1, schools closed, plus a teacher workday Jan. 4, 2027 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 4 (11 weekdays), back Tue., Jan. 5 |
| February break (the PDF says "Fall Break") | Feb. 15-17, 2027, plus teacher workdays Feb. 18-19 | Mon.-Fri., Feb. 15-19 (5 weekdays) |
| Spring Break | Apr. 5-9, 2027, schools closed | Mon.-Fri. (5 weekdays), back Mon., Apr. 12 |

Students get a full week off in September and again in February because the district adds teacher workdays after the break days. The PDF uses the name Fall Break for both, so we call the second one the February break.

## Which days are no school in Fulton County?

| Date | Day | What the calendar lists |
|---|---|---|
| July 27-31, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Teacher workdays (student holiday) |
| Aug. 3, 2026 | Monday | First day of school |
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day (schools closed) |
| Oct. 12, 2026 | Monday | Holiday (schools closed); the PDF does not name it |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Election Day, remote learning |
| Dec. 18, 2026 | Friday | Last day of first semester |
| Jan. 5, 2027 | Tuesday | First day of second semester |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) Holiday (schools closed) |
| Mar. 15, 2027 | Monday | Holiday (schools closed); the PDF does not name it |
| May 27, 2027 | Thursday | Last day of school |
| May 28, 2027 | Friday | Professional Development/Teacher Workday |
| May 31, 2027 | Monday | Memorial Day (offices closed) |

The PDF lists no early-release days and no make-up days. It says the year has 179 instructional days for students: 88 in the first semester and 91 in the second.

## What happens on Election Day in Fulton County Schools?

Nov. 3, 2026 appears as "Election Day (remote learning)". Students are not off; they learn remotely. Check with your school for how remote days work.

## Is the 2027-28 Fulton County calendar already approved?

Yes. The 2027-2028 Student Instructional Calendar is marked "Approved 12/18/2025", and the Board Bulletin for Dec. 19, 2025 says the board approved it. Our weekdays are checked against the dates.

| Item | 2026-27 | 2027-28 |
|---|---|---|
| First day | Mon., Aug. 3, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 2, 2027 |
| Labor Day | Mon., Sept. 7, 2026 | Mon., Sept. 6, 2027 |
| Fall Break | Sept. 21-23, 2026, then workdays | Mon., Sept. 27 to Fri., Oct. 1, 2027 |
| Thanksgiving | Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Nov. 22-26, 2027 |
| Last day of first semester | Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | Fri., Dec. 17, 2027 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027 | Mon., Dec. 20 to Fri., Dec. 31, 2027 |
| First day of second semester | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 4, 2028 |
| MLK Day | Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | Mon., Jan. 17, 2028 |
| February break | Feb. 15-19, 2027 | Feb. 21-25, 2028 |
| Spring break | Apr. 5-9, 2027 | Apr. 3-7, 2028 |
| Last day | Thu., May 27, 2027 | Thu., May 25, 2028 |
| Instructional days | 179 (88 + 91) | 180 (88 + 92) |

The 2027-28 PDF prints a key beside each month: "21-25 Winter Break" next to February 2028 and "3-7 Spring Break" next to April 2028, both Monday to Friday. It also lists Jan. 3, 2028 as "Students Off" and Oct. 11, 2027 as "Students Off".
