**The Michigan High School Athletic Association (MHSAA) lists its 2026 11-player football finals at Ford Field in Detroit on Friday and Saturday, Nov. 27 and 28, with four games each day starting at 9:30 a.m. ET and ending with a 7 p.m. ET game.** The matchups were set after the Nov. 21 semifinals, and all 10 finals are on the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network. The two 8-player finals were on Nov. 21 at the Superior Dome in Marquette. The table gives every 11-player game by division, time and matchup, with Central Time for the western Upper Peninsula, and the champions from 2025 follow it.

## What time are the MHSAA football finals at Ford Field?

MHSAA's football page lists the 11-player games by division. Ford Field's own events page shows "MHSAA Football Finals" on 11/27 and 11/28. The page gives the times in Eastern Time (ET). Menominee, Iron Mountain and the rest of the western Upper Peninsula are on Central Time (CT), one hour earlier, so the CT column is our subtraction of one hour.

| Day | Division | Game time (ET) | Game time (CT) | Matchup (record) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 27 | D8 | 9:30 a.m. | 8:30 a.m. | Beal City (13-0) vs. Hudson (12-1) |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | D4 | 12:30 p.m. | 11:30 a.m. | Dearborn Divine Child (12-1) vs. Grand Rapids Kenowa Hills (12-1) |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | D6 | 4 p.m. | 3 p.m. | Jackson Lumen Christi (11-2) vs. Almont (13-0) |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | D2 | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | Orchard Lake St. Mary's (10-3) vs. Muskegon Mona Shores (12-1) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | D7 | 9:30 a.m. | 8:30 a.m. | Ottawa Lake Whiteford (11-2) vs. Ithaca (12-1) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | D3 | 12:30 p.m. | 11:30 a.m. | Zeeland West (11-2) vs. Harper Woods (11-2) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | D5 | 4 p.m. | 3 p.m. | Frankenmuth (12-1) vs. Grand Rapids West Catholic (11-2) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | D1 | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | Detroit Catholic Central (12-1) vs. Romeo (13-0) |

Records are as MHSAA prints them, and the page gives no home or away team. The Detroit Lions' 2026 schedule has them hosting Chicago on Thursday, Nov. 26, at 1 p.m. (CBS), the day before the first Finals games, according to the team's site. For Thanksgiving week games, see our [Thanksgiving football schedule](/news/thanksgiving-football-schedule-2026-nfl-college-games/), and the [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) confirms the holiday is Nov. 26.

## When are the 8-player finals?

MHSAA's page names the Superior Dome in Marquette, on the Northern Michigan University campus, for the 8-player finals. Its schedule and sidebar list Saturday, Nov. 21: Division 1 at 11 a.m. ET and Division 2 at 2 p.m. ET (10 a.m. and 1 p.m. CT). One block on the same page says "Nov. 22", which is a Sunday, so the Nov. 21 date that the page states twice is the one used here.

## What is the road to the finals?

| Format | Round | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| 11-player | Districts | Oct. 30-Nov. 7 |
| 11-player | Regionals | Nov. 13-14 |
| 11-player | Semifinals | Nov. 21 |
| 11-player | Finals | Nov. 27-28 |
| 8-player | Regionals | Oct. 30-Nov. 7 |
| 8-player | Semifinals | Nov. 14 |
| 8-player | Finals | Nov. 21 |

The 11-player semifinals and the 8-player finals share Saturday, Nov. 21, per MHSAA's sidebar.

## How to watch the MHSAA football finals

MHSAA's Nov. 20, 2026 story says "all 10 MHSAA Football Finals will broadcast exclusively on the NFHS Network," and that "a subscription to the NFHS Network starts at $13.99 per month." The games are archived on MHSAA.tv shortly after they end, and live audio of the Finals is at MHSAANetwork.com.

## Tickets and parking for Ford Field

MHSAA has not announced 2026 prices, parking or gates, and it links Ticketmaster for [Friday](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/08006554C2754E56) and [Saturday](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/08006554C38D4F0C). In 2025, tickets were $20 for all four games in one day, at the door or online through Ticketmaster, parking was $8 and the gates were A, D and G, according to MHSAA's Nov. 24, 2025 release.
