**Dev Deepawali 2026 is Tuesday, Nov. 24 in Varanasi, according to Drik Panchang, and the Government of India's Incredible India tourism site also lists 24 November 2026.** Drik gives the Pradoshakal muhurat, the evening window it lists for the festival, as 5:08 p.m. to 7:47 p.m. Indian Standard Time (IST). In the United States that is 6:38 a.m. to 9:17 a.m. ET and 3:38 a.m. to 6:17 a.m. PT on the same Tuesday, and the table below converts every time to Central, Mountain and London clocks.

## Dev Deepawali 2026 times in IST, ET, CT, MT, PT and London

Drik Panchang, a panchang (almanac) publisher and not an official body, lists these Varanasi times for 2026: Purnima (full moon) tithi begins at 11:42 p.m. IST on Nov. 23 and ends at 8:23 p.m. IST on Nov. 24. We converted them with IST as Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus 5:30. U.S. clocks have been on standard time since Nov. 1, so every U.S. time is standard time, and Toronto matches ET.

Varanasi's evening falls in the early morning in North America. Temples and community groups outside India set their own programs and times, so check your local temple for an evening event; none is listed here.

## Which date is Dev Deepawali 2026?

Drik Panchang's 2026 page reads "Dev Deepavali on Tuesday, November 24, 2026." The Free Press Journal, in a story updated Nov. 12, 2026, wrote: "This year, the festival will be celebrated on Tuesday, November 24." The tourism site says the festival falls on Kartik Purnima, fifteen days after Diwali; see our story on [Diwali 2026](/news/diwali-2026-date-lakshmi-puja-time-usa/).

Some blogs give Wednesday, Nov. 25. Drik's calculation does not support that, because the Purnima tithi it lists ends at 8:23 p.m. IST on Nov. 24.

Two other observances share the day. Drik lists Guru Nanak Jayanti on Tuesday, Nov. 24, 2026, and our story on [Guru Nanak Jayanti 2026](/news/guru-nanak-jayanti-2026-date-november-24-gurpurab-purnima-time-us/) has its own Purnima times. Drik's New Delhi page also gives Tuesday, Nov. 24, 2026 for Karthigai Deepam.

## What is planned in Varanasi on Nov. 24?

The program below comes from news reports, not from an official schedule, and may change.

- **Diyas.** Oneindia Hindi (Nov. 12), Bharat Samachar TV (Nov. 13) and Organiser (Nov. 15) report a plan for more than 11 lakh (1.1 million) earthen lamps on the ghats. Organiser wrote that the Uttar Pradesh government "is planning more than 11 lakh earthen lamps."
- **Laser show and green fireworks.** Organiser wrote that "a laser show and green fireworks are on the programme." Bharat Samachar TV places the laser show at Chet Singh Ghat and the green fireworks in front of Kashi Vishwanath Dham.
- **Ganga Maha Aarti.** Oneindia Hindi and Bharat Samachar TV both report a Ganga Maha Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat on the evening of Nov. 24. No clock time was announced in those reports.
- **Boats and security.** Indian Masterminds reported on Nov. 19 that Varanasi Police are preparing a 7.5-km two-way boat lane from Namo Ghat to Assi Ghat, with life-jacket rules. The same outlet reported a deployment of more than 4,500 police personnel.
