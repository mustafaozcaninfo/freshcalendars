**Baltimore's Monument Lighting is Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026, from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. ET at Mount Vernon Place, with fireworks at 8 p.m.**, according to the Mount Vernon Place Conservancy. The Conservancy says the event is in its 55th year and calls it the unofficial opening of the holiday season in Baltimore. Mark it on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and see the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) for the weeks before.

The organizer's page lists a time for each stage act. The schedule below is taken from it, and a printable sheet carries the start, the stage window, the fireworks and last call.

## What is the Monument Lighting 2026 schedule?

The Conservancy lists the event as beginning at 5 p.m. All times are ET, in the evening.

| Time (Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026) | What happens |
|---|---|
| 5:00 p.m. | Event begins |
| 5:20 p.m. | Baltimore School for the Arts (BSA) Dance Department |
| 5:30 p.m. | BSA vocal department |
| 5:45 p.m. | Center Stage Charmz |
| 6:05 p.m. | The Peabody Brass Ensemble |
| 6:35 p.m. | Mariah Bonner and Clayton Bryant |
| 7:05 p.m. | Emmanuel Episcopal Church Choir |
| 7:30 p.m. | Morgan State University Choir |
| 8:00 p.m. | Fireworks |
| 8:15 p.m. | Last call at bars |

The page does not give a moment when the monument's lights come on. It lists the program from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. and says the fireworks will go off at 8 p.m. We did not find an admission price, 2026 road closures or a broadcast time, so check mountvernonplace.org before you go.

## Where is the Monument Lighting, and what is there to eat?

The Conservancy gives the address as 699 Washington Place. Lead sponsor Mercy Medical Center is named on the page. Nearly 20 local food vendors will provide food from sweet to savory, and the Conservancy's beverage concessions will pour local beer, mead, wine, and hot and spiked cider, the Conservancy says.

## How hard is parking at the Monument Lighting?

"Parking in Mount Vernon on the night of Monument Lighting will be difficult," the Conservancy says, and street parking "is available but will be scarce." It links a parking lot map and says several bus lines serve Mount Vernon.

## Is the 2026 Monument Lighting at the same time as last year?

The start and fireworks times match 2025, and the date moves from Dec. 4 to Dec. 3. The Baltimore Banner reported that the 54th annual lighting began at 5 p.m. on Dec. 4, 2025, and that a fireworks show would wrap up the event around 8 p.m.

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Edition | 54th | 55th |
| Date | Thu., Dec. 4 | Thu., Dec. 3 |
| Start | 5 p.m. | 5 p.m. |
| Fireworks | Around 8 p.m. (Banner) | 8 p.m. (Conservancy) |

Dec. 3, 2026, Dec. 4, 2025 and Dec. 5, 2024 are all Thursdays, and each is the first Thursday of December.

## Can weather cancel the fireworks?

In 2024, CBS News Baltimore reported that the fireworks and light show were canceled because of heavy wind. The Conservancy's 2026 page does not say what happens in bad weather.

## Is there a watch party near the monument?

Visit Baltimore's event listing shows a Hotel Revival watch party on Thursday, Dec. 3, from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. at 101 West Monument St. This is a third-party listing, not part of the Conservancy's program.

## Which Monument Lighting times to mark?

Mark 5 p.m. for the start, 5:20 p.m. for the first stage act and 8 p.m. for the fireworks, with last call at 8:15 p.m. The printable sheet holds those times on one page.
