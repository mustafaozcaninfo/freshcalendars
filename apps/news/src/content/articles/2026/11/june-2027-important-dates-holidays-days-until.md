**June 2027 begins on Tuesday, June 1 and has one federal holiday: Juneteenth, observed on Friday, June 18, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** Juneteenth itself falls on Saturday, June 19. Flag Day is Monday, June 14, Father's Day is Sunday, June 20 and the Belmont Stakes is Saturday, June 5. The countdowns below are counted from Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026.

## Juneteenth 2027: observed on Friday, June 18

OPM's federal holiday list shows "Friday, June 18" for Juneteenth National Independence Day, and its footnote says that if a holiday falls on a Saturday, the preceding Friday is the observed day for most federal employees. Juneteenth falls on Saturday, June 19, 2027. The long weekend therefore runs from Friday, June 18 to Sunday, June 20, which is also Father's Day. It is the only federal holiday in June. The [May 2027 important dates](/news/may-2027-important-dates-holidays-days-until/) story covers the month before.

GOV.UK lists no bank holiday in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland in June 2027; the Spring bank holiday is Monday, 31 May.

## Moon phase times in Eastern Time for June 2027

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes times in Universal Time (UT). Daylight time is in effect all month, so New York is four hours behind UT on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). The full moon is at 00:44 UT on June 19, which is the evening of Friday, June 18 in New York. Our [2027 Moon phase calendar](/news/moon-phases-2027-full-moon-calendar/) has the other months.

## June solstice 2027 time in each U.S. time zone

USNO lists the June solstice on June 21, 2027 at 14:11 UT. That is 10:11 a.m. EDT, 9:11 a.m. Central Daylight Time, 8:11 a.m. Mountain Daylight Time and 7:11 a.m. Pacific Daylight Time on Monday, June 21. The [2027 seasons story](/news/seasons-2027-equinox-solstice-dates-times/) has all four dates and times.

## Flag Day and Father's Day in June 2027

Federal law, 36 U.S.C. 110(a), says "June 14 is Flag Day," which is Monday, June 14, 2027. The Father's Day law, 36 U.S.C. 109(a), says "The third Sunday in June is Father's Day." In 2027 that is Sunday, June 20.

## Belmont Stakes, Formula 1 and Le Mans in June 2027

The New York Racing Association (NYRA) lists the Belmont Stakes on June 5, 2027, at Belmont Park, a Saturday. Formula 1's 2027 calendar shows the Monaco Grand Prix on "04 - 06 Jun" and the Portugal Grand Prix on "18 - 20 Jun". The 24 Hours of Le Mans site lists the 2027 edition "from 9 to 13 June", which is the event window; the race day and start time are not given there.

## SAT, Shavuot and dates not yet set

The College Board lists the SAT on June 5, 2027, with registration by May 21, 2027 and changes or late registration on May 25, 2027. No ACT date for June 2027 is announced yet. Hebcal says Shavuot "begins at sundown on Thursday, June 10, 2027, and ends at nightfall on Saturday, June 12, 2027." Eid al-Adha is expected around May 16, so it falls in May.

Several June events have no confirmed 2027 date here. NBC Sports reported June 17-20 for the U.S. Open golf at Pebble Beach in a 2026 article, but the U.S. Golf Association (USGA) page could not be checked. Wimbledon's 2027 start, the NBA Finals, the Stanley Cup Final and Trooping the Colour are not yet announced.
