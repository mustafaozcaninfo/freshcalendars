**Lewisville Independent School District (LISD) marks Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, Monday, Dec. 21 to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and Monday, March 15 to Friday, March 19, 2027 as holidays**, according to the 2026-27 academic calendar PDF linked from the district's "Calendars" page. Students return Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027.

The calendar prints the word "Holiday" for those weeks and does not name Thanksgiving or spring break, so those labels are our reading. This story adds the weekday for every day off, the four nine-week periods with their day counts, the new 2026-27 bell times and a comparison with Plano and Dallas. A printable school-year calendar is at the bottom of the page.

## Lewisville ISD days off in 2026-27

Dates before today, Nov. 5, 2026, are marked past. The words are the calendar's own; the weekdays are ours.

| Date | What the calendar lists |
|---|---|
| Wed., Aug. 12, 2026 (past) | First Day of School |
| Mon., Sept. 7, 2026 (past) | Holiday (Labor Day) |
| Fri., Oct. 9, 2026 (past) | Student Holiday, Staff Day |
| Mon., Oct. 12, 2026 (past) | Holiday |
| Tue., Nov. 3, 2026 (past) | Student Holiday, Staff Day |
| Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Holiday |
| Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | Student & Staff Early Release |
| Mon., Dec. 21 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | Holiday |
| Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Holiday |
| Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | Staff Day |
| Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | Students Return |
| Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | Holiday |
| Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | Student Holiday, Staff Day |

| Fri., March 26, 2027 | Holiday |

| Mon., May 31, 2027 | Holiday |

The calendar also lists staff days on Aug. 3-7, 10 and 11 before the first day. The [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the winter weeks.

## Is school open on March 29, 2027?

The calendar marks Monday, March 29, 2027 as a "Bad Weather Day". It does not call it a make-up day or say whether classes meet, so check with the district before you plan around it. Friday, March 26 is a holiday on the calendar, which means the [March 2027 printable calendar](/march-calendars.html) shows spring break week, a holiday Friday and then March 29 with its own label. Spring break week is also the end of the third nine weeks: that period runs through March 12, and the fourth starts March 22.

## How long are the Lewisville ISD nine-week periods?

The calendar prints the periods and day counts below. Adding them gives 174 days by our sum. It also prints a first semester of 84 days and a second of 90.

| Period | Dates | Days |
|---|---|---|
| First 9 Weeks | Aug. 12 - Oct. 8, 2026 | 41 |
| Second 9 Weeks | Oct. 13 - Dec. 18, 2026 | 43 |
| Third 9 Weeks | Jan. 5 - March 12, 2027 | 47 |
| Fourth 9 Weeks | March 22 - May 21, 2027 | 43 |
| Total (our sum) | | 174 |

The first semester's 84 days are the first two periods (41 plus 43), and the second semester's 90 are the last two (47 plus 43).

## What are the new Lewisville ISD school hours?

The district's staff page says "The Board voted to change school start and end times for the 2026-2027 school year in January 2026." The calendar's "New School Hours for 2026-2027" box gives the same times. By our arithmetic each level's day is 7 hours 25 minutes from start to end, and the three start times are 35 minutes apart.

| Level | Start | End |
|---|---|---|
| Elementary | 7:40 a.m. | 3:05 p.m. |
| Middle | 8:15 a.m. | 3:40 p.m. |
| High | 8:50 a.m. | 4:15 p.m. |

## When is the last day of school in Lewisville ISD?

The calendar does not label a last day of school. The fourth nine weeks ends Friday, May 21, 2027, and the calendar lists that day as a student early release. Monday, May 31 is a holiday. The calendar does not list early-release dismissal times that we could read reliably, and we found no graduation dates or 2027-28 calendar on the district's site as of Nov. 5, 2026.

## How does Lewisville ISD compare with Plano and Dallas?

Plano dates come from our [Plano ISD calendar story](/news/plano-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), which cites the Plano Independent School District's 2026-27 calendar PDF dated April 20, 2026. Dallas dates come from our [Dallas ISD calendar story](/news/dallas-isd-calendar-2026-27-holidays-breaks/) and the Dallas Independent School District's 2026-27 calendar PDF.

| | Lewisville ISD | Plano ISD | Dallas ISD |
|---|---|---|---|
| First day | Wed., Aug. 12, 2026 | Tue., Aug. 11, 2026 | Tue., Aug. 11, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving week | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 4 |
| Spring break | March 15-19 | March 15-19 | March 15-19 |
| Last day | Not labeled; fourth nine weeks ends May 21 | Fri., May 21, 2027 | Wed., May 26, 2027 |

All three have the same spring week, March 15-19. Lewisville and Plano both list Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 and Tuesday, Jan. 5 as the return; Plano also lists Jan. 4 as a student holiday, and Dallas lists winter break through Jan. 4. Lewisville starts a day after the other two. For other districts, see our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).
