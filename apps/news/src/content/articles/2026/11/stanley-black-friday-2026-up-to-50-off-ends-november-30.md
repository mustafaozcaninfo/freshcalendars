**Stanley's Black Friday sale, advertised as "Up to 50% off," ends Nov. 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Standard Time (PST), according to the homepage of stanley1913.com, which we read on Nov. 25.** That is 2:59 a.m. ET on Tuesday, Dec. 1. The homepage adds "Exclusions apply" and gives no start date. The table below converts the deadline for every US time zone and lists prices for 20 tumblers, bottles, mugs and jugs, with the percent off worked out from Stanley's own listed prices.

## When does the Stanley Black Friday sale end in each time zone?

Stanley writes "Ends 11/30 at 11:59 PM PST." Pacific Time is on standard time in late November, so PST is the right label. Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, and Nov. 30 is Cyber Monday, so the sale's last day is the Monday after Black Friday. The conversions are ours.

| Time zone | Sale ends |
|---|---|
| Pacific (PST) | Mon., Nov. 30, 11:59 p.m. |
| Alaska | Mon., Nov. 30, 10:59 p.m. |
| Hawaii | Mon., Nov. 30, 9:59 p.m. |
| Mountain | Tue., Dec. 1, 12:59 a.m. |
| Central | Tue., Dec. 1, 1:59 a.m. |
| Eastern (ET) | Tue., Dec. 1, 2:59 a.m. |

Stanley states the deadline in Pacific time. The [Cyber Monday 2026 guide](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/) covers start times at other stores, and the date itself is on [When is Cyber Monday?](/when-is/cyber-monday/).

## Stanley's club free shipping offer ends Nov. 26

Stanley also lists "Stanley 1913 Club members save with free shipping," auto-applied in checkout when logged into an account. The offer "Ends 11/26 at 11:59 PM PST," which is 2:59 a.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 27. The main sale ends Nov. 30. An earlier club offer, an extra 10% off sale items, ended Nov. 22.

## Shipping: free over $50, order by Dec. 15

Stanley's site lists free shipping "On all orders over $50." It also says "Order by 12/15 for guaranteed standard shipping" and "Order By 12/15 → Get by 12/24," so a standard-shipping order placed by Tuesday, Dec. 15 is promised for Thursday, Dec. 24. ## What has Stanley not said about the sale?

The homepage and the sale page do not give a start date or a year. They say "Exclusions apply" without naming the items. Stanley has not issued a press release that we found, so the deadline here is the one printed on its site.
