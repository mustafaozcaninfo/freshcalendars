**The New York Transit Museum's Holiday Nostalgia Train runs five Sundays in 2026, Nov. 29 and Dec. 6, 13, 20 and 27, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., on the F and Q lines of the Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA) subway, according to the [museum's Holiday Nostalgia Rides page](https://www.nytransitmuseum.org/holidaynostalgiarides).** Trains leave 2 Av-Houston St uptown at 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m. and 4 p.m., and leave 96 St-2 Av downtown at 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 3 p.m. and 5 p.m. Rides are free with your subway fare.

The museum's page, read Nov. 26, 2026, gives the rule "every Sunday between Thanksgiving and New Year's from 10am to 5pm" and does not list calendar dates. Thanksgiving is Thu., Nov. 26, so the Sundays that follow are Nov. 29 and Dec. 6, 13, 20 and 27. 6sqft, in a Nov. 10 story, lists the same five dates.

## Which Sundays does the Holiday Nostalgia Train run in 2026?

The same eight departures are listed for every ride day. Uptown trains start at 2 Av-Houston St on the F line; downtown trains start at 96 St-2 Av on the Q line. The museum gives no arrival times at the stops in between, and none are guessed here.

| Date | Uptown from 2 Av-Houston St (F) | Downtown from 96 St-2 Av (Q) |
|---|---|---|
| Sun., Nov. 29 | 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m., 4 p.m. | 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 3 p.m., 5 p.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 6 | 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m., 4 p.m. | 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 3 p.m., 5 p.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 13 | 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m., 4 p.m. | 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 3 p.m., 5 p.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 20 | 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m., 4 p.m. | 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 3 p.m., 5 p.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 27 | 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m., 4 p.m. | 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 3 p.m., 5 p.m. |

The museum's rule stops before New Year's, so Dec. 27 is the last ride listed. No source lists a ride on Sun., Jan. 3, 2027. Times are in Eastern Time (ET).

## What are the Holiday Nostalgia Train stops, and which platform do you board?

The route has 11 stops. Uptown trains call at them in the order below, and downtown trains run the list in reverse. The museum says to board the F platform for stops between 2 Av-Houston St and Lexington Av-63 St, and the Q platform for stops between Lexington Av-63 St and 96 St-2 Av.

Lexington Av-63 St is the one stop where the F and Q platforms meet.

## What cars does the Holiday Nostalgia Train use?

The museum says route and vintage equipment are subject to change for all rides. Newsroom reports describe 1930s R1-R9 series cars. 6sqft counts eight 1930s R1-9 cars with "rattan seats, paddle ceiling fans, roll signs, and period advertisements," and amNewYork says the cars date back to the days of the Great Depression. The car models come from those two newsrooms.

## What should you know before you ride?

- **Fare:** the museum says rides are free with your subway fare. The MTA lists the fare at $3 for most riders on subways and local, limited, rush and Select Bus Service buses.
- **Weather:** rides depend on weather, and timing, routes and equipment may change, the museum says.
- **Comfort:** while some vintage cars are air-conditioned, many are not, and restrooms are not available on the trains.
- **Accessibility:** some but not all of the rides use stations that are Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) accessible. Accommodation requests go to the museum by email at least two weeks ahead.

## How does 2026 compare with the 2025 Holiday Nostalgia Train?

In 2025, ABC7 New York reported that the rides ran "every Sunday in December from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.," with the same departure times and stops. The 2026 rule starts after Thanksgiving, which adds Sun., Nov. 29 to the usual December Sundays and makes five ride days.

## Is the Holiday Train Show at Grand Central open too?

The New York Transit Museum's exhibits page lists the Holiday Train Show as opening Friday, Nov. 13, at Grand Central. amNewYork reported the free show at the Grand Central Gallery and Store runs "through February of next year," open 10 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. on weekdays and 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekends. The exact end date has not been announced.
