**Harbor Freight's homepage on Nov. 24, 2026 lists early access for Inside Track Club members on Monday, Nov. 23, Tuesday, Nov. 24 and Wednesday, Nov. 25, and says "Everyone saves Thursday, 11/26 - Monday, 11/30."** The company is based in Calabasas, Calif. The public sale starts on Thanksgiving Day and runs through Cyber Monday. Store hours, items and prices are not posted yet.

## What are the Harbor Freight Black Friday 2026 sale dates?

The homepage banner reads "Congratulations, You've Unlocked Early Access!" and shows three member days: "Mon. 11/23", "Tue. 11/24" and "Wed. 11/25". Early access is for Inside Track Club members. The banner gives no times.

Thanksgiving is [Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026](/when-is/thanksgiving/), so the public sale covers five days, Nov. 26 to Nov. 30.

| Date | Day | Who can shop the sale | Confirmed by Harbor Freight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 23 | Monday | Inside Track Club members | Early access day |
| Nov. 24 | Tuesday | Inside Track Club members | Early access day |
| Nov. 25 | Wednesday | Inside Track Club members | Early access day |
| Nov. 26 | Thursday, Thanksgiving Day | Everyone | Sale starts |
| Nov. 27 | Friday, Black Friday | Everyone | Inside the Nov. 26-30 range |
| Nov. 28 | Saturday, Small Business Saturday | Everyone | Inside the Nov. 26-30 range |
| Nov. 29 | Sunday | Everyone | Inside the Nov. 26-30 range |
| Nov. 30 | Monday, Cyber Monday | Everyone | Sale ends |

The day labels for Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Small Business Saturday and Cyber Monday are calendar dates, not Harbor Freight's wording.

## How much is the Inside Track Club?

Harbor Freight's own join page was not readable by automated tools, so the price comes from news outlets. SlashGear wrote on July 8, 2026: "As of July 2026, an annual Inside Track Club membership costs $29.99." America Online (AOL), on Mar. 19, 2026, described the membership as costing "$29.99 per year." Confirm the price when you join.

## Is Harbor Freight open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Harbor Freight has not posted Thanksgiving or Black Friday store hours on any page we could read. The homepage lists Thursday, Nov. 26 as a sale day, which does not say whether stores are open. Each store's hours are on its page at harborfreight.com/storelocator.

## What has Harbor Freight not posted yet?

The official [Black Friday page](https://www.harborfreight.com/black-friday) said on Nov. 24 that Black Friday deals were still to come and told shoppers to "Check back" for the deals.

- Item deals and prices
- Purchase limits and any free shipping threshold
- Store hours for Thanksgiving Day and Black Friday
- Start times for the early access days

SlashGear reported on Nov. 17, 2026 a pre-Black Friday round of discounts running "through November 25." That comes from the outlet, not from the homepage banner.
