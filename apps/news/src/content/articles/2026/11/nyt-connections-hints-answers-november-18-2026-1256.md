If two entries seem to belong together but a third and fourth will not come, you may be looking at a decoy pair. Save your attempts for sets that survive a check against every other color.

## Why each red herring nearly fits

French is the loudest decoy. It names the country's people and language, so it pulls toward the Cities in France group, yet no French city goes by that name, and it finishes the ___ Fry phrase.

Nice does the opposite. It is a city, but it is also a plain adjective like Juicy and Sour, so a quick solver may park it with the lemon words and leave the cities one short.

Small and Deep are adjectives as well, and a lemon can be small or its color deep, which tempts you to add them to the descriptor group. Vocal is an adjective too, and it only works once you hear the phrase with Fry after it.

Honey is a flavor word as well, so it can drift toward Sour and Juicy, but it belongs with Buzz, Comb and Venom.
