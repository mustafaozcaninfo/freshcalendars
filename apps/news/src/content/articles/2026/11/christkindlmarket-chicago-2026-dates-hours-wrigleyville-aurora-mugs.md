All three Christkindlmarket Chicago markets open Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, the market's 30th anniversary, and are free to visit, according to NBC Chicago and Choose Chicago, the city's official tourism organization. The open-air German-style market returns to Daley Plaza downtown, to Gallagher Way in Wrigleyville and to RiverEdge Park in Aurora. The Christkindlmarket's own site was not reachable for us, so the dates here are confirmed by two independent sources that agree.

The three markets keep different calendars. Daley Plaza and the Aurora market both close on Christmas Eve, while the Wrigleyville market stays open through New Year's Eve. The sections below put the dates, the regular and holiday hours, and the holidays each market is open side by side, and the mug lineup is at the end.

## Christkindlmarket Chicago 2026 dates and locations

The market marks its 30th year in 2026, and Daley Plaza downtown is still a location, per NBC Chicago and Choose Chicago. Each market opens on Nov. 20; the closing dates and the vendor counts differ.

| Market | Address | 2026 dates | Days open | Vendors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daley Plaza | 50 W. Washington St., Chicago | Nov. 20 to Dec. 24 | Daily | Not stated |
| Wrigleyville at Gallagher Way | 3635 N. Clark St. | Nov. 20 to Dec. 31 | Daily | 28 |
| Aurora at RiverEdge Park | 360 N. Broadway | Nov. 20 to Dec. 24 | Thursdays to Sundays | 60-plus |

## Daley Plaza, Wrigleyville and Aurora hours for 2026

The Aurora hours below are confirmed by NBC Chicago and the Aurora Area Convention & Visitors Bureau. The Daley Plaza and Wrigleyville hours are as reported by NBC Chicago; the market's own site was not reachable for us to confirm.

| Market | Regular hours | Holiday and special hours |
|---|---|---|
| Daley Plaza | Sun. to Thu. 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.; Fri. and Sat. 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. | Thanksgiving (Nov. 26) and Christmas Eve (Dec. 24) 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. |
| Wrigleyville | Mon. to Thu. 3 p.m. to 9 p.m.; Fri. and Sat. 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.; Sun. 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. | Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Dec. 21-23 and Dec. 28-30 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.; closed Christmas Day (Dec. 25) |
| Aurora | Thu. 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Fri. and Sat. 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.; Sun. 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. | Wed., Nov. 25 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Thanksgiving (Nov. 26) and Christmas Eve (Dec. 24) 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Mon., Dec. 21 and Wed., Dec. 23 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. |

## Which Christkindlmarket is open on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Eve

Because the three markets end on different days, the holiday most readers ask about splits them. All three open on Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve, but only Wrigleyville runs past Dec. 24.

| Day | Daley Plaza | Wrigleyville | Aurora |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving (Thu., Nov. 26) | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. |
| Christmas Eve (Thu., Dec. 24) | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. (last day) | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. (last day) |
| Christmas Day (Fri., Dec. 25) | Season ended | Closed | Season ended |
| Dec. 26 to Dec. 30 | Season ended | Open (see hours) | Season ended |
| New Year's Eve (Thu., Dec. 31) | Season ended | 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. (last day) | Season ended |

## The 2026 Christkindlmarket mugs

Choose Chicago lists four collectible mugs for the 30th year. There is a flute-shaped mug with a pearl finish, a nod to pearl as a traditional 30th-anniversary symbol, and a green Christmas-tree-shaped mug meant for non-alcoholic drinks. A ceramic beer stein carries market scenes and skyline details, and new this year is a mini stein shot cup.

Mugs can be filled with glühwein, hot cocoa, hot cider or chai, per Choose Chicago. The glühwein is imported German hot spiced wine from Original Nürnberger Glühwein. Prices for the 2026 mugs have not been announced; in 2025, each mug was $20 at all locations, per NBC Chicago's 2025 mug reveal.

Choose Chicago also notes a preview party, "'Twas the Night Before Christkindlmarket," that gives early access to a limited group of ticket holders. Its 2026 date and price have not been announced, and neither has the opening-ceremony time.
