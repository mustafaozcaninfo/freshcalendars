**Dick's Sporting Goods says its 2026 holiday deals are valid Sunday, Nov. 22, through Saturday, Dec. 5, and that doors open on Black Friday, Nov. 27, at 6 a.m. local time, according to its Nov. 10 release.** The sale runs 14 days. All Dick's locations are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26, and the company has not said when stores close on Black Friday. The timeline, the deals, a 2025 comparison and a one-page printable follow.

## Dick's Black Friday 2026 timeline, Nov. 22 to Dec. 11

| Date | Day | What Dick's and Golf Galaxy published |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 22, 2026 | Sunday | Holiday deals begin |
| Nov. 26, 2026 | Thursday | Thanksgiving: all Dick's stores closed; dicks.com open |
| Nov. 27, 2026 | Friday | Black Friday: Dick's doors open at 6 a.m. local time; Golf Galaxy open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. with the giveaway |
| Nov. 30, 2026 | Monday | Cyber Monday: the Nov. 22 to Dec. 5 deals are still valid |
| Dec. 5, 2026 | Saturday | Holiday deals end |
| Dec. 6 to 11, 2026 | Sunday to Friday | Deal Days on dicks.com and the app |

The weekdays are our computation. Our [Black Friday date page](/when-is/black-friday/) confirms Nov. 27, and you can mark the sale on the [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars.

## Is Dick's open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. The Nov. 10 release says all Dick's locations "will be closed on Thanksgiving Day to allow teammates to spend time with their families before doors open on Black Friday at 6:00 a.m local time." The Oct. 15 hiring release says the same for all Dick's Sporting Goods, Inc. stores and distribution centers in the U.S. on Nov. 26. Shoppers can order at dicks.com and in the Dick's app, which the release says are open 24/7.

The release adds that extended hours will be available "across most" Dick's, House of Sport, Public Lands, Going Going Gone! and Golf Galaxy stores through the season, and points shoppers to dicks.com or the app for local schedules. Dick's store locator blocked our automated check, so look up your own store's page for its Black Friday closing time and its December hours. Christmas Eve and Christmas Day hours are not announced.

## What are Golf Galaxy's Black Friday 2026 hours?

Golf Galaxy stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day and "will open on Black Friday at 7 a.m. and close at 8 p.m. local time," according to its Nov. 10 release. That is an hour after Dick's 6 a.m. opening. At store open on Nov. 27, every Golf Galaxy location holds its first-ever "Pick a Tag. Win Some Swag." giveaway, and the first 30 participants are guaranteed to win.

Golf Galaxy's listed deals are up to 60% off golf apparel, up to 50% off golf shoes, $500 off the Garmin Approach R50 launch monitor, $100 off Ghost Golf bags, and Buy More Save More with free personalization on select golf balls.

## What are Dick's Deal Days 2026?

Dick's Deal Days returns for a second holiday season from Sunday, Dec. 6, through Friday, Dec. 11, on dicks.com and the app. The release says it has exclusive offers for ScoreCard loyalty members and app users. It starts the day after the Dec. 5 holiday deals end.

## How do Dick's 2026 and 2025 Black Friday plans compare?

Dick's Nov. 13, 2025 release is the source for last year's column.

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Holiday deals | Wed., Nov. 26 to Mon., Dec. 1 (6 days) | Sun., Nov. 22 to Sat., Dec. 5 (14 days) |
| Thanksgiving | Stores closed | Stores closed |
| Black Friday opening | 6:00 a.m. | 6:00 a.m. |
| Black Friday closing | 10:00 p.m. | Not announced |
| Deal Days | Sun., Dec. 7 to Wed., Dec. 10 (4 days) | Sun., Dec. 6 to Fri., Dec. 11 (6 days) |

The 2026 sale is eight days longer and begins five days before Black Friday. The 2025 closing time is last year's, not a prediction for Nov. 27.
