**ESPN says its fantasy football playoffs begin in Week 15 and finish in Week 18, after a 14-week regular season, which on the 2026 National Football League (NFL) schedule means Thursday, Dec. 17, 2026 to Jan. 9-10, 2027.** The 14 weeks and the playoff weeks come from ESPN Fan Support's "Length of Fantasy Football Season" page, updated Oct. 5, 2026; the dates come from NFL.com. Yahoo's game-dates page sets its default trade deadline at Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, still ahead, and its default playoffs at Dec. 22 to Jan. 4. Sleeper posts no default, so its leagues follow the commissioner's settings. Game times are Eastern Time (ET), and the one-page printable below holds the weeks, dates and Christmas games.

## Which NFL dates are the fantasy football playoff weeks in 2026?

ESPN's page says "ESPN has set fourteen (14) weeks in the regular season" and "Playoffs will begin in Week 15 and finish in Week 18." The page does not say how many teams qualify or how the rounds split, so this story does not either. The table pairs ESPN's default weeks with NFL.com's game days.

| NFL week | First to last game day | Under ESPN's default | Lineup-lock note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 14 | Thu., Dec. 10 (Vikings at Patriots, 8:15 p.m. ET) to Mon., Dec. 14 (Steelers at Jaguars, 8:15 p.m. ET) | Last regular-season week | Thursday and Monday night games |
| Week 15 | Thu., Dec. 17 (49ers at Chargers, 8:15 p.m. ET) to Mon., Dec. 21 (Patriots at Chiefs, 8:15 p.m. ET); Sat., Dec. 19 has games at 5:00 p.m. and 8:20 p.m. ET | Playoffs begin | Thursday game starts the week |
| Week 16 | Thu., Dec. 24 (Texans at Eagles, 8:15 p.m. ET) to Mon., Dec. 28; NFL.com says two Week 16 games will be played Saturday at 4:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. ET on NFL Network (matchups not named) | Playoff week | Games on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day |
| Week 17 | Thu., Dec. 31 (Ravens at Bengals, 8:15 p.m. ET) to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 (Texans at Packers, 8:15 p.m. ET) | Playoff week | Starts on New Year's Eve, a Thursday |
| Week 18 | Jan. 9-10, 2027 (Saturday: 1:00 p.m. ET on Netflix, 4:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN/ABC) | Playoffs finish | NFL.com says game times will be determined and announced |

NFL.com lists Week 18 as "January 9-10" on its important dates page. Its Week 18 schedule page says three games will be played Saturday (1:00 p.m. ET on Netflix, 4:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN/ABC) and that game times will be determined and announced. Mark the weeks on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html).

## Which Week 16 games lock lineups early on Christmas?

NFL.com's Week 16 page lists Texans at Eagles on Thursday, Dec. 24, at 8:15 p.m. ET on Prime Video. Friday, Dec. 25 has three games: Packers at Bears at 1:00 p.m. ET and Bills at Broncos at 4:30 p.m. ET, both on Netflix, then Rams at Seahawks at 8:15 p.m. ET on FOX. The full Week 16 board is in our [NFL Week 16 schedule](/news/nfl-week-16-schedule-2026-printable-pick-em-sheet-game-times/), and the [Week 15 schedule](/news/nfl-week-15-schedule-2026-printable-pick-em-sheet-game-times/) covers the opening playoff week.

Yahoo's lineup help page says players lock at "The start of the player's real-life game." On a platform that works that way, a Packers, Bears, Bills, Broncos, Rams or Seahawks player locks on Friday, before the Sunday games. Check your own platform's lock rule before Dec. 24; this story has no lock rule for the others.

## When is the fantasy football trade deadline in 2026?

Yahoo's Important Game Dates page lists a default trade deadline of Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026 for its public leagues, which falls in Yahoo's Week 12 (Nov. 24 to 30). Its default playoffs start Tuesday, Dec. 22 and end Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, so Yahoo's default final is NFL Week 17, a week before ESPN's. ESPN's help page, updated Aug. 11, 2026, says "In all ESPN Public fantasy leagues, there is a universal Trade Deadline set by ESPN." It gives no 2026 date, so check your league settings for the day. Sleeper's page, dated Apr. 22, 2026, says "Commissioners can manage the trade deadline at any time in the league's General Settings." Yahoo's separate help pages list no default; the dates above come from its game-dates page.

The NFL's own trade deadline is a different date: Nov. 10, 2026, per NFL.com's important dates page. See our [NFL trade deadline 2026 story](/news/nfl-trade-deadline-2026-november-10-time-et/) for the time.

## Do any NFL teams have a bye during the fantasy playoffs?

No. NFL.com's bye-week list runs from Week 5 to Week 14, with no byes in Week 12. Week 14 byes are the Arizona Cardinals and Dallas Cowboys, and Week 13 byes are the Ravens, Colts, Raiders and Jets. Weeks 15 to 18 have none.

## How do you use the fantasy football playoff weeks sheet?

The landscape sheet lists the Week 14 opener and closer, the Christmas Eve and Christmas Day games in Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific time, and a line per playoff week for your opponent and your league's trade deadline. The sheet does not print Week 15, 17 or 18 kickoff times; they are in the table above. Write your own league's weeks in if your commissioner changed them.
