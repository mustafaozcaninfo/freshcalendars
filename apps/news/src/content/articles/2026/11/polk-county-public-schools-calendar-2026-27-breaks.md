**Polk County Public Schools (PCPS) in Florida has no school for students from Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, the Thanksgiving week the district calls "Fall Break."** Winter break begins Monday, Dec. 21, students return Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027, spring break is March 15-19, 2027, and the last day is Thursday, May 27, 2027, by the district's 2026-2027 instructional calendar. The next day off is Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026.

The district's "Printable School Calendars" page lists the 2026-2027 calendar as "Approved 10/22/2024 / Revised 2/24/2026." The PDF itself is still titled tentative and says "Dates are subject to change." Check both before booking travel.

## Polk County school breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break (Thanksgiving week) | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | 12 | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 |
| Spring break | Mon., March 15 to Fri., March 19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 22, 2027 |

The district lists Dec. 21 as the start of winter break and Jan. 1 as a New Year's Day holiday. Jan. 4 and 5 are a teacher workday and staff development, so the 12 weekdays run from Dec. 21 through Jan. 5. Return days are read from the calendar, which marks no closure on those dates. ## Which Polk County days end early?

The calendar lists 10 early dismissal days, and says "Students are dismissed from school 150 minutes (2.5 hours) early on each of these days."

| Date | Weekday |
|---|---|
| Sept. 4, 2026 | Friday |
| Oct. 2, 2026 | Friday |
| Oct. 30, 2026 | Friday |
| Nov. 20, 2026 | Friday |
| Dec. 18, 2026 | Friday |
| Jan. 15, 2027 | Friday |
| Feb. 12, 2027 | Friday |
| March 12, 2027 | Friday |
| April 23, 2027 | Friday |
| May 27, 2027 | Thursday |

Dec. 18 is also the end of the first semester, which the calendar counts as 86 days, and the last school day before winter break. Third quarter begins Jan. 6, 2027.

## Could storm make-up days cancel part of Polk County's breaks?

They could. The calendar reads: "Storm Make-Up Days: 11/23/26, 11/24/26, 1/5/27 and 5/27/27 (Last Student Day)." That puts two days of the fall break week and Jan. 5, during winter break, on the list of days that could become school days after a storm. The calendar adds: "Reminder: Non-Refundable Travel Tickets Should Not Be Purchased in the Event that Storm Make-Up Days Are Used as Student Attendance Days and/or Teacher Work Days."

The calendar does not say that any of these days has been used, and it does not say how many lost days would trigger each one. The printable marks Nov. 23, Nov. 24 and Jan. 5 as unconfirmed.

## When does the Polk County school year start and end?

Classes start Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026 and end Thursday, May 27, 2027 with an early dismissal.
