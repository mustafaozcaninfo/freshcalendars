**Thursday's Blossom from Merriam-Webster, Nov. 19, 2026, puts E in the middle of A, I, M, N, O and T.** Beebom, Word.Tips and NYTSolvers all print this flower and one long pangram. The hints below close in on that word without writing it.

## Today's petals: E in the center

- **Center:** E
- **Petals:** A, I, M, N, O, T

Three petals (A, I and O) are vowel letters and the center E makes four, so short words come easily and the consonants M, N and T do the shaping. Rules for the center letter are on the [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works). Yesterday's flower is on the [Nov. 18 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-18-2026/).

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- Hint 1: The solver pages share one pangram, and it is long.
- Hint 2: It has 11 letters, four more than the flower holds.
- Hint 3: Every extra slot is filled by a petal that already appeared.
- Hint 4: It begins with the petal A and ends with the center letter E.
- Hint 5: It is a chemistry term for a kind of salt, and the -ate ending is the usual sign of one.

## Why four doubled letters make the word easy to lose

Seven letters need seven slots. This pangram has eleven, so four letters are used a second time. Hunting for a word with each letter once will never reach it, and that is the trap on a day like this. Count the slots first: if you have placed a seven-letter skeleton, expect to fill four more by repeating A, I, N and T.

| Letter | Role | Times in the pangram |
|---|---|---|
| E | Center | 1 |
| A | Petal | 2 |
| I | Petal | 2 |
| M | Petal | 1 |
| N | Petal | 2 |
| O | Petal | 1 |
| T | Petal | 2 |

Counts come from the word itself and add to 11. Three letters read 1 and four read 2.
