## Saturday's Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints, Nov. 7, 2026

- **Pinpoint #921, first nudge:** the five clue words share one word that carries several unrelated meanings, so stop looking for a single theme.
- **Pinpoint #921, second nudge:** one meaning is a person who trains a team or a single athlete, and another is a vehicle that carries many passengers.
- **Pinpoint #921, third nudge:** think of a railway carriage, a section of an aircraft sold at a mid-range fare, and a fashion house known for handbags.
- **Crossclimb #921, the locked ends:** the two end words are an adjective for something fine and a feeling of looking forward, and together they name a famous cape.
- **Crossclimb #921, the first two rungs:** a precious yellow metal comes first, followed by what a shop did with its stock.
- **Crossclimb #921, the middle rung:** a word that means either the bottom of a shoe or a flat fish.
- **Crossclimb #921, the last two rungs:** an unspecified amount, then the place you live.

## Crossclimb #921 ladder map, Nov. 7, 2026

Seven rows give six moves, and each move swaps a single letter. Positions run 1 to 4 from left to right, and no word appears in this table.

| Step | Letter position that changes |
|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 3 |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 1 |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 4 |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 3 |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 1 |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 3 |

Position 3 moves three times, position 1 twice and position 4 once, while position 2 never changes. If the ladder will not budge, leave the second letter alone and start with the third.

## Starting points for Queens, Tango and Zip

**Queens #921.** Begin with the smallest colored region, since it has the fewest places to hold a crown. Every cell it touches in a row, column or diagonal then becomes unavailable.

**Tango #761.** Scan for three-in-a-row traps: when two matching symbols sit together, the cell on each side must be the other symbol. Count each row and column, because a full quota of one symbol settles the rest.

**Zip #600.** Trace from the number 1 and from the last number toward the middle, and note any cell with only one exit. Those forced cells give you path segments you can join.

## Saturday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 7, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #921:** Different definitions of “coach”.
- **Crossclimb #921:** Good, Gold, Sold, Sole, Some, Home, Hope. Gold, Sold, Sole, Some and Home fill the middle in that order; Good and Hope are the two ends.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row | Good | none |
| Rung 1 | Gold | L for O |
| Rung 2 | Sold | S for G |
| Rung 3 | Sole | E for D |
| Rung 4 | Some | M for L |
| Rung 5 | Home | H for S |
| Bottom row | Hope | P for M |

Gold is the metal, sold is what the shop did, sole is a shoe's underside, some is an unspecified amount and home is where you live. Good and Hope, read as a pair, give "good hope," as in the Cape of Good Hope.
