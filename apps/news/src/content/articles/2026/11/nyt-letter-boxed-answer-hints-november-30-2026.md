## Which letter pairs make Monday's box hard?

Twelve letters give 108 ordered steps between different sides. Twenty of them appear in no word at all, and 9 more appear in exactly one word.

The dead steps cluster on a few letters. M cannot go to K, T, D or R, so after an M only C, E, L, N or O can follow. D cannot go to K, T, M or N. C cannot reach M, F or N. The other dead steps are K to M, T to M, T to N, T to D, F to K, F to N, F to D, L to F and L to R.

F is the letter to watch. It sits in 221 words, fewer than any other letter, and K is next at 281. Only 12 words end in an F.

| Sides | Words with a step between them |
|---|---|
| K/O/T and M/I/F | 603 |
| K/O/T and E/L/N | 759 |
| K/O/T and D/R/C | 709 |
| M/I/F and E/L/N | 678 |
| M/I/F and D/R/C | 536 |
| E/L/N and D/R/C | 793 |

The thinnest link joins M/I/F and D/R/C at 536 words, and E/L/N and D/R/C is the busiest at 793.

The list has 1,258 words. Among the 35 days we saved, Oct. 27 to Nov. 30, that is the 22nd smallest, with 13 days larger and the median at 1,207. Sunday's list had 709 words and 5 routes, so Monday's has about 9 times as many.

The Times' own pair is one of the 47. Any two-word route is two under the par of 4, yet the routes differ by up to 9 letters, so a shorter pair is a different way to be correct. The total for the Times' route sits in the answer section below.

## What is the longest word in the list?

It has 15 letters. One word each runs to 14 and 13 letters, 4 words have 12 and 17 have 11. The longest word does cover a lot of the box, and it opens 5 of the 47 routes.

Even so, the best any word does is touch 10 of the 12 letters. Only one word reaches 10, and 14 more touch 9. With two letters always missing, a one-word answer cannot exist today.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, two fewer than the par of 4.
2. The first word has 9 letters and the second has 10. The list holds 89 words of 9 letters and 42 of 10.
3. The first word opens on the M/I/F side and closes on the D/R/C side. The second opens on that same D/R/C side and closes on the E/L/N side.
4. The first word leaves out M, O, T and N. The second word brings all four.
5. The first word starts with F and ends in D. The second starts with D and ends in N.
