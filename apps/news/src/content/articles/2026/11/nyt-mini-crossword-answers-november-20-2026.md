**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, has 10 clues on a 5-by-5 grid with 6 black squares, and the official data credits Joel Fagliano.** Every hint opens on a tap; the filled grid sits in the marked spoiler section. The 9-Across clue mentions a personal identification number (PIN), and its answer is a three-letter machine.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (3 letters), *"That ship ___ sailed"*: the verb that completes the saying, in the form that goes with "he" or "she"; it starts with the first letter of 1-Down and ends with the first letter of 3-Down.
- **4-Across** (4 letters), *As animals, they squeak; as objects, they click*: the plural of a small rodent, and also the plural of a pointing device on a desk; it starts with the second letter of 1-Down and ends with the first letter of 5-Down.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Vowelless way of saying "Yes"*: a hummed agreement written with consonants only, where one letter appears four times; it starts with the last letter of 1-Down and ends with the first letter of 7-Down.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *Do the butterfly, perhaps*: the butterfly here is a stroke in a pool; it starts with the last letter of 2-Down and ends with the second letter of 7-Down.
- **9-Across** (3 letters), *Place to enter a PIN*: a street machine that hands out cash; it starts with the last letter of 3-Down and ends with the last letter of 7-Down.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (3 letters), *"Let me think ..."*: the sound of someone pondering aloud; it has no vowel, and its first letter starts 1-Across.
- **2-Down** (4 letters), *Prepares to throw a dart*: a verb for lining up a shot; it starts with the second letter of 1-Across and ends with the first letter of 8-Across.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *The "uh" vowel sound at the starts of words like "agree" and "occur"*: the name linguists give to that weak, unstressed sound; it starts with the last letter of 1-Across and ends with the first letter of 9-Across.
- **5-Down** (4 letters), *Give off*: a verb for releasing light, heat or a sound; it starts with the last letter of 4-Across and ends with the second letter of 9-Across.
- **7-Down** (3 letters), *"Delicious!"*: a contented hum over good food; all three letters are the same, and it starts with the last letter of 6-Across.

## The double-meaning clue and the vowelless entries

4-Across is the clue where the wordplay is least literal, because its two halves describe the same plural noun in two settings. "Squeak" belongs to a living creature and "click" belongs to a gadget you press, so the clue works by splitting one word into two jobs. Once you see that, the four letters fall into place from the four Down entries that cross it.

The other oddity is 6-Across, whose clue announces that its answer has no vowels. That is a promise about the whole entry, so the crossing Downs have to supply every letter without a single a, e, i, o or u in that row. Count the grid and the pattern is plain: three of the 10 answers, 6-Across, 1-Down and 7-Down, contain no vowel letter. The letter M fills 7 of the 19 white squares, more than a third of the white squares in the grid.
