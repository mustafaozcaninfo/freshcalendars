**World Children's Day is Friday, Nov. 20, 2026,** according to the United Nations (UN), whose observance page lists the date as "20 November" and says it marks "the adoption of the Convention on the Rights of the Child on November 20." Below are the treaty's key dates and counts from the UN Treaty Collection, the weekday of Nov. 20 through 2031 and a free activity sheet. The United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF) gives the 2026 theme as "Children are speaking up: My day, my rights, my future"; the UN page shows no year-specific theme.

## What does World Children's Day mark?

The UN's observance page says the day marks the adoption of the Convention on the Rights of the Child. The UN Treaty Collection records the treaty as concluded at "New York, 20 November 1989" and adopted by General Assembly resolution 44/25 on 20 November 1989. It entered into force on "2 September 1990, in accordance with article 49(1)."

## Convention on the Rights of the Child in numbers

All figures come from the UN Treaty Collection page checked on Nov. 4, 2026.

| Item | What the Treaty Collection lists |
|---|---|
| Concluded | New York, 20 November 1989 |
| Adopted | General Assembly resolution 44/25, 20 November 1989 |
| Entered into force | 2 September 1990 |
| Parties | 196 |
| Signatories | 140 |
| United States | Signature on Feb. 16, 1995; no ratification listed |

## What is the World Children's Day 2026 theme?

The UNICEF page for the day, checked Nov. 10, 2026, reads "Children are speaking up: My day, my rights, my future." It says World Children's Day "will take place on 20 November 2026" and that "around the world, children are speaking up about their lives, their rights, and the future they want." The UN observance page shows no year-specific theme. For comparison, the UNICEF Romania page for 2025 gave the theme "Listen to children, stand up for the rights of every child, every day." That was last year's theme.

## What weekday is Nov. 20 through 2031?

The date is fixed, so the weekday moves. The weekdays are our calculation.

| Year | Date | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Nov. 20 | Friday |
| 2027 | Nov. 20 | Saturday |
| 2028 | Nov. 20 | Monday |
| 2029 | Nov. 20 | Tuesday |
| 2030 | Nov. 20 | Wednesday |
| 2031 | Nov. 20 | Thursday |

Mark it on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) or the [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html). The next day, Saturday, Nov. 21, is National Adoption Day on the coalition's page; see [National Adoption Day 2026](/news/national-adoption-day-2026-november-21/). A week earlier, [World Kindness Day](/news/world-kindness-day-2026-friday-november-13-kindness-dates-printable/) falls on Friday, Nov. 13, and the UN's other rights observance, [Human Rights Day](/news/human-rights-day-2026-december-10/), follows on Dec. 10.

## Ways to take part in 2026, from UNICEF's page

UNICEF's global page lists these ideas for 2026:

- Tell adults what kind of future you want.
- Help children know their rights.
- Take the survey.
- Come run with your family and wear blue for children's rights.

In 2025, UNICEF Romania's page listed other ideas, among them the #KidsTakeOver challenge, coloring company logos blue online and, for teachers, the World's Largest Lesson. Those were 2025 ideas.

## What is on the free activity sheet?

The one-page sheet carries the Nov. 20 date, three treaty facts (conclusion in New York on 20 November 1989, entry into force on 2 September 1990, and the UN's description of the day), a checklist of the four 2026 ideas above and lines for notes.
