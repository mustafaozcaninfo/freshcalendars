## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *___ as a button*: an adjective for something small and charming; it starts with C and ends with E.
- **5-Across** (4 letters), *Computer operating system since 1969*: a family of systems whose name is a four-letter coinage; it starts with U and ends with X.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Units of ice cream or beer*: a liquid measure, where two cups make one; it starts with P and ends with S.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *Green-skinned monster, perhaps*: a fairy-tale giant with a bad temper; it starts with O and ends with E.
- **9-Across** (4 letters), *___ as a pin*: a word for tidy and well kept; it starts with N and ends with T.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (3 letters), *Container for ice cream or beer*: a small drinking vessel, also a baking measure; its middle letter opens 5-Across, and its last letter opens 6-Across.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Worker's collective*: a group that bargains with an employer for its members; it starts with U and ends with N.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Slightest bit of color*: a faint trace of hue, as when a white wall picks up a hint of pink; it starts with T and ends with E.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *Background actor*: a person in the crowd of a film scene who has no lines; it starts with E and ends with A.
- **7-Down** (3 letters), *Word before "... go!"*: the middle word of a three-word starting call at a race; it starts with S and ends with T.

## The countdown clue and the Monday comparison

7-Down is the clue furthest from a plain definition. It gives no meaning at all, only the last word of a phrase and a dotted gap before it. The quotation marks tell you the clue is a spoken line, and the ellipsis says one word is missing right in front of "go". Think of the three beats said aloud at the start of a sprint, and the answer is the one in the middle. The last letter of 8-Across is this answer's middle square, which helps.

A smaller echo sits on the left. 1-Down and 6-Across share their wording, "ice cream or beer", yet one wants a container and the other a unit of measure. They meet at one square, the last of 1-Down and the first of 6-Across.
