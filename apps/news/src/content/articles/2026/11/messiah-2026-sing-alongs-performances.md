**The Los Angeles Master Chorale's Great Messiah Sing-Along is Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, at 7 p.m. PT (10 p.m. ET) at Walt Disney Concert Hall**, according to the chorale's event page. It is the one sing-along among the five Messiah listings confirmed from official pages for 2026; Chicago, Philadelphia, New York and Boston list performances to attend or watch. The table puts them side by side, and a one-page printable keeps the dates.

## Los Angeles Messiah sing-along at Walt Disney Concert Hall

The Los Angeles Master Chorale calls the event "Two Thousand Voices at Walt Disney Concert Hall" and says tickets are sold on its page. Its description says Grant Gershon conducts with soloists and a chamber orchestra, and that the audience sings the chorus parts, up to the "Hallelujah" chorus. The chorale says you do not have to be a professional singer to take part.

## What to bring to a Messiah sing-along

The Los Angeles Master Chorale says Grant Gershon Silver Anniversary vocal scores are available for purchase in the lobby for anyone who does not know the words. The page gives no price for the scores or for tickets, so check the ticket page before you go. If you already own a score, the chorale's page invites you to "grab your score".

## Messiah in Chicago, Philadelphia, New York and Boston

The Chicago Symphony Orchestra lists Handel's Messiah on Thursday, Dec. 17, Friday, Dec. 18 and Saturday, Dec. 19 at Symphony Center, with tickets from $69.00 including fees. It also lists a free preconcert conversation 75 minutes before each performance.

The Philadelphia Orchestra lists "Dec 4 - Dec 5, 2026" at Marian Anderson Hall in the Kimmel Center, with the Philadelphia Symphonic Choir. Times and prices were not shown on the page.

Trinity Church Wall Street lists "Messiah" on Friday, Dec. 4, 2026, at 7 p.m. ET as a livestream on YouTube, with the Trinity Choir and Trinity Baroque Orchestra under conductor Nicholas McGegan. The page gives no price.

In its 2026-27 season announcement of March 24, 2026, the Handel and Haydn Society lists Messiah at Symphony Hall in Boston on Nov. 27, 28 and 29, the Friday to Sunday after Thanksgiving. Times and prices were not in that announcement.

## Messiah dates not confirmed in Washington and London

We could not confirm a date for the Kennedy Center's Messiah Sing-Along or the National Symphony Orchestra's Messiah in Washington, because the Kennedy Center's pages refused our automated reads. Check kennedy-center.org. We found no December 2026 Messiah listing from the Royal Albert Hall or the Southbank Centre in London. The Tabernacle Choir's Dec. 17-19 concerts are Christmas concerts, not Messiah, so they are left out.
