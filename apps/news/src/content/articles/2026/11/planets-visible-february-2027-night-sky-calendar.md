**Jupiter reaches opposition on Wednesday, Feb. 10, 2027, at 7:29 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), and Mars follows on Friday, Feb. 19, at 10:51 a.m. EST**, according to the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ). The Moon phases come from the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO). Both oppositions fall in one month, and the table below puts them next to the Moon and Mercury.

All times are EST, which applies all of February. Print the Full Moon night on one page with the [February 2027 night sky sheet](#downloads).

NAOJ publishes in Japan Standard Time (JST), nine hours ahead of Universal Time (UT), and lists the Mercury elongation by the hour only ("15h" on Feb. 3 JST), so it is "about 1 a.m." in EST. We converted every NAOJ time, so Jupiter's "09:29" on Feb. 11 JST is 7:29 p.m. EST on Feb. 10. The USNO times are in UT, also converted. NASA's eclipse tables use Terrestrial Dynamical Time (TD), about 69 seconds ahead of UT, and we converted those too.

## Which planets are visible in February 2027?

Jupiter and Mars are the two planets with oppositions this month. A planet at opposition is opposite the Sun in the sky, so it rises around sunset and stays up all night. Jupiter's opposition comes 8 days and 15 hours before Mars's.

| Planet | NAOJ date (EST) | What the date means |
|---|---|---|
| Jupiter | Opposition, Wed., Feb. 10, 7:29 p.m. | Opposite the Sun |
| Mars | Opposition, Fri., Feb. 19, 10:51 a.m. | Opposite the Sun |
| Mercury | Greatest elongation east, Feb. 3; stationary Feb. 8; inferior conjunction Feb. 18 | East of the Sun at the start of the month, between Earth and the Sun on Feb. 18 |

An elongation is the angle between a planet and the Sun. A planet east of the Sun sets after it, so Mercury's Feb. 3 elongation puts it in the evening sky after sunset. At inferior conjunction on Feb. 18, Mercury passes between Earth and the Sun. NAOJ gives dates, not rise and set times, and the USNO service used here covers only the Sun and Moon, so we do not print planet times. This guide does not place Venus or Saturn. For exact times in your city, use a planetarium app. NASA's "What's Up" skywatching guide had no February 2027 edition on Nov. 22, 2026.

The full list of 2027 oppositions is in our [planets in 2027 calendar](/news/planets-2027-calendar-jupiter-mars-opposition-saturn-venus-elongation-dates/). The month before is in the [January 2027 night sky calendar](/news/planets-visible-january-2027-night-sky-calendar/).

## What are the Moon phases in February 2027?

The USNO lists four primary phases: new Moon on Saturday, Feb. 6, first quarter on Sunday, Feb. 14, full Moon on Saturday, Feb. 20 and last quarter on Sunday, Feb. 28. Jupiter's opposition comes about four days after the new Moon. The full Moon on Feb. 20 comes the day after Mars's opposition. For every phase of the year, see the [Moon phases 2027 guide](/news/moon-phases-2027-full-moon-calendar/).

## When do the Sun and Moon set in New York in February 2027?

The USNO gives these times for New York in EST.

| Date | Sunset | End of civil twilight | Moonrise | Moonset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon., Feb. 1 | 5:13 p.m. | 5:42 p.m. | 3:49 a.m. | 12:33 p.m. |
| Mon., Feb. 15 | 5:31 p.m. | 5:59 p.m. | 11:19 a.m. | 2:23 a.m. |
| Sat., Feb. 20 | 5:37 p.m. | 6:04 p.m. | 5:32 p.m. | 6:28 a.m. |
| Sun., Feb. 28 | 5:46 p.m. | 6:13 p.m. | 1:39 a.m. | 10:27 a.m. |

Sunset comes 33 minutes later on Feb. 28 than on Feb. 1. On Feb. 1 and Feb. 28 the Moon is not up at the end of twilight, so there is no moonlight after dusk. On Feb. 15 the Moon has been up since 11:19 a.m., and on Feb. 20 it rises at 5:32 p.m., just before sunset.

## Is there an eclipse in February 2027?

There are two, and only the lunar one is in the U.S. sky. NASA lists an annular solar eclipse on Feb. 6, 2027, with greatest eclipse at 16:00:47 TD, visible from South America, Antarctica and western and southern Africa. The path crosses Chile, Argentina and the Atlantic. It is not visible from the United States.

NASA also lists a penumbral lunar eclipse on Feb. 20, 2027, with greatest eclipse at 23:14:06 TD, about 6:13 p.m. EST, in a region that includes the Americas. It is a very faint eclipse that is hard to notice, so do not expect a visible darkening. In New York the Moon rises at 5:32 p.m. that day, according to the USNO. The printable lists the Feb. 20 moonrise and moonset for New York, Chicago, Denver and Los Angeles.

## Is there a meteor shower in February 2027?

No major one. The American Meteor Society (AMS) calendar has no major shower in February, and the next is the Lyrids, active April 14-30, 2027.
