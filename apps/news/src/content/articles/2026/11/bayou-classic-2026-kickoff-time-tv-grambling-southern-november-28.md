**The 53rd Bayou Classic, Southern vs. Grambling State, is Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, with kickoff at 1 p.m. local time (CT, or 2 p.m. ET) at Caesars Superdome in New Orleans, according to the Superdome.** The stadium's event page also lists "a week of events featuring a parade, fan festival and battle of the bands," and tickets are on sale through Ticketmaster. The official Bayou Classic site says "All events are subject to change."

The Superdome is in New Orleans, so 1 p.m. is Central Time. Doors open at 11:30 a.m. CT, per the Bayou Classic events page. The 2026 TV network and stream have not been announced; the table and printable below list the game channel as to be announced (TBA).

## What time does the Bayou Classic kick off in each time zone?

| Time zone | Kickoff on Sat., Nov. 28 |
|---|---|
| Central (CT, local in New Orleans) | 1 p.m. |
| Eastern (ET) | 2 p.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | noon |
| Pacific (PT) | 11 a.m. |

Doors open at 11:30 a.m. CT (12:30 p.m. ET). The Bayou Classic site lists the halftime show and the postgame trophy presentation without times.

## Bayou Classic week events, Nov. 24-28

The official events page lists nine events. Times are local Central Time on the official page; the ET column adds one hour.

| Event | Day | Time (CT) | Time (ET) | Place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Welcome Press Conference](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) | Tue., Nov. 24 | 10 a.m. | 11 a.m. | Caesars Superdome |
| [Bayou Classic Thanksgiving Day Parade](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) | Thu., Nov. 26 | 3 p.m. | 4 p.m. | Downtown New Orleans |
| [Bayou Business Bowl student pitch](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) | Fri., Nov. 27 | 10 a.m. to noon | 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. | Hilton New Orleans Riverside, Jefferson Ballroom |
| [Coaches luncheon](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) (invitation only) | Fri., Nov. 27 | noon | 1 p.m. | Hyatt Regency New Orleans |
| [Beats Before Battle](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) | Fri., Nov. 27 | 3 to 6 p.m. | 4 to 7 p.m. | Caesars Superdome, gate C plaza level (clear bag policy) |
| [Greek Show and Battle of the Bands](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) | Fri., Nov. 27 | Doors 5 p.m., event 6 p.m. | Doors 6 p.m., event 7 p.m. | Caesars Superdome, gate C; separate Ticketmaster tickets |
| [Special Olympics Unified Rivalry Flag Football](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) | Sat., Nov. 28 | 9 a.m. | 10 a.m. | Pan American Stadium, City Park (free) |
| [Bayou Classic Fan Festival](https://mybayouclassic.com/events) | Sat., Nov. 28 | 9 a.m. to noon | 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. | Champions Square at the Superdome (free) |
| [53rd Bayou Classic game](https://www.caesarssuperdome.com/events/detail/bayou-classic-2) | Sat., Nov. 28 | Doors 11:30 a.m., kickoff 1 p.m. | Doors 12:30 p.m., kickoff 2 p.m. | Caesars Superdome |

The Battle of the Bands is a separate ticket from the game. The Superdome page links the game tickets and the events page links the Greek Show and Battle of the Bands tickets, both on Ticketmaster.

## Who won the Bayou Classic in 2025, and what channel carried it?

In 2025, Southern beat Grambling State 28-27 in the 52nd Bayou Classic on Sat., Nov. 29, 2025, according to Southern University Athletics ("Southern Closes Out 2025 Season With Thrilling Comeback Win Over Grambling in 52nd Bayou Classic"). That is last year's result; the 2026 game decides this year's.

In 2025, NBC Sports listed the game on NBC and Peacock at 2 p.m. ET. The 2026 network is not announced, so check the Superdome and Bayou Classic pages before Nov. 28.
