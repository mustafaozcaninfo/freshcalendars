**Morton's The Steakhouse is serving a dine-in Four-Course Feast for $72 per person on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and says reservations are strongly encouraged.** Its Thanksgiving page lists a dine-in menu at "$72 per person" and marks it "Dine In" "Thursday, November 26" and "Available while supplies last. Valid dine-in only."

If you want to cook at home, Morton's also sells a Sides To Go package for $139 that serves 4 to 6 people, with pickup only on Wednesday, Nov. 25. The page says "Pre-orders end Monday, November 23 at 8 PM," so that deadline has passed, and pickup on Wednesday, Nov. 25, is for orders already placed. Morton's is owned by Landry's, Inc.

## Morton's Thanksgiving dine-in Four-Course Feast: what $72 includes

The $72-per-person price buys four courses. Morton's lists a few swaps "upon request" and paid add-ons.

| Course | Dish | Also offered |
|---|---|---|
| First | Butternut Squash Bisque with cinnamon crème fraîche | Lobster Bisque upon request |
| Second | Fall Harvest Salad with arugula, fennel, apple, pomegranate, walnuts and goat cheese | Caesar or Chopped House salad upon request |
| Third | Roasted Turkey Roulade with sage and cranberry stuffing, sour cream mashed potatoes, green bean casserole, cranberry sauce and turkey gravy | Add sweet potato casserole for two, $15; add lobster macaroni shells and cheese, $20 |
| Dessert | Spiced Pumpkin Torte with whipped cream and cinnamon | Upgrade to the Hot Chocolate Cake, $10, or Salted Caramel Apple Pie, $12 |

Morton's says "Sales tax and gratuity not included. No substitutions. While supplies last," and that the feast is "Not available in Biloxi, San Juan, or international locations." A wine flight called Thanksgiving Uncorked pairs five wines in 3-ounce pours for $39 a person at dine-in, or $49 a person in Maryland, Mississippi, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Virginia. Two bottles to bring home are $85, available for pickup in select locations on Wednesday, Nov. 25, and only with a Sides To Go package or an entrée; the bottles are unavailable in those same five states.

## Morton's Thanksgiving Sides To Go: $139 package and a la carte prices

The Sides To Go package is $139 and "Serves 4-6 People." It has no turkey. Pickup is only on Wednesday, Nov. 25, between 1 and 7 p.m., and the food arrives cold: Morton's says the sides "are not hot or ready-to-eat upon pickup and must be reheated at home before serving," with reheating instructions in each package.

| Sides To Go | Price | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| Package: mashed potatoes, green bean casserole, au gratin potatoes, creamed spinach, sage and cranberry stuffing, turkey gravy, cranberry relish, egg and onion bread (2 loaves) | $139 | 4 to 6 |
| A la carte side (choose one), 2-pound servings | $29 each | not listed |

A la carte, each $29 side is a 2-pound serving: sour cream mashed potatoes, green bean casserole, sweet potato casserole, twice baked au gratin potatoes, creamed spinach, or sage and cranberry stuffing with turkey gravy.

## What a Morton's Thanksgiving costs per person in 2026

Morton's prices the dinner by the person and the sides by the package. The per-person figures below are Fresh Calendars' arithmetic from those prices, before sales tax, gratuity and any add-ons.

| What you buy | Morton's price | Per person |
|---|---|---|
| Dine-in Four-Course Feast | $72 per person | $72 (set by Morton's) |
| Dinner for two | $144 before tax, tip, add-ons and wine | $72 |
| Dinner for two with the wine flight | $222, or $242 in the five named states | $111, or $121 |
| Sides To Go package | $139 for 4 to 6 people | $23.17 to $34.75 |

The $23.17-to-$34.75 range is $139 divided by 6 people and by 4 people. It covers sides only, because the package has no turkey. For a dinner with the wine flight, $222 is $144 plus two $39 pours; $242 is $144 plus two $49 pours in Maryland, Mississippi, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Virginia.
