**Westminster Abbey's First Eucharist of Christmas (Midnight Mass) is on Thursday 24 December 2026 from 11.00pm to 12.30am, free but with booking required, the Abbey says.** London is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in December, so that is 6 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and 3 p.m. Pacific Time (PT). The table lists every Christmas service the Abbey, St Paul's Cathedral and Southwark Cathedral have published, with the ticket rule for each, and a printable keeps most of them together.

## Do you need a ticket for the Abbey's Christmas services?

The Abbey says "some of our most popular services require free tickets to be booked in advance." Its page says tickets for the Advent and Christmas services "will be released on Monday 16th November and Wednesday 18th November" at 9:30am and 2:30pm. Both dates have passed. We could not see current availability, so check the [Abbey's Advent and Christmas page](https://www.westminster-abbey.org/worship-music/services-times/advent-and-christmas/) before you travel. The Abbey's watch page says services can be watched "live or on demand"; it has announced no Christmas broadcast schedule.

## When does the St Paul's Cathedral carol ballot close?

St Paul's says its Family Carols on 19 December and its Christmas Carol Services on 23 and 24 December are "free but ticketed", and the ballot "will close on 26th November". That is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, which is also U.S. Thanksgiving. The cathedral says it will then "contact both successful and unsuccessful ballot entrants." The pages give no closing time. Its Christmas Day Festal Evensong on Friday, Dec. 25 at 3:00pm is "free and unticketed".

The St Paul's Christmas Eve midnight service and Christmas Day morning Eucharist times are not on the pages we could read. Check the cathedral's What's On page for them. For a similar ballot, see [King's College's Nine Lessons and Carols](/news/nine-lessons-and-carols-2026-kings-college-time-ballot/), whose ballot closed Nov. 16.

## Which Christmas services in London need no ticket?

From the sources above, these need no booking or ticket:

- Westminster Abbey Holy Communion, Friday, Dec. 25 at 8.00am, and Evensong at 3.00pm.
- St Paul's Festal Evensong, Friday, Dec. 25 at 3:00pm.
- Southwark Cathedral's Crib Service at 4:00pm and The First Mass at Christmas at 10:00pm on Thursday, Dec. 24.
- Southwark's Christmas Eucharist with Carols at 9:00am and Choral Eucharist at 11:00am on Friday, Dec. 25.

Southwark's First Mass starts at 10 p.m., not midnight, and runs to 11:30pm.
