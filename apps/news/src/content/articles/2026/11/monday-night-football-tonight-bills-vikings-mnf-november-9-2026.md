## When did the Bills and Vikings last play?

The most recent meeting was Sunday, Nov. 13, 2022, when the Vikings beat the Bills 33-30 in overtime at Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, New York. NBC Sports headlined its recap "Vikings seal win over Bills after chaotic finish to regulation, overtime," and Sports Illustrated and FOX Sports also covered the game.

The last game in Minnesota was on Sept. 23, 2018, a 27-6 Bills win, per the Vikings. The Vikings lead the regular-season series 9-6-0, and the Vikings add that the teams have not met on a day other than Sunday since a Thursday Night Football game in 1982, which Buffalo won 23-22.
