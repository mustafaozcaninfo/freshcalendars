**The 2027 Navy Federal Credit Union NHL Stadium Series is Saturday, Feb. 20, 2027, with the Vegas Golden Knights at the Dallas Stars at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, according to the Dallas Stars and the National Hockey League (NHL).** The game begins at 7 p.m. Central Time (CT) and is broadcast on the American Broadcasting Company (ABC). The Stars are the home team. Below: the start time in five zones, the season's three outdoor games, and what each team has done outdoors.

## What time does the Stadium Series start in each time zone?

The Stars' announcement says the game "will begin at 7 p.m. CT." NHL.com's own story gives the same start as 8 p.m. ET. February is standard time in the U.S., and the UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). Every other row is converted from 7 p.m. CT.

| Zone | Start on Saturday, Feb. 20, 2027 |
|---|---|
| Central Time (Dallas, Arlington) | 7 p.m. (the Stars' time) |
| Eastern Time | 8 p.m. (also NHL.com's time) |
| Mountain Time | 6 p.m. |
| Pacific Time | 5 p.m. |
| UK (GMT) | 1 a.m. on Sunday, Feb. 21 |

## What channel is the Stadium Series on?

ABC, per both the Stars and the City of Arlington. A Canadian broadcaster and a streaming plan were not announced in the sources we read, so none is listed here.

## How do you get Stadium Series tickets?

The NHL said on May 18, 2026, that tickets would go on sale Thursday, May 21, 2026, at 10 a.m. CT through SeatGeek, with experience packages through NHL Experiences. The league's Stadium Series page of frequently asked questions says tickets are now available and that the ticket is fully mobile. The sources give no ticket prices, so none appear here.

## Which outdoor games are on the 2026-27 NHL calendar?

The NHL's July 16, 2026 schedule release names three outdoor games this season. The Heritage Classic has been played. For the Winter Classic start time, see our [Winter Classic 2027 story](/news/nhl-winter-classic-2027-date-time-avalanche-mammoth-salt-lake-city/).

| Game | Date | Place | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 Tim Hortons NHL Heritage Classic | Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026 | Winnipeg | Played |
| Discover NHL Winter Classic | Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026 | Rice-Eccles Stadium, Salt Lake City (Utah Mammoth host the Avalanche) | Dec. 31 on the NHL's schedule |
| Navy Federal Credit Union NHL Stadium Series | Saturday, Feb. 20, 2027 | AT&T Stadium, Arlington, Texas | Feb. 20 on the NHL's schedule |

Our [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) holds the Winter Classic date and the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html) holds this one.

## What have the Stars and Golden Knights done outdoors?

Dallas has hosted before. The City of Arlington says this is the second time the Stars and the state of Texas host an NHL outdoor game. The first was the 2020 Winter Classic at the Cotton Bowl, where the Stars beat the Nashville Predators 4-2 on Jan. 1, 2020, before a crowd of 85,630, per NHL.com.

For Vegas, NHL.com calls this the Golden Knights' third outdoor game.

| Team | Outdoor game | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Stars | 2020 Winter Classic, Cotton Bowl, Jan. 1, 2020 | Won 4-2 over Predators |
| Golden Knights | Lake Tahoe, 2021 | Lost 3-2 to Colorado |
| Golden Knights | 2024 Winter Classic | Lost 3-0 to Seattle |

For comparison, the 2026 Stadium Series was Sunday, Feb. 1, 2026, at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa. The Tampa Bay Lightning beat the Boston Bruins 6-5 in a shootout, in front of a crowd of 64,617, according to NHL.com's recap.
