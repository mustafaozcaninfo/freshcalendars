**PetSmart's early Black Friday offers end Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, including an extra 20% off thousands of gifts online with code EARLYBF20, according to the retailer's November sale page.** PetSmart is based in Phoenix. A few offers run to Nov. 29, and free pet photos with Santa are set for Dec. 12-13. PetSmart has not posted what follows the early round from Nov. 26, so check its sale page after the deadline.

## Is PetSmart open on Thanksgiving 2026?

PetSmart has not posted 2026 hours for Thanksgiving (Thursday, Nov. 26) or Black Friday (Nov. 27). Last year, Fox Business reported on Nov. 26, 2025 that "PetSmart will also be closed for Thanksgiving but will reopen at 7 a.m. on Black Friday." That is 2025 information, not a 2026 plan. ## What is Petco doing for Black Friday 2026?

Petco's Oct. 13, 2026 release, issued through PR Newswire, promises savings across Black Friday and Cyber Week, "including buy-one-get-one-free (BOGO) promotions on Holiday products." It says that "Every week during Nov. 21 through Dec. 25, Petco is treating everyone on their 'Nice List'" and lists hundreds of gifts at $10 or less. Petco gives no percentages or Black Friday dates. In 2025, Fox Business reported that "PetCo will be closed on Thanksgiving, but most will open at 8 a.m. the following morning for Black Friday." Petco has not posted 2026 hours in the sources we read.

## When are the free PetSmart Santa photos?

PetSmart's homepage lists free pet photos with Santa in store on Saturday, Dec. 12 and Sunday, Dec. 13, from 2 to 5 p.m. local store time. Petco's release says its free Santa photos "return to select Petco stores on December 12." For mall Santas, see [Santa at the mall 2026](/news/santa-at-the-mall-2026-arrival-dates-simon-malls/).
