faq:
  - q: "When is spring break 2027 for colleges?"
    a: "It depends on the school. Among 37 universities, breaks run from Georgetown's Saturday, Feb. 27 start to Cornell's Sunday, Apr. 4 end. The week of Mar. 15-19 is the most common, with 16 schools."
  - q: "When is spring break 2027 at Yale?"
    a: "Yale College's recess begins after the last academic obligation on Friday, Mar. 5, and classes resume Monday, Mar. 22, 2027, which is 10 weekdays. The calendar says dates are subject to change."
  - q: "Which universities have the earliest spring break in 2027?"
    a: "Georgetown (Main Campus) starts Saturday, Feb. 27 and classes resume Monday, Mar. 8. Temple's break is Monday, Mar. 1 to Sunday, Mar. 7."
  - q: "Why do Stanford and Dartmouth not have a spring break in March?"
    a: "Both use quarter or term calendars. Stanford's winter quarter classes end Mar. 12 and spring quarter starts Monday, Mar. 29; Dartmouth's spring term classes begin Mar. 29."
  - q: "Is a college spring break the same week as a school district's?"
    a: "Sometimes. Dallas, Austin, Orange County, Fla., and Clark County, Nev., are off Mar. 15-19, the same week as 16 of these universities. Check each district in our school district spring break story."

**College spring break 2027 falls between Saturday, Feb. 27 and Sunday, Apr. 4 at 37 universities, and 16 of them are off the week of Mar. 15-19, according to each school's registrar calendar for 2026-27.** We checked the pages on Nov. 4, 2026. The table below gives every break as the school lists it, the weekdays off and the first weekday back, and the week-by-week grouping shows which schools share a week. Families with children in kindergarten through 12th grade (K-12) can compare with our [spring break 2027 story for 19 large school districts](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

## Which week is spring break 2027 at most universities?

Mar. 15-19 is the most common week in our list, with 16 of the 37 schools. Mar. 8-12 follows with 15 schools, counting Yale, whose recess covers Mar. 8-19. Counts are for the schools we checked, not all U.S. colleges.

| Weekdays off | Schools | Universities |
|---|---|---|
| Mar. 1-5 | 2 | Georgetown (Main Campus); Temple |
| Mar. 8-12 | 15 | Boston University; Duke; Notre Dame; University of Mississippi (Ole Miss); University of North Carolina (UNC) at Chapel Hill; University of Pennsylvania; University of Utah; University of Virginia (UVA); Virginia Tech; Yale College; Arizona State; Penn State; University of South Carolina; Louisiana State University (LSU); University of Tennessee |
| Mar. 15-19 | 16 | Harvard Graduate School of Arts and Sciences (GSAS); Rutgers University-New Brunswick; Texas Christian University (TCU); University of Florida; University of Illinois (tentative); Vanderbilt; University of Iowa; University of Maryland; University of Southern California; Florida State; North Carolina (NC) State; Ohio State; University of Colorado Boulder; University of Kansas; University of Kentucky; University of Texas (UT) at Austin |
| Mar. 22-26 | 3 | University of Oregon (quarter system); University of Wisconsin-Madison; Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) |
| Mar. 29-Apr. 2 | 1 | Cornell |

## What do Stanford, Dartmouth and Chicago do instead?

Three universities run quarter or term calendars and show no March spring break in 2027. Stanford's winter quarter classes end Mar. 12 and spring quarter starts Monday, Mar. 29. Dartmouth's spring term classes begin Mar. 29. The University of Chicago lists "Spring Quarter Begins" on Monday, Mar. 22. The gap between quarters is their break. The University of Oregon, also on quarters, does list a spring vacation, Mar. 20-28, and its spring term begins Mar. 29.

Siblings at different schools can plan around a shared week. Sixteen schools are off Mar. 15-19: Harvard GSAS, Vanderbilt, TCU, Kansas, Ohio State, UT Austin, Florida, Rutgers, Illinois, Iowa, Kentucky, Southern California, Florida State, North Carolina (NC) State, Maryland and Colorado Boulder.
