**Connections #1247, the Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, puzzle from The New York Times, was edited by Wyna Liu.** Ten of the 16 tiles are two-word phrases, which leaves a lot of half-words to juggle, and the scoring rules are on [our Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works). Two hints per group are below; the answers follow under a spoiler heading.

## Today's 16 words

Steak Knife, Card Shark, Con Job, Boot-Cut, Navy Seal, Shell Game, Mint Leaf, Gamma Ray, Tape Dispenser, Low-Rise, Fun Sponge, Rip-Off, Skinny, Flimflam, Grapefruit Spoon, Distressed

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Each is a trick for getting someone's money under false pretenses.
- Hint 2: One hides a pea under one of three cups; another repeats nearly the same syllable twice.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Each describes a cut or finish you could pick when shopping for denim.
- Hint 2: One sits below the natural waist; one has been worn and faded on purpose.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Run a finger along each one and you would feel a row of tiny teeth.
- Hint 2: One is a breakfast utensil for a citrus half; one sits on an office desk and tears off sticky strips.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: The last word of each one lives in the ocean.
- Hint 2: One is a burst of high-energy radiation; one is an expert who tends to win at poker.

## Solving tips

Begin with the denim. Those tiles are words you would see on a jeans tag, and none has a second meaning strong enough to pull it away.

Two tiles sound like they belong with the scams because one names a hustler and one starts with a sea word. Read the last word of every two-word tile on its own before you lock in that group.

## Why the traps worked

Card Shark sounds like the person running a con, so it looks like a fifth scam. It belongs to purple, where its last word is a fish.

Shell Game pulls the other way. Shell is a sea word, yet it comes first in the tile, and the group needs the creature at the end, so it stays with the schemes. Rip-Off can suggest ripped knees on a pair of jeans, and Navy is one of the most common denim colors.
