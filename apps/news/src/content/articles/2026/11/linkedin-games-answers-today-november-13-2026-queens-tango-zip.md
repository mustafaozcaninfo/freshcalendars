## Pinpoint and Crossclimb nudges for Friday, Nov. 13

- **Pinpoint #927, first nudge:** this is a geography category, so think of maps rather than meanings.
- **Pinpoint #927, second nudge:** each clue names a cluster of land in the water, not a single piece of land.
- **Pinpoint #927, third nudge:** try adding the word "Islands" after each clue; the category is what they all have in common.
- **Crossclimb #927, the locked ends:** the top word is a waterfowl and the bottom word is where it swims, both four letters.
- **Crossclimb #927, rungs 1 and 2:** a hard rubber disc in hockey, then a rock genre.
- **Crossclimb #927, rung 3:** wordplay that trades on a double meaning, plural.
- **Crossclimb #927, rungs 4 and 5:** writing tools, then a verb for being undecided.

## Step map for the Crossclimb #927 ladder

Seven words, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word appears in this table.

| Move | Letter position that changes | Kind of letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 3 | consonant |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 4 | consonant |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 2 | vowel |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 4 | consonant |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 2 | vowel |

Position 1 changes only on the very first move. From then on that letter is fixed for every rung and for the bottom row, so once rung 1 is in place you can write its first letter into all the rest. Positions 2 and 4 change twice each, position 3 once. The two vowel moves are the fourth and sixth, and both happen in position 2, which leaves just two letter slots to juggle on the middle rungs.

## Starting points for Queens #927, Tango #767, Zip #606 and Mini Sudoku #459

**Queens #927.** Look for a color region that fits inside a single row or column. A crown there rules out the rest of that line for every other region.

**Tango #767.** Begin with any row or column that already shows two matching symbols side by side, because the cells at both ends must then be the opposite symbol.

**Zip #606.** Mark the cells that touch a wall or the grid edge, since they have fewer ways out, and join the numbered dots in order from there.

**Mini Sudoku #459.** Pick the digit that appears most often in the starting grid and see where its last copies must go.

## Friday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 13, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #927:** Names of island groups (archipelagoes), when followed by "Islands".
- **Crossclimb #927:** Duck, Puck, Punk, Puns, Pens, Pend, Pond. Puck, Punk, Puns, Pens and Pend fill the middle in that order; Duck and Pond are the locked ends.

The P ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top row | Duck | none | none |
| Rung 1 | Puck | P for D | 1 |
| Rung 2 | Punk | N for C | 3 |
| Rung 3 | Puns | S for K | 4 |
| Rung 4 | Pens | E for U | 2 |
| Rung 5 | Pend | D for S | 4 |
| Bottom row | Pond | O for E | 2 |

Duck and Pond are the waterfowl and its water. Puck is the hockey disc, punk a rock genre, puns wordplay, pens writing tools and pend means to hang undecided.

After Duck becomes Puck, P holds the first spot through the whole ladder, so only the last three letters ever move. The vowel swaps are Puns to Pens and Pend to Pond, both in the second position.
