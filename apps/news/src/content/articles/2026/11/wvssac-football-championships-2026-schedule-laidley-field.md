**The West Virginia Secondary School Activities Commission (WVSSAC) lists its 2026 football championships for Fri., Dec. 4 and Sat., Dec. 5 at Laidley Field in Charleston, in a table that reads "Football | Dec. 4-5 | Charleston | Laidley Field / UC Stadium."** The eight semifinals come first, on Fri., Nov. 27 and Sat., Nov. 28, in four classes: Class Four-A (AAAA), AAA, AA and A. Which class plays on which finals day, and the kickoff times, are not yet announced.

## WVSSAC football championships 2026: finals dates and site

The WVSSAC's "Championship Dates, Locations for 2026-27" page names Charleston and Laidley Field, also called University of Charleston (UC) Stadium. WV MetroNews reported on Feb. 22, 2024 that the football championships "will move to University of Charleston Stadium for at least the next three years," with a mutual option for the 2027 title games.

In 2025, WV MetroNews posted the finals schedule on Nov. 23, after the semifinals: AAAA on Fri., Dec. 5 at noon, A on Fri., Dec. 5 at 7 p.m., AA on Sat., Dec. 6 at noon and AAA on Sat., Dec. 6 at 7 p.m. That is last year's pattern, not a 2026 schedule.

## WVSSAC football semifinals 2026: all eight games

WV MetroNews (Nov. 7, 2026) and the television newsroom at wchstv.com (WCHS), Nov. 22, 2026, agree on every pairing and time below. WCHS reports that the higher seed hosts and that the winners advance to Laidley Field on Dec. 4-5. All times are Eastern Time (ET).

| A | No. 4 East Hardy (11-1) at No. 1 Tug Valley (11-1) | Sat., Nov. 28 | 1 p.m. |

## Which 2025 finalists could meet again?

All four 2025 AAAA and AA finalists are in this year's semifinals. Morgantown and Martinsburg are in different AAAA semifinals, so a rematch of the 2025 AAAA final is possible on Dec. 4-5. Frankfort and Bluefield are also in different AA semifinals, so a rematch of the 2025 AA final is possible too. Neither is set: each rematch needs both teams to win their semifinals on Nov. 27-28.

## Who won the 2025 WVSSAC football championships?

All four 2025 finals were played at UC Stadium at Laidley Field. Results come from WV MetroNews and The Intelligencer.

| Class | 2025 final | Date |
|---|---|---|
| AAAA | Morgantown 28, Martinsburg 21 | Dec. 5, 2025 |
| AAA | Princeton 36, Nitro 35 | Sat., Dec. 6, 2025 |
| AA | Frankfort 49, Bluefield 42 | Sat., Dec. 6, 2025 |
| A | Wheeling Central Catholic 56, Clay-Battelle 34 | Fri., Dec. 5, 2025 |
