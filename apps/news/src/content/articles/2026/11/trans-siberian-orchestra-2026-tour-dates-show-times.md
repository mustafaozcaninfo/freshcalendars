Trans-Siberian Orchestra (TSO) opened its 2026 Winter Tour on Thursday, Nov. 19, at the Resch Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin, and plays 56 cities through Wednesday, Dec. 30, according to [TicketNews](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/trans-siberian-orchestra-unveils-30th-anniversary-the-ghosts-of-christmas-eve-winter-tour/). The tour page on [trans-siberian.com](https://www.trans-siberian.com/tour) lists the show as "The Ghosts of Christmas Eve: The Best Of TSO And More!" with "tickets starting as low as $59," plus tax where applicable. The table gives every city's local show times and the same times in Eastern Time (ET), and a printable is at the end.

## Where does Trans-Siberian Orchestra play in 2026, and when are the shows?

TSO's tour page loads its dates by script, so the dates and times below come from the full lists that [Ultimate Classic Rock](https://www.ultimateclassicrock.com/trans-siberian-orchestra-winter-tour-2026/) and [Blabbermouth.net](https://blabbermouth.net/news/trans-siberian-orchestra-announces-30th-anniversary-tour-for-november-december-2026) both published on Sept. 9, 2026. The two lists agree on all 56 cities, venues and times. Venue releases dated Sept. 9 from the Covelli Centre, Target Center and Rocket Arena announced the tour, which venues billed as the 30th anniversary winter tour. Each venue name links to the Ultimate Classic Rock list, except six that link to a page from the venue or the Hershey Harrisburg Regional Visitors Bureau that confirms its own row.

Local times are as listed. The ET column adds one hour for Central Time (CT), two for Mountain Time (MT) and three for Pacific Time (PT). Phoenix is on Mountain Standard Time (MST) in December, two hours behind ET. Toronto is on ET. City names follow the way the sources print them. Several arenas carry a sponsor's initials. Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU) names the Manchester arena and Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU) the Worcester one; Allentown's takes the initials of the utility once called Pennsylvania Power and Light (PPL), and Albany's those of a health insurer that began as Mohawk Valley Physicians' Health Plan (MVP). Birmingham's Legacy Arena is part of the Birmingham-Jefferson Convention Complex (BJCC).

## How many Trans-Siberian Orchestra shows are matinees?

The 56 cities add up to 97 shows by our count of the lists. Forty-one cities have two shows on their date, a matinee and an evening show, and 15 have a single evening show: Council Bluffs, Wilkes-Barre, Nashville, Charleston, Fresno, Las Vegas, Allentown, Austin, Raleigh, Bossier City, Charlottesville, Birmingham, Albany, Jacksonville and Sunrise. Of the 41 two-show cities, 32 start the first show at 3 p.m. local. Six start at 2:30 p.m., and Boise (2 p.m.), Portland (3:30 p.m.) and Chicago (2:45 p.m.) each have their own start. Uncasville, Portland and Chicago have an 8 p.m. evening show; every other evening show starts at 7 p.m. or 7:30 p.m. local.

Every date on the tour has two stops. By weekday, the 56 cities fall on 12 Saturdays, 12 Sundays, 10 Wednesdays, 8 Thursdays, 8 Fridays and 6 Tuesdays, with none on a Monday. On Saturdays and Sundays, 24 shows each are listed.

## Is Trans-Siberian Orchestra playing in Canada in 2026?

Toronto is the only Canadian stop. TSO plays Scotiabank Arena on Tuesday, Dec. 29, at 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. Toronto is on ET, so those are the same times in the ET column. Kansas City plays the same night at 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. local, which is 4 p.m. and 8:30 p.m. ET.

## Where does the 2026 tour end?

Two cities close the tour on Wednesday, Dec. 30: Buffalo at KeyBank Center and St. Louis at Enterprise Center, both at 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. local. TicketNews names St. Louis as the final stop. The Buffalo show is 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. ET, and the St. Louis show is 4 p.m. and 8:30 p.m. ET.

## How does the 2026 tour compare with 2025?

In 2025, Blabbermouth and Loudwire reported on Aug. 28, 2025, that TSO would play 106 concerts in 64 markets, and [Loudwire](https://loudwire.com/trans-siberian-orchestra-2025-north-american-tour/) listed Nov. 13, 2025, in Green Bay as the opener. The 2026 list has 56 cities, 97 shows by our count, and a Nov. 19 Green Bay opener.
