**Cracker Barrel Old Country Store, Inc. said in its Oct. 20, 2026 release that Thanksgiving Heat & Serve pickup runs from Nov. 21 through Nov. 29.** Pre-orders are open at CrackerBarrel.com and in the Cracker Barrel app. The Turkey Family Dinner is typically priced around $119.99 and the Turkey Feast around $189.99, and prices may vary by location. Nation's Restaurant News reported the same Nov. 21-29 pickup window the same day.

The release also lists new and returning holiday dishes, and it says nothing yet about Christmas meals. This story is one of our [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/) entries; the tables below are built from the company's own text.

## When is Cracker Barrel Heat & Serve pickup in 2026?

Pickup is available Nov. 21 through Nov. 29, according to the release. The company does not list pickup hours by day, so the table below only gives the weekday of each date in the window. Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, falls in the middle of it (see our [Thanksgiving 2026 date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/)).

| Nov. 25 | Wednesday | Yes |
| Nov. 26 | Thursday | Yes, Thanksgiving Day |
| Nov. 27 | Friday | Yes |

## Cracker Barrel Thanksgiving Heat & Serve meals and prices

Cracker Barrel says the complete meals are ready to heat and serve in two hours or less. Both are priced "typically", and the company adds that prices may vary based on location.

| | Turkey Family Dinner | Turkey Feast |
|---|---|---|
| Typical price | Around $119.99 | Around $189.99 |
| Feeds | 4-6 people | 8-10 people |
| Turkey | One oven-roasted turkey breast | Two oven-roasted turkey breasts |
| Sides | Sweet potato casserole, green beans | Sweet potato casserole, green beans, mashed potatoes |
| Also served with | Cornbread dressing, gravy, cranberry relish, sweet rolls | Cornbread dressing, gravy, cranberry relish, sweet rolls |
| Dessert | None listed | Pumpkin pie and pecan pie |

Holiday pies are sold separately and are typically priced around $14.49 each, in flavors including pumpkin, pecan, chocolate pecan and cinnamon roll, according to the release.

## What is new on the Cracker Barrel holiday menu?

The company says the holiday menu began Oct. 20 and is available at its locations nationwide for a limited time throughout the holiday season. It did not give an end date. The campaign is called "Get Your Season's Worth."

| Item | Status | What the release says |
|---|---|---|
| Cinnamon Swirl Pancakes | New | The Cinnamon Swirl French Toast flavors as buttermilk pancakes with cinnamon filling and cream cheese icing, with two eggs and bacon or sausage |
| Country Fried Turkey Sandwich | New | Crispy turkey tenderloin, gravy and cranberry relish on a toasted bun |
| Country Fried Turkey | Returning | Hand-breaded turkey tenderloins with herbed pan gravy, country sides, cranberry relish, and biscuits or corn muffins |
| Cinnamon Swirl French Toast Breakfast | Returning | Cinnamon streusel bread with two eggs and bacon or sausage |
| Rich Do-It-Yourself Hot Chocolate | Returning | Whipped cream, chocolate drizzle and mix-and-match toppings |
| Cinnamon Swirl Latte | Returning | Hot or iced, with whipped cream, icing drizzle and cinnamon |

Sarah Moore, Chief Marketing Officer at Cracker Barrel, said in the release: "Some of the best holiday memories happen when you're not planning on making them."

## Is Cracker Barrel open on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

USA Today reported on Oct. 21 that Cracker Barrel plans to serve a traditional Thanksgiving meal at its restaurants on Thursday, Nov. 26, and that Thanksgiving hours may vary by location. Food Network reported on Nov. 3 that the restaurants are open on Thanksgiving Day and serve that meal starting at 11 a.m. local time. We did not find a closing time. Check your local restaurant before you go.

## Does Cracker Barrel have a Christmas Heat & Serve meal in 2026?

Not yet announced. The Oct. 20 release covers Thanksgiving Heat & Serve meals only, and Cracker Barrel has not published Christmas pickup dates or Christmas Eve and Christmas Day hours. Christmas Day is Friday, Dec. 25, 2026. We will add the dates when the company announces them. A [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html) is a place to pencil them in once they are out.
