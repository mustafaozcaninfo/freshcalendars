## Where the T starts in grid #1755

Look at the top word's middle square. The tile sitting there is a T, and that square is not its home. In the shortest route we list, that one tile is swapped four times, more than anything else on the board, and only the fourth swap settles it. That last move also brings a second tile home.

On the way it stops on the left edge, then the right edge, then the bottom row, so expect to park it in a few wrong squares while other tiles go home.

## Start from the top of grid #1755

Row 1 has three green tiles, at the left end, next to it, and at the right end. That makes it the nearest word to done. Row 3 is the opposite: only the center square is home, so four of its five tiles have to arrive from elsewhere. Row 5 sits between them with two greens, both in corners.

Column 1, column 3 and column 5 each hold two greens. Add the row and column counts and you reach 12, because five of the seven greens sit where a row meets a column and count twice, while the other two, in row 1 and row 4, count once.

Five letters repeat: L, E, S, P and N take up 12 of the 21 tiles.

| Letter | Tiles | Start in the right square |
|---|---|---|
| L | 3 | 1 |
| E | 3 | 0 |
| S | 2 | 0 |
| P | 2 | 1 |
| N | 2 | 0 |

Ten of those 12 tiles start in the wrong square, so a yellow L, E, S, P or N tells you little: the same letter has two or three legal homes. Every other letter appears once and has a single destination.
