## Quordle #1766 hints, word by word

The words run in alphabetical order, not in board order.

**Word 1**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 12th letter of the alphabet and ends with the 8th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels, side by side, in the middle.
- Hint 3: To drain away slowly, the way minerals wash out of soil with water.

**Word 2**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 13th letter and ends with the 14th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels again, side by side, right after the first letter.
- Hint 3: To feel and show sorrow for someone who has died.

**Word 3**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 16th letter and ends with the 25th.
- Hint 2: One vowel, in second place, and the 1st and 4th letters match.
- Hint 3: Soft, wet and mushy, like the inside of a ripe fruit.

**Word 4**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 18th letter and ends with the 5th.
- Hint 2: Three vowels, and the middle letter of the word is one of them.
- Hint 3: The path you pick to get from where you are to where you are going.

## Where the letters of Leach, Mourn, Pulpy and Route sit

The endings are H, N, Y and E, all different. U is the one letter in three words, Mourn, Pulpy and Route, and Leach is the only word without it. Pairs overlap lightly: Leach and Pulpy share L, Leach and Route share E, Mourn and Pulpy share only U, and Mourn and Route share O, U and R. Leach and Mourn share nothing.

| Letter | Slots used | Words |
|---|---|---|
| U | 3 | Mourn, Pulpy, Route |
| L, E, O, R, P | 2 each | L in Leach and Pulpy; E in Leach and Route; O and R in Mourn and Route; P twice in Pulpy |
| A, C, H, M, N, Y, T | 1 each | one word each |

Those rows add up to 20 slots and 13 distinct letters. Route holds three vowels, Leach and Mourn two each, and Pulpy one plus Y, so the vowel count is eight. Pulpy is the only word that doubles a letter, and its two Ps sit in first and fourth place.

A and Y each appear once, while U is the only vowel shared by three words. That is why Roate, with its O, A and E, scores best on Word 4 yet misses Word 3 entirely.
