## How Quordle works

You get nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Each guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**The word that starts with J**

- Letters: starts with J and ends with T, with an O, an I and an S inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: a horizontal beam that holds up a floor or a ceiling.

**The word that starts with C**

- Letters: starts with C and ends with K, with an R and two more of the same vowel letter inside; one vowel letter that appears twice, so this word repeats a letter.
- Meaning: a dishonest person, or the curve of a bent arm.

**The word that starts with D**

- Letters: starts with D and ends with Y, with an E, an L and an A inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: to put something off until later, or the wait that follows.

**The word that starts with I**

- Letters: starts with I and ends with Y, with a C, a second I and an L inside; one vowel letter besides the Y, appearing twice, so this is the second word today that repeats a letter.
- Meaning: in a cold and unfriendly way.

## A starter strategy for today

Two of today's words end in the same letter, so a late guess can fit more than one board, which makes the end of a game worth watching. For an opener, try Alone, with A, L, O, N and E: each of the four words contains at least one of those five letters, so every board gets a clue on guess one. Finish the board with the most green tiles first, and skip any board that is already solved.
