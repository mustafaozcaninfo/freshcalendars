## Hints for Pinpoint #942 and Crossclimb #942

- **Pinpoint #942, what is confirmed:** two sources agree on only the first three clue words, so this page covers three of the five.
- **Pinpoint #942, the first three:** each one is a physical thing, not an action, and all three belong to one kind of machine.
- **Crossclimb #942, the chain:** seven words of four letters, joined by six one-letter swaps.
- **Crossclimb #942, the two ends:** read together, the two end words make one familiar two-word phrase about people flocking to a place in search of a fortune.
- **Crossclimb #942, the middle:** one word means to grip or keep, one is a flower, one is a sly plan or trick, one is a gap and one is a garden tool's cousin, a flexible pipe.

## Crossclimb #942 as a letter-position map

Positions run from the left, 1 to 4, and no word appears here. Six swaps, in order:

| Swap | Position that changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 1 |
| 5 | 2 |
| 6 | 4 |

Counted by position, the first and fourth letters change twice each and the second and third once each. Every position changes at least once. The first and fourth slots each move once in the first half of the chain and once in the second, while the third slot moves only at swap 3 and the second only at swap 5.

## Where to begin Queens #942, Tango #782, Zip #621 and Mini Sudoku #474

**Queens #942.** Find the smallest colored region; with only a few cells to choose from, each option rules out neighbors quickly.

**Tango #782.** Look for two matching symbols side by side: the cells on either end of that pair must hold the opposite symbol.

**Zip #621.** Mark the squares with the fewest open neighbors, since the path has to pass through them in a forced way.

**Mini Sudoku #474.** Scan for a digit that already appears in several boxes; it can only fit in a few cells of the rest.

## Saturday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 28, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #942:** Parts of a submarine (Nerdschalk) or Submarine parts (TechWiser). The first three clue words in order are Fin, Airlock and Ballast tanks.
- **Crossclimb #942:** Gold, Hold, Hole, Hose, Rose, Ruse, Rush. Hold, Hole, Hose, Rose and Ruse fill the middle; Gold and Rush are the two ends.

The chain with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| End | Gold | none | none |
| Step 1 | Hold | H for G | 1 |
| Step 2 | Hole | E for D | 4 |
| Step 3 | Hose | S for L | 3 |
| Step 4 | Rose | R for H | 1 |
| Step 5 | Ruse | U for O | 2 |
| Other end | Rush | H for E | 4 |

Gold and Rush together make the phrase "gold rush". One source lists the chain from Rush up to Gold, so which end sits on top is not stated here.

## What the three Pinpoint clue words do on a submarine

- **Fin:** a fixed or movable surface that steadies the boat and helps steer it.
- **Airlock:** a chamber that lets divers pass between the hull and the water.
- **Ballast tanks:** compartments that flood to dive and empty to surface.

The fourth and fifth Pinpoint clue words appeared on one page only and are left out. Queens, Tango, Zip and Mini Sudoku solutions are also left out: no two sources confirmed them as text. Crossclimb clue texts differed between sources, so the hints above are written from the words themselves.
