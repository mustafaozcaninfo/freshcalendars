**The Chicago Bulls open December 2026 at home against the New York Knicks on Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 7 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), on NBC and Peacock, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The NBA lists 12 Bulls games for the month, and a one-page printable of all of them is further down.

The source is the NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF from NBA Communications, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." Its times are in ET, with a local time for each home team; the table below gives CT first and ET second. Weekdays are our own calculation.

## Bulls game times and TV for every December game

Seven of the 12 games are at the United Center and five are on the road. "None listed" means the NBA's schedule shows no national network for that game.

The Dec. 1 game against the Knicks is the only one with a national network listed. The schedule does not include local broadcasts; the Bulls list those at nba.com/bulls/schedule.

## Is there a Bulls game between Dec. 4 and Dec. 11?

No. After the Dec. 3 game against Cleveland, the next Bulls game on the schedule is Sunday, Dec. 13, against Houston. The NBA's August PDF has no regular-season games for any team from Dec. 4 to Dec. 11.

That span holds the Emirates NBA Cup knockout rounds. NBA.com's Cup page, updated Nov. 7, 2026, lists the quarterfinals on Dec. 4-5, the semifinals on Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9 and the championship on Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. The Bulls are in East Group C and lost 125-111 to Boston on Oct. 30, with group games left against Atlanta on Nov. 13, Washington on Nov. 20 and at Charlotte on Nov. 25, so whether they play in the knockouts depends on those results. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) lists every round, and the [Bulls' November schedule](/news/chicago-bulls-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-nba-cup-printable/) covers the group games.

## Back-to-backs, road trip and the Heat games

The table gives three back-to-back pairs: Sunday-Monday, Dec. 13-14 (both at home), Friday-Saturday, Dec. 18-19 (at San Antonio, then at Dallas) and Tuesday-Wednesday, Dec. 29-30 (at Atlanta, then home against Boston). From Dec. 18 to Dec. 23 the Bulls play four straight games on the road, ending with two at Miami.

Miami is on the schedule three times in 10 days: at the United Center on Dec. 14, then in Miami on Dec. 21 and Dec. 23. Both road games start at 6:30 p.m. CT (7:30 p.m. ET). For the Heat's side of those games, see the [Miami Heat December 2026 schedule](/news/miami-heat-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-christmas-printable/).

## No Bulls game on Christmas Day

The schedule lists no Bulls game on Friday, Dec. 25, or on Thursday, Dec. 24. The Bulls play at Miami on Wednesday, Dec. 23, then at home against Detroit on Saturday, Dec. 26, at 7 p.m. CT (8 p.m. ET). The month ends at home against Boston on Wednesday, Dec. 30.
