**Pokémon GO's official site lists December 2026 Community Day for Saturday, Dec. 12, and Sunday, Dec. 13, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time on both days.** Saturday features Vulpix and Alolan Vulpix, Sunday features Zorua and Pawniard. The dates for Max Battle Day, Raid Day and Hatch Day are set too. This page puts every official December date in one table and adds a printable to tick off.

## Pokémon GO events in December 2026, by date

Weekdays are computed from the dates. Every row comes from pokemongo.com, read Nov. 23, 2026, and the site notes that "Upcoming events are subject to change."

| Date | Event | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Tue., Dec. 1 | Twilight Trails season ends | 10 a.m. local time |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | Max Battle Day | Time not yet announced |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | Community Day, day 1 | 2 to 5 p.m. local time |
| Sun., Dec. 13 | Community Day, day 2 | 2 to 5 p.m. local time |
| Sat., Dec. 19 | Raid Day | Time not yet announced |
| Sat., Dec. 26 | Hatch Day | Time not yet announced |

The lead-in event is Twilight Wishes, which runs Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 10 a.m. to Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, at 8 p.m. local time. The Twilight Trails season page gives its span as Sept. 8 at 10 a.m. to Dec. 1, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time. The next season's name and start are not yet announced, and so are any December holiday event, Wild Area or New Year's event.

## Which Pokémon are featured on Community Day, Dec. 12-13, 2026?

The two days have separate lineups.

| | Saturday, Dec. 12 | Sunday, Dec. 13 |
|---|---|---|
| Featured Pokémon | Vulpix, Alolan Vulpix, Grookey, Scorbunny, Lechonk, Tinkatink | Zorua, Pawniard, Sobble, Nickit, Frigibax |
| May also appear | Piplup | Gible and Deino |
| Time | 2 to 5 p.m. local time | 2 to 5 p.m. local time |

## What are the Community Day bonuses and ticket?

Pokémon GO says the bonuses are active from Saturday, Dec. 12, at 9 a.m. to Sunday, Dec. 13, at 9 p.m. local time, which is wider than the 2 to 5 p.m. event hours. They are:

- 2× experience points (XP), 2× Stardust and 2× Candy for catches.
- A doubled chance of Candy XL for Trainers at level 31 and above.
- Eggs hatch at half distance.
- Up to two Special Trades per day at 50% less Stardust.

A Kingambit evolution is available until 9 p.m. local time on Sunday, Dec. 13: use a Bisharp as your buddy and defeat a Dark- or Steel-type raid boss. A US$4.99 ticket (or the equivalent pricing tier in local currency) is offered.

## How do December 2026 Community Days compare with 2025?

In 2025 the weekend was Saturday and Sunday, Dec. 6-7, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. Saturday featured Karrablast, Shelmet, Vanillite, Sprigatito, Fuecoco and Pawmi, and Sunday featured Solosis, Flabébé, Pikipek, Jangmo-o, Rookidee and Quaxly.

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Community Day | Sat.-Sun., Dec. 6-7 | Sat.-Sun., Dec. 12-13 |
| Hours | 2 to 5 p.m. local time | 2 to 5 p.m. local time |
| Max Battle Day | Sun., Dec. 14 | Sat., Dec. 5 |
| Other day announced | Research Day, Sat., Dec. 20 | Raid Day, Sat., Dec. 19 |

The 2025 dates come from the official Community Day page and the Season 21 save-the-date. The 2026 weekend is six days later in the month, and Max Battle Day moves from a Sunday after Community Day to a Saturday before it.

## Weekly events: Spotlight Hour and Raid Hour

A June 2026 official post placed Spotlight Hour from 6 to 7 p.m. local time under GO Battle Thursdays, so on Thursday rather than Tuesday. Whether that schedule holds in December is not confirmed, and December's Spotlight Hour Pokémon are not yet announced. This page gives no Raid Hour time.
