## Where each Pips board starts on Nov. 16

Every board has at least one cell the rules pin down before you place a domino. Cells are named by row and column from the top left, so R1C1 is the top-left cell.

**Easy.** R4C1 is a one-cell region that must be less than 1, so it holds a 0. Only the 1-0 domino has a 0, and R3C1 is the only cell next to R4C1, so that domino is fixed first.

**Medium.** R4C4 is a one-cell region that must be less than 5, and its only neighbor is R4C3. That makes R4C3 and R4C4 one domino. R4C3 is also in a two-cell region that must total more than 10, so it holds at least a 5 and R4C4 holds a smaller value.

**Hard.** R6C3 must add up to 0. Its only neighbor is R7C3, and R7C3 must be less than 3, so one domino covers both: 2-0 or 0-0. That leaves R8C3 and R8C4 as a pair, and R8C4 must add up to 6.

## Monday's Pips hints, Nov. 16, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 6-1, 4-1, 1-0, 5-6, 1-3)**

- Hint 1: R2C3, R3C3 and R3C4 together add up to 3, so no 4, 5 or 6 can go in any of them.
- Hint 2: R2C2 and R3C2 must be equal. Only 1 and 6 appear on more than one half.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 5-2, 0-5, 2-4, 6-1, 1-1, 6-4, 6-5)**

- Hint 1: R1C2 and R1C3 add up to 10, so they hold a 4 and a 6, or two 5s.
- Hint 2: R1C1 is a one-cell region that must be more than 4, so it holds a 5 or a 6.

**Hard (sixteen dominoes on an eight-row board)**

- Hint 1: R1C1, R2C9 and R4C1 must each add up to 3. Six halves show a 3, two of them on the 3-3 domino.
- Hint 2: R3C7, R3C8, R4C7 and R5C7 must all show the same value. A 5 appears on three halves and a 6 on two, so neither can fill four cells.

## Reading the boards before you check

Compare the sums first. Easy has one sum, over three cells, so most of its board is settled by the equal pair and the two one-cell limits. Medium leans on less-than and more-than regions, which allow several values each, so expect a few moves to hold more than one fit until the dominoes run out. Hard has seven sums, three of them one-cell targets of 3. Hard has three doubles: 3-3, 4-4 and 0-0. Easy has none and Medium has one, 1-1.
