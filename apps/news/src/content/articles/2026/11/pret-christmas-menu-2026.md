**Pret A Manger's Christmas menu was on its United Kingdom (UK) website on 5 November 2026, with a new Christmas Hot Baguette and the Christmas Sandwich back, according to Pret's own Christmas page.** The page carries no date and Pret's media centre listed no 2026 Christmas release that day, so Pret has not published a launch date. In 2025, Pret said festive favourites were available from 4 November.

## What is the new Christmas Hot Baguette?

Pret describes it as "Christmas dinner, minus the carving": thick slices of British chicken with Port & orange cranberry sauce, herby pork stuffing and gooey, melty Grevé cheese. It is chicken, not turkey. The separate "Christmas dinner, in a baguette" has thick slices of British turkey with port and orange cranberry sauce and herby pork stuffing, plus peppery rocket, a dab of mayo and crispy onions in a white baguette.

## Is the Pret Christmas Sandwich back, and who gets the money?

Pret calls the Christmas Sandwich "iconic" and says every one sold supports The Pret Foundation's work to break the cycle of homelessness. The page gives no 2026 amount per sandwich. In 2025 the sandwich was the Christmas Lunch Sandwich and Pret said it would donate £1 from each one to the foundation.

## Pret Gingerbread Latte and festive drinks

The Gingerbread Latte is back. The Chocolate Orange Latte blends Pret's espresso with chocolate and orange flavours, and the Orange Hot Chocolate combines rich chocolate with zesty orange. Both are finished with a swirl of chocolate whipped cream. Pret's page does not say whether the two orange drinks are new.

## Pick your Pair calories and dietary flags

Pret labels Pick your Pair "new for Christmas" and lists four items. The table gives the figures from each product page. We did not read the offer's pairing rules or price, so check them in store or in the app.

| Pick your Pair item | kcal per serving | Vegetarian |
|---|---|---|
| Brie, Pistachio & Cranberry Half Baguette | 296 | Yes (not vegan) |
| Pret's Christmas Half Baguette | 352 | No |
| Pret's Christmas Half Sandwich | 275 | No |
| Christmas Ham Hock Side Soup | 121 | No |

The Christmas Ham Hock Side Soup is the lightest of the four at 121 kcal and the Christmas Half Baguette the highest at 352 kcal. None of the items we checked is marked vegan.
