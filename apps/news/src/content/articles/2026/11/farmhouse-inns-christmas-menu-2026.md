**Farmhouse Inns sells any two courses from its Turkey & Tinsel menu for £13.99 per person in 2026, according to the pub chain's own Christmas pages read on 23 November.** A third course is £3 extra, so three courses come to £16.99 (our sum). Christmas Day is Friday 25 December, and the chain publishes a £10 deposit but no price for it. Farmhouse Inns is a carvery pub chain, and its promoter line in the menu PDF gives Burton on Trent as its address.

## Farmhouse Inns festive offers and prices for 2026

Every price and date the Farmhouse Inns Christmas pages publish, in one table. Weekdays are our arithmetic from the dates.

| Offer | Date | Price | Deposit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turkey & Tinsel, 2 courses | Set by each pub | £13.99 per person | Not given |
| Turkey & Tinsel, 3 courses | Set by each pub | £16.99 (£13.99 + £3) | Not given |
| Breakfast with Santa | Sun. 6, Sat. 12, Sun. 13, Sat. 19, Sun. 20, Wed. 23 Dec | Adult £11.99, child £10.99 | £5 per adult and child |
| Breakfast with Santa, Christmas Eve | Thu. 24 Dec | Adult £12.99, child £11.99 | £5 per adult and child |
| Dinner with Santa | Mon. 21 and Tue. 22 Dec | Adult £12.99, child £11.99 | £5 per adult and child |
| Christmas Day | Fri. 25 Dec | Not published | £10 per adult and £10 per child |
| Christmas Day takeaway | Fri. 25 Dec | Two courses and a drink, £37.99 | Not given |
| Boxing Day breakfast buffet | Sat. 26 Dec | From £7.99 | Not given |

The chain's questions page says you will need to pay a deposit for any of its festive events and that all of its pubs offer different menus. Mark the dates on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and see [when Christmas Day falls](/when-is/christmas/).

## How do Farmhouse Inns Christmas Day bookings work in 2026?

The Christmas Day page says "For just one tasty price, you can choose any starter, main and dessert." The price is not on the page or in the PDF, so it is set by each pub and not published centrally. The menu is "Available 25th December 2026", pre-orders opened on 21 October 2026, and the page gives no sitting times.

| Date | What the page says | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Dec 2026 | "Payment in full will be required no later than 1st December 2026, and at the time of booking for any reservations made after this date." | Tue. |
| 11 Dec 2026 | Bookings made on or before this day settle menu choices and final payment by 11 December. A full refund if you cancel before it; deposits are non-refundable on or after it | Fri. |
| 18 Dec 2026 | Bookings made after 11 December settle choices and final payment by 18 December. "No further bookings will be accepted after December 18th 2026" | Fri. |

The 1 December and 11 December rules sit on the same page and do not match. The questions page says "please check the terms and conditions in your booking confirmation", so go by that document. The deposit is £10 per adult and £10 per child, so a family of two adults and two children pays £40 to book (our arithmetic).

## When are Farmhouse Inns Breakfast with Santa and Dinner with Santa?

Breakfast with Santa runs on seven dates in 2026. The page says 6 December is for special educational needs (SEN), "an SEN-friendly and accessible event". Deposits are due no later than 48 hours before, and cancelling more than 48 hours before earns a full deposit refund. For an event on Saturday 12 December, 48 hours earlier is Thursday 10 December at the same time of day. The page says "Not all of our pubs offer Breakfast with Santa."

Dinner with Santa runs on Monday 21 and Tuesday 22 December at £12.99 for adults and £11.99 for children, with a £5 deposit per adult and child. A family of two adults and two children pays £10.99 x 2 + £11.99 x 2 = £45.96 for Breakfast with Santa on a regular date and £49.96 on Christmas Eve (our arithmetic from the page's prices).

## How does Farmhouse Inns compare with Toby Carvery?

Both are carvery chains with a Breakfast with Santa. Toby Carvery lists Christmas Day dining "from £67.99" on its Christmas Day booking page, read on 21 November in our [Toby Carvery Christmas menu story](/news/toby-carvery-christmas-menu-2026/). Farmhouse Inns publishes no Christmas Day price, so the two cannot be compared on that. Breakfast with Santa can be.

| | Farmhouse Inns | Toby Carvery |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas Day | Price not published, £10 deposit | From £67.99 |
| Breakfast with Santa, regular date | Adult £11.99, child £10.99 | £12.99 |
| Breakfast with Santa, Christmas Eve | Adult £12.99, child £11.99 | £13.99 |
| Breakfast with Santa dates | 6, 12, 13, 19, 20, 23, 24 Dec | 5, 6, 12, 13 and 19 to 24 Dec |

Toby Carvery's page does not say whether its Breakfast with Santa price is for adults or children. Other pub chains are in the [Harvester Christmas menu story](/news/harvester-christmas-menu-2026/) and the [Wetherspoon Christmas menu story](/news/wetherspoon-christmas-menu-2026/).
