## How Strands works

The board is six letters wide and eight tall. Theme words are made by linking letters that touch, up, down, sideways or diagonally, and every letter is used exactly once. One special answer, the spangram, names the theme and stretches from one side of the board to the opposite side. Find three non-theme words and you earn a hint that highlights the letters of a theme word.

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: It is the sound people write for sleep.
- Hint 2: It is not a normal word, and it is made of one letter repeated.
- Hint 3: Eight letters, all the same, and the letter is the last in the alphabet.

## Theme word hints

There are seven theme words, all about resting or sleeping:

- **6 letters, starts with C:** a short sleep during the day.
- **4 letters, starts with D:** to sleep lightly or drift off.
- **6 letters, starts with D:** to be half asleep.
- **6 letters, starts with S:** a midday rest.
- **5 letters, starts with S:** the plain word for what this puzzle is about.
- **7 letters, starts with S:** a gentle word for sleep.
- **6 letters, starts with S:** a short, light sleep, or what you do with an alarm clock button.

## Solving tips

Start with the spangram. At eight letters it crosses the board from one side to the other; on today's board it begins at the top edge and reaches the bottom edge. The board has ten Z tiles: eight belong to the spangram, and the other two sit inside Doze and Snooze. Slumber is the longest theme word. Doze is the only four-letter theme word, so look for it once the long words are out of the way. If you get stuck, spell any ordinary words you can see to earn a hint.
