**HBO Max's home page lists its Basic With Ads plan at "$5.49 per month" for the first 12 months, valid "from November 23, 2026, through November 30, 2026," and Paramount+ offers any monthly plan at $2.99 a month for the first 2 months, ending 11/30/26.** Both pages were read again on Nov. 25, 2026, and neither shows a time of day for the end. Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is Monday, Nov. 30; see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/) and [when Cyber Monday falls](/when-is/cyber-monday/).

| [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/) | $5.49 a month for 12 months, Basic With Ads, plus taxes | Then-current price, now $10.99 a month plus taxes | New and returning customers on hbomax.com, U.S. only | Nov. 30, 2026 |
| [Apple TV](https://www.apple.com/apple-tv-plus/) | $6.99 a month for 6 months | Not on the page | Eligible new and qualified returning subscribers | Nov. 30, 2026, 8:00 p.m. PT (11 p.m. ET) |

Apple's page calls its deal a "Limited-time offer" and does not say Black Friday; it ends on the same day as the Black Friday deals. HBO Max says the offer is once per account and also runs through Apple, Google Play, Roku, Samsung and Xumo, and for new customers through Amazon Fire. Paramount+ is $2.99 only for the first 2 months.

## What do the HBO Max, Paramount+, Apple TV and Disney+ and Hulu offers cost?

The sums use the prices on each page, before tax.

| Offer | Cost at the offer price | Compared with |
|---|---|---|
| HBO Max Basic With Ads, 12 months | 12 x $5.49 = $65.88 | $131.88 at $10.99 a month for 12 months, $66.00 more |
| Paramount+ monthly plan, 2 months | 2 x $2.99 = $5.98 | Regular plan prices are not on the page |
| Apple TV, 6 months | 6 x $6.99 = $41.94 | Price after the 6 months is not on the page |
| Disney+ and Hulu bundle (ad-supported), 12 months | 12 x $4.99 = $59.88 | $155.88 at $12.99 a month for 12 months, $96.00 more |
| Disney+ and Hulu bundle Premium, 12 months | 12 x $14.99 = $179.88 | $263.88 at $21.99 a month for 12 months, $84.00 more |

HBO Max's $5.49 holds for 12 months, so the discount is the larger sum; Paramount+ and Apple TV give shorter stretches, and the Disney+ and Hulu bundle also runs 12 months. Paramount+ and HBO Max both renew without a reminder from this page, so cancel before the first full-price bill if you do not want to keep the plan.

## Are the 2026 deals better than Black Friday 2025?

Last year's HBO Max offer was lower. In 2025, TV Guide and Cord Cutters News reported an HBO Max deal at $2.99 a month for 12 months, which ended Dec. 1, 2025. That is 12 x $2.99 = $35.88, so the 2026 price of $5.49 costs $30.00 more over a year.

Disney+ ran a bundle deal in 2025, and its 2026 bundle has the same ad-supported price. Its 2025 page, headed "The 2025 Disney+ & Hulu Black Friday Deal Has Ended," lists "$4.99/month for 12 months," then "auto-renews at $12.99/month or then-current regular monthly price," ending "11:59 PM PST on 12/1/25." It was for the ad-supported bundle and for new and eligible returning subscribers. For 2026, Hulu's page lists the same $4.99 a month for 12 months, then $12.99, and ends at 11:59 PM PST on 11/30/26, one day earlier than the 2025 offer. Eligibility, the 12-month cost table and end times by U.S. time zone are in our [Hulu and Disney+ Black Friday deal story](/news/hulu-disney-plus-black-friday-2026-deal-4-99-bundle-ends-november-30/).
