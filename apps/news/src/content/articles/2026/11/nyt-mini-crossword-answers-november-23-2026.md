**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, is a 5-by-5 grid with 10 clues, and the official data credits Joel Fagliano.** Every hint opens on a tap. Black squares sit only in the top-left and bottom-right corners, so most entries run the full width or height.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *Instrument you might "slap"*: a low-pitched stringed instrument whose name also belongs to a fish; its first letter starts 1-Down and its second starts 2-Down.
- **5-Across** (5 letters), *São ___, Brazil*: a five-letter given name that follows São; its first letter starts 5-Down and its last letter is the second letter of 4-Down.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Newly elected senator El-Sayed*: the senator's first name; it starts with the second letter of 5-Down and ends with the third letter of 4-Down.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *"I don't know why it's doing that ..."*: an adjective meaning strange, what you mutter at a glitch you cannot explain; it starts with the third letter of 5-Down and ends with the last letter of 4-Down.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *Lazy A.I. content*: a four-letter word for sloppy filler made with no effort; it starts with the last letter of 5-Down.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Biblical city with language problems*: the Genesis city where the builders stopped understanding one another; it starts with the first letter of 1-Across.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *The "A" of A/V*: the five-letter word for sound in an audiovisual pair; it starts with the second letter of 1-Across.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Drink noisily*: a five-letter verb for sucking a drink up with a sound; it starts with the third letter of 1-Across and ends with the last letter of 8-Across.
- **4-Down** (4 letters), *Word after "Going once, going twice ..."*: the auctioneer's closing word, in the past tense; it starts with the last letter of 1-Across and ends with the last letter of 7-Across.
- **5-Down** (4 letters), *Mutt's "mitts"*: a four-letter plural noun for a dog's feet; it starts with the first letter of 5-Across and ends with the first letter of 8-Across.

## Clue types in the Nov. 23 Mini

Counted from the 10 clue texts in the saved data, each clue sorted once, in this order of priority:

| Clue type | Count | Clues |
|---|---|---|
| Fill-in-the-blank (a gap marked with a blank line) | 1 | 5-Across |
| Quotation (the clue is a quoted phrase or ends in a quoted lead-in) | 2 | 7-Across, 4-Down |
| Contains an abbreviation or initialism | 2 | 8-Across, 2-Down |
| Plain definition or name | 5 | 1-Across, 6-Across, 1-Down, 3-Down, 5-Down |

The least literal clue is 5-Down. The quotation marks around "mitts" are the signal: the setter is using a slang word for hands as a stand-in for something else, and the possessive "Mutt's" tells you whose. Swap the slang word for the plain body part it replaces, then move that part to the owner the clue names, and the plural four-letter answer follows.

The other clues are direct. The 2-Down clue gives you half of an abbreviation and asks for the word the letter stands for, and the 8-Across clue spells out A.I. with periods. The rules are on our [Mini Crossword page](/news/topic/nyt-mini-crossword/#how-it-works), and today's 23 open squares leave only the two corner black squares.
