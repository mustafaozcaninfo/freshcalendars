**The 2026 National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) women's volleyball national semifinals are Thursday, Dec. 17 and the national championship game is Sunday, Dec. 20 at the Alamodome in San Antonio, according to ESPN Press Room.** Below: the round-by-round dates computed from the NCAA's own formula, the Alamodome's listed times in four U.S. zones, and a one-page printable.

The selection show is Sunday, Nov. 29, at 6 p.m. ET on ESPN, according to ESPN Press Room, which says it unveils the full bracket. The first-serve times, the regional host sites and the seeds are not yet announced either. This story lists only what the NCAA, ESPN, the Alamodome and San Antonio Sports have published, and marks every computed day as computed.

## NCAA volleyball tournament 2026 round dates

The NCAA's bid specifications set a date formula for each round. The first and second rounds are played "First Thursday-Friday or Friday-Saturday following Thanksgiving," the regionals the "Weekend following First/Second Rounds (Friday-Saturday)," and the semifinals and finals on "Thursday and Saturday after regionals," with "2026-27 December 17-19 or 20" as the dates. Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026 (see [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/)). The table applies that formula; the days for the first and second rounds and the regionals are our computation, not an NCAA announcement.

| Round | Sites | Days | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| First and second rounds | 16 campus sites | Thu.-Fri., Dec. 3-4 or Fri.-Sat., Dec. 4-5 (computed) | NCAA bid specifications |
| Regionals | 4 sites | Fri.-Sat., Dec. 11-12 (computed) | NCAA bid specifications |
| National semifinals (the final four) | Alamodome, San Antonio | Thu., Dec. 17 | ESPN Press Room |
| National championship game | Alamodome, San Antonio | Sun., Dec. 20 | ESPN Press Room |

The exact days for each campus site come with the bracket, which ESPN says is unveiled on Nov. 29. Fans call the semifinal round the Final Four; the NCAA and ESPN call them the national semifinals.

## What time are the volleyball semifinals and final in San Antonio?

The Alamodome's event page lists "Dec 17 Time: 5:30PM" and "Dec 20 Time: 2:30PM." The page does not call these first-serve times, and the NCAA has not yet posted first-serve times, so treat them as the venue's listed event times. We converted them from Central Time (CT).

| Day | Alamodome listed event time (CT) | Eastern Time (ET) | Mountain Time (MT) | Pacific Time (PT) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 17, national semifinals | 5:30 p.m. | 6:30 p.m. | 4:30 p.m. | 3:30 p.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 20, national championship game | 2:30 p.m. | 3:30 p.m. | 1:30 p.m. | 12:30 p.m. |

## Where is the 2026 NCAA volleyball championship and how do tickets work?

ESPN Press Room says "This season's championship will take place at the San Antonio Alamodome." San Antonio Sports says the event is hosted by the University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA) and San Antonio Sports, with the action on December 17 and 20. The Alamodome says tickets are on sale and are available through ncaatickets.com or ticketmaster.com. Its box office hours are to be announced.

## Where can I watch the NCAA volleyball tournament in 2026?

ESPN Press Room says "The 2026 season will conclude with the 2026 NCAA Women's Volleyball Tournament, entirely on ESPN." A later ESPN Press Room release, dated Nov. 10, 2026, says ABC will air the NCAA Division I Women's Volleyball Championship and the regional finals. The channel for the other matches is not named, so check ESPN's listings once the bracket is out on Nov. 29.

## What happened in the 2025 NCAA volleyball final?

In 2025, Texas A&M beat Kentucky 3-0 in the final on Sunday, Dec. 21, 2025, at T-Mobile Center in Kansas City, Missouri, according to Sports Illustrated (SI) and the Bryan, Texas, television station (KBTX), which both reported the result that night. SI headlined it Texas A&M's first national title in program history. That was last year's event; the 2026 final is in San Antonio.
