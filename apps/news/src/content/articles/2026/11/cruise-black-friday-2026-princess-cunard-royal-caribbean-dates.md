**Princess Cruises and Cunard both take Black Friday cruise bookings through Monday, Dec. 7, 2026, while Virgin Voyages' Black Friday Sale ends Thursday, Dec. 3, according to each line's own sale page or release.** Royal Caribbean, Celebrity and Norwegian Cruise Line show Black Friday offers without a booking window. The table lists each line's window, offer and promo code, and a printable carries the same rows.

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026; see [when is Black Friday](/when-is/black-friday/). Weekdays below are computed. Each cruise line name links to the page the row came from.

Offers vary by sailing. Princess says "Instant savings per stateroom vary by cruise length and stateroom type," and Cunard says "Onboard credit varies by voyage length and stateroom category booked."

## Which cruise Black Friday sale ends first?

Three dates stand out, in order.

1. **Tuesday, Dec. 1:** Royal Caribbean lists Black Friday (Nov. 27), Cyber Monday (Nov. 30) and Travel Tuesday (Dec. 1) on its page and in an Oct. 8 guide, but gives no end date for its offers. Dec. 1 is the last day it names, not a confirmed deadline.
2. **Thursday, Dec. 3:** Virgin Voyages' Black Friday Sale. Its page says "Offer ends December 3, 2026."
3. **Monday, Dec. 7:** Princess and Cunard. Both windows run past Cyber Monday, Nov. 30, which the [Cyber Monday 2026 guide](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/) covers for retailers.

## What is Princess Cruises' Trade Black for Blue Sale?

Princess's offer page, titled "Trade Black for Blue Sale - Up to 50% Off," says "Offers valid on bookings made 11/06/2026 - 12/07/2026" and says "enjoy up to $400 instant savings, up to 50% off, 50% off your deposit." Its terms read "Offer Expiration: Monday, December 7, 2026" and "Promo codes: KQS | NDS," and state no end time or time zone. The site menu banner reads "Up to $800 instant savings + up to 50% + free 3rd & 4th guests," which differs from the $400 on the offer page; the table uses the offer page.

Cruise Industry News reported on Nov. 9, 2026 a past-guest sale for Nov. 13 to 23, 2026, which has ended. That was not on the Princess page we read, so it is reported only.

A Princess Australia page (princess.com/en-au) says "This Offer Has Expired." That is the Australian market's Blue Friday, not the U.S. sale above.

### How does Princess compare with Blue Friday 2025?

In 2025, Princess said in a Nov. 12 release that its first Blue Friday offer ran through Monday, Nov. 24, 2025, with instant savings up to $250. A second phase from Nov. 25 to Dec. 2, 2025 offered up to $800 and ended "at 11:59 p.m. PST on December 2, 2025," meaning Pacific Standard Time (PST). The 2026 page lists one window, Nov. 6 to Dec. 7, and instant savings of up to $400.

## What is Cunard's Black Friday Sales Event?

Cunard announced the sale on Nov. 3, 2026 in a PR Newswire release datelined Santa Clarita, Calif. It covers "new bookings made between November 10 and December 7, 2026," on 161 voyages worldwide and select sailings across all four Cunard Queens. The release says "Reference promotion code RD1." Cunard calls it its "best sale of the year"; that is the company's wording. The cunard.com pages returned an access error when we checked, so the release is the source.

## Does Royal Caribbean have a Black Friday 2026 sale?

Its page shows deals but has not posted the booking window. It says "Black Friday is November 27th, 2026," "Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026" and "Travel Tuesday is December 1, 2026," and advertises "Up to $900 off," a Cyber Bonus of up to $100 onboard credit, and "3rd and 4th guests sail free." It also mentions special rates for Florida residents and others near select departure ports. It lists no promo code. Dates for Royal Caribbean found elsewhere are not from the line, so they are left out here.

## What are Virgin, Celebrity and Norwegian offering?

Virgin Voyages' Black Friday Sale offers "up to $800 in free drinks," depending on cabin category and voyage length, plus a second Sailor at 80% off on voyages with travel dates from Nov. 20, 2026 to Oct. 28, 2028 on Scarlet Lady, Valiant Lady, Brilliant Lady and Resilient Lady. Its page says "Offer ends December 3, 2026" and does not post a start date.

Celebrity's page, headed "Black Friday Kickoff," offers 75% off the second guest's cruise fare, up to $900 off per stateroom and an additional $100 off Europe, Caribbean and Alaska cruises, and gives no dates. Norwegian's page shows 50%-off-all-cruises banners without dates. Cruise Industry News reported on Nov. 8, 2026 that Norwegian's Black Friday sale "kicked off" on Nov. 5 with 50% off all cruises plus Free at Sea; the end date is not confirmed.

## Do Carnival, Holland America and Disney have a Black Friday sale?

Carnival's deals page showed no 2026 Black Friday sale on Nov. 16, and its page could not be read on Nov. 25. Holland America's deals page now lists "Black Friday: Up to 30% off, free crew appreciation + more" and states no end date in its text. Disney Cruise Line's page could not be reached, so its plans are not confirmed.
