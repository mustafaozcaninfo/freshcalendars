**Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026 on LinkedIn is Pinpoint #929 and Crossclimb #929, confirmed by Nerdschalk and Try Hard Guides, with the answers kept in the spoiler section.** Hints come first, in steps.

## Pinpoint and Crossclimb nudges for Sunday, Nov. 15

- **Pinpoint #929, first nudge:** the five clues are not one kind of thing; they share a feature that one part of each can have.
- **Pinpoint #929, second nudge:** that part is a mechanism that keeps something shut, and a key or a code opens it.
- **Pinpoint #929, third nudge:** the category starts with "Things with" and ends in a one-word mechanism. The last two clues stretch the meaning furthest.
- **Crossclimb #929, the locked ends:** both are five-letter music words: one names a set of lowered notes, the other the symbol that does the reverse.
- **Crossclimb #929, rung 1:** the slender strips that tilt on a window covering.
- **Crossclimb #929, rung 2:** a sports fan's numbers, in their short, plural form.
- **Crossclimb #929, rung 3:** to put your view into plain words, the way a lawyer would in court.
- **Crossclimb #929, rungs 4 and 5:** a long, unblinking look, then splitting something so each person gets a part.

## Reading the Crossclimb #929 ladder without the words

Seven words of five letters, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 5. No word appears in this section.

| Move | Letter position that changes |
|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 1 |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 2 |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 5 |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 4 |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 2 |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 5 |

Positions 2 and 5 each change twice, positions 1 and 4 once each, and position 3 never. Only the first move touches the first letter, so the other six words open with the same letter. Moves 3 and 6 both work on the last letter.

## Starting points for Queens #929, Tango #769, Zip #608 and Mini Sudoku #461

**Queens #929.** Look for a color region that fits inside a single row or column; its crown rules out the rest of that line.

**Tango #769.** Two matching symbols side by side force the cell on each end to be the other symbol.

**Zip #608.** Corners and dead ends have only one way in, so draw those stretches of the path first.

**Mini Sudoku #461.** Pick the digit that appears most often so far and place it in each box where only one cell is left for it.
