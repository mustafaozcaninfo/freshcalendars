**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Joel Fagliano.** Two black squares sit in the top-left corner and two in the bottom-right, so every entry runs three to five letters. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *Preschoolers sing them*: the alphabet song, written as three letters plus a plural S.
- **5-Across** (4 letters), *Give applause*: bring your palms together, once.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Constantly complaining*: an adjective for a child who moans about everything; it ends in Y.
- **7-Across** (4 letters), *Remote button with a house icon*: the same word as the place you live.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *Bad comment to hear from your barber*: the word people say right after a small mistake; it starts with a double O.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Sneeze sound*: how a sneeze is spelled in comics, starting with A.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Goodyear aircraft*: the airship that floats over stadiums, not a plane.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Some decorations on Christmas trees*: the striped hooked candies, plural.
- **4-Down** (3 letters), *Undercover agent*: a secret operative in a three-letter word.
- **6-Down** (3 letters), *"Pinball Wizard" band, with "The"*: the British rock group whose name is also a question word.

## Solving tips

Start with 4-Down and 6-Down, the only three-letter answers. 1-Across and 1-Down share their first square, and 6-Across crosses all five Down answers, so filling it checks the whole middle row at once. 7-Across and 8-Across, on the two bottom rows, each cross four Down answers, so save them for last.
