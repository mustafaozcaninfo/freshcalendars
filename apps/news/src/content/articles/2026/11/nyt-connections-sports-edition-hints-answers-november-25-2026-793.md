This board leans on the hardwood: a fan of that sport will see court words almost everywhere. The rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works). ## Wednesday's hints for #793

Sort the tiles by what a referee, a recruiter or a phrase would do with them.

- **Hint 1 (yellow):** Each tile is something an official can call against a player.
- **Hint 2 (yellow):** Two of the four tiles are two words long.
- **Hint 1 (green):** The tiles are lines you would read in a player's profile.
- **Hint 2 (green):** One tile is two words, and the other three are single words.
- **Hint 1 (blue):** One short word you might say while breathing in goes before each tile.
- **Hint 2 (blue):** The shortest tile has three letters, and two tiles share a length of six.
- **Hint 1 (purple):** One five-letter word that means ahead goes before each tile.
- **Hint 2 (purple):** One tile is a number, and no tile runs past six letters.

## Why a basketball fan grabs the wrong tiles

The yellow group has four spots, yet the board offers more than four ways to hear basketball. A tile can name a player's role, a famous pair of syllables or a spot in a team's building, and each one pulls the same way. The test is simple: would a referee blow a whistle for it? If the tile describes a rule broken on the court, it can join the yellow group; if it only sounds like the sport, keep looking for a phrase or a profile line that holds it.

Three tiles deserve a second look before you lock anything in. One reads as a name that a court fan knows, one reads as where a player lines up, and one reads as the people who run a club. None of them is a foul. Try the opening-word hints on each and see which group takes it. A [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) keeps the streak tidy.

## Wednesday's answers, #793, Nov. 25, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it. Beebom and Nerdschalk print the same four groups and sixteen entries, and the group names below are as those sites print them.

- **Yellow, Basketball Violations:** Traveling, Double Dribble, Backcourt, Shot Clock
- **Green, Bio Info for a College Athlete:** Hometown, High School, Class, Position
- **Blue, Air ___:** Bud, Jordan, Force, Hockey
- **Purple, Front ___:** Office, Row, Runner, Seven

## What each entry means

- **Traveling, Double Dribble, Backcourt, Shot Clock:** each is a basketball violation, per the published group title. We add no rule details beyond that.
- **Hometown, High School, Class, Position:** each is a piece of biography a school or team lists for a college athlete, per the published group title.
- **Bud, Jordan, Force, Hockey:** each word follows Air to make a phrase: Air Bud, Air Jordan, Air Force and Air Hockey. We say nothing more about any of them.
- **Office, Row, Runner, Seven:** each word follows Front to make a phrase: Front Office, Front Row, Front Runner and Front Seven.
