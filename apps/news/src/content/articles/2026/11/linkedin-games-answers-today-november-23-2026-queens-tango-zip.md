## Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints for Monday, Nov. 23

- **Pinpoint #937, first hint:** the five clue words are all living things, and each one is a different kind.
- **Pinpoint #937, second hint:** the first two clue words each have a second life as an everyday word that is not a creature at all; do not let them steer you.
- **Pinpoint #937, third hint:** the last two clue words are the easy ones, and they belong to the same category as the first three.
- **Crossclimb #937, the ladder:** seven words of four letters, linked by six single-letter swaps.
- **Crossclimb #937, the locked ends:** the top and bottom words are a pair that you can act out with one part of your body.
- **Crossclimb #937, the first move:** the first swap changes the first letter, and the second swap changes the last.

## How many Pinpoint #937 clues to open

Pinpoint reveals its clue words one at a time, and today the first two are the weakest. Both of them can be read in a second, non-living sense, so a guess made after two clues may point at the wrong field. Opening a third clue is a fair price here: it is the first one that settles which kind of thing the list is about, and the fourth and fifth then confirm it. That is our reading of the clue words, not a LinkedIn rule.

## Where the Crossclimb #937 letters move

Six moves, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word of the ladder appears here.

| Position | Times it changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 1 |

The moves run through positions 1, 4, 3, 1, 3, 1. Position 2 never moves, so every rung shares the same second letter with its neighbors. The last four moves swap only the first and third letters, which gives a way to check a middle guess: if two neighbors differ in more than one position, one of them is misplaced.

## Starting points for Queens #937, Tango #777, Zip #616 and Mini Sudoku #469

**Queens #937.** Look for a region that sits inside a single row or column; its crown rules that line out for every other region.

**Tango #777.** Check pairs of identical neighbors first: the cells on both sides of a pair must hold the opposite symbol.

**Zip #616.** Trace the path between the two numbers that are closest together on the grid, then extend outward from there.

**Mini Sudoku #469.** Start in the 2x3 box with the most clues and ask which digit has only one cell left to go.

## Monday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 23, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #937:** Types of birds. The five clue words in order are Rail, Swift, Quail, Owl and Eagle.
- **Crossclimb #937:** Hand, Land, Lane, Lake, Sake, Save, Wave. Land, Lane, Lake, Sake and Save fill the middle in that order; Hand and Wave are the locked ends. One source notes the order may run either way.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top row | Hand | none | none |
| Rung 1 | Land | L for H | 1 |
| Rung 2 | Lane | E for D | 4 |
| Rung 3 | Lake | K for N | 3 |
| Rung 4 | Sake | S for L | 1 |
| Rung 5 | Save | V for K | 3 |
| Bottom row | Wave | W for S | 1 |

Hand and Wave are a gesture: a hand wave. They differ in positions 1, 3 and 4, so no single swap joins them, and that is why they are the last rows to fill. Rail and Swift are the two Pinpoint traps: a rail is also a track for trains and Swift is also a word for fast, but all five clue words are birds.
