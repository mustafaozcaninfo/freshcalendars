**Northwest Independent School District (ISD) students have no school Nov. 23-27, 2026, Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 5, 2027, and March 15-22, 2027**, by the district's 2026-2027 Instructional Calendar at nisdtx.org. This is Northwest ISD in Justin, Texas, in the Fort Worth area, not Northside ISD in San Antonio, which has its [own calendar story](/news/northside-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/). The last day of school is Friday, May 21, 2027.

The district PDF says "*Calendar is subject to change." nisdtx.org announced on Jan. 13, 2026 that the Board of Trustees approved the calendars for 2026-2027 and 2027-2028; the post gives no approval date. Every date below comes from the district PDF.

## Northwest ISD breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates. Return days are our reading, since the calendar marks no closure on them.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving week | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | 12 | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 |
| Spring break | Mon., March 15 to Fri., March 19, 2027, plus Mon., March 22 | 6 | Tue., March 23 |

Plan the week on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html). For neighbors, see the [Keller ISD calendar](/news/keller-isd-calendar-2026-27/) and the [Fort Worth ISD calendar](/news/fort-worth-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/).

## Which Northwest ISD days are off for students?

Every student holiday on the calendar. The PDF legend says "Students do not report to school" on student holidays.

| Date | Weekday | What the district lists |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day student-staff holiday |
| Oct. 9, 2026 | Friday | Student holiday, teacher flex |
| Oct. 12, 2026 | Monday | Student holiday, teacher planning |
| Nov. 2, 2026 | Monday | Student-staff holiday |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Student holiday, teacher professional learning |
| Nov. 23-24, 2026 | Monday-Tuesday | Student holiday, teacher flex |
| Nov. 25-27, 2026 | Wednesday-Friday | Student-staff holiday |
| Dec. 21-31, 2026 | Monday-Thursday | Student-staff holiday |
| Jan. 1, 2027 | Friday | Student-staff holiday |
| Jan. 4-5, 2027 | Monday-Tuesday | Student holiday, teacher professional learning |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Student-staff holiday |
| Feb. 12, 2027 | Friday | Student-staff holiday |
| Feb. 15, 2027 | Monday | Student holiday, teacher planning |
| March 5, 2027 | Friday | Student holiday, teacher professional learning |
| March 15-19, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Spring break |
| March 22, 2027 | Monday | Student holiday, teacher planning |
| March 26, 2027 | Friday | Student and staff holiday |
| April 23, 2027 | Friday | Student holiday, teacher flex |

That is 34 weekdays off: 1 in September, 2 in October, 2 in early November, 5 for Thanksgiving week, 12 for winter break, 1 in January after the break, 2 in February, 1 on March 5, 6 for spring break and the Monday after, 1 on March 26 and 1 on April 23. The PDF does not name Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day or Good Friday; by date, Jan. 18 is the Martin Luther King Jr. Day Monday, Feb. 15 is the Presidents Day Monday and March 26 is the Friday before Easter, March 28, 2027 (our reading).

## When do Northwest ISD students return after winter break?

Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027. The district lists Dec. 21-31 and Jan. 1 as student-staff holidays and Jan. 4-5 as student holidays with teacher professional learning. It marks Jan. 6 as the beginning of the third quarter.

## Which long weekends does Northwest ISD have this year?

Four stretches come from the calendar's own dates.

| Stretch | Days off | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 9 to Mon., Oct. 12, 2026 | 4 days | Two student holidays |
| Sat., Oct. 31 to Tue., Nov. 3, 2026 | 4 days | Student-staff holiday, then a student holiday |
| Fri., Feb. 12 to Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | 4 days | Student-staff holiday, then a student holiday |
| Fri., March 26 to Sun., March 28, 2027 | 3 days | Student and staff holiday |

## Does Northwest ISD have early release days?

The calendar marks two: Friday, Dec. 18, 2026, the end of the second quarter, and Friday, May 21, 2027, the last day of school. The district lists these early release end times:

| Campus type | Early release end time |
|---|---|
| Hicks Early Childhood Center | 12:10 p.m. |
| Elementary school | 11:45 a.m. |
| Middle school | 12:55 p.m. |
| High school | 12:30 p.m. |
| Steele Early College High School | 12:20 p.m. |
| Special Programs Center | 12:30 p.m. |

## When does the Northwest ISD school year start and end?

Students start Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026, after staff professional learning Aug. 3-11. The last day of school is Friday, May 21, 2027, the end of the fourth quarter. The calendar lists State of Texas Assessments of Academic Readiness (STAAR) testing for Dec. 1-11, 2026 and April 6-30, 2027. It does not list graduation dates. District offices are closed May 28 and 31, after the last day.
