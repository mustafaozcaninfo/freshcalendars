**Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen, Inc. says its 2026 Cajun-Style Turkey is pickup only at participating restaurants, and its turkey page now says "Digital ordering is closed."** Online ordering ran from Monday, Oct. 19 to Thursday, Nov. 19, and online order pickup began Oct. 23. Popeyes' questions page says "Popeyes is not offering Cajun-Style Turkey delivery or shipping in 2026." Popeyes describes the turkey as pre-cooked, so the job at home is to thaw, heat and serve; sides are not included.

The turkey page says you can still "Stop in and order at the counter" at participating restaurants, and the questions page adds that availability varies by restaurant, so call yours before you drive. We added a pickup-to-table timeline for Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, computed from Popeyes' own thawing times.

## Popeyes Cajun turkey 2026 order and pickup dates

| Step | Date | What Popeyes says |
|---|---|---|
| Online ordering starts | Monday, Oct. 19 | "ordering begins October 19, 2026" |
| Online pickup starts | Friday, Oct. 23 | "Pickup for app and website turkey pre-orders begins October 23, 2026." |
| Online ordering ends | Thursday, Nov. 19 | "until Thursday, November 19, 2026" |
| Counter purchase | Participating restaurants, availability varies | "Digital ordering is closed, but you can still get your Cajun-Style Turkey at participating Popeyes restaurants." |
| Delivery and shipping | Not offered | "Popeyes is not offering Cajun-Style Turkey delivery or shipping in 2026." |

## How do you order a Popeyes turkey?

Digital ordering closed on Nov. 19, so the route now is a participating restaurant. Popeyes' turkey page links to its [restaurant locator](https://www.popeyes.com/store-locator), and its questions page says to contact your local restaurant about remaining in-restaurant availability; an in-restaurant reservation is paid for at pickup. If you ordered online before Nov. 19, you can still pick up on the date in your order. Popeyes also lists Kroger-family stores in select markets across 33 states as sellers, with grocery prices expected to range from $59.99 to $69.99. The page does not state a weight or quantity limits.

## Does Popeyes ship turkey in 2026?

No. Pickup is the only option this year. In 2025, FOX 11 Los Angeles reported that the 11 to 13-pound turkeys started at $54.99 for in-restaurant pickup and $99 for online delivery while supplies lasted. The 2026 page lists no delivery price because it offers no delivery.

## How much does a Popeyes turkey cost in 2026?

Popeyes' page lists no price and its questions page says prices vary by restaurant and purchase method. Sporked reported on Oct. 20, 2026 that the turkey "costs $59.99 to pickup in-store," and Hip2Save reported on Nov. 4, 2026 a pre-cooked turkey at a local restaurant for $59.99. Prices are set by participating restaurants, so confirm yours when you order.

## Popeyes turkey thaw and heat timeline for Thanksgiving

Popeyes' thawing page says: "Thaw for 72 hours in the refrigerator." Counting back 72 hours from Thursday, Nov. 26, the thaw starts Monday, Nov. 23. The heating steps add about 2 hours (1½ hours covered plus 30 minutes uncovered) and a 20-minute rest. The times below are our computation from Popeyes' figures, not a Popeyes schedule.

| Day | Step | Popeyes' instruction |
|---|---|---|
| Monday, Nov. 23 | Move the turkey to the refrigerator | "Thaw for 72 hours in the refrigerator." |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Preheat the oven | "Preheat a conventional oven to 375˚F." |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Heat covered, about 1½ hours | "Cover tightly with aluminum foil and heat on the lower rack for 1½ hours." |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Heat uncovered, 30 minutes or more | Remove the foil and keep heating uncovered for 30 minutes and until the internal temperature reaches 140˚F |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Rest, then carve | "Let the turkey rest 20 minutes before carving." |

Popeyes also lists a cold-water thaw: keep the turkey completely submerged and continuously under running water for the whole thaw, with water below 70°F. For a turkey that is thawed early, the page says to heat and eat within 3 days of thawing and to refrigerate leftovers promptly and eat them within 3 days.
