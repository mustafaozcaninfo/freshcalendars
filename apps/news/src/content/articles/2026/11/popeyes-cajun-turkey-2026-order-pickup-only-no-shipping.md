**Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen, Inc. says its 2026 Cajun-Style Turkey is pickup only at participating restaurants, with ordering open since Oct. 19 and pickup since Oct. 23.** The company's turkey page says "No delivery or shipping" and "Popeyes is not offering turkey delivery or shipping this year." Popeyes describes the turkey as pre-cooked, so the job at home is to thaw, heat and serve; sides are not included.

The page is undated and does not give a last pickup day, so the dates below are the ones it states: "Ordering starts October 19" and "Digital order pickup starts October 23 at participating locations." We added a pickup-to-table timeline for Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, computed from Popeyes' own thawing times.

## Popeyes Cajun turkey 2026 order and pickup dates

| Step | Date | What Popeyes says |
|---|---|---|
| Ordering starts | Monday, Oct. 19 | "Ordering starts October 19." |
| Pickup starts | Friday, Oct. 23 | "Online pre-orders are available for restaurant pickup starting October 23." |
| Where | Participating restaurants | "Available for pickup at participating Popeyes restaurants." |
| Delivery and shipping | Not offered | "No delivery or shipping." |
| Last day to order or pick up | Not announced | Not on the page as of Nov. 17, 2026 |

## How do you order a Popeyes turkey?

Popeyes' page links to a [pre-order menu](https://www.popeyes.com/menu/item/item_40655?isPreorder=true) and to its [restaurant locator](https://www.popeyes.com/store-locator), and says you can check on or manage a pre-order for pickup. Because only participating restaurants take part, the locator is the place to confirm yours does. The page does not state a weight or quantity limits.

## Does Popeyes ship turkey in 2026?

No. Pickup is the only option this year. In 2025, FOX 11 Los Angeles reported that the 11 to 13-pound turkeys started at $54.99 for in-restaurant pickup and $99 for online delivery while supplies lasted. The 2026 page lists no delivery price because it offers no delivery.

## How much does a Popeyes turkey cost in 2026?

Popeyes' page lists no price. Sporked reported on Oct. 20, 2026 that the turkey "costs $59.99 to pickup in-store," and Hip2Save reported on Nov. 4, 2026 a pre-cooked turkey at a local restaurant for $59.99. Prices are set by participating restaurants, so confirm yours when you order.

| Year | Pickup price reported | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Starting at $54.99 (11 to 13 lb) | FOX 11 Los Angeles, Oct. 15, 2025 |
| 2026 | $59.99 | Sporked, Oct. 20, 2026; Hip2Save, Nov. 4, 2026 |

## Popeyes turkey thaw and heat timeline for Thanksgiving

Popeyes' thawing page says: "Thaw for 72 hours in the refrigerator." Counting back 72 hours from Thursday, Nov. 26, the thaw starts Monday, Nov. 23. The heating steps add about 2 hours (1½ hours covered plus 30 minutes uncovered) and a 20-minute rest. The times below are our computation from Popeyes' figures, not a Popeyes schedule.

| Day | Step | Popeyes' instruction |
|---|---|---|
| Monday, Nov. 23 | Move the turkey to the refrigerator | "Thaw for 72 hours in the refrigerator." |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Preheat the oven | "Preheat a conventional oven to 375˚F." |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Heat covered, about 1½ hours | "Cover tightly with aluminum foil and heat on the lower rack for 1½ hours." |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Heat uncovered, 30 minutes or more | Remove the foil and keep heating uncovered for 30 minutes and until the internal temperature reaches 140˚F |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Rest, then carve | "Let the turkey rest 20 minutes before carving." |

Popeyes also lists a cold-water thaw: keep the turkey completely submerged and continuously under running water for the whole thaw, with water below 70°F. For a turkey that is thawed early, the page says to heat and eat within 3 days of thawing and to refrigerate leftovers promptly and eat them within 3 days.
