Side 3 closes nearly half the list (531 of 1,101 words, with the S alone ending 441), while side 4 opens plenty of words and ends hardly any. Side 1 begins the fewest.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' own solution is two words, four fewer than par.
2. The pair is 15 letters long, tied with three other routes for the shortest total, and one word is far longer than the other. The long one ties for the longest word in the list.
3. The first word starts on the T/N/Y side and ends on the A/I/U side; the second word starts on that same last letter.
4. The first word holds the T and the B; the second holds the X and the Y.
5. The first word starts with T and ends in A. The second starts with A and ends in S.
