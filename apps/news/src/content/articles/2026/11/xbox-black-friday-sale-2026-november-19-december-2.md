**The Xbox Black Friday sale launches Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, at 9 p.m. Pacific Time and runs through Wednesday, Dec. 2, according to Xbox Wire's Nov. 16 post by Joe Skrebels.** The post labels the start "9 PM PST," which is midnight Eastern Time (ET) on Friday, Nov. 20. The table below converts it for every US zone and Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), and a free printable wish list holds the three dates.

Xbox says "our Black Friday Deals kick off on November 19 and run through December 2" and to "come back and check for additional offers starting on November 23." The post gives no end time and no Cyber Monday offer. Discounts in it are US-only.

## What time does the Xbox Black Friday sale start in each time zone?

Pacific Standard Time (UTC-8) is in effect, so ET is three hours ahead of Pacific Time (PT). Conversions to ET, Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT) and GMT are ours; the post gives only the Pacific time.

| Zone | Sale starts |
|---|---|
| PT | Thu., Nov. 19, 9 p.m. (the post's time) |
| MT | Thu., Nov. 19, 10 p.m. |
| CT | Thu., Nov. 19, 11 p.m. |
| ET | Fri., Nov. 20, 12 a.m. (midnight) |
| GMT | Fri., Nov. 20, 5 a.m. |

For East Coast shoppers the sale opens at midnight, so the first full day is Friday, Nov. 20. Black Friday is Nov. 27. Our [when is Black Friday](/when-is/black-friday/) page has the retail date.

| Date | Day | What happens | Source wording |

The sale spans 14 days, counting both ends. From Wednesday, Nov. 18, the start is 1 day away and the Nov. 23 offers are 5 days away.

## Which Xbox games are on sale for Black Friday 2026?

The [Xbox Wire post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/16/xbox-black-friday-sale-2026/) lists these game deals. The sale window is Nov. 19 through Dec. 2; the post does not give a separate end date per game.

The post gives discounts as percentages and states no prices here, so check each price on the Microsoft Store. Console prices for Xbox Series X|S and headset prices are not announced in the post.

## Are there Xbox controller and accessory deals?

Xbox offers free engraving and up to 15% off all controller purchases made with Xbox Design Lab, from Nov. 19 through Dec. 2. It also lists savings on select Xbox wireless accessories, without prices.

## Is there an Xbox Game Pass Black Friday offer in 2026?

Yes, for new members only, and the post gives no price. It says the "subscription continues automatically at regular monthly price unless cancelled" and that Game Pass Ultimate includes 15 hours of cloud playtime per month.

A second offer pairs hardware: buy an eligible Samsung TV and get an Xbox Nano Controller plus two months of Game Pass Ultimate for new members, available through Jan. 17 per the post.

## How does the 2026 Xbox sale compare with 2025?

In 2025, Xbox Wire's Nov. 20 post said "these offers kick off today and run through December 3." Its Game Pass offer was "14 days for $1; then $14.99/mo. unless cancelled," for new subscribers only. The 2026 post gives no Game Pass price.

| Item | 2025 (Xbox Wire, Nov. 20, 2025) | 2026 (Xbox Wire, Nov. 16, 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Thu., Nov. 20 | Thu., Nov. 19, 9 p.m. PT |
| End | Dec. 3 | Dec. 2 |
