**International Day for Tolerance is Monday, Nov. 16, 2026,** according to the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO), and it falls on Nov. 16 every year. UNESCO's page ties the day to a 1996 UN General Assembly resolution and a 1995 declaration. Below are the countdown, the two origin dates, the weekday through 2031 and a free one-page activity sheet.

## Why is International Day for Tolerance on Nov. 16?

The date has two roots on UNESCO's page for the day. UNESCO's Member States adopted a Declaration of Principles on Tolerance on Nov. 16, 1995. A year later, in 1996, the UN General Assembly adopted Resolution 51/95, which proclaimed Nov. 16 as International Day for Tolerance.

| Year | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1995 | UNESCO's Member States adopted the Declaration of Principles on Tolerance on Nov. 16 | UNESCO |
| 1996 | The UN General Assembly adopted Resolution 51/95 proclaiming Nov. 16 as the day | UNESCO |

The UN's own observances page lists the day as repeating every year on Nov. 16. It does not give the resolution or the 1995 declaration, so those two facts rest on UNESCO's page.

## What is the UNESCO-Madanjeet Singh Prize?

UNESCO says the prize rewards significant activities in the scientific, artistic, cultural or communication fields. The UN observances page lists a "2026 award ceremony of UNESCO-Madanjeet Singh Prize for the Promotion of Tolerance and Non-Violence" but gives no date for it, so the ceremony is not on the printable.

## Is there a 2026 theme?

UNESCO's page lists none, and neither page names a 2026 campaign slogan. This story will not invent one; the date and the origins are the confirmed facts.

## How do classrooms and groups mark the day?

The sources we checked describe activities, not a requirement for schools. These prompts are our own and carry no UNESCO or UN endorsement:

- Ask students to describe a time someone listened to a view different from theirs.
- Have each person write one sentence on what tolerance looks like in their class, team or workplace.
- Read UNESCO's page for the day aloud and ask what questions it raises.

In the UK, [Anti-Bullying Week 2026](/news/anti-bullying-week-2026-uk-november-16-20-odd-socks-day/) is a separate observance with its own story. For a related UN day on rights, see [Human Rights Day 2026](/news/human-rights-day-2026-december-10/) and [World Children's Day 2026](/news/world-childrens-day-2026-friday-november-20-rights-of-the-child/).
