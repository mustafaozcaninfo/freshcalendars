**Sonos's homepage, read Nov. 25, 2026, advertises "Up to 25% off" for Black Friday, with the offer "valid on select products through 11/30."** The Era 100 speaker is $179, down from $219. The table below lists the 10 discounted prices on sonos.com with the dollars saved and the percent off we computed.

Prices are from Sonos's product pages on Nov. 25, 2026, and can change. Dollars saved and percent off are computed from the sale and regular prices; Sonos does not print the percent. The Beam price and its $160 saving also match MacRumors' Nov. 19 report.

Sonos Ace Ultra, at $449, shows no sale price. The banner says "up to 25% off"; the prices above are the ones Sonos's own pages show, and the computed percent for the Beam (32%) and the Ace headphones (34%) is above 25%. The largest dollar cut is the Arc Ultra at $250.

Sonos's product pages also list bundles. The 2 Room Set with Era 100 is $358 against $438, an $80 saving.

## When does the Sonos Black Friday sale end?

Sonos says the offer is valid "through 11/30," which is Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, Cyber Monday. Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27. Sonos gives no end time and no extension. The date also appears in our [Black Friday 2026 end dates by store](/news/when-does-black-friday-end-2026-sale-end-dates-by-store/) and [Cyber Monday 2026 start times](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/). See [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/) and [when Cyber Monday falls](/when-is/cyber-monday/), and mark both on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

Sonos's sale start date is not on sonos.com. The sale was live by Nov. 19, as MacRumors reported that day.

## How long can you return a Sonos order from the sale?

Sonos's product pages say: "Order by 11/30 and you've got 45 days to return it." The pages do not say which day the 45 days start from. If the count starts on Nov. 30, day 45 is Thursday, Jan. 14, 2027. That date is computed from the 45 days, not given by Sonos; read the return terms at checkout.

Sonos's shipping page says "we do not offer Saturday, Sunday or holiday shipping/delivery." Sonos does not give a 2026 Christmas shipping cutoff on the pages we read.
