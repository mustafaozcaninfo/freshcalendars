**Connections: Sports Edition #796 is the Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026 puzzle from The Athletic and New York Times Games, on the Saturday of Thanksgiving weekend.** Each color has two hints behind it, and the answers wait under the spoiler heading.

Several tiles on this board can be read two ways. That is the whole game today, so the traps get their own section, set out tile by tile below the hints and well above the answers. How the game works is on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works). Friday's board is on [Sports Edition #795](/news/nyt-connections-sports-edition-hints-answers-november-27-2026-795/), and the main game has its own page, [Connections for Nov. 28](/news/nyt-connections-hints-answers-november-28-2026-1266/).

## Saturday's hints for #796

Count letters first.

- **Hint 1 (yellow):** Each tile is a name you see on a screen when a game is on.
- **Hint 2 (yellow):** Two tiles have three letters, one has four and one has five.
- **Hint 1 (green):** Each tile is a surname, and all four people share one job on the field.
- **Hint 2 (green):** Three tiles have four letters, and one has six.
- **Hint 1 (blue):** Each tile is the first name a fan would use for a driver.
- **Hint 2 (blue):** Two tiles have five letters, one has four and one has three.
- **Hint 1 (purple):** The same word can follow each of the four tiles.
- **Hint 2 (purple):** The tiles have three, five, six and seven letters, so no two match.

## Tiles that can be read two ways on #796

Magic is also the name of an NBA team, the Orlando Magic, so it can pull you toward basketball. Max and Prime both read like streaming brands, because Max is a streaming service, and that look-alike pair is worth a second thought. Rice and Ward are plain words as well as surnames, and Branch is an everyday word too. Jersey can suggest an item of football clothing before it suggests anything else.

Fox may make you think of an animal. Slow down. Before you lock in any group of four, test each tile against the other fifteen and ask whether a second reading would send it somewhere else on the board. ## Saturday's answers, #796, Nov. 28, 2026 (spoilers)

Still solving? Stop here. Beebom and Nerd's Chalk print the same four groups and sixteen entries, and the group names below are as Nerd's Chalk prints them. Most Valuable Player (MVP) is the award named in the green group.

- **Yellow, NFL Broadcasters:** CBS, ESPN, Fox, Prime
- **Green, WRs to Win Super Bowl MVP:** Branch, Kupp, Rice, Ward
- **Blue, F1 Champions, Familiarly:** Kimi, Lando, Lewis, Max
- **Purple, _____ Number:** Bib, Jersey, Magic, Retired

## Who or what each entry is

We confirmed no league pages today, so we add nothing about individual careers and describe each group only by its name.

- **CBS, ESPN, Fox, Prime:** each is a broadcaster of National Football League (NFL) games, per the group name.
- **Branch, Kupp, Rice, Ward:** each is the surname of a wide receiver (WR) who was named Super Bowl MVP, per the group name.
- **Kimi, Lando, Lewis, Max:** each is the familiar first name of a Formula 1 (F1) champion, per the group name.
- **Bib, Jersey, Magic, Retired:** each can be followed by "number." A bib number is the number pinned to a runner or other athlete. A jersey number is the one on a player's shirt. A magic number is the combined wins and losses that clinch a playoff spot or title. A retired number is one a team no longer gives to any player.
