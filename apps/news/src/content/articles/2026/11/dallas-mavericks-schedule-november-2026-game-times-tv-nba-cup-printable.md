**The Dallas Mavericks host Sacramento on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, at 7 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The NBA lists 14 Mavericks games in November, and a one-page printable of all of them is further down.

The source is the NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF from NBA Communications, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." Its times are in ET, so Dallas fans subtract one hour for CT; the table below gives both. Weekdays are our own calculation. The Mavericks' previous game was at home against Houston on Friday, Oct. 30.

## Which Mavericks games count in the NBA Cup?

The schedule flags three November Mavericks games as Emirates NBA Cup group play: against Utah on Fri., Nov. 6, at Denver on Tue., Nov. 24 and at Phoenix on Fri., Nov. 27. A fourth Mavericks group game, against Houston, was on Fri., Oct. 30. Group play runs from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, per NBA.com's Aug. 12, 2026 release, with quarterfinals on Dec. 4 and 5 and the championship on Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round.

## Back-to-backs and road trips in the Mavericks' November

Reading the dates in the table shows how the month is built. These are our own counts from the NBA's schedule:

- **Back-to-backs:** Sun., Nov. 8 and Mon., Nov. 9 (both at home), Sat., Nov. 14 and Sun., Nov. 15 (both on the road), and Sat., Nov. 21 and Sun., Nov. 22 (home, then at Utah).
- **A three-game road trip:** the Mavericks play at Minnesota on Nov. 12, at Cleveland on Nov. 14 and at Boston on Nov. 15.
- **A second road trip to close the month:** at Utah on Nov. 22, at Denver on Nov. 24 and at Phoenix on Nov. 27.
- **Thanksgiving week:** the Mavericks play at Denver on Tue., Nov. 24 and at Phoenix on Fri., Nov. 27. They have no game on Thanksgiving Day itself, which is Thu., Nov. 26, 2026. Our [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has the holiday.

Seven home games and seven road games make 14 in all, from Nov. 2 to Nov. 30.
