**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, puts W in the center, with A, D, E, L, O and S on the petals.** Beebom, Connections Solver and Word.Tips list these letters. Hints come first, and the answers sit in a marked section at the bottom.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** W (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** A, D, E, L, O, S

Three letters are vowels: A, E and O. The center W is a consonant, so each word needs at least one of those three vowels. Rules and scoring are on our [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works). Yesterday's board is on the [Nov. 9 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-9-2026/).

## Pangram hints, from light to heavy

Several pangrams exist today, so the hints fit more than one word.

1. **Length:** the shortest is 8 letters and the longest is 10, so every pangram repeats at least one letter.
2. **Count:** Beebom and Word.Tips list seven; Connections Solver's count reads three.
3. **The everyday one:** a past-tense verb, 9 letters, meaning gulped down.
4. **The family:** five of the seven are built around a four-letter word for timber, joined to letters from the rest of the board.
5. **First letters:** the seven start with A, D, L, S and W, and three of them start with A.

Beebom and Word.Tips each print all seven pangrams. Other lists add rarer words, and the game may not accept them.

## What the letters allow

The board is friendly to doubled letters, and the long words show it.

- **Double O carries many of them.** Five of the nine long words we confirmed (listed below) contain two O's.
- **Double E is just as common.** Six of the nine repeat the E, and two of them use it four times.
- **S, L and D double up too.** The S appears twice or more in five of them, the L twice or more in four, and the D twice or more in two.
- **A is the scarcest.** Only one of the nine uses the A twice, four use it once and four skip it.
