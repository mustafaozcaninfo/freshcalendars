## Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints for Friday, Nov. 27

- **Pinpoint #941, first hint:** the category opens with the words "Different ways to," so it names methods, not objects.
- **Pinpoint #941, second hint:** every clue is a single moment you could watch happen, and none of them is a thing you can hold.
- **Pinpoint #941, third hint:** the fifth clue carries a note in brackets that gives a condition for it to count.
- **Crossclimb #941, the ladder:** seven words of four letters, joined by six one-letter swaps.
- **Crossclimb #941, the locked ends:** the top and bottom rows share one clue about a person who is very enthusiastic and knowledgeable about a subject.
- **Crossclimb #941, the middle clues:** one rung is something you are required to have, one is to give food to, one is to experience an emotion, one is the outer part of some fruit and one is a quick look.

## Which Crossclimb #941 letters move on each rung

Six swaps, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word of the ladder appears here.

| Position | Times it changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |

The swaps run through positions 3, 1, 4, 1, 4, 1. The second letter never moves, and the third moves only on the very first step. After that the ladder works only on the first and last letters, so from the second rung down you are choosing a new opening or closing letter for a frame that stays put. That makes the first letter the one to test first on each middle clue.

## Starting points for Queens #941, Tango #781, Zip #620 and Mini Sudoku #473

**Queens #941.** Begin with any region that covers only one row or column; its queen settles that line for every other region.

**Tango #781.** If a symbol already appears the full number of times in a line, the empty cells left in it take the other symbol.

**Zip #620.** Trace the path backward from the highest number as well as forward from 1; the two ends are the most constrained.

**Mini Sudoku #473.** Look at the row, column and box that already hold the most digits, since fewer candidates fit there.

## Friday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 27, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #941:** Different ways to score points in sports. The five clue words in order are Try, Goal, Ace, Home run and Free throw (worth 1 if made).
- **Crossclimb #941:** Nerd, Need, Feed, Feel, Peel, Peek, Geek. Need, Feed, Feel, Peel and Peek fill the middle in that order; Nerd and Geek are the locked ends.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top row | Nerd | none | none |
| Rung 1 | Need | E for R | 3 |
| Rung 2 | Feed | F for N | 1 |
| Rung 3 | Feel | L for D | 4 |

Clues as printed by Nerdschalk: Need, "Requirement"; Feed, "Provide with sustenance"; Feel, "Experience, as an emotion"; Peel, "Outer part of some fruit"; Peek, "Quick look (not to be confused with a mountain)". Nerd and Geek share one clue, which asks for two words for someone very enthusiastic and knowledgeable about a subject.

## Five games behind the Pinpoint #941 clues

The five clue words come from different games: Try points to rugby, Goal to soccer or hockey, Ace to tennis, Home run to baseball and Free throw to basketball. The sources do not give a point value for any of them except the bracket on the last clue, which says a free throw is worth 1 if it is made. Point values per game are left out because they were not checked against each league's rulebook for this page.

Queens, Tango, Zip and Mini Sudoku solutions are left out: no two sources confirmed them as text.
