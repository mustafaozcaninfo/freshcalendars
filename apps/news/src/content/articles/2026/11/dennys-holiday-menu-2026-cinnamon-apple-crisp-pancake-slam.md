**Denny's Family Turkey Bundle can be ordered on Dennys.com through Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, for Thanksgiving pickup, and again Dec. 14-22 for Christmas pickup, Denny's said in a Nov. 3 release distributed by GlobeNewswire.** Orders need 24 hours' advance notice. The same release, datelined Spartanburg, S.C., announced the holiday menu, a new $6.99 Triple Play menu and catering. The tables below sort the menu, the dates and what Denny's has not said.

## What is on Denny's 2026 holiday menu?

| Item | New or returning | What it is | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cinnamon Apple Crisp Pancake Slam | Returning | Two buttermilk pancakes with cinnamon crumb topping, apple crisp, salted caramel sauce and powdered sugar, with eggs, hash browns, and bacon or sausage | Not given |
| Turkey & Dressing Dinner | Returning | Carved turkey breast, stuffing, turkey gravy and cranberry sauce, with two sides and dinner bread | Not given |
| Cinnamon Roll Cold Brew & Milk Shake | New | Cinnamon roll flavor in iced cold brew or a hand-dipped vanilla ice cream shake, both with whipped cream | Not given |
| Pecan Pie and Pumpkin Pie | Returning, per Full Service Restaurant (FSR) Magazine | By the slice or as a whole pie | Not given |

Source: Denny's release, Nov. 3, 2026; FSR Magazine, Nov. 10, 2026. The release describes the Slam as "Two buttermilk pancakes cooked with cinnamon crumb topping, finished with warm apple crisp, salted caramel sauce, and powdered sugar." The release does not label the pies new or returning; FSR Magazine calls them returning. Items are at participating locations for a limited time, and Denny's gave no end date. FSR Magazine and Sporked both described the menu on Nov. 10.

## How does Denny's $6.99 Triple Play menu work?

The release says Triple Play Combo Meals are "Available now nationwide" and start "at just $6.99," with guests mixing and matching a drink, a starter and an entree. FSR Magazine adds that they are available all day.

| Pick | Choices | Upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Drink | Soft drink, iced tea, lemonade or coffee | Cold Brew or Milk Shake |
| Starter | Maple Stuffed Donut Holes or Chips & Salsa | Side Garden Salad or Cup of Soup |
| Entree | Includes the Everyday Value Slam, Super Slam and a new Crispy Chicken Sandwich | Not given |

Denny's describes the sandwich as "A golden-fried chicken breast on a brioche bun with a variety of bold sauces." The release gives no upgrade prices.

## Denny's turkey bundle order windows and gift card bonus dates

The Family Turkey Bundle is "available for a limited time exclusively through Dennys.com." Denny's did not post a price or list what the bundle contains.

| Date | What happens | Source wording |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 20 to Wed., Nov. 25 | Thanksgiving bundle orders open | "can be placed Nov. 20-25 for Thanksgiving pickup" |
| Wed., Nov. 25 | Last day to order for Thanksgiving | 24 hours' advance notice |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving; last day of the first gift card bonus window | "between Oct. 28 and Nov. 26, 2026" |
| Mon., Dec. 14 to Tue., Dec. 22 | Christmas bundle orders | "Dec. 14-22 for Christmas pickup" |
| Fri., Dec. 25 | Christmas Day | Hours not posted |
| Sat., Dec. 26 | Last day of the second gift card bonus window | "Dec. 1 and Dec. 26, 2026" |

The gift card offer reads: "Guests who purchase a $25 gift card between Oct. 28 and Nov. 26, 2026, or Dec. 1 and Dec. 26, 2026, will receive a $5 bonus card to use on a future visit." The two windows leave Fri., Nov. 27 to Mon., Nov. 30 outside the dates the release prints.
