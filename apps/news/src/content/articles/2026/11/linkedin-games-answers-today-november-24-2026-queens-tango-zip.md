## Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints for Tuesday, Nov. 24

- **Pinpoint #938, first hint:** the first four clue words are each the name of one person, and the fifth names two.
- **Pinpoint #938, second hint:** the people share a source: they come from works written for the stage, not from real life.
- **Pinpoint #938, third hint:** the clue words lead you to the titles of those works, not to the people's jobs or home countries.
- **Crossclimb #938, the ladder:** seven words of four letters, joined by six one-letter swaps.
- **Crossclimb #938, the locked ends:** together the top and bottom words make a compound word for a spot on the web.
- **Crossclimb #938, the middle clues:** one rung is a color word, one is a stack, and one is a pipe-like object.

## Who is in the Pinpoint #938 list

The Pinpoint clue words come in a mixed shape today. The first four are single names, of 5, 8, 9 and 7 letters. The fifth joins two names with a short word. A guess that fits a group of single people can still fit the last clue, because the pair is the point of that entry. Open the clues one at a time and wait for the fifth before you commit if your first idea was about one person's trait. That is our reading of the clue words, not a LinkedIn rule.

## Where the Crossclimb #938 letters move

Six moves, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word of the ladder appears here.

| Position | Times it changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 0 |

The moves run through positions 3, 1, 2, 3, 2, 3. The fourth letter never moves, so every word shares its last letter. The top and bottom words differ in positions 1, 2 and 3, which is a quick test: if a pair of neighbors differs in two places, one of them is in the wrong row.

## Starting points for Queens #938, Tango #778, Zip #617 and Mini Sudoku #470

**Queens #938.** Find the smallest colored region first; with few cells, its crown is the easiest to pin down.

**Tango #778.** Scan each row and column for two matching symbols side by side, then fill the cell next to them with the other symbol.

**Zip #617.** Start from the number 1 and look at which neighbor the path has to take to avoid cutting off a corner.

**Mini Sudoku #470.** Count how often each digit already appears, and begin with the one that has the most placements.

## Tuesday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 24, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #938:** Title characters from Shakespeare's plays. The five clue words in order are Timon, Cressida, Cleopatra, Othello and Romeo and Juliet.
- **Crossclimb #938:** Home, Hope, Pope, Pipe, Pile, Pale, Page. Hope, Pope, Pipe, Pile and Pale fill the middle in that order; Home and Page are the locked ends.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top row | Home | none | none |
| Rung 1 | Hope | P for M | 3 |
| Rung 2 | Pope | P for H | 1 |
| Rung 3 | Pipe | I for O | 2 |
| Rung 4 | Pile | L for P | 3 |
| Rung 5 | Pale | A for I | 2 |
| Bottom row | Page | G for L | 3 |

Home and Page form the compound word homepage, the part of a website many users see first. In Pinpoint, the last entry names two people but is a single play, which is why it belongs in the list.
