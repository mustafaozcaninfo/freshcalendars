The National Guard turns 390 on Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026, counting from Dec. 13, 1636, the founding date given by the U.S. Army (Army.mil). Its article says "on Dec. 13, 1636, the colonial leaders formed three militia regiments of men between the ages of 16 and 60." The day is not a federal holiday, and the National Guard Bureau (NGB) had not posted 2026 birthday events as of Nov. 21, 2026.

## When is the National Guard birthday in 2026?

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Birthday | Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026 | Computed from the calendar |
| Anniversary | 390th (2026 minus 1636) | Army.mil |
| Founding | Dec. 13, 1636, Massachusetts | Army.mil, Dec. 13, 2023 |
| Next birthday | Monday, Dec. 13, 2027, the 391st | Computed |

Army.mil's 2023 article says "the Massachusetts General Court established an official militia for the first time in the American Colonies," and that "December 13th commemorates the 387th anniversary of the founding of the National Guard." The 390th follows by counting three more years. The Defense Visual Information Distribution Service (DVIDS) quoted "the Dec. 13, 1636, birthday of the National Guard" in a Dec. 12, 2025 story on the 389th. No statute or proclamation for the birthday turned up in the sources used here.

## Is Dec. 13 a federal holiday?

No. The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) federal holiday page lists only "Friday, December 25" (Christmas Day) for December 2026. Dec. 13, 2026 falls on a Sunday. See the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [US holidays list](/holidays/).

## What happens on the National Guard's birthday?

The National Guard Bureau had not posted 2026 birthday events, a theme or a logo as of Nov. 21, 2026. In 2025, DVIDS reported that the New York National Guard "saluted the National Guard's 389th Birthday during a Dec. 12 ceremony," a day before the date. Check your state Guard's own page for a local ceremony.

## When is the Space Force birthday in 2026?

The Space Force birthday falls on Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, its 7th, counted from Dec. 20, 2019. That day the President signed the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year 2020 at Joint Base Andrews, Maryland, saying "With my signature today, you will witness the birth of the Space Force" and that it "will be now officially the sixth branch of the United States Armed Forces," per GovInfo. The 8th is Monday, Dec. 20, 2027.

## How do other service birthdays line up?

The Marine Corps marked its 251st on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026; our [Marine Corps birthday story](/news/marine-corps-birthday-2026-november-10-251st/) has the Defense Commissary Agency (DeCA) release. Army.mil says "Established on June 14, 1775, the Army is a year older than the United States itself." The Army's next birthday is Monday, June 14, 2027, the 252nd.
