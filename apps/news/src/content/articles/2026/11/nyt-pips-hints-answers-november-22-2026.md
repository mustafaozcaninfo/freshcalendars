## The forced first move on each Pips board, Nov. 22

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left. A search of every legal placement against the saved data backs each line, and it finds one arrangement on Easy and Hard and two on Medium.

**Easy.** R5C1, R5C2 and R5C3 must add up to 15. The set tops out at 5 and shows a 5 on exactly three halves, the double 5-5 and one end of 3-5, so the whole bottom row is made of 5s. If the double slid right onto R5C2 and R5C3, the 3-5 would put its 3 on R4C1, and R4C1 must stay below 3. So the double sits on R5C1 and R5C2, and the 3-5 stands on R5C3 with its 3 above.

**Medium.** R3C5 must be below 2, and the set has no 0, so it holds the 1 of the 4-1. The 4 has two neighbors to choose from, R3C4 and R2C5. R2C5 and R1C5 must total more than 10, which a 4 cannot reach even beside a 6, so the 4 lands on R3C4.

**Hard.** R2C1, R2C3 and R3C5 each add up to 0 on their own, and the set has three halves showing a 0: the 5-0, the 0-3 and the 6-0. Each of those dominoes fills one zero cell, so three of the board's single cells are settled before anything else. The 6-0 spans R2C3 and R2C4, the neighbor that must be above 4.

## Pips hints for Nov. 22, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 3-5, 3-2, 5-5, 2-0, 4-0)**

- Hint 1: Start with the bottom row, three cells that add up to 15, in a set whose highest half is a 5.
- Hint 2: R3C3 and R4C3 add up to 3, and no half in the set shows a 1.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 2-6, 4-5, 3-3, 3-6, 4-1, 2-3, 3-5)**

- Hint 1: R3C5 must stay below 2, and no half in the set shows a 0.
- Hint 2: R2C5 and R1C5 must total more than 10, and a 6 is in that pair; two halves in the set show a 6.

**Hard (14 dominoes on a seven-column board)**

- Hint 1: R2C1, R2C3 and R3C5 each add up to 0 alone, and three halves in the set show a 0.
- Hint 2: R1C7, R2C7, R3C7 and R4C7 must all be equal, so the number needs four halves in the set; 0, 5 and 6 have fewer.

## Why Sunday's Hard board reads like a lock

Thirteen of Hard's 18 regions hold one cell, so most of the board is a list of exact values rather than a puzzle of trade-offs. Three cells want a 0, R1C3 wants a 3, R1C4 and R1C5 want a 4, and R2C5 and R4C3 want a 1. The tension sits in the two equal regions: column 7 spans four cells, and the 2-by-2 block of R3C1, R3C2, R4C1 and R4C2 needs one value on all four. Easy puts its three 5s in one row, and Medium has only two single-cell regions.
