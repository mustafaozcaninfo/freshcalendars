## When is Christmas in Rockefeller Center 2026 on NBC?

Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026, 8 to 10 p.m. ET and PT, on NBC, simulcast on Peacock and in Spanish on Telemundo. Cynthia Erivo hosts the 94th annual special, which includes the tree lighting. The release does not name performers yet. In 2025, the special aired Wednesday, Dec. 3, 8 to 10 p.m., according to NBCUniversal's Oct. 30, 2025 release.

Dec. 2 is a two-special night: Christmas with Snoop follows from 10 to 11 p.m. and streams on Peacock the next day, with an encore Dec. 17 at 10 p.m.

## Which NBC specials air in Thanksgiving week?

Four NBC programs air on Nov. 25 and 26, around [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/), and the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade moved to Saturday, Nov. 28:

- **Wed., Nov. 25, 8-9 p.m.:** Countdown to Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, hosted by Jack McBrayer, simulcast on Peacock.
- **Wed., Nov. 25, 9-11 p.m.:** A Saturday Night Live Thanksgiving.
- **Thu., Nov. 26, 8:30 a.m.:** NBC and Peacock encore the 2025 Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones. NBC has not announced when the rerun ends.
- **Thu., Nov. 26, 12-2 p.m.:** the National Dog Show Presented by Purina, in its 25th anniversary year, hosted by John O'Hurley and David Frei, with more than 2,000 dogs and features by Mary Carillo.
- **Sat., Nov. 28, 8:30 a.m. ET/CT/MT, 7:30 a.m. PT:** the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, postponed from Thursday because of extreme weather in New York City (a high wind warning), per NBC Insider (updated Nov. 25, 2026). It also airs on Telemundo and streams on Peacock. The end time has not been announced.

For the full holiday-week TV list, including football, see our [Thanksgiving 2026 TV schedule](/news/thanksgiving-2026-tv-schedule-macys-parade-nfl-games/).

## What are the NBC music and live specials in December?

- **Thu., Dec. 10, 8-11 p.m.:** NBC100: The Live Celebration, simulcast on Peacock. The release says it is live from the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles at 8 p.m. ET on NBC, so West Coast viewers should check local listings for the time in their zone.
- **Mon., Nov. 30, 10-11 p.m.:** Christmas at Graceland, where music stars celebrate Elvis Presley.
- **Tue., Dec. 8, 8-10 p.m.:** Alan Jackson: The Last Show, his final concert, at Nissan Stadium in Nashville, streaming on Peacock the next day.
- **Fri., Dec. 11, 8-10 p.m.:** A Saturday Night Live Christmas, with an encore Dec. 21 at 9 p.m.
- **Wed., Dec. 16, 9-11 p.m.:** Dolly Parton's Mountain Magic Christmas.

## What airs on NBC on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve?

On Thursday, Dec. 24, It's a Wonderful Life airs from 8 to 11 p.m. ([when is Christmas](/when-is/christmas/)). Christmas Eve Mass follows from 11:30 p.m. to 1 a.m., a Mass led by Pope Leo from St. Peter's Basilica in Rome, simulcast in Spanish on Telemundo. On Friday, Dec. 25, Telemundo carries Mensaje Navideño del Papa at 6 a.m. ET, and NBC airs the Grinch cartoon and film from 8 to 11 p.m.

On Thursday, Dec. 31, NBC100: The Countdown runs 8 to 10 p.m. with Savannah Guthrie and Craig Melvin, and 100 memorable moments. A Toast to 2026!, hosted by Hoda Kotb and Jenna Bush Hager, runs 10:30 p.m. to 12:30 a.m. Telemundo's Bienvenido 2027 begins at 8 p.m. ET. On Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, the 138th Rose Parade, presented by Honda, airs at 11 a.m. ET on NBC, simulcast on Peacock and in Spanish on Telemundo. The release gives no Pacific time for it.
