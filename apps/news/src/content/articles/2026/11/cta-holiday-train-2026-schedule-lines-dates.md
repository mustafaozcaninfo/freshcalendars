**The Allstate-sponsored Chicago Transit Authority (CTA) Holiday Train runs Nov. 27 to Dec. 22, 2026, starting Friday, Nov. 27, on the Orange and Green lines, with its first departure from Midway toward the Loop at 3:00 p.m., according to [CTA's Holiday Train & Bus page](https://www.transitchicago.com/holidayfleet/).** The Holiday Bus began its run on Tuesday, Nov. 24, on the #56 Milwaukee route. The tables below give every departure time by line, the Santa photo stops and the bus route for each day, read from CTA's schedule on Nov. 24.

CTA lists its train times as "All times pm" and does not name a time zone; Chicago is on Central Time (CT), and all times here are CT. The train, CTA says, "will travel all rail lines" in regular rail service and stops at all stations along each route, and normal CTA fares apply.

## How to track and board the Holiday Train and Bus

CTA says both vehicles are in-service trains and buses that cannot wait long at stations or stops, so space is limited and boarding is not guaranteed. Its tips:

- Track the train on [CTA Train Tracker](https://www.transitchicago.com/traintracker/), which shows the Holiday Train with a candy cane, or follow the bus on the [holiday bus tracker](https://www.transitchicago.com/holidaybustracker/). The bus shows on the map only while it is in service.
- Board near the start of the route; riders often stay on until the end of the line.
- On the train, cars toward the front or back are less crowded than those next to Santa's sleigh.
- Small collapsible strollers are encouraged.
- To photograph the train, CTA suggests boarding first and taking photos when you get off at your destination. On the bus, take photos with Santa only while the bus is stopped, ideally just before leaving by the rear doors.

## Why the Holiday Train delivers food baskets

CTA says the train is in its 35th year of operation. It began on the Blue Line in 1992, when a "Season's Greetings from the CTA" sign was put on an out-of-service train used to deliver food to charities. Today Santa waves to riders from his sleigh on an open-air flatcar carrying his reindeer and decorated holiday trees. CTA says it will donate approximately 400 food baskets to community organizations this year and that the effort has supported more than 11,900 families since 1992. Allstate sponsors the Holiday Train and Bus.

In 2025, CTA's Nov. 17 release said the train launched from Midway on the Orange Line on Friday, Nov. 28, 2025, and the bus started Tuesday, Nov. 25, 2025.
