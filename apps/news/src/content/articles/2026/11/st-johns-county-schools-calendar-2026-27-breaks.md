**The St. Johns County School District (SJCSD) 2026-2027 master calendar starts Thanksgiving Break on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, Winter Break on Monday, Dec. 21 and Spring Break on Monday, March 15, 2027, and ends the year for students on Friday, May 28, 2027.** Students start Monday, Aug. 10, 2026. The next student holiday is Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026. The district's [master calendar page](https://www.stjohns.k12.fl.us/calendar/) says the board approved the calendar on Sept. 11, 2025.

Our printable, the St. Johns County Schools Calendar 2026-27, puts every date below on 11 mini months from August 2026 to June 2027. Download it under the story.

## St. Johns County break dates for 2026-27

| Break | Students out | Classes resume | Weekdays off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 | 5 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | 11 |
| Spring Break | Fri., March 12 to Fri., March 19, 2027 | Mon., March 22, 2027 | 6 |

The winter count includes Monday, Jan. 4, which the calendar lists as "Teacher Planning Day-Student Holiday." The calendar's own Winter Break is Dec. 21, 2026, to Jan. 1, 2027, so students are out one extra weekday. The spring count includes Friday, March 12, also a Teacher Planning Day and student holiday, one day before Spring Break begins. Thursday, March 11 is when the third quarter ends.

Counting weekends, our math gives a 10-day stretch for spring: Friday, March 12 through Sunday, March 21, with classes back Monday, March 22. For winter, students are away from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Monday, Jan. 4, which is 17 days in a row, after dismissal 1 hour early on Friday, Dec. 18. For other districts, see our [winter break roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) and the [spring break 2027 list](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

## Which St. Johns County days could become hurricane make-up days?

The calendar's key reads "Denotes hurricane make-up days." It marks Labor Day (Sept. 7), Sept. 25, Oct. 12, Thanksgiving Break and Jan. 4. Those days could become school days if storms close schools. The calendar publishes no further rule on how the district would use them, so check with the district before booking travel on any of these dates.

## When are early release days in St. Johns County?

The calendar lists a weekly early release every Wednesday: "Elementary @1:45, Middle @12:50, High @2:50." Separately, all schools are dismissed 1 hour early on Dec. 18, 2026, and May 28, 2027, the last days before Winter Break and summer.
