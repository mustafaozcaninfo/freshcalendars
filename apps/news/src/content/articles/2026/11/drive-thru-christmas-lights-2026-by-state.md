**Nine drive-thru Christmas light shows list 2026 dates on their own websites, checked Nov. 23, 2026, and the per-car prices they post run from $5 at Wayne County Lightfest in Michigan to about $80+ at Speedway Christmas in North Carolina.** Six of the nine open between Nov. 20 and Nov. 26. The table gives each show's dates, nightly hours, price and closed nights, and a one-page printable planner keeps them on the fridge.

## Which drive-thru light show costs the least per car?

The prices the shows post, in order from lowest, are below. Three shows post no car price, so they have no place in the ranking.

| Rank | Show | Posted price per car |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wayne County Lightfest | $5 |
| 2 | Tanglewood Festival of Lights | $20 to $23 Mon. to Thu., $25 to $28 Fri. to Sun. (cash or card) |
| 3 | Bright Nights at Forest Park | $30 plus $2 fee through Dec. 27, then $20 plus $2 |
| 4 | Gift of Lights | $35 |
| 5 | Lights Under Louisville | From $42.99 |
| 6 | Speedway Christmas | About $80+ per vehicle, up to 15 passengers |

Tanglewood's lowest prices are four family nights in Forsyth County: Monday, Nov. 16, Tuesday, Nov. 17, Monday, Nov. 23 and Tuesday, Nov. 24, at $10 cash or $13 by credit card. Bright Nights lists oversized vehicles with 9 to 16 seats at $50 plus $2. Gift of Lights charges $60 for a bus, limousine or RV, and Lake Lanier Islands lists a $100 shuttle and a $250 bus. Shady Brook Farm says prices vary by date and the gate price is $5 more than the advance price. Prairie Lights posts a $15 early-bird discount for Nov. 30 to Dec. 4, 2026 with code EARLY26, but no base price.

## Which drive-thru light shows need tickets in advance?

- **Required:** Bright Nights says tickets must be purchased in advance. Lights Under Louisville says all tickets must be bought in advance and will not be sold at the gate.
- **Recommended:** Shady Brook Farm recommends advance tickets, and the gate price is $5 more.
- **Not required or sold at the gate:** Tanglewood does not require advance tickets for general admission. Lake Lanier Islands sells tickets online or at the gate. Gift of Lights sells online tickets that are valid any open evening.
- **Not confirmed:** The Speedway Christmas ticket page could not be read, so whether it uses timed tickets is not confirmed. Wayne County Lightfest and Prairie Lights list no ticket rule in the text we read.

## Which light shows close on Christmas Eve or Christmas Day?

Christmas Eve is Thursday, Dec. 24 (see [when is Christmas Eve](/when-is/christmas-eve/)). Speedway Christmas says the show is closed on Dec. 24, 2026. Wayne County Lightfest's last night is that Thursday, so it has no Christmas Day show. Magical Nights of Lights is open Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve, and Lights Under Louisville lists Christmas Day hours of 5:30 to 10 p.m. Gift of Lights runs Dec. 20 to 24 until 10 p.m., the same hours as its Friday and Saturday nights.

Shows that end earlier also matter for last-minute plans: Prairie Lights ends Dec. 31 and Tanglewood ends Jan. 1, 2027. On Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, Lights Under Louisville is open 5:30 to 10 p.m. and Gift of Lights and Prairie Lights open that night. Bright Nights is closed Monday, Nov. 30 and Tuesday, Dec. 1.

## Where to tune the radio at a drive-thru light show

Two shows name a station. Speedway Christmas plays its music on 101.3 FM, and Shady Brook Farm says to "turn your dial to 90.7 FM" for holiday music on its own station. Speedway Christmas also asks drivers to turn off their headlights while driving through the display. The other pages list no radio station.

## Is Shady Brook Farm's drive-through light show ending?

Shady Brook Farm says "2026 marks the final season of our classic drive-through experience." The show runs nightly from Nov. 21 to Jan. 3, then Fridays and Saturdays until Jan. 30. Hours are 5 to 10 p.m. Sunday to Thursday and 5 to 11 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. The page does not say what replaces the classic show.
