**Connections: Sports Edition #782, the Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026 puzzle from The Athletic and New York Times (NYT) Games, puts names from several sports on one board and leaves the sorting to you.** Two hints per color open one tap at a time. Answers stay below the spoiler heading.

One entry carries a fresh piece of sports news, covered after the answers. The rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works).

## Saturday's hints, #782, Nov. 14, 2026

Sort the sixteen tiles by what they are before you sort them by sport: some are surnames, some are first names, some are ordinary words.

- **Yellow hint 1:** Every tile is a move in a sport played on ice.
- **Yellow hint 2:** Each is a kind of jump, and two of the four start with the same letter.
- **Green hint 1:** All four are surnames of players on one National Basketball Association (NBA) roster.
- **Green hint 2:** The surnames run from five letters up to nine, with two of them at five.
- **Blue hint 1:** These are baseball names, the kind a broadcaster can say without adding a surname.
- **Blue hint 2:** All four are five or six letters long, and each belongs to someone who has won the same award in the same league.
- **Purple hint 1:** Change the first letter of each tile and you get a surname.
- **Purple hint 2:** The replacement letter is different on all four tiles, and the new names belong to pro football players.

## What makes Saturday's board slippery

Three of the colors are built from names, and the fourth is built from plain words, so a quick scan finds more "names" than any one group can hold. A first guess based on type alone can cost a mistake.

Start with the group whose rule you can state in one sentence, then let the leftovers sort themselves. ## Saturday's answers, #782, Nov. 14, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it. Beebom and Nerdschalk list the same four groups and sixteen entries.

- **Yellow, "Figure Skating Jumps":** Axel, Loop, Lutz, Salchow
- **Green, "Golden State Warriors":** Curry, Green, Horford, Lendeborg
- **Blue, "AL MVPs, Familiarly":** Aaron, Mookie, Shohei, Yordan
- **Purple, "NFL RBs, With the First Letter Changed":** Hook, Naylor, Tall, Talker

## Who each entry is

- **Golden State Warriors:** Stephen Curry, Draymond Green, Al Horford and Yaxel Lendeborg are all on the Warriors roster at NBA.com.
- **AL MVPs, Familiarly:** MLB.com's Most Valuable Player award history lists Aaron Judge (New York Yankees; American League (AL) winner in 2022, 2024 and 2025), Mookie Betts (Boston; 2018), Shohei Ohtani (Los Angeles Angels; 2021 and 2023) and Yordan Alvarez (Houston; 2026).
- **Purple wordplay:** Hook becomes Cook, Naylor becomes Taylor, Tall becomes Hall and Talker becomes Walker, all surnames of NFL running backs (RBs).
- **Figure Skating Jumps:** four jump types in figure skating.

## This year's winner on the board

Yordan is the newest name in the blue group: MLB.com lists Alvarez as the 2026 AL winner. Judge, Betts and Ohtani are the other three names. Green holds the other trap, since Green is a surname that reads like a color and sits with the Warriors, not with anything else on the board.
