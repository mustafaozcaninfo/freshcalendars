**Parliament's Christmas recess 2026 runs from Friday 18 December 2026 to Monday 4 January 2027 for both the House of Commons and the House of Lords, according to UK Parliament's recess dates.** Thursday 17 December is the last sitting day and the Parliament calendar lists Tuesday 5 January 2027 as the first sitting day back. Below are the Scottish Parliament, Senedd and Northern Ireland Assembly breaks beside it, the 2027 recesses to Whitsun and last year's dates.

## When does Parliament break for Christmas 2026?

UK Parliament's data service lists a "Christmas recess 2026" for the Commons and the Lords with the same two dates: Friday 18 December 2026 and Monday 4 January 2027. The columns on those pages mark the first and last days when the House does not sit. The day pages on the same service confirm it:

| Date | Commons | Lords |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday 17 December 2026 | Sitting day | Sitting day |
| Friday 18 December 2026 | Adjournment day (Christmas recess) | Adjournment day (Christmas recess) |
| Monday 4 January 2027 | Adjournment day (Christmas recess) | Adjournment day (Christmas recess) |
| Tuesday 5 January 2027 | Sitting day | Sitting day |

That gives 18 days of recess from 18 December to 4 January, counting both ends and weekends. In 2025, UK Parliament listed the Christmas recess as Friday 19 December 2025 to Sunday 4 January 2026, which is 17 days.

## When does Parliament return in January 2027?

Both Houses are shown as sitting on Tuesday 5 January 2027. The recess table runs to Monday 4 January, and the calendar lists that day as an adjournment day for the Commons and the Lords. UK Parliament's own pages are the reference for the return, so check the calendar again closer to the date.

## How do the five Christmas breaks compare?

Each body sets its own break. Days off count from the first to the last recess day, both included.

| Body | First recess day | Last recess day | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| House of Commons | Fri. 18 Dec. 2026 | Mon. 4 Jan. 2027 | 18 |
| House of Lords | Fri. 18 Dec. 2026 | Mon. 4 Jan. 2027 | 18 |
| Scottish Parliament | 19 Dec. 2026 | 3 Jan. 2027 | 16 |
| Senedd | Mon. 21 Dec. 2026 | Sun. 10 Jan. 2027 | 21 |
| Northern Ireland Assembly | 12 Dec. 2026 | 3 Jan. 2027 | 23 |

The Scottish Parliament lists its break as "Christmas recess 2026-27: 19 December 2026 to 3 January 2027". The Senedd lists "Monday 21 December 2026 - Sunday 10 January 2027" under confirmed recess dates. The Northern Ireland Assembly published "Christmas 2026: 12 December 2026 to 3 January 2027" in all-party notice (APN) 532 on 5 May 2026. The same notice lists a Business Committee on 5 January 2027 and a plenary sitting on 11 January 2027. The Scottish and Welsh sources give no return date, so none is given here.

## Which recesses does Parliament list for 2027?

UK Parliament's recess pages for the Commons and the Lords list these 2027 breaks:

| Recess | First day | Last day | House |
|---|---|---|---|
| February recess 2027 | Fri. 12 Feb. 2027 | Sun. 21 Feb. 2027 | Commons and Lords |
| Easter recess 2027 | Fri. 26 March 2027 | Sun. 11 April 2027 | Commons and Lords |
| Whitsun recess 2027 | Fri. 28 May 2027 | Sun. 6 June 2027 | Commons and Lords |
| Summer recess 2027 | Thu. 29 July 2027 | Sun. 5 Sept. 2027 | Lords only |

The Commons list has no summer 2027 row yet. Each recess runs to the Sunday shown, and the pages give no return dates, so none is inferred here. Of the [bank holidays in 2027](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/), Good Friday (26 March) and Easter Monday (29 March) fall in the Easter recess and the spring bank holiday (31 May) in the Whitsun recess; the [Christmas bank holidays](/news/uk-christmas-bank-holidays-2026-boxing-day-substitute-december-28/) fall in the Christmas break.

## Who decides the recess, and can it change?

The House agrees its Christmas adjournment on a motion. In the Commons debate of 4 December 2003, the record reads "Motion made, and Question put forthwith, pursuant to Standing Order No. 25 (Periodic Adjournments); Question agreed to." Dates can change if the House is recalled or agrees new dates.

Canada runs a separate calendar: see the [House of Commons of Canada sitting calendar for 2027](/news/canada-house-of-commons-sitting-calendar-2027-returns-january-25/).
