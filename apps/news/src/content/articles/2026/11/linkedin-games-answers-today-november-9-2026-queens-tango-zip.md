## Pinpoint and Crossclimb nudges for Monday, Nov. 9

- **Pinpoint #923, first nudge:** the five clues are separate items from one list, and none of them describes another.
- **Pinpoint #923, second nudge:** the category is a set of components, so ask what the clues are pieces of.
- **Pinpoint #923, third nudge:** the whole is something you hear on a broadcast before deciding what to wear tomorrow.
- **Crossclimb #923, the locked ends:** the game's clue reads "Two groups of animals commonly adopted as pets", so the top and bottom words are plural and four letters each.
- **Crossclimb #923, rungs 1 and 2:** one is a polka pattern, the next means to lavish affection on someone.
- **Crossclimb #923, rung 3:** a couple's outing together.
- **Crossclimb #923, rungs 4 and 5:** a partner or companion, then a yoga floor pad.

## Step map for the Crossclimb #923 ladder

Seven words, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word appears in this table.

| Move | Letter position that changes | Kind of letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 3 | consonant |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 4 | vowel |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 2 | vowel |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 4 | consonant |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 1 | consonant |

Positions 1 and 4 each change twice, while 2 and 3 change once. The only two vowel moves are back to back, so rungs 1, 2 and 3 are where a vowel swap is worth testing, and the last three moves are all consonants. Rungs 2, 3 and 4 also tell one connected story, which helps when the clues for two of them feel interchangeable.

## Starting points for Queens #923, Tango #763, Zip #602 and Mini Sudoku #455

**Queens #923.** Find the smallest colored region on the board. With only a few cells to choose from, every crown placed there removes its row, column and neighbors from the larger regions.

**Tango #763.** Scan for the = and x marks between neighboring cells first, since they fix a pair of symbols outright. Then see which line already has half its suns or moons.

**Zip #602.** Trace backward from the highest number as well as forward from 1, and give any cell with a single open neighbor its path early.

**Mini Sudoku #455.** Start with the row, column or box that has the most numbers filled in and list what is missing there.
