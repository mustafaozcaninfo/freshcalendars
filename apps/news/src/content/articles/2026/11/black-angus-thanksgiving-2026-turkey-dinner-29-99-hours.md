**Black Angus Steakhouse says its once-a-year Traditional Turkey Dinner costs $29.99 ($31.99 in Hawaii) on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, when its restaurants are open from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.** The chain's Thanksgiving page reads "We're open Thanksgiving Thursday, November 26. Join us and dine on our once-a-year turkey dinner with all the sides or enjoy one of our signature steaks or Prime Rib." A "Limited menu [is] served on Thanksgiving Day."

The same page lists a separate fully cooked Thanksgiving dinner to go that feeds 4 for $89.99 ($99.99 in Hawaii), with pre-orders that ran Nov. 14-24 and ended Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 6 p.m. The page does not say the time zone of that 6 p.m. cutoff, or whether the to-go dinner for 4 can be picked up on Nov. 25 or 26. The figures below are quoted from the Black Angus page read Nov. 25, 2026; the per-person and comparison numbers are our arithmetic from the chain's prices.

## Black Angus Thanksgiving turkey dinner: price and what is on the plate

The page sells the turkey dinner two ways. The dine-in plate is a flat price per person; the to-go box feeds 4.

| Item | Price | In Hawaii |
|---|---|---|
| Traditional Turkey Dinner (dine-in, Nov. 26 only) | $29.99 | $31.99 |
| Kids' Dinner (dine-in, Nov. 26 only) | $12.99 | $14.99 |
| Thanksgiving Meals To-Go (feeds 4) | $89.99 | $99.99 |

Black Angus says the dine-in "Traditional turkey dinner includes: Sliced turkey, sage dressing, homestyle mashed potatoes, turkey gravy, broccoli with garlic butter, roasted sweet potatoes, fresh cranberry sauce, garden salad and pumpkin pie," while supplies last, "Available for dine-in and takeout." The turkey dinner is "not available for dine-in until Thanksgiving Day, November 26, 2026."

## Black Angus Thanksgiving to-go dinner for 4: pre-order window

The to-go box is "fully-cooked and includes: sliced roasted turkey, garden salad, home-style mashed potatoes with gravy, sage dressing, sweet potatoes, fresh broccoli with garlic butter, fresh cranberry sauce, pumpkin pie and molasses bread," at "$89.99/$99.99 in Hawaii | Feeds 4 people." Black Angus said to "Pre-order your to-go turkey dinner for 4 November 14-24" and that you "can pick-up your to-go, fully-cooked turkey dinner for 4 as early as November 14." Orders "Must order at least 24 hours in advance," and "All pre-orders end Tuesday, November 24 at 6pm."

Dividing each Black Angus price by the people it serves gives the per-person cost below. The dine-in prices are already per person; the to-go box is one price for 4.

| Option | Price | Serves | Per person (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| To-go turkey dinner for 4 | $89.99 ($99.99 in Hawaii) | 4 | $22.50 ($25.00 in Hawaii) |
| Dine-in turkey dinner | $29.99 ($31.99 in Hawaii) | 1 | $29.99 ($31.99 in Hawaii) |
| Dine-in kids' dinner | $12.99 ($14.99 in Hawaii) | 1 | $12.99 ($14.99 in Hawaii) |

Four dine-in turkey dinners at $29.99 come to $119.96, so the $89.99 to-go box for 4 is $29.97 less than four plates. A table of two adults and two children dining in works out to $85.96 ($29.99 twice plus $12.99 twice). These comparisons are our arithmetic from the chain's listed prices.
