The 2026 Rawlings Gold Glove Award winners were announced Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, on ESPN, according to MLB.com. Each league has 10 winners, one at each position. Below are the full list by league and position, how the winners are chosen, the Platinum Glove winners and the other Major League Baseball (MLB) awards winners of November.

## Who won the 2026 Gold Glove Awards?

MLB.com's list of winners gives these names. Its headline for the story reads "First-timers make up bulk of Gold Glove winners." The team names are MLB.com's; the table is ordered by position.

| Position | American League (AL) winner | National League (NL) winner |
|---|---|---|
| Catcher | Dillon Dingler, Tigers | Gabriel Moreno, Diamondbacks |
| First base | Christian Walker, Astros | Matt Olson, Braves |
| Second base | Cole Young, Mariners | Brice Turang, Brewers |
| Shortstop | Taylor Walls, Rays | Geraldo Perdomo, Diamondbacks |
| Third base | Caleb Durbin, Red Sox | Alex Bregman, Cubs |
| Left field | Cody Bellinger, Yankees | Jackson Chourio, Brewers |
| Center field | Ceddanne Rafaela, Red Sox | Pete Crow-Armstrong, Cubs |
| Right field | Wilyer Abreu, Red Sox | Corbin Carroll, Diamondbacks |
| Pitcher | Tanner Bibee, Guardians | Nolan McLean, Mets |
| Utility | Ezequiel Duran, Rangers | Mauricio Dubón, Braves |

## Which teams have the most 2026 Gold Glove winners?

This is our count from the list above. The Boston Red Sox (Durbin, Rafaela, Abreu) and the Arizona Diamondbacks (Moreno, Perdomo, Carroll) each have three winners, the most of any team. The Milwaukee Brewers (Turang, Chourio), the Atlanta Braves (Olson, Dubón) and the Chicago Cubs (Bregman, Crow-Armstrong) have two each.

## How are Gold Glove winners chosen?

Rawlings says each manager and up to six coaches on his staff vote from a pool of qualified players in their league, and they cannot vote for their own team. MLB.com says those votes make up 75% of the selection total, and the Society for American Baseball Research (SABR) Defensive Index (SDI) counts for the other 25%. This year's field had three finalists at each position in each league, 60 names in all, per Rawlings and MLB.com. The award dates to 1957, per Rawlings.

## Who won the Platinum Glove?

MLB.com says the Platinum Glove Awards, one for the best overall defensive player in each league, were announced on Friday, Nov. 6, at the Rawlings Gold Glove Dinner in New York City: Yankees outfielder Cody Bellinger in the AL and Cubs outfielder Pete Crow-Armstrong in the NL. Rawlings had run the fan vote at Rawlings.com from 9:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Sunday, Nov. 1 to 11:59 p.m. ET on Thursday, Nov. 5, and said a combination of the national fan vote and the SDI decides who takes the honor.

## Who won the other MLB awards in November 2026?

MLB.com's awards guide gives these dates and winners. The Baseball Writers' Association of America (BBWAA) votes on the BBWAA awards. The Silver Slugger Awards on Nov. 17 are the next to be announced.

| Date | Award | Winners per MLB.com |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 6 | Platinum Glove Awards | Cody Bellinger, Yankees (AL); Pete Crow-Armstrong, Cubs (NL) |
| Fri., Nov. 6 | Heart & Hustle Award | Bobby Witt Jr., Royals |
| Mon., Nov. 9 | BBWAA Rookie of the Year | Kevin McGonigle, Tigers (AL); Sal Stewart, Reds (NL) |
| Tue., Nov. 10 | BBWAA Manager of the Year | Will Venable, White Sox (AL); Pat Murphy, Brewers (NL) |
| Wed., Nov. 11 | BBWAA Cy Young | Cam Schlittler, Yankees (AL); Jacob Misiorowski, Brewers (NL) |
| Wed., Nov. 11 | BBWAA Relief Pitcher of the Year | Louis Varland, Blue Jays (AL); Mason Miller, Padres (NL) |
| Thu., Nov. 12 | BBWAA M.V.P. | Yordan Alvarez, Astros (AL); Pete Crow-Armstrong, Cubs (NL) |
| Tue., Nov. 17 | Silver Slugger | Not yet announced |
