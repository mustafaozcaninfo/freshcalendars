The 2026 Rawlings Gold Glove Award winners are revealed tonight, Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, at 8:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on ESPN, according to Rawlings and MLB.com. Rawlings says the winners are unveiled in a one-hour, special-edition "Baseball Tonight" broadcast. Below are the show time in every U.S. time zone and the United Kingdom, all 60 finalists by league and position, the Platinum Glove vote window and the remaining Major League Baseball (MLB) award dates. The winners are not known yet; this story lists the finalists only.

Rawlings lists 8:30 p.m. ET. The other zones are our conversion. U.S. clocks went back to standard time at 2 a.m. today, and the table uses that standard time. In the UK, the show falls after midnight, on Monday, Nov. 2.

## How are Gold Glove winners chosen?

Rawlings says each manager and up to six coaches on his staff vote from a pool of qualified players in their league, and they cannot vote for their own team. MLB.com says those votes make up 75% of the selection total, and the Society for American Baseball Research (SABR) Defensive Index (SDI) counts for the other 25%. Utility uses a separate formula based on SABR's work, per Rawlings. The award dates to 1957, per Rawlings.

## When does Platinum Glove voting open and close?

The Rawlings Platinum Glove Award is presented by SABR. Fan voting begins online at Rawlings.com at 9:30 p.m. ET on Sunday, Nov. 1, an hour after the Gold Glove show starts, and stays open until 11:59 p.m. ET on Thursday, Nov. 5. Rawlings says a combination of the national fan vote and the SDI decides who takes the honor. The Platinum Glove winners are unveiled at the Rawlings Gold Glove Award Ceremony in New York City on Friday, Nov. 6.
