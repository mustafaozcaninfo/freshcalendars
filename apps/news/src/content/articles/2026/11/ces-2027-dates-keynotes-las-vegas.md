**CES 2027 takes place Jan. 6-9, 2027, in Las Vegas, according to the Consumer Technology Association (CTA), which runs the show: Wednesday, Jan. 6 through Saturday, Jan. 9.** The first keynote comes the night before the show floor opens, when Waymo's co-chief executive officer (CEO) speaks at 6:30 p.m. Pacific Standard Time (PST) on Tuesday, Jan. 5. Below: every keynote CTA has listed, converted to Eastern Time (ET), Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), Central European Time (CET) and Japan and Korea time for readers following from abroad.

## CES 2027 dates and what happens each day

CTA's Nov. 2, 2026 release says "CES 2027 takes place Jan. 6-9, 2027, in Las Vegas." That is 63 days from Nov. 4. Pacific Time (PT) runs on PST in January, which is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) minus 8 hours. The table uses only what CTA has published as of Nov. 4, 2026.

| Day | What CTA has listed |
|---|---|
| Tue., Jan. 5 | Waymo keynote, 6:30 p.m. PST |
| Wed., Jan. 6 | Show opens; State of the Industry Address, 8:30 a.m. PST; Stellantis keynote after it; Lenovo keynote, 5 p.m. PT |
| Thu., Jan. 7 | No keynote listed |
| Fri., Jan. 8 | No keynote listed |
| Sat., Jan. 9 | Last day of CES 2027 |

CTA's keynotes page lists three keynotes and says "Stay tuned for more announcements," so Thursday and Friday may change. Mark the dates on your [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html) or on a [printable yearly calendar](/printable-yearly-calendar.html).

## CES 2027 keynote times by time zone

The conversions are ours, from the Pacific times in CTA's releases: ET is 3 hours ahead of PST, GMT 8 hours, CET 9 hours, and Japan Standard Time (JST) and Korea Standard Time (KST) 17 hours. Japan and Korea share the same clock time.

| Keynote | Pacific (PST) | Eastern (ET) | GMT | CET | Japan and Korea (JST, KST) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Waymo, Tue., Jan. 5 | 6:30 p.m. | 9:30 p.m., Jan. 5 | 2:30 a.m., Wed., Jan. 6 | 3:30 a.m., Jan. 6 | 11:30 a.m., Jan. 6 |
| State of the Industry Address, Wed., Jan. 6 | 8:30 a.m. | 11:30 a.m., Jan. 6 | 4:30 p.m., Jan. 6 | 5:30 p.m., Jan. 6 | 1:30 a.m., Thu., Jan. 7 |
| Lenovo Tech World, Wed., Jan. 6 | 5 p.m. | 8 p.m., Jan. 6 | 1 a.m., Thu., Jan. 7 | 2 a.m., Jan. 7 | 10 a.m., Jan. 7 |

Stellantis is not in the table because CTA gives no start time for it, only that it follows the address.

## Who is speaking at CES 2027?

- **Waymo:** Tekedra Mawakana, co-CEO, at 6:30 p.m. PST on Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, in the Palazzo Ballroom at the Venetian, per CTA's Sept. 21 release.
- **Stellantis:** Antonio Filosa, CEO, "will deliver a keynote on the opening day of CES 2027 on January 6," per CTA's Sept. 8 release. He speaks "following the CTA State of the Industry Address at 8:30 a.m. PST on Wednesday, January 6, 2027," in the Palazzo Ballroom at the Venetian.
- **Lenovo:** Yuanqing Yang, chairman and CEO, at Lenovo Tech World on Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027, at 5 pm PT at the Sphere in Las Vegas, per CTA's Oct. 7 release. Its words: "Attendance will be limited to credentialed CES attendees." Lenovo says "additional details regarding ticketing, logistics and speakers will be announced at a later date."

## CES 2027 registration and pass prices

CTA's homepage says "Registration for CES 2027 is now open!" Its Conference page, which names CES 2027, listed these prices on Nov. 4, 2026:

| Pass | Through Nov. 30 | Dec. 1 to Jan. 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Pass | $149 | $349 |
| Premium Pass | $1450 | $1750 |

CTA describes the Standard Pass as "best for exploring the exhibit floor and keynote experiences." Prices can change, so confirm at ces.tech before you buy.

## CES 2027 hall hours and last year's turnout

CTA has not posted 2027 exhibit hall hours on a dated page, so none are printed here. CTA said in September that CES 2027 welcomes a Chilean global pavilion presence for the first time.

For scale, a CTA audit released March 31, 2026 counted 148,392 participants and more than 4,100 exhibitors at CES 2026. That is last year's show, not a forecast for 2027.
