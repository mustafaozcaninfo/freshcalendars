**The Europa League and Conference League play on Thursday, Dec. 10, 2026, with kickoffs from 10:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the fixture lists the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) updated on Nov. 5.** Europa League Matchday 6 and Conference League Matchday 5 are both that day, and the Conference League league phase ends on Thursday, Dec. 17. Below: all 54 December games in four time zones, the British clubs, how to watch and the dates after the league phase.

UEFA lists kickoffs in Central European Time (CET, UTC+1) and says on both fixture pages that kickoffs are 21:00 unless another time is listed. In December the UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, one hour behind CET), Eastern Standard Time is UTC-5 (six hours behind CET) and Pacific Time (PT) is nine hours behind. The UK, ET and PT columns are our conversion from UEFA's CET times, not an official schedule. Games with no time on UEFA's page are shown at its default 21:00 CET, and UEFA can still move them, so check UEFA before each matchday. Bold names are English or Scottish clubs. The [Champions League Matchday 6 story](/news/champions-league-december-2026-matchday-6-kickoff-times/) covers Dec. 8-9, and the [November Europa League and Conference League schedule](/news/europa-league-conference-league-november-2026-schedule-kickoff-times-et/) covers the games before.
