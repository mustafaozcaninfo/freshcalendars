## Which letters start and end the most words?

Every route has to chain: the last letter of one word is the first letter of the next. So a letter that opens few words, or closes few, limits what can sit next to it. The table counts all 920 words.

| Letter | Side | Words starting with it | Words ending with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H | 1 | 52 | 24 |
| R | 1 | 113 | 141 |
| A | 1 | 90 | 87 |
| T | 2 | 109 | 127 |
| P | 2 | 216 | 34 |
| L | 2 | 82 | 87 |
| Y | 3 | 16 | 134 |
| F | 3 | 109 | 15 |
| G | 3 | 78 | 31 |
| X | 4 | 0 | 11 |
| I | 4 | 5 | 41 |
| E | 4 | 50 | 188 |

Three letters stand out. The X begins nothing, so it cannot be the join between two words, and the I opens just 5 words. The Y is the reverse of that: 134 words end in it but only 16 begin with it. P is the busiest opener at 216 and a weak closer at 34.

## How hard is the X today?

The X sits in 19 words of the list, 11 of them ending in it. Because no word starts with X, it can never be the letter that hands off from one word to the next. A first word ending in X would strand the route, since nothing opens on X. So the X sits inside one of the words, or at the very end of the last word.

Crossing between sides is easier on some pairs than others. Sides 2 and 3 (T/P/L and Y/F/G) share only 200 words with a hop between them, against 617 for sides 1 and 4 and 597 for sides 2 and 4.

## Longest words and whether one word is enough

The longest word in the list has 12 letters, and two words reach it: Featherlight and Afterfeather. Ten more words have 11. Length is not the same as coverage, though. The most distinct letters any single word touches is 9 of the 12, so a one-word solution is out and the answer needs at least two words.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, three fewer than the par of 5.
2. The first word has 6 letters and the second has 8, chosen from 146 words of the first length and 67 of the second.
3. The first word opens on the Y/F/G side and closes on the T/P/L side. The second opens on that same T/P/L side and closes on the H/R/A side.
4. The X is in the second word, and the first word is the one that uses the I.
5. The first word starts with F and ends in T. The second starts with T and ends in R.
