**St. Augustine's Nights of Lights runs nightly from Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, through Monday, Jan. 18, 2027**, Visit St. Augustine, the area's visitors bureau, said in its July 29, 2026 release. That is 59 nights, counted inclusive. "Each evening, Historic Downtown St. Augustine glows with more than three million white lights," the release says. All times below are Eastern Time (ET).

## How does the Old Town Trolley Nights of Lights tour work?

Visit St. Augustine lists the tour nightly from Nov. 22, 2026, to Jan. 18, 2027, 6 to 9 p.m. Tours depart every quarter hour beginning at 6 p.m. from the Visitor Information Center at 10 West Castillo Drive. The 30- to 45-minute tour makes no stops, the listing says.

The listing gives "starting at $72.99 per row," for up to four people. The operator, Old Town Trolley Tours, says general passes start at $72.99 and a higher tier of passes at $199.99, and that times vary by date. Children 3 and under may ride on a lap; ages 4 and older need a seat.

## What does parking cost during Nights of Lights?

The City of St. Augustine says special rates apply at the Historic Downtown Parking Facility from Nov. 21, 2026, through Jan. 18, 2027.

| Day (2026) | Rate on the city page |
|---|---|
| Sat., Nov. 21, 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. | $20 |
| Sat., Nov. 21, after 1 p.m. | $40 |
| Sun., Nov. 22, all day | $20 |

Those are the only examples the page prints in this guide; the city's date-by-date table has the other days, so check it before you drive in. St. Johns County residents with a registered vehicle pay $3 ("your cost is always $3"). A city headline mentions a free shuttle from Light-Up! Night through Jan. 2, 2027, but the release itself could not be read, so its stops and hours are not confirmed.

## St. Augustine Christmas Parade and Colonial Night Watch on Dec. 5

The 68th Christmas Parade is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, from 9 to 10:30 a.m. It starts at the Shrine of Our Lady of La Leche at Mission of Nombre de Dios and ends behind the Visitor Information Center, Visit St. Augustine says. That evening the Colonial Night Watch torch-lit parade begins at 7 p.m. For other cities, see our [Christmas parade dates by city](/news/christmas-parades-2026-us-cities-dates-times-routes/).

## Regatta of Lights and scenic cruises

The Regatta of Lights is Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, from 6 to 7:30 p.m. Boats gather near Vilano Beach Pier, go south to the Bridge of Lions, then turn back. Other harbors are in our [boat parade schedule](/news/holiday-boat-parades-2026-dates-times-by-city/).

The scenic cruise listing names a 6 p.m. run on Nov. 21 and 6:30 p.m. runs from Dec. 18, 2026, through Jan. 2, 2027. Its page shows conflicting price tables, so no cruise price is printed here; check the booking page.
