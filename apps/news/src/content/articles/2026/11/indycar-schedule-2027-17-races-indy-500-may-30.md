**The 2027 IndyCar season has 17 races, opening on the Streets of St. Petersburg on Sun., Mar. 7, 2027, and ending at WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca on Sun., Sept. 5, 2027, according to IndyCar's 2027 schedule.** The 111th Indianapolis 500 is Sun., May 30. IndyCar released the schedule in two phases, on Aug. 12 and Oct. 23, 2026, and start times are not yet announced.

The table below lists every race with its weekday, computed from the dates IndyCar published, and a track type that is our own count from the venue names. A one-page copy is in the downloads box with this story. To fit the races against other plans, use the [2027 printable yearly calendar](/printable-yearly-calendar.html).

## What is the full 2027 IndyCar race schedule?

| Race | Date | Event and track | City | Track type (our count) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sun., Mar. 7 | Streets of St. Petersburg | St. Petersburg, Florida | Street |
| 2 | Sat., Mar. 13 | Phoenix Raceway | Avondale, Arizona | Oval |
| 3 | Sun., Mar. 21 | Streets of Arlington | Arlington, Texas | Street |
| 4 | Sun., Apr. 4 | Barber Motorsports Park | Birmingham, Alabama | Road course |
| 5 | Sun., Apr. 18 | Streets of Long Beach | Long Beach, California | Street |
| 6 | Sat., May 15 | Indianapolis Motor Speedway (IMS) Road Course | Indianapolis | Road course |
| 7 | Sun., May 30 | 111th Indianapolis 500 | Indianapolis | Oval |
| 8 | Sun., June 6 | Chevrolet Detroit Grand Prix, Streets of Detroit | Detroit, Michigan | Street |
| 9 | Sun., June 13 | World Wide Technology Raceway | Madison, Illinois, near St. Louis | Oval |
| 10 | Sun., June 27 | Road America | Elkhart Lake, Wisconsin | Road course |
| 11 | Sun., July 4 | Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course | Lexington, Ohio | Road course |
| 12 | Sun., July 18 | Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya | Barcelona, Spain | Road course |
| 13 | Sun., Aug. 1 | Portland International Raceway | Portland, Oregon | Road course |
| 14 | Sun., Aug. 15 | Streets of Markham (Ontario Honda Dealers Indy at Markham) | Markham, Ontario | Street |
| 15 | Sat., Aug. 28 | Milwaukee Mile, Race 1 | West Allis, Wisconsin | Oval |
| 16 | Sun., Aug. 29 | Milwaukee Mile, Race 2 | West Allis, Wisconsin | Oval |
| 17 | Sun., Sept. 5 | WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca | Monterey, California | Road course |

IndyCar describes Phoenix as a "1-mile desert oval" and the Indianapolis 500 is run at Indianapolis Motor Speedway. The other track types come from the venue names: Streets of St. Petersburg, Arlington, Long Beach, Detroit and Markham are street circuits, and the road courses are Barber, the IMS Road Course, Road America, Mid-Ohio, Barcelona, Portland and Laguna Seca. By that count the season has 5 oval races, 5 street races and 7 road-course races.

## When is the 2027 Indy 500?

IndyCar says the 111th Running of the Indianapolis 500 presented by Gainbridge is set for Sunday, May 30, 2027, race 7 of 17. The road-course race at IMS comes two weeks earlier, on Sat., May 15. Dates for Indy 500 practice, qualifying and Carb Day are not in the announcements we read. Phase Two also mentions three additional telecasts during the two days of qualifying for the Indianapolis 500, without dates. ## How does the 2027 IndyCar season break down by month?

These counts are ours, taken from the race dates above.

| Month | Races | Race days |
|---|---|---|
| March | 3 | Mar. 7, 13 and 21 |
| April | 2 | Apr. 4 and 18 |
| May | 2 | May 15 and 30 |
| June | 3 | June 6, 13 and 27 |
| July | 2 | July 4 and 18 |
| August | 4 | Aug. 1, 15, 28 and 29 |
| September | 1 | Sept. 5 |

The longest break between races is 27 days, from Long Beach on Apr. 18 to the IMS Road Course on May 15. Fourteen races are on Sundays and three are on Saturdays: Phoenix on Mar. 13, the IMS Road Course on May 15 and Milwaukee Mile Race 1 on Aug. 28. The race at Mid-Ohio falls on July 4, which is Independence Day.

## Which 2027 IndyCar races are new or notable?

IndyCar calls Barcelona on July 18 "the historic addition of Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya," the one race outside North America. Markham, Ontario, hosts the only race in Canada on Aug. 15. Phoenix on Mar. 13 is described as "the series' return to historic Phoenix Raceway." Road America on June 27 will be the series' 37th race there, and St. Petersburg will be the 17th time the championship opens in the Sunshine City. The Milwaukee Mile hosts what IndyCar calls "a pivotal doubleheader Saturday and Sunday, Aug. 28-29," and the season ends on Sunday, Sept. 5.

## Where can you watch the 2027 IndyCar season?

IndyCar says that for the third consecutive season, all races will be broadcast on FOX in the United States.

## What is not yet announced for 2027?

IndyCar says tune-in times for next season's events will be announced at a later date, so no race has a start time yet. Indy 500 practice, qualifying and Carb Day dates are also not yet listed in the announcements. This story will be updated when IndyCar publishes them.
