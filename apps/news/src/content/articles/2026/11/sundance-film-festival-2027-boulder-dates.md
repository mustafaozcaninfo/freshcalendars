**The 2027 Sundance Film Festival takes place Jan. 21-31, 2027, in Boulder, Colorado, with at-home online screenings from Thursday, Jan. 28, to Sunday, Jan. 31, the Sundance Institute says.** It is the festival's first year in Boulder. The 2026 edition was held in Park City and Salt Lake City, Utah, from Jan. 22 to Feb. 1, per the Institute's 2026 guide. The film lineup "will be announced in December," with no day given as of Nov. 4, 2026.

## Sundance 2027 festival calendar by day

The Institute's pages give these dates. It has not posted screening times, so the table lists days only.

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Jan. 8, 2027 | Single film tickets go on sale | Institute, Oct. 21, 2026 |
| Thu., Jan. 21 | Festival opens; world premieres begin; press and industry (P&I) screenings begin | Institute, July 29, 2026 |
| Fri., Jan. 22 | Festival gala | Institute, July 29, 2026 |
| Tue., Jan. 26 | Last day of world premieres | Institute, July 29, 2026 |
| Wed., Jan. 27 | Legacy program begins (runs through Sun., Jan. 31); last day of P&I screenings at Cinemark Century | Institute, Oct. 21 and July 29, 2026 |
| Thu., Jan. 28 | At-home online screenings begin | Institute, July 29, 2026 |
| Fri., Jan. 29 | Jury and audience awards announced | Institute, July 29, 2026 |
| Sun., Jan. 31 | Festival and online screenings end | Institute, July 29, 2026 |

The Institute wrote that "World premieres will be held Thursday, January 21, to Tuesday, January 26," and that the Legacy program, announced Oct. 21, 2026, runs from Jan. 27 through Sunday, Jan. 31. Jan. 27, 2027, falls on a Wednesday. The festival spans 11 days, counting both Jan. 21 and Jan. 31.

## Can you watch Sundance 2027 online?

Yes, for the last four days. The Institute lists "at-home online screenings from Thursday, January 28, to Sunday, January 31." That window opens the day before the awards announcement on Friday, Jan. 29. The Institute pages we used do not say which films will be online, so the choice waits for the December lineup.

## When do Sundance 2027 tickets go on sale?

Single film tickets go on sale starting Jan. 8, 2027, the Institute said in its Oct. 21, 2026 announcement. In-person ticket packages and passes and online ticket packages and passes were already on sale at that time. We did not find prices on the Institute pages we used, so none are listed here.

## When is the Sundance 2027 lineup announced?

December 2026, per the Institute's July 29, 2026 post. The Institute has not given a day. This story will carry the date once the Institute announces it.

## Where are the Sundance 2027 venues in Boulder?

The Institute says its venues "are all within about a 2-mile radius." Its Feb. 24, 2026 venue list groups them by use.

| Type | Venues |
|---|---|
| Theaters | Boedecker Theater (Dairy Arts Center); Boulder High School Auditorium; Boulder Theater; Casey Middle School Auditorium; Chautauqua Auditorium; Cinemark Century Boulder; eTown Hall; Gordon Gamm Theater (Dairy Arts Center); Macky Auditorium Concert Hall (University of Colorado (CU) Boulder); Muenzinger Auditorium (CU Boulder); Roe Green Theatre (CU Boulder) |
| Talks | Canyon Theater (Boulder Public Library); Dairy Arts Center; eTown Hall; Old Main (CU Boulder) |

The Institute pages we used do not list street addresses.
