**Peloton's Black Friday sale takes up to $1,300 off its equipment, according to onepeloton.com on Nov. 25, 2026, with the Bike+ at $1,795 and new Treads from $1,995.** Peloton has not posted an end date; the pages say only "Limited time offer; subject to change." Financing at 0% annual percentage rate (APR) ends Jan. 12, 2027 for those who qualify. The table gives each machine's regular price, discount, sale price and 36-month payment, and a one-page [printable price list](#print-the-peloton-black-friday-2026-price-list) holds the same numbers.

## Peloton Black Friday 2026 prices for each Bike, Tread and Row+

The sale price is the figure Peloton uses as the base for its financing line ("Based on a price of ..."), and the discount comes from each product page's footnote ("Black Friday Sale: Get $X off"). Black Friday itself is Friday, Nov. 27; see [when Black Friday is](/when-is/black-friday/). The monthly amounts are Peloton's 0% APR payments over 36 months, before membership, taxes and fees.

| Machine | Regular price | Off | Sale price | Per month for 36 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bike | $1,695 | $400 | $1,295 | $35.97 |
| Bike+ | $2,695 | $900 | $1,795 | $49.86 |
| Tread Flex | from $2,195 | $200 | $1,995 | $55.42 |
| Tread Vision | from $3,495 | $700 | $2,795 | $77.64 |
| Tread+ Vision | from $6,695 | $1,300 | $5,395 | $149.86 |
| Row+ | $3,495 | $650 | $2,845 | $79.03 |
| Refurbished Bike+ | $1,395 | $400 | $995 | $27.64 |

The Tread+ Vision footnote adds a $299 assembly fee. The $1,995 Tread Flex is the "New Treads starting at $1,995" line on the deals page, and the Tread+ Vision's $1,300 is the "up to" figure in the headline. The refurbished Bike+ is listed "while supplies last"; Peloton's refurbished page also shows a refurbished Bike at $1,145 with no discount. Peloton also lists $50 off the Starter Accessories Set and $100 off the Ultimate Accessories Set for the Bike and Bike+.

## What does a Peloton cost in the first year with the membership?

Peloton's page says the All-Access Membership is required "to access all Peloton content and applicable features" on its hardware, and the membership page lists it at $49.99 a month. The deals page mentions 1 month free on machine rows. As one example, our calculation for a Bike+ at the $1,795 sale price is 11 paid months at $49.99, or $549.89, which brings the first year to $2,344.89 before tax and delivery fees. The same sum for the $1,295 Bike is $549.89 plus $1,295, or $1,844.89.

## When does the Peloton 0% APR offer end?

Peloton says: "Limited-time offer of 0% APR for all term lengths ends January 12, 2027 for those who qualify." The offer is subject to credit check and approval, and the rate "will be 0% APR or 4.99% APR based on eligibility." The Peloton sale itself has no end date on the pages we read, so Jan. 12 is the only deadline Peloton states. Put it on a [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html).

## Can you return a Peloton bought on sale?

Peloton's 30-day home trial says: "If it's not for you, we'll pick it up and refund your order, excluding the return fee." It is for first-time purchasers, one per household, in the 48 contiguous states. Delivery fees may apply in remote areas such as Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico. The pages we read give no holiday delivery cutoff, and no refurbished Tread price.
