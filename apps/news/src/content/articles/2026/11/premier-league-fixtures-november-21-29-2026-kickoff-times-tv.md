## Why is Nov. 21 the first Premier League round after a break?

The Premier League lists an international break on 9-17 Nov, between Matchweek 10 and Matchweek 11, so Saturday, Nov. 21 is the first round after it. Matchweek 11 runs Nov. 21-23 and Matchweek 12 runs Nov. 27-29, per NBC Sports.

## What time is Liverpool v Manchester United?

Liverpool host Manchester United on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 4:30pm UK time, on Sky Sports in the UK, per the Premier League. That is 11:30 a.m. ET and 8:30 a.m. PT. NBC Sports has not yet listed a US network for the match.

## What time is Everton v Liverpool?

The league lists Everton v Liverpool on Sunday, Nov. 29 at 12pm UK time on TNT Sports. That is 7 a.m. ET and 4 a.m. PT, the earliest start of the 17 timed matches here, on the same calendar day in both zones.

## What time is Arsenal v Manchester City?

Arsenal v Manchester City closes Matchweek 12 on Sunday, Nov. 29 at 4:30pm UK time, on Sky Sports, per the Premier League. That is 11:30 a.m. ET and 8:30 a.m. PT. Earlier in the round, Manchester City play Fulham on Saturday, Nov. 21 at 12:30pm UK time (7:30 a.m. ET) on TNT Sports, and Arsenal travel to Newcastle United the same day at 5:30pm (12:30 p.m. ET) on Sky Sports.

## Which matches have no kick-off time yet?

Three matches have no time on the league's fixtures page: Tottenham Hotspur v Ipswich Town on Saturday, Nov. 21, and Leeds United v Coventry City and Manchester United v Brentford on Saturday, Nov. 28. The league's TV announcement page lists the next block of live-TV selections, for January, in the week commencing Nov. 30, which is approximate. Check the fixtures page before you plan around any of them.

## How can you watch Premier League matches in the US?

NBC Sports says that in the USA you can watch all 380 Premier League games during the 2026-27 season across NBC, USA and Peacock. For Nov. 21 it lists Bournemouth v Nottingham Forest, Aston Villa v Sunderland, Chelsea v Leeds United and Coventry City v Crystal Palace at 10 a.m. ET, "Watch live on Peacock and Multiview". For the other matches it shows the US channel as not yet announced.
