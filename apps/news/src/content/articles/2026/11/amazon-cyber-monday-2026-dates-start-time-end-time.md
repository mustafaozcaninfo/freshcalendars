**Amazon's Cyber Monday 2026 is Monday, Nov. 30, and deals drop at midnight and 8 a.m. PT that day, Amazon's press release says, which is 3 a.m. and 11 a.m. ET by our conversion.** Amazon's Cyber Monday guide adds that its Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deal events "kick off at 12:01 a.m. PST on November 19 and run through November 30." The event is already under way, and Cyber Monday is its last day.

For the other days of the sale, see our story on [Amazon's Black Friday 2026 dates](/news/amazon-black-friday-2026-sale-dates-when-does-it-start/). This one covers the Cyber Monday hours, the Prime rules and what Amazon has not said.

## What time do Amazon's Cyber Monday 2026 deals drop?

Amazon's Nov. 9 release says "For the first time ever, deals will drop twice on both Black Friday and Cyber Monday at midnight and 8 a.m. PT." Pacific Standard Time (PST) is in effect on Nov. 30, so the conversion below is a fixed offset. Amazon gave the times in Pacific time only; the other columns are our conversion.

| Zone | First drop | Second drop |
|---|---|---|
| Pacific (PT) | 12:00 a.m. Mon., Nov. 30 | 8:00 a.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 1:00 a.m. | 9:00 a.m. |
| Central (CT) | 2:00 a.m. | 10:00 a.m. |
| Eastern (ET) | 3:00 a.m. | 11:00 a.m. |
| Alaska | 11:00 p.m. Sun., Nov. 29 | 7:00 a.m. Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Hawaii | 10:00 p.m. Sun., Nov. 29 | 6:00 a.m. Mon., Nov. 30 |

The same two drops happen on Black Friday, Fri., Nov. 27. For Alaska and Hawaii, the first Cyber Monday drop lands on Sunday night.

## When does Amazon's Cyber Monday sale end?

Amazon says the events "run through November 30" and has not published an end hour. Its Nov. 9 release lists the dates as November 19-30, and its deals page says the events "run now through November 30." Whether sales close at 11:59 p.m. PT or earlier on Nov. 30 is not announced. Single deals can end sooner, so check each deal's own countdown.

The sale lasts 12 days if you count Nov. 19 and Nov. 30. Our [Cyber Monday 2026 guide by store](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/) lists how other retailers set their windows.

## Do you need Prime for Amazon Cyber Monday deals?

Amazon's pages do not say that Cyber Monday deals in general need Prime. Some offers do. The release says the extra 7% back on eligible Amazon.com purchases requires an "Eligible Prime membership." Others are open to everyone: "all Amazon customers can access up to 75% off select subscriptions and bundles" on Prime Video.

| Membership | Price, per Amazon |
|---|---|
| Prime | $14.99 a month or $139 a year; free 30-day trial if eligible |
| Prime for Young Adults (ages 18 to 24) | Six months free, then $7.49 a month or $69 a year |
| Prime Access (qualifying government-assistance or income-verified customers) | $6.99 a month after a 30-day trial |

Amazon says the discounted options carry the same benefits at 50% of the cost.

## What is Amazon's return window for Cyber Monday orders?

Amazon's return policy page says most items bought between Nov. 1 and Dec. 31, 2026 can be returned through Jan. 31, 2027. Apple-branded products bought in that window can be returned through Jan. 15, 2027. Check your order's return date in your Amazon account. For other stores, see our [holiday return policies by store](/news/holiday-return-policies-2026-return-deadlines-by-store/).

- Thu., Nov. 19: events started at 12:01 a.m. PST (3:01 a.m. ET).
- Fri., Nov. 27: Black Friday, drops at midnight and 8 a.m. PT.
- Mon., Nov. 30: Cyber Monday, drops at midnight and 8 a.m. PT; events run through this day.
