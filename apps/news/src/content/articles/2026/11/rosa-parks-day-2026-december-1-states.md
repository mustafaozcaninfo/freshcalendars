**Rosa Parks Day is Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026, under the laws of Ohio, Texas and Tennessee,** which each name Dec. 1 in their statutes. Missouri's law points to Feb. 4 and Michigan's to the first Monday after it, Feb. 8, 2027. None of the laws quoted here closes state offices or gives a day off. The table below lists all five states, and a free printable carries the same rows.

## Rosa Parks Day by state, 2026-27

Each row below comes from the state's own statute or bill page. Our check of official statute pages found no Rosa Parks Day law we could quote for other states. The weekdays are our calculation.

| State | Date in 2026-27 | What the law calls it | Citation and year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ohio | Tue., Dec. 1, 2026 | "Rosa Parks Day" | Ohio Revised Code 5.2231, effective Apr. 14, 2006 |
| Texas | Tue., Dec. 1, 2026 | "Rosa Parks Day" | HB 3481, 87th Legislature, effective Sept. 1, 2021 |
| Tennessee | Tue., Dec. 1, 2026 | "Mrs. Rosa L. Parks Day," a day of special observance | SB 500, 111th General Assembly, Public Chapter 122, signed Apr. 15, 2019 |
| Missouri | Thu., Feb. 4, 2027 | "Rosa Parks Day," proclaimed by the governor | RSMo 9.163, effective Aug. 28, 2006 (H.B. 1256) |
| Michigan | Mon., Feb. 8, 2027 | "Mrs. Rosa L. Parks day" | Michigan Compiled Laws (MCL) 435.111, Act 28 of 1997 |

## Why does Rosa Parks Day have two dates?

Ohio's statute says Dec. 1 honors "the woman who helped usher in the modern civil rights movement on that day in 1955 by refusing to give up her seat on a bus in Montgomery, Alabama." Texas HB 3481 uses similar words: Dec. 1 is Rosa Parks Day "in honor of Rosa Louise McCauley Parks for her courageous act of refusing to give up her seat on a Montgomery, Alabama, bus to protest segregation which helped launch the civil rights movement in the United States."

Feb. 4 is the date Missouri's statute sets: "The governor shall issue annually a proclamation setting apart the fourth day of February as 'Rosa Parks Day'." Michigan's law moves the day to a Monday, "the first Monday following February 4 of each year." The statute texts we read do not say why Feb. 4 was chosen, so we do not.

## Is Rosa Parks Day a holiday?

None of the laws quoted here closes state offices or gives a day off. They name the day. Ohio designates it, Texas declares it, Tennessee calls it "a day of special observance," and Missouri's governor proclaims it. Michigan's law "encourages individuals, educational institutions, and social, community, religious, labor, and business organizations to pause on Mrs. Rosa L. Parks day and reflect upon..." in the statute's words. Check your own employer or school for hours.

## What else is on the calendar around Rosa Parks Day?

Feb. 4 and Feb. 8 both fall in Black History Month. Later in December come Human Rights Day on [Thursday, Dec. 10](/news/human-rights-day-2026-december-10/) and [Bill of Rights Day on Dec. 15](/news/bill-of-rights-day-2026-december-15/).
