## How Strands works

The board is six letters wide and eight tall. Theme words are made by linking letters that touch, up, down, sideways or diagonally, and every letter is used exactly once. One special answer, the spangram, names the theme and stretches from one side of the board to the opposite side. Find three non-theme words and you earn a hint that highlights the letters of a theme word.

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: It is what a trainer calls a set of muscles worked together, like "legs" or "back."
- Hint 2: It is two words, of six and five letters.
- Hint 3: Eleven letters, and the first letter is M.

## Theme word hints

There are six theme words, and each one is a muscle or a set of muscles, often in its gym nickname:

- **4 letters, starts with L:** the wide muscles of the back, short form.
- **4 letters, starts with P:** the chest muscles, short form.
- **5 letters, starts with Q:** the front of the thighs, short form.
- **6 letters, starts with B:** the muscles at the front of the upper arm.
- **8 letters, starts with O:** the muscles along the sides of the waist.
- **10 letters, starts with H:** the muscles at the back of the thighs.

## Solving tips

Start with the top row. One theme word fills it from end to end, spelled from right to left. Then find the spangram. It starts at the left edge in the second row, first column, and runs down the first column to the fourth row. From there it zigzags to the right through the third, fourth and fifth rows, crosses the fourth row to the right edge and ends one row up, in the third row, sixth column. The longest theme word has ten letters, only one fewer than the spangram, so length alone will not tell them apart. The six theme words use 37 letters, and with the spangram they fill all 48 tiles. If you get stuck, spell any ordinary words you can see to earn a hint.
