**The first 2026 party night for Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party is Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, at Magic Kingdom, 7 p.m. to midnight Eastern Time (ET), according to Walt Disney World.** The event is held select nights through Tuesday, Dec. 22, 25 nights in all. The table below lists every night with its weekday and whether the $10 Annual Passholder and Disney Vacation Club (DVC) discount applies, and a printable calendar carries the same dates.

## Which nights is Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party held in 2026?

Walt Disney World lists 12 nights in November (Nov. 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27 and 29) and 13 in December (Dec. 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20 and 22). Counting the weekdays for 2026, six nights are Sundays, six are Tuesdays, six are Fridays and five are Thursdays. Monday, Nov. 9 and Wednesday, Nov. 25 are the only nights on those weekdays, and none of the nights is a Saturday.

Two dates sit next to holidays. Nov. 25 is the night before Thanksgiving, and Nov. 27 is Black Friday. For the date of the holiday itself, see [when is Christmas Day](/when-is/christmas-day/).

## What time does the party start, and when can you enter?

The party is a five-hour event from 7 p.m. to midnight. Ticketholders are admitted to Magic Kingdom as early as 4 p.m. on the valid date of their ticket, according to Walt Disney World. The event tickets are date-specific and do not require an additional day theme park ticket or theme park reservation.

## How much do Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party tickets cost in 2026?

| Item | Price or rule | Source detail |
|---|---|---|
| Event ticket | $189 to $229 per ticket, plus tax | Price varies by event date |
| Children ages 3 to 9 | Save $10 per ticket | Walt Disney World |
| Annual Passholders and DVC Members | Save $10 per ticket | Nov. 8 to 20 and Nov. 29 to Dec. 3 |

The page does not list a price for each night, so this story does not either. The Passholder and DVC discount covers 11 of the 25 nights. Disney does not say here whether it can be combined with the children's discount.

## What is included at the party?

Walt Disney World names these shows on the event page:

- Mickey's Once Upon a Christmastime Parade
- Minnie's Wonderful Christmastime Fireworks Show over Cinderella Castle
- Frozen Holiday Surprise, with Elsa, Anna, Olaf, Kristoff and the Snowgies at Cinderella Castle

Minnie's Wonderful Christmastime Fireworks Dessert Party at Tomorrowland Terrace is an add-on that is purchased separately. Show times are not given on the page we checked.
