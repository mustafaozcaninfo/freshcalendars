**A 2027 vision board needs a plan beside it, so our free goal worksheet pairs one year goal page with 12 check-ins, one on the last day of each month from Sunday, Jan. 31 to Friday, Dec. 31, 2027.** The advice behind it comes from the National Institutes of Health (NIH): "Write down the steps that will help you to achieve them," and "A journal, app, or online diary will help keep your focus." (Wellness@NIH, "Maintaining Healthy Habits," Feb. 3, 2020).

The NIH page also says a habit is easier to keep when the goal matters to you: "If the goal matters to you and resonates with your present needs and interests." That is why the goal page asks for the reason as well as the goal. The check-in dates and the layout are our own choices, not an NIH or government method.

## What goes on the 2027 goal page?

The page has six lines to fill in: the goal in one sentence, why it matters to you, three written steps, and where you keep notes (a journal, app or online diary, the three options NIH names). Pin the vision board images next to it, on a cork board or a wall, so the pictures and the written steps sit together.

## Monthly check-in dates for 2027

We set each check-in on the last day of the month and computed the weekday for every date. The right column adds the 2027 federal holidays from the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) in the same month, which helps if you want to check in during a long weekend.

| Check-in | Date | OPM federal holiday that month |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sun., Jan. 31 | Fri., Jan. 1 (New Year's Day); Mon., Jan. 18 (Martin Luther King Jr. Day) |
| 2 | Sun., Feb. 28 | Mon., Feb. 15 (Washington's Birthday) |
| 3 | Wed., Mar. 31 | None |
| 4 | Fri., Apr. 30 | None |
| 5 | Mon., May 31 | Mon., May 31 (Memorial Day) |
| 6 | Wed., June 30 | Fri., June 18 (Juneteenth, observed; June 19 is a Saturday) |
| 7 | Sat., July 31 | Mon., July 5 (Independence Day, observed; July 4 is a Sunday) |
| 8 | Tue., Aug. 31 | None |
| 9 | Thu., Sept. 30 | Mon., Sept. 6 (Labor Day) |
| 10 | Sun., Oct. 31 | Mon., Oct. 11 (Columbus Day) |
| 11 | Tue., Nov. 30 | Thu., Nov. 11 (Veterans Day); Thu., Nov. 25 (Thanksgiving) |
| 12 | Fri., Dec. 31 | Fri., Dec. 24 (Christmas, observed; Dec. 25 is a Saturday) |

Four of the 12 dates fall on a weekend: Jan. 31, Feb. 28, July 31 and Oct. 31. Check-in 5 lands on Memorial Day itself, Monday, May 31. Move any of them by a day or two; the boxes on the sheet have room for your own date.

## Can I check in on the full Moon instead?

You can. The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) lists the 2027 full Moons in Eastern Time: Jan. 22, Feb. 20, Mar. 22, Apr. 20, May 20, June 18, July 18, Aug. 17, Sept. 15, Oct. 15, Nov. 13 and Dec. 13. Write them over the month-end dates if you like a mid-month rhythm.
