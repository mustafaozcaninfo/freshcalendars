**The New York State Public High School Athletic Association (NYSPHSAA) lists its football championships for Friday and Saturday, Dec. 4 and 5, 2026, at Syracuse University's domed stadium in Syracuse.** The 10 semifinals come first, on Nov. 27 and 28, at Middletown High School (East) and Vestal High School (West). NYSPHSAA's page gives dates and places but no times; the times below come from the Times Union of Albany, the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network and the Utica Observer-Dispatch, and each is labeled by how many sources back it.

## When are the NYSPHSAA football semifinals?

NYSPHSAA's schedule lists "Football - East Semifinals" at Middletown High School and "Football - 8-Man Regional Championship & West Semifinals" at Vestal High School, both "Fri, Nov 27 - Sat, Nov 28". The Times Union's Nov. 24, 2026 bracket story gives the game times, and they match the NFHS Network's event listings. The two sites play the same class at the same time. Numbers in parentheses are seeds as the Times Union gives them.

| Day | Class | Time (ET) | East, at Middletown | West, at Vestal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 27 | D | 3 p.m. | Greenwich (2) vs. Tuckahoe (1) | Dolgeville (3) vs. Randolph (6) |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | A | 6 p.m. | Burnt Hills-Ballston Lake (2) vs. Somers (1) | Auburn (3) vs. Hilton (5) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | C | noon | Schuylerville (2) vs. Bronxville (1) | Chenango Forks (4) vs. Chautauqua Lake/Westfield/Brocton (6) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | B | 3 p.m. | Glens Falls (2) vs. Westlake (1) | Maine-Endwell (4) vs. East/World of Inquiry (5) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | AA | 6 p.m. | Albany Christian Brothers Academy (2) vs. Mamaroneck (1) | Syracuse Christian Brothers Academy (3) vs. Rush-Henrietta (5) |

## Which day is my class's final in Syracuse?

Classes D and A play Friday, Dec. 4; Classes C, B and AA play Saturday, Dec. 5. Only the Class D time has two sources: the NFHS Network lists the "2026 NYSPHSAA Class D Football Championship" for "Dec 4, 2026, 8:00 PM UTC", which is 3 p.m. ET, and the Utica Observer-Dispatch's Nov. 13, 2026 bracket story agrees. The other four times are in the Utica paper alone, and NYSPHSAA has not posted them.

| Day | Class | Time (ET) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Dec. 4 | D | 3 p.m. | NFHS Network and Utica Observer-Dispatch |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | A | 6 p.m. | Utica Observer-Dispatch only |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | C | noon | Utica Observer-Dispatch only |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | B | 3 p.m. | Utica Observer-Dispatch only |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | AA | 6 p.m. | Utica Observer-Dispatch only |

The same slots are what NYSPHSAA used last year. Its Dec. 2, 2025 release listed Class D at 3 p.m. and Class A at 6 p.m. on Friday, Dec. 5, 2025, then Class C at noon, Class B at 3 p.m. and Class AA at 6 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 6. That supports the Utica list but does not confirm 2026.

## Who plays in the finals?

The finalists are the winners of the Nov. 27-28 semifinals, so no finals matchups exist yet. NYSPHSAA's page also lists an "8-Man Regional Championship" at Vestal High School on those days; it does not list an 8-player final at the Dome.

## How can fans watch the NYSPHSAA finals?

NYSPHSAA's Dec. 2, 2025 release says games stream on the NFHS Network, "the official broadcasting partner of the NYSPHSAA State Championships", and the network's page already lists the 2026 Class D final. No other broadcast is announced for 2026.

## Tickets and parking at the Syracuse dome

NYSPHSAA has not announced 2026 ticket prices, a ticket link or parking. In 2025, it said "Ticket prices are $16.00 per person (including all fees) for each day of the competition", sold at Ticketmaster or at the Dome ticket window, and that the Dome is a cashless venue. The same release called 2025 the 32nd straight year the finals returned to the dome.
