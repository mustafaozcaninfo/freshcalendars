**Alzheimer's Society's Elf Day 2026 is Wednesday 2 December,** according to the charity's Elf Day page (read Nov. 7, 2026), and registrations opened on 1 September 2026. Below are the sign-up facts, a countdown with suggested dates, the next four Elf Day dates and a one-page printable planner.

## What date is Elf Day 2026?

The charity's page says: "This year Elf Day will be the 2 December." It also says: "On the first Wednesday of December every year, we celebrate Elf Day." The page describes it as a time when workplaces, clubs, groups of friends and families across the UK come together to make a difference for people affected by dementia. Mark the day on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## How do you sign up for Elf Day?

Per the charity, "Elf Day will be open to registrations from the 1 September 2026." Signing up brings a "free digital fundraising pack, full of tips, ideas and resources." The charity says it is "only offering digital fundraising guides to help reduce our impact on the environment", so there is no printed pack to wait for.

On the sign-up page, organisers "must be over 16 years old". The form offers the digital guide in Welsh and asks whether you host at a school or at work. It lists payment by debit or credit card, cheque, bank transfer or a secure online fundraising page. The Elf Day page also lists phone, cheque, a JustGiving page and an online donation form for paying in.

| Item | What Alzheimer's Society says |
|---|---|
| Date | 2 December 2026 (first Wednesday of December every year) |
| Registrations | Open from 1 September 2026 |
| Who can take part | "Anyone can take part in Elf Day, from workplaces, clubs and schools to groups of friends" |
| Organiser age | Must be over 16 years old |
| Pack | Free digital fundraising pack; no printed guides |
| Paying in | Aim to pay in within 28 days after the activity |
| 2026 target | "We need to raise £115,000" |

The charity says £100 could help fund a Community Dementia Adviser to provide face-to-face support for families. It is registered in England and Wales (296645) and the Isle of Man (1128), according to its help page.

## What Elf Day ideas does the charity suggest?

The page suggests you "dress up as an elf to collect sponsors and donations, run desk games, host bake sales or take on festive challenges." It also mentions fancy dress contests. The page also suggests checking whether your workplace offers matched giving.

## What countdown dates could an organiser use for Elf Day?

These steps are our suggestions, not the charity's rules. Weekdays and day counts are our calculation. The pay-in date counts 28 days from 2 December, the window the sign-up page gives for organisers.

| Suggested date | Days before 2 Dec. | Step |
|---|---|---|
| Mon. 9 November | 23 | Register and read the digital pack |
| Mon. 16 November | 16 | Pick activities and tell your group |
| Mon. 23 November | 9 | Set up a payment route and donation tally |
| Mon. 30 November | 2 | Check costumes, games and bake sale items |
| Wed. 2 December | 0 | Elf Day: run activities and collect donations |
| Wed. 30 December | 28 days after | Pay in by this date to meet the 28-day aim |

## When is Elf Day in 2027 and later?

Elf Day is the first Wednesday of December, so the date moves each year. These dates are our calculation from the charity's rule; the charity has not announced them.

| Year | Elf Day |
|---|---|
| 2026 | Wednesday 2 December |
| 2027 | Wednesday 1 December |
| 2028 | Wednesday 6 December |
| 2029 | Wednesday 5 December |
| 2030 | Wednesday 4 December |
