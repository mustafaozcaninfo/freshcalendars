**Barnes & Noble's Black Friday page offers "up to 50% off the year's biggest reads" ahead of Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, and its homepage banners show 50% off select paperbacks, hardcovers, kids' books, the Criterion Collection and holiday boxed cards (barnesandnoble.com, read Nov. 24).** The pages give no start or end dates. The table below lists every offer, and a one-page printable follows.

The banners say "select" for paperbacks and hardcovers, so the 50% does not cover every title. The gift card page does not state an end date, and we did not read the offer's terms page.

## When does the Barnes & Noble Black Friday sale start and end?

Barnes & Noble does not say. Its deals were live on the site on Tuesday, Nov. 24, 2026, three days before Black Friday. Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26 and Cyber Monday is Monday, Nov. 30; the retailer has announced no Cyber Monday offer. ## Is Barnes & Noble open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Barnes & Noble has not posted Thanksgiving Day or Black Friday store hours for 2026, and we found no 2026 newsroom report on them. Hours are listed per store, so look up your location on stores.barnesandnoble.com before you go. For the holiday itself, see [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

## What does a Barnes & Noble membership add during the sale?

Two memberships appear on Barnes & Noble's membership page.

| | Rewards Membership | Premium Membership |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free, "available separately" | "$39.99 a year" |
| Discount | None listed | 10% "Everyday Membership Discount" on eligible purchases at B&N and Paper Source stores and BN.com |
| Earning | 1 stamp for every $10 spent on eligible items in a single transaction; 10 stamps convert to a $5 reward | Not given |
| Shipping | Not given | Free standard shipping on U.S. orders, exclusions apply |
| Other | Stamps expire after 12 months of account inactivity | Free drink size upgrades at B&N Cafés, a free tote bag each membership year, exclusive offers |

By our arithmetic from the published rule, $100 of eligible spending in a single transaction earns 10 stamps, which convert to a $5 reward. Barnes & Noble's page does not say whether the 10% Premium discount applies to sale items, so do not assume the two combine. At 10% off, a $39.99 membership would need about $400 of eligible purchases to pay for itself, again our arithmetic and before any exclusions.
