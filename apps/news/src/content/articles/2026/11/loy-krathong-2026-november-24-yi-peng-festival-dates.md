**Loy Krathong 2026 centers on the full moon of Tuesday, Nov. 24, according to the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT), which wrote on Nov. 5 that "celebrations" take place "on different dates across the country."** The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) lists that full moon at 9:53 a.m. ET, which is 9:53 p.m. in Thailand. The table below gives nine festivals with their official dates, and a second table converts Thai clock times to ET, CT and PT.

## Loy Krathong 2026 festival dates by city

TAT's newsroom (Nov. 5 and Nov. 18, 2026) and the Thai government portal thailand.go.th list these 2026 events. Dates are as published; organizers can change them.

| Festival | Dates, Nov. 2026 | Where and when | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sukhothai candle festival | 15-24 | Sukhothai Historical Park; show at 7 p.m., extra show Nov. 24 at 8:30 p.m. | TAT, thailand.go.th |
| Bangkok Loi Krathong Festival | 17-29 | Rattanakosin waterways | TAT, Nov. 5 |
| Ayutthaya Loi Krathong Festival | 21-24 | Free, 4:30 p.m. to 10 p.m. daily | TAT, Nov. 18 |
| Tak Loi Krathong Sai Festival | 21-24 | Lan Krathong Sai | TAT, Nov. 18 |
| Loi Krathong Kap Kluai Mueang Mae Klong | 21-24 | Samut Songkhram | TAT, Nov. 18 |
| Kasetsart University Loi Krathong | 23-24 | Kasetsart University | TAT, Nov. 5 |
| Somma Nam Khuen Peng Seng Prathip | 23-24 | Somdet Phra Srinagarindra Park, Roi Et | TAT, Nov. 18 |
| Chiang Mai Yi Peng Festival | 23-25 | Temples, historic sites and the city moat | TAT, Nov. 18 |
| Chiang Mai KhomLoi Sky Lanterns Festival | 24-25 | Cultural Center Lanna, Mae On District | TAT, Nov. 5 |

The Sukhothai festival is the only one of the nine that lists show times on the government portal. The Bangkok festival has the longest run, Nov. 17-29.

## Chiang Mai Yi Peng 2026: dates and sky lanterns

TAT said the Chiang Mai Yi Peng Festival 2026, Nov. 23-25, "will illuminate temples, historic sites and the city moat." It also announced "authorised sky lantern releases on 24–25 November under aviation safety controls in designated areas" and "a grand krathong procession from Tha Phae Gate to Chiang Mai Municipality on 25 November." No clock times for the releases were in the sources used here.

The Nation Thailand reported on Nov. 3 that Chiang Mai airport would limit flights "from 7pm to 1am the following morning on November 24 and 25," and that 108 flights were cancelled from Nov. 21 to 28. Only that newsroom was found reporting it, so check with your airline before you book.

## Loy Krathong 2026 times in ET, CT and PT

Thailand time is UTC+7. In the United States, ET is on standard time (EST), 12 hours behind Thailand, so Thai evening events fall in the morning of the same date in North America. USNO gives the full moon as 14:53 UT.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Sukhothai show | 7:00 p.m. | 7:00 a.m. | 6:00 a.m. | 4:00 a.m. |

## Is Loy Krathong on Nov. 24 or Nov. 25?

TAT says the full moon falls on Nov. 24, and USNO puts it at 9:53 p.m. Thailand time that day. Some travel sites say Nov. 25. TAT's own listings span both days: the Chiang Mai sky lanterns run Nov. 24-25 and the Chiang Mai procession is on Nov. 25. Check each organizer's program for the night you plan to attend. This story did not find an official holiday list that confirms whether Nov. 24 is a public holiday in Thailand.

## What to know before floating a krathong

Pattaya Mail reported on Oct. 21 that "the Ministry of Culture is working with 33 agencies to establish 14 guidelines for Loy Krathong 2026," and that the ministry encourages krathongs made of natural materials. The Nation Thailand reported on Nov. 13 that Bangkok will open 35 parks in 22 districts on Nov. 24 for krathong floating.

For scale, The Nation Thailand reported that the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration collected 391,027 krathongs in 2025. That is a 2025 count, not a 2026 figure.

## When is Loy Krathong 2027?

Thai dates for 2027 and 2028 are not announced. USNO lists the next full moons as Nov. 14, 2027 at 03:26 UT and Nov. 2, 2028 at 09:17 UT; the 2027 full moon in that lunar month is expected in mid-November, and the festival date is not announced.
