**The Howard County Public School System (HCPSS) in Maryland has winter break from Dec. 24, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, and spring break from Mar. 22 to Mar. 29, 2027, by its 2026-2027 School Year Key Dates.** The last scheduled day is Tuesday, June 8, 2027, a 3-hour early dismissal. This is Howard County, Maryland, whose seat is Ellicott City.

The dates come from the system's [2026-2027 Key Dates PDF](https://www.hcpss.org/f/news/calendars/datesonly202627.pdf) and its [Dates Only calendar page](https://www.hcpss.org/calendar/2026-27-dates-only/). A July 2026 HCPSS News post, [calendar adjustments](https://news.hcpss.org/news-posts/2026/07/hcpss-2026-2027-school-year-calendar-adjustments/), reports that the Howard County Board of Education approved changes on June 25.

## Howard County school breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the calendar's dates. Thanksgiving week is the odd one: students have no full school day in it.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving | Wed., Nov. 25 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 (Nov. 23 and 24 are early dismissals) | 3 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter break | Thu., Dec. 24, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 7 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Spring break | Mon., Mar. 22 to Mon., Mar. 29, 2027 | 6 | Tue., Mar. 30, 2027 |

Return days are inferred: the calendar lists no closure on Nov. 30, Jan. 4 or Mar. 30. Compare Maryland neighbors in our [Montgomery County](/news/montgomery-county-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) and [Anne Arundel County](/news/anne-arundel-county-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) calendar stories.

## What does Howard County Thanksgiving week 2026 look like?

The calendar has schools close 3 hours early on Monday, Nov. 23 and Tuesday, Nov. 24. On Nov. 23 it lists elementary and middle school parent/teacher conferences and a high school staff professional day; Nov. 24 is parent/teacher conferences. Wednesday, Nov. 25 is "Schools closed for students – Parent/Teacher Conferences", and Nov. 26-27 are "Schools and offices closed*". Put the week on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) pages when you plan childcare around it.

## Howard County winter break 2026

The calendar line reads "12/24/2026 – 01/01/2027 | Schools closed* – Winter Break (Offices Closed 12/24, 12/25, 01/01)". That is seven weekdays, Thursday, Dec. 24 to Friday, Jan. 1. The calendar lists no closure on Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, the first weekday after the break. For other districts, see the [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

## Howard County spring break 2027

The calendar lists "03/22 – 03/29/2027 | Schools closed* – Spring Break (Offices Closed 03/26, 03/29)". Students are out for six weekdays, Monday, Mar. 22 to Monday, Mar. 29. The asterisk appears on the calendar's closure lines; this story uses it only as the calendar's own mark.

## When is the last day of school in Howard County in 2027?

Tuesday, June 8, 2027 is the "Last Scheduled Day", a 3-hour early dismissal. The calendar adds: "If inclement weather days are used, may become a full day". Monday, June 7 carries the same note.

## How do inclement weather days work in the HCPSS calendar?

The calendar lists "06/09 - 06/16/2027 | May be used as inclement weather days": June 9-11 and June 14-16, six weekdays. The July 2026 HCPSS News post says three additional days, June 14-16, were added, for a total of six inclement weather days. If all six were used, the year would run to Wednesday, June 16, our count. The post also says Jan. 14-15, 2027 are now full days for all students, and Feb. 11-12 are now early dismissals for elementary schools for parent-teacher conferences. The printable lists June 9-16 as unconfirmed.

## When does the 2027-28 HCPSS school year start?

Not yet posted. As of Nov. 4, 2026, the HCPSS calendar page showed no 2027-28 calendar.

## When does the Howard County school year start in 2026-27?

K-12 students start Monday, Aug. 24, 2026, and pre-K/RECC students Thursday, Aug. 27. The free printable puts the system's dates on one page, and our [printable school-year calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html) page has more layouts.
