**T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, according to ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) and LiveNOW from FOX, both dated Nov. 23, 2026.** The three chains and their parent company have not posted holiday hours on its own sites that we could read, so every fact here is attributed to the newsroom that reported it. The third source is a Gray Television station (WBAY).

## Is TJ Maxx open on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

No. ABC7 Los Angeles lists "T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods - Closed" and adds, "All stores will be closed on Thanksgiving Day." LiveNOW from FOX, in a story timed 11:22 a.m. EST on Nov. 23, 2026, writes that all three "are closed on Thanksgiving Day." [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26.

The parent company of T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods says on its homepage, updated September 2026, that it runs "5,200+ stores in 10 countries on 3 continents." Its brand sites and investor pages did not load for us, so the closure is not a company statement.

## Is Marshalls open on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

No. Marshalls is on the closed list at ABC7 and LiveNOW, and WBAY (Gray Television) also lists it as "Closed" in its Black Friday 2026 store hours page, updated Nov. 20, 2026.

## TJ Maxx Black Friday 2026 hours

Only WBAY gives a time: 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. for T.J. Maxx and HomeGoods on Friday, Nov. 27. No second newsroom or company page we could read confirms it, and WBAY's page was first published Nov. 24, 2025, so the time may carry over from last year. For Marshalls, WBAY says only "Regular hours listed at this time." Check your store's page on the brand's site before you go.

For other chains, see the [Target hours](/news/is-target-open-thanksgiving-2026-black-friday-hours/), the [mall hours](/news/are-malls-open-thanksgiving-2026-simon-mall-black-friday-hours/) and the [Black Friday shopping planner](/news/black-friday-2026-shopping-planner-printable-store-opening-times/).

## HomeGoods and Sierra on Thanksgiving

HomeGoods is on the closed list at ABC7, LiveNOW and WBAY. Sierra appears as closed only at WBAY, so that one rests on a single newsroom. For Black Friday, WBAY tells readers to "Check store for holiday hours." None of the three sources lists HomeSense.

## Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and returns

Hours for Thursday, Dec. 24 and Friday, Dec. 25, 2026 are not yet announced. The holiday return window for 2026 and any online Black Friday sale are not yet announced either.
