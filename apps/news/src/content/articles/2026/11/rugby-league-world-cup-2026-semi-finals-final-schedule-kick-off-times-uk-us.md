**Australia and New Zealand meet in the Rugby League World Cup 2026 men's final on Sunday, Nov. 15, at Suncorp Stadium in Brisbane, kicking off at 8:35am Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), according to the tournament's official site, rlwc2026.com, and Sky Sports.** That is 3:35 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), 12:35 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) and 6:35pm Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST) in Brisbane. The women's final is also Australia v New Zealand, at 5:10am GMT, per the International Rugby League (IRL) fixture list.

Tonga beat Samoa 20-18 in the last group game on Nov. 1, the IRL reported, and then lost to New Zealand in a semi-final. The UK is on GMT and US clocks fell back on Nov. 1, so Eastern Time is 5 hours behind GMT and Pacific Time is 8 hours behind. Sydney is on Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT), 11 hours ahead of GMT. Brisbane is 10 hours ahead, because Queensland has no daylight saving time.

## What time are the women's and wheelchair finals?

New Zealand beat Samoa 32-0 in Newcastle and Australia beat Fiji 30-14 in Sydney in the women's semi-finals, the IRL reported. The official site and Sky Sports both show the women's semi-finals at 6:45am GMT and the final at 5:10am GMT.

The wheelchair tournament is finished. On Tuesday, Nov. 10, England beat Australia 86-18 and France beat Ireland 82-26 in the semi-finals, the IRL reported. On Friday, Nov. 13, England beat France 50-38 in the final at the entertainment centre in Wollongong, which began at 8:45am GMT (3:45 a.m. ET), the IRL said. Australia took third place with a 70-24 win over Ireland.

## How did the semi-finalists reach the knockout stage?

The IRL draw has Australia, New Zealand, Fiji and Cook Islands in Group A. Group B is England, Samoa and Lebanon, and Group C is Tonga, Papua New Guinea and France. Group B and Group C teams do not play inside their own pool. The IRL says the top two from Group A go to the semi-finals, and so do the top two teams from a combined table of Groups B and C.

Sky Sports adds that the top team of the combined table faces the Group A runner-up. Its listing put New Zealand in semi-final 1, so the combined-table winner played New Zealand in Newcastle and the second-placed team played Australia in Sydney. That pairing is our reading of Sky Sports' schedule, not a separate IRL statement.

Group A finished with Australia top of Pool A after a 66-6 win over Cook Islands in Townsville, the IRL reported, and New Zealand second after a 78-0 win over Fiji on the Gold Coast. Tonga and Samoa took the two places from the combined table, and England went out in the group stage.

## Where can I watch the Rugby League World Cup 2026 in the UK, US and Australia?

The IRL named Premier Sports as the exclusive broadcaster for the UK and Ireland on Aug. 18, 2026. On Oct. 8, it said every game from the men's, women's and wheelchair tournaments is streamed live on Premier Sports Player in 129 more countries, including major markets such as the United States. Premier Sports also covers Canada. Coverage runs from Oct. 15 to Nov. 15. The IRL also said BBC Radio 5 Live has live coverage of the men's and women's finals on Sunday, Nov. 15, with live text on the BBC Sport website and app.

A US television channel and an Australian broadcaster were not named in the sources we checked, so check your local listings. ## Where are the Rugby League World Cup 2026 knockout games played?

The IRL says Brisbane's Suncorp Stadium hosts the men's and women's finals on Sunday, Nov. 15. The semi-finals were played at McDonald Jones Stadium in Newcastle on Nov. 7 and Allianz Stadium in Sydney on Nov. 8. The wheelchair final was at the entertainment centre in Wollongong on Friday, Nov. 13. The tournament opened at Sydney's Allianz Stadium on Thursday, Oct. 15, with Australia v New Zealand, which Australia won 24-22.
