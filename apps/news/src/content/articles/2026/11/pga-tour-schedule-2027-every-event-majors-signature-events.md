## What is different about The Sentry in 2027?

The Sentry is played at Torrey Pines in San Diego on Jan. 25-30 as a full-field event, and the PGA Tour says it "will feature the only scheduled Saturday finish on Tour." Its week is the only one in the table that ends on a Saturday, so its round days are not computed as Thursday to Sunday. It is the second of three California events to open the year, with The American Express and the AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am.

## Which events are the eight Signature Events in 2027?

The PGA Tour says the eight Signature Events stay the same as 2026, starting with the AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am on Feb. 1-7 and ending with the Travelers Championship on June 21-27. The table lists 700 FedExCup points for each of them. Per the tour, the Cadillac Championship is now March 1-7 at Trump National Doral and the Arnold Palmer Invitational presented by Mastercard moves to March 15-21.

| Signature Event | Tour week | Course | Days after the previous one |
|---|---|---|---|
| AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am | Feb. 1-7 | Pebble Beach Golf Links | First of the year |
| The Genesis Invitational | Feb. 15-21 | Riviera Country Club | 14 |
| Cadillac Championship | March 1-7 | Trump National Doral | 14 |
| Arnold Palmer Invitational presented by Mastercard | March 15-21 | Bay Hill | 14 |
| RBC Heritage | April 12-18 | Harbour Town | 28 |
| Truist Championship | May 10-16 | Quail Hollow | 28 |
| Memorial Tournament presented by Workday | May 31-June 6 | Muirfield Village | 21 |
| Travelers Championship | June 21-27 | TPC River Highlands | 21 |

The gaps are computed from the Monday that starts each tour week. The tour says Signature Events include the top 50 players from the previous year's FedExCup standings plus in-season qualifiers through the Aon Next 10 and Aon Swing 5. The Genesis Invitational, Arnold Palmer Invitational and Memorial Tournament, the three hosted by players, again have a 36-hole cut to the top 50 and ties, plus anyone within 10 shots of the lead.

## What are the 2027 men's golf major dates?

The four major venues in 2027 are Augusta National Golf Club (the Masters), PGA Frisco (PGA Championship), Pebble Beach Golf Links (U.S. Open) and the Old Course at St Andrews (The Open Championship), the PGA Tour says. The tour week dates are in the table; the Thursday-Sunday round days are computed and not confirmed by the tournaments.

| Major | Tour week | Computed round days | Venue | Gap from the previous major |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Masters Tournament | April 5-11 | April 8-11 | Augusta National Golf Club, Augusta, Georgia | First of the year |
| PGA Championship | May 17-23 | May 20-23 | PGA Frisco, Frisco, Texas | 42 days |
| U.S. Open | June 14-20 | June 17-20 | Pebble Beach Golf Links, Pebble Beach, California | 28 days |
| The Open Championship | July 12-18 | July 15-18 | Old Course at St Andrews, Fife, Scotland | 28 days |

The PGA Championship's own site lists "May 17-23, 2027" at Fields Ranch East at PGA Frisco in Frisco, Texas. The Open's site says "Golf's original championship returns to St Andrews" on "11-18 July 2027" for the 155th Open, so the R&A's championship week starts a day before the tour's Monday-to-Sunday July 12-18 listing. The Masters and U.S. Open round days are not confirmed here by Augusta National or the U.S. Golf Association, so check them before booking. Pebble Beach Golf Links also hosts the Pro-Am on Feb. 1-7, alongside Spyglass Hill Golf Course, per the tour. ## Which 2027 PGA Tour events are new or on the same week as another?

The Sompo Championship is the one new event. It is the penultimate event of the regular season, on July 26-Aug. 1 at the North Course at Silverado Resort in Napa, California, which hosted a tour event from 2014 to 2025, the tour says. GO by Raymond James follows on Aug. 2-8 at Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, North Carolina.

Four weeks list two events: Feb. 15-21 (The Genesis Invitational and a TBD event), May 10-16 (Truist Championship and ONEflight Myrtle Beach Classic), July 5-11 (Genesis Scottish Open and the Louisville event at Hurstbourne Country Club) and July 12-18 (The Open Championship and Corales Puntacana Championship). The table lists 300 FedExCup points for the Louisville event, the Myrtle Beach Classic, the Corales Puntacana Championship and the TBD event.

## When are the 2027 FedExCup Playoffs?

The three Playoffs weeks are the FedEx St. Jude Championship (Aug. 9-15, TPC Southwind in Memphis), the BMW Championship (Aug. 16-22, Liberty National in Jersey City) and the Tour Championship (Aug. 23-29, East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta). The tour says the top 70 players advance to the Tour Championship, a 30-man field, where the FedExCup champion is crowned. In 2026, the Tour Championship was Aug. 27-30 at East Lake, the PGA Tour said on Aug. 25, 2026.
