**The Philadelphia 76ers host Chicago on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) in South Philadelphia, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The NBA lists 14 76ers games in November, and a one-page printable of all of them is further down.

The source is the NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF from NBA Communications, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." Its times are in ET, so the table needs no conversion except for the two road games in the Central Time (CT) zone, which also get a CT time. Weekdays are our own calculation. The 76ers' previous game was at Charlotte on Saturday, Oct. 31.

## Which 76ers games count in the NBA Cup?

The schedule flags four 76ers games as Emirates NBA Cup group play: against New York on Fri., Oct. 30, at Cleveland on Fri., Nov. 6, against Miami on Wed., Nov. 25 and at Indiana on Fri., Nov. 27. Group play runs from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, per NBA.com's Aug. 12, 2026 release, and the championship is on Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round, including the Dec. 4 and 5 quarterfinals.

## Back-to-backs and Thanksgiving week in the 76ers' November

The 76ers play two sets of back-to-back games in November, one around mid-month and one that closes the month on the road. These are our own counts from the NBA's schedule:

- **Back-to-backs:** Sun., Nov. 15 and Mon., Nov. 16 (home against Indiana, then at Brooklyn), and Fri., Nov. 27 and Sat., Nov. 28 (both on the road, at Indiana then at Houston).
- **Home and away:** the month splits evenly, seven home games and seven road games, ending with four of the last six on the road.
- **Thanksgiving week:** Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026. The 76ers host Portland on Mon., Nov. 23 and Miami on Wed., Nov. 25 (the night before), then play at Indiana on Fri., Nov. 27 (the day after) and at Houston on Sat., Nov. 28. The schedule lists no 76ers game on Thanksgiving Day itself. Our [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has the holiday.
