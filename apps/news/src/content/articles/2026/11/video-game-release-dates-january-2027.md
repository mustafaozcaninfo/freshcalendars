**Thirteen video games have a January 2027 release date on their publishers' own pages, and six of them land on Thursday, Jan. 28.** PlayStation.Blog has Until Dawn 2 on PS5 for that day, and Nintendo has Metroid Ravenous exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2. The table gives each date a weekday, platforms and the publisher's source, and a second table sorts the games by platform.

## Which January 2027 games come to Switch 2, PS5, Xbox and PC?

Counted from the table above, PC has nine of the 13 games, PS5 has eight, Xbox has five and Switch 2 has three.

| Platform | January 2027 games |
|---|---|
| Nintendo Switch 2 | Danganronpa 2x2 (Jan. 14), Metroid Ravenous (Jan. 28), Witchbrook (Jan. 28) |
| PS5 | Danganronpa 2x2, Stranger Than Heaven, Titan Quest II, Forza Horizon 6, Until Dawn 2, Fate/Extra Record, Witchbrook, Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy |
| Xbox Series X\|S | Danganronpa 2x2, Stranger Than Heaven, Titan Quest II, Witchbrook, Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy |
| PC | Armatus, Danganronpa 2x2, Stranger Than Heaven, Titan Quest II, Prison of Husks, Hollow Home, Witchbrook, Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy, Tropico 7 |

The Steam pages for Armatus, Prison of Husks, Hollow Home and Tropico 7 list PC only, and the sources we read do not name consoles for them. Owlcat Games says the Switch 2 version of Dark Heresy comes later, so it is not in the Switch 2 row. Forza Horizon 6 is listed here for its PS5 version only.

## Which games launch on Jan. 28, 2027?

Six, counted from the table: Until Dawn 2 on PS5, Metroid Ravenous on Switch 2, Fate/Extra Record on PS5 and PS4, Witchbrook, Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy and Tropico 7. No other day in the table has more than two. Chucklefish lists Witchbrook at "$24.99" and Kalypso Media's Steam page lists Tropico 7 at $54.99 for the Standard edition and $69.99 for the Deluxe edition. Nintendo's Sept. 11, 2026 announcement says pre-orders for Metroid Ravenous begin now.

## What time does Forza Horizon 6 launch on PS5 on Jan. 26?

The Irish PlayStation Store shows "26/1/2027 04:00 PM UTC" for the PS5 version. UTC is Coordinated Universal Time, and in January that is 11 a.m. ET. The store lists the game at €69.99 and gives no US price. We found no launch hour for any other January game, so we list none.

## Which January 2027 dates are partly confirmed?

Danganronpa 2x2 has a date for North America and Europe only. Stranger Than Heaven's Sega page lists Japanese yen prices and no US price, so we print none. Titan Quest II goes into full release on Jan. 19 at "$49.99 / €49.99 for PC and $59.99 / €59.99 for Consoles," according to the publisher's Games Press release.
