**The ACT on Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026 has a regular registration deadline of Friday, Nov. 6 and a late deadline of Sunday, Nov. 29, both at 11:59 p.m. Central Time (CT), according to ACT's 2026-27 national test dates page.** That is 36 days from the regular deadline to test day and 13 days from the late deadline (our count). ACT's score page lists online scores on Thursday, Dec. 17 and paper scores on Tuesday, Dec. 22. Print the [checklist](#printable-checklist) and tick it off.

## What are the December 2026 ACT dates?

ACT's test dates page shows five dates for this test. The weekdays and the costs in the last column are our additions; the costs come from ACT's current fee list, an undated page.

| Date | What happens | What missing it costs |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 6 | Regular registration deadline | Nothing extra if you register by this date |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Late registration deadline | $42 late registration fee |
| Mon., Dec. 7 | Photo upload and standby deadline | Standby costs $75, refunded if you are denied admission |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | Test day, in by 8:00 a.m. local time | Late arrivals cannot be admitted; moving to another date costs a $49 change fee |
| Thu., Dec. 17 | Initial online score release | Writing scores may take longer |
| Tue., Dec. 22 | Initial paper score release | Writing scores may take longer |

All registration deadlines run to 11:59 p.m. CT, so a student in the Eastern time zone has until 12:59 a.m. the next day (our conversion).

## When are the December ACT scores released?

The test dates page shows only Dec. 22. The scores page splits it in two: its table of 2026-27 initial score release dates lists Dec. 17, 2026 for online scores and Dec. 22, 2026 for paper scores for the Dec. 12 test. ACT's page does not say which a given student gets, so check your ACT account.

Three lines on the scores page set expectations. "Over 97% of ACT scores are available online within 1-4 weeks after your test date." "Processing writing scores may take longer than the multiple-choice section." And "Your scores will not be reported to score recipients until all scores from the event are available." If you take the optional Writing test, that last line matters for any college deadline. We did not find a college score report date on the pages we used, so check each school's own deadline.

## What does it cost to register for the December ACT?

By ACT's current fee list, the ACT with English, Math and Reading is $70. Adding Science is $5 more ($75), adding Writing is $25 more ($95), and Science plus Writing is $100. Late registration carries a $42 fee, so the base ACT registered between Nov. 7 and Nov. 29 comes to $112 (our sum). Changing the test date costs $49, and standby is $75.

## What should I bring on Dec. 12?

ACT's test day page says: "Plan to be inside the test center no later than 8:00 a.m. to allow time for check-in." That is local time. "Late arrivals cannot be admitted." Bring:

- An acceptable photo ID. It "must be a hard plastic card - paper or electronic IDs are not accepted."
- Sharpened No. 2 pencils with erasers. ACT says no pens or mechanical pencils.
- A permitted calculator, for the math section only.

ACT lists what to leave out too: no textbooks, dictionaries (except approved bilingual), scratch paper or notes; no highlighters, colored pens or correction fluid or tape. Phones, smart watches, fitness bands and devices with Wi-Fi or recording must be off and out of sight.

## How long is the ACT?

ACT's page gives "2 hours and 45 minutes" and "171 questions" for the full test with Science. Writing is separate.

| Section | Minutes | Questions | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 35 | 50 | Yes |
| Math | 50 | 45 | Yes |
| Reading | 40 | 36 | Yes |
| Science | 40 | 40 | Optional |
| Writing | 40 (one essay, after a 5-minute break) | 1 essay | Optional |

Without Science, the three required sections add up to 125 minutes (2 hours 5 minutes) and 131 questions, our arithmetic from the table.
