**Quordle #1765 arrives Tuesday, Nov. 24, 2026, from Merriam-Webster.** You get three nudges for each word, a table that scores five first guesses against all four boards, and the solutions at the very end.

Today's 20 letter slots collapse to only 12 different letters. The rules sit on the [Quordle guide](/news/topic/quordle/#how-it-works), and Monday's solutions are on the [Quordle #1764 page](/news/quordle-hints-answers-november-23-2026-1764/).

## Quordle #1765 hints, word by word

The words below run in alphabetical order, which is not the order of the boards.

**Word 1**

- Hint 1: Opens with the 2nd letter of the alphabet and closes with the 20th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels, next to each other, in the middle.
- Hint 3: A wild creature, often a big and fierce one.

**Word 2**

- Hint 1: Same first and last letter as Word 1.
- Hint 2: Two vowels, and they are the same letter, side by side.
- Hint 3: A shove upward, such as a lift in morale or a rise in a score.

**Word 3**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 4th letter and ends with the 5th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels with two consonants wedged between them.
- Hint 3: A person judged slow to learn, once made to wear a pointed paper cap in class.

**Word 4**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 15th letter and ends with the 25th.
- Hint 2: Only one vowel, and it is the first letter; the 2nd and 3rd letters match.
- Hint 3: In a way that seems strange or unexpected.

## Five first guesses scored on all four boards

Seven of the 20 slots are vowels. G is a green tile, Y a yellow one, scored the way the game colors repeated letters. Columns follow the board layout.

| Opener | Top left | Top right | Bottom left | Bottom right | Colored tiles in all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stare | 1G 0Y | 0G 2Y | 1G 3Y | 0G 0Y | 7 |
| Toast | 0G 0Y | 3G 0Y | 3G 0Y | 0G 1Y | 7 |
| Adieu | 0G 3Y | 0G 0Y | 0G 2Y | 1G 0Y | 6 |
| Crane | 1G 2Y | 0G 0Y | 1G 1Y | 0G 0Y | 5 |
| Lunch | 3G 0Y | 0G 0Y | 0G 0Y | 0G 1Y | 4 |

Stare and Toast tie at seven colored tiles, yet they split differently: Stare gives two greens and five yellows, Toast six greens and one yellow. Four of Stare's seven tiles land on the bottom-left board, and Toast puts three greens on each of two boards.

Lunch is the only opener here that makes three greens on the top-left board, and it leaves two boards fully gray. No opener in the table colors more than one tile on the bottom-right board.

## Letters shared by Dunce, Boost, Beast and Oddly

Boost and Beast hold three letters in common, B, S and T, and both end in S then T. Dunce and Beast share only E, Dunce and Oddly share only D, and Boost and Oddly share only O. Dunce has no letter in common with Boost.

| Letter | Slots used | Words |
|---|---|---|
| D | 3 | Dunce, Oddly (twice) |
| O | 3 | Boost (twice), Oddly |
| B, S, T, E | 2 each | B, S, T in Boost and Beast; E in Dunce and Beast |
| U, N, C, A, L, Y | 1 each | one word each |

Those rows add up to 20 slots and 12 distinct letters. Boost and Oddly are the two words with a doubled letter, O in the first and D in the second. Dunce, Boost and Beast hold two vowels apiece, and Oddly holds one, so the vowel count is seven.

A, U and Y each turn up only once. Toast greens six tiles largely because its S and T sit where the S then T ending falls on two boards.
