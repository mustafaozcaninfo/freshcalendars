**Garland Independent School District (ISD) in Texas has no school for students Nov. 23-27, 2026, for Thanksgiving Break, and Winter Break runs Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, by its 2026-27 Academic Calendar.** Spring Break is Monday-Friday, March 15-19, 2027, and the last day of class is Thursday, May 27, 2027. The district's PDF is marked "Updated: 12/03/25; subject to change."

The next break is Thanksgiving, which starts Monday, Nov. 23. The district's [school year calendar](https://www.garlandisd.net/calendars/school-year-calendar) web page lists the same dates as the PDF, apart from one gap covered below.

## Garland ISD breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates. Return days are inferred from the calendar, which shows no closure on Nov. 30 or March 22 and starts a grading period on Jan. 5.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Classes resume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Break (past) | Mon., Oct. 12 to Fri., Oct. 16, 2026, plus Mon., Oct. 19 | 6 | Tue., Oct. 20, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027, plus Mon., Jan. 4 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon., March 15 to Fri., March 19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 22, 2027 |

## Does Garland ISD have a fall break in 2026?

Yes, and it has already happened. The PDF lists "Oct. 12-16: Fall Break," then "Oct. 19: Staff development/ Student holiday" and "Oct. 20: Second grading period begins." Students were out for eight days in a row, Oct. 12-19. The table on the district's school year calendar web page leaves the fall break out, so a reader who checks only the web page will not see it. The break still counts in the year's totals below.

## How many school days and long weekends does Garland ISD have?

Between the first day, Aug. 10, 2026, and the last day, May 27, 2027, there are 209 weekdays by our count. The calendar takes 34 of them off for students: Labor Day (1), fall break and the Monday after (6), Nov. 3 (1), Thanksgiving Break (5), Winter Break (10 weekdays, Dec. 21 to Jan. 1), Jan. 4 (1), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (1), Feb. 15-16 (2), Spring Break (5), March 26 (1) and April 16 (1). The remaining 175 match the PDF's own totals of "First semester: 82 days" and "Second semester: 93 days."

| Grading period | Dates | Days listed |
|---|---|---|
| First | Aug. 10-Oct. 9, 2026 | 44 |
| Second | Oct. 20-Dec. 18, 2026 | 38 |
| Third | Jan. 5-March 12, 2027 | 46 |
| Fourth | March 22-May 27, 2027 | 47 |

Long weekends are our computation from the dates. Monday-Tuesday, Feb. 15-16, 2027, attach to the weekend, so students are out four days in a row, Feb. 13-16. Easter Sunday falls on March 28, 2027 (our computation), and Friday, March 26 is a holiday, which gives a three-day weekend, March 26-28. Friday, April 16 makes another three-day weekend, April 16-18.

## Garland ISD winter break 2026 and the return day

Winter Break covers Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. The PDF prints it as "Dec. 21-31: Winter Break" and again for the first of January. Students get one more day: Monday, Jan. 4 is a staff development and student holiday. Classes resume Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, the day the third grading period begins. Friday, Dec. 18 is an early dismissal day and the end of the second grading period.

## Garland ISD spring break 2027

Spring Break is March 15-19, 2027. The third grading period ends Friday, March 12, and the fourth begins Monday, March 22. Our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) shows how other districts set theirs.

## When is the last day of school in Garland ISD?

Classes began Monday, Aug. 10, 2026. The last day of class is Thursday, May 27, 2027, an early dismissal day, and the fourth grading period ends that day. The PDF shows Friday, May 28 as a staff development and student holiday and Monday, May 31 as Memorial Day. The district's web page lists the last day as "HS Student early dismissal/staff development," which ties the early dismissal to high school students; the PDF says only "Early dismissal."
