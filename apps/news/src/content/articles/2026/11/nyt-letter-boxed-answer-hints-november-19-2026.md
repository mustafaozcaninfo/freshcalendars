## Which letter pairs never occur today?

Letters on one side cannot touch, which leaves 108 ordered pairs that can. In the list, 20 of them never occur: C then N, J or B; Z then S, T, N, J, R or B; S then Z, J or R; O then J; J then C, Z, S, T, R or B; and R then J. The J sits in 11 of the 20 and the Z in 8, so these two are the awkward letters. Two more pairs occur in exactly one word: B then N, and B then Z.

The longest word has 14 letters and only one word reaches that length. No word covers the box: the widest reach 9 of the 12 letters, and 17 words tie at that figure. Chaining every pair of words that share a join letter and use all twelve letters gives 92 two-word routes.

The routes run from 14 to 24 letters in total, and 81 words in the list have 9 letters.
