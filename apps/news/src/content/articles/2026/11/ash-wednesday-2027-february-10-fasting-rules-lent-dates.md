**Ash Wednesday 2027 is Wednesday, Feb. 10, and Easter Sunday is March 28, according to the U.S. Catholic bishops' 2027 liturgical calendar.** The calendar comes from the Secretariat of Divine Worship of the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB). Below are every Friday in Lent with its rule, Ash Wednesday and Easter through 2031, and a one-page tracker to print.

The day before is Mardi Gras on Feb. 9; the [Mardi Gras and Lent 2027 calendar](/news/mardi-gras-2027-february-9-ash-wednesday-february-10-lent-calendar/) has that date and a Lent printable. For Holy Week openings and closings, see [Easter 2027: Good Friday and Easter Monday](/news/easter-2027-march-28-good-friday-easter-monday-whats-open/). The month pages are the [February 2027 printable calendars](/february-calendars.html) and [March 2027 printable calendars](/march-calendars.html), and [when is Easter](/when-is/easter/) gives the date for any year.

## Which days in Lent 2027 call for fasting or abstinence?

Canon 1251 in the Code of Canon Law reads: "Abstinence from meat, or from some other food as determined by the Episcopal Conference, is to be observed on all Fridays, unless a solemnity should fall on a Friday. Abstinence and fasting are to be observed on Ash Wednesday and Good Friday." The dates below come from the USCCB 2027 calendar.

| Date | Day in the USCCB calendar | Fasting | Abstinence from meat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wed., Feb. 10 | Ash Wednesday | Yes | Yes |
| Fri., Feb. 12 | Friday after Ash Wednesday | No | Yes |
| Fri., Feb. 19 | Lenten Weekday | No | Yes |
| Fri., Feb. 26 | Lenten Weekday | No | Yes |
| Fri., Mar. 5 | Lenten Weekday | No | Yes |
| Fri., Mar. 12 | Lenten Weekday | No | Yes |
| Fri., Mar. 19 | Saint Joseph, Spouse of the Blessed Virgin Mary (solemnity) | No | No, under canon 1251 |
| Fri., Mar. 26 | Good Friday | Yes | Yes |

The Friday that stands out is March 19, 2027. The USCCB calendar lists the Solemnity of Saint Joseph on that Friday, and canon 1251 exempts a Friday on which a solemnity falls. Local bishops can give their own guidance, so a parish or diocese may say more than the canon does.

Holy Week has its own days in the same calendar: Palm Sunday on March 21, Holy Thursday on March 25, Good Friday on March 26 and Holy Saturday on March 27. Because Holy Thursday is March 25, the USCCB calendar moves the Annunciation to Monday, April 5, 2027. Pentecost Sunday is May 16, 2027.

## Who has to fast and abstain, and how much can a person eat?

Canon 1252 sets the ages: "The law of abstinence binds those who have completed their fourteenth year. The law of fasting binds those who have attained their majority, until the beginning of their sixtieth year." The USCCB flyer "Lenten Fasting & Abstinence" (2021) puts it as ages 18 to 59 for fasting on Ash Wednesday and Good Friday, and ages 14 and older for abstinence on every Friday during Lent.

The flyer defines fasting this way: "When fasting, a person is permitted to eat one full meal, as well as two smaller meals that together are not equal to a full meal." It lists the physically or mentally ill, and pregnant or nursing women, among those excused.

Ash Wednesday is not on the canon's list of holy days of obligation (canon 1246), yet canon 1251 still names it a day of fasting and abstinence.

## When is Ash Wednesday from 2027 to 2031?

Easter in 2027 and 2028 comes from the USCCB calendars. The 2029 to 2031 rows are computed with the method the U.S. Naval Observatory publishes in "The Date of Easter", counting back 46 days. The Observatory says Easter can never occur before March 22 or later than April 25.

| Year | Ash Wednesday | Easter Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Wed., Feb. 10 | Sun., Mar. 28 |
| 2028 | Wed., Mar. 1 | Sun., Apr. 16 |
| 2029 | Wed., Feb. 14 | Sun., Apr. 1 |
| 2030 | Wed., Mar. 6 | Sun., Apr. 21 |
| 2031 | Wed., Feb. 26 | Sun., Apr. 13 |

The 2028 dates match the USCCB's 2028 calendar, which prints Ash Wednesday on March 1 and Easter on April 16. In 2029 Ash Wednesday lands on Valentine's Day, Feb. 14.

## When is Orthodox Easter in 2027?

The Orthodox Church in America lists Pascha, the Orthodox Easter, on Sunday, May 2, 2027. That is five weeks after the Western date of March 28.
