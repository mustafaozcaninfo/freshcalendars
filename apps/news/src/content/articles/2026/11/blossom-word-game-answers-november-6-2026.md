**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Friday, Nov. 6, 2026, centers on O, with E, F, N, R, S and U around it.** Beebom, Connections Solver and Word.Tips all agree on those letters. This page gives graded hints and a count of the long words first, then keeps every answer for a marked spoiler section.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** O (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** E, F, N, R, S, U

Every word today needs the O in the center; the rules and the scoring are on our [Blossom page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works).

The board holds three vowel letters in all, the center O plus E and U on the petals. F is the one petal that is hard to place, so a lot of good words simply skip it.

## Long-word count before the spoilers

This board is built for length. The three lists agree on 23 words of 9 letters or more, and the longest has 15 letters. Here is how those 23 split by size:

Eleven of the 23 end in the same quality-noun pattern, either a -ness or a -nesses ending, so one ending covers nearly half the long list. None of those 11 needs the petal F, which fits with only four pangrams being confirmed.

## Pangram hints

- **How many:** four pangrams are confirmed by the answer lists we checked.
- **Lengths:** they run 11, 10, 10 and 8 letters.
- **Starting letters:** two begin with F, one with N and one with U.
- **The 8-letter one:** it is a verb form, and it describes going ahead of something else.
- **The 11-letter one:** it is a plural noun for those who come first and signal what follows.

## Today's Blossom pangrams and long words (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it yourself.

- **Forerunners** (11 letters)
- **Nonferrous** (10 letters)
- **Unforeseen** (10 letters)
- **Foreruns** (8 letters)

Each uses O, E, F, N, R, S and U at least once. Four pangrams are confirmed by the answer lists we checked, and the game may not accept every word that solver sites list.

### Long words by word family

All three sources list these words of 9 letters or more, grouped by what they share. Forerunners, Nonferrous and Unforeseen appear in the table too, since they are long enough.

| Family | Words |
|---|---|
| -nesses (plural of a -ness noun) | Erroneousnesses (15), Sensuousnesses (14), Sonorousnesses (14), Onerousnesses (13), Sorenesses (10), Sournesses (10), Onenesses (9) |
| -ness | Erroneousness (13), Sensuousness (12), Sonorousness (12), Onerousness (11) |
| Ending in -ous | Nonsensuous (11), Nonferrous (10), Erroneous (9), Furfurous (9), Susurrous (9) |
| Starting with fore- | Forerunners (11), Forerunner (10), Forenoons (9), Foreseers (9) |
| Everything else | Unforeseen (10), Froufrous (9), Nonsenses (9) |

That is 7 and 4 words in the two -ness groups, 5 ending in -ous, 4 starting with fore-, and 3 others, which adds up to the 23 counted above. Foreruns, at 8 letters, is a pangram but falls below the 9-letter cutoff.
