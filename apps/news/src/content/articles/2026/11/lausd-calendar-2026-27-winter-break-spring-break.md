**Winter recess for Los Angeles Unified School District (LAUSD) students runs Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Friday, Jan. 8, 2027**, according to the district's "Instructional School Calendar 2026-2027." Spring recess is Monday through Friday, March 22-26, 2027, and the last day of instruction is Friday, June 4, 2027.

## LAUSD 2026-27 recesses at a glance

| Break | Dates | Weekdays with no school |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving week | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 |
| Winter recess | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 8, 2027 | 15 |
| Spring recess | Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027 | 5 |

The weekday counts are our arithmetic from the printed dates; the winter count includes Jan. 1. The calendar is the district's single-track (traditional) calendar. It was marked "Board Approved 3/11/2025," and LAUSD's payroll "Tentative Basis Dates" page says the Board of Education approved the 2026-2027 Single-Track Instructional Calendar that day. LAist reported the same day that the board had approved calendars with mid-August starts.

## What to know about the three-week winter recess

The recess covers three school weeks: Dec. 21-25, Dec. 28-31 plus Jan. 1, and Jan. 4-8. The second semester begins Monday, Jan. 11, 2027. Compare that with Clark County School District in Nevada, whose winter break runs Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 and whose classes resume Tuesday, Jan. 5 (see our [Clark County calendar story](/news/san-diego-unified-calendar-2026-27-breaks/)). LAUSD students stay home four more weekdays, Jan. 5-8. For the other large districts, see our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/), and plan the dates on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

One footnote changes the spring. It says: "If a school selects Monday, January 11, 2027, as a Pupil Free Day, then Monday, June 7, 2027, becomes an Instructional Day." So a school that uses Jan. 11 as a pupil-free day gives up the June 7 pupil-free day. Ask your school which it chose.

## When does the LAUSD school year start and end?

Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026 is a Pupil Free Day, and Wednesday, Aug. 12 is the First Day of Instruction. The last day of instruction is Friday, June 4, 2027. The calendar counts 84 instructional days in the fall semester, 96 in the spring semester and 180 in total.

## Is LAUSD closed for the whole Thanksgiving week?

Yes, on this calendar. Monday through Wednesday, Nov. 23-25, 2026 are marked "Unassigned Day (No School)," and Thursday and Friday, Nov. 26-27 are the Thanksgiving Holiday. That is five weekdays, the same count as the spring recess. The Harvard Elementary page for the district's calendar at a glance lists the same week. Our [Thanksgiving break roundup](/news/thanksgiving-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) compares other districts.

## When is LAUSD spring break 2027?

Spring Recess is Monday, March 22 through Friday, March 26, 2027. Farm Workers Day on Wednesday, March 31 is a separate day with no school. See the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html) and our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) for other districts.

## Can the LAUSD calendar change?

A footnote says the Board's adoption of the Instructional Calendars "is tentative and conditioned on and subject to the outcome of negotiations with the unions regarding their members' work year calendars." Check with your school before booking travel. We did not find a 2027-28 calendar, so there are no dates for it yet.
