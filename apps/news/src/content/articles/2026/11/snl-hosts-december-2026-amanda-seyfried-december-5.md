**Amanda Seyfried hosts Saturday Night Live (SNL) on Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, with Don Toliver as musical guest, according to TheWrap and TVLine.** The cold open starts at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC. The tables below add the full season 52 host list, the December calendar with each date's status, and last December's hosts for comparison. A free printable tracker follows.

## Who hosts SNL on Dec. 5, 2026?

TheWrap reported on Nov. 14, 2026 that NBC "has tapped “You Can See Everything” star Amanda Seyfried to host its forthcoming Dec. 5 episode." It added: "The episode will mark Seyfried's “SNL” hosting debut." TVLine, on Nov. 15, wrote that she "will make her "SNL" hosting debut on Saturday, December 5."

Toliver is the musical guest. TheWrap said "It will also see Toliver perform on “SNL” for the first time." Music Times called Seyfried "Season 52's fourth first-time host." Toliver's album NITROUS is due Dec. 4, the day before, according to Stereogum and HotNewHipHop (both Nov. 18, 2026).

## What time is SNL on Dec. 5, and where can you watch?

TVLine says the cold open "begins at 11:30 p.m. ET, which is 10:30 p.m. CT or 8:30 PT," and that the show "airs on NBC and can be streamed live on Peacock." Peacock's own page lists "Saturdays at 11:30p ET/8:30p PT." We computed Mountain Time from the Eastern start.

| Eastern (ET) | 11:30 p.m. |

## Is SNL new on Nov. 21, Nov. 28, Dec. 12 and Dec. 19?

NBC has not announced a new episode for Nov. 21 or Nov. 28, and has not said whether the show is off those nights. Music Times reports "There will be no new episode on November 21 or November 28." TVLine wrote on Nov. 14 that "It's possible the show may take a break until after Thanksgiving." The 2026 Thanksgiving date is on the [when is Thanksgiving page](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

For Dec. 12 and Dec. 19, no host or musical guest is announced. Music Times says more episodes "are expected" on those dates; NBC has not confirmed them in the sources we checked on Nov. 20, 2026.
