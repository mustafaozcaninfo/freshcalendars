**The Carabao Cup quarter-finals are played Dec. 15, 16 and 17, 2026, with Liverpool v Bournemouth at Anfield on Wednesday, Dec. 16 at 8pm Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), which is 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to Liverpool FC.** Manchester City host Bradford City on Tuesday, Dec. 15 and Crystal Palace host Sunderland on Wednesday, Dec. 16. Fleetwood Town v Everton follows on Thursday, Dec. 17, confirmed by Everton FC.

The table has each tie in UK, Eastern and Pacific time with the broadcaster and who confirmed it, and a printable with a score column is further down.

## Carabao Cup quarter-final fixtures and kick-off times

The draw was made on Oct. 28, 2026, and the home side is named first. Goal reported that all four quarter-finals will be played in the week commencing Dec. 14; Everton FC and the Premier League put the Fleetwood tie on Thursday, Dec. 17. UK times are Greenwich Mean Time; Eastern and Pacific times are our conversion.

| Date | Tie (home side first) | UK (GMT) | ET | PT | UK broadcaster | Confirmed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue., Dec. 15 | Manchester City v Bradford City, Etihad Stadium | 8pm | 3 p.m. | 12 p.m. | Sky Sports | Sky Sports and Rush the Kop |
| Wed., Dec. 16 | Crystal Palace v Sunderland, Selhurst Park | 7:30pm | 2:30 p.m. | 11:30 a.m. | Sky Sports | Crystal Palace FC |
| Wed., Dec. 16 | Liverpool v Bournemouth, Anfield | 8pm | 3 p.m. | 12 p.m. | Independent Television (ITV) and Sky Sports | Liverpool FC |
| Thu., Dec. 17 | Fleetwood Town v Everton, Highbury Stadium | 8pm | 3 p.m. | 12 p.m. | Sky Sports and ITV | Everton FC |

Manchester City v Bradford City is the one tie confirmed by newsrooms rather than a club. Sky Sports lists "Manchester City vs Bradford - Tuesday, December 15, 8pm - live on Sky Sports", and Rush the Kop gives the same date, time and channel. Crystal Palace say the tie is "kicking-off at 19:30 GMT" and "will be broadcast live on Sky Sports". Liverpool say it "will be shown live on ITV and Sky Sports in the UK".

## Is Fleetwood Town v Everton confirmed?

Yes. Everton FC said on Nov. 4, 2026 that the quarter-final "away to Fleetwood Town has been confirmed for Thursday 17 December" and "will kick off at 8pm (GMT)." The club says the tie against the League Two side at Highbury Stadium "will be shown live on Sky Sports and ITV." The Premier League moved Nottingham Forest v Everton to Sunday, Dec. 20 (2pm GMT) because of the tie, and puts the cup match on the preceding Thursday, Dec. 17. The Eastern and Pacific times in the table are our conversion of the 8pm GMT kick-off.

## How can you watch the quarter-finals in the US?

CBS Sports holds the U.S. rights to English Football League (EFL) competitions through 2027-28, TV Tech reported on July 18, 2024, and its deal covers "at least 250 EFL matches each season across the Championship, League One, League Two, Carabao Cup". Which platform shows each quarter-final has not been announced. For round four, the EFL's U.S. listings put every tie on Paramount+; we have not seen the same for the quarter-finals.

## How did the last eight get here?

Fleetwood Town drew 1-1 with Arsenal and won 5-3 on penalties, Liverpool drew 1-1 with Chelsea and won 6-5 on penalties, and Manchester City drew 2-2 with Brighton & Hove Albion and won 4-3 on penalties, according to British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC) Sport. Bradford City, Bournemouth, Crystal Palace, Sunderland and Everton won in normal time. Goal describes Manchester City as the holders and Bradford City as a League One side. Every result is in our [Carabao Cup fourth-round results story](/news/carabao-cup-fourth-round-2026-schedule-kickoff-times-tv-us-uk/).

## What comes after the quarter-finals?

The EFL's round dates, reported by The League Paper via Yahoo Sports on June 23, 2026, put the semi-final first legs in the week commencing Monday, Jan. 11, 2027 and the second legs in the week commencing Monday, Feb. 1, 2027. The final is scheduled for Sunday, March 21, 2027. The semi-final draw date has not been announced, and we have not confirmed a venue for the final.
