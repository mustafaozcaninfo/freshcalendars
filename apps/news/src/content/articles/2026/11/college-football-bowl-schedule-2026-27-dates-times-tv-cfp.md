**College football's 2026-27 postseason runs from Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, to Monday, Jan. 25, 2027, with 37 bowl games, the Army-Navy Game and 11 College Football Playoff (CFP) games, according to ESPN's schedule checked Nov. 1.** Below: every date, kickoff in Eastern Time (ET) and channel, a count by network, what the times mean on the West Coast, and a one-page watch sheet.

The matchups do not exist yet. Teams are named on CFP Selection Day, Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, per FOX Sports, so this story lists games and kickoffs; it does not guess who plays.

## How many bowl and playoff games are there, and when?

The counts below come from our tally of ESPN's schedule, not from a separate release. ESPN's own June 3 announcement described "32 games across ESPN, ABC and the ESPN App from Dec. 12-Jan. 2"; the ESPN and American Broadcasting Company (ABC) bowl games in our table (not counting CFP games) also add up to 32.

| Phase | Dates | Games |
|---|---|---|
| Bowl games before Christmas | Dec. 12-24 | 17 bowls, plus the Army-Navy Game |
| CFP first round | Dec. 18-19 | 4 games at campus sites |
| Bowl games after Christmas | Dec. 26-Jan. 2 | 20 bowls |
| CFP quarterfinals | Dec. 30 and Jan. 1 | 4 games |
| CFP semifinals | Jan. 14-15, 2027 | 2 games |
| CFP national championship | Jan. 25, 2027 | 1 game |

The 20 bowls after Christmas are 16 on Dec. 26-31 and four on Jan. 2. Saturday, Dec. 26 has the most games on one day, five. Dec. 23, Dec. 31 and Jan. 2 have four each.

## Which days have no bowl games?

ESPN's schedule has no college bowl games on Friday, Dec. 25 (Christmas Day) or Sunday, Dec. 27. From our count of the table, Dec. 12 to Jan. 2 spans 22 days and 16 have a game; the six without are Dec. 13, 14, 17, 20, 25 and 27. For the days around them, see the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html).
