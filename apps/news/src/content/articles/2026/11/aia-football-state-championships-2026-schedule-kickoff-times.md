**The Arizona Interscholastic Association (AIA) lists its 2026 football state championship games from Saturday, Nov. 28 to Saturday, Dec. 5, with the 4A, 5A, 6A and Open finals on Dec. 4-5 at Arizona State University's (ASU) Mountain America Stadium in Tempe, according to its AZPreps365 championship site.** The 2A and 3A finals are Nov. 28 at Mountain Pointe High School in Phoenix. Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so ET is 2 hours ahead of the Arizona times below. All of the schedule changes if AIA moves a game: AZPreps365 says playoff games are subject to time and date changes and venue relocation.

## AIA football state championship schedule and kickoff times

AZPreps365's "2026-27 State Tournament Schedule" gives the date, time and site of each final. The ET column is computed by Fresh Calendars: Arizona stays on Mountain Standard Time (MST, UTC-7) all year, and ET is UTC-5 after Nov. 1, 2026.

| Class | Date | Kickoff (MST) | Kickoff (ET) | Venue | Matchup (seeds) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2A | Sat., Nov. 28 | 3 p.m. | 5 p.m. | Mountain Pointe High School, Phoenix | No. 1 Scottsdale Christian vs. No. 2 San Tan Charter |
| 3A | Sat., Nov. 28 | 7:30 p.m. | 9:30 p.m. | Mountain Pointe High School, Phoenix | No. 1 Benjamin Franklin vs. No. 2 Round Valley |
| 4A | Fri., Dec. 4 | 3 p.m. | 5 p.m. | ASU Mountain America Stadium, Tempe | Not yet set |
| 5A | Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | 9 p.m. | ASU Mountain America Stadium, Tempe | Not yet set |
| 6A | Sat., Dec. 5 | 12 p.m. | 2 p.m. | ASU Mountain America Stadium, Tempe | Not yet set |
| Open | Sat., Dec. 5 | 6 p.m. | 8 p.m. | ASU Mountain America Stadium, Tempe | Not yet set |

The 1A final was played Saturday, Nov. 14 at Coronado High School in Scottsdale at 6 p.m., per the same schedule. No score is listed in the pages read, so none is given here.

## Which teams are in the 2A and 3A finals?

The AZPreps365 brackets show the semifinal results from Saturday, Nov. 21.

| Class | Semifinal results | Final, Nov. 28 |
|---|---|---|
| 2A | Scottsdale Christian 50, Chandler Prep 7; San Tan Charter 38, Willcox 15 | No. 1 Scottsdale Christian vs. No. 2 San Tan Charter, 3 p.m. MST |
| 3A | Benjamin Franklin 44, Show Low 24; Round Valley 29, Thatcher 14 | No. 1 Benjamin Franklin vs. No. 2 Round Valley, 7:30 p.m. MST |

## When are the 4A, 5A, 6A and Open semifinals?

The finalists are not set. AZPreps365 lists the 6A, 5A and 4A semifinals on Friday, Nov. 27 at 7 p.m., played at the higher seed, and the Open semifinals on Saturday, Nov. 28 at 6 p.m. at neutral sites still to be determined. The 3A and 2A semifinals were Saturday, Nov. 21 at 6 p.m.

## How to watch the Arizona state football finals

AZPreps365 says "live streaming rights of all postseason events are held by NFHSNetwork.com," the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network. It lists no television broadcast for the finals.

## Tickets and parking for the AIA finals

AZPreps365 says tickets are digital only, sold through GoFan. State championship games are $15 for adults and $7.50 for students with a high school ID, and games at ASU are $20 for adults and $7.50 for students. Earlier rounds are $10 for adults and $5 for students, and children 5 and under are free.

Parking for 2026 is not announced. In 2025, AZPreps365's Dec. 2, 2025 notice listed the same ASU slate, 4A at 3 p.m. and 5A at 7 p.m. on Friday, Dec. 5, and 6A at noon and Open at 6 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 6, with adult tickets at $20, student tickets at $7.50 and free parking in surface lots except around Desert Financial Arena.
