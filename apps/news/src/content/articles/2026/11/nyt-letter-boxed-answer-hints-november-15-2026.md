**Letter Boxed from The New York Times (NYT) for Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026 has the sides I/U/N, V/Y/O, T/C/M and E/R/P, and a par of 4, according to the puzzle data on the Letter Boxed page.** With a par of 4, the question is how far below it a solver can go, and the rules are on our [Letter Boxed page](/news/topic/nyt-letter-boxed/#how-it-works). The saved list has 1,016 words. Below are the par math, the awkward letters, five hints and four warm-up words, with the Times' solution at the end.

## The four sides

| Side | Letters |
|---|---|
| 1 | I, U, N |
| 2 | V, Y, O |
| 3 | T, C, M |
| 4 | E, R, P |

Twenty-four ordered pairs are barred by the side rule alone, since each letter has two side-mates.

## How far under par 4 can a solver get?

Par 4 means four words or fewer. The saved list sets a floor of two. The widest single word touches 10 of the 12 letters, and two words reach that, so no word finishes the box alone.

| Words in the finish | Against par 4 | Routes in the saved list |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 under | 0 |
| 2 | 2 under | 66 |
| 3 | 1 under | 45,773 |
| 4 | Level with par | Not counted |

The three-word count covers chains where the first two words leave at least one letter out. Total length varies far more than word count does: the 66 two-word routes run from 13 to 24 letters.

| Join letter | Two-word routes |
|---|---|
| C | 47 |
| E | 8 |
| T | 6 |
| R | 3 |
| N | 1 |
| I | 1 |

The C alone carries 47 of the 66, so a first word that ends on a C is the quickest road to a two-word finish.

## Which letters are hardest to place?

The V is the trouble. It sits in 72 words, opens 22 and closes none. Of the 108 ordered pairs on different sides, 11 never appear in the list, and 8 of those 11 involve the V: V then U, N, T, C, M or P, plus C then V and P then V. The other three are T then N, M then R and C then P.

| Letter | Words containing it | Words starting with it | Words ending with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| V | 72 | 22 | 0 |
| U | 258 | 16 | 28 |
| Y | 222 | 8 | 142 |
| P | 330 | 161 | 45 |
| O | 679 | 59 | 75 |

The Y starts only 8 words, the fewest of the twelve letters, and the U starts 16.

| Side | Letters | Words that start on it | Words that end on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I, U, N | 127 | 187 |
| 2 | V, Y, O | 89 | 217 |
| 3 | T, C, M | 486 | 295 |
| 4 | E, R, P | 314 | 317 |

Side 3 opens 486 words, more than any other side. The longest word runs 16 letters, and nothing else comes close to it in this list.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' own solution uses two words, the lowest count this list allows.
2. The longer word has 10 letters, one of 45 that long. The shorter has 4 letters, one of 213.
3. The first word starts and ends on the T/C/M side, and the second word begins on that side too.
4. The R, the U and the V sit in the first word. The Y, the P and the O sit in the second.
5. The first word starts with C and ends in T. The second starts with T and ends in O.
