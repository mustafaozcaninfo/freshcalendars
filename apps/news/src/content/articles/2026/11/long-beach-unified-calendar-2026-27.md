**Long Beach Unified School District (LBUSD) in Long Beach, California has no school for students from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and the last day of school is Thursday, June 10, 2027**, according to its "2026-2027 School Calendar." Spring break is April 5-9, 2027. The story lists every no-school weekday with the district's own label, compares the dates with three other California districts, and offers a printable school-year calendar.

## Long Beach Unified no-school days in 2026-27

The PDF splits days off between a holidays box and a special-dates list. The table merges them in date order. The district's wording is in quotes; weekdays and counts are our arithmetic from its dates. Students started Tuesday, Aug. 25, 2026 ("First Day of School for Students").

| Dates | Weekdays off | What the calendar calls it |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Sept. 7, 2026 | 1 | "Labor Day" |
| Mon., Nov. 2, 2026 | 1 | "Professional Development- No School for Students" |
| Wed., Nov. 11, 2026 | 1 | "Veterans Day" |
| Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | "Fall Break" (also "Thanksgiving" on Nov. 26-27) |
| Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | "Winter Break"; "Christmas" Dec. 24-25 and "New Year's Day Holiday" Jan. 1 fall inside it |
| Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | 1 | "M. L. King, Jr. Day" |
| Mon., Feb. 8, 2027 | 1 | "Lincoln's Day" |
| Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | 1 | "Washington's Day" |
| Fri., April 2, 2027 | 1 | "Admission Day" (listed in the holidays box) |
| Mon.-Fri., April 5-9, 2027 | 5 | "Spring Break" |
| Mon., May 31, 2027 | 1 | "Memorial Day" |

That adds up to 28 weekdays between the first and last day. "Juneteenth" on Friday, June 18 is in the holidays box too, but it falls after the last day. The first semester ends Friday, Jan. 22 ("Fall Semester Ends") and the spring semester begins Monday, Jan. 25. Put the break on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When is Long Beach Unified winter break 2026?

The calendar lists "Dec 21-Jan 1 Winter Break": Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, 10 weekdays. Counting the weekends on both sides, students are out from Saturday, Dec. 19 to Sunday, Jan. 3, 16 days. The calendar prints no return day, so check with your school before you plan the first week of January.

## What are Long Beach Unified's Thanksgiving dates?

The PDF prints "Nov 23-27 Fall Break." The district's calendar page words the same week differently: "Monday, November 23 through Wednesday, November 25: Thanksgiving Recess (all schools)" and "Thursday, November 26 and Friday, November 27: Thanksgiving Holiday (all schools)." Both labels cover the full week, five weekdays.

Before that, the calendar page lists "Tuesday, November 3 through Tuesday, November 10: Parent Conferences - Minimum Day for Elem/K8 students only" and "Friday, November 20: Minimum Day for High Schools and Middle Schools Only."

## When is Long Beach Unified spring break 2027?

"Apr 5-9 Spring Break" is Monday to Friday, April 5-9, 2027. Friday, April 2, the weekday before the break, is listed as "Admission Day" in the holidays box. The calendar prints no return day after the break. The third quarter ends Tuesday, March 30. High schools hold Open House on March 29, middle schools on March 30 and elementary and K-8 schools on March 31. See the week on the [April 2027 calendar](/april-calendars.html).

## What is the last day of school at Long Beach Unified?

"Jun 10 Last Day of School for Students" is Thursday, June 10, 2027. Memorial Day, May 31, comes first and Juneteenth, June 18, comes after. The calendar also lists "Oct 27 First Quarter Ends." Back to School Nights are Sept. 17 for elementary and K-8, Sept. 24 for middle and Oct. 1 for high schools. No board approval date is printed, and the calendar page links a "2027-2028 Traditional School Calendar" whose dates we did not read. The page also lists a separate calendar for two other schools, which we did not read.

## How does Long Beach compare with other California districts?

Dates for the other three districts come from our own stories on the Los Angeles Unified School District (LAUSD), the San Diego Unified School District (SDUSD) and the Fresno Unified School District, each built from that district's calendar. Weekdays are our count.

| District | Winter break | Weekdays off | Spring break | Last day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long Beach Unified | Dec. 21-Jan. 1 | 10 | April 5-9, 2027 | June 10, 2027 |
| [Los Angeles Unified](/news/lausd-calendar-2026-27-winter-break-spring-break/) | Dec. 21-Jan. 8 | 15 | March 22-26, 2027 | June 4, 2027 |
| [San Diego Unified](/news/san-diego-unified-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) | Dec. 21-Jan. 4 | 11 | March 29-April 2, 2027 | May 26, 2027 |
| [Fresno Unified](/news/fresno-unified-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) | Dec. 21-Jan. 8 | 15 | March 22-29, 2027 | June 10, 2027 |

Long Beach has the shortest winter break of the four and the latest spring break start. Its June 10 last day matches Fresno's. The [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) compares more districts.
