**The Great British Bake Off Series 17 final airs on Channel 4 on Tuesday 24 November 2026 at 8 p.m. UK time, according to Raidió Teilifís Éireann (RTÉ) News and The Scotsman.** That is 3 p.m. ET and 12 p.m. PT. Mo Mahir, Moyin Odeniran and Tom Whittaker are the three bakers left. Channel 4's programme page lists Episodes 1 to 9 and has no entry for Episode 10 yet, so the date and time come from the two newsrooms.

The table below sets all of Series 17 side by side with Channel 4's air dates and runtimes, and a one-page night sheet with the start time in UK and US zones is linked further down.

## When is the Bake Off 2026 final, and who is in it?

RTÉ News reported on 17 November 2026 that "The Great British Bake Off final will take place Tuesday, 24 November at 8pm" and that it "will be contested between Mo Mahir, Moyin Odeniran and Tom Whittaker." The Scotsman, on 18 November 2026, also puts the final on Channel 4 at 8 p.m. on Tuesday 24 November.

Gabe Capes, a 32-year-old drag artist, left in the Patisserie Week semi-final on Tuesday 17 November, RTÉ reported, and both RTÉ and The Scotsman say Mo was named Star Baker that week. Channel 4's own page has not yet added the final, so recheck it for any change.

## What time is the Bake Off final in the US and UK?

The United Kingdom is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in late November, and the United States is on standard time. We computed the zones below from 8 p.m. UK time.

| Where | Time on Tuesday 24 November 2026 |
|---|---|
| United Kingdom | 8 p.m. GMT |
| US East Coast | 3 p.m. ET |
| US West Coast | 12 p.m. PT |

Channel 4 is a UK service. In the US the show is on Netflix as The Great British Baking Show, and Netflix has not posted the date the final reaches US viewers, so check the title page there.

## How long is the final, and what is the theme?

Channel 4 has not announced a runtime or a theme for the final. Its page lists 58 minutes for each of Episodes 4 to 9. Channel 4's page for last year's Series 16 lists the final, Episode 10, as first shown on Tuesday 4 November 2025 at 58 minutes.

## Who judges and presents Bake Off in 2026?

Channel 4's page says "Twelve new bakers and a brand-new judge" started the series, and "Nigella Lawson joins Paul Hollywood, Noel Fielding and Alison Hammond." Its press release of 8 September 2026 has Paul and Nigella judging, with Alison and Noel welcoming the 12 bakers.

## Is there an Extra Slice after the 2026 final?

Channel 4 has not announced a date for An Extra Slice for 2026. Last year, Channel 4's press release on the Series 16 final also promoted An Extra Slice; Channel 4 has not listed one for 2026 yet.

## What happened in the Bake Off 2025 final?

In 2025, Channel 4 said Jasmine, 23, from London won Series 16, with Tom and Aaron as fellow finalists. That is last year's result and does not hint at this year's.
