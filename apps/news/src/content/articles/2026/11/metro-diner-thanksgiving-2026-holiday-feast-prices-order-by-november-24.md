**Metro Diner says to order its pre-cooked Holiday At Home Feast by Tuesday, Nov. 24, 2026, to pick it up in time for Thanksgiving dinner on Thursday, Nov. 26.** The Thanksgiving page reads "Thanksgiving Day: Order by Tuesday, November 24th," and describes "Metro Diner's heat-and-serve Holiday At Home Feast" of "Roasted Turkey or Baked Ham plus all your favorite traditional holiday sides."

Every feast is "pre cooked" and sold cold, so it is a heat-and-serve meal you warm at home. The feast comes in three sizes, and the page prints two different prices for the largest one, so the checkout total is the figure to trust. Metro Diner also stays open for an early dine-in Thanksgiving, which is covered below.

## Metro Diner Thanksgiving 2026 Holiday Feast prices and sizes

The Thanksgiving page lists the Holiday At Home Feast in three sizes. The feast for 8 appears twice on the page at two prices.

| Feast size | Price on the page |
|---|---|
| Feeds 1 | $17.99 |
| Feeds 4 | $67.99 |
| Feeds 8 | $133.99 (also printed lower on the page as $139.99) |

The page's words are "Feeds 1 ($17.99), 4 ($67.99), or 8 ($133.99, pictured)," sold pre cooked and cold, and, further down, "Feeds 1 ($17.99), 4 ($67.99), or 8 ($139.99)," again sold cold. Because the two prices for 8 disagree, confirm the total before you pay. Metro Diner marks the feast "While supplies last."

## What is in the Metro Diner Holiday At Home Feast

Each feast has a main and a set of traditional sides, with a choice at three of them. The page lists:

- Choice of Roasted Turkey or Baked Ham
- Mashed Potatoes & Gravy
- Steamed Green Beans or Street Corn (slightly spicy)
- Savory Cornbread Stuffing or Macaroni & Cheese
- Cranberry Sauce
- Hawaiian Dinner Rolls

The page says "À la carte sides available" and offers a Pumpkin Bread Pudding dessert add-on, which it describes as serving "6-8" in the menu and "8+" in the frequently asked questions. Turkey or ham on its own "is not available online," and the page says to call your local diner for it.

## Metro Diner Thanksgiving order deadline and timeline

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026; the date is on our [Thanksgiving 2026 page](/when-is/thanksgiving/). The page sets the order deadline and window below.

| Date | What Metro Diner says or to do |
|---|---|
| Oct. 1 to Nov. 24 | Order window; "Other Orders: Order 24 hours in advance" |
| Tue., Nov. 24 | Thanksgiving Day order deadline: "Thanksgiving Day: Order by Tuesday, November 24th" |

The page says "Preorder your Turkey or Ham Feast by Tuesday the 24th to pick up in time for Thanksgiving Dinner," and that the feast is "available for pick-up only."

## How to heat the Metro Diner Holiday Feast

The feast is handed to you cold, so you heat it at home. Metro Diner says "There are different instructions for 1 serving vs. 4&8"; the steps below are the ones it lists for the feasts that feed 4 and 8.

| Item | Metro Diner's instructions |
|---|---|
| Roasted Turkey | "preheat oven to 350˚, heat for 35 minutes with the lid on or until the internal temperature reaches at least 165˚" |
| Baked Ham | "preheat oven to 350˚, heat for 30 minutes with the lid on," then remove the lid and "heat for an additional 15 minutes or until internal temperature reaches at least 165˚" |
| Stuffing, green beans, mashed potatoes, mac & cheese | "preheat oven to 350˚, heat for 35 minutes with the lid off or until the internal temperature reaches at least 165˚" |
| Gravy | "heat separately in a sauce pan, on a medium heat, constantly stirring until hot" |

## Is Metro Diner open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Metro Diner says its diners stay "open until 2:30pm on Thanksgiving Day," so you can "join us in-diner for an early Thanksgiving Dinner with guest favorite holiday dishes like our traditional Turkey Plate, holiday special Country Fried Turkey, or unique-to-Metro Stuff 'N Waffles." The page does not give the opening time, the pickup hours or the dine-in Turkey Plate price. Metro Diner also lists a "$10 bonus card with every $50 spent on gift cards."
