**Clear Creek Independent School District (ISD) in League City, Texas, lists Thanksgiving Break Nov. 23-27, Winter Break Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, and Spring Break March 8-12, 2027, on its 2026-2027 Academic Calendar.** Students return Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, and the last day of school is Thursday, May 20, 2027, an early release day.

The district links the calendar as a PDF from its [Calendar page](https://www.ccisd.net/calendar). The next day off for students is Monday, Nov. 23, the start of Thanksgiving week. Fall Break and the Sept. 7, Sept. 21 and Nov. 3 days off have already passed.

## Clear Creek ISD breaks in 2026-27

The calendar lists these weeks under "Student/Teacher Holidays". Weekdays off and return days are our count from the printed dates.

| Break | Dates on the calendar | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Break (past) | Fri., Oct. 9 and Mon., Oct. 12, 2026 | 2 | Tue., Oct. 13, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (Jan. 4 is a student holiday) |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 8-12, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 15, 2027 |

## When do Clear Creek ISD students come back in January?

On Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027. The calendar prints "Second Semester January 5 - May 20" and "First Semester August 12 - December 18". Monday, Jan. 4 is a Teacher In-Service/Student Holiday, so with Winter Break that is 11 weekdays in a row without classes, counted from Dec. 21.

## Do Clear Creek ISD bad-weather days mean school on Oct. 13 or April 12?

Oct. 13, 2026 has passed as a day off. April 12, 2027 is a student holiday on the calendar. Both dates sit in two lists: Teacher In-Service/Student Holiday and "Bad Weather Days", with the legend "Bad Weather Make-Up Day". They are student holidays that become school days only if the district needs to make up a weather closure. The calendar does not say how the district decides.

## Clear Creek ISD grading periods

The calendar grid marks four grading periods: Aug. 12 to Oct. 8, Oct. 14 to Dec. 18, Jan. 5 to March 5, and March 15 to May 20. The third period ends on the early release day before Spring Break.

## When is the last day of school in Clear Creek ISD?

Thursday, May 20, 2027, marked "First and Last Day of School" in the legend and listed as a Student Early Release day. The first day was Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026 for most grades, after a Transition Day on Tuesday, Aug. 11.
