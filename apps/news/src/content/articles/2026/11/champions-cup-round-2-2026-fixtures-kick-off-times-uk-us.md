## Where can you watch Champions Cup Round 2 in the UK and U.S.?

What Hi-Fi says: "Rugby fans in the UK will need a Premier Sports subscription to watch Champions Cup rugby." For the U.S. it names FloRugby, with "comprehensive coverage of the 2026/27 Champions Cup in the USA." We found no per-match U.S. list, no Ireland broadcaster and no statement on whether games air live or delayed.

Two games are marked free in the sources. Planet Rugby lists Cardiff v Bath as free on S4C and BBC iPlayer, and What Hi-Fi notes that France.TV shows Montpellier v Bulls and Bordeaux-Bègles v Stormers free in France. Those offers are tied to those countries.

## Which clubs are in the Champions Cup 2026-27?

Planet Rugby reported on July 1, 2026 that the competition has 24 clubs in four pools of six. The Challenge Cup has 18 clubs in three pools of six, and its Round 2 is also on Dec. 11-13. Round 1 was played Oct. 16-18, 2026; we list no Round 1 results.

Bordeaux-Bègles are the defending champions. RTÉ Sport and The Irish Times reported that they beat Leinster 41-19 in the final on Sat., May 23, 2026 at San Mamés in Bilbao. Round 2 has Bordeaux-Bègles at home to the Stormers on Sun., Dec. 13 at 3:15 p.m. GMT (10:15 a.m. ET).
