**York's Christmas market opens Thursday 12 November 2026 and runs to 21 December, and Cardiff's opens the same day and runs to Wednesday 23 December**, according to Visit York and the Cardiff Christmas Market's own site. Bath's follows on Thursday 26 November, Lincoln Cathedral holds a ticketed market on 3 to 5 December and Belfast's opens on 14 November. Our comparison table, a week-by-week grid and a one-page printable are below.

## What are the York Christmas market 2026 dates and hours?

Visit York lists "12 November – 21 December 2026" and hours of "10.30am – 7pm, Wednesday–Monday". It adds that the market is "closed Tuesdays for a weekly rest day" and "closes at 5pm on Monday 21 December". The market sits on Parliament Street and St Sampson's Square.

By our count, that is 40 dates between opening and closing, and five of them are Tuesdays (17 and 24 November, 1, 8 and 15 December), leaving 35 trading days.

## What are the Bath Christmas market 2026 opening times?

The Bath Christmas Market site gives "Thursday 26th November to Sunday 13th December 2026 (inclusive)", which is 18 dates by our count. Hours differ by day:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Wednesday | 10am to 7pm |
| Thursday to Saturday | 10am to 8pm |
| Sunday | 10am to 6pm |

The site's address is 36 Stall Street, Bath, BA1 1QH. It does not state a number of chalets, so we do not give one.

## What are the Cardiff Christmas market 2026 dates?

The Cardiff Christmas Market runs "from Thursday 12 November to Wednesday 23 December", 42 dates by our count. Hours are 10am to 6pm Monday to Saturday and 10am to 5pm on Sundays, "with a late night to 7pm every Thursday in December". The Thursdays in December before it closes are 3, 10 and 17 December. The site does not name streets on the page we read, so we say only that it is in the city centre.

## Is there a Christmas market at Lincoln Cathedral in 2026?

Lincoln Cathedral's homepage announces "Lincoln's first-ever Cathedral Christmas Market", "taking place 3–5 December", and says tickets are on sale. That is Thursday to Saturday in 2026. The page does not print a ticket price or opening hours, so check the cathedral's site before you plan a trip.

## When does the Belfast Christmas market open in 2026?

Belfast City Council's Christmas lights page says the switch-on is on Saturday 14 November 2026, 6.30pm to 7.30pm at Belfast City Hall, and that the market "will open at 12 noon on the same day, Saturday 14 November". The council has not yet announced a closing date or daily hours for 2026. For comparison, last year's run was 15 November to 22 December 2025, according to the council's Christmas page.
