## Is Aldi open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. FOX 4 wrote on Nov. 19, 2026 that Aldi stores will be closed on Thanksgiving, according to the company's holiday policy. Yahoo Shopping, in its Nov. 18, 2026 update, lists Aldi as "Closed on Thanksgiving Day." [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26.

Both are newsroom reports, not an Aldi statement. For other chains, see [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/) and the [Publix holiday hours](/news/is-publix-open-thanksgiving-2026-christmas-eve-hours/).

## Is Aldi open on Black Friday and the day before?

Aldi has not announced hours for Wednesday, Nov. 25 or Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27, 2026. Regular hours vary by store, so check the store locator on aldi.us. The dates sit on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## Is Aldi open on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's?

Aldi has not announced its 2026 Christmas hours. The best guide is the policy Yahoo News reported on Oct. 27, 2025, citing Aldi's website: "the chain closes its locations on New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving and Christmas," with "Limited hours" scheduled on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve. Yahoo News added that these special holiday hours vary by store location, as Aldi's website explains.

Under that 2025-reported policy, stores would close Friday, Dec. 25, 2026 and Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and run limited hours on Thursday, Dec. 24 and Thursday, Dec. 31. None of that is confirmed for 2026. The days appear on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

Past times are mixed. In 2025, FOX 35 Orlando reported "Aldi stores will be open until 7 p.m." on New Year's Eve; that is one 2025 report. Christmas Eve 2025 reports disagreed, so no time is given here.

## What is Aldi's Thanksgiving meal deal in 2026?

NBC 5 Dallas-Fort Worth reported on Nov. 12, 2026: "Aldi says shoppers can feed 10 people for less than $39 with its Thanksgiving meal deal." The turkey size, item list and effective dates were not read, so they are not given here.

For comparison, Aldi's newsroom said in its Oct. 15, 2025 release: "For just $40*, shoppers can build a full spread for 10," including "a 14-pound turkey." That was the 2025 deal. Other chains' meal deals are in the [Thanksgiving meal deals story](/news/thanksgiving-meal-deals-2026-aldi-lidl-target-sams-club-prices-dates/).

## What Aldi has not announced

Not yet announced for 2026: hours for Wednesday, Nov. 25 and Friday, Nov. 27, the Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve closing times, and confirmation of the Christmas Day and New Year's Day closures.
