**New York City (NYC) high school applications for fall 2027 close Friday, Dec. 4, 2026, and offers are scheduled for Wednesday, March 3, 2027, after 3 p.m., according to the NYC Public Schools "Fall 2027 High School Admissions Timeline."** Dec. 4 is 27 days after Nov. 7. The same page says the dates "are subject to change" and that any changes "will be announced and communicated directly to families."

The High School and LaGuardia applications both close that day. The timeline gives no closing time for Dec. 4, so submit well before the end of the day rather than at the last minute. Our one-page printable, "NYC High School Admissions Dates 2026-27," is below the story; it counts down to Dec. 4 and notes the March 3 offer date in its footer note.

## Which NYC high school admissions dates are left?

The application opened Tuesday, Oct. 6, 2026, and Specialized High Schools Admissions Test (SHSAT) registration closed Friday, Oct. 30. Everything still ahead as of Nov. 7 is below. Days left count from Nov. 7.

| Date | Day | Days left | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 10 | Tuesday | 3 | Virtual Fall General Information Session in Mandarin, 6-7 p.m. | NYC Public Schools, High School page |
| Nov. 14 | Saturday | 7 | SHSAT weekend testing | Testing Calendar |
| Nov. 15 | Sunday | 8 | SHSAT weekend testing | Testing Calendar |
| Nov. 18 | Wednesday | 11 | SHSAT School Day | High School page, Testing Calendar |
| Nov. 21 | Saturday | 14 | SHSAT weekend testing | Testing Calendar |
| Nov. 23 | Monday | 16 | Virtual Fall General Information Session, 5-6 p.m. | High School page |
| Dec. 4 | Friday | 27 | High School and LaGuardia applications close | High School page |
| March 3, 2027 | Wednesday | 116 | High school offer release, after 3 p.m. | High School page |

The page lists the Nov. 10 and Nov. 23 sessions as virtual. Weekdays and day counts are computed from the calendar.

## What should families do before Dec. 4?

NYC Public Schools' own instructions point to a short list:

- **Confirm who can apply.** NYC lists two requirements: a NYC resident, and a current eighth grade or first-time ninth grade student.
- **Apply to 12 or more options.** The page says "we recommend you apply to at least twelve" and that you can add any number of programs. Twelve is a recommendation, not a limit.
- **Rank in your true order.** NYC says to "always place the programs on your application in your true order of preference."
- **Submit online or through a school counselor.** Both routes are listed.
- **Check your SHSAT date.** The Specialized High Schools page says "all registered students will be scheduled for a SHSAT testing date in November."
- **Upload auditions before the deadline.** All audition schools, including LaGuardia High School, accept virtual auditions, and they "must be uploaded to MySchools during the application period."

Students can also apply to the eight testing Specialized High Schools and to any or all of LaGuardia High School's six arts programs.

## What changes for the SHSAT this year?

NYC says that "beginning in fall 2026, the SHSAT will move to a computer-adaptive test." Students who take it this fall "will have the opportunity to sign up for a Test View in the Spring of 2027." The Specialized High Schools page adds that after the test, "in the spring," NYC will contact students if they are eligible for a Discovery program.

## When do SHSAT results come out?

The fall 2026 score release and the specialized high school offer date were not listed when we checked on Nov. 7. Last year, the NYC Public Schools page said "As of March 5, results are now available" for the Fall 2025 test. Treat that as last year's timing, not this year's date.

## What has not been announced?

As of Nov. 7, 2026, we found no LaGuardia audition dates, SHSAT make-up dates or waitlist and appeal dates on the pages above. The closing time on Dec. 4 is also missing. Check MySchools and your child's school for updates.
