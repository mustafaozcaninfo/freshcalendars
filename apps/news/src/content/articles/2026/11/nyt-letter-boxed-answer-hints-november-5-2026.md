## Hard letters and rare pairs on these sides

We counted these from the day's own dictionary, so they describe this box only.

The J is the awkward one. It appears in 34 of the 1491 words, only 24 words begin with it, and only 2 end with it. Wherever it shows up, its neighbors are A, I, B, W or D; it never touches E, T, C, H, L or S. The most common pairings are J followed by A (18 words) and J followed by I (13 words).

Across the three other sides, 108 ordered letter pairs are possible. Sixteen of them never occur in any word. Eleven of those involve the J. The other five are B followed by W, C followed by B, C followed by D, L followed by H and T followed by D. Five more pairs show up in exactly one word each: B followed by H, D followed by J, I followed by J, J followed by W and W followed by T.

Word endings matter too. In the dictionary, 530 words end in S, more than any other letter, while only 20 end in W.
