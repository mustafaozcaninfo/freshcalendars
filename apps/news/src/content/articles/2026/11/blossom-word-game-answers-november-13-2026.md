**Blossom from Merriam-Webster for Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, puts R in the center, ringed by A, C, E, F, L and N.** Beebom and Connections Solver print the same letters. Hints come first, and the pangrams are saved for a marked section at the bottom.

## Today's flower: R in the middle

- **Center:** R (it has to appear in every word)
- **Petals:** A, C, E, F, L, N

Only A and E are vowels, which leaves five consonants fighting for room. With R required in the middle, every word must carry it; the rules are on our [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works). The flower before this one is on the [Nov. 12 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-12-2026/).

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

Two pangrams are listed, and they are closely related.

1. **Count:** Beebom and Word.tips list two, and two more sites agree.
2. **Size:** one has 9 letters and the other has 10.
3. **Link:** the shorter one sits inside the longer one with nothing in between, so solving one gives you the other.
4. **Meaning:** the shorter one is an adjective for someone paid per job instead of held on a payroll; the longer one names the person.
5. **Starts:** both begin with the same four letters.

## How one find leads to the other

Seven letters are required, so the 9-letter pangram has two spares and the 10-letter one has three. Count them and a pattern shows.

- **E repeats twice.** Each pangram holds three E's, so the spares are mostly E.
- **R is the only difference.** The 10-letter pangram has two R's, the 9-letter one has one.
- **A, C, F, L and N appear once.** None of them doubles in either word.

So the longer pangram is the shorter one with the center letter added at the end. For the bonus, that means a player who spots the 9-letter word should try one more letter before moving on, because the second pangram is a single keystroke away. The same holds in reverse: if you find the long word first, remove the last letter and test what is left.
