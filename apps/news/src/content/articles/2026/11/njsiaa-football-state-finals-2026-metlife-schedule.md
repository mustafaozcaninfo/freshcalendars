**The New Jersey State Interscholastic Athletic Association (NJSIAA) lists the 2026 football state finals at MetLife Stadium on Nov. 28 or 29, and newsrooms report seven games: four on Saturday, Nov. 28, from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. ET and three on Sunday, Nov. 29, from noon to 6 p.m. ET.** The NJSIAA page names the venue and the two days but gives no kickoff times. The times and pairings below come from Central Jersey Sports Radio and the Courier-Post, both Nov. 18, 2026, and NorthJersey.com on Nov. 22 lists the same times and pairings.

## NJSIAA state finals 2026 schedule at MetLife

NJSIAA's football page says "State Finals: 11/28/2026 or 11/29/2026 | MetLife." Its 2026-2027 Football Tournament Regulations, posted Aug. 19, 2026, list the Public and Non-Public State Finals for "November 28 or 29, 2026" and add that "NJSIAA reserves the right to adjust, change or cancel dates, times, and locations for any round of competition." The pairings are marked as reported because no NJSIAA page we read prints them.

| Day | Kickoff (ET) | Group | Reported matchup (records) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 10 a.m. | Non-Public B | Paramus Catholic (7-5) vs. DePaul (6-5) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 1 p.m. | Group 2 | Glassboro (12-1) vs. Caldwell (11-1) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 4 p.m. | Group 3 | Seneca (10-3) vs. Old Tappan (12-0) |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 7 p.m. | Non-Public A | Don Bosco Prep (9-2) vs. Bergen Catholic (7-4) |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Noon | Group 5 | Washington Township (12-0) vs. Passaic Tech (10-3) |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | 3 p.m. | Group 1 | Schalick (13-0) vs. Newton (13-0) |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | 6 p.m. | Group 4 | Winslow (9-3) vs. Northern Highlands (9-3) |

High School On SI (Nov. 5, 2026) gives the same two days: "State championship dates: Saturday, Nov. 28, and Sunday, Nov. 29," at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford.

## Do Thanksgiving teams play in the finals?

Central Jersey Sports Radio reported on Sept. 16, 2026 that all finals are at MetLife on Thanksgiving weekend and that NJSIAA Executive Director Colleen Maguire said "teams will be allowed to play on Thanksgiving." Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, two days before the first kickoff; the [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) confirms it.

## How did the rounds before the finals fall?

| Round | Dates (NJSIAA regulations) |
|---|---|
| Sectional finals | Nov. 13 or 14, 2026 |
| Semifinals | Nov. 20 or 21, 2026 |
| State finals | Nov. 28 or 29, 2026 |

The regulations say the higher United Power Ranking (UPR) team hosts a public-school semifinal, and the higher seed hosts a non-public one.

## How do 2026 and 2025 compare?

NJSIAA's 2025 finals guide shows a split schedule. In 2026 all seven games are at MetLife, unlike 2025's split.

| Year | Date | Venue | Kickoffs (ET) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Fri., Nov. 28 | MetLife Stadium | 10 a.m., 1, 4 and 7 p.m. |
| 2025 | Sun., Nov. 30 | Rutgers' stadium | Noon and 3 p.m. |
| 2025 | Wed., Dec. 3 | Rutgers' stadium | 5 p.m. |
| 2026 | Sat., Nov. 28 | MetLife Stadium | 10 a.m., 1, 4 and 7 p.m. (reported) |
| 2026 | Sun., Nov. 29 | MetLife Stadium | Noon, 3 and 6 p.m. (reported) |
