## What is the World Usability Day 2026 theme?

The organizer's Themes page says: "2026 Theme: Designing a Sustainable Future with Ethical AI." The Bratislava chapter page on the same site repeats it: "The theme of this year's World Usability Day is Designing a Sustainable Future with Ethical AI." The Themes page for the previous year shows a different subject.

|---|---|---|

| 2025 | Emerging Technologies and the Human Experience | World Usability Day, Themes page |

## Which World Usability Day events are on Nov. 12?

The organizer's site carries a page for each local chapter. Two of them list in-person events on Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026. The organizer shows no complete list of Ethical AI events, so a city that is not below may still hold one.

| City | Date | Time listed |
|---|---|---|
| Rome | Nov. 12, 2026 | 09:00 to 17:00 |
| L'Aquila | Nov. 12, 2026 | From 09:00 CET |

The Austin chapter's listing is not in the table because the organizer's pages disagree: the home page says the in-person event is on Friday, Nov. 13, while the same listing shows Nov. 12 from 08:30 to 18:30. Check with that chapter before you plan around either date.

## Everyday usability checks for World Usability Day

The organizer does not publish a checklist for the day, so the printable carries a short one of our own: finish one task in an app and note where you hesitate, ask someone else to try it while you watch, enlarge the text on a screen, and read one AI message or setting and ask what it tells you. They need no equipment, and each takes a few minutes.
