## Thursday's Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints, Nov. 5, 2026

- **Pinpoint #919, first nudge:** the hidden link is one short word that sits in front of each clue to make a familiar term.
- **Pinpoint #919, stronger nudge:** that word is something hard you could pick up on a beach or find in an old wall, and it also names a prehistoric era.
- **Crossclimb #919, the middle rungs:** five four-letter words, starting with a word for a discount event and ending with a word for the back of something, like a deer's rear legs.
- **Crossclimb #919, more help:** between those, look for a word meaning of sound mind, the grains on a beach and the part of your arm you write with.
- **Crossclimb #919, the locked ends:** both are kinds of moving air. One is a strong blast at sea; the other is the everyday word for air on the move.

## Crossclimb #919 ladder map, Nov. 5, 2026

The table tracks which letter position changes at each step, without naming the words. Positions run 1 to 4 from left to right.

| Step | Letter position that changes |
|---|---|
| Top end to rung 1 | 1 |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 3 |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 4 |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 1 |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 2 |
| Rung 5 to bottom end | 1 |

Three of the six steps swap the first letter, so if you are stuck, try a new opening letter before touching the middle of the word.

## Getting started on Queens, Tango and Zip

**Queens #919.** Start with the smallest colored region. If it covers only one row or one column, its queen already rules out that whole line for every other color.

**Tango #759.** Three of the same symbol can never sit side by side, so any two matching symbols in a row tell you what the cells on both sides must be.

**Zip #598.** Trace backward from the highest number. The end of the path often has fewer ways in than the start, which trims the choices quickly.
