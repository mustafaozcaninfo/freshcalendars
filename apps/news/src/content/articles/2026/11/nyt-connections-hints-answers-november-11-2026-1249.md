Here are two hints per color, followed by the answers in a marked section.

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Each describes a way of treating illness, often outside a hospital.
- Hint 2: One looks at the whole person instead of a single symptom; one relies on leaves, roots and plant extracts.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Play 18 holes and you will cross each of these spots.
- Hint 2: One is the long grass bordering the fairway; one is where every hole begins.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Each one goes flat or pops once it loses what is inside.
- Hint 2: One is a prank cushion that makes a silly noise when you sit; one is the rubber ring around a car wheel.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Look at the last letters of each one; they read the same left to right and right to left.
- Hint 2: One ends with a short word for statistics; one ends with a word meaning more red.

## Solving tips

The golf course is the cleanest place to start, since its tiles mean little outside the game. Then move on to the things you inflate.

Tea is the trap. Four tiles make a tea phrase, and they are spread across all four colors, so a tea guess will fail. For purple, read each remaining tile backward from its end.

## Why the traps worked

Green, Herbal, Afternoon and Bubble all go in front of tea. One sits in each group, so the tea idea never pays off.

Green has a second pull as a color, and Tee Box sounds like a box of tea when you say it aloud, a pun that only feeds the trap. Folk and Traditional both work as kinds of music as well as medicine, which can send a solver hunting for a song group that is not there.
