**Today's Connections from The New York Times (NYT) is puzzle #1249, for Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, edited by Wyna Liu.** One group is kinds of medicine, one is parts of a golf course, one is things filled with air, and one is entries whose last letters form a palindrome. Green is a color as well as a golf term, and a couple of the medicine words sound like music. Below are two levels of hints for each color, then the full solution and an explanation of the tricky words in a marked spoiler section.

## Today's 16 words

Shredder, Herbal, Patoot, Whoopie Cushion, Green, Folk, Bubble, Rough, Afternoon, Rheostats, Traditional, Tire, Sand Trap, Holistic, Air Mattress, Tee Box

## How the game works

Sort the 16 entries into four groups of four. You can make four mistakes. The groups run from yellow to green, blue and purple, with purple the group that depends most on wordplay.

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Each word can come before "medicine" or "remedy."
- Hint 2: One uses plants, one treats the whole person, one is handed down by ordinary people, and one is the long-established practice of a culture.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Think of the places a golfer's ball can land.
- Hint 2: One is where the hole sits, one is the long grass beside the fairway, one is a bunker, and one is where each hole begins.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Each item needs air inside it to work.
- Hint 2: One is a bed you pump up, one floats away and pops, one sits on a wheel, and one is a prank you sit on.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Read the end of each word backward.
- Hint 2: The last four, five or six letters of each entry read the same in both directions: a word for midday, a short horn sound, a word for numbers, and a word meaning more red.

## Solving tips

The golf course is the easiest group, but Green can pull you toward the colors, and nothing else on the board is a color word. Take the four places on the course first. Herbal and Holistic are clearly medicine words, and Folk and Traditional join them, even though both also describe music. Watch Patoot and Shredder: one sounds like a joke and one like an office machine, yet both end in a palindrome. Leave purple for last and read the endings backward.

**Shredder and Green.** Shredder contains the color red, which seems to pair it with Green. It ends in Redder, a palindrome, so it goes in purple.
