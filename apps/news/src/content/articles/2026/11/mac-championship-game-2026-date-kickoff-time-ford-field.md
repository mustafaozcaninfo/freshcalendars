**The 2026 Mid-American Conference (MAC) Football Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, at noon ET on ESPN, according to the MAC.** The conference's schedule release of Mar. 24, 2026 says "the 2026 season will culminate with the 30th MAC Football Championship Game on Saturday, December 5 at Noon ET (ESPN)," and its kickoff-attendees release puts the game at Ford Field in Detroit.

Which two teams play is not set yet. Under the MAC's format, the top two teams in the conference standings meet, so the matchup is set after the final regular-season games.

## What time does the MAC Championship Game start where you live?

| Time zone | Kickoff on Sat., Dec. 5 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET, local in Detroit) | noon |
| Central (CT) | 11 a.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 10 a.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | 9 a.m. |
| United Kingdom (GMT) | 5 p.m. |

The MAC's kickoff-attendees release also gives "Noon ET/9 am PT (ESPN)." The other rows are computed from noon ET. The Ford Field website lists the event as starting at 12:00 PM, with tickets through Ticketmaster. It posts no gate times, parking details or prices, so check fordfield.com and the MAC closer to the game.

## Where does the MAC game fall on Group of Five title weekend?

Five conference title games are listed for Dec. 4-5, 2026. The MAC game is the earliest Saturday kickoff in this table.

| Game | Date | Kickoff (ET) | Network | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun Belt Championship | Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | ESPN | Sun Belt, May 27, 2026 |
| Conference USA Championship | Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | CBS Sports Network | Liberty athletics site |
| MAC Championship, Detroit | Sat., Dec. 5 | noon | ESPN | MAC, Mar. 24, 2026 |
| American Conference Championship | Sat., Dec. 5 | 8 p.m. | ABC | American, May 27, 2026 |

The Mountain West title game is also on Friday, Dec. 4, on FOX; its kickoff time is not confirmed in the sources used here. Our [conference championship games schedule](/news/college-football-conference-championship-games-2026-schedule-times-tv/) lists every league's title game, and the [Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) title game](/news/acc-championship-game-2026-noon-et-abc-charlotte/) shares the noon ET slot on Dec. 5, on ABC in Charlotte.

## How does the MAC game fit the College Football Playoff calendar?

The National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) lists Selection Day for Sun., Dec. 6, 2026, the day after the MAC game, and the first round for Dec. 18-19. Mark the dates on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and see the [Big 12 title game](/news/big-12-championship-game-2026-friday-december-4-kickoff-time-abc/) for the Friday kickoff.

## Who won the 2025 MAC Championship Game?

In 2025, Western Michigan beat Miami (Ohio) 23-13 on Sat., Dec. 6, 2025, at Ford Field, the MAC reported ("Western Michigan Captures 2025 MAC Football Title"). That is last year's result. The sources used here do not say where the game is played after 2026.
