Hard leans on five greater-than and less-than regions today, and the symbols are explained on [our Pips page](/news/topic/nyt-pips/#how-it-works). ## Where each Pips board starts on Nov. 5

Each board gives up one move before any guessing. R2C3 means row 2, column 3, counted from the top left.

**Easy.** Three cells are dead ends: R1C3 touches only R1C2, R5C3 only R5C2 and R3C2 only R3C1. Pair those, and R1C1 can join nothing but R2C1, and R5C1 nothing but R4C1, so the shape of the whole board is set. R1C3 must stay under 2, so the top-right domino is the 6-0 or the 5-1.

**Medium.** R3C5 must be exactly 1 and its only neighbor is R3C4, so those two share a domino. Three dominoes carry a 1: the 5-1, the 1-2 and the 4-1. Whichever you pick puts its other half in R3C4, which must differ from R3C3.

**Hard.** R7C8 touches only R7C7, and R7C7 must be a 2. The 2-1, the 6-2 and the 2-3 carry a 2, but R7C8 has to be more than 3, so the 6-2 is the only fit: 2 in R7C7, 6 in R7C8. The corner region of R2C1, R3C1 and R3C2 then takes three 6s, and since R2C1 touches only R3C1, the 6-6 covers those two.

## Thursday's Pips hints, Nov. 5, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 6-0, 6-6, 5-2, 4-4, 5-1)**

- Hint 1: Two pairs must each total 11. That takes a 5 and a 6, and the set has exactly two 5s, on the 5-2 and the 5-1.
- Hint 2: R3C1 and R3C2 share a domino, and both belong to the four-cell region down the left side that adds up to 14.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 5-3, 4-0, 5-1, 2-3, 1-2, 4-1, 3-3)**

- Hint 1: R1C4 and R2C4 must match. The value 3 shows up on four halves, more than any other on this board.
- Hint 2: Whatever lands on R3C2 must be a 2, so it comes from the 2-3 or the 1-2. R4C2 below it belongs to the bottom row, which totals 8.

**Hard (fifteen dominoes on a seven-row board)**

- Hint 1: R4C5, R4C6, R5C5 and R5C6 make a square of matching values. The 3s and the 6s each appear on six halves, and the 6s are needed in other places.
- Hint 2: R4C7 and R5C7 add up to 3, and R6C7 just below them must be under 2.

## Reading the boards before you check

Easy is pure arithmetic today: three sums and two upper limits. Medium uses sums, an equal pair, a not-equal pair and two greater-than cells. On Hard, R2C7, R3C7 and R4C4 have no condition, so fill them last; they take whatever halves the regions around them leave over. The 3-3, 6-6 and 1-1 are the doubles on Hard.
