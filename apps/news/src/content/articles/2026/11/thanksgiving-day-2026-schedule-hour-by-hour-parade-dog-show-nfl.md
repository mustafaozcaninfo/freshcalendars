**The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade begins at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, NBCUniversal says, and Bears at Lions kicks off at 1 p.m. ET, according to NFL.com.** The National Dog Show airs from noon to 2 p.m. local time on NBC, per the American Kennel Club (AKC); NBC10 Philadelphia gives the date as Thursday, Nov. 26. That makes two kinds of events on one day: games that start at one moment everywhere, and broadcasts that start at the same clock time in every zone. Below are the day in Eastern Time (ET), the same day for a Pacific Time (PT) viewer and a dinner plan.

NBCUniversal also says the parade airs live on Telemundo and lists an encore on NBC at 2 p.m. ET/PT. It does not list when the live broadcast ends.

## Which Thanksgiving events air at the same clock time everywhere?

Two groups behave differently. The parade and the dog show are tied to local clocks: NBCUniversal says NBC and Peacock "kick off the holiday season at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones", and the AKC says NBC airs the dog show "from noon to 2 p.m. local time". The NFL games are tied to one moment, so their clock time shifts by zone.

That gap changes the order of the day. In Eastern Time the Lions kick off 1 hour after the dog show starts, so the two overlap. In Central Time both start at noon. In Mountain Time the Lions start at 11 a.m., an hour before the dog show, and in Pacific Time at 10 a.m., 2 hours before it. The dog show is on NBC and the Lions game is on CBS, so the two overlap on different networks.

The conversions are ours: Pacific Time is 3 hours behind Eastern. A Pacific viewer waits 1 hour 30 minutes between the parade start and the first kickoff. An Eastern viewer waits 4 hours 30 minutes.

## Where does dinner fit between the games?

Kickoffs are 3 hours 30 minutes apart, from 1 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. ET, then 3 hours 50 minutes apart, to 8:20 p.m. ET. The same gaps hold in every zone, by our subtraction. The sources list no game length, so the time from one game's end to the next kickoff is not computed here.

Our advice: pick the dinner time first, then see which kickoff it lands near.
