**Letter Boxed from The New York Times (NYT) for Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 has the sides W/H/C, I/G/O, N/E/T and X/R/Y and a par of 5, according to the puzzle data on the Letter Boxed page.** The day's word list has 660 entries. ## How many words start and end on each letter?

Every route hands over a letter: one word ends where the next begins. These counts come from the 660 words in the list.

| Letter | Side | Words starting with it | Words ending with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| W | 1 | 54 | 25 |
| H | 1 | 60 | 14 |
| C | 1 | 100 | 56 |
| I | 2 | 28 | 22 |
| G | 2 | 20 | 118 |
| O | 2 | 42 | 38 |

| E | 3 | 43 | 91 |
| T | 3 | 146 | 48 |
| X | 4 | 4 | 13 |
| R | 4 | 89 | 88 |
| Y | 4 | 11 | 62 |

T opens the most words at 146, and G closes the most at 118 while opening only 20. H ends just 14 words, the fewest of any letter. Y is the lopsided one: 62 words finish on it, yet 11 start with it, so a route that wants to continue past a Y needs one of those few. X is the scarcest letter overall, sitting in 56 words; the next lowest is Y, in 108.

## Which steps between letters never happen here?

There are 108 ordered steps from one side to a different side. In this list 24 never occur inside any word: W to G, W to X, H to G, H to N, H to T, H to X, C to G, C to N, C to X, G to W, G to C, G to T, G to X, N to X, T to C, T to G, T to X, X to W, X to G, X to N, Y to W, Y to H, Y to C and Y to G. Y has four dead exits, and X is the hardest to enter: only I, O and E can lead straight into it. Four steps appear in exactly one word: I to W, I to H, G to H and R to W.

W/H/C and X/R/Y is the thin link at 104 words, about a third of the 289 on the next lowest pair.
