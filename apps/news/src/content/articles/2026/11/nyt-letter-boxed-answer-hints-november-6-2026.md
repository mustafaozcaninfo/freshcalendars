## Hard letters and rare pairs on these sides

These counts come from the day's own dictionary and describe only this box.

The V is the weakest link. It sits inside 441 words but ends only 10 of them, and the K sits in 274. Ten ordered pairs from different sides never occur in any word: F before K, F before M, F before N, K before M, K before V, M before V, V before D, V before K, V before M and V before N. In plain terms, the V never comes right before D, K, M or N, and never right after K or M.

Some pairs exist but barely. K followed by F, M followed by F, F followed by D and L followed by R each show up in exactly one word. M followed by K appears in 2 words, and K followed by N in 4.

The most common last letter is D, with 429 of the 1851 words.
