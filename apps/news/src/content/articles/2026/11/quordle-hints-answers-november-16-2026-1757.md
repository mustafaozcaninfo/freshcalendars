## How do the four words overlap?

Four words of five letters fill 20 slots, but only 12 different letters appear. Eight slots are repeats, and one of them is a letter used twice inside a single word. Each word begins with a different letter, so no first-letter pattern links two boards.

| Words containing the letter | Letters | What it means for play |
|---|---|---|
| 3 of 4 | 2 | Two vowels each cover three boards, so a yellow on either one is worth tracking everywhere |
| 2 of 4 | 3 | Three letters split across two boards each |
| 1 of 4 | 7 | Seven letters belong to a single board |

Every word holds at least two vowels, and one holds three. Because the two shared vowels together touch all four boards, a first guess built on them still has consonants to find afterward.

## How five common opening words score on today's four boards

Each cell lists green tiles (G, right letter, right spot) and yellow tiles (Y, right letter, wrong spot) that the opener would earn against that word.

| Opener | Word 1 | Word 2 | Word 3 | Word 4 | Colored tiles in all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crane | 0G 2Y | 0G 2Y | 0G 0Y | 0G 1Y | 5 |
| Stare | 0G 2Y | 0G 3Y | 0G 1Y | 0G 1Y | 7 |
| Audio | 0G 2Y | 1G 0Y | 0G 2Y | 1G 3Y | 9 |
| Pilot | 1G 0Y | 0G 1Y | 0G 2Y | 0G 2Y | 6 |
| Round | 1G 1Y | 0G 2Y | 2G 0Y | 1G 2Y | 9 |

Audio and Round tie at nine colored tiles, yet Round turns four of them green and Audio only two. Round is the one opener here with greens on three different boards. Crane and Stare finish with no green at all, so everything they find has to be placed by later guesses.

Word 4 gives the most back, with 11 colored tiles across the five openers, while Word 3 gives the least, with 7. Crane touches Word 3 not at all, which tells you where the second guess should go after it.
