## Is Ross open on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

No. The Ross homepage says: "Ross is closed Thursday in observance of the Thanksgiving Holiday." The page is undated, and its footer reads "©2026 Ross Stores, Inc." [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26.

## What are Ross Black Friday hours in 2026?

Ross says "Extended holiday hours start Friday," which is Nov. 27, but names no opening or closing time. Ross Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 hours are not yet announced. The Ross Contact Us page says: "Hours vary by store location and time of year." It sends shoppers to the Ross Store Locator for current hours in their area.

## Is dd's Discounts open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Not yet announced. Ross Stores' third-quarter 2026 release, dated Nov. 19, says the company also operates 384 dd's Discounts stores in 24 states, next to 1,993 Ross locations in 45 states, the District of Columbia, Guam and Puerto Rico, for 2,377 stores at the end of the period. The release has no holiday hours, and the dd's Discounts site shows none. Check the store locator or call your store.

## Is Burlington open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Not yet announced. The Burlington homepage lists no holiday hours, and we did not find a store count on pages we could read. The homepage does carry a coat offer: "Buy a coat now through 11/28 and get $5* off a $25+ purchase redeemable 12/5/26-12/24/26." Nov. 28 is a Saturday, Dec. 5 a Saturday and Dec. 24 a Thursday.

## Return windows after Black Friday at Ross and Burlington

Ross says returns need "the original receipt within 30 days of purchase." Older receipts get store credit, and returns without a receipt need a valid picture ID. Counting 30 days from Black Friday, Nov. 27, a Ross purchase reaches the limit on Sunday, Dec. 27, 2026 (computed from the 30-day rule).

Burlington says: "All items must be returned within 30 days of purchase. Please bring your original receipt." The same count puts a Nov. 27 purchase at Dec. 27.
