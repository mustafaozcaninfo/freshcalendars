**Ulta Beauty stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and open at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday, Nov. 27, according to Ulta's own Black Friday page, which we read on Nov. 25.** Ulta lists no closing time yet. Its online Cyber Monday sale starts earlier than the holiday itself, on Sunday, Nov. 29.

## Is Ulta open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. Under a "Coming Thurs, Nov 26" heading, Ulta's page says: "These products & 600 more start online at 12:01am CT. Stores are closed." Ulta gives online times in Central Time (CT) only, so shoppers elsewhere can use the conversions below. The Thursday deals are online only, through ulta.com and the app.

## What time does Ulta open on Black Friday 2026?

Under "Coming Fri, Nov 27," Ulta's page says: "These products & 250 more start online at 12:01am CT. In store at 6am local time." Each store opens at 6 a.m. on its own clock. Because of time zones, an Eastern store opens three hours of real time before a Pacific one.

Ulta's pages do not give a Black Friday closing time or hours for Saturday, Nov. 28, Sunday, Nov. 29 or Monday, Nov. 30. Check your own store's page for its Friday hours.

Ulta states every time in CT. The other columns are our conversion. Late-night starts land on the previous calendar day in the western zones.

Ulta writes that the Black Friday sale ends "at midnight CT on November 28, 2026." We read that as the end of Nov. 28, which is the same moment Cyber Monday starts. That reading is ours, not Ulta's.

## When does Ulta's Cyber Monday sale start and end?

Ulta's Cyber Monday page says the sale "begins November 29, 2026, at 12:00 a.m. CT and runs through December 1, 2026, at 2:00 a.m. CT." That is 50 hours, and it begins a day before Cyber Monday itself on Monday, Nov. 30. Our [Cyber Monday date page](/when-is/cyber-monday/) confirms the holiday date, and a [Cyber Monday start-time list across retailers](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/) compares other stores.

The page says to shop online at ulta.com and through the Ulta Beauty app, and that Ulta Beauty Rewards members can earn points on qualifying purchases. It names hair brands such as Redken, Olaplex and Living Proof, and fragrance brands such as Lancôme, Armani Beauty and Marc Jacobs. Ulta's Black Friday page lists a separate Big Holiday Beauty Sale, "In store & online Nov 29-Dec 24."

## How much can you save?

Both the Black Friday and Cyber Monday pages say savings reach up to 50% off select beauty products, with discounts varying by brand and offer. Ulta's Black Friday page also notes that select products may sell out and that no rain checks are issued.
