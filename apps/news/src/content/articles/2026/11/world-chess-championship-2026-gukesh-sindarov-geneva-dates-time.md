**The 2026 World Chess Championship between Gukesh Dommaraju and Javokhir Sindarov is tied at 1½–1½ after three draws, the International Chess Federation (FIDE) said on Nov. 26, 2026; Game 4 is Sat., Nov. 28 at 3 p.m. in Geneva, which is 9 a.m. Eastern Time (ET).** World Chess, the organizer, lists the start as "3pm CET," meaning Central European Time. The 14 classical games run through Fri., Dec. 11, with a tie-break, if needed, set for Sat., Dec. 12. The match is played at the Fondation Martin Bodmer in Cologny, Geneva, and FIDE streams the games on its YouTube channel.

## World Chess Championship 2026 dates in Geneva

FIDE's regulations say the match is "organised from 22 November to 13 December 2026 in Geneva." The schedule starts with arrivals on Fri., Nov. 20; the opening ceremony was held on Sun., Nov. 22, with a media day, and a technical meeting followed on Mon., Nov. 23. World Chess puts the ceremony at the Palais des Nations.

The venue is the Fondation Martin Bodmer, a library and museum in Cologny, Geneva, according to FIDE's Sept. 14, 2026 announcement of the regulations, which gives the match dates as Nov. 22 to Dec. 12. Its title sponsor is Salesforce, so FIDE calls the event the Salesforce FIDE World Championship Match 2026. Tickets are on sale on FIDE's ticket site: a Very Important Person (VIP) Day Pass, which includes an hour in the playing hall, and a Fan Zone Pass. Geneva Tourism lists prices from 19 Swiss francs (CHF).

FIDE's July 28, 2026 announcement had said the match would run "from November 25 to December 15." The later regulations moved it, so older pages may show different dates. Mark the days on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

FIDE's announcement names "reigning World Champion Gukesh D and Challenger Javokhir Sindarov." Gukesh will "defend the crown he won in Singapore in December 2024." FIDE says Sindarov earned the challenge "by winning the FIDE Candidates Tournament 2026 in Cyprus," after his victory at the 2025 FIDE World Cup.

## How is the World Chess Championship 2026 decided?

Article 4.1 sets 14 classical games, and article 4.4.1 says 7.5 points wins. Article 4.2.2 gives the time control: "120 minutes for the first 40 moves, followed by 30 minutes for the rest of the game with an increment of 30 seconds per move starting from move 41." Article 4.3 bars a draw by agreement before Black's 40th move.

If the match is level, article 4.4.2 sets the tie-break:

1. Four games at 15 minutes plus a 10-second increment.
2. Then two games at 10 minutes plus 5 seconds.
3. Then two games at 3 minutes plus 2 seconds.
4. Then single 3+2 games until the match is decided.

There is a 10-minute pause between tie-break games. The tie-break time on Sat., Dec. 12 is not yet announced.

## How much is the prize fund?

Article 4.5.1 sets the prize fund in US dollars (USD) at "2,500,000 USD net of any applicable local taxes". Article 4.5.2(a) pays 200,000 USD for each game won. FIDE says the winner receives USD 1.3 million and the runner-up USD 1.2 million if the final classical score is 7.5-6.5 with 13 decisive games, or 8-6 with all 14 decisive, and the same split applies if the tie-break decides the match.

## Where to watch the World Chess Championship 2026

FIDE's Nov. 26, 2026 announcement calls the FIDE YouTube channel "the official broadcast," and says the games "can be watched on the FIDE YouTube channel with live commentaries by top Grandmasters Peter Leko and Peter Svidler." Maurice Ashley is the host. FIDE gives no broadcast start time.

In Geneva, FIDE says the Fan Zone shows a live broadcast from the playing hall on a big screen. Fan Zone activities include simuls, tournaments, master classes and grandmaster Q&As on selected days.
