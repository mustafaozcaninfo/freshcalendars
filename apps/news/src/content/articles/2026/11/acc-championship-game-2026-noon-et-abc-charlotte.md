**The 2026 Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) Football Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina, with kickoff at noon ET on ABC, according to the ACC.** The conference's announcement is titled "2026 ACC Football Championship Game to kick off at noon ET on ABC" (Jan. 26, 2026), and Fox Sports confirms the date, site, time and network in its 2026 ACC schedule, updated Aug. 19, 2026.

The Charlotte television station (WBTV) reported on Nov. 9, 2026 that "the 22nd annual Atlantic Coast Conference Championship game for 2026 will be the final one in Charlotte." That is one outlet's report; this page does not cover what the ACC plans next. Which two teams play is not set yet.

## Where does the ACC game fall on conference title Saturday?

Three Power Four title games share Dec. 5. The ACC game is the earliest of the three by ET kickoff.

| Southeastern Conference (SEC) Championship Game, Atlanta | 4 p.m. | ABC | ESPN Press Room |

## How does the ACC game feed the College Football Playoff?

The National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) says that in 2026-27 "conference champions from the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC are in automatically" in a 12-team field. ESPN Press Room lists the College Football Playoff (CFP) first round on Dec. 18-19 and the national championship on Jan. 25, 2027, in Las Vegas. Follow the [CFP rankings schedule](/news/college-football-playoff-rankings-2026-schedule-dates-times/) for the weeks before the bracket is set, and mark the dates on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html).

## How will the ACC pick its title-game teams?

ESPN reported on July 15, 2026 that the ACC implemented a new tiebreaker policy for the title game. "The No. 1 tiebreaker remains head-to-head," ESPN wrote, and a later step uses SportSource Analytics' Team Success Ranking: "It will come down to body of work." ESPN's report is the only source used here, so the order of any steps in between is left out.

## Who won the 2025 ACC Championship Game?

In 2025, Duke beat No. 17 Virginia 27-20 in overtime on Sat., Dec. 6, 2025, in Charlotte, ABC11 reported ("Duke wins its eighth ACC football title with 27-20 OT thriller against No. 17 Virginia"). The report called the Blue Devils "unranked" going in. That is last year's result.

The free one-page card lists the noon ET kickoff, the ABC channel, the venue and the times in Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific, with a My pick column. Streaming and ticket details are listed on it as not confirmed.
