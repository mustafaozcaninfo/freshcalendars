**On Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) runs 8 a.m. to midnight, the Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority (MARTA) runs its Sunday schedule and San Francisco's Muni runs a weekend schedule**, according to each agency's own page. Thanksgiving Day is on the [federal holiday list](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/) of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management.

Washington splits its service: Metrobus is on a Sunday schedule, but Metrorail keeps its 6 a.m. to midnight hours. Other large systems had not posted Thanksgiving 2026 service when we checked on Nov. 17, 2026. We found no fare-free service.

## Which agencies run a Sunday schedule on Thanksgiving 2026?

Four systems name a Sunday or weekend schedule for Thursday, Nov. 26. BART says its hours that day will be 8 a.m. to midnight. MARTA runs a Sunday schedule on bus, rail and streetcar. Muni lists a weekend schedule with Sunday operators, and Caltrain lists a weekend schedule.

Washington is the exception to the pattern. WMATA's Thanksgiving page puts Metrobus on a Sunday schedule and gives Metrorail hours of 6 a.m. to midnight. The same page says regular fares are in effect on holidays.

## What changes on Black Friday, Nov. 27, 2026?

The agencies split. BART returns to regular weekday service, 5 a.m. to midnight. MARTA moves to Saturday schedules, with rail on a "Modified Saturday." Muni runs its weekday non-school schedule. Caltrain lists a modified schedule and says its timetables will be released a few weeks before the holiday, so look again closer to the date.

Chicago has a seasonal addition. The Chicago Transit Authority's Holiday Fleet page lists the Holiday Train on the Orange and Green lines on Friday, Nov. 27, and a Holiday Bus on the #22 Clark route that day from about 12:45 p.m. to 8 p.m. It also lists the #56 Milwaukee route on Wednesday, Nov. 25, from about 11:55 a.m. to 4 p.m.

## Is parking free at BART and Metro stations on Thanksgiving?

At BART, yes at most stations. Parking will be free at all stations except Milpitas and Berryessa/North San Jose on Thanksgiving Day, and BART says the day after Thanksgiving is not a parking holiday, so normal parking rules apply on Nov. 27. In Washington, WMATA says parking at Metro-owned garages and lots is free on federal holidays, and Thanksgiving is one.

## What have New York and other systems not posted?

The Long Island Rail Road's timetables page lists "Thanksgiving Day added/adjusted service, effective Thursday, November 26, 2026" for the Babylon, City Terminal Zone, Far Rockaway, Long Beach, Port Jefferson, Port Washington and Ronkonkoma branches. The listing does not say which schedule applies, so check your branch's timetable.

We did not find 2026 Thanksgiving service on the official pages of the New York City subway and buses, Metro-North, NJ Transit, PATH, SEPTA, Metra, LA Metro, King County Metro, Sound Transit, RTD, TriMet and DART. The Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority says its routes "operate on weekend schedules for most major federal and state holidays," but its Thanksgiving entry says "Check back here for details." We left Amtrak extra service out for the same reason. Agencies can post later, so confirm on the agency's page the day before you travel.
