**The Colorado High School Activities Association (CHSAA) plays seven football state championships in 2026: the 6-Man, 8-Man, 1A and 2A finals at Dutch Clark Stadium in Pueblo on Fri., Nov. 27 and Sat., Nov. 28, and the 3A, 4A and 5A finals on Sat., Dec. 5 at Canvas Stadium in Fort Collins.** Kickoffs run from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. Mountain Time (MT); Eastern Time (ET) is 2 hours later. Every game is in the table below, with a printable schedule after it.

## CHSAA football state championships 2026: all seven finals

Four finals are set. The 6-Man and 8-Man matchups come from CHSAANow's Nov. 24, 2026 previews, and the 1A and 2A matchups from its Nov. 21 semifinal roundup, which says "All four championship games are at Dutch Clark Stadium in Pueblo." The 3A, 4A and 5A finals have a date, time and site but no finalists until the semifinals are played. Numbers are playoff seeds as CHSAA prints them. The ET column adds 2 hours to each MT kickoff. Class names link to the CHSAA bracket pages.

| Class | Date | Kickoff (MT) | Kickoff (ET) | Stadium | Matchup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [6-Man](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/6-man) | Fri., Nov. 27 | 12:30 p.m. | 2:30 p.m. | Dutch Clark Stadium, Pueblo | No. 1 Idalia (13-0) vs. No. 2 Peetz (12-1) |
| [8-Man](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/8-man) | Fri., Nov. 27 | 5 p.m. | 7 p.m. | Dutch Clark Stadium, Pueblo | No. 1 McClave vs. No. 3 West Grand |
| [1A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/1a) | Sat., Nov. 28 | 12:30 p.m. | 2:30 p.m. | Dutch Clark Stadium, Pueblo | No. 4 Burlington vs. No. 11 Limon |
| [2A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/2a) | Sat., Nov. 28 | 5 p.m. | 7 p.m. | Dutch Clark Stadium, Pueblo | No. 4 Delta vs. No. 3 Wellington |
| [3A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/3a) | Sat., Dec. 5 | 10 a.m. | noon | Canvas Stadium, Fort Collins | Semifinal winners |
| [4A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/4a) | Sat., Dec. 5 | 2 p.m. | 4 p.m. | Canvas Stadium, Fort Collins | Semifinal winners |
| [5A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/5a) | Sat., Dec. 5 | 6 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Canvas Stadium, Fort Collins | Semifinal winners |

CHSAANow's Sept. 9, 2026 release puts the 3A, 4A and 5A games at Canvas Stadium, home of Colorado State University (CSU) in Fort Collins, on Dec. 5. Sports Illustrated's Nov. 5, 2026 playoff guide agrees. The sources read do not mark a home team for the finals.

## Which finalists won a title in 2025?

CHSAANow's Dec. 6, 2025 recap, "Football: All Seven State Champions Awarded," lists last year's results. Three 2025 champions are back in this year's finals, and Cherry Creek and Palmer Ridge, a 2025 champion and a 2025 finalist, are still alive in the semifinals.

| Class | 2025 final result | Back in 2026? |
|---|---|---|
| 6-Man | Idalia def. Stratton 38-8 | Idalia plays Peetz in the final |
| 8-Man | Haxtun def. Akron 54-28 | Neither team is in the final |
| 1A | Limon def. Buena Vista 14-10 | Limon plays Burlington in the final |
| 2A | Wellington def. Elizabeth 35-0 | Wellington plays Delta in the final |
| 3A | Pomona def. Windsor 17-14 | Neither team is in the semifinals |
| 4A | Dakota Ridge def. Palmer Ridge 38-14 | Palmer Ridge is in the 3A semifinals; Dakota Ridge is not in the 4A semifinals |
| 5A | Cherry Creek def. Ralston Valley 56-28 | Cherry Creek is in the 5A semifinals |

## How do fans get tickets and watch the Colorado finals?

For the Pueblo games, CHSAANow's 6-Man preview says tickets are sold through CSU Pueblo. A 2026 ticket price was not posted in the sources read. For Canvas Stadium, 2026 ticket details are not yet announced. In 2025, CHSAANow's Canvas Stadium information page said the games were televised live on Altitude and streamed on the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network, and that tickets were sold only through GoFan. No 2026 broadcaster is announced.
