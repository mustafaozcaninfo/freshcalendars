**The Saint Louis Art Museum lists its Kwanzaa Celebration 2026 on Sunday, Dec. 27, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. CST, free, and four other organizers have posted 2026 dates.** Brooklyn Children's Museum runs Celebrate Kwanzaa from Saturday, Dec. 26, to Wednesday, Dec. 30. Children's Museum Houston, the DuSable Black History Museum and Education Center in Chicago and the National Great Blacks in Wax Museum in Baltimore complete the list. The table below puts the five pages together with the day's principle, which no single organizer page does.

## Kwanzaa events in 2026 by city

Kwanzaa 2026 runs from Saturday, Dec. 26, to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, according to [Maulana Karenga's site](https://www.maulanakarenga.org/kwanzaa/). Times are local to each city; all five pages were read on Nov. 24, 2026.

| Date | City | Event and organizer | Time | Cost and registration | Principle that day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sat., Dec. 26 to Wed., Dec. 30 | Brooklyn, NY | [Celebrate Kwanzaa, Brooklyn Children's Museum](https://www.brooklynkids.org/programs/kwanzaa/) | 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. each day | Museum ticket per day; price not listed on the event page | Umoja on Dec. 26, Nia on Dec. 30 |
| Sat., Dec. 26 | Baltimore, MD | [Kwanzaa 2026 - It's All About The Children, National Great Blacks in Wax Museum](https://www.greatblacksinwax.org/kwanzaa) | 6 p.m. ET (doors 5:45 p.m.) | Registration required per the Kwanzaa page; no price listed | Umoja |

| Mon., Dec. 28 | Houston, TX | [Celebrating Kwanzaa, Children's Museum Houston](https://www.cmhouston.org/events/celebrating-kwanzaa) | 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. CT (10 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET) | With general admission; "Register for Event" button | Ujima |
| Tue., Dec. 29 | Chicago, IL | [The Du Kwanzaa Celebration, DuSable Black History Museum](https://www.dusablemuseum.org/events) | 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. CT (11 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET) | Venue, price and registration not on the page | Ujamaa |

The principle column follows the order on the [Official Kwanzaa Website](https://www.officialkwanzaawebsite.org/); the [Kwanzaa 2026 dates and principles story](/news/kwanzaa-2026-dates-seven-principles-calendar-printable/) explains each one.

## What does the Saint Louis Art Museum list?

The museum's event page reads "Sunday, December 27, 2026 | 10 am–3 pm CST | Free." It is held at the museum on One Fine Arts Drive in Forest Park. Registration is not mentioned on the page. Dec. 27 is the second day of Kwanzaa, which the Official Kwanzaa Website pairs with Kujichagulia (Self-Determination).

## What are the Brooklyn Children's Museum dates?

The Brooklyn Children's Museum lists "Saturday, December 26 – Wednesday, December 30" and "10:00 am – 5:00 pm." The page shows no year; the weekdays match 2026. It links a separate ticket for each day, from 12/26 to 12/30, and does not list a price on the event page.

## What do the Houston, Chicago and Baltimore pages say?

Children's Museum Houston lists "Monday, December 28, 2026" and "9:00 am - 5:00 pm" for Celebrating Kwanzaa at 1500 Binz St., with general admission. The page has a "Register for Event" button and says workshops are limited to 15 participants aged 5 and older.

The DuSable Black History Museum and Education Center lists "December 29, 2026 10:00 am - 5:00 pm" for The Du Kwanzaa Celebration on its events page. The page does not give a venue, price or registration, so confirm those with the DuSable.

The National Great Blacks in Wax Museum lists Kwanzaa 2026 - It's All About The Children on Saturday, Dec. 26, at 6 p.m., with doors at 5:45 p.m., at Henderson-Hopkins Elementary, 2100 Ashland Avenue. No end time or price is listed. The two museum pages disagree on registration. The Kwanzaa page says registration is required and asks visitors to complete the form below it, while the street fair page lists the same event as registration closed. Check the form before you go.

## Which Kwanzaa events are not posted yet?

As of Nov. 24, 2026, the official pages of the Smithsonian National Museum of African American History and Culture, the Smithsonian Anacostia Community Museum, the Charles H. Wright Museum of African American History in Detroit, the Apollo Theater in New York and the American Museum of Natural History had not posted 2026 Kwanzaa dates. No dates from earlier years are listed here for them.
