## How Wordle works

You get six tries to find a five-letter word. After each guess, a green tile means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means the letter is in the word but in another spot, and gray means it is not in the word at all. A letter that appears twice is only marked twice if you guess it twice.

## Six clues, gentle to strong

1. **Part of speech:** it is a noun.
2. **Topic:** a reptile with no legs.
3. **Vowels:** two vowel letters, I and E. The I comes second and the E comes fourth.
4. **Repeated letter:** no, all five letters are different.
5. **First letter:** V.
6. **Strong nudge:** a venomous snake.

## Guessing tips for today

The word ends in R, so a guess that puts R in the last spot can turn a tile green right away. A starter with I, E and a common consonant, such as "raise," shows whether the I lands in the second spot and the E in the fourth. Because no letter repeats, you can spend each guess on new letters without testing doubles.
