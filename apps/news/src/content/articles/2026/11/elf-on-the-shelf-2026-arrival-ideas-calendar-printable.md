**Most Scout Elves return the night of Thanksgiving or on Dec. 1, according to The Elf on the Shelf's November prep checklist.** In 2026 that means Thursday, Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving Day, per the Office of Personnel Management (OPM)) or Tuesday, Dec. 1. By our count that leaves 29 nights to Christmas Eve for the first date and 24 for the second. Below is a table with one easy idea for each of the 29 nights, and a free printable idea calendar you can stick on the fridge.

## When do Scout Elves return in 2026?

The Elf on the Shelf blog says: "Most Scout Elves return the night of Thanksgiving or on December 1." The page also says "this is the month to finalize your elf's big arrival date" and allows for "somewhere in between." The page shows no date, so we do not call it a 2026 page. The brand says Scout Elves return each season, and we use its word.

| Return night | 2026 date | Weekday | Nights to Christmas Eve (our count) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving night | Nov. 26 | Thursday | 29 |
| Dec. 1 | Dec. 1 | Tuesday | 24 |
| Christmas Eve | Dec. 24 | Thursday | last night counted |

OPM lists Thanksgiving Day 2026 as "Thursday, November 26." Both counts include the first and the last night. The brand's pages we read give no departure date. Some families choose Christmas Eve as the last night; that is a family choice, so the table stops there only because it is the last night before Christmas Day. For the holiday itself, see [when Thanksgiving falls](/when-is/thanksgiving/) each year.

## What should you prepare before the Scout Elf returns?

The checklist advises preparing supplies for the first 3 to 5 days and suggests you "set some calendar alerts so you'll remember a few days before your elf's big arrival." That is the job of the idea table and the printable below. Mark the return night on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html), then keep the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) beside the idea sheet for the rest of the run.

## Where can you get the printable Elf on the Shelf idea calendar?

The free one-page sheet lists all 29 nights with the date, both night counts and a tick box. T numbers count from Thanksgiving night and D numbers from Dec. 1, so a family on the Dec. 1 start reads the D column and ignores the five nights before it. It is plain text and boxes, with no drawing of the elf. For the official ideas, The Elf on the Shelf runs its own [Elf Ideas page](https://elfontheshelf.com/scout-elf-ideas-intro/); we link to it and do not copy it.
