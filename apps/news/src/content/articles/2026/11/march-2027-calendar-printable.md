**March 2027 starts on Monday, Mar. 1 and has 31 days, no federal holiday and 23 working days.** The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists Washington's Birthday on Feb. 15 and Memorial Day on May 31 in 2027, with nothing between them. The free one-page printable marks 10 days from official sources, with the clock change, the first day of spring and every Moon phase with its time.

## March 2027 at a glance

The month begins on a Monday and ends on a Wednesday. Because 31 days is four full weeks plus three days, those three weekdays each occur five times. The other four weekdays occur four times. Both a Monday-start and a Sunday-start calendar fit it in five rows.

| | March 2027 |
|---|---|
| First day | Monday, Mar. 1 |
| Last day | Wednesday, Mar. 31 |
| Days | 31 (23 weekdays, 8 weekend days) |
| Five-time weekdays | Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday |
| Four-time weekdays | Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday |
| Federal holidays | None |
| Working days | 23 |
| First day of spring | Sat., Mar. 20, 4:25 p.m. EDT |
| Full Moon | Mon., Mar. 22, 6:44 a.m. EDT |

The 23 working days are the weekdays, since the month holds no federal holiday. ## March 2027 Moon phases and the spring equinox

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes Moon phases in Universal Time (UT). Clocks in most of the U.S. change on Sunday, Mar. 14, 2027 at 2 a.m. local time, according to 15 U.S.C. 260a, so the New Moon is in Eastern Standard Time (EST) and the other three phases are in Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). Our [clock change 2027](/news/clock-changes-2027-when-does-daylight-saving-time-start-again-march-14/) story has the details.

| Event | UT | Eastern time |
|---|---|---|
| New Moon | Mar. 8, 09:29 | Mon., Mar. 8, 4:29 a.m. EST |
| First Quarter Moon | Mar. 15, 16:25 | Mon., Mar. 15, 12:25 p.m. EDT |
| March equinox (spring begins) | Mar. 20, 20:25 | Sat., Mar. 20, 4:25 p.m. EDT |
| Full Moon | Mar. 22, 10:44 | Mon., Mar. 22, 6:44 a.m. EDT |
| Last Quarter Moon | Mar. 30, 00:54 | Mon., Mar. 29, 8:54 p.m. EDT |

The last quarter Moon is on Mar. 30 in Universal Time but still Monday evening, Mar. 29, on the U.S. East Coast. The equinox time comes from USNO's Earth's Seasons data, which lists the March equinox as the start of spring in the Northern Hemisphere.

## State holidays in March 2027: Texas, Vermont and California

Federal offices work the whole month, yet three states give March its own days. The Federal Reserve's holiday schedule for 2027 has no closure in March either, so banks keep normal hours.

| Date | Holiday | State | What the state's schedule says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tue., Mar. 2 | Town Meeting Day | Vermont | State employee holiday |
| Tue., Mar. 2 | Texas Independence Day | Texas | Offices open with a skeleton crew |
| Fri., Mar. 26 | Good Friday | Texas | Optional holiday |
| Wed., Mar. 31 | Holiday on March 31 | California | State employee holiday |

The Texas rows come from the Comptroller's fiscal 2027 schedule, Vermont's from its Department of Human Resources and California's from the California Department of Human Resources (CalHR), which lists the day by its date. Illinois has no state holiday in March: its 2027 list goes from Washington's Birthday on Feb. 15 to Memorial Day on May 31, according to the Illinois Department of Central Management Services. In the UK, St. Patrick's Day on Wednesday, Mar. 17 is a bank holiday in Northern Ireland only, GOV.UK says.

Sunday, Mar. 14 carries two big events: Selection Sunday for both NCAA tournaments, according to NCAA.com, and the 99th Oscars at 7 p.m. ET on ABC, according to the Academy. The men's Final Four is on Saturday, Apr. 3 in Detroit, per NCAA.com, and our [college basketball 2026-27 key dates](/news/college-basketball-2026-27-key-dates-season-start-march-madness-printable/) story has every round. ## Tax deadlines and the New York to London time gap in March 2027

The Internal Revenue Service says S corporations file Form 1120-S by the 15th day of the third month, which is Monday, Mar. 15, 2027 for calendar-year filers. In Canada, the RRSP contribution deadline for 2026 returns is Monday, Mar. 1, 2027 by the 60-day count; our [RRSP deadline story](/news/rrsp-deadline-2027-march-1-canada-tax-season-dates/) notes the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) had not posted its own 2027 date.

The U.S. clock change is on Mar. 14 and the UK change on Sunday, Mar. 28 at 1 a.m., according to GOV.UK, so the gap between New York and London changes twice, computed from the offsets. Our [UK clocks 2027 story](/news/uk-clocks-go-forward-2027-march-28-bst/) has the detail.

| Dates | New York | London | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar. 1 to Mar. 13 | EST, UTC-5 | GMT, UTC+0 | 5 hours |
| Mar. 14 to Mar. 27 | EDT, UTC-4 | GMT, UTC+0 | 4 hours |
| From Mar. 28 | EDT, UTC-4 | BST, UTC+1 | 5 hours |
