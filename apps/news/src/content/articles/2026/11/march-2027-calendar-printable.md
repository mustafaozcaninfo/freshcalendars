**March 2027 starts on Monday, Mar. 1 and has 31 days, no federal holiday and 23 working days.** The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists Washington's Birthday on Feb. 15 and Memorial Day on May 31 in 2027, with nothing between them. The free one-page printable at the bottom of this story marks 14 dates from official sources, including every Moon phase with its time.

## March 2027 at a glance

The month begins on a Monday and ends on a Wednesday. Because 31 days is four full weeks plus three days, those three weekdays each occur five times. The other four weekdays occur four times. Both a Monday-start and a Sunday-start calendar fit it in five rows.

| | March 2027 |
|---|---|
| First day | Monday, Mar. 1 |
| Last day | Wednesday, Mar. 31 |
| Days | 31 (23 weekdays, 8 weekend days) |
| Five-time weekdays | Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday |
| Four-time weekdays | Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday |
| Federal holidays | None |
| Working days | 23 |
| First day of spring | Sat., Mar. 20, 4:25 p.m. EDT |
| Full Moon | Mon., Mar. 22, 6:44 a.m. EDT |

The 23 working days are the weekdays, since the month holds no federal holiday. ## March 2027 Moon phases and the spring equinox

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes Moon phases in Universal Time (UT). Clocks in most of the U.S. change on Sunday, Mar. 14, 2027 at 2 a.m. local time, according to 15 U.S.C. 260a, so the New Moon is in Eastern Standard Time (EST) and the other three phases are in Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). Our [clock change 2027](/news/clock-changes-2027-when-does-daylight-saving-time-start-again-march-14/) story has the details.

| Event | UT | Eastern time |
|---|---|---|
| New Moon | Mar. 8, 09:29 | Mon., Mar. 8, 4:29 a.m. EST |
| First Quarter Moon | Mar. 15, 16:25 | Mon., Mar. 15, 12:25 p.m. EDT |
| March equinox (spring begins) | Mar. 20, 20:25 | Sat., Mar. 20, 4:25 p.m. EDT |
| Full Moon | Mar. 22, 10:44 | Mon., Mar. 22, 6:44 a.m. EDT |
| Last Quarter Moon | Mar. 30, 00:54 | Mon., Mar. 29, 8:54 p.m. EDT |

The last quarter Moon is on Mar. 30 in Universal Time but still Monday evening, Mar. 29, on the U.S. East Coast. The equinox time comes from USNO's Earth's Seasons data, which lists the March equinox as the start of spring in the Northern Hemisphere.

## March 2027 holidays and key dates

No date in the table is a federal holiday. St. Patrick's Day and Pi Day are calendar dates, and Pi Day falls on Sunday, Mar. 14, the same day as the clock change, Selection Sunday and the Oscars. Each other row comes from the organization that sets it.

Ash Wednesday, the start of Lent, was Feb. 10, according to the Church of England, which is why Mothering Sunday lands on Mar. 7 and Palm Sunday on Mar. 21. Eid al-Fitr depends on the sighting of the Moon, so it is not on the printable.
