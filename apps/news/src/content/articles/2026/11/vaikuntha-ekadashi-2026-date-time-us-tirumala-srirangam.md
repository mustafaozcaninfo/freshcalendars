**Vaikuntha Ekadashi, also called Mukkoti Ekadashi, falls on Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, according to Drik Panchang's calculation for New Delhi.** The Ekadashi tithi (lunar day) begins at 11:39 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Saturday, Dec. 19, and ends at 9:44 a.m. ET on Sunday, Dec. 20. The Hans India and United News of India (UNI) report that Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) plans a 10-day Vaikunta Dwara Darshan at Tirumala from Dec. 20 to 29. Below are the times in four U.S. zones, the darshan days and what is still not announced.

## Vaikuntha Ekadashi 2026 times in ET, CT, MT and PT

Drik Panchang, an almanac publisher and not an official body, gives the Ekadashi tithi for New Delhi as beginning at 10:09 p.m. Indian Standard Time (IST) on Dec. 19, 2026, and ending at 8:14 p.m. IST on Dec. 20. Its parana (fast-breaking) time is 7:10 a.m. to 9:13 a.m. IST on Dec. 21. We converted those times with IST as Eastern Time plus 10 hours 30 minutes, which holds in December when the U.S. is on standard time. Central, Mountain and Pacific Time are one, two and three hours behind ET.

| Time zone | Ekadashi begins (Sat., Dec. 19) | Ekadashi ends (Sun., Dec. 20) | Parana window (Sun., Dec. 20) |
|---|---|---|---|
| IST, New Delhi (source) | 10:09 p.m. | 8:14 p.m. | 7:10 a.m. to 9:13 a.m. on Mon., Dec. 21 |
| Eastern Time (ET) | 11:39 a.m. | 9:44 a.m. | 8:40 p.m. to 10:43 p.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | 10:39 a.m. | 8:44 a.m. | 7:40 p.m. to 9:43 p.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 9:39 a.m. | 7:44 a.m. | 6:40 p.m. to 8:43 p.m. |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 8:39 a.m. | 6:44 a.m. | 5:40 p.m. to 7:43 p.m. |

These are New Delhi-based times converted to U.S. clocks. A U.S. temple sets its own fasting and parana times from local sunrise, so the figures above can differ from the notice at your temple. Ask the temple before you plan the fast.

## Is Gita Jayanti the same day as Vaikuntha Ekadashi?

In the New Delhi reckoning, yes. Drik Panchang lists Gita Jayanti and Mokshada Ekadashi on Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, the same day it gives for Vaikuntha Ekadashi. A calendar set to a U.S. city can show Gita Jayanti on Saturday, Dec. 19, because the tithi begins on Saturday morning in the U.S. (11:39 a.m. ET in the table). Confirm the day with your temple instead of relying on one app.

## Tirumala Vaikunta Dwara Darshan dates, Dec. 20-29

As reported by The Hans India (Nov. 6, 2026) and UNI (Nov. 5, 2026) from a TTD review meeting, the 10-day Vaikunta Dwara Darshan is "scheduled from December 20 to 29." TTD's own sites returned server errors when we checked, so the dates rest on those two newsrooms. The Hans India adds how the days split.

| Day | Date | As reported by The Hans India |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sun., Dec. 20 | Darshan tokens by online allotment |
| 2 | Mon., Dec. 21 | Darshan tokens by online allotment |
| 3 | Tue., Dec. 22 | Darshan tokens by online allotment |
| 4 | Wed., Dec. 23 | Sarva Darshan |
| 5 | Thu., Dec. 24 | Sarva Darshan |
| 6 | Fri., Dec. 25 | Sarva Darshan, with special arrangements |
| 7 | Sat., Dec. 26 | Sarva Darshan |
| 8 | Sun., Dec. 27 | Sarva Darshan |
| 9 | Mon., Dec. 28 | Sarva Darshan |
| 10 | Tue., Dec. 29 | Sarva Darshan |

The Hans India wrote that tokens are to be allotted online for the first three days, that "Sarva Darshan will be provided for the remaining seven days" and that "special arrangements will also be made for Sarva Darshan on December 25." The split into days 1-3 and 4-10 is the newspaper's report, not a TTD notice we could read.

## What has Tirumala and Srirangam not announced?

- **Online token registration window and token release dates:** not yet announced.
- **Rs 300 Special Entry Darshan release date:** not yet announced.
- **Number of token days:** reported as the first three days by The Hans India; treat it as unconfirmed until TTD posts it.
- **Srirangam Sri Ranganathaswamy temple, Paramapada Vasal (Sorgavasal) opening date for 2026:** not yet announced; the temple had not posted it on Nov. 20.

Check the TTD site and the temple's own notice for each of these before you travel.
