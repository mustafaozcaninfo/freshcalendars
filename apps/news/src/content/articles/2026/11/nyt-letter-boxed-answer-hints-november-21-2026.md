## The longest word and the pairs that never occur

The longest word in the dictionary has 15 letters, and it is the only one that long. It uses 8 different letters and touches all four sides, yet it is no use for a two-word finish: the longest word in any two-word route has 13 letters. Another 18 words have 13 or 14 letters.

Moving between two letters of the same side is forbidden, which rules out 24 ordered pairs. That leaves 108 pairs across sides, and only one of them, C followed by P, never occurs in the dictionary. Three more occur in a single word each: P then K, T then N and K then C.

With one dead pair among 108, the box rarely blocks a word. The choice today is among many routes, and the R alone joins 85 of the 189 routes.
