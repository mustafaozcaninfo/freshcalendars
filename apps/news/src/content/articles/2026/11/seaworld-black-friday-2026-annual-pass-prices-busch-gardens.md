**SeaWorld Orlando listed its Platinum Pass at $351 a year, down from $549, on its limited-time offers page on Nov. 23, 2026, with each product labeled "Black Friday".** That is $198 off, and the page covers "11 parks". Busch Gardens Tampa Bay's single-day ticket was $71.99 (was $150.99) on its ticket page the same day. SeaWorld has not posted an end date on its offer pages.

## SeaWorld Orlando Black Friday 2026 pass prices

SeaWorld Orlando's banner reads "Save up to 60% on Tickets, Fun Cards, & Passes." Prices are per person as shown online on Nov. 23, 2026, and can change. The page says "Low monthly payments. No down payment. 0% annual percentage rate (APR) financing available." The saving and percentage columns are ours, worked from the annual prices.

| Pass | Monthly (was) | Annual | Regular annual | Saving | Saving % | Page's wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | $14.25 ($16.50) | $171.00 | $198.00 | $27.00 | 14% | "Save Up to 20%" |
| Silver | $16.50 ($23.25) | $198.00 | $279.00 | $81.00 | 29% | "Save Up to 25%" |
| Gold | $18.50 ($26.25) | $222.00 | $315.00 | $93.00 | 30% | "Save Up to 30%" |
| Platinum | $29.25 ($45.75) | $351.00 | $549.00 | $198.00 | 36% | "Save 35%" |

The Platinum Pass covers "11 parks"; the page does not list them in the text read. At the annual price the Bronze saving is below the page's "up to 20%", while Silver and Platinum come out above their percentages on the page.

## Which dates are blocked out for Quick Queue and other add-ons?

SeaWorld Orlando's page lists Quick Queue, Reserved Seating, Very Important Person (VIP) Tour and animal tours as blocked out Nov. 20-29 and Dec. 19-31, 2026. A Quick Queue bought in the sale cannot be used on Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, or Black Friday, Nov. 27, and not between Dec. 19 and 31. The page gives no Thanksgiving Day or Black Friday park hours; check the park's hours calendar.

## Do the passes include the Christmas events?

SeaWorld Orlando's Christmas Celebration runs "November 6 - January 4, 2027 on select dates" and is included with a SeaWorld Annual Pass and with admission, its event page says. Busch Gardens Tampa Bay's Christmas Town runs on select dates from Nov. 13, 2026, through Jan. 4, 2027, and is included with admission and the annual pass. Check each park's calendar for the dates.
