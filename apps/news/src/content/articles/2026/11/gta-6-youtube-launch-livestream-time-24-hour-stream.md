**YouTube says a 24-hour Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI launch stream starts Thursday, Nov. 19 at 12 a.m. ET on youtube.com/youtube, with a pre-show hosted by RDCWorld at 11 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Nov. 18,** according to the YouTube Official Blog post of Nov. 12, 2026. The stream runs 24 hours, so by our conversion it ends at 12 a.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 20.

## GTA 6 launch stream times in ET, CT, MT, PT and GMT

YouTube gives its times in Eastern Time only. The other columns are our conversion of those times.

| Moment | ET | CT | MT | PT | GMT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-show starts | 11 p.m. Wed., Nov. 18 | 10 p.m. Wed., Nov. 18 | 9 p.m. Wed., Nov. 18 | 8 p.m. Wed., Nov. 18 | 4 a.m. Thu., Nov. 19 |
| Stream starts | 12 a.m. Thu., Nov. 19 | 11 p.m. Wed., Nov. 18 | 10 p.m. Wed., Nov. 18 | 9 p.m. Wed., Nov. 18 | 5 a.m. Thu., Nov. 19 |
| Stream ends (24 hours later) | 12 a.m. Fri., Nov. 20 | 11 p.m. Thu., Nov. 19 | 10 p.m. Thu., Nov. 19 | 9 p.m. Thu., Nov. 19 | 5 a.m. Fri., Nov. 20 |

For viewers on the West Coast, the pre-show begins at 8 p.m. on Wednesday, and the stream proper begins at 9 p.m. the same evening. Pacific viewers see the stream start before the calendar turns to launch day.

## Who is on the YouTube GTA VI stream?

YouTube describes "a 24 hour stream packed with live gameplay, creator podcasts, and original content featuring top creators like Caylus, Zhong and Kat, Jelly, Rivers, Jake Baldino, Cinna and more!" The pre-show is "hosted by RDCWorld."

YouTube says the marathon "will wrap with live on-the-ground coverage from Miami, bringing viewers to the action alongside creators and special guests." The post does not give a time for the Miami segment or a full order of segments. An hour-by-hour run of show is not announced.

## How to find the YouTube GTA 6 stream

The address is youtube.com/youtube. YouTube also says fans can jump straight into the global broadcast by clicking its custom Grand Theft Auto VI-themed logo, which it calls a Yoodle, on the YouTube homepage.

## Is this a Rockstar stream?

No. It is YouTube's stream on YouTube's own channel, announced by YouTube. Rockstar Games has not announced a launch livestream of its own, and no Rockstar source for this event was found.

## How the stream lines up with the game's launch hours

The stream's start is a clock time in Eastern Time. The game's release hour is set differently by platform:

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| PlayStation (support page) | "Grand Theft Auto VI releases at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation®5 consoles." |
| Vice (Nov. 13, 2026) | Reported that the U.S. PlayStation Store lists a release time of 9 p.m. PT on Nov. 18, which is 12 a.m. ET on Nov. 19. |
| Xbox Wire (Nov. 10, 2026) | Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S starts "at 1am ET." |

If the U.S. PlayStation Store listing that Vice reported holds, the YouTube stream and that listing share the same 12 a.m. ET start. Xbox Cloud Gaming begins an hour later, at 1 a.m. ET, which is 10 p.m. PT on Wednesday by our conversion. A single worldwide release moment is not confirmed, and because PlayStation says "midnight local time," players in time zones ahead of the U.S. reach launch day earlier.
