**Fifteen official observances fall between Monday, Dec. 7 and Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026, and Thursday, Dec. 10 holds two of them: Human Rights Day and, in the UK, Christmas Jumper Day.** Each comes from the United Nations (UN) observances list, U.S. law, a church calendar or an organizer's own page. A planner for the week follows the table.

Only days with a named setter are listed. Commercial "national food days" are left out, and so are the Nobel Prize day on Dec. 10 and the National Guard birthday on Dec. 13, because we found no official page we could read for either. Hanukkah began at sundown on Friday, Dec. 4, so it runs through the whole week and shows on its last two days.

## Monday and Tuesday: Pearl Harbor and the Immaculate Conception

The law says: "December 7 is National Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day." Under 36 U.S. Code 129 the flag goes to half-staff each Dec. 7. The 2025 proclamation was dated Dec. 5, 2025; the 2026 proclamation has not been issued as of Nov. 13. Our [Pearl Harbor story](/news/national-pearl-harbor-remembrance-day-2026-december-7-flags-half-staff/) has the rest. The UN list shows International Civil Aviation Day on 07 Dec; the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) theme for 2026 is not yet announced.

The Liturgy Office of England and Wales lists "Tuesday | 8 | The Immaculate Conception of the Blessed Virgin Mary | solemnity". In the United States, the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB) lists "December 8, the solemnity of the Immaculate Conception" among its holy days of obligation. The USCCB rule that lifts the Mass obligation on a Saturday or Monday names only Jan. 1, Aug. 15 and Nov. 1, as our [Immaculate Conception story](/news/immaculate-conception-2026-holy-day-of-obligation-december-8/) explains.

## Wednesday and Thursday: corruption, genocide, human rights and jumpers

The UN list shows two days on 09 Dec: International Anti-Corruption Day and the International Day of Commemoration and Dignity of the Victims of the Crime of Genocide and of the Prevention of this Crime. The 2026 theme of the Anti-Corruption Day is not yet announced from a source we could confirm.

The UN's Human Rights Day page gives the 2026 theme as "Making Humanity Together" with the line "Human rights are our shared story. Our shared power. Our shared future." The day marks the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, proclaimed 10 December 1948; see [Human Rights Day 2026](/news/human-rights-day-2026-december-10/).

Save the Children UK says: "The official date is Thursday 10 December 2026" for Christmas Jumper Day. It is a UK charity day, and our [Christmas Jumper Day planner](/news/christmas-jumper-day-2026-thursday-10-december-planner/) covers it.

## Friday to Sunday: mountains, Hanukkah and Guadalupe

The UN lists International Mountain Day on 11 Dec. The FAO gives the 2026 theme as "Mountain pastoralists: Sustaining rangelands, knowledge and livelihoods". Its page also says the 2026 observance event is in New York on 15 December, so the event date differs from the Dec. 11 day.

Hebcal gives the eighth candle as "Friday, December 11". It says Chanukah "begins at sundown on Friday, 4 December 2026 and ends at nightfall on Saturday, 12 December 2026"; our [Hanukkah 2026 story](/news/hanukkah-2026-dates-calendar/) has the dates. Hebcal's page gives no clock times, so none are printed here.

Saturday, Dec. 12 also carries two UN days, the International Day of Neutrality and International Universal Health Coverage Day. The England and Wales calendar reads "Saturday | 12 | Weekday of Advent, Our Lady of Guadalupe"; see [Our Lady of Guadalupe Day 2026](/news/our-lady-of-guadalupe-day-2026-december-12-las-posadas-december-16-24/). The same calendar lists "Sunday | 13 | 3rd Sunday of Advent".
