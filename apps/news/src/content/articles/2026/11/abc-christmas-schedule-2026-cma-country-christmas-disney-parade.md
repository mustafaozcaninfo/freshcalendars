**American Broadcasting Company (ABC) lists 24 holiday airings from Wed., Nov. 25 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026, and the Disney Parks Magical Christmas Day Parade is on Fri., Dec. 25 at 10:00 a.m. EST, according to ABC's Oct. 27, 2026 release "ABC Decks the Halls with Festive Holiday Programming for 2026."** The release says "all times listed are Eastern/Pacific." Every row below comes from it.

## What time is the Disney Christmas Day parade on ABC?

ABC's release gives four zone times for the parade on Fri., Dec. 25, 2026, and the Mountain and Pacific entries are the same:

| Zone | Time as ABC lists it |
|---|---|
| Eastern | 10:00 a.m. EST |
| Central | 9:00 a.m. CST |
| Mountain | 5:00 a.m. MST |
| Pacific | 5:00 a.m. PST |

The release names no hosts or performers for the 2026 parade. Local ABC stations set their own times in the Central and Mountain zones, so check your listings.
