## Which letters cross between boards today?

The four words hold 20 letter slots but only 13 different letters, so seven slots repeat something. Five of those are letters shared between two words, and two come from words that use one letter twice. Five letters each belong to two words, and eight belong to one; none reaches three.

| Words containing the letter | Letters | What it means for play |
|---|---|---|
| 3 or 4 of 4 | 0 | No single tile can be tracked across three grids |
| 2 of 4 | 5 | A yellow or green settles part of two boards at once |
| 1 of 4 | 8 | A gray here tells you nothing about the other three grids |

One word links to each of the other three through a shared letter, and another links to just one. That makes the first an anchor for the whole set and the second a board you will probably solve alone. Two words repeat a letter within themselves, which is worth remembering before you rule a double out.

Beebom, Ponly and Analytics Insight list these four words in this order and each labels the puzzle #1759. Ponly and Analytics Insight place them on the grid as Creek top left, Macho top right, Slush bottom left and Minus bottom right.

| Letter | Words containing it |
