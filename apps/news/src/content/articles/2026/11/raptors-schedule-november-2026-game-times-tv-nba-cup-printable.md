**The Toronto Raptors play at the LA Clippers on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, at 7 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 10 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.**

The NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change," lists 14 Raptors games in November. Toronto is on Eastern Time, and Canada and the U.S. ended daylight saving time on Nov. 1, so every time below is standard time. The table adds a Pacific column (ET minus 3 hours) and the local time the NBA prints for road games.

## How many home and road games do the Raptors have in November?

By our count from the NBA schedule, the Raptors play 14 games from Nov. 1 to Nov. 29: 8 at home and 6 on the road. The road games are at the Lakers, Clippers, Denver, Milwaukee, Philadelphia and Detroit. The home games are Nov. 9, 11, 15, 17, 18, 23, 27 and 29.

The schedule also has three back-to-backs, by our count: Nov. 1 and 2 at the Lakers and Clippers, Nov. 11 and 12 (Boston at home, then at Philadelphia) and Nov. 17 and 18 (the Knicks, then Washington, both at home). The Knicks visit Toronto twice in three days, on Nov. 15 and Nov. 17.

## Which Raptors games count in the NBA Cup?

The NBA says group play runs from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, with Cup nights in November on Nov. 6, 13, 20, 24, 25 and 27. The Raptors are in East Group A with the Pistons, Magic, Bucks and Nets, according to NBA.com's group preview. Their first group game was at home against Orlando on Oct. 30. In November the schedule flags three more: Fri., Nov. 6 at Milwaukee, Fri., Nov. 20 at Detroit and Fri., Nov. 27 against Brooklyn.
