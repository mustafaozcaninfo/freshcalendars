**New Year's Eve 2026 is Thursday, Dec. 31, and a free four-page printable covers the night: a 25-square bingo card, resolution cards with a 2027 check-in grid, a 2026 look-back sheet and a midnight countdown card.** New Year's Day 2027 is Friday, Jan. 1, listed as a federal holiday in the 2027 table of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM). OPM's list does not include New Year's Eve.

## New Year's Eve bingo printable: 25 squares

Page 1 is a five-by-five card under the letters B, I, N, G and O. The center square is free ("Happy 2027!") and the other 24 hold party moments, among them "Someone says 'Happy New Year' early," "A toast," "A resolution is shared," "Confetti," "Countdown from 10," "Fireworks, outside or on a screen" and "A story about this year." None names a brand or a person, so the card works at any party.

To play, print one copy for each guest and mark squares as they happen. Five in a row, across, down or corner to corner, wins. For a caller game, cut a copy into squares and draw them from a bowl.

## New Year's resolution printable for 2027

Page 2 holds four resolution cards. Each has three lines ("I will," "Why it matters," "My first step") and a row of 12 boxes from Jan. to Dec. 2027 to tick at a monthly check-in.

Above each box is the weekday of the 1st of that month, computed from OPM's 2027 table, which has Jan. 1 on a Friday. 2027 is not a leap year.

## 2026 year in review printable

Page 3 is a look-back with six prompts and a one-word line: "My best day of 2026," "A new place I went," "A book or song I loved," "Something I learned," "What I am proudest of" and "People I want to thank." Each has two writing lines. Fill it in before midnight, or read the answers aloud around the table, then keep it with the resolution cards so the 2026 answers and the 2027 plans sit side by side.

## Midnight countdown card

Page 4 has ten boxes, numbered 10 down to 1, each with a tick box, then a banner reading "Happy New Year 2027" with the date, Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. A row of circles marks the time zone (ET, CT, MT, PT or other), and three lines record where the group watched the countdown, who counted with them and a first wish for 2027.

The card sets no time. Midnight comes at the local clock of each place, which is why the page asks for the time zone. For the Times Square ball drop, see the [ball drop story](/news/times-square-ball-drop-2026-time-how-to-watch-time-zones/).
