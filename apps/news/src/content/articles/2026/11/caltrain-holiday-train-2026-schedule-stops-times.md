**Caltrain's Holiday Train runs Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, leaving San Francisco at 4:20 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 7:20 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), and arriving at San Jose Diridon at 5:19 p.m. PT, according to Caltrain.** The northbound trip leaves San Jose Diridon at 6:11 p.m. PT and reaches San Francisco at 7:25 p.m. PT. Tickets for the onboard ride are sold out, and the tables below list every stop time, the free station parties and the ways to see the train without a ticket.

## Caltrain Holiday Train 2026 stop times, southbound and northbound

Times come from Caltrain's Holiday Train schedule; the Eastern Time column adds three hours. Ticket holders board only at San Francisco, San Mateo, Palo Alto and San Jose Diridon, Caltrain says: "These are the only stations where ticket holders may board." Millbrae and Santa Clara are hop-off stops.

## Are Caltrain Holiday Train tickets sold out?

Yes. The Holiday Train page says: "Tickets are all sold out, thanks for your support of the Holiday Train!" Sales opened at noon on Wednesday, Nov. 18, "through separate Eventbrite pages for the northbound and southbound trips," Caltrain said. Caltrain does not state a ticket price on the pages read for this story.

On board, Caltrain lists "a beautifully decorated interior, carols performed by Ensemble Continuo, a reading of holiday stories and a complimentary goodie bag."

## How to see the Holiday Train without a ticket

The station parties are free for everyone. Caltrain says families can enjoy free entertainment at San Francisco, San Mateo, Palo Alto and San Jose Diridon stations before the train arrives, with "music, food, entertainment and a chance to meet Santa Claus himself." The decorated train also runs as a regular service train through Sunday, Dec. 13, Caltrain says.

| Where | Station party hours (PT) | Station party hours (ET) |
|---|---|---|
| San Francisco | 3 to 4:30 p.m. | 6 to 7:30 p.m. |
| San Mateo | 4 to 7:30 p.m. | 7 to 10:30 p.m. |
| Palo Alto | 4:30 to 7 p.m. | 7:30 to 10 p.m. |
| San Jose Diridon | 5 to 6:30 p.m. | 8 to 9:30 p.m. |
| Any Caltrain station | Decorated train in regular service through Sunday, Dec. 13 | Same dates |

The news page also names community events at other stops: the Millbrae Holiday Tree Lighting, Winter Wonderland and "Santa Mateo" in San Mateo, the South Bay Historical Railroad Society in Santa Clara, and Christmas in the Park in San Jose.

## Toy drive: what to bring to a station

Caltrain encourages attendees to "bring a new, unwrapped gift to any participating station for the Ronald McDonald House's Comfort and Joy program." The Holiday Train page also carries an online donation link.

## How the 2026 Holiday Train compares with 2025

In 2025, Caltrain ran its first onboard Holiday Train on Saturday, Dec. 13, with eight stops each way, including Mountain View and Redwood City. Tickets went on sale at noon on Thursday, Nov. 20, 2025, and the southbound trip left San Francisco at 4:50 p.m. and arrived at San Jose Diridon at 5:50 p.m. In 2026 the train runs eight days earlier in December, leaves San Francisco 30 minutes earlier and lists six stations in each direction.
