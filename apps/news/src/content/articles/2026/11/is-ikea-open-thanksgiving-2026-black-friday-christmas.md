**IKEA stores in the United States are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, according to IKEA US's help page and all six store pages.** IKEA's help page also lists Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, as closed for all stores, warehouses, deliveries and customer services.

## IKEA holiday hours 2026 at six stores

IKEA's help page says "Special holiday hours will be posted to store-specific pages on our website," so the hours below come from six store pages, each re-read on Nov. 25, 2026. Weekdays are computed here.

| Store | Regular hours | Thu., Nov. 26 | Thu., Dec. 24 | Fri., Dec. 25 | Thu., Dec. 31 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Brooklyn](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/stores/brooklyn/) | Mon.-Fri. and Sun. 11 a.m.-8 p.m.; Sat. 10 a.m.-9 p.m. | Closed | 11 a.m.-5 p.m. | Closed | 11 a.m.-6 p.m. |
| [Burbank](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/stores/burbank/) | Mon.-Thu. and Sun. 10 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri.-Sat. 10 a.m.-10 p.m. | Closed | 10 a.m.-5 p.m. | Closed | 10 a.m.-6 p.m. |
| [Schaumburg, Ill.](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/stores/schaumburg/) | Mon.-Sat. 10 a.m.-9 p.m.; Sun. 10 a.m.-8 p.m. | Closed | 10 a.m.-5 p.m. | Closed | 10 a.m.-6 p.m. |
| [Paramus, N.J.](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/stores/paramus/) | Mon.-Fri. 10 a.m.-8 p.m.; Sat. 10 a.m.-9 p.m.; Sun. closed | Closed | 10 a.m.-5 p.m. | Closed | 10 a.m.-6 p.m. |
| [Houston](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/stores/houston/) | Mon.-Sat. 10 a.m.-9 p.m.; Sun. 10 a.m.-8 p.m. | Closed | 10 a.m.-5 p.m. | Closed | 10 a.m.-6 p.m. |
| [Atlanta](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/stores/atlanta/) | Mon.-Sun. 11 a.m.-8 p.m. | Closed | Not shown | Closed | Not shown |

Every store page lists Thanksgiving and Christmas Day as closed. Atlanta's page does not show Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve hours, so check it before you go. Hours differ by store, and the other IKEA locations are on their own pages.

## Is IKEA open on Black Friday 2026?

No IKEA store page lists special hours for Friday, Nov. 27, so the regular hours in the table above are the only ones shown. IKEA's Black Friday page says "Save $10 for every $100 spent thru 11/30*" and that IKEA Family and Business Network members save $10 off qualifying purchases for every $100 spent, with maximum savings of $100. The offer is valid through Monday, Nov. 30, 2026. Look up your store's page before you drive over.

## IKEA Black Friday deal runs through Monday, Nov. 30

The offer is for IKEA Family and Business Network members, and IKEA says joining is free. IKEA's offers page repeats the member offer as valid through Nov. 30, 2026. The early deals on sofas, dining tables, dinnerware, curtains, rugs and bed frames that the page listed earlier ended Tuesday, Nov. 24.

## IKEA on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve

Thursday, Dec. 24 is a short day at the five stores that list it: Brooklyn runs 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. and the other four run 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Friday, Dec. 25 is closed everywhere, and Thursday, Dec. 31 closes at 6 p.m. at the same five stores. IKEA's pages do not list hours for New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, 2027.

Paramus lists Sunday as closed, which matters for the Sundays of Nov. 29 and Dec. 6, 13, 20 and 27. The other five pages list Sunday hours.
