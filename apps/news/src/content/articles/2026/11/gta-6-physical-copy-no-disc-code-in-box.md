**The boxed Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI has no disc: PlayStation's support page, read Nov. 18, 2026, says "Physical (Code-In-Box) versions will only contain a game download code inside the box to support pre-load. A disc will not be included in the box."** Take-Two Interactive and GameStop describe the box the same way. A disc version has not been announced.

## Who says what about the GTA 6 box

Four sources describe the boxed edition. Their words are quoted as printed.

| Source | Exact words | Date |
|---|---|---|
| PlayStation support | "Physical (Code-In-Box) versions will only contain a game download code inside the box to support pre-load. A disc will not be included in the box." | Read Nov. 18, 2026 |
| Take-Two Interactive | "The physical version of Grand Theft Auto VI, containing a download code inside the box," available "starting November 12, 2026 to support pre-loading." | June 24, 2026 |
| GameStop (U.S.) | "This product will only contain a download code inside the box to support pre-load starting on November 12." | Read Nov. 18, 2026 |
| JB Hi-Fi (Australia) | "You can collect your GTA VI code-in-box edition pre-order from JB from 12 November." | Date not shown |

Rockstar Games publishes the game, and Take-Two Interactive owns Rockstar. Take-Two's release gave the November 12 date for the physical version in June. PlayStation later moved pre-load for digital pre-orders to Nov. 10, and its support page says the boxed code supports pre-load too.

## How the code in the GTA 6 box works

The box holds a code, and the game comes from a download. PlayStation lists the steps for a PS5:

1. Sign in and select your avatar at the top of the screen.
2. Select Redeem Code from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter your code.

"If your code is missing or unreadable, please contact the retailer," PlayStation says. The same page says the console must be signed in to the account used to pre-order the game or to enter the code.

PlayStation asks for 212 GB of storage before the download. To check it, go to the home screen and select Settings, then Storage, per PlayStation. More on the download is in [the 212 GB download guide](/news/gta-6-download-size-212-gb-ps5-pre-load-install-fix/).

## What the boxed GTA 6 costs

GameStop lists the game at $79.99. Take-Two's June 24 release gave $79.99 for the Standard Edition, and the PlayStation Store lists "Standard Edition: $79.99". By those three sources, the boxed copy costs the same as the digital Standard Edition at GameStop.

Upgrading is a store matter. PlayStation says upgrades to the Ultimate Edition, from the digital Standard Edition and from the code-in-box Standard Edition, are available only from PlayStation Store. The Ultimate Edition is $99.99 on the PlayStation Store. [The edition and price breakdown](/news/gta-6-price-editions-standard-ultimate-upgrade/) covers what each tier includes.

## What is not announced for the GTA 6 physical edition

- **A disc version.** None of the sources above announces one.
- **Midnight store openings.** The GameStop, Best Buy, Walmart and Target pages we could read announce none.
- **Prices at other U.S. retailers.** Only GameStop's $79.99 is confirmed here.
