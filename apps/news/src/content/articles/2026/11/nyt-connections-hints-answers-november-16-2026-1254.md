## Why the traps worked

All four groups share one idea, the four seasons, so the trap is that the same entry can attach to more than one of the four words. Garden is the widest case: spring garden, summer garden and fall garden are all everyday phrases, so it only lands in Winter ___ once the other three groups are full. Cleaning is the other near miss, because fall cleaning is also a phrase people use, but Spring ___ has the idiom.

Four entries are foods that also look like a group of their own: Chicken, Onion, Sausage and Melon. They are split across three seasons, so a food category does not exist here.
