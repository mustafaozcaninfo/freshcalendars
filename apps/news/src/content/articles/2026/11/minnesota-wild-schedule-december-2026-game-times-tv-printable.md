**The Minnesota Wild play 12 games in December 2026, nine of them at Grand Casino Arena in St. Paul, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 9 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 10 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), at Anaheim, and ends Monday, Dec. 28, at home against Vegas.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Minnesota, read Nov. 12, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to CT, which is St. Paul time, and ET, and worked out the weekdays. U.S. TV is the last column and Canadian TV the one before it. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, which can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Wild December 2026 game times with U.S. and Canadian TV

In the U.S. the feed lists Wild+, the team's streaming service, and a second Wild outlet for every game except Dec. 22, and adds other outlets for some games. "Regional channel" is a local or team outlet the feed lists under a short code, and "Local station" is a Twin Cities over-air station. Canadian listings are Sportsnet channels, Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports, and The Sports Network (TSN) 4 for the Dec. 15 game. "None listed" means the feed shows no Canadian broadcaster.

## How many Wild games are at Grand Casino Arena in December 2026?

Nine: Dec. 5, 8, 12, 15, 18, 19, 22, 26 and 28. The feed names the arena Grand Casino Arena. Our own count from the feed: three away games, at Honda Center in Anaheim on Dec. 1, Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on Dec. 3 and Nationwide Arena in Columbus on Dec. 10.

Winnipeg visits twice, on Dec. 12 and Dec. 26, and the other side of those games is in our [Jets December schedule](/news/winnipeg-jets-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/). Anaheim appears twice as well, as the Dec. 1 opponent and again at Grand Casino Arena on Dec. 18, and Los Angeles appears at Crypto.com Arena on Dec. 3 and at Grand Casino Arena on Dec. 22.

## Wild seven-game home stand in December 2026

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Seven straight home games, Dec. 12 to Dec. 28:** the Jets, Maple Leafs, Ducks, Canadiens, Kings, Jets again and Golden Knights. The Wild's previous game, Dec. 10 at Columbus, is away.
- **Back-to-back nights, Dec. 18 and 19:** the Ducks on Friday at 7 p.m. CT (8 p.m. ET) and the Canadiens on Saturday at 6 p.m. CT (7 p.m. ET).
- **Earliest start, Mon., Dec. 28:** against Vegas, 1 p.m. CT (2 p.m. ET), with Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada.
- **No game:** Dec. 23 to Dec. 25. The feed shows no game on Dec. 29, 30 or 31 either, so Dec. 28 is the month's last.

The [Oilers December schedule](/news/edmonton-oilers-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/) covers Edmonton's visit on Dec. 8, and the [Maple Leafs December schedule](/news/toronto-maple-leafs-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/) covers Toronto's trip on Dec. 15.

## Which Wild game is a national ESPN+ broadcast?

The Dec. 22 home game against the Kings, at 7 p.m. CT (8 p.m. ET). The feed lists ESPN+, Hulu and Disney+ in the U.S. and no Wild+ or other Wild outlet for that game, and no Canadian broadcaster.
