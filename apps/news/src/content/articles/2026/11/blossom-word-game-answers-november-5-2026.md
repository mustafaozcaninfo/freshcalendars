**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, puts D in the center, with the petals B, E, I, S, T and U.** Beebom and Connections Solver print the same seven letters, and Beebom and Word.Tips list the same 13 pangrams. Below are the letters, words to try, a count of the pangrams by length and graded hints, with the answers held back for a marked spoiler section.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** D (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** B, E, I, S, T, U

Three of the six petals are vowel letters (E, I and U), and the other three are consonants, so D has plenty of vowels to lean on.

## Words to try

Solver sites list these words, and each one contains D and uses only today's letters. Four-letter starters: Debt and Dues. Five letters: Dudes, Debit and Duets. Longer: Duties (6 letters), then Bedside, Subdued and Studied (7 letters each). Subdued and Dudes both repeat D, which is worth remembering if D is the lit petal.

## How the points work with today's letters

A pangram earns 7 extra points, and each use of the highlighted bonus petal earns 5 more. With S lit, Studied would add 5 for its one S, while a pangram that repeats S would collect the bonus twice. The 12-word limit and the other rules are on the [Merriam-Webster Blossom page](https://www.merriam-webster.com/games/blossom/).

## Pangram hint

- **Shortest pangram** (7 letters): it uses each of the seven letters exactly once.
- **Longest pangram** (13 letters): it begins with the center letter.
- **Middle length** (9 letters): four pangrams share this length, and two of them begin with S.
