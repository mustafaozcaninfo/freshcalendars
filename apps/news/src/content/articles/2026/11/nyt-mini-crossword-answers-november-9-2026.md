## The parking clue and this week's grids

1-Across is the clue with the loosest tie to its answer. "Paradoxically" warns that the surface reading contradicts itself: a place with only a little parking should not be described by a word for abundance. Read the answer as a three-letter noun with two meanings, one of them a plot of land and the other a big quantity. Crossing 1-Down gives you the first letter.

Four black squares at the corners leave 21 white cells. The pairs of three-letter entries (1-Across and 8-Across, 4-Down and 5-Down) are the short ones; the middle three rows run edge to edge.
