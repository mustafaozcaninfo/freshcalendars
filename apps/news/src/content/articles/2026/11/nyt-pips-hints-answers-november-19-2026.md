## The forced first move on each Pips board, Nov. 19

Cells are named by row, then column, counted from the top left, so R1C1 is the top-left cell.

**Easy.** R1C5 adds up to 1 by itself, so it holds a 1, and the set has one domino with a 1. That domino carries a 4 on its other end. R1C5 touches R2C5 and R1C6, and a 4 on R2C5 would need R2C4 to be 7 for the sum of 11, which no half can show. So the domino lies across R1C5 and R1C6, with the 4 on R1C6.

**Medium.** R2C2 must be more than 4, and the largest count in the set is 5, since no domino has a 6. R2C2 touches R2C1, R2C3 and R3C2. R2C3 is less than 2, and no domino pairs a 5 with a 0 or a 1, so R2C3 cannot be the partner. The 5 on R2C2 shares its domino with R2C1 or R3C2.

**Hard.** R5C5 touches no cell except R4C5, and the two must be equal, so one domino fills both cells. Only a double shows the same value twice, and the set has three. A second start is R7C5: it adds up to 4 and touches only R8C5, so the domino with a 4 lies across them.

## Pips hints for Nov. 19, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 5-0, 6-6, 5-4, 1-4, 0-6)**

- Hint 1: R1C5 adds up to 1, and the set has exactly one half that shows a 1.
- Hint 2: Two regions ask for matching values, and the set has one double. Values 0, 4 and 5 each appear on exactly two halves.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 0-1, 2-4, 5-5, 1-1, 5-3, 4-1, 0-2)**

- Hint 1: R2C2 must be more than 4, and the largest pip count in the set is 5.
- Hint 2: R2C3 is less than 2, so it holds a 0 or a 1, and R3C4 has no rule at all.

**Hard (twelve dominoes on a ten-row board)**

- Hint 1: R5C5 has a single neighbor, R4C5, and the two must be equal. Think about which dominoes show one value twice.
- Hint 2: R5C1 adds up to 2 and touches only R4C1. Five halves in the set show a 2.

## Reading Thursday's boards before you look

Medium is the only board where one cell carries no rule. Hard has no less-than and no free cell, and its one-cell regions are R5C1, R7C5 and R9C1. Easy has two equal regions out of six, and its only more-than region is R2C1.
