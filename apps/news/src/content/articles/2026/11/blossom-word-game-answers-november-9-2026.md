**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, centers on I, with B, G, L, M, N and O around it.** Beebom and Connections Solver agree on the letters. Clues come first, and the answers wait in a marked section at the bottom.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** I (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** B, G, L, M, N, O

Only I and O are vowels. Words built here need I plus a run of consonants, with O as the one optional vowel. The scoring and rules are on our [Blossom page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works), and today's lone center vowel is the part that sets this board apart.

## Pangram hints for a two-vowel board

- **Length:** 8 letters, so one letter appears twice.
- **Repeat:** the doubled letter is a vowel.
- **Start:** B.
- **Ending:** the last three letters are I, N and G in that order.
- **Meaning:** flowering, in full color.

Two sources print the pangram, Beebom and Word.Tips, and Connections Solver's masked hint, which begins with B and L and hides six more letters, fits it. Other solver sites list a few rarer words that use all seven letters. The game may not accept them, so we name none.

## What the letters allow

The seven letters have a few traits worth checking before you type.

- **An -ing ending is possible.** I, N and G are all on the board, so a short word plus -ing can work, as long as the stem uses only today's letters.
- **The ending covers the center.** A word ending in -ing already holds the required I, so its stem can be built from O and the consonants alone.
- **Double letters carry the long words.** The three long words we confirmed repeat letters: one uses I three times, one uses B twice, and one uses N three times.
- **The doubled O shows up twice.** The pangram has two O's, and so does one of the three long words.

## Today's Blossom pangram and long words (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it yourself.

- **Blooming** (8 letters)

It uses B, G, I, L, M, N and O, with the O twice.

### Long words by length

Beebom, Connections Solver and Word.Tips all list these three words of 9 letters or more. Solver lists run longer, but we print only what three sources agree on, and the game may not accept every word they show.

| Length | Words |
|---|---|
| 10 letters | Immingling |
| 9 letters | Bilimbing, Nonillion |

Immingling has three I's, two M's, two N's and two G's. Bilimbing repeats I three times and B twice. Nonillion has three N's, two O's, two I's and two L's, and it is the only one of the three without an -ing ending.
