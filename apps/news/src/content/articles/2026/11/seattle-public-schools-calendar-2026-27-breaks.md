**Seattle Public Schools (SPS) students have no school on Thursday, Nov. 26 and Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, from Monday, Dec. 21 to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, Feb. 15-19 and April 12-16, 2027**, by the district's "2026-27 School Year Dates" page, posted Aug. 10, 2026. The last day of school is Monday, June 21, 2027, a 1-hour early dismissal. The Sept. 7, Oct. 9 and Nov. 11 days off have passed, so Thanksgiving is next.

The dates are agreed by SPS and the Seattle Education Association (SEA) in the Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA). The district's page says the CBA formula sets the first day of school as the first Wednesday of September.

## When are the SPS breaks in 2026-27?

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates.

| Break | No school | Weekdays off | Classes resume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving and Native American Heritage Day | Thu., Nov. 26 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 2 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Mid-winter break (includes Presidents Day) | Mon., Feb. 15 to Fri., Feb. 19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., Feb. 22 |
| Spring break | Mon., April 12 to Fri., April 16, 2027 | 5 | Mon., April 19 |

The district does not print a return day. Mon., Jan. 4 is read from the PDF calendar, which marks no closure that day, and the other return days follow the same reading. Plan the first one on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html), and compare other districts in our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

## What happens if SPS closes for snow?

The PDF calendar sets aside three emergency closure makeup days and numbers them. Jan. 28, 2027 is "1 of 3," Mon., June 22 is "2 of 3" and Tue., June 23 is "3 of 3." Its note reads: "Schools closed, however, in the event of an emergency closure, such as a snow day, these days will be used to makeup for that closure." The page calls Jan. 28 a "potential makeup day" and lists June 22 and 23 as "potential emergency closure makeup days." The calendar does not say how many closures would trigger each day.

## What are the SPS trimester, semester and quarter dates?

Trimesters apply to K-5 students in elementary and K-8 schools. Semesters and quarters apply to K-8, middle and high schools.

| Term | Dates |
|---|---|
| Trimester 1 | Sept. 2 to Dec. 1, 2026 |
| Trimester 2 | Dec. 2, 2026 to March 18, 2027 |
| Trimester 3 | March 19 to June 21, 2027 |
| Semester 1 | Sept. 2, 2026 to Jan. 27, 2027 |
| Semester 2 | Jan. 29 to June 21, 2027 |
| Quarter 1 | Sept. 2 to Nov. 5, 2026 |
| Quarter 2 | Nov. 6, 2026 to Jan. 27, 2027 |
| Quarter 3 | Jan. 29 to April 8, 2027 |
| Quarter 4 | April 9 to June 21, 2027 |

## When is spring break and mid-winter break in 2027?

Mid-winter break is Feb. 15-19 and spring break is April 12-16, 2027. Quarter 3 ends April 8, the Thursday before spring break. For other districts' spring weeks, see our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

## When does the Seattle school year start and end?

Students in grades 1-12 start Wednesday, Sept. 2, 2026. Preschool and Kindergarten Family Connections run Sept. 2-4, and those students start Tuesday, Sept. 8. The last day is Monday, June 21, 2027.
