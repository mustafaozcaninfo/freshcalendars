**The Pittsburgh Penguins play 13 games in December 2026, eight of them at PPG Paints Arena, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Wednesday, Dec. 2, at 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) at St. Louis and ends Thursday, Dec. 31, at 2 p.m. ET at Ottawa.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Pittsburgh, read Nov. 11, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to ET, which is Pittsburgh time, and worked out the weekdays. The table puts SportsNet Pittsburgh, national U.S. TV and Canadian TV side by side. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Penguins December 2026 game times and TV channels

SportsNet Pittsburgh is the home-market channel the feed lists for the Penguins, on home and away games. Turner Network Television (TNT) and HBO Max carry the Dec. 8 game nationally in the U.S., and ESPN carries the Dec. 10 game. Sportsnet, Sportsnet 360, Sportsnet+ and Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports are the Canadian networks the feed lists for Pittsburgh games. For the Dec. 31 game at Ottawa, the feed lists the home broadcasters The Sports Network 5 (TSN5) and Réseau des sports 2 (RDS2). "None listed" means the feed shows no Canadian broadcaster.

## How many Penguins games are at PPG Paints Arena in December 2026?

Eight: Dec. 5, 6, 8, 15, 17, 18, 22 and 29. The five road games are at St. Louis (Dec. 2), Florida (Dec. 10), Tampa Bay (Dec. 12), Carolina (Dec. 26) and Ottawa (Dec. 31). The month's last home game is the Hurricanes on Tuesday, Dec. 29.

Tampa Bay and Carolina each appear twice. The Penguins play at Tampa Bay on Saturday, Dec. 12, and host the Lightning on Tuesday, Dec. 15. They play at Carolina on Saturday, Dec. 26, and host the Hurricanes on Tuesday, Dec. 29. ## Afternoon starts, back-to-backs and days off

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Afternoon starts:** Sat., Dec. 5, vs. New Jersey, and Sun., Dec. 6, vs. San Jose, both at 3 p.m. ET; Sat., Dec. 12, at Tampa Bay, at 3:30 p.m. ET; and Thu., Dec. 31, at Ottawa, at 2 p.m. ET.
- **Back-to-backs:** Dec. 5 and Dec. 6, both at home, and Dec. 17 and Dec. 18, both at home.
- **No game:** Dec. 23, 24 and 25, around [Christmas Day](/when-is/christmas-day/), and Dec. 19, 20 and 21.
- **National TV:** Chicago at Pittsburgh on Dec. 8 (TNT, HBO Max) and Pittsburgh at Florida on Dec. 10 (ESPN).
