**The Houston Texans play the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday Night Football (SNF), Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, at 8:20 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on NBC, according to the National Football League (NFL).** The game is at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh. ## What channel is Texans at Steelers on?

NBC, per NFL.com and both teams' schedules. We did not use announcer names, pregame start times or streaming details for this game, because no official 2026 page we reached lists them.

## Can the Texans-Steelers kickoff still change?

For Sunday Night Football in Weeks 5 to 13, the NFL says it will "decide and announce no later than 12 days in advance of the game." For Dec. 6 that point was Tuesday, Nov. 24. For Weeks 14 to 17, changes are "generally" made no later than six days before the game.

As of Nov. 25, the NFL, Steelers and Texans schedules all still list Texans at Steelers at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. The [NFL Week 13 schedule and pick'em sheet](/news/nfl-week-13-schedule-2026-printable-pick-em-sheet-game-times/) has the rest of that weekend.

## When did the Texans and Steelers last play?

The teams met in the Wild Card round on Monday, Jan. 12, 2026, at Acrisure Stadium, and the Texans won 30-6, per the 2025 schedules on NFL.com and steelers.com. Their last regular-season meeting was Sunday, Oct. 1, 2023, in Houston, another 30-6 Texans win, per the Steelers' 2023 schedule.
