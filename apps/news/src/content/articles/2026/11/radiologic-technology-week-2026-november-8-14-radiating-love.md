**National Radiologic Technology Week (NRTW) 2026 runs Nov. 8-14,** with the theme "Radiating Love," according to the American Society of Radiologic Technologists (ASRT). Below are what the theme honors, the anniversary the week marks, a day-by-day planner with our own suggestions and a free one-page sheet.

## What is the Radiologic Technology Week 2026 theme?

ASRT's NRTW page says the week "will be celebrated Nov. 8–14 with the theme 'Radiating Love.'" The theme honors the "compassion, dedication and commitment" of imaging and radiation therapy professionals. Nov. 8 is a Sunday and Nov. 14 is a Saturday, so the week runs a full Sunday to Saturday (our calculation).

## Why is Radiologic Technology Week in November?

ASRT says the week is held to "mark the anniversary of Wilhelm Conrad Roentgen's discovery of the x-ray on Nov. 8, 1895." Counting from 1895, Nov. 8, 2026 is the 131st anniversary (our calculation). The week starts on the anniversary itself this year.

## How can you celebrate during NRTW 2026?

ASRT's page has a "Ways to Celebrate" section that covers planning, contacting media, requesting proclamations, presenting to young audiences and public forums. It also offers downloadable templates and infographics, hashtags for social media, and theme gifts from ASRT and Jim Coleman Ltd. The page lists the ASRT Live session "Expect the Unexpected: Rare Pathology in Medical Imaging" for Thursday, Nov. 12, at noon Central time. It also says "Enter by Nov. 14 to claim your prizes and national ASRT honors" for the Planting Seeds Contest.

## What is International Day of Radiology on Nov. 8, 2026?

The International Day of Radiology (IDoR) falls on Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, the same day NRTW starts. Its homepage says: "On November 8, medical imaging professionals from across the world will celebrate the 15th International Day of Radiology (IDoR 2026)." The site is an initiative of the European Society of Radiology (ESR) and uses the hashtag #IDoR2026. The homepage lists no IDoR 2026 theme or November event dates beyond Nov. 8.

## When is Radiologic Technology Week 2027?

Not yet announced. ASRT's page covered 2026 only when we checked on Nov. 6, 2026.
