**National Radiologic Technology Week (NRTW) 2026 runs Nov. 8-14,** with the theme "Radiating Love," according to the American Society of Radiologic Technologists (ASRT). Below are what the theme honors, the anniversary the week marks, a day-by-day planner with our own suggestions and a free one-page sheet.

## What is the Radiologic Technology Week 2026 theme?

ASRT's NRTW page says the week "will be celebrated Nov. 8–14 with the theme 'Radiating Love.'" The theme honors the "compassion, dedication and commitment" of imaging and radiation therapy professionals. Nov. 8 is a Sunday and Nov. 14 is a Saturday, so the week runs a full Sunday to Saturday (our calculation).

## Why is Radiologic Technology Week in November?

ASRT says the week is held to "mark the anniversary of Wilhelm Conrad Roentgen's discovery of the x-ray on Nov. 8, 1895." Counting from 1895, Nov. 8, 2026 is the 131st anniversary (our calculation). The week starts on the anniversary itself this year.

## How can you celebrate during NRTW 2026?

ASRT's page has a "Ways to Celebrate" section that covers planning, contacting media, requesting proclamations, presenting to young audiences and public forums. It also offers downloadable templates and infographics, hashtags for social media, and theme gifts from ASRT and Jim Coleman Ltd. The page lists an ASRT Live webcast on Nov. 12 and a contest deadline of Nov. 14; check ASRT's page for the details of each.

## When is Radiologic Technology Week 2027?

Not yet announced. ASRT's page covered 2026 only when we checked on Nov. 6, 2026. The 2025 theme used by some third-party pages is not the 2026 theme.

The one-page sheet lists the Nov. 8-14 dates, the "Radiating Love" theme, the Nov. 8, 1895 anniversary and a tick-box checklist of appreciation ideas, with space to write your own. Its source is the ASRT page listed below.
