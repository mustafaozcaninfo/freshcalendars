## Tuesday's letters and the center T

- **Center:** T
- **Petals:** A, C, E, O, P, R

Three of the seven letters are vowels (A, E, O) and four are consonants. Monday's flower, centered on P, is on the [Nov. 23 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-23-2026/). With 24 pangrams in play, today's [Blossom rules and scoring](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works) matter less than the center: every find must contain the T.
