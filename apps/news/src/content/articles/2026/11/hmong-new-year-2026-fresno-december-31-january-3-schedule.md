**The Fresno Hmong New Year runs Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026 to Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027 at the Fresno Fairgrounds, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. each day, according to The Hmong Inc., the organizer.** Its page describes four days of "cultural/religious ceremony, sport, music, food and family at the turn of the year." The celebration crosses into 2027, so the final two days are in January. The day-by-day program and the status of the St. Paul event are below.

## Fresno Hmong New Year 2026 schedule by day

The Hmong Inc. lists a program for each day. Hours are Pacific Time.

| Day | Hours | Program listed by The Hmong Inc. |
|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. | Opening day: Opening Ceremony and Procession, traditional and formal programs, the marketplace opens |
| Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | 6 to 11 p.m. | New Year's Eve party |
| Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. | Sport and courtship: ball toss (pov pob) and courtship games, spinning tops and volleyball, qeej and music on the main stage |
| Sat., Jan. 2, 2027 | 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. | The pageant: Miss Hmong Grand International preliminaries, Mrs Hmong USA and Mr Hmong USA, volleyball finals |
| Sun., Jan. 3, 2027 | 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. | Coronation of the titleholders and closing ceremony; the marketplace closes at sundown |

The page greets visitors with "Nyob Zoo Xyoo Tshiab," Happy New Year.

## Where is it, and what does it cost?

The festival is at the Fresno Fairgrounds, 1121 S Chance Ave., Fresno, California 93702. The page lists fairgrounds parking off Chance Ave. and accessible parking and seating. It does not post a ticket price or a parking price, so neither is given here. The organizer's phone number on the page is (559) 552-2001.

## What is Hmong New Year?

The Minnesota Historical Society's MNopedia calls the St. Paul event "a weekend celebration held at the end of November" and says that "Since 1977, Hmong people have gathered in St. Paul to celebrate the harvest." Hamline University describes the new year celebration as typically held in late November, the harvesting season. The 2026 Fresno edition falls in a different part of the calendar, Dec. 31 to Jan. 3.

Pov pob, the ball toss on Fresno's Jan. 1 program, is described by MNopedia as a courtship ritual in which "a young man tosses a small cloth-sewn ball (pob) back and forth with a young woman." Hamline University notes that a hu plig ceremony "is done to call a soul back to its host body," and St. Olaf College calls these activities "collectively known as 'soul calling,' or hu plig."
