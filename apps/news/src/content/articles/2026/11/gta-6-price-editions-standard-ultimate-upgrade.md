**Take-Two Interactive prices Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, and the PlayStation Store shows the same two prices.** The Xbox store lists a separate Ultimate Edition Upgrade at $20.00. That matches the $20.00 gap between the editions (our arithmetic: $99.99 minus $79.99). The game launches Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## GTA 6 editions and U.S. prices by store

| Edition | U.S. price | Where it is sold | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Edition (digital) | $79.99 | Take-Two release; PlayStation Store | Take-Two; PlayStation Store |
| Standard Edition (code-in-box) | $79.99 | GameStop product page | GameStop |
| Ultimate Edition | $99.99 | PlayStation Store; Xbox store | Take-Two; PlayStation Store; Xbox store |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade | $20.00 | Xbox store | Xbox store |

The code-in-box version holds only a download code. PlayStation says no disc is in the box, and [the physical copy story](/news/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc-code-in-box/) covers how it works. On PlayStation, upgrades to the Ultimate Edition from the digital Standard Edition and from the code-in-box Standard Edition "are available only from PlayStation Store," per PlayStation's support page. The upgrade price on the PlayStation Store was not read, so none is given here.

## Pre-order bonus deadline: what each source says

Take-Two wrote that "All Grand Theft Auto VI pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026, will include the Vintage Vice City Pack." The Xbox store words its offer differently, and retailers set their own dates.

| Source | Offer in its own words | Cut-off it prints |
|---|---|---|
| Take-Two | "will include the Vintage Vice City Pack" | before Nov. 20, 2026 |
| Xbox store (Ultimate Edition) | "The Vintage Vice City Pack; One Month of GTA+" | "Purchase prior to 19 November 2026 23:59:59" |
| PlayStation Store | GTA+ subscription | "must be claimed by March 31, 2027" |
| JB Hi-Fi (Australia) | "Pre-order GTA VI at JB to receive the Vintage Vice City Pack" | "Offer ends 18 November 2026" |

The last day for the bonus reads as Thursday, Nov. 19 on the Xbox store, ending at 23:59:59, and "before November 20" at Take-Two points to the same day. Neither source prints a time zone, so the exact moment is not confirmed. The JB Hi-Fi offer in Australia ends sooner, on Wednesday, Nov. 18.

## Do GTA 6 editions differ by console?

The sources give one release date, Nov. 19, for both platforms. The sources do not list what the Ultimate Edition contains beyond the bonus lines in the table, so no contents list is given. Take-Two's release names the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox Wire says Grand Theft Auto VI "must be purchased separately." Every official launch date is listed in [the release date and countdown story](/news/gta-6-october-22-2026-what-is-confirmed-release-countdown/).

## Can you get a refund on a GTA 6 pre-order?

PlayStation and Microsoft word their rules differently.

- **PlayStation Store:** if you paid for a pre-order more than 14 days before the release date, you may cancel at any time before the release date and receive a refund. The refund applies "Provided that you have not started downloading or streaming the main part of the digital product pre-order." PlayStation adds that bonus content "will stop working if you cancel your pre-order."
- **Microsoft Store:** "Digital goods like apps, games, add-on content, subscriptions, movies, TV shows, and books aren't refundable." Microsoft's page then lists exceptions for offers or applicable law.

Refund rules for GameStop and JB Hi-Fi code-in-box orders were not read.

## Is GTA 6 on sale for Black Friday 2026?

No official Black Friday sale lists it as of Nov. 22, 2026. PlayStation's Black Friday 2026 deals post, for a sale that runs through Monday, Nov. 30, does not name Grand Theft Auto VI among its discounts, and the Xbox Black Friday Sale post, for a sale that runs through Dec. 2, does not either. Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar Games have said nothing about a discount. The game launched on Nov. 19, eight days before Black Friday, and the PlayStation Store lists the Standard Edition at $79.99.
