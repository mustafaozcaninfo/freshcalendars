**Kentucky's candidate filing deadline for 2027 offices is 4:00 PM on Friday, Jan. 8, 2027, the primary is Tuesday, May 18 and the general election is Tuesday, Nov. 2, according to the Kentucky State Board of Elections' "2027 Kentucky Election Calendar."** Signatures on candidate filing forms for a 2027 office may be gathered starting today, Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026. The table below sets every primary and general election date side by side, with weekdays and times as the board prints them. The board's PDF carries no printed issue date; we read it on Nov. 4, 2026.

## Kentucky 2027 election dates: primary and general side by side

| Step | Primary (May 18) | General (Nov. 2) |
|---|---|---|
| Mail-in absentee request portal opens (45 days before) | Sat., April 3 | Sat., Sept. 18 |
| Last day to register | Mon., April 19 | Mon., Oct. 4 |
| Registration books closed | Tue., April 20 through Mon., May 24 | Tue., Oct. 5 through Mon., Nov. 8 |
| Mail-in absentee portal closes, 11:59 PM (14 days before) | Tue., May 4 | Tue., Oct. 19 |
| Excused in-person absentee (a designated reason is required) | Wed., May 5, Thu., May 6, Fri., May 7 and Mon., May 10, Tue., May 11, Wed., May 12 | Wed., Oct. 20, Thu., Oct. 21, Fri., Oct. 22 and Mon., Oct. 25, Tue., Oct. 26, Wed., Oct. 27 |
| Military-overseas application deadline (7 days before) | Tue., May 11 | Tue., Oct. 26 |
| No-excuse in-person early voting | Thu., May 13 through Sat., May 15 | Thu., Oct. 28 through Sat., Oct. 30 |
| Election Day, polls 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. prevailing time | Tue., May 18 | Tue., Nov. 2 |

All dates are 2027. The calendar says the primary falls on the first Tuesday after the third Monday in May.

## Which Kentucky offices are on the 2027 ballot?

The board's "Kentucky Election Schedule 2026-2036" marks only state officers in its 2027 column: governor, lieutenant governor, secretary of state, attorney general, auditor of public accounts, state treasurer and commissioner of agriculture. This story gives dates and offices only; the board's pages list the candidates once they file.

## When can Kentucky candidates file for 2027 offices?

The calendar sets three candidate dates:

- **Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026:** the earliest date for affixing signatures on candidate filing forms for a 2027 office.
- **Friday, Jan. 8, 2027, 4:00 PM:** the filing deadline for candidates who must run in the primary.
- **Thursday, April 1, 2027, 4:00 PM:** the deadline for the statement of candidacy for independent and political organization candidates.

From today, that is 65 days to the Jan. 8 deadline, 195 days to the May 18 primary and 363 days to the Nov. 2 general election.

## What are the Kentucky registration and party-change deadlines?

To vote in a party's 2027 primary, a voter must change party affiliation by Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, the last day to do so under Kentucky Revised Statutes (KRS) 116.055, per the calendar.

The last day to register for the primary is Monday, April 19, 2027. Online registration must be finished by 4:00 PM local time, and the calendar says a mailed form postmarked Tuesday, April 20 still counts. For the general election the last day is Monday, Oct. 4, with mailed forms postmarked Oct. 5.

The calendar then lists the registration books as closed from Tuesday, April 20 through Monday, May 24, and from Tuesday, Oct. 5 through Monday, Nov. 8. It cites KRS 116.045(2): "the fourth Tuesday preceding through the first Monday following" the election. The board's calendar does not explain how a mailed form postmarked April 20 fits with books that close that day, so check your registration with your county clerk well before April 19.

## How do absentee and early voting work in Kentucky in 2027?

Three routes appear on the calendar. Mail-in absentee requests go through a portal that opens 45 days before each election and closes at 11:59 PM 14 days before. Excused in-person absentee voting, which needs a designated reason, runs on six weekdays before each election. No-excuse in-person early voting is the Thursday to Saturday before the election week: May 13-15 for the primary and Oct. 28-30 for the general election.

Mail-in ballots "must be received by the County Clerk before 6 p.m. prevailing time," the calendar says. Military and overseas applications are due 7 days before: Tuesday, May 11 and Tuesday, Oct. 26.

## What does "prevailing time" mean for Kentucky polls?

Polls are open 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. prevailing time, which is the local time in each county. Kentucky is split between Eastern Time (ET) and Central Time (CT), so a county on Central Time closes at 6 p.m. CT, which is 7 p.m. ET. A county on Eastern Time closes at 6 p.m. ET. The 4:00 PM and 11:59 PM deadlines above are printed without a zone, other than the online registration deadline, which is 4:00 PM local time; ask your county clerk if you are unsure which clock applies.
