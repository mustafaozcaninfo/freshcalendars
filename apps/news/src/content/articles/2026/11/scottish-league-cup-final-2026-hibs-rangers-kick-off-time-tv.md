**Hibernian will face Rangers in the 2026 Scottish League Cup final, branded the Premier Sports Cup, on Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026, with kick-off at 3 p.m. at Barclays Hampden in Glasgow, live on Premier Sports, according to the Scottish Professional Football League (SPFL).** That is 10 a.m. ET, 9 a.m. CT and 7 a.m. PT. The SPFL's draw page names the venue as Hampden Park; Barclays Hampden is the sponsor's name for it.

## What time is the Hibs v Rangers kick-off in US time zones?

The SPFL gives 3.00pm, and Scotland is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in December. The other rows are Fresh Calendars' conversion from that time.

| City or zone | Offset on Dec. 13 | Kick-off, Sun., Dec. 13 |
|---|---|---|
| Glasgow and London (GMT) | UTC+0 | 3 p.m. |
| Central Europe (CET) | UTC+1 | 4 p.m. |
| Eastern (ET) | UTC-5 | 10 a.m. |
| Central (CT) | UTC-6 | 9 a.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | UTC-7 | 8 a.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | UTC-8 | 7 a.m. |

## How did Hibernian and Rangers reach the final?

The SPFL results page lists both semi-finals: Rangers beat Aberdeen 1-0 on Sat., Oct. 31, and Hibernian beat Hearts 2-1 on Sun., Nov. 1. The Edinburgh Reporter called the Hibs result "a memorable 2-1 Premier Sports Cup semi-final win over Hearts at Barclays Hampden."

| Semi-final | Date | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [SPFL results: Rangers](https://spfl.co.uk/league/league-cup/results/2637157) | Sat., Oct. 31, 2026 | Rangers 1-0 Aberdeen |
| [SPFL results: Hibernian](https://spfl.co.uk/league/league-cup/results/2637157) | Sun., Nov. 1, 2026 | Hearts 1-2 Hibernian |

Hibernian's League Cup wins on the SPFL's list are 1972-73 and 1991-92, and the club lost the 2021-22 final 2-1 to Celtic. Rangers' most recent win on the same list is 2023-24, 1-0 against Aberdeen. Last season St Mirren beat Celtic 3-1 at Hampden Park on Sun., Dec. 14, 2025, per [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11781/13482203/st-mirren-3-1-celtic-wilfried-nancy-still-waiting-for-first-win-as-jonah-ayungas-double-secures-league-cup-glory-for-buddies).

## Where will the fans sit and what do tickets cost?

The SPFL's Nov. 3 notice splits the stadium between the two clubs and sets the prices below.

| Club | Stands |
|---|---|
| [Hibernian](https://spfl.co.uk/news/202627-premier-sports-cup-final-details-confirme) | South, East and North |
| [Rangers](https://spfl.co.uk/news/202627-premier-sports-cup-final-details-confirme) | South, West and North |

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| [Adult, most of the stadium](https://spfl.co.uk/news/202627-premier-sports-cup-final-details-confirme) | £42 |
| [Adult, North and South Stands](https://spfl.co.uk/news/202627-premier-sports-cup-final-details-confirme) | £49 |
| [Concession, whole stadium](https://spfl.co.uk/news/202627-premier-sports-cup-final-details-confirme) | £27 and £30 |

Tickets are issued digitally through the Barclays Hampden app. The clubs sell tickets and announce sale details on their own channels; no on-sale date is confirmed.

## Where to watch the League Cup final

The SPFL says the final is live on Premier Sports. A U.S. or other international broadcaster has not been announced by the SPFL, so none is listed here.
