**Swiss Chalet's Festive Scratch & Win contest runs from Nov. 1, 2026 to 11:59:59 p.m. ET on Sunday, Dec. 27, 2026, or until the game cards run out, whichever comes first, according to the official rules on swisschalet.com.** The Festive Special itself has no posted end date or price. Below are the meal, the full prize table, who can enter and the dates to mark, with a free printable.

## What is in the Swiss Chalet Festive Special 2026?

Swiss Chalet's festive page describes a quarter of its Rotisserie Chicken served with its Signature Chalet Dipping Sauce, plus a dinner roll, a choice of side, stuffing, cranberry and five Lindor chocolate truffles. It also lists a Festive Scratch & Win Card and says: "Hurry, it's only here for a limited time."

A Festive Family Feast is on the same page. Swiss Chalet says its Family Pak, with all the fixings, feeds 4.

| Detail | What Swiss Chalet says (page read Nov. 19, 2026) |
|---|---|
| Price | Not posted |
| Menu start and end dates | Not posted |
| White or dark meat choice | Not given |
| Takeout or delivery | Not given |

Swiss Chalet announced Festive Special Day, to start on Nov. 16, in a Nov. 12, 2026 release. It said it would donate and deliver over 10,000 warm Festive Special meals to community organizations.

## When does Swiss Chalet's Scratch & Win end?

Rule 1 sets the window: the contest starts at 12:00:01 a.m. ET on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026 and is scheduled to end at 11:59:59 p.m. ET on Sunday, Dec. 27, 2026, "or whenever official game cards are exhausted, whichever occurs first." Rule 5 puts the supply at 2,623,700 game cards.

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sun., Nov. 1, 2026 | Contest starts; gift card offer starts, while supplies last | Rules, Rule 1; gift card page |
| Mon., Nov. 16, 2026 | Festive Special Day | Nov. 12 release |
| Sun., Dec. 27, 2026 | Contest scheduled to end; no-purchase essays must be received | Rules, Rules 1 and 4 |
| Mon., Dec. 28, 2026 | Food prizes and the gift card coupon can be redeemed | Rules; gift card page |
| Sun., Feb. 28, 2027 | Last day to redeem food prizes; claims must be received | Rules |

## Who can enter, and how many game cards do you get?

Rule 2 says the contest is open to legal residents of Canada (excluding Québec) who have reached the legal drinking age in their province or territory of residence.

Rule 3 gives one game card with each of these: Festive Special, Festive Special Double Leg, Half Chicken, 1/3 and 1/2 Rack, Chicken and Rack combos, Festive Chicken On a Bun, Festive Hot Chicken Sandwich or Festive Poutine. A Festive Family Feast gets "four (4) Game Cards with each purchase."

Rule 4 offers a route with no purchase: a handwritten essay of 50 words or more on a favourite holiday memory, mailed to Recipe Unlimited, 199 Four Valley Drive, Vaughan, Ontario L4K 0B8 and "received no later than December 27, 2026."

## What are the Swiss Chalet Scratch & Win prizes?

Rule 7 of the official rules lists every prize with its approximate retail value, the number available and the odds per game card at the start of the contest. The rules add that odds vary by location and change as cards are handed out.

The food prizes are "valid only at any subsequent visit between December 28, 2026 and February 28, 2027." Food prize claims "must be received by no later than February 28, 2027," and non-food prize claims carry the same Feb. 28, 2027 deadline.

## What is the Swiss Chalet gift card offer?

On its gift card page, Swiss Chalet says a gift card of $50 or more bought in one of its restaurants comes with a free Quarter Chicken Dinner. The offer is available starting Nov. 1, 2026, "only while supplies last." The coupon is valid for redemption Dec. 28, 2026 to Feb. 28, 2027 for dine-in only, and only one coupon may be redeemed per table per visit.

## What has Swiss Chalet not said yet?

The festive page posts no price, no menu start or end date and no holiday hours for Dec. 24, 25, 26, 31 or Jan. 1.
