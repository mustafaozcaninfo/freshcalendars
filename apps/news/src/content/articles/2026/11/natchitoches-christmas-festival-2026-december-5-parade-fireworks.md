**The Natchitoches Christmas Festival lists its Festival of Lights Parade for Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, at 1 p.m. Central Time (CT)**, which is 2 p.m. ET, and fireworks over Cane River Lake at 6 p.m. CT (7 p.m. ET). The festival's [schedule page](https://natchitocheschristmas.com/schedule) is the source for the times below. Mark the date on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

This is the 100th Natchitoches Christmas Festival, running Nov. 21, 2026, to Jan. 6, 2027, with "Christmas Cheer Since 1927" on the home page. The first festival was in 1927, so 2026 is its 100th year. The home page says "over 300,000 lights and 100 plus set pieces are on display every night at dusk."

The home page also carries a line about an "Annual Festival on December 6th." The schedule page lists Saturday, Dec. 5, and Dec. 6, 2026, is a Sunday, so this story uses Dec. 5.

## When is the Natchitoches Christmas parade in 2026?

The parade page says "Festival of Lights Parade • Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026 at 1PM." It leaves from the grounds of Northwestern State University and travels through the streets of the Historic District. The festival posts a route map on its parade page; we did not read street names from it.

## What is the Dec. 5 festival day timeline?

| Time (CT) | Time (ET) | What the schedule lists |
|---|---|---|
| All day | All day | Front Street and Downtown Riverbank, $20 admission |
| 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | Festival of Lights Parade |
| 3:30 to 5:30 p.m. | 4:30 to 6:30 p.m. | Mike McKenzie Band, Riverbank Stage |
| 6 p.m. | 7 p.m. | Fireworks Celebration, Cane River Lake |
| 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. | 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. | Johnny Earthquake and The Moondogs, Riverbank Stage |

## Natchitoches Christmas Festival admission calendar by Saturday

The festival's frequently asked questions page says "Admission is only charged on select Saturdays throughout the Season: November 21st & 28th and December 5th, 12th & 19th." Of those five Saturdays, Nov. 21 has already happened.

| Saturday | Admission | What is confirmed |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 21 | $20 after 1 p.m.; ages 6 and under free | Turn on Holidays, 4:30 to 9 p.m.; drone show and fireworks, 7 p.m. (past) |
| Nov. 28 | $20 after 1 p.m.; ages 6 and under free | Fireworks time not posted |
| Dec. 5 | $20 all day on Front Street and Downtown Riverbank | Parade 1 p.m.; fireworks 6 p.m.; two bands |
| Dec. 12 | Charged, per the festival's questions page | Schedule details not posted |
| Dec. 19 | Charged, per the festival's questions page | Schedule details not posted |

The home page says every Saturday brings "live music, kids activities, fireworks, and much more," but the schedule page lists fireworks times only for Nov. 21 and Dec. 5.

## What does the Natchitoches Christmas Festival cost, and are there free days?

The tickets page says "Armbands are $20. Children 6 and under are free and do not require an armband." It also says no cash will be accepted at ticket booths this year and that all armband and vendor sales are cashless. Online sales link to TicketSpice.

The schedule page lists free days: Nov. 27, Dec. 25 and Dec. 31. It adds that "there is no admission to enter the Riverbank area Sunday through Friday."

## Natchitoches Christmas lights in 2026: when do they end?

The home page says the season "concludes on January the 6th, the Epiphany," in 2027. Lights are on every night at dusk, per the home page; it gives no clock time.

## What is not announced for the Natchitoches festival yet?

The festival had not posted these items on Nov. 25, 2026: Riverbank hours, parking prices, shuttles, 2026 road closures, a rain date, and bag, pet or cooler rules (its questions page has no answers on them). Last year, the Natchitoches Times reported on Dec. 4, 2025, that barricades went up at 5 a.m. downtown, including the Church Street Bridge, and that parking was banned on Williams Avenue and the East Second Street area from 10 a.m. on festival day. Treat that as 2025 only.
