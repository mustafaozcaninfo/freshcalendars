**Jefferson County Public Schools (JCPS) students in Louisville are off Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and the last day of school is Monday, May 24, 2027**, according to the district's "School Calendar 2026-27" parent and family PDF. Students started Thursday, Aug. 6, 2026. Today, Tuesday, Nov. 3, is Election Day, a no-school day for students on the calendar. The next break is Thanksgiving, Wednesday, Nov. 25 through Friday, Nov. 27.

## When are the JCPS breaks in 2026-27?

The calendar's "No School for Students" legend lists each break below. The weekday counts and return dates are our reading of the sheet.

| Break | Dates | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break (past) | Mon., Sept. 28 to Fri., Oct. 2, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Oct. 5 |
| Thanksgiving break | Wed., Nov. 25 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 3 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Spring break | Mon., March 29 to Fri., April 2, 2027 (plus Fri., March 26) | 5, or 6 with March 26 | Mon., April 5, 2027 |

Plan the December weeks on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html), and compare other districts in our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) and [Thanksgiving break 2026 roundup](/news/thanksgiving-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

## Which JCPS days are off for students, including the teacher-only days?

The calendar separates "No School for Students" from "Teacher Attendance Only" days. Students are off on both kinds, so this table puts them together with the weekday.

| Date | Weekday | What the calendar lists |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day, no school for students |
| Sept. 28 to Oct. 2, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Fall break; Sept. 28 is also a Professional-Development Flex Day |
| Nov. 2, 2026 | Monday | Professional-Development Gold Day, teacher attendance only |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Election Day, no school for students |
| Nov. 25-27, 2026 | Wednesday-Friday | Thanksgiving break |
| Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Winter break |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Martin Luther King Jr. Day |
| Feb. 15, 2027 | Monday | Professional-Development Gold Day, teacher attendance only (Presidents' Day) |
| March 26, 2027 | Friday | Professional-Development Flex Day, teacher attendance only |
| March 29 to April 2, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Spring break |
| April 30, 2027 | Friday | Oaks Day, no school for students |
| May 18, 2027 | Tuesday | Election Day, no school for students |
| May 31, 2027 | Monday | Memorial Day |

The Professional-Development Flex and Gold days are easy to miss because they sit beside holidays and breaks. Our free printable marks each of them as "PD day, no school" next to the breaks and holidays. For the whole year on paper, use the [printable school-year calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html).

The legend also lists family-teacher conferences on Sept. 23 and Feb. 16. The sheet does not make clear whether students attend, so check with your school before you plan around them.

## When is the last day of school at JCPS in 2027?

Monday, May 24, 2027 is the last day for students, and teachers finish Tuesday, May 25. The calendar lists make-up days for students on May 25, 26, 27, 28, June 1 and June 2, 2027, and says "Inclement weather will alter the school calendar." Mark the end of the year on the [May 2027 calendar](/may-calendars.html).

| Possible make-up day | Weekday |
|---|---|
| May 25, 2027 | Tuesday |
| May 26, 2027 | Wednesday |
| May 27, 2027 | Thursday |
| May 28, 2027 | Friday |
| June 1, 2027 | Tuesday |
| June 2, 2027 | Wednesday |

Memorial Day, Monday, May 31, falls between the last two groups. The high school grading period also ends May 24.

## How does JCPS 2026-27 compare with 2025-26?

Spring break moves from early April to the end of March. In 2025-26, the district's calendar listed the first day as Aug. 7, 2025 and the last as May 22, 2026.

| Date | 2026-27 | 2025-26 |
|---|---|---|
| First day for students | Thu., Aug. 6, 2026 | Thu., Aug. 7, 2025 |
| Fall break | Sept. 28 to Oct. 2 | Sept. 30 to Oct. 3 |
| Thanksgiving break | Nov. 25-27 | Nov. 26-28 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 | Dec. 22 to Jan. 2 |
| Spring break | March 29 to April 2, 2027 | April 6-10, 2026 |
| Oaks Day | Fri., April 30, 2027 | May 1, 2026 |
| Last day for students | Mon., May 24, 2027 | May 22, 2026 |

The Louisville Courier Journal, in a July 8, 2026 story by Kathryn Muchnick, and Spectrum News 1 also reported the 2026-27 dates. Spectrum News 1 wrote, "The first day of school is Thursday, Aug. 6 for students, but teachers will begin on Monday, Aug. 3." Both match the district PDF. See how other districts schedule spring break in our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

## Are the 2027-28 JCPS dates set?

Not in the calendar this story covers. The Louisville Courier Journal reported on Oct. 27, 2026 that draft calendars for 2027-28 and 2028-29 have five fewer instructional days, and that a board vote was expected at its November meeting. It also reported a 2027-28 start of Aug. 5, 2027 and an end of May 19, 2028. Those dates are a proposal, not an adopted calendar, and we have not confirmed them with the district.

## What else is on the JCPS calendar?

- Teachers' first day is Monday, Aug. 3, 2026, and Tuesday, Aug. 4 is a Non-Flex In-Service day, teachers only.
- High school grading periods end Sept. 15, Nov. 5, Dec. 18, Feb. 17, April 8 and May 24.
- Magnet and optional program applications are listed for Nov. 2 to Dec. 18, 2026, so that window is open now.
