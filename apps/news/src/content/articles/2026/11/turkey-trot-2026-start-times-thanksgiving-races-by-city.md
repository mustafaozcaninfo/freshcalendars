**The Buffalo Turkey Trot starts at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026**, according to the race page of the Young Men's Christian Association (YMCA) Buffalo Niagara, and the Manchester Road Race in Connecticut starts at 10:00 a.m. ET, according to its Race Roster page. The tables below cover 10 Thanksgiving Day races, read on their official pages on Nov. 13, 2026: start times in local time and in ET, fees, the next price change and packet pickup. A one-page printable checklist collects the deadlines and pickup dates.

## When does the Buffalo Turkey Trot start in 2026?

The 131st Annual YMCA Buffalo Niagara Turkey Trot is on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, with a 9:00 a.m. start that the page marks as sharp, according to the race's RunSignup page. It is an 8K, which the page gives as 4.97 miles, and the field is capped at 14,000. The course is new for 2026 and starts and finishes at the Buffalo Convention Center. The YMCA Buffalo Niagara page puts the start line on Franklin Street between West Chippewa and West Tupper streets and the finish at Franklin and West Eagle streets. Race-morning bib pickup is at the Buffalo Convention Center from 7 to 8:15 a.m.

## When does the Dallas Turkey Trot start in 2026?

The Dallas YMCA Turkey Trot is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, according to Race Roster. The timed 8 Mile or 5K starts at 8:30 a.m. CST, which is 9:30 a.m. ET, and a second start is at 9:00 a.m. CST (10:00 a.m. ET). The start is at Dallas City Hall, 1500 Marilla Street, and the race benefits the YMCA of Metropolitan Dallas.

The Atlanta Track Club page lists the later Atlanta prices as the price "until sell out".

## Who runs these races, and which edition is it?

- **Buffalo:** the 131st edition, benefiting YMCA Buffalo Niagara.
- **Manchester:** the 90th edition, according to Patch (Apr. 6, 2026).
- **Cincinnati:** the 117th year, according to Western & Southern, which says the 2026 courses are updated and the start is staggered. Proceeds support charities including Big Brothers Big Sisters.
- **Denver:** the 53rd year, benefiting Mile High United Way.
- **Detroit:** the race page says it has run for "over 40 years".
- **San Diego-North County:** the 3rd annual, benefiting Active Kids Alliance.
- **Dallas:** benefiting the YMCA of Metropolitan Dallas.
- **Atlanta:** benefiting the Atlanta Track Club Foundation's Kilometer Kids.
- **San Jose:** organized by the Silicon Valley Leadership Group Foundation.

We could not confirm 2026 details for the Seattle and Chicago races from their official pages, so they are not in the tables.
