**Roku's Black Friday sale runs Nov. 19-30, 2026, with the Streaming Stick at $19.99, down from a manufacturer's suggested retail price (MSRP) of $39.99, according to Roku's Nov. 9 press release on Business Wire.** The last day is Cyber Monday, Nov. 30. The table below lists every price Roku named, with the dollars saved computed from the release and the percent off as Roku printed it.

## Roku Black Friday 2026 prices: sale price, MSRP and savings

Roku's release covers organic light-emitting diode (OLED) TVs as well. In it, Roku said it "announced 12 days of Black Friday savings, with a deal for every budget," and that shoppers can "save more than 50% on select Roku TV smart projectors, Roku streaming players, and Roku TV models." Roku also says deals are "available at Roku.com and major retailers nationwide." Dollars saved are computed from the two prices; percent off is Roku's figure.

Two more TV deals have no MSRP in the release: the 50-inch Roku Select Series 4K TV at $199.99 (Roku says Select Series models are up to 43% off) and the 65-inch Roku Plus Series at $429.99 (Plus Series up to 42% off). For smart projectors Roku gives only a saving of "up to 53%" and no price.

The $500 cut on the 65-inch Pro Series LX OLED TV is the largest in dollars. The Streaming Stick's 50% is the deepest percent among the priced products, and the projector figure of up to 53% is the highest percent Roku names.

## When does the Roku Black Friday sale end?

Roku's release dates the sale Nov. 19-30. Counting from Nov. 19, Black Friday (Fri., Nov. 27) is day 9 of the 12, and Cyber Monday (Mon., Nov. 30) is day 12. Roku's release gives no closing time for the device and TV prices.

Roku's deals page, which we read on Nov. 25, 2026, states two cutoffs for subscription offers:

| Offer | Roku's wording | In Eastern Time |
|---|---|---|
| Roku subscriptions | "Roku Subscriptions offers end 11/30/26 at 11:59 PM PST." | 2:59 a.m. ET, Tue., Dec. 1 |
| Apple TV | "Apple TV: Offer expires on November 30, 2026 8pm PT." | 11 p.m. ET, Mon., Nov. 30 |

The Eastern Time column is converted from Roku's Pacific time. Roku has not announced an extension past Nov. 30. Subscription prices are not confirmed on the deals page, so none are listed.

## Where are the Roku Black Friday deals sold?

Roku says only that the deals are at Roku.com and major retailers nationwide. The links in its release send the Hiro TVs to Target. Two Roku Streamer players are a Walmart deal: "Starting Nov. 30, Walmart shoppers can save on two Roku streaming players throughout the holiday season." The Roku Streamer is $19.88 against an MSRP of $29.88, and the Roku Streamer Plus is $34.88 against $49.88. A retailer's own price can differ from Roku's list, so check the store page at checkout.

## Roku's 2025 sale prices against 2026

In 2025, RTTNews reported from Roku's Nov. 12, 2025 release that deals began Nov. 20, 2025. It reported Roku Ultra at $69.99 and the Streaming Stick 4K at $24.99.

| Product | 2025 sale price (RTTNews) | 2026 sale price (Roku) | 2026 minus 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roku Ultra | $69.99 | $89.99 | $20 more |
| Roku Streaming Stick 4K | $24.99 | $49.99 | $25 more |

Both 2026 sale prices are above last year's. The 2026 Ultra is still $60 under its $149.99 MSRP, and the 2026 Streaming Stick 4K is $30 under $79.99.
