**McDonald's holiday drinks for 2026 were set to arrive at participating U.S. restaurants on Tuesday, Nov. 3, according to Nation's Restaurant News and Dexerto.** The limited-time line has five drinks: a Cookie Butter Frappé, Latte and Iced Coffee, plus a Frosted Cranberry Splash made with Sprite and a Red Bull Frosted Cranberry Energizer. McDonald's had not posted the lineup on a page we could read, so every fact here comes from the newsrooms named in the text.

## Which McDonald's holiday drinks are on the 2026 menu?

Nation's Restaurant News reported that McDonald's is launching a line of limited-time holiday drinks on Nov. 3. Dexerto and MoneyTalksNews describe the two cranberry drinks, and Tasting Table reviewed the three cookie butter coffees. The table sorts all five by format and caffeine base.

| Drink | Format | Base | What else is in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frosted Cranberry Splash | Not stated | Sprite | Orange and cranberry flavors, cold foam, cranberry sprinkles |
| Red Bull Frosted Cranberry Energizer | Not stated | Classic Red Bull or Red Bull Zero | Cold foam and sprinkles |
| Cookie Butter Frappé | Blended | Coffee | Caramel cookie crumbs and vanilla whipped topping, per Tasting Table |
| Cookie Butter Latte | Hot | Coffee | Caramel cookie crumbs and vanilla whipped topping, per Tasting Table |
| Cookie Butter Iced Coffee | Iced | Coffee | Caramel cookie crumbs and vanilla whipped topping, per Tasting Table |

The hot, iced and blended labels for the three coffees come from the Tasting Table review alone. The sources we found do not say whether the two cranberry drinks are served over ice or blended, so the table leaves that open.

## Is the Red Bull cranberry drink the first Red Bull at McDonald's?

No. Good Morning America reported on Aug. 11, 2026 that McDonald's was adding Red Bull Energizer drinks, available from Aug. 17, starting with a Red Bull Dragonberry Energizer. The same report said McDonald's was also introducing Red Bull Zero to its permanent drink lineup. The holiday Energizer is a seasonal flavor on an existing Red Bull menu, and Dexerto reported that customers can choose between classic Red Bull and Red Bull Zero.

## How much do the holiday drinks cost and how long do they last?

McDonald's has not announced pricing for the new drinks, Dexerto reported, so there is no national price to list. Prices at individual restaurants may differ, and we have not printed any local figure. The drinks are available for a limited time at participating locations across the U.S.; neither Dexerto nor Sporked found an end date from McDonald's.

## How can you check your local McDonald's?

Every newsroom says the drinks are at "participating" restaurants, which means a restaurant near you may not carry all five. Open the McDonald's app, pick your restaurant and look at its menu before you go. The drinks are limited time, and no end date has been announced.
