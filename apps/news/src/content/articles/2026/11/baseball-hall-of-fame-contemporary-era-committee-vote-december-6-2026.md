The Contemporary Baseball Era Committee votes on eight non-player candidates on Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, in San Diego, and the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum says the results are announced live on MLB Network's "MLB Tonight" at 8 p.m. ET. A free printable ballot with each candidate's record and the Class of 2027 dates is below.

## Who is on the 2026 Contemporary Era ballot?

The Hall of Fame announced the finalists on Nov. 2, 2026. This is the non-player ballot: managers and executives. The table uses the Hall's own wording for each record.

| Candidate | Record as the Hall lists it |
|---|---|
| Sandy Alderson | Became the Athletics general manager at age 35 and built the team that won three American League pennants |
| Dusty Baker | Managed the Giants, Cubs, Reds, Nationals and Astros for 26 seasons |
| Bruce Bochy | Managed the Padres, Giants and Rangers over 28 seasons |
| Joe Maddon | Managed the Angels, Rays and Cubs over 19 seasons |
| Lou Piniella | Managed 23 seasons for the Yankees, Reds, Mariners, Devil Rays and Cubs |
| Brian Sabean | Joined the Yankees front office after a stint as head coach at the University of Tampa |
| Ted Turner | Purchased the Atlanta Braves in 1976 |
| Bill White | Served as the president of the National League from 1989-94 |

## What time are the results in each U.S. time zone?

The Hall says the committee meets to discuss the eight candidacies on Dec. 6 in San Diego, and the results air at 8 p.m. ET / 5 p.m. PT on Sunday, Dec. 6. By our conversion:

| Zone | Results on Sun., Dec. 6 |
|---|---|
| Eastern | 8 p.m. |
| Central | 7 p.m. |
| Mountain | 6 p.m. |
| Pacific | 5 p.m. |

## How many votes does a candidate need?

The Hall says a candidate who receives votes on 75 percent of the ballots cast by the committee earns election. The electorate is a 16-member panel appointed by the Hall of Fame Board, and 75 percent of 16 is 12, so 12 votes is the threshold by our computation. The Hall's Era Committees rules page says electors may vote for as few as zero and as many as three eligible candidates.

The Hall has not published the 16 names; it said they "will be announced later this fall."

## What happened in the 2025 vote?

Last year's vote was the Contemporary Baseball Era players ballot. The Hall said Jeff Kent was named on 14 of 16 ballots and was the only candidate to reach the 75-percent threshold. Carlos Delgado received 9 votes, and Don Mattingly and Dale Murphy 6 each. That is last year's result on a different ballot and says nothing about 2026.

## What are the Class of 2027 dates?

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026 | Contemporary Baseball Era Committee results, 8 p.m. ET, MLB Network | Hall of Fame, Nov. 2, 2026 |
| Wednesday, Dec. 9, 2026 | Ford C. Frick Award winner announced at Baseball's Winter Meetings in San Diego, according to the Hall; time not announced | Hall of Fame, Sept. 28, 2026 |
| Tuesday, Jan. 19, 2027 | Baseball Writers' Association of America (BBWAA) election results; time not announced | Hall of Fame, Nov. 2, 2026 |
| July 23-26, 2027 | Hall of Fame Weekend 2027 in Cooperstown | Hall of Fame, Sept. 28, 2026 |
| Saturday, July 24, 2027 | Awards Presentation | Hall of Fame, Sept. 28, 2026 |
| Sunday, July 25, 2027 | Class of 2027 inducted in Cooperstown | Hall of Fame, Nov. 2, 2026 |

The Frick Award ballot has ten finalists, chosen by the 14 living Frick Award recipients: Buddy Blattner, Ken Coleman, Dizzy Dean, Earl Gillespie, Merle Harmon, Ned Martin, Monte Moore, Gustavo Lopez Moreno, Bill O'Donnell and Jack Quinlan. The Hall's page lists the Frick date as scheduled; Major League Baseball (MLB) has not confirmed the Winter Meetings dates on its own site. For the January date, keep the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html) handy.

## When is the next non-player election?

The Era Committees rules page says a Contemporary Baseball Era non-player election is held once every three years beginning in 2023. It lists this vote for December 2026 (Class of 2027), the Classic Era for December 2027 (Class of 2028) and the Contemporary Players ballot for December 2028 (Class of 2029).
