### Why 10 is the minimum

Sixteen tiles start out of place. A loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops. The best grouping of these 16 tiles has 6 loops, which leaves 10 swaps, and no route can use fewer.
