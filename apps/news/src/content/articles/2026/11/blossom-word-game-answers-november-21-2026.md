**Saturday's Blossom from Merriam-Webster, Nov. 21, 2026, puts S in the center with G, H, I, N, R and U on the petals.** Three solution pages agree on the pangrams, and there are four of them. Below: a length guide, a tick-box self-check and graded hints before the spoiler.

## Saturday's letters and what the center asks

- **Center:** S
- **Petals:** G, H, I, N, R, U

Two petals, I and U, are vowel letters, so most of the shaping falls to the consonants around the S. The rules are on our [Blossom page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works), and today the center S is the one letter every entry must carry. Friday's flower is on the [Nov. 20 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-20-2026/).

## Four pangrams, four lengths

Merriam-Webster shows no official word list, so the pangrams here are the ones the published solution pages list: four, of 7, 8, 9 and 9 letters. Seven letters is the floor for any pangram, so the shortest one uses each of S, G, H, I, N, R and U exactly once. The 8-letter one has a single spare slot, and the two 9-letter ones have two spare slots each.

| Pangram | Letters | Spare slots | Where the spare slots go |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7 | 0 | none, each letter once |
| 2 | 8 | 1 | one letter used twice |
| 3 | 9 | 2 | two letters each used twice |
| 4 | 9 | 2 | one letter used three times |

Spotting the seven-letter word first is the quickest route in. The longer ones are that skeleton stretched: a plural ending, a front piece, or a doubled consonant swapped into a different word shape. If a long find is not on your list, check whether one of the letter slots is doing double duty.

## Check a find against the seven letters

Write S, G, H, I, N, R and U in a row and tick each one found in your word.

| Tick | Letter | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | S | Center |
| 2 | G | Petal |
| 3 | H | Petal |
| 4 | I | Petal |
| 5 | N | Petal |
| 6 | R | Petal |
| 7 | U | Petal |

Seven ticks is a pangram. Six ticks with the S present is a strong long word, and seven ticks without the S is not allowed. A nine-letter word can still miss a tick, so count ticks, not length.

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- Hint 1: Four pangrams are listed, and none is longer than nine letters.
- Hint 2: The shortest has seven letters, so every letter appears once.
- Hint 3: Three of the four end in the same three letters.
- Hint 4: The 8-letter one is the 7-letter one with one more letter at the very end.
- Hint 5: Of the two 9-letter pangrams, one starts with a petal letter and the other with the center.

## Spreading one short word into three longer ones

Each added slot has to be a letter already in play, which narrows the choices fast. A plural adds a spare slot at the back. A front piece adds two spare slots at once. A word that doubles a consonant in its middle can reach nine letters without sharing a root with the short one. When a search returns only the seven-letter word, try those three moves in turn.

### Pangram

The solution pages list these four. Each holds S, G, H, I, N, R and U at least once.

- **Rushing** (7 letters)
- **Rushings** (8 letters)
