**Meta is selling the Meta Quest 3S at 15% off through 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time (PT) on Nov. 30, 2026, according to Meta's Nov. 20 blog post: $296.99 for the 128GB model and $381.99 for 256GB.** The sale ends at 2:59 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Tuesday, Dec. 1. The older Quest 3 (512GB) is not discounted, and two game bundles run to Jan. 4, 2027.

## How much is the Meta Quest 3S on Black Friday 2026?

Meta's post says "Now through 11:59 pm PT on November 30, 2026, you can get Meta Quest 3S for 15% off," at "just $296.99" for the 128GB model. It adds that the 256GB model is "$381.99," which it calls "a savings of $68." The list prices come from Meta's Apr. 16, 2026 post, which raised Quest 3 and 3S prices starting Apr. 19. The dollars saved in the table are our subtraction.

| Headset | List price (Apr. 19, 2026) | Black Friday price | You save |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta Quest 3S, 128GB | $349.99 | $296.99 | $53.00 |
| Meta Quest 3S, 256GB | $449.99 | $381.99 | $68.00 |
| Meta Quest 3, 512GB | $599.99 | No discount | $0 |

One detail: 15% of $349.99 is $297.49, while Meta's post prints $296.99, so the table uses Meta's figure. The 256GB saving of $68 matches Meta's own number. Meta's footnote says the discount "applies to Meta Quest 3S (128GB and 256GB) only" and "excludes refurbished products." Pricing is for the US here; Meta says "Pricing varies by region."

## When does the Meta Quest deal end in your time zone?

Meta gives the deadline in Pacific Time. Converted, the sale ends on the first day of December in every US zone below.

| Zone | Deal ends |
|---|---|
| Pacific Time (PT) | Mon., Nov. 30, 11:59 p.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | Tue., Dec. 1, 12:59 a.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | Tue., Dec. 1, 1:59 a.m. |
| Eastern Time (ET) | Tue., Dec. 1, 2:59 a.m. |

Nov. 30 is also Cyber Monday, so the Quest 3S price Meta posted covers both days. ## Is the Meta Quest 3 on sale for Black Friday?

Not in Meta's post. The Quest 3 (512GB) is listed at $599.99 in Meta's April pricing post and gets no price cut in the Black Friday post. It does qualify for the bundles below.

## Which games come with a Quest headset?

Two offers run through Jan. 4, 2027, a date after the sale ends. Meta offers "1,800 in-game credits ($55 total value) with an eligible headset" for the golf game Golf+. Resident Evil 4 comes "with your purchase of an eligible Meta Quest 3 or 3S headset," and its game value is $39.99. Both dates are in the table on the printable and on our [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## How does 2026 compare with Black Friday 2025?

In 2025, Android Central reported on Nov. 25, 2025 that Meta was "discounting the Meta Quest 3S by up to $50, bringing the starting price down to $249.99," with offers "live until Dec. 1, 2025." The 2026 price for the 128GB model is $296.99, which is $47.00 higher. Meta raised the list price in April 2026, so the 15% cut starts from a higher number. These are different years' offers, listed here as reported.

## Are Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta glasses on sale too?

The same Meta post lists Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 and Oakley Meta glasses at 25% off from Nov. 20 to 30, 2026.

## What has Meta not announced for Quest Black Friday 2026?

Meta has not announced a 2026 Meta Horizon Store game sale or a promo code in the post we read. For comparison, UploadVR reported that the 2025 store sale was 40% off with code BFCM25 through Dec. 2, 2025. We list no retailer prices here because Meta's post gives none. Meta's post also lists the Quest 3S sale for other regions: North America, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, Australia, New Zealand and Singapore from Nov. 20 to 30, with Japan on Nov. 13 to Dec. 4.
