**Bob Evans says its dining rooms are open from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, while Chick-fil-A says all U.S. restaurants are closed that day.** Both facts come from the chains' own pages, as do the meal pickup windows and order deadlines for Maggiano's Little Italy, Morton's The Steakhouse and The Capital Grille below. Cracker Barrel's site blocked our checks, so its status rests on two newsrooms. Chains whose hours are not confirmed are listed separately, with what is missing. For grocery stores, see [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/); the date itself is on [When is Thanksgiving?](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

Each chain name links to the page the fact came from. Franchise and location hours can differ, so call your restaurant.

## What to know before you go

- Reserve or order early: Bob Evans' feast closes Nov. 25 and The Capital Grille's pre-orders close Nov. 24.
- Pickup at Maggiano's, The Capital Grille and Morton's includes Wednesday, Nov. 25, a day before Thanksgiving.
- Chick-fil-A's closed page names no year; the chain describes it as a day for its teams to spend with family and friends.
