**The National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division II and Division III football playoffs play 24 second-round games on Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, with the first kickoffs at noon Eastern Time (ET), according to the NCAA's bracket pages.** Eight are in Division II and 16 in Division III; nine start at noon ET, 13 at 1 p.m., one at 2 p.m. and one at 3 p.m.

Every game is below in ET, Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT) and Pacific Time (PT), with the Division II seeds and first-round scores and a two-page printable.

## Second-round kickoff times by hour

| Kickoff (ET) | Division II | Division III | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noon | 1 | 8 | 9 |
| 1 p.m. | 5 | 8 | 13 |
| 2 p.m. | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| 3 p.m. | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| All games | 8 | 16 | 24 |

NCAA.com's Division II and Division III championship schedules give the kickoffs in ET ("12 p.m.", "1 p.m."), and the bracket pages give the same times in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), for example "11/28 - 5:00PM UTC". CT, MT and PT are one, two and three hours behind ET.

## When are the next rounds of the playoffs?

The NCAA's Division II bracket lists the quarterfinals for Dec. 5 and the semifinals for Dec. 12, both with times to be announced. NCAA.org lists the Division II championship for December 19, 2026, at McKinney Independent School District (ISD) Stadium in McKinney, Texas; the [Division II championship guide](/news/ncaa-division-ii-football-championship-2026-december-19-mckinney/) has the final's kickoff and the other small-college title games. The NCAA.com Division II schedule names Dec. 5 a Friday, but Dec. 5, 2026 is a Saturday, and the bracket gives only the date. In Division III, NCAA.com lists the third round for Saturday, Dec. 5, the quarterfinals for Saturday, Dec. 12, the semifinals for Saturday, Dec. 19 and the final for Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027.

Last season's champions, from the NCAA.com history tables: Ferris State beat Harding 42-21 for the 2025 Division II title in McKinney, Texas, and UW-River Falls beat North Central (IL) 24-14 for the Division III title in Canton, Ohio. Ferris State and Harding are both still alive this year.
