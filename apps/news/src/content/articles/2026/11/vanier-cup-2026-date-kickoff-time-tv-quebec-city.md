**The 61st Vanier Cup is set for Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, at noon ET at Stade Telus-Université Laval in Quebec City, according to Laval's Rouge et Or and the Western Mustangs.** Western plays the Montréal Carabins, Western said on Nov. 21, 2026. Below: the kickoff in every time zone, how both teams got there and the ticket prices.

## What time is the Vanier Cup in 2026?

Laval's Vanier Cup page and calendar list the game on Nov. 28 at 12:00 at the Université Laval stadium. Western's newsroom says "The game is set for 12:00 pm on Saturday, November 28, 2026." Destination Québec cité lists 12h00PM. 3DownNation reports kickoff at 12:15 p.m. ET; this story uses noon ET, the time the host and Western give.

## How did Western and Montréal reach the Vanier Cup?

Western beat Queen's 36-27 in Kingston on Nov. 14 to win the Ontario University Athletics (OUA) title, the Yates Cup, as Canadian Television (CTV) News London and Education News Canada reported. A week later, Montréal hosted the University of British Columbia (UBC) in the Uteck Bowl.

| Date | Game | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Sat., Nov. 14 | Yates Cup (OUA final), Kingston | Western 36, Queen's 27 |
| Sat., Nov. 21 | Mitchell Bowl | Western 64, Saint Mary's 14 |
| Sat., Nov. 21 | Uteck Bowl, Montréal | Montréal 30, UBC 13 |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 61st Vanier Cup, Quebec City | Noon ET |

3DownNation and The Ubyssey both report the 30-13 Uteck Bowl score. 3DownNation calls it Montréal's second consecutive Vanier Cup berth.

## How much are Vanier Cup 2026 tickets?

Laval lists seated tickets at $30 for adults and $24 for students, service fees included. General admission follows in limited quantity, and children 5 and under attend free without a seat. Tickets are sold through the link on Laval's Vanier Cup page. Destination Québec cité says tickets start at $34 in advance. Prices are in Canadian dollars; the two sources differ, so check the seller before you buy.

## What channel is the Vanier Cup on?

Not confirmed here. The national university sports body had not posted a readable 2026 where-to-watch page on Nov. 23; check its [broadcast information page](https://en.usports.ca/Broadcasts/Broadcast_Info). The halftime show and weather are not announced either.

## Who won the Vanier Cup last year?

The Montréal Carabins beat the Saskatchewan Huskies 30-16 in the 60th Vanier Cup on Saturday, Nov. 22, 2025, at Mosaic Stadium in Regina, before an announced crowd of 8,896, per the Saskatchewan Huskies. Montréal is the defending champion.
