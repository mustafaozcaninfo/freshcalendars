## How Wordle works

You get six tries to find a five-letter word. After each guess, a green tile means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means the letter is in the word but in another spot, and gray means it is not in the word at all. A letter that appears twice is only marked twice if you guess it twice. Gray tiles matter too.

## Six clues, gentle to strong

1. **Part of speech:** it is an adjective.
2. **Topic:** it describes food that is full of nuts or tastes like them, and in casual speech it can also mean silly.
3. **Vowels:** one vowel letter, U, in the second spot, and the word ends in Y.
4. **Repeated letter:** one. The letter T appears twice, side by side in the third and fourth spots.
5. **First letter:** N.
6. **Strong nudge:** a brownie packed with walnuts is described this way: "This brownie is really ___."

The word starts with N and ends with Y, so a guess with N first and Y last can turn two tiles green right away. The double T sits in the middle. A guess with two T tiles in a row tests it fast. Try it. Apart from the final Y, U is the only vowel.
