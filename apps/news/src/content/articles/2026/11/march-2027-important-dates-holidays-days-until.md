**March 2027 begins on Monday, Mar. 1 and has no federal holiday, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** U.S. clocks go forward at 2 a.m. on Sunday, Mar. 14, the same day as the 99th Oscars. Spring begins on Mar. 20, St. Patrick's Day is Mar. 17 and Easter is Mar. 28. The countdowns below are counted from Friday, Nov. 20, 2026.

## March 2027 month layout and the days with a note

March 2027 has 31 days and runs from a Monday to a Wednesday, so it holds five Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays. A Sunday-start calendar needs five rows, and the last one stops on Wednesday, Mar. 31.

## No federal holiday between Washington's Birthday and Memorial Day

OPM's federal holiday list has Washington's Birthday on Monday, Feb. 15, 2027 and then Memorial Day on Monday, May 31, so March has none. Good Friday on Mar. 26 is not on that list either; the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) is the one U.S. calendar here that closes for it, and our [Easter 2027 guide](/news/easter-2027-march-28-good-friday-easter-monday-whats-open/) covers what is open that weekend. The previous month in this series is the [February 2027 important dates](/news/february-2027-important-dates-holidays-days-until/) story, which has the Feb. 15 holiday.

## Clock changes in March 2027: the U.S. on Mar. 14, the UK on Mar. 28

Federal law, 15 U.S.C. 260a, starts the period "at 2 o'clock antemeridian on the second Sunday of March," and the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) says it "begins at 2:00 a.m. on the second Sunday of March." In 2027 that is Sunday, Mar. 14. Our [clock change 2027 story](/news/clock-changes-2027-when-does-daylight-saving-time-start-again-march-14/) has the full rules.

The UK moves two weeks later. GOV.UK lists 28 March for the 2027 spring change, with clocks going forward at 1 a.m.; our [UK clocks 2027 story](/news/uk-clocks-go-forward-2027-march-28-bst/) has the detail. Between the two dates the gap between New York and London is 4 hours, computed from the offsets.

| Dates | New York | London | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar. 1 to Mar. 13 | EST, UTC-5 | GMT, UTC+0 | 5 hours |
| Mar. 14 to Mar. 27 | EDT, UTC-4 | GMT, UTC+0 | 4 hours |
| From Mar. 28 | EDT, UTC-4 | BST, UTC+1 | 5 hours |

## Equinox and Moon phase times in Eastern Time

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes times in Universal Time (UT). New York is five hours behind UT on Eastern Standard Time (EST) before Mar. 14 and four hours behind on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) after it. The equinox is at 20:25 UT on Saturday, Mar. 20, which is 4:25 p.m. EDT; our [Nowruz 2027 story](/news/nowruz-2027-date-equinox-time/) converts it for 11 cities.

| Event | UT | Eastern Time |
|---|---|---|
| New moon, Monday, Mar. 8 | 09:29 | 4:29 a.m. EST |
| First quarter, Monday, Mar. 15 | 16:25 | 12:25 p.m. EDT |
| March equinox, Saturday, Mar. 20 | 20:25 | 4:25 p.m. EDT |

| Last quarter, Mar. 30 UT | 00:54 | 8:54 p.m. EDT, Monday, Mar. 29 |

The last quarter moon is on Mar. 30 in UT but still Monday, Mar. 29 in New York.

## Oscars, baseball and March Madness in March 2027

The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences said on Apr. 7, 2026 that "the 99th Oscars will take place on Sunday, March 14, 2027," on ABC from the Dolby Theatre, live at 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT. The Academy's key dates page lists finals voting on Thursday, Mar. 4. Our [Oscars 2027 calendar](/news/golden-globes-oscars-2027-dates-nominations-how-to-watch-awards-calendar/) has the rest of the awards season.

MLB.com said on July 16, 2026 that Opening Night is Wednesday, Mar. 24 on Netflix and that "Traditional Opening Day is set for Thursday, March 25, with 14 games on the schedule." More dates are in our [MLB Opening Day 2027 story](/news/mlb-opening-day-2027-date-march-25-spring-training-all-star/).

The NCAA said on May 7, 2026 that the 76-team men's tournament "will begin March 16" and that the women's Opening Round is Mar. 17-18. NCAA.com's tournament pages give Selection Sunday as Mar. 14. The [college basketball key dates](/news/college-basketball-2026-27-key-dates-season-start-march-madness-printable/) story lists the rounds.

## Easter weekend, Purim, Mothering Sunday and Nowruz

Easter Sunday is Mar. 28. GOV.UK lists Good Friday on Friday, Mar. 26 and Easter Monday on Monday, Mar. 29 as UK bank holidays. Palm Sunday, Mar. 21, is computed from Easter, the Sunday a week earlier. The Church of England puts UK Mothering Sunday three weeks before Easter Day, on Mar. 7; see our [Pancake Day and Mothering Sunday 2027 story](/news/pancake-day-2027-february-9-mothering-sunday-2027-march-7-uk/).

Purim begins at sundown on Monday, Mar. 22 and ends at nightfall on Tuesday, Mar. 23, according to Chabad.org and MyJewishLearning. The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) says the International Day of Nowruz is Mar. 21. In Miami, the [Ultra Music Festival](/news/ultra-music-festival-2027-miami-dates-march-26-28/) is Mar. 26-28, on the Easter weekend.

## Tax and savings deadlines in March 2027

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) says S corporations file Form 1120-S by the 15th day of the third month, which is Monday, Mar. 15, 2027 for calendar-year filers. In Canada, the RRSP contribution deadline for 2026 returns is Monday, Mar. 1, 2027 by the 60-day count; our [RRSP deadline story](/news/rrsp-deadline-2027-march-1-canada-tax-season-dates/) notes the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) had not posted its own 2027 date.
