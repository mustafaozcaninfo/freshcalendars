**Ace Hardware's Ace Rewards members get 50% off one regularly priced item $30 or less on Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 only, with the online code BF26, according to the retailer's Nov. 2 release from Oak Brook, Ill.** Holiday Weekend Savings follow Nov. 28-30 (15% off regularly priced merchandise, online code HOLIDAY26). Separate flash sale items are valid Nov. 25-29. The tables below add sale and regular prices with savings, and a one-page printable follows.

## Is Ace Hardware open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Ace has not posted Thanksgiving hours for 2026. Its stores are locally owned, so hours are set store by store. Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26. ## How do the Ace Rewards Black Friday offers work?

The release says Ace Rewards members get more holiday savings through exclusive offers. Two are named:

- Black Friday Deal, Nov. 27 only: 50% off one regularly priced item $30 or less. For online transactions, use code BF26.
- Holiday Weekend Savings, Nov. 28-30: 15% off your purchase of regularly priced merchandise. For online transactions, use code HOLIDAY26.

## Does Ace deliver and assemble grills for free?

The release says free assembly and delivery is available at participating Ace stores on grills and push lawn mowers priced at $399 and up. Participation varies by store.

## What has Ace Hardware not announced for 2026?

- Thanksgiving Day store hours.
- Early access for Ace Rewards members.
- A Cyber Monday (Nov. 30) event; the Holiday Weekend Savings end that day.
- A Small Business Saturday (Nov. 28) offer.
