**Winterlude 2027 runs from Jan. 29 to Feb. 15, 2027,** Canadian Heritage says on its Winterlude page (modified Oct. 29, 2026): "From January 29 to February 15, 2027, join us for the 49th edition of Winterlude in Canada's Capital Region." The program is not yet announced. Print our one-page [Winterlude 2027 weekend planner](/news/downloads/winterlude-2027/winterlude-2027-weekend-planner.pdf) to keep the dates.

## Winterlude 2027 compared with 2026

| | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Edition | 48th | 49th |
| Dates | Jan. 30 to Feb. 16 | Jan. 29 to Feb. 15 |
| Program | Unveiled Jan. 14, 2026 | Not yet announced |

The 2026 line comes from Canadian Heritage's Jan. 14, 2026 news release, which said "The official sites are Confederation Park and Jacques-Cartier Park" and placed Snowflake Kingdom "at the northern part of Jacques-Cartier Park." Those are last year's sites; no 2027 sites are announced yet. The 2027 edition starts a day earlier in the calendar than 2026 did.

## Is Family Day on the last day of Winterlude 2027?

Yes for Ontario, where Ottawa is. Order in Council 653/2026 says "Family Day, the third Monday of February of every year, is a holiday." The third Monday of February 2027 is Feb. 15, computed from that rule, which is also the last day of Winterlude. Our [Family Day 2027 story](/news/family-day-2027-canada-february-15-provinces-whats-closed/) covers which provinces list the day, and the [Canada statutory holidays 2027 list](/news/canada-statutory-holidays-2027-federal-general-holidays/) covers the federal ones.

## Will the Rideau Canal Skateway be open?

The National Capital Commission (NCC) says the Rideau Canal Skateway has "a total length of 7.8 km" and that "The skating season typically runs from January to early March, but it is weather-dependent." No 2026-27 opening date is announced, so Winterlude's dates do not promise ice on the first day. The 2026 Winterlude listing for the Skateway says: "This is a free event." Check the 2027 listing when it appears.
