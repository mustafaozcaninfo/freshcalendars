**Williamson County Schools (WCS) students have no school Nov. 23-27, 2026, from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, and from Monday, Mar. 15 to Friday, Mar. 19, 2027**, according to the district's 2026-2027 WCS Student School Calendar. Students return Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, and the last day for students is Thursday, May 27, 2027. The PDF says "Approval Date: November 17, 2025" and is linked from the district's calendar page in Franklin, Tennessee.

The next date to know is Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026: the calendar lists Veterans' Day as "Students in School." Students in the separate Franklin Special School District follow that district's own calendar, not this one.

## When are the Williamson County Schools breaks in 2026-27?

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates; the return days marked "our reading" are the first school day after each break.

| Break | Dates | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break (past) | Mon.-Fri., Oct. 12-16, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Oct. 19 (our reading) |
| Thanksgiving | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 (our reading) |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (second semester begins) |
| Mid-Winter Break | Fri., Feb. 12 to Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | 2 | Tue., Feb. 16 (our reading) |
| Spring break | Mon.-Fri., Mar. 15-19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., Mar. 22 (our reading) |

The first semester ends Friday, Dec. 18, 2026, a half day for students with 39 grading days. The calendar prints "1st Semester = 83 Instructional Days" and "2nd Semester = 94 Instructional Days," so 83 plus 94 instructional days. For the weeks around the breaks, see our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## Which Williamson County Schools days are no-school days?

The calendar uses three labels. "No Students" means "Students do not report to school. Teachers will report for an Admin or PD Day." (professional development). "No School" means "Students and teachers do not report. Twelve-month employees report to work on this day." "System Closed" means "All schools and the district offices are closed."

| Date | Weekday | What the district lists | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day | No School, System Closed |
| Oct. 12, 2026 | Monday | Columbus Day, students on fall break | No School |
| Oct. 12-16, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Fall Break | No School; System Closed Oct. 14-16 |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Election Day | No Students |
| Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Thanksgiving Holiday | No School, System Closed |
| Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027 | Monday-Monday | Winter Break | No School |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Martin Luther King, Jr. (MLK) Day | No School, System Closed |
| Feb. 12-15, 2027 | Friday-Monday | Mid-Winter Break; Presidents' Day | No Students |
| Mar. 15-19, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Spring Break | No School, System Closed |
| Mar. 26, 2027 | Friday | Spring Holiday | No School, System Closed |

Presidents' Day, Mon., Feb. 15, 2027, falls in the Friday-to-Monday Mid-Winter Break, so students are out four days in a row with the weekend.

## What are the WCS early release and late start dates?

The calendar prints two schedules. Kindergarten through fifth grade (K-5) has 11 early release dates of 113 minutes; grades 6-12 have 27 late start dates of 45 minutes.

| Semester | K-5 early release (113 min.) |
|---|---|
| First | Aug. 26, Sept. 16, Sept. 30, Oct. 28, Nov. 18, Dec. 9, 2026 |
| Second | Jan. 13, Jan. 27, Feb. 10, Feb. 24, Mar. 10, 2027 |

| Semester | Grades 6-12 late start (45 min.) |
|---|---|
| First | Aug. 18, 24, 31; Sept. 8, 14, 21, 28; Oct. 5, 19, 26; Nov. 2, 9, 16, 30; Dec. 7, 2026 |
| Second | Jan. 5, 11, 19, 25; Feb. 1, 8, 22; Mar. 1, 8, 22, 29; Apr. 5, 2027 |

A footnote marks Nov. 18 with an asterisk: "Approval Date for Early Release Change: August 17, 2026 (Change to November 18th from November 11th)." So the K-5 early release is Wednesday, Nov. 18, not Veterans' Day, Nov. 11.

## What happens if Williamson County Schools miss more than 10 weather days?

The calendar says: "Up to ten (10) inclement weather days are accumulated in the school calendar through extended work (stockpile) days and will not be made up." If more than 10 days are missed, "the Calendar Committee will make a recommendation to the Superintendent of Schools" from this list: Mid-Winter Break; Second Semester Professional Development Day; Spring Holiday; Spring Break days (Monday, then Tuesday, then Wednesday); and additional days at the end of the school year. The calendar does not print the date of the Second Semester Professional Development Day.
