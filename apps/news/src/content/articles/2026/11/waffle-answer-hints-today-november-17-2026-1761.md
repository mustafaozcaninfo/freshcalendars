The three-of-a-kind letter is A. It sits in Raise at row 1, column 2, in Rabbi at row 2, column 1 and in Bayou at row 3, column 2. In the starting grid the A tiles are at row 2, column 5; row 3, column 4; and row 5, column 3, so none starts home.

### A 10-swap route to finish

The minimum is 10 swaps, found by an exhaustive search and replayed on the starting grid to reach the solved one. Play them in order, because each one assumes the earlier ones are done. Swaps 5, 6, 9 and 10 put two tiles home at once; the other six fix one each. Swaps 6 and 10 are the two that bring an L tile into column 3, so the route closes on the double L.

### Why the count is 10

Fourteen tiles are misplaced, and a loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops. Ten swaps means 4 loops.
