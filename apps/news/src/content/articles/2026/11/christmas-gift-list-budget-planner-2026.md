**Our free Christmas gift list and budget planner for 2026 puts 18 gift rows, a budget column and the carriers' ship-by dates on one US Letter page**, built from the U.S. Postal Service (USPS) 2026 Holiday Shipping Deadlines and the FedEx dates reported by Value Added Resource on Sept. 24, 2026. Christmas Day is Friday, Dec. 25, 2026, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) federal holiday list.

Each row has a person, a gift idea, a budget, what you spent, an order-by date and Bought, Wrapped and Shipped boxes. A totals box holds the total budget, total spent and what is left. The text columns are blank lines for you to fill in by hand.

## How do you use the gift list and budget planner?

1. Write one total budget in the totals box.
2. Split it across people in the Budget column, then add a gift idea for each.
3. Write an order-by date for any gift that ships, using the table below.
4. Enter the price in Spent when you buy, tick the boxes as each gift is bought, wrapped and shipped, and update Left.

For a yardstick, the National Retail Federation (NRF) said in its Oct. 16, 2025 release that shoppers planned to spend an average of $627.93 per person on gifts in 2025. That is last year's survey figure. The NRF's 2026 survey release of Oct. 20, 2026 (8,094 adults surveyed Oct. 1-7) gives no per-person figure in the text we could read, so we print none for 2026.

## What is the 2026 ship-by date for each carrier?

The USPS dates are recommended send-by dates for delivery before Dec. 25 in the lower 48 states, "recommended dates, not guaranteed cutoffs." FedEx dates are for Dec. 24 delivery, from the FedEx 2026 Peak Last Day to Ship table as reported by Value Added Resource, because FedEx pages block automated access.

The order-by column is our own planning suggestion, not a carrier rule. It counts back two business days (Monday to Friday) from the ship date, so a seller has time to pack and hand off the parcel. Change the margin to suit the store.

## What does the NRF expect for 2026 holiday spending?

The NRF's Oct. 15, 2026 release forecasts that November and December 2026 retail sales will grow 4.2% over 2025, to total spending of $1.013 trillion. In 2025, the same release says, holiday sales rose 3.1% over 2024 to $972 billion. It is a forecast of retail sales overall, not of what one person will spend, so it is no guide to your own budget.

## When should you order so a gift ships on time?

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) says in its business guide to the Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule that a seller must have a reasonable basis to ship within the time it states, or within 30 days if it states none. Read the shipping estimate on each order before you write an order-by date in the planner.
