**Dune: Part Three opens only in cinemas and IMAX on Friday, Dec. 18, 2026, according to Warner Bros. Discovery's (WBD) title page for the film.** The page lists the release date as "December 18, 2026," credits Denis Villeneuve as director, and sets the third film on the same Friday that Disney lists for Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday. Below are the release date, how it fits the earlier Dune films, what the Dec. 18 weekend holds, a countdown and a tracker to print.

## When is the Dune: Part Three release date?

Warner Bros. Discovery's title page lists Dune: Part Three for "December 18, 2026" and says the film is "only in cinemas and IMAX December 18." The same page names Denis Villeneuve as director and lists the cast as Timothée Chalamet, Zendaya, Jason Momoa, Florence Pugh, Rebecca Ferguson, Isaach De Bankolé, Charlotte Rampling, Anya Taylor-Joy, Robert Pattinson, Javier Bardem, Nakoa-Wolf Momoa and Ida Brooke.

Dec. 18, 2026 is a Friday. Our guide to [new movies in theaters in December 2026](/news/new-movies-in-theaters-december-2026-release-dates-avengers-doomsday-dune/) has the full month lineup.

## How does Dec. 18, 2026 fit the earlier Dune films?

Each Dune film has opened on a Friday. The first film reached theaters and the HBO Max streaming service on the same day, Oct. 22, 2021, per Engadget. The Motion Picture Association (MPA) reported that Dune: Part Two moved up two weeks to March 1, 2024 and secured IMAX 70mm screens. The gaps below are our calculation from those dates.

| Film | Release date | Weekday | Gap from previous film |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dune | Oct. 22, 2021 | Friday | — |
| Dune: Part Two | March 1, 2024 | Friday | 861 days (about 28 months) |
| Dune: Part Three | Dec. 18, 2026 | Friday | 1,022 days (about 34 months) |

## Why do Dune: Part Three and Avengers: Doomsday share Dec. 18?

Two studio tentpoles list the same Friday. Warner Bros. Discovery puts Dune: Part Three on Dec. 18, 2026, and Disney's page for Avengers: Doomsday lists "December 18, 2026" as well.

| Date | Film | Studio |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | Dune: Part Three | Warner Bros. Discovery |
| Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | Avengers: Doomsday | The Walt Disney Company (Marvel Studios) |

For the Marvel film's reported runtime, tickets and its own countdown, see our [Avengers: Doomsday release date story](/news/avengers-doomsday-release-date-december-18-countdown-tickets-runtime/).
