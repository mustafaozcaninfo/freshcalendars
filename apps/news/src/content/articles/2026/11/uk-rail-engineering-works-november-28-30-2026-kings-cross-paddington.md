**No trains run between London King's Cross and Peterborough from Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026 to 06:40 on Monday, Nov. 30, and none run to or from London Paddington on Saturday and Sunday**, according to National Rail's engineering works pages read on Nov. 28. The King's Cross restart is 06:40 UK time (GMT), which is 1:40 a.m. ET. National Rail lists these as planned engineering works. Tube strike dates are in our [London Tube strike](/news/london-tube-strike-dates-november-december-2026-january-2027/) story.

The table below lists every closure National Rail shows on its planned works index for the weekend, with what runs instead and when each reopens. A one-page printable of the main closures is attached.

## What runs at King's Cross this weekend?

National Rail's page for the East Coast Digital Programme works says "No trains will run between London Kings Cross and Peterborough all weekend," and recommends you change your journey at Peterborough or Bedford. Operator by operator, it lists:

| Operator | What National Rail says |
|---|---|
| Grand Central | No services all weekend; on Monday the 07:48 King's Cross to Sunderland will not run |
| Great Northern and Thameslink | A shuttle train service between Letchworth Garden City and Cambridge / Ely / Kings Lynn; buses between Bedford and Peterborough via St Neots / Huntingdon, and between Hitchin and Peterborough calling at all stations |
| Hull Trains | 2 trains each way on Saturday, 3 each way on Sunday |
| LNER | On Monday the 06:33 King's Cross to Newcastle and the 06:40 King's Cross to Leeds will not run; a rail replacement coach runs between Peterborough and Bedford |
| Lumo | Reduced service between Newcastle and Edinburgh / Glasgow Queen Street only |

National Rail says tickets are valid at no extra cost on Thameslink services between London St Pancras International and Bedford, on London Overground services between London Liverpool Street and Enfield Town (paper tickets), and on listed Underground and London bus routes. It says "Check before you travel."

## When can you travel to Paddington again?

National Rail's Dolphin Junction upgrade page closes all lines between Maidenhead and London Paddington on Saturday, Nov. 28 and Sunday, Nov. 29. No trains run between Maidenhead or Heathrow Airport and Paddington all weekend. Heathrow Express runs no trains between Paddington and Heathrow Airport. The Elizabeth line runs a limited service of 2 trains per hour between Reading and Maidenhead and none between Maidenhead and Abbey Wood.

GWR runs no trains between Maidenhead and Paddington in either direction; services are diverted or shortened to Reading, Swindon or London Euston, and the Sunday night sleeper starts from London Euston at 23:15. National Rail warns that replacement vehicles may be busier than usual and to allow extra time. Elizabeth line tickets are accepted between Ealing Broadway and Stratford on London Bus and Underground at no extra cost. The page covers Saturday and Sunday only, so confirm Monday trains before you leave. For the longer Paddington closure over the holidays, see [UK trains at Christmas 2026](/news/uk-trains-christmas-2026-no-trains-december-25-26-paddington-closed/).

## What about Brighton and the Windrush line?

On Sunday, Nov. 29, National Rail says engineering work between Three Bridges and Brighton closes all lines. Southern buses run between Three Bridges and Preston Park, Three Bridges and Lewes, Balcombe and Brighton, Haywards Heath and Plumpton, and Plumpton and Lewes. Thameslink buses run between Three Bridges and Brighton all day, with a reduced Thameslink service between Gatwick Airport and Three Bridges.

On the Windrush line, all weekend there are no trains between Highbury & Islington and New Cross / New Cross Gate, or between Surrey Quays and Clapham Junction. A reduced service of 2 trains per hour runs between New Cross Gate and Crystal Palace / West Croydon from all day Saturday until 08:00 on Sunday.

## Is this a strike?

No: National Rail lists every closure above as engineering works. We have not confirmed whether a strike affects trains this weekend, and the Transport for London (TfL) pages for Tube and Elizabeth line closures beyond those above were not available to us. Strike dates are in our [London Tube strike dates story](/news/london-tube-strike-dates-november-december-2026-january-2027/). The Monday restart also falls on [St Andrew's Day](/news/st-andrews-day-2026-scotland-bank-holiday-monday-november-30/), a bank holiday in Scotland.

Mark Saturday, Nov. 28 (King's Cross to Peterborough and Paddington closures start), Sunday, Nov. 29 (Brighton buses) and Monday, Nov. 30 at 06:40 UK time (King's Cross to Peterborough trains resume).
