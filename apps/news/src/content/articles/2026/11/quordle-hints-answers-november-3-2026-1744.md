## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**The word that starts with T**

- Letters: starts with T, ends with L, and has an R, an I and an A inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: a formal hearing of evidence in court, or a test of how well something works.

**The word that starts with S**

- Letters: starts with S, ends with D, and has a T, an A and an I inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: calm, serious and a little old-fashioned in manner.

**The word that starts with L**

- Letters: starts with L, ends with L, and has an E, a V and a second E inside; only one vowel letter, and it appears twice, so this is the one word today that repeats letters (the L as well as the E).
- Meaning: flat and even, with no part higher than another.

**The word that starts with B**

- Letters: starts with B, ends with S, and has an O, an N and a U inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: an extra payment or reward added to what is usual.

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess such as Raise, with R, A, I, S and E, puts at least one letter into all four boards, because each word holds at least one of those five letters. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
