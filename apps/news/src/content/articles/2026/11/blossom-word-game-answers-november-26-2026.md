**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, puts A in the center with E, H, N, P, S and T on the petals, and four lists agree on eight pangrams.** The counts on solver sites split over a simple rule: a pangram must use all seven letters. Hints come first, with no word given away.

## Thanksgiving Day's flower: center A

- **Center:** A
- **Petals:** E, H, N, P, S, T

Minute Cryptic, Word.Tips, LadyPuzzle and Beebom print this letter set. Only A and E are vowels, so the center does most of the work and every find must hold it. ## Which petals does a near miss skip?

A long word full of the right letters can still fall one short. Check each petal off before you count a find as a pangram.

| Tick | Letter | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | Center |
| 2 | E | Petal |
| 3 | H | Petal |
| 4 | N | Petal |
| 5 | P | Petal |
| 6 | S | Petal |
| 7 | T | Petal |

Seven ticks make a pangram; six do not. Keep a tally on the [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) or the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).
