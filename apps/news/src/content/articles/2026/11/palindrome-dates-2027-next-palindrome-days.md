In 2027 no date is an 8-digit palindrome, but 11 days of July 2027 read the same both ways in the US short form M/D/YY, from 7/2/27 to 7/29/27, according to our calculation at Fresh Calendars. The next 8-digit palindrome date is Sunday, Feb. 3, 2030 (03022030, day first); the next in US form is Saturday, March 2, 2030. Below is every format to 2040 and a free printable.

Every date here was computed by Fresh Calendars: we wrote each date in each format, removed the slashes or dashes and reversed the digits. No outside source prints these dates.

## Which dates in 2027 are palindromes?

None in the 8-digit forms. The year 2027 reversed is 7202, which is not a valid month and day in either order, so MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY and YYYY-MM-DD (International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 8601, which the [ISO help page](https://help.iso.org/en/articles/376284-iso-8601-international-standard-for-date-and-time-format) describes as "YYYY-MM-DD (year-month-day)" and our [ISO 8601 guide](/news/what-is-iso-8601/)) all come up empty.

The short US form gives July 2027 its run. Written as M/D/YY with no leading zeros, July 2 is 7/2/27 (7227) and July 20 to 29 are 7/20/27 to 7/29/27, for example 7/22/27 (72227).

|---|---|---|---|

| July 20, 2027 | Tuesday | 7/20/27 | 72027 |
| July 21, 2027 | Wednesday | 7/21/27 | 72127 |

| July 29, 2027 | Thursday | 7/29/27 | 72927 |

Day-first readers get one: 7/2/27 in D/M/YY is Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027. The same digits, 7227, give two different days depending on which number is the month.

## How do you check a palindrome date yourself?

1. Pick a format, such as MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Write the date in it and remove the separators: 03/02/2030 becomes 03022030.
3. Reverse the digits: 03022030 reversed is 03022030.
4. If both strings match, the date is a palindrome in that format.

Leading zeros decide the result. 7/22/27 works as five digits (72227), while 07/22/27 gives 072227, which reverses to 722270 and fails.
