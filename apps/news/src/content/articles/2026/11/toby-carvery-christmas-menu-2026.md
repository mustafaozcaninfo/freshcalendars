**Toby Carvery lists Christmas Day 2026 dining from £67.99 and a Boxing Day meal from just £27.79, according to the pub chain's own booking pages read on 21 November 2026.** Both are starting prices, and the chain says a deposit may be required at booking. Toby Carvery is part of Mitchells & Butlers. The pages publish no adult or child split, no dish list and no festive start or end dates.

## What are the Toby Carvery Christmas prices for 2026?

Every 2026 price Toby Carvery has published, by day. Christmas Day is Friday 25 December and Boxing Day is Saturday 26 December.

| What | When | Price | Source page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Festive dining | Monday to Friday | From £14.79 | Festive Menu 2026 |
| Festive dining | Saturdays | From £16.79 | Festive Menu 2026 |
| Festive dining | Sundays | From £19.79 | Festive Menu 2026 |
| Christmas Day dining | Friday 25 December | From £67.99 | Book for Christmas Day 2026 |
| Boxing Day meal | Saturday 26 December | From £27.79 | Book for Boxing Day 2026 |
| Breakfast with Santa | Sat 5 and Sun 6 December | £12.99 | Breakfast with Santa |
| Breakfast with Santa | Sat 12 and Sun 13 December | £12.99 | Breakfast with Santa |
| Breakfast with Santa | Sat 19 to Wed 23 December | £12.99 | Breakfast with Santa |
| Breakfast with Santa | Thu 24 December (Christmas Eve) | £13.99 | Breakfast with Santa |

The Christmas Day figure is a "from" price. The page does not call it an adult price and publishes no children's price. Prices may differ by pub, so check your local Toby Carvery when you book.

## How much is Toby Carvery on Christmas Day 2026?

The Book for Christmas Day 2026 page says "Christmas Day dining available from £67.99." It adds that "at the time of booking you may be required to pay a deposit." The deposit amount and any booking deadline are not published on that page.

## How much is Toby Carvery on Boxing Day 2026?

The Boxing Day page invites diners to "a delicious Boxing Day meal from just £27.79", again with a possible deposit at booking. That is £40.20 less than the Christmas Day starting price on the chain's pages, a difference computed from the two figures. Boxing Day falls on a Saturday in 2026; our [UK Christmas bank holidays 2026 story](/news/uk-christmas-bank-holidays-2026-boxing-day-substitute-december-28/) covers the public holidays by nation.

## When is Toby Carvery Breakfast with Santa in 2026?

The Breakfast with Santa page lists four date blocks. The first three are a "Special Offer" at £12.99: 5 and 6 December (Saturday and Sunday), 12 and 13 December (Saturday and Sunday), and 19 to 23 December (Saturday to Wednesday). Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December, is £13.99. The page states no adult and child split. Mark the dates on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## What does the Toby Carvery festive menu cost on ordinary days?

The Festive Menu 2026 page says: "Festive dining is available from £14.79 Monday to Friday, from £16.79 on Saturdays and from £19.79 on Sundays." The Sunday starting price is £5.00 above the weekday one. The page names no start or end dates, no dishes, and no two-course, three-course or children's prices, so none are given here. Last year's prices were not found on the official pages, so no year-on-year comparison is made.
