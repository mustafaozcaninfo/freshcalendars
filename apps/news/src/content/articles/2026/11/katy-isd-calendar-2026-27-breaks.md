**Katy Independent School District (ISD) has no school from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, 2026**, according to its "Instructional Calendar 2026-2027", which the Katy ISD Board of Trustees approved on Jan. 20, 2026. Winter break runs **Monday, Dec. 21 through Friday, Jan. 1**, and spring break is **Monday, March 8 through Friday, March 12, 2027**. The last day of school is Wednesday, May 26, 2027. Fall break (Oct. 9-13) has already passed, so Thanksgiving week is the next break.

## Katy ISD break dates for 2026-27

The district's calendar labels the breaks "Students and Staff". The weekday counts and return days in this table are our reading of the PDF.

| Break | Dates | Weekdays off | Back in class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break (past) | Fri., Oct. 9 to Tue., Oct. 13, 2026 | 3 | Wed., Oct. 14 |
| Thanksgiving week | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (Jan. 4 is a campus day with no students) |
| Spring break | Mon.-Fri., March 8-12, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 15 |
| Easter-weekend holiday | Fri., March 26 to Mon., March 29, 2027 | 2 | Tue., March 30 |

Thanksgiving Day itself is Thursday, Nov. 26. The Friday-to-Monday holiday in late March is printed on the PDF as "Holiday • Students and Staff" with no name; it falls on the Friday and Monday around Easter. The calendar does not mark Veterans Day, Nov. 11, as a holiday. Mark winter break on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html) and spring break on the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html).

The district's Jan. 20, 2026 release, "Katy ISD Board of Trustees Approves Fall Breaks for 2026-2027, 2027-28 Instructional Calendars", says the board added an October fall break to the calendar.

## Is the first day of spring semester Jan. 4 or Jan. 5 in Katy ISD?

It is Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027. The PDF lists Monday, Jan. 4 as "Campus Day • No Students" and Jan. 5 as "First Day of Spring Semester". Students are off for 17 days counting weekends, from Saturday, Dec. 19 to Monday, Jan. 4. The last day before winter break is Friday, Dec. 18, an early dismissal day that the calendar marks as the end of the semester.

## Which Katy ISD days off do summaries miss?

The calendar has more no-student days than the named breaks. Five Fridays and Mondays are "Campus Professional Learning Day" with no students, and each one lengthens a weekend.

| Date | Weekday | What the calendar lists | Days off counting weekends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Holiday (Labor Day by date; past) | 3, Sat. Sept. 5 to Mon. Sept. 7 |
| Sept. 18, 2026 | Friday | Campus Professional Learning Day (past) | 3, Fri. Sept. 18 to Sun. Sept. 20 |
| Oct. 23, 2026 | Friday | Campus Professional Learning Day | 3, Fri. Oct. 23 to Sun. Oct. 25 |
| Nov. 2, 2026 | Monday | Campus Professional Learning Day (past) | 3, Sat. Oct. 31 to Mon. Nov. 2 |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Holiday (Martin Luther King Jr. Day by date) | 3, Sat. Jan. 16 to Mon. Jan. 18 |
| Feb. 12-15, 2027 | Friday, then Monday | Campus Professional Learning Day, then Holiday (Presidents Day by date) | 4, Fri. Feb. 12 to Mon. Feb. 15 |
| March 26-29, 2027 | Friday, then Monday | Holiday; the district does not name it | 4, Fri. March 26 to Mon. March 29 |
| April 9, 2027 | Friday | Campus Professional Learning Day | 3, Fri. April 9 to Sun. April 11 |

The Feb. 12 and Feb. 15 pair is the easiest to miss: a four-day weekend sits between two ordinary school weeks. Our [printable school-year calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html) page and the printable below mark each of these days.

## How does Katy ISD spring break 2027 compare with other Houston-area districts?

Katy shares March 8-12, 2027 with Houston ISD and Fort Bend ISD. Cy-Fair ISD and Conroe ISD break a week later. That matters for families with children in two districts.

| District | Spring break 2027 | First day | Last day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Katy ISD | March 8-12 | Aug. 12, 2026 | May 26, 2027 |
| Houston ISD | March 8-12 | Aug. 10, 2026 | May 28, 2027 |
| Fort Bend ISD | March 8-12 | Aug. 11, 2026 | May 26, 2027 |
| Cy-Fair ISD | March 15-19 | Aug. 12, 2026 | May 27, 2027 |
| Conroe ISD | March 15-19 | Aug. 12, 2026 | May 27, 2027 |

Each row comes from that district's own 2026-27 calendar PDF, listed under sources. See our [Houston ISD calendar story](/news/houston-isd-calendar-2026-27-holidays-breaks/) and [Cy-Fair ISD calendar story](/news/cypress-fairbanks-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) for those districts' other days off, and the [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) for more districts.

## How many school days and bad weather days does Katy ISD have?

The calendar counts 172 instruction days: 81 in the fall semester and 91 in the spring. It says it has "enough minutes to cover two bad weather days", and it lists no make-up dates.

## What are the first and last days of the 2026-27 school year?

Students began on Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026, listed as "First Day of School and Fall Semester". The last day is Wednesday, May 26, 2027, an early dismissal day. The PDF does not print grading-period dates, so we do not list them.

## What does Katy ISD's 2027-28 calendar look like?

The same Jan. 20, 2026 release says the board adopted the 2027-28 calendar too. Per the release, the first day is Aug. 11, 2027, fall break is Oct. 8-12, 2027, spring break is March 13-17, 2028, and the last day is May 24, 2028.
