**Wildwood serves its Christmas set menu from Tuesday 17 November 2026 to Monday 4 January 2027 at "3 courses from £34.95", according to the Wildwood Restaurants Christmas page.** The set menu has six starters, seven mains and four desserts, and parties of 6 or more pay a £5 per person deposit. Our research desk read the Wildwood page and the set menu PDF on 23 November; the prices, dates and dishes below follow their wording.

## What does the Wildwood Christmas menu cost in 2026?

The Christmas page says "3 courses from £34.95". It gives no 2-course price, so none is shown here. The Early Bird offer is worded "Early Bird Christmas – 3 Courses & Prosecco £34.95", at the same price, and the page states no end date for it.

The set menu runs 49 calendar days with both ends counted. Wildwood does not state Christmas Day opening or menu, Boxing Day, New Year's Eve or children's prices on its Christmas page. Mark the dates on a [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and see [when Christmas Day falls](/when-is/christmas/): Friday in 2026.

## What do the extras on the Wildwood set menu cost?

The PDF prices four add-ons on top of the £34.95. The total in the last row is our arithmetic, not a Wildwood price.

| Extra | Added cost | Calories |
|---|---|---|
| Pigs in Blankets side | £6.95 | 567 kcal |
| Wildwood Winter Grog cocktail, maximum two per person | £9.95 | not listed |
| 10oz British rib-eye steak instead of another main | £6 supplement | 901 kcal |
| Wagyu Burger instead of another main | £2 supplement | 1155 kcal |
| Rib-eye, side and one cocktail with the set menu | £57.85 | £34.95 + £6 + £6.95 + £9.95 |

The cocktail is Bacardi Dark rum, Cointreau, a cinnamon and mint infusion, honey and fresh lemon. An optional service charge of 10% is added to the bill.

## What are Wildwood's booking rules for 6 or more?

Wildwood's page states three rules for parties of 6 or more and one for cancellations.

| Rule | What Wildwood says | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | "Parties of 6 or more pay £5 per person within 72 hours of enquiry." | 6 people: £30; 10 people: £50 |
| Pre-order | "Parties of 6 or more must complete a pre-order at least 7 days before the booking." | Sat. 19 Dec booking: by Sat. 12 Dec |
| Cancellation | Cancelling within 48 hours of a booking for 6 or more forfeits the deposit | Deposit lost |
| No-show | A no-show with a pre-order is charged 100% of the order | Full order |

Bookings can be made online, in branch, by email or on 020 3949 8800. Wildwood does not say whether the deposit comes off the final bill. An enquiry made on Monday 23 November has its 72 hours end on Thursday 26 November, at the same time of day.
