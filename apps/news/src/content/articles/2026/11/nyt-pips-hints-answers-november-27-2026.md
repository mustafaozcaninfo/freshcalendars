Second, how often each pip value appears among the dominoes' halves.

| Level | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | Pips in total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 1 | 2 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 2 | 30 |
| Medium | 2 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 41 |

Easy is built around its two equal regions, one of which links three cells, and its set has no 3 or 4 at all. Medium has no equal region; its five "total" regions that compare against a number (three less than, two more than) sit next to three sums. Hard is almost all sums: 15 of 19 regions, and 13 of its regions are a single cell. Its set holds six 0s and six 3s, but only two 1s and two 5s, and five of the six 0s are fixed by regions alone: one four-cell block that adds up to 0 and one single cell that must be less than 1.

## The forced first move on each board

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left. A search of every legal placement against the saved data finds one arrangement on Easy.

**Easy.** R3C3, R3C4 and R4C4 must be equal, which needs one value three times. Counting the table above, only one value appears three times in the whole set, so all three cells hold it and the two dominoes that carry that value start here. That leaves no copy of it for R2C3 and R2C4, which add up to 2, so they take the pair that sums to 2 without it.

**Medium.** R2C2 adds up to 5 on its own, so it holds a 5. Its neighbors are R2C3 and R3C2, and R3C2 belongs to a pair that totals less than 3. In every valid placement of the search, R3C2 holds a 2 and the 5-5 domino lies across R2C2 and R2C3. Begin with R2C2.

**Hard.** R4C1, R4C2, R5C1 and R5C2 add up to 0, so all four are 0. Only the 0-0 domino can lie wholly inside that block, which makes it the first domino to place. The other two zeros there belong to dominoes that stick out of the block through R4C1 and R4C2. Another cell that needs a 0 is R2C6, which must be less than 1.

## Pips hints for Nov. 27, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 1-1, 6-5, 6-0, 5-2, 2-2)**

- Hint 1: R3C3, R3C4 and R4C4 must be equal, and only one value in the set appears three times.
- Hint 2: R2C3 and R2C4 add up to 2, and the value from Hint 1 is already used up by then.

**Medium (eight dominoes: 1-1, 6-1, 2-2, 1-2, 5-0, 5-5, 3-4, 0-3)**

- Hint 1: R2C2 adds up to 5 by itself.
- Hint 2: R2C3 with R2C4, and R5C1 with R5C2, must each total more than 8, and the set has only one 6.

**Hard (15 dominoes on a frame 5 rows by 6 columns)**

- Hint 1: R4C1, R4C2, R5C1 and R5C2 add up to 0.
- Hint 2: Only one domino in the set is 0-0, and it is the only one that fits entirely inside that block.

## Which Pips dominoes can be turned around on Nov. 27

Hard has two dominoes that swap ends without breaking a rule: the 2-4 at R1C5 and R1C6, and the 4-0 at R1C2 and R1C1. Both sit in the top row, where the sums of 6 are written as three cells and two cells. Easy and Medium have no domino that turns alone.
