**Labor Day falls on Monday, Sept. 6, 2027, the only federal holiday in September, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** The month starts on a Wednesday and ends on a Thursday. Day counts below run from Monday, Nov. 23, 2026.

## September 2027 calendar grid and the dates on it

September 2027 has 30 days, so it holds five Wednesdays and five Thursdays and four of every other weekday. On a Sunday-start grid the first row begins with Wednesday, Sept. 1 and the fifth row runs from Sunday, Sept. 26 to Thursday, Sept. 30.

| Sun | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| 5 | 6: Labor Day | 7: First Quarter | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11: Patriot Day |
| 12: Grandparents Day | 13 | 14: Voter Registration Day | 15: Full Moon | 16 | 17: Constitution Day | 18 |
| 19 | 20 | 21 | 22 | 23: Equinox | 24 | 25 |
| 26: Gold Star Mother's Day | 27 | 28 | 29: New Moon (10:36 p.m.) | 30 | | |

The New Moon is at 02:36 Universal Time (UT) on Sept. 30, which is 10:36 p.m. on Wednesday, Sept. 29 in New York, so it sits under Sept. 29. The [September 2027 printable calendar](/september-calendars.html) has plain grids with room for your own plans.

To count from another day, add the days left in the current month to the days in each full month before your date. From Nov. 23 there are 8 days left in November, then 31, 31, 28, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31 and 31 days in December through August, so Sept. 1 is 8 + 31 + 31 + 28 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 31 = 282 days away.

## Which holidays fall in September 2027?

Labor Day is the only federal holiday. OPM's list shows "Monday, September 06" for Labor Day, and the next federal holiday on it falls in October. Our [Labor Day page](/when-is/labor-day/) and the [2027 federal holidays list](/news/federal-holidays-2027-observed-dates-list/) have the rest of the year. The month before is in [August 2027 important dates](/news/august-2027-important-dates-holidays-days-until/).

Several observances in federal law fall on weekdays or weekends this month but are not days off:

| Observance | Rule in 36 U.S.C. | 2027 date |
|---|---|---|
| Patriot Day | "September 11 is Patriot Day." (§144) | Saturday, Sept. 11 |
| National Grandparents Day | "the first Sunday in September after Labor Day" (§125) | Sunday, Sept. 12 |
| Constitution Day and Citizenship Day | September 17 (§106) | Friday, Sept. 17 |
| National Hispanic Heritage Month | September 15 through October 15 (§126) | Wednesday, Sept. 15 to Friday, Oct. 15 |
| Gold Star Mother's Day | "The last Sunday in September" (§111) | Sunday, Sept. 26 |

National Voter Registration Day is not in federal law: its organizer says it "Will Return on September 14, 2027", a Tuesday. Prisoner of War/Missing in Action Recognition Day is set by presidential proclamation each year, so its 2027 date is not yet announced.

## Do Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur fall in September 2027?

No. Chabad lists Rosh Hashanah as beginning at sundown on Friday, Oct. 1, 2027 and ending at nightfall on Sunday, Oct. 3, and Hebcal lists Yom Kippur as beginning at sundown on Sunday, Oct. 10. Searches for the High Holy Days in September 2027 land a few days early. The full list of dates is in our [2027 Jewish holidays guide](/news/jewish-holidays-2027-dates-passover-rosh-hashanah-yom-kippur-hanukkah/).

## Moon phase times in Eastern Time for September 2027

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes times in UT. Daylight time is in effect all month, so New York is four hours behind UT on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). September has one full moon and one new moon, and the August new moon was on Aug. 31 at 1:41 p.m. EDT. Our [2027 Moon phase calendar](/news/moon-phases-2027-full-moon-calendar/) has the other months.

## When is the September equinox in 2027?

The September equinox is on Thursday, Sept. 23, 2027 at 06:02 UT, which is 2:02 a.m. EDT, according to USNO. The last quarter moon is the same morning, at 6:20 a.m. EDT. Other season dates are in our [2027 equinox and solstice times](/news/seasons-2027-equinox-solstice-dates-times/).

## Are there eclipses or meteor showers in September 2027?

No eclipse. NASA's list of 2027 eclipses has the February 6 annular eclipse, penumbral lunar eclipses on Feb. 20, July 18 and Aug. 17, and the total solar eclipse on Aug. 2, with nothing in September. The American Meteor Society (AMS) calendar shows no major shower peaks in September; it lists the Southern Taurids as active from Sept. 20, with a peak on Oct. 14. See the [2027 eclipse overview](/news/eclipses-2027-dates-times-total-solar-eclipse-august-2/) for the year.

## Sports and events in September 2027

The Ryder Cup at Adare Manor in Ireland has an event week of "September 13th - 19th in 2027", according to Limerick City and County Council, and Raidió Teilifís Éireann (RTE) reported competition "from 17-19 September", so the matches run from Friday, Sept. 17 to Sunday, Sept. 19. The Berlin Marathon organizer lists the race for Sunday, Sept. 26, 2027.

The NFL's 2027 season kickoff date and the U.S. Open tennis dates are not yet announced. Rugby World Cup 2027 in Australia opens just after the month, on Oct. 1.

## Canada and UK dates in September 2027

Canada's Interpretation Act defines Labour Day as "the first Monday in September", which is Monday, Sept. 6, and says the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation "is observed on September 30", a Thursday. See the [2027 Canada statutory holidays](/news/canada-statutory-holidays-2027-federal-general-holidays/) for what each date means by province. GOV.UK lists the summer bank holiday on Monday, Aug. 30, 2027 in England and Wales and none in September. Mexico's Independence Day, Sept. 16, is a Thursday.
