**Thursday's Blossom from Merriam-Webster, Nov. 12, 2026, puts C in the middle, with D, E, I, N, T and U on the petals.** Beebom and Connections Solver print the same seven letters. Nudges sit up top, and the full word lists wait in a marked section at the end.

## The seven letters around C

- **Center:** C (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** D, E, I, N, T, U

Three vowels, E, I and U, share the flower with four consonants. The rules are on our [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works). The previous flower is on the [Nov. 11 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-11-2026/).

## Pangram nudges, from light to heavy

Several words use all seven letters, so each nudge fits more than one.

1. **Count:** Beebom and Word.Tips both list five.
2. **Span:** the shortest has 7 letters and the longest has 10.
3. **Clean one:** exactly one of the five uses every letter a single time.
4. **Openers:** three begin with I, one with U and one with T.
5. **Endings:** four of the five finish with ED.

## What the five pangrams share

Seven letters are required, so the 10-letter pangram carries three spares, each 8-letter pangram carries one, and the 7-letter pangram carries none. The spares fall on a few letters.

- **D doubles twice.** One 8-letter pangram and the 10-letter one each use it two times.
- **N doubles twice.** The 10-letter pangram and one other 8-letter pangram use it two times.
- **I and E double once each.** I repeats in the 10-letter pangram, and E repeats in the remaining 8-letter pangram.
- **C, T and U never repeat.** Each appears once in all five.

A prefix pattern runs through the list: four of the five open with the pair IN or UN, three with IN and one with UN. The fifth starts with TU.
