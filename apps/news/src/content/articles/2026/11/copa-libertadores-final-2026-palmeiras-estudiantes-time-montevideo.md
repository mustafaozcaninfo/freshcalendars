**The 2026 Copa Libertadores final is Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, with Palmeiras and Estudiantes de La Plata kicking off at 3 p.m. ET at the Estadio Centenario in Montevideo, Uruguay, according to beIN Sports, the U.S. rights holder.** beIN's Nov. 16 article describes the match as live and exclusive on beIN Sports and does not give a channel number. The South American Football Confederation (CONMEBOL) lists the final at 17:00 local time.

Brazilian outlets agree on the hour for Brazil: two Brazilian outlets, dgabc.com.br and Tribuna do Sertão, both reported on Oct. 31 that the match is at 5 p.m. Brasília time. On Nov. 28 Montevideo, Buenos Aires and Brasília are all three hours ahead of ET, which gives the table below.

## Libertadores final kickoff by time zone

Every row is computed from 5 p.m. in Montevideo, which is 20:00 UTC. beIN also prints "1:00 PM PT" in its article, but 3 p.m. ET is noon PT, so the table uses noon.

## How did Palmeiras and Estudiantes reach the final?

CONMEBOL's site reports that Palmeiras won on penalties and is the first finalist, and that Estudiantes is the other. In the semifinals, per CONMEBOL, Palmeiras beat Fluminense 5-3 on penalties after a 2-2 aggregate (Oct. 14 and 21), and Estudiantes beat Flamengo 3-2 on penalties after a 0-0 aggregate (Oct. 15 and 22).

In 2025, Flamengo beat Palmeiras 1-0 in the final on Nov. 29 at the Estadio Monumental in Lima, according to Al Jazeera.

## Where to watch the Libertadores final

In the U.S., beIN Sports has the final live and exclusively, per its own article. Broadcasters in Brazil, Argentina and the UK are not yet announced on the broadcasters' own pages, so none is listed here. CONMEBOL's site does not list ticket or gate details for the final in the pages checked.

## What the Libertadores champion earns

beIN Sports reported on Oct. 7, 2026 that the champion receives $25 million and the runner-up $7 million. These are beIN's figures; CONMEBOL's own pages checked did not state them.

The winner then goes on to the club competition run by Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA). In the [Intercontinental Cup schedule](/news/fifa-intercontinental-cup-2026-qatar-dates/), FIFA lists the Libertadores winners against Toluca in the Derby of the Americas on Wed., Dec. 9, in Doha, with kickoff not announced.
