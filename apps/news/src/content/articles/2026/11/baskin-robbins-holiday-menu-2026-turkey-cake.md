**Baskin-Robbins Rewards members can save $5 on Turkey Cake orders placed Nov. 16 to 26, 2026, according to a Nov. 2 release from Baskin-Robbins' parent company, Inspire Brands.** Nov. 26 is Thanksgiving Day, so the member window closes on the holiday itself. The all-guest $5 offer ran for orders placed Nov. 1 to 15 and is over.

Baskin-Robbins did not give a price for the Turkey Cake or anything else in the release. For how other chains compare, see our [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/).

## Baskin-Robbins Turkey Cake discount dates for 2026

| Window | Who saves $5 | Status on Nov. 19 |
|---|---|---|
| Orders placed Nov. 1-15 | All guests | Over |
| Orders placed Nov. 16-26 | Baskin-Robbins Rewards members | Open |

Source: Inspire Brands release, Nov. 2, 2026. The Baskin-Robbins home page shows the Turkey Cake offer as "Expires 11/26/2026." The release gives no pickup cutoff for Thanksgiving Day, so confirm your shop's pickup time when you order.

## What is a Baskin-Robbins Turkey Cake?

The release calls it "an all-ice cream cake, complete with sugar cone legs glazed with caramel praline topping." It needs no oven and no carving. It can be made in any Baskin-Robbins flavor, including the Dessert Table Collection flavors. Guests pre-order through the Baskin-Robbins app or BaskinRobbins.com at participating shops nationwide, and the release says shop participation may vary.

## What is in the Baskin-Robbins Dessert Table Collection?

The collection joined the menu Nov. 1 "for a limited time." No end date is given. The Baskin-Robbins home page names Triple Treat Feast and Frosted Sugar Cookie as the new flavors.

| Item | What is in it |
|---|---|
| Triple Treat Feast Ice Cream | Spice Cake and Pumpkin Pie flavored ice cream with pecan pralines, pumpkin pie spice cake pieces and brown sugar swirls |
| Frosted Sugar Cookie Ice Cream | Sugar Cookie flavored ice cream with Sugar Cookie Dough Pieces and pink confetti icing swirls |
| Triple Treat Feast Sundae | Three scoops of Triple Treat Feast Ice Cream with caramel praline topping, whipped cream, chopped almonds and a cherry |
| Frosted Sugar Cookie Shake | Frosted Sugar Cookie Ice Cream with milk and simple syrup |

Source: Inspire Brands release, Nov. 2, 2026.

## What Baskin-Robbins has not said

The sources we read do not state any prices, the end date of the Dessert Table Collection, the December 2026 Flavor of the Month, Thanksgiving Day pickup cutoffs or shop hours that day. If you need the cake on Nov. 26, order ahead and check with your shop.
