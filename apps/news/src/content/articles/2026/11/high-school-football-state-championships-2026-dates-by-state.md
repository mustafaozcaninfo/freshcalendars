**The University Interscholastic League (UIL) plays the Texas high school football state championships Wednesday to Saturday, Dec. 16-19, 2026, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, and the Georgia High School Association (GHSA) plays Tuesday and Wednesday, Dec. 15-16, at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.** Michigan's 8-player final on Saturday, Nov. 21 opens the title weekends among the states below, each read on its own association's page on Nov. 6. Below: a state-by-state table, the weekends in order and the states that had not posted a date.

## High school football state championships 2026: dates and stadiums by state

Every row comes from the association's own page. Weekdays are computed from the 2026 calendar. Where a page names no stadium, the table says so.

| State | Association | Final dates | Stadium and city |
|---|---|---|---|
| Michigan | Michigan High School Athletic Association (MHSAA) | 11-player: Fri.-Sat., Nov. 27-28; 8-player: Sat., Nov. 21 | Ford Field, Detroit (11-player); Superior Dome, Marquette (8-player) |
| Indiana | Indiana High School Athletic Association (IHSAA) | Fri.-Sat., Nov. 27-28 | Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis; see our [IHSAA football tournament story](/news/ihsaa-football-tournament-2026-schedule-sectionals-regionals-state-finals/) |
| Alabama | Alabama High School Athletic Association (AHSAA) | Wed.-Sat., Dec. 2-5 (Super 7 Finals) | Mobile; the page names no stadium |
| Ohio | Ohio High School Athletic Association (OHSAA) | Thu.-Sat., Dec. 3-5 | Tom Benson Hall of Fame Stadium, Canton; see our [OHSAA football playoffs story](/news/ohsaa-football-playoffs-2026-schedule-regional-dates-state-finals-canton/) |
| Tennessee | Tennessee Secondary School Athletic Association (TSSAA) | Thu.-Sat., Dec. 3-5 (BlueCross Bowl) | Finley Stadium, Chattanooga |
| South Carolina | South Carolina High School League (SCHSL) | Fri.-Sat., Dec. 4-5 | South Carolina State University, Orangeburg |
| Pennsylvania | Pennsylvania Interscholastic Athletic Association (PIAA) | Thu.-Sat., Dec. 10-12 | Chapman Field, Cumberland Valley High School |
| North Carolina | North Carolina High School Athletic Association (NCHSAA) | Thu.-Sat., Dec. 10-12 | Kenan Stadium, Chapel Hill; Helen & Leonard Moretz Stadium, Hickory; Barker-Lane Stadium, Buies Creek |
| Georgia | Georgia High School Association (GHSA) | Tue.-Wed., Dec. 15-16 | Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta |
| Texas | University Interscholastic League (UIL) | Wed.-Sat., Dec. 16-19 (classes 1A to 6A) | AT&T Stadium, Arlington |

The MHSAA page lists "Friday and Saturday, November 27 and 28" with no year; those weekdays match 2026. The Indiana dates and venue are from the IHSAA's 2026-27 State Tournament Series page. The Ohio venue is from the OHSAA's 2026 tournament coverage page. The Pennsylvania page, dated Aug. 27, 2026, gives no city for Chapman Field. The NCHSAA says it will finalize which class plays at which of its three sites "after the final regional contests."

## Which state championship weekends come first?

In order of the first final day: Michigan's 8-player final (Nov. 21), then Michigan's 11-player finals and Indiana (Nov. 27-28), then Alabama (Dec. 2-5). Ohio, Tennessee and South Carolina start Dec. 3 or 4, ahead of Pennsylvania and North Carolina on Dec. 10-12. Georgia is Dec. 15-16 and Texas, Dec. 16-19, is last on this list. The Texas and Georgia finals overlap on Wednesday, Dec. 16.

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, so the Michigan and Indiana finals fall on the Friday and Saturday after it. For college football that weekend, see our [Football Championship Subdivision (FCS) playoffs story](/news/fcs-playoffs-2026-bracket-schedule-dates-championship-nashville/).

## Which states had not posted finals dates on Nov. 6?

Florida, California, Minnesota, Kentucky, Mississippi, Louisiana, Illinois, New Jersey, New York, Arizona, Oklahoma and Virginia were not posted or not readable on an official page on Nov. 6, so no date is printed here for them.

## What is not confirmed yet?

The GHSA page gives the date and stadium, and we could not confirm game times by class, so none are listed. The Alabama and Ohio pages read on Nov. 6 do not name a stadium; the Ohio venue comes from the OHSAA's tournament coverage page.
