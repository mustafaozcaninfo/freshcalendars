## Pangram hint

- **Pangram** (9 letters, starts with A): a spirit distilled from fermented cider, spelled as a fruit followed by a short name; A and P each appear twice and it ends in -jack.

More than half of the list, 20 of 37 words, starts with P, and the *pa* pair alone has 10. Next come *pe* with 8 and *ap* with 6.
