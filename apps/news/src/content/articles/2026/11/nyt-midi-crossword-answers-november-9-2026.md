**The New York Times (NYT) Midi Crossword for Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, is titled "Suggestion Boxes" and has 31 clues on a nine-by-nine grid, with Ian Livengood listed as constructor.** Every clue below carries a hint that stays closed until you tap it. The answers are kept in a marked section at the bottom.

## Where "Suggestion Boxes" shows up in the grid

The official data gives the title and no explanation, so here is only what the grid itself shows. Three Across entries of nine letters fill rows 3, 5 and 7 from edge to edge: clues 11, 14 and 22. Each is two words, a four-letter word followed by a five-letter word. Look at columns 3 through 5 of those rows and you will find the same three letters stacked in each one, so the title may be nudging you to compare them.

Between the theme rows the grid goes quiet. Row 4 has only three white squares, in the middle, so the three long entries cannot lean on each other. Each one is crossed by nine Down entries, which is where most of your letters will come from.

## The clue with the loosest wordplay

Pick 26-Across, "Streaker in space." It has five letters and no question mark, yet "streaker" here has nothing to do with a dash across a stadium: it means anything that leaves a streak of light as it moves. The answer is a visitor from the outer solar system that leaves a bright trail behind it. It sits in row 8, directly under the last theme row.
