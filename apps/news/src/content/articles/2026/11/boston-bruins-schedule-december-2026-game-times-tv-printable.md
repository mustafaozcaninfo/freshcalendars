**The Boston Bruins play 14 games in December 2026, eight of them at TD Garden, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against Colorado and ends Wednesday, Dec. 30, at 7:30 p.m. ET against Edmonton.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Boston, read Nov. 11, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to ET, which is Boston time, and worked out the weekdays. The table puts the New England Sports Network (NESN), national U.S. TV and Canadian TV side by side. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Bruins December 2026 game times and TV channels

NESN is the home-market channel the feed lists for Boston. Turner Network Television (TNT), truTV and HBO Max carry the Dec. 1 game nationally in the U.S., and TNT and HBO Max the Dec. 15 game. Sportsnet, Sportsnet One and Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports are the Canadian networks the feed lists, and Prime Video carries the Dec. 30 game in Canada. "None listed" means the feed shows no Canadian broadcaster.

## How many Bruins games are at TD Garden in December 2026?

Eight: Dec. 1, 5, 12, 15, 17, 20, 26 and 30. The six road games are at Florida (Dec. 8), Tampa Bay (Dec. 10), Carolina (Dec. 13), New Jersey (Dec. 19 and Dec. 27) and the Islanders (Dec. 22). Boston's last game of the month, the Oilers on Dec. 30, is at home.

New Jersey is the one opponent Boston plays three times: at home on Saturday, Dec. 12, and at Prudential Center on Dec. 19 and Dec. 27. The Islanders and Florida each appear twice, one home and one away. Another NHL team's month is in our [Canadiens December schedule](/news/montreal-canadiens-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## Afternoon starts and back-to-back days

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Afternoon starts:** Sat., Dec. 5, vs. Detroit; Sat., Dec. 12, vs. New Jersey; Sun., Dec. 13, at Carolina; and Sun., Dec. 20, vs. Philadelphia, all at 1 p.m. ET; plus Sat., Dec. 19, at New Jersey, at noon ET.
- **Back-to-backs:** Dec. 12 at home and Dec. 13 at Carolina; Dec. 19 at New Jersey and Dec. 20 at home; Dec. 26 at home and Dec. 27 at New Jersey.
- **No game:** Dec. 23, 24, 25 and 31.
