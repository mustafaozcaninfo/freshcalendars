The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) posted 86 toy recalls from Jan. 1 to Nov. 12, 2026, covering about 2.42 million units, in the recall data it publishes on SaferProducts.gov. The count is ours: every CPSC recall notice whose title names a toy, read on Nov. 16, 2026. The full list is below, newest first, with the units, hazard, remedy and recall number for each, and a printable toy-box checklist is at the end. With Christmas gifts going on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and into [the gift list planner](/news/christmas-gift-list-budget-planner-2026/), the list is a way to check a toy before it is wrapped.

## Which toys did CPSC recall in November 2026?

Four recalls were posted from Nov. 1 through Nov. 12. All four were sold online, according to CPSC's notices.

| Date | Product (CPSC recall page) | Hazard | Units | Remedy | Recall no. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 12 | [Kakgold 4WD Stunt Vehicle Toys](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Kakgold-Recalls-4WD-Remote-Controlled-Car-Toys-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Battery-Ingestion-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Toys) | Battery ingestion | About 294 | Refund | 27-085 |
| Nov. 12 | [Baby Rattle and Teether Toy Sets](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/SHEIN-Distribution-Corporation-Recalls-Baby-Rattle-and-Teether-Toy-Sets-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Choking-Violate-Mandatory-Small-Parts-Ban) | Choking or small parts | About 2,657 | Refund | 27-083 |
| Nov. 5 | [Arlopu Slide Playsets](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Arlopu-Recalls-Slide-Playsets-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-and-Death-from-Head-and-Neck-Entrapment-Hazards) | Entrapment | About 10,318 | Repair | 27-066 |
| Nov. 5 | [Magnetopia Magnetic Building Cubes and Blocks](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Magnetopia-Recalls-Magnetic-Building-Cubes-and-Blocks-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Magnet-Ingestion-Hazard) | Magnet ingestion | About 7,600 | Refund | 27-076 |

The Nov. 12 Shein notice covers baby rattle and teether toy sets sold on US.Shein.com. The Kakgold remote-control cars were sold on Amazon.com from July through September 2026. The Arlopu slide playsets were sold on Amazon.com and Walmart.com, and CPSC's notice says "Arlopu has received one report of an entrapment incident involving these playsets, resulting in abrasions on a child's neck." The Magnetopia cubes were sold on Magnetopia.com.

## How to check a toy against the CPSC list

1. Find the toy's recall number or name in the table above, or search CPSC's [Recalls page](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls).
2. Open the recall page linked in the first column and compare the product, model and sale dates.
3. Follow the remedy on that page. The table shows which remedy CPSC lists for each recall.
4. If you believe you have found an unsafe product, CPSC says to "report it to CPSC on SaferProducts.gov."
5. To keep up, CPSC says: "Never miss a recall – subscribe for recall emails." If the recall search has problems, CPSC lists its Consumer Hotline at info@cpsc.gov or 800-638-2772.
