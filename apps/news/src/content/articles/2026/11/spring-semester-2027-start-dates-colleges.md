Spring 2027 classes begin on Monday, Jan. 11 at 12 of the 24 big universities whose registrar calendars we read on Nov. 4, 2026: Ohio State, the University of Texas (UT) at Austin, Florida, Penn State, Arizona State University (ASU), Kentucky, the University of Southern California (USC), NC State, Utah, the University of Georgia (UGA), Louisiana State University (LSU) and Indiana Bloomington. Seven more start Tuesday, Jan. 19, the day after Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) Day on Monday, Jan. 18.

Florida State University (FSU) and the University of North Carolina (UNC) at Chapel Hill start earliest, on Wednesday, Jan. 6. The University of Maryland, College Park starts latest, on Wednesday, Jan. 27. Every date below is 2027, and every registrar warns that calendars can change.

Notes on the table: Arizona State lists Jan. 11 for Session C, and its add date is Jan. 17 (drop Jan. 24). Penn State's regular add date is Jan. 17 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time (ET). The University of Texas lists Jan. 19 for undergraduate adds. The University of Georgia and Florida list drop/add windows ending Jan. 15 (Florida's runs Jan. 11 to 15). UNC lists late registration ending Jan. 12, and its break runs from Friday, Mar. 5 at 5 p.m. until classes resume Mar. 15. The University of Arizona's page shows a regular session from Wed., Jan. 13 to May 5, and no other dates. Indiana's registration bulletin gives only the first day, Jan. 11.

## When do spring 2027 finals end?

Among the schools that list the end of finals, the earliest end date is Apr. 30 (Florida, FSU and Colorado Boulder) and the latest is May 20 (Maryland). Wisconsin-Madison lists its spring recess as Mar. 20 to 28, the latest start of any break in the table. Our companion story, [college spring break 2027 dates at universities](/news/college-spring-break-2027-dates-universities/), covers the breaks in full.

## Which universities are not in the table?

The calendars for Texas A&M, Michigan, Michigan State, Purdue, the University of Central Florida (UCF), New York University (NYU), Texas Tech, Georgia Tech and Alabama were not readable when we checked on Nov. 4, 2026. Check the registrar for those. The University of Houston is also left out until we confirm its page.
