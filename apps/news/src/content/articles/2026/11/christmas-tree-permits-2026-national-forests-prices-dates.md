## Free Christmas tree permits for fourth graders

Four forests in the table tie a free tree to the fourth-grade Every Kid Outdoors pass. The [Every Kid Outdoors](https://www.everykidoutdoors.gov/) site describes the free pass for fourth graders and says nothing about trees, so the tree rule below belongs to each forest.

- **White River:** fourth graders with a valid pass "can recieve a free Holiday Tree either online through Recreation.gov or through our local District Offices," the listing says. The pass "does not waive the Recreation.gov purchase processing fee," and the listing does not give the amount.
- **Arapaho and Roosevelt:** fourth graders with a pass qualify for a free permit through Recreation.gov "with a $2.50 reservation fee," according to the forest. The Elk Creek area "will not be open for drive-in tree cutting this coming season."
- **Bridger-Teton:** "Present your pass or voucher to receive a permit and tag for one free Christmas tree," the listing says. It gives no 2026 dates.
- **Mount Hood, 2025 season:** the page last updated Dec. 31, 2025 said fourth graders "must have their Every Kid Outdoors pass or voucher with them to receive the permit," and that those permits "are not available from local vendors." It also listed permits at "$5/permit/tree" and a Recreation.gov service fee of $2.50. The forest had not posted 2026 terms.

## Cutting rules the forests print

Rules differ by forest, so the ones below are credited to the page that states them.

- **Arapaho and Roosevelt:** cut the tree "six inches or less from the ground." The trunk must be six inches or less in diameter, "strictly enforced." No cutting within 75 feet of any road or trail. Permits must be visible on the tree or on your dash.
- **Pike and San Isabel:** trees up to 15 feet tall or 6 inches in diameter at ground level.
- **Mount Hood, 2025 season:** a stump "no more than 6 inches from the ground," no cutting within 100 feet of any highway, and the online permit and cutting guidelines printed and on your person. The annual limit was 5 trees per household.
- **Eldorado:** one tree per permit, and permits are valid Nov. 9 to Dec. 31, 2026.
- **Tahoe:** the listing says "The 2026 cutting areas have changed for Truckee and American River Ranger Districts," and asks buyers to review the maps closely.

## What the forests have not posted for 2026

- **Prices:** Arapaho and Roosevelt, Coconino, Kaibab, Pike and San Isabel, Tahoe and Bridger-Teton do not show a 2026 price on the pages we read.
- **Cutting dates:** Kaibab, Tahoe and Bridger-Teton show none.
- **Mount Hood:** the 2026 season, price and sale date.

Listings can change, so confirm the forest's own page before you buy.
