**The Tampa Bay Buccaneers visit the Chicago Bears on Sunday Night Football (SNF) on Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, with kickoff at 8:20 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on NBC, according to the NFL.com Week 9 schedule.** The game is at Soldier Field, and the Bears list kickoff as 7:20 p.m. Central Time (CT). It is Week 9 of the National Football League (NFL) season. Clocks already fell back on Nov. 1, so 8:20 p.m. ET is Eastern Standard Time (EST).

The Buccaneers are 3-5 and the Bears are 6-2, per the NFL.com standings and the Buccaneers game center.

## What time is Buccaneers at Bears in my time zone?

Kickoff is 8:20 p.m. EST. The other rows are our conversion from that time; the UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in November.

## What channel is Buccaneers vs. Bears on?

NBC. The NFL.com Week 9 schedule lists "Buccaneers at Bears, Sunday, November 8th, 8:20 PM, NBC," and Buccaneers.com says the game "will air locally on NBC." The NFL's injury page lists the Spanish-language broadcast as NBC/Universo.

| Option | What the source says | Source |

| NBC | TV broadcast of the game | NFL.com, Buccaneers.com |
| NBC/Universo | Spanish-language | NFL.com injury page |

| Peacock | "Subscribe to Peacock to stream all 18 Sunday Night Football games" | NBC Sports |

The Peacock line is NBC Sports' season-level wording; neither club's page mentions Peacock for this game, so check Peacock on Sunday. The NBC booth is Mike Tirico (play-by-play), Chris Collinsworth (analyst) and Melissa Stark (sideline reporter), per Buccaneers.com.

For radio, the Bears flagship is ESPN Chicago (1000 AM, 100.3 FM HD2). The Buccaneers flagship is 97.9 FM (98ROCK) in Tampa Bay and Sarasota.

## When did the Buccaneers and Bears last meet?

The Buccaneers.com matchup history lists the most recent meeting as 2023, a 27-17 Buccaneers win in Tampa. We did not find a 2024 or 2025 meeting in the sources we read, and the exact 2023 date is not confirmed here.

## Who is on the Buccaneers and Bears injury report?

The NFL's page listed three Buccaneers as not participating in practice on Nov. 4: linebacker Yaya Diaby, wide receiver Emeka Egbuka and defensive tackle Elijah Roberts. It listed five Bears with the same status: Theo Benedet, T.J. Edwards, Grady Jarrett, Kyle Monangai and Zavier Scott; the page does not print a date for the Bears list. Reports change through the week, so check the final Friday report on NFL.com.
