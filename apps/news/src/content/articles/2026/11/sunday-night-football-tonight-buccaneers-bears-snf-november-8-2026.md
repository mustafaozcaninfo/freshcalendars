**The Chicago Bears beat the Tampa Bay Buccaneers 27-24 on Sunday Night Football (SNF) on Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, according to the NFL.com Week 9 page.** Kickoff was at 8:20 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on NBC. The game was at Soldier Field, and the Bears listed kickoff as 7:20 p.m. Central Time (CT). It was Week 9 of the National Football League (NFL) season. Clocks had fallen back on Nov. 1, so 8:20 p.m. ET was Eastern Standard Time (EST).

Going in, the Buccaneers were 3-5 and the Bears 6-2, per the NFL.com standings and the Buccaneers game center. NFL.com lists them at 3-6 and 7-2 after the game.

## What channel was Buccaneers vs. Bears on?

NBC. The NFL.com Week 9 schedule lists "Buccaneers at Bears, Sunday, November 8th, 8:20 PM, NBC," and Buccaneers.com says the game "will air locally on NBC." The NFL's injury page lists the Spanish-language broadcast as NBC/Universo.

## When did the Buccaneers and Bears last meet?

## Who is on the Buccaneers and Bears injury report?

The NFL's page listed three Buccaneers as not participating in practice on Nov. 4: linebacker Yaya Diaby, wide receiver Emeka Egbuka and defensive tackle Elijah Roberts. It listed five Bears with the same status: Theo Benedet, T.J. Edwards, Grady Jarrett, Kyle Monangai and Zavier Scott; the page does not print a date for the Bears list.
