**Columbus Day falls on Monday, Oct. 11, 2027, the only federal holiday in October, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** The month starts on a Friday and ends on a Sunday. Day counts below run from Monday, Nov. 23, 2026.

## October 2027 calendar grid and the dates on it

October 2027 has 31 days, so it holds five Fridays, five Saturdays and five Sundays and four of every other weekday. On a Sunday-start grid the first row holds only Friday, Oct. 1 and Saturday, Oct. 2, and the sixth row is Sunday, Oct. 31 alone.

To count from another day, add the days left in the current month to the days in each full month before your date. From Nov. 23 there are 7 days left in November, then 31, 31, 28, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31, 31 and 30 days in December through September, so Oct. 1 is 7 + 31 + 31 + 28 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 31 + 30 + 1 = 312 days away.

## Which holidays fall in October 2027?

Columbus Day is the only federal holiday. OPM's list shows "Monday, October 11" for Columbus Day. Our [2027 federal holidays list](/news/federal-holidays-2027-observed-dates-list/) has the rest of the year. The month before is in [September 2027 important dates](/news/september-2027-important-dates-holidays-days-until/).

Leif Erikson Day is Saturday, Oct. 9. Federal law says "The President may issue each year a proclamation designating October 9 as Leif Erikson Day," so the 2027 proclamation is not yet issued and the day is not a day off. Halloween is Sunday, Oct. 31, and our [Halloween page](/when-is/halloween/) has the date in other years.

## When are the Jewish holidays in October 2027?

All four fall in October, and Yom Kippur ends on Columbus Day. Hebcal lists these dates; each holiday starts at sundown the evening before the day it names.

| Holiday | Begins | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| Rosh Hashanah | Sundown, Friday, Oct. 1 | Nightfall, Sunday, Oct. 3 |
| Yom Kippur | Sundown, Sunday, Oct. 10 | Nightfall, Monday, Oct. 11 |
| Sukkot | Sundown, Friday, Oct. 15 | Nightfall, Friday, Oct. 22 |
| Simchat Torah | Sundown, Saturday, Oct. 23 | Nightfall, Sunday, Oct. 24 |

The full list for the year is in our [2027 Jewish holidays guide](/news/jewish-holidays-2027-dates-passover-rosh-hashanah-yom-kippur-hanukkah/).

## Moon phase times in Eastern Time for October 2027

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes times in Universal Time (UT). Daylight saving time ends on Sunday, Nov. 7, 2027, the "first Sunday of November" in federal law (15 U.S.C. 260a), so New York is four hours behind UT on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) all of October. Our [2027 Moon phase calendar](/news/moon-phases-2027-full-moon-calendar/) has the other months.

## Are there eclipses or meteor showers in October 2027?

No eclipse. NASA lists the 2027 solar eclipses on Feb. 6 (annular) and Aug. 2 (total) and the penumbral lunar eclipses on Feb. 20, July 18 and Aug. 17, with nothing in October. See the [2027 eclipse overview](/news/eclipses-2027-dates-times-total-solar-eclipse-august-2/) for the year.

The American Meteor Society (AMS) lists the Orionids as active from Oct. 2 to Nov. 7, with the peak on the night of Oct. 21-22, when the Moon is about 67% lit and waning gibbous. Our [2027 meteor shower ranking](/news/meteor-showers-2027-calendar-peak-dates-moon-phase-best-nights/) compares it with the other showers.

## UK and India dates in October 2027

GOV.UK lists no bank holiday in October 2027 in England and Wales. The Government of India has not yet published its 2027 holiday list, so Diwali 2027 is not given here.
