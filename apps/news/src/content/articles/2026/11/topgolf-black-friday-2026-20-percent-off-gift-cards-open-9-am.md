**Topgolf takes 20% off gift cards bought online from Nov. 27 to Nov. 30, 2026, up to $200 off per person, and opens its venues at 9 a.m. on Black Friday, Nov. 27, the company said in a Nov. 19 press release.** Topgolf will also be open on Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26, from 3PM to 10PM. The release gives no Black Friday closing time.

## Topgolf holiday dates, Nov. 23 to Dec. 31, 2026

Every date below comes from Topgolf's [Nov. 19, 2026 press release](https://press.topgolf.com/2026-11-19-TOPGOLF-UNWRAPS-FORE-THE-HOLIDAYS-WITH-FESTIVE-POP-UPS,-THANKSGIVING-TRADITIONS,-AND-UNBEATABLE-PARTY-PERKS). Weekdays are ours.

| Date | Offer or hours | Where it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 23-26 | "Giving Thanks for Shanks" (Shanksgiving), with an online Shanksgiving Giveaway | Online and all venues |
| Nov. 23-29 | $100 off any event package for 7+ guests hosted during Thanksgiving week, code THANKFUL100 | All venues, booked online or through a local event sales team |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving Day: open 3PM-10PM | All venues, local time |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Black Friday Doorbuster: venues open at 9 a.m. | Venues; local pricing and availability at topgolf.com |
| Nov. 27-30 | 20% off Topgolf Gift Cards, max $200 off per person | Online only |
| Mondays, Nov. 30 to Dec. 28 | Merry Mondays: half-off golf for a holiday party hosted that day | Event bookings |
| Dec. 1-25 | One-hour game play bonus card with a gift card order of $75 or more | Online or in-venue |
| Thu., Dec. 24 | Christmas Eve: open until 5PM | Venues, local time |
| Fri., Dec. 25 | Christmas Day: open 3PM-10PM | Venues, local time |
| Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 | $15 Game Play Bonus Card for every event attendee, valid through Mar. 31, 2027 | Events hosted in that window |

The release says to check topgolf.com/holidays or the Topgolf app for local hours on Thanksgiving Day and through the holiday season.

## What time does the Topgolf gift card sale start and end?

The terms give the window in Pacific Time (PT): "from November 27, 2026 at 12:00am PT through November 30, 2026 at 11:59pm PT." The conversions to Mountain Time (MT), Central Time (CT) and Eastern Time (ET) are ours.

| Zone | Starts | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| PT | Fri., Nov. 27, 12:00 a.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 11:59 p.m. |
| MT | Fri., Nov. 27, 1:00 a.m. | Tue., Dec. 1, 12:59 a.m. |
| CT | Fri., Nov. 27, 2:00 a.m. | Tue., Dec. 1, 1:59 a.m. |
| ET | Fri., Nov. 27, 3:00 a.m. | Tue., Dec. 1, 2:59 a.m. |

Eastern shoppers therefore have until the early hours of Dec. 1.

## How does the 20% off gift card deal work?

- The discount applies automatically at checkout; no promo code is needed.
- Cards must be bought online only at topgolf.cashstar.com. It is not valid for gift cards bought in-venue, on bulk orders or from third-party websites or retailers.
- The discount covers gift card value only, not shipping or handling fees.
- "Maximum discount of $200 per person across all transactions during the promotion period." Orders may include multiple gift cards.
- The cards are not for resale, and the offer cannot be combined with another coupon, offer or promotion. Topgolf may modify or end the offer without notice.

## Is Topgolf open on Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Christmas?

Yes on all three. The release says: "Topgolf will be open to guests on Thanksgiving Day from 3PM – 10PM." Black Friday venues "open early at 9 a.m., with our lowest prices ever," and the release points to topgolf.com for local pricing, booking details and availability. It lists Christmas Eve as open until 5PM and Christmas Day as 3PM to 10PM, and the bonus card terms add that venues "may be closed or open limited hours on 12/25/2026".

The release does not name a time zone, so the hours are each venue's local time. Other chains' Thanksgiving hours are in [restaurants open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/restaurants-open-thanksgiving-2026-hours-by-chain/).

## What is the Topgolf December gift card bonus?

For a single order of $75 or more in U.S. Topgolf Gift Cards placed from 12:00am PT on Dec. 1 to 11:59pm PT on Dec. 25, 2026, Topgolf gives one bonus card good for one free hour of game play. That window ends at 2:59 a.m. ET on Dec. 26.

- Orders can be online at topgolf.cashstar.com or in-venue at a participating U.S. venue during business hours.
- Limit one bonus card per qualifying order and three per person across all online orders.
- Bonus cards are valid for visits Dec. 26, 2026 through Feb. 28, 2027, and not on Dec. 31, 2026. They cannot be used before Dec. 26.
- The $75 minimum excludes shipping and handling fees. A cancelled, refunded or charged-back order voids the bonus card.

This bonus is separate from the Nov. 27-30 discount, and the terms for both say they are not valid with any other coupon, offer or promotion. ## What else does Topgolf offer this holiday season?

- **Jingle Bell Lounge:** the holiday pop-up opens Nov. 19 and runs through the New Year, according to the release.
- **Merry Mondays:** host a holiday party on any Monday from Nov. 30 to Dec. 28 for half-off golf.
- **$15 Game Play Bonus Card:** each attendee of an event hosted Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 gets one, valid through Mar. 31, 2027, regardless of event size or package.
- **Angry Birds Sweepstakes:** play Angry Birds at Topgolf Nov. 16-30 for a chance at a $250 Topgolf gift card; winners are announced Dec. 1. The rules say one $250 gift card is available per participating venue and entry is free.

## What has Topgolf not said?

- A Black Friday closing time; the release gives the 9 a.m. opening only.
- Game play prices for Black Friday; the release says only "our lowest prices ever" and points to topgolf.com for local pricing.
- An end date for the THANKFUL100 code beyond "Thanksgiving week" (Nov. 23-29 in the release).
