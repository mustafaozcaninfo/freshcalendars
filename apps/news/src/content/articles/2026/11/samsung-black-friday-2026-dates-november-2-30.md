**Samsung Black Friday 2026 runs from Nov. 2 to Nov. 30, 2026 on Samsung.com, according to Samsung Electronics America's Black Friday page, which we read on Nov. 18.** That is 29 days, from a Monday to Cyber Monday. The page lists seven savings lines and no prices, and it does not mention a separate Cyber Monday sale. The tables below count the days left and group the savings by product.

## How many days are left in Samsung's Black Friday sale?

Samsung's page says the sale "runs from November 2 to November 30, 2026 on Samsung.com." The weekdays, holiday names and day counts are our computation; the window is Samsung's. The count includes the day itself.

| Date | Weekday | What it is | Days left, counting that day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 2 | Mon. | Sale starts, per Samsung | 29 |
| Nov. 18 | Wed. | Day this page was read; third week of the sale | 13 |
| Nov. 26 | Thu. | Thanksgiving | 5 |
| Nov. 27 | Fri. | Black Friday | 4 |
| Nov. 30 | Mon. | Cyber Monday; last day of the window | 1 |

Put the dates on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html), and see the dates of [Black Friday](/when-is/black-friday/) and [Cyber Monday](/when-is/cyber-monday/).

## What are the seven Samsung Black Friday savings lines?

Samsung's page gives savings amounts, not prices. The wording below is Samsung's; the TV names use the display terms quantum dot light-emitting diode (QLED) and light-emitting diode (LED).

| Type | Product | What the page says |
|---|---|---|
| Phones, tablets, wearables | [Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra](https://www.samsung.com/us/offer/black-friday) | "Get up to $995 in total savings." |
| Phones, tablets, wearables | [Galaxy Watch Ultra2](https://www.samsung.com/us/offer/black-friday) | "Save up to $250 instant trade-in credit or $100 credit without trade-in." |
| Phones, tablets, wearables | [Galaxy S26 Ultra + Galaxy Buds4 Pro bundle](https://www.samsung.com/us/offer/black-friday) | "Bundle and save $174.99" |
| TVs and audio | [Neo QLED TV](https://www.samsung.com/us/offer/black-friday) | "Save up to $1,200" |
| TVs and audio | [Mini LED TV](https://www.samsung.com/us/offer/black-friday) | "Get up to $300 off" |
| TVs and audio | [The Frame Pro + Q-Series Soundbar](https://www.samsung.com/us/offer/black-friday) | "Save up to $300 when you bundle The Frame Pro and Q-series Soundbar" |
| Appliances | [Bespoke appliances](https://www.samsung.com/us/offer/black-friday) | "Save an additional $100 when you buy 2 or more Bespoke products" |

Five of the seven lines say "up to," so the amount you get depends on the model and, for the watch, on whether you trade in a device. The page gives the event window, not an end date for each offer, so offers may change. Check the product page before you order.

## What has Samsung not announced for Black Friday 2026?

- A separate Cyber Monday sale, or any deal after Nov. 30.
- Dates for a Discover Samsung event.
- Sale prices or regular prices.
- Whether offers change from week to week.
- Member-only or early-access dates.
- Samsung Experience Store hours on Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, or Black Friday, Nov. 27.
