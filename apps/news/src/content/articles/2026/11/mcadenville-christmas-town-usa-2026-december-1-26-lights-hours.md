**The Town of McAdenville says its Christmas Town U.S.A. lights run Dec. 1-26, 2026, from 5:30 p.m. to 10 p.m. ET each night**, with the tree lighting at Dynamo 31 on Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 5:30 p.m. The town's page says visitors can walk or drive through downtown. Pair the dates with the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

The town traces the tradition of the lights to 1956, when the McAdenville Men's Club and W. J. Pharr started it. Its Yule Log Parade began in 1949.

## What are the McAdenville Christmas Town hours in 2026?

The town's page says "Green, red and white lights will shine from 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM each night." The season is Dec. 1-26, 2026, so there are 26 nights.

## When is the McAdenville tree lighting in 2026?

The tree lighting is Dec. 1, 2026, a Tuesday. The town invites visitors to "join us at Dynamo 31, 102 Main Street in McAdenville at 5:30pm to celebrate." That is the same time the nightly lights begin.

## Is there a fee, and where do you park?

The town's Christmas Town page does not list a fee or a ticket. It links a "2026 Trip Planner" PDF, [Planning a Trip to Christmas Town 2026](https://townofmcadenville.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Planning-A-Trip-to-Christmas-Town-2026.pdf), which we could not read; use it for parking and routes. The page says you can drive through town or park and walk with family and friends.
