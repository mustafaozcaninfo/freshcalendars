**Tyson Fury vs. Anthony Joshua is Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, at Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales, and streams live on Netflix, according to Netflix Tudum.** Matchroom Boxing lists the same date and puts Walker vs Rocha on Dec. 5 and Dmitry Bivol vs Callum Smith on Dec. 12; Queensberry lists Cacace vs Ball on Dec. 19, and Most Valuable Promotions (MVP) lists Jake Paul vs Cédric Doumbé on Dec. 24. None of the pages we read posts a confirmed start time yet, so times below are conversions you can use once one appears, in Eastern Time (ET) and Pacific Time (PT).

## When is Fury vs Joshua, and how do you watch it?

Netflix Tudum gives "Friday, Dec. 11, 2026" at "Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales" and says the event will stream live on Netflix globally. Members pay nothing extra. Tudum lists Deontay Wilder vs Jai Opetaia, Martin Bakole vs Solomon Adebayo and Da'Mazion Vanhouter vs Michael Essomba on the card, says the start time will follow on its page, and posts no ring-walk time. Matchroom's event page shows "Friday 11 December 2026". We give no odds or predictions.

## When are Bivol vs Smith and the Dec. 12 cards?

Matchroom's page for Bivol vs Callum Smith shows "Saturday 12 December 2026" and calls it the Unified World Light Heavyweight Championship, in Yekaterinburg, Russia. Matchroom's Monte Carlo Showdown page shows Soro vs Liddard on the same date at Salle des Étoiles in Monte Carlo, Monaco. Neither page states a broadcaster or a start time.

## What are the Diaz vs Chaves, Walker vs Rocha, Cacace vs Ball and Paul vs Doumbé dates?

Walker vs Rocha is "Saturday 05 December 2026" on Matchroom's page, and Matchroom's events list gives Civic Hall in Wolverhampton, UK. Diaz vs Chaves is "Thursday 10 December 2026" at Caribe Royale in Orlando, Florida, per Matchroom. Queensberry's events page lists Anthony Cacace vs Nick Ball for "Saturday, 19 December 2026" at the O2 Arena in Belfast, as a WBA super-featherweight championship.

MVP's event page lists Jake Paul vs Cédric Doumbé on "Thu - Dec 24, 2026" at Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai, with Chantelle Cameron vs Mikaela Mayer, Alistair Overeem vs Jimi Manuwa and Jahmal Harvey vs Craig Morgan on the card. The page shows both 4:00 AM and 9:00 AM for that date without naming a time zone, so we print no time. No broadcaster is named on the pages we read.

## What do UK and Russian start times mean in ET and PT?

The United Kingdom, including Cardiff and Belfast, is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in December, which is ET plus five hours. Yekaterinburg is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus five, so it is ET plus ten hours. The table converts example local start times; they are not announced times.

| If the card starts at | Where | ET | PT |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 p.m. local | UK (GMT) | 2 p.m. | 11 a.m. |
| 9 p.m. local | UK (GMT) | 4 p.m. | 1 p.m. |
| 7 p.m. local | Yekaterinburg (UTC+5) | 9 a.m. | 6 a.m. |

Fury vs Joshua has no confirmed time. Neither Netflix nor Matchroom has posted one, so we print none.
