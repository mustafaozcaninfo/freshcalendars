**Tyson Fury vs. Anthony Joshua is Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, at Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales, and streams live on Netflix, according to Netflix Tudum.** Matchroom Boxing lists the same date and puts Walker vs Rocha on Dec. 5 and Dmitry Bivol vs Smith on Dec. 12; Queensberry lists Cacace vs Ball on Dec. 19. None of the pages we read posts a start time yet, so times below are conversions you can use once one appears, in Eastern Time (ET) and Pacific Time (PT).

## When is Fury vs Joshua, and how do you watch it?

Netflix Tudum gives "Friday, Dec. 11, 2026" at "Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales" and says the event will stream live on Netflix globally. Members pay nothing extra. Tudum says the full fight card and start time will follow on its page, and no ring-walk time is posted. Matchroom's event page shows "Friday 11 December 2026". We give no odds or predictions.

## When are Bivol vs Smith and the Dec. 12 cards?

Matchroom's page for Bivol vs Smith shows "Saturday 12 December 2026" and calls it the Unified World Light Heavyweight Championship, in Yekaterinburg, Russia. Matchroom's Monte Carlo Showdown page shows Soro vs Liddard on the same date at Salle des Étoiles in Monte Carlo, Monaco. Neither page states a broadcaster or a start time.

## What are the Walker vs Rocha and Cacace vs Ball dates?

Walker vs Rocha is "Saturday 05 December 2026" on Matchroom's page, and Matchroom's events list gives Civic Hall in Wolverhampton, UK. Queensberry's events page lists Anthony Cacace vs Nick Ball for "Saturday, 19 December 2026" as a WBA super-featherweight championship. We could not load Queensberry's page when we wrote this, so we give only the city, Belfast, and no venue; confirm both on Queensberry's page.

## What do UK and Russian start times mean in ET and PT?

The United Kingdom, including Cardiff and Belfast, is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in December, which is ET plus five hours. Yekaterinburg is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus five, so it is ET plus ten hours. The table converts example local start times; they are not announced times.

| If the card starts at | Where | ET | PT |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 p.m. local | UK (GMT) | 2 p.m. | 11 a.m. |
| 9 p.m. local | UK (GMT) | 4 p.m. | 1 p.m. |
| 7 p.m. local | Yekaterinburg (UTC+5) | 9 a.m. | 6 a.m. |

Fury vs Joshua has no official time. Neither Netflix nor Matchroom has posted one, so we print none.
