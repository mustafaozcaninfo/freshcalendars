## The forced first move on each board

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left. A search of every legal placement against the saved data finds eight arrangements for Easy, 40 for Medium and one for Hard.

**Easy.** R2C4 is less than 1, so it takes a 0. The four cells R1C1, R1C2, R2C1 and R3C1 add up to 1, and the only 1 in the set rides on 1-5. A 1 at R1C1 or R2C1 would have to bring its 5 into the same region, so those two cells take 0s. All eight arrangements agree on that.

**Medium.** R1C2 touches only R1C3 and R2C2, and each of those sits in a pair that must total more than 9. With 6 as the top value, every cell in such a pair holds at least 4, so R1C2's domino needs two halves of 4 or more. Only 6-5 qualifies, and in all 40 arrangements it has R1C2 as one end.

**Hard.** R5C1 adds up to 0, and R6C1 adds up to 6. The set has no 6-0, so R6C1 cannot pair upward and its domino runs sideways through R6C2. Only 2-6 and 6-3 hold a 6, and the search gives R6C1 the 6-3 domino, with a 3 at R6C2. R6C5 then takes the other 6.

## Pips hints for Nov. 30, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 2-0, 0-0, 6-0, 0-3, 1-5)**

- Hint 1: R2C4 is less than 1, so a 0 goes there.
- Hint 2: R3C2 is more than 2, and only the 3 in 0-3, the 5 in 1-5 and the 6 in 6-0 are high enough.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 6-5, 5-1, 5-0, 0-3, 2-1, 4-0, 1-1)**

- Hint 1: The cells in each pair that totals more than 9 hold at least 4 apiece.
- Hint 2: R3C3 and R3C4 add up to 1, so one takes a 0 and the other a 1.

**Hard (15 dominoes on a frame 6 rows by 5 columns)**

- Hint 1: R5C1 adds up to 0, which leaves R6C1, a 6, one neighbor to pair with.
- Hint 2: R1C1 and R1C2 total more than 9, so both hold at least 4, and the 5-5 double would suit that pair.

## Which Pips dominoes can be turned around on Nov. 30

Easy has no domino that swaps ends without breaking a rule, even though the board has eight arrangements, which come from larger shifts across the 1-5, 0-3, 2-0 and 6-0 dominoes. Medium has two that turn alone: 6-5 and 4-0, and both keep every region satisfied. Hard allows no turn at all, because it has a single arrangement.
