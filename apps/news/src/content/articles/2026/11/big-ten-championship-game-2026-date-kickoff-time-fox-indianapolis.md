**The 2026 Discover Big Ten Football Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis, with an 8 p.m. ET kickoff on FOX, according to the Big Ten.** The conference's game page says tickets are "on sale now on Ticketmaster" and lists no prices. The Indiana Sports Corp calls it the 16th annual title game.

The matchup is not set. The Big Ten says "the two teams with the best records in regular-season conference play will qualify for the championship game," with tiebreakers applied if necessary.

## What time does the Big Ten title game start where you live?

Computed from the 8 p.m. ET kickoff the Big Ten lists:

| Where | Kickoff |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET), Indianapolis | 8 p.m., Sat., Dec. 5 |
| Central (CT) | 7 p.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 6 p.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | 5 p.m. |
| Alaska | 4 p.m. |
| Hawaii | 3 p.m. |
| United Kingdom (GMT) | 1 a.m., Sun., Dec. 6 |

## Where do you watch the Big Ten Championship Game?

FOX is the official broadcast partner of the game, per the Big Ten, and the Big Ten page says it will air live on FOX at 8 p.m. ET. FOX Sports lists the game as "8 p.m. ET (FOX, FOX One)," so it also streams on FOX One.

## What did the 2025 Big Ten title game look like?

In 2025, Indiana beat Ohio State 13-10 on Sat., Dec. 6 at Lucas Oil Stadium, in what NBC News (Dec. 7, 2025) called Indiana's first Big Ten title since 1967. Sports Illustrated listed that game's kickoff as 8 p.m. ET on FOX. That is last year's result; the 2026 teams are not yet decided.

## How does the Big Ten title game fit into the College Football Playoff?

The College Football Playoff (CFP) takes conference champions from the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC), Big Ten, Big 12 and Southeastern Conference (SEC) automatically, according to the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) on NCAA.com. ESPN's press room lists the first round for Dec. 18-19 and the national championship for Jan. 25, 2027, in Las Vegas.

The SEC title game is also Saturday, Dec. 5, at 4 p.m. ET on ABC, ESPN's press room announced on June 10, 2026. The Big Ten game is the last of the day; the [conference championship schedule](/news/college-football-conference-championship-games-2026-schedule-times-tv/) and the [SEC title game story](/news/sec-championship-game-2026-date-kickoff-time-tv-atlanta/) have the rest, and the [ACC game](/news/acc-championship-game-2026-noon-et-abc-charlotte/) has its own page.

## How long will Indianapolis host the game?

Indiana Sports Corp said on July 23, 2024, that Indianapolis will host the game at Lucas Oil Stadium through 2028.
