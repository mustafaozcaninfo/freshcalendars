**Scooter's Coffee's holiday menu has been in stores since Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, at all 900+ locations in 32 states, while supplies last, the Omaha, Neb., chain said in an Oct. 29 release.** It has seven drinks and two bakery items. App users could order it early, from Thursday, Oct. 29, to Saturday, Oct. 31, through the Scooter's Coffee mobile app only.

Our [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/) lists one Scooter's item. The table below has all nine, with what is in each. Scooter's gave no prices in the release.

## What is on the Scooter's Coffee holiday menu for 2026?

| Item | What is in it | Forms |
|---|---|---|
| Colada Craze Red Bull Infusion | Red Bull Yellow Edition, pineapple, coconut, sweet cream, coconut cold foam | Iced or blended |
| Sleighcation Pineapple Smoothie | Pineapple puree blended with your choice of base; a Vertigo Smoothie version adds Red Bull Yellow Edition | Not given |
| Butterscotch Caramelicious | The guest-favorite Caramelicious with butterscotch, whipped cream, caramel drizzle, salted toffee pieces | Not given |
| Butterscotch Chai | Butterscotch and chai spice, whipped cream, salted toffee pieces | Hot, iced or blended |
| Candy Cane Chaos Mocha | Espresso, mocha and peppermint, whipped cream, mocha drizzle, peppermint chips | Hot, iced or blended |
| Candy Cane Chaos Hot Chocolate | The same drink without espresso | Not given |
| Santa's Cookie Latte | Espresso, white mocha and cookie butter, whipped cream, green sprinkles | Hot, iced or blended |
| Bah Humbug Butterscotch Muffin | Butterscotch, cinnamon and vanilla, crackly sugar top | Bakery item |
| Stocking Stuffer Sugar Cookies | Bite-size, with red and green sprinkles | Bakery item |

Source: Scooter's Coffee, Oct. 29, 2026. The release says espresso and chai items come hot, iced or blended and Red Bull Infusions come iced or blended. It does not say which drinks are new this year and which are returning, so the table does not label them.

## What is in the Candy Cane Chaos Mocha?

Espresso, mocha and peppermint, topped with whipped cream, mocha drizzle and peppermint chips. The Candy Cane Chaos Hot Chocolate is the same drink without espresso. Either one can be made with sugar-free peppermint and mocha flavorings, Scooter's says, though other ingredients may contain sugar.

## Which Scooter's holiday drinks have no espresso?

The Colada Craze Red Bull Infusion uses Red Bull Yellow Edition with pineapple, coconut, sweet cream and coconut cold foam. The Sleighcation Pineapple Smoothie blends pineapple puree with the base you choose, and a Vertigo Smoothie version with Red Bull Yellow Edition is also offered. The Butterscotch Chai pairs butterscotch with chai spice. For a plain chocolate drink, the Candy Cane Chaos Hot Chocolate leaves out the espresso.

## How long does the Scooter's holiday menu last?

Scooter's gave no end date. Its release says the menu is in stores "Sunday, Nov. 1, at all 900+ locations in 32 states, while supplies last." Check with your store before you plan a trip for late December. ## Is there a Scooter's app coupon for new users?

Yes, with no dates given. New app users get a free medium drink coupon after a first app purchase of $5 or more. The coupon expires 14 days after it is issued and covers regular-priced drinks only, with no modifications or prepackaged drinks. It is limited to one per customer, valid at participating locations and cannot be combined with other offers.
