**Golden Corral held Military Appreciation Night on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, from 4 p.m. to close, with a free "thank you" buffet and beverage for anyone who served in the U.S. military, according to the company's own page, read Nov. 14, 2026.** The page gives no time zone, so hours were local to each restaurant. Pages written for last year show Tuesday, because in 2025 the night fell on Tuesday, Nov. 11. In 2026 it was a Wednesday. The restaurant is one of the chains on our [Veterans Day free meals list](/news/veterans-day-free-meals-discounts/).

The 2025 column comes from Golden Corral's Oct. 24, 2025 release, carried by Franchising.com. It said the night was available at all locations for dine-in only and that military identification was not required to receive the complimentary meal.

## Who gets the free meal at Golden Corral?

Golden Corral's page said any person who had served in the U.S. military qualified, and listed retirees, veterans, active duty, National Guard and Reserves. The offer was a free "thank you" buffet and beverage.

Only the person who served eats free. The page said other guests paid the regular dinner menu pricing. Golden Corral also said there were no take-out meals and no rain checks, so the free dinner had to be eaten in the restaurant on Nov. 11.

## Do you need military ID or proof of service?

The 2026 page did not address identification. Last year's release said military identification was not required. Our [Veterans Day free meals guide](/news/veterans-day-free-meals-discounts/) lists the IDs other chains accept.

## Is every Golden Corral taking part?

The 2025 release said the night was available at all Golden Corral locations. The 2026 page did not say which restaurants took part.

## How long has Military Appreciation Night run?

Golden Corral says the night began in 2001 and that 2026 marked its 26th anniversary. The company says it has served over 6.8 million complimentary meals since then. It also says Golden Corral restaurants and their guests have donated over $21.5 million to the Disabled American Veterans (DAV) organization over the past 25 years.

For 2024, the 2025 release reported more than $1 million raised for local DAV chapters and more than 185,000 complimentary meals on the night. Golden Corral's page, read Nov. 14, gave no 2026 figures and no 2027 date.
