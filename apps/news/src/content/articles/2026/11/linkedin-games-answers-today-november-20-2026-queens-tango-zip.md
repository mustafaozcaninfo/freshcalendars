**LinkedIn's Friday set for Nov. 20, 2026 is Pinpoint #934 and Crossclimb #934, as numbered by Nerdschalk and Try Hard Guides; the solutions are in the spoiler section at the bottom.** Nudges come first.

## Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints for Friday, Nov. 20

- **Pinpoint #934, first hint:** all five clues are words of exactly three letters.
- **Pinpoint #934, second hint:** the clues are not a set of things you can hold or see; they belong together because of how they are written, not what they mean.
- **Pinpoint #934, third hint:** say the five clues in a row, and look for a field of study they all come from.
- **Crossclimb #934, the ladder:** seven words of four letters, so six moves with one letter changed in each.
- **Crossclimb #934, the locked ends:** the clue is an old saying with two blanks, and the top and bottom words fill them in order.
- **Crossclimb #934, the middle:** the first middle word names a state of mind, and the first move changes the first letter.

## How many Pinpoint #934 clues do you need?

Two clues leave many categories open, because two short words can sit in many lists. A third clue narrows the choices, and the fourth and fifth let you test a guess. This is our reading, not a LinkedIn rule. Today the five clues share a kind of shortening, so a guess made after clue three can be checked against clues four and five for free.

## Reading the Crossclimb #934 ladder without the words

Seven words, six moves, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word of the ladder appears in this section.

| Move | Letter position that changes |
|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 1 |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 4 |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 1 |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 3 |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 1 |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 2 |

The first letter changes three times, and positions 2, 3 and 4 change once each. Three of the six moves are at the front of the word.

## How far apart the Crossclimb #934 locked ends sit

The two locked words differ in all four positions, yet the ladder needs six moves. Two positions are changed a second time on the way, and the first position is changed three times in all.

## Starting points for Queens #934, Tango #774, Zip #613 and Mini Sudoku #466

**Queens #934.** Begin with the smallest color region: with only a few cells, each one you can rule out sharpens the rest of the board.

**Tango #774.** Scan for two matching symbols side by side; the cells on both ends must take the other symbol.

**Zip #613.** Look at where the numbered dots sit near an edge, since the path has fewer ways to run there.

**Mini Sudoku #466.** Count how often each digit is already placed, and start with the digit that appears most.
