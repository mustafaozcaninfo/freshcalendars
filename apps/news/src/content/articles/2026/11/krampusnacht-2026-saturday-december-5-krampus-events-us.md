**Krampusnacht 2026 is Saturday, Dec. 5, the eve of St. Nicholas Day on Sunday, Dec. 6.** The University of Wisconsin-Madison College of Letters & Science says that on "the fifth of December, also known as Krampusnacht, Krampus tags along with St. Nicholas." Four U.S. events have posted a 2026 date and start time for that Saturday: a New Orleans parade at 7:30 p.m. Central Time (CT), a Philadelphia festival from 6 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), a Harrisburg event from noon and a trail race in Lyme, New Hampshire, that starts at 7 a.m. A one-page printable lists them with the times in four zones.

## What time is the Krampus parade in New Orleans?

The Krewe of Krampus lists its Krampus NOLAuf parade for "Saturday, December 5, 2026" with a "7:30pm Start" in the Bywater (8:30 p.m. ET), and says it is "completely free to attend and open to the public." The route is posted as a map image on the krewe's page, so look at the map there. One line in the page body also mentions December 6th, so confirm the night on the [krewe's page](https://kreweofkrampus.com/krampus-nolauf-parade) before you go.

## What is on at Fort Mifflin in Philadelphia?

Fort Mifflin, at 6400 Hog Island Rd., calls it "its first-ever Krampusnacht Festival on December 5th only," and its page shows "6PM." PhillyVoice reported on Nov. 6, 2026 that the festival runs 6 p.m. to 10:30 p.m. and that general admission starts at $32.18, including fees. No 2026 date for a Philadelphia Krampuslauf has been posted. The festival is one of several seasonal events in the city; see the [U.S. Christmas market dates](/news/us-christmas-markets-2026-opening-dates-philadelphia-dc-detroit-nyc/) for others.

## When is Pennsylvania Krampusnacht in Harrisburg?

The organizer's vendor form for the '26 Pennsylvania Krampusnacht at the Zembo Shrine, 2801 N 3rd St, lists the date as "December 5th, 2026, 12-8pm," with a rain date of December 6th from 12 p.m. to 6 p.m. The form says "12 and under is free." The adult price is not posted.

## What is the Krampuslauf in Lyme, N.H.?

The Krampuslauf is a trail race at Dartmouth Skiway, 39 Grafton Turnpike Rd, on Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026. UltraSignup lists three starts: the 9-hour race at 7:00 a.m., the 10K at 9:00 a.m. and the 5K at 10:00 a.m. Registration closes Thursday, Dec. 3, at 11:59 p.m. ET. The entry fee is not shown on the page we checked.

## Why is Krampusnacht the night before St. Nicholas Day?

The Austrian National Tourist Office says celebrations "begin on 5 December, the eve of St. Nicholas Day," and that the Krampus tradition "remains alive in many rural areas of Austria." Krampus is described as a companion of St. Nicholas, who "presents well-behaved children with sweets and nuts on 6 December." Its Austria page lists three dates in a row.

| Date in 2026 | Weekday | Tradition in Austria | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dec. 4 | Friday | Barbara branches | Austrian National Tourist Office |
| Dec. 5 | Saturday | Krampus parade | Austrian National Tourist Office |
| Dec. 6 | Sunday | St. Nicholas | Austrian National Tourist Office |

The Library of Congress notes that St. Nicholas visits children "on the night of December 5 (St. Nicholas Eve) or on December 6 (St. Nicholas's Day)," so the night varies by tradition. For the saint's own date in 2026, see [St. Nicholas Day 2026 and the eve on Dec. 5](/news/saint-nicholas-day-2026-december-6-eve-december-5/).
