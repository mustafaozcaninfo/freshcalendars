## Which Pixel Black Friday end date is right: Dec. 1 or Dec. 5?

Google's page says "Promotions run from November 19, 2026 at 12:00 am PST through December 1, 2026 at 11:59 pm PST." The footnote for each phone says the offer "ends December 5, 2026 at 11:59 PM PST." The Pixel Watch 5, Buds Pro 2 and Buds 2a footnotes say Dec. 1. Because the phone terms are the fine print for each offer, the table uses the footnote dates. The page does not explain the difference.

## What are the terms of Google's Pixel 11 Black Friday offer?

The phone footnotes say the following.

- The deal applies when "Unlocked" is chosen from the "Choose a carrier" menu on the Google Store. Choosing "Google Fi" or any other carrier makes the purchase ineligible for that promotion.
- It is for US residents with US shipping addresses only, and the buyer must be 18 or older.
- It runs "while supplies last and subject to availability."
- Except for eligible phone trade-in, it cannot be combined with promotional codes or other bundle offers.

Google also lists trade-in amounts on the page: "up to $510 back with eligible trade-in" for the Pixel 11, "up to $600 back through eligible trade-in offers" for the Pixel 11 Pro series and "up to $900 back with trade-in" for the Pixel 11 Pro Fold.

The Google Fi footnote has its own terms: "Promotions end 1/05/27 at 9:59am PT, or while supplies last," for new users only, with 24 monthly bill credits after phone activation.

## What did 9to5Google report about Pixel prices?

These figures are reported by 9to5Google, not on the Google page we read. In its Nov. 19, 2026 story, 9to5Google gave sale prices of $699 for the Pixel 11 (from $899), $1,649 for the Pixel 11 Pro Fold (from $1,899), $169 for the Pixel Buds Pro 2 (from $229) and $99 for the Pixel Buds 2a (from $129). It also reported an emailed promo code for an extra $50 off unlocked Pixel 11 phones that "expires on December 1."

## How does this compare with Google's 2025 Black Friday dates?

In 2025, 9to5Google reported that the first set of Google Store deals ended Dec. 2, 2025, with Pixel 10 series deals running until Dec. 6, 2025 at 11:59 p.m. PT. The 2026 phone deadline, Dec. 5, is one day earlier than 2025's, and Black Friday itself also falls one day earlier this year. The Black Friday page we read does not list Christmas shipping cutoffs for the Google Store, a Pixel Tablet deal or a quantity limit beyond "while supplies last."
