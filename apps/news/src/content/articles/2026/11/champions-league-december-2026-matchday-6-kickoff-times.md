**Champions League matchday 6 is Tuesday, Dec. 8, and Wednesday, Dec. 9, 2026, with 18 games at 12:45 p.m. or 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA).** That is 9:45 a.m. or noon Pacific Time (PT). Arsenal host Real Madrid on Dec. 9, and Barcelona host Manchester City on Dec. 8, both at 3 p.m. ET.

UEFA's fixtures page, last updated Nov. 4, 2026, says "Kick-offs are 21:00 unless stated; all times CET." Central European Time (CET) is the only zone it gives, so the table converts every game to ET, PT and Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). December has no daylight saving time in the U.S. or UK, so the gaps are fixed: ET is 6 hours behind CET and GMT is 1 hour behind.

<p class="callout"><b>Two early slots.</b> Four games start at 18:45 CET, which is 12:45 p.m. ET and 9:45 a.m. PT: Viking v Feyenoord and Villarreal v Sabah on Dec. 8, and Real Betis v Como and Slovan Bratislava v Shakhtar Donetsk on Dec. 9. The other 14 are at 21:00 CET, 3 p.m. ET.</p>

## What time are Arsenal vs Real Madrid and Barcelona vs Manchester City?

Arsenal v Real Madrid is Wednesday, Dec. 9, at 21:00 CET: 3 p.m. ET, noon PT and 8pm GMT. Barcelona v Manchester City is Tuesday, Dec. 8, at the same times. Aston Villa v Paris Saint-Germain and Manchester United v Leipzig are also on Dec. 8 at 3 p.m. ET. Paris Saint-Germain also feature in our [Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) Intercontinental Cup 2026 dates in Qatar](/news/fifa-intercontinental-cup-2026-qatar-dates/) story.

## When do the 12:45 p.m. ET games start on Dec. 8 and 9?

The early slot has two games on each day. Viking v Feyenoord and Villarreal v Sabah are on Tuesday, Dec. 8; Real Betis v Como and Slovan Bratislava v Shakhtar Donetsk are on Wednesday, Dec. 9. All four are listed by UEFA at 18:45 CET, and every other game is at 21:00 CET.
