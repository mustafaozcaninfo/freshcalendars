The 12 days of Christmas 2026 start on **Friday, Dec. 25**, when Christmas Day is counted as day one, and day 12 is **Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027**. Counted from Saturday, Dec. 26, day 12 is **Wednesday, Jan. 6**, which is Epiphany on fixed-date calendars. The U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB) lists a feast or memorial for every one of those days, and a table puts both counts next to each feast.

## What are the dates of the 12 days of Christmas in 2026?

The table gives both counts for each date. Weekdays are computed for 2026 and 2027; the feast column quotes the day titles on the USCCB's Daily Bible Readings calendar for [December 2026](https://bible.usccb.org/readings/calendar/202612) and [January 2027](https://bible.usccb.org/readings/calendar/202701).

| Mon., Dec. 28 | Day 4 | Day 3 | Feast of the Holy Innocents, Martyrs |

On Dec. 25 the USCCB calendar lists four Masses: Vigil, Night, Dawn and Day. Dec. 27 is a Sunday in 2026, and the calendar lists the Holy Family that day. ## Do the 12 days of Christmas start on Dec. 25 or Dec. 26?

Both counts are used. Counting Christmas Day as day one ends on Jan. 5. Counting from Dec. 26 ends on Jan. 6. The sources read do not say which count a church must follow, so neither is presented here as the official one.

Merriam-Webster defines Twelfth Night as "the evening or sometimes the eve of Epiphany." With Epiphany on Jan. 6, 2027, that points to the evening of Jan. 5 or of Jan. 6. It defines Twelfth Day as Epiphany, so the Dec. 26 count ends on Twelfth Day.

## When is Epiphany in 2027: Jan. 3 or Jan. 6?

It depends on the church calendar. The USCCB calendar lists The Epiphany of the Lord on Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027, and lists Jan. 6 as "Wednesday after Epiphany." The Lutheran Church-Missouri Synod (LCMS) calendar lists "Epiphany" on "January 6, 2027."

| Calendar | Epiphany 2027 | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| USCCB (U.S. Catholic) | Jan. 3 | Sunday |
| LCMS (Lutheran) | Jan. 6 | Wednesday |

For churches that keep the Julian calendar, see [Orthodox Christmas 2027 and Three Kings Day](/news/orthodox-christmas-2027-january-7-three-kings-day-january-6/). The Catholic feasts for the rest of the year are in the [Catholic liturgical calendar for 2027](/news/catholic-liturgical-calendar-2027-year-b/). For Jan. 1 obligations, see [Holy Days of Obligation 2027](/news/holy-days-of-obligation-2027-us-dates/).

## What comes after day 12 on the U.S. Catholic calendar?

The USCCB calendar lists The Baptism of the Lord on Sunday, Jan. 10, 2027, and Monday of the First Week in Ordinary Time on Monday, Jan. 11. Those days fall outside both counts.

## Is Jan. 6, 2027, a holiday?

On the Puerto Rico Department of Education's 2026-2027 calendar, Jan. 6, 2027, is listed as "Día festivo: Día de Reyes," a school holiday. For the Christmas run-up, see the [Christmas 2026 countdown](/news/christmas-2026-calendar-printable-countdown/).
