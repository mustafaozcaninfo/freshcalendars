**The Las Vegas Raiders play the Cleveland Browns on Sunday Night Football (SNF), Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026, at 8:20 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on NBC, according to the National Football League (NFL).** The game is at Huntington Bank Field in Cleveland. It was first set for 1 p.m. ET on FOX; the NFL moved it on Nov. 17.

Below are the kickoff in every U.S. zone and in the United Kingdom, what the flex changed, how the teams arrive, the last meeting and the next Monday game. A one-page watch guide is at the end.

## What time is Raiders at Browns in my time zone?

Clocks are on standard time, so the 8:20 p.m. ET kickoff is 8:20 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST), as the Browns' schedule prints it. We worked out the other zones from that listing.

| Time zone | Sun., Nov. 29 (Raiders at Browns) |
|---|---|
| Eastern | 8:20 p.m. |
| Central | 7:20 p.m. |
| Mountain | 6:20 p.m. |
| Pacific | 5:20 p.m. |
| Alaska | 4:20 p.m. |
| Hawaii | 3:20 p.m. |
| United Kingdom (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT) | 1:20 a.m., Mon., Nov. 30 |

## What channel is Raiders at Browns on?

NBC, per the NFL's Nov. 17 release and the Browns' schedule, which also lists Universo for Spanish-language viewers. Peacock's NFL page says the service carries Sunday Night Football all season. We did not use announcer names or a Peacock start time from any listing.

## Why did the NFL move Raiders at Browns to Sunday night?

The NFL's Nov. 17 Week 12 flex release moved three games. The Raiders say theirs was set for 10 a.m. PT (1 p.m. ET) on FOX and now airs at 5:20 p.m. PT on NBC. They add that it is the Raiders' first primetime game this season.

| Game | New time | Network |
|---|---|---|
| Raiders at Browns | Sun., Nov. 29, 8:20 p.m. ET | NBC (Sunday Night Football) |
| Patriots at Chargers | 4:05 p.m. ET | CBS |
| Titans at Jaguars | 1 p.m. ET | FOX |

The release does not say which slots the Patriots-Chargers and Titans-Jaguars games left. Our [NFL Week 12 schedule and pick'em sheet](/news/nfl-week-12-schedule-2026-printable-pick-em-sheet-game-times/) has the full weekend.

## How do the Raiders and Browns arrive?

NFL.com's standings page of Nov. 24 lists the Raiders at 4-7 and the Browns at 6-4.

| Team | Record | Schedule note |
|---|---|---|
| Raiders | 4-7 | Lost 38-24 at the Denver Broncos on Sun., Nov. 22; bye in Week 13, then the Los Angeles Chargers on Sun., Dec. 13 at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS |
| Browns | 6-4 | Coming off their Week 11 bye; the Cincinnati Bengals on Sun., Dec. 6 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS |

## When did the Raiders and Browns last play?

The Browns won 24-10 at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas on Sun., Nov. 23, 2025, according to the Browns' game recap.
