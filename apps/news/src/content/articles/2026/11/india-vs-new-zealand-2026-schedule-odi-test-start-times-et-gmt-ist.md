**India lead New Zealand 2-1 in the five-match One Day International (ODI) series, and the fifth ODI is Sunday, Nov. 15 at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, with a 3 p.m. New Zealand start, which is 9 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Saturday, Nov. 14, 2 a.m. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and 7:30 a.m. India Standard Time (IST) on Nov. 15, according to the International Cricket Council (ICC) and New Zealand Cricket (NZC) schedules.** The first Test starts Wednesday, Nov. 18 at 5 p.m. ET, which is Thursday, Nov. 19 at 11 a.m. in Wellington.

The tour has five Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is), five ODIs and two Tests, according to the ICC's June 3, 2026 announcement of New Zealand's home summer. The T20I series ended on Nov. 1. The schedule table lists all 12 matches, and the conversion table further down covers the matches still to play.

## India vs New Zealand ODI results so far

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) lists the first ODI as an India win by five wickets under the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) method, with New Zealand all out for 185 in 40.2 overs and India on 143 for 5 after 22.4. Agence France-Presse (AFP) reported India's four-run DLS win in the rain-hit second ODI in Wellington, and Radio New Zealand (RNZ) and the NZ Herald both reported that the third ODI in Hamilton was abandoned without a ball bowled. The Associated Press reported the fourth ODI as a New Zealand win by 244 runs.

## What time is India vs New Zealand in ET, PT, GMT and IST?

The start times are New Zealand local time. New Zealand Daylight Time (NZDT) is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) plus 13 hours from late September to early April, according to the New Zealand Government's daylight saving page. U.S. clocks fell back at 2 a.m. on Nov. 1, so ET is UTC minus 5 hours and Pacific Time (PT) is UTC minus 8 hours. The United Kingdom has been on GMT since Oct. 25. India is on IST, UTC plus 5 hours 30 minutes.

Those offsets shift the calendar date for North American readers. An ODI starting at 3 p.m. on a New Zealand Wednesday is 9 p.m. ET on the Tuesday. A Test starting at 11 a.m. on a Thursday begins at 5 p.m. ET on the Wednesday.

## India vs New Zealand squads for the Tests

India's 16-player Test squad, announced Oct. 16 according to the ICC, includes Jasprit Bumrah and has Rishabh Pant subject to fitness. Shubman Gill captained the first three ODIs, and Shreyas Iyer leads India in the last two, the ICC reported. New Zealand named a 14-player Test squad on Nov. 11 under Tom Latham, with Rachin Ravindra, Tom Blundell and Devon Conway aiming to be available for the first Test, according to RNZ, and Matt Henry also back from injury, according to 1News.
