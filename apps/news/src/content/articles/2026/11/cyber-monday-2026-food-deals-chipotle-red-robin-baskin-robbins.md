**Seven chains post a Cyber Monday 2026 food deal on their own websites, read Nov. 27, and four of them end on Monday, Nov. 30: Chipotle's $0 delivery fee with code CYBER26, Red Robin's Buy $100 Get $25 gift card offer, Baskin-Robbins' free sundae and Outback Steakhouse's two $10 bonus cards.** Krispy Kreme and Subway gift card bonuses run to Dec. 31, and Auntie Anne's to Jan. 31, 2027. The list uses only the chains' own pages.

## Cyber Monday 2026 restaurant deals by end date

The table is sorted by the day each offer ends, earliest first. Each chain name links to the page the row came from. None of the pages shows a publish date, so every row is as read on Nov. 27, 2026 (Outback's terms were read Nov. 25).

| Chain | Offer | How to get it | Ends | Limits from the page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Chipotle](https://www.chipotle.com/) | $0 delivery fee, code CYBER26 | Chipotle.com or Chipotle app | Nov. 30, 2026 (valid 11/27-11/30) | $10 minimum before tax and fees; 5 entrées max per order; higher menu prices and, in most states, extra delivery fees apply |
| [Red Robin](https://www.redrobin.com/) | Buy $100, get $25 | Online gift card purchases in the U.S. | Nov. 30, 2026 (valid 11/27-11/30) | Online only |
| [Baskin-Robbins](https://www.baskinrobbins.com/) | Buy one 2-scoop sundae, get one free | Join Baskin-Robbins Rewards; participating U.S. shops | Nov. 30, 2026 | Single use; one free 2-scoop sundae with a 2-scoop sundae purchase |
| [Outback Steakhouse](https://www.outback.com/gift-cards) | Two $10 bonus cards per $50 in gift cards | Buy gift cards (bought Nov. 25-30) | Nov. 30, 2026 | First bonus card valid Jan. 1 to Feb. 7, 2027, second Feb. 15 to March 28, 2027 |
| [Krispy Kreme](https://www.krispykreme.com/) | $5 bonus eGift card per $25 in eGift cards | Buy eGift cards | Dec. 31, 2026 | eGift cards only |
| [Subway](https://www.subway.com/en-US) | Free Footlong per $50 gift card purchase | Buy gift cards | Dec. 31, 2026 | One Footlong for every $50 |
| [Auntie Anne's](https://www.auntieannes.com/) | $5 reward per $25 in gift cards | Online only | Jan. 31, 2027 (Nov. 4, 2026 start) | Online only |

Chipotle's page says the delivery fee waiver is "valid 11/27-11/30/2026 only" and that delivery carries higher menu prices and, outside a handful of states, additional fees. It is a delivery-fee waiver, not a food discount, and the page does not use the words "Cyber Monday". The Baskin-Robbins page shows a "Sweet Cyber Weekend Savings" section and no start date. None of the pages states a time of day for any offer.

## Which Cyber Monday food deals end Nov. 30?

Four of the seven. Chipotle, Red Robin and Outback are all cut off after Monday, Nov. 30, and Baskin-Robbins' sundae offer "expires 11/30/2026". Red Robin's offer is a separate one from its Buy $50, Get $10 online gift card bonus, which the [restaurant gift card bonus story](/news/restaurant-gift-card-deals-2026-bonus-cards-cheesecake-factory-panera-red-robin/) covers with the other bonus-card chains. Outback's bonus cards do not work until January, so the Nov. 30 date is the last day to buy, not to spend.

## Which gift card bonuses are still on sale after Cyber Monday?

Three. Krispy Kreme says "Now thru 12/31, purchase $25 in eGift cards and receive a $5 Bonus eGift card." Subway says "Get a free Footlong for every $50 gift card purchased through 12/31." Auntie Anne's says "Buy $25 in Auntie Anne's gift cards and get a $5 reward. Online Only. 11/04/26-01/31/2027." Mark Dec. 31 and Jan. 31 on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) if you plan to buy.

One offer ends before Cyber Monday. Crumbl's page says "Get $50 in gift cards for just $40, in-store only, November 22-29", so its last day is Sunday, Nov. 29.

## Chains with no Cyber Monday offer posted

Domino's, Taco Bell, Dunkin', Wendy's, Starbucks, Chili's, Olive Garden, Panera and Cinnabon had posted no Cyber Monday offer as of Nov. 27, 2026.

For McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Shake Shack, Church's, Grubhub, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Texas Roadhouse, Dairy Queen, Denny's, Applebee's and Jersey Mike's, no official 2026 offer could be confirmed. Coupon sites list codes for some of these that the chains have not posted, so they stay out of the table.
