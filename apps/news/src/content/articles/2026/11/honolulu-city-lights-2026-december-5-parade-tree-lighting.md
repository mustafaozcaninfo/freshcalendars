**Honolulu City Lights Opening Night is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026: the ceremony begins at 5:30 p.m. Hawaii-Aleutian Standard Time (HST) and the lights go on at about 6 p.m.**, the official Honolulu City Lights Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) page says. In Eastern Time (ET) that is 10:30 p.m. and about 11 p.m. HST is ET minus five hours in December.

The parade steps off at about 6 p.m. HST and the first unit reaches Honolulu Hale at about 6:30 p.m. The city's Department of Transportation Services (DTS) lists the road closures below. Kapolei lights its own tree on Saturday, Nov. 28. Pair the dates with the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html), and see [when Christmas falls](/when-is/christmas/).

| Time (HST) | Time (ET) | What | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11:30 a.m. | 4:30 p.m. | Punchbowl Street closes for the block party | DTS |

| 11 p.m. | 4 a.m., Sun., Dec. 6 | Parade roads reopen | DTS |
| 11:30 p.m. | 4:30 a.m., Sun., Dec. 6 | Punchbowl Street reopens | DTS |

Eastern times are the Honolulu times plus five hours. The FAQ also lists a Free Photo with Santa at Mission Memorial Auditorium on opening night. The printable opening-night planner holds the ceremony, lights and Kapolei rows on one page.

## Where does the Honolulu City Lights parade start?

The sources describe the route two ways. The FAQ says the parade travels on King Street from River Street to Kawaiahao Street. DTS lists "Aala Park to King St., eastbound to Honolulu Hale," and Hawaii News Now reported on Nov. 16 that it "will begin at 6 p.m. from Aala Park." The parade heads east on King Street to Honolulu Hale either way.

## What roads close for Honolulu City Lights?

| Event (Sat., Dec. 5, 2026) | Closes (HST) | Reopens (HST) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening Night Block Party | 11:30 a.m. | 11:30 p.m. | Punchbowl St. from Beretania St. to S. King St. |
| Mayor's Annual Tree Lighting Ceremony and Parade | 5 p.m. | 11 p.m. | Aala Park to King St., eastbound to Honolulu Hale |

Both are DTS entries marked Approved. The FAQ says to park before the parade-related street closures start; free public parking is at the Frank F. Fasi Municipal lot, entered from Beretania Street. TheBus detours and a Honolulu Police Department advisory for 2026 are not announced.

The same list shows other O'ahu parades that day, with road closures from 8:30 a.m. to 11 a.m. (Mililani Ohana Holiday Parade), 9 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. (Kaneohe Christmas Parade) and 5:30 p.m. to 8:30 p.m. (Waianae Coast Rotary Community Christmas Parade).

## When does Kapolei City Lights open in 2026?

Kapolei City Lights Opening Night is Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, from 5 p.m. to 9 p.m. HST (10 p.m. to 2 a.m. ET), the honolulu.gov events list says. Hawaii News Now reported on Nov. 10 that the Kapolei tree will be lit that day.

| Kapolei event, Sat., Nov. 28 | Closes (HST) | Reopens (HST) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| City Lights Hoolaulea | 11 a.m. | 11 p.m. | Segments of Uluohia St., Kamaaha Ave., Alohikea St. and Ala Kahawai St. |
| City Lights Parade | 4 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Fort Barrette Rd./Kapolei Pkwy. to Kealakapu St. |

In Eastern Time the Hoolaulea closure runs 4 p.m. to 4 a.m. and the parade closure 9 p.m. to 1 a.m.

## How long do the Honolulu City Lights displays run?

The FAQ says the display is open daily from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. HST (1 p.m. to 3 a.m. ET) on the Frank F. Fasi Civic Center Grounds and runs through December. No last day is posted. The city's 50-foot holiday tree and other outdoor decorations are lighted nightly at about 6 p.m. Hawaii News Now reported on Nov. 16 that the Cook pine was trimmed from 55 feet to about 40 feet, so the tree height differs by source. The event is free, presented as a gift to the community.

In 2025, the mayor's office said opening night was Nov. 29 with tree lighting at 6 p.m. and the parade leaving 'A'ala Park at 6:15 p.m. The Holiday Tree Exhibition inside Honolulu Hale closed Friday, Dec. 26, 2025, and outdoor displays began powering down Monday, Dec. 29, 2025. No 2026 end dates are posted. Hawaii News Now calls this the 42nd annual celebration.

Fireworks and a 2026 theme are not announced.
