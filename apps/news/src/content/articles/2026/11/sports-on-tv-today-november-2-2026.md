## Bears at Seahawks on Monday Night Football

NFL.com listed Bears at Seahawks on Monday, Nov. 2, at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN at Lumen Field in Seattle. The Chicago Bears' how-to-watch page, dated Oct. 28, said the game "kicks off at 7:15 p.m. CT Monday, Nov. 2 in Seattle" and listed ESPN+ for streaming and NFL+ for streaming in market. The game was 5:15 p.m. PT in Seattle. The Monday schedule for later weeks is in our [Monday Night Football guide](/news/monday-night-football-tonight-cowboys-eagles-mnf-schedule-2026/).

## NBA and NHL on Monday night

NBA.com listed 15 games, with Thunder at Cavaliers at 7 p.m. ET on Peacock and NBC Sports Network and the other 14 on NBA League Pass. The last three tips, Raptors at Clippers, Suns at Warriors and Heat at Lakers, were at 10 p.m. ET. Arenas and every time zone are in [NBA games today, Nov. 2](/news/nba-games-today-november-2-2026/).

## Premier League and Paris Masters tennis

Newcastle United v Everton kicked off at 8 p.m. UK time (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT), which is 3 p.m. ET, on Sky Sports in the UK and USA Network in the United States, per the Premier League fixtures page. Every kickoff of the weekend is in our [Premier League fixtures this weekend](/news/premier-league-fixtures-this-weekend-october-31-november-2-2026-kickoff-times-tv/) story.
