## Bears at Seahawks on Monday Night Football

NFL.com lists Bears at Seahawks on Monday, Nov. 2, at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN at Lumen Field in Seattle. The Chicago Bears' how-to-watch page, dated Oct. 28, says the game "kicks off at 7:15 p.m. CT Monday, Nov. 2 in Seattle" and lists ESPN+ for streaming and NFL+ for streaming in market. The game is 5:15 p.m. PT in Seattle. Last week's result and the rest of the Monday schedule are in our [Monday Night Football guide](/news/monday-night-football-tonight-cowboys-eagles-mnf-schedule-2026/).

## NBA and NHL on Monday night

NBA.com lists 15 games, with Thunder at Cavaliers at 7 p.m. ET on Peacock and NBC Sports Network and the other 14 on NBA League Pass. The last three tips, Raptors at Clippers, Suns at Warriors and Heat at Lakers, are at 10 p.m. ET. Arenas and every time zone are in [NBA games today, Nov. 2](/news/nba-games-today-november-2-2026/).

## Premier League and Paris Masters tennis

Newcastle United v Everton kicks off at 8 p.m. UK time (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT), which is 3 p.m. ET, on Sky Sports in the UK and USA Network in the United States, per the Premier League fixtures page. Every kickoff of the weekend is in our [Premier League fixtures this weekend](/news/premier-league-fixtures-this-weekend-october-31-november-2-2026-kickoff-times-tv/) story.
