**The New York Times (NYT) Midi Crossword for Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, is titled "Catty Corner" and has 24 clues on a 9-by-9 grid, credited to Shannon Rapp.** Each clue has a hint behind a tap, and the answers wait in the marked section at the end.

## What the title "Catty Corner" points to

Read the title as an instruction. The animal in "Catty" has been taken out of two corners, and the square it would fill stays empty: the official data accepts those two squares only when they are left blank. Each empty square is the first square of both an Across and a Down entry, so four entries start one box early with nothing in it.

The first pair is 1-Across and 1-Down, which share the top-left corner of the grid. The second pair is 18-Across and 18-Down, which share the square at row 6, column 6. Put the two squares side by side on the grid and they fall on one diagonal, 5 rows and 5 columns apart. That diagonal is the "corner" half of the joke. The same three letters are missing from both squares, so a solver who spots the trick in one corner has the other.

Start with 1-Down: its clue names something that arrives in a mailbox before a holiday, and the crossing 1-Across names a ballplayer. Both answers need the same missing letters in front of what you type.

## The least literal clue today

12-Down: *Rooms where things get steamy?* The question mark warns that "steamy" is not about romance. Think of a room built to be hot and damp on purpose, where people sit on wooden benches. The answer has 6 letters, it is plural, and it begins and ends with S.
