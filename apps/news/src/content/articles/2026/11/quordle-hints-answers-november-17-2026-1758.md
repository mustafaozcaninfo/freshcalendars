## How do the four words overlap?

Twenty slots hold 12 different letters, which leaves eight repeats. Seven of those repeats are letters that turn up in a second or third word; the eighth is a letter used twice inside one word. Counting only A, E, I, O and U, two words hold two vowels and the other two hold one.

| Words containing the letter | Letters | What it means for play |
|---|---|---|
| 3 of 4 | 2 | A yellow or green on either one is worth carrying to three boards |
| 2 of 4 | 3 | Each splits the grid in half, so one clue serves two boards |
| 1 of 4 | 7 | Seven letters are single-board evidence, and a gray on one rules out nothing elsewhere |

A first guess that holds both three-board letters can mark three grids at once. The one grid it cannot reach with them has to be solved from its own letters.

## How five opening words score on today's four boards

Each cell lists green tiles (G, right letter, right spot) and yellow tiles (Y, right letter, wrong spot) that the opener earns against that word.

| Opener | Word 1 | Word 2 | Word 3 | Word 4 | Colored tiles in all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crane | 1G 0Y | 0G 2Y | 0G 1Y | 1G 2Y | 7 |
| Slate | 2G 0Y | 0G 1Y | 1G 2Y | 0G 1Y | 7 |
| Audio | 0G 1Y | 0G 2Y | 0G 0Y | 0G 2Y | 5 |
| Snowy | 1G 1Y | 0G 3Y | 1G 1Y | 0G 2Y | 9 |
| Tongs | 0G 3Y | 0G 3Y | 1G 1Y | 0G 3Y | 11 |

Tongs scores 11 colored tiles and touches every board with at least two, yet only one of them is green. Slate has the most greens, three, but they fall on two boards and Word 2 and Word 4 get one tile each. Audio, built from vowels, scores five in all and leaves Word 3 blank.

Word 2 and Word 4 return the most, 11 colored tiles each across the five openers. Word 3 returns eight, the fewest, with Word 1 at nine.

Spoilers ahead, so stop here if you are still playing.

Beebom and Ponly both list these four words in this order and both label the puzzle #1758. On the grid, Slung sits top left, Wagon top right, Testy bottom left and Organ bottom right.

| Letter | Words containing it |
|---|---|

G and N sit in the same three words, so one guess that pins both settles most of three boards. Testy is the outlier: it shares only S with Slung and nothing with Wagon or Organ.
