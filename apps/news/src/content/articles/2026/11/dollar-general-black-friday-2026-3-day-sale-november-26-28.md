**Dollar General's three-day sale runs Thursday to Saturday, Nov. 26-28, 2026, with a one-day flash sale on Black Friday, Nov. 27, according to the retailer's homepage and its announcement as reported by Mass Market Retailers on Nov. 23.** The Goodlettsville, Tenn., chain also posts a Wednesday-only coupon for Nov. 25 and delivery offers that run to Nov. 30. The calendar below sorts every dated offer by day, and a one-page printable follows.

## Dollar General deal calendar, Nov. 25 to Nov. 30

The weekdays are our computation; the offers come from the dollargeneral.com homepage (read Nov. 24) and the announcement as reported by Mass Market Retailers.

| Date | What is on | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Wed., Nov. 25 | $5 off $25 with Dollar General (DG) Digital Coupons, "This Wednesday Only" | Homepage |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | 3-day sale starts; delivery fee waived on one same-day myDG Delivery order | Homepage, announcement |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Flash sale, "One Day Only"; Black Friday deals; $10 off qualifying $40 delivery orders starts | Homepage, announcement |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | 3-day sale ends; coupons on the site expire | Homepage, announcement |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Delivery offers continue | Announcement |
| Mon., Nov. 30 | Last day of both delivery offers | Announcement |

The coupons on the homepage show an expiry of "Sat Nov 28 23:59:59 UTC 2026". That is 6:59 p.m. ET on Saturday, our conversion. The Wednesday coupon's terms beyond its headline are not posted on the page. Mark the days on our [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars.

## What are Dollar General's 3-day sale deals on Nov. 26-28?

Dollar General announced the deals and Mass Market Retailers reported them on Nov. 23. The sale runs in stores, online and in the DG app, while supplies last.

- Buy one qualifying toy, get a second for 75% off; Pokémon products are excluded.
- 50% off select Comfort Bay throws and blankets.
- Nutribullet blenders at $25 (regularly $40) and glass food storage sets at $10 (regularly $20).
- Select gift cards and multipacks (McDonald's, Roblox, Panera, Xbox) at 20% off during the event.
- More than 2,000 items priced at $1 or less, including gift wrap, stocking stuffers, seasonal décor and cleaning supplies.

Johanna Blankush, senior vice president and general merchandise manager at Dollar General, said: "Whether customers are shopping for gifts or stocking up on everyday essentials, we're here to help make holiday shopping more affordable and convenient."

## What is the Dollar General Black Friday flash sale on Nov. 27?

The homepage lists a flash sale on Friday, Nov. 27, "One Day Only". The announcement names these Black Friday items:

| Deal on Fri., Nov. 27 | Price or discount |
|---|---|
| Select Christmas trees, ornaments and lights | 50% off |
| Air fryers (flash sale) | $25, from $40 |
| Vivitar Bluetooth speaker systems (flash sale) | $20, from $30 |
| Lowe's gift cards | 15% off |

## How do Dollar General's delivery offers work?

The homepage offers same-day delivery with "One free delivery fee with myDG, other fees may apply." The announcement gives the dates. From Nov. 26 through Nov. 30, the delivery fee is waived on one same-day myDG Delivery order. From Nov. 27 through Nov. 30, qualifying delivery orders of $40 get $10 off.

## Is Dollar General open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Dollar General has not posted Thanksgiving Day or Black Friday hours, and store hours vary by location. Use the store locator on dollargeneral.com before you go. The 3-day sale starts on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, and the announcement says shoppers can use stores, online or the app. For other chains, see our [Thanksgiving 2026 open and closed list](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/) and the [Ollie's Black Friday hours](/news/ollies-black-friday-2026-hours-thanksgiving-closed-daily-deals/). As of Nov. 24, neither Dollar Tree nor Family Dollar had posted 2026 holiday hours or a Black Friday event on its own site.
