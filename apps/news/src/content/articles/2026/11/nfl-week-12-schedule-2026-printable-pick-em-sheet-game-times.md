## What is the Thanksgiving Eve game on Wednesday, Nov. 25?

Packers at Rams kicks off Wednesday night, the eve of Thanksgiving, at 8 p.m. ET on Netflix, according to NFL.com. The Packers list it at 7 p.m. CT and the Rams at 5 p.m. PT, which matches the table above. Our [Thanksgiving football schedule](/news/thanksgiving-football-schedule-2026-nfl-college-games/) covers the five NFL games of the holiday weekend with the college games.

## How many days of NFL games are in Week 12?

Five game days spread over six. NFL.com lists games on Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Sunday and Monday, with none on Saturday, Nov. 28. Thanksgiving Day alone has three games, and Black Friday has Broncos at Steelers at 3 p.m. ET on Prime Video.

## Can the Week 12 Sunday and Monday games still move?

Yes, for Sunday afternoon games. The NFL's flexible scheduling procedures say only Sunday afternoon games, or games still to be determined, can move. Sunday Night Football can be flexed in any week from Week 11 to 17 and Monday Night Football in Weeks 12 to 17, with notice "no later than 12 days before the game." Counting back from Nov. 29 and Nov. 30, that puts the decisions by Tuesday, Nov. 17 for Sunday night and Wednesday, Nov. 18 for Monday night, by our calculation. The Thanksgiving and Wednesday games are not covered by the flex rule. Check NFL.com before game day.
