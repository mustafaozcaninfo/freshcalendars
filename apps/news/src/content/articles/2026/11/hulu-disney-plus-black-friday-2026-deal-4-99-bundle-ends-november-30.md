**Disney+ says the Disney+, Hulu Bundle with ads is $4.99 a month for 12 months in its 2026 Black Friday deal, and sign-up ends at 11:59 p.m. PT on Monday, Nov. 30.** Disney+ lists the same moment as 2:59 a.m. ET on Tuesday, Dec. 1. After the 12 months the plan renews at $12.99 a month, or the then-current regular monthly price, unless you cancel.

The offer is on the Hulu and Disney+ home pages and in a Disney+ article revised Nov. 25, 2026. For all streaming offers ending Nov. 30, see the [streaming Black Friday roundup](/news/streaming-black-friday-2026-deals-paramount-plus-hbo-max-peacock/). This story covers the Disney+ and Hulu bundle only.

## When does the Disney+ and Hulu offer end by time zone?

Disney+ gives the start and end in ET and PT. The Central, Mountain, Alaska and Hawaii rows are by our conversion from those times.

| Zone | Offer started | Offer ends |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | Tue., Nov. 24, 3:00 a.m. | Tue., Dec. 1, 2:59 a.m. |
| Central (CT) | Tue., Nov. 24, 2:00 a.m. | Tue., Dec. 1, 1:59 a.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | Tue., Nov. 24, 1:00 a.m. | Tue., Dec. 1, 12:59 a.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | Tue., Nov. 24, 12:00 a.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 11:59 p.m. |
| Alaska | Mon., Nov. 23, 11:00 p.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 10:59 p.m. |
| Hawaii | Mon., Nov. 23, 10:00 p.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 9:59 p.m. |

Because the end falls after midnight in the Eastern, Central and Mountain zones, it is Dec. 1 on the clock there but still Nov. 30 on the West Coast. Do not wait for the last hour, since a payment problem leaves no time to retry.

## What does the Disney+ and Hulu deal cost over 12 months?

Both plans below are offered at the promo price for 12 months. The regular figure uses the price Disney+ names for month 13 on, so it assumes no change in the then-current price.

| Plan | Promo price | 12 months on the offer | 12 months at the regular price | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Disney+, Hulu Bundle (with ads) | $4.99 a month | $59.88 | $155.88 at $12.99 | $96.00 less (61.6%) |
| Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium | $14.99 a month | $179.88 | $263.88 at $21.99 | $84.00 less (31.8%) |

The Premium plan has fewer ads, but Disney+ says ads are served in select live and linear content. Over 24 months, if the price after month 12 stays at $12.99, the ad plan totals $215.76, or $8.99 a month on average, against $311.76 at the regular price. The Premium plan at $21.99 after the first year totals $443.76 over 24 months.

## Who can get the Disney+ and Hulu deal?

Disney+ says the offer is open to new and eligible returning subscribers who are U.S. residents age 18 or older. Current Disney+, Hulu, ESPN and bundle subscribers are not eligible. The offer:

- is for the ad-supported bundle and the Premium bundle only, and does not include ESPN;
- cannot be combined with a free trial or any other promotional offer or pricing, including another bundle;
- cannot be redeemed with a gift card;
- is lost if you switch plans after redeeming it.

To claim it, open the Disney+ home page, pick the bundle with ads or the Premium bundle, enter account and payment details, and check the price, 12-month length and renewal terms before you finish.

## What happens after 12 months, and how do you cancel?

The plan renews on its own at $12.99 a month (Premium: $21.99), or the then-current regular monthly price. Disney+ says you can cancel anytime, effective at the end of your billing period, and that there are no refunds or credits for partial months. Neither page gives step-by-step cancel directions, so check the Disney+ or Hulu Help Center. A plan started on Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 reaches month 13 in late November 2027; your billing date may differ, so set a reminder a few days before it.

Disney+ says select Hulu content shows in the Disney+ app, and the rest is only in the Hulu app. The [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html) has room to mark the renewal date.
