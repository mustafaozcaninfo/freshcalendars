## Six woven entries behind the title "Mixed Results"

Six clues in the data carry a plus sign between two short phrases: 27-Across, 33-Across, 58-Across, 66-Across, 90-Across and 96-Across. Each is 13 letters long. The plus sign means the entry is a blend: answer the two phrases separately, then shuffle their letters together, each answer keeping its own order, until they fill the 13 squares. In all six, one part has six letters and the other seven.

A blend alone would leave a solver with a string that reads as neither part, so each long entry has a partner. The six "See" clues (26-Across, 36-Across, 55-Across, 68-Across, 88-Across and 100-Across) each point to a long entry and want a 7-letter answer that says what the blend means in plain terms. That is the "mixed results" of the title: two sets of results from one pair of clues. The partner always sits in the same row as its long entry, with one black square between them.
