**The Toronto Maple Leafs play 13 games in December 2026, including Boxing Day at the Montreal Canadiens on Saturday, Dec. 26, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** Five games are at home and eight are on the road, and the month opens Thursday, Dec. 3, at home against Florida.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Toronto, read Nov. 10, 2026. It gives each start in UTC. We converted the times to ET, which is Toronto time, and calculated the weekdays. The feed lists broadcasters and they can change. A one-page printable is further down.

## What time is Leafs at Canadiens on Boxing Day 2026?

Toronto visits the Canadiens at Centre Bell on Saturday, Dec. 26, at 7 p.m. ET. The feed lists Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada and no U.S. broadcaster. Boxing Day itself is covered in our [Canada Boxing Day date guide](/when-is/ca-boxing-day/). The Leafs have no game on Dec. 23, 24 or 25, so Boxing Day is their first game after Tuesday, Dec. 22, at Detroit.

## Back-to-backs, the afternoon game and the California finish

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Back-to-backs:** Sat., Dec. 12 and Sun., Dec. 13, both at home; Mon., Dec. 21 at home and Tue., Dec. 22 at Detroit; Tue., Dec. 29 at Anaheim and Wed., Dec. 30 at Los Angeles.
- **Afternoon game:** Mon., Dec. 21, against Washington at 2 p.m. ET, the only start before 7 p.m. in the month.
- **Late starts:** both California games begin at 10 p.m. ET, against the Ducks on Dec. 29 and the Kings on Dec. 30.
- **Home games:** Dec. 3, 12, 13, 19 and 21, all at Scotiabank Arena.
- **No game:** Dec. 23, 24, 25 and Dec. 31.

## When do the Maple Leafs play next after December?

The first January game is Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027, at the San Jose Sharks, 7 p.m. ET at SAP Center at San Jose, listed on Sportsnet in Canada.
