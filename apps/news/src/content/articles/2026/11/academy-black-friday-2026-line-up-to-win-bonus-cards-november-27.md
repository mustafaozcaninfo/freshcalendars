**Academy Sports + Outdoors says the first 100 customers in line at each of its stores on Black Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, get a bonus card worth $20 to $500, according to its Nov. 9 release.** The Katy, Texas, retailer also lists five holiday shopping windows from Oct. 25 to Dec. 25. The date calendar, the Line-Up to Win details and a one-page printable follow.

## Academy 2026 holiday shopping dates, Oct. 25 to Dec. 25

| Dates (2026) | Weekdays | Academy's name for the window |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 25 to Nov. 18 | Sunday to Wednesday | Discover Gifts You Want to Give at Academy |
| Nov. 19 to Nov. 25 | Thursday to Wednesday | Black Friday Starts Now |
| Nov. 26 to Nov. 28 | Thursday to Saturday | Black Friday Doorbusters |
| Nov. 29 to Dec. 6 | Sunday to Sunday | Cyber Week |
| Dec. 17 to Dec. 25 | Thursday to Friday | Last-Minute Holiday Shopping: same-day delivery and last-minute gift options |

The weekdays are our computation. Thursday, Nov. 26, is Thanksgiving Day and Friday, Nov. 27, is Black Friday. Mark the windows on the [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars.

## How does Academy's Line-Up to Win work on Black Friday?

The release says Academy "brings back the excitement of lining up early with its Line-Up to Win activation at all of its stores" on Friday, Nov. 27. The first 100 customers in line at each location receive a bonus card valued from $20 to $500. The card is for in-store holiday shopping from Nov. 27 through Nov. 29, a Friday-to-Sunday window.

The release links to separate rules. We did not read them, so card terms, the line-up start time and how values are assigned are not covered here. Check Academy's rules before you plan a trip.

## Is Academy open on Thanksgiving 2026, and what are the Black Friday hours?

Academy has not confirmed either in its release. Doorbusters run Nov. 26 to 28, which includes Thanksgiving Day, and the release does not say whether that day is online only. Academy's Black Friday and store-hours pages blocked our automated check, so look up your own store's page on academy.com.

Yahoo Shopping lists 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. for Black Friday in a page updated Nov. 16, 2026. That is one newsroom, and Academy has not confirmed it in the release.

## When is Academy Cyber Week 2026?

Cyber Week runs Sunday, Nov. 29, to Sunday, Dec. 6. Monday, Nov. 30, falls inside it. Academy's release does not name a separate Cyber Monday event or list deal prices, so no products or prices are given here. For other chains, see the [Black Friday 2026 store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/), the [ad tracker](/news/black-friday-2026-ad-tracker-walmart-target-best-buy/) and [Dick's Sporting Goods' Black Friday plans](/news/dicks-sporting-goods-black-friday-2026-deals-november-22-hours/).

## What is Academy's 25 Days of Fun?

Beginning Tuesday, Dec. 1, Academy launches "25 Days of Fun," which the release says is available exclusively to myAcademy Rewards members in the Academy app. Through Christmas Day, members get a new surprise each day, including app-exclusive 24-hour promotions and interactive activities.

## What has Academy not announced?

- Thanksgiving Day store status.
- Black Friday opening and closing times.
- Whether the Nov. 26 doorbusters are online only.
- Deal prices and products for any window.
