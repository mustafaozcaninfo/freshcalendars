**The 2026 Mountain West (MW) Football Championship Game is Friday, Dec. 4, at 8 p.m. CT, which is 9 p.m. ET, on FOX, according to the MW.** Its broadcast release says the game is "slated for Friday, Dec. 4, at 8 p.m. CT/7 p.m. MT/6 p.m. PT/4 p.m. HT" and "will be nationally televised on FOX for the seventh consecutive year." HT is Hawaii Time.

The matchup is not set. The MW says the game "will feature the top two teams that finish the Conference season with the highest winning percentages," so it is decided in the final games of Fri., Nov. 27, and Sat., Nov. 28.

## What time does the Mountain West title game start in each time zone?

| Time zone | Kickoff on Fri., Dec. 4 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | 9 p.m. |
| Central (CT) | 8 p.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 7 p.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | 6 p.m. |
| Hawaii (HT) | 4 p.m. |

The MW gives the Central, Mountain, Pacific and Hawaii times. The Eastern time is computed from the 8 p.m. CT start.

## Which teams can still reach the Mountain West title game?

The MW's 2026 football membership is Air Force, Hawai'i, Nevada, New Mexico, Northern Illinois University (NIU), North Dakota State, San José State, University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP) and Wyoming. In the standings read on Nov. 25, 2026, five teams are at 5-2 or better in conference play. The "best" and "worst" columns below are our arithmetic from those records and the final schedule, not the MW's scenarios.

| Team | Conference record (overall) | Last conference game | Best / worst finish |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Mexico | 6-1 (9-2) | Fri., Nov. 27, vs. Air Force, 4:30 p.m. ET, The CW | 7-1 / 6-2 |
| North Dakota State | 6-2 (9-2) | Fri., Nov. 27, at San José State, 3:30 p.m. ET, CBS Sports Network | 7-2 / 6-3 |
| Wyoming | 6-2 (7-4) | Done; plays non-conference UConn Sat., Nov. 28, noon ET | 6-2 / 6-2 |
| Air Force | 5-2 (9-2) | Fri., Nov. 27, at New Mexico, 4:30 p.m. ET, The CW | 6-2 / 5-3 |
| UNLV | 5-2 (7-4) | Sat., Nov. 28, vs. Nevada, 9 p.m. ET, CBS Sports Network | 6-2 / 5-3 |

The times are the MW's Week 13 release (Nov. 24, 2026), converted to ET. Nevada (4-3) can finish no higher than 5-3, a .625 winning percentage, below Wyoming's finished .750, so by our count Nevada cannot reach the top two. San José State, Hawai'i, UTEP and NIU are further back.

One result would settle part of it. In Air Force at New Mexico, a New Mexico win makes the Lobos 7-1 (.875), above the best North Dakota State can do (7-2, .778). By our arithmetic that clinches New Mexico a spot and first place. No other clinch follows from the standings alone.

## Where will the Mountain West championship game be played?

The host is not named yet. The MW's Week 12 scenarios release tied "hosting rights" to the top finish, saying Wyoming "clinches a berth and hosting rights with a win over New Mexico." That release came before Week 12 was played; with Wyoming finished at 6-2 and New Mexico ahead at 6-1, the host depends on who finishes first. Ties are settled by the MW's published tiebreaking procedures, and this page does not predict one. Tickets are not announced either: the MW had posted no ticket details as of Nov. 25.

## Who won the 2025 Mountain West championship game?

In the 2025 title game, Boise State beat UNLV 38-21 for a third straight Mountain West title, CBS Sports reported from The Associated Press. That is last year's result. The MW's Week 13 release adds that if UNLV reaches this year's game, the Rebels would be the second program ever with at least three straight MW title-game appearances, after Boise State's four straight from 2017 to 2020.

## What else is on title-game weekend, Dec. 4-5?

The MW game shares Friday with the [Pac-12 game at Boise State](/news/pac-12-championship-game-2026-boise-state-hosts-december-4-cbs/) and the [Big 12 title game](/news/big-12-championship-game-2026-friday-december-4-kickoff-time-abc/). On Saturday, the [Mid-American Conference title game](/news/mac-championship-game-2026-date-kickoff-time-ford-field/) is at Ford Field.
