## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Imagine a paint-swatch card with one color family on it; each tile is a name printed there.
- Hint 2: The entries have five, five, nine and nine letters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Walk a dealership lot; each tile describes how one vehicle is sized, built or driven.
- Hint 2: The entries have six, six, seven and eleven letters.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: A cartoon dad in Springfield would happily spend an evening on any of these.
- Hint 2: The entries have four, six, seven and ten letters.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: One short verb fits in front of every tile, and it makes a small noise when you do it.
- Hint 2: Counting letters only, the entries have seven, seven, eleven and seventeen letters.

## Solving tips

Start with the tile that has the most letters. It is a whole phrase, so ask what action you could carry out on it.

## Why these four tiles mislead in puzzle #1264

Each row is a tile that can pass for a member of a different group, and the reason it belongs where it does.

| Tile | Group it tempts | Why it does not go there | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bubblegum | Things that are popped | Gum bubbles do pop, but the word is also a bright pink | Yellow |
| Popcorn | Things Homer Simpson likes, next to Donuts and Beer | It is a snack, yet it is also something you pop | Purple |
| Bottle of Champagne | Things Homer Simpson likes, as a drink beside Beer | The cork gets popped, so it joins the popped group | Purple |
| Compact | Shades of pink, as a makeup case beside Blush | Here it names a car size | Green |
| Donuts | Kinds of cars, as a stunt drivers perform | On this board it is a food Homer likes | Blue |
| Wheelie | Kinds of cars, as a vehicle trick | A wheelie is popped on a bike | Purple |
| Coral | A reef | It is a pink shade with Blush, Bubblegum and Carnation | Yellow |

Two decoy lanes cross on this board. Beer, Donuts and Popcorn make a tidy snack trio, but one of them is in a different group from the other two. The motor-vehicle stunts, Donuts and Wheelie, also end up in two groups, neither of them the car group.
