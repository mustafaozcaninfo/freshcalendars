**Connections: Sports Edition #780, the Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026 board from The Athletic and New York Times (NYT) Games, mixes American leagues with English soccer.** Each color gets two hints you can open one at a time. Every answer, and the team or club behind each tile, waits under the spoiler heading.

If you need a refresher on grouping and mistakes, the rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works).

## Thursday's hints, #780, Nov. 12, 2026

Say each tile out loud as a plural and as a surname before you guess. Thursday's board rewards that habit.

- **Yellow hint 1:** Think of one player from a pro basketball club, named the way a broadcaster might say "a Celtic" or "a Knick."
- **Yellow hint 2:** All four clubs play in the half of the league that sits west of the Mississippi, give or take a few cities.
- **Green hint 1:** Four teams that share a home state, and one of them is a college program rather than a pro club.
- **Green hint 2:** Football, hockey and women's pro basketball fill the other three spots; two of the four play in the same city on Lake Erie.
- **Blue hint 1:** These four are surnames, and the men who carry them all coach soccer clubs in England's top division this season.
- **Blue hint 2:** One of them took over a South Coast club this summer, and one led his side up from the second tier.
- **Purple hint 1:** Each tile needs a five-letter word added after it to become a piece of sports gear.
- **Purple hint 2:** Two of those gear items go on water, one on a mountain and one on pavement.

## What makes Thursday's board slippery

Three tiles from three different colors read like nature words: something in the sky, something that falls in winter and a flower. That pull toward a nature group is the main decoy. A fourth tile is both a color and a college. Before you lock a group, ask whether the word is a name someone wears on a jersey or a word that finishes a compound.

## Thursday's answers, #780, Nov. 12, 2026 (spoilers)

Turn back now if you still want to solve it. Beebom and Word.tips print the same four groups and all sixteen tiles.

- **Yellow, "A Western Conference NBA player":** Laker, Rocket, Sun, Warrior
- **Green, "Teams in New York State":** Bills, Liberty, Orange, Sabres
- **Blue, "Premier League managers":** Emery, Lampard, Moyes, Rose
- **Purple, "___board":** Skate, Snow, Surf, Wake

## Which NBA clubs are behind the yellow tiles?

Add an S back on and each tile becomes a club listed in the Western Conference on the NBA's team page.

| Tile | Club | Official page |
|---|---|---|
| Laker | Los Angeles Lakers | nba.com |
| Rocket | Houston Rockets | nba.com |
| Sun | Phoenix Suns | nba.com |
| Warrior | Golden State Warriors | nba.com |

## Which New York State teams are in green?

Two Buffalo clubs, one New York City team and one university make the group.

| Tile | Team | League | Official page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bills | Buffalo Bills | NFL | buffalobills.com |
| Sabres | Buffalo Sabres | National Hockey League (NHL) | nhl.com/sabres |

## Who are the four Premier League managers?

The Premier League's own list of managers for the 2026/27 season, published Aug. 5, 2026, names all four.

| Tile | Manager | Club |
|---|---|---|
| Emery | Unai Emery | Aston Villa, since October 2022 |
| Moyes | David Moyes | Everton, second spell since January 2025 |
| Rose | Marco Rose | Bournemouth, new for 2026/27 |
| Lampard | Frank Lampard | Coventry City, promoted under him |

Rose is an easy tile to park in the wrong spot. It reads like a garden word, and with Sun and Snow nearby a nature group looks tempting. Lampard and Moyes are the easy entry points for anyone who follows English soccer.

## What do the purple tiles complete?

Put board after each one: skateboard, snowboard, surfboard and wakeboard. Sun is the tile that most often gets dragged into this group, since sunboard looks almost like a word, yet it belongs to Phoenix in yellow.
