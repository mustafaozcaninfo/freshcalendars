**The San Antonio Spurs and New Orleans Pelicans play the NBA Paris Game on Thursday, Jan. 14, 2027, at Accor Arena, tipping off at 2 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), and meet again in Manchester, England, on Sunday, Jan. 17, at 10:30 a.m. ET, according to NBA.com's 2026-27 key dates.** Both games stream on Prime Video. Below: both tip times in six time zones, how the two games fit together and the other international game this season.

## When are the NBA Paris and Manchester games in 2027?

NBA Communications announced the games on May 20, 2026. The Spurs and Pelicans "will play regular-season games at the Accor Arena in Paris, France, on Thursday, Jan. 14, 2027," and "at Co-op Live in Manchester, England, on Sunday, Jan. 17, 2027." The same two teams play both games, three days apart. NBA.com's key dates, updated Oct. 28, 2026, list the times:

| Game | Date | Teams | Arena | Tip-off (ET) | Streaming |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NBA Paris Game | Thu., Jan. 14, 2027 | Spurs vs. Pelicans | Accor Arena, Paris | 2 p.m. | Prime Video |
| NBA Manchester Game | Sun., Jan. 17, 2027 | Spurs vs. Pelicans | Co-op Live, Manchester | 10:30 a.m. | Prime Video |

## What time do the NBA Europe games start in your time zone?

The Eastern times are the NBA's. The NBA does not print local times, so every other column is our conversion for January 2027, when Eastern Standard Time is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) minus 5 hours. The United Kingdom is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, UTC+0) and Paris on Central European Time (CET, UTC+1).

| Game | Eastern | Central | Mountain | Pacific | UK (GMT) | Paris (CET) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paris, Thu., Jan. 14 | 2 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. |
| Manchester, Sun., Jan. 17 | 10:30 a.m. | 9:30 a.m. | 8:30 a.m. | 7:30 a.m. | 3:30 p.m. | 4:30 p.m. |

The Paris game is an 8 p.m. start in Paris and the Manchester game is a 3:30 p.m. start in Manchester. Mark both on your [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html) or your [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html).

## Where can you watch the Paris and Manchester games?

NBA.com's key dates list Prime Video for both games. Its "How to watch NBA games in 2026-27" page, published Aug. 13, 2026, says "Prime Video will stream the NBA Paris Game 2027 presented by Tissot on Thursday, Jan. 14," and lists the European games (Jan. 14 in Paris, Jan. 17 in Manchester) under Prime Video. The NBA has not confirmed which broadcaster carries the games in the United Kingdom.

## Are tickets still available for the NBA Paris Game?

The official NBA Paris Game page says "General Sale tickets are now sold out." The NBA has not published a ticket status for Manchester, so check the arena and league pages.

## Which other NBA game is played abroad this season?

The Denver Nuggets play the Indiana Pacers at an arena in Mexico City on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, at 5 p.m. ET on NBA TV, according to NBA.com's key dates; NBA Communications said ESPN carries it in Mexico. The key dates list no London or Berlin regular-season game for 2026-27. Last season the NBA played in Berlin on Jan. 15, 2026, and in London on Jan. 18, 2026.
