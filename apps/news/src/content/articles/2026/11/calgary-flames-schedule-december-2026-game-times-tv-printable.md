**The Calgary Flames play 13 games in December 2026, six of them at the Scotiabank Saddledome, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 5 p.m. Mountain Time (MT), which is 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), at Detroit, and ends Thursday, Dec. 31, at home against Winnipeg.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Calgary, read Nov. 12, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to MT, which is Calgary time, and ET, and worked out the weekdays. Canadian TV is the first channel column and the U.S. listing the second. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Flames December 2026 game times with Sportsnet and U.S. TV

Every game lists a Sportsnet channel in Canada: Sportsnet West, Sportsnet One or Sportsnet, by our count from the feed. Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports is listed for the Dec. 5 game against Washington, and TSN3 for the Dec. 31 game against Winnipeg. In the U.S. column, the market is the one the feed names, "Regional channel" is a local or team outlet the feed lists under a short code, and "None listed" means the feed shows no U.S. broadcaster.

## How many Flames games are at the Scotiabank Saddledome in December 2026?

Six: Dec. 3, 5, 8, 26, 29 and 31. The first three come in one home stand after the opener at Detroit. The Dec. 5 game, at 1:30 p.m. MT (3:30 p.m. ET), is the only home start before 6 p.m. MT in the feed. Our own count from the feed: seven away games.

Vancouver visits on Sat., Dec. 26, and the other side of that game is in our [Canucks December schedule](/news/vancouver-canucks-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/). Buffalo appears twice: at the Saddledome on Dec. 3 and at KeyBank Center on Dec. 15.

## Flames six-game road trip in December 2026

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Six straight away games, Dec. 10 to Dec. 22:** the Islanders (UBS Arena), Flyers (Xfinity Mobile Arena), Sabres (KeyBank Center), Lightning (Benchmark International Arena), Panthers (Amerant Bank Arena) and Stars (American Airlines Center).
- **Back-to-back nights, Dec. 10 and 11:** the Islanders on Thursday and the Flyers on Friday, both at 5 p.m. MT (7 p.m. ET).
- **Earliest start, Sat., Dec. 19:** at Florida, 11 a.m. MT (1 p.m. ET), on Sportsnet.
- **No game:** Dec. 23 to Dec. 25.

The Flyers' month is in our [Flyers December schedule](/news/philadelphia-flyers-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/), and the [Oilers December schedule](/news/edmonton-oilers-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/) has the other Alberta team's.

## Where are the Flames on New Year's Eve 2026?

At home. The Jets visit the Scotiabank Saddledome on Thursday, Dec. 31, at 6 p.m. MT (8 p.m. ET), the month's last game. The feed lists Sportsnet West and TSN3 in Canada and no U.S. broadcaster.
