**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Joel Fagliano.** A black square fills each corner, so the top and bottom rows hold three letters and the three middle rows run the full width. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (3 letters), *Awesome song, informally*: slang for a great track, and also the fruit spread you put on toast.
- **4-Across** (5 letters), *Word after "Midas" or "magic"*: the sense you use with your fingertips.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *World's most populous country*: the South Asian nation whose capital is New Delhi.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Holy person*: someone a church has canonized; it ends in T.
- **8-Across** (3 letters), *Ticker symbol for Harley-Davidson on the N.Y.S.E., appropriately*: a farm animal that bikers also use as a nickname for a big motorcycle.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Biblical whale bait*: the prophet swallowed by a great fish; it starts with J.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Sound*: the word for the sound track of a recording, as opposed to its video.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Hosting, informally*: acting as master of ceremonies, written as two initials with a verb ending added.
- **4-Down** (3 letters), *"___ the season!"*: the old contraction of "it is" from the carol "Deck the Halls."
- **5-Down** (3 letters), *Pharrell's signature item of apparel*: something worn on the head.

## Solving tips

Start with 6-Across, a plain geography fact, and 1-Down, a Bible story most people know. 4-Down runs down the left edge through the first letters of 4-, 6- and 7-Across, and 5-Down runs down the right edge through their last letters, so those two short answers check the three long rows. 3-Down is the trickiest entry: it is an abbreviation turned into a verb.
