**The Chicago Bulls play at Philadelphia on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The NBA lists 13 Bulls games in November, and a one-page printable of all of them is further down.

The source is the NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF from NBA Communications, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." Its times are in ET, so Chicago fans subtract one hour for CT; the table below gives both. Weekdays are our own calculation. The Bulls' previous game was at Boston on Friday, Oct. 30.

## Which Bulls games count in the NBA Cup?

The schedule flags three November Bulls games as Emirates NBA Cup group play: against Atlanta on Fri., Nov. 13, against Washington on Fri., Nov. 20 and at Charlotte on Wed., Nov. 25. A fourth Bulls group game, at Boston, was on Fri., Oct. 30. Group play runs from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, per NBA.com's Aug. 12, 2026 release, with quarterfinals on Dec. 4 and 5 and the championship on Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round.

## Back-to-backs and home stands in the Bulls' November

Reading the dates in the table shows how the month is built. These are our own counts from the NBA's schedule:

- **Back-to-backs:** Fri., Nov. 13 and Sat., Nov. 14 (home, then at Minnesota), and Sun., Nov. 22 and Mon., Nov. 23 (both at home).
- **Two three-game home stands:** Nov. 8, 11 and 13 at the United Center, and again Nov. 20, 22 and 23.
- **Three road games mid-month:** the Bulls play at Minnesota on Nov. 14, at Cleveland on Nov. 16 and at Brooklyn on Nov. 18, between the Nov. 13 and Nov. 20 home games.
- **Thanksgiving week:** the Bulls play at Charlotte on Wed., Nov. 25 and host Indiana on Sat., Nov. 28. They have no game on Thanksgiving Day itself, which is Thu., Nov. 26, 2026. Our [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has the holiday.

Seven home games and six road games make 13 in all, from Nov. 2 to Nov. 28.
