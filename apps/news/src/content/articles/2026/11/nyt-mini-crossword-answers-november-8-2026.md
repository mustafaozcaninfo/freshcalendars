**Across**

- **1-Across** (5 letters), *Thin winter coat?*: the question mark hints that "coat" means a layer of tiny ice crystals on a window or lawn; it starts with F and ends with T.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *When morning people like to wake up*: an adverb for well before the usual hour; it starts with E and ends with Y.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Option on the "How would you like to receive your receipt?" screen*: the digital choice that sends a message to your inbox instead of printing paper; it starts with E and ends with L.

- **9-Across** (5 letters), *Villain's facial expression*: a scornful look with a curled lip; it starts with S and ends with R.

## The coat clue and the shape of the grid

The least literal clue is 1-Across. "Thin winter coat?" does not ask about outerwear. The question mark tells you the word coat is used for a covering that forms on a cold morning, and you can picture it on a car windshield. Solve 1-Down first if you want a start, since its first letter is the same as 1-Across.

The fourth row is the odd one. 8-Across answers a spoken line, so the grid packs several words into one entry; its last letter is also the fourth letter of 5-Down, which gives you a check.

The grid has 25 squares and none are black. Every cell is shared by one Across and one Down answer.
