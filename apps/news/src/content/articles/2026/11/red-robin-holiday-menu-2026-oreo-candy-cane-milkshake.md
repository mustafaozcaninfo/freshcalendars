**Red Robin's holiday drinks run now through Sunday, Jan. 3, 2027, at participating U.S. locations, Red Robin Gourmet Burgers said in a Nov. 3, 2026 release on PR Newswire.** The lineup is a Peppermint Espresso Martini, Santa's Little Helper Dirty Soda and an Oreo Candy Cane Milkshake. From Thursday, Nov. 19, that is 45 days left. The table below shows what is in each drink and which can be spiked, and a second table sorts the gift card terms Red Robin has dated from those it has not.

For how other chains compare, see our [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/). Red Robin gave no drink prices in the release.

## What are the Red Robin holiday drinks for 2026?

| Drink | What is in it | Spike option | Dine-in only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peppermint Espresso Martini | Grey Goose vodka, peppermint, espresso, cold foam, crushed peppermint | Made with vodka | Yes, per Red Robin's holiday page |
| Santa's Little Helper Dirty Soda | Original or Sugarfree Red Bull, raspberry, vanilla, club soda, cold foam, lime wedge | Grey Goose vodka | The spiked version, per the holiday page |
| Oreo Candy Cane Milkshake | Vanilla soft serve, peppermint, Oreo cookies, whipped cream, crushed candy cane | Baileys | Not given |

Source: Red Robin release, Nov. 3, 2026; Red Robin holiday page. The release says the dirty soda "comes with a cherry on top" and invites guests to spike it "for an extra kick." It calls the milkshake "the Oreo fan-favorite" that "is back on the nice list" and says guests can spike it "for an adults-only twist." Red Robin says guests must be 21 or older for alcohol.

## Is the Red Robin Oreo Candy Cane Milkshake back in 2026?

Yes. The release says the Oreo fan-favorite is back on the nice list. It is made with vanilla soft serve, peppermint, Oreo cookies, whipped cream and crushed candy cane. For an adults-only version, guests can add Baileys. Our [Chick-fil-A peppermint milkshake 2026 story](/news/chick-fil-a-peppermint-milkshake-2026/) covers another chain's peppermint shake.

## Are the new Red Robin spiked dirty sodas holiday-only?

No. Nation's Restaurant News reported on Nov. 3, 2026 that the new Spiked Dirty Sodas are available year-round. The release lists four: Coconut Cola with Malibu Rum, Vanilla Dr Pepper with Jack Daniel's, Fruity Fizz with Tito's and Island Breeze with Malibu Rum. Red Robin also offers non-alcoholic bottomless dirty sodas. Only Santa's Little Helper is the holiday dirty soda, and the holiday page lists its spiked version as dine-in only.

## Red Robin gift card bonus dates for 2026

Red Robin's gift card page dates one offer: "Offer valid 11/2/2026 through 12/31/2026, for U.S. online gift card purchases only." Buy $50 in online gift cards in any combination of amounts and get a $10 Bonus Buck. From Nov. 19, that leaves 42 days to Thursday, Dec. 31.

| Offer | Status | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Buy $50, get $10 | Confirmed on the gift card page | Nov. 2 to Dec. 31, 2026, U.S. online purchases |
| Buy $30, get $5 | Listed on the holiday page without dates | Not given |
| Buy $100, get $25 | Listed on the holiday page without dates | Not given |

The holiday page shows all three tiers with an asterisk but no dates, so only the $50 tier is confirmed for 2026 here. The Bonus Buck must be used in one visit. It is not valid on online catering, third-party delivery or buying gift cards, has no cash value and applies at participating U.S. locations. Red Robin does not state the redemption window on the gift card page; the reward carries a printed expiration. Our [restaurant gift card deals story](/news/restaurant-gift-card-deals-2026-bonus-cards-cheesecake-factory-panera-red-robin/) compares Red Robin's offer with other chains.

## What else is on Red Robin's holiday menu?

The release also names Big Yummm Deals, a value menu with meals under $10, dine-in at participating locations for a limited time. Red Robin's holiday page also lists the Prime Rib Stack Burger as a limited-time item. Red Robin has not posted 2026 Thanksgiving or Christmas hours in the sources we read, so check your restaurant before you go.
