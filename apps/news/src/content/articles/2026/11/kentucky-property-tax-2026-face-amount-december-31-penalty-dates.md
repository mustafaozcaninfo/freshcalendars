**The Kentucky Department of Revenue's schedule, tied to Kentucky Revised Statutes (KRS) 134.015, lets property owners pay a bill at the amount printed on it, with no discount or penalty, from Nov. 2 through Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026.** The statute says "all property taxes are due and payable on or before December 31 of the assessment year." Louisville and Lexington sheriffs post different calendars after that, and a $2,000 bill is $420 higher once the 10% penalty and the sheriff's add-on apply.

## Kentucky property tax dates for 2026 bills

The Department of Revenue lists the state schedule in its collection guide, which carries no date. KRS 134.015 sets the same periods in law.

| Period | Dates | What you pay |
|---|---|---|
| Discount | Sept. 15 to Sunday, Nov. 1 | 2% discount |
| Face amount | Monday, Nov. 2 to Thursday, Dec. 31 | Amount on the bill |
| Delinquent | Friday, Jan. 1 to Sunday, Jan. 31, 2027 | 5% penalty |
| Delinquent | After Jan. 31, 2027 | 10% penalty plus 10% sheriff add-on fee |
| Transfer to county clerk | Thursday, April 15, 2027 | Certificate of delinquency |

The Department of Revenue says certificates of delinquency are sold from July 14 through Aug. 28. Its guide lists those dates without a year.

## Lexington and Louisville schedules side by side

Counties set the collection start, so the sheriff of your county is the source for your dates. The Fayette County Sheriff, which serves Lexington, lists dates with years. The Jefferson County Sheriff, which serves Louisville, lists months and days without a year; the dates below apply the sheriff's current schedule to this tax season.

| Period | Fayette County (Lexington) | Jefferson County (Louisville) |
|---|---|---|
| 2% discount | Oct. 1 to Sun., Nov. 1, 2026 | Nov. 1 to Tue., Dec. 1 |
| Face amount | Mon., Nov. 2 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | Wed., Dec. 2 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| 5% penalty | Fri., Jan. 1 to Sun., Jan. 31, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5 to Fri., Feb. 5, 2027 |
| 10% penalty plus 10% add-on | Mon., Feb. 1 to Thu., April 15, 2027 | Sat., Feb. 6 to Thu., April 15, 2027 |
| Unpaid bills | Go to the Fayette County Clerk after April 15 | Go to the county clerk at close of business April 15 |

KRS 134.015 allows the department to set an alternative schedule when the regular one is delayed. Under it, taxes are due two full months after the bills are mailed, and the 2% discount applies within one full month. The Department of Revenue says an alternative schedule gives owners 30 days to pay at each stage when collections start after Nov. 1. Louisville's page does not say why its dates run later, so a Louisville owner should follow that page, not the state's Dec. 31 date.

Fayette's sheriff says bills were mailed in the last week of September 2026 and should have arrived by Oct. 1, 2026.

The Daviess County Sheriff in Owensboro posts a third schedule with no year shown: a 2% discount from Nov. 10 to Dec. 10, the base amount from Dec. 11 to Jan. 10, a 5% penalty from Jan. 11 to Feb. 10 and 21% from Feb. 11 to April 25. Boone, Kenton, Hardin, Warren and other counties publish their own dates, and those were not checked for this story.

## What does a late Kentucky property tax payment cost?

KRS 134.015 sets the 2% discount for payment in full by Nov. 1, no penalty from Nov. 2 to Dec. 31, a 5% penalty in January and 10% after Jan. 31. KRS 134.119 entitles the sheriff to "ten percent (10%) of the total taxes due plus ten percent (10%) of the ten percent (10%) penalty for all delinquent taxes." Adding the three pieces gives 10% + 10% + 1% = 21% of the tax, the figure the Department of Revenue gives from Feb. 1 and Fayette's sheriff gives after April 15. That sum is our calculation from the two statutes. The example uses a made-up $2,000 bill; your bill shows your own amount.

| Payment made | Calculation | Total on a $2,000 bill |
|---|---|---|
| With 2% discount | $2,000 less 2% | $1,960 |
| At face amount | No discount or penalty | $2,000 |
| With 5% penalty | $2,000 plus 5% | $2,100 |
| After the 5% month | $2,000 plus 21% | $2,420 |

Unpaid bills on April 15 move to the county clerk with a lien, per the Department of Revenue, and interest at 1% per month plus fees is added. KRS 134.122 has the sheriff file unpaid claims with the county clerk on April 15 under the regular schedule.

## How do I pay a Fayette County property tax bill?

The Fayette County Sheriff accepts cash, check or money order in person and at Republic Bank lobbies. Online payment by card or direct debit goes through [secure2.kentucky.gov/PropertyTax/Fayette](https://secure2.kentucky.gov/PropertyTax/Fayette), and the sheriff says a convenience fee applies. Checks are payable to "Fayette County Sheriff." Louisville's schedule page does not list payment methods, so the payment options are not covered here for Jefferson County.
