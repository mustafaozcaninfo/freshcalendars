New York is **5 hours behind London** from Nov. 1, 2026 until Sunday, March 14, 2027, then 4 hours behind for two weeks until the UK and European Union (EU) clocks go forward on Sunday, March 28, 2027, and 5 hours again after that, under the U.S. law (15 U.S.C. 260a) and the UK government's clock-change dates. The one-page [US-Europe time difference card](/news/downloads/time-difference-2026-27/us-europe-time-difference-winter-2026-27.pdf) puts the city grid on paper.

Every hour count on this page is our calculation, made with the Time Zone Database of the Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA), the clock data that phones and computers use.

## How many hours behind Europe is each U.S. city?

From Nov. 1, 2026 until March 14, 2027, New York is on Eastern Time (ET), the U.S. is on standard time, and the UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). Central Europe is on Central European Time (CET), which covers Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Rome and Amsterdam. Dublin and Lisbon match London. The table shows hours behind, for three periods.

| U.S. city | London, Dublin, Lisbon | Paris, Berlin (CET) | Athens |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Chicago | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| Denver | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| Phoenix | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| Los Angeles | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| Anchorage | 9 | 10 | 11 |
| Honolulu | 10 | 11 | 12 |

The same figures hold again from March 28 to Oct. 30, 2027, for every city except Phoenix and Honolulu, which sit one hour further behind once Europe moves its clocks (table below).

## Why does New York drop to 4 hours behind London in March 2027?

The U.S. and Europe change clocks on different Sundays. Under 15 U.S.C. 260a, daylight saving time begins "at 2 o'clock antemeridian on the second Sunday of March" and ends on "the first Sunday of November." In 2027 that is March 14 and Nov. 7 (our calculation).

GOV.UK says UK clocks "go forward 1 hour at 1am on the last Sunday in March, and back 1 hour at 2am on the last Sunday in October." Its table lists 28 March and 31 October for 2027. EU Directive 2000/84/EC sets summer time from "1.00 a.m., Greenwich Mean Time, on the last Sunday in March" to the same hour on the last Sunday in October, so all EU countries change at the same moment as the UK.

The gap therefore moves three times in the next year:

| Dates | New York behind London | New York behind Paris, Berlin |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 1, 2026 to March 14, 2027 | 5 hours | 6 hours |
| March 14 to March 28, 2027 | 4 hours | 5 hours |
| March 28 to Oct. 31, 2027 | 5 hours | 6 hours |
| Oct. 31 to Nov. 7, 2027 | 4 hours | 5 hours |

The last row comes from putting the two laws side by side: UK clocks go back on Oct. 31, 2027, a week before the U.S. does on Nov. 7. Our [UK clocks go forward story](/news/uk-clocks-go-forward-2027-march-28-bst/) covers the March 28 change, when the UK moves to British Summer Time (BST) and central Europe to Central European Summer Time (CEST). For the U.S. date, see [when daylight saving time starts again on March 14](/news/clock-changes-2027-when-does-daylight-saving-time-start-again-march-14/).

## How far behind is each U.S. city from March 14 to March 28, 2027?

Daylight saving time has begun in the U.S., but Europe has not changed yet. Arizona and Hawaii do not change, so the gap there stays the same as in winter.

| U.S. city | London, Dublin, Lisbon | Paris, Berlin | Athens |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| Chicago | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Denver | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| Phoenix | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| Los Angeles | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| Anchorage | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| Honolulu | 10 | 11 | 12 |

## What are the hours after March 28, 2027?

Once Europe goes forward, the gaps return to winter values for most cities and grow by one hour for Phoenix and Honolulu.

| U.S. city | London, Dublin, Lisbon | Paris, Berlin | Athens |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Chicago | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| Denver | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| Phoenix | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| Los Angeles | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| Anchorage | 9 | 10 | 11 |
| Honolulu | 11 | 12 | 13 |

## What time is it in London when it is 9 a.m. in New York?

From Nov. 1, 2026 to March 14, 2027, it is 2 p.m. in London. We computed each conversion with the Intl time-zone functions in Node.js, using Dec. 15, 2026 and March 20, 2027 as the test days.

| New York time (ET) | London, Nov. 1 to March 14 | Paris, Berlin | Athens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 a.m. | 2 p.m. | 3 p.m. | 4 p.m. |
| 12 p.m. | 5 p.m. | 6 p.m. | 7 p.m. |
| 5 p.m. | 10 p.m. | 11 p.m. | 12 a.m., next day |

Between March 14 and March 28, 2027, every converted time is one hour earlier:

| New York time (ET) | London, March 14 to 28 | Paris, Berlin | Athens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 a.m. | 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | 3 p.m. |
| 12 p.m. | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | 6 p.m. |
| 5 p.m. | 9 p.m. | 10 p.m. | 11 p.m. |

On the West Coast, 9 a.m. in New York is 6 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) in both periods, so Los Angeles keeps the same 3-hour gap with New York all year (our calculation).

## Does a standing call move when the clocks change?

A call fixed on New York time stays put on the New York clock but lands at a different London hour. A 9 a.m. call is 2 p.m. in London until March 14, 2027, then 1 p.m. for two weeks, then 2 p.m. again from March 28. A call fixed on London time moves the other way on U.S. clocks, so recheck any recurring invite in the weeks of March 14 and March 28, 2027.

## Dates to mark

- Sunday, March 14, 2027: U.S. clocks go forward at 2 a.m.; New York is 4 hours behind London.
- Sunday, March 28, 2027: UK and EU clocks go forward at 1 a.m. GMT; the gap returns to 5 hours.
- Sunday, Oct. 31, 2027: UK and EU clocks go back; New York is 4 hours behind London for one week.
- Sunday, Nov. 7, 2027: U.S. daylight saving time ends; the gap returns to 5 hours.
