**The Douglas County School District (DCSD) in Colorado has Thanksgiving break from Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, winter break from Monday, Dec. 21 to Thursday, Dec. 31, and spring break from Monday, March 15 to Friday, March 19, 2027**, according to the district's "Calendar at a Glance" page. All three are labeled "Schools Closed." The last day of school for students is Thursday, May 27, 2027. This is the Castle Rock, Colorado district, not Douglas County in Georgia or Nevada.

Fall break (Oct. 12-16) is already past. The next day without students is tomorrow: Friday, Nov. 6, a professional development day. This story adds a count of student days off by the page's three labels, weekdays off by month, the weekdays of school between breaks and the 2027-28 dates already posted on the same page. All counts are our arithmetic from the district's dates. A printable school-year calendar is at the bottom.

## Douglas County School District breaks at a glance

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Break (No Students) | Mon.-Fri., Oct. 12-16, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Oct. 19, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving Break (Schools Closed) | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break (Schools Closed) | Mon., Dec. 21 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | 9 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break (Schools Closed) | Mon.-Fri., March 15-19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 22, 2027 |

The page prints no return days. They are the next weekdays that it does not list as off, which is our reading, not the district's. For winter break that matters: Monday, Jan. 4 is "Teacher Work Day (No Students)," and Friday, Jan. 1 is listed only as "New Years Day (District Offices Closed)." ## Which Douglas County holidays does the page leave unclear?

Four holidays carry only "(District Offices Closed)": Labor Day (Mon., Sept. 7, 2026), Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) Day, listed as "MLK Holiday" (Mon., Jan. 18, 2027), Presidents Day (Mon., Feb. 15, 2027) and Memorial Day (Mon., May 31, 2027, after the last day). The page does not say whether students attend on those days, so we list them apart and do not count them as days off. New Years Day (Fri., Jan. 1, 2027) and the Thanksgiving Holiday on Nov. 26-27 carry the same label; the Nov. 26-27 days sit inside the break already marked "Schools Closed." Check with your school before you plan around Sept. 7, Jan. 18 or Feb. 15.

The page and the district's separate [Academic Calendars](https://www.dcsdk12.org/schools-academics/academic-calendars) page also give different board approval dates: "June 18, 2024" on the first and "June 9, 2026" on the second. We use the dates on the "Calendar at a Glance" page and do not choose between the two approval dates.

## How many school days fall between Douglas County breaks?

The stretches below are weekdays between the district's listed days off for students. They include Labor Day, MLK Day and Presidents Day, because the page does not say those are student days off, so each stretch that holds one is an upper bound.

| Stretch | Dates | Weekdays |
|---|---|---|
| First day to Sept. 21 | Aug. 12 to Sept. 18, 2026 | 28 (includes Sept. 7) |
| Sept. 21 to fall break | Sept. 22 to Oct. 9, 2026 | 14 |
| Fall break to Nov. 6 | Oct. 19 to Nov. 5, 2026 | 14 |
| Nov. 6 to Thanksgiving | Nov. 9 to Nov. 20, 2026 | 10 |
| Thanksgiving to Dec. 18 | Nov. 30 to Dec. 17, 2026 | 14 |
| Winter break to Feb. 12 | Jan. 5 to Feb. 11, 2027 | 28 (includes Jan. 18) |
| Feb. 12 to spring break | Feb. 15 to March 12, 2027 | 20 (includes Feb. 15) |
| Spring break to April 23 | March 22 to April 22, 2027 | 24 |
| April 23 to last day | April 26 to May 27, 2027 | 24 |

Jan. 1 is left out of the table because it falls between the Dec. 31 end of winter break and the Jan. 4 work day. The 176 weekdays are not a district count of instructional days; the page does not give one. It also notes that the board may amend the calendar to "Provide additional school days on Saturdays, during vacations, or at the end of the present calendar" if schools must close.

## When does Douglas County School District start winter break and spring break?

Winter break starts Monday, Dec. 21, 2026, after Friday, Dec. 18, a teacher work day with no students. It runs through Thursday, Dec. 31. The page lists Christmas Holiday on Dec. 24-25 as "District Offices Closed" inside the break. The next weekday after Jan. 1 is Jan. 4, a teacher work day, so Tuesday, Jan. 5 is our reading of the return.

Spring break is Monday through Friday, March 15-19, 2027, and no other days off sit beside it in the list. The [March 2027 printable calendar](/march-calendars.html) shows the week. The last day of school is Thursday, May 27, 2027.
