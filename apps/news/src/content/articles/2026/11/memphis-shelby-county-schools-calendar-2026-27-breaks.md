**Memphis-Shelby County Schools (MSCS) students have no school Nov. 23-27, 2026, from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, Mar. 15-19, 2027 and on Fri., Mar. 26 and Mon., Mar. 29, 2027**, by the district's 2026-27 Student Calendar. The last day for students is Thursday, May 20, 2027. Both calendar PDFs carry the footer "This calendar is pending approval by the Tennessee Department of Education," and neither is dated; we found no later version, board vote or state approval.

The next day off is Veterans Day, Wed., Nov. 11. Aug. 3 (first day), Sept. 7 and Oct. 12-19 have passed.

## When are the MSCS breaks in 2026-27?

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates.

| Break | No school | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break (past) | Mon., Oct. 12 to Mon., Oct. 19, 2026 | 6 | Tue., Oct. 20 |
| Thanksgiving break | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring break | Mon., Mar. 15 to Fri., Mar. 19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., Mar. 22 |
| Spring Break II | Fri., Mar. 26 and Mon., Mar. 29, 2027 | 2 | Tue., Mar. 30 |

The district's calendar marks the return from winter break ("Students Return" on Jan. 5, 2027) and the start of Q4 on Mar. 22. The other return days are our reading of the calendar, which marks no closure on those dates. Plan the Thanksgiving week on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html), and compare other districts in our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

## Why does MSCS have two spring breaks?

The student calendar lists a second break, "Spring Break II," on Fri., Mar. 26 and Mon., Mar. 29, 2027. Easter Sunday 2027 is March 28, and Mar. 26 falls on Good Friday. With the weekend, students are out four days in a row, Fri., Mar. 26 to Mon., Mar. 29, and return Tue., Mar. 30. The district does not say why it set the days; the link to Easter is our computation.

The teacher calendar treats the two days differently. Mar. 26 is "Spring Break II (Teachers Out)," while Mar. 29 is a Teacher Stockpile Professional Development (PD) day. The first spring break, Mar. 15-19, ends the week before Q4 begins on Mon., Mar. 22. For other districts' spring weeks, see our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

The calendar prints no early dismissal days and no weather make-up days, so it gives no guidance on how a snow or storm closure would be made up.

## When are MSCS parent-teacher conferences?

The calendar lists conferences from 4 to 7 p.m. by school level. They are not student days off.

| Level | Fall 2026 | Winter 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| High school | Thu., Sept. 10 | Thu., Jan. 21 |
| Middle school | Tue., Sept. 15 | Tue., Jan. 26 |
| Elementary school | Thu., Sept. 17 | Thu., Jan. 28 |

## What do teachers do on MSCS break days?

The teacher calendar marks Nov. 23-25 as Stockpile PD days, inside the week students are out for Thanksgiving. It also lists teacher in-service days inside two student breaks: Oct. 19 and Feb. 15. Check the teacher calendar for your school's rules before planning around these days.

## When does the MSCS school year start and end?

Students started Monday, Aug. 3, 2026, "First Day for Students | Beginning of Q1." Q3 and the second semester begin Tue., Jan. 5, 2027, after winter break, and Q4 begins Mon., Mar. 22. The last day is Thursday, May 20, 2027, "End of Q4/S2."
