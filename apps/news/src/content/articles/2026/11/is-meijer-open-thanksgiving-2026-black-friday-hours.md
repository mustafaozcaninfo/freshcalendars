**Meijer stores are open on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, from 6 a.m. to 5 p.m., according to New Tang Dynasty Television (NTD) and the Gray Television station (WBAY).** Meijer's own hours page returned an access block to automated requests and could not be read, so the hours rest on those two newsrooms. Meijer says it runs more than 500 supercenters, grocery stores, neighborhood markets and express locations across six states.

## Meijer hours and offers, Nov. 22 to Dec. 24, 2026

Weekdays are computed. The Basis column separates what two newsrooms confirm, what one source reports and what Meijer itself published.

| Date | Meijer | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Sun., Nov. 22 to Sat., Nov. 28 | Meijer brand food purchases fund pantry meals, up to 4 million | Meijer release, Nov. 18, 2026 |
| Thu., Nov. 26, Thanksgiving Day | Open 6 a.m. to 5 p.m. | NTD and WBAY |
| Through Thu., Nov. 26 | Meijer brand frozen turkeys 79 cents a pound or less | Meijer release, Nov. 4, 2026 |
| Fri., Nov. 27, Black Friday | 6 a.m. to 12 a.m. | WBAY only; check your store |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | Simply Give Double Match Day | Meijer release, Nov. 18, 2026 |
| Thu., Dec. 24, Christmas Eve | Not announced | See last year's report below |

## What time does Meijer close on Thanksgiving 2026?

At 5 p.m. NTD wrote on Nov. 23, 2026: "According to posted weekly ads, Meijer locations will be open from 6 a.m. to 5 p.m." WBAY, in its Black Friday store hours list updated Nov. 20, 2026, gives the same: "Thanksgiving: 6 a.m. to 5 p.m."

The hours match last year. In 2025, CBS Detroit listed Meijer as open 6 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Thanksgiving. [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26. Other chains are in [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/), [Walmart's hours](/news/is-walmart-open-thanksgiving-2026-black-friday-6-am/) and [Target's hours](/news/is-target-open-thanksgiving-2026-black-friday-hours/).

## Meijer Black Friday 2026 hours

WBAY lists Meijer at 6 a.m. to 12 a.m. (midnight) on Friday, Nov. 27, 2026. No second newsroom confirms it, so treat it as one report and check your store. Meijer's Black Friday ad and sale dates are not announced in the sources used. The days sit on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## Meijer Christmas Eve hours

Christmas Eve is Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026, and Meijer has not announced its 2026 Christmas hours. Last year, Parade reported on Dec. 23, 2025 that Meijer was open on Christmas Eve with many stores closing as early as 6 p.m., and closed on Christmas Day. Those are 2025 reports, not 2026 hours. See the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) for the dates.

## Is the Meijer pharmacy open on Thanksgiving?

Pharmacy hours for Thanksgiving are not posted in the sources used here. Meijer Express and gas station hours are not posted either. Check your store's pharmacy before you need a refill; the printable has a line to write the hours in.

## Meijer turkey price and food pantry dates

Meijer's Nov. 4, 2026 release says: "Meijer brand frozen turkeys for 79 cents a pound or less through Nov. 26." A footnote adds that frozen turkeys are 79 cents a pound with a $25 purchase in Wisconsin. The release says more than 80 Meijer brand holiday items are on sale through Dec. 24.

Meijer's Nov. 18, 2026 release says: "The Thanksgiving purchase initiative runs Nov. 22–28, 2026." Eligible Meijer brand foods "can generate up to 4 million meals" for local food pantries. The same release names the Meijer Simply Give Double Match Day on Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026.
