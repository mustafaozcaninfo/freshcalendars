**The Boston Celtics open December 2026 at home against the Charlotte Hornets on Wednesday, Dec. 2, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The month's headline date is Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, when the Celtics host Miami at 2:30 p.m. ET on ABC and ESPN.

The NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change," gives every game in ET and lists the local time of the home arena. All but one Celtics December game is in Boston, so ET is the local time; the exception is Chicago. The table below covers the 10 December games with set dates, and a printable of the same rows is at the end. Two more Celtics games, unassigned as of the Aug. 13 schedule, fall from Dec. 4 to Dec. 10.

## Celtics games in December 2026, date by date

The NBA's August schedule gives dates for 10 Celtics games in December: eight at home and two on the road. Two more games are unassigned (see below). Weekdays are our own calculation.

| Date | Game | Time (ET) | National TV |

| Wed., Dec. 2 | Charlotte at Boston | 7:30 p.m. | None listed |
| Sun., Dec. 13 | Boston at Brooklyn | 3 p.m. | None listed |

| Wed., Dec. 16 | Washington at Boston | 7 p.m. | ESPN |

| Sun., Dec. 20 | Detroit at Boston | 8 p.m. | None listed |
| Wed., Dec. 23 | Portland at Boston | 7:30 p.m. | None listed |

| Wed., Dec. 30 | Boston at Chicago | 8 p.m. (7 p.m. local, Central Time (CT)) | None listed |

"None listed" means the NBA's schedule shows no national network for that game. Local broadcasts are not in the schedule and are not covered here.

## Celtics Christmas Day game 2026

The Celtics host Miami on Friday, Dec. 25, at 2:30 p.m. ET on ABC and ESPN, according to the NBA's schedule. It is the earliest tip-off of the Celtics' December; the next earliest is the 3 p.m. game at Brooklyn on Dec. 13. For the full holiday slate, see our [Christmas Day 2026 NBA and National Football League (NFL) schedule](/news/christmas-day-2026-games-nba-nfl-schedule-times-tv/) and the [When is Christmas?](/when-is/christmas/) date page.

## Celtics games Dec. 4-10: two dates still unassigned

NBA.com's Aug. 13, 2026 schedule release says the schedule "includes defined dates and opponents for 80 of each team's 82 games." The two unassigned games for each team, to be played from Friday, Dec. 4, through Thursday, Dec. 10, "will be determined based on the results of Group Play games in the Emirates NBA Cup 2026." The Celtics' two dates and opponents are not yet announced. After the Dec. 2 game against Charlotte, the next dated game is Sunday, Dec. 13, at Brooklyn.

The Celtics play in East Group C with the Hawks, Hornets, Bulls and Wizards, and two Group Play games remain: at Washington on Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 8 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock, and Atlanta at Boston on Friday, Nov. 27, at 8 p.m. ET, the Group Play finale, per NBA.com's East Group C preview. Those results feed the Dec. 4-10 games.

The same release puts the Knockout Rounds on Prime Video: quarterfinals on Friday, Dec. 4, and Saturday, Dec. 5, semifinals on Tuesday, Dec. 8, and/or Wednesday, Dec. 9, and the championship on Friday, Dec. 11, at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round.

## Home stand and national TV in the Celtics' December

After the Dec. 13 game at Brooklyn, the schedule has seven straight Celtics home games, from Monday, Dec. 14, through Monday, Dec. 28. The Celtics then close the month on the road at Chicago on Wednesday, Dec. 30, at 7 p.m. local time, which is 8 p.m. ET.

National TV carries three December games: ESPN for Washington on Dec. 16, ABC and ESPN on Christmas Day, and NBC and Peacock for Cleveland on Dec. 28.
