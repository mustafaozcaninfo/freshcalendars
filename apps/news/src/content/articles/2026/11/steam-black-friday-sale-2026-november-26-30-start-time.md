**Steam's Black Friday 2026 runs from Thursday, Nov. 26, at 10 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) to Monday, Nov. 30, at 10 a.m. PT, according to Valve's "Upcoming Steam Events" page for developers, read Nov. 18, 2026.** That is 1 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on both days. Valve says it is not hosting an official Black Friday fest, so there is no store-wide seasonal sale: the Discounts & Events page becomes the hub instead. The start and end in each US zone, the UK, central Europe and Sydney, plus Valve's Steam sale dates to July 2027 and a free printable, are below.

Valve's wording: "From November 26th 10AM PT through November 30th 10AM PT, the Discounts & Events page (which otherwise exists year-round on Steam) will host dedicated theming and artwork, and will receive additional promotion on the Steam storefront."

## What time does the Steam Black Friday sale start in my time zone?

Nov. 26 is Thanksgiving Thursday and Nov. 30 is Cyber Monday, so the window covers exactly four days, 96 hours. US clocks are on standard time on both dates, the UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and central Europe is on Central European Time (CET). Times below are computed from Valve's 10 a.m. PT, with Mountain Time (MT), Central Time (CT), Alaska Standard Time (AKST), Hawaii-Aleutian Standard Time (HST) and Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT) added.

| Time zone | Starts | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | Thu., Nov. 26, 1 p.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 1 p.m. |
| Central (CT) | Thu., Nov. 26, noon | Mon., Nov. 30, noon |
| Mountain (MT) | Thu., Nov. 26, 11 a.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 11 a.m. |
| **Pacific (PT), Valve's time** | **Thu., Nov. 26, 10 a.m.** | **Mon., Nov. 30, 10 a.m.** |
| Alaska (AKST) | Thu., Nov. 26, 9 a.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 9 a.m. |
| Hawaii (HST) | Thu., Nov. 26, 8 a.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 8 a.m. |
| UK (GMT) | Thu., Nov. 26, 6 p.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 6 p.m. |
| Central Europe (CET) | Thu., Nov. 26, 7 p.m. | Mon., Nov. 30, 7 p.m. |
| Sydney (AEDT) | Fri., Nov. 27, 5 a.m. | Tue., Dec. 1, 5 a.m. |

## Is the Steam Black Friday sale an official Steam sale?

Not in the way the Winter Sale is. Valve writes: "Just like in 2025, Steam is not hosting an official Black Friday fest of its own." It adds: "Instead, the Discounts & Events page will function as a hub for Black Friday discounts across all genres."

For developers, Valve says Nov. 26 through Nov. 30 "is still a great option for running discounts; just be aware that the usual discount cooldown applies and is not exempt as are our seasonal sales." In plain terms, each developer chooses whether to discount a game, so not every title will be cheaper, and a game that was just discounted may be held back by the cooldown. Valve has not published discount sizes or a list of games.
