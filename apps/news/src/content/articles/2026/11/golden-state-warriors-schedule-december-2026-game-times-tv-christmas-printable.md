**The Golden State Warriors host Utah on Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026, at 8 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), which is 11 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), at Chase Center in San Francisco, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) official schedule.** That game is on NBC and Peacock.

The NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change," gives each game's ET time and the local time at the home arena. It lists 12 Warriors games in December. The table below converts every tip-off to PT and ET, and to Central Time (CT) for road games in the Central zone, and a printable of the same rows is further down.

## Golden State Warriors December 2026 schedule and game times

Weekdays and the home and away counts are our own calculation from the NBA's PDF. The Warriors are in the Pacific zone, so PT and ET stay three hours apart all month.

## Warriors Christmas Day game 2026 against Denver

The Warriors host the Denver Nuggets on Christmas Day, Fri., Dec. 25, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. PT (10:30 p.m. ET), per the NBA's schedule. It is the only Warriors game on ABC and ESPN in December. For the full holiday lineup across the league, see our [Christmas Day 2026 games guide](/news/christmas-day-2026-games-nba-nfl-schedule-times-tv/), and our [guide to when Christmas falls](/when-is/christmas/) marks the date on the calendar.

## The empty stretch on the Warriors' December calendar

After the Dec. 1 home opener, the NBA's schedule lists no Warriors game until Sat., Dec. 12 at Cleveland. The PDF lists no games for any team from Thu., Dec. 3 to Fri., Dec. 11, 2026, the Emirates NBA Cup knockout window. NBA.com, in its Aug. 12, 2026 group-play announcement, set the Cup quarterfinals for Dec. 4-5 and the championship for Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Whether the Warriors play in that window depends on their group-play results. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) lists the group and knockout dates.

## Warriors road trip and home games in December 2026

By our count, 5 of the 12 games are at Chase Center and 7 are away. The month's longest block is a five-game road trip, and two sets of games fall on back-to-back nights.

| Stretch | Dates | Games |
|---|---|---|
| Road trip | Dec. 12 to Dec. 20 | 5 road games: Cleveland, Memphis, Indiana, Toronto, Milwaukee |
| Back-to-back | Dec. 22 and Dec. 23 | Home against Dallas, then at the LA Clippers |
| Home games | Dec. 25 and Dec. 27 | Denver on Christmas, then Minnesota |
| Back-to-back | Dec. 27 and Dec. 28 | Home against Minnesota, then at Phoenix |

The Warriors close the month at home against Houston on Wed., Dec. 30.

## Warriors national TV games in December 2026

The NBA's PDF lists national TV only: NBC and Peacock for the Dec. 1 home game against Utah and the Dec. 22 home game against Dallas, and ABC and ESPN for the Dec. 25 Christmas game against Denver. The other nine December games show no national network there. Local broadcast details are on the Warriors' official schedule page at [nba.com/warriors/schedule](https://www.nba.com/warriors/schedule).
