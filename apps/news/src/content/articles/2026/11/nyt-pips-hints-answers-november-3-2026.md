## How Pips works

You place every domino so that each colored region meets its condition. A number means the pips in the region add up to it; = means every half shows the same value; ≠ means all are different; > and < set a total above or below a number; a region with no condition takes anything. Rows are counted from the top and columns from the left, so R2C5 is the second row, fifth column.

## Solving tips

Look for single cells with a limit: a cell that must be more than 4, as R1C2 is on Tuesday's Medium board, can only hold a 5 or a 6 there. An "equal" region shrinks your options fast once one value is known; a double such as the 4-4 can supply two matching halves at once. On the Hard board, count the single cells with an exact sum: three need a 0, two need a 2, one needs a 3 and one needs a 6, so seven of the 28 cells are fixed from the start. R1C2 and R1C5 must each be less than 2, which makes them a 0 or a 1.
