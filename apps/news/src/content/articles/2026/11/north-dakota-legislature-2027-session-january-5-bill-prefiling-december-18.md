**North Dakota's 2027 legislative session begins Tuesday, Jan. 5, and legislators must prefile bills by Friday, Dec. 18, 2026, according to the North Dakota Legislative Council's tentative 2027 deadlines.** The council's list has 20 dated rows. The table below gives each with its weekday and legislative day, and a free printable tracker keeps them in order.

The list is marked tentative, so dates can change. Its footer reads "June 2026." A footnote says a Joint Rule (JR) reference to the agency and Supreme Court filing deadline, changed in House and Senate Rules (H/SR) during the 2026 special session, "inadvertently was overlooked," so the Nov. 25 deadline may be revised. The Legislative Assembly's home page also describes the session as tentatively scheduled to run from Jan. 5 to April 30, 2027.

## North Dakota legislative session 2027 deadlines by date

## When do North Dakota bills have to be introduced?

Prefiling closes first: Friday, Dec. 18, 2026 (H/SR 401(3)). Once the session opens on Jan. 5, each representative may introduce no more than five bills after day 3, Monday, Jan. 11, and the representatives' deadline is day 8, Monday, Jan. 18. Senators follow on day 13, Monday, Jan. 25, and after Jan. 18 each senator may introduce no more than three bills. A Senate bill with an appropriation clause cannot be introduced after Jan. 18.

Resolutions have a separate clock: Thursday, Jan. 28 for most, and Friday, Feb. 12 for constitutional amendments and study resolutions.

## What is crossover in the North Dakota Legislature?

Crossover is the date set for bills and resolutions to move from the chamber where they started to the other one. Joint Rule 203 sets it for Friday, Feb. 26, 2027, legislative day 37, and bills and resolutions must be reported out of committee in the house of origin by Tuesday, Feb. 23. The chambers recess March 1 and 2 and reconvene Wednesday, March 3. In the second house, bills and resolutions must be reported out of committee by Wednesday, April 7.

## Why does North Dakota's session open on Jan. 5?

Article IV, Section 7 of the Constitution has the Legislative Assembly meet at the seat of government in December after the election for organizational and orientation purposes, then recess until noon on "the first Tuesday after the third day in January," and no later than Jan. 11. Jan. 3, 2027 is a Sunday, so the first Tuesday after it is Jan. 5. The organizational session runs Tuesday through Thursday, Dec. 1-3, 2026.

## How long can the 2027 session last?

The council's list limits the session to 80 legislative days, ending Friday, April 30. The Constitution says no regular session may exceed 80 natural days during the biennium, and defines a natural day as 24 consecutive hours. Because a legislative day ends at 7:00 a.m. the next day, the two counts are different measures.

For the last session, the Legislative Assembly's page says it convened Tuesday, Jan. 7, 2025 and adjourned Saturday, May 3, 2025. The council's footnote adds that in 2025 the House and Senate were in session on Martin Luther King Day and George Washington's Birthday, and that generally both chambers have met on Good Friday. The list shows those days, Jan. 18, Feb. 15 and March 26, 2027, as legislative days 8, 28 and 55.
