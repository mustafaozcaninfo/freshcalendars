## What do the letter pairs and routes look like today?

Letters on one side cannot touch, which leaves 108 ordered pairs that can. In the word list, 13 of them never occur: F then D, B, H or N; B, T or G then F; B, H or T then G; and L then B, H or R. Seven of the 13 involve the F, so the F is the awkward letter here. Only one word in the list holds each of F then G, H then F, H then N and T then D.

The longest words run 13 letters, and three words tie at that length. No single word covers the box, because the widest touches 9 of the 12 letters. Counting every pair of words that chain and use all twelve letters gives 32 two-word routes.

| Join letter | Two-word routes |
|---|---|
| R | 12 |
| G | 10 |
| E | 5 |
| D | 4 |
| A | 1 |

Those routes use 13 different first words and 21 different second words, and they run from 15 to 21 letters in total.
