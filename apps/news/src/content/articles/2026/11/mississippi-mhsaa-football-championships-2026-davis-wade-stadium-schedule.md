**The Mississippi High School Activities Association (MHSAA) lists its 2026 football championships for Thursday through Saturday, Dec. 3-5, at Davis Wade Stadium on the Mississippi State University campus in Starkville.** Seven games are scheduled, one per classification, and the MHSAA page says "All times are Central." The table converts each kickoff to Eastern Time, and the teams are not announced yet.

## When are the Mississippi MHSAA football championship games?

The MHSAA published the schedule on Oct. 19, 2026. Darkhorse Press reported the same seven times on Nov. 24, 2026. The Eastern column is our addition of one hour to each Central time.

| Day | Class | Kickoff (CT) | Kickoff (ET) | Matchup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 4A | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | Not announced |
| Thu., Dec. 3 | 6A | 7:30 p.m. | 8:30 p.m. | Not announced |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 2A | 4 p.m. | 5 p.m. | Not announced |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 7A | 7:30 p.m. | 8:30 p.m. | Not announced |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 1A | noon | 1 p.m. | Not announced |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 3A | 3:30 p.m. | 4:30 p.m. | Not announced |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 5A | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Not announced |

A July 15, 2026 Clarion Ledger report, carried by Yahoo Sports, said the MHSAA board voted to hold the 2026 Blue Cross Blue Shield of Mississippi Gridiron Classic on Dec. 3-5 in Starkville. That report listed the 6A game at 7 p.m.; the MHSAA's later Oct. 19 page and Darkhorse Press both say 7:30 p.m., which is the time used here.

## Which day does my class play?

- **Thursday, Dec. 3:** 4A and 6A.
- **Friday, Dec. 4:** 2A and 7A.
- **Saturday, Dec. 5:** 1A, 3A and 5A.

Out-of-state relatives should check the time zone first. The schedule is in Central Time, so the Saturday 1A game at noon is 1 p.m. in New York.

## Who plays in the 2026 title games?

The MHSAA page lists no matchups. Darkhorse Press reported on Nov. 24, 2026 that 14 teams will compete for state championships at Davis Wade Stadium and that the teams "will be announced later" that week. The Matchup column stays open until the MHSAA or a newsroom publishes the pairings.

## How to buy tickets and watch the games

The MHSAA says tickets are "Sold on GoFan" and tells fans to buy before game day. Every game is listed as live on the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network. The MHSAA gave no ticket prices, parking details or bag policy in its Oct. 19 page, and no television broadcast beyond that stream.

## What was parking like in 2025?

In 2025, Mississippi State's newsroom said the two lots, "Barnes and Noble Parking Lot – G1" and "Soccer Press Box Parking Lot – G2", would close "at 2 p.m. on Thursday and Friday and at 9 a.m. on Saturday." The 2025 Gridiron Classic was Dec. 4-6. The 2026 parking plan is not announced, so check the MHSAA and the university before you drive.

## Who won in 2025?

A Dec. 7, 2025 report carried by Yahoo Sports said that Raleigh beat Noxubee County 12-6 in the 3A title game. The sources read for this story do not give the 2025 results for the other six classes.
