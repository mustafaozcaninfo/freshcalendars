**Five Below says it will hold one-day, in-store doorbuster deals on Nov. 14, Nov. 27, Dec. 5 and Dec. 12, 2026, with a new mystery dumpling free with any $30 purchase on Black Friday, Nov. 27, while supplies last, according to its Oct. 27 release.** Nov. 14 has already passed. The company has not announced Black Friday store hours or what goes on sale each day. The dated table, a 2025 comparison and a one-page printable follow.

## Five Below doorbuster dates for 2026

| Date | Day | What Five Below published | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 14, 2026 | Saturday | One-day in-store doorbuster deals | Passed |
| Nov. 27, 2026 | Friday, Black Friday | One-day in-store doorbusters, plus a free mystery dumpling with any $30 purchase while supplies last | Upcoming |
| Dec. 5, 2026 | Saturday | One-day in-store doorbuster deals | Upcoming |
| Dec. 12, 2026 | Saturday | One-day in-store doorbuster deals | Upcoming |

The weekdays are our computation. Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, the day before. Our [Black Friday date page](/when-is/black-friday/) confirms Nov. 27, and the [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars have room to mark the days.

## How does the free Five Below mystery dumpling work?

The release says the mystery dumpling is "free with any $30 purchase on Black Friday, while supplies last." It is new this year, tied to Black Friday only, and listed under in-store deals. The release does not say how many dumplings each store gets, whether it is one per customer, or how the $30 is counted. Dumplings are one of the squishy lines the release names in its holiday gift ideas, with Taba-licious and NeeDoh Cool Canes.

## What does Five Below sell on doorbuster days?

The release says shoppers can "stop by in store on each doorbuster day for deals spanning squishies, plush, toys, crafts and more, only available that day." It does not list products or prices for any single day. It says stocking stuffers start at just $1 and that most items are priced between $1 and $5.

The release also names these gift ideas:

- Advent calendars and a giant nutcracker inflatable.
- LEGO sets, ButterLuvs plush, remote-control (R/C) vehicles, Squishmallows, slime, and Fuggler and Sanrio collectibles.
- Peanuts, KPop Demon Hunters, Bluey and the Grinch products.
- Beauty and tech, including speakers and digital cameras.

## Is Five Below open on Thanksgiving 2026, and what are its Black Friday hours?

Five Below has not said. The Oct. 27 release gives no opening or closing times and does not say whether stores open on Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26. The company's own website refused our automated check, so look up your store's page on locations.fivebelow.com for its Black Friday hours. No Cyber Monday or online offer is announced either; the [Cyber Monday 2026 sale times](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/) guide covers other retailers.

## How do Five Below's 2026 and 2025 holiday plans compare?

Five Below's Nov. 6, 2025 release is the source for last year's column.

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Stores | Over 1,900 in 46 states | More than 2,000 in 47 states |
| Doorbuster dates in the release | None named | Nov. 14, Nov. 27, Dec. 5, Dec. 12 |
| Black Friday gift | None named | Free mystery dumpling with $30 |
| Toys for Tots | 16th year | 17th consecutive season |
| Seasonal hiring | 25,000+ crew members | 50,000+ crew members, stores and Ship Centers |

The 2026 release adds specific dates and a Black Friday gift that the 2025 release did not name. The 2025 figures are last year's, not a prediction for this season.

## When can shoppers support Toys for Tots at Five Below?

Five Below says its 17th consecutive season with Toys for Tots runs from Nov. 1 through Dec. 24, with in-store donations at checkout.

## What has Five Below not announced?

- Black Friday 2026 store hours.
- Whether stores open on Thanksgiving Day.
- The products on sale on each doorbuster day.
- Any Cyber Monday or online offer.
