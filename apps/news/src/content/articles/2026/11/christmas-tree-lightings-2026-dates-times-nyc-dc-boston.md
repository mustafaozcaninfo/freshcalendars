## When is the Rockefeller Center Christmas tree lighting in 2026?

NBCUniversal says the lighting is part of "Christmas in Rockefeller Center," which "will air live on Wednesday, Dec. 2 at 8 p.m. ET on NBC" and is simulcast on Peacock. The special runs two hours, so 8 p.m. to 10 p.m. ET. Telemundo airs it live at 9 p.m. ET. NBC-owned television stations add a live hour starting at 7 p.m. ET; the release says to check local listings. It gives no clock time for the moment the tree is lit.

Cynthia Erivo makes her debut as host. NBCUniversal lists Kelly Clarkson, Kirk Franklin, Wynonna Judd, Alanis Morissette, Brad Paisley and Alex Warren among its performers, with more acts to be announced, plus the Radio City Rockettes. The release describes the tree as a 76-foot-tall, 50-foot-wide Norway spruce from West Edmeston, N.Y. amNY and NY1 reported the same tree origin on Oct. 26, 2026, and the tree stays on display through Jan. 9, 2027. Our [NYC holiday calendar](/news/nyc-holiday-calendar-2026-rockefeller-tree-macys-parade/) tracks the rest of the New York season.

## How do you get tickets to the National Christmas Tree lighting?

The NPS says the ceremony takes place Dec. 3 on the Ellipse in Washington, D.C. Tickets came from a lottery on Recreation.gov that opened at 10 a.m. ET on Wednesday, Oct. 28 and closed at 3 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026. There is no charge to receive tickets, and each winner can get up to five. The NPS said it would notify winners on Thursday, Nov. 12, and the lottery is over, so the tickets are not on sale.

The NPS release does not give a start time, and we found none elsewhere. The National Park Foundation's thenationaltree.org says the ceremony premieres nationwide on Saturday, Dec. 5 on the foundation's YouTube channel and will air on other national and local media channels. The page gives public visiting from Dec. 5 in one place and from Dec. 7 in another, running through Jan. 1, 2027, so check it before you go.

## When is the Boston Common tree lighting in 2026?

The City of Boston lists the Boston Common Tree Lighting for Dec. 3, 2026, 6 p.m. to 8 p.m., at 139 Tremont Street, and calls it free. The tree is a gift from the Province of Nova Scotia; the city says 2026 marks the 55th year Nova Scotia has given Boston a tree in thanks for relief efforts. Mayor Michelle Wu hosts with Nova Scotia Premier Tim Houston. At about 8:15 p.m., right after the Common ceremony, the lights on the Commonwealth Avenue Mall are switched on. The city says the arrival of the tree, a 46-foot white spruce, will be celebrated on Boston Common at approximately 11 a.m. on Tuesday, Nov. 24. The 2026 TV broadcast is not yet announced.

For comparison, in 2025 the Parks Department said the lighting was Thursday, Dec. 4, 6 to 8 p.m., with a Channel 5 broadcast from 7 p.m. Treat that as last year's schedule until the city posts a 2026 broadcast.

Copley Square has its own event on Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, 5 p.m. to 6 p.m. at 560 Boylston Street, also free. The city lists musical performances by vocalist Julia Chisholm, the Boston Children's Chorus and Rey Royale, visits from Santa Claus and Rudolph, and the grand reopening of Copley Square Park.

## When is the Capitol Christmas Tree lighting in 2026?

The Architect of the Capitol (AOC) says the lighting takes place during a ceremony Wednesday, Dec. 2, on the West Front Lawn, and gives no start time. The tree, a 76-foot Engelmann spruce from the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests in Idaho, arrived Friday, Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. After the ceremony, the tree is lit each evening from dusk until 11 p.m. through early January 2027, and visiting is free with no ticket. The cross-country trip is in our [Capitol Christmas Tree tour story](/news/us-capitol-christmas-tree-2026-tour-schedule-stops-dates/).

## When is the Chicago Christmas tree lighting in 2026?

The City of Chicago said the Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony was Friday, Nov. 20, 2026. Festivities began at 4 p.m., the ceremony at 6 p.m., and the official lighting, with performances and fireworks, was at about 6:30 p.m. The city's release does not give a cost.

## What is the date of The Grove tree lighting in Los Angeles?

The Grove's site listed its Tree Lighting Celebration for Nov. 16, 2026, hosted by Lance Bass, as part of the 25th annual Christmas at The Grove. The time and ticket terms are not posted.
