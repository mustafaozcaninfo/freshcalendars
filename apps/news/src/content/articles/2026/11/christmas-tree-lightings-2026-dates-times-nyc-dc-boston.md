**The National Christmas Tree lighting is Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026, on the Ellipse in Washington, D.C.**, according to the National Park Service (NPS), and Boston Common lights its tree the same day from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m., according to boston.gov. Rockefeller Center's lighting is reported for Wednesday, Dec. 2, by amNY and NY1.

This story puts the confirmed dates in one table, shows how to attend each in person, and lists the cities that have not posted a 2026 date. For the lottery behind the Washington ticket, see our [National Christmas Tree lighting story](/news/national-christmas-tree-lighting-2026-ticket-lottery-october-28/). ## When is the Rockefeller Center Christmas tree lighting in 2026?

The official Rockefeller Center site was not readable when we checked, so this date rests on two newsrooms. amNY and NY1 both reported on Oct. 26, 2026 that the lighting is on Wednesday, Dec. 2, during NBC's "Christmas in Rockefeller Center." NY1 quotes the line "The tree lighting ceremony will take place on Wednesday, Dec. 2."

Both say the tree is a Norway spruce from West Edmeston, New York. amNY gives its height as 76 feet. The 2026 broadcast time is not yet announced. Our [NYC holiday calendar](/news/nyc-holiday-calendar-2026-rockefeller-tree-macys-parade/) tracks the rest of the New York season.

## How do you get tickets to the National Christmas Tree lighting?

The NPS says the ceremony takes place Dec. 3 on the Ellipse in Washington, D.C. Tickets come from a lottery on Recreation.gov that opened at 10 a.m. ET on Wednesday, Oct. 28 and closes at 3 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026. There is no charge to enter or to receive tickets, and each winner can get up to five. Winners are notified Thursday, Nov. 12.

The NPS release does not give a start time, and we found none elsewhere. The National Park Foundation's thenationaltree.org says the ceremony premieres nationwide on Saturday, Dec. 5 on the foundation's YouTube channel and will air on other national and local media channels, and that the grounds are open to the public from Dec. 7, 2026 through Jan. 1, 2027.

## When is the Boston Common tree lighting in 2026?

The City of Boston lists the Boston Common Tree Lighting for Dec. 3, 2026, 6 p.m. to 8 p.m., at 139 Tremont Street, and calls it free. The tree is a gift from the Province of Nova Scotia; the city says 2026 marks the 55th year Nova Scotia has given Boston a tree in thanks for relief efforts. Mayor Michelle Wu hosts with Nova Scotia Premier Tim Houston. At about 8:15 p.m., right after the Common ceremony, the lights on the Commonwealth Avenue Mall are switched on. The 2026 TV broadcast and the tree's arrival date are not yet announced.

For comparison, in 2025 the Parks Department said the lighting was Thursday, Dec. 4, 6 to 8 p.m., with a Channel 5 broadcast from 7 p.m. Treat that as last year's schedule until the city posts a 2026 broadcast.

Copley Square has its own event on Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, 5 p.m. to 6 p.m. at 560 Boylston Street, also free. The city lists music from vocalist Joelle James, the Boston Children's Chorus and Symphonic of Emerson College, visits from Santa Claus and Rudolph, and the grand reopening of Copley Square Park.

## When is the Capitol Christmas Tree lighting in 2026?

The Architect of the Capitol (AOC) has not announced the lighting date. It says the tree, a 76-foot Engelmann spruce from the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests in Idaho, arrives Friday, Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. The lighting ceremony is on the West Front Lawn. After it, the tree is lit each evening from dusk until 11 p.m. through early January 2027, and visiting is free with no ticket. The cross-country trip is in our [Capitol Christmas Tree tour story](/news/us-capitol-christmas-tree-2026-tour-schedule-stops-dates/).

## What is the date of The Grove tree lighting in Los Angeles?

The Grove's site lists its Tree Lighting Celebration for Nov. 16, 2026, hosted by Lance Bass, as part of the 25th annual Christmas at The Grove. The time and ticket terms are not posted.
