**Fairfax County Public Schools (FCPS) starts the 2027-28 school year on Monday, Aug. 23, 2027, and ends it on Wednesday, June 14, 2028**, according to a July 2026 FCPS bulletin on the School Board's vote. The board approved the 2027-28 and 2028-29 calendars, with 10 votes in favor and one against. FCPS's Standard School Year Calendar page adds the rest: winter break from Dec. 20, 2027 to Jan. 2, 2028 and spring break on April 10-14, 2028. The table below lists every day off, and a free printable school-year calendar is at the bottom.

## Which FCPS days off fall in 2027-28?

The bulletin says the approved calendars "maintain 180 instructional days and 15 professional workdays for staff." Staff-only professional workdays on Aug. 13 and 16-20, 2027 come before the first day. The days below are off for students, with weekdays computed from the dates on the FCPS calendar page.

| Date | Day | What the calendar says |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 3 and 6, 2027 | Friday and Monday | Labor Day break |
| Oct. 11, 2027 | Monday | Holiday: Yom Kippur and Indigenous Peoples' Day |
| Oct. 12, 2027 | Tuesday | Student holiday (professional workday) |
| Oct. 29, 2027 | Friday | Holiday: Diwali |
| Nov. 1, 2027 | Monday | Student holiday (professional workday) |
| Nov. 2, 2027 | Tuesday | Student holiday (professional workday), Election Day |
| Nov. 24-26, 2027 | Wednesday-Friday | Thanksgiving break |
| Dec. 20, 2027 to Jan. 2, 2028 | Monday-Sunday | Winter break |
| Jan. 17, 2028 | Monday | Holiday: Martin Luther King Jr.'s Birthday |
| Jan. 21, 2028 | Friday | Student holiday (professional workday) |
| Feb. 21, 2028 | Monday | Holiday: Washington's Birthday and Presidents' Day |
| Mar. 6 and 7, 2028 | Monday and Tuesday | Student holidays (professional workdays); Mar. 7 is the Presidential Primary |
| Mar. 31, 2028 | Friday | Student holiday (professional workday) |
| Apr. 10-14, 2028 | Monday-Friday | Spring break |
| May 5, 2028 | Friday | Holiday: Eid al-Adha |
| May 29, 2028 | Monday | Holiday: Memorial Day |

Adding the weekdays in the table gives 33 days off for students by our count: 10 in winter break, five in spring break, three at Thanksgiving, two at Labor Day, and 13 other holidays and student holidays. The calendar page does not print a return day after a break; by our reading classes resume Monday, Jan. 3, 2028 after winter break and Monday, April 17, 2028 after spring break. Lay out the holiday weeks on the [December printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## What changes from the 2026-27 FCPS calendar?

The 2026-27 dates come from the same FCPS calendar page and from our story on the [2026-27 FCPS calendar](/news/fairfax-county-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/).

| Item | 2026-27 | 2027-28 |
|---|---|---|
| First day | Mon., Aug. 24, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 23, 2027 |
| Last day | Wed., June 16, 2027 | Wed., June 14, 2028 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 3, 2027 | Dec. 20, 2027 to Jan. 2, 2028 |
| Spring break | Mar. 22-26, 2027 | Apr. 10-14, 2028 |
| Eid al-Fitr | Mar. 9, 2027, a holiday | Feb. 27, 2028, an observance only |

The school year opens one day earlier and closes two days earlier. Winter break is 10 weekdays in both years. Spring break is five weekdays in both, but it moves from late March into mid-April. The Board's bulletin says the approved calendars keep "a full two-week winter break, a full week of spring break" and add "additional full five-day instructional weeks." FFXnow, a local news site, reported on July 15, 2026 that the 2027-28 year has 27 five-day weeks and 2028-29 has 26; FCPS's bulletin does not give those counts.

## Are Veterans Day, Rosh Hashanah and Lunar New Year days off in 2027-28?

No. The FCPS calendar lists four dates as religious and cultural observances only, so they are school days:

- Rosh Hashanah, Saturday, Oct. 2, 2027, falls on a weekend.
- Veterans Day, Thursday, Nov. 11, 2027.
- Lunar New Year, Wednesday, Jan. 26, 2028.
- Eid al-Fitr, Sunday, Feb. 27, 2028, falls on a weekend.

Yom Kippur (Monday, Oct. 11, 2027), Diwali (Friday, Oct. 29, 2027) and Eid al-Adha (Friday, May 5, 2028) are holidays. Juneteenth (observed), Monday, June 19, 2028, falls after the last day.

## Which FCPS days are early release in 2027-28?

The quarter-end days are two-hour early releases, and so is the last day of school, for middle and high school students.

| Date | Day | Early release |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 28, 2027 | Thursday | End of first quarter, 2-hour early release |
| Jan. 20, 2028 | Thursday | End of second quarter, 2-hour early release |
| Mar. 30, 2028 | Thursday | End of third quarter, 2-hour early release |
| June 14, 2028 | Wednesday | Last day of school, two-hour day for middle and high school students |

## When does FCPS start and end school in 2028-29?

The bulletin gives Aug. 21, 2028 to June 15, 2029 for the 2028-29 school year. The FCPS calendar page lists winter break as Dec. 25, 2028 to Jan. 7, 2029 and spring break as March 26-30, 2029.
