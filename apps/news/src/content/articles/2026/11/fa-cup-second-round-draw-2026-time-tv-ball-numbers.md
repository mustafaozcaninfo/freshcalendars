The Football Association (The FA) says the Emirates FA Cup second round proper draw will be made on Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, live on Turner Network Television (TNT) Sports, at approximately 6.45pm Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). That is 1:45 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), a conversion worked out by Fresh Calendars. The 40 ties follow on the weekend of Saturday, Dec. 5.

## FA Cup second round draw at a glance

| Item | Detail | UK time (GMT) | Eastern Time (ET) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draw | Live at Plymouth Parkway | Approximately 6.45pm, Mon., Nov. 9 | Approximately 1:45 p.m. |
| Plymouth Parkway v Berkhamsted | Last of the first round proper fixtures | 7.30pm | 2:30 p.m. |
| Second round ties | 40 ties | Weekend of Sat., Dec. 5 | Weekend of Sat., Dec. 5 |

The FA gives its times in GMT only. The UK is on GMT in November, and US clocks went back on Nov. 1, so ET is five hours behind. The club's name is spelled Berkhamsted in the FA's ball list and Berkhamstead in its venue sentence; the list spelling is used here. For the clubs already through, see the [FA Cup first round fixtures and TV picks](/news/fa-cup-first-round-2026-27-fixtures-tv-picks-kickoff-times/).

## How to watch the second round draw

The FA says the draw "will be available to watch on TNT Sports 1, HBO Max, and the TNT Sports YouTube channel." The page does not list rights by country, so check your own provider before the evening.

## Which ball numbers are still undecided?

The FA's list has 40 numbered entries, from ball 1, Dover Athletic, to ball 40, Salisbury. Five entries named two clubs when the list was published on Nov. 6, because those first round ties had not finished:

| Ball number | Entry as listed by The FA |
|---|---|
| 6 | Oldham Athletic or Sheffield Wednesday |
| 16 | Plymouth Parkway or Berkhamsted |
| 23 | Cray Wanderers or Stockport County |
| 29 | Wimborne Town or Crawley Town |
| 33 | Stafford Rangers or Grimsby Town |

The FA has not named the pairings for those five balls. Each "or" will become one club once its first round tie is played. This page does not reprint the other 35 entries, and the FA's own list is the one to check against.

## When are the 40 second round ties played?

The FA says the 40 ties take place on the weekend of Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026. The day and kick-off time of each tie are not announced. The FA's fixtures page adds that "fixture dates and times may change, subject to TV selections and replays."
