The Football Association (The FA) made the Emirates FA Cup second round proper draw on Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, and 20 ties were drawn for the weekend of Saturday, Dec. 5. Eight of them are live on Turner Network Television (TNT) Sports and HBO Max, two also on BBC Two. The third round draw follows on Monday, Dec. 7, before the Guiseley v Notts County tie.

## How to watch the second round

All eight selected ties are live on TNT Sports and HBO Max: Wimborne Town v Bradford City, Barrow v Kidderminster Harriers and Plymouth Parkway v Barnet on TNT Sports 1, with Guiseley v Notts County and AFC Fylde v Luton Town also on TNT Sports 1, and Crewe Alexandra v Plymouth Argyle on TNT Sports 2. BBC Sport also shows Dover Athletic v Leicester City and Salisbury v Blackpool live on BBC Two and iPlayer, alongside TNT Sports 2. The FA does not list rights by country, so check your own provider. Each club in a televised tie receives a £60,000 fee; second round winners receive £79,500 and losing clubs £21,200 from the prize fund.

## Which clubs filled the five undecided balls?

When The FA published its numbered list on Nov. 6, five entries named two clubs because those first round ties had not finished. The draw report shows which club went through in each case:

| Ball number | Entry on Nov. 6 | Club in the second round draw |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Oldham Athletic or Sheffield Wednesday | Oldham Athletic |
| 16 | Plymouth Parkway or Berkhamsted | Plymouth Parkway |
| 23 | Cray Wanderers or Stockport County | Stockport County |
| 29 | Wimborne Town or Crawley Town | Wimborne Town |
| 33 | Stafford Rangers or Grimsby Town | Grimsby Town |

Plymouth Parkway, a seventh-tier side, beat Berkhamsted 2-1 after extra time on Nov. 9, with Rocky Neal scoring both goals from the penalty spot, according to The FA. The FA's draw report lists the 20 ties and mentions no first round replays.

## When is the FA Cup third round draw?

The third round draw takes place before the Guiseley v Notts County tie on Monday, Dec. 7, 2026, live on TNT Sports and HBO Max. Coverage starts at 6.30pm GMT (1:30 p.m. ET, a conversion worked out by Fresh Calendars) and the match kicks off at 7.30pm GMT. The FA says further details will be announced in due course, so the exact draw time is not yet announced.
