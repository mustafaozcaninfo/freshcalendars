## The forced first move on each Pips board, Nov. 21

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left. A search of every legal placement against the saved data backs each line.

**Easy.** R1C3 must be less than 1, so it holds a 0, and the set has one 0, on the 0-1. That domino's 1 has three possible homes: R1C2, R1C4 or R2C3. R1C2 and R2C3 each belong to a three-cell equal region, which needs a number that shows up on three ends, and the 1 shows up once. So the 1 goes to R1C4.

**Medium.** R4C3 adds up to 6 by itself, and the set has a single end showing a 6, on 1-6. That domino starts on R4C3 and its 1 climbs to the cell above.

**Hard.** R5C2 and R5C3 together must pass 10, so they hold a 6 beside a 5 or a second 6. R5C3 touches only R5C2 and R6C3. Across all 32 arrangements the 6 sits on R5C3 and the 3 that R6C3 needs sits right below it, so the 6-3 goes down first.

## Pips hints for Nov. 21, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 0-1, 4-2, 5-2, 3-2, 4-4)**

- Hint 1: R1C3 must stay below 1, and only one end in the set shows a 0.
- Hint 2: Two regions of three cells each ask for equal values, and only 2 and 4 appear on three ends.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 2-2, 2-4, 4-4, 1-6, 4-5, 1-2, 2-3)**

- Hint 1: R4C3 adds up to 6 on its own, and the set has one end showing a 6.
- Hint 2: R3C1, R3C2 and R4C2 must all differ, though five ends in the set show a 2.

**Hard (14 dominoes on a six-row board)**

- Hint 1: R5C2 and R5C3 must total more than 10, so a 6 is in that pair.
- Hint 2: R5C1 and R6C4 each add up to 2 alone, and five ends in the set show a 2.
