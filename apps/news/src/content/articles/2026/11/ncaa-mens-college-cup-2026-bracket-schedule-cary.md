**The 2026 National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I men's soccer tournament opens Thursday, Nov. 19, and ends with the final on Monday, Dec. 14, at 7 p.m. ET at WakeMed Soccer Park in Cary, North Carolina, according to the NCAA's printed bracket.** Georgetown is the No. 1 national seed. Below: all 16 first-round kickoffs in four U.S. time zones, the 16 second-round hosts with their times, the round dates and a printable watch guide.

## NCAA men's soccer tournament 2026 dates by round

NCAA.com says the field has 48 teams: automatic qualifiers from 23 conferences and 25 at-large selections. The opening four rounds are hosted at campus sites. The semifinals and final are in Cary, and NCAA.org lists the event as "December 11 & 14, 2026" at WakeMed Soccer Park.

| Round | Date | Where | Kickoff (ET) |
|---|---|---|---|
| First round | Thu., Nov. 19 | Campus sites | 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. (table below) |
| Second round | Sun., Nov. 22 | Campus sites | Noon to 7 p.m. (table below) |
| Third round | Sat., Nov. 28 and Sun., Nov. 29 | Campus sites | Not yet announced |
| Quarterfinals | Fri., Dec. 4 and Sat., Dec. 5 | Campus sites | Not yet announced |
| Semifinals | Fri., Dec. 11 | First Horizon Stadium at WakeMed Soccer Park, Cary | 6 p.m. and 8:30 p.m. |
| Final | Mon., Dec. 14 | First Horizon Stadium at WakeMed Soccer Park, Cary | 7 p.m. |

The bracket says the order of the two semifinal games is decided after the quarterfinals. Its printed note reads "All games times are Eastern standard time." Weekdays are computed from the calendar; see the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Where is the 2026 Men's College Cup and are tickets on sale?

The semifinals and final are in Cary, North Carolina. NCAATickets.com says "The 2026 Men's College Cup is heading to Cary, NC on December 11 & 14." The page does not post face-value ticket prices, so none are listed here.

## How to watch the NCAA men's soccer tournament

The NCAA article says ESPNU will provide live coverage of the semifinals and final on Dec. 11 and 14. ESPN Press Room said on Aug. 6, 2026, that "ESPN networks will once again exclusively present the NCAA Div. I Men's College Cup." The NCAA has not listed TV or streaming for the first four rounds on the bracket or in its article. Each host school posts its own stream link, so check the host's athletics site for the Nov. 19 and Nov. 22 games.

## Last year's final: Washington and NC State

In 2025, Washington beat NC State 3-2 in overtime in the final on Monday, Dec. 15, 2025, at First Horizon Stadium at WakeMed Soccer Park, according to an Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) release. That was last year's result. Both teams are in the 2026 field: Washington (10-4-5) hosts Cleveland State and NC State (7-6-6) hosts the College of Charleston in the first round.
