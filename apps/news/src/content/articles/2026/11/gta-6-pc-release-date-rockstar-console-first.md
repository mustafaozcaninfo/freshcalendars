**No PC version of Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI has been announced: Take-Two Interactive's Nov. 9, 2026 earnings release lists the game for "PS5, Xbox Series X|S" on Nov. 19, 2026 and no other platform.** Rockstar Games' own game page lists the same two consoles. Rockstar and Take-Two have given no PC date, no window, no Steam or Epic Games Store listing and no system requirements.

What can be added is history. Take-Two's filings show how long Rockstar's last two big games took to reach PC, and the gaps are worked out below. They are past gaps, nothing more.

## What Take-Two and Rockstar list for Nov. 19

| Source | What it says | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Take-Two Interactive, fiscal second quarter 2027 results (filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)) | Release table row: "Rockstar Games, Grand Theft Auto VI, PS5, Xbox Series X\|S, November 19, 2026". No PC row | Nov. 9, 2026 |
| Rockstar Games, game page | "Coming November 19, 2026", with PS5 and Xbox Series X\|S listed. No PC | Read Nov. 18, 2026 |
| Xbox Wire (Microsoft) | Cloud play on Xbox One and Xbox Series X\|S starting at 1 a.m. ET on Nov. 19 | Nov. 10, 2026 |
| PlayStation (US) | Released for PlayStation 5 and PS5 Pro on Nov. 19, 2026 | Read Nov. 18, 2026 |

The [release countdown story](/news/gta-6-october-22-2026-what-is-confirmed-release-countdown/) covers the other confirmed dates.

## How long Rockstar's last releases took to reach PC

| Game | Console launch | PC launch | Gap (our arithmetic) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grand Theft Auto V | September 2013 (PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360) | April 2015 | 19 months | Take-Two annual report filed May 22, 2015 |
| Grand Theft Auto V, from its PlayStation 4 and Xbox One release | November 2014 | April 2015 | 5 months | Take-Two annual report filed May 22, 2015 |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | Oct. 26, 2018 | Nov. 5, 2019 | 375 days, a little over 12 months | Take-Two annual report filed May 14, 2019; Rockstar Newswire and Video Games Chronicle, Oct. 4, 2019 |

Take-Two's 2015 report says Grand Theft Auto V was released on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in September 2013, "on Sony's PlayStation 4 and Microsoft's Xbox One in November 2014, and on the PC in April 2015." The 2019 report says "On October 26, 2018, Rockstar Games launched Red Dead Redemption 2." On Oct. 4, 2019, Rockstar's Newswire headline read "Red Dead Redemption 2 Coming to PC November 5th," and Video Games Chronicle reported "The PC version will launch on November 5."

## What those gaps are and are not

They are two data points from earlier games. Rockstar and Take-Two have announced no PC version, date or window for Grand Theft Auto VI, and the gaps do not predict one.

Applied mechanically, and only as arithmetic, 375 days after Nov. 19, 2026 is Nov. 29, 2027, and 19 months after November 2026 is June 2028. Those are not forecasts and no source gives either date. A PC version could come on a different schedule, or not at all.

## Can a PC stream GTA 6?

Xbox Wire says the game will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, starting at 1am ET. The post names those consoles and does not mention streaming to a PC, so cloud play on a PC is not announced. Microsoft adds that cloud availability, supported devices and features "may vary by market and device."

PlayStation's US game page lists "Remote Play supported" without further detail. That page is for PS5 and PS5 Pro. More on the Xbox side is in the [Xbox One cloud gaming story](/news/gta-6-xbox-one-cloud-gaming-ps4-platforms/).

## What is not announced

- A PC version, its date or a window
- A Steam or Epic Games Store listing
- PC system requirements
- A Nintendo Switch 2 version
- Cloud play on a PC
