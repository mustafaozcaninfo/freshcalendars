**IHG Hotels & Resorts' Cyber Sale takes bookings through Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026, Wyndham Rewards' sale through Friday, Dec. 4, and Marriott Bonvoy's sale ends Tuesday, Dec. 1, according to each company's own release or sale page.** IHG and Wyndham offer up to 30% off. The table lists each chain's discount, book-by date, stay-by date and promo code, and a printable carries the same rows.

## Hotel Black Friday and Cyber Week deals by chain

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, Cyber Monday is Monday, Nov. 30 and Travel Tuesday is Tuesday, Dec. 1; see [when is Cyber Monday](/when-is/cyber-monday/). Days left are counted from Sunday, Nov. 22. Weekdays are computed.

The discount is "up to"; participating hotels and terms vary.

## How long can you wait to book a hotel Cyber Week deal?

Marriott closes first. Its release says the Cyber Week Sale "runs from November 24 through Travel Tuesday, December 1, 2026," nine days from Nov. 22. IHG follows on Dec. 3, then Wyndham on Dec. 4.

The stay windows differ by months. IHG's release says "All stays must be completed by April 30, 2027." Marriott's app rate covers stays "from November 29, 2026, through May 2, 2027." Wyndham's page says "Stays at participating hotels in the U.S. and Canada must be completed by July 31, 2027," with Caribbean and Latin America stays by Oct. 31, 2027.

## What is IHG's Cyber Sale 2026?

IHG's Nov. 17, 2026 release says "IHG One Rewards members can save up to 30% on bookings at participating IHG Hotels & Resorts properties," and "Everyone Else Saves 20% Globally." Bookings run "from November 17 to December 3," and the offer applies "when booked by Dec. 3, 2026 for travel through April 2027." The release lists no promo code.

## What is Wyndham's Cyber Week Flash Sale?

Wyndham's page says "Save up to 30% (or More!)," scaled by length of stay: 10% for one night, 15% for two, 20% for three and 30% for four or more. Wyndham Rewards Earner cardmembers "save an extra 5%." Guests must "book the Wyndham Rewards Rate or Insider Rate at participating hotels by Dec. 4, 2026." Booking opened Nov. 19, 2026. The page lists no promo code, and UpgradedPoints reported on Nov. 19 that "there's no promo code required for this offer."

## What is Marriott Bonvoy's Cyber Week Sale?

Marriott International's Nov. 12, 2026 release says Bonvoy members booking in the app "can save 25% on stays from November 29, 2026, through May 2, 2027." The release also says "members and nonmembers who book on Marriott.com can save 15%" and mentions no promo code. UpgradedPoints (Nov. 17, 2026) and Milesopedia (Nov. 16, 2026) reported that no code is needed. The [Travel Tuesday 2026 story](/news/travel-tuesday-2026-december-1-hopper-travel-deal-tuesday/) has the sale beside Hopper's and eDreams' dates.

### How does Marriott's 2026 sale compare with 2025?

In 2025, Marriott said in a Nov. 12, 2025 release that its sale ran Nov. 25 through Dec. 2, 2025, with 25% off in the app, 20% for members online and 15% for non-members, for stays Nov. 30, 2025 to Feb. 22, 2026. The 2026 stay window, to May 2, 2027, is longer.

## What about Hilton and Hyatt?

Hilton's offers page shows "Save up to 20% + earn Bonus Points" and "The Hilton Sale is on!" with no booking or stay dates, so Hilton has not posted dates for it. Hyatt has not announced a 2026 offer as of Nov. 22. In 2025, Hyatt said in a Nov. 13, 2025 release that members in the Americas could save up to 30% "when booked by December 11, 2025" for travel between Nov. 20, 2025 and April 30, 2026.
