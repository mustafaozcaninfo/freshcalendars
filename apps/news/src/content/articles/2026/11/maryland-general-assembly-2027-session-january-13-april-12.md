**The Maryland General Assembly convenes in Annapolis at noon on Wednesday, Jan. 13, 2027, and adjourns sine die on Monday, April 12, according to the Maryland Department of Legislative Services (DLS).** The Maryland State Archives' Maryland Manual Online lists the 451st session as running "January 13 - April 12, 2027." The DLS "Dates of Interest" sheet, stamped Sept. 8, 2026, numbers every day of the 90-day session.

## Maryland General Assembly 2027 session dates by legislative day

Jan. 13 is legislative day 1 and April 12 is day 90, so weekends count. The table repeats the DLS wording, with capitals lowered where DLS prints them; the day numbers are DLS's where it prints them.

| Date | Weekday | Day | What happens (DLS wording) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 20, 2026 | Friday | before | "Deadline for Requesting Bills for Prefile" |
| Dec. 10, 2026 | Thursday | before | "Deadline for Introduction of Bills for Prefile" |
| Jan. 13, 2027 | Wednesday | 1 | General Assembly convenes (noon, Wednesday) |
| Jan. 20 | Wednesday | 8 | "Inauguration of the Governor and the Lieutenant Governor (Wednesday)" |
| Jan. 22 | Friday | 10 | "Final date for the Governor to introduce budget bill"; also the capital budget bill, the Senate and House bill request guarantee date and the final date for executive orders reorganizing the Executive Branch |
| Jan. 25 | Monday | 13 | "Administration bills introduced in the Senate after this date referred to Senate Rules Committee" |
| Feb. 8 | Monday | 27 | Senate bill introduction date |
| Feb. 11 | Thursday | 30 | "Day before House Bill Introduction Date; 'Hopper' will close at 5:00 P.M." |
| Feb. 12 | Friday | 31 | House bill introduction date |
| Feb. 21 | Sunday | 40 | "'Green Bag' appointments submitted by Governor (Delivered on Friday, February 19)" |
| Mar. 8 | Monday | 55 | "Final date for introduction of bills and LBIs without suspension of the Rules" |
| Mar. 16 | Tuesday | 63 | "Committee Reporting Courtesy Date" |
| Mar. 22 | Monday | 69 | "Opposite Chamber Bill Crossover Date" |
| Apr. 5 | Monday | 83 | "Budget bill to be passed by both Chambers" |
| Apr. 12 | Monday | 90 | Adjournment sine die (Monday) |
| May 2 | Sunday | after | "20th Day after adjournment — Final date for presentment of bills to the Governor" |
| May 12 | Wednesday | after | "Final date for an extended session" |

LBIs are legislative bond initiatives. The State of the State Address (at noon) and the election of the State Treasurer are listed as to be determined, so the date is not yet set.

## When are Maryland bills due for introduction?

Senate bills introduced after Monday, Feb. 8 (day 27) are referred to the Senate Rules Committee. House bills introduced after Friday, Feb. 12 (day 31) are referred to the House Rules and Executive Nominations Committee. The DLS sheet cites Senate Rule 32(b) and House Rule 32(b) for those two referrals. The House "hopper" closes at 5:00 p.m. on Thursday, Feb. 11, the day before.

Bills can still be introduced after those dates only with the rules suspended, up to Monday, March 8 (day 55), when DLS lists the "final date for introduction of bills and LBIs without suspension of the Rules." That follows Article III, Section 27, which bars introducing a bill "during the last thirty-five calendar days" without suspension.

## What is the Maryland crossover date in 2027?

The crossover date is Monday, March 22, 2027 (day 69). DLS describes it this way: "Each Chamber to send to other Chamber those bills it intends to pass favorably." Two weeks later, on Monday, April 5 (day 83), the budget bill is to pass both chambers. Sine die follows a week after that.

## How does Maryland's 2027 calendar compare with 2026?

The DLS 2026 sheet for the 449th session and the Maryland Manual give "January 14 - April 13, 2026." Every main date in 2027 is one day earlier on the calendar.

| Milestone | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| General Assembly convenes | Wed., Jan. 14 | Wed., Jan. 13 |
| Senate bill introduction | Feb. 9 | Mon., Feb. 8 |
| House bill introduction | Feb. 13 | Fri., Feb. 12 |
| Day 55, introduction without suspension | Mar. 9 | Mon., Mar. 8 |
| Crossover | Mar. 23 | Mon., Mar. 22 |
| Sine die | Mon., Apr. 13 | Mon., Apr. 12 |

The budget deadline also moves. In 2026 the rule was the third Wednesday in January, Jan. 21, 2026. For 2027 DLS lists Friday, Jan. 22 as the final date for the Governor to introduce the budget bill.

## Why does the session open on Jan. 13 and last 90 days?

The DLS sheet quotes Article III, Section 14 of the Maryland Constitution, which has the General Assembly convene on the "second Wednesday of January." Article III, Section 15(1) sets a session of "not longer than ninety days," which the General Assembly or the Governor can extend by up to thirty days. DLS lists May 12 as the final date for an extended session. The Governor's term commences "on the third Wednesday of January next ensuing his election" (Article II, Section 1), which gives Wednesday, Jan. 20 for the inauguration, as DLS lists. A newly elected Governor must submit the budget "not later than ten days after the convening of the General Assembly" (Article III, Section 52(3)); DLS gives Jan. 22 as the final date.

## What happens after sine die?

Under Article III, Section 30, bills must be presented to the Governor "no later than 20 days after adjournment," which DLS puts at Sunday, May 2. After that, DLS lists:

| Date | What takes effect |
|---|---|
| Tue., June 1 | Earliest date for non-emergency bills to take effect |
| Thu., July 1 | "Budgetary, tax, and revenue bills to take effect" |
| Fri., Oct. 1 | "Usual effective date for bills" |

## When can bills be prefiled for 2027?

DLS's prefiling sheet sets two dates before the session: "Friday, November 20, 2026 – Deadline for Requesting Bills for Prefile" and "Thursday, December 10, 2026 – Deadline for Introduction of Bills for Prefile." Both fall weeks before the session opens on Wednesday, Jan. 13.
