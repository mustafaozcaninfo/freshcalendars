## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *Answer that shouldn't lead off the Acrosses?*: a word for the opposite list of clues, and the opposite of up; it starts with D, the first letter of 1-Down, and ends with N, the first letter of 4-Down.
- **5-Across** (4 letters), *Big name in smart rings*: a wellness-tracker brand worn on a finger; it starts with O, the second letter of 1-Down, and ends with A, the second letter of 4-Down.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Silences notifications from, as a text thread*: a verb in the third person singular; it starts with the first letter of 6-Down, has T in the middle as the third letter of 2-Down, and ends with the last letter of 4-Down.
- **7-Across** (4 letters), *Actor Baldwin of "30 Rock"*: the actor's first name; it starts with A, the middle letter of 6-Down, and ends with C, the fourth letter of 3-Down.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *What "P" stands for in a Prius*: the gear on the shifter you use when the car is not moving; it starts with the last letter of 6-Down and ends with the last letter of 3-Down.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Childbirth support person*: a trained helper who stays with a parent during labor; it starts with D, the first letter of 1-Across, and ends with A, the second letter of 8-Across.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *North Carolina's ___ Banks*: the word that fills the blank before the barrier islands; it starts with O, the second letter of 1-Across, and ends with R, the third letter of 8-Across.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Total emotional mess*: slang for a person who has fallen apart; it starts with W, the third letter of 1-Across, and ends with K, the last letter of 8-Across.
- **4-Down** (3 letters), *"Illmatic" rapper*: a New York artist known by a three-letter stage name; it starts with N, the last letter of 1-Across, and ends with S, the last letter of 6-Across.
- **6-Down** (3 letters), *Helpful diagram at a mall*: the "you are here" sign; it starts with M, the first letter of 6-Across, and ends with P, the first letter of 8-Across.

## How the clues sort on Wednesday

The 10 clues were sorted by hand from the clue text in the saved data into four types, one type per clue.

| Clue type | Clues | Entries |
|---|---|---|
| Fill-in-the-blank | 1 | 2-Down |
| Question-mark wordplay | 1 | 1-Across |
| Proper-noun clue (a person, brand or artist) | 3 | 5-Across, 7-Across, 4-Down |
| Plain definition or description | 5 | 6-Across, 8-Across, 1-Down, 3-Down, 6-Down |

Half the clues ask for a definition. The answers to three others have to be known rather than worked out, so a solver who does not recognize the ring brand can rely on the crossings. 8-Across looks like a definition but works as a trivia clue about a car's shifter, with the answer being a gear position.

The clue with the least literal wordplay is 1-Across, *Answer that shouldn't lead off the Acrosses?* The question mark signals that the clue describes the answer's job in the puzzle, not a thing in the world. It does not define the word. It tells you the word belongs to a list other than the one it is sitting in. Read it as a riddle: which word, among the labels solvers see on screen, would be wrong at the top of the Across list? The crossings help here, because 1-Down and 4-Down both start in this row.
