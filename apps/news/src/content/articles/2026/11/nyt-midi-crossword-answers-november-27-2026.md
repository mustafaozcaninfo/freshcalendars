## How does "Second Helpings" connect to Thursday's Midi?

Comparing the two saved grids square by square shows a literal repeat in the frame. The 11 black squares sit exactly where Thursday's did, so the clue numbers, the entry lengths and the shape of every slot match: seven 3-letter, fourteen 4-letter, six 5-letter, one 6-letter and three 9-letter entries on both days. What changes is the lettering, since only 14 of the 70 letter squares keep Thursday's letter.

Three long entries share letters with their Thursday counterparts:

- **11-Across**: the last five squares of row 3 hold the same five letters as Thursday's 11-Across, in the same squares, though today's clue is about golf and has nothing to do with the holiday.
- **15-Across**: the first three letters match Thursday's 15-Across in the same squares.
- **21-Across**: the clue itself ends "like from yesterday's Midi?" The two 9-letter entries share only their final letter.

The data stores letters, numbers and clue text, with no shading, so it cannot say which squares are shaded. What it does show is the overlap above, and no whole answer from Thursday returns today.

## Which clue is the least literal today?

2-Down, *Animals that can take your breath away?* The question mark flags wordplay. One reading is awe at wildlife; the other is a physical one, where the animals leave you short of air. The second reading is the one the grid wants, and the four squares it fills are a plural noun. If you have 2-Down's top letter, the Across entry at 1 will cross it and agree.
