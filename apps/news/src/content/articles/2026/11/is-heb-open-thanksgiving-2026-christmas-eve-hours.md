**Most H-E-B stores open at 6 a.m. and close at noon Central Time on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, according to Fox 7 Austin, Austinot and Fox 4 Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).** H-E-B's own pages had not posted 2026 hours we could read as of Nov. 21, so every 2026 hour below comes from those newsrooms, not from the company.

## Is H-E-B open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Yes, for six hours. Fox 7 Austin wrote on Nov. 19, 2026 that most Austin-area stores will open at 6 a.m. and close at noon. Austinot, on Nov. 20, said they "open at 6:00 AM and close promptly at 12:00 PM." Fox 4 reported the same hours for DFW. Fox 26 Houston said H-E-B "closes early at noon" and gave no opening time, so the Houston start is not confirmed here. San Antonio hours are not in any source we found. For other chains, see [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/); the date itself is on the [Thanksgiving page](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

## Is the H-E-B pharmacy open on Thanksgiving?

No. Fox 7 Austin says "Pharmacy counters will be closed," and Austinot says in-store pharmacies are closed all day. Both reports cover the Austin area.

## H-E-B curbside and delivery hours on Thanksgiving

Fox 4 reports curbside pickup in DFW from 7 to 11 a.m. and home delivery from 7 to 10 a.m. Austinot gives Austin curbside as 7 a.m. to 11 a.m. and home delivery as 7 a.m. to 10 a.m. In both metros, curbside ends an hour before the noon store close, and delivery ends two hours before it.

## Central Market Thanksgiving hours

Fox 4 reports Central Market stores in DFW open 8 a.m. to 2 p.m., "according to the company's published Thanksgiving schedule." Austinot lists the North Lamar and Westgate stores in Austin at the same 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. In 2025, Fox 4 reported the same 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. for Central Market and 6 a.m. to noon for Mi Tienda. Mi Tienda hours for 2026 are not announced.

H-E-B has not announced Christmas hours that we could find. The 2025 values come from Spectrum News (South Texas and El Paso), published Dec. 23, 2025.
