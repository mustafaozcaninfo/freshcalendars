**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, is a 5-by-5 grid with 10 clues, and the official data credits Joel Fagliano.** Every hint opens on a tap. Two pairs of black squares trim the corners, so most entries are short and the middle row runs edge to edge.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (4 letters), *"Should that be the case ..."*: a conditional opener made of two two-letter words run together; its first letter starts 1-Down and its last letter starts 4-Down.
- **5-Across** (4 letters), *Two cents, so to speak*: the thing you offer when you weigh in uninvited; a window gives you one too; it starts with the second letter of 1-Down and ends with the second letter of 4-Down.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Culminating test*: the exam that closes a course, and also the name of a tournament's last round; it starts 6-Down and ends 4-Down.
- **7-Across** (4 letters), *A couple of bucks, perhaps*: read "bucks" as males of a hooved woodland animal, not money, and give that animal in the singular; it starts with the second letter of 6-Down and ends with the fourth letter of 3-Down.
- **8-Across** (4 letters), *Throws into the mix*: a verb for putting more in the pot, and the job a plus sign does in a sum; it starts with the last letter of 6-Down and ends with the last letter of 3-Down.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (5 letters), *Covered in vines, like old college buildings*: an adjective made from a climbing plant's name plus -ED; it starts with the first letter of 1-Across and ends with the second letter of 8-Across.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Punished for speeding*: a past-tense verb for being made to pay a penalty on the road; it starts with the second letter of 1-Across and ends with the third letter of 8-Across.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *In 1991, Walmart overtook this store as the nation's largest retailer*: a department store name that opens and closes with the same letter; it starts with the third letter of 1-Across.
- **4-Down** (3 letters), *Bird that can hunt in total darkness*: a night-flying bird of prey, famous for its hoot; it starts with the last letter of 1-Across and ends with the last letter of 6-Across.
- **6-Down** (3 letters), *Medicine-approving org.*: a three-letter initialism for an agency, with a vowel at the end; it starts with the first letter of 6-Across and ends with the first letter of 8-Across.

## The bucks clue and the grid's layout

The least literal clue today is 7-Across. Nothing in the printed words points to an animal: "A couple of bucks" reads first as a small sum of money, and "perhaps" is the setter's wink that the obvious sense is a decoy. Once you hear "bucks" as the male of a species, the plural turns into "a couple of" one creature and the answer drops to a single word of four letters. The other nine clues are direct definitions, a fill-in prompt or an abbreviation tag.

The layout tells you where to begin. The four black squares sit in the left column (rows 1 and 2) and the right column (rows 4 and 5), which cuts 4-Down to three letters at the top right and 6-Down to three letters at the bottom left.

Crossings per entry, from the grid:

- **1-Across, 5-Across, 7-Across and 8-Across** (4 letters each): 4 crossings apiece.
- **6-Across** (5 letters): 5 crossings, with every Down entry.
- **1-Down, 2-Down and 3-Down** (5 letters each): 5 crossings apiece, with every Across entry.
- **4-Down and 6-Down** (3 letters each): 3 crossings apiece.

Four entries tie at five crossings: 6-Across and the three long Downs. Of those, 6-Across has the plainest clue, and it is the only Across entry that supplies a letter to every Down entry. The two 3-letter Downs touch only three others each, so they are better as finishers.
