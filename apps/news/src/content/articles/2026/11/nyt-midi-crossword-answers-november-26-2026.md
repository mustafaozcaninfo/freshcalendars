**Thursday's New York Times (NYT) Midi Crossword, dated Nov. 26, 2026, is titled "Talkin' Turkey" and has 31 clues on a 9-by-9 grid by Ian Livengood.** It lands on Thanksgiving Day, and each clue has a hint behind a tap above the marked answer section.

## What does the "Talkin' Turkey" title tell you about the grid?

The title names no answer, so the clues carry the holiday. One Across clue mentions tryptophan. Another speaks of Thanksgiving side dishes and then adds "interpreted differently," with a reference to shaded squares and their full entries. The saved data holds the words and letters of the grid but no shading, so the clue text is all we have to go on for that part. Two of the three 9-letter entries run Across through rows 3 and 7, each with no black square in it, and the clue about shaded squares belongs to the lower one. Every Down entry that reaches row 3 or row 7 crosses one of those two long rows.

## Which clue is the least literal today?

21-Across, *Thanksgiving side dishes ... or, interpreted differently, the shaded squares vis-à-vis their full entries*. It is a clue with two jobs. Read it once as an ordinary definition and you have a 9-letter answer for what sits next to the bird. Read it a second time and the clue asks how the shaded squares relate to the whole entry. The answer does not change between the two readings; only the way you look at it does. Count the letters, check the first and last, and the crossing Down entries in all nine columns will confirm it.
