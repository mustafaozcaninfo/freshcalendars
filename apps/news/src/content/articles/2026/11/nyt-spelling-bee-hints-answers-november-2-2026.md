**Monday's New York Times (NYT) Spelling Bee for Nov. 2, 2026 sets D at the hub and circles it with A, C, E, T, V and X.** The letters V and X are the unusual guests, and they steer the whole solve toward 35 words and 176 points behind a single pangram. Hints, counts and one clue come first, with the answers held back for the spoiler section.

Genius needs 123 points, and the full rules are on our [Spelling Bee page](/news/topic/nyt-spelling-bee/#how-it-works).

## A clue for the pangram

- **Pangram** (9 letters, starts with E): to dig something out of the ground, the way an archaeologist clears a buried ruin; the second letter is X and two A letters appear.

This is a verb-heavy board. Of the 35 answers, 26 end in -ed, so nearly every find is a past-tense form, and the longer -ed verbs are where the score lives. Only eight words run just four letters, yet Genius still asks for 123 points. Watch for doubles as well: eleven answers repeat a letter back to back, and six squeeze in three of a single letter.
