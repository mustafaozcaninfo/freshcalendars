**Stellar Blade Complete Edition launches on Nintendo Switch 2 at 10 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, according to a Shift Up Corp. press release dated Sept. 10.** The company gives the moment as 00:00 Korea Standard Time (KST) and Japan Standard Time (JST) on Nov. 6, so the game carries two dates depending on where you live. The list price is $59.99.

## Stellar Blade Switch 2 release time by time zone

Shift Up describes a "simultaneous worldwide release" at "6 November 2026 at 00:00 KST/JST (5 November, 07:00 PST / 15:00 GMT)." That is one instant. We converted it for the other zones below. U.S. clocks went back on Nov. 1, so every U.S. zone is on standard time, and the UK is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT).

| Region | Launch time | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Hawaii (HST) | 5:00 a.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| Alaska (AKST) | 6:00 a.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| Pacific (PST) | 7:00 a.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| Mountain (MST) | 8:00 a.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| Central (CST) | 9:00 a.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| Eastern (EST) | 10:00 a.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| UK (GMT) | 3:00 p.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| Central Europe (CET), such as Paris or Berlin | 4:00 p.m. | Thu., Nov. 5 |
| Korea (KST) | 12:00 a.m. | Fri., Nov. 6 |
| Japan (JST) | 12:00 a.m. | Fri., Nov. 6 |
| Sydney, Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT), UTC+11 | 2:00 a.m. | Fri., Nov. 6 |

The 07:00 Pacific Standard Time (PST) and 15:00 GMT rows come from Shift Up. The other rows are our conversions from 00:00 KST. Nov. 5, 2026 is a Thursday and Nov. 6 is a Friday.

## Why Stellar Blade is dated Nov. 5 and Nov. 6

The two dates describe the same moment. Midnight on Nov. 6 in Korea and Japan falls on the afternoon or morning of Nov. 5 in the Americas and Europe. Nintendo UK's store page lists the release date as 5 November 2026, while Gematsu, reporting the announcement on Sept. 9, headlined it as November 6. Both are consistent with Shift Up's 00:00 KST and JST time. If you are in the Americas, plan for Thursday.

## Stellar Blade Switch 2 price and what is included

Shift Up lists a manufacturer's suggested retail price (MSRP) of $59.99, £54.99, €59.99, ¥7,980 and ₩69,800. Nintendo UK's page lists Shift Up as publisher and a 25.3 GB download size, and says the edition includes Stellar Blade x NieR:Automata and Stellar Blade x Goddess of Victory: Nikke content.

RPGSite reported on Sept. 13 that the Switch 2 cartridge holds the full game, with no key card or extra download. It also reported four free Bayonetta-inspired outfits for Eve on Switch 2, PlayStation 5 and PC on release day, with the fourth not yet revealed as of September.

## What is not confirmed

- **U.S. Nintendo eShop page:** we could not reach it, so we do not state its details.
- **Physical editions beyond the standard cartridge, preload date and performance modes:** the sources we read do not cover them.
