**Maxton Hall Season 3 premieres Wednesday, Dec. 9, 2026 on Prime Video, according to Amazon, and its finale arrives Dec. 23.** Amazon's page, updated Nov. 9, 2026 with the trailer, says the final season has six episodes: two on Dec. 9, then two each week until the series finale. That puts episodes 3 and 4 on Dec. 16, a date computed from Amazon's schedule. Each drop is dated below, and the same dates sit on a one-page printable watch tracker.

## When does Maxton Hall Season 3 come out?

Amazon writes: "Opening its doors one last time, Maxton Hall Season 3 will premiere on Prime Video on December 9, 2026." It adds that the season "will be the show's last chapter, concluding the story of Ruby and James." Dec. 9, 2026 is a Wednesday.

## What is the Maxton Hall Season 3 episode schedule?

Amazon says "the first two episodes will release on December 9" and "two new episodes will release each week until the series finale on December 23." Six episodes in three weekly drops end on Wednesday, Dec. 23. Amazon prints only the first and last dates, so the middle date is ours: one week after the premiere. We checked all three weekdays against the 2026 calendar.

| Episodes | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Wed., Dec. 9, 2026 | Amazon: premiere |
| 3 and 4 | Wed., Dec. 16, 2026 | Computed from Amazon's two-episodes-a-week schedule |
| 5 and 6 | Wed., Dec. 23, 2026 | Amazon: series finale |

Amazon has not announced episode titles or runtimes. Mark the three Wednesdays on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) or a [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html).

## What time does Maxton Hall Season 3 release?

Amazon has not announced a release time. Season 2's Prime Video title page dated its episodes Nov. 6, 2025 in the U.S. listing and shows no time, while Amazon's filming-locations page says Season 2 returns on November 7. Whether a Season 3 episode appears the evening before in the Americas is unconfirmed, and no 2026 time is given here until Amazon posts one.

## How does Season 3's release compare with Seasons 1 and 2?

Season 1 launched May 9, 2024 with all six episodes at once, per Amazon's "How to watch" page. Season 2's Prime Video title page lists six episodes: three on Nov. 6, 2025, then one each on Nov. 13, 20 and 27. Season 3 is the first of the three with two episodes a week.

| Season | First episodes | Episodes | Rollout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thu., May 9, 2024 | 6 | All at once |
| 2 | Thu., Nov. 6, 2025 (U.S. listing) | 6 | 3, then 1 a week for 3 weeks |
| 3 | Wed., Dec. 9, 2026 | 6 | 2, then 2 a week for 2 weeks |

From first episode to finale, Season 2 spanned 21 days (Nov. 6 to 27, 2025) and Season 3 spans 14 days (Dec. 9 to 23, 2026). Season 2's episodes were dated on Thursdays; Season 3 moves to Wednesdays.

## Who is in Season 3 and what is it based on?

Amazon says Harriet Herbig-Matten and Damian Hardung return in the lead roles. Season 3 is based on the third book in the series, Save Us. Prime Video streams the show in more than 240 countries and regions worldwide, per Amazon.

## Where is Maxton Hall filmed?

Amazon's filming-locations page describes Marienburg Castle, a 19th-century neo-Gothic castle near Hanover, as the school's setting: "Marienburg Castle remains the school's beating heart." The hero photo shows the castle.
