**Seattle, San Diego, San Antonio and Austin libraries are closed on Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026, Christmas Eve, according to each system's own holiday page, read on Nov. 23, 2026.** Boston Public Library says all locations are open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. that day, Toronto Reference Library closes at 1 p.m., and Los Angeles does not list Dec. 24.

All seven systems list Friday, Dec. 25 as closed. The table below runs from Wednesday, Dec. 23 through New Year's Day 2027, so it also answers whether libraries are open on New Year's Eve. ## Which libraries are closed on Christmas Eve 2026?

Four of the seven: Seattle, San Diego, San Antonio and Austin. Seattle's page lists "Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026" among its closures, and San Diego's lists "Thursday, Dec. 24 - Christmas Eve (Library Observed Holiday)". San Antonio's closure calendar says "All libraries will be closed" for the day, and Austin's page lists "Thursday & Friday, December 24 & 25".

Toronto Reference Library shows "Closes early: 1:00pm". Boston's page says "The day before Christmas Day / All locations will be open 9:00 am - 5:00 pm." Los Angeles lists Christmas on Dec. 25 only. For other offices that day, see [state offices closed on Christmas Eve](/news/state-offices-closed-christmas-eve-2026-by-state/) and the [post office and bank hours for Dec. 24 to 26](/news/is-post-office-open-christmas-eve-2026-december-26-banks/).

## Are libraries open on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day 2027?

On Thursday, Dec. 31 Boston is open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ("The day before New Year's Day"), Austin closes at 5 p.m., Toronto Reference Library closes at 1 p.m. and San Diego is closed. Seattle, San Antonio and Los Angeles do not list Dec. 31 on the pages we read.

On Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, Seattle, San Diego, Austin, Los Angeles and Toronto Reference Library are closed. Boston's page shows New Year's Day 2026 only, and San Antonio's closure calendar did not show Jan. 1 on Nov. 23, so neither is confirmed.

## Which libraries stay closed for several days in a row?

San Antonio's calendar lists "All libraries will be closed" on Thursday, Dec. 24, Friday, Dec. 25, Saturday, Dec. 26 and Sunday, Dec. 27, four days straight. Toronto Reference Library is closed all day Dec. 25, 26 and 27, and its page labels Sunday, Dec. 27 a "Scheduled Sunday Closure". Austin closes at 5 p.m. on Wednesday, Dec. 23, then stays closed on Dec. 24 and 25.

## Can you use the library online or return books on a closed day?

Seattle Public Library says "We are open 24/7 online at www.spl.org". San Diego's page says "Book drops will remain open at all Libraries on holidays (unless otherwise noted)." The other pages we read did not say.

## Which library systems are not in the table?

The New York Public Library, Brooklyn, Queens, Philadelphia, Houston, Washington, Denver, San Francisco, Miami-Dade and Dallas are not in the table: their pages block automated reading or had no 2026 Christmas list we could confirm on Nov. 23, 2026. Dallas posted November dates only. Chicago Public Library's holiday page showed no Dec. 24 or Dec. 31 entry we could confirm. Check each system's own holiday page.
