**Connections: Sports Edition #787, the Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026 puzzle from The Athletic and New York Times (NYT) Games, leans on place names and surnames that pull in the wrong direction.** Each color has two tap-to-open hints, and the answers wait under the spoiler heading.

Plenty of tiles here could sit with more than one neighbor. The rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works).

## Thursday's hints, #787, Nov. 19, 2026

Look at the board twice: once for cities and schools, once for names of people.

- Hint 1 (yellow): All four tiles are places, and the puzzle ties them to one division of the National Football Conference (NFC).
- Hint 2 (yellow): The tiles give the home city only; the nicknames are left off.
- Hint 1 (green): Each tile is a surname, and the puzzle ties all four to one position.
- Hint 2 (green): Every one of these surnames runs between four and six letters.
- Hint 1 (blue): One US state connects the four tiles, though the state itself is not on the board.
- Hint 2 (blue): One of the four tiles is made of two words.
- Hint 1 (purple): A single team nickname is shared by all four tiles, though the teams are not in the same league.
- Hint 2 (purple): Three of the four stand for college programs; the fourth is a major-league city.

## Why Thursday's tiles tempt the wrong group

Two tiles on the board name big American cities that both host pro teams, so a quick solver may reach for a "pro cities" idea and spend a mistake. Another pair ends in the same word, and that is a spelling accident, not a theme. Two more tiles read like ordinary towns or capital-style place names, which invites a "places" group that does not exist.

The cleanest way in is to ask of each place tile whether a person's name would fit instead. Surnames are rarer on this board than places, so the color made only of surnames is the easiest to fill once the place tiles are set aside. ## Thursday's answers, #787, Nov. 19, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it. Beebom and Nerdschalk list the same four groups and sixteen entries.

- **Yellow, "NFC East Teams":** Dallas, New York, Philadelphia, Washington
- **Green, "Hall of Fame Catchers":** Berra, Fisk, Mauer, Piazza
- **Blue, "Associated With Indiana":** Assembly Hall, Bloomington, Cignetti, Mendoza
- **Purple, "Pirates":** East Carolina, Hampton, Pittsburgh, Seton Hall

## Who each entry is

- **Berra, Fisk, Mauer, Piazza:** each is a catcher in the Hall of Fame, per the group.
- **Assembly Hall, Bloomington, Cignetti, Mendoza:** each is associated with Indiana, per the group.
- **Dallas, New York, Philadelphia, Washington:** each stands for a team in the NFC East, per the group.
- **East Carolina, Hampton, Pittsburgh, Seton Hall:** each is a team nicknamed the Pirates, per the group.
