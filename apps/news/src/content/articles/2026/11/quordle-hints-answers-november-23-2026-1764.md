## One solved word opens the others: Rainy, Baggy, Caddy and Movie

Rainy, Baggy and Caddy all share two fixed tiles: A in second place and Y in fifth. Solve any one of them and those two tiles can be locked into guesses for the other two boards. Movie is the odd one out, closing on E and holding no A or Y at all.

| Pattern | Words | Count |
|---|---|---|
| A in 2nd, Y in 5th | Rainy, Baggy, Caddy | 3 |
| Double letter, 3rd and 4th | Baggy, Caddy | 2 |
| No repeated letter | Rainy, Movie | 2 |

Baggy and Caddy go further: each repeats one letter in the third and fourth places, G in Baggy and D in Caddy, so a guess shaped like the first can be reshaped for the second by swapping only the first letter and the doubled one. Rainy breaks the pattern with the I and N in the middle.

Counted as slots, A and Y fill three of the 20 each. I, G and D fill two each. The other eight letters, R, N, B, C, M, O, V and E, appear once. That adds up to 13 distinct letters.

Movie carries three of the seven vowels, and Rainy has two. Baggy and Caddy have one each. The letter Y closes three of the four words, which a vowel-hunting opener such as Adieu cannot find.
