**The National Hockey League (NHL) plays 15 games on Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, from 1 p.m. to 8 p.m. ET, according to the NHL schedule.** The Thanksgiving Showdown on Turner Network Television (TNT) is the evening doubleheader: the Rangers visit the Blackhawks at 5:30 p.m. ET, then the Penguins visit the Sabres at 8 p.m. ET, per NHL.com.

The table gives every start in Eastern Time (ET), Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT) and Pacific Time (PT), and a free printable watch sheet follows it. For the date itself, see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/).

## Is the Thanksgiving Showdown different in 2026?

Yes. NHL.com's Aug. 26, 2026 TNT release says NHL on TNT's annual Thanksgiving Showdown on Black Friday, Nov. 27, "will feature a doubleheader beginning with the Rangers visiting the Blackhawks at 5:30 p.m., followed by the Penguins at Buffalo Sabres at 8 p.m." Both games are in the evening, and the table lists TNT, truTV and HBO Max for each.

In 2025, NHL.com's "NHL On Tap" column listed the Showdown as the New York Rangers at the Boston Bruins at 1 p.m. ET, "the NHL Thanksgiving Showdown ... for the fifth time." That matchup is gone from the Showdown label this year. The Rangers still play on Black Friday, at Chicago, while the Bruins host the Maple Leafs at 1 p.m. ET on NESN.

The ESPN national schedule, also published by NHL.com on Aug. 26, 2026, lists no ESPN or ABC game from Nov. 25 to Nov. 27.
