**Quordle #1752 from Merriam-Webster is the Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026 puzzle.** Each board gets a letters clue and a meaning clue, and a letter tracker shows where the four words lean on each other. The answers are at the bottom, behind a spoiler warning.

## Clues for the four boards

Boards run in the order of the answer list: top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right.

**Top left**

- Letters: starts with the 15th letter of the alphabet and ends with the 20th.
- Meaning: done openly and in plain view, with nothing hidden.

**Top right**

- Letters: starts with the 19th letter and ends with the 18th.
- Meaning: to do with the sun, as in a panel that turns daylight into power.

**Bottom left**

- Letters: starts with the 18th letter, ends with the 12th, and one letter appears twice.
- Meaning: to take great pleasure in something, or a noisy, lively party.

**Bottom right**

- Letters: starts with the second letter and ends with the 15th.
- Meaning: a shout of approval, said to a performer after a job well done.

## Quordle letter tracker for Nov. 11

Twenty tiles, but only nine different letters. That is a small pool, so a letter you confirm on one board is worth testing on the others straight away.

| Board | Letters also found on another board | Letters found on this board only |
|---|---|---|
| Top left | 4 | 1 |
| Top right | 4 | 1 |
| Bottom left | 4 | 0 |
| Bottom right | 4 | 1 |

Counts are different letters, not tiles. The bottom left repeats one letter, so it holds four different letters in five tiles, and every one of them also turns up elsewhere.

| Boards paired | Letters shared |
|---|---|
| Top left and top right | 2 |
| Top left and bottom left | 3 |
| Top left and bottom right | 3 |
| Top right and bottom left | 2 |
| Top right and bottom right | 3 |
| Bottom left and bottom right | 2 |

One letter is on all four boards, and two more cover three boards each. Three letters appear on two boards, and three appear on just one. Every word has exactly two vowel tiles, so vowels will not tell the boards apart; the consonants will.

**A suggested order.** Start with the bottom left: all of its letters are shared, so each green or yellow tile you find there carries over. Go next to the top left, which has three letters in common with both the bottom left and the bottom right. Leave the top right for later, since it shares the fewest letters with the top left and bottom left.
