## How the 97 points split by word length

Points are 1 for a four-letter word, 1 per letter for longer words, and a 7-point bonus for each pangram.

| Group | Words | Points | Share of 97 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4-letter answers | 6 | 6 | 6.2% |
| 5-letter answers | 8 | 40 | 41.2% |
| 6-letter answers | 5 | 30 | 30.9% |
| 7-letter answer that is not the pangram | 1 | 7 | 7.2% |
| Pangram (7 letters plus the 7-point bonus) | 1 | 14 | 14.4% |
| Total | 21 | 97 | 100% |

- **The gap:** Genius is 68 points, 62 more than the four-letter words can give.
- **Longer words:** the 14 answers with 5 letters or more that are not the pangram carry 77 points, 79.4% of the total.
- **Without the pangram:** the other 20 answers give 83 points, 15 more than Genius needs.

## What makes Monday's words hard

- **Doubled letters:** 3 answers repeat a letter back to back. Two are five-letter words, one starting CA and one CI, with a doubled R; the third is a six-letter CR word with a doubled P.
- **Letter pairs found once:** six pairs appear in only one answer each: AI, PA, PP, PY, RC and RY. The six-letter CR word holds two of them, PP and PY.
- **C twice:** 3 answers use the center letter twice, one six-letter word starting AC and two five-letter words starting CI.
- **Rare outer letters:** V is in 5 answers and Y in 7, against 18 for A, 14 for I, 15 for R and 11 for P.
- **Repeats of any kind:** 11 of the 21 answers use some letter more than once.
- **One starter:** only one answer starts with V, one with R and one with PA.
