**Christ the King Sunday 2026 is Sunday, Nov. 22**, listed by the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB) as "The Solemnity of Our Lord Jesus Christ, King of the Universe." The Church of England calls the same day "Christ the King" and "The Sunday next before Advent." The First Sunday of Advent follows a week later, on Nov. 29.

Below: the four readings, the dates through 2031, why the feast moved, and a free printable.

## What are the Christ the King 2026 readings?

The USCCB readings page for Nov. 22, 2026 lists these four. The Gospel is the Last Judgment passage.

| Reading | Citation |
|---|---|
| First reading | Ezekiel 34:11-12, 15-17 |
| Psalm | Psalm 23:1-2, 2-3, 5-6 |
| Second reading | 1 Corinthians 15:20-26, 28 |
| Gospel | Matthew 25:31-46 |

The Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA) labels 2025-2026 as Year A of the Sunday lectionary and 2026-2027 as Year B, so Nov. 22 closes Year A. Year B begins on the First Sunday of Advent, Nov. 29.

## When is Christ the King Sunday through 2031?

The day is the Sunday before the First Sunday of Advent, which is the Sunday nearest Nov. 30. The dates below are our computation from that rule, with weekdays checked. The lectionary letters follow the three-year A, B, C rotation from the ELCA labels; no church has published these years' dates on the pages we used.

| Year | Christ the King Sunday | First Sunday of Advent | Lectionary year ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Sun., Nov. 22 | Sun., Nov. 29 | A |
| 2027 | Sun., Nov. 21 | Sun., Nov. 28 | B |
| 2028 | Sun., Nov. 26 | Sun., Dec. 3 | C |
| 2029 | Sun., Nov. 25 | Sun., Dec. 2 | A |
| 2030 | Sun., Nov. 24 | Sun., Dec. 1 | B |
| 2031 | Sun., Nov. 23 | Sun., Nov. 30 | C |

## Why is the feast on the last Sunday before Advent?

Pope Pius XI created the feast. In the encyclical Quas Primas, dated Dec. 11, 1925, he wrote: "We institute the Feast of the Kingship of Our Lord Jesus Christ to be observed yearly throughout the whole world," and set it for "the last Sunday of the month of October."

Vatican News says that "with the liturgical reform of 1969, it was moved to the last Sunday of the liturgical year." That is the Sunday now before Advent. United Methodist Discipleship Ministries uses the name "Reign of Christ or Christ the King Sunday" and also calls it "the last Sunday of the Christian year."

## Is Christ the King Sunday also World Youth Day?

In local churches, yes. Vatican News reported on Nov. 22, 2020 that Pope Francis said: "Beginning in 2021, the celebration of World Youth Day in the local churches will take place on Christ the King Sunday." In his message for the 41st World Youth Day, Pope Leo says it is observed in "our local Churches on 22 November," the Solemnity of Our Lord Jesus Christ, King of the Universe. The 2026 theme is "Take courage! I have overcome the world" (John 16:33). The international World Youth Day is Aug. 3-8, 2027, per the message title.

## Do Catholics have to go to Mass on Nov. 22, 2026?

Christ the King is not a separate holy day of obligation. Because Nov. 22 is a Sunday, the usual Sunday obligation applies: canon 1247 of the Code of Canon Law says, "On Sundays and other holy days of obligation, the faithful are obliged to participate in the Mass." A holy day of obligation in Advent is covered in [Immaculate Conception 2026](/news/immaculate-conception-2026-holy-day-of-obligation-december-8/).

As of Nov. 19, the Holy See Press Office calendar of liturgical celebrations led by Pope Leo for November and December 2026 lists no papal Mass on Nov. 22. Mass times are set by each parish and are not covered here.
