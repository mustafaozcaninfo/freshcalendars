**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, has L in the center with A, D, E, I, N and T on the petals, and every pangram printed here is longer than the flower.** Hints come first, with no pangram given away.

## Saturday's letters: center L

- **Center:** L
- **Petals:** A, D, E, I, N, T

Minute Cryptic, Word Tips and ConnectionSolver print this letter set, and Beebom's page agrees. Merriam-Webster's own page does not show the letters in text, so the set rests on those lists. A, E and I are the vowels, which leaves D, L, N and T as the four consonants. Yesterday's page covers the [Nov. 27 Blossom](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-27-2026/), and with L in the middle today the scoring rules on the [Blossom rules page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works) apply as usual.

## Why every pangram runs long

A pangram uses all seven letters, so the shortest possible one has 7. The pangrams printed below have 9 or 10 letters. That means each one spends two or three letters on repeats, and the repeats are the trap: a word can hold six of the seven letters twice over and still miss the seventh. Count distinct letters, not word length.

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- **Hint 1:** The lists hold more pangrams than we print. We print six.
- **Hint 2:** One of the six has 9 letters. The other five have 10.
- **Hint 3:** The 9-letter word begins with a vowel petal, A.
- **Hint 4:** Three of the 10-letter words begin with the petal D, and the other two begin with I and T.
- **Hint 5:** Two of the three D words match in their first nine letters and differ only in the last vowel.
- **Hint 6:** One of the 10-letter words names a person who dabbles in a field for the love of it, and a second is that word's plural.
- **Hint 7:** The center L appears twice in only one of the six.

## Today's Blossom pangrams and their repeats (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it yourself.

### Pangram

Each word uses every one of A, D, E, I, L, N and T and no other letter. At least two solver lists print each of these six.

- **Alienated** (9 letters)
- **Delineated** (10 letters)
- **Dilettante** (10 letters)
- **Dilettanti** (10 letters)
- **Initialled** (10 letters)
- **Tendential** (10 letters)

### Which letters repeat

| Word | Length | Extra letters | Repeated letters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alienated | 9 | 2 | A twice, E twice |
| Delineated | 10 | 3 | D twice, E three times |
| Dilettante | 10 | 3 | T three times, E twice |
| Dilettanti | 10 | 3 | T three times, I twice |
| Initialled | 10 | 3 | I three times, L twice |
| Tendential | 10 | 3 | T twice, E twice, N twice |

Only Initialled doubles the center L. The solver lists hold many more pangrams than these six, including 11-letter scientific family names, which are not printed here. They also include words the game may not accept, so a word on a list is not a promise.
