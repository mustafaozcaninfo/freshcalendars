The White House held the 2026 National Thanksgiving Turkey Pardon on Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 2:00 p.m. ET on the South Lawn, at the Helipad, according to its Thanksgiving page, read Nov. 25, 2026. President Donald Trump and First Lady Melania Trump hosted it. Thanksgiving Day 2026 is Thursday, Nov. 26, so the ceremony came two days before it.

This year's birds were raised at Deans Gap Farm in West Virginia and made a ceremonial arrival at the Willard InterContinental Washington, DC, before the pardon, the White House says. Two newsrooms, News Channel 3 in Kalamazoo, Michigan (wwmt.com) and 12 News in Clarksburg, West Virginia, both reporting on Nov. 19, 2026, name the growers as Scott and Becky Rightsell of Deans Gap Farm (12 News places the farm in Fort Seybert, West Virginia). Both say the turkeys will live at Texas A&M University in College Station, Texas, where agriculture students care for them.

## How do the 2026 turkey name votes work?

The White House ran a two-round vote. Voting on the names ended Sunday, Nov. 22, at 5 p.m. ET. Round two opened Monday, Nov. 23, at 8 a.m. ET, to vote on which turkey receives the official pardon. The name pairs in the poll were Commander & Chief, Freedom & Feathers and Honey & Ham. 12 News reports the names were chosen from a list made by students at Brandywine Elementary. The White House says voters named the turkeys Freedom and Feathers, and Freedom received the official pardon.

The event is organized each year by the National Turkey Federation (NTF), which raises and presents the birds to the president. In 2025 the ceremony was the 78th National Thanksgiving Turkey Presentation, the federation said; the 2024 pardon was the 77th.

## When past turkey pardon ceremonies were held

From 2021 through 2025 the ceremony landed on different weekdays, but from 2022 to 2025 it fell on the Monday or Tuesday of Thanksgiving week; the 2026 ceremony was on a Tuesday. The table below lists the date, the day of the week, how many days before Thanksgiving it fell, the turkeys and the location reported for each year.

| Year | Pardon date | Day | Days before Thanksgiving | Turkeys | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | Nov. 19 | Friday | 6 | Peanut Butter and Jelly | Rose Garden |
| 2022 | Nov. 21 | Monday | 3 | Chocolate and Chip | South Lawn |
| 2023 | Nov. 20 | Monday | 3 | Liberty and Bell | White House |
| 2024 | Nov. 25 | Monday | 3 | Peach and Blossom | Rose Garden |
| 2025 | Nov. 25 | Tuesday | 2 | Gobble and Waddle | Rose Garden |

The 2021 ceremony was held on Friday, Nov. 19 in the White House Rose Garden, where President Joe Biden pardoned Peanut Butter and Jelly, according to ABC News. President Biden pardoned Chocolate and Chip on the South Lawn on Monday, Nov. 21, 2022, The Washington Times reported, and Liberty and Bell at the White House on Monday, Nov. 20, 2023. On Monday, Nov. 25, 2024, he spared Peach and Blossom in the Rose Garden, The Washington Times reported. President Donald Trump pardoned Gobble and Waddle on Tuesday, Nov. 25, 2025, the federation said.

## What happened at the 2025 ceremony

The 2025 National Thanksgiving Turkeys, Gobble and Waddle, were raised in Kenly, North Carolina, by grower Travis Pittman, the National Turkey Federation said in its Nov. 25, 2025 release. President Trump pardoned the pair at the 78th presentation. The federation said Gobble and Waddle would return to North Carolina to live at North Carolina State University in Raleigh. The 2025 ceremony was held in the Rose Garden, ABC News reported.

The federation's 2026 chairwoman is Christy Puffenbarger, elected by its board as the first woman to lead the turkey federation, the National Turkey Federation announced on Feb. 23, 2026. In 2025 the chairman was Jay Jandrain, president and chief executive of Butterball.
