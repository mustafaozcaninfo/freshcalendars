**The Temple Square Christmas lights run Nov. 27, 2026, through Jan. 1, 2027, from 6 a.m. to 8 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. to 10 p.m. Mountain Standard Time (MST)**, Church News reports. In Eastern Time (ET) that is 8 a.m. to 10 a.m. and 6:30 p.m. to midnight. The grounds are at 50 West North Temple in Salt Lake City, a Salt Lake City events listing says.

No lighting ceremony is listed for opening night. Pair the dates with the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and see [when Christmas falls](/when-is/christmas/).

## What are the Temple Square Christmas lights hours?

| Window | Mountain (MST) | Eastern (ET) |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 6 a.m. to 8 a.m. | 8 a.m. to 10 a.m. |
| Evening | 4:30 p.m. to 10 p.m. | 6:30 p.m. to midnight |

Church News and the Salt Lake City events listing give the same two windows. Counted from Nov. 27, 2026, through Jan. 1, 2027, the run is 36 nights. In 2025, the Church newsroom said the lights ran from Friday, Nov. 28, 2025, through Thursday, Jan. 1, 2026, daily from 6 a.m. to 8 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. to 10 p.m. The windows are unchanged, and the 2026 start is a day earlier on the calendar.

## How do you get Tabernacle Choir Christmas concert tickets?

The Choir's Christmas concerts are Dec. 17, 18 and 19, 2026, at 8 p.m. MST in the Conference Center. Tickets are free and were given out by random drawing. Registration ran from Nov. 2 through Nov. 15 and is closed, the Church newsroom said on Nov. 2.

Tickets go to selected individuals by Dec. 1. A standby line forms in the Tabernacle on Temple Square 90 minutes before the 8 p.m. MST concert time, the newsroom said. That is 6:30 p.m. MST, or 8:30 p.m. ET.

## Is the First Presidency Christmas Devotional live?

The devotional is Sunday, Dec. 6, at 6 p.m. MST, which is 8 p.m. ET. Church News says it will not be a live ticketed event and is a prerecorded broadcast instead. On Sunday, Dec. 20, the Music & the Spoken Word Christmas broadcast begins at 9:30 a.m. MST, 11:30 a.m. ET.

## Does public transit run on holidays in Salt Lake City?

The Utah Transit Authority (UTA) holiday service page lists no bus or light-rail service on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The page does not name a year, so check it before you travel. The lights stay on through Jan. 1, 2027, which is New Year's Day.
