The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) lists **Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026** as the Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB) payment date, with the next one on **Thursday, Dec. 10**. Both dates come from the CRA's payment dates page, last updated Oct. 14, 2026. The benefit year runs from July 2026 to June 2027, and the CRA says payments "will be issued on the 10th of each month, starting in July 2026."

For other federal payments in the same weeks, see our guide to [Canada Pension Plan (CPP), Old Age Security (OAS) and Canada Child Benefit (CCB) dates for November and December 2026](/news/canada-benefit-payment-dates-november-december-2026-cpp-oas-ccb/).

## Ontario Trillium Benefit payment dates for the rest of 2026

The CRA lists a date for every month of 2026. The weekdays below are our calculation from those dates.

| Month | OTB payment date (CRA) | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| January | Jan. 9 | Friday |
| February | Feb. 10 | Tuesday |
| March | Mar. 10 | Tuesday |
| April | Apr. 10 | Friday |
| May | May 8 | Friday |
| June | June 10 | Wednesday |
| July | July 10 | Friday |
| August | Aug. 10 | Monday |
| September | Sept. 10 | Thursday |
| October | Oct. 9 | Friday |
| **November** | **Nov. 10** | **Tuesday** |
| **December** | **Dec. 10** | **Thursday** |

The CRA's page did not list January to June 2027 dates when we read it, so they are not yet posted. The CRA's Ontario page says payments are issued on the 10th of each month for the July 2026 to June 2027 benefit year.

## What does the Ontario Trillium Benefit combine?

The CRA describes the OTB as "the combined payment of the Ontario energy and property tax credit, the Northern Ontario energy credit, and the Ontario sales tax credit." The Government of Ontario gives these maximums for the 2026 benefit year (July 2026 to June 2027). Your own amount can be lower and is set by your return.

| Credit | 2026-27 maximum (Government of Ontario) |
|---|---|
| Northern Ontario Energy Credit (NOEC) | $189 for single people; up to $290 for families |
| Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit (OEPTC) | $1,307 if you are between 18 and 64 years old; $1,488 if you are 65 or older |
| Ontario Sales Tax Credit (OSTC) | Up to $378 |

The CRA's Ontario page puts the sales tax credit at a "maximum annual credit of $378 for each adult and each child in a family."

## Monthly or one payment: how Ontario describes the choice

For an entitlement over $500, the Government of Ontario says you can be paid monthly from July 2026 or in one payment in June 2027, with "the same total amount regardless of which option you choose." If your 2026 annual entitlement is $500 or less, the CRA says it is issued in one lump-sum payment in the first payment month, usually July.

## What if the payment has not arrived by Nov. 10?

The CRA says to wait 10 working days after the payment date before contacting it. Count from Nov. 10 for the November payment and from Dec. 10 for the December one.
