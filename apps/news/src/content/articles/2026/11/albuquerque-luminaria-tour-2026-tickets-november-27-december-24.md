**ABQRide, the City of Albuquerque's transit department, puts Luminaria Tour tickets on sale at 8:00 a.m. Mountain Time (MT) on Black Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, which is 10 a.m. ET.** The price is $1.50, tickets are sold online only, and the tour runs Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026. Mark both days on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

ABQRide's Luminaria Tour page says tickets "typically sell out within a few hours of sales." Plan to be ready when the sale opens.

## When do Luminaria Tour tickets go on sale, and what do you need?

The sale starts Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. MT. In Eastern Time that is 10 a.m., because Mountain Time is two hours behind. ABQRide's page lists these terms:

- Price: $1.50.
- Tickets will only be sold online.
- Limit of 5 tickets maximum per purchase.

A checklist for Friday morning:

1. Open luminariatour.com a few minutes before 8:00 a.m. MT (10 a.m. ET).
2. Decide the departure time you want, and a second choice.
3. Count your group: no more than 5 tickets per purchase.
4. Have your payment ready; there is no sale at the door, since tickets are online only.

## What time do the Albuquerque Luminaria Tour buses leave on Dec. 24, 2026?

ABQRide says four tours originate from the Downtown Convention Center on Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026. The city's events page lists the window as 5:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. at the Albuquerque Convention Center.

| Tour | Departs (MT) | Departs (ET) |
|---|---|---|
| First | 5:30 p.m. | 7:30 p.m. |
| Second | 5:45 p.m. | 7:45 p.m. |
| Third | 6:00 p.m. | 8:00 p.m. |
| Fourth | 6:15 p.m. | 8:15 p.m. |

The tour begins and ends at the Albuquerque Convention Center. Buses pick up on 2nd Street, on the east side of the building.

## What else is on in Albuquerque around Christmas Eve 2026?

Visit Albuquerque says "the largest display takes place on Christmas Eve in the Old Town Plaza and Country Club neighborhood." It also lists the Old Town Holiday Stroll on Dec. 4, 2026, in Old Town. Old Town closures and parking for Christmas Eve 2026 have not been announced in the sources we checked.

## What is the Canyon Road Farolito Walk in Santa Fe in 2026?

Tourism Santa Fe lists the Canyon Road Farolito Walk on Christmas Eve (Dec. 24). Its December events page posts no 2026 hours, street closures, shuttle or cost, so check the city before you travel.

## Luminaria or farolito: what is the difference?

The New Mexico Tourism Department says the answer depends on where you are. "The paper lanterns are known as farolitos in northern New Mexico." In that part of the state, "luminaria still refers to the original bonfires." South of Santa Fe, "paper lanterns are more commonly called luminarias." The state adds that "the terminology can shift by region, family, community, and culture."
