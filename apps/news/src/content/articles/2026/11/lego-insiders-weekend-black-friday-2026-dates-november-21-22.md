**LEGO Insiders Weekend took place Saturday, Nov. 21, and Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, and LEGO's Black Friday event runs from Friday, Nov. 27, to Cyber Monday, Nov. 30, according to LEGO's official shop pages read Nov. 16.** Insiders Weekend is for members, and joining is free. LEGO's pages named no deals when read on Nov. 16. The dated table, steps to join and a printable checklist follow.

## What is LEGO Insiders Weekend 2026?

LEGO describes Insiders Weekend as "an annual event just for our members" with "exclusive deals for LEGO Insiders," held the weekend before the Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales. Its page says the event is "filled with surprises, gifts with purchases and much more," and that members "earn at least 5% back in LEGO® Insiders points for discounts on future LEGO® purchases." When we read the page on Nov. 16, its heading still said the event was coming, so the specific deals, any points multiplier and the gift sets were not announced then.

## How do you join LEGO Insiders?

LEGO's help page says you can become an Insider anytime, and it names no join deadline. Insiders Weekend ended Sunday, Nov. 22, and we suggest joining before you shop so the account is ready.

1. Go to LEGO.com/Insiders and follow the instructions to create an account, or use the [LEGO Insiders join page](https://www.lego.com/en-us/insiders/join).
2. In a LEGO Store, ask a Brick Specialist to sign you up.
3. Check the age line: LEGO's help page asks "If you're at least 18 years old, why not become a LEGO Insider?"
4. Sign in before you shop.

## What do LEGO's Black Friday 2026 deals include?

LEGO's page says the "once-a-year event runs all weekend, from Black Friday on 11/27 to Cyber Monday on 11/30," with "Discounts, gifts with purchase and more, starting November 27." On Nov. 16 it told shoppers to check back for offers, sets and gifts with purchase, and named none. Neither LEGO page names a sale between the two weekends. For other retailers' dates, see our [Cyber Monday 2026 sale times](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/), and for holiday sets, [LEGO advent calendars for 2026](/news/lego-advent-calendars-2026-prices-whats-inside/).

## Are LEGO Stores open on Thanksgiving 2026?

LEGO's store pages carry no chain-wide Thanksgiving or Black Friday notice. One store page, LEGO Store Brandon Exchange in Brandon, Fla., lists Thursday, Nov. 26, as closed for Thanksgiving. Treat that as one store only and check your own store's page on lego.com.
