## Hints for every clue

- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Stringed instrument propped up by an endpin*: a bowed instrument that a player holds between the knees while seated; its first letter is the second letter of 1-Down and its last letter is the second letter of 5-Down.

## The bracketed clue is the least literal one

9-Across, *[This lecture is soooo boring!]*, does not define a word. The square brackets mark a reaction, something a character would do rather than say, and the stretched "soooo" tells you how long the reaction lasts. Read it as stage direction in a script: what sound or action would the speaker make during a dull talk? The answer is a word for that reaction, not a word about lectures. 1-Across plays a related game, because it describes what a keyboard key does instead of naming the key.

## Which crossings open which entries?

With no black squares, every one of the 25 squares is shared by one Across and one Down, so each answer leaks five letters into the grid.

- Start with 1-Across: it hands you the first letter of every Down, because all five Down answers begin in row 1.
- Then take 4-Down, a plain baking clue and a safe anchor that places the fourth letter of each Across.
- Next, 6-Across gives the second letter of every Down, since the instrument clue fills a full row.
- Then 8-Across, a fill-in phrase with one natural completion, adds the fourth letter of every Down.

Solved in that order, the hardest entry, the film role in 2-Down, arrives with its first, second and fourth letters already placed. The bracketed 9-Across row then comes last and closes the fifth letter of every Down.
