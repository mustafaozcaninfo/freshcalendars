**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, centers on H, with E, N, O, R, S and T around it.** Beebom and Connections Solver list these letters, and Word.Tips' lists fit them. Hints come first, and the answers wait in a marked section at the bottom.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** H (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** E, N, O, R, S, T

Only E and O are vowels, so every word needs one of them next to the H. Scoring and rules are on our [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works). Yesterday's flower, centered on W, is on the [Nov. 10 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-10-2026/).

## Pangram hints, from light to heavy

There are many pangrams, so each hint fits several words.

1. **Size:** the shortest uses exactly seven letters, and the longest runs to 11.
2. **Tally:** Beebom and Word.Tips both list 22.
3. **No repeats:** four of the 22 use each of the seven letters exactly once.
4. **Openers:** six begin with S, six with H, four with N, two with T and two with O; one each begins with E and R.
5. **Pairs:** eight of the 22 open with SH or TH.

Other lists add a 23rd, but Beebom does not call it a pangram.

## How the long pangrams reuse letters

The four 11-letter pangrams each carry four spare letters, because seven are needed and eleven are used. The spares pile up on a few letters.

- **E repeats in all four.** Three use it twice and one uses it three times.
- **S is the heavy hitter.** Three of the four repeat it, twice at three uses and once at four.
- **N and R are rarer.** Two of the four use N twice, and only one uses R, three times in all.
- **H, O and T never double.** Each appears exactly once in every 11-letter pangram.

## Why H in the center favors SH and TH openers

H joins S or T in two of the most common English letter pairs, and today's pangrams show it: 8 of the 22 begin with SH or TH, and 6 more begin with H on its own. Among the 20 long words all three sources list, 17 start with H, SH or TH. Starting a search with those pairs covers most of the ground.
