Global Entrepreneurship Week 2026 runs Monday, Nov. 16 to Sunday, Nov. 22, according to the Global Entrepreneurship Network, the organizer, whose page reads "Save the Date: Nov. 16-22, 2026." The week is seven days long, counting both ends. Below are each day with a live count from today, the figures the organizer has and has not published, and a free planner PDF.

## What does the organizer say about the 2026 edition?

The Global Entrepreneurship Network announced the 2026 dates on Jan. 22, 2026, in an article headed "After Its Largest Year Ever, Global Entrepreneurship Week Set to Return in November 2026."

| Item | 2026 | Last year (2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Countries and territories taking part | Not yet announced | 200 |
| People taking part each November | Not yet announced | 10 million |
| Events | Not yet published | 55,000+ in the article body, 50,000 in its Quick Facts |

The 2025 event count is contradictory on the organizer's own pages, and its site navigation gives yet another figure, so this story quotes none of them as a total. The 200 countries and territories and the 10 million people are the organizer's 2025 figures, and the 10 million is described as a yearly total each November.

## How do you find local events?

Local event dates are not verified here. The organizer's Global Entrepreneurship Week site is the place to look, and each event has its own organizer, schedule and registration rules. The organizer shows no registration deadline for the week itself.

The planner PDF has a seven-day grid and a short checklist: find an event, save its registration link, block the time, then write down one action for the month after. It carries no event listings, only the dates the organizer confirms.

## How does it differ from Small Business Saturday?

Global Entrepreneurship Week is an organizer's campaign for founders and students, not a shopping event. Readers looking for the retail side of small business can read the story on [Small Business Saturday 2026](/news/small-business-saturday-2026-participating-stores-events/), which covers participating stores and events.
