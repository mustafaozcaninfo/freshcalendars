**Letter Boxed from The New York Times (NYT) for Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, which is Thanksgiving Day, has the sides S/W/E, O/A/I, B/K/N and P/H/M, and a par of 4, according to the puzzle data on the Letter Boxed page.** The saved word list has 1,441 entries, and 36 pairs of them touch every letter. With a par of 4 and the rules on our [Letter Boxed page](/news/topic/nyt-letter-boxed/#how-it-works), the sections below cover openers and closers per letter, the letter pairs that never meet, three look-alike words that fail, five hints and four warm-up words. The Times' solution comes last.

## Which letters start and end the most words?

A route chains words, so the last letter of one is the first of the next. A letter that opens or closes few words narrows the choices around it. The counts use all 1,441 words.

| Letter | Side | Words starting with it | Words ending with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | 1 | 237 | 468 |
| W | 1 | 85 | 25 |
| E | 1 | 25 | 211 |
| O | 2 | 61 | 76 |
| A | 2 | 88 | 147 |
| I | 2 | 30 | 78 |
| B | 3 | 137 | 31 |
| K | 3 | 101 | 35 |
| N | 3 | 124 | 174 |
| P | 4 | 192 | 56 |
| H | 4 | 105 | 71 |
| M | 4 | 256 | 69 |

S is the standout at both ends, closing 468 words, and M opens the most at 256. E is the opposite case: 211 words end in it, but only 25 begin with it. W closes just 25 words, so a W in the middle of a route has to sit inside a word.

## Which letter pairs never meet on this box?

Of the 108 ordered pairs that cross from one side to another, 6 never appear next to each other in any dictionary word: W then B, B then P, B then M, K then P, K then M and M then K. K and M are the strictest pair, since neither order shows up at all. Only a handful of pairs appear exactly once: K then W, H then N and N then P.

The sides matter more than single pairs. Counting words that contain at least one step between two given sides:

| Sides | Words with a step between them |
|---|---|
| S/W/E and O/A/I | 814 |
| S/W/E and B/K/N | 539 |
| S/W/E and P/H/M | 607 |
| O/A/I and B/K/N | 946 |
| O/A/I and P/H/M | 951 |
| B/K/N and P/H/M | 102 |

The B/K/N and P/H/M pair is the weak link, with 102 words against 539 or more for every other pair.

## Three tempting words that fail on this box

These look playable but break a rule. Each was checked against the sides and the saved dictionary.

| Word | Why it fails |

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, two fewer than the par of 4.
2. Both words have 7 letters, and the saved list holds 153 words of that length.
3. The first word opens and closes on the P/H/M side. The second word opens on that same side and closes on the S/W/E side.
4. The K and the W are in the first word. The B, the P and the N are in the second.
5. The first word starts with M and ends in H. The second starts with H and ends in E.
