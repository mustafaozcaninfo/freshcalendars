## What do doubled letters do to the colors?

Quordle colors one tile per letter in your guess. If your guess holds a letter once and the answer holds it twice, you get one tile for it and nothing at all for the other copy, so the second copy stays unmarked and the board looks less complete than it is. To see both copies, the guess needs both.

| Word | Different letters | Letters used twice | Where the pair sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Word 1 | 4 | 1 | side by side |
| Word 2 | 3 | 2 | interleaved |
| Word 3 | 5 | 0 | none |
| Word 4 | 4 | 1 | two spots apart |

The four words hold 20 letter slots but only 13 different letters. Four slots are repeats inside one word, and three more are letters that cross between boards. One word holds just three different letters, because both of its pairs are doubled.

Words 1, 2 and 4 therefore reward a first guess with a repeated letter in the right place. Word 3 is the opposite, a clean five-letter board.

## How five opening words score on today's four boards

Each cell lists green tiles (G, right letter, right spot) and yellow tiles (Y, right letter, wrong spot) the opener earns against that word.

| Opener | Word 1 | Word 2 | Word 3 | Word 4 | Colored tiles in all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crane | 1G 2Y | 1G 1Y | 0G 1Y | 0G 1Y | 7 |
| Round | 0G 2Y | 1G 0Y | 2G 1Y | 0G 0Y | 6 |
| Dairy | 0G 1Y | 0G 1Y | 1G 2Y | 1G 1Y | 7 |
| Gored | 2G 1Y | 1G 0Y | 1G 2Y | 0G 0Y | 7 |

Crane, Dairy and Gored tie at seven colored tiles, and Dairy is the one of the three that reaches every grid. Round and Gored each land two greens on a single board. Brick colors the fewest, five. Round and Gored both leave Word 4 blank.

Across the five openers Word 3 returns 11 colored tiles, the most, while Word 4 returns just five. Word 1 gives 10 and Word 2 gives six.

## Today's Quordle answers (spoilers)

Spoilers ahead, so stop here if you are still playing.

- **Word 1: Green**
- **Word 2: Cocoa**
- **Word 3: Rowdy**
- **Word 4: Alibi**

Beebom and Ponly list these four words in this order and label the puzzle #1760; Nerd's Chalk prints the same four in the same order for Nov. 19. Only one of them places the words on the grid, so this page leaves the layout out. It covers the daily Classic game only.

| Doubled letter | Word | Positions |
|---|---|---|
| E | Green | 3 and 4 |
| C | Cocoa | 1 and 3 |
| O | Cocoa | 2 and 4 |
| I | Alibi | 3 and 5 |

Dairy shows the cost of a single copy: its I turns the third spot of Alibi green and leaves the fifth spot unmarked. Crane, with one E in the last spot, earns a single yellow on Green though two E tiles are there. Gored lands O on the second spot of Cocoa and says nothing about the O in the fourth.

Three letters cross between boards: R in Green and Rowdy, O in Cocoa and Rowdy, A in Cocoa and Alibi. That links the set in a chain, Green to Rowdy to Cocoa to Alibi, so Rowdy and Cocoa each touch two boards and Green and Alibi touch one.
