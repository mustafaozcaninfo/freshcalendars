**Cherry Creek School District has no school for students or staff from Monday, March 15 to Friday, March 19, 2027, and the last day of school is Friday, May 28, 2027**, according to its "2026-2027 School Year Calendar, All Schools," adopted by the Board of Education on Jan. 8, 2024. The story counts every weekday without students, flags the Wednesday before Thanksgiving that other lists miss, and covers the preschool differences, plus a printable Cherry Creek School District (CCSD) calendar.

## Cherry Creek no-school days in 2026-27

The PDF is a color grid with no holiday names. Its legend marks "No School for Students and Staff," "K-12 Non-contact" and "K-8 Non-contact/conferences," and says "Days marked with colors below are non-pupil contact days." The table lists every one in date order. Weekdays, holiday names by date and counts are our arithmetic from the district's dates.

| Fri., Sept. 18, 2026 | 1 | K-12 non-contact |

| Fri., Nov. 6, 2026 | 1 (K-8 only) | K-8 non-contact/conferences |

Adding the rows, K-12 students have 29 weekdays without school between Aug. 17, 2026 and May 28, 2027, and K-8 students have four more for conferences. The district's PDF does not print the 29; it is our sum. Plan the next break on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Is Wednesday before Thanksgiving a school day in Cherry Creek?

No. Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026 is marked "K-12 Non-contact," so students are home, though the legend treats it as a work day for staff. The district's calendar page lists "No School ~ Thanksgiving Break ~ All Schools" for Nov. 26-27. Together, K-12 students are off Wednesday, Nov. 25 through Friday, Nov. 27, three weekdays. A list that shows only Nov. 26-27 leaves out the Wednesday.

## When is Cherry Creek winter break 2026?

No school for students and staff runs from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, ten weekdays. Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 is a K-12 non-contact day, so students are out through that Monday. The calendar does not label a first day back; Tuesday, Jan. 5 is the first weekday it does not mark, which is our reading. Denver Public Schools lists winter break as Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 with a first class day of Jan. 5, per our [Denver story](/news/denver-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), and Jeffco's break runs Dec. 21-Jan. 5, per our [Jeffco story](/news/jeffco-public-schools-calendar-2026-27/).

## When is Cherry Creek spring break 2027?

The calendar marks Monday to Friday, March 15-19, 2027 as no school for students and staff. It does not print the words "spring break," so these are the dates and not a district label. Thursday and Friday, March 4-5, are K-8 conference days just before. Douglas County School District also lists March 15-19, per our [Douglas County story](/news/douglas-county-school-district-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), while Jeffco's week is March 22-26. See the week on the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html).

## When did Cherry Creek school start, and when does it end?

Students started on Monday, Aug. 17, 2026; the legend marks "School Starts," with teacher work days Aug. 10-14 before it. The last day is Friday, May 28, 2027, marked "Last day of School." Memorial Day, Monday, May 31, falls after it. The calendar has no start dates for kindergarten, sixth grade or ninth grade, and no graduation dates.

## How is the Cherry Creek preschool calendar different?

The district's separate "2026-2027 Cherry Creek Schools Preschool Calendar," revised on 4/2/26, has its own dates. Preschool started Wednesday, Aug. 19, 2026, two days after K-12, and its last day is Thursday, May 27, 2027, a day before K-12. Preschool is off Nov. 25-27, and the preschool PDF also has Friday staff work days with no students. Use that PDF for preschool and pre-kindergarten dates.

## Print the Cherry Creek School District calendar

The free printable, "Cherry Creek School District Calendar 2026-27," puts the first day, the breaks, the no-school days and the last day on one page, with room for conferences and picture day. The calendar can be amended: it says "If for any reason the school district must close schools for more than two days, this 2026-2027 calendar will be amended by the Board of Education to provide additional school days." Check the district's [Calendar page](https://www.cherrycreekschools.org/fs/pages/10099) before you book travel, and use the [printable school-year calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html) to plan around it.
