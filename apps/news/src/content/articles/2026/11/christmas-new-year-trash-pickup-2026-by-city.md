**Christmas Day (Friday, Dec. 25, 2026) and New Year's Day (Friday, Jan. 1, 2027) both fall on a Friday, so in the cities that have posted, Friday trash pickups move to Saturday, Dec. 26 and Saturday, Jan. 2.** Phoenix, Oklahoma City and Charlotte say so for Christmas on their pages read on Nov. 3, 2026, Fort Worth says so in its 2026 holiday PDF read on Nov. 6, 2026, and Phoenix and Oklahoma City say so for New Year's Day, on their official pages read on Nov. 3, 2026. The table says "not yet posted" where a page has nothing for 2026-27.

## Which cities have confirmed Christmas Day 2026 pickup changes?

Phoenix says "Solid waste collections will not be provided on Christmas Day (Friday, December 25, 2026)" and that residents with a Friday collection day will have trash and recycling collected on Saturday, Dec. 26. Charlotte says the city will be closed on Friday, Dec. 25, with no collection, and that Friday routes run on a one-day delay, collected on Saturday, Dec. 26. Oklahoma City's table lists Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, with a makeup day of Saturday, Dec. 26. Jacksonville says customers with a Friday pickup will be serviced on Saturday, Dec. 26, 2026. Fort Worth's 2026 holiday schedule PDF says "Friday collection moves to Saturday" for Christmas, Friday, Dec. 25, 2026.

San Diego lists "Friday, Dec. 25, 2026 – Christmas Day" among the holidays with no collection of trash, recyclables or organic waste, and collects one day later for the remainder of that week. Denver lists Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, under observed holidays with collections delayed one day, and says a collection that falls on or after the holiday is delayed one day for the rest of that week only.

## Is trash picked up on New Year's Day 2027?

Phoenix says collections will not be provided on New Year's Day (Friday, Jan. 1, 2027), and Friday collections move to Saturday, Jan. 2. Oklahoma City lists New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, with a makeup day of Saturday, Jan. 2.

Jacksonville's page says waste collection schedules change only on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day, and that for all other holidays waste is collected on the regular day, so we read New Year's Day as a regular day there. Fort Worth's PDF has a New Year's row for Jan. 1, 2026 only, so no 2027 rule is posted. Charlotte's page covers 2026 only. It says collection ran as scheduled on New Year's Day 2026, which is not a 2027 rule. San Diego and San Francisco list Jan. 1, 2026 only, and Denver and Philadelphia show nothing for 2027 yet. Austin's page says that if your collection day falls on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day or New Year's Day, or after the holiday in the same week, it slides one day later; that standing rule would put Friday routes on Saturday, and My Schedule on the city page shows your address.

## What do cities without a posted schedule say?

Houston lists Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, and in its 2027 list New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, as city holidays, but gives no collection text for either yet. Recology San Francisco lists Christmas, Friday, Dec. 25, as to be determined. Columbus has not posted dates; its Oct. 8, 2026 bulletin says that if your collection day falls on or after the holiday, pickup will be delayed one day. Seattle's 2026 notice is not out; in 2025, SPU said that if a regular collection day falls on a holiday, service is delayed one day. Philadelphia's city holidays page says collection is one day behind for the rest of the week after a holiday, and Philly311 says collection does not take place on the day of a city-observed holiday; its Christmas and New Year's 2026 tables are not posted yet.
