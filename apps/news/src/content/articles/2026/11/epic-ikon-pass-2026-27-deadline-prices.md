**The Epic Pass goes off sale after Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, with an adult price of $1,245, according to a Vail Resorts release dated Nov. 16.** Vail's release says "December 6 is the last chance to purchase an Epic Pass for the 2026/27 season" and gives no time of day. From Nov. 17 that leaves 19 days.

Ikon, Mountain Collective and Indy have not set the same kind of deadline on the pages checked, so the table below lines up all four pass families. A printable version of the table is included.

Prices are for adults. Ikon's are starting prices for ages 23 and up, and Mountain Collective's adult price is for ages 19 and up. The Epic Pass has "unrestricted access" per Vail, so no blackout dates are listed for it.

## When does the Epic Pass go off sale?

Vail Resorts says Epic Passes go off sale after Dec. 6, which falls on a Sunday in 2026. The release gives no cutoff time. Its other figures:

- Epic Pass: $1,245 for an adult and $995 for a young adult, with "unrestricted access to 90+ resorts."
- Epic Local Pass: $959 for an adult and $759 for a young adult, with access to 50+ resorts plus 10 days at Vail Mountain, Whistler Blackcomb and Beaver Creek.
- Epic Day Pass: from $50 to $106 a day.
- Young adults ages 13-30 save 20% on an Epic Pass or Epic Local Pass.
- Season-long Epic Pass holders receive five Epic Friend Tickets offering 50% off adult lift tickets.
- Vail's Lodging Cyber Sale runs from Nov. 23 to Dec. 7.

Vail's release names the Epic Pass; it does not give a separate date for the Epic Local Pass, the Epic Day Pass or regional Epic passes. Do not assume they end on Dec. 6.

## When does the Ikon Pass go off sale in 2026?

Ikon has not announced its 2026-27 off-sale date on the pages checked. Last year, Ikon's Nov. 26, 2025 release was headlined "Don't Miss out on the Joy - Ikon Pass Goes off Sale December 11", so Dec. 11, 2025 was last year's date. That is a pattern to watch, not a 2026 date.

Alterra Mountain Company's compare page lists the Ikon Pass from $1,529, the Ikon Base Pass from $1,099 and the Ikon Session Pass from $369 for ages 23 and up. Ikon Base Pass blackout dates are Dec. 26-30, 2026, Jan. 16-17, 2027 and Feb. 13-14, 2027. Dec. 26 is the Saturday after Christmas Day, so the first block covers the week between Christmas and New Year's Day; the other two blocks are Saturday-Sunday weekends. The Ikon Base Pass's $1,099 starting price is $140 above the Epic Local Pass's $959 adult price (computed from the two pages), and the passes cover different resorts, so compare the access lists as well as the prices.

## Mountain Collective and Indy Pass: no last day shown

Mountain Collective's site lists passes as on sale for the 2026-27 season, with an adult (ages 19 and up) price of $759. No last day to buy is shown.

The Indy Pass shop lists the 2026-27 adult Indy Base Pass at $419 and Indy+ at $469. Both show a waitlist button, and the shop says "restrictions have been placed on the number of passes sold for the 26/27 season." It shows no sale-end date.
