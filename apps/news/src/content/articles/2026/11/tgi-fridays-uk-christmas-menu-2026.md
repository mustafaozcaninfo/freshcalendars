**TGI Fridays UK's 2026 Christmas menu is two courses from £24.95 or three courses from £30.95, and it is available now, according to the chain's Christmas page.** Our research desk read the page, the festive menu PDF and the booking terms on 22 November. Below are every dish and supplement, the days that need a £5 deposit and the gift card window.

## How much is the TGI Fridays Christmas menu in 2026?

The Christmas page says: "Two courses from £24.95 or three courses from £30.95." It also says "The 2026 Christmas menu is available now" and "Christmas bookings are now open". The pages give no end date for the menu.

| Option | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 2 courses | Appetizer and main | From £24.95 |
| 3 courses | Appetizer, main and dessert, or a "Sip & Sleigh" cocktail instead of dessert | From £30.95 |

In 2025, Retail Times reported a "Festive set menu – from £24.95", "available from 10th November in restaurants nationwide". The 2026 pages give no start date.

## What dishes are on the TGI Fridays festive menu?

The PDF lists the dishes below, with the "(VG)" and "(V)" marks as printed on the menu. We read the text from an image of the PDF, so spellings follow the menu. Prices appear only for supplements.

| Course | Dishes |
|---|---|
| Appetizers | Fridays Signature Whiskey-Glazed Sesame Chicken Strips; Tomato Bruschetta (VG); Mozzarella Parcels, 5 pieces (V); Pigs in Blankets, with a cranberry and orange glaze or the Signature Whiskey-Glaze |
| Mains | Festive Half-Rack of Baby Back Ribs; Very Merry Vegan Burger (VG); 'Santa Pants' King Prawn Pasta; Sizzling Fajitas, Cajun Chicken or Garlic & Chilli Veg (VG); Triple Stacked Jingle Bell Burger; Sirloin Steak |
| Desserts | Black Forest Brownie Blizzard (V); Festive Wonderland Cheesecake (V); Chocolate Orange Brownie (VG) |
| Sides | House Fries (VG); Chef's Vegetables (VG); House Salad (V); Spicy Rice (VG); Maple-Glazed Corn Ribs (VG); Creamy Mash (V); Cheese & Bacon Loaded Fries; Pigs in Blankets |

## Which TGI Fridays Christmas dishes cost extra?

| Item | Extra cost |
|---|---|
| Full rack instead of the Half-Rack of Baby Back Ribs | +£5 |
| Triple Stacked Jingle Bell Burger | +£5 |
| Sirloin Steak | +£5 |
| Garlic & Chilli King Prawns added to the Sirloin Steak | +£6 |
| Cheese & Bacon Loaded Fries | +£2 |
| Pigs in Blankets as a side | +£2 |

The other dishes carry no supplement on the PDF.

## What are the Sip & Sleigh cocktails?

A three-course meal can swap dessert for a "Sip & Sleigh" cocktail. The PDF names five: Ultimate Claus for Celebration; Cinnamon Pornstar Martini; Holy Duck, It's Tropical Christmas!; Santa Pants; and Santa's Surprise Shake, which is listed as 0% alcohol. Cocktail prices are not listed.

## Which dates need a £5 TGI Fridays deposit?

The Christmas page says: "Bookings for 6 and over require a £5 deposit per person." The TGI Fridays UK booking terms count adults and children and name the dates. They add that the £5 per guest is taken by debit or credit card from the person making the booking.

| Booking date | What the terms say | Deposit |
|---|---|---|
| Fri. 13 Nov to Sun. 6 Dec | Fridays to Sundays between 9 November and 10 December 2026 | £5 per guest, groups of 6 or more |
| Mon. 7 to Thu. 10 Dec | The Friday to Sunday wording does not cover these days | Not named in the terms |
| Fri. 11 Dec to Sat. 26 Dec | "all weekdays between 11th December 2026 and 26th December 2026 (inclusive)" | £5 per guest, groups of 6 or more |
| Thu. 31 Dec | "bookings for 31st December" | £5 per guest, groups of 6 or more |

The weekdays are our calculation from the terms' dates. The page uses the word "weekdays" for 11 to 26 December, and 26 December 2026 is a Saturday; check the wording with the restaurant for that day. The terms also say that festive and key date terms "are subject to change and will be released closer to the date".

## Can you cancel or change a TGI Fridays Christmas booking?

The terms give a full refund "for reservations cancelled at least 14 days in advance of the booking date". Date or time changes need "at least 24 hours prior to the booking time". A table booked for Friday 18 December 2026, for example, would need cancelling by Friday 4 December for the refund.

## Is there a TGI Fridays Christmas gift card offer?

Yes. The terms list "20% off digital e-gift vouchers via the TGI Fridays website between 23rd November to 30th November 2026 (inclusive)", with the code GC20TGI26, "for purchases up to £250 in one transaction". The offer starts Monday 23 November and ends Monday 30 November.

The early-booking free drink for groups of 7 or more (Heineken, Prosecco, Coca-Cola or Diet Coke) applied only to tables booked before 8 November 2026. It is closed to new bookings.

## What does TGI Fridays not say about Christmas 2026?

The pages read do not list a children's festive price, cocktail prices, a menu end date, or opening hours for Christmas Day, Boxing Day or New Year's Eve. For 31 December, the terms say only that bookings need a deposit.
