**Fenwick's 2026 Christmas window on Northumberland Street in Newcastle shows The Gruffalo's Child and opened for public viewing on Monday 2 November 2026**, after the live reveal at 6.30pm UK time on Sunday 1 November (1:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET)), according to Fenwick. Below: the Newcastle Christmas 2026 dates in one table, last year's windows, and a printable.

Fenwick's page says the windows stay open "throughout the festive season". It gives no end date, viewing hours or accessibility details, so this story does not either.

## What are the Newcastle Christmas 2026 dates?

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday 1 November 2026, 6.30pm UK time | Fenwick window reveal, live on Fenwick's official Instagram and YouTube channels | Fenwick |
| Monday 2 November 2026 | Fenwick Christmas windows open for public viewing, then throughout the festive season | Fenwick |
| Thursday 12 November 2026 | Christmas Lights Switch-On at Grey's Monument: live entertainment from 6pm, lights on at 7pm, free | Newcastle City Council; Newcastle's Christmas website |
| Thursday 19 November 2026 | Christmas Market opens, around the Monument and on Nelson Street | Newcastle City Council |
| Wednesday 23 December 2026 | Christmas Market in place until this date | Newcastle's Christmas website |

The market is open from 10am to 8pm Monday to Saturday and from 10am to 5pm on Sundays, according to the Newcastle's Christmas website, which is run by Newcastle NE1 Ltd. The council's announcement of 13 August 2026 says the festive season begins with the switch-on; the times above come from the Newcastle's Christmas event page.

## When can you see the Fenwick window?

From Monday 2 November 2026, per Fenwick. The reveal itself took place on Sunday 1 November and was shown live on Fenwick's official Instagram and YouTube channels at 6.30pm. Fenwick says children and families from its partner schools, hospitals and charities were guests, alongside winners of the reveal ballot.

## What is the Fenwick window theme for 2026?

The 2026 window brings The Gruffalo's Child to life, according to Fenwick's release of 1 November 2026. It is based on the book by Dame Julia Donaldson and Axel Scheffler, and was made with Macmillan Children's Books and Magic Light Pictures. Fenwick says the story follows the Gruffalo's Child into the snowy wood, with Fox, Owl and Snake, and ends at home by the Christmas tree.

Royal Northern Sinfonia recorded the soundtrack, a festive arrangement of Grieg's In the Hall of the Mountain King by Ian Anderson, conducted by James Weeks. Fenwick also sells a limited edition Gruffalo's Child Christmas bauble at £18 at Fenwick Newcastle and online, and says a donation from sales goes to the National Literacy Trust.

| Year | Theme | Dates, per Fenwick |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol, illustrated by Chris Riddell | Unveiled Sunday 2 November 2025; on view until early January 2026 |

The 2025 and 2024 details come from Fenwick's releases of 2 November 2025 and 3 November 2024, and the [2025 Fenwick blog post](https://fenwick.co.uk/blogs/daily-muse/fenwicks-2025-christmas-windows-bring-charles-dickens-a-christmas-carol-to-life). The 2023 theme is from Fenwick's [2023 window page](https://www.fenwick.co.uk/blogs/daily-muse-kids/fenwick-2023-christmas-window). Those are past years; Fenwick has not published a 2026 end date.
