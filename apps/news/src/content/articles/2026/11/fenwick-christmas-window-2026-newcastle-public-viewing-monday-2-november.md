**Fenwick's Christmas windows open for public viewing on Monday 2 November 2026 on Northumberland Street in Newcastle**, after the live reveal at 6.30pm UK time on Sunday 1 November (1:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET)), according to Fenwick's reveal page. Below: the Newcastle Christmas 2026 dates in one table, last year's windows, and a printable.

Fenwick's page says the windows stay open "throughout the festive season". It gives no end date, viewing hours or accessibility details, so this story does not either.

## What are the Newcastle Christmas 2026 dates?

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday 1 November 2026, 6.30pm UK time | Fenwick window reveal, live on Fenwick's official Instagram and YouTube channels | Fenwick |
| Monday 2 November 2026 | Fenwick Christmas windows open for public viewing, then throughout the festive season | Fenwick |
| Thursday 12 November 2026 | Christmas Lights Switch-On (time and location not yet announced) | Newcastle City Council |
| Thursday 19 November 2026 | Christmas Market opens, around the Monument and on Nelson Street | Newcastle City Council |
| Wednesday 23 December 2026 | Christmas Market in place until this date | Newcastle's Christmas website |

The market is open from 10am to 8pm Monday to Saturday and from 10am to 5pm on Sundays, according to the Newcastle's Christmas website, which is run by Newcastle NE1 Ltd. The council's announcement of 13 August 2026 says the festive season begins with the switch-on and did not give a time for it.

## When can you see the Fenwick window?

From Monday 2 November 2026, per Fenwick. The reveal itself was on Fenwick's official Instagram and YouTube channels at 6.30pm on Sunday 1 November. For public safety, Fenwick says a section of Northumberland Street will be closed on the evening of the reveal.

The ballot for reveal-night places is closed, and Fenwick says all randomly selected winners have been notified. Anyone without a place can see the windows from Monday.

## What is the Fenwick window theme for 2026?

Fenwick had not named the 2026 theme on its reveal page when we checked on 1 November. Some content sites name one, but no newsroom or Fenwick source confirms it, so we leave it out.

## What did the Fenwick windows show in 2023, 2024 and 2025?

| Year | Theme | Dates, per Fenwick |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol, illustrated by Chris Riddell | Unveiled Sunday 2 November 2025; on view until early January 2026 |
| 2024 | The 12 Days of Christmas reimagined by Chris Riddell | Open to view from Monday 4 November 2024 throughout the festive season |
| 2023 | The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, The Witch and The Wardrobe | Unveil date not given on the official page |

The 2025 and 2024 details come from Fenwick's releases of 2 November 2025 and 3 November 2024, and the [2025 Fenwick blog post](https://fenwick.co.uk/blogs/daily-muse/fenwicks-2025-christmas-windows-bring-charles-dickens-a-christmas-carol-to-life). The 2023 theme is from Fenwick's [2023 window page](https://www.fenwick.co.uk/blogs/daily-muse-kids/fenwick-2023-christmas-window). Those are past years; the 2026 end date has not been published.
