The 36th College Football Awards show airs Friday, Dec. 11, 2026, from 7 to 9 p.m. ET on ESPN, the network said in its Nov. 24 press release. The National College Football Awards Association (NCFAA) named three finalists for each of nine awards, and the full list is in the table below with a free watch-week printable.

## What time is the College Football Awards show in each time zone?

ESPN gives the time as 7 to 9 p.m. ET on Friday, Dec. 11, and says the two-hour special comes from its Bristol, Conn., studios. Kevin Negandhi, Taylor McGregor and Booger McFarland host, with Jen Lada reporting from New York City ahead of the 92nd Heisman Trophy Presentation. The other zones are our conversion.

| Zone | Start | End |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern | 7 p.m. | 9 p.m. |
| Central | 6 p.m. | 8 p.m. |
| Mountain | 5 p.m. | 7 p.m. |
| Pacific | 4 p.m. | 6 p.m. |

In 2025 the show aired Friday, Dec. 12, from 7 to 9 p.m. ET on ESPN. The 2026 slot is the same time one day earlier in the calendar.

## Who are the 2026 College Football Awards finalists?

ESPN's release lists these finalists for the awards presented during the show. The position labels are ESPN's.

| Award | ESPN's label | Finalists |
|---|---|---|
| Chuck Bednarik Award | Defensive Player of the Year | Colin Simmons (Texas), Leonard Moore (Notre Dame), Chris Cole (Georgia) |
| Biletnikoff Award | Outstanding Receiver | Jesse Legree (Oregon State), Jeremiah Smith (Ohio State), Malachi Toney (Miami) |
| Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award | Outstanding Placekicker | Luke Drzewiecki (Minnesota), Nolan Hauser (Clemson), Sam Keltner (Oklahoma State) |
| Ray Guy Award | Punter of the Year | Bailey Ettridge (Oregon), James Wagenseller (Army), Tyler White (Texas A&M) |
| Maxwell Award | Player of the Year | Jeremiah Smith (Ohio State), Devon Dampier (Utah), Darian Mensah (Miami) |
| Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award | Best Quarterback | Devon Dampier (Utah), Darian Mensah (Miami), Gio Lopez (Wake Forest) |
| Outland Trophy | Most Outstanding Interior Lineman | Xavier Gilliam (Tennessee), A.J. Holmes Jr. (Texas Tech), Kade Pieper (Iowa) |
| Paycom Jim Thorpe Award | Best Defensive Back | Ashton Hampton (Clemson), Jelani McDonald (Texas), Leonard Moore (Notre Dame) |
| Doak Walker Award | Premier Running Back | DeSean Bishop (Tennessee), Aiden Flora (Iowa State), Nate Sheppard (Duke) |

The Davey O'Brien Foundation's own Nov. 24 announcement names the same three quarterbacks: Dampier, Lopez and Mensah.

## Which players are finalists for two awards?

Counting the table, nine awards with three finalists each make 27 finalist spots, held by 23 players. Four players hold two spots.

| Player | School | Awards |
|---|---|---|
| Jeremiah Smith | Ohio State | Maxwell, Biletnikoff |
| Leonard Moore | Notre Dame | Bednarik, Thorpe |
| Devon Dampier | Utah | Maxwell, Davey O'Brien |
| Darian Mensah | Miami | Maxwell, Davey O'Brien |

The Maxwell Award field is Smith, Dampier and Mensah, so all three are also finalists for a position award. Miami holds three finalist spots: receiver Malachi Toney and the two for Mensah.

## What else is recognized during the show?

ESPN says the show also covers the Home Depot Coach of the Year, the NCFAA Contributions to College Football Award, the Walter Camp All-America Team, the Buddy Teevens Award, the Allstate American Football Coaches Association (AFCA) Good Works Captain, the William V. Campbell Trophy, the John Mackey Award, the Rimington Trophy, the Wuerffel Trophy, the Burlsworth Trophy, the Butkus Award and the Nagurski Trophy. The release does not name finalists for those honors.

## How does the show fit with Lombardi and Heisman week?

Two other awards land around the show. The Lombardi Award site says its winner is announced at a gala on Dec. 9, 2026, a Wednesday, and lists four finalists: Chris Cole (Georgia), Sammy Omosigho (UCLA), Tobi Osunsanmi (Indiana) and Colin Simmons (Texas). The gala time and venue are not yet announced.

The Heisman Trophy Trust's key dates on heisman.com list the winner announcement at the Heisman ceremony on Dec. 12, 2026, a Saturday. The start time is not yet announced; see our [Heisman Trophy 2026 ceremony story](/news/heisman-trophy-2026-ceremony-date-finalists-voting-deadline/) for the ballot deadline and last year's time.

| Sat., Dec. 12 | Heisman Trophy winner announced | Not yet announced |
