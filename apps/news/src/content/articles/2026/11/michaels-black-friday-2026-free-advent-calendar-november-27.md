**Michaels says its ten-day Black Friday sale runs Friday, Nov. 20, through Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026, and that free "Countdown to Craftsmas" advent calendars go out at checkout starting Black Friday, Nov. 27, with 500 per store, according to its Nov. 10 release.** Cyber Week follows from Monday, Nov. 30, to Saturday, Dec. 5. The day-by-day calendar, the discounts by category, how the free calendar works and a printable follow.

|---|---|---|
| Nov. 11, 2026 | Wednesday | Black Friday pricing starts on artificial Christmas trees |

The weekdays are our computation. Mark the dates on the [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars. The sale is a ten-day event; Retail Dive, in its Nov. 12 report, agrees on the dates and on the 500 calendars per store.

## How do I get the free Michaels advent calendar?

Starting Black Friday, Nov. 27, Michaels stores hand out the free Countdown to Craftsmas calendar at checkout. With just 500 per store, Michaels says, "they won't last long." Each of the 25 doors links by QR code to step-by-step tutorials, and from Dec. 1 to 25 a new door opens each day.

The release says the doors reveal:

- Screen-free projects, such as holiday crafts, friendship bracelets and Creatology Color Your Own mugs.
- In-store holiday experiences, including cookie decorating, wooden ornament painting and holiday junk journaling.
- Decorating and styling tips, such as hand-lettered gift tags and Christmas tree headbands.
- Online and in-store offers on yarn, ribbon and strung beads.

Select calendars also hold prize doors. One calendar in every Michaels store includes a $100 gift card, and one customer wins a $10,000 grand prize. Michaels says no purchase is necessary and points to official rules at michaels.com/lp/holiday-challenge-terms-conditions.

## Michaels Black Friday and Cyber Week discounts by category

Both periods are listed at up to 70% off. These are the offers the release names, with its wording for each period:

| Category | Black Friday, Nov. 20-29 | Cyber Week, Nov. 30 to Dec. 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Yarn | Buy one, get one 50% off all yarn | 35% off all yarn |
| Fine art and canvas | Buy one, get one 50% off marker, pen and pencil sets; buy one, get two free canvas | 70% off canvas |
| Ribbon and bows | Buy one, get three free bows and ribbons | 65% off Christmas ribbon |
| Frames | Buy one, get two free shadow boxes, display cases and tabletop frames | Up to 65% off frames |
| Artificial trees | Up to 70% off select pre-lit trees from Nov. 11; 4-ft. trees as low as $20 | Not listed |
| Holiday decorations | 60% off wreaths and garlands; ornaments $4 each | Not listed |

Doorbusters run Nov. 25-29 and include Lexington rolling carts, Artist's Loft canvas packs, shatterproof ornaments and gift wrap. Michaels Rewards members can earn $15 when they spend $50 from Nov. 20 to 29. Michaels also lists "Mega Deals" on seasonal items such as poinsettias, candles and greenery.

## Is Michaels open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Michaels has not said. The Nov. 10 release gives neither Thanksgiving Day status nor Black Friday opening and closing times, and michaels.com blocked our automated check of its store pages. Check your store's page before you go. ## How does Michaels' 2026 Black Friday compare with 2025?

Michaels' Nov. 12, 2025 release described a "ten-day Black Friday sales event from November 21 to November 30." The 2026 event is also ten days, one day earlier, Nov. 20 to 29. Free advent calendars with a gift card and grand prize are in this year's release; this page found no free in-store kids' events confirmed for 2026. Another advent calendar story: [LEGO advent calendars 2026](/news/lego-advent-calendars-2026-prices-whats-inside/).

## What has Michaels not announced?

- Thanksgiving Day status and Black Friday store hours.
- A separate Cyber Monday event; the release says "Cyber Week."
- Free in-store kids' events for 2026.
