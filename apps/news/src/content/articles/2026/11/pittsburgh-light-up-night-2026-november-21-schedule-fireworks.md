**Light Up Night 2026 in Pittsburgh is Saturday, Nov. 21, starting at 4 p.m. and ending with a 9:30 p.m. fireworks finale**, according to the City of Pittsburgh and the Pittsburgh Downtown Partnership. It is the 65th Highmark Light Up Night, a count that CBS Pittsburgh and a second local newsroom (see sources) both gave on Nov. 5, 2026.

The Downtown Partnership's page lists every tree lighting, fireworks show and stage set. The table below puts them in time order, and the printable timeline keeps the evening on one sheet. The month sits on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html), and the rest of the season is on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Light Up Night 2026 schedule: tree lightings, fireworks and stages

All times are Eastern Time on Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, from the Downtown Partnership unless noted.

| Time | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 4 p.m. | Light Up Night begins (City of Pittsburgh) | Downtown Pittsburgh |
| 4:00 | Confluence Theater of Pittsburgh; Joe Grushecky & The Houserockers at 6:00 and 7:20; Justin Fabus at 8:15 | Highmark Stage, Stanwix St. and Penn Ave. |
| 4:00 | SLiM Tha DJ; Neon Bridges at 6:30; Daya at 8:30 | Xfinity Holiday Jam stage, Arts Landing |
| 4:00 | Chantal Joseph, with more acts through the evening | City-County Building stage, 414 Grant St. |
| 5 p.m. | City of Pittsburgh Tree Lighting with Zambelli Rooftop Fireworks | City-County Building |
| 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 p.m. | Holiday light show | City-County Building stage |
| 6:30 p.m. | University of Pittsburgh Medical Center (UPMC) Ultimate Holiday Tree Lighting and American Cancer Society Tribute of Light Celebration | PPG Plaza |
| 7 p.m. | Highmark Christmas Tree Lighting with Zambelli Rooftop Fireworks | Stanwix St. and Penn Ave. |
| 8 to 8:10 p.m. | Sister Bridges Lighting and Cosmologyscape: A Dream for Pittsburgh | Ft. Duquesne Blvd. and Sixth St. |
| 8:30 p.m. | Headliner Daya, Xfinity Holiday Jam | Arts Landing |
| 9:30 p.m. | Xfinity Fireworks Spectacular with Supersized Zambelli Grand Finale | Ft. Duquesne Blvd., 9th Street Bridge and Arts Landing |

One local report listed the PPG Plaza lighting at 6 p.m.; we use the Downtown Partnership's 6:30 p.m. The City says its tree is the 113th official City tree lighting. The bridge lighting is short: it runs from 8 p.m. to 8:10 p.m., so get to Ft. Duquesne Boulevard early if you want to see the sister bridges switch on before walking the few blocks to Arts Landing for Daya at 8:30.

## Where to watch the 9:30 p.m. Light Up Night fireworks

The Downtown Partnership says the finale is best seen from the 9th St. (Rachel Carson) Bridge, Arts Landing and the area overlooking the Allegheny River along Ft. Duquesne Boulevard. It also lists a Rachel Carson Bridge party marking the bridge's 100th anniversary. No time is given.

## What else is open on Light Up Night 2026?

The City says the vendor market at the City-County Building closes at 7 p.m. and the Gingerbread Display closes at 8 p.m. The Downtown Partnership lists Pirate Parrot and Pierogies photo opportunities on Stanwix at 4:00 and a live ice carving at Fifth Avenue Place at 4:00.

The Peoples Gas Holiday Market in Market Square runs Nov. 20 to Dec. 24, CBS Pittsburgh and a second local newsroom agree. VisitPittsburgh says The Rink at PPG Place is open to the public from Nov. 13, 2026 to Feb. 28, 2027. For other rinks, see our [ice skating rinks guide](/news/ice-skating-rinks-2026-27-opening-dates-bryant-park-wollman-chicago-la-london/).

## What is not announced for Light Up Night 2026?

As of Nov. 18, we found no overall end time, TV or stream, parking rates or rain policy. Pittsburgh Regional Transit lists no extra service on rideprt.org. The City had not posted road closure times, so follow posted detours downtown.

## How does 2026 compare with Light Up Night 2025?

In 2025 Light Up Night was Saturday, Nov. 22, with the finale fireworks at 9:30 p.m. and The Calling as the headliner, according to a City news release dated Nov. 13, 2025. The finale time is the same. Only the headliner changed.

Other cities light trees on different dates; our [Christmas tree lighting dates](/news/christmas-tree-lightings-2026-dates-times-nyc-dc-boston/) story covers them, and the [Christmas parades](/news/christmas-parades-2026-us-cities-dates-times-routes/) story lists parade times.
