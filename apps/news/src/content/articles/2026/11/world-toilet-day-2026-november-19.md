**World Toilet Day is Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026,** according to the United Nations (UN), and this year's campaign is "Safe toilets. Equal futures." The UN's observance page says World Toilet Day 2026 "is about sanitation and gender equality." Below are the latest sanitation figures from the World Health Organization (WHO) and the United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF), the date through 2031 and a free one-page sheet.

## What is the World Toilet Day 2026 theme?

The UN page gives the campaign title as "Safe toilets. Equal futures." and says the day is about sanitation and gender equality. Its calls to action include:

- "Help raise awareness of the link between sanitation and gender equality."
- "We must invest in gender-responsive sanitation and make toilets a force for equality."
- "Women must help shape sanitation solutions, working alongside men."

The page also asks, "What action will you take for World Toilet Day 2026?" The UN's page for the day offers downloadable resources for campaigns. It says the day is about the link between sanitation and gender equality, with "not everyone faces the same impacts" from the lack of safe sanitation.

## How many people lack safe sanitation?

The UN page cites WHO and UNICEF for 3.4 billion people living without access to safe sanitation. The figures come from the report "Progress on Household Drinking Water and Sanitation 2000-2024: special focus on inequalities," which WHO and UNICEF announced on Aug. 26, 2025.

| Measure | Figure | Data year | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| People without safely managed sanitation | 3.4 billion | 2024 | WHO and UNICEF, Aug. 26, 2025 |
| Of them, people who practice open defecation | 354 million | 2024 | WHO and UNICEF, Aug. 26, 2025 |
| People without basic hygiene services at home | 1.7 billion | 2024 | WHO and UNICEF, Aug. 26, 2025 |
| Of them, people with no facilities at all | 611 million | 2024 | WHO and UNICEF, Aug. 26, 2025 |
| Share using a safely managed sanitation service | 58% (4.8 billion people) | 2024 | WHO Sanitation fact sheet, Nov. 3, 2026 |

## Is World Toilet Day an official UN day?

Yes. The UN General Assembly adopted resolution 67/291, "Sanitation for All," on July 24, 2013, according to the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), and the UN lists the day on its observances page. The UN's Sustainable Development Goal target 6.2 sets the 2030 aim: "By 2030, achieve access to adequate and equitable sanitation and hygiene for all and end open defecation, paying special attention to the needs of women and girls and those in vulnerable situations."

## Which water and sanitation dates come next?

World Water Day is March 22, according to the UN, which is Monday, Mar. 22, 2027 (our calculation). Its 2026 theme was "Where water flows, equality grows," and the 2027 theme is not announced.
