**Kroger stores are open on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, with reduced hours and closed pharmacies, according to FOX 4 Dallas-Fort Worth, ABC7 Los Angeles and New Tang Dynasty Television (NTD).** Kroger's own hours page (kroger.com/i/holiday) could not be read, so every time here is attributed to a newsroom. The newsrooms disagree on closing time: 4 p.m. or 5 p.m.

## What time does Kroger close on Thanksgiving 2026?

FOX 4 Dallas-Fort Worth reported on Nov. 19, 2026: "Stores will open with reduced hours." It gave no time. Three later reports, all dated Nov. 23, 2026, give two different answers.

| Source | Closing time, Thu., Nov. 26 | Stores named |
|---|---|---|
| LiveNOW from FOX | 4 p.m. ("according to the company's website") | Kroger |
| NTD | 4 p.m. | Kroger |
| ABC7 Los Angeles | 5 p.m. local time | Kroger, Fred Meyer, Ralphs and its other subsidiaries |

The sources do not agree, and this page does not pick one. Hours vary by store, so check your store's page on kroger.com or the Kroger app before you go. For stores in other chains, the [Publix Thanksgiving hours](/news/is-publix-open-thanksgiving-2026-christmas-eve-hours/) and the [Aldi Thanksgiving closure](/news/is-aldi-open-thanksgiving-2026-christmas-eve-hours/) are covered separately.

## Is the Kroger pharmacy open on Thanksgiving?

No. FOX 4 wrote "Pharmacies and clinics will be closed." ABC7 wrote "Pharmacies and clinics will be closed for the Thanksgiving holiday." NTD wrote that "on-site pharmacies and clinics will be closed." Fill any prescription before Thursday, Nov. 26. For drugstore chains, see the [Walgreens and CVS Thanksgiving hours](/news/is-walgreens-open-thanksgiving-2026-pharmacies-cvs/).

## Harris Teeter, Ralphs and Fred Meyer Thanksgiving hours

Harris Teeter is a Kroger company. ABC7 reports its stores are open on Thanksgiving until 2 p.m. local time and reopen Friday, Nov. 27 at 8 a.m. LiveNOW from FOX says "All Harris Teeter stores are open at 6 a.m. and close by 2 p.m., according to its website." The 2 p.m. close appears in both reports. The 6 a.m. opening is LiveNOW's alone, and the Friday 8 a.m. reopening is ABC7's alone.

| Store | Thanksgiving status | Pharmacy | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Kroger](https://www.kroger.com/) | Open, closes 4 p.m. or 5 p.m. by source | Closed | LiveNOW from FOX, NTD, ABC7 |
| [Ralphs](https://www.ralphs.com/) | Open until 5 p.m. local time | Closed (Kroger pharmacies) | ABC7 |
| [Fred Meyer](https://www.fredmeyer.com/) | Open until 5 p.m. local time | Closed (Kroger pharmacies) | ABC7 |
| [Harris Teeter](https://www.harristeeter.com/) | Open until 2 p.m. | Not named | ABC7, LiveNOW from FOX |
| [Pick 'n Save](https://www.picknsave.com/) | Opens 6 a.m., closing times may vary | Closed all day | Gray Television local report, Nov. 20, 2026 |

ABC7 says "Kroger, Fred Meyer, Ralphs and its other subsidiaries" stay open until 5 p.m. local time. No source read for this story gives separate hours for King Soopers, Fry's, Smith's, Dillons or Mariano's, so those are not announced here. The pharmacy column for Ralphs and Fred Meyer follows the reports that Kroger's pharmacies and clinics are closed; no source lists those two banners by name on pharmacies.

## Is Kroger open the day after Thanksgiving, and what about Christmas?

NTD says normal hours resume Friday, Nov. 27. That is one source, and Black Friday deals are not covered here. Kroger's hours for Christmas Eve (Thursday, Dec. 24) and Christmas Day (Friday, Dec. 25) are not announced. The dates sit on the [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars, and [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) is on Thursday, Nov. 26.

## What is Kroger selling for Thanksgiving 2026?

In its Nov. 5, 2026 release from Cincinnati, Kroger said shoppers can pick up "heat-and-eat holiday sides available in the fresh cases" and "single-slice holiday pies" at the bakery tables. The release says Thanksgiving groceries can be delivered "in as little as 30 minutes." It lists no hours or prices.
