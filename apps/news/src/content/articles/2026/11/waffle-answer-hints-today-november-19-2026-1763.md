### The swap route, move by move

The minimum is 10 swaps, found by an exhaustive search and replayed on the starting grid to reach the solved one. Play them in order, because each assumes the earlier ones are done. Swaps 3, 5, 8, 9 and 10 put two tiles home at once; swaps 1, 2, 4, 6 and 7 fix one each.

| Swap | Squares | Letters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Row 1, column 3 and row 4, column 3 | I and V |
| 2 | Row 1, column 4 and row 4, column 5 | I and E |
| 3 | Row 2, column 1 and row 4, column 1 | L and U |
| 4 | Row 2, column 3 and row 4, column 3 | R and I |
| 5 | Row 2, column 5 and row 5, column 3 | L and O |
| 6 | Row 3, column 1 and row 4, column 5 | O and I |
| 7 | Row 3, column 2 and row 5, column 2 | V and N |
| 8 | Row 3, column 4 and row 4, column 3 | A and R |
| 9 | Row 3, column 5 and row 4, column 5 | S and O |
| 10 | Row 5, column 2 and row 5, column 4 | V and E |

- **Swap 1:** V reaches row 1, column 3, the top of Vital. The I it replaces drops into row 4, column 3 as a stop on the way.
- **Swap 2:** E takes row 1, column 4, which finishes Bevel. The I goes to row 4, column 5, another temporary stop.
- **Swap 3:** L and U trade places in column 1, and both land home for Build.
- **Swap 4:** I moves up from the parked square to row 2, column 3, and R goes down in its place.
- **Swap 5:** L and O trade, and both finish: O in row 2, column 5 and L in row 5, column 3.
- **Swap 6:** I reaches row 3, column 1, the start of Intro. The tile it replaces, O, goes to row 4, column 5.
- **Swap 7:** N lands in row 3, column 2 and the V moves down to row 5, column 2.
- **Swap 8:** A and R trade places, and both are home: R ends Intro's fourth letter and A fills Vital's fourth letter.
- **Swap 9:** S and O trade, completing Loose and Intro together.
- **Swap 10:** V and E trade in row 5, which finishes Delve.

### Why the count is 10

Fifteen tiles are misplaced, and a loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops. Ten swaps means 5 loops.
