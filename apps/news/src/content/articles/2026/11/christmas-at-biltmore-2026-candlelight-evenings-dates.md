**Biltmore's Candlelight Christmas Evenings run Nov. 3, 2026 to Jan. 9, 2027, with tickets starting at $135**, according to The Biltmore Company's event and ticket pages, read Nov. 15, 2026. Biltmore says Candlelight is not available on Nov. 17, Nov. 26 or Dec. 24. The calendar below marks every night, and a one-page printable keeps the dates in one list.

## What does a Biltmore Candlelight ticket cost?

Biltmore's ticket page lists Candlelight Christmas House & Grounds at "starting at $135" for Nov. 3, 2026 to Jan. 9, 2027. Prices by date are not posted beyond that, and the page says prices vary by date. Biltmore also lists a discount for ages 16 and younger.

| Ticket | Price on Biltmore's page | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Candlelight Christmas House & Grounds | Starting at $135 | Nov. 3, 2026 to Jan. 9, 2027 |
| Christmas House & Grounds (daytime) | Starting at $110 | Now through Jan. 10 |
| Candlelight Exclusive Tour House & Grounds | Starting at $360 | Varies by date |
| House & Grounds | Starting at $70 | Resumes Jan. 11 |
| Titanic: The Human Story | From $105 | Opens Dec. 26 |
| Annual Pass | $209, down from $299 | Passholders get discounts on Titanic and Candlelight tickets |

The Exclusive Tour page says the tour takes 8 guests, lasts 1.5 hours, needs advance reservations and has a minimum age of 10. The ticket page lists a discount for ages 10 to 16 on it.

## How does Candlelight entry work?

Biltmore says the reservation time on your admission ticket is your Biltmore House entry time. It asks guests to arrive no later than that time: "If you arrive late, we will do our best to accommodate you, but we may be unable to do so." The entry time slots themselves are not posted on the pages we read.

The ticket includes all-day estate access: daytime admission to Antler Hill Village & Winery, the gardens, shops and restaurants. Biltmore says you can use the daytime part on the day of your evening visit or the day after, and the Winery visit includes a complimentary tasting. The evening itself is built around live music and the idea of being a guest at the first Vanderbilt family Christmas in 1895.

### Christmas Day entry

Biltmore's note says that on Christmas Day, estate entry begins at 1 p.m. for Passholders and overnight guests and at 4 p.m. for non-Passholders with Candlelight tickets. Dec. 25 is a Friday this year, and Candlelight is offered that night.
