**Six Flags says guests at participating parks can "buy a day" and get the 2027 spring and summer season included, with the Black Friday offer valid through Nov. 30, 2026, according to its Nov. 4 release.** On Nov. 18, the Silver Pass cost $59 at Six Flags New England and $99 at Kings Island, with 10 other parks between $65 and $89 on their own pages. Black Friday is Nov. 27; the offer runs three days past it, to Monday, Nov. 30.

## Six Flags Silver Pass price by park, cheapest first

Prices were read from each park's page at sixflags.com on Nov. 18, 2026. Each carries the note "Plus processing fee of up to $9.99 & applicable taxes"; the California's Great America and Magic Mountain pages say "Taxes may apply." Six Flags can change prices. The Silver Pass card reads "Silver Pass Unlimited Visits & Free Parking" next to a one-visit Good Any Day Ticket at the same price. The bundle is the Silver Pass plus a dining and drink plan.

Kings Island, Kings Dominion and Carowinds list no "was" price for the bundle on the pages read. Within the 12 parks, the Silver Pass is $59 at the cheapest and $99 at the highest.

## What does "buy a day, get the 2027 season" cover?

The release says "Buy a day" guests get "the 2027 spring and summer season included, providing months of amusement park fun for the price of a single visit." Park pages word it differently:

- Kings Island, Kings Dominion and Carowinds: the pass page reads "Thru Labor Day 2027."
- Six Flags Fiesta Texas: the banner reads "Buy a Day, Get the Year Free. Ends 11/30."
- Most other park pages: "Black Friday Deal: Buy a Day, Get the Summer Free. Ends 11/30."

Knott's Berry Farm and Canada's Wonderland are excluded, the release says. Whether a Silver Pass bought now covers 2026 holiday events is not confirmed by the pages read; check your park's pass comparison page first.

## How does the free bring-a-friend ticket work?

Six Flags says each new and renewing season pass, and each new membership, receives a bring-a-friend-for-free ticket. The offer is valid through Nov. 30, and "redemption dates vary based on park operating schedule, see each park website for details." Each park's use-by date is in the price table.

- Cedar Point: a Silver Pass bought by 11/30 gets a bring-a-friend ticket, the banner says.
- Kings Island: buy between 11/2 and 11/30 and use the ticket by 12/31/26, the page says.
- Carowinds: buy by 11/30 and use the ticket by 1/3/27, the page says.

Seven parks list use-by dates from Dec. 31, 2026 to Jan. 5, 2027; the other five list May 31, 2027.

## What does the Gold Pass cost at Kings Island, Kings Dominion and Carowinds?

Three parks list a higher tier on their season pass pages, read Nov. 18, 2026.

| Park | Higher tier | New price | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kings Island | Gold, "Valid all 2026 & 2027" | $135 | $125 |
| Kings Dominion | Gold | $105 | $95 |
| Carowinds | Gold-tier Season Pass, "Valid all 2026 & 2027" | $105 | $95 |

Prestige pass prices were not collected.

## Is there a resort deal with the pass sale?

The release also says guests who book two nights at participating Six Flags resorts can receive a third night free.

## Which Six Flags parks have holiday events this season?

The release lists Holiday in the Park at Great Adventure and Over Georgia from Nov. 21, 2026, to Jan. 3, 2027, and WinterFest at Carowinds from Nov. 27 to Jan. 3. The full list is in [Six Flags Holiday in the Park 2026 dates by park](/news/six-flags-holiday-in-the-park-2026-dates-by-park/).

## What has Six Flags not announced?

- A chain-wide start date for the sale; Kings Island's page says "Buy between 11/2 and 11/30."
- Whether a Silver Pass bought now covers 2026 holiday events.
