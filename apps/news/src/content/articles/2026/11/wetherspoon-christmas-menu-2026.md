**J D Wetherspoon's Christmas menu is on sale from Thursday 12 November until Thursday 31 December 2026, excluding Christmas Day, according to its "Christmas at Wetherspoon 2026" page of 15 September.** The page names a turkey main, four burgers, two pizzas and four small plates. It prints no prices. Wetherspoon's website blocks scripted access, so our research desk read the page on 14 November and the dishes below follow its wording.

## What is on the Wetherspoon Christmas menu 2026?

The dishes the page names, grouped by course:

| Course | Dish | What the page says |
|---|---|---|
| Main | Turkey Christmas dinner | Four turkey breast slices, a pork, sage & onion stuffing, roasted Chantenay carrots and parsnips, Maris Piper mash, two pigs-in-blankets, peas, cranberry sauce and gravy |
| Burger | Big cheese burger | Choose plant-based patty, 6oz beef patty, grilled chicken breast or fried buttermilk chicken |
| Burger | Brie & bacon burger | Same four patty choices |
| Burger | Mushroom & garlic burger | Same four patty choices |
| Burger | Chicken & stuffing burger | Same four patty choices |
| Pizza | 11" festive feast pizza | Named on the page, no toppings listed |
| Pizza | 11" Brie & garlic mushroom pizza | Named on the page, no toppings listed |
| Small plate | Breaded Camembert bites | Marked as new; in the "any 3 for" deal |
| Small plate | Pigs-in-blankets with cranberry sauce | In the "any 3 for" deal |
| Small plate | 8" festive feast pizza | In the "any 3 for" deal |
| Small plate | 8" Brie & garlic mushroom pizza | In the "any 3 for" deal |

Wetherspoon adds that "Deli Deal® dishes and children's menu festive options" are also available and points to the main Christmas menu for details. The page names none of them, so they are not listed here. A plant-based patty is one of the four patty options on the burgers. The page names no desserts or drinks offers.

## How much does the Wetherspoon Christmas menu cost?

The official page gives no prices, so none appear here. Prices vary by pub; the Wetherspoon app and the menu in your pub show what you pay.

## How many days does the Wetherspoon Christmas menu run?

Counting 12 November and 31 December, the window is 50 calendar days. Christmas Day is excluded, which leaves 49 days of service.

| Measure | Result | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| First day | Thursday 12 November 2026 | Wetherspoon page |
| Last day | Thursday 31 December 2026 | Wetherspoon page |
| Calendar days, both ends counted | 50 | Day count |
| Days without the menu | 1 (Friday 25 December) | Excluded on the page |
| Days with the menu | 49 | 50 minus 1 |
| Days before Christmas Day | 43 (12 November to 24 December) | Day count |
| Days after Christmas Day | 6 (26 to 31 December) | Day count |

## Is Wetherspoon open on Christmas Day 2026?

Wetherspoon has not published a 2026 Christmas Day opening list that we found, so opening is not confirmed here. The Christmas menu does not run on 25 December. Check your pub's page before you travel. Boxing Day and New Year's Eve 2026 hours are not announced either.
