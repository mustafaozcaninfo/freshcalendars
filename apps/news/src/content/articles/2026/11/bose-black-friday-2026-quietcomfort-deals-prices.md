## Which QuietComfort model costs less than its 2nd Gen?

Three pairs on the page show the model without "2nd Gen" at a lower sale price.

| Model without "2nd Gen" | Price | 2nd Gen model | Price | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| QuietComfort Headphones | $169 | QuietComfort Headphones (2nd Gen) | $199 | $30 |

The first pair has the same $359 regular price, so the headphones without a generation label carry the bigger saving, $190 against $160. The Ear Cushion Kit Set versions follow the same order: $224 for the headphones without a generation label and $248 for the 2nd Gen. Subtracting the headphone price leaves $55 and $49 for the cushion kit.

## What are the biggest Bose Black Friday savings in dollars?

The Home Theater Set is $450 off, $2,246 against $2,696. The Smart Ultra Soundbar and Surround Sound Set are each $350 off, at $649 and $1,447. The SoundLink Flex Portable Speaker (2nd Gen) is $99, $60 off, and the QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen) are $229, $70 off. The smallest cut is $9, on the AirFly SE adapter at $25.99.

In its home theater banner, Bose says "Save up to 15% on new Lifestyle Collection bundles." The Lifestyle Ultra Soundbar, the Lifestyle Ultra Subwoofer and the two aviation headsets show no regular price, so the table lists them with no discount shown.

## When do Bose's Black Friday deals end?

Bose does not say. The page has no start date, no end date and no year, and carries only a general line that "Bose offers seasonal promotions, including Black Friday deals on select audio products. Available discounts, eligible products, and promotional terms may vary." The page also shows a "Holiday price match promise": "We'll match a lower price through January 8, 2027 for purchases made Oct 30 – Dec 25." We did not read the full terms.

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is Monday, Nov. 30. Those are calendar dates; Bose has not tied its sale to either. Mark them on our [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/).
