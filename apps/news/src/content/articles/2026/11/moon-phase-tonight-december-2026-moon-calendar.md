**The full moon of December 2026 is at 8:28 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST) on Wednesday, Dec. 23**, which is 01:28 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) on Thursday, Dec. 24, according to the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO). In New York the Moon is 100% lit that Wednesday and rises at 3:58 p.m., then sets at 8:07 a.m. the next morning in the table below. The date shifts by zone, so a calendar that says "Dec. 24" is using UTC.

This page gives the moon phase tonight for any December date: phase, percent lit, moonrise and moonset for New York on all 31 days, plus each main phase in Eastern, Pacific and UTC time. For the Moon today, see our [November moon calendar](/news/moon-phase-tonight-october-27-november-2026-moon-calendar/).

## Is the December 2026 full moon a supermoon?

NASA's supermoon page lists "December 24, 2026 (01:28 UTC)" among its upcoming supermoons, and the Nov. 24 full moon as well. NASA defines a supermoon as a full moon that happens when the Moon's orbit is closest to Earth, called perigee. The page gives no perigee distance or date for December, so this story gives none. For the other full moon names and the supermoon season, see our [supermoon guide for November and December](/news/supermoon-2026-beaver-moon-cold-moon-geminids-solstice/).

## Moon phase on Christmas Eve, Christmas and New Year's Eve

On Thursday, Dec. 24 the Moon is a waning gibbous, 99% lit, and rises at 5:15 p.m. in New York. On Friday, Dec. 25 it is 96% lit and rises at 6:36 p.m., setting at 8:57 a.m. On Thursday, Dec. 31 it is a waning crescent, 41% lit, with moonrise at 12:40 a.m. and moonset at 11:42 a.m.; our [New Year's Eve moon story](/news/new-years-eve-2026-moon-phase-moonrise-time-midnight-new-york/) covers midnight.

## How bright is the Moon on the December sky dates?

The Moon's light matters most on the meteor shower nights. USNO puts the Moon at 18% lit on Sunday, Dec. 13 and 26% on Monday, Dec. 14, when the American Meteor Society (AMS) lists the Geminids peak as Dec. 13-14; see our [Geminid moon viewing times](/news/geminid-meteor-shower-2026-peak-december-13-14-moon-viewing-times/). The AMS lists the Ursids peak as Dec. 21-22, and USNO has the Moon 92% lit on Dec. 21 and 97% on Dec. 22, so it is bright; our [Ursid moonset times](/news/ursid-meteor-shower-2026-peak-december-21-22-moonset-times/) story has the city table. The AMS figures on its own page differ from USNO's, so we use USNO's throughout.
