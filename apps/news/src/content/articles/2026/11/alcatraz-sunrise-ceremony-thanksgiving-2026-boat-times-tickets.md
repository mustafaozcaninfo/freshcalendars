**The Indigenous Peoples Thanksgiving Sunrise Gathering on Alcatraz Island is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, with boats leaving Pier 33 in San Francisco every 15 minutes from 4:15 to 6:00 a.m. PT, according to the organizer, the International Indian Treaty Council (IITC).** Tickets are $15.50. Many attendees and news outlets also call the gathering Unthanksgiving Day. A free one-page morning timeline is further down this page.

## When do the boats leave Pier 33 for the Alcatraz sunrise ceremony?

IITC's page lists the event as "Thursday November 26, 2026," "November 26 @ 4:00 am – 9:00 am PST." It says: "Boats depart from Pier 33 every 15 minutes from 4:15 – 6:00 AM." and "All boats return by 9:00 AM." Indybay, a community news site, says the last sailing off the island is 8:45 a.m.; IITC gives 9:00 a.m. as the return time.

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) lists sunrise in San Francisco at 7:02 a.m. PST on Nov. 26, 2026. The boats run well before that.

## What is the morning timeline in Pacific and Eastern Time?

November 26 is after daylight saving time ends, so Pacific Time (PT) is on standard time and Eastern Time (ET) is three hours ahead. We converted IITC's times and USNO's sunrise.

## How do you get a ticket for the Alcatraz sunrise ceremony?

IITC lists tickets at "$15.50, children under 5 free."

1. Advance tickets went on sale Oct. 24, per IITC; the online link is on [IITC's event page](https://www.iitc.org/event/indigenous-peoples-thanksgiving-sunrise-gathering-on-alcatraz-island-2026/). We could not confirm whether they have sold out.
2. The ticket booth opens at 4:00 a.m. PT and, in IITC's words, "limited tickets will be for sale."
3. Indybay quotes the organizers: "If it does sell out only a very small number of tickets will be available the morning of the ceremony."
4. Pick a boat between 4:15 and 6:00 a.m. PT.

## Is Alcatraz open on Thanksgiving, and how does the ceremony differ?

The National Park Service (NPS) says: "Alcatraz is open every day except Christmas, Thanksgiving and New Year's day." Regular day tours do not run on Thanksgiving; the ceremony has its own boats from Pier 33. See our [list of national park sites open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/national-parks-open-thanksgiving-2026-statue-of-liberty-alcatraz/).

## How does the November gathering compare with the October one?

IITC also holds a sunrise gathering on Indigenous Peoples Day in October. The 2026 Indigenous Peoples Day gathering was its 47th annual, on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026, from 5:00 to 8:00 a.m. on the island. CalMatters reported more than 2,000 spectators at that gathering.

## How can you listen if you are not on the island?

IITC says the event will be broadcast live on 94.1 FM and online at kpfa.org, and will be "simulcast via IITC's Facebook page from 6:00-8:00 AM." Those are Pacific times, so 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. ET.

## Why do people gather on Alcatraz at sunrise?

IITC says: "The Native American occupation of this site began on November 20, 1969. The occupation ended June 11, 1971." CalMatters reports that the occupation began in November 1969 and that federal agents removed the remaining occupants on June 11, 1971.

## What else happens on Thanksgiving morning?

The National Day of Mourning takes place at noon ET on Cole's Hill in Plymouth, Massachusetts, the same day; noon ET is 9 a.m. PT.
