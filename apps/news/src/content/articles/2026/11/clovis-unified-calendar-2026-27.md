**Clovis Unified School District (CUSD) in California has spring break from Friday, March 19 to Monday, March 29, 2027, and winter break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Monday, Jan. 4, 2027**, according to its 2026-2027 student calendar PDF. School starts Monday, Aug. 17, 2026 and ends Friday, June 4, 2027. The district PDF prints the spring break start as March 19; some calendar sites show March 22-29.

## Clovis Unified no-school days in 2026-27

The district's holidays box lists each day off. The table keeps its wording, adds the weekday and counts the weekdays off. Elementary students also have "Elem Conference Day (No school for elementary students) Oct 30," so that row is not in the total.

| Dates | Weekdays off | What the calendar calls it |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Sept. 7, 2026 | 1 | "Sept 7 - Labor Day" |
| Fri., Oct. 30, 2026 | elementary only | "Elem Conference Day (No school for elementary students)" |
| Wed., Nov. 11, 2026 | 1 | "Nov 11 - Veterans Day" |
| Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | "Nov 23 - 27 – Thanksgiving Break" |
| Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | 11 | "Dec 21 - Jan 4 - Winter Break" |
| Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | 1 | "Jan 18 - Martin Luther King Day" |
| Mon., Feb. 8, 2027 | 1 | "Feb 8 - Lincoln's Birthday" |
| Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | 1 | "Feb 15 - Washington's Birthday" |
| Fri., March 19 to Mon., March 29, 2027 | 7 | "Mar 19 - Mar 29 - Spring Break" |
| Mon., May 31, 2027 | 1 | "May 31 - Memorial Day" |
| Total, all students | 29 | |

The holidays box also lists "Jul 2- Independence Day" (before school starts) and "June 17 - Juneteenth" (after the last day); neither is in the table. The PDF prints no count of school days, so none is given here.

## When is Clovis Unified winter break 2026?

The holidays box reads "Dec 21 - Jan 4 - Winter Break": Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Monday, Jan. 4, 2027. That is 11 weekdays off, and the first weekday after the printed break is Tuesday, Jan. 5. Some calendar sites end the break on Jan. 1, which is not what the district prints. Mark the dates on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When is Clovis Unified spring break 2027?

The district prints "Mar 19 - Mar 29 - Spring Break." March 19, 2027 is a Friday and March 29 is a Monday, so the weekdays off are March 19, March 22-26 and March 29: seven in all. The first weekday after the printed break is Tuesday, March 30. Some calendar sites show March 22-29 and miss the Friday, March 19 start. The [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html) shows the days, and our story on [spring break 2027 dates for school districts](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) covers other districts.

## What are Clovis Unified's Thanksgiving and Veterans Day dates?

Thanksgiving break is "Nov 23 - 27," Monday to Friday, five weekdays. The district's school-year page says "Schools closed. District office closed Nov. 26-27." Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, reads "Schools and district offices closed." The page lists Oct. 30 as "No school for elementary school children. Parent conferences."

## Why is Clovis Unified's Lincoln's Birthday on Feb. 8?

The calendar prints "Feb 8 - Lincoln's Birthday," a Monday, followed by "Feb 15 - Washington's Birthday," also a Monday. Lincoln's birthday itself is Feb. 12, and [Capistrano Unified](/news/capistrano-unified-calendar-2026-27/) closes on that Friday; Clovis moves the day off to Monday, Feb. 8. Both days are single Mondays, one week apart.

## What are the grading and early-dismissal dates at Clovis Unified?

The calendar prints "Every Wednesday 90-minute early dismissal for elementary students only." Grading periods differ by level.

| Level | Dates printed |
|---|---|
| Secondary (7-12) | Sept 25, Nov 6, Dec 18, Feb 19, April 16, June 4 |
| Elementary | Sept 25, Dec 4, Feb 19, Apr 30 |
| Elementary End of Quarter | Oct 23, Jan 15, Mar 18, June 4 |

This story uses only the 2026-27 student calendar. The PDF prints no board approval date.
