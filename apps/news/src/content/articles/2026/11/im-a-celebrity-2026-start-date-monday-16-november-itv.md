I'm A Celebrity returns for 2026 on Monday, 16 November at 9pm Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) on ITV1, Capital and What To Watch both report. The two newsrooms say the series launches that night, a day later than first planned.

The press centre of Independent Television (ITV) could not be reached for this story, so each date below is carried by two independent newsrooms: Capital, part of Global, and What To Watch, part of Future plc. What To Watch says the series starts on 16 November "at 9 p.m. GMT" and will air on ITV1; Capital lists the broadcaster's channels and streaming services, including ITV1 and Scottish Television (STV). The date, 16 November 2026, falls on a Monday.

Ant McPartlin and Declan Donnelly host again, both sources report, and What To Watch says the show "returns to its familiar home in the Australian jungle, near Murwillumbah in New South Wales." TV Guide and PinkNews, two more newsrooms, also carry the Monday, 16 November launch, and TV Guide gives 9pm on ITV1.

## I'm A Celebrity 2026 line-up

ITV announced the line-up before the launch, TV Guide and PinkNews report. TV Guide counts 11 campmates and notes that Leigh Francis, who had been reported as joining, is not on ITV's list. Ade Adepitan is the first wheelchair user to take part, PinkNews reports.

|---|---|
| Wayne Rooney | Football legend |

| Claire Sweeney | Soap icon |
| Mariella Frostrup | Broadcaster and journalist |

## I'm A Celebrity 2026 start time in US and Canadian time zones

The 9pm GMT launch is early evening in the United Kingdom and afternoon across North America. GMT is the same as Coordinated Universal Time in November, and United States and Canadian clocks are on standard time after the November change, so the table below converts the UK broadcast time. ITV has not announced an official way to watch the series in the US or Canada.

| Time zone | Launch time, Monday, 16 November |
|---|---|
| Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), United Kingdom | 9:00pm |
| Eastern Time (ET) | 4:00pm |
| Central Time (CT) | 3:00pm |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 2:00pm |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 1:00pm |

## Why I'm A Celebrity 2026 starts on a Monday

The launch moved back a day because of a Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) fixture. What To Watch says the date "was pushed back a day, following a scheduling clash with England's UEFA Nations League football match against Spain." Capital reports that now that ITV holds the UEFA Nations League rights, "bosses have had to go back to the drawing board."

England Football, run by The Football Association, lists the fixture as "Matchday 6: Spain v England" at 7.45pm GMT on Sunday, 15 November 2026. That Sunday-night match is the reason the jungle launch sits on the Monday.

## I'm A Celebrity 2026 launch week

Here is how the weekend into Monday lines up, with each event in UK time. The printable watch guide converts the launch time into Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific time as well.

| Date | Event | Time (GMT) |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday, 15 November | Spain v England, UEFA Nations League | 7.45pm |
| Monday, 16 November | I'm A Celebrity 2026 launch on ITV1 | 9.00pm |

## I'm A Celebrity…Unpacked 2026 on ITV2

The spin-off I'm A Celebrity…Unpacked returns live on ITV2, Capital reports, hosted by Sam Thompson and Kemi Rodgers. What To Watch names the same hosts and says it returns from 16 November 2026. The air time for Unpacked has not been announced.

## How long I'm A Celebrity ran in 2025

In 2025, the series began on Sunday, 16 November and ended with the final on Sunday, 7 December, a run of 21 days, Capital and Heart report; Angry Ginge won. ITV has not announced the 2026 final date.
