**The 45th Ford Holiday River Parade and river lighting ceremony begin Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, at 6 p.m. Central Time (CT) at the Tobin Center in San Antonio**, according to the San Antonio River Walk Association. That is 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET). A live broadcast starts at 7:05 p.m. CT (8:05 p.m. ET) from the Arneson River Theatre; the association does not name a channel for 2026.

The association's event page returned an access error to automated readers on Nov. 25, so the date and time also rest on [Visit San Antonio](https://www.visitsanantonio.com/event/45th-annual-ford-holiday-river-parade/13573/), Texas Public Radio (TPR) and San Antonio television station (KSAT) 12. The parade falls the day after Thanksgiving; see [When is Thanksgiving?](/when-is/thanksgiving/) and the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) to plan the weekend.

## What time does the River Walk parade reach each seating area?

The ticket page on the association's store lists prices including fees, and the parade reaches each viewing area at a different time. Only some areas are shown here; the page runs through Area W.

| Viewing area | Parade reaches it | Central (CT) | Eastern (ET) | Price with fees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Area L | 6:00 p.m. | 6:00 p.m. | 7:00 p.m. | $49.69 to $117.49 |
| Area A | 6:05 p.m. | 6:05 p.m. | 7:05 p.m. | $19.95 to $32.25 |
| Parade Plaza | 7:15 p.m. | 7:15 p.m. | 8:15 p.m. | $14.53 |
| Area W | 7:50 p.m. | 7:50 p.m. | 8:50 p.m. | not checked |

Across the areas on that page, tickets run from $14.53 to $117.49 including fees. The ticket page showed no sold-out notice on Nov. 25. The last area time is 7:50 p.m., so the parade takes about 1 hour 50 minutes to pass from the first area listed to the last. The association did not post a parade end time.

## Where is the free viewing area for the river parade?

The association lists a free viewing area on the east side of the River Walk between Pecan Street and Richmond Avenue. It is first come, first served, and visitors bring their own chair. Reserved seating and restaurant dinner packages are also offered, and the association says tickets are on sale now.

## What is the theme and who leads the 2026 parade?

TPR and KSAT 12 reported on July 7, 2026 that the Witte Museum, celebrating its 100th anniversary, is the grand marshal, and that the theme is "Holiday Wishes Come True." Visit San Antonio lists the event as the 45th annual. The association says the river lights brighten from sundown to sunrise through early January.
