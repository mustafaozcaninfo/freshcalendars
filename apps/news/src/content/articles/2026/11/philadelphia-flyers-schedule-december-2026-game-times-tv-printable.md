**The Philadelphia Flyers play 11 games in December 2026, six at home and five on the road, starting Thursday, Dec. 3, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against the Islanders, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** Home games are at Xfinity Mobile Arena, and the month ends Tuesday, Dec. 29, at the Seattle Kraken.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Philadelphia, read Nov. 12, 2026. It gives each start in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). We converted the times to ET, which is Philadelphia time, and calculated the weekdays. The feed lists broadcasters and they can change. A one-page printable is further down.

## Flyers game times and TV for every December game

Network names are as the feed lists them. Turner Network Television (TNT) carries the Dec. 16 game. Sportsnet, Sportsnet One, Sportsnet+, Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports and TSN3 are Canadian networks. In the US column, Wild+, "Wild's own channel", "Blue Jackets' own channels" and "Seattle local channel" are how we read the feed's short codes for the home teams' own or local outlets. Scripps Sports, Detroit SportsNet, Chicago Sports Network (CHSN) and New England Sports Network (NESN) are local outlets. "None listed" means the feed shows no Canadian broadcaster.

## Which Flyers games are on national TV?

The feed lists Rangers at Flyers on Wed., Dec. 16, at 7:30 p.m. ET on TNT, truTV and HBO Max, with no NBC Sports Philadelphia listing. The Dec. 3 opener against the Islanders is listed on ESPN+, Hulu and Disney+ in the US. The Dec. 29 game at Seattle lists Prime Video along with NBC Sports Philadelphia and a Seattle local channel.

## Home games, road trips and the break before Christmas

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Home games (6):** Dec. 3, 11, 14, 16, 18 and 21, all at Xfinity Mobile Arena.
- **Road games (5):** Dec. 5 at Minnesota, Dec. 8 at Winnipeg, Dec. 20 at Boston, Dec. 27 at Chicago and Dec. 29 at Seattle.
- **Back-to-back:** Sun., Dec. 20, at Boston at 1 p.m. ET, then Mon., Dec. 21, at home against Columbus at 7 p.m. ET.
- **Start after 7:30 p.m. ET:** Sat., Dec. 5, at 8 p.m. in Minnesota and Tue., Dec. 29, at 9:40 p.m. in Seattle.
- **Start before 7 p.m. ET:** Sun., Dec. 20, at 1 p.m. in Boston and Sun., Dec. 27, at 3 p.m. in Chicago.
- **No game:** Dec. 22 to Dec. 26, so the Flyers play Dec. 21 at home and not again until Dec. 27 in Chicago. They also have no game on Dec. 30 or Dec. 31.

The first January game is Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, at the San Jose Sharks, 4 p.m. ET (1 p.m. PT), listed on NBC Sports Philadelphia in the NHL's January feed.
