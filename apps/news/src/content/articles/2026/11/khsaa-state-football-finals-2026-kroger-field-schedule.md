**The Kentucky High School Athletic Association (KHSAA) will play all six 2026 state football finals at Kroger Field in Lexington on Friday, Dec. 4, and Saturday, Dec. 5, with kickoffs at noon, 4 p.m. and 8 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on both days, according to its ticket page dated Nov. 20, 2026.** Central Time (CT) kickoffs, ticket prices and the fourth-round pairings that decide each final are below, followed by a printable schedule.

## KHSAA state football finals 2026: kickoff times and how each final is set

KHSAA's ticket page says: "The games will again be played at Kroger Field at the University of Kentucky in Lexington." It lists the schedule as "All times Eastern." The CT column subtracts one hour and is computed by Fresh Calendars; fans in western Kentucky, such as Owensboro and Paducah, are on Central Time. Each final pairs the winners of that class's two fourth-round games, played Friday, Nov. 27.

| Class | Day | Kickoff (ET) | Kickoff (CT) | Who plays in the final |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | Fri., Dec. 4 | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | Raceland-Pikeville winner vs. Bethlehem-Kentucky Country Day winner |
| 2A | Fri., Dec. 4 | 4 p.m. | 3 p.m. | Belfry-Owensboro Catholic winner vs. Beechwood-Lexington Christian winner |
| 4A | Fri., Dec. 4 | 8 p.m. | 7 p.m. | Covington Catholic-Paducah Tilghman winner vs. North Oldham-Highlands winner |
| 3A | Sat., Dec. 5 | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | Rockcastle County-Christian Academy-Louisville winner vs. Lloyd Memorial-Lexington Catholic winner |
| 6A | Sat., Dec. 5 | 4 p.m. | 3 p.m. | Trinity (Louisville)-Frederick Douglass winner vs. Madison Central-DuPont Manual winner |
| 5A | Sat., Dec. 5 | 8 p.m. | 7 p.m. | Atherton-Pulaski County winner vs. Owensboro-Woodford County winner |

Note the order: Saturday's games run 3A, 6A, then 5A, not in class order.

## What are the fourth-round games on Friday, Nov. 27?

Each KHSAA class bracket page is headed "Pairings for Friday, November 27." In each pairing, the school after "at" hosts the game.

| Class | Game 1 | Game 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | Raceland at Pikeville | Bethlehem at Kentucky Country Day |
| 2A | Belfry at Owensboro Catholic | Beechwood at Lexington Christian |
| 3A | Rockcastle County at Christian Academy-Louisville | Lloyd Memorial at Lexington Catholic |
| 4A | Covington Catholic at Paducah Tilghman | North Oldham at Highlands |
| 5A | Atherton at Pulaski County | Owensboro at Woodford County |
| 6A | Trinity (Louisville) at Frederick Douglass | Madison Central at DuPont Manual |

KHSAA's Nov. 20 release says "Kickoff times will be announced on Monday," which is Nov. 23. Those times were not posted when this story was written. The ticket page calls this round "the semifinals." The round falls the day after [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/), Thursday, Nov. 26, and the finals weekend follows one week later, at the start of the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## How much are KHSAA state finals tickets in 2026?

All ticketing is digital only through GoFan, "the official ticket system for the KHSAA," at gofan.co/app/school/KHSAA?activity=Football. KHSAA says advance purchases include parking charges and credit card processing fees, and that there has been "no change in ticket pricing from 2025." Prices below come from the Nov. 20 ticket page; "after midnight" means just after midnight on the first day of the finals, Dec. 4.

| Seat area | Full set (all six games) | Single game | Price change after midnight Dec. 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| General admission, rest of stadium | $80 | $17, parking included | Set rises to $95; single game $20 if available on game day |
| Elijah Craig Club (formerly Longship Club), premium seats | $135 | $27 up to the day before the game | Set rises to $150 if sets remain; single game $30 if available |
| Central Bank Club (formerly Field Level Club) | No advance sets | $17 | $20; sold only after the semifinals |

Elijah Craig Club fans get an armband in the Commonwealth Tower Lobby. The Central Bank Club seats are part of the home-team allocation on the Alumni Drive side.

## Who won the KHSAA finals in 2025?

KHSAA's 2025 championship releases name these winners. Five of the six are in this year's fourth round.

| Class | 2025 champion | 2026 status |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | Kentucky Country Day | Hosts Bethlehem |
| 2A | Lexington Christian | Hosts Beechwood |
| 3A | Christian Academy-Louisville | Hosts Rockcastle County |
| 4A | Boyle County | Not in the 4A fourth round |
| 5A | Owensboro | At Woodford County |
| 6A | Trinity | At Frederick Douglass |

In 2025, Christian Academy-Louisville beat Murray 42-7 in 3A, and Owensboro beat Pulaski County 35-7 in 5A. Owensboro and Pulaski County play in different 2026 fourth-round games, so they can meet again only in the final.

## How to watch the KHSAA finals

KHSAA's information page says all games of the 2026 State Football Finals can be watched live on the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network. It also lists audio for select championship events. Gate times, parking lots and bag rules were not on the pages read, so none are given here.
