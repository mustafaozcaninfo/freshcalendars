**2027 has 52 weeks and 1 day**: 365 days, because it is not a leap year, and 52 × 7 is 364. Friday, Jan. 1 and Friday, Dec. 31 are the same weekday, which gives the year 261 weekdays by our count of Monday to Friday. The 11 holidays on the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) federal list all fall on weekdays in 2027, leaving 250 federal workdays. The tables below add the week numbers and weekday count for each month.

## Weeks, days and weekdays in 2027

| Measure | 2027 |
|---|---|
| Days | 365 (52 weeks and 1 day) |
| Weekdays, Monday to Friday | 261 |
| Saturdays and Sundays | 104 (52 of each) |
| Fridays | 53 |
| Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Thursdays | 52 each |
| Federal holidays on weekdays | 11 |
| Federal workdays | 250 |

The extra day is why one weekday gets an extra turn. A year of 365 days ends on the same weekday it began, so 2027 starts and ends on a Friday and holds 53 of them. Every other weekday appears 52 times. OPM does not publish a count of federal workdays; 250 is our arithmetic from its holiday list.

## How the 52 numbered weeks are counted

Numbered weeks follow the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) date standard, ISO 8601. Markus Kuhn's reference, hosted by NASA Goddard, gives the rule: "Week 01 of a year is per definition the first week that has the Thursday in this year", which is "the week that contains the fourth day of January." Weeks start on Monday.

For 2027 that makes Monday, Jan. 4 the first day of week 1. Friday, Jan. 1 to Sunday, Jan. 3 belong to week 53 of 2026, which is a 53-week year. Week 52 of 2027 runs Monday, Dec. 27 to Sunday, Jan. 2, 2028, so Saturday and Sunday, Jan. 1 and 2, 2028 count as part of 2027. The numbers for every week are in our [2027 week numbers guide](/news/week-numbers-2027-calendar-printable/), and [why some years reach 53 weeks](/news/why-some-years-have-53-weeks/) explains the rule.

## What 53 Fridays mean for paydays

Pay schedules count in 52-week and 26-week units, but 2027 has 365 days. The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) lists the pay periods per year in Table 3 of Publication 15-T (2026 edition): weekly 52, biweekly 26, semimonthly 24, monthly 12 and daily 260.

A year with 53 Fridays pushes some paydays past those totals. A weekly Friday payday pays 53 times in 2027. A biweekly Friday schedule that starts Friday, Jan. 1 pays 27 times, because Jan. 1 plus 26 two-week steps lands on Dec. 31; the months with three paychecks are in our [biweekly payday calendar for 2027](/news/three-paycheck-months-2027-biweekly-payday-calendar/). A schedule that starts Friday, Jan. 8 stays at 26.

## When does the 2027 federal leave year start?

OPM's leave year fact sheet says leave year 2027 begins Jan. 10, 2027 and ends Jan. 8, 2028, and that "a leave year begins on the first day of the first full biweekly pay period in a calendar year." That is 364 days, or 26 biweekly pay periods, by our arithmetic. An agency's payroll system can set its own dates.
