**The Missouri State High School Activities Association (MSHSAA) plays the seven 2026 Show-Me Bowl football finals Thursday, Dec. 3, through Saturday, Dec. 5, at Craig Field at Spratt Memorial Stadium on the Missouri Western State University campus in St. Joseph, according to its Information Central page read Nov. 26, 2026.** The 8-Man final opens the week at 7 p.m. Central Time (CT); Classes 6, 2 and 4 follow Friday and Classes 5, 1 and 3 on Saturday. Every kickoff, the semifinal times and a printable schedule are below.

## MSHSAA football state championships 2026: every Show-Me Bowl kickoff

MSHSAA bills the week as the "59th MSHSAA 11-Man Football Championships" and the "39th 8-Man Football Championships." Its [Information Central page](https://www.mshsaa.org/Content/Football/InformationCentral.aspx) lists "December 3, 2026 (8-Man)," "December 4, 2026 (Classes 6,2,4)" and "December 5, 2026 (Class 5,1,3)." Times are local to St. Joseph, which is on Central Time; the Eastern Time (ET) column adds one hour and is computed by Fresh Calendars. All matchups were still to be determined.

| Day | Date | Class | Kickoff (CT) | Kickoff (ET) | Matchup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thu. | Dec. 3 | 8-Man | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | To be determined |
| Fri. | Dec. 4 | Class 6 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | To be determined |
| Fri. | Dec. 4 | Class 2 | 3 p.m. | 4 p.m. | To be determined |
| Fri. | Dec. 4 | Class 4 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | To be determined |
| Sat. | Dec. 5 | Class 5 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | To be determined |
| Sat. | Dec. 5 | Class 1 | 3 p.m. | 4 p.m. | To be determined |
| Sat. | Dec. 5 | Class 3 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | To be determined |

MSHSAA's [championship schedule PDF](https://www.mshsaa.org/resources/pdf/MSHSAA%20Championship%20Schedule.pdf) agrees on the dates: the 39th Show-Me Bowl (8-Man) on Dec. 3 and the 59th Show-Me Bowl (Classes 1-6) on Dec. 4-5, both at Spratt Memorial Stadium in St. Joseph. Note the order: Friday runs Classes 6, 2, 4 and Saturday runs 5, 1, 3, not class by class.

## What changed from the 2025 Show-Me Bowl schedule?

In 2025 the finals were Dec. 4-6 at the same stadium. Class 6 kicked off Saturday, Dec. 6, at 7 p.m., according to High School on SI (Dec. 4, 2025). In 2026 Class 6 opens the Friday at 11 a.m. CT, so Class 6 plays the first game of the Classes 1-6 weekend instead of the last. Lee's Summit beat Nixa 41-37 in the 2025 Class 6 final, per the Jefferson City News Tribune (Dec. 7, 2025) and 810 Varsity (Dec. 6, 2025).

## When are the 2026 MSHSAA state semifinals?

MSHSAA's [state championship bracket pages](https://www.mshsaa.org/Activities/DistrictWinners.aspx?alg=19&class=1) list the semifinal times for each class. Winners advance to the finals above. Matchups for Classes 1-5 are on those pages; they are not repeated here.

| Class | Semifinal times | Final (CT) |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | Sat., Nov. 28, 2 p.m. (both games) | Sat., Dec. 5, 3 p.m. |
| Class 2 | Sat., Nov. 28, 1 p.m. (both games) | Fri., Dec. 4, 3 p.m. |
| Class 3 | Sat., Nov. 28, 1 p.m. and 2 p.m. | Sat., Dec. 5, 7 p.m. |
| Class 4 | Sat., Nov. 28, 1 p.m. (both games) | Fri., Dec. 4, 7 p.m. |
| Class 5 | Sat., Nov. 28, 1 p.m. (both games) | Sat., Dec. 5, 11 a.m. |
| Class 6 | Fri., Nov. 27, 7 p.m. and Sat., Nov. 28, 1 p.m. | Fri., Dec. 4, 11 a.m. |

The Class 6 semifinals, per the bracket and High School on SI (Nov. 23, 2026): St. Louis University High (SLUH), 9-3, at Jackson, 12-0, on Friday, Nov. 27 at 7 p.m.; and Rockhurst, 8-4, at Blue Springs, 10-2, on Saturday, Nov. 28 at 1 p.m. In the quarterfinals Jackson beat Christian Brothers College 45-28, SLUH beat Troy Buchanan 28-14, Blue Springs beat Nixa 42-14 and Rockhurst beat Liberty North 17-12. The semifinal times above sit on the [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) weekend of the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## How to watch the Show-Me Bowl on MSHSAA TV

MSHSAA says: "All seven games will stream exclusively on MSHSAA TV and the MSHSAA TV suite of apps (iPhone, Android, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV and Android TV)." The price: "Individual games are available for $20, or fans can purchase a $25 championship pass for access to all seven games across all three days of competition."

## What is not announced for the finals yet?

MSHSAA's Information Central page shows no gate ticket price or sales platform, so none is given. The finals matchups are set only after the Nov. 27-28 semifinals, and no 8-Man semifinal details were on the pages read. The page links three PDFs for game day: [spectator guidelines](https://www.mshsaa.org/resources/Activities/Football/MWSU%20Prohibited%20and%20Permitted%20Items.pdf), a [parking map](https://www.mshsaa.org/resources/Activities/Football/MoWest%20Parking%20Map.pdf) and the [tailgate policy](https://www.mshsaa.org/resources/Activities/Football/Tailgate-Policy-Revisions-9.2024%20(1).pdf).

## Where are the Missouri football finals played after 2026?

KQ2 reported on Sept. 18, 2026: "Missouri high school football state championship games will be played in St. Joseph at Spratt Stadium through 2030." The St. Joseph Convention and Visitors Bureau lists the [2026 state football championships](https://stjomo.com/calendar-event/2026-mshsaa-state-football-championships/) for Dec. 3-5 at Spratt Stadium, 4525 Downs Drive.
