**Ember Inns' 2026 festive set menu runs from 1 to 31 December at "2 courses from just £19.49 for adults and from £9.99 for children", according to the pub chain's own Christmas pages read on 23 November 2026.** Christmas Day and Boxing Day have separate menus, and both need a pre-order by Tuesday 1 December. The four Ember Inns menus, their deposits and the cut-off dates are in the tables below.

## What do the four Ember Inns Christmas menus cost?

Every price is a "from" price. Ember Inns lists no serving times, opening hours or calorie counts on these pages.

| Menu | Dates | Adult | Child | Deposit per person |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Festive set menu, 2 courses | 1-31 Dec 2026, not 25 or 26 Dec | from £19.49 | from £9.99 | £5 |
| Christmas Day, 3 courses | Fri. 25 Dec 2026 | from £65.95 | from £32.95 | £10 |
| Boxing Day, 3 courses | Sat. 26 Dec 2026 | from £29.50 | from £11.99 | £5 |
| Breakfast with Santa | Sat. 12 and Sun. 13 Dec 2026 | £12.99 per person | see below | none given |

The festive page also says "three courses for under £27", the only 3-course figure given for that menu. It names Fireside Roasted Tomato Soup, Hand-carved Turkey and Sticky Toffee Christmas Pudding, and a second page adds Steak & Venison Pie, Gressingham Confit Duck Leg and Mushroom & Chestnut Suet Pudding. A children's festive menu exists, but its contents are not listed. Mark the dates on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When is the Ember Inns Christmas pre-order deadline?

For Christmas Day, "your pre-order is required by 1st December", and full payment is also due by then. For Boxing Day, "a pre-order is required by 1st December". Tuesday 1 December 2026 is eight days after 23 November, our count. The festive set menu is "available by pre-order only", with the deposit taken at booking.

| Item | Date | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas Day and Boxing Day pre-order | Tue. 1 Dec 2026 | Required by this date; Christmas Day also needs full payment |
| Christmas Day dinner | Fri. 25 Dec 2026 | £10 per person deposit |
| Boxing Day | Sat. 26 Dec 2026 | £5 per person deposit; the balance is paid on the day |
| New Year's Eve | Thu. 31 Dec 2026 | Deposit £5 or £10 by page; confirm with the pub |

Christmas Day dishes named on the Ember Inns page are the Ultimate Christmas Duo, Best Grilled Salmon and Festive Hand-carved Roast Beef, with vegetarian and vegan options. The page adds that availability may vary by pub.

## What are the Boxing Day dishes at Ember Inns?

The Boxing Day menu names Gressingham Confit Duck Leg, Festive Hunter's Chicken Schnitzel and Pulled Ham Hock & Welsh Rarebit Burger, and one page lists a Steak & Venison Pie as well. A children's menu is available. [When Boxing Day falls in the UK](/when-is/uk-boxing-day/) is on our calendar site.

## When do Ember Inns deposits stop being refundable?

The deposits page says "deposits are non-refundable within 14 days of dining". Counting back 14 days from the dining date gives these dates, our calculation; the page does not give dates.

| Dining date | Deposit non-refundable from |
|---|---|
| Fri. 25 Dec 2026, Christmas Day | Fri. 11 Dec 2026 |
| Sat. 26 Dec 2026, Boxing Day | Sat. 12 Dec 2026 |
| Thu. 31 Dec 2026, New Year's Eve | Thu. 17 Dec 2026 |

## What is Ember Inns Breakfast with Santa in 2026?

Breakfast with Santa takes place on Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 December 2026 for "£12.99 per person". Each child gets a Kids' Breakfast (Classic or Veggie), a Fruit Shoot, a festive activity pack, a Christmas gift and a meet-and-greet with Santa. Adults get a complimentary tea or black coffee. "A minimum purchase of one Adult Classic Breakfast is required per booking", and booking in advance is needed. No session times are published.

## How do Ember Inns prices compare with Toby Carvery and Harvester?

On Christmas Day, Ember Inns' £65.95 adult price is £2.04 below the [Toby Carvery](/news/toby-carvery-christmas-menu-2026/) figure of £67.99 and £4.04 below the [Harvester](/news/harvester-christmas-menu-2026/) figure of £69.99 per adult, computed from the three chains' own pages. For Boxing Day, Ember Inns at £29.50 is £1.71 above Toby Carvery's £27.79. Harvester has published no Boxing Day price. All are "from" prices, and the pages do not say the menus have the same courses.

| Chain | Christmas Day | Boxing Day |
|---|---|---|
| Ember Inns | from £65.95 | from £29.50 |
| Toby Carvery | from £67.99 | from £27.79 |
| Harvester | from £69.99 per adult | not published |

Other chains are covered in the [Wetherspoon Christmas menu story](/news/wetherspoon-christmas-menu-2026/).
