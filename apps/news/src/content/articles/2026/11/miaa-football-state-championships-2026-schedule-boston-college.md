**The Massachusetts Interscholastic Athletic Association (MIAA) will play its eight 2026 football championships from Friday, Dec. 4, to Sunday, Dec. 6, and Boston College's Alumni Stadium hosts four of them.** The MIAA posted the schedule on Nov. 23, 2026. Games are Division 1 to Division 8, every kickoff is Eastern Time (ET), and all eight are streamed live on the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network. The table gives each game by day, time, matchup and stadium.

## What is the MIAA football championships schedule for 2026?

The MIAA's announcement says "The 2026 MIAA Football Championships will be played December 4-6." Numbers before team names are the seeds as the MIAA prints them.

| Day | Kickoff (ET) | Division | Matchup | Stadium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | D7 | No. 7 Rockland vs. No. 9 Amesbury | Merrimack College, North Andover |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 7 p.m. | D8 | No. 1 Lee vs. No. 3 Nashoba Valley Tech | Worcester State University, Worcester |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 11 a.m. | D5 | No. 1 Shawsheen Valley Tech vs. No. 7 Foxborough | Veterans Memorial Stadium, Quincy |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 11:30 a.m. | D1 | No. 1 Saint John's Prep vs. No. 2 Saint John's | Alumni Stadium, Boston College |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 3 p.m. | D3 | No. 1 Mansfield vs. No. 3 Marshfield | Alumni Stadium, Boston College |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 3 p.m. | D6 | No. 1 Norwell vs. No. 3 Abington | Veterans Memorial Stadium, Quincy |
| Sun., Dec. 6 | 11:30 a.m. | D2 | No. 1 Catholic Memorial vs. No. 3 Billerica | Alumni Stadium, Boston College |
| Sun., Dec. 6 | 3 p.m. | D4 | No. 2 Tewksbury vs. No. 5 Walpole | Alumni Stadium, Boston College |

The MIAA lists the Division 1 teams as Saint John's Preparatory School and Saint John's High School, and Lee as Lee Middle/High School. Nashoba Valley Technical High School is shortened to Nashoba Valley Tech above.

## Why are the finals not at Gillette Stadium?

The 2025 championships were played Thursday, Dec. 4, to Saturday, Dec. 6, 2025, at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, according to the MIAA's Nov. 24, 2025 announcement. Boston.com reported on Nov. 2, 2026 that the MIAA said using Gillette "is no longer an option." A Nov. 3 report from Boston public media said scheduling conflicts with the Revolution and Patriots were the reason.

## Tickets and how to watch the MIAA football finals

The MIAA says: "Tickets are $22 per person (including fees) and will be available via GoFan for advance purchase." Tickets "also can be purchased on game day at each of the four championship venues." Its release adds: "All eight championship games will be streamed live on the NFHS Network."
