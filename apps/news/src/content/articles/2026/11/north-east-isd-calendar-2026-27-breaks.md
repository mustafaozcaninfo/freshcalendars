**North East Independent School District (NEISD) in San Antonio, Texas, lists Thanksgiving break for Nov. 23-27, 2026, winter break as "12/21/2026-1/4/2027" and spring break for March 8-12, 2027**, on its "Calendars" page. Traditional campuses started Aug. 17 and the last day of classes is Thursday, May 27, 2027.

The page says "Academic Calendars are Subject to Change." It gives no board approval date, so every date here is as posted by the district on Nov. 5, 2026. This story adds what the page does not: weekdays off by month, school days between breaks, the three NEISD calendars side by side, early release dates from a second district page, and a comparison with Northside Independent School District (NISD).

## NEISD breaks at a glance

Weekday counts and return days are our arithmetic from the district's dates.

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | School resumes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student holiday plus Columbus/Indigenous Peoples' Day | Fri., Oct. 9 and Mon., Oct. 12, 2026 | 2 | Tue., Oct. 13, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break | "12/21/2026-1/4/2027," Mon., Dec. 21 to Mon., Jan. 4 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 8-12, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 15, 2027 |

The district prints no return day for any of these. We use the next weekday its list does not show as off. The winter break dates mean 17 days in a row without school, from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Monday, Jan. 4, and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the weeks.

The list also shows Labor Day on Sept. 7, Martin Luther King Jr. Day on Jan. 18, 2027 and Presidents' Day on Feb. 15, 2027, all Mondays. Good Friday on March 26 and Battle of Flowers on April 23, 2027, both Fridays, are listed too. Battle of Flowers is a San Antonio Fiesta holiday.

## Is there a fall break at NEISD?

The district lists no fall break week. For traditional campuses it lists "Student Holiday: 10/9/2026" and "Columbus/Indigenous Peoples’ Day: 10/12/2026," a Friday and a Monday, so the weekend becomes four days. Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, is on the same list. Both dates have passed. Castle Hills Elementary, the year-round campus, has a student intersession Oct. 5-16, 2026.

## How does NEISD choose its calendar?

The district says it gives campuses two calendar options for review. Campuses vote, the option with the most votes goes to the school board, and once the district calendar is approved the other NEISD calendars are presented for approval. Families can apply for School Choice to attend a campus outside their area, and graduation dates are on a separate page the district links.
