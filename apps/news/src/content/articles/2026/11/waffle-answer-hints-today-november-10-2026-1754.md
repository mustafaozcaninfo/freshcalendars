## Repeated letters in grid #1754

The 21 tiles hold four E's, three N's and three I's; every other letter appears once. That is 10 tiles from just three letters.

Only one of the ten starts in its right square: an E in the bottom-right corner. The other three E's, all three N's and all three I's begin elsewhere. When a letter turns up several times, a yellow tile tells you less, because the same letter has several legal homes. An E that is wrong in one square may be right in a neighboring one, and an N or I behaves the same way.

Treat the singletons first. A letter that appears once has exactly one destination, so moving it pins down a square and shrinks the choices left for the repeats.
