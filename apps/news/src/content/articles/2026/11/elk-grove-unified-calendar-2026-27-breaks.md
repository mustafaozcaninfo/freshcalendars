**Elk Grove Unified School District (EGUSD) students are off Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 for Thanksgiving, from Wednesday, Dec. 23, 2026 to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 for winter break, and from Monday, March 15 to Friday, March 19, 2027 for spring break**, according to the "2026-2027 School Year Calendar (Traditional)" PDF the district posted this fall. The last day of school on that calendar is Thursday, May 27, 2027.

The district's older board-approved calendar says something different about winter break, and copies of that 2024 version exist. This story shows both side by side, adds the weekday for every date, lays out the Modified Traditional and Track calendars in their own table, and compares Elk Grove with Fresno, Los Angeles and San Diego. A printable school-year calendar is at the bottom of the page.

## Elk Grove Unified traditional calendar breaks at a glance

The district's [Calendars page](https://www.egusd.net/StudentsFamilies/FormsQuick-Links/Calendars/index.html) lists 2026-27 PDFs in English and Spanish: the main calendar, Traditional, Modified Traditional and Tracks A to D. The table uses the Traditional PDF. Weekdays off are our arithmetic, and the return days are our reading of the grid.

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | Students return (our reading) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Wed., Dec. 23, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 8 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (Mon., Jan. 4 is an inservice day) |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 15-19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 22, 2027 |

Winter break includes Friday, Dec. 25 and Friday, Jan. 1. The [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the weeks around it.

## Which Elk Grove Unified winter break dates are right?

Two district PDFs disagree. The traditional-calendar PDF the district posted this fall prints "Dec. 23 - Jan. 1, Winter Break" and marks Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 "IS 4", an inservice day with no students. The board version, approved 11/19/24, prints "Dec. 21 - Jan. 4, Winter Break, Trad."

| | Board version (approved 11/19/24) | Newer Traditional PDF |
|---|---|---|
| Winter break listed | Dec. 21 - Jan. 4 | Dec. 23 - Jan. 1 |
| First day off | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 | Wed., Dec. 23, 2026 |
| Last day of the break | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 |
| Weekdays off (our count) | 11 | 8 |
| Mon., Dec. 21 and Tue., Dec. 22 | Break | School days |
| Mon., Jan. 4 | Break | Inservice day, no students |

We use the Traditional PDF's dates as the current ones because it is the newer file. The district's page does not explain the change, and we do not guess at one. If your plans depend on Dec. 21-22, confirm with your school before you book travel.

## Elk Grove Unified Modified Traditional and Track calendars

The board PDF also lists dates for the other calendars. These differ from the Traditional calendar, so they sit in their own table. The sources we read do not say which schools use which calendar; check your school's page. Track spring breaks are not listed on the track calendars.

| Calendar | First day | Winter break | Spring break | Last day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Modified Traditional | Thu., Aug. 13, 2026 | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 11, 2027 | Mon.-Fri., March 15-26, 2027 | Fri., June 11, 2027 |
| Track A | Mon., Aug. 17, 2026 | Wed., Dec. 23, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Not listed on the track calendars | Fri., June 25, 2027 |
| Tracks B and C | Wed., July 15, 2026 | Track C: Dec. 23 to Jan. 1; Track B not listed in the board PDF's winter-break line | Not listed on the track calendars | Fri., June 25, 2027 |
| Track D | Wed., July 15, 2026 | Wed., Dec. 23, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Not listed on the track calendars | Thu., May 27, 2027 |

Modified Traditional winter break is 16 weekdays by our count, and its spring break is 10 weekdays, March 15 to 26.

## How does the Elk Grove Unified calendar compare with Fresno, Los Angeles and San Diego?

Dates for the other three districts come from our stories on [Fresno Unified](/news/fresno-unified-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), [Los Angeles Unified](/news/lausd-calendar-2026-27-winter-break-spring-break/) and [San Diego Unified](/news/san-diego-unified-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), which cite each district's own 2026-27 calendar. Elk Grove's column is the Traditional calendar.

| | Elk Grove Unified | Fresno Unified | Los Angeles Unified | San Diego Unified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First day | Thu., Aug. 13, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 17, 2026 | Wed., Aug. 12, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 10, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 | Nov. 23-27 |
| Winter break | Dec. 23 to Jan. 1 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 8 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 8 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 4 |
| Winter weekdays off (our count) | 8 | 15 | 15 | 11 |
| Spring break | March 15-19 | March 22-29 | March 22-26 | March 29 to April 2 |
| Last day | Thu., May 27, 2027 | Thu., June 10, 2027 | Fri., June 4, 2027 | Wed., May 26, 2027 |

Elk Grove's winter break is the shortest of the four, and its spring break comes a week before Fresno's and Los Angeles's and two weeks before San Diego's. Its last day is one day after San Diego's.
