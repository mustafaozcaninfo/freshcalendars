**Winterlicious 2027 takes place from Jan. 29 to Feb. 11, 2027,** the City of Toronto says on its Winterlicious participation page (modified June 4, 2026): "Apply by August 20 to participate in Winterlicious 2027, which takes place from January 29 to February 11." The reservation date, the restaurant list and the 2027 prices are not yet announced. Keep the dates with our [Winterlicious 2027 dates sheet](/news/downloads/winterlicious-2027/winterlicious-2027-dates-planner.pdf).

## Winterlicious 2027 dates by weekday

The City gives the first and last day. The weekdays and the weekend split are computed by us from that range.

| Date | What it is |
|---|---|
| Friday, Jan. 29 | Winterlicious starts |
| Saturday, Jan. 30 | Weekend |
| Sunday, Jan. 31 | Weekend |
| Monday, Feb. 1 | Weekday |
| Tuesday, Feb. 2 | Weekday |
| Wednesday, Feb. 3 | Weekday |
| Thursday, Feb. 4 | Weekday |
| Friday, Feb. 5 | Weekday |
| Saturday, Feb. 6 | Weekend |
| Sunday, Feb. 7 | Weekend |
| Monday, Feb. 8 | Weekday |
| Tuesday, Feb. 9 | Weekday |
| Wednesday, Feb. 10 | Weekday |
| Thursday, Feb. 11 | Winterlicious ends |

That is 14 days with two full weekends, Jan. 30-31 and Feb. 6-7. Valentine's Day, Sunday, Feb. 14, and Ontario's Family Day, Monday, Feb. 15, 2027, fall after the last day. Ottawa's Winterlude has the same opening day and runs to Feb. 15; see our [Winterlude 2027 dates story](/news/winterlude-2027-dates-ottawa-gatineau/) and the [Family Day 2027 story](/news/family-day-2027-canada-february-15-provinces-whats-closed/).

## When did Winterlicious bookings open in past years?

The City has not announced the 2027 reservation date. Its past releases show the timing before each start.

| Year | Release | Date | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | "Reservations are open now." | Jan. 16, 2025 | 15 days before the Jan. 31 start (the release gives Jan. 31 to Feb. 13) |
| 2026 | "Savour the season: book your Winterlicious table now" | Jan. 19, 2026 | 11 days before the Jan. 30 start |

The gaps of 15 and 11 days would put the 2027 booking release between Thu., Jan. 14 and Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 if the pattern held. That is a past pattern, not a promise. The City's participation page lists "late September 2026" under "Upcoming Registration Dates" for Winterlicious 2027.

## What does Winterlicious cost, and where is it held?

The City's program page describes a "three-course prix fixe menu" at "more than 200 Toronto restaurants." The 2027 prices and restaurant list are not yet announced. Last year's figures, from the City's Jan. 19, 2026 release, are labeled as 2026:

| | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Dates | Jan. 30 to Feb. 12 | Jan. 29 to Feb. 11 |
| Lunch | C$20 to C$55 | Not yet announced |
| Dinner | C$25 to C$75 | Not yet announced |
| Restaurants | "more than 240 local restaurants" (Jan. 30, 2026 release) | Not yet announced |

The 2026 release said "Winterlicious returns from Friday, January 30 to Thursday, February 12." It also said "Launched in 2003, the Winterlicious and Summerlicious programs were created by the City." Prices are in Canadian dollars (C$). The 2027 edition starts a day earlier in the calendar than 2026 did.
