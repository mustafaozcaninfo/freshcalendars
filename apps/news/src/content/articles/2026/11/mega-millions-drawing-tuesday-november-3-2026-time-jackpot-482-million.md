**The next Mega Millions drawing is Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, at 11 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), with an estimated jackpot of $482 million (estimated cash value $203.6 million), according to the Texas Lottery and the California Lottery as of Nov. 1.** That is 10 p.m. Central Time (CT), 9 p.m. Mountain Time (MT) and 8 p.m. Pacific Time (PT). The Friday, Oct. 30 drawing had no jackpot winner. Its numbers were 5, 10, 38, 49 and 68 with Mega Ball 17, per the Texas Lottery. Below are the ticket cutoffs by state and every drawing date through December.

## Mega Millions drawing time on Tuesday, Nov. 3

The Florida Lottery says Mega Millions drawings are held every Tuesday and Friday night at 11 PM Eastern Time. The New Jersey Lottery says they are held at approximately 11:00 pm. The Texas Lottery notes that it broadcasts the drawings every Tuesday and Friday evening at 10:12 p.m. CT, which is 11:12 p.m. ET by our conversion.

The Eastern time is the Florida Lottery's. The other three rows are our conversions.

## What is the Mega Millions jackpot for Nov. 3?

The Texas Lottery lists a current estimated annuitized jackpot of $482 million for 11/03/2026 and an estimated cash value of $203.6 million. The California Lottery's page, last updated Nov. 1 at 7:45 p.m., shows the same $482 million estimate. The Oct. 30 drawing had an estimated jackpot of $463 million and no jackpot winner, and the Texas Lottery lists it as a roll.

Five white balls are drawn from 1 to 70 and one Mega Ball from 1 to 24. megamillions.com lists jackpot odds of 1 in 290,472,336 and says tickets cost $5.00 per play, with a multiplier included. The multiplier is 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X or 10X and applies to non-jackpot prizes.

## Mega Millions ticket cutoff by state

The drawing is at 11 p.m. ET, but sales stop earlier. Each state lottery sets its own cutoff, so the table gives what five official pages say. We converted to ET only where the page names a zone or the zone is Eastern.

| State | Official cutoff | In ET |
|---|---|---|
| Pennsylvania | Sales end at 9:59 p.m. on drawing nights | 9:59 p.m. |
| Florida | Tickets may be purchased until 10:00 p.m. ET | 10:00 p.m. |
| New Jersey | Purchase prior to 10:45 pm on Tuesday and Friday | Page gives no zone |
| Texas | No tickets during Draw Break, 9:45-10:15 p.m. CT | 10:45-11:15 p.m. |
| California | Draw entry closes at 7:45 p.m. on the day of the draw | 10:45 p.m. if local time |

California's page prints no zone. If it means local time in California, the cutoff is 10:45 p.m. ET. For any other state, ask the retailer or read your own lottery's page.
