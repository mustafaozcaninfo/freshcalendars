**Christmas Movie Bingo 2026 from Fresh Calendars is a free printable set of six different 5x5 cards and one caller sheet, with the season's movie nights from Hallmark, Lifetime, Netflix, Apple TV and ABC listed below.** Each card has a free center square and 24 holiday movie moments, from "Hot cocoa with marshmallows" to "Ugly sweater contest."

## What is in the Christmas movie bingo printable?

Two PDFs, both on US Letter paper:

- **Christmas Movie Bingo 2026: 6 Cards** has one card per page, six pages in all. Every card is a different shuffle of 24 of the 36 squares around a free center, with a line for the player's name.
- **Christmas Movie Bingo 2026: Caller Sheet** lists all 36 squares, numbered 1 to 36, each with a tick box. The same number sits in the corner of each card square.

The squares are plot moments written by Fresh Calendars, such as "Snow starts falling during a kiss," "Bakery or tree farm needs saving," "A town festival is in danger" and "Gingerbread house contest." They name no film, channel, studio or character, so the cards work for any Christmas movie on any channel or service.

## How do you play Christmas movie bingo?

1. Print one card per player and keep the caller sheet for one person.
2. Pick a holiday movie and press play.
3. When a square's moment happens on screen, the caller ticks it on the caller sheet and says its number. Players mark the same square on their cards.
4. The first player with five marked squares in a row, a column or a diagonal calls "Bingo." The free center counts as marked.
5. For a longer game, play four corners (all four corner squares) or blackout (every square on the card).
