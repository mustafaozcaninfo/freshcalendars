**The New York Times (NYT) Crossword for Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, was constructed by Dena Verkuil and edited by Will Shortz: 74 clues, 33 Across and 41 Down, in a 15-by-15 grid.** None of the 74 clues ends in a question mark, and two entries run to 14 letters. Every clue has a hint behind a tap, and the answers close the page.

## Wordplay in the Nov. 23 Crossword without a question mark

A search of the puzzle data finds no clue ending in "?", the usual flag for a pun. The tricks are marked differently, and these six are worth a look. Each is explained without its answer, with the length in letters.

- **9-Down, "[This is a library!]" (3 letters).** The square brackets mean the clue is a sound or action, not a definition. Think of what a librarian does with a finger to the lips.
- **56-Across, "Fish found in Tahiti (in more ways than one!)" (3 letters).** "Found in" is literal: the answer sits inside the island's name, and it is also a fish. Read the name aloud, then look for three letters in the middle.
- **61-Down, "Get a lode of this!" (3 letters).** The odd spelling of "lode" is the hint: a lode is a mineral deposit, and the answer is the raw material in it.
- **32-Across, "Goes to Montauk from Memorial Day to Labor Day, say" (7 letters).** A season is used as a verb. The answer is plural in form, and it ends in the letter S.
- **13-Across, "\"That is to say ...\"" (5 letters).** The trailing ellipsis marks a spoken phrase that introduces a rewording; the answer runs two words together.
- **59-Across, "Words from a bartender … or a hint to the ends of 16-, 26- and 48-Across" (14 letters).** The clue names three other entries and asks you to look at their last letters, so it is easiest after those three are filled in. It runs four words together.

## How the Nov. 23 grid is built

The grid is 15 columns by 15 rows, 225 squares in all: 38 black and 187 holding a letter, a black share of 16.9 percent. The black pattern is unchanged by a half-turn. Friday's grid, on our [Nov. 20 Crossword page](/news/nyt-crossword-answers-november-20-2026/), had 39 black squares and 66 clues, so Monday has one black square fewer and eight more clues.
