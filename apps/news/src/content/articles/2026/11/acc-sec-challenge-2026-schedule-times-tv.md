**The Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) and the Southeastern Conference (SEC) play 32 basketball games from Tue., Dec. 1 to Thu., Dec. 3, 2026, with Duke at Florida at 9:30 p.m. ET on ESPN the first night, according to ESPN.** ESPN calls it the fourth annual SEC/ACC Men's and Women's Basketball Challenge. In its words: "The SEC/ACC Men's Challenge will be contested Tuesday, Dec. 1 and Wednesday, Dec. 2, while the Women's Challenge is slated for Wednesday, Dec. 2 and Thursday, Dec. 3. Games will air across ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, SEC Network and ACC Network and stream on the ESPN App."

ESPN lists each tip in Eastern Time (ET). The tables below add Central (CT), Mountain (MT) and Pacific (PT) times, computed from the ET tip, and a printable watch guide follows them. In each matchup the home team is named second. Two short names in the tables: Louisiana State University (LSU) and Southern Methodist University (SMU).

## Who won the men's ACC/SEC Challenge last year?

In 2025, the SEC won the men's challenge 9-7. The SEC's Dec. 4, 2025 recap says the Southeastern Conference "defeated the ACC 9–7 to claim the ACC/SEC Men's Basketball Challenge," for the second straight year. That is the 2025 men's result; the 2026 games decide this year's.
