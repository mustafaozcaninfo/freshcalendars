**Northside Independent School District (NISD) in San Antonio has no school from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 for Thanksgiving break**, according to its 2026-2027 Academic Calendar, approved Dec. 9, 2025. Winter break runs **Monday, Dec. 21 through Friday, Jan. 1**, and spring break is **Monday, March 8 through Friday, March 12, 2027**. The last day of school is Wednesday, May 26, 2027.

## Northside ISD 2026-27 breaks and long weekends

| Break | Dates | Days off counting weekends |
|---|---|---|
| Fall student holidays (already past) | Fri., Oct. 9, Mon., Oct. 12 and Tue., Oct. 13, 2026 | 5 days, Fri. Oct. 9 through Tue. Oct. 13 |
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 9 days, Sat. Nov. 21 through Sun. Nov. 29 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027, plus Mon., Jan. 4 | 17 days, Sat. Dec. 19 through Mon. Jan. 4 |
| Presidents Day weekend | Fri., Feb. 12, Mon., Feb. 15 and Tue., Feb. 16, 2027 | 5 days, Fri. Feb. 12 through Tue. Feb. 16 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 8-12, 2027, plus Mon., March 15 | 10 days, Sat. March 6 through Mon. March 15 |
| Friday and Monday holidays | Fri., March 26 and Mon., March 29, 2027 | 4 days, Fri. March 26 through Mon. March 29 |

Teacher work days are Aug. 3-7, 2026, and the first day of school is Monday, Aug. 10. The first semester runs Aug. 10 to Dec. 18 and the second runs Jan. 4 to May 26, 2027. Thursday, May 27 is a teacher work day. The calendar calls Feb. 16 a "Bad Weather Makeup Day/ Student Holiday/Staff Dev.", so that fifth day of the February weekend is a day off unless a weather closure makes the district use it.

Counting every weekday from Aug. 10, 2026 through May 26, 2027, and counting Feb. 16 as a day off, the calendar has 34 weekdays without classes: 1 in September, 3 in October, 6 in November, 9 in December, 3 in January, 3 in February, 8 in March and 1 in April. The calendar does not list early release days or graduation dates, so none are shown here.

## When does Northside ISD start in 2027-28?

The board adopted the 2027-28 calendar in the same Dec. 9, 2025 action. The district's news release says key dates include the first day of school on Aug. 9, 2027, with the last day on May 25, 2028. This story covers only the 2026-27 year; check the district's calendar page before planning around next year.
