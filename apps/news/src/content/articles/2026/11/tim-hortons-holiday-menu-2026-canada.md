**Tim Hortons said on Nov. 12, 2026 that its holiday menu, a partnership with Warner Bros. Consumer Products inspired by New Line Cinema's film Elf (ELF), is available starting that day at participating Tims restaurants across Canada.** The lineup has new donuts, Timbits, two Chaos drinks and a Maple Soda Swirl. Below are the items, a 2025 comparison and a printable checklist with Holiday Cheer Day on Saturday, Dec. 12.

## What is on the Tim Hortons holiday menu in 2026?

Tim Hortons published the list in a Nov. 12, 2026 release on its newsroom. It names Canadian restaurants only. It lists no prices and gives no end date for the menu.

| Item | Type | What Tim Hortons says |
|---|---|---|
| ELF Cola Donut | Donut | Festive sprinkles and a fondant inspired by the dinner scene in the film |
| ELF Chaos Donut | Donut | Mini marshmallows, crushed candy, maple drizzle and fondant inspired by Buddy the Elf's spaghetti breakfast |
| ELF Snowball Timbits | Timbits | Chocolate Timbits rolled in icing sugar |
| ELF Chaos Latte | Drink | Maple brown butter, mini marshmallows, crushed candy and spaghetti-inspired whipped topping |
| ELF Chaos Iced Capp | Drink | Same flavour combinations as the Chaos Latte |
| Maple Soda Swirl | Drink | Coca-Cola with maple brown butter syrup, cream and maple brown butter cold foam |
| Candy Cane Hot Chocolate | Drink, returning | Regular hot chocolate or white hot chocolate |
| Maple Bacon | Food | On breakfast sandwiches, wraps and Grilled Cheese Melts |
| ELF Meal | Combo | Maple Bacon Grilled Cheese Melt, ELF Cola Donut and a medium fountain Coca-Cola |

Maple Bacon is available "starting today and throughout the holiday season," made with 100 per cent Canadian maple syrup, according to the release. Hope Bagozzi, Chief Marketing Officer for Tim Hortons, said: "We've created a festive menu inspired by some of the movie's most memorable moments and flavours."

## When is Tim Hortons Holiday Cheer Day 2026?

On Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, participating restaurants host Holiday Cheer Day from 2 to 5 p.m. Guests can decorate one free donut per guest, decorate an ornament and more. Tim Hortons says more details, including participating locations, "will be available in the coming weeks," so the list of restaurants is not yet announced.

## What are the ELF cups and gift card?

The release says limited-edition ELF x Tim Hortons cups, donut boxes, gift cards and other packaging feature nods to moments from the film. The ELF gift card has a pearl finish and is available at participating restaurants starting Nov. 12 until supplies last.

## How does the 2026 menu compare with 2025?

Tim Hortons announced its 2025 holiday menu on Nov. 12, 2025, the same date as this year's launch, through Canada Newswire. The 2025 release centred on Biscoff items.

| | 2025 menu | 2026 menu |
|---|---|---|
| Theme | Biscoff | ELF |
| Baked goods | Biscoff Boston Cream, Biscoff Filled Croissant | ELF Cola Donut, ELF Chaos Donut, ELF Snowball Timbits |
| Drinks | Biscoff Latte, Biscoff Iced Capp | ELF Chaos Latte, ELF Chaos Iced Capp, Maple Soda Swirl |
| Returning | Candy Cane Hot Chocolate and Candy Cane White Hot Chocolate | Candy Cane Hot Chocolate, regular or white |
