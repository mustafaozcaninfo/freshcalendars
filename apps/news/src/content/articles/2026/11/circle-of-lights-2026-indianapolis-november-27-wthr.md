**Circle of Lights in Indianapolis is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, with festivities from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. ET on Monument Circle**, the Downtown Indy Alliance says. A child picked through the coloring contest of the city's electric utility flips the switch before 7 p.m., and Indianapolis television station (WTHR) Channel 13 broadcasts the ceremony exclusively from 6 p.m. to 7 p.m.

The Soldiers and Sailors Monument is the centerpiece, lit as a tree of lights. The Alliance calls the night free and says it celebrates 64 years of festive décor and bright bulbs; International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 481 presents it. Pair the date with the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## What is the Circle of Lights 2026 schedule?

| Time (Fri., Nov. 27, 2026, ET) | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 5 p.m. | Festivities begin with live music, vendors and holiday activities | Downtown Indy Alliance |
| 6 p.m. to 7 p.m. | WTHR Channel 13 broadcasts the ceremony | Downtown Indy Alliance |
| Before 7 p.m. | A child from the coloring contest of the city's electric utility takes the stage and flips the switch | Downtown Indy Alliance |
| 8 p.m. | Festivities end | Downtown Indy Alliance |

The Alliance did not publish the exact minute of the switch. Santa, artists and food trucks are listed among the attractions. The coloring contest drop-off ran from Nov. 1 to noon on Nov. 16, and the winning child was not named.

## What time was the switch in 2024?

In 2024, the Downtown Indy release said the event returned on Friday, Nov. 29, the day after Thanksgiving. Pre-event festivities started at 5 p.m., an on-stage pre-show at 5:30 p.m. and post-lighting activations ran 7 p.m. to 8 p.m. The switch was flipped alongside Santa just after 6:50 p.m.

| | 2024 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Fri., Nov. 29 | Fri., Nov. 27 |
| Festivities start | 5 p.m. | 5 p.m. |
| Switch | Just after 6:50 p.m. | Before 7 p.m. |
| Broadcast | Not in the release | WTHR Channel 13, 6 p.m. to 7 p.m. |

The 2024 time is a hint for when to look up, not a 2026 fact. Plan to be on the Circle by 6:30 p.m. if you want to be sure of the moment.
