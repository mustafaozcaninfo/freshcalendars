**Old Navy's Black Friday 2026 sale starts Nov. 23 and goes through Dec. 1, according to the retailer's "Black Friday and Cyber Monday Deals 2026" page, which we read on Nov. 17.** That is nine days, from a Monday to a Tuesday. Old Navy's page gives no Thanksgiving status and no store hours. The calendar below marks each day, and a one-page printable follows.

## Old Navy's Black Friday sale, day by day

The page says "Ginormous discounts coming your way for Black Friday 2026, starting November 23rd" and "going all the way through to December 1st!" The weekdays and holiday names are our computation; the sale dates are Old Navy's.

| Date | Weekday | What is known |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 23 | Mon. | Sale starts, per Old Navy |
| Nov. 24 | Tue. | Sale on, per Old Navy |
| Nov. 25 | Wed. | Sale on, per Old Navy |
| Nov. 26 | Thu. | Thanksgiving; sale on, store status not announced |
| Nov. 27 | Fri. | Black Friday; sale on, opening time not announced |
| Nov. 28 | Sat. | Small Business Saturday; sale on, per Old Navy |
| Nov. 29 | Sun. | Sale on, per Old Navy |
| Nov. 30 | Mon. | Cyber Monday; sale on, no separate deal named |
| Dec. 1 | Tue. | Sale ends, per Old Navy |

Put the window on our [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars.

## What is the Old Navy Extra code?

On Nov. 17 the Old Navy page showed a banner reading "50% Off Early Black Friday Deals + Extra 30% Off Purchase," with a promo code (Extra, shown in capital letters) and "Exclusions apply." The banner gives no end date, so it may not cover the whole sale. Today.com, in its Old Navy Black Friday 2026 deals guide updated Nov. 16, reported that early sales are "as high as 65% off" and that you can "take 30% off at checkout with the code Extra." Those figures are Today.com's reporting; Old Navy's page does not state the 65% figure.

## Is Old Navy open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Old Navy has not said. Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026. The Black Friday page has no Thanksgiving status, and the [Gap Inc. newsroom](https://www.gapinc.com/en-us/newsroom) on Nov. 17 listed no Old Navy store-hours release. Its holiday release was the Nov. 5 "All Aboard the Jingle Jet: Introducing Old Navy's Holiday 2026 Campaign," which is not a sale-date or hours announcement. For chains that have published hours, see the [Black Friday 2026 store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/) and the [Kohl's Black Friday 2026 dates](/news/kohls-black-friday-2026-dates-early-access-november-6-store-hours/).

## Does Old Navy have a Cyber Monday 2026 deal?

The page carries "Cyber Monday" in its title, and calls the clothing deals "Old Navy's Black Friday & Cyber Monday clothing deals." It names no separate Cyber Monday offer. Cyber Monday is Nov. 30, which falls inside the Nov. 23 to Dec. 1 window. Other retailers' dates are in our [Cyber Monday 2026 guide](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/).

## What has Old Navy not announced for 2026?

- Whether stores open on Thanksgiving.
- The Black Friday opening time.
- A separate Cyber Monday deal.
- Navyist Rewards early access.
- Gap and Banana Republic Black Friday dates, which are not on Old Navy's page.

## What to check before you go

- Your local store's hours on the Old Navy store page, since the sale page lists none.
- The banner's exclusions and whether the Extra code still applies to your items.
- The order date against Dec. 1, the last day the page names.
