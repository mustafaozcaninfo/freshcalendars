## Which letter pairs make Sunday's box hard?

Count the steps from one side to a different one and there are 108 ordered pairs. In this list 20 of them never occur inside a word, and 7 more occur in a single word only.

The Q causes most of it. Every word that holds a Q (51 of 709) puts a U right after it, so from Q the only step that exists is Q to U. Nothing ends in Q, and G, V, U, Y and K never step into it. The rest of the dead steps are G to K, V to N, V to K, L to R, Y to N, K to G and K to V.

Seen by side, the thinnest link is between Q/I/L and R/Y/K:

| Sides | Words with a step between them |
|---|---|
| A/G/V and U/E/N | 476 |
| A/G/V and Q/I/L | 304 |
| A/G/V and R/Y/K | 302 |
| U/E/N and Q/I/L | 417 |
| U/E/N and R/Y/K | 358 |
| Q/I/L and R/Y/K | 250 |

Letter by letter, V (142 words), Y (150) and K (163) trail after Q, while R sits in 432 words and A in 414.

## How many two-word solutions does the dictionary allow?

Five. A two-word route needs the second word to start where the first ends, with all 12 letters used between them. This list has 5 such pairs, the Times' own included, and none shorter than 14 letters in total:

| Total letters | Routes |
|---|---|
| 14 | 1 |
| 15 | 1 |
| 16 | 2 |
| 18 | 1 |
| 14 to 18 | 5 |

Saturday's list allowed 238 routes. Across the 34 days we saved, 709 words is the fifth smallest list, after 371, 592, 660 and 661. With so few routes, a par of 5 is generous: three words more than the Times' own route uses.

## Is the longest word any help?

The longest word in the list has 13 letters, and 5 more run to 11 letters. Length does not buy coverage, though. The best word touches 10 different letters, two others touch 9, and none reaches all 12. A one-word answer cannot exist here.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, three fewer than the par of 5.
2. The first word has 11 letters and the second has 3. The list holds 5 words of 11 letters and 92 of 3 letters.
3. The first word opens on the Q/I/L side and closes on the R/Y/K side. The second word opens and closes on the R/Y/K side, with a letter from A/G/V in the middle.
4. The first word covers 10 of the 12 letters and leaves out only A and K. The second word brings both.
5. The first word starts with Q and ends in Y. The second starts with Y and ends in K.
