**The Boston Tea Party 253rd anniversary reenactment is Wednesday, Dec. 16, 2026, at Old South Meeting House in Boston: doors open at 5:00 p.m. and the reenactment begins at 6:15 p.m., according to Revolutionary Spaces, the nonprofit that runs the venue.** Tickets are on sale now. Below are the 2026 prices next to 2025's, a short table of the 1773 facts and a free one-page event-night plan.

## When and where is the Boston Tea Party reenactment in 2026?

Revolutionary Spaces lists "Wednesday, December 16, 2026," with "Doors Open: 5:00 PM" and "Reenactment Begins: 6:15 PM," at Old South Meeting House, 310 Washington Street, Boston, MA 02108. Its upcoming-programs listing names the event "Boston Tea Party 253rd Anniversary Commemoration" at 6:15 p.m. on Dec. 16.

| Time (ET) | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 5:00 p.m. | Doors open | Revolutionary Spaces |
| 6:15 p.m. | Reenactment begins | Revolutionary Spaces |

Revolutionary Spaces says general admission seating is open and available on a first-come basis. Doors open 75 minutes before the start, by our count.

## How much are Boston Tea Party reenactment tickets in 2026 and 2025?

Prices below come from the Revolutionary Spaces pages for each year. The 2025 event was Tuesday, Dec. 16, 2025, and its page now says it is sold out and offers a waitlist. The change column is our subtraction.

| Ticket | 2026 | 2025 (last year) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adults (18-64) | $45 | $40 | +$5 |
| Seniors (65 and older) | $40 | $30 | +$10 |
| Youth (17 and under) | $40 | $30 | +$10 |
| Members | $35 | $25 | +$10 |
| Premium (2026) / top tier (2025) | $75, sold out | $60, limited to 35 | +$15 |

The 2025 top-tier ticket was listed under a different name from 2026's premium seats, so they may not be the same package. Revolutionary Spaces shows 2026 premium seats (all ages) as sold out.

Two more prices are listed for 2026:

- **Groups of 10 or more**, booked by email: adults $34, seniors $30, youth $30, members $26.
- **Member presale:** $28. Revolutionary Spaces said it would email members on Monday, Oct. 26 with how to buy presale tickets.

The 2025 event now shows as sold out with a waitlist, which is the only year-over-year signal on the pages. Revolutionary Spaces says 2026 tickets are on sale now.

## What happened in the 1773 Boston Tea Party?

The National Park Service (NPS) timeline says the protesters destroyed "all 342 chests of East India Company Tea aboard the three ships." The meeting that led to it moved to Old South Meeting House because the space was larger.

| Fact | What the NPS says |
|---|---|
| Ships | Three: the Dartmouth, the Eleanor and the Beaver |
| Dartmouth | An East India merchant vessel carrying 114 chests of tea |
| Chests destroyed | All 342 chests of East India Company tea |
| Crowd at Old South | "Nearly 5,000 people, a third of Boston's entire population" |

The NPS page was last updated Dec. 20, 2023.

## What has Revolutionary Spaces not posted for Dec. 16?

- A livestream or a free viewing area.
- Road closures or Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority (MBTA) changes.
- The route reenactors take to the waterfront.

The Boston Tea Party Ships & Museum is a separate site. It lists general admission "From $36.00" and tour hours of Monday to Sunday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., and shows no Dec. 16, 2026 program. Its tickets are for museum tours, not the Old South reenactment.
