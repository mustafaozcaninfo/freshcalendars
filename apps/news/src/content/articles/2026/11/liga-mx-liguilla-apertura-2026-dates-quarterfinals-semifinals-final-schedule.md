**The Liga MX (Mexico's top soccer league) Apertura 2026 Liguilla (playoffs) quarterfinal second legs (vuelta) are Sat., Nov. 28 and Sun., Nov. 29, 2026, starting at 8 p.m. ET with Atlas v Toluca, according to ligamx.net and Televisa Univision Deportes Network (TUDN).** The first legs were played Nov. 25-26 and Cruz Azul leads León 4-0 on aggregate. The regular season has ended, and there was no Play-In.

Below: the quarterfinal pairings, first-leg scores, second-leg kickoffs in ET, CT and PT, the later rounds and the one exception that can move the Final. A one-page printable calendar is included.

## Liguilla Apertura 2026 dates, round by round

Each round has a first leg (ida) and a second leg (vuelta).

| Stage | Leg | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Jornada 17 (last regular-season round) | Single match | Fri., Nov. 20 to Sun., Nov. 22 (played) |
| Quarterfinals (Cuartos de Final) | First legs | Wed., Nov. 25 and Thu., Nov. 26 (played) |
| Quarterfinals | Second legs | Sat., Nov. 28 and Sun., Nov. 29 |
| Semifinals | First legs | Wed., Dec. 2 and Thu., Dec. 3 |
| Semifinals | Second legs | Sat., Dec. 5 and Sun., Dec. 6 |
| Final | Two legs | Thu., Dec. 10 to Sun., Dec. 13 |

Source: Liga MX, TUDN, Excélsior and Infobae, Nov. 2026. Excélsior and Infobae, both on Nov. 27, give the semifinal dates; kickoff times are not yet set. Mark the days on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Who plays whom in the quarterfinals, and what were the first-leg scores?

Liga MX and TUDN list the quarterfinals as 1 v 8, 2 v 7, 3 v 6 and 4 v 5, each over two legs. Claro Sports (Nov. 26), Infobae (Nov. 27) and Excélsior (Nov. 27) agree on the first-leg results.

| Tie | First leg (date, venue) | Score | Aggregate after first leg |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Guadalajara v 8 Pachuca | Thu., Nov. 26, Estadio Hidalgo | Pachuca 2-1 Guadalajara | Pachuca 2-1 |
| 2 Cruz Azul v 7 León | Thu., Nov. 26, Estadio León | León 0-4 Cruz Azul | Cruz Azul 4-0 |
| 3 América v 6 Monterrey | Wed., Nov. 25, Monterrey home stadium | Monterrey 0-0 América | Level 0-0 |
| 4 Atlas v 5 Toluca | Wed., Nov. 25, Estadio Nemesio Diez | Toluca 2-0 Atlas | Toluca 2-0 |

Source: Liga MX and TUDN (pairings); Claro Sports, Infobae and Excélsior (scores), Nov. 26-27, 2026. Scorers named in the reports: Helinho 2 for Toluca, Oussama Idrissi 2 for Pachuca and José Paradela 2 for Cruz Azul. First-leg kickoffs were 7 p.m. Mexico City (8 p.m. ET) for Toluca v Atlas and Pachuca v Guadalajara, and 9:10 p.m. (10:10 p.m. ET) for Monterrey v América and León v Cruz Azul, per TUDN and Mediotiempo.

## How does the Liguilla format work?

The top eight teams in the general table qualify, with pairings 1 vs. 8, 2 vs. 7, 3 vs. 6 and 4 vs. 5.

- In the quarterfinals and semifinals, a tie on aggregate goes to the team that finished higher in the table, per Claro Sports.
- The Final goes to extra time and penalties if it is tied, per Claro Sports.

## Is there a Play-In in the Apertura 2026?

No. Claro Sports says "No, no hay Play In en el Apertura 2026," and TUDN says the Play-In was eliminated. The final regular-season table on ligamx.net shows no Play-In.

## What time are the second legs in ET, CT and PT?

Liga MX, TUDN and Excélsior (Nov. 27) agree on these kickoffs. Mexico City does not observe daylight saving time. Since U.S. clocks fell back on Nov. 1, Mexico City is 1 hour behind ET and equal to CT, and 2 hours ahead of PT.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sat., Nov. 28 | Atlas v Toluca | Estadio Jalisco | 7:00 p.m. | 8:00 p.m. | 5:00 p.m. |
| Sat., Nov. 28 | América v Monterrey | Estadio Banorte | 9:10 p.m. | 10:10 p.m. | 7:10 p.m. |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Guadalajara v Pachuca | Estadio Akron | 7:07 p.m. | 8:07 p.m. | 5:07 p.m. |
| Sun., Nov. 29 | Cruz Azul v León | Estadio Banorte | 9:00 p.m. | 10:00 p.m. | 7:00 p.m. |

Source: Liga MX (ligamx.net), TUDN and Excélsior, Nov. 27, 2026; ET and PT converted from Mexico City time.

## Where can U.S. viewers watch the quarterfinal second legs?

Liga MX's page lists no U.S. channel for the second legs, and no 2026 broadcaster schedule for these matches was on a page we could read, so U.S. channels are not yet announced here. English-language U.S. rights are not announced.

## What is next after the quarterfinals?

The semifinals are Wed., Dec. 2 and Thu., Dec. 3 (first legs) and Sat., Dec. 5 and Sun., Dec. 6 (second legs), per Excélsior and Infobae. Semifinal kickoff times are not yet set. The Final is Dec. 10-13, per TUDN and Excélsior.

## Can Toluca move the Final to December 24-27?

Yes. TUDN says the Final would be played Dec. 24-27 instead of Dec. 10-13 if Toluca reaches it, because "Toluca comienza su participación en la Copa Intercontinental el 9 de diciembre," the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) Intercontinental Cup, per Excélsior. If Toluca is out, the Final stays on Dec. 10-13. Toluca leads Atlas 2-0 after the first leg.
