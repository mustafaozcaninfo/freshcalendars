**The Kansas City Chiefs play the Los Angeles Rams on Thursday Night Football (TNF), Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Prime Video, according to the National Football League (NFL) Week 13 schedule on NFL.com.** The Rams' schedule shows the same game at 5:15 p.m. PST, and the Chiefs' schedule puts it at SoFi Stadium.

Below are the kickoff in every U.S. zone and in the United Kingdom, where to watch, both teams' records before Week 12, their last meeting and what each plays first. A one-page watch guide is at the end.

## What time is Chiefs at Rams in my time zone?

Clocks are on standard time. The Rams list 5:15 p.m. PST and the Chiefs 7:15 p.m. CST, which matches the 8:15 p.m. ET kickoff. We worked out the other zones from that listing.

## What channel is Chiefs at Rams on?

NFL.com lists Prime Video, and both teams' schedules agree. The Chiefs' schedule adds a local Kansas City broadcast on Channel 41, so the game also airs there. The Rams' schedule lists only Prime Video, and we found no confirmed Los Angeles station.

Amazon says: "Football fans can watch Thursday Night Football exclusively on Prime Video." Pregame coverage "begins each Thursday at 7 p.m. ET with games kicking off at 8:15 p.m. ET." Amazon offers a free 30-day trial to people who are not Prime members. It lists Al Michaels as the season's play-by-play voice, but no official page names the crew for this game, so we leave names out.
