**The New York Times (NYT) Mini Crossword for Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, is a five-by-five grid with 10 clues by Sam Ezersky.** One black square sits in the bottom-left corner, so every row and column is full width except the bottom row and the first column. Below is a hint for every clue, each one hidden until you open it, then the full answers in a marked spoiler section.

## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (5 letters), *Insurance filing*: a request you send an insurer after a loss, and also what you do when you say something is true; it starts with C.
- **6-Across** (5 letters), *Bar game?*: a party game where dancers bend backward to pass under a pole; it starts with L.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *To no ___ (uselessly)*: the word that fills the blank to mean without success; it starts with A and ends with L.
- **8-Across** (5 letters), *Pie you likely wouldn't see at a Thanksgiving table*: a round, cheesy Italian dish, the kind the clue says does not belong on a Thanksgiving table; it has a double Z.
- **9-Across** (4 letters), *Much-loved*: beloved, and also the word that opens a letter; it starts with D.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (4 letters), *Sudden sound of thunder*: the sharp crack that comes with lightning, and also what hands do to applaud; it starts with C.
- **2-Down** (5 letters), *Hopping mad*: furiously angry, or blue-gray from a bruise; it starts with L.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *Elicit oohs and aahs from*: to astonish someone; it starts with A and ends with E.
- **4-Down** (5 letters), *Spanish island known for its nightlife*: a Mediterranean island off Spain's east coast that is famous for clubs and dance music; it starts with I.
- **5-Down** (5 letters), *Back tooth*: a large, flat tooth at the back of the mouth, used for grinding; it starts with M.

## Solving tips

Start with 4-Down, a geography fact, and 1-Across, a word from paperwork. 1-Across and 1-Down share their first square. Because the black square is in the bottom-left corner, 9-Across begins in the second column, and 1-Down is the only Down answer with four letters. Every Down answer except 1-Down runs the full five squares, so each one crosses all five Across rows.
