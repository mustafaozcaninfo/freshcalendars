**The American Automobile Association (AAA) Tow to Go program has two holiday windows in 2026: 6 p.m. local time Wednesday, Nov. 25 to 6 a.m. Monday, Nov. 30, and 6 p.m. Thursday, Dec. 24 to 6 a.m. Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, according to The Auto Club Group's Tow To Go page.** The ride is free to members and non-members, and the number is (855) 286-9246. The service covers the states and cities listed below, which are The Auto Club Group's territory, and the page warns it may not be available in rural areas or during severe weather. AAA has not yet issued its 2026 holiday announcement; last year's came on Dec. 19.

## AAA Tow to Go 2026 windows and lengths

The page lists Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, and a second row for Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, and New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. The times are marked "(local time)" and the page names no time zone. The lengths below are our own arithmetic from those start and end times.

| Window | Starts | Ends | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving, Thu., Nov. 26 | Wed., Nov. 25, 6 p.m. local time | Mon., Nov. 30, 6 a.m. local time | 4 days 12 hours |
| Christmas Day and New Year's Day | Thu., Dec. 24, 6 p.m. local time | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027, 6 a.m. local time | 10 days 12 hours |
| Last year: 2025 holidays | Wed., Dec. 24, 2025, 6 p.m. | Fri., Jan. 2, 2026, 6 a.m. | 8 days 12 hours |

Last year's window comes from AAA's Dec. 19, 2025 release, which said Tow to Go would run from 6 p.m. on Wednesday, Dec. 24, 2025 through 6 a.m. on Friday, Jan. 2, 2026. The 2026-27 window is two days longer and reaches through the weekend after New Year's Day. It covers Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, Thursday, Dec. 31, and New Year's Day. Mark both on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), or print the wallet card below.

The page also shows five earlier 2026 activations that began at 6 p.m.: Feb. 27, March 16, May 22, July 3 and Sept. 4. The program also runs on other holiday weekends.

The page is The Auto Club Group's. It does not say whether other AAA clubs run the same program, so drivers outside these areas should ask their own club. The page also warns that Tow to Go may not be available in rural areas or during severe weather, and that some rural areas may have fewer drivers.

## What Tow to Go does and does not do

- **It is free.** The page calls it a free confidential ride available to AAA members and non-members.
- **It takes the driver and the vehicle.** The AAA tow truck transports the vehicle and driver home or somewhere safe within 10 miles.
- **It cannot be booked.** The page says appointments cannot be scheduled in advance.
- **It is a safety net.** The page says it is designed for people who did not plan ahead, so the ride-share or designated driver plan still comes first.
- **It may not reach everywhere.** Rural areas and severe weather can limit it.

The page says that since 1998 the program has removed nearly 30,000 impaired drivers from the road.

## How to call Tow to Go

Call the toll-free number, (855) 2-Tow-2-Go, which is (855) 286-9246, during a window above. Save it before you go out. Our printable wallet card carries the number and both windows, with a Done column to tick off each one.
