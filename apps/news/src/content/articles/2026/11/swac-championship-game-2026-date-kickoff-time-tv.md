**The 2026 Pepsi SWAC Football Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5, at 2 p.m. ET (1 p.m. CT) on ESPN2, with Alabama A&M at Prairie View A&M, according to the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC).** The conference announced the matchup on Nov. 21, 2026, and the game is at Panther Stadium in Prairie View, Texas.

The SWAC's May 27, 2026 schedule release gave the same slot: "will air live on ESPN2 on Saturday, December 5 at 2:00 p.m. EST."

## How did Prairie View A&M and Alabama A&M reach the SWAC final?

Prairie View A&M won its second straight West Division title with a win over Alabama State on Nov. 21, the SWAC said, and the school's athletics site reports the score as 45-10. The Panthers also beat Alabama A&M 31-10 on Nov. 7, which Alabama A&M Athletics cites as the head-to-head result behind the hosting rights.

Alabama A&M (9-3 overall, 6-2 SWAC) won the East with a 51-19 victory at Mississippi Valley State on Nov. 21, its first East title since 2021, per its athletics site. The SWAC adds that Alabama A&M's last conference title was in 2021 and that Prairie View A&M is looking to repeat after winning in 2025.

## How much are tickets and where do Alabama A&M fans sit?

Alabama A&M Athletics lists tickets at $60 in the Alabama A&M seating sections, bought through the Prairie View A&M ticket portal. The release names these sections:

| Group | Sections |
|---|---|
| Alabama A&M fans (about 1,500 bench seats, visiting sideline) | 117 to 121 |
| Alabama A&M band | 114 to 116 |
| Prairie View A&M band | 122 to 124 |

General-public tickets go on sale Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 9 a.m. CT, the SWAC said. Prices for the home sections are not yet announced in the sources used here, and no stadium capacity is given.

## Who won the 2025 SWAC Championship Game?

In 2025, Prairie View A&M won 23-21 over Jackson State at Veterans Memorial Stadium on Saturday, Dec. 6, according to the SWAC. It was the Panthers' first league title since 2009. That is last year's result.

## What does the SWAC champion play next?

The SWAC champion meets the Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference (MEAC) champion in the Cricket Celebration Bowl on Saturday, Dec. 12, at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta. The bowl, a game for historically Black colleges and universities (HBCU), calls it "the HBCU national championship game matching the champions of the MEAC and SWAC." The ESPN Events schedule puts kickoff at noon ET on ABC.
