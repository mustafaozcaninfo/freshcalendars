**The New York Rangers play the New Jersey Devils three times in December 2026, starting Monday, Dec. 7, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) at Madison Square Garden (MSG), according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The Rangers have 14 games in the month, seven at home and seven on the road, and the first is Thursday, Dec. 3, against San Jose.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for the Rangers, read Nov. 10, 2026. It gives each start in UTC. We converted the times to ET, which is New York time, and calculated the weekdays. The feed lists broadcasters and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Rangers game times and TV for every December game

MSG Networks is the Rangers' regional outlet; the feed lists its MSG, MSG 2 and MSG Sportsnet (MSGSN) channels for the Rangers' games. Turner Network Television (TNT), truTV and HBO Max carry the game at Philadelphia nationally in the U.S. Sportsnet, Sportsnet+ and Télé-Diffuseurs Associés (TVA) Sports are Canadian networks. "None listed" means the feed shows nothing in that column.

## Rangers vs. Devils in December 2026: three dates

Only one opponent shows up more than once. The Devils visit the Garden on Monday, Dec. 7, host the Rangers at Prudential Center in Newark on Tuesday, Dec. 15, and return to New York on Tuesday, Dec. 22. Each starts at 7 p.m. ET and each lists MSG for the Rangers. The Rangers' other New York-area opponent, the Islanders, are at UBS Arena on Sunday, Dec. 20.

## Back-to-backs and odd start times in the Rangers' December

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Back-to-backs:** Mon., Dec. 7 at home against the Devils and Tue., Dec. 8 at Carolina; Tue., Dec. 15 at New Jersey and Wed., Dec. 16 at Philadelphia.
- **Not at 7 p.m. ET:** Sun., Dec. 13 (6 p.m.), Wed., Dec. 16 (7:30 p.m.), Sun., Dec. 27 (5 p.m.) and Thu., Dec. 31 (9 p.m.). Every other game starts at 7 p.m.
- **Home games:** Dec. 3, 5, 7, 13, 18, 22 and 29, all at Madison Square Garden.
- **No game:** Dec. 23, 24, 25 and 26, so the Rangers play Tuesday, Dec. 22, then Sunday, Dec. 27, at St. Louis.

## What time do the Rangers play on New Year's Eve 2026?

The Rangers close the month at the Dallas Stars on Thursday, Dec. 31, at 9 p.m. ET (8 p.m. Central Time) at American Airlines Center. The feed lists Sportsnet in Canada and MSGSN for the Rangers' broadcast. For the holiday itself, see our [New Year's Eve date guide](/when-is/new-years-eve/).

## When do the Rangers play next after December?

The first game of 2027 is Sunday, Jan. 3, against the Columbus Blue Jackets at Madison Square Garden, 6 p.m. ET, on MSG in the feed. The Rangers visit Columbus on Tuesday, Jan. 5, at 7 p.m. ET.
