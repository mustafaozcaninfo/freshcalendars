**Hyde Park Winter Wonderland opens on Thursday 19 November 2026 and runs to Sunday 3 January 2027, every day except Christmas Day, with entry by pre-booked timed ticket for everyone aged 2 and over**, according to the event's official tickets guide. Below are the opening time for each day, computed from the guide, the advance ticket prices it shows and what the organisers have not yet announced. A printable calendar of the opening times is further down.

By our count the season spans 46 calendar days, 45 of them open. In 2025, the official help centre gave Friday 14 November 2025 to Thursday 1 January 2026, so the 2026 season starts five days later and ends two days later.

## What time does Winter Wonderland open each day in 2026?

The guide says the park stays open until 10pm every evening, but "some mornings will open a little later", and "any dates not listed below will open at 10am". London is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in winter, five hours ahead of Eastern Time (ET), so a 12pm London opening is 7 a.m. ET.

| Dates in 2026 and 2027 | Opens | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Thu 19 to Fri 20 Nov | 12pm | 2 |
| Mon 23 to Thu 26 Nov | 12pm | 4 |
| Fri 27 Nov | 11am | 1 |
| Mon 30 Nov | 12pm | 1 |
| Tue 1 Dec | 2pm | 1 |
| Wed 2 Dec | 12pm | 1 |
| Thu 3 to Fri 4 Dec | 11am | 2 |
| Mon 7 to Fri 11 Dec | 11am | 5 |
| Mon 14 to Fri 18 Dec | 11am | 5 |
| Mon 28 Dec to Fri 1 Jan | 11am | 5 |
| Fri 25 Dec | Closed | 1 |
| Every other date, including all Saturdays and Sundays | 10am | 18 |

The day counts are ours. They add up to 46: eight days at 12pm, 18 at 11am, one at 2pm, 18 at 10am and the Christmas Day closure. That includes 21 to 24 December, 26 and 27 December and 2 and 3 January at 10am, because none of them is in the guide's later-opening list. The guide says the final day, Sunday 3 January, is also a Family Fun Day and that more details will be revealed soon.

## How much are Winter Wonderland tickets in 2026?

"Whether you're 2 or 92, you must have an entry ticket," the guide says, and you "must pre-book a specific date and hour" of arrival. Tickets can be bought on arrival, but popular slots sell out. Advance prices are available until 23:59 the day before the visit. New for 2026, booking fees are included in the ticket price.

| Slot type | Advance price on the guide | When |
|---|---|---|
| Off-Peak (green slots) | £1.00 | The homepage says Monday to Thursday throughout November |
| Standard (gold slots) | £5.50 | Not specified |
| Peak | £8.25 | Mostly later in the day and weekends, per the guide |

These are the advance prices shown on the guide, and the guide does not say whether they change on the day.

## Is Winter Wonderland free in 2026?

Entry needs a booked ticket in every case. The guide's wording calls Off-Peak slots "always free to enter" while still requiring a ticket, yet both the guide and the homepage list them at £1.00, so check the price at checkout. The homepage also says that spending £25 online unlocks free event entry. It does not give the terms in the text we read.

## Which November dates are Off-Peak?

The homepage offers Off-Peak sessions for £1 "every Monday - Thursday throughout November". Counting only the days the event is open, that is Thursday 19 November, Monday 23 to Thursday 26 November and Monday 30 November. The Monday 30 November opening is at 12pm. The guide does not say what the Off-Peak pricing rule is in December.

## What are the age limits and bag rules?

The homepage lists age limits for three attractions: Munich Looping 8 and over, Après-Ski Party Funhouse 4 and over, and Ice Sculpting Workshops 12 and over. Prices for attractions such as the ice rink, Magical Ice Kingdom, circus, Bar Ice and Giant Wheel have not been announced in the sources we read.

The Getting Here page says bags must be A4 size or smaller and may be searched. Suitcases, scooters and bikes are not permitted, and there is no left luggage; the nearest is at Victoria Station.

## How do you get to Hyde Park Winter Wonderland?

The official nearest Underground stations are Hyde Park Corner and Knightsbridge on the Piccadilly line and Marble Arch on the Central line. The page says these stations are "expected to be busy and can sometimes be closed". For step-free access it points to Green Park or Bond Street. It does not list any 2026 station closures.
