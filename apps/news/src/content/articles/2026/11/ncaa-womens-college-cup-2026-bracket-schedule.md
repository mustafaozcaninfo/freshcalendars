**The 2026 National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) women's soccer bracket sends 64 teams into a first round on Nov. 20 or 21, and the Women's College Cup semifinals are Thursday, Dec. 10 with the final Sunday, Dec. 13, at First Horizon Stadium at WakeMed Soccer Park in Cary, North Carolina.** The final kicks off at 7 p.m. ET on ESPN2, according to the NCAA's printable bracket. Below: every first-round kickoff in four U.S. time zones, the dates of each round with weekdays, a count of games by start time and a printable watch guide.

The NCAA held its selection show on Monday, Nov. 16, according to the NCAA.com preview of Nov. 9. Where that preview and the published bracket differ on first-round days, this story follows the bracket.

## Women's College Cup 2026 dates by round

The bracket prints one date or date pair for each round. NCAA.org lists the College Cup dates as Dec. 10 and 13, 2026, at WakeMed Soccer Park in Cary. We added the weekdays.

| Round | Dates (bracket) | Weekdays | Kickoff (ET) | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First round | Nov. 20 or 21 | Fri. or Sat. | 2 p.m. to 10 p.m., set per game | Not named |
| Second and third rounds | Nov. 27 and 30 | Fri. and Mon. | Not in the bracket | Not named |
| Quarterfinals | Dec. 5 | Sat. | Not in the bracket | Not named |
| College Cup semifinals | Dec. 10 | Thu. | 6 p.m. or 8:30 p.m. | ESPNU |
| National championship | Dec. 13 | Sun. | 7 p.m. | ESPN2 |

The bracket marks the semifinal slots as "6 or 8:30 p.m." and notes that the order of the two semifinal games is determined after the quarterfinals. It adds that the second semifinal begins 30 minutes after the first one ends. Nov. 27 is the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026); see the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) to mark the dates.

## No. 1 seeds and how the 64-team bracket is split

The bracket has four quadrants of 16 teams. Each quadrant holds seeds 1 through 8, and all eight seeded teams host. The No. 1 seeds are North Carolina (17-2-0, hosting UNC Asheville on Nov. 21 at 6 p.m. ET), Vanderbilt (16-1-1, hosting UT Martin on Nov. 20 at 7 p.m.), Ohio State (15-2-1, hosting Lipscomb on Nov. 20 at 6 p.m.) and Michigan State (15-4-2, hosting Central Connecticut State on Nov. 21 at 3:30 p.m.). The records are as printed on the bracket.

## Where to watch and buy tickets for the Women's College Cup

The bracket names ESPNU for both semifinals and ESPN2 for the final. It names no network for the first three rounds or the quarterfinals, so check the host schools' athletics sites and the NCAA bracket page for those games. NCAATickets.com says "The 2026 Women's College Cup is heading to Cary, NC on December 10 & 13" and has a tickets page. Ticket prices at face value were not posted when we checked.

## What happened in the 2025 women's soccer final?

In 2025, Florida State beat Stanford 1-0 on Monday, Dec. 8, 2025, in Kansas City, according to Stanford Athletics and Florida State Athletics. Stanford's site says Florida State defeated No. 1 Stanford to win the 2025 women's soccer national championship, and Florida State Athletics headlined it the program's fifth national championship. That was last year's final; the College Cup returns to Cary in 2026.
