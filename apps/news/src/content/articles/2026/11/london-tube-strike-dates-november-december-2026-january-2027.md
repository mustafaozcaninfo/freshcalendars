**Train drivers on the London Underground who belong to the Rail, Maritime and Transport (RMT) union plan eight 24-hour strikes, the first starting at noon on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026**, according to Time Out London, IanVisits and Railnews. Time Out lists the dates through Thursday, Feb. 25, 2027, and says each strike ends at noon the next day. The table below adds the day each strike ends and the days it covers, and a printable planner is attached.

## Tube strike dates from November 2026 to February 2027

All three outlets give the same eight start dates and the same noon start. Time Out London, published Monday, Nov. 16, prints "12pm" for every start and end and gives the end of each strike. IanVisits and Railnews, both published Friday, Nov. 6, say each strike lasts 24 hours from noon.

| Strike | Starts (noon) | Ends (noon) | Days it touches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thu., Nov. 26, 2026 | Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | Thursday evening, Friday morning |
| 2 | Thu., Dec. 17, 2026 | Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | Thursday evening, Friday morning |
| 3 | Sat., Dec. 19, 2026 | Sun., Dec. 20, 2026 | Saturday afternoon and evening, Sunday morning |
| 4 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | Monday evening, Tuesday morning |
| 5 | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 | Thu., Jan. 7, 2027 | Wednesday evening, Thursday morning |
| 6 | Wed., Feb. 17, 2027 | Thu., Feb. 18, 2027 | Wednesday evening, Thursday morning |
| 7 | Tue., Feb. 23, 2027 | Wed., Feb. 24, 2027 | Tuesday evening, Wednesday morning |
| 8 | Thu., Feb. 25, 2027 | Fri., Feb. 26, 2027 | Thursday evening, Friday morning |

The last column is our own reading of the noon-to-noon times: a strike that starts at noon covers the evening of its first day, and one that ends at noon the next day covers that morning. The sources do not list individual rush hours.

## Which mornings and holiday dates fall inside a strike?

Because every strike ends at noon, the morning of the second day falls inside it each time: Friday, Nov. 27, Friday, Dec. 18, Sunday, Dec. 20, Tuesday, Jan. 5, Thursday, Jan. 7, Thursday, Feb. 18, Wednesday, Feb. 24 and Friday, Feb. 26. That is our calculation from the Time Out times.

Three dates stand out on a calendar:

- **Friday, Nov. 27, 2026** is Black Friday, and the morning after the first strike begins.
- **Saturday, Dec. 19, 2026** is the last Saturday before Christmas, and the third strike starts at noon.
- **Monday, Jan. 4 and Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027** are the first Monday and Wednesday of 2027, and a strike starts at noon on each.

No strike appears in the lists on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day or Boxing Day, or on Friday, Jan. 1. For rail closures on those days, see our story on [UK trains at Christmas 2026](/news/uk-trains-christmas-2026-no-trains-december-25-26-paddington-closed/). If you are going to the [London New Year's Day Parade](/news/london-new-years-day-parade-2027-start-time-route-tickets/), Jan. 1 is not a strike day in any of the three lists.

## Why are Tube drivers striking?

Time Out says the dispute is over "a so-called voluntary four-day working week alongside a new Trains Modernisation Framework". Railnews quotes RMT general secretary Eddie Dempsey: "London Underground is attempting to dress up a wholesale attack on drivers' working conditions as a voluntary four-day week." Time Out quotes Nick Dent, Transport for London (TfL) director of customer operations: "This will also mean less time at work for Tube drivers."

Railnews also reports that the unions are divided. Finn Brennan of the train drivers' union, the Associated Society of Locomotive Engineers and Firemen (ASLEF), said: "We are surprised that the RMT has, once again, decided to take strike action." Time Out reports that ASLEF has not announced any London Underground strikes.

## Will the Elizabeth line and Overground run?

Time Out says the Elizabeth line is "not a London Underground service" and "runs normally on tube strike days", and that no London Overground strikes are planned. This point rests on Time Out alone.

## Which Tube lines will run during a strike?

That is not confirmed. IanVisits says the impact will be confirmed closer to the date, and we have not found a TfL service plan for any strike. Check TfL's journey planner before each date.
