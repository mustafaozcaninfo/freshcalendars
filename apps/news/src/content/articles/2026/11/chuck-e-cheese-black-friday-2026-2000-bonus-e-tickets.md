**Chuck E. Cheese gives 2,000 bonus E-Tickets for every $25 in gift cards bought Nov. 27-30, 2026, according to the company's newsroom release published Nov. 9, 2026.** That is Black Friday through Cyber Monday. Through Dec. 31 the same purchase earns 1,000 bonus E-Tickets. The release lists U.S. locations as closed on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26.

## How many bonus E-Tickets does a Chuck E. Cheese gift card earn?

The release says: "Through Dec. 31, guests receive 1,000 bonus E-Tickets for every $25 in Chuck E. Cheese gift cards purchased, with a maximum of four bonus E-Ticket codes per guest at participating locations while supplies last." For Nov. 27-30, "the same purchase earns 2,000 bonus E-Tickets."

The table is our arithmetic from those two rates. It assumes each $25 earns one code, and uses the release's four-code limit as the ceiling. The release does not say how the cap counts codes; the full terms are at chuckecheese.com/gift-cards.

| Gift card spend | Codes | Bonus E-Tickets, 1,000 rate (through Dec. 31) | Bonus E-Tickets, Nov. 27-30 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $25 | 1 | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| $50 | 2 | 2,000 | 4,000 |
| $75 | 3 | 3,000 | 6,000 |
| $100 | 4 | 4,000 | 8,000 |

Per the release's four-code limit, the most a guest can earn is 8,000 bonus E-Tickets on Nov. 27-30 or 4,000 at the 1,000 rate. The release does not state when the 1,000 rate began, only that it runs through Dec. 31.

## What are the terms of the gift card offer?

- The offer applies "only to gift cards purchased at Chuck E. Cheese fun centers and online at chuckecheese.com."
- Bonus E-Tickets "are delivered via email immediately after purchase and are valid Jan. 1-March 31, 2027."
- Codes are limited to participating locations, while supplies last.
- Full terms are at chuckecheese.com/gift-cards.
- The Fun Pass page says: "Chuck E. Cheese gift cards cannot be used to purchase Fun Passes or Memberships."

The release lists a Fun Pass as starting at $54.99 and monthly options from $7.99 to $24.99 a month. It announces no Fun Pass discount.

## Is Chuck E. Cheese open on Thanksgiving and Christmas 2026?

Hours below are for U.S. locations, from the Nov. 9 release, which advises guests to check chuckecheese.com for local hours.

| Date | Hours |
|---|---|
| Thursday, Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving) | Closed |
| Thursday, Dec. 24 (Christmas Eve) | Closing at 5 p.m. |
| Friday, Dec. 25 (Christmas Day) | Closed |
| Thursday, Dec. 31 (New Year's Eve) | Regular hours |
| Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 (New Year's Day) | Regular hours |

The release gives no Black Friday hours, so check the location page before you go. Other stores' Thanksgiving closings and Black Friday openers are in the [Black Friday 2026 giveaways by store](/news/black-friday-2026-giveaways-first-customers-by-store/) story.

## What is the #RobotBananaChallenge?

The release says that starting Nov. 27, families can recreate the Robot Banana Dance from the holiday special. Videos can be featured on screens at participating locations Dec. 20-26. "Videos must be posted by a parent or guardian age 18 or older by Dec. 16."

## What has Chuck E. Cheese not announced?

- Black Friday hours at any location.
- Clock times or a time zone for the Nov. 27-30 window; the release gives dates only.
- A promo code or a Fun Pass discount; none is in the release.
