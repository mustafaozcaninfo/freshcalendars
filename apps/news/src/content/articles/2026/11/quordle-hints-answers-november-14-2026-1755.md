## How five common opening words score on today's four boards

Each cell gives green tiles (G, right letter, right spot) and yellow tiles (Y, right letter, wrong spot) the opener would earn against that word.

Stare and Crane tie at nine colored tiles, yet they get there differently. Stare banks four greens, the most of any row, and Crane only two, with a lone green on each of two boards. Crane's strength is Word 2, where it lights four tiles, the highest single cell in the table.

Adieu finishes last with five tiles. It spends four of its five letters on vowels and still leaves one board completely dark: not one green and not one yellow on Word 4.

Word 4 is the board that stays darkest overall. Add its column and the five openers earn six colored tiles together, against seven on Word 3, eleven on Word 1 and thirteen on Word 2. Word 3 is nearly as stubborn, with one colored tile from each of three openers. If your first guess leaves a board blank, expect to spend the second guess there.
