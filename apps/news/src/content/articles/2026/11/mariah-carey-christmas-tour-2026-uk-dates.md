**Mariah Carey plays three UK arena shows on her Christmastime Tour: Birmingham on Friday, Nov. 27, London on Monday, Nov. 30 and Manchester on Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026, according to the Official Charts Company.** Time Out UK and PA Media (the Press Association) list the same three dates. Only Birmingham has a show time on an official page: Live Nation UK, the promoter's ticket page, gives 7:30 p.m. GMT (Greenwich Mean Time), or 2:30 p.m. ET. The table below adds Carey's other 2026 dates, and the printable sheet is at the end.

## What time does the Birmingham show start in ET?

Live Nation UK's page for the Birmingham show lists doors at 18:00 (6 p.m. GMT), the show at 19:30 (7:30 p.m. GMT) and a curfew of 23:00 (11 p.m. GMT), with DJ Suss One as support. The UK is on GMT in late November, five hours ahead of Eastern Time, so doors are at 1 p.m. ET, the show at 2:30 p.m. ET and the curfew at 6 p.m. ET. London and Manchester use the same clock, so a start of 7:30 p.m. there would also be 2:30 p.m. ET, but neither venue has posted a time.

## Which dates outside the UK are confirmed?

In Brazil, CNN Brasil reported on June 1, 2026 that Carey plays São Paulo on Nov. 17 and Rio de Janeiro on Nov. 21, with Farmasi Arena as the Rio venue. The Rio Times (Sept. 4, 2026) also gives Farmasi Arena and Nov. 21. The two reports name different São Paulo venues and The Rio Times lists a second São Paulo night on Nov. 18 that CNN Brasil does not, so the table gives no São Paulo venue and no second night.

PA reports one Malta show at the Malta Fairs and Conventions Centre near Valletta without a date. The Italian news agency Italpress reports Tuesday, Dec. 8. Only Italpress gives the date, and the promoter has not posted it on a page we could read, so the table marks it "reported".

## Are there Mariah Carey Christmas shows in the U.S. in 2026?

No U.S. Christmas dates have been announced for 2026. In 2025, a Live Nation release dated Sept. 15, 2025 announced 10 shows at Dolby Live in Las Vegas from Nov. 28 to Dec. 13, 2025, each at 8 p.m. The 2025 run is last year's schedule and not a 2026 date.

## Where to buy UK tickets

General sale opened Friday, Sept. 25, 2026 at 10 a.m. BST (British Summer Time), according to the Official Charts Company and Time Out UK. PA gave the date as Sept. 25. Tickets are through Live Nation UK, and Brazil tickets are through Eventim Brasil. Time Out UK reports Birmingham tickets from £60. We did not find an official price list for London or Manchester.
