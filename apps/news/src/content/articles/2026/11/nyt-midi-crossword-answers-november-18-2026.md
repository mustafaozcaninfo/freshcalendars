The title is a weather report, and the grid takes it seriously. The data holds one clue that talks about the theme: 6-Down, which says it is a hint to "this puzzle's shaded answers, phonetically." That entry has 10 letters and runs from the top row to the bottom row of column 7, so it crosses ten Across entries, one per row, with 4, 4, 4, 3, 8, 3, 3, 4, 4 and 3 letters from the top down.

The saved data does not mark which squares are shaded, so this page does not guess. Read the 6-Down clue twice instead: it asks you to say the answer aloud and listen for what the shaded entries sound like.
