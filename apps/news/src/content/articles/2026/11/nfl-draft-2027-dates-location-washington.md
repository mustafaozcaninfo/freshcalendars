**The 2027 NFL Draft will take place Thursday, April 29, to Saturday, May 1, 2027, in Washington, D.C., the NFL announced on NFL.com on June 25, 2026.** The league has not yet announced start times, which rounds fall on which day or the TV networks. This page lays out the road from Super Bowl 2027 (LXI) to Round 1 and the rule that sets the pick order, with a printable sheet of both.

## Where is the 2027 NFL Draft?

On the National Mall in Washington. The NFL's announcement says the National Mall will anchor "the Draft Theater in front of the Capitol," and that the NFL Draft Experience will span Pennsylvania Avenue NW between 3rd and 7th Streets. The Associated Press (AP), in a June 25, 2026, report carried by NBC4 Washington, wrote that the three-day draft "will begin on Thursday, April 29, and continue through Saturday, May 1," with "a variety of free activities available for fans."

The NFL's event page offers early-access registration for Very Important Person (VIP) ticket packages. No other 2027 ticket or registration details are published yet.

## How is the NFL draft order set?

The NFL's tiebreaking procedures set the order by how each club's season ends. There is no 2027 order yet, because the regular season is still being played.

| Picks | Who selects | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 18 | Clubs not in the playoffs | Reverse-standings order |
| 19 to 24 | Wild Card round losers | Playoff round of elimination |
| 25 to 28 | Divisional round losers | Playoff round of elimination |
| 29 and 30 | Conference championship losers | Playoff round of elimination |
| 31 | Super Bowl loser | Next-to-last |
| 32 | Super Bowl winner | Last |

In the NFL's words: "The winner of the Super Bowl game shall select last and the Super Bowl loser will select next-to-last." When clubs have the same record, the procedures break the tie by the aggregate won-lost-tied percentage of their regular-season opponents, and the lowest percentage picks first.

## What did the 2026 NFL Draft schedule look like in Pittsburgh?

Last year's draft was held at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh. These are the 2026 times from NFL.com, shown only as a reference: the NFL has not said whether 2027 uses the same split or start times.

| 2026 day | Rounds | Start (ET) |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday, April 23 | Round 1 | 8 p.m. |
| Friday, April 24 | Rounds 2-3 | 7 p.m. |
| Saturday, April 25 | Rounds 4-7 | Noon |

On television in 2026, ESPN Press Room said ESPN, for the 47th year, and ABC, for the eighth year, would "once again bring fans all 257 picks." NFL.com listed NFL Network, NFL+, ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPN Deportes. The 2027 networks are not yet announced.
