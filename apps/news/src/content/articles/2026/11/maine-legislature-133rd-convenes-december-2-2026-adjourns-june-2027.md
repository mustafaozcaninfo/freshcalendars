**Maine's 133rd Legislature convenes Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026, in Augusta, with members sworn in at 10 a.m., according to the Legislative Council's convening schedule.** The Maine State Legislature's page sets a statutory adjournment date of the third Wednesday in June for the first regular session, which is Wednesday, June 16, 2027. The table below puts every dated step in order, and a free printable tracker keeps them on one sheet. Facts were read on Nov. 12, 2026.

Maine's first regular session starts before the new year. The Constitution (Article IV, Part 3, Section 1) says the Legislature "shall convene on the first Wednesday of December following the general election."

## What happens on Dec. 2, 2026, the first day?

The Legislative Council's schedule for the Pre-Legislative Conference, which runs "November 30 – December 3, 2026," lists Wednesday, Dec. 2 as the "Convening of the 133rd Maine Legislature." The program lists these events.

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 10:00 AM | Swearing-in of members by Gov. Janet T. Mills |
| Time not listed | Election of a presiding officer in each chamber, the Secretary of the Senate and the Clerk of the House of Representatives |
| 2:00 PM | Joint Convention of the 133rd Legislature: election of constitutional officers |

The Legislature has a Senate of 35 members and a House of Representatives of 151 members, plus three non-voting tribal members from the Penobscot Nation, the Passamaquoddy Tribe and the Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians. The Dec. 2 votes decide who presides in each chamber, and this page names no nominees.

## Who does the joint convention elect on Dec. 2?

The Constitution has the Legislature choose three officers by joint ballot of the Senators and Representatives in convention. The Secretary of State and the Treasurer are chosen biennially at the first session (Article V, Part 2, Section 1 and Part 3, Section 1). The Attorney General is chosen biennially the same way (Article IX, Section 11). The schedule lists the 2 p.m. convention as the election of constitutional officers.

## When does the Maine Legislature adjourn in 2027?

The statutory adjournment date for the first regular session is the third Wednesday in June, which is June 16, 2027. The Legislature's page calls it a statutory date; no actual adjournment date is posted. For comparison, the 132nd Legislature's second regular session adjourned sine die on Wednesday, April 29, 2026 at 8:32 PM, according to the House page (last edited Nov. 9, 2026). That is last Legislature's result, not a forecast.

The second regular session convenes on the first Wednesday after the first Tuesday in January, which is Wednesday, Jan. 5, 2028. Its statutory adjournment date is the third Wednesday in April, Wednesday, April 19, 2028.

## What deadlines follow convening day?

- **Governor's term:** Article V, Part 1, Section 2 gives the governor four years "from the first Wednesday of January next following the election," Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027 by our count. For other offices, see [when 2026 election winners take office](/news/when-do-2026-election-winners-take-office-congress-governors/).
- **Initiative petitions:** Article IV, Part 3, Section 18 sets "on or before the 50th day after the date of convening of the Legislature in first regular session." Counting 50 days from Dec. 2 gives Thursday, Jan. 21, 2027.
- **Bill requests:** Joint Rule 204 sets state agency bill requests "by 4:00 p.m. on the first Wednesday in December," which would be Dec. 2, 2026 if the 133rd keeps the rule. Each Legislature adopts its own joint rules. For the 132nd, Rule 202 set legislator bill requests "by 4:00 p.m. on January 10, 2025," last Legislature's date.
- **Cloture:** Rule 203 says "The Legislative Council shall set a cloture date." The 133rd's date is not posted.

## When do Maine laws passed in 2027 take effect?

A non-emergency law takes effect 90 days after the recess of the session in which it passed, under Article IV, Part 3, Section 16. The recess date is not announced, so there is no effective date to give. An emergency law needs a two-thirds vote of all members elected to each House.
