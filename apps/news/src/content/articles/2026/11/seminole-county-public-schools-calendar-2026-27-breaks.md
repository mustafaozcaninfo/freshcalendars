**Seminole County Public Schools (SCPS) in Florida starts classes Monday, Aug. 10, 2026, closes schools Dec. 21-31 for winter break and March 15-19, 2027 for spring break, and ends the year for students Tuesday, May 25, 2027.** Those dates come from the district's "Student Calendar 2026-2027, Board Approved 5/13/2025," linked from its calendar page. Students return from winter break Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027.

Some websites give a different last day and a longer winter break. The district's PDF shows May 25 as the last day of student attendance; May 27 is a teacher post-planning day. It shows classes resuming Jan. 6, not later.

## Seminole County school breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays are our count from the district's dates.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving week | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter break and the days after it | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | 12 | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 |
| Spring break and the Monday after | Mon., March 15 to Mon., March 22, 2027 | 6 | Tue., March 23, 2027 |

The calendar lists "Schools Closed/Winter Break" for Dec. 21-31, "School System Closed" for Jan. 1, and Jan. 4 and 5 as closed for students. Spring break is March 15-19, and March 22 is closed for students as a teacher workday. Return days are read from the calendar, which marks no closure on those dates. ## What are Seminole County's make-up days?

The calendar marks five days as "Student Make-up Day, if needed." Make-up days like these replace school days lost to closures, such as a hurricane week; the PDF does not name the trigger. The calendar does not say any has been used or how many lost days would trigger each one.

| Make-up day | Date | Weekday | What it would change |
|---|---|---|---|
| #1 | Jan. 5, 2027 | Tuesday | Students would be in class the day before classes resume |
| #2 | Jan. 4, 2027 | Monday | Winter break would end a day earlier |
| #3 | Oct. 12, 2026 | Monday | A single Monday off would become a school day |
| #4 | March 22, 2027 | Monday | Spring break would be followed by a school day, not a weekday off |
| #5 | May 26, 2027 | Wednesday | School would run one day past the May 25 last day |

The numbers show the order the district gives them, not the order they fall on the calendar. Check the district's calendar page for any change before booking travel around a make-up day. The printable lists them as unconfirmed.

## Closed schools or closed school system: what is the difference?

The calendar uses two labels. "Schools Closed" appears on Nov. 23-25, Dec. 21-31 and March 15-19, and "School System Closed" on Sept. 7, Nov. 26-27, Jan. 1, Jan. 18 and Feb. 15. "Schools Closed for Students/Teacher Workday" marks Oct. 12, Jan. 4, Jan. 5 and March 22: no students, but a workday for teachers. The PDF does not define the labels beyond that.

## Which Seminole County days end early?

The calendar lists "Early Dismissal for Students" on Dec. 16-18, 2026, and May 21, 24 and 25, 2027. It does not give dismissal times in the PDF. May 25, the last day, is one of them.

## What is the 2027-28 Seminole County school calendar?

The district's "Student Calendar 2027-2028," approved 5/13/25, has the first day on Tuesday, Aug. 10, 2027. Winter break is Dec. 20-31, 2027, and classes resume Wednesday, Jan. 5, 2028. The last day is Wednesday, May 24, 2028.
