**Target's Cyber Monday 2026 deals go live Monday, Nov. 30 at 2 a.m. CT, according to Target's Nov. 27 fact sheet.** That is 3 a.m. ET. Target's release calls the event an online-only sale, "exclusively on Target.com and the Target app," so stores do not carry the Cyber Monday deals. Target has not said when the sale ends. Below: the start time in every U.S. zone, the deals by category, and the return deadlines that follow.

## What time does Target's Cyber Monday sale start in each time zone?

Target states the start in Central Time (CT). The other zones are our conversion.

| Zone | Start of Target Cyber Monday |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | 3 a.m. Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Central (CT), Target's time | 2 a.m. Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Mountain, and Arizona | 1 a.m. Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Pacific (PT) | 12 midnight, Sunday night into Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Alaska | 11 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 29 |
| Hawaii | 10 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 29 |

In Pacific Time the sale opens at midnight, and in Alaska and Hawaii it opens on Sunday night, Nov. 29, before Monday begins there.

## Is Target's Cyber Monday in stores?

No. The sale is online only, on Target.com and the Target app. Stores keep their holiday hours, which Target extends from Nov. 29 through Dec. 23, and the Nov. 27 fact sheet says Black Friday stores open at 6 a.m. local time. Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, stores close by 8 p.m. local time, and on Friday, Dec. 25 all stores are closed. Our [Target Thanksgiving and Black Friday hours story](/news/is-target-open-thanksgiving-2026-black-friday-hours/) has the store side.

## What are Target's Cyber Monday 2026 deals?

Target's release lists these offers by category.

| Category | Cyber Monday offer | Brands named |
|---|---|---|
| Tech and gaming | Up to 50% off | Beats, Bose, HP and Hiro; savings also from Apple and Oura Ring |
| Toys | Up to 50% off select toys | LEGO, Disney, MrBeast, B. toys and more |
| Apparel and accessories | Up to 50% off | Cat & Jack, Wild Fable, A New Day, Goodfellow & Co |
| Jewelry, handbags, accessories | Up to 50% off | Vera Bradley and more |
| Beauty | 30% off select | Athena Club, Kitsch, La Roche-Posay, Morphe and more |
| Home | 40% off select holiday bedding; up to 50% off select floorcare and kitchen appliances | Shark, Nutribullet, Ninja, Hamilton Beach, KitchenAid |
| Wellness | 25% off vitamins and nutrition | Ritual, Clean Simple Eats, Liquid I.V. |

Target gives no percentage for the Apple and Oura Ring savings. The fact sheet adds a Cyber Monday Deal of the Day: 50% off shoes and slippers for all.

## What does Target Plus add on Dec. 1-2?

On Tuesday, Dec. 1, and Wednesday, Dec. 2, select Target Plus brand partners offer additional deals, according to the release.

| Category | Target Plus offer | Brands named |
|---|---|---|
| Toys | Up to 60% off | Joyfy, Costway |
| Christmas trees, lighting, decor | Up to 60% off | Not listed |
| Cookware | Up to 50% off | Gotham Steel, Granitestone |
| Shoes | Up to 50% off | Cushionaire, HeyDude |

## When does Target's Cyber Monday sale end?

Target has not announced it. Neither the fact sheet nor the release gives an end date or time. Last year's release (Nov. 28, 2025) said the sale started Sunday, Nov. 30, 2025 at 2 a.m. CT and "will run through Monday, Dec. 1." The 2026 start is on a Monday, while the 2025 start was on a Sunday. Target also says a new Deal of the Day is available every day from Nov. 1 through Dec. 24.

## What are Target's Black Friday, gift card and price match dates?

Target's Black Friday deals run Nov. 22-29, with new weekend deals Nov. 26-29. The 10% off Target GiftCards offer for Target Circle members returns Dec. 5-6, online and in stores. Target's Holiday Price Match Guarantee runs now through Dec. 24 and matches Target's own prices if they go lower later in the season. The fact sheet does not say whether it covers Cyber Monday prices.

## What are Target's return deadlines for holiday purchases?

Target gives extra time for qualifying purchases made Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, 2026. The return window begins Dec. 26, 2026.

| Item | Return by |
|---|---|
| Apple, Beats and mobile phones (unlocked or pre-paid) | Friday, Jan. 8, 2027 |
| Electronics and entertainment (excluding Apple and Beats) | Sunday, Jan. 24, 2027 |
| Most Target Plus items (excluding seasonal holiday decor) | Sunday, Jan. 24, 2027 |

Counting Dec. 26 as day one, the Jan. 8 deadline gives 14 days and the Jan. 24 deadline gives 30 days.
