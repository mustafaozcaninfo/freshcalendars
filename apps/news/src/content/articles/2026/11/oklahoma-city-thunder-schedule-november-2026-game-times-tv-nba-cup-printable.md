**The Oklahoma City Thunder play at Cleveland on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, at 6 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The NBA lists 15 Thunder games in November, and a one-page printable of all of them is further down.

The source is the NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF from NBA Communications, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change." Its times are in ET, so Oklahoma City fans subtract one hour for CT; the table below gives both. Weekdays are our own calculation. The Thunder's previous game was at Houston on Saturday, Oct. 31.

## Which Thunder games count in the NBA Cup?

The schedule flags four November Thunder games as Emirates NBA Cup group play: at Memphis on Fri., Nov. 6, at the LA Clippers on Fri., Nov. 13, against New Orleans on Fri., Nov. 20 and against Minnesota on Wed., Nov. 25. Group play runs from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, per NBA.com's Aug. 12, 2026 release, with quarterfinals on Dec. 4 and 5 and the championship on Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round.

## Back-to-backs and road trips in the Thunder's November

Reading the dates in the table shows how the month is built. These are our own counts from the NBA's schedule:

- **Back-to-backs:** Fri., Nov. 6 and Sat., Nov. 7 (at Memphis, then home), Tue., Nov. 10 and Wed., Nov. 11 (both at Portland), and Sun., Nov. 22 and Mon., Nov. 23 (at Miami, then at Indiana).
- **A four-game western road trip:** the Thunder play at Portland on Nov. 10 and 11, at the LA Clippers on Nov. 13 and at Sacramento on Nov. 15 before returning home.
- **Road-heavy month:** 10 of the 15 games are away and only five are at home in Oklahoma City, the home dates falling on Nov. 4, 7, 18, 20 and 25.
- **Thanksgiving week:** the Thunder host Minnesota on Wed., Nov. 25 and play at Milwaukee on Sat., Nov. 28. They have no game on Thanksgiving Day itself, which is Thu., Nov. 26, 2026. Our [Thanksgiving date page](/when-is/thanksgiving/) has the holiday.

Five home games and 10 road games make 15 in all, from Nov. 2 to Nov. 30.
