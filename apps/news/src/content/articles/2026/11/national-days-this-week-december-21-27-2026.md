**The winter solstice, Christmas Day and the start of Kwanzaa fall between Monday, Dec. 21 and Sunday, Dec. 27, 2026, and each comes from a different official source.** The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) sets the solstice, the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) the federal holiday, and GOV.UK and the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) the UK and Canadian days. A planner for the week follows the table.

Only days with a named setter are listed. Commercial "national food days" are left out.

## When is the winter solstice on Dec. 21, 2026?

The USNO seasons data gives the December solstice as Dec. 21, 2026 at 20:50 UT. That is the time in each U.S. zone below.

| Zone | Local time, Monday, Dec. 21 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (EST) | 3:50 p.m. |
| Central (CST) | 2:50 p.m. |
| Mountain (MST) | 1:50 p.m. |
| Pacific (PST) | 12:50 p.m. |

Sunset times by city are in our [winter solstice story](/news/winter-solstice-2026-december-21-shortest-day-sunset-times/). The Chinese festival tied to the solstice has its own page: [Dongzhi Festival 2026](/news/dongzhi-festival-2026-december-22-winter-solstice/).

## Which Christmas days are public holidays in the U.S., UK and Canada?

OPM's federal holiday list reads "Friday, December 25 | Christmas Day". As of Nov. 13, 2026 there is no federal closure order for Thursday, Dec. 24 or Saturday, Dec. 26, and Christmas Eve is not a federal, UK or Canadian holiday in these sources; see [Is Christmas Eve a federal holiday?](/news/is-christmas-eve-a-federal-holiday-2026/).

GOV.UK lists "25 December | Friday | Christmas Day" in all three UK regions. Its rule: "If a bank holiday is on a weekend, a 'substitute' weekday becomes a bank holiday, normally the following Monday." The Boxing Day substitute is "28 December | Monday", which falls after this week; the [UK Christmas bank holidays story](/news/uk-christmas-bank-holidays-2026-boxing-day-substitute-december-28/) has the full list.

The CRA's public holidays page, dated Jan. 6, 2026, reads "Christmas Day – Friday, December 25, 2026" and "Boxing Day – Saturday, December 26, 2026". For the working day that follows, see [Boxing Day 2026 in Canada](/news/boxing-day-2026-canada-saturday-december-26-day-off-monday-december-28/).

## When does Kwanzaa start in 2026?

The National Park Service's African Burial Ground National Monument says "Kwanzaa begins on December 26, with the principle of Umoja, which calls for unity", concluding "January 1, with the principle of Imani". In 2026 Dec. 26 is a Saturday. The page was updated May 1, 2019. Our [Kwanzaa 2026 story](/news/kwanzaa-2026-dates-seven-principles-calendar-printable/) lists the seven principles by day.

## What does the church calendar list for Christmas week?

The Liturgy Office of England and Wales lists Thursday, Dec. 24 as a "Weekday of Advent", Friday, Dec. 25 as "The Nativity of the Lord" (solemnity), Saturday, Dec. 26 as "St Stephen, the first Martyr" (feast) and Sunday, Dec. 27 as "The Holy Family of Jesus, Mary and Joseph" (feast).

Santa tracking has no 2026 time yet; the [Tracks Santa story](/news/norad-tracks-santa-2026-when-tracking-starts-phone-number/) has the phone number and any start announcement.
