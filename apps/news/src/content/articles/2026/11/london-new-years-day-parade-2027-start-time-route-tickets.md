**London's New Year's Day Parade is on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and starts at 1 p.m. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), which is 8 a.m. Eastern Time (ET)**, according to Time Out and The Late Edition. Time Out lists a 1pm start; The Late Edition lists 13:00 to 16:30. We could not open the organiser's own site, londonparade.com, which refused automated access, so confirm the details there before you travel. Below: the start in U.S. time zones, the route, what is free and what is not yet announced.

## What time is the London New Year's Day Parade in U.S. time zones?

London is on GMT (UTC+0) in January, which is five hours ahead of Eastern Time. The 4:30 p.m. finish is listed by The Late Edition only. The rows other than London are our conversion.

| Where | Start | Finish (The Late Edition) |
|---|---|---|
| London, GMT | 1 p.m. | 4:30 p.m. |
| New York and Toronto, Eastern Time (ET) | 8 a.m. | 11:30 a.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | 7 a.m. | 10:30 a.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 6 a.m. | 9:30 a.m. |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 5 a.m. | 8:30 a.m. |

For the holiday dates around it, see our [UK bank holidays 2027 dates](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/).

## What is the London New Year's Day Parade route?

Time Out says the route "will move from Piccadilly, through Regent Street and St James's, along Pall Mall and through Whitehall, ending at Westminster." The Late Edition lists Piccadilly, Regent Street, St James's and Pall Mall, then Trafalgar Square and down Whitehall. The two sources agree on the start on Piccadilly and an end near Westminster. They differ on the finish street, so we do not name one.

| Order | Stretch | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Piccadilly (start) | Time Out, The Late Edition |
| 2 | Regent Street | Time Out, The Late Edition |
| 3 | St James's | Time Out, The Late Edition |
| 4 | Pall Mall | Time Out, The Late Edition |
| 5 | Trafalgar Square | The Late Edition |
| 6 | Whitehall, ending near Westminster | Time Out, The Late Edition |

## Is the parade free, and where can you stand?

The Late Edition says: "It is free to stand and watch." Grandstand ticket holders arrive from noon, per the same guide. Grandstand prices and the seller for 2027 are not yet announced, so we give no price.

The route table above is the best guide to where to stand: Piccadilly is the start, Pall Mall and St James's are in the middle, and Whitehall is near the end. Neither source names a viewing spot, so check londonparade.com for access points.

## Who performs in the parade?

Time Out says more than 8,000 performers from across the globe take part. The Late Edition describes mostly American high school and college marching bands, plus London borough entries, heritage vehicles and floats. Headliners for 2027 are not yet announced.

## What time is the London parade on PBS?

Time Out says that in America the parade is televised on the Public Broadcasting Service (PBS). The 2027 air time is not yet announced, and PBS stations set their own times, so check your local PBS station listings. No UK broadcaster has been announced in the sources we used.

## What is not yet announced?

- The 2027 headliners
- Transport for London (TfL) road and Tube closures for Jan. 1, 2027

## What happens the night before?

The Greater London Authority (GLA) says on nye.london.gov.uk: "Tickets for The Mayor of London's New Year's Eve fireworks are now on sale. Ticket prices are £20 to £55, depending on viewing area and whether you live in London."
