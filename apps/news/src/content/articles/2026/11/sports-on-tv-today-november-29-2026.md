## What is on national TV and what is on League Pass?

Of the 30 events, the NFL's 10 games each list a national network (CBS, FOX or NBC), and Formula 1 is on Apple TV, which holds the 2026 U.S. rights. In the NBA, Celtics at Spurs on NBA TV is the only national broadcaster nba.com lists; the other nine games show NBA League Pass, and the page does not list local channels. The NHL's Wild at Blackhawks is on NHL Network; the Canucks game lists NESN locally and Sportsnet in Canada.

## Which hour has the most games?

The most events starting at one time is 1 p.m. ET, with six NFL games: three on CBS and three on FOX. The next wave is 4:05 p.m. ET and 4:25 p.m. ET, with Patriots at Chargers on CBS and Commanders at Cardinals and Seahawks at 49ers on FOX. The NBA's four 6 p.m. ET tips are the next largest group.

NFL.com lists ten Sunday games in Week 12: six at 1 p.m. ET, one at 4:05 p.m. ET, two at 4:25 p.m. ET and Raiders at Browns at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. ## Formula 1, Davis Cup and Premier League on Sunday morning

Formula 1 lists the Qatar Grand Prix on Sunday, Nov. 29 as 57 laps or 120 minutes, "19:00 - 21:00" local time at Lusail International Circuit, which is 11 a.m. ET. The U.S. rights are with Apple TV, according to Apple's newsroom.

The Davis Cup Final is at "15:00 CET (14:00 GMT)" in Bologna, Italy, per daviscup.com: Central European Time (CET), 9 a.m. ET. The site lists no U.S. channel. Our [Davis Cup Final 8 schedule](/news/davis-cup-final-8-2026-bologna-schedule-time-zones-teams/) has the full week in four time zones.

The Premier League's fixture release lists UK times in Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and UK broadcasters only. Everton v Liverpool is 12:00 GMT (7 a.m. ET), four matches are 14:05 GMT (9:05 a.m. ET) and Arsenal v Manchester City is 16:30 GMT (11:30 a.m. ET). It names no U.S. channel, so the table says so.
