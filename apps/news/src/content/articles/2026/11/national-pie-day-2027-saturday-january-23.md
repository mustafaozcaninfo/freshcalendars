National Pie Day is Jan. 23, which falls on Saturday in 2027, according to the American Pie Council (APC). The council's website says it "invites everyone from sea to shining sea to celebrate National Pie Day" on Jan. 23. This story adds a weekday table through 2032 that we computed, a side-by-side of National Pie Day and Pi Day, and a free plan sheet.

## What does the American Pie Council say about National Pie Day?

The council's page suggests "a small slice of heaven" as a meal ender or as a treat "just because," and asks people to share with #NationalPieDay. It does not say who founded the day, so we do not name a founder and do not credit the council with starting it.

## What weekday is National Pie Day through 2032?

The date is fixed at Jan. 23, so only the weekday moves. We computed these from the calendar.

| Year | Jan. 23 falls on |
|---|---|
| 2027 | Saturday |
| 2028 | Sunday |
| 2029 | Tuesday |
| 2030 | Wednesday |
| 2031 | Thursday |
| 2032 | Friday |

Mark the day on the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html). The same date is also National Handwriting Day, which marks John Hancock's birthday; see our [National Handwriting Day 2027 story](/news/national-handwriting-day-2027-saturday-january-23/).

## How is National Pie Day different from Pi Day?

The two share a sound, not a date. Pi Day is held on March 14, and March 14 matches the first digits of pi, 3.14. A House resolution in the 111th Congress in 2009 (House Resolution 224, published on GovInfo) states that "March 14, 2009, is an appropriate day for 'National Pi Day'."

| Day | Date | What it marks | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Pie Day | Jan. 23 (Saturday in 2027) | Eating and sharing pie | American Pie Council |
| Pi Day | March 14 | The number pi | House Resolution 224 (2009), GovInfo |

NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) runs a yearly "NASA Pi Day Challenge," which belongs to the March observance.

## Are there National Pie Day 2027 deals?

Not yet announced. On Nov. 4, 2026, neither Marie Callender's nor Bakers Square had posted a National Pie Day 2027 offer on its own site. We will check the chains' own pages in the week before Jan. 23 and will not treat any past offer as this year's. For a look at how a food-day deal story reads once offers are posted, see our [National Sandwich Day 2026 deals](/news/national-sandwich-day-2026-deals-tuesday-november-3-subway-mcalisters-capriottis/) report. If you bake at home, [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/) has federal food handling guidance; we give no storage rules here.
