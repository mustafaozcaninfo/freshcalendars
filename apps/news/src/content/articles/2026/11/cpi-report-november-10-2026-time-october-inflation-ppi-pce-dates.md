**The Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) is scheduled to publish the Consumer Price Index (CPI) for October 2026 on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to its CPI release schedule.** The table below puts the CPI, Producer Price Index (PPI) and Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) dates from November 2026 to March 2027 in one place, and a printable carries the same rows.

## What time is the CPI report on Nov. 10, 2026?

The BLS schedule lists "October 2026" with a release date of Nov. 10, 2026, a Tuesday, at 8:30 a.m. ET. The September 2026 CPI summary repeats it: "The Consumer Price Index news release for October 2026 is scheduled to be published on Tuesday, November 10, 2026." The BLS pages we checked on Nov. 1 show no schedule change or delay note for 2026 releases.

In 2025, the BLS archive lists the October 2025 CPI as "Not published because of 2025 lapse in federal government appropriations."

## What did the September CPI report say?

The BLS released the September 2026 CPI at 8:30 a.m. ET on Wednesday, Oct. 14, 2026. Its summary says: "Over the last 12 months, the all items index increased 3.7 percent before seasonal adjustment." The index "increased 0.6 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in September," and "the all items less food and energy index rose 2.6 percent over the year." The October figures are not out yet, and this story makes no forecast of them.

## When is the PPI report for October 2026?

The BLS lists the Producer Price Index for October 2026 on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET, three days after the CPI. The November PPI is Tuesday, Dec. 15, 2026, five days after that month's CPI on Thursday, Dec. 10.

## When is the BEA Personal Income and Outlays release?

The BEA release schedule, last modified Nov. 1, 2026, lists Personal Income and Outlays for October 2026 on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET, and for November 2026 on Wednesday, Dec. 23, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET. That release contains the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index. The BEA schedule does not name the PCE index itself.
