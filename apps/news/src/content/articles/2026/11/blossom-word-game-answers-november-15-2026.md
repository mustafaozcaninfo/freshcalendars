**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, puts G in the center, with A, B, E, I, M and N on the petals.** Beebom, Word.Tips and Connections Solver agree on the letters. Hints sit up top, and the words wait in a marked section at the bottom.

## Today's petals: G in the center

- **Center:** G (every word needs it)
- **Petals:** A, B, E, I, M, N

Three of the seven letters are vowel letters (A, E and I) and four are consonants (B, G, M and N). Because today's center is a consonant, the center-letter rule is worth a look on our [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works). Yesterday's flower is on the [Nov. 14 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-14-2026/).

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

1. **Count:** two pangrams are listed by both cross-check sources, Beebom and Word.Tips.
2. **Lengths:** one has 9 letters and the other has 7.
3. **Short one:** its seven letters are the seven tiles, each used once, and it ends in the same three letters as a very common word ending.
4. **Long one:** it repeats two letters, one vowel and one consonant, and G is not one of them.

## How the two pangrams fit together

A 7-letter pangram has no spare letters, so the short pangram is simply a way of ordering G, A, B, E, I, M and N. The 9-letter pangram adds two more tiles on top of those seven. Both sources list the same two, and the letter counts below come straight from the words.

| Pangram | Letters | A | B | E | G | I | M | N |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short one | 7 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Long one | 9 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |

E and N double, and the other five letters stay single. Take one E and one N out of the long word and the seven tiles left spell the short word once rearranged, since M and A swap places. The longest word on the sources' lists, at 11 letters, is a different kind of find: it skips A, so it never counts as a pangram, and it leans on the center letter more than either pangram does.

### Pangrams

Beebom and Word.Tips both list these two. Each uses A, B, E, G, I, M and N at least once.

- **Bemeaning** (9 letters)
- **Beaming** (7 letters)

Beaming uses the same letters as Bemeaning minus one E and one N, with the A moved ahead of the M. For the longest word, Word.Tips and Connections Solver list Embiggening at 11 letters, with G three times.
