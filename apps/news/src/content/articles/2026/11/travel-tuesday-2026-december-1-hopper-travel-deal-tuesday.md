**Travel Tuesday 2026 is Tuesday, Dec. 1, and Hopper says its Travel Deal Tuesday offers up to 50% off hotels in top domestic and international destinations that day, according to its Nov. 11, 2026 release.** Marriott Bonvoy and eDreams have also posted their own booking windows. The table lists each brand's dates, and a printable deadline sheet carries the same rows.

## When is Travel Tuesday 2026?

Dec. 1 is the Tuesday after Thanksgiving weekend. Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, per the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) federal holiday list, which puts Black Friday on Nov. 27 and Cyber Monday on Nov. 30. See [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) and [when is Cyber Monday](/when-is/cyber-monday/) for those dates; store-side sale times are in the [Cyber Monday 2026 guide](/news/cyber-monday-2026-date-sale-start-times-by-store/).

## What is Hopper's Travel Deal Tuesday?

Hopper says the event "returns December 1st for its tenth consecutive year" and promises "significant savings and exclusive deals on flights, hotels and more" on the Hopper app and Hopper.com. It also says "With domestic airfares up 40% year over year, Travel Deal Tuesday is a chance to stretch your travel budget further," a Hopper figure. The company gives no start hour or time zone for Dec. 1.

The organizer site says "dozens of participating destinations will offer deals 15-50% off on select hotels." Hopper's release names these partners so far:

- **Airlines:** Avianca, Condor Airlines, Gol, Royal Jordanian, Singapore Airlines and Scandinavian Airlines
- **Tourism boards:** Amelia Island Tourist Development Council, Tourism Australia and Visit Orlando

Hopper says it created the event in 2017. In 2025, its Nov. 24 release said the ninth Travel Deal Tuesday fell on Dec. 2 and that deals would start Nov. 25 and run through the day. A 2026 start date before Dec. 1 has not been announced.

## What are Marriott Bonvoy and eDreams offering?

Marriott's release says its Cyber Week Sale "runs from November 24 through Travel Tuesday, December 1, 2026." Marriott Bonvoy app users "can save 25% on stays from November 29, 2026, through May 2, 2027," and "members and nonmembers who book on Marriott.com can save 15%."

eDreams, an online travel agency, says on its newsroom page that "Travel Tuesday 2026 falls on Tuesday 1 December 2026," that app deals go live from Thursday, Nov. 19 and that "All deals will remain live until Tuesday 1 December 2026." It lists hotels up to 40% and car hire up to 20%. Its UK sister brand Opodo posts the same terms.

## Which travel brands have not posted Travel Tuesday offers?

As of Nov. 20, 2026, Southwest, Alaska, JetBlue, Frontier, Spirit, Allegiant, Delta and United had not posted 2026 Travel Tuesday offers on their own pages. The same goes for Hilton, InterContinental Hotels Group (IHG), Hyatt, Expedia and Amtrak. Cruise lines run their own Black Friday sales, with windows ending Dec. 7 for Princess and Cunard; those are in the [cruise Black Friday 2026 story](/news/cruise-black-friday-2026-princess-cunard-royal-caribbean-dates/).
