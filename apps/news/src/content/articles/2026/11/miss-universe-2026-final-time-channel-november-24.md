**The 75th Miss Universe final airs live Tuesday, Nov. 24, 2026 at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Telemundo, according to an NBCUniversal Newsgroup and Telemundo release dated Oct. 28, 2026.** The release says the broadcast airs "coast-to-coast on Telemundo in the United States, with a simultaneous livestream on Peacock." Reelz lists an English-language telecast at 8 p.m. ET on its own page.

## What time is Miss Universe 2026 in each time zone?

NBCUniversal gives "7pm ET/6pm CT/4pm PT," and Telemundo repeats the same times in its Nov. 6 announcement. The other rows are computed from 7 p.m. ET, which is UTC-5 on Nov. 24 because daylight saving time ended Nov. 1.

| Place | Start time | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern Time (ET) | 7 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |
| Central Time (CT) | 6 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 5 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |
| Pacific Time (PT) | 4 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |
| Atlantic Standard Time (AST), Puerto Rico | 8 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |
| Mexico City (UTC-6) | 6 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |
| London (GMT) | 12 a.m. (midnight) | Wed., Nov. 25 |
| Bangkok | 7 a.m. | Wed., Nov. 25 |
| Manila | 8 a.m. | Wed., Nov. 25 |

The Eastern, Central and Pacific times are NBCUniversal's. Mountain, Puerto Rico, Mexico City, London, Bangkok and Manila are computed.

## Where can you watch the Miss Universe 2026 final?

In the United States, Telemundo carries it live with a Peacock livestream. For Latin America, the release says the final airs live "on the Telemundo Internacional cable network" and "on the Universal+ streaming service." Jacky Bracamontes and Carlos Adyan are the lead hosts.

## Telemundo says 7 p.m. ET, Reelz lists 8 p.m. ET: which is it?

The two sources differ. Telemundo's Spanish-language broadcast starts at 7 p.m. ET, per NBCUniversal and Telemundo. [Reelz's Miss Universe page](https://www.reelz.com/show/miss-universe/) lists "75th Miss Universe Final Competition" on "Tue, Nov 24" at "8ET / 5PT · Live." Reelz has not said on that page whether its telecast is a different feed, so check your provider's guide for the channel you plan to use.

## Where is the 75th Miss Universe held, and are tickets still on sale?

Telemundo says only that the show airs live from Puerto Rico. A Philippine newsroom reported on July 23, 2026 that the competition is set for Nov. 24, and both it and the Puerto Rican newspaper El Nuevo Día name the Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot in San Juan as the venue. El Nuevo Día reported on Oct. 3, 2026 that tickets for the final were sold out ("la totalidad de los boletos para la gala final").

## How does the 2026 start time compare with last year's?

| | 2026 | 2025 (last year) |
|---|---|---|
| Edition | 75th | 74th |
| Date | Tue., Nov. 24 | Thu., Nov. 20 |
| Telemundo start | 7 p.m. ET (live broadcast) | Special at 7 p.m. ET, live pageant from 8 p.m. ET |
| Venue | Puerto Rico (Coliseo de Puerto Rico, per two newsrooms) | Impact Challenger Hall, Bangkok, Thailand |
| Streaming | Peacock livestream | Peacock and the Telemundo app |

In 2025, Fátima Bosch of Mexico was crowned. A viewer used to the 8 p.m. ET pageant start should note that Telemundo's 2026 broadcast starts at 7 p.m. ET.

## What has Miss Universe not announced for 2026?

The sources used here do not give start times for the preliminary competition or the national costume show, the number of contestants, or ticket prices. Telemundo's release names only Jacky Bracamontes and Carlos Adyan as lead hosts.
