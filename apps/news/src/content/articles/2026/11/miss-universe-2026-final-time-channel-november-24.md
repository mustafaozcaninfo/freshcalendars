**The 75th Miss Universe final airs live Tuesday, Nov. 24, 2026 in two United States broadcasts: Telemundo at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) in Spanish and Reelz at 8 p.m. ET in English.** Telemundo's live broadcast "airs coast-to-coast on Telemundo in the United States, with a simultaneous livestream on Peacock," per an NBCUniversal Newsgroup and Telemundo release dated Oct. 28, 2026. Reelz says it will air "the English language production of the historic 75th Miss Universe pageant" live on Tuesday, Nov. 24 "at 8pm ET/ 5pm PT," in its Nov. 4, 2026 release.

## Where can you watch the Miss Universe 2026 final?

There are two confirmed U.S. broadcasts on Nov. 24, each with its own language, channel and start time.

| Language | Channel | Streaming | Start (ET / PT) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | Telemundo | Peacock | 7 p.m. / 4 p.m. | NBCUniversal and Telemundo, Oct. 28, 2026 |
| English | Reelz | Reelz+ app, YouTube @Reelzchannel | 8 p.m. / 5 p.m. | Reelz, Nov. 4, 2026 |

Reelz says the pageant "can be viewed on cable, satellite and streaming services nationwide," as well as on the Reelz+ app and through "a live all access membership on YouTube @Reelzchannel." For Latin America, the Telemundo release says the final airs live "on the Telemundo Internacional cable network" and "on the Universal+ streaming service." Jacky Bracamontes and Carlos Adyan are the lead hosts of the Telemundo broadcast; the Reelz release names no hosts.

## What time is Miss Universe 2026 in each time zone?

The times below are for the Telemundo broadcast, which NBCUniversal and Telemundo give as "7pm ET/6pm CT/4pm PT." The Reelz English-language telecast starts one hour later, at 8 p.m. ET. The other rows are computed from 7 p.m. ET, which is UTC-5 on Nov. 24 because daylight saving time ended Nov. 1.

|---|---|---|
| Eastern Time (ET) | 7 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |

| Mexico City (UTC-6) | 6 p.m. | Tue., Nov. 24 |
| London (GMT) | 12 a.m. (midnight) | Wed., Nov. 25 |

| Manila | 8 a.m. | Wed., Nov. 25 |

The Eastern, Central and Pacific times are NBCUniversal's. Mountain, Puerto Rico, Mexico City, London, Bangkok and Manila are computed.

## Why do Telemundo and Reelz list different start times?

Telemundo's Spanish-language broadcast starts at 7 p.m. ET, per NBCUniversal and Telemundo, while Reelz's English-language telecast starts at 8 p.m. ET, per the Reelz release. Neither release explains the one-hour difference, so check your provider's guide for the feed and channel you plan to use.

## What airs on Reelz before the Nov. 24 final?

The Reelz release lists two earlier live nights from Puerto Rico. The National Costume Extravaganza airs Saturday, Nov. 21 at 6 p.m. ET (3 p.m. PT), and the Preliminary Competition airs Sunday, Nov. 22 at 6 p.m. ET (3 p.m. PT).

| Event | Date | Start (ET / PT) | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Costume Extravaganza | Sat., Nov. 21 | 6 p.m. / 3 p.m. | Reelz |
| Preliminary Competition | Sun., Nov. 22 | 6 p.m. / 3 p.m. | Reelz |
| 75th Miss Universe final (Spanish) | Tue., Nov. 24 | 7 p.m. / 4 p.m. | Telemundo, Peacock |
| 75th Miss Universe final (English) | Tue., Nov. 24 | 8 p.m. / 5 p.m. | Reelz |

## Where is the 75th Miss Universe held, and are tickets still on sale?

A Telemundo release of Nov. 21, 2025 says the 75th edition takes place in November 2026 "at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico," and the Nov. 4, 2026 Reelz release places the pageant in Puerto Rico. A Philippine newsroom reported on July 23, 2026 that the competition is set for Nov. 24, and both it and the Puerto Rican newspaper El Nuevo Día give the fuller name, the Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot in San Juan. El Nuevo Día reported on Oct. 3, 2026 that tickets for the final were sold out ("la totalidad de los boletos para la gala final").

## How does the 2026 start time compare with last year's?

| | 2026 | 2025 (last year) |
|---|---|---|
| Edition | 75th | 74th |
| Date | Tue., Nov. 24 | Thu., Nov. 20 |
| Telemundo start | 7 p.m. ET (live broadcast) | Special at 7 p.m. ET, live pageant from 8 p.m. ET |
| Venue | Coliseo de Puerto Rico (Telemundo release) | Impact Challenger Hall, Bangkok, Thailand |
| Streaming | Peacock (Telemundo); Reelz+ app (Reelz) | Peacock and the Telemundo app |

In 2025, Fátima Bosch of Mexico was crowned. A viewer used to the 8 p.m. ET pageant start should note that Telemundo's 2026 broadcast starts at 7 p.m. ET, while Reelz carries the English telecast at 8 p.m. ET.

## What has Miss Universe not announced for 2026?

The sources used here do not give the number of contestants or ticket prices. Telemundo's release names only Jacky Bracamontes and Carlos Adyan as lead hosts, and the Reelz release names no hosts for its telecast.
