**A classic Thanksgiving dinner for 10 costs $58.63 in 2026, or about $5.86 per person, up more than 6% from $55.18 in 2025, the American Farm Bureau Federation (AFBF) said on Nov. 18, 2026.** It is the first overall increase since 2022. The item-by-item table below puts the 2026 price next to 2025 with the dollar change, the regional prices follow, and a free printable shopping list leaves blank columns for what your own store charges. Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, Nov. 26; see [when is Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) for the date itself.

## How much did each Thanksgiving dinner item cost in 2026?

The AFBF survey prices 12 items. The 2026 prices and AFBF's percent changes come from its Nov. 18, 2026 release and Farm Bureau Intel article; the 2025 prices come from its Nov. 19, 2025 release. The dollar change is our subtraction.

| Item | 2026 | 2025 | Change in dollars (our math) | AFBF change from 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16-lb turkey | $23.68 | $21.50 | +$2.18 | +10.1% |
| 30-oz pumpkin pie mix | $4.24 | $4.16 | +$0.08 | +1.9% |
| 1 gal whole milk | $4.05 | $3.73 | +$0.32 | +8.6% |
| 1-lb veggie tray, carrots and celery | $1.31 | $1.36 | -$0.05 | -3.7% |
| Misc. ingredients to prepare the meal | $3.84 | $3.61 | +$0.23 | +6.4% |
| 1 dozen dinner rolls | $3.81 | $3.56 | +$0.25 | +7% |
| 2 frozen pie crusts | $3.28 | $3.37 | -$0.09 | -2.7% |
| 1 lb frozen peas | $1.99 | $2.03 | -$0.04 | -2% |
| 12-oz fresh cranberries | $2.48 | $2.28 | +$0.20 | +8.8% |
| Half pint whipping cream | $1.84 | $1.87 | -$0.03 | -1.6% |
| 3 lb sweet potatoes | $4.20 | $4.00 | +$0.20 | +5% |
| 14-oz cubed stuffing mix | $3.91 | $3.71 | +$0.20 | +5.4% |
| **Total for 10** | **$58.63** | **$55.18** | **+$3.45** | **More than 6% (our math: +6.3%)** |

Eight of the 12 items cost more than in 2025 and four cost less: the veggie tray, the pie crusts, the peas and the whipping cream. The turkey alone adds $2.18 of the $3.45 rise, or 63%. Milk added the next most, $0.32, and the four cheaper items subtract $0.21 between them.

## Why did the turkey price rise in 2026?

The AFBF put the 16-pound turkey at $23.68, or $1.48 a pound, up about 10% from last year, and said turkey had the largest increase of the survey. In 2025 the same bird was $21.50, or $1.34 a pound; the AFBF did not give a per-pound change, and our division makes it +10.4%.

Farm Bureau Intel gave two reasons. Strong demand and tight inventories have pushed up turkey prices in 2026, it said, and high fuel costs for harvesting and transportation have raised production costs. It also tied the higher whole milk price to increased demand for powdered protein, which is made from milk.

Faith Parum, Ph.D., the AFBF's deputy chief economist, said: "The overall increase in our annual survey reflects the rising costs of getting food from the farm to your dinner table." She also said high diesel prices and farm labor shortages strain the farm level of the supply chain. AFBF President Zippy Duvall said food prices are a real concern for families, farm families included, and urged Congress to "pass the farm bill and labor reform."

## Which region has the most expensive Thanksgiving dinner?

The AFBF listed 2026 prices for the classic dinner and the expanded menu by region. It gave 2025 classic prices for the same regions in last year's release; the change columns are our math.

| Region | Classic 2026 | Classic 2025 | Change | Expanded 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northeast | $61.57 | $60.82 | +$0.75 (+1.2%) | $86.62 |
| West | $58.74 | $61.75 | -$3.01 (-4.9%) | $81.75 |
| Midwest | $56.89 | $54.38 | +$2.51 (+4.6%) | $79.68 |
| South | $54.33 | $50.01 | +$4.32 (+8.6%) | $76.91 |

The Northeast is the most expensive region in 2026 and the South the cheapest, as the 2025 survey also showed for the South. The West was the most expensive region in 2025 at $61.75 and is the only region where the classic dinner fell, to $58.74.

The expanded menu adds boneless ham, russet potatoes and frozen green beans. Together they add $23.09 for a total of $81.72, the AFBF said. In 2025 the expanded menu was $77.09.

## Does the 2026 dinner cost a record?

No. The 2026 total is $5.42, or 8.5%, less than the 2022 record of $64.05, the AFBF said. Last year's survey, released Nov. 19, 2025, found $55.18 for 10 people, about $5.52 per person and down 5% from 2024, so 2026 reverses that decline.

## When do whole turkeys go on sale before Thanksgiving?

Volunteer shoppers checked prices the first week of November, in all 50 states and Puerto Rico. The AFBF noted that whole turkeys often go on sale right before Thanksgiving, and that U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) data showed lower feature prices in the second week of November. Your own total may therefore land below $58.63 if you buy a turkey on sale. Retailer meal bundles are listed in our [Thanksgiving meal deals comparison](/news/thanksgiving-meal-deals-2026-aldi-lidl-target-sams-club-prices-dates/), and a frozen bird needs days in the refrigerator, so check the [thaw dates](/news/when-to-thaw-thanksgiving-turkey-2026/) before you buy.
