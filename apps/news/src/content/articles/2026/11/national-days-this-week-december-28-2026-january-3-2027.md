**New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, is the one federal holiday in the week of Monday, Dec. 28 to Sunday, Jan. 3, and the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists it as "Friday, January 01 | New Year's Day".** The same week has a UK bank holiday on Monday, Dec. 28, Edinburgh's Hogmanay from Dec. 29, and perihelion, Earth's closest point to the Sun, at 9:33 p.m. EST on Saturday, Jan. 2, per the U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO). The planner for Dec. 28 to Jan. 3 sits below the two tables.

Each day in the tables has a body that sets it; commercial "national food days" have none and are left out.

## When is perihelion in January 2027?

The USNO seasons data lists Perihelion on Jan. 3, 2027 at 02:33 UT. In the U.S. that is still Saturday, Jan. 2 for every zone below.

| Zone | Local time, Saturday, Jan. 2 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (EST) | 9:33 p.m. |
| Central (CST) | 8:33 p.m. |
| Mountain (MST) | 7:33 p.m. |
| Pacific (PST) | 6:33 p.m. |

Universal Time (UT) is the reference time the USNO uses. For New Year's Eve skies, see our [moon phase and moonrise story](/news/new-years-eve-2026-moon-phase-moonrise-time-midnight-new-york/).

## Which New Year holidays apply in the U.S., UK and Canada?

OPM's federal holiday list reads "Friday, January 01 | New Year's Day" for 2027. Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026 is not on the 2026 federal list. Store and service hours for both days are in [What's open on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day](/news/whats-open-new-years-eve-new-years-day-2027/).

GOV.UK's bank holiday data lists "Boxing Day" on 2026-12-28 and "New Year's Day" on 2027-01-01 for England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Scotland also lists "2nd January" on 2027-01-04, the Monday after this week. The full lists are in [UK bank holidays 2027](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/).

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) public holidays page lists "Christmas Day – Friday, December 25, 2026" and "Boxing Day – Saturday, December 26, 2026" and has no 2027 dates yet. New Year's Day 2027 falls on a Friday. For federal public servants, Public Services and Procurement Canada says "If a holiday falls on your day of rest, the holiday is moved to your first scheduled working day following the day of rest." Its page lists New Year's Day and Boxing Day as designated paid holidays.

## When does Kwanzaa end in 2026-27?

The National Park Service's African Burial Ground National Monument says "Kwanzaa begins on December 26, with the principle of Umoja, which calls for unity", concluding "January 1, with the principle of Imani". The page was last dated May 1, 2019. Each day's principle is in our [Kwanzaa 2026 story](/news/kwanzaa-2026-dates-seven-principles-calendar-printable/).

## What does the Catholic calendar list for Dec. 28-Jan. 3?

The USCCB's 2027 liturgical calendar lists Monday, Dec. 28, 2026 as The Holy Innocents, Martyrs (feast), Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 as the Solemnity of Mary, the Holy Mother of God, marked "Holyday of Obligation", and Sunday, Jan. 3 as "USA: The Epiphany of the Lord". The Holy Family feast falls the Sunday before, on Dec. 27. [Holy days of obligation 2027](/news/holy-days-of-obligation-2027-us-dates/) has the full U.S. list.

## What is on in Edinburgh before New Year?

Edinburgh's Hogmanay site says "Celebrations kick off on 29 December 2026." It dates the Torchlight Procession to Dec. 29, the Night Afore Party to Dec. 30, and the Hogmanay Street Party and Hogmanay in the Gardens to Dec. 31. The [Edinburgh Christmas and Hogmanay story](/news/edinburghs-christmas-2026-dates-big-wheel-hogmanay-torchlight/) has the dates and tickets. For midnight in other places, see the [New Year's countdown by time zone](/news/new-years-countdown-2027-midnight-every-time-zone/). The Rose Parade also falls on Jan. 1; [its story](/news/rose-parade-2027-date-start-time-by-time-zone-route-floatfest/) has the start time by zone.
