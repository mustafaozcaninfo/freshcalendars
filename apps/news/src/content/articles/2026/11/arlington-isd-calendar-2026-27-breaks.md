**Arlington Independent School District (ISD) in Texas lists Nov. 23-27, Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, and March 15-19, 2027, as Student & Staff Holiday weeks on its 2026-2027 student academic calendars.** Students return Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027, and the last day of school is Wednesday, May 26, 2027. The board approved the dates on Jan. 22, 2026.

The district posts an elementary and a secondary PDF on its [District Events Calendar](https://www.aisd.net/district/about/calendars/) page. The next days off are the Thanksgiving week starting Monday, Nov. 23; the Nov. 2-3 days off have passed.

## What are Arlington ISD's breaks in 2026-27?

The district's own label for all three weeks is "Student & Staff Holiday". It does not print the words "spring break" or "winter break"; those are the common names, added here. Weekdays off and return days are our count from the district's dates. The calendar shows no closure on Nov. 30 or March 22.

| Week | District label | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving week | Student & Staff Holiday, Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter break | Student & Staff Holiday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, then Student Holiday Jan. 4-5 | 12 | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 |
| Spring break | Student & Staff Holiday, March 15-19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 22, 2027 |

## When do Arlington ISD students return after winter break?

On Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027, by the calendar grid. The date list prints no return day, but the grid marks Jan. 6 as the start of the second semester. Students have two student holidays first: Monday-Tuesday, Jan. 4-5. With the weekdays from Dec. 21 to Jan. 1, that is 12 weekdays without classes. Dec. 17-18 are early dismissal days before the break; the table below shows which schools they apply to.

## Every Arlington ISD day off and early dismissal

"Student & Staff Holiday" is the district's label for days when neither group is at school. "Student Holiday" is the label for days when students are off. Past dates are in the past tense.

Counting every weekday in that list from Sept. 7, 2026 on, students have 34 weekdays off, 32 if the two bad-weather makeup days are used. Plan the first break on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Do the Arlington ISD bad-weather makeup days mean school on March 26 or April 23?

Not by the calendar as printed. Both days are labeled "Student & Staff Holiday/Bad Weather Makeup Day", and the legend marks them "Inclement Weather Makeup Day (if needed)". They are days off unless the district needs them to make up a weather closure. The calendar does not say how the district decides.

## Are Arlington ISD early dismissal days different for elementary and secondary?

Yes. The two calendars match except for early dismissal days. Secondary means junior high (grades 7-8) and high school (grades 9-12).

| Early dismissal | Elementary (Pre-K-6) | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 17, 2026 | Regular day | Early Dismissal |
| Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | Early Dismissal | Early Dismissal |
| Tue., May 25, 2027 | Regular day | Early Dismissal |
| Wed., May 26, 2027 | Last Day of School/Early Dismissal | Last Day of School/Early Dismissal |

## How does Arlington ISD compare with Fort Worth and Dallas ISD?

All three districts list Nov. 23-27 and March 15-19. They differ on winter break and the last day. Fort Worth ISD's values come from its traditional calendar, in our [Fort Worth ISD story](/news/fort-worth-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/); Dallas ISD's from our [Dallas ISD story](/news/dallas-isd-calendar-2026-27-holidays-breaks/). Another north Texas district is in our [Lewisville ISD story](/news/lewisville-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/).

| | Arlington ISD | Fort Worth ISD | Dallas ISD |
|---|---|---|---|
| First day | Wed., Aug. 12, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 10, 2026 | Tue., Aug. 11, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving | Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Nov. 23-27, 2026 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, plus Jan. 4-5 student holidays | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027; Jan. 4 is a staff day | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Return day | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | Jan. 5 is a professional development day; the next grading period starts Jan. 6 |
| Spring break | March 15-19, 2027 | March 15-19, 2027 | March 15-19, 2027 |
| Last day | Wed., May 26, 2027 | Fri., May 21, 2027 | Wed., May 26, 2027 |

## When is the last day of school in Arlington ISD?

Wednesday, May 26, 2027, listed as "Last Day of School/Early Dismissal". Secondary schools also dismiss early the day before, Tuesday, May 25. The first day of school was Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026.
