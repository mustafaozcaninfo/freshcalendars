**BJ's Restaurant & Brewhouse began its all-new holiday menu nationwide on Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, with a reimagined Biscoff Brown Butter Pizookie, BJ's Restaurants, Inc. said in a Nov. 5 release on PR Newswire.** This is the restaurant chain, not BJ's Wholesale Club; for the club, see [is BJ's Wholesale open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/is-bjs-open-thanksgiving-2026-closed-november-26-free-turkey/). The menu is available for a limited time with no end date given. Gift card bonuses run Nov. 5 through Dec. 31, and the table below lists every tier with the value per dollar. The release lists no prices, which vary by restaurant.

## What is on the BJ's holiday menu in 2026?

The release calls the menu a "Brewhouse twist on steakhouse-inspired favorites." It lists these items:

- **Hawaiian Rib Eye:** a 16-ounce marinated ribeye with a Hawaiian glaze, a grilled pineapple ring and seasoning salt.
- **Truffle Mac & Cheese:** cavatappi in white truffle oil and white cheddar sauce, topped with Gruyère and Jack cheese and a Parmesan panko crust, served hot in a skillet.
- **Loaded Mashed Potatoes:** Jack and cheddar cheese, smoked bacon, green onions and sour cream.
- **Truffle Fries.**
- **Biscoff Brown Butter Pizookie:** a new warm brown butter cookie base with vanilla bean ice cream, a cookie butter drizzle, Biscoff crumbles and a whole Biscoff cookie.
- **Cocktails:** the Glittery Goose Sangria and the Caramel Macchiato Martini. The release gives no description of either.
- **27th Annual Grand Cru:** a limited-time Belgian-style holiday ale.

## BJ's gift card bonus tiers by date, 2026

The release says that from Nov. 5 through Dec. 31, guests can use these offers. The last column is our math: the bonus card divided by the amount bought.

| Channel | Buy | Get | Dates | Bonus per dollar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Online eGift cards | $50 | $10 bonus card | Nov. 5 to Dec. 31 | 20% |
| Online eGift cards | $100 | $20 bonus card and a 20% off Very Important Person (VIP) card | Nov. 5 to Dec. 31 | 20% |
| Online eGift cards, Cyber Weekend | $100 | $25 bonus card and a 20% off VIP card | Fri., Nov. 27 to Mon., Nov. 30 | 25% |
| In restaurant | $50 | $10 bonus card | Nov. 5 to Dec. 31 | 20% |
| In restaurant | $100 | $20 bonus card, a 20% off VIP card and two holiday pint glasses | Nov. 5 to Dec. 31 | 20% |

Source: BJ's Restaurants, Inc. release, Nov. 5, 2026. The release does not give the terms of the 20% off VIP card. The $50 offer still applies during Cyber Weekend. The release says offers are subject to terms, conditions and availability, and additional restrictions may apply.

Cyber Weekend adds $5 to the $100 eGift bonus, from $20 to $25. A $100 purchase then gives $125 to spend, against $120 on the other days.

## When can you use BJ's bonus cards?

The release says "Bonus cards and 20% off VIP cards are valid January 4-31, 2027." That is Monday, Jan. 4 to Sunday, Jan. 31, a 28-day window that opens four days after the Dec. 31 end of the offers. Mark it on a [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html), and the Cyber Weekend purchase days on a [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

The in-restaurant offers exclude Arizona and Texas, where offers differ. The release does not say what they are.

## How does the 2026 offer compare with 2025?

In 2025, BJ's said in a Nov. 12, 2025 release that its offers ran "Now through January 1, 2026," with a $10 bonus card on a $50 eGift card and a $25 bonus card on $100 during Cyber Weekend. The 2026 offers run from Nov. 5 and end Dec. 31, a day earlier than last year's Jan. 1 end.
