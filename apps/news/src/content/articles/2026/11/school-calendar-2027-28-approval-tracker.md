**As of Nov. 4, 2026, six large U.S. school districts have an approved 2027-28 calendar with a first day we could read, and the earliest is Monday, Aug. 2, 2027 in Hawaii, according to the Hawaii State Department of Education (HIDOE).** The latest of the six is Tuesday, Aug. 24, 2027 at Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (CMS), whose Board of Education approved its calendar on April 14, 2026. This is a tracker: we update it as boards vote and districts post.

## What the approved 2027-28 calendars say

Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools lists "Aug 24 First Day of School" and "June 7th Last Day of School" on a calendar the Board of Education approved April 14, 2026. Winter break is "Dec 20-31" and spring break "Apr 10-14". Our [CMS 2026-27 story](/news/charlotte-mecklenburg-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) covers the current year.

Hawaii's "2027-2028 OFFICIAL SCHOOL CALENDAR" reads "Approved May 15, 2025", with "August 2: Students' First Day" and "May 24: Last Day for Students & Second Semester Ends". Winter break is December 20-31 and spring break March 13-17. See our [Hawaii 2026-27 story](/news/hawaii-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) for this year.

Katy ISD's board release of Jan. 20, 2026 gives the first day as Aug. 11, 2027 and the last day as May 24, 2028, with fall break Oct. 8-12, 2027 and spring break March 13-17, 2028. Northside ISD's board approved the 2026-27 and 2027-28 calendars together, with first days of Aug. 10, 2026 and Aug. 9, 2027 and last days of May 26, 2027 and May 25, 2028.

Fairfax County Public Schools said in a bulletin sent July 13, 2026: "The 2027-28 school year will begin on August 23, 2027." The last day is June 14, 2028, with a two-week winter break and a one-week spring break. FFXnow reported July 15, 2026 that the board adopted the calendar July 9. Palm Beach County's PDF is stamped "School Board Approved 8/12/2026" and shows Tuesday, Aug. 10, 2027 as the first day. The School District of Lee County's 2027-28 PDF, marked as board approved 11/04/2025, starts students Tuesday, Aug. 10, 2027 and ends Tuesday, May 30, 2028 with an early dismissal, as our [Lee County story](/news/lee-county-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) notes.

## Approved calendars where we did not read the dates

Three Florida districts say a board approved 2027-28 calendars, but we did not confirm their dates. Polk County Public Schools lists "2027-2028 (Approved 2/24/2026)" on its calendars page; the dates sit in a PDF we did not check. Broward County Public Schools said Dec. 18, 2025 that "the School Board has approved two school year calendars"; the 2027-28 dates are not confirmed on the district site. Hillsborough County Public Schools has an article titled "School Board Approves 2027-2028 Student Academic Calendar"; we did not read the vote date or the dates.

## Which districts post 2027-28 calendars without a stated approval?

Chicago Public Schools' family calendar for SY27-28 (last update 3.5.26) says "Students begin classes on Monday, August 23, 2027 and end on Friday, June 9, 2028", with winter break December 20 to 31, 2027 and spring break March 20 to 24, 2028. It does not state an approval. Orange County Public Schools shows Tuesday, Aug. 10 as the first day and Wednesday, May 24 as the last, with winter break Dec. 20-31 and spring break March 13-17.

The Clark County School District PDF is marked "*Subject to change". It lists "Monday, August 9 - Classes Begin" and "No School December 20 - December 31", with classes resuming Jan. 4, 2028. It shows no last day or spring break. Columbus City Schools in Ohio, outside the 30 largest districts, posted a traditional calendar: "Aug. 19 First Day of School for Grades 1-12", "Dec. 20-31" winter break, "Apr. 14-21" spring break and "May 25 Last Day for Students".

## How the 2027-28 first days compare with 2026-27

Where our own 2026-27 stories give the first day, the 2027-28 start is one calendar day earlier and falls on the same weekday, with one exception. The dates come from our district stories and the documents above.

|---|---|---|---|
| Hawaii | Mon., Aug. 3, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 2, 2027 | One day earlier |
| Northside ISD | Mon., Aug. 10, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 9, 2027 | One day earlier |
| Clark County | Mon., Aug. 10, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 9, 2027 | One day earlier |
| Lee County | Tue., Aug. 11, 2026 | Tue., Aug. 10, 2027 | One day earlier |
| Orange County | Tue., Aug. 11, 2026 | Tue., Aug. 10, 2027 | One day earlier |

The weekday shift is a calendar fact: a date lands one weekday later each year. Our 2026-27 stories link from each district's name in the [kindergarten registration roundup](/news/kindergarten-registration-2027-28-dates-age-cutoffs-by-district/) and its related stories.

## Which districts have nothing posted for 2027-28?

On Nov. 4, 2026, the calendar pages of Houston Independent School District, Dallas Independent School District, the School District of Philadelphia, San Diego Unified, Cypress-Fairbanks ISD, Memphis-Shelby County Schools and the Wake County Public School System (WCPSS) showed nothing for 2027-28. We could not check NYC, Los Angeles Unified School District (LAUSD), Miami-Dade, Gwinnett, Montgomery County in Maryland, Prince George's, Duval, Austin, Denver, Cobb, Baltimore County or Fort Worth, because their sites refused automated access or could not be read. They are listed as not confirmed.
