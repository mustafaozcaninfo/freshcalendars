**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, puts E in the center, with C, D, I, R, T and V on the petals.** Beebom, Word.Tips and Connections Solver agree on the letters. Hints come first, and the words wait in a marked section at the bottom.

## Today's petals: E in the center

- **Center:** E (every word needs it)
- **Petals:** C, D, I, R, T, V

Only two of the seven letters are vowel letters (E and I), while five are consonants (C, D, R, T and V). The center is a vowel, which matters for how today's rule plays out; the [Blossom hub](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works) explains the center-letter rule. Yesterday's flower is on the [Nov. 15 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-15-2026/).

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- Hint 1: Beebom and Word.Tips list three pangrams, not one.
- Hint 2: Two have 9 letters and one has 7.
- Hint 3: The short one starts with V and uses every tile exactly once.
- Hint 4: Both long ones end in the same three letters and start with D.
- Hint 5: One long pangram repeats I and E; the other repeats only E.

## What separates the three pangrams

With seven tiles in play, a 7-letter pangram has no spare letters, so the short answer is one ordering of C, D, E, I, R, T and V. Each long pangram is that same set plus two extra tiles. The counts below were computed from the words themselves.

| Pangram | Letters | C | D | E | I | R | T | V |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short one | 7 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| First long one | 9 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Second long one | 9 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

C, D, R, T and V stay single in all three. Only E and I ever repeat. Set the two long words side by side and eight of their nine letters match: one holds an extra I where the other holds an extra E, and nothing else differs.

### Pangrams

Beebom and Word.Tips both list these three. Each uses C, D, E, I, R, T and V at least once.

- **Decretive** (9 letters)
- **Directive** (9 letters)
- **Verdict** (7 letters)

Verdict uses each letter once. Directive adds a second I and a second E to those seven, and Decretive adds two more E, which gives it three in all. Connections Solver's masked pangrams, which show only the first two letters and a count of the rest, fit the same three words.
