**Maggiano's Little Italy says its Thanksgiving 2026 carryout bundles serve 4 to 5 people and cost $210 for a cold-prepared bundle picked up Wednesday, Nov. 25, or $220 for a hot-prepared bundle picked up Thursday, Nov. 26.** Its Thanksgiving page reads "Our hot and cold prepared carryout bundles serve 4-5 people. Cold-prepared bundles are $210 and available for pick-up Wednesday, November 25th. Hot-prepared bundles are $220 and available for pick-up Thursday, November 26th."

Maggiano's, part of Brinker International, also asks guests to "Please place orders 24 hours prior to desired pick-up time" and says to "Call your nearest Maggiano's to order today." The page lists a new $50 sides-only bundle and invites Thanksgiving Day dine-in reservations, but it does not state dine-in hours or prices, how many the sides-only bundle serves, or pickup hours. Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026; the date is on our [Thanksgiving 2026 page](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

## Maggiano's Thanksgiving 2026 carryout bundle prices and pickup dates

| Item | What Maggiano's says |
|---|---|
| Cold-prepared bundle | "$210 and available for pick-up Wednesday, November 25th" |
| Hot-prepared bundle | "$220 and available for pick-up Thursday, November 26th" |
| Serves | "Our hot and cold prepared carryout bundles serve 4-5 people" |
| Order timing | "Please place orders 24 hours prior to desired pick-up time" |
| Sides-only bundle | New sides-only bundle, $50 |
| How to order | "Call your nearest Maggiano's to order today" |

The only difference the page gives between the two bundles is the temperature, the price and the pickup day: cold for Wednesday, hot for Thanksgiving Day itself.

## What is inside the Maggiano's Thanksgiving carryout bundle

Maggiano's lists one carryout bundle menu for both the cold and hot versions: Chef's Signature Caesar; Traditional Roasted Turkey with Gravy and Sage & Sausage Stuffing; Country-Style Smoked Ham with Whipped Sweet Potatoes; Spaghetti & Meatballs; Four-Cheese Ravioli; Garlic Mashed Potatoes; Cranberry Relish; Pumpkin Praline Cheesecake; Chocolate Layered Cake; and Freshly Baked Bread.

The page adds a new sides-only bundle for $50 that lists Sweet Potatoes, Mashed Potatoes, Creamed Corn, Stuffing, Cranberry and Gravy. It does not say how many people the sides-only bundle serves.

## Maggiano's Thanksgiving carryout bundle cost per person

Maggiano's prices the bundles for the group, not per plate. Dividing each price by the 4 to 5 people the page says a bundle serves gives the range below. The per-person figures are our arithmetic from the chain's prices, not Maggiano's own numbers.

| Bundle | Price | Serves | Per person |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold-prepared | $210 | 4 to 5 | $42.00 to $52.50 |
| Hot-prepared | $220 | 4 to 5 | $44.00 to $55.00 |
| Sides-only | $50 | Not given | Not given |

The hot bundle costs $10 more than the cold one for the same menu, which adds about $2 to $2.50 a person at the sizes the page gives.

## When to order your Maggiano's Thanksgiving bundle

Maggiano's says to place orders 24 hours before the pickup time you want, so the order-by day sits one day before each pickup day. The steps below count back from Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26.

| When | Step |
|---|---|
| Tue., Nov. 24 | Last day to order a $210 cold-prepared bundle for a Wed., Nov. 25 pickup under the 24-hour rule |
| Wed., Nov. 25 | Pick up the cold bundle; last day to order a $220 hot-prepared bundle for a Thu., Nov. 26 pickup |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Pick up the hot-prepared bundle on Thanksgiving Day, then reheat and serve |

Maggiano's lists a reheating-instructions link for the bundles. The page gives no pickup hours, so confirm the time when you order.

## Maggiano's Thanksgiving dine-in: what the page says

For guests who want to eat in the restaurant, Maggiano's writes: "It's never too early to reserve your seasonal gathering with us on Thanksgiving Day, complete with a menu featuring the best flavors fall has to offer. This Thanksgiving, treat your loved ones to Chef-curated dishes served in our signature Family Style portions by booking your meal with us early!" The page shows reservation and menu buttons.

The page does not list a dine-in price per adult or child, or Thanksgiving Day hours, so the reservation and menu links for your restaurant are where to confirm them.
