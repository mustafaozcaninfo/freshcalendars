**Pizza Hut's Triple Treat Box costs $21.99 in 2026 and is available from Nov. 16 through Dec. 28, according to Nation's Restaurant News (NRN) and WhatNow.** Both reported it on Nov. 17. Pizza Hut's own pages could not be reached when this story was written, so every 2026 fact here rests on those two newsrooms, which agree.

The box holds two medium, one-topping pizzas, breadsticks and cinnamon sticks, packaged together in a festive box, per NRN. WhatNow describes a stacked carry-out box. For other chains' holiday items, see the [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/) and the [Jimmy John's Christmas Vacation Feast](/news/jimmy-johns-christmas-vacation-feast-2026/).

## What does the Pizza Hut Triple Treat Box cost in 2026?

NRN reports $21.99 this year, "or about $5.50 per person." WhatNow says the box is available at participating locations "with prices starting at $21.99." That wording leaves room for local differences. Neither report gives another price.

## Pizza Hut Triple Treat Box 2026 vs 2025

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $19.99 (NRN's report of 2025) | $21.99 |
| Pizzas | Two medium, one-topping | Two medium, one-topping |
| Sides | Five breadsticks | Breadsticks |
| Dessert | Choice of dessert (Pizza Hut's 2025 release) | Cinnamon sticks |
| Start | Nov. 11, 2025 release says "available now" | Mon., Nov. 16 |
| End | No end date given | Mon., Dec. 28 |
| Days available | Not given | 43 |
| Per person, 4 people | About $5.00 (computed) | About $5.50 (NRN) |

The 2026 box costs $2.00 more than NRN's 2025 figure. Pizza Hut's 2025 release lists five breadsticks and a choice of dessert; the 2026 reports name breadsticks and cinnamon sticks without a breadstick count. The per-pizza figures divide the box price by two pizzas and count the sides as free, so they compare years rather than price a pizza on its own.

## Pizza Hut holiday box dates to mark

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| Monday, Nov. 16 | Box becomes available at participating locations |
| Thursday, Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving |
| Friday, Dec. 25 | Christmas Day |
| Monday, Dec. 28 | Last day of the box |

From Nov. 16 through Dec. 28 is 43 days with both ends counted. The box is still on sale after Christmas, through the Monday after. Check the date against a [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html) or a [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## What the reports do not say about the Triple Treat Box

Neither NRN nor WhatNow states whether the box is carryout only or also delivery or app orders. Neither gives delivery fees, Hut Rewards points or online-only terms. Pizza Hut's 2025 release said the box was available "for a limited time only at participating Pizza Hut locations nationwide," and gave no end date that year.
