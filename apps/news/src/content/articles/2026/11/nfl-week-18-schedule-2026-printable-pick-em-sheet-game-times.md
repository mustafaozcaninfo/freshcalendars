**The National Football League (NFL) lists 16 Week 18 games for Saturday, Jan. 9, and Sunday, Jan. 10, 2027, and NFL.com shows every kickoff as to be determined (TBD).** The matchups are set; the days, times and networks are not. A one-page pick'em sheet below leaves a day and time line for each game to fill in later. Times are Eastern Time (ET), and NFL.com's Week 18 page was checked on Nov. 3, 2026.

## What time slots does the NFL use in Week 18?

NFL.com's Week 18 page says: "In Week 18, three games will be played on Saturday (1:00 PM ET on Netflix, 4:30 PM ET and 8:00 PM ET on ESPN/ABC) with the remainder to be played on Sunday afternoon (1:00 PM ET and 4:25 PM ET), or Sunday night (8:20 PM ET)." The NFL has not said which games fill which slot, which game is Sunday night, or any per-game kickoff time or network beyond the Saturday ones above.

## When will the Week 18 kickoff times be announced?

After Week 17. NFL.com says: "Specific dates, start times, and networks for Week 18 matchups will be determined and announced following the conclusion of Week 17." The league's [flexible scheduling procedures](https://www.nfl.com/legal/flexible-scheduling-procedures) agree: "The schedule for Week 18 will be announced at the conclusion of Week 17," and add that "the scheduling of the Saturday, Sunday afternoon, and the Sunday night games is not assigned." Week 17 ends with Monday night's game on Jan. 4, 2027, so expect the slots after that. Check NFL.com then, because nothing in the Week 18 table above is final.

## How do you use the NFL Week 18 pick'em sheet?

The one-page sheet lists all 16 games in NFL.com's order, each with a blank "Day/time" line, a blank for your pick and one for the final score. Because the slots are not assigned yet, nothing is pre-printed: fill in each day and time once the league announces them. The tiebreaker line asks for the total points in the Sunday night game, which NFL.com's framework places at 8:20 p.m. ET (the matchup is not announced), and the foot of the sheet lists the slots.
