**Bodhi Day 2026 falls on Tuesday, Dec. 8, according to the Honpa Hongwanji Mission of Hawaii, which says Mahayana Buddhist tradition holds that Shakyamuni Buddha attained awakening at age 35 under a Bodhi tree "on December 8th."** The Mission calls Bodhi Day "the date of the attainment of perfect awakening by Shakyamuni Buddha." Dec. 8, 2026 is a Tuesday; we computed the weekday. Services and retreats cluster around it: Honolulu on Sunday, Dec. 6, San Francisco Zen Center retreats from Nov. 28, and a Toronto service on Dec. 20.

Two of the services listed fall on Sundays: Honolulu on Dec. 6 and Toronto on Dec. 20. The Dec. 8 holiday itself is a Tuesday.

## What is the Honolulu Bodhi Day service?

The Hawaii Buddhist Council, which the Mission describes as "comprised of seven Buddhist denominations in Hawaii," sponsors the service at the Hawaii Betsuin, 1727 Pali Highway, Honolulu. It runs Sunday, Dec. 6, from 9:30 to 10:30 a.m. Hawaii time, or 2:30 to 3:30 p.m. ET. The event page asks visitors to enter the parking lot from Lusitana St.

## What is Rohatsu Sesshin at San Francisco Zen Center?

San Francisco Zen Center (SFZC) says Rohatsu Sesshin "commemorates Buddha's enlightenment and closes the Fall Practice Period." It runs two in-person retreats, and sesshin is a Soto Zen meditation retreat with formal zendo meals, Dharma talks, sitting and walking meditation, and private interviews with senior teachers, SFZC says.

- **City Center, San Francisco:** Nov. 28 at 7 p.m. to Dec. 5 at 6 p.m. Pacific. SFZC's page said to register by Friday, Nov. 13, so registration is closed. A shorter online version, the Three-Day Rohatsu Sesshin, Online 12/3-12/5, has its own page.
- **Green Gulch Farm:** Sunday, Dec. 6 at 3 p.m. to Sunday, Dec. 13 at 2 p.m. Pacific, with an orientation at 3 p.m. on Dec. 6. SFZC asks people to "apply by Friday, November 27," and says the in-person sesshin is for people with experience in formal Zen practice.

## Is there a Bodhi Day service in Toronto?

Toronto Buddhist Church lists a "Bodhi Day Service (Jodo-e) – In Person and Zoom – English" on Sunday, Dec. 20, 2026, from 11 a.m. to noon ET. People who want to join virtually should email the church office at tbc@tbc.on.ca for the Zoom link. The church's page does not give a reason for the Dec. 20 date.
