**The 2027 International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF) World Junior Championship (WJC) runs from Saturday, Dec. 26, 2026 to Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027 in Edmonton and Red Deer, Alberta, and Canada opens against Switzerland at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on the first night, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) site NHL.com and Daily Faceoff.** Both published the schedule on Sept. 22, 2026 and agree on every game and time. The IIHF and Hockey Canada pages blocked automated access, so we cite those two outlets. Mountain Time (MT), the Alberta clock, is two hours behind ET.

## What are the knockout dates and times?

NHL.com says the top four teams in each group advance to the quarterfinals. It lists the Saturday, Jan. 2 quarterfinals as Quarterfinals 1 and 3 in Red Deer and Quarterfinals 2 and 4 at Rogers Place; Daily Faceoff does not give those venues. The matchups are not set until the round robin ends. Daily Faceoff names the Jan. 5 games the Bronze Medal Game and the Gold Medal Game, and NHL.com calls the last one the championship game.

## When does Canada play at the 2027 World Juniors?

All four round-robin games for Canada are at Rogers Place, at 7 p.m. ET, and the last one is a New Year's Eve game. Canada's opener falls on Boxing Day, which our [Boxing Day 2026 story](/news/boxing-day-2026-canada-saturday-december-26-day-off-monday-december-28/) covers for readers planning the long weekend. For another hockey date on Dec. 31, see our [NHL Winter Classic 2027 story](/news/nhl-winter-classic-2027-date-time-avalanche-mammoth-salt-lake-city/).

| Date | Game | Place | ET | MT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sat., Dec. 26 | Canada vs Switzerland | Edmonton | 7:00 p.m. | 5:00 p.m. |
| Mon., Dec. 28 | Canada vs Latvia | Edmonton | 7:00 p.m. | 5:00 p.m. |
| Tue., Dec. 29 | Norway vs Canada | Edmonton | 7:00 p.m. | 5:00 p.m. |

## Where can you watch the World Juniors 2027?

NHL.com lists NHL Network in the United States, and TSN and Réseau des sports (RDS) in Canada. Check the listings nearer the date for the channel of each game.

## What do World Juniors 2027 tickets cost?

Daily Hive reported on Sept. 22, 2026 that single-game tickets start at $40. It listed full-event packages at $1,107.60 to $2,707.60 for Edmonton (16 games) and $495 to $745 for Red Deer (14 games). We could not confirm what single-game tickets are on sale today or when more go on sale, so check Hockey Canada for current availability.

## Who won the last World Juniors?

Sweden beat Czechia 4-2 in the 2026 final on Jan. 5, 2026 in St. Paul, Minnesota, The Hockey News reported on Jan. 6, 2026. It was the last tournament before Edmonton and Red Deer.
