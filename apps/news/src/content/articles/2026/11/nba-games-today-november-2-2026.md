**There were 15 National Basketball Association (NBA) games on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, with the first tip-off at 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the national TV game, Thunder at Cavaliers, on Peacock and NBC Sports Network, per the NBA.com games page.** The Thunder won it 117-112. The last three games started at 10:00 p.m. ET. The other 14 games showed NBA League Pass.

NBA.com's games page listed the matchups, times and TV on Oct. 31, 2026, and the final scores below come from the same page, re-read on Nov. 14. The home team, away team and arena for each game come from the [CBS Sports schedule](https://www.cbssports.com/nba/schedule/20261102/), which carries the same 15 matchups.

## How the night unfolds from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. ET

The games fell into six start times: one game at 7:00 p.m. ET, five at 7:30 p.m., three at 8:00 p.m., two at 8:30 p.m., one at 9:00 p.m. and three at 10:00 p.m. ET.

The three 10:00 p.m. ET games, Raptors at Clippers, Suns at Warriors and Heat at Lakers, were all in California, so they started at 7:00 p.m. PT.

## Where were the NBA games on Nov. 2 shown?

NBA.com listed Thunder at Cavaliers on Peacock and NBC Sports Network, and NBA League Pass for the other 14 games. It listed no other channel.

## Is Monday part of the NBA Cup?

No. NBA.com said Group Play for the Emirates NBA Cup 2026 has 60 games from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, on Oct. 30, Nov. 6, Nov. 13, Nov. 20, Nov. 24, Nov. 25 and Nov. 27. Nov. 2 was not on that list, so Monday's games were regular-season games only. The next Cup night was Friday, Nov. 6.
