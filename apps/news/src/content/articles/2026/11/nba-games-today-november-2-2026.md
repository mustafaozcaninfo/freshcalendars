## How the night unfolds from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. ET

The listings group into six start times: one game at 7:00 p.m. ET, five at 7:30 p.m., three at 8:00 p.m., two at 8:30 p.m., one at 9:00 p.m. and three at 10:00 p.m. ET.

The three 10:00 p.m. ET games, Raptors at Clippers, Suns at Warriors and Heat at Lakers, are all in California, so they start at 7:00 p.m. PT. If you are on the East Coast and want to see all three tip off, that is the final hour of the slate.

## Where to watch the NBA games tonight

NBA.com lists Thunder at Cavaliers on Peacock and NBC Sports Network, and shows NBA League Pass for the other 14 games. It does not list a channel for any other game. Free streams found on social media are not licensed. Stick to the league, the teams' partners or your TV provider.

## Is Monday part of the NBA Cup?

No. NBA.com says Group Play for the Emirates NBA Cup 2026 has 60 games from Oct. 30 to Nov. 27, on Oct. 30, Nov. 6, Nov. 13, Nov. 20, Nov. 24, Nov. 25 and Nov. 27. Nov. 2 is not on that list, so Monday's games are regular-season games only. The next Cup night is Friday, Nov. 6.
