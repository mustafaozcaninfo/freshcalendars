**Aldi's 2026 Advent calendars arrive in its stores on Wednesday, Nov. 4, while supplies last: more than 25 calendars, with prices starting at $1.99, according to Aldi's Oct. 30, 2026 newsroom release.** The same week has an Aldi Finds window that opened Nov. 4, a holiday decorating contest that takes entries from Nov. 9, and Thanksgiving meal prices that run through Nov. 25. This story puts the dates, prices and rules from Aldi's own pages in one place, adds our own per-item math for the calendars and the drink and dessert pairings, and ends with what Aldi has not said yet. A one-page [printable of the November dates](#aldi-holiday-dates-printable) is at the bottom.

Aldi says the 2026 assortment adds categories such as beauty, baking, stationery, magnetic building toys and Aldi-branded merchandise. The company also says sales of select Advent calendars rose nearly 20% last year. In the release, Aldi U.S. Director of Communications Bridget Kozlowski asks: "Who says the best part of the holidays has to take place only one day?"

The Hot Sauce calendar is described by 25 bottles, one more than the 24 days of the baking and sensory calendars.

## How Aldi's Wine and Cheese Advent calendars pair

Aldi built the cheese calendar around the wine one. Its release says the Emporium Selection Advent Cheese Calendar, $16.99, is designed to pair with the Wine Advent Calendar, $59.99, with each day's wine matched to a cheese. The wine calendar is refreshed for 2026 with new wines, including some from Greece and France. Together the two cost $76.98 by our math, before tax.

Two limits apply. First, the release does not say how many wines or cheeses each calendar holds, so we cannot give a per-bottle or per-day price. Second, Aldi's winter seasonal post says holiday alcohol is sold at select Aldi locations, and that online alcohol delivery is offered where state and local laws permit. The Advent release does not say whether every store carries the Wine Advent Calendar. If you want the pair, check your store's page or call ahead before you plan around it.

The Barissimo Coffee and Cocoa Advent Calendar, $16.99, is the other food and drink calendar in the named group, with a new cocoa variety and updated coffee flavors. The Choceur Kids Advent Calendar, $1.99, holds chocolates, and the Pure Being Dog Advent Calendar, $7.99, holds toys and treats for dogs.

## Aldi Deck-Out holiday decorating contest: dates, prizes and rules

Aldi announced the Deck-Out holiday decorating challenge on Nov. 2, 2026. Entrants submit photos of a holiday display at ALDIDeckOut.com, with up to 100 words of description. The entry window is "beginning Nov. 9, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. ET through Nov. 22, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET," in Aldi's words. By our conversion, 10:00 a.m. ET on Nov. 9 is 9:00 a.m. Central Time (CT), 8:00 a.m. Mountain Time (MT) and 7:00 a.m. Pacific Time (PT); 11:59 p.m. ET on Nov. 22 is 8:59 p.m. PT.

| Prize | How many | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Grand prize | 1 | $10,000 in Aldi gift cards, a custom Deck-Out trophy and a holiday dinner shopping list the winner curates, delivered |
| Honorable mentions | Up to 10 | $1,000 in Aldi gift cards each and a holiday dinner spread |
| Every winner | All | A spread of Aldi Winter Seasonal favorites |

The entry rules, per Aldi's release:

- Entrants must be legal residents, 18 or older, of the contiguous 48 states or the District of Columbia.
- Residents of Utah, Colorado, Montana, Wyoming, Idaho, New Mexico, Oregon and Washington are excluded.
- No purchase is necessary, and Aldi products are not required in the display.
- The judges are the Aldi Quarter Club, a group of 25 Aldi "Super Fans."

Aldi had not announced a date for naming the winners as of Nov. 4. The entry window closes on a Sunday, Nov. 22, by our calendar check, which is also the weekend before Thanksgiving week. Read the official rules at ALDIDeckOut.com before you enter, because this story covers only what Aldi's release states.

## Aldi holiday closures: Thanksgiving and Christmas Day

Aldi's store information page says its stores close on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, New Year's Day and Easter Sunday. Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, Nov. 26, so the last full shopping day before it is Wednesday, Nov. 25, the same day Aldi's Thanksgiving meal prices end. The page does not give hours for Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve in the pages we read, and we do not have them; check your own store's hours. ## Shopping Aldi's Wednesday drops: our tips

The advice in this section is ours, built on what Aldi says about timing. It is not an Aldi recommendation.

1. **Check Wednesday.** Aldi's Finds weeks and the Advent calendar arrival both start on Wednesdays, and Aldi says Advent calendars are available "while supplies last." If one calendar is the reason for the trip, going earlier in the week gives you more days to find it than waiting for the last weekend of the month.
2. **Search the lineup before you drive.** The full lineup is on Aldi's Advent calendar web page. The release names ten of more than 25 calendars, so look up the rest there.
3. **Check alcohol separately.** Aldi says holiday alcohol is at select locations, so confirm that your store carries the Wine Advent Calendar and the holiday wines before you plan the pairing.
4. **Write the Thanksgiving dates down.** The turkey is available from Nov. 4 and the pie mix from Nov. 11, and the price window ends Nov. 25. The printable below lists each date.
5. **Put the Deck-Out dates on the calendar.** Entries open at 10:00 a.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 9 and close at 11:59 p.m. ET on Sunday, Nov. 22.
6. **Plan for the Thursday closure.** Aldi stores close on Thanksgiving Day, so any ingredient you are missing needs to be bought by Wednesday, Nov. 25.

For other retailers' holiday sale dates this week, see our [holiday deals this week, Nov. 2-8, 2026](/news/holiday-deals-this-week-november-2-8-2026-target-best-buy-starbucks/) roundup.

- **The other 15 or more Advent calendars.** The release says the assortment has over 25 and names ten; Aldi points to its Advent calendar page for the rest.
- **Products and prices for Aldi Finds.** Neither the current week (Nov. 4-10) nor the upcoming week (Nov. 11-17) lists any on Aldi's pages as of Nov. 4.
- **Finds weeks after Nov. 17.** The Nov. 18-24 and Nov. 25 to Dec. 1 weeks are not posted.
- **A holiday price-cut program.** Aldi's Sept. 23 release said fall price reductions, worth an estimated $86 million in savings, "will be in effect from Sept. 23 until at least Nov. 3." We have found no separate holiday price-cut announcement as of Nov. 4.
- **Deck-Out winner dates.** The release gives entry dates only.
- **Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve store hours.** Not confirmed from Aldi.
- **Prices for the turkey, cranberries, sweet potatoes and pie mix.** The Thanksgiving meal page we read does not list them.
