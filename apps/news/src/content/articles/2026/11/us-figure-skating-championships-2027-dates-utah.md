**The 2027 U.S. Figure Skating Championships run Tuesday, Jan. 5 to Sunday, Jan. 10, 2027 in Utah, with practices and competition at the Maverik Center, according to U.S. Figure Skating.** NBC Sports lists NBC, Peacock and NBCSN for live coverage on Jan. 4-10. The event-by-event schedule and start times are not yet announced.

U.S. Figure Skating's own watch schedule lists the event in "Salt Lake City, Utah". Its March 19, 2026 release says the championships "return to Utah for the first time since 1999" and are "held in partnership with the Utah Sports Commission".

## What we know and what is not yet announced

| Question | Answer as of Nov. 4, 2026 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Dates | Tue., Jan. 5 to Sun., Jan. 10, 2027 | U.S. Figure Skating |
| Venue | Maverik Center, Utah (Salt Lake City, Utah per U.S. Figure Skating) | U.S. Figure Skating |
| TV and streaming | NBC, Peacock, NBCSN, Jan. 4-10 | NBC Sports |
| Event-by-event schedule | Not yet announced | none posted |
| Start times | Not yet announced | none posted |
| Channel for each event | Not yet announced | none posted |
| Team selection for 2027 Worlds | No official statement found | none |

NBC Sports adds "All times Eastern, check local listings, subject to change." Its coverage window starts Jan. 4, a day before the Jan. 5 start listed by U.S. Figure Skating, and the release does not explain the difference.

## Day by day: Jan. 4 to Jan. 10, 2027

| Date | What is confirmed |
|---|---|
| Mon., Jan. 4 | NBC Sports coverage window begins |
| Tue., Jan. 5 | Championships begin |
| Wed., Jan. 6 to Sat., Jan. 9 | Event order not yet announced |
| Sun., Jan. 10 | Last day of the championships |

For a sense of the shape, the 2026 championships in St. Louis had junior and novice events on Jan. 5-6 at a separate rink and the championship events on Jan. 7-10 at Enterprise Center, per a U.S. Figure Skating release of Jan. 2, 2026. That release put the women's free skate on Friday, Jan. 9, 2026, and the final group of the free dance and the men's free skate on Saturday, Jan. 10, 2026. That was 2026; the 2027 order has not been posted.

## What time will it be in Utah, and what time on the East Coast?

No start time is posted yet. Utah is in the Mountain Time (MT) zone, which is 2 hours behind Eastern Time (ET) in January. A session at 7 p.m. MT, for example, would be 9 p.m. ET. That is an illustration of the conversion, not a scheduled time. NBC Sports says its times are Eastern.

## How to watch the 2027 U.S. Figure Skating Championships

NBC Sports' release of Sept. 17, 2026 says "On Jan. 4-10, NBC Sports presents live coverage of the 2027 Prevagen U.S. Figure Skating Championships," and its table lists NBC, Peacock and NBCSN. The headline of that release says Peacock streams every event live. Which sessions air on which channel is not yet announced. For comparison, the 2026 championships were televised on USA and NBC, per U.S. Figure Skating; USA Network is not on NBC's 2027 list.

## 2027 U.S. Figure Skating Championships tickets

U.S. Figure Skating put all-session tickets on sale at 12 p.m. ET on Thursday, March 26, 2026. They cover "all championship-level competition and practices at the Maverik Center". Prices include taxes and fees.

| Section | All-session price |
|---|---|
| P1A | $1,500 |
| P1B | $1,000 |
| P2 | $600 |
| P3 | $300 |

U.S. Figure Skating said juvenile-junior tickets would be available in the fall; we could not confirm their status.

## After Utah: the road to Worlds

The International Skating Union (ISU) says the 2027 World Championships are in Tampere, Finland, from March 17 to 21, 2027. U.S. Figure Skating's watch schedule lists Four Continents for Feb. 11-14, 2027 in Astana and the 2027 U.S. Synchronized Skating Championships in Henderson, Nevada, Feb. 22-27, 2027. How the U.S. teams for those events will be chosen is not stated in the sources we checked.
