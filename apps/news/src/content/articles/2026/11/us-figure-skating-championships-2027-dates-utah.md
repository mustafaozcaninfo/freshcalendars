**The 2027 U.S. Figure Skating Championships are listed for Tuesday, Jan. 5 to Sunday, Jan. 10, 2027 in Utah, with championship competition Thursday, Jan. 7 to Sunday, Jan. 10 at the Maverik Center, according to U.S. Figure Skating.** Championship official practices are Wednesday, Jan. 6. NBC Sports lists NBC, Peacock and NBCSN for live coverage on Jan. 4-10. Which session airs on which channel is not yet announced.

U.S. Figure Skating's own watch schedule lists the event in "Salt Lake City, Utah". Its March 19, 2026 release says the championships "return to Utah for the first time since 1999" and are "held in partnership with the Utah Sports Commission".

NBC Sports adds "All times Eastern, check local listings, subject to change." Its coverage window starts Jan. 4, a day before the Jan. 5 start listed by U.S. Figure Skating, and the release does not explain the difference.

## Session schedule: Jan. 6 to Jan. 10, 2027

U.S. Figure Skating's release of Oct. 1, 2026 lists these sessions. It does not say which time zone the times use.

| Date | Session | Time | Event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wed., Jan. 6 | Practices | Not listed | Championship official practices |
| Thu., Jan. 7 | 1 | 12:55 p.m. | Championship pairs short program |
| Thu., Jan. 7 | 2 | 3:05 p.m. | Championship rhythm dance |
| Thu., Jan. 7 | 3 | 7:10 p.m. | Championship women's short program |

| Fri., Jan. 8 | 5 (medal event) | 6:15 p.m. | Men's short program (Groups 2 and 3) and women's free skate (Groups 2 and 3) |
| Sat., Jan. 9 | 6 (medal event) | 2 p.m. | Championship free dance |
| Sat., Jan. 9 | 7 (medal event) | 5:10 p.m. | Championship pairs free skate |
| Sun., Jan. 10 | 8 (medal event) | 12:45 p.m. | Championship men's free skate |

The release does not say what is scheduled on Jan. 4 or Jan. 5. For comparison, the 2026 championships in St. Louis had junior and novice events on Jan. 5-6 at a separate rink and the championship events on Jan. 7-10 at Enterprise Center, per a U.S. Figure Skating release of Jan. 2, 2026.

## What time will it be in Utah, and what time on the East Coast?

Utah is in the Mountain Time (MT) zone, which is 2 hours behind Eastern Time (ET) in January. The Oct. 1 release gives the times without naming a zone, so check the zone on the tickets or the NBC listings before you plan around a session. If the times are Utah local time, 12:55 p.m. on Jan. 7 would be 2:55 p.m. ET. That is our conversion, not a listed time. NBC Sports says its own times are Eastern.

## How to watch the 2027 U.S. Figure Skating Championships

NBC Sports' release of Sept. 17, 2026 says "On Jan. 4-10, NBC Sports presents live coverage of the 2027 Prevagen U.S. Figure Skating Championships," and its table lists NBC, Peacock and NBCSN. The headline of that release says Peacock streams every event live. Which sessions air on which channel is not yet announced. For comparison, the 2026 championships were televised on USA and NBC, per U.S. Figure Skating; USA Network is not on NBC's 2027 list.

## 2027 U.S. Figure Skating Championships tickets

U.S. Figure Skating put all-session tickets on sale at 12 p.m. ET on Thursday, March 26, 2026. They cover "all championship-level competition and practices at the Maverik Center". Prices include taxes and fees.

| Section | All-session price |
|---|---|
| P1A | $1,500 |
| P1B | $1,000 |
| P2 | $600 |
| P3 | $300 |

Single-session tickets for championship-level events went on sale Oct. 1, 2026, for the eight sessions above plus the Jan. 6 official practices. Prices vary by session and are on U.S. Figure Skating's ticket page, which we did not copy here. Tickets for junior through juvenile events are to go on sale in late November, according to the Oct. 1 release.

## After Utah: the road to Worlds

The International Skating Union (ISU) says the 2027 World Championships are in Tampere, Finland, from March 17 to 21, 2027. U.S. Figure Skating's watch schedule lists Four Continents for Feb. 11-14, 2027 in Astana and the 2027 U.S. Synchronized Skating Championships in Henderson, Nevada, Feb. 22-27, 2027. How the U.S. teams for those events will be chosen is not given in the sources we checked.
