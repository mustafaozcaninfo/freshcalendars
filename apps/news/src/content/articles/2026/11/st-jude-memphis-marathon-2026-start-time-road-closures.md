The St. Jude Memphis Marathon and half marathon start at 8 a.m. Central Time (CT), which is 9 a.m. ET, on Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, according to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital's schedule of events. Race day is 14 days after Nov. 21, 2026. Both distances start at B.B. King Blvd. and Beale St. The 5K and 10K start an hour earlier, at 7 a.m. CT.

St. Jude says all in-person distances are officially sold out, so the details below are for runners already entered, volunteers and spectators. Times for the course closures are estimates that St. Jude says are "subject to change."

## What are the St. Jude Marathon start times by time zone?

St. Jude lists times in Central Time only. The other columns are computed from them.

| Start, Sat., Dec. 5 | Where | Central | Eastern | Mountain | Pacific |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5K and 10K wheelchair | B.B. King Blvd. and Madison Ave. | 6:55 a.m. | 7:55 a.m. | 5:55 a.m. | 4:55 a.m. |
| 5K and 10K | B.B. King Blvd. and Madison Ave. | 7 a.m. | 8 a.m. | 6 a.m. | 5 a.m. |
| Marathon and half marathon wheelchair | B.B. King Blvd. and Beale St. | 7:55 a.m. | 8:55 a.m. | 6:55 a.m. | 5:55 a.m. |
| Marathon and half marathon | B.B. King Blvd. and Beale St. | 8 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 7 a.m. | 6 a.m. |
| Kids' Fun Run | B.B. King Blvd. and Madison Ave. | 1 p.m. | 2 p.m. | noon | 11 a.m. |

The Kids' Fun Run is for children ages 12 and under who are accompanied by an adult. Corrals open at 12:30 p.m. CT. The race page does not confirm a distance.

|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 3, noon to 7 p.m. | Expo and packet pickup | Renasant Convention Center |
| Fri., Dec. 4, noon to 8 p.m. | Expo and packet pickup | Renasant Convention Center |
| Fri., Dec. 4, 6 a.m. | Union Avenue closes completely | Union Ave. |

| Sat., Dec. 5, by 6 a.m. | Be downtown and parked | Downtown Memphis |

Race packets are picked up at the expo. The schedule page puts gear check at Union Ave. and 4th St., while the race-week page says it is "located near the finish line on Hernando St." Check the location in the race-week email before race morning.

## Which Memphis streets close for the St. Jude Marathon?

St. Jude's race-day guide says road closures run Friday, Dec. 4 and Saturday, Dec. 5. Union Avenue closes completely from 6 a.m. Friday to 5 p.m. Saturday, a window of 35 hours (our arithmetic).

On Saturday, closures start as early as 12 a.m., on Union Ave. and B.B. King Blvd., and the last roads, on North Pkwy., reopen by about 3:30 p.m. St. Jude says each road reopens once the last runner has passed. The guide names these course streets:

- Union Ave., Danny Thomas Blvd., B.B. King Blvd. and A.W. Willis Ave.
- Front St., Beale St., North Pkwy. and Poplar Ave.
- Riverside Dr., Stonewall St. and Jackson Ave.

St. Jude campus gates are at N. 4th St. and A.W. Willis Ave. The guide lists the Tennessee Welcome Center, 119 N. Riverside Dr., and Beale St. as Saturday rideshare drop-off points.

## When should you park downtown?

Because of the closures, St. Jude says participants and spectators should plan to be downtown and parked no later than 6 a.m. on race day. The race-week page says to buy parking by Dec. 4 at stjude.org/marathonparking to lock in the special $20 rate. Other parking prices are not listed on the pages we read.

## What are the time limits at the St. Jude Marathon?

| Distance | Time limit |
|---|---|
| Marathon | 6.5 hours |
| Half marathon | 6.5 hours |
| 10K | 1.5 hours |
| 5K | 1.5 hours |

At those limits, a runner starting at 8 a.m. CT has until 2:30 p.m. CT to finish the marathon or half marathon (our arithmetic). The 10K and 5K limits from a 7 a.m. start run to 8:30 a.m. CT.

## When was the 2025 race?

The 2025 race was Saturday, Dec. 6, according to The Daily Memphian's Dec. 6, 2025 report; in 2026 it falls on the first Saturday of December, Dec. 5. St. Jude's schedule page lists the next two dates as Sat., Dec. 4, 2027 and Sat., Dec. 2, 2028.
