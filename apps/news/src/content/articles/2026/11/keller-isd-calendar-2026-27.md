**Keller Independent School District (KISD) students have no school Nov. 23-27, 2026, Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 5, 2027, and March 15-19, 2027**, by the district's 2026-2027 Instructional Calendar, linked from kellerisd.net. The last day of school is Wednesday, May 26, 2027, an early release day. The Oct. 9-16 fall break has passed, so Thanksgiving break is next.

The district PDF prints no issue date. Community Impact reported on Dec. 30, 2025 that the board "unanimously approved its academic calendar for the 2026-27 school year" at its Dec. 18 regular meeting, and that "KISD also has two instructional days that could be used in the event of bad weather." The calendar itself does not name those days. The dates below come from the district PDF, not from news summaries.

## Keller ISD breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates. Return days are our reading, since the calendar marks no closure on them.

| Break | No school | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break | Fri., Oct. 9 (student holiday), then Mon., Oct. 12 to Fri., Oct. 16, 2026 | 6 | Mon., Oct. 19 |
| Thanksgiving break | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027, plus Tue., Jan. 5 | 12 | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 |
| Spring break | Fri., March 12 (student holiday), then Mon., March 15 to Fri., March 19, 2027 | 6 | Mon., March 22 |

Plan the week on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html). For neighboring districts, see the [Fort Worth ISD calendar](/news/fort-worth-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) and the [Arlington ISD calendar](/news/arlington-isd-calendar-2026-27-breaks/).

## When do Keller ISD students return after winter break?

By our reading, Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027. The district lists Dec. 21-31 and Jan. 1-4 as district holidays (winter break) and Jan. 5 as a student holiday (a teacher work day). It marks nothing on Jan. 6; that date is the start of a grading-period bracket on the calendar. A Community Impact summary lists winter break as "Dec. 21, 2026-Jan. 6, 2027," which differs from the PDF, so confirm the first day back with the district before you book travel.

## Which long weekends does Keller ISD have this year?

Three stretches come from the calendar's own dates, counted by us.

| Stretch | Days off | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 9 to Sun., Oct. 18, 2026 | 6 weekdays | Student holiday (teacher work day), then fall break |
| Fri., Feb. 12 to Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | 4 days | Student holiday (professional development), then Presidents Day |
| Fri., March 26 to Mon., March 29, 2027 | 4 days | KISD holiday, then a student holiday (teacher work day) |

March 26, 2027 is Good Friday. The district lists it only as a "KISD Holiday."

## Does Keller ISD have early release days?

Yes. Thursday, Dec. 17, 2026 is an early release day for Grades 9-12, and Friday, Dec. 18 is one for all schools, the last school day before winter break. In May, Tuesday, May 25, 2027 is early release for Grades 9-12 and Wednesday, May 26, the last day of school, is early release.

## When does the Keller ISD school year start and end?

Students start Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026, after teachers' prep days, Aug. 3-11. The last day of school is Wednesday, May 26, 2027.
