**Zizzi serves its Christmas Set Menu from Tuesday 17 November 2026 to Monday 4 January 2027 at £29.95 for three courses or £24.95 for two, according to the Zizzi Restaurants Ltd Christmas page.** The 20% off the 3-course menu ends on Friday 27 November, and every Zizzi restaurant is closed on Christmas Day. Our research desk read the Zizzi pages on 22 November; the dates, prices and dishes below follow their wording.

## What does the Zizzi Christmas menu cost in 2026?

Zizzi's Christmas page says: "Our 3-course Christmas Set Menu is £29.95, while our 2-course option is £24.95." The menu page lists "3 courses from £29.95". Retail Times gave the same two prices on 17 September and again on 11 November.

| Option | Price | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 3 courses | £29.95 | Zizzi Christmas page |
| 2 courses | £24.95 | Zizzi Christmas page |
| Difference | £5.00 | 3-course price minus 2-course price |
| 3 courses with 20% off, 17 to 27 November | £23.96 | Our arithmetic; Zizzi does not print it |

The page does not give a children's price, a weekday and weekend split or a deposit, so none is shown here.

## Which Zizzi Christmas dates matter in 2026?

The last column counts days after Sunday 22 November, the day the pages were read.

| Date | What happens | Days after 22 Nov |
|---|---|---|
| Tue. 17 Nov 2026 | Set menu starts; 20% off starts | Already started |
| Fri. 27 Nov 2026 | 20% off the 3-course menu ends | 5 |

| Fri. 25 Dec 2026 | All restaurants closed | 33 |
| Thu. 31 Dec 2026 | New Year's Eve: UK and Republic of Ireland (ROI) restaurants open, most until 10 p.m., some may close earlier | 39 |

The set menu runs for 49 calendar days with both ends counted. Zizzi's wording on the 20% offer is "Save 20% on our 3-course Christmas Set Menu for up to 16 guests, all day, every day from 17th - 27th November." ## What is on the Zizzi Christmas Set Menu?

The menu page lists nine starters, nine mains and seven desserts. It groups dishes under "Vegetarian" and "Vegan" headings but shows no allergen symbols, so check allergens with the restaurant.

| Course | Dishes on the menu page |
|---|---|
| Starters | Garlic & Mozzarella Bombe; Cheesy Chilli Garlic Bread; Garlic Bread with Mozzarella; Garlic Bread with Mozzarella & Caramelised Onions; Mozzarella Arancini; Black Truffle Arancini; Mushroom Brindisi; Bruschetta Classica; Aubergine Fritti |
| Mains | Funghi Risotto; Pinoli; Margherita; Pomodoro; Vegan Margherita; Aubergine Parmigiana; Vegan Pomodoro; Vegan Lentil Ragu; Vegan Baked Gnocchi |
| Desserts | Pistachio Christmas Tree; Salted Caramel Chocolate Brownie; Honeycomb Cheesecake; Tiramisu; Limoncello Basque; Raspberry Collins Cheesecake; Fudge Cake |

## Is Zizzi open on Christmas Day, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve?

Zizzi's page says: "All Zizzi restaurants are closed on Christmas Day." It adds that restaurants are open on Christmas Eve, without hours. For New Year's Eve it says: "All UK and ROI restaurants will be open on New Year's Eve. Most are open until 10 PM. Some may close earlier." ROI is the Republic of Ireland.

A 3-course New Year's Eve menu can be booked "at select Central London restaurants". Zizzi gives no price for it. Boxing Day opening is not given on the pages we read.

## What else has Zizzi announced for Christmas 2026?

Zizzi sells physical gift cards and instant eGift cards, which it says you can send "whenever you like". Two earlier offers have ended: early booking by 26 October, and a free magnum of Prosecco for the first 100 qualifying bookings of four or more. Retail Times reported that Zizzi held a Festive Bombe Day on 17 November, with thirty bombes per restaurant while stocks lasted, and on 17 September that its Christmas advert was its first TV campaign since November 2021.
