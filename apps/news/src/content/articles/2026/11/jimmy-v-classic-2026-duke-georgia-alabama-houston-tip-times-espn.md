**The Jimmy V Classic 2026 is Tuesday, Dec. 8 at Madison Square Garden in New York, with Alabama vs. Houston at 6:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and Georgia vs. Duke at 9 p.m. ET, both on ESPN, according to ESPN Events.** Below: both games in ET, Central, Mountain and Pacific time, what the ranks mean, the 2025 results and a one-page viewing card.

ESPN's Sept. 28 schedule release says "December is highlighted by the 32nd Jimmy V Men's Classic presented by Modelo at Madison Square Garden on December 8." ESPN Events described the event on Oct. 8 as "the annual men's college basketball doubleheader" that "will air on ESPN."

## What time are the Jimmy V Classic games on Dec. 8, 2026?

The ET times are ESPN Events' own. Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT) and Pacific Time (PT) are converted from those ET times.

| Tip-off (ET) | CT | MT | PT | Game | Network |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6:30 p.m. | 5:30 p.m. | 4:30 p.m. | 3:30 p.m. | Alabama vs. Houston | ESPN |
| 9 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 7 p.m. | 6 p.m. | Georgia vs. Duke | ESPN |

Dec. 8, 2026 is a Tuesday. ESPN Events says the first matchup "will feature ESPN preseason Top-25 ranked teams Alabama vs. Houston" and the nightcap "will feature Georgia vs. ESPN preseason #2 ranked Duke."

## Which ranks does ESPN print for the teams?

ESPN's Sept. 28 release lists No. 16 Alabama vs. No. 17 Houston and Georgia vs. No. 2 Duke. Those are ESPN's preseason rankings as printed in the release, not the Associated Press poll, and the current poll ranks of these teams are not printed here. Georgia carries no rank in the release.

## Where are the tickets and what does the event support?

ESPN Events says tickets are on sale through [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/3B006515D5CB98C3), and its [tickets page](https://espnevents.com/jimmy-v-classic/tickets/) reads "Tickets On Sale Now!" without listing prices. ESPN's release says 2026 marks "ESPN's 20th V Week for Cancer Research" and that the classic is part of that annual week. The V Foundation for Cancer Research calls itself "a leading non-profit organization dedicated to achieving Victory Over Cancer" at [v.org](https://www.v.org/).

## What happened at the 2025 Jimmy V Classic?

ESPN's June 4, 2025 release set the 2025 doubleheader for Tuesday, Dec. 9, 2025 on ESPN, with Clemson vs. Brigham Young University (BYU) at 6:30 p.m. ET and Florida vs. UConn at 9 p.m. ET. Those are last year's games; the 2026 field is different.

| 2025 tip-off (ET) | Game | Final |
|---|---|---|
| 6:30 p.m. | Clemson vs. BYU | BYU 67, Clemson 64 |
| 9 p.m. | Florida vs. UConn | UConn 77, Florida 73 |

Clemson Athletics reported the BYU win came on a buzzer-beater "as time expired." UConn Athletics posted the 77-73 score over Florida.
