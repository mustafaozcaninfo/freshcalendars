## How the four Quordle words overlap

Count the tiles: 20 in all, spread over 13 different letters. Five of those letters turn up in two words, and the other eight each belong to a single word. Nothing reaches three words.

| Word pair | Letters shared |
|---|---|
| Words 1 and 2 | 2 |
| Words 1 and 4 | 1 |
| Words 3 and 4 | 2 |
| Words 1 and 3 | 0 |
| Words 2 and 3 | 0 |
| Words 2 and 4 | 0 |

Read the table as a chain. Word 2 links only to Word 1, Word 1 also links to Word 4, and Word 4 links to Word 3. Word 1 and Word 4 sit in the middle, each touching two neighbors, so a clue earned on them travels furthest. Words 2 and 3 are the two ends and have nothing in common with each other.

The pair with two shared letters at the right-hand end of the chain also opens identically: Words 3 and 4 begin with the same two letters, and those are exactly the two letters they share. A guess that starts with that pair gets scored on both boards at once.

Doubles matter too. Two words repeat a letter inside themselves, Word 3 and Word 4, so each shows only four different letters, while Words 1 and 2 show five. A first guess with five different letters will never match a doubled word tile for tile.

Vowels are scarce: six of the 20 tiles, covering four vowel letters. Words 1 and 4 have one vowel tile each, Word 2 has two different vowels and Word 3 has one vowel repeated.
