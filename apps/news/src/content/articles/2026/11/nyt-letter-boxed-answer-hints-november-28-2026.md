## Which letter-to-letter steps never occur on this box?

Of the 108 ordered steps from one side to a different side, 5 never appear inside any word of the list: P to N, P to B, B to M, B to P and T to N. Another 5 appear in exactly one word: L to R, M to L, D to T, T to L and T to D.

B is the letter to watch. It sits in 422 words, the fewest of the 12 letters, and it cannot step to M or P. Next comes U, in 570 words and the last letter of only 39.

How many words use at least one step between two given sides:

| Sides | Words with a step between them |

M/D/P and B/R/T is the thin link at 379 words. A/U/O does the heavy lifting, with 1,212 to 1,333 words on each of its three links.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, three fewer than the par of 5.
2. The first word has 8 letters and the second has 6, and the list holds 188 words of 8 letters.
3. The first word opens on the A/U/O side and closes on the B/R/T side. The second word opens on that same side and closes on the M/D/P side.
4. The B and the M are in the first word. The P and the I are in the second.
5. The first word starts with A and ends in T. The second starts with T and ends in D.
