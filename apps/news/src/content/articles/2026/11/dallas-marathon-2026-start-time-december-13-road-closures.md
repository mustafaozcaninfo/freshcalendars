The Dallas Marathon starts at 8:00 a.m. Central on Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026, which is 9 a.m. ET, according to the race's own site, dallasmarathon.com. The Full (26.2 miles), Half Marathon (13.1), Half Marathon Walk and 50K Ultra Marathon (31 miles) all start at Dallas City Hall Plaza, and the relay start is also 8:00 a.m.

One fact is still open: the City of Dallas had not posted a 2026 road closure advisory as of Nov. 21, 2026. The race says all Saturday and Sunday events are sold out.

Runners must be 16 or older for the Half Marathon, Full Marathon, Ultra Marathon and relays, the organizer says. Entries for the Friday Night Lights Mile were still open on the homepage, which said "Limited spots remain."

## Where is the Dallas Marathon expo, and is there race-morning packet pick-up?

The expo is in Hall A of the Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center, 650 S. Griffin St., Dallas, TX 75202. It is open Friday, Dec. 11 from 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Saturday, Dec. 12 from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. The organizer says admission is free and that it is the official packet pick-up for all participants.

The Sunday events page says there is "NO packet pick-up available on race morning." Plan to collect your packet on Friday or Saturday.

## What are the Dallas Marathon cutoffs?

Every Sunday event has a course split at mile 7.8, at Richmond Ave. and Greenville Ave., at 10:45 a.m. The 50K has a second cutoff at mile 18.5, at Winsted Dr. and the White Rock Lake Trail, at 12:00 p.m. Those are the only cutoff points the organizer lists on its events page. The organizer says it offers support vehicles for anyone needing assistance back to Dallas City Hall Plaza.

The race publishes a [2026 course map for Sunday (PDF)](https://res.cloudinary.com/enlightenment/image/upload/v1792549407/dallas-marathon/maps/2026/2026_DM_-_Graphical_Map_-_SUNDAY.pdf). The participants page says all events start and end at the intersection of Marilla St. and S. Akard St., and that there are 17 aid stations along the marathon, half marathon and 50K course.

Not yet announced. The City of Dallas Office of Special Events page had no 2026 advisory on Nov. 21, 2026. The last city traffic advisory for the race we found was posted on Dec. 9, 2024 and covered Friday to Sunday, Dec. 13 to 15, 2024. That is last year's pattern only; this year's closures and times may differ; the 2024 notice went out four days before race weekend.
