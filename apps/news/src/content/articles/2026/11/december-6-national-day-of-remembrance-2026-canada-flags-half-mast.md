**Flags on federal buildings in Canada, including the Peace Tower, are half-masted from sunrise to sunset on Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, for the National Day of Remembrance and Action on Violence Against Women.** Canadian Heritage lists the day among its Special Days in "Rules for half-masting the National Flag of Canada". As of Nov. 8, 2026, its half-masting notices page lists notices up to Remembrance Day on Nov. 11 and none yet for Dec. 6.

## What the federal rule says about Dec. 6

The masting rules list "December 6, National Day of Remembrance and Action on Violence Against Women" as a Special Day. On Special Days the flag is half-masted "on all federal buildings and establishments in Canada, including the Peace Tower, from sunrise to sunset". The rules page is the federal source; provinces and cities follow their own directions, covered below.

## Why are flags half-masted on Dec. 6?

Women and Gender Equality Canada says: "It has been over 30 years since the murder of 14 young women at Polytechnique Montréal (December 6, 1989)." It adds that "this act of violent misogyny shook our country and led Parliament to designate December 6 as The National Day of Remembrance and Action on Violence Against Women."

The National Day of Remembrance Act (S.C. 1991, c. 36) was assented to on Oct. 29, 1991. Section 2 says "Throughout Canada, in each and every year, the 6th day of December shall be known under the name of 'National Day of Remembrance and Action on Violence Against Women'". The Act names the day. It does not create a holiday.

## Federal half-mast days, Dec. 2026 to Dec. 2027

Canadian Heritage's Special Days that fall in the next 12 months are below, with weekdays computed from the calendar.  The two September Sundays follow the "second Sunday" and "last Sunday" wording in the rules.

| Apr. 9, 2027 | Friday | Vimy Ridge Day (Peace Tower only) |

| Nov. 11, 2027 | Thursday | Remembrance Day |

Dec. 6 falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027. ## How do the federal, Ontario and British Columbia rules compare?

| Government | What it says about Dec. 6 | Year of the source |
|---|---|---|
| Canada (Canadian Heritage) | Flag half-masted on all federal buildings and establishments in Canada, including the Peace Tower, sunrise to sunset | Rules page, checked Nov. 8, 2026 |
| Ontario | "Ontario annually half-masts flags from sunrise to sunset" on Dec. 6 | Page updated Sept. 25, 2026 |
| British Columbia | "Canadian flags on all B.C. government buildings will be flown at half-mast on December 6, ... from sunrise until sunset" | Deputy Minister's Bulletin, Dec. 2, 2022 |

The British Columbia line is the province's 2022 direction; a 2026 notice had not been posted. For the same remembrance season, the November story on the [International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women and the 16 Days](/news/international-day-elimination-violence-against-women-2026-november-25-16-days/) covers the campaign that runs "November 25 to December 10", per Women and Gender Equality Canada. The 2026 theme has not been announced.

## What happens in Montréal on Dec. 6?

The City of Montréal has not announced its 2026 commemoration. In 2024, according to its release of Dec. 5, 2024, the 14 light beams on Mount Royal were lit at 5:10 p.m., and a ceremony was held at the chalet du Mont-Royal. The hero photo is a file photo of a past commemoration, year unknown.
