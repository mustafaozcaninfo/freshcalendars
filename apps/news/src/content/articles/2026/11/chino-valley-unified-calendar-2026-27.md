**Chino Valley Unified School District (CVUSD) in California has Christmas/Winter Break from Friday, Dec. 18, 2026 through Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, and students return Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027**, according to its board-approved "2026-2027 Student Attendance Calendar." The calendar is titled "180 School Days - Traditional," and school ends Thursday, May 27, 2027. The first and last days, every no-school weekday counted to 180 and a printable school-year calendar follow.

## CVUSD first and last days, 2026-27

| Event | Date on the calendar | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| "First Day of School" | Aug. 10, 2026 | Monday |
| "Last Day of School" | May 27, 2027 | Thursday |

Teachers have workdays before the start: "New Teacher Workday" on Aug. 4-5, "K-6 Teacher Workday" on Aug. 6 and "All Teacher Workday" on Aug. 7. After the last day, "May 28 All Teacher Workday" is followed by Memorial Day on Monday, May 31, 2027. The calendar also lists "June 18 Juneteenth Observed," which falls after the school year ends.

## How many CVUSD no-school days are in 2026-27?

Aug. 10, 2026 through May 27, 2027 spans 209 weekdays. Weekends are not counted, and Christmas and New Year's Day fall inside winter break, so they add nothing extra.

| Dates | Weekdays off | What the calendar calls it |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Sept. 7, 2026 | 1 | "Labor Day" |
| Wed., Nov. 11, 2026 | 1 | "Veterans' Day" |
| Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | "Thanksgiving Break" |
| Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 to Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | 13 | "Christmas/Winter Break" |
| Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | 1 | "Martin Luther King Day" |
| Mon., Feb. 8, 2027 | 1 | "Lincoln's Birthday" |
| Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | 1 | "Washington's Birthday" |
| Mon.-Fri., March 22-26, 2027, and Mon., March 29 | 6 | "Spring Break" |
| **Total no-school weekdays** | **29** | |

209 weekdays minus 29 no-school weekdays leaves 180, which matches the calendar's "180 School Days." That count follows from the list above; the calendar prints only the "180" in its title.

## When is CVUSD winter break 2026?

The calendar lists "Dec 18-Jan 5 Christmas/Winter Break." Dec. 18 is also marked "7-12 Teacher Workday/TK-12 Non-School Day," so the break starts on a Friday. Counting weekdays, it is 13: Dec. 18, then Dec. 21-25, Dec. 28-Jan. 1, and Jan. 4-5. Students are back Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027. See the weeks on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When is CVUSD Thanksgiving break 2026?

"Nov 23-27 Thanksgiving Break" runs Monday to Friday, five weekdays, with Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, Nov. 26. "Nov 11 Veterans' Day" is a Wednesday, a single day off earlier in the month.

## When is CVUSD spring break 2027?

The calendar lists "Mar 22-29 Spring Break," from Monday, March 22 through Monday, March 29, 2027. That is eight calendar days and six weekdays, because the break includes two Mondays. Students return Tuesday, March 30. Mark it on the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html). Nearby districts print their own dates: see the [Riverside Unified calendar](/news/riverside-unified-calendar-2026-27/), the [Garden Grove Unified calendar](/news/garden-grove-unified-calendar-2026-27/) and the [Los Angeles Unified calendar](/news/lausd-calendar-2026-27-winter-break-spring-break/).

## Which CVUSD days off fall in January and February?

Three single Mondays are listed: "Jan 18 Martin Luther King Day," "Feb 8 Lincoln's Birthday" and "Feb 15 Washington's Birthday," the district's wording for the February holiday. Lincoln's Birthday and Washington's Birthday are a week apart, so each stands alone.

## Does the CVUSD calendar cover year-round schools?

No. The dates above come from the traditional calendar. The district's [School Year Calendars](https://www.chino.k12.ca.us/138686_3) page lists a separate "2026/2027 - Multi-Track Student Calendar" for multitrack schools, which follow their own dates, and a 2027-28 traditional calendar. Districts can change dates; check the district page before you book travel.
