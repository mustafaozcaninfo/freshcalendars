**April 2027 begins on Thursday, Apr. 1 and has no federal holiday, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** Tax Day is Thursday, Apr. 15, Passover begins at sundown on Wednesday, Apr. 21 and the NFL Draft starts on Thursday, Apr. 29. The countdowns below are counted from Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026.

## No federal holiday between Washington's Birthday and Memorial Day

OPM's federal holiday list has Washington's Birthday on Monday, Feb. 15, 2027 and then Memorial Day on Monday, May 31, so April has none. The previous month in this series is the [March 2027 important dates](/news/march-2027-important-dates-holidays-days-until/) story.

Easter is not in April this year. GOV.UK lists Good Friday on 26 March and Easter Monday on 29 March, so no England and Wales bank holiday falls in April 2027.

## Tax Day 2027: the Thursday, April 15 deadline

Federal law, 26 U.S.C. 6072(a), sets the return due date "on or before the 15th day of April following the close of the calendar year." For 2026 returns that is Thursday, Apr. 15, 2027, and because it is a weekday the date does not move. The same day is the first installment date for 2027 estimated tax under 26 U.S.C. 6654(c)(2). The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) has not yet posted its 2027 filing-season opening date. Our [HSA contribution deadline story](/news/2026-hsa-contribution-deadline-april-2027/) covers the HSA date.

## Passover, Earth Day and Showa Day in April 2027

Hebcal says Passover "begins in the Diaspora at sundown on Wednesday, 21 April 2027" and "ends at nightfall on Thursday, 29 April 2027." Chabad.org lists 2027 as April 21-29, with Seders on the nights of April 21 and 22. Our [Passover 2027 story](/news/passover-2027-holi-2027-dates-sundown-times/) has sundown times by city.

Earth Day is Thursday, Apr. 22. Earthday.org says "The theme for Earth Day 2027, Food for Our Planet." In Japan, the Cabinet Office lists Showa Day on April 29, which is Thursday, Apr. 29, 2027 and the start of Golden Week, followed by Constitution Day on May 3, Greenery Day on May 4 and Children's Day on May 5.

## Boston Marathon, Coachella and the NFL Draft in April 2027

The Boston Athletic Association (B.A.A.) lists the Boston Marathon on "Apr 19th, 2027," a Monday. That is Patriots' Day in Massachusetts and Maine, the third Monday of April by computation.

Coachella's page lists Apr. 9-11 and Apr. 16-18, 2027 and shows passes as sold out; our [Coachella and Stagecoach 2027 story](/news/coachella-stagecoach-2027-dates-lineup-indio/) has the rest. NFL.com says "The 2027 NFL Draft presented by Bud Light will take place Thursday, April 29 to Saturday, May 1, 2027, in Washington, D.C." More is in our [NFL Draft 2027 story](/news/nfl-draft-2027-dates-location-washington/).
