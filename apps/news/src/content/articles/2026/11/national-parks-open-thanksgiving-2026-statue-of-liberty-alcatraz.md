**On Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, Alcatraz, Ellis Island and the Statue of Liberty ferry are closed, while Mount Rushmore, the Grand Canyon South Rim and Muir Woods list no Thanksgiving closure**, according to each site's National Park Service (NPS) hours page and the ferry operator, Statue City Cruises. The rules below are year-round rules printed on those pages, read Nov. 17, 2026, not announcements made for this year.

## Which national park sites close on Thanksgiving?

Five sites state the closure in their own words: Alcatraz, Ellis Island, Carlsbad Caverns National Park, Ford's Theatre National Historic Site and the Pearl Harbor National Memorial visitor center. The NPS Statue of Liberty page says the Statue "is only accessible via ferry. Please check the ferry schedule." The ferry company, Statue City Cruises, says on its questions page: "The only days we're closed are Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day (December 25th)." Ellis Island and the Statue of Liberty ferries therefore do not run on Nov. 26.

## National park hours on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day

Christmas Day is Dec. 25 and New Year's Day is Jan. 1, 2027. "Not listed" means the site's hours page names no closure for that day; it does not confirm the site is open.

## Which sites stay open on Thanksgiving?

The Grand Canyon South Rim is open "24 hours a day, 365 days a year," and Muir Woods says it is "open 365 days a year!" Muir Woods requires a parking reservation for visitors who drive, and its hours run from 8:00 AM until about sunset. Mount Rushmore's visitor facilities are open "all year, seven days a week," except Dec. 25. The Wright Brothers National Memorial closes only on Christmas Day and is open 9:00 am to 5:00 pm the rest of the year. The Liberty Bell Center page reads "Open daily, 9 am-5 pm" and lists no Thanksgiving closure. Confirm any of these with the site before you travel, because the pages are general hours and not holiday notices.

## Are national park entrance fees waived on Thanksgiving?

No. Nov. 26 and Nov. 27 are not NPS fee-free days. The last 2026 fee-free day was Veterans Day, Nov. 11; our [national parks free days story](/news/national-parks-free-days-october-27-veterans-day-2026/) has who qualified and what the waiver did not cover.
