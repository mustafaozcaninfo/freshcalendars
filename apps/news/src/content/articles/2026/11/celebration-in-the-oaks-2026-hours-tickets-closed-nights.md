**Celebration in the Oaks runs Nov. 27, 2026, through Jan. 1, 2027, in New Orleans City Park and is closed Dec. 7, 14, 24 and 31**, according to the park's event site, read Nov. 26. Hours are 6 to 10 p.m. Central Time (CT) Monday to Thursday, 5 to 10 p.m. Friday and Saturday and 5 to 9 p.m. Sunday. Eastern Time (ET) is one hour later.

The park's [event page](https://neworleanscitypark.org/event/celebration-in-the-oaks/) gives the season as "November 27, 2026 - January 1, 2027." Counted that way the run is 36 nights: 4 closed, 4 Bike Nights, 7 Value Nights and 21 regular nights. The calendar below places each night; for the whole month see the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and for the holiday itself [when Christmas falls](/when-is/christmas/).

| Monday to Thursday | 6 to 10 p.m. | 7 to 11 p.m. |
| Friday and Saturday | 5 to 10 p.m. | 6 to 11 p.m. |

The event site lists the same hours for the Driving Tour and the Walking Tour. Last entry is a half hour before closing for both. The site also lists "Christmas Hours: 5pm-10pm" without naming the nights, so check the ticket page for the date you pick. The park does not post separate Value Night hours; it lists nightly hours by weekday.

| Walking Tour, Value Night | $20 per person | |

Children 2 and under are free on the Walking Tour. The park's ticket page lists these prices, read Nov. 26; it posts no member dollar prices, child prices for ages 3 and up, or ride prices for Carousel Gardens and Storyland.

All tickets must be bought in advance, and the entire event is cashless, the questions page says. There are no refunds. A date change costs $7.99 and must be made at least 24 hours ahead. A Driving Tour ticket is valid only for the date and time printed on it. Members get discounted pricing on up to eight tickets, with no amount posted. Friends of City Park members get one free Dasher Pass Driving Tour, the park said on Nov. 2.

## Which nights are Value Nights at Celebration in the Oaks?

The park's questions page says: "There are seven Value Nights: November 29, December 8, 9, 10, 15, 16 and January 1." They fall on a Sunday, then Tuesday to Thursday on Dec. 8 to 10, Tuesday and Wednesday on Dec. 15 and 16, and Friday, Jan. 1. A New Orleans television station reported a different Value Night list on Nov. 5, 2026; the park's list is the one used here.

## What is new at Celebration in the Oaks in 2026?

The park's Nov. 2 announcement lists 45 exhibits along Palm Drive, a Mardi Gras-inspired tree and more uplighting on the Roosevelt Mall live oaks. The Walking Tour adds an 18-foot walk-through mushroom, a 22-foot prism tree and a reimagined Peppermint Post Office. The Holiday Express train scenes add Three Little Pigs, Mama Duck and Her Ducklings and a 40-foot "I Heart City Park" sign. The event site advertises over one million twinkling lights.

## Where do you enter and park for Celebration in the Oaks?

The Driving Tour entrance is at 4 Friederichs Avenue and the Walking Tour entrance at 7 Victory Avenue. All parking in City Park is free, the questions page says, and Irby Field parking at 25 Dreyfous Drive is open weather permitting. The Driving Tour runs rain or shine. The Walking Tour does not run on Bike Nights, and pets are not allowed on it; pets may stay in the car on the Driving Tour. No outside food is allowed, and hot chocolate and marshmallow roasting are complimentary. The Walking Tour has group discounts for 50 or more.
