**Labour Day 2027 is Monday, Sept. 6, according to the Government of Alberta's general holidays table, which lists "September 6" for 2027.** The U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) puts Labor Day on the same date, "Monday, September 06". Canada's long weekend runs from Saturday, Sept. 4 to Monday, Sept. 6, 2027. The date for 2026 was Sept. 7, and the Alberta table shows Sept. 4 for 2028.

New Zealand waits until October. A free long-weekend planner for Canada comes with the story.

## Labour Day 2027 dates in Canada, the US and New Zealand

| Country | Holiday | 2027 date | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canada (Alberta table) | Labour Day | Monday, Sept. 6 | Government of Alberta |
| United States | Labor Day | Monday, Sept. 6 | OPM |
| New Zealand | Labour Day | Monday, Oct. 25 | Employment New Zealand |

Employment New Zealand lists Labour Day as the "4th Monday in October", Monday 25 October in 2027 and Monday 26 October in 2026. Australian states set their own Labour Day dates; none had posted 2027 dates on an official page we could read.

## What is closed on Labour Day in Canada?

It depends on who runs the workplace.

- **Federally regulated workplaces.** Section 166 of the Canada Labour Code lists Labour Day among the general holidays, alongside New Year's Day, Canada Day and Christmas Day. It covers federally regulated employers such as banks, airlines and telecom companies.
- **Alberta and British Columbia.** Alberta lists Labour Day as a general holiday. British Columbia's statutory holidays page lists "Labour Day | Monday, September 7" for 2026; its 2027 row was not posted when this story was written.
- **Canada Post.** It says it is "closed on national, provincial and territorial holidays, with no collection or delivery of mail on those days." Its 2027 schedule is not yet posted, so the rule is the guide for Sept. 6.
- **Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).** Its public holidays page lists "Labour Day – Monday, September 7, 2026", with no 2027 row yet.

Rules in Ontario, Quebec and the other provinces, and individual stores, were not confirmed here. Ask your employer. The [Canada holidays calendar](/canada-holidays/) shows the other days off, and [the 2027 federal general holidays](/news/canada-statutory-holidays-2027-federal-general-holidays/) are covered in their own story.

## When is Labour Day from 2027 to 2030?

Labour Day is the first Monday of September. Alberta's table matches that rule for 2026, 2027 and 2028, and the 2029 and 2030 dates are computed from the same rule, counting forward from the first day of each September.

| Year | Labour Day | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Monday, Sept. 6 | Alberta general holidays table |
| 2028 | Monday, Sept. 4 | Alberta general holidays table |
| 2029 | Monday, Sept. 3 | Computed from the first-Monday rule |
| 2030 | Monday, Sept. 2 | Computed from the first-Monday rule |

Sept. 1, 2027 is a Wednesday, which is why the first Monday is the 6th. See the [September 2027 printable calendar](/september-calendars.html) for the full month, and the [2027 long weekends by province](/news/canada-long-weekends-2027-by-province-vacation-planner/) for the rest of the year.
