**Epic's Black Friday sale on the Epic Games Store runs Nov. 17 to Dec. 1, 2026, according to Epic Games' "2026 Q4 Promotional Guide" for developers on its Unreal Engine forums.** The guide, posted Aug. 24, 2026, calls it a Premium Editions sale: "focused exclusively on Premium Editions - such as Deluxe, Gold, Premium, or similar versions." It also dates Epic's Holiday Sale (Dec. 10 to Jan. 7) and leaves a gap between the two sales. The dates, with weekdays and gaps computed, and a free printable calendar are below.

The guide gives no start or end hour: it says "You can find information about the dates and launch times in the Discount Management Tool," which is a developer tool. No 2026 hour is confirmed.

## When does the Epic Games Store Black Friday sale 2026 start and end?

Epic lists "November 17 - December 1 | 14 Days." Nov. 17 is a Tuesday and Dec. 1 is a Tuesday, so the sale ends the Tuesday after Thanksgiving week. Two newsrooms agree on the dates: games.gg wrote on Nov. 13 that the sale "runs from November 17 to December 1, 14 days in total," and JWave in Brazil reported on Nov. 18 a sale "entre 17 de novembro e 1º de dezembro."

JWave alone gave an hour, 1 p.m. Brasília time. Epic's guide does not confirm it, so it is not treated as the start time. Last year, Video Games Chronicle reported on Nov. 18, 2025: "The sale will run from today until December 2 at 11 AM ET / 4 PM GMT." That was the 2025 sale, also for Premium Editions; the 2026 hour is not confirmed.

## What is Epic's full sale calendar for the fourth quarter?

Every row below is from Epic's 2026 Q4 guide, with the weekdays computed from the dates.

| Epic promotion | Dates | Epic's length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Halloween Sale | Mon., Oct. 19 to Mon., Nov. 2 | 14 days | Over |
| Epic Savings: Nov | Thu., Nov. 12 to Thu., Nov. 26 | 14 days | Overlaps the Black Friday sale |
| **Black Friday: Premium Editions Sale** | **Tue., Nov. 17 to Tue., Dec. 1** | **14 days** | Premium Editions only |
| **Holiday Sale** | **Thu., Dec. 10 to Thu., Jan. 7, 2027** | **28 days** | Base games and downloadable content (DLC) or add-ons |

The guide's lengths are the gaps between the dates. The Black Friday sale spans 15 calendar days if both Nov. 17 and Dec. 1 are counted, and the Holiday Sale 29.

## Is there a gap between the Black Friday sale and the Holiday Sale?

Yes, on Epic's schedule. The Black Friday sale ends Tue., Dec. 1 and the Holiday Sale starts Thu., Dec. 10, so the guide lists no sale from Wed., Dec. 2 to Wed., Dec. 9, which is 8 days. The Epic Savings: Nov promotion and the Black Friday sale overlap from Nov. 17 to Nov. 26, 10 days. The guide does not say how the two interact on a given game.

## What are the Epic free games for Nov. 19, 2026?

Worthplaying and Clouddosage, both on Nov. 12, 2026, report Alien: Isolation and Mark of the Deep free from Nov. 19 to Nov. 26, 2026. Worthplaying wrote "gratis starting November 19 until November 26, 2026." The free games after Nov. 26 are not yet announced. Epic has also not announced mystery free games for December, and no dates are given here.

## How does the Epic sale compare with Steam, Xbox and PlayStation?

Epic's sale overlaps the other PC and console stores. Valve lists its Black Friday event for Nov. 26 at 10 a.m. PT to Nov. 30 at 10 a.m. PT, and the Steam Winter Sale from Dec. 17 to Jan. 4, 2027; see our [Steam sale calendar](/news/steam-winter-sale-2026-dates-december-17-steam-sale-calendar-2027/).

| Epic Games Store | Holiday Sale | Dec. 10 to Jan. 7, 2027 | Epic Games |

Console sales are covered in the [PlayStation Store Black Friday 2026](/news/playstation-black-friday-2026-ps-store-deals-november-4-30/) and [Nintendo Black Friday 2026](/news/nintendo-black-friday-2026-deals-switch-2-bundle-dates/) stories.

## What has Epic not announced for the 2026 sales?

Epic has not announced a 2026 coupon or Epic Rewards rate, the Holiday Sale hour or any named game discounts on an official page, so no prices are listed. The guide gives no discount percentages.
