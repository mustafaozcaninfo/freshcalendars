**Verizon says Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday is Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, on its holiday deals page, which we read on Nov. 18.** Its holiday deals started Nov. 5, according to a Verizon newsroom post of that date. The top bundle is the iPhone 18 Pro with an Apple Watch SE 3 and AirPods 5 free. The tables below list every offer on the page with its condition and monthly price.

## What are the Verizon Black Friday 2026 dates?

Verizon's page gives two dates, and its newsroom post gives the start. Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, is our addition from the calendar. Verizon has not posted an end date for the deals.

| Date | Day | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 5 | Thu. | Verizon's holiday deals start, per its newsroom post |
| Nov. 26 | Thu. | Thanksgiving |
| Nov. 27 | Fri. | Black Friday, per Verizon |
| Nov. 30 | Mon. | Cyber Monday, per Verizon |

The newsroom post says: "Starting today, November 5, and running throughout the holiday season, customers can unwrap amazing savings." Put the dates on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/).

## What does the Verizon iPhone 18 Pro deal include?

Verizon's page offers four bundles, each marked "With Simplicity Plan. Watch w/ service plan req'd to get" the earbuds. Verizon's newsroom post adds that you get a smartwatch and earbuds "when you purchase your phone on a 36-month device payment plan." The full terms are on Verizon's offer page behind each Details link, which we did not read, so trade-in and new-line requirements are not listed here.

| Phone | Free with it | Condition on the page |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 18 Pro | Apple Watch SE 3 and AirPods 5 | With Simplicity Plan; watch with service plan required |
| Galaxy S26 Ultra | Galaxy Watch9 and Galaxy Buds4 | With Simplicity Plan; watch with service plan required |
| Pixel 11 Pro XL | Pixel Watch 5 and Pixel Buds 2a | With Simplicity Plan; watch with service plan required |
| motorola razr+ 2026 | moto watch ultra and moto buds 2 plus | With Simplicity Plan; watch with service plan required |

## What has Verizon not announced for Black Friday 2026?

- An end date for its Black Friday or holiday deals.
- Store hours for Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, or Black Friday, Nov. 27. Its deals page says "Hours will vary from store to store"; use the store locator on verizon.com.
- The terms behind each Details link, which this page does not reproduce.

Verizon's newsroom post also says daily drops through Verizon Shine "are available only while supplies last."
