**Verizon says Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday is Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, on its holiday deals page, which we read on Nov. 25.** A Verizon newsroom post dated Nov. 5 says the savings run throughout the holiday season. The top bundle is the iPhone 18 Pro with an Apple Watch Series 12 and an iPad (A16) free. The tables below list every offer on the page with its condition and monthly price.

## What are the Verizon Black Friday 2026 dates?

Verizon's page gives two dates, and its newsroom post gives several offer dates. Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, is our addition from the calendar. Verizon has not posted one end date for the whole sale.

| Date | Day | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 5 | Thu. | Date of Verizon's holiday deals newsroom post |
| Nov. 24 | Tue. | iPhone 18 Pro watch-and-iPad bundle available, per the newsroom post |
| Nov. 25 | Wed. | Home internet choice of extra offers begins, through Nov. 30, per the newsroom post |
| Nov. 26 | Thu. | Thanksgiving; Galaxy S26 Ultra bundle available and Meta Glasses offer window opens, per Verizon |
| Nov. 27 | Fri. | Black Friday, per Verizon |
| Nov. 30 | Mon. | Cyber Monday, per Verizon; Meta Glasses offer ends |

The newsroom post says: "Throughout the holiday season, customers can unwrap amazing savings on the hottest tech." The Meta Glasses terms on the page give the window as 11.26.26 to 11.30.26. Put the dates on our [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html), and see [when Black Friday falls](/when-is/black-friday/).

## What does the Verizon iPhone 18 Pro deal include?

Verizon's page offers four bundles, each marked as needing a Simplicity Plan and a service plan for the watch. Verizon's newsroom post says new and existing customers on Simplicity can get the phones "with no trade-in needed," and that "when you purchase your phone on a device payment plan, you can get a smartwatch and tablet on us." The Pixel and razr+ bundles also include Meta Glasses worth up to $299. The page lists the Galaxy S26 Ultra bundle with a Galaxy Watch9 and Galaxy Buds4, while the newsroom post names a Galaxy Watch9 and Galaxy Tab S10 FE 5G, so check which one Verizon shows at checkout. The full terms are behind each Details link on Verizon's offer page.

| Phone | Free with it | Condition on the page |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 18 Pro | Apple Watch Series 12 and iPad (A16) | With Simplicity Plan; service plan for watch and iPad required |
| Galaxy S26 Ultra | Galaxy Watch9 and Galaxy Buds4 (page) | With Simplicity Plan; watch with service plan required |
| Pixel 11 Pro XL | Pixel Watch 5, Tab S10 FE 5G and Meta Glasses (up to $299) | New line on Simplicity Plan; service plan for watch and tablet required |
| motorola razr+ 2026 | moto watch ultra, Galaxy Tab S10 FE 5G and Meta Glasses (up to $299) | New line on Simplicity Plan; service plan for watch and tablet required |

## What has Verizon not announced for Black Friday 2026?

- One end date for its Black Friday or holiday deals. Only some offers show one: the Meta Glasses offer ends 11/30 and the Pixel 11 Pro XL's Save $1300 says "Ends Monday."
- Store hours for Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, or Black Friday, Nov. 27. Its deals page says "Hours will vary from store to store"; use the store locator on verizon.com.
- The terms behind each Details link, which this page does not reproduce.

Verizon's newsroom post says daily drops through Verizon Shine "are available only while supplies last."
