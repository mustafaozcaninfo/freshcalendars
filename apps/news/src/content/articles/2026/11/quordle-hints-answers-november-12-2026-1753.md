## Which Quordle board to start on today

Twenty tiles use 15 different letters, so five tiles are repeats of a letter seen on another board. That is a thin overlap. No letter sits on all four boards, and no word repeats a letter inside itself.

| Board | Different letters | Also on another board | On this board only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top left | 5 | 2 | 3 |
| Top right | 5 | 3 | 2 |
| Bottom left | 5 | 2 | 3 |
| Bottom right | 5 | 2 | 3 |

| Boards paired | Letters shared |
|---|---|
| Top left and top right | 1 |
| Top left and bottom left | 1 |
| Top left and bottom right | 0 |
| Top right and bottom left | 1 |
| Top right and bottom right | 2 |
| Bottom left and bottom right | 1 |

The top right is the only board that shares at least one letter with each of the other three. A green or yellow tile earned there is worth testing on every other board. The top left and bottom right share nothing, so what you learn on one of them says nothing about the other.

**A suggested order.** Open the top right. Move to the bottom right next, since it shares two letters with the top right and one with the bottom left. The bottom left comes third. Leave the top left for last: three of its five letters appear nowhere else, so it needs its own guesses.

**First-guess coverage.** One letter covers three boards, and three more cover two boards each. Those four letters account for 9 of the 20 tiles. A first word built from as many of them as possible, plus a vowel, tests most of the board at once. Six of the 20 tiles are vowel tiles, spread over four different vowel letters, and two words have two of them while the other two have one.
