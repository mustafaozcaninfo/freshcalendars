**The Transportation Security Administration (TSA) screened 2,924,593 people on Sunday, Dec. 28, 2025, its highest count of the Dec. 18-31 stretch last year, and the same weekday in 2026 is Sunday, Dec. 27.** Christmas Day 2026 is Friday, Dec. 25, according to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM). The table below pairs every day from Dec. 18 to 30, 2026 with the same weekday 364 days earlier, using TSA's published daily checkpoint numbers.

These are last year's counts, not a forecast. Neither TSA nor the American Automobile Association (AAA) had released a 2026 year-end projection as of Nov. 10, 2026.

## How the counts line up when you count from Christmas Day

Holiday travel follows the holiday, not the weekday. This table counts days before and after Dec. 25 in each year and puts the 2026 date beside them. TSA's 2024 page figures in this window start Dec. 19.

The two methods give different answers for the peak. In 2025 the highest count was the Sunday three days after Christmas (Dec. 28), which is Monday, Dec. 28 if you count from Christmas Day in 2026 and Sunday, Dec. 27 if you match the weekday. In 2024 the highest count was Friday, Dec. 27, two days after Christmas, which is Dec. 27 in 2026 by that count. TSA's numbers show where the crowds were; they do not say where they will be.

## What the 2025 and 2024 numbers show

- Christmas Day had the lowest count of Dec. 18-31 in both years: 2,066,118 in 2025 and 2,039,342 in 2024.
- Christmas Eve was also low: 2,236,205 in 2025 and 2,068,817 in 2024.
- The 2025 peak of 2,924,593 was about 42% above Christmas Day 2025 (our calculation) and 76,437 above the 2024 peak of 2,848,156.
- On Dec. 19-23 and Dec. 26-30, every count in both years fell between 2,651,437 and 2,924,593.
- The day after Christmas was not quiet: 2,716,278 people were screened on Dec. 26, 2025 and 2,743,795 on Dec. 26, 2024.

## Holidays, ID rules and a printable travel calendar

OPM lists Christmas Day on Friday, Dec. 25, 2026 and New Year's Day on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. As of Nov. 10, no 2026 executive order giving federal employees Dec. 24 or Dec. 26 off has been announced; our [Christmas Eve federal holiday explainer](/news/is-christmas-eve-a-federal-holiday-2026/) tracks it. Our [When is Christmas?](/when-is/christmas/) page has the date, and a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) lets you mark flights.

TSA says REAL ID enforcement began May 7, 2025, and travelers without an acceptable form of ID must pay a $45 fee. Its ConfirmID page says the fee applies for travel on or after Feb. 1 (2026) and lets you use ConfirmID for 10 days from the date of travel listed on the receipt; details are in our [TSA ConfirmID fee story](/news/tsa-confirmid-45-fee-no-real-id-holiday-travel-2026/).
