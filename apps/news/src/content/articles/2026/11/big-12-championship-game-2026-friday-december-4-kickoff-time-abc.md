**The 2026 Big 12 Championship Game is Friday, Dec. 4, 2026, kicking off at 7 p.m. CT (8 p.m. ET) on ABC from AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, the Big 12 Conference said on Nov. 9, 2026.** ESPN's college football broadcast release of May 27, 2026 lists the same night: "The Big 12 gets the ABC action started on Friday, Dec. 4, at 8 p.m., live from AT&T Stadium in Arlington."

The Big 12 calls it the "final edition of one of the Conference's cornerstone events" and "its 25th and final Championship Game." Which two teams play is not set yet.

| Time zone | Kickoff on Fri., Dec. 4 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | 8 p.m. |
| Central (CT, local in Arlington) | 7 p.m. |

| Hawaii | 3 p.m. |
| United Kingdom (GMT) | 1 a.m. Sat., Dec. 5 |

The Big 12 gives 7 p.m. CT and ESPN gives 8 p.m. ET; the other rows are computed from those. All zones are on standard time on Dec. 4.

## Why is the Big 12 title game on a Friday night?

ESPN reported on Jan. 20, 2026 that the game was moving to Friday, Dec. 4 at 8 p.m. ET, "a change from its recent home on Saturday at noon." It "will remain on ABC," ESPN wrote, and Sports Illustrated confirmed the move on Jan. 21. The Big 12's May 27, 2026 TV release then listed it as the bookend of the regular season, with a 7 p.m. CT kickoff on ABC.

Last year's game was on Saturday. The Big 12 said in its Nov. 29, 2025 release that the 2025 championship "will kick off at 11 a.m. CT on Saturday, December 6" at AT&T Stadium on ABC, which is noon ET.

## Who plays in the Big 12 Championship Game?

Not decided. On the Big 12's standings page on Nov. 19, 2026, Utah was 7-0 in conference play, Texas Tech 6-1 and Brigham Young University (BYU) 5-2. The top two teams in the conference standings meet, and ties are broken by the Big 12's published tiebreaker policy, posted on its [2026 Football Tiebreaker Central](https://admin.big12sports.com/sports/2026/11/16/2026-football-tiebreaker-central.aspx) page. The matchup is set after the last regular-season games.

## What does "final edition" mean for the Big 12 game?

The Big 12 said the 2026 game is the 25th and final Championship Game. The conference tied the change to "the College Football Playoff's approved expansion to a 24-team field beginning with the 2027-28 season." From then on, the Big 12's "automatic qualifier will be awarded to its regular-season champion." That is the Big 12's own wording; this page does not cover the College Football Playoff (CFP) rules for 2026.

## What do Big 12 Championship Game tickets cost?

General tickets went on sale at 10 a.m. CT on Friday, Aug. 14, 2026 and "will be sold exclusively on SeatGeek," the Big 12 said on Aug. 13. Prices start at $109 for the general public and $75 for students through their university's ticket office.
