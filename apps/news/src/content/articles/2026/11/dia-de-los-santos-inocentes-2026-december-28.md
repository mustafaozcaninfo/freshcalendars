**Día de los Santos Inocentes 2026 falls on Monday, Dec. 28, the day the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB) lists as the Feast of the Holy Innocents, Martyrs.** The USCCB gives the rank as a Feast, and Saint Mary's Press shows Dec. 28, 2026 as a Monday. The tables below add the weekday of Dec. 28 through 2032, the Christmas-season dates around it and what the town of Ibi, Spain, says about its flour battle that day.

## When is the Feast of the Holy Innocents in 2026?

The USCCB's page for December 28 carries the heading "Feast of the Holy Innocents, Martyrs." Saint Mary's Press lists the Gospel for Dec. 28, 2026 as Matthew 2:13-18. In that passage, as the USCCB Bible prints it, "When Herod realized that he had been deceived by the magi, he became furious," and he ordered the killing of boys two years old and under in and around Bethlehem. Verse 18 begins "A voice was heard in Ramah."

Dec. 28 is not a holy day of obligation in the United States. The USCCB lists six: Jan. 1 (Mary, Mother of God), the Ascension, Aug. 15, Nov. 1, Dec. 8 and Dec. 25.

## What is the flour battle in Ibi on Dec. 28?

The Ayuntamiento de Ibi, the town hall in the province of Alicante, writes: "El 28 de diciembre se celebra la fiesta de «Los Enharinados» («Els Enfarinats»)," which means the festival of the Floured Ones is held on Dec. 28. Its page adds that the route ends after 2 p.m.

The Generalitat Valenciana's tourism site says the winter festival's central day is Dec. 28, "coincidentes con el día de los Santos Inocentes" (the same day as the Holy Innocents), and that the festival runs from Dec. 13 (Santa Lucía) to Jan. 6. For 2026 that is Dec. 13, 2026 to Jan. 6, 2027.

Ibi has not posted a 2026 program or start time, so none is listed here. Check the [town hall's winter festival page](https://www.ibi.es/turismo/fiestas-de-invierno-enfarinats-tapats-danzas/) before you plan a trip.

## Is Dec. 28 a public holiday in Spain?

Dec. 28 is not on the national list for 2026. A resolution of Nov. 18, 2025, published in the Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE) on Nov. 24, 2025, sets the 2026 calendar of non-working days for the General State Administration. It lists Jan. 1, Jan. 6, April 3, May 1, Oct. 12, Dec. 8 and Dec. 25 nationwide. That calendar governs administrative deadlines; it is not the labor calendar, and we did not check regional or other countries' rules.
