Niagara Falls Tourism lists two fireworks shows at 8pm and midnight on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, while the City of Calgary and the Old Port of Montréal list midnight fireworks, according to each organizer's event page. Calgary's event is free and starts at 7 p.m. Below: the three cities side by side, what Toronto has and has not said, and what midnight in each place is on another coast.

## Where are the Canada New Year's Eve fireworks on Dec. 31, 2026?

All times are local: Calgary time in Calgary, Eastern Time (ET) in Niagara Falls and Montreal. Alberta did not turn its clocks back on Nov. 1, 2026, so this winter Calgary is one hour behind Eastern Time, not two.

| City | Where | Earlier show | Midnight fireworks | Free? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Calgary](https://www.calgary.ca/csps/recreation/events/new-years-eve.html) | Eau Claire Plaza and Prince's Island Park | 8 p.m. countdown for kids; activities from 7 p.m. | Yes, from Prince's Island Park | Yes, per the City of Calgary | City of Calgary |
| [Niagara Falls](https://niagarafallstourism.com/holidays/new-years-eve) | Queen Victoria Park (concert) | Fireworks at 8pm | Yes | Page lists no price for the concert | Niagara Falls Tourism |
| [Montreal](https://www.oldportcorporation.com/node/2554) | Jacques-Cartier Pier, Old Port | Music from 7:30 p.m. | Yes, at midnight | Page lists no price | Old Port of Montréal |

## What is on in Calgary on New Year's Eve 2026?

The City of Calgary says to "ring in 2027 with free festivities at Eau Claire Plaza and Prince's Island Park." Starting at 7 p.m. there are family-friendly activities, live entertainment, food trucks and an outdoor skating and dance party, with the countdown for kids at 8 p.m. The midnight fireworks launch from Prince's Island Park and are timed to music on 90.9 FM. The city's page does not name performers.

## What does Niagara Falls have on Dec. 31, 2026?

Niagara Falls Tourism lists "two spectacular fireworks shows at 8pm and midnight," plus light displays and events through the evening. A concert at Queen Victoria Park is headlined by country singer Riley Green, with The Reklaws, Tenille Townes and Sacha. The page does not list a price or a start time for the concert.

The tourism office's fireworks page says the show is "extended to nine minutes" on New Year's Eve. The Winter Festival of Lights runs through the season; its nightly fireworks are in our [Niagara Winter Festival of Lights schedule](/news/niagara-falls-winter-festival-of-lights-2026-27-fireworks-schedule/).

## What is the Grand Minuit 2027 program in Montreal?

The Old Port of Montréal says it will again gather at the Jacques-Cartier Pier to celebrate the New Year, with a countdown at midnight. Its Grand Minuit page lists this running order:

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:30 p.m. | DJ Tupi Collective |
| 9 p.m. | Qualité Motel |
| 10:30 p.m. | A medley with performers including Patrick Watson, Anachnid and Boogàt |
| Midnight | Fireworks |
| 12:10 a.m. | High Klassified |
| 1 a.m. | Nana Zen |
| 2 a.m. | End of the event |

The Old Port page does not state a price; it links to grandminuit.ca.

## Is Toronto's New Year's Eve 2026 announced?

Not yet. The City of Toronto's New Year's Eve page, last modified Sept. 17, 2026, says only "See you on December 31." Treat the following as last year's plan, not this year's: on Dec. 31, 2025 the City's release said a free waterfront celebration began at 10 p.m. and that at midnight it would present a 10-minute fireworks show.

On transit, the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) said in December 2025 that service would be free from 7 p.m. on Wed., Dec. 31 until 8 a.m. on Thu., Jan. 1. The TTC's 2026 holiday page has no Dec. 31 entry yet and lists Sunday service on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 (New Year's Day).

We found no 2026 program yet for Ottawa, Vancouver, Edmonton, Halifax or Quebec City.

## What is midnight in Calgary on another coast?

Calgary's midnight is 1 a.m. in Montreal and Niagara Falls, because Alberta stayed on its summer clock this winter and is one hour behind Eastern Time. Eastern midnight is 11 p.m. in Calgary and 9 p.m. in the U.S. Pacific time zone.

| Moment | Eastern Time | Calgary | U.S. Pacific |
|---|---|---|---|
| Niagara Falls first fireworks, 8 p.m. ET | 8 p.m. | 7 p.m. | 5 p.m. |
| Calgary kids' countdown, 8 p.m. Calgary time | 9 p.m. | 8 p.m. | 6 p.m. |
| Midnight in Niagara Falls and Montreal | 12 a.m. | 11 p.m. | 9 p.m. |
| Midnight in Calgary | 1 a.m. | 12 a.m. | 10 p.m. |
