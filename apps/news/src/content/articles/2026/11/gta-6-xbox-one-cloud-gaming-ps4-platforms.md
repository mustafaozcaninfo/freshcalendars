**An Xbox One can stream Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI starting at 1 a.m. ET on Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, according to Microsoft's Xbox Wire post of Nov. 10.** The game is not listed as a native release for Xbox One or PS4. Take-Two Interactive's announcement names PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Microsoft's words: "Grand Theft Auto VI will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, starting at 1am ET."

## Where GTA 6 can be played at launch

| Platform | Status at launch | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PS5 | Released Nov. 19, 2026 | PlayStation |
| PS5 Pro | Released Nov. 19, 2026, as "PS5 Pro Enhanced" | PlayStation |
| Xbox Series X | Native play, available to pre-install | Microsoft |
| Xbox Series S | Native play, available to pre-install | Microsoft |
| Xbox One | Cloud streaming only | Microsoft |
| PS4 | Not listed | PlayStation, Take-Two |
| PC | Not announced | Take-Two |
| Nintendo Switch 2 | Not announced | Take-Two |

Take-Two's June 24 announcement says the game is "Launching November 19, 2026, for the PlayStation 5 computer entertainment systems and Xbox Series X|S." Its Nov. 9 earnings release lists "PS5, Xbox Series X|S" for the title, with no PC row. Rockstar's game page also lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. For the PC question, see [GTA 6 PC release date](/news/gta-6-pc-release-date-rockstar-console-first/).

## How does GTA 6 run on Xbox Cloud Gaming?

Microsoft says "The game uses Xbox Series S graphics settings when cloud-streamed with Xbox Cloud Gaming." It also says that starting Nov. 11, "players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a Game Pass subscription."

The post adds that "Cloud gaming availability, purchasing options, supported devices, account eligibility, and features may vary by market and device. Grand Theft Auto VI must be purchased separately."

## What does the PS5 Pro version add?

PlayStation's page labels the game "PS5 Pro Enhanced," with improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows. The page gives no frame rate or resolution number. It also says "Remote Play supported."

## Is GTA 6 on Game Pass or PlayStation Plus, and is there an online mode?

Neither subscription is mentioned as including the game. Microsoft says it must be purchased separately, and inclusion in Game Pass or PlayStation Plus is not announced.

On online play, PlayStation's question-and-answer section asks whether the game has multiplayer modes or features and answers: "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience." No online mode or date is announced. The same page says online features require an account for PlayStation and are subject to terms of service.
