## The Midi Golf theme

The title is the one the puzzle carries as published. Read it as a nudge: two answers are golf scores, but their clues point somewhere else (military slang and a musical), so a clue that sounds like plain trivia may have a golf meaning too.

## Solving tips

Start with the ten three-letter entries: 1-Across, 17-Across, 27-Across, 30-Across and 32-Across, plus 6-Down, 7-Down, 12-Down, 15-Down and 29-Down. 1-Across and 1-Down share their first square. The two 10-letter Down entries, 3-Down and 5-Down, sit in the third and eighth columns and each cross ten Across answers, so every letter you place in one of them is checked by a second entry. 14-Across, the eight-letter entry, crosses both of them.
