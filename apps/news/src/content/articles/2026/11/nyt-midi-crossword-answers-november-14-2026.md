**The New York Times (NYT) Midi Crossword for Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026, is titled "Wine Selections" and has 38 clues on an 11-by-9 grid, credited to Jeremy Newton.** Each clue gets a hint behind a tap, and the answers sit in a marked section at the bottom.

## What "Wine Selections" asks of the solver

Three clues share an opening: "Wine best enjoyed while..." followed by a place or activity and a question mark. They are 14-Across, 19-Across and 24-Across, and each answer fills 7 squares. Read the second half of each clue as a scene, then look for an ordinary word or phrase that fits it and whose tail also works as a clipped wine name.

The three entries sit in rows 3, 5 and 7 of the 9-row grid and step to the left as you go down: they start in columns 5, 3 and 1. The grid's 5-Down is the one 9-letter Down that crosses all three, so any letter you place there pays off in the theme.

The three 9-letter Downs, 3-Down, 5-Down and 7-Down, are the visual decoys. They run through the theme rows at columns 3, 6 and 9, yet their clues carry no question mark and no wine.

## The least literal clue today

24-Across: *Wine best enjoyed while visiting Disney World?* The question mark warns that "visiting" is not a plain verb here. Split the answer into two pieces. The first is a man's first name; the second is a clipped name for a red grape, which Friday's Midi also leaned on. Together, the two pieces also form a casual phrase for strolling into a room, which is how the clue disguises the wine.
