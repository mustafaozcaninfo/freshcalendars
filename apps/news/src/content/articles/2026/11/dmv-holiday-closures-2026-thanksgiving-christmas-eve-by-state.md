**Six of the seven DMV pages we read list Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, as a closure: California, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, the District of Columbia, Washington and Indiana.** Maryland's Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA) says it closes Thursday, Nov. 26, and "will resume normal operations on Friday, November 27." The table gives each state's Thanksgiving and Christmas dates from its own page, read Nov. 25, 2026, and a printable checklist carries the Black Friday column. The date itself is on [When is Thanksgiving?](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

"Not on the closure list" and "Not listed" mean the date is absent from that state's page, which is not a promise that offices are open. Maryland is the only state whose page says normal operations on Nov. 27.

## Which states close the DMV on Black Friday?

Six DMV pages name Nov. 27. California lists "Thursday, November 26 and Friday, November 27 – Thanksgiving." North Carolina closes "Friday, Nov. 27, 2026." Pennsylvania's PennDOT Driver and Vehicle Services lists "November 27, 2026 – Day after Thanksgiving." The District of Columbia DMV says all its locations are closed "on Thursday, November 26, and Friday, November 27, 2026." Washington's Department of Licensing (DOL) calls its Nov. 27 closure Native American Heritage Day. The Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) lists "Lincoln's Birthday Friday November 27, 2026," a holiday Indiana moves to that date. Texas state offices also close that day on the state's schedule, which is not the same as a driver license office (see below).

So the split is plain: California, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, the District of Columbia, Washington and Indiana close, while Maryland says normal operations resume. Confirm the hours on your state's site before you go.

## Is the DMV open on Christmas Eve 2026?

Thursday, Dec. 24 is a closure in two of the seven DMV pages. North Carolina lists "Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026," and Indiana lists "Washington's Birthday Thursday December 24, 2026," another holiday it moves to a December date. California, Pennsylvania, the District of Columbia and Washington do not list Dec. 24, and Maryland's notice covers only Thanksgiving. Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, is a closure on every page that lists December dates. North Carolina adds Monday, Dec. 28, 2026, so its offices are shut for five days in a row with the weekend. The state-office story on [Christmas Eve by state](/news/state-offices-closed-christmas-eve-2026-by-state/) covers other state workers, and the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the dates.

## What does the Texas row mean?

The Texas Comptroller's state holiday schedule lists "Thanksgiving Day | Office closed | 11-26-26," "Day after Thanksgiving | Office closed | 11-27-26," "Christmas Eve Day | Office closed | 12-24-26," "Christmas Day | Office closed | 12-25-26" and "New Year's Day | Office closed | 01-01-27." It is the schedule for state agencies. The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS), which runs driver license offices, has no 2026 notice we could read, so check DPS before you go.

## What can you do online in Maryland over the holiday?

Maryland's notice, dated Nov. 17, 2026, says customers can complete "more than 60 transactions with myMVAOnline Services accounts." Its self-service Vehicle Emissions Inspection Program (VEIP) kiosks "are available 24 hours each day, throughout the holiday weekend." The notice does not give December dates.

## States that have not posted 2026 closures

We did not find a 2026 holiday closure page for New York, Florida, the Georgia Department of Driver Services, the Illinois Secretary of State, the Ohio BMV, Virginia, the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission, the Michigan Secretary of State, Arizona's Motor Vehicle Division, the Massachusetts Registry of Motor Vehicles, Oregon, Tennessee, Colorado, Minnesota, Missouri and Kentucky. They are not marked closed or open here. Check your state's page before you go. Many county-run offices, including New York county DMVs and Florida tax collectors, set their own closures.
