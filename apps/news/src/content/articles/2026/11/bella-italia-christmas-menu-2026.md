**Bella Italia's Christmas menu runs from Tuesday 10 November 2026 to Wednesday 6 January 2027 at £22.95 for two courses or £27.95 for three, according to the Bella Italia Christmas page.** Twelve restaurants open on Christmas Day, walk-in only. Our research desk read the Bella Italia pages on 22 November; the dates, prices and offers below follow their wording and Retail Times' report of 18 November.

## What does the Bella Italia Christmas menu cost in 2026?

The Christmas page says: "Our Celebration Menu is 2 courses for £22.95, or 3 courses for £27.95 at all locations."

| Option | Price | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 2 courses | £22.95 | Bella Italia Christmas page |
| 3 courses | £27.95 | Bella Italia Christmas page |
| Difference | £5.00 | 3-course price minus 2-course price |

The page names Christmas Camembert Pizza, Christmas Carbonara, Truffle and Pumpkin Ravioli and Mince Pie Cheesecake. It does not give prices or courses for them, and no supplements, so none are shown here. Its line "Kids Eat Free Sunday to Thursday!" is a general offer; the terms are not on the page and no kids' Christmas menu price is given.

## Which Bella Italia festive dates matter in 2026?

The last column counts days after Sunday 22 November, the day the pages were read.

| Date | What happens | Days after 22 Nov |
|---|---|---|
| Tue. 10 Nov 2026 | Christmas menu starts | Already started |
| Tue. 24 Nov 2026 | Bottomless Carbonara starts | 2 |

## Which Bella Italia restaurants open on Christmas Day 2026?

The Christmas page says "12 Bella Italias open on Christmas Day", on a walk-in basis only. They are Edinburgh Hanover, Edinburgh Northbridge and ten restaurants in London. Christmas Day is a Friday in 2026. The page does not say the rest of the chain opens, and it gives no Boxing Day, New Year's Eve or New Year's Day hours.

## How do Bella Italia's bottomless offers work?

Bella Italia's homepage lists "Bottomless Carbonara: 24–26 November" and "Bottomless Pigs in Blankets: 10 December". Retail Times, on 18 November, added the terms in the table. It also reported Bottomless Dough Balls from Tuesday 17 to Thursday 19 November, which has ended.

| Term | Retail Times report |
|---|---|
| Bottomless Carbonara | Buy any drink and pay full price for the first Carbonara; average cost £17 |
| Carbonara exclusions | "Excludes Premium and Festive Carbonara dishes" |
| Bottomless Pigs in Blankets | Average cost £5.29, with a main from the A La Carte menu; tied to Festive Jumper Day |
| Time limit | "All three bottomless offers are available for 90 minutes" |
| Other terms | Subject to availability; cannot be combined with other promotions or discounts |
| Excluded sites | Center Parcs locations, London Paddington and Edinburgh Northbridge |
| How to claim | Show the promotional email or social post |

Edinburgh Northbridge is on both lists: open on Christmas Day, excluded from the bottomless offers.

## What gift card and group booking offers has Bella Italia announced?

The homepage says: "20% extra value on all gift cards purchased between 2–26 November 2026." For parties, the Christmas page says groups of up to 20 book online ("No enquiry needed!"), while a prompt on the same page reads "Group of up to 19?". Groups of 21 or more enquire. Deposit and pre-order rules are not given on the pages we read.
