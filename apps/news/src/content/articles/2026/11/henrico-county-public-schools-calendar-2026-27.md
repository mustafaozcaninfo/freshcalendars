**Henrico County Public Schools (HCPS) in Virginia lists "Schools closed - Winter Break" for Dec. 21-31, 2026 and Jan. 1, 2027, and schools reopen Monday, Jan. 4, 2027**, according to the "2026 - 2027 School Calendar" on the district's Calendars page. Thanksgiving closings come first, Wednesday to Friday, Nov. 25-27. The last day of school is Friday, June 4, 2027. The staggered first days by grade, every student no-school weekday counted and a printable follow.

## What is next on the Henrico County schools calendar?

| Dates | What the district lists |
|---|---|
| Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27, 2026 | "Schools closed - Thanksgiving Holiday." |
| Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | "Schools closed - Winter Break." |
| Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | "Schools reopen following Winter Break." |
| Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | "Schools closed - Martin Luther King Jr. Day." |

The weeks around the break are on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When do Henrico students start school in 2026?

Start days are staggered by grade, according to the district's calendar page.

| Date | Who starts |
|---|---|
| Mon., Aug. 24, 2026 | Half of pre-K and kindergarten students; grades 1-5; grades 6 and 9 |
| Tue., Aug. 25, 2026 | Second half of pre-K and kindergarten students; grades 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12 |
| Wed., Aug. 26, 2026 | "All students attend" |

On Aug. 25, grades 1-5, 6 and 9 also attend. The page does not say how pre-K and kindergarten families are split between the two days.

## Every HCPS student no-school day in 2026-27

The table lists each weekday students are off, using the district's labels. Weekends are skipped, and Dec. 25 and Jan. 1 fall inside winter break, so they add nothing extra.

By month, that is 1 in September, 6 in November, 9 in December, 3 in January, 2 in February, 4 in March, 2 in April and 1 in May, or 28 weekdays. October and August have none. The count follows from the district's list; the page prints no total. It also lists half days on Fri., March 26 and Fri., June 4, which are not counted as days off.

## Which Henrico days off are observances and Wellness Days?

HCPS lists three observance days as student holidays: Yom Kippur on Monday, Sept. 21, 2026, Diwali on Monday, Nov. 9, 2026 and Eid al-Fitr on Wednesday, March 10, 2027. Each is labeled "Student and teacher holiday." It also lists two Wellness Days, Monday, Nov. 2, 2026 and Monday, Feb. 15, 2027, with "Schools and central offices closed." Feb. 15, 2027 is the date of the federal Presidents Day, but the district lists it only as a Wellness Day.

## When is HCPS winter break 2026?

The calendar lists Dec. 21-31, 2026 and Jan. 1, 2027 as "Schools closed - Winter Break," ten weekdays. "Schools reopen following Winter Break" on Monday, Jan. 4, 2027. Monday, Jan. 18 follows as "Schools closed - Martin Luther King Jr. Day," and Jan. 20 is the end of the second quarter.

## When is HCPS spring break 2027?

Spring Break is listed for March 29-April 2, 2027, Monday through Friday. The page prints no return date; the next weekday after the break is Monday, April 5. Friday, March 26 is a student half day ("End of quarter/Student half day/Flexible staff clerical day*"). See the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html), and compare other districts on the [spring break 2027 list](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

## When is the last day of school in Henrico County?

"End of quarter/Student half day/Last day of school" is listed for Friday, June 4, 2027. Memorial Day, Monday, May 31, 2027, is "Schools closed" the week before. The district lists quarter ends on Oct. 28, 2026, Jan. 20, March 26 and June 4, 2027. The calendar page lists no make-up or weather days, and the 2027-28 calendar is a separate page not covered here.
