The California International Marathon (CIM) starts at 7:00 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) on Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, near Folsom Dam in Folsom, and finishes at the State Capitol in Sacramento, according to the race's General Information page. Wheeled athletes and ambulatory athletes with disabilities go first, at 6:53 a.m. The finish line closes at 1:30 p.m. sharp.

The Sacramento Running Association (SRA) organizes the race. It says CIM is certified and sanctioned by USA Track & Field (USATF) and is a Boston Marathon qualifying course, and that it will host the 2026 USATF Road Marathon Championships on Dec. 6. Elite start times, a TV or stream plan, a 5K and a kids run were not announced on the race site when we checked on Nov. 21, 2026.

## What is the CIM race-morning timeline?

The table lists the official times in order, then adds our estimate of where the last runner allowed on the course is at each point. The estimate is our calculation, not a race figure: it starts the clock at 7:30 a.m., when the start line closes and sweep runners begin, and holds the 13:44 minutes per mile pace the race uses for reopening roads.

| Time (PT) | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 6:53 a.m. | Wheeled athletes and ambulatory athletes with disabilities start | Race info page |
| 7:00 a.m. | Marathon mass start near Folsom Dam | Race info page |
| 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. | Eight light rail stations closed | SacRT |
| 7:30 a.m. | Start line closes; sweep runners start | Race info page |
| About 10:30 a.m. | Mile 13.1 at 13:44 per mile from 7:30 a.m. (our estimate) | Our calculation |
| About 10:35 a.m. | Mile 13.5, near the start of relay leg 2 (our estimate) | Our calculation |
| About 12:05 p.m. | Mile 20 (our estimate) | Our calculation |
| 1:30 p.m. | Finish line closes; a 6-hour finish at 13:44 per mile (our estimate: mile 26.2 about 1:30 p.m.) | Race info page |

The race says a California Highway Patrol officer in a vehicle traveling at about 13:44 minutes per mile will open the roads behind the car to regular traffic. Relay leg 2 starts near the 13.5-mile mark, the race's information page says.

## What time is the CIM start in other time zones?

The 7:00 a.m. PT mass start is 8:00 a.m. Mountain Time (MT), 9:00 a.m. Central Time (CT) and 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time (ET). The 6:53 a.m. wheeled start is 9:53 a.m. ET, and the 1:30 p.m. finish close is 4:30 p.m. ET. The race gives its times in Pacific Time, and the start-line close of 7:30 a.m. is 10:30 a.m. ET.

## Which light rail stations close on CIM day?

Sacramento Regional Transit (SacRT) says these stations are closed from 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. on Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026:

- Archives Plaza
- 9th & K
- 8th & O
- 8th & Capitol
- 7th & Capitol
- 8th & K
- 7th & I
- Sacramento Valley Station

SacRT adds that several bus routes and light rail lines will be temporarily affected over the same hours. Check [SacRT's CIM page](https://www.sacrt.com/CIM) before you travel.

## What are the CIM road closures and shuttle times?

The race lists closures on its road-closure page, which says times are subject to change. It shows, for example, the Folsom Lake Crossing closed from 2 a.m., Capitol Mall from 4 a.m. to 7 p.m. and L Street from 4 a.m. to 3 p.m. The race has not dated that page 2026, and a dated 2026 notice has not been posted, so confirm each street in race week.

The race site also lists shuttles for race morning, and has not dated that page 2026 either. Location A, the Convention Center at K Street and 13th Street, has buses leaving by 4:30 a.m.; Location B, 1780 Tribute Road, by 5 a.m.; Locations C and D, 261 and 2475 Iron Point Road, between 5 a.m. and 5:30 a.m.; and Location E, 6845 Douglas Boulevard, between 5:15 a.m. and 6 a.m.

## Where is the CIM Expo and when can you pick up a bib?

The CIM Expo is at the convention center at 1400 J Street, Sacramento, in Hall D, with entry through the North Lobby on J Street.

| Day | Hours (PT) |
|---|---|
| Fri., Dec. 4, 2026 | 1:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. PT (opens 4 p.m. ET) |
| Sat., Dec. 5, 2026 | 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. PT (opens noon ET) |

All packets must be picked up at the Expo; there is no race-day pick-up, and every runner needs a government-issued photo ID. A proxy needs a copy of the runner's final instructions, the QR code and a copy of the runner's photo ID. Relay packets cannot be divided for individual pick-up.

## Is CIM 2026 sold out?

The race says its three standard registration tiers, Blue ($180), Orange ($200) and Gold ($230) plus fees, sold out "at record speed." Transfers were available until Nov. 16. The relay price was not on the pages we checked.

## Where can spectators follow CIM?

The race says to follow runners and view results through its official tracking app, and it partners with Waze for real-time traffic around closures. Spectators are not permitted in the start area.
