**The Home Depot's Black Friday Savings Event runs Thursday, Nov. 5, through Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, in stores and online, per the company's Oct. 29 announcement.** The event is 21 days long and ends the day before Thanksgiving. Home Depot's 2026 holiday hours page lists stores as closed on Thanksgiving and open at 6 a.m. on Black Friday. The table below puts the sale phases and store hours in one place, and there is a one-page printable.

## Home Depot sale dates and store hours, Oct. 29 to Jan. 1

| Date | Day | What The Home Depot published |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 29, 2026 | Thursday | Gift savings begin and last throughout the season (Sept. 29 release) |
| Nov. 5, 2026 | Thursday | Black Friday Savings Event begins, in store and online |
| Nov. 25, 2026 | Wednesday | Black Friday Savings Event ends |
| Nov. 26, 2026 | Thursday | Thanksgiving: stores closed |
| Nov. 27, 2026 | Friday | Black Friday: stores open at 6 a.m. |
| Dec. 24, 2026 | Thursday | Christmas Eve: stores close at 5 p.m. |
| Dec. 25, 2026 | Friday | Christmas Day: stores closed |
| Dec. 31, 2026 | Thursday | New Year's Eve: stores close at 6 p.m. |
| Jan. 1, 2027 | Friday | New Year's Day: stores open 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. |

The weekdays and the 21-day length are our computation. Our [Black Friday date page](/when-is/black-friday/) confirms Nov. 27, and you can mark the sale on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## What is in the Home Depot Black Friday Savings Event?

The release names three groups of offers. Product selection, pricing and promotions "may vary between stores and online," the company says.

| Group | Offers named in the release |
|---|---|
| Power tools and gifts | Select power tool buy one, get one (BOGO) offers: buy a participating tool or battery kit from participating brands, including Milwaukee, and get a free select power tool. Savings on Hubspace smart home products, tool kits and accessories |
| Holiday decor | 6.5-foot light-emitting diode (LED) pre-lit artificial Christmas trees for $49.98; select 3.5-foot LED pre-lit inflatables for $19.98; 100-count LED string lights as low as $5.98 |
| Major appliances | Up to an extra $500 on refrigeration purchases of $2,498 or more; up to $300 on laundry of $1,698 or more; up to $300 on cooking of $1,498 or more; up to $150 on dishwashers of $798 or more; maximum discount $1,250 |

Rodes Bazzel, senior vice president of merchandising at The Home Depot, said: "Black Friday is an amazing opportunity for customers to find great value on all they need for the holiday season."

## Is Home Depot open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. The Home Depot's 2026 holiday hours list Thursday, Nov. 26, as closed. Stores open at 6 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 27. The event ends Wednesday, Nov. 25, the day before Thanksgiving. For other retailers' Thanksgiving and Black Friday hours, see our [Black Friday 2026 store hours guide](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/).

## What is the difference between the Oct. 29 savings and the Nov. 5 event?

The company's Sept. 29 holiday decor release says: "Customers can find savings on gifts for everyone on their lists beginning Oct. 29 and lasting throughout the season." That release also says holiday decor reaches stores beginning the week of Oct. 5. The Black Friday Savings Event is the separate Nov. 5 to 25 event described in the Oct. 29 announcement. Lowe's, which also starts deals on Oct. 29, publishes a different schedule; see our [Lowe's Black Friday 2026 story](/news/lowes-black-friday-2026-deals-start-october-29-12-days-of-deals-november-16/). Our [Black Friday 2026 ad tracker](/news/black-friday-2026-ad-tracker-walmart-target-best-buy/) follows the other big chains.

## What has Home Depot not announced?

- The end time of the event on Nov. 25.
- Whether a separate Black Friday or Cyber Monday sale follows after Nov. 25.
- Per-day doorbusters.
