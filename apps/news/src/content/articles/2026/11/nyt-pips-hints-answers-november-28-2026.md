Easy carries three doubles out of five dominoes and skips 2 and 4 entirely. Medium holds no 3 and has three values that appear exactly three times, which matches its two three-cell equal blocks and a three-cell block that adds up to 12. ## The forced first move on each board

Cells are named by row, then column, from the top left. A search of every legal placement against the saved data finds one arrangement for Easy and Hard, and two for Medium that differ only in which way two dominoes lie.

**Easy.** R1C1 adds up to 1 alone, so it holds a 1, and its only neighbor is R2C1. The set has two 1s: one for R1C1, the other for R3C3 or R4C3, which add up to 1 together. The set's lone 3 is the catch. Next to R3C3 or R4C3 it would land on R3C2, R4C2 or R4C4, and each of those sits in a region (equal pair, or a sum of 6) that would need a second 3. So the 1-3 domino goes at the top, and the 6-1 domino goes beside R3C3.

**Medium.** R2C3 and R2C4 total less than 1, so both hold 0. The set carries exactly two 0s, one in 1-0 and one in 0-5, so the two dominoes that carry them go there. In the search's placement, both stand on end, 1-0 on column 3 and 0-5 on column 4, which also settles R1C4 (more than 1).

**Hard.** R1C4, R2C2, R4C1 and R6C3 each add up to 0 by themselves. The set holds four 0s, in 0-3, 5-0, 2-0 and 4-0, so each of those dominoes has one half on one of those cells. The four-cell equal block in the top left cannot use 0 and must take a value that appears at least four times.

## Pips hints for Nov. 28, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 6-6, 5-5, 6-1, 1-3, 0-0)**

- Hint 1: R1C1 adds up to 1 by itself.
- Hint 2: R3C3 and R4C3 add up to 1 as well, which uses up both 1s in the set.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 6-1, 1-0, 2-2, 0-5, 2-6, 4-4, 6-4)**

- Hint 1: R2C3 and R2C4 together total less than 1.
- Hint 2: The set holds two 0s, so 1-0 and 0-5 are the dominoes that go there.

**Hard (16 dominoes on a frame 8 rows by 4 columns)**

- Hint 1: R1C4, R2C2, R4C1 and R6C3 each add up to 0.
- Hint 2: The set has four 0s, so every one is used by those cells.

## Which Pips dominoes can be turned around on Nov. 28

No domino on Easy, Medium or Hard swaps ends without breaking a rule. Medium has one other flexibility: two dominoes can trade between lying down and standing up, with every cell holding the same number. The placements below print the Times' own data for each domino.
