**Cabarrus County Schools students are out Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 by our reading of the district's 2026-27 Traditional Academic Calendar, and spring break is March 8-12, 2027.** The calendar's own Winter Break line reads "December 23, 2026 - Jan 1, 2027," but Dec. 21-22 and Jan. 4 are staff development days, and the district says students are out of school on all of them.

The dates come from the district's [plain text calendar](https://www.cabarrus.k12.nc.us/58075_4) and its [PDF](https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/3048/2026-2027_ccs_academic_calendar_approved_10132025.pdf), both read Nov. 13, 2026; the PDF's file name dates the approval Oct. 13, 2025. Weekdays and return days are our calendar check. The first day of school for all students was Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026.

## Cabarrus County Schools breaks in 2026-27

| Break | Students out | Students return (our reading) |
|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving Break | Wed., Nov. 25 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter break, with staff development days | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon., March 8 to Fri., March 12, 2027 | Mon., March 15, 2027 |

Plan the weeks on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html).

## When is Cabarrus County Schools' winter break in 2026?

The district's Winter Break line runs from Wednesday, Dec. 23, 2026 to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, eight weekdays by our count.

Students are out longer. The district lists staff development days on Monday and Tuesday, Dec. 21-22, and on Monday, Jan. 4, and states: "Capped/Uncapped designations for staff only. Students are out of school on all staff development days." That makes 11 weekdays without school, from Monday, Dec. 21 to Monday, Jan. 4, and students return Tuesday, Jan. 5. The calendar does not print a return date; that follows from its entries. Quarter 2 ends Friday, Dec. 18, with early dismissal.

Every break, holiday and staff development day on the traditional calendar, with weekdays. Entries before Nov. 13 are past.

The calendar has no line named Presidents' Day, and the staff development days on Feb. 15-16, 2027 are the nearest entries. Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, is listed among the district's breaks and holidays as an uncapped workday for staff; the calendar does not separately say whether students attend, so it is not in the table.

## Which Cabarrus County weekends run long in 2026-27?

By our count from the dates above:

| Weekend | Days off | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 5-7, 2026 | Sat.-Mon. | Labor Day |
| Oct. 10-13, 2026 | Sat.-Tue. | Staff development days Oct. 12-13 |
| Jan. 16-18, 2027 | Sat.-Mon. | Martin Luther King Jr. Day |
| Feb. 13-16, 2027 | Sat.-Tue. | Staff development days Feb. 15-16 |
| March 26-29, 2027 | Fri.-Mon. | Good Friday and a staff development day |
| April 16-19, 2027 | Fri.-Mon. | Staff development days |
| May 29-31, 2027 | Sat.-Mon. | Memorial Day |

Good Friday, March 26, and Monday, March 29, 2027 give a four-day weekend, and so do Friday, April 16 and Monday, April 19. The calendar lists no Easter Monday.

## Which days have early dismissal?

The calendar states: "Each end-of-quarter day includes early dismissal (capped for staff)." Quarter 1 ends Oct. 9, 2026, Quarter 2 ends Dec. 18, 2026 and Quarter 3 ends March 19, 2027, all Fridays. The PDF also marks Wednesday, May 26, 2027, the last day of school for traditional students, as "End Of Quarter; Early Dismissal."

## What is the school year length and the weather make-up rule?

The PDF lists Semester 1 at 84 days, Semester 2 at 90 days and 1045.76 total hours of instruction. Its inclement weather note says North Carolina law requires that all hours missed be made up to provide 1,025 hours of instruction per academic year, and that up to 30 hours may be used for remote learning. The calendar lists no make-up days.

## What about the Early College and Restart schools?

The district posts a separate Early College calendar, marked "Approved 10/13/2025" in its PDF. It uses the same dates for Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Break, winter break, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, spring break and Good Friday. The last day of school for Early Colleges is Thursday, May 13, 2027, and graduation is Saturday, May 15 at West Cabarrus High School.

A separate Restart Calendar PDF covers a group of schools the district lists. Those schools follow different dates that this story does not cover. Traditional high school exams run Dec. 14-18, 2026 and May 20, 21 and 24-26, 2027, and graduation ceremonies are May 26, 27 and 28, 2027 at Cabarrus Arena & Events Center.

## How does Cabarrus County compare with Charlotte-Mecklenburg and Guilford County?

| District | Winter break (district's wording) | Spring break | Last day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cabarrus County Schools | Dec. 23, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, with staff days Dec. 21-22 and Jan. 4 | March 8-12, 2027 | May 26, 2027 |
| [Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools (CMS)](/news/charlotte-mecklenburg-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 | March 29 to April 2, 2027 | June 9, 2027 |
| [Guilford County Schools (GCS)](/news/guilford-county-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, plus a Jan. 4 workday | March 22-26, 2027 | June 10, 2027 |

Cabarrus County's spring break comes the earliest of the three and its last day is two weeks or more before the others, by the dates each district lists. For Wake County, see the [Wake County Public School System calendar](/news/wake-county-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-traditional/).
