## Tips for guessing today

Four different letters fill five tiles, so a first guess made of five different letters can test all four at most. A word with a doubled tile punishes a guess that uses each letter once: you may see one green or yellow tile and miss that the letter has a twin.

Both vowel letters are the same, which means a guess loaded with a spread of different vowels will return mostly gray. Try a second guess that repeats a letter you already have in yellow or green.
