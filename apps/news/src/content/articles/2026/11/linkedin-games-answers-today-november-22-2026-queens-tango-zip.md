## Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints for Sunday, Nov. 22

- **Pinpoint #936, first hint:** all five clues describe one everyday word, each through a different meaning.
- **Pinpoint #936, second hint:** think of two things that belong together, and of a verb that doubles an amount.
- **Pinpoint #936, third hint:** one clue is about sports or games, another about a small flame.
- **Crossclimb #936, the ladder:** seven words of five letters, linked by six single-letter swaps.
- **Crossclimb #936, the locked ends:** read together, the top and bottom words form a two-word phrase about bold, decisive actions.
- **Crossclimb #936, the middle:** the first middle word is a person who acts a part, and the first swap changes the third letter.

## How many Pinpoint #936 clues to open

Each clue is another sense of a single word, and the senses are not the same part of speech: four read as nouns and one as a verb. That is our reading of the clue wording, not a LinkedIn rule. A noun-heavy list means two clues can still fit several words, so opening a third before guessing is reasonable, and the verb clue is the quickest filter for a candidate.

## Where the Crossclimb #936 letters move

Six moves, positions counted from the left, 1 to 5. No word of the ladder appears here.

| Position | Times it changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 0 |
| 5 | 1 |

The moves run through positions 3, 1, 3, 1, 5, 1, so the first letter alternates with the others for most of the climb. Positions 2 and 4 never move.

## Why the Crossclimb #936 ends make one phrase

The locked words differ in positions 1, 3 and 5 only, which would take three swaps in a straight line. The ladder takes six because the middle words return to position 1 and 3 on the way. Once the five middle words are in, the top and bottom rows read as one phrase, which is a quick way to confirm the order.

## Starting points for Queens #936, Tango #776, Zip #615 and Mini Sudoku #468

**Queens #936.** Begin with the smallest colored region and mark which rows and columns its cells share.

**Tango #776.** Scan for three cells in a line where two already match; the third has to differ.

**Zip #615.** Work backward from the highest number as well as forward from the lowest, so the two ends meet.

**Mini Sudoku #468.** Pick the row or column with the most digits placed and fill its last gap first.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in | Position |
