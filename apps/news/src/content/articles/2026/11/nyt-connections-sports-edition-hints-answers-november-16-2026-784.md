**Connections: Sports Edition #784, the Monday, Nov. 16, 2026 puzzle from The Athletic and New York Times (NYT) Games, is a board where several tiles wear two hats.** Two hints per color open one tap at a time. Answers stay below the spoiler heading.

Monday's tiles are mostly single words, and a few of them double as part of a pro team's name. The rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works).

## Monday's hints, #784, Nov. 16, 2026

Read the board once for places and once for people before you tap anything. Some tiles answer to both.

- Hint 1 (yellow): Each tile stands for a pro team from the same corner of California.
- Hint 2 (yellow): The teams play four different sports, and one of them has a championship claim for this year.
- Hint 1 (green): Every tile is the start of a city or state name, and all of them are football places.
- Hint 2 (green): One of the four tiles starts the locations of more than one team in the same league.
- Hint 1 (blue): These four surnames share one school, not one team.
- Hint 2 (blue): The league's player pages list two of them with the same college, one a quarterback and one a running back.
- Hint 1 (purple): Each tile is a thing drawn inside a basketball logo.
- Hint 2 (purple): The four are a tool, a structure, an animal and a figure from folklore.

## What makes Monday's board slippery

One tile has a second life as part of a National Football League (NFL) team name, and that is the main trap. A tile that is half a team name can look like a place word when it is really a nickname, or the other way round. A tile that is a person's surname can look like a team when it is really a school link.

Start with the group where you are surest of the rule, then see which tile still has two homes. If you track your streak on paper, a [printable weekly calendar](/printable-weekly-calendar.html) holds the week. Sunday's page is [Connections: Sports Edition #783](/news/nyt-connections-sports-edition-hints-answers-november-15-2026-783/), and the main game has its own [Connections hints and answers for Nov. 16](/news/nyt-connections-hints-answers-november-16-2026-1254/).

## Monday's answers, #784, Nov. 16, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it. Beebom and Nerdschalk list the same four groups and sixteen entries.

- **Yellow, "A Bay Area Athlete":** Earthquake, Giant, Shark, Valkyrie
- **Green, "First Word in NFL Team Locations":** Green, Kansas, New, San
- **Blue, "Stanford Alumni":** Elway, Ledecky, Luck, McCaffrey
- **Purple, "Found in NBA Team Logos":** Bridge, Horse, Leprechaun, Pickaxe

## Who each entry is

- **Earthquake:** the San Jose Earthquakes, a Major League Soccer (MLS) club that plays at PayPal Park in San Jose, per MLSSoccer.com.
- **Giant:** the San Francisco Giants, an MLB club in the National League (NL) West, per MLB.com.
- **Shark:** the San Jose Sharks of the National Hockey League (NHL), per NHL.com.
- **Valkyrie:** the Golden State Valkyries of the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA). Their website says they won the 2026 WNBA championship in Game 7 at Chase Center.
- **Green, Kansas, San, New:** NFL.com lists the Green Bay Packers, Kansas City Chiefs and San Francisco 49ers. Four teams' locations start with New: the New England Patriots, New York Giants, New York Jets and New Orleans Saints.
- **McCaffrey:** Christian McCaffrey is a running back who went to Stanford and plays for the San Francisco 49ers, per NFL.com.
- **Luck:** Andrew Luck is a quarterback who went to Stanford, per NFL.com.
- **Elway, Ledecky:** no official page we could read confirms them today. The puzzle groups them with the Stanford alumni.
- **Bridge, Horse, Leprechaun, Pickaxe:** the puzzle places each in an NBA team logo. We print no team pairing because no team page we could read describes its logo.

## Where the overlaps sit on Monday's board

| Tile | Tempting group | Where it goes | Why it tempts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Giant | First word in NFL locations | Bay Area athlete | The New York Giants are on NFL.com's team list |
| San | Bay Area athlete | NFL locations | San Jose and San Francisco clubs sit in the yellow group |
| McCaffrey | Bay Area athlete | Stanford alumni | He plays for the San Francisco 49ers |

San is the only tile with a double pull from two different teams' cities: the 49ers on the football side and the Earthquakes and Sharks on the yellow side. Giant and McCaffrey each point to a team whose city name starts with San or New, which is why neither can be placed from its team alone.
