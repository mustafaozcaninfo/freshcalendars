**The indoor team golf league founded by Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy (TGL), presented by SoFi, opens its 2027 season on Sunday, Dec. 27, 2026, with a doubleheader at 1 p.m. ET on ESPN and 3 p.m. ET on ABC, according to the league's schedule page.** The Professional Golfers' Association (PGA) Tour says the 2027 schedule has 14 regular-season matches and a new play-in match for the fourth playoff spot. The table below puts every match in Eastern, Central and Pacific time with its network, and a one-page printable schedule follows.

Two things are not set. The league lists the two Jan. 17 matches with a start time still to be determined (TBD), and the play-in, semifinal and Finals dates and times are not yet announced.

## What is on the Dec. 27 opening doubleheader?

The PGA Tour's Oct. 27, 2026 report says the season starts at SoFi Center in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida. In the first match, Boston Common Golf (Rory McIlroy) plays Motor City Golf Club (Brooks Koepka), the expansion team's league debut. In the second, Jupiter Links Golf Club (Tiger Woods) meets defending SoFi Cup champion Los Angeles Golf Club, a rematch of the 2026 Finals, per the same report.

Motor City is the seventh team in the league, according to ESPN's Oct. 1, 2026 media-rights release.

## Which TGL matches are on ABC, ESPN and ESPN2?

The schedule page assigns one network to each match. ABC carries three, up from one in 2026 per the PGA Tour.

| Network | Matches | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| ABC | 3 | Dec. 27 (3 p.m. ET), Jan. 9, Jan. 10 |
| ESPN | 7 | Dec. 27 (1 p.m. ET), Jan. 3, Jan. 12, both Jan. 17 matches, both Feb. 23 matches |
| ESPN2 | 4 | Jan. 5, Jan. 31, Feb. 9, Feb. 22 |

ESPN's Oct. 1, 2026 release says matches also stream on the ESPN app with an ESPN Unlimited subscription and in the Disney+, Hulu, ESPN bundle. The schedule page does not assign streaming to individual matches.

## Which days of the week does TGL play in 2027?

Seven of the 14 matches are on Sundays and five are on Tuesdays. The other two are Saturday, Jan. 9 and Monday, Feb. 22. The five Sunday matches with a time start between 1 p.m. and 7 p.m. ET, and all five Tuesday matches start at 5 p.m. ET or later.

## TGL playoffs 2027: play-in, semifinals and Finals

The PGA Tour's report describes the format. The new play-in match decides the fourth playoff team, and its winner joins the top three in the SoFi Cup standings for two semifinal matches. The semifinal winners meet in a best-of-three Finals. The PGA Tour, quoting the ESPN media-rights release of the same day, gives the playoff window as "March 1-23." The play-in, semifinal and Finals dates and times are not yet announced.

## TGL tickets for the 2027 season

FirstCall Golf and TheGolfWire both reported on Oct. 27, 2026 that a presale ran that day at TGLGolf.com/tickets and that general sales opened Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026 at 10 a.m. ET. Prices are not part of the sources used here.
