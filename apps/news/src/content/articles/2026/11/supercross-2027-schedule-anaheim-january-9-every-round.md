**The 2027 Supercross season opens Saturday, Jan. 9, 2027, at Angel Stadium in Anaheim, California, and runs 17 rounds to Saturday, May 15, at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City, according to the official Monster Energy American Motorcyclist Association (AMA) Supercross schedule.** Pro Motocross follows, and the SuperMotocross (SMX) World Championship Final is set for Sept. 25. TV for 2027 and start times for most rounds are not yet announced.

The table below adds the weekday, the 250 class and the marks for every round. The 450SX class is listed at every round. A one-page copy is in the downloads box with this story, and the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html) shows the opening weeks by day.

## What is the full 2027 Supercross schedule?

| Round | Date | City | Stadium | 250 class and marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sat., Jan. 9 | Anaheim, CA | Angel Stadium | 250SX West |
| 2 | Sat., Jan. 16 | San Diego, CA | Snapdragon Stadium | 250SX West |
| 3 | Sat., Jan. 23 | Anaheim, CA | Angel Stadium | 250SX West, Triple Crown |
| 4 | Sat., Jan. 30 | San Antonio, TX | Alamodome | 250SX West |
| 5 | Sat., Feb. 6 | Tampa, FL | Raymond James Stadium | 250SX East |
| 6 | Sat., Feb. 13 | Glendale, AZ | State Farm Stadium | 250 East/West Showdown |
| 7 | Sat., Feb. 20 | Detroit, MI | Ford Field | 250SX East |
| 8 | Sat., Feb. 27 | Arlington, TX | AT&T Stadium | 250SX West, Triple Crown |
| 9 | Sat., Mar. 6 | Daytona Beach, FL | Daytona International Speedway | 250SX East |
| 10 | Sat., Mar. 13 | Indianapolis, IN | Lucas Oil Stadium | 250SX East |
| 11 | Sat., Mar. 20 | Seattle, WA | Lumen Field | 250SX West |
| 12 | Sat., Apr. 3 | Foxborough, MA | Gillette Stadium | 250SX East, Triple Crown |
| 13 | Sat., Apr. 10 | Baltimore, MD | M&T Bank Stadium | 250SX East |
| 14 | Sat., Apr. 17 | East Rutherford, NJ | MetLife Stadium | 250SX East |
| 15 | Sat., Apr. 24 | Pittsburgh, PA | Acrisure Stadium | 250 East/West Showdown |
| 16 | Sat., May 8 | Denver, CO | Empower Field at Mile High | 250SX West |
| 17 | Sat., May 15 | Salt Lake City, UT | Rice-Eccles Stadium | 250 East/West Showdown |
Triple Crown and East/West Showdown are the marks the official schedule lists. The weekdays are our check of each date: all 17 rounds fall on a Saturday.

## What do the 2027 Supercross dates add up to?

These counts are ours, taken from the official schedule.

| Count | Result | Rounds |
|---|---|---|
| 250SX West rounds | 7 | 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 11 and 16 |
| 250SX East rounds | 7 | 5, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13 and 14 |
| 250 East/West Showdown | 3 | 6 (Glendale), 15 (Pittsburgh) and 17 (Salt Lake City) |
| Triple Crown | 3 | 3 (Anaheim), 8 (Arlington) and 12 (Foxborough) |
| Anaheim nights | 2 | 1 (Jan. 9) and 3 (Jan. 23) |

Rounds 1 to 11 run on 11 straight Saturdays, from Jan. 9 to Mar. 20. Saturday, Mar. 27 is open, so Seattle on Mar. 20 and Foxborough on Apr. 3 are two weeks apart. Saturday, May 1 is also open, between Pittsburgh on Apr. 24 and Denver on May 8. The last three rounds are Pittsburgh, Denver and Salt Lake City. California hosts three rounds (Anaheim twice and San Diego), and Texas and Florida host two each. NBC Sports reported on Oct. 1, 2026 that San Antonio and Baltimore join Supercross, and its headline names them as the additions.

## When does the 2027 Supercross season start and end?

The opener is Round 1 on Saturday, Jan. 9, 2027, at Angel Stadium in Anaheim, and the finale is Round 17 on Saturday, May 15, at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City. For comparison, the 2026 season began on Saturday, Jan. 10 at Angel Stadium of Anaheim, according to an NBC Sports release of Jan. 6, 2026. Fans planning around May can use the [May printable calendar](/may-calendars.html).

Times for most rounds are not yet posted. The Eastern Time (ET) equivalent is ours; the zones are Pacific Time (PT), Central Time (CT) and Mountain Time (MT).

| Round | City | Date | The time SMX lists | ET equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 4 | San Antonio | Sat., Jan. 30 | 6:00 p.m. CT | 7:00 p.m. ET |
| 5 | Tampa | Sat., Feb. 6 | 7:00 p.m. ET | 7:00 p.m. ET |
| 6 | Glendale | Sat., Feb. 13 | 5:00 p.m. MT | 7:00 p.m. ET |
| 13 | Baltimore | Sat., Apr. 10 | 3:00 p.m. ET | 3:00 p.m. ET |
| 17 | Salt Lake City | Sat., May 15 | 5:00 p.m. MT | 7:00 p.m. ET |

For last season's reference, NBC Sports said the 2026 opener at Angel Stadium aired on Peacock and NBC Sports Network (NBCSN), calling it "the race at 7 p.m. ET on Peacock and NBCSN." That is 2026, not a 2027 plan.
