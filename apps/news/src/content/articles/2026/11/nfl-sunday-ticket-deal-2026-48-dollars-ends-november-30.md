**YouTube says its NFL Sunday Ticket sale ends Nov. 30, 2026, with the package at $48 for new users and $62 for returning users, according to its Sunday Ticket page read on Nov. 21.** The National Football League (NFL) schedule has 68 Sunday afternoon CBS and FOX games set from Nov. 22 through Jan. 3, 2027, so a new user who buys now pays $6.00 for each of the 8 Sundays left. YouTube's page shows no time of day for the end.

## NFL Sunday Ticket price for new and returning users

| | New users | Returning users |
|---|---|---|
| One payment | $48 | $62 |
| Four monthly payments | $12 a month for 4 months | $15.50 a month for 4 months |

Source: [YouTube's Sunday Ticket page](https://tv.youtube.com/sundayticket), Nov. 21, 2026. Its fine print says the four payments are non-cancelable, the plan auto-renews at full price, there are no refunds, and Colorado, Maine, Utah and Wisconsin get single payment only. An active YouTube TV Plan is required, and digital-only games and commercial use are excluded.

Reported regular prices, not from YouTube's sale page: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/10/youtube-nfl-sunday-ticket-2026-details/) (Sept. 10, 2026) and [Cord Cutters News](https://cordcuttersnews.com/youtube-tv-youtube-announced-2026-nfl-sunday-ticket-pricing-starting-at-just-192-a-year/) (Feb. 6, 2026) both reported a full-season 2026 price for existing customers of $378 a year through YouTube TV and $480 through YouTube Primetime Channels. The two outlets disagree on the full-season price for new users, so it is left out.

## Who gets the $48 new-user price?

YouTube says "the new user price is available only if you have never purchased or subscribed to NFL Sunday Ticket in the past via YouTube or YouTube TV," and that this "includes previous subscriptions through a YouTube TV bundle." Everyone else pays the returning-user price of $62. Per YouTube's [terms](https://www.youtube.com/go/nflsundayticket/terms), buyers must be 18 or older.

## Which games does Sunday Ticket carry?

YouTube describes Sunday Ticket as a package that broadcasts all out-of-market regular season Sunday games. The terms exclude "locally broadcast Fox and CBS games, Sunday Night Football on NBC, select digital-only games." A reader in one market therefore loses that market's local CBS and FOX games from the counts below. This page does not state whether playoff games are included.

## What does Sunday Ticket cost per Sunday left?

Computed from YouTube's one-payment prices and the Sundays on the NFL schedule: 8 Sundays from Nov. 22 through the Week 18 weekend, or 7 from Dec. 6 on.

| Buy on | Sundays left | New user ($48) | Returning user ($62) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun., Nov. 22 | 8 | $6.00 | $7.75 |
| Mon., Nov. 30 | 7 (Dec. 6 to Week 18) | $6.86 | $8.86 |

Buying on Nov. 30 leaves out Nov. 22 and Nov. 29, which is 19 games set. Week 18 is set for Jan. 9-10, 2027, with every game time still TBD, so the Sunday count includes Jan. 10 as the NFL lists the weekend.

## What else ends on Dec. 2?

YouTube lists a Sports and News Plan offer for new YouTube TV users that ends Dec. 2, 2026: $49.99 a month for the first 6 months, then $71.99 a month. Check YouTube's page for what the plan includes.
