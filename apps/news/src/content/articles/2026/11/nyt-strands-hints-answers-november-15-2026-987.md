## How Strands works

The board is six letters wide and eight tall. Theme words are made by linking letters that touch, up, down, sideways or diagonally, and every letter is used exactly once. One special answer, the spangram, names the theme and stretches from one side of the board to the opposite side. Find three non-theme words and you earn a hint that highlights the letters of a theme word.

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: It names the kind of character all five theme words are.
- Hint 2: It is two words, and the first is the name of a caped comic-book hero.
- Hint 3: Thirteen letters, and the first letter is B.

## Theme word hints

There are five theme words, and each one is a rogue from the same comic-book city:

- **5 letters, starts with J:** a grinning prankster, clown-faced.
- **7 letters, starts with P:** a bird that cannot fly, dressed in a top hat and monocle.
- **7 letters, starts with R:** a villain who leaves puzzles and questions behind.
- **7 letters, starts with F:** a glowing insect, and the name of a fiery villain.
- **9 letters, starts with S:** a field scarer, and a villain who spreads fear.

## Solving tips

Start with the spangram. On today's board it starts at the left edge in the fourth row, winds up and across the middle of the board to the right edge in the fourth row, then loops back left along the fifth row and ends in the sixth row. The five theme words use 35 letters, so the spangram and the theme words together fill all 48 tiles. If you get stuck, spell any ordinary words you can see to earn a hint.
