**No. 1 Georgia plays at No. 17 South Carolina on Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on the American Broadcasting Company (ABC), according to ESPN's Week 12 schedule.** ESPN lists 22 games with a team in the College Football Playoff (CFP) selection committee's Nov. 10 rankings, every one with a set time and network as of Nov. 16. ## Which Week 12 games have two ranked teams?

Three of the 22 games match two CFP-ranked teams.

- **LSU at Tennessee, 3:30 p.m. ET, ABC.** No. 21 LSU visits No. 8 Tennessee at Neyland Stadium in Knoxville.
- **Georgia at South Carolina, 7:30 p.m. ET, ABC.** No. 1 Georgia visits No. 17 South Carolina at Williams-Brice Stadium in Columbia, S.C.
- **Kentucky at Missouri, 7:30 p.m. ET, SEC Network.** No. 11 Kentucky visits No. 22 Missouri at Memorial Stadium in Columbia, Mo.

The committee's Nov. 10 top four were Georgia, Utah, Alabama and UCLA, as ESPN's Andrea Adelson reported via ABC7 on Nov. 11. Georgia (No. 1) is at South Carolina, Utah (No. 2) at TCU, Alabama (No. 3) hosts Chattanooga and UCLA (No. 4) is at Michigan.
