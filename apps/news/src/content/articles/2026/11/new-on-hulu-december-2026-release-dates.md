**Only Murders in the Building season 6, a Hulu Original, opens with two episodes on Tuesday, Dec. 8, 2026, according to Disney's "Next on Disney+ and Hulu: December 2026" post of Nov. 19.** The day-by-day table below lists every dated Hulu title in that post, and a free printable watch list follows at the end.

Hulu and Disney can update the list, so confirm a date on Hulu before you plan a watch night. Raising Chelsea season 2 is the one Hulu title listed for December without a date.

## Which Hulu seasons and finales are dated in December 2026?

These are the premieres and endings the post marks with "Premiere" or "Finale" for Hulu, in date order.

- Tue., Dec. 1: Phony season 1 (Hulu Original), finale episode
- Wed., Dec. 2: All's Fair season 2 and Rivals season 2, part 2 (both Hulu Originals), finale episodes
- Thu., Dec. 3: Dance Moms: A New Era season 3, premiere
- Tue., Dec. 8: Only Murders in the Building season 6 (Hulu Original), two-episode premiere
- Wed., Dec. 9: Style Wars season 1 (Hulu Original), finale episodes
- Thu., Dec. 10: The Kardashians season 8 and Scrubs season 2, finale episodes
- Fri., Dec. 11: Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3, finale episodes
- Fri., Dec. 25: Tokyo Revengers: War of the Three Titans Arc (Hulu Original), finale episode
- Mon., Dec. 28: Teen Titans Go! season 9, no label in the post
- Thu., Dec. 31: Duck Dynasty: FlashQuack season 1, no label in the post

Of the 20 rows on Dec. 1, 18 are films printed with a year and one is a holiday special. Five titles carry the "Hulu Original" tag on more than one date: Tokyo Revengers (Dec. 4, 11, 18 and 25), Only Murders (Dec. 8, 15, 22 and 29), Merry Berry Love, Style Wars and The Perfect Lie (two dates each).

## What is Only Murders in the Building season 6 on Hulu?

Disney's post prints "Only Murders in the Building (Season 6) (Hulu Original) - Two-Episode Premiere" under Tuesday, Dec. 8. It prints "Only Murders in London (Season 6) (Hulu Original) - New Episodes" under Tuesday, Dec. 15, and the same title with "New Episode" under Dec. 22 and Dec. 29. The post does not explain the change of name and gives no release time. A podcast, "Only Murders in the Building Podcast (Season 6)", is listed on the same four Tuesdays.

## Which holiday films and specials are on Hulu in December 2026?

The post lists the holiday films on Tuesday, Dec. 1: the Home Alone films, The Santa Clause 2 and 3, and Miracle on 34th Street (1947). Holiday specials from ABC are dated Dec. 1 (The Wonderful World of Disney: Holiday Spectacular), Dec. 2 (CMA Country Christmas and Dancing with The Holidays) and Dec. 25 (Disney Parks Magical Christmas Day Parade). Bob's Burgers: On the Fort Day of Christmas, a holiday short, arrives Monday, Dec. 7. On Friday, Dec. 18 the post lists iHeartRadio Jingle Ball 2026; the [Jingle Ball dates, lineup and ABC special](/news/iheartradio-jingle-ball-2026-dates-lineup-by-city-abc-special/) story has the show details.

## What does Disney+ with Hulu cost in December 2026?

The Disney+ welcome page, checked Nov. 20, 2026, lists the Disney+ and Hulu bundle with ads at $12.99 a month, with an offer of $11.99 a month for the first six months. The bundle without ads is $21.99 a month, with an offer of $19.99 a month for the first six months. Hulu's own page did not show a standalone price, so none is given here. For the Disney+ side of the schedule, see the [Disney+ November 2026 list](/news/new-on-disney-plus-november-2026-release-dates/).
