## Hints for every clue

**Across**

- **1-Across** (3 letters), *Puzzle solver's cry of delight*: the exclamation that follows a sudden click of understanding; its three letters open the first three Down answers, in order.
- **4-Across** (5 letters), *Batting practice enclosures*: netted structures a hitter steps into, in the plural; it starts with C and ends with the letter that opens 6-Down.
- **7-Across** (5 letters), *Like dishes in the sink*: an adjective for things waiting to be washed; it starts with D, the third letter of 1-Down, and ends with Y.
- **8-Across** (5 letters), *Opposite of 7-Across*: the adjective you would want after washing; it starts with C, the last letter of 1-Down, and ends with N.
- **9-Across** (3 letters), *Upper-left keyboard key*: a shortened name for a key that backs out of a menu or closes a window; it starts with E, the last letter of 3-Down, and ends with C.

**Down**

- **1-Down** (4 letters), *Rock band with an "electric" name*: a band whose name is two pairs of letters, borrowed from kinds of electric current; it starts with A, the first letter of 1-Across, and ends with C.
- **2-Down** (4 letters), *Frozen rain*: pellets of ice that fall from a storm cloud; it starts with H, the middle letter of 1-Across, and ends with L.
- **3-Down** (5 letters), *What a subject and verb must do*: a grammar rule about matching number; it starts with A, the last letter of 1-Across, and ends with a double E.
- **5-Down** (4 letters), *Figs. that rise in traffic jams*: a plural abbreviation entered as one word, for times a navigation app keeps pushing later; it starts with E and ends with S.
- **6-Down** (4 letters), *Make sure everyone is on the same page, with "up"*: a verb that pairs with "up" to mean line things up together; it starts with S, ends with C and shares that C with 9-Across.

## The abbreviation clue in Monday's Mini

The clue with the least literal wordplay is 5-Down. "Figs." carries a dot, and a dot after a clipped word tells you the clue itself is abbreviated: it stands for a word meaning numbers, so no fruit is involved. "Rise in traffic jams" then reads as a number growing, which sends many solvers toward counts of cars or minutes. The answer is a shortened phrase too, and it is plural to match "Figs." The crossing letters from 4-Across and 9-Across fix its first and last squares.
