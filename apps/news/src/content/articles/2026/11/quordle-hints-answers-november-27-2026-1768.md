## What the doubled letters in Ninja and Chuck do to yellow tiles

Ninja has two Ns and Chuck has two Cs. A guess holding a single N or C can color only one tile for that letter per word, so a lone yellow never tells you the letter is doubled. Scored on Slant, for example, Ninja gets one yellow for its N, even though the word holds two.

Crane holds one N and one C as well, so each of those letters fills three slots across the board: N in Crane and twice in Ninja, C in Crane and twice in Chuck. A also fills three slots, one in each of three words.

| Letter | Slots used | Words |
|---|---|---|
| A | 3 | Crane, Steam, Ninja |
| N | 3 | Crane once, Ninja twice |
| C | 3 | Crane once, Chuck twice |
| E | 2 | Crane, Steam |
| R, S, T, M, I, J, H, U, K | 1 each | one word each |

Those rows add up to 20 slots and 13 distinct letters. Crane and Steam share A and E, Crane and Ninja share A and N, Crane and Chuck share C, and Steam and Ninja share A. Steam and Chuck share nothing, and neither do Ninja and Chuck.
