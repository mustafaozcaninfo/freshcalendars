**The Colorado General Assembly convenes Monday, Jan. 11, 2027, for the First Regular Session of the Seventy-sixth General Assembly, according to House Joint Resolution (HJR) 26-1031.** The resolution was signed June 3, 2026, and the General Assembly's home page repeats the date. The Legislative Council Staff (LCS) schedule dated Aug. 25, 2026 counts Jan. 11 as day 1 and ends with the 120th day, Monday, May 10, 2027.

## Colorado legislative session 2027 dates

| Milestone | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Newly elected members: deadline to request three of five bills | Tue., Dec. 15, 2026 | LCS schedule |
| Filing one of each member's three bills | Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 | LCS schedule |
| General Assembly convenes (day 1) | Mon., Jan. 11, 2027 | HJR 26-1031 |
| 120th day | Mon., May 10, 2027 | LCS schedule |

The convening time is not in the 2027 documents.

## Why does the 2027 session start on a Monday?

The Denver Gazette and Colorado Politics both reported on April 21, 2026 that the schedule "shifts in years when a new governor is elected," and that lawmakers must first certify the results of the general election. The 2026 session convened on Wednesday, Jan. 14, according to the LCS 2026 schedule. The 2027 session opens three days earlier.

## How does 2027 compare with the 2026 session?

| | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Convenes | Wed., Jan. 14 | Mon., Jan. 11 |
| Ends | Adjourned sine die May 13, 2026 | 120th day, Mon., May 10 |
| Days, first to last | 120 (our count, Jan. 14 to May 13) | 120 (Jan. 11 to May 10) |

The 2026 dates come from the LCS 2026 schedule ("January 14 (Wednesday) General Assembly Convenes") and the General Assembly's home page, which says the Second Regular Session of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly adjourned sine die on May 13, 2026. The 2027 schedule lists May 7 as the day to reconvene for adjournment sine die if the houses adjourn to a day certain, so a single 2027 sine die date is not set in the documents.
