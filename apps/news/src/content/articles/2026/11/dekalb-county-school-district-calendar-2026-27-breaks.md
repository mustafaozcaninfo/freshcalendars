**DeKalb County School District (DCSD) in Georgia has no school from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 for Thanksgiving break**, according to its 2026-2027 Calendar, which the Board of Education approved on Nov. 10, 2025. Winter break runs **Monday, Dec. 21 through Friday, Jan. 1**, and spring break is **Monday, Apr. 5 through Friday, Apr. 9, 2027**. The last day of school is Thursday, May 27, 2027.

## DeKalb County 2026-27 breaks

| Break | Dates | Students return |
|---|---|---|
| Fall Break (already past) | Tue.-Fri., Oct. 6-9, 2026 | Monday, Oct. 12 |
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday, Nov. 30 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | Tuesday, Jan. 5 (Jan. 4 is a day without students) |
| February Break | Tue.-Fri., Feb. 16-19, 2027, after Presidents' Day on Feb. 15 | Monday, Feb. 22 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., Apr. 5-9, 2027 | Monday, Apr. 12 |

July 27-31, 2026 are pre-planning days when students do not report, and the first day of school is Monday, Aug. 3. The first semester ends Friday, Dec. 18, and the second begins Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027. Some third-party calendar sites list different dates; the board-approved calendar is the one used here.

## Which days are no school or no students in DeKalb County?

The table lists every closure and every day the calendar says students do not report, with the weekday and the day classes resume.

| Date | Day | What the calendar lists | Back in class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day, schools closed (past) | Tue., Sept. 8 |
| Oct. 5, 2026 | Monday | Teachers' Virtual Workday, students do not report (past) | Mon., Oct. 12 (Oct. 6-9 is Fall Break) |
| Oct. 6-9, 2026 | Tuesday-Friday | Fall Break, schools closed (past) | Mon., Oct. 12 |
| Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Thanksgiving Break, schools closed | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Dec. 21, 2026-Jan. 1, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Winter Break (Dec. 21-31) and New Year's Day (Jan. 1), closed | Tue., Jan. 5 |
| Jan. 4, 2027 | Monday | Post/Pre-Planning Day, students do not report | Tue., Jan. 5 |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Day, closed | Tue., Jan. 19 |
| Feb. 15-19, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Presidents' Day (Feb. 15) and February Break (Feb. 16-19), closed | Mon., Feb. 22 |
| Mar. 15, 2027 | Monday | Professional Development Day, students do not report | Tue., Mar. 16 |
| Apr. 5-9, 2027 | Monday-Friday | Spring Break, schools closed | Mon., Apr. 12 |

Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026 is listed as an Independent Learner/Professional Development Day. The calendar also prints "Indigenous Peoples' Day/Columbus Day" on Monday, Oct. 12 without marking it closed, so it is a regular day on this calendar. The calendar's legend lists no early release days. After the last day, Friday, May 28 is a Post Planning Day, Memorial Day is Monday, May 31, and Juneteenth, June 19, 2027, falls on a Saturday.

## Inclement weather days inside the DeKalb breaks

Four days inside breaks carry a second label. The calendar marks Monday and Tuesday, Nov. 23-24, 2026 as Inclement Weather Day 1 and Inclement Weather Day 2, and does the same for Monday and Tuesday, Apr. 5-6, 2027. All four sit inside the Thanksgiving and spring breaks, which are shown as no-school weeks.

The calendar does not say how those days would be used, so this story does not guess. If school is closed for weather, watch the district's notices for what changes.
