**NYC Restaurant Week returns from Jan. 19 to Feb. 11, 2027, with reservations opening Jan. 6 at 10 a.m. ET**, according to [NYC Tourism + Conventions](https://www.nyctourism.com/restaurant-week/), which runs the program (its Restaurant Week page, read Nov. 26, 2026). The program offers prix-fixe meals at $30, $45 and $60 at hundreds of restaurants in all five boroughs. The table below sorts every day of the run into program days, optional Sundays and excluded Saturdays, and a printable planner keeps the dates on one page.

## NYC Restaurant Week 2027 dates and rules

The page says "the program runs from January 19 through February 11, but restaurants may participate for one, two, three or all four weeks." Saturdays are excluded and Sundays are optional. A restaurant that joins for two weeks will not honor the menu on every date, so check its own hours before you book. The page tells diners to do that.

By our calendar arithmetic, Jan. 19, 2027 is a Tuesday, the day after Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Mon., Jan. 18). Feb. 11 is a Thursday, and the run covers 24 calendar days. Valentine's Day, Sun., Feb. 14, falls three days after it ends; see the [Valentine's Day 2027 dates and printables](/news/valentines-day-2027-sunday-february-14-dates-printables/).

## When do NYC Restaurant Week reservations open?

Reservations open Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027, at 10 a.m. ET, and the page says the full list of participating restaurants is viewable starting Jan. 6, 2027, at 10am. The page does not name a booking platform or say whether any card sponsor gets early access, so those details are not yet announced.

## What do the $30, $45 and $60 menus cost?

The official page lists three price options, $30, $45 and $60, which restaurants "may offer during meal periods of their choosing (lunch, dinner or both)." For 2027 the page does not say which courses each price includes, whether tax, tip and drinks are extra, or how many restaurants take part; it says only "hundreds."

In 2026, NYC Tourism + Conventions said in its [Jan. 7, 2026 release](https://www.business.nyctourism.com/press-media/press-releases/nyc-winter-outing-2026) that NYC Restaurant Week offered "prix-fixe two-course lunches and three-course dinners for $30, $45 and $60." That is last year's structure; 2027 terms are not confirmed.
