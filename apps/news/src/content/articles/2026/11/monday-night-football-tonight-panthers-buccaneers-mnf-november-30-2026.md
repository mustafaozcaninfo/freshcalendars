**The Carolina Panthers play the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Monday Night Football (MNF), Monday, Nov. 30, 2026, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on ESPN, according to the National Football League (NFL) Week 12 schedule on NFL.com.** The Buccaneers' schedule lists the same time as "8:15 PM EST", and their game center puts the game at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa. It is a National Football Conference (NFC) South game.

Below are the kickoff in every U.S. zone and in the United Kingdom, how to watch, the NFC South standings, the October meeting and the next games. A one-page watch guide is at the end.

## What channel and stream carry Panthers at Buccaneers?

NFL.com and the Buccaneers list ESPN. The Buccaneers' how-to page names NFL+ for mobile viewing in the team's market, NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV for viewers outside it and a Game Pass service for international viewers. That page lists the broadcast crew as to be determined. Yahoo Sports reported a crew on Nov. 24, but no official page confirms it, so we leave names out.

## Could the Monday game still move?

The NFL's 2026 flexible scheduling procedures say: "For Monday Night Football in Weeks 12-17, the NFL will decide and announce no later than 12 days in advance of the game." Counting back from Nov. 30 gives Nov. 18. The league's Nov. 17 Week 12 schedule change moved Raiders-Browns to Sunday Night Football and also covered Patriots-Chargers and Titans-Jaguars. Panthers-Buccaneers was not changed, so the game stays on Monday.

## Where do Panthers and Buccaneers stand in the NFC South?

NFL.com's standings on Nov. 24 show Carolina leading the division, with three teams tied behind it.

| NFC South team | Record |

| Buccaneers | 3-7 |

The Buccaneers fell to 3-7 with a 31-21 loss to the Lions in Week 11, per their Nov. 24 power rankings. We did not find an official Panthers result for Week 11, so only their 5-5 record is given. Going by those standings, a Carolina win makes it 6-5 against Tampa Bay at 3-8. A Tampa Bay win makes it 4-7 against Carolina at 5-6, which cuts the gap between them to one game.

## What happened when the Panthers and Buccaneers met in October?

In Week 7, on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, the Buccaneers beat the Panthers 43-13 at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, per the Panthers' game center. Monday's game follows that meeting.
