**Chiquito serves its Christmas menu from 10 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, with the Celebration Menu at 2 courses from £20.50 or 3 courses from £25.50, according to the chain's Christmas page.** No restaurant is scheduled to open on Christmas Day. Our research desk read the Chiquito pages and its Christmas flyer on 22 November; the dates and prices below follow their wording.

## What does the Chiquito Celebration Menu cost in 2026?

The Christmas page says: "Enjoy 2 courses from £20.50 or 3 courses from £25.50, with plenty of Chiquito favourites to choose from." Its questions page says the Celebration Menu prices apply "at all locations", and no location exception is given. The flyer's first page shows the same two prices, £20.50 and £25.50.

## How do Chiquito's prices compare with Frankie & Benny's and Bella Italia?

All three chains list the Celebration Menu from 10 November 2026 to 6 January 2027. The last two columns are our arithmetic on the published prices.

| Chain | 2 courses | 3 courses | Chiquito 2 courses is | Chiquito 3 courses is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chiquito | From £20.50 | From £25.50 | n/a | n/a |
| Frankie & Benny's | £21.50 | From £25.50 | £1.00 lower | The same |
| Frankie & Benny's The Strand | £23 | From £26 | £2.50 lower | £0.50 lower |
| Bella Italia | £22.95 | £27.95 | £2.45 lower | £2.45 lower |

Frankie & Benny's says its prices apply "at all locations, except for Frankie & Benny's The Strand". Bella Italia says its prices apply "at all locations". Chiquito and Frankie & Benny's both say "from" for some prices, so the final bill can vary. See the full [Frankie & Benny's Christmas menu story](/news/frankie-and-bennys-christmas-menu-2026/) and the [Bella Italia Christmas menu story](/news/bella-italia-christmas-menu-2026/).

## Which Chiquito Christmas dates matter in 2026?

The last column counts days after Sunday 22 November, the day the pages were read.

| Date | What happens | Days after 22 Nov |
|---|---|---|
| Tue. 10 Nov 2026 | Christmas menu starts | Already started |
| Fri. 25 Dec 2026 | No restaurant scheduled to be open | 33 |
| Wed. 6 Jan 2027 | Last day of the Christmas menu | 45 |

The menu runs for 58 calendar days with both ends counted. Chiquito recommends booking early "to secure your preferred date and time" and says bookings are open for Christmas 2026. Mark the dates on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and a [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html).

## Is Chiquito open on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Eve?

Not on Christmas Day. The Christmas page says: "None of our restaurants are scheduled to be open on Christmas day." Bella Italia, by contrast, says 12 of its restaurants open on Christmas Day. Chiquito's Christmas page does not list Boxing Day, New Year's Eve or New Year's Day hours.

## What does Chiquito's Christmas page not say?

Deposit terms, a children's Christmas price and Boxing Day and New Year opening are not given on Chiquito's Christmas page. We found no 2025 Celebration Menu price to compare with, so the table above sets Chiquito against two other chains instead.
