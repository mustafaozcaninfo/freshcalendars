**Fort Worth Independent School District (FWISD) students have no school Nov. 23-27, 2026, Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, and March 15-19, 2027**, by the district's 2026-2027 Traditional Calendar, linked from its "Academic Calendars" page. The last day of school is Friday, May 21, 2027. The fall break and Indigenous Peoples' Day days off, Oct. 9 and 12, have passed, so Thanksgiving break is next.

The district's traditional calendar says its dates are subject to change and that the board approved it on 4/28/26. An amended version dated May 11, 2026 lists "175 Student Days x 450 Minutes." The dates below match the calendars the district posts, including the Accelerated Day School Year (ADSY) version for some campuses, covered further down.

## Fort Worth ISD breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates. Return days are our reading, since the calendar marks no closure on them, except Jan. 5, which the district lists.

| Break | No school | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break | Fri., Oct. 9, 2026 | 1 | Tue., Oct. 13 (after Indigenous Peoples' Day, Mon., Oct. 12) |
| Thanksgiving break | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (Jan. 4 is a staff day) |
| Spring break | Mon., March 15 to Fri., March 19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 22 |

## Is there a four-day weekend in March 2027?

Yes. Good Friday is March 26 and Monday, March 29 is a staff day, so students are out Friday, March 26 to Monday, March 29, 2027, two weeks after spring break ends.

## How does the ADSY calendar differ at Fort Worth ISD?

The district says ADSY days are 25 days added to the traditional calendar for some campuses in its Elevate program, for acceleration, small-group support and enrichment. The ADSY PDF reads "175 Student Days + 25 ADSY Summer = 200 Student Days." Its breaks match the traditional calendar, so only the summer block differs.

| | Traditional | ADSY |
|---|---|---|
| First day | Mon., Aug. 10, 2026 | Mon., Aug. 10, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving break | Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Nov. 23-27, 2026 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 |
| Spring break | March 15-19, 2027 | March 15-19, 2027 |
| Last day before summer | Fri., May 21, 2027 | Fri., May 21, 2027 |
| Student days | 175 | 175 + 25 ADSY summer = 200 |
| ADSY summer block | None | Mon., May 24 to Tue., June 29, 2027 |

The ADSY calendar lists these elementary schools (15): Harlean Beal, George C. Clarke, Clifford Davis, Diamond Hill, East Handley, Eastern Hills-West Handley, W.M. Green, Rufino Mendoza, Hazel Harvey Peace, T.A. Sims, W.J. Turner, Van Zandt-Guinn, Maudrie M. Walton, Westcreek and Western Hills. Its middle schools (4) are William James, William Monnig, Morningside and Wedgwood. Schools in the district's Focus program use the traditional calendar, per the ADSY PDF.

## When does the Fort Worth ISD school year start and end?

Students start Monday, Aug. 10, 2026. The last day of school is Friday, May 21, 2027, and Memorial Day, May 31, falls after it. ADSY campuses continue to Tuesday, June 29, 2027.
