## Pinpoint and Crossclimb nudges for Wednesday, Nov. 18

- **Pinpoint #932, first nudge:** the five clues are 500, 21, Uno, Crazy Eights and 52 Pickup, and each one is a name.
- **Pinpoint #932, second nudge:** two clues are bare numbers, and Uno is the Spanish word for one. Ask what comes in a set of 52.
- **Pinpoint #932, third nudge:** the category has four words, and the last one has five letters.
- **Crossclimb #932, the ladder:** all seven words have four letters.
- **Crossclimb #932, the locked ends:** the top is a basin in a kitchen or the thing a stone does in water, and the bottom is something you wash with.
- **Crossclimb #932, the middle:** five words sit between the ends, and the first move changes the third letter.

## Reaching the Pinpoint #932 category from the clues

Pinpoint asks for what five clues share, so sort them by type before guessing. Here three clues are numerals or number words (500, 21, Uno), and two carry a number inside a longer name (Crazy Eights, 52 Pickup). The 52 points to one piece of equipment that comes in a fixed set. A category of four words starting "Names of" fits that, with the last word naming what the five clues are.

## Reading the Crossclimb #932 ladder without the words

Seven words of four letters, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word appears in this section.

| Move | Letter position that changes |
|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 3 |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 2 |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 3 |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 2 |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 3 |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 4 |

Position 1 never changes, so all seven words begin with the same letter. Position 3 changes three times, position 2 twice and position 4 once. The first five moves alternate between the third and second letters, and only the last move touches the fourth.

## How far apart the Crossclimb #932 locked ends sit

The two locked words share one letter, and it sits in the same place in both: the first. Positions 2, 3 and 4 all differ. Covering three differing positions takes six moves because positions 2 and 3 are each visited more than once on the way.

## Starting points for Queens #932, Tango #772, Zip #611 and Mini Sudoku #464

**Queens #932.** Look for a color region confined to one row or column; that line cannot hold a crown from any other region.

**Tango #772.** When two identical symbols sit side by side, the cells on both ends of the pair must take the other symbol.

**Zip #611.** Mark the cells with the lowest and highest numbers, then check which walls leave only one exit from each.

**Mini Sudoku #464.** Count how many digits each row, column and box already holds, and start with the line that is missing only one or two.
