**Aldi's 2026 advent calendars arrived in its stores on Wednesday, Nov. 4, while supplies last, and prices start at $1.99**, according to the grocer's newsroom release from Batavia, Ill., dated Oct. 30, 2026. Aldi says the assortment has "over 25 calendars." The release names 10 of them, with prices from $1.99 to $59.99, and they are all in the table below.

## Aldi advent calendars 2026: the 10 named calendars and prices

Sorted from lowest to highest price. Tags and prices are as printed in Aldi's release; the last column is our reading of each calendar's name.

| Calendar | Tag | Price | Kind (from the name) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choceur Kids Advent Calendar | Refreshed | $1.99 | Kids |
| Pure Being Dog Advent Calendar | Refreshed | $7.99 | Pet |
| Creativity for Kids Sensory Advent Calendar | New | $14.99 | Kids, sensory |
| Burman's Hot Sauce Advent Calendar | Refreshed | $14.99 | Food |
| Emporium Selection Advent Cheese Calendar | Refreshed | $16.99 | Food |
| Barissimo Coffee and Cocoa Advent Calendar | Refreshed | $16.99 | Drink |
| Crofton 24 Days of Baking Advent Calendar | New | $19.99 | Baking |
| Stationery Advent Calendar | New | $19.99 | Stationery |
| Aldi Super Fan Advent Calendar | New | $29.99 | Aldi fans |
| Wine Advent Calendar | Refreshed | $59.99 | Drink |

## What the prices add up to

These totals are our arithmetic on the 10 prices above, not figures from Aldi.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Calendars under $15 | 4 (Choceur Kids, Pure Being Dog and the two at $14.99) |
| Calendars at $20 or less | 8 |
| Cheapest | Choceur Kids, $1.99 |
| Most expensive | Wine Advent Calendar, $59.99 |
| Gap between the two | $58.00 |
| All 10 together | $203.90 |

Four of the 10 are new this year: the Aldi Super Fan, Crofton 24 Days of Baking, Creativity for Kids Sensory and Stationery calendars. The other six are refreshed versions of earlier calendars. Door counts are not listed with the prices, so this page does not work out a price per day.

## Aldi wine advent calendar 2026

The Wine Advent Calendar is $59.99 and is tagged refreshed in the release. It is the highest price among the 10 named calendars. Wine sales follow each state's alcohol rules, so check with your local store before you go.

## When did Aldi advent calendars come out last year?

In 2025, Aldi's calendars arrived Nov. 5. NewsNation reported on Nov. 1, 2025 that they would be available "starting Nov. 5," and Cheapism wrote on Nov. 10, 2025 that "On November 5, Aldi launched a core set of seasonal must-have Advent calendars." The 2026 date is one day earlier in the month.

The Shelby Report (Nov. 2, 2026) also reported that Aldi was bringing back its advent calendars Nov. 4, "while supplies last," with "more than 25 varieties starting at $1.99." Mashed (Nov. 3, 2026) said the Crofton calendar "lands at Aldi on November 4." Mashed also lists other calendars it found, which are not in Aldi's release and are left out here.

## Dates to plan around

Advent 2026 begins on Sunday, Nov. 29, the first Sunday of Advent (see [when Advent starts](/news/when-does-advent-start-2026-november-29-advent-wreath-candles/)). Dec. 1, 2026 is a Tuesday, so a 24-door calendar opened from Dec. 1 ends on Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24. These dates come from the calendar, not from Aldi. Because Aldi says "while supplies last," buying before December leaves the most choice. Aldi has not said anything about purchase limits or online sales for 2026.
