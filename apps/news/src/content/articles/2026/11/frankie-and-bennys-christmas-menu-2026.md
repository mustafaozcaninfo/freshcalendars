**Frankie & Benny's serves its Christmas menu from 10 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, with the Celebration Menu from £21.50 for two courses, according to the chain's Christmas page.** The Strand in London charges £23 for two courses, and no restaurant is scheduled to open on Christmas Day. Our research desk read the Frankie & Benny's pages on 22 November; the dates and prices below follow their wording.

## What does the Frankie & Benny's Celebration Menu cost in 2026?

The Christmas page lists "Two courses from £21.50" and "Three courses from £25.50". The Best Christmas Restaurant page words the first price as "2 courses for £21.50, or 3 courses from £25.50 at all locations". The Strand has its own prices: "The Strand is offering 2 courses for £23, or 3 courses from £26."

| Option | Celebration Menu | The Strand, London | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 courses | From £21.50 | £23 | £1.50 |
| 3 courses | From £25.50 | From £26 | £0.50 |

The difference column is our arithmetic on the two published prices. Both pages say "from" for three courses, so the final bill can vary. The pages do not list per-dish prices, supplements, a children's Christmas menu price or a deposit.

## Which Frankie & Benny's Christmas dates matter in 2026?

The last column counts days after Sunday 22 November, the day the pages were read.

| Date | What happens | Days after 22 Nov |
|---|---|---|
| Tue. 10 Nov 2026 | Christmas menu starts | Already started |
| Fri. 25 Dec 2026 | No restaurant scheduled to be open | 33 |
| Wed. 6 Jan 2027 | Last day of the Christmas menu | 45 |

The menu runs for 58 calendar days with both ends counted. The page says: "None of our restaurants are scheduled to be open on Christmas day." Mark the dates on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and a [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html), and see [when Christmas Day falls](/when-is/christmas/).

## Is Frankie & Benny's open on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Eve?

Not on Christmas Day: the Christmas page and the Best Christmas Restaurant page both say none of the restaurants are scheduled to be open. Neither page lists Boxing Day, New Year's Eve or New Year's Day hours. Opening times at each site are not listed on the Christmas page.

## What is on the Frankie & Benny's Christmas menu?

The Christmas page names these festive items without giving a course or a price.

| Type | Named on the Christmas page |
|---|---|
| Food | Mozzarella Sticks (cheese-pull); Festive Fondue Melt Pizza; Cinnamon Bun Cheesecake; Loaded Crimbo Burger; Cherry Chocolate Sundae; Pigs in Blankets; Festive Loaded Fries |
| Drinks | Jack Frost Fizz; Merry Berry Float (alcohol-free); Gingerbread Latte syrup |

## How do you book a Frankie & Benny's Christmas meal for a group?

The page says: "Book online for groups of 19 or fewer." For 20 or more, you send an enquiry and the Group Bookings team gets in touch. It adds that festive dates are filling up, so enquire early. It also says "Kids Eat Free between Christmas and New Year", without giving the exact days or terms.
