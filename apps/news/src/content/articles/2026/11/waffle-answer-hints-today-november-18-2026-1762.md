Sort the 21 tiles by letter and you get 12 different letters. Two letters fill four tiles each, three letters fill two each, and seven letters appear once. The singles are the useful ones: each has exactly one square it can go to, so any swap that moves one is a certain move.

One of the four-tile letters has a single green tile and the other has two. Of the three pairs, none has a green tile.

### Why each swap is forced

The minimum is 10 swaps, found by an exhaustive search and replayed on the starting grid to reach the solved one. Play them in order, because each assumes the earlier ones are done. Swaps 1, 3, 4, 5, 9 and 10 put two tiles home at once; swaps 2, 6, 7 and 8 fix one each.

| Swap | Squares | Letters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Row 1, column 2 and row 2, column 5 | O and Y |
| 2 | Row 1, column 3 and row 4, column 3 | R and B |
| 3 | Row 1, column 4 and row 4, column 1 | O and E |
| 4 | Row 2, column 1 and row 3, column 2 | B and H |
| 5 | Row 2, column 3 and row 4, column 3 | K and R |
| 6 | Row 3, column 1 and row 3, column 5 | O and A |

- **Swap 1:** the only Y tile sits in row 2, column 5, and Y has just one home, the second letter of Cyber. The O it trades with lands in column 5 for Rotor.
- **Swap 2:** brings B to the third letter of Cyber. The R it sends down to row 4, column 3 is a parking spot, since R is needed higher in column 3.
- **Swap 3:** E takes the fourth letter of Cyber, finishing row 1, and the O lands in row 4, column 1 for Chaos.
- **Swap 4:** the only H moves to row 2, column 1 for Chaos, and B drops into Abort.
- **Swap 5:** the only K goes to row 4, column 3, and the parked R rises to row 2, column 3, so Broke has its second and fourth letters.
- **Swap 6:** A reaches row 3, column 1, which finishes Chaos. The O it sends right is not home yet.
- **Swap 7:** R takes row 3, column 4 for Abort. The E goes to row 5, column 4, a stop on the way to its square beside it.
- **Swap 8:** the only T moves to the end of Abort, finishing row 3.
- **Swap 9:** the only W settles in row 5, column 2, and the O it displaces lands in row 4, column 5, finishing Rotor.
- **Swap 10:** A and E trade places in row 5. Broke and Swear both close on this move.

### Why the count is 10

Sixteen tiles are misplaced, and a loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops. Ten swaps means 6 loops.
