**Frisco King, a new Paramount+ series from Taylor Sheridan starring Samuel L. Jackson, premieres Friday, Dec. 18, 2026, according to the Paramount+ Sneak Peak page "What's New On Paramount+ In December," published Nov. 19.** The day-by-day table below counts every dated film and series on that page and its companion movie page, a separate table lists live sports in ET, and a free printable watch list closes the story.

Neither page gives a release time for any title, so times are not yet announced. Dates for library titles are subject to change, per the page.

## Which Paramount+ premieres and finales are dated in December 2026?

The page names these in its opening and its day listings.

- Thu., Dec. 3: By Any Means, streaming premiere of the 2026 theatrical film with Mark Wahlberg and Yahya Abdul-Mateen II
- Tue., Dec. 8: The Elite: Army Rangers, new docuseries
- Wed., Dec. 9: Lil Kev season 2, Kevin Hart's animated comedy
- Thu., Dec. 10: Who The Hell Is Hamish?, new Paramount+ series
- Fri., Dec. 11: Garfield, new animated series (the page calls it a new 2D-animated series)
- Fri., Dec. 18: Frisco King, new Paramount+ series, and the Tulsa King season 4 finale
- Thu., Dec. 31: The Day, new Paramount+ series

Dexter: Resurrection season 2 and Yellowjackets season 4 bring new episodes "throughout the holiday month," and the page lists no episode dates. For the Dexter schedule, see the [Dexter: Resurrection season 2 episode schedule](/news/dexter-resurrection-season-2-release-schedule-episode-time-paramount-plus/).
