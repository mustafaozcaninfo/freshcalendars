## Why are there two Friday night games in 2027?

Both Friday kick-offs, at 8:10 p.m. GMT on Feb. 5 and Mar. 5, involve Ireland. The Irish Times headline on Mar. 9, 2026 said Ireland would have two more Friday night games in 2027, and its report said: "Ireland and England begin next year's championship in the Aviva Stadium on Friday, February 5th, at 8.10pm." For viewers in Eastern Time those are 3:10 p.m. starts on a weekday.

The final round is on Super Saturday, Mar. 13, with three matches: Italy v Wales at 2:10 p.m. GMT, England v Scotland at 4:40 p.m. GMT and Ireland v France at 8:10 p.m. GMT. In Eastern Time that is 9:10 a.m., 11:40 a.m. and 3:10 p.m., the day before U.S. clocks move forward on Mar. 14.

## Where are the French home games in 2027?

Scottish Rugby lists the Stade de France in Paris for France v Scotland. The Irish Times reported on Mar. 9, 2026 that "the Stade de France has still to be confirmed for the French home ties," and we did not find a French federation statement confirming the venue. Treat Paris as the host city and the stadium as unconfirmed until the federation announces it. The Six Nations' own [fixtures page](https://www.sixnationsrugby.com/en/m6n/fixtures/202700) is the place to check for any late change.

## How can you watch the Six Nations in the U.S. and Canada?

The 2027 broadcasters in the United States and Canada are not yet announced in the sources we found. In 2026, NBC Sports said all Six Nations matches streamed live on Peacock, with select windows on NBC. In Canada in 2026, Premier Sports became "the exclusive rights holder," according to Planet Rugby and Advanced Television, both on Jan. 7, 2026. Last year's deals can change.

## When is the 2027 Women's Six Nations?

Planet Rugby reported on May 13, 2026 that the women's championship starts on Sat., Apr. 10, 2027 and ends on Sat., May 15, 2027. Scottish Rugby lists the opening match as Scotland v Ireland at Murrayfield. The round dates are Apr. 10, Apr. 17, Apr. 24, May 8 and May 15, with each round's three matches on the same Saturday. We have no kick-off times for the women's matches.
