## Pinpoint and Crossclimb nudges for Wednesday, Nov. 11

- **Pinpoint #925, first nudge:** the five clues are not a family of things. What joins them is one symbol.
- **Pinpoint #925, second nudge:** that symbol is a single letter, and it stands for something different in each clue.
- **Pinpoint #925, third nudge:** read each clue as a short form. Ask which single letter could stand for all five at once.
- **Crossclimb #925, the locked ends:** the top and bottom words are four letters each. Read in order, they name a familiar half of something measured in holes.
- **Crossclimb #925, the middle:** five rungs, and each is one letter away from the rung above it. Start from whichever neighbor you are surest of.

## How the locked pair gives away the ladder

Crossclimb locks the first and last rows, and today those two words form a phrase when read top to bottom. Solving that phrase before touching the middle is a legitimate shortcut: it tells you the exact first and last words, and each one is only a single letter away from its neighbor. The top word's neighbor differs from it in the first position. The bottom word's neighbor differs from it in the first position as well, so both ends of the ladder are opened by an opening-letter swap.

## Step map for the Crossclimb #925 ladder

Seven words, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word appears in this table.

| Move | Letter position that changes | Kind of letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 2 | vowel |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 3 | consonant |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 4 | vowel |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 1 | consonant |

Position 1 changes in three of the six moves, the first, the fourth and the last. The only two vowel moves are the second and the fifth, and the fifth is the one that alters the final letter. A quick test for any stuck rung: if neither neighbor accepts a new first letter, the change is somewhere inside the word.
