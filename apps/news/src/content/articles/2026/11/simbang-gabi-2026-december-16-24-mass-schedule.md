**Simbang Gabi 2026 begins Wednesday, Dec. 16 and ends Thursday, Dec. 24, under the Archdiocese of Manila's 2026 guidelines, as a Philippine broadcaster reported on Nov. 13, 2026.** The United States Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB) describes the same novena as running from Dec. 16 until Christmas Eve. In the Diocese of Arlington in Virginia, the Mass list printed on its Simbang Gabi 2026 page starts a night earlier, on Tuesday, Dec. 15, and holds 30 Masses through Dec. 24. The tables give the weekday for every night and each Arlington parish time, and a printable tracker is at the end.

## What are the Simbang Gabi 2026 dates?

The Nov. 13 broadcast report on the guidelines says "the nine-day Simbang Gabi in the Philippines begins on December 16, 2026 and ends on the 24th of December 2026." Counting nine nights from Dec. 16 ends on Christmas Eve. Christmas Day follows on Friday, Dec. 25; see [when is Christmas Day](/when-is/christmas-day/).

## How do Philippine and U.S. Simbang Gabi Masses compare?

The Diocese of Arlington says Simbang Gabi was introduced to the Philippines "by Spanish friars in the 16th century," with Masses at dawn "when the roosters crow to announce the coming of the new day (hence the name, Misa de gallo)." In the United States, it says, "winter weather in most parts of the country makes it impractical to hold the Mass celebrations at dawn," so local parishes "celebrate Simbang Gabi in the evenings."

| | Philippines, 2026 | Diocese of Arlington, 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Dates | Dec. 16 to Dec. 24 | Dec. 15 to Dec. 24 |
| Evening Masses | May begin as early as 6:00 p.m. under Manila's guidelines | Most parish Masses listed, from 4:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. |
| Dawn Masses | Manila's circular sets 5:30 a.m. for dawn celebrations | St. Leo the Great, Fairfax, 5:00 a.m.; Good Shepherd, Alexandria, 6:00 a.m. on Dec. 19 |

The Manila rules come from two reports of the same circular. The Nov. 13 report quotes it: "Aguinaldo/Simbang Gabi Masses can start as early as 6:00 p.m. for evening Masses," and "the last Aguinaldo/Simbang Gabi Mass can begin at 5:30 a.m." The news service of the Catholic Bishops' Conference of the Philippines (CBCP), CBCP News, reported on Nov. 16 that the circular "also allows evening Simbang Gabi Masses to begin as early as 6 p.m." and that "dawn celebrations may start at 5:30 a.m." The circular is dated Nov. 12. These are rules for the Archdiocese of Manila, not a list of parish times.

An older USCCB column in its "Entre Amigos" series, which carries no date, calls Simbang Gabi or Misa de Gallo "a novena of Masses" starting "on December 16 until Christmas Eve" in the Philippines. It says Filipino communities in the United States celebrate "from December 15 to December 23." The Arlington page for 2026 runs to Dec. 24, so check each parish's own dates.

## What about Simbang Gabi near me outside Arlington?

Other dioceses and parishes publish their own lists. No 2026 schedule for another U.S. city or for Manila Cathedral was confirmed for this story, so none is listed. Look on your diocese's website or call the parish office, and use the printable tracker to write down the times you find.
