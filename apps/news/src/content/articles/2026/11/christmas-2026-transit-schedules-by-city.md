**On Friday, Dec. 25, 2026, the Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority (MBTA), TriMet, the Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority (MARTA), Sound Transit and Capital Metro run Sunday-type service**, according to each agency's own holiday page. Three of them also offer free rides on New Year's Eve, Thursday, Dec. 31: the MBTA, TriMet and Capital Metro.

Christmas Eve is Thursday, Dec. 24, and New Year's Day is Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. We read the pages on Nov. 23, 2026; agencies can still change them, so confirm on the agency's page the day before you travel.

## Christmas Eve to New Year's Day transit service by agency

Entries are the agencies' own wording or a close paraphrase. Sound Transit's columns are ST Express, Sounder commuter rail, T Line and Link light rail.

| Agency | Christmas Eve, Thu., Dec. 24 | Christmas Day, Fri., Dec. 25 | New Year's Eve, Thu., Dec. 31 | New Year's Day, Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [MBTA (Boston)](https://www.mbta.com/holiday) | Subway, bus, Commuter Rail and The Ride on a regular weekday schedule | Subway, bus and The Ride on a Sunday schedule with Friday late-night extended hours; Commuter Rail on a weekend schedule; no ferry | Fare-free from 8 p.m.; subway on a regular weekday schedule until 3 p.m., then more service later than usual | Same as Christmas Day |
| [TriMet (Portland)](https://trimet.org/schedules/holidays) | Regular schedule | Buses, Metropolitan Area Express (MAX) and Streetcar on a Sunday schedule; bus lines that do not normally run Sundays are not in service; no Westside Express Service (WES) | All service free after 8 p.m.; MAX runs about every 30-35 minutes until about 3 a.m. | Same as Christmas Day |
| [MARTA (Atlanta)](https://www.itsmarta.com/holiday-operating-schedule.aspx) | Sunday (bus, rail, streetcar) | Sunday (bus, rail, streetcar) | Sunday on bus and streetcar; rail "Modified Sunday" | Sunday on bus and streetcar; rail "Modified Sunday" |
| [Sound Transit (Seattle area)](https://www.soundtransit.org/Rider-Guide/holiday-service) | ST Express weekday; Sounder reduced weekday; T Line reduced weekday; Link weekday | ST Express, T Line and Link Sunday; Sounder no service | ST Express and Link weekday*; Sounder reduced weekday; T Line weekday | ST Express, T Line and Link Sunday; Sounder no service |
| [Capital Metro (Austin)](https://www.capmetro.org/alerts/2026-holiday-service-schedule) | Regular service | Reduced Sunday service; no rail, UT Service, Night Owl or Express service; Transit Store closed | Regular bus service; rail extended until 2:30 a.m.; free rides after 5 p.m. | Sunday-level service; no rail, UT Service, Night Owl or Express service; Transit Store closed |
| [Metro Transit (Minneapolis-St. Paul)](https://metrotransit.org/holiday-fares) | Not listed | Listed as a holiday service day | Not listed | Listed as a holiday service day |

*Sound Transit: "Additional service may be provided on ST Express and Link if resources allow."

## Is MARTA really on a Sunday schedule on Christmas Eve?

Yes. MARTA's 2026 holiday table lists Sunday service on bus, rail and streetcar for Thursday, Dec. 24, the same as on Christmas Day. The MBTA, TriMet and Capital Metro run regular service on Christmas Eve, and Sound Transit runs weekday or reduced weekday service by mode. A weekday timetable does not apply in Atlanta on Dec. 24.

On New Year's Eve and New Year's Day, MARTA keeps Sunday service on bus and streetcar and lists rail as "Modified Sunday." The page does not define that term, so check the rail line's schedule page.

## Which transit agencies are free on New Year's Eve 2026?

Three of the agencies we checked name free rides, each with a start time. Late-night service also differs.

| Agency | Free from | Late-night service on Dec. 31 |
|---|---|---|
| MBTA (Boston) | 8 p.m. on subway, ferry, bus and The Ride | Subway service increases after 3 p.m. and runs later than usual |
| TriMet (Portland) | After 8 p.m., all service | MAX light rail about every 30-35 minutes until about 3 a.m. |
| Capital Metro (Austin) | After 5 p.m. | Rail extended until 2:30 a.m. |

The MBTA adds that Commuter Rail is free on trains that arrive at or leave North Station or South Station from 8 p.m. through the end of service. We found no free New Year's Eve rides on the MARTA, Sound Transit or Metro Transit pages.

## What changes in Portland and Austin on Christmas Day?

TriMet says bus lines that normally do not run on Sundays are not in service on Dec. 25 and Jan. 1, and the Westside Express Service (WES) commuter train has no service. Capital Metro runs reduced Sunday service with no rail on Dec. 25, and Sunday-level service with no rail on Jan. 1. Capital Metro's Transit Store is closed on both days. The MBTA's Charlie Service Center closes early at 2 p.m. on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.

## Which systems have not posted Christmas 2026 service?

We did not find 2026 Christmas or New Year's service for the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (WMATA), Chicago Transit Authority (CTA), Southeastern Pennsylvania Transportation Authority (SEPTA), NJ Transit, LA Metro, San Francisco Muni, the Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA) in New York, Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART), Dallas Area Rapid Transit (DART), Houston Metro, King County Metro or Miami-Dade, so none of their 2026 values are listed here.

Last year's notices show what these agencies posted, not what they will do in 2026:

| Agency | What it posted in December 2025 |
|---|---|
| BART | Sunday service, 8 a.m. to midnight, on Dec. 25, 2025 and Jan. 1, 2026; regular weekday service with extended late-night service on New Year's Eve |
| MTA New York | "On Thursday, January 1, the subway will run on a Sunday schedule." |
| Regional Transportation District (RTD) in Denver | "limited Sunday/Holiday schedule on Christmas Day, Thursday, Dec. 25, 2025" |
