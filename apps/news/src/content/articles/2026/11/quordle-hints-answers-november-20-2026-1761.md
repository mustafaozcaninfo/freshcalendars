**Friday, Nov. 20, 2026 is Quordle #1761 from Merriam-Webster.** Four words get three clues each, then comes a look at which letters the boards share, and five opening guesses scored on every grid. The answers are at the bottom.

Thursday's words are on the [Quordle #1760 page](/news/quordle-hints-answers-november-19-2026-1760/). Today's four words barely overlap, so a good first guess has to pick its letters carefully; the scoring rules are on the [Quordle guide](/news/topic/quordle/#how-it-works).

## Clues for Quordle words 1 to 4

Words run in grid order: Word 1 is top left, Word 2 top right, Word 3 bottom left, Word 4 bottom right.

**Word 1**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 7th letter of the alphabet and ends with the 6th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels sit back to back, right in the middle of the word.
- Hint 3: The heavy sadness that follows losing someone you love.

**Word 2**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 13th letter and ends with the 18th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels, split by a single consonant, with one more consonant on each side.
- Hint 3: Small in importance, or a person who is not yet a legal adult.

**Word 3**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 1st letter and ends with the 5th.
- Hint 2: Three vowels, and a consonant tucked between each pair of them.
- Hint 3: To treat someone cruelly, or to use something in the wrong way.

**Word 4**

- Hint 1: Starts with the 11th letter and ends with the 2nd.
- Hint 2: Only this word of the four repeats a consonant.
- Hint 3: Chunks of grilled meat pushed onto a skewer.

## Which letters do Grief, Minor, Abuse and Kebab share?

| Letter | Words | Positions |
|---|---|---|
| E | Grief, Abuse, Kebab | 4, 5 and 2 |
| B | Abuse, Kebab | 2; 3 and 5 |
| A | Abuse, Kebab | 1; 4 |
| R | Grief, Minor | 2; 5 |
| I | Grief, Minor | 3; 2 |

Eight other letters appear in one word only: G, F, M, N, O, U, S and K. Five shared letters plus eight single ones make the 13.

No shared letter holds the same spot in two words, so a guess can turn a letter green on one board and yellow on another. Amber shows it. Its A is green in Abuse, its B green in Kebab, its E green in Grief and its R green in Minor, one green per board. Kebab has two Bs, and Amber's single B marks only the third spot, so the fifth stays unmarked.

The overlaps split the set into two clusters. Abuse and Kebab share three letters, A, B and E. Grief and Minor share two, R and I. Only E bridges the clusters, and it skips Minor, which is why Minor touches nothing outside Grief.
