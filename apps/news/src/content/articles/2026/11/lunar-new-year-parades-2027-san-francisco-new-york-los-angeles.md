**San Francisco's Chinese New Year Parade is Saturday, Feb. 20, 2027, at 5:15 p.m. Pacific Time (PT)**, stepping off at Second and Market Streets, according to the parade's organizer. New York's parade is the next day, and Los Angeles holds its Golden Dragon Parade a week earlier.

Lunar New Year's Day itself is Saturday, Feb. 6, 2027, per the NYC Public Schools central calendar. The zodiac animal, the date and the lunar months are in our [Lunar New Year 2027 story](/news/lunar-new-year-2027-year-of-the-goat/) and the [Chinese lunar calendar 2027 guide](/news/chinese-lunar-calendar-2027-months-no-leap-month/). One naming note: the San Francisco organizer calls 2027 the "Year of the Ram", while most calendars say Goat. The table below lists the big-city parades in date order, with our Eastern Time (ET) conversion where a start time is posted.

## San Francisco Chinese New Year Parade: start, seats and TV

The organizer says the parade, "celebrating the Year of the Ram, will be on Saturday, February 20, 2027 at 5:15 PM, stepping off at Second and Market Streets." Its tickets page says it is a free event for all to attend, but tickets must be purchased for bleacher seats. Its home page says: "Mark your calendars, the Chinese New Year Parade will return on February 20, 2027." The route end and the 2027 TV channel are not announced.

## NYC Lunar New Year parade 2027: Firecracker Ceremony and Chinatown parade

The Better Chinatown Society posted its 2027 dates on Aug. 20, 2026. The 29th New Year Firecracker Ceremony and Cultural Festival is Saturday, Feb. 6, 2027, from 11:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. at Sara D. Roosevelt Park at Grand Street. The parade and festival is Sunday, Feb. 21, 2027, at 1:00 p.m. in Chinatown. The organizer's Chinese-language line for the parade still reads 2026年2月21日, last year's year; the English line says 2027, and Feb. 21, 2027 is a Sunday. No route is posted.

NYC Public Schools lists Lunar New Year 2027 on Saturday, Feb. 6, so it is not a school day off. Midwinter recess is Mon., Feb. 15 to Fri., Feb. 19, 2027, with schools closed.

## Los Angeles Golden Dragon Parade 2027

The Chinese Chamber of Commerce of Los Angeles lists the 128th Annual Golden Dragon Parade for Feb. 13, 2027, from 1:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. The parade starts at 4:00 p.m. ET. The page we read does not give a route.

## Which Lunar New Year parades are not announced yet?

- London: Visit London says the 2027 programme "has not been revealed yet".
- Vancouver Chinatown Spring Festival Parade, Chicago's Chinatown parade and the Washington, D.C. Chinese New Year Parade: no 2027 date is confirmed on an official page we checked, so none is given here.
- San Francisco: route end and TV channel. New York and Los Angeles: routes.
