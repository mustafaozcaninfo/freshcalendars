**Costco's Black Friday page says holiday savings are valid Nov. 27 to 30, 2026, Friday through Monday, according to Costco Wholesale.** Its pre-Thanksgiving page starts earlier, with savings valid Nov. 16 to 30. Costco says it will be closed Thursday, Nov. 26, for Thanksgiving. The table below lays out all four 2026 windows and where each applies, and there is a one-page printable.

## Costco's four holiday savings windows for 2026

| Window | Dates | Weekdays | Where it applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holiday savings (general) | Oct. 30 to Nov. 30 | Friday to Monday | Valid dates vary by item |
| Pre-Thanksgiving | Nov. 16 to 30 | Monday to Monday | U.S. warehouses and Costco.com, unless otherwise noted |
| Thanksgiving Day | Nov. 26 | Thursday | Warehouses closed, so savings are on Costco.com |
| Black Friday | Nov. 27 to 30 | Friday to Monday | Dates as listed on Costco's Black Friday page |

The weekdays are our computation. Costco's Black Friday page also says: "Online-only promotions valid only for purchases on Costco.com" and "Active Costco membership required for warehouse purchases." Costco's pre-Thanksgiving page adds that pricing on Costco.com may be higher than Costco warehouse pricing.

## How does 2026 compare with Costco's 2025 Black Friday dates?

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Black Friday savings | Nov. 28 to Dec. 1 | Nov. 27 to 30 |
| Pre-Thanksgiving savings | Not confirmed here | Nov. 16 to 30 |
| Thanksgiving Day | Not confirmed here | Warehouses closed Nov. 26 |

In 2025, Costco's holiday savings page was titled "Holiday Savings Nov. 28-Dec. 1" and its items read "Valid 11/28/25 - 12/1/25." Both windows run Friday through Monday.

## Is Costco open on Thanksgiving and Black Friday 2026?

Costco says that in observance of Thanksgiving it will be closed Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026. That matches the closure list in our [Costco holiday hours and closures story](/news/costco-holiday-hours-closures-2026-2027/). The Black Friday, Nov. 27, warehouse hours are not posted on the savings pages, so check your own warehouse page before you go.

## What has Costco not said about Black Friday 2026?

- Black Friday warehouse hours.
- Any early access for members; the savings pages state none.

## Kohl's and Macy's Black Friday 2026 dates

Kohl's and Macy's have not yet announced their 2026 Black Friday dates. For reference, Macy's 2025 schedule, in a release distributed by Business Wire on Nov. 6, 2025, had Black Friday Early Access Nov. 10 to 13, "Black Friday Starts Now" deals Nov. 14 to 22 and Black Friday Week sales Nov. 23 to 30. Macy's stores were closed on Thanksgiving 2025 and reopened Nov. 28, 2025, from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.
