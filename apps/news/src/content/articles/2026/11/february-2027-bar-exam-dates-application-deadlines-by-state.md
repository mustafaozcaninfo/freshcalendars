The February 2027 bar exam is set for Tuesday, Feb. 23, and Wednesday, Feb. 24, 2027, according to the National Conference of Bar Examiners (NCBE). As of Wed., Nov. 4, New York's application period has closed, Pennsylvania's timely deadline has passed and California's first-time filers are in a $50 late-fee window that ends Nov. 30. Below are the deadlines for five states in date order, a count of days left and a checklist for this month.

## When is the February 2027 bar exam?

NCBE's [Uniform Bar Exam (UBE) test administration page](https://www.ncbex.org/exams/ube) lists Feb. 23-24, 2027, then July 27-28, 2027, and Feb. 22-23, 2028. The state boards we checked name the same two days: the [State Bar of California](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/admissions/examinations/california-bar-examination/february-2027-california-bar-exam) says Tuesday, Feb. 23, and Wednesday, Feb. 24, 2027, the [Pennsylvania Board of Law Examiners](https://www.pabarexam.org/bar_exam_information/227ExamInfo.htm) says the exam is scheduled in person in Pittsburgh and Lancaster on those days, and the [New York State Board of Law Examiners](https://www.nybarexam.org/) says the February 2027 exam is scheduled for Feb. 23-24, 2027. From Nov. 4 the first day is 111 days away (our math).

NCBE's [NextGen page](https://www.ncbex.org/exams/nextgen) says that between July 2026 and July 2028 most jurisdictions will transition to the NextGen UBE. It lists Connecticut, Guam, Idaho, Maryland, Missouri, Northern Mariana Islands, Oregon, Palau, the Virgin Islands and Washington under July 2026, and lists Florida, New York, California and Texas among the jurisdictions moving later. We have not confirmed which exam format any state gives in February 2027, so check your board's page.

## California bar exam February 2027 deadline and late fees

The State Bar of California says the application opened Oct. 1 after 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time (PT), the timely filing deadline was Nov. 2 and the final filing and reinstatement deadline is Jan. 4. In between, a $50 late fee applies Nov. 3-30 and a $250 late fee applies from Dec. 1, 2026, to Jan. 4, 2027. A first-time filer who has not yet applied is in the $50 window today.

Applicants who took the July 2026 exam again, which the State Bar calls immediate repeaters, have their own dates: timely filing by Nov. 13, a $50 late fee Nov. 14 to Dec. 8, and a $250 late fee Dec. 9, 2026, to Jan. 4, 2027. California gives its own exam rather than the UBE, administered entirely in person, with a multiple-choice part of 200 questions.

## Pennsylvania, Florida, Tennessee and New York deadlines

**Pennsylvania.** The board lists a timely deadline of Oct. 30, a late deadline of Nov. 30 and a final deadline of Dec. 15, 2026. The exam is in person in Pittsburgh and Lancaster. The page does not list late fee amounts.

**Florida.** The Florida Board of Bar Examiners says the timely filing deadline for the February 2027 examination is Nov. 16, 2026, and that all filing requirements must be met by that date to avoid late filing fees. The board's schedule table lists other dates and fees that conflict with that sentence, so we leave them out; check the board's page for the fee and later deadlines.

**Tennessee.** The board's site announced that February 2027 applications would open Oct. 5. Its calendar shows a final deadline for the February 2027 exam on Dec. 1 (the calendar entry prints no year) and a deadline to withdraw with a refund on Feb. 1.

**New York.** The board says applications for the February 23-24, 2027 exam were open through Oct. 31, 2026, and describes the February filing period as Oct. 1-31.

## Which deadlines are not confirmed?

We could not load official pages for Texas, Illinois, New Jersey, Georgia, Arizona, Colorado, Massachusetts, Virginia, Washington, North Carolina or Ohio, so no deadline for those states appears here. No board we checked states a results release date for the February 2027 exam. Fees for New York, Pennsylvania and Tennessee were not confirmed either.

## What to do this month for the February 2027 bar exam

1. Open your own board's page and note its timely, late and final deadlines; they differ by state, as the table shows.
2. If you are in California and have not filed, the $50 late-fee window runs through Mon., Nov. 30.
3. If you are in Florida, the timely deadline is Mon., Nov. 16, and every filing requirement must be met by then.
4. If you are in Pennsylvania, the late deadline is Mon., Nov. 30 and the final deadline is Tue., Dec. 15.
5. Mark Feb. 23-24, 2027 on the [2027 printable yearly calendar](/printable-yearly-calendar.html) and the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) for the filing days.
