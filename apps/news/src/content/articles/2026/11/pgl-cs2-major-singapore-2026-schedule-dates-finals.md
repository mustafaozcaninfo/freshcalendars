**The Counter-Strike 2 (CS2) Major in Singapore runs Nov. 24 to Dec. 13, 2026, with 32 teams and a playoff at the Singapore Indoor Stadium on Dec. 10-13, according to the organizer's page at pglesports.com.** The organizer calls it the second CS2 Major of the year, with "three Swiss stages, one playoff." Below are the dates of each stage, the playoff start times in U.S., UK and Singapore time, ticket prices by day and a free printable stage calendar.

## CS2 Major Singapore 2026 stage dates

| Stage | Dates (2026) | Teams | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Tue., Nov. 24 to Fri., Nov. 27 | 16 teams | Swiss, Bo1 / Bo3 |
| Stage 2 | Sat., Nov. 28 to Tue., Dec. 1 | 8 invited + 8 from Stage 1 | Swiss |
| Stage 3 | Wed., Dec. 2 to Sun., Dec. 6 | 8 invited + 8 from Stage 2 | Swiss, Bo3 |
| Playoffs | Thu., Dec. 10 to Sun., Dec. 13 | Top 8 from Stage 3 | Best-of-5 grand final |

The format column uses the organizer's wording: Bo1 is a best-of-one map match and Bo3 a best-of-three. The organizer's page gives the Stage 2 and playoff formats only as "Swiss" and a best-of-5 grand final. The Kallang, the venue's operator, lists the event as starting Nov. 25. This story uses the organizer's Nov. 24 date.

The organizer has not published the venue for Stages 1 to 3, match times for those stages, or the playoff split into quarterfinal and semifinal days. Valve has not announced dates for Viewer Pass or Pick'Em.

## What time does the CS2 Major final start?

The venue lists session start times in Singapore Time (SGT), which is UTC+8: 5 p.m. on Dec. 10, 11 and 12, and 2 p.m. on Sunday, Dec. 13. These are session starts, not the time of each match. Doors open at 3:30 p.m. on Dec. 10-12 and 12:30 p.m. on Dec. 13.

| Session | Singapore (SGT) | ET | PT | UK (GMT) | Central Europe (CET) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 10 | 5 p.m. | 4 a.m. | 1 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Fri., Dec. 11 | 5 p.m. | 4 a.m. | 1 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | 5 p.m. | 4 a.m. | 1 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Sun., Dec. 13 (grand final day) | 2 p.m. | 1 a.m. | 10 p.m. Sat., Dec. 12 | 6 a.m. | 7 a.m. |

Conversions are ours, from the venue's local times. On Dec. 13 the Sunday session in Singapore falls on Saturday evening on the U.S. West Coast.

## Ticket prices by day at the Singapore Indoor Stadium

The Kallang says tickets went on sale Feb. 25, 2026, at 10 a.m. through Ticketmaster Singapore, online and by hotline, with a limit of 10 tickets per transaction. Prices are in Singapore dollars and exclude the booking fee.

| Day | Price (S$) |
|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 10 | S$144 |
| Fri., Dec. 11 | S$169 |
| Sat., Dec. 12 | S$294 |
| Sun., Dec. 13 | S$369 |

The Kallang describes the stadium as having a capacity of over 12,000 spectators, and the prize pool as US$1,250,000. It calls this the first CS2 Major in Southeast Asia.
