**The Virginia General Assembly convenes Wednesday, Jan. 13, 2027, and prefiling ends at 10:00 a.m. that day, according to the Division of Legislative Services (DLS).** The constitution sets the meeting day as the second Wednesday in January. The division's 2027 prefiling schedule, adopted under 2026 House Joint Resolution 30, runs from Nov. 16, 2026 to Jan. 13, 2027, and is the only part of the 2027 calendar published so far.

## Virginia General Assembly 2027 session dates

| Milestone | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Prefiling began | Mon., July 20, 2026 | DLS: "Prefiling begins Monday, July 20, 2026" |
| General Assembly convenes (day 1) | Wed., Jan. 13, 2027 | Constitution, Art. IV, Sec. 6: "The General Assembly shall meet once each year on the second Wednesday in January." |
| Prefiling ends | Wed., Jan. 13, 2027, 10:00 a.m. | DLS 2027 prefiling schedule |
| Session length | 30 days, extendable by up to 30 | Constitution, Art. IV, Sec. 6 |
| Reconvened session | Sixth Wednesday after adjournment, up to 3 days | Constitution, Art. IV, Sec. 6 |

The constitution says "no regular session of the General Assembly convened in an odd-numbered year shall continue longer than thirty days;" and that "any regular session may be extended for a period not exceeding thirty days." The reconvened session "shall not continue longer than three days."

## Virginia prefiling deadlines for the 2027 session

Prefiling lets members and the public see bills before the session starts. The division's calendar lists these steps. Days left count from today, Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026.

| Date | Days from Nov. 10 | Deadline (DLS wording) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Nov. 16, 2026 | 6 | "Last day for committees to act on legislation continued from the 2026 Regular Session by midnight" |
| Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 | 20 | "All requests for drafts of legislation to be prefiled to DLS by 5:00 p.m." |
| Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | 51 | "All drafts of legislation to be prefiled available for review by midnight" |
| Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 | 57 | "All requests for drafts, redrafts, and corrections of legislation creating or continuing a study to DLS by 5:00 p.m.; All requests for redrafts and corrections for legislation to be prefiled to DLS by 5:00 p.m." |
| Tue., Jan. 12, 2027 | 63 | "All drafts of legislation to be prefiled available by noon" |
| Wed., Jan. 13, 2027 | 64 | "Prefiling ends at 10:00 a.m." |

The calendar numbers session days from Jan. 13 as day 1, so Jan. 31 is day 19.

## When does the governor's budget proposal come due?

Section 2.2-1509(E) of the [Code of Virginia](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter15/section2.2-1509/) says: "On or before December 20 of the year immediately prior to the beginning of each regular session held in an odd-numbered year of the General Assembly, the Governor shall submit to the presiding officer of each house printed copies of all gubernatorial amendments proposed to the general appropriation act." December 20, 2026 is a Sunday.

## What did the 2025 session calendar look like?

The last odd-year session is the nearest comparison. The division's 2025 calendar, adopted under House Joint Resolution 430, convened the General Assembly on Wednesday, Jan. 8, 2025, at noon. The 2027 column below is our count with the same day numbers from Jan. 13, 2027. It is not adopted, and the Assembly may set different dates.

| Session day | 2025 date and entry | Same day in 2027 (computed, not adopted) |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Fri., Jan. 17: "All bills and joint resolutions filed with clerk by 3:00 p.m." | Fri., Jan. 22 |
| 28 | Tue., Feb. 4: "Each house to complete work on its own legislation except the Budget Bill" (crossover) | Tue., Feb. 9 |
| 30 | Thu., Feb. 6: "Houses of introduction to complete work on the Budget Bill" | Thu., Feb. 11 |
| 36 | Wed., Feb. 12: "Last day for each house to complete work on the Budget Bill and revenue bills of the other house and appoint conferees" | Wed., Feb. 17 |
| 41 | Mon., Feb. 17: "Last day for any committee action on legislation by midnight" | Mon., Feb. 22 |
| 46 | Sat., Feb. 22: "Adjournment Sine Die" | Sat., Feb. 27 |

In 2025, prefiling ended on day 6, Monday, Jan. 13, at 10:00 a.m. In 2027 it ends on day 1. The 2025 calendar said "Reconvened Session is scheduled for April 2, 2025," and the House Clerk's minutes list "April 02, 2025 - Reconvened Session." The constitution puts the reconvened session on the sixth Wednesday after adjournment, so an adjournment on Feb. 27, 2027 would mean Wednesday, April 7, 2027. That is our count, not a scheduled date.

## When does the governor act on bills, and when do new laws start?

Under Article V, Section 6(c), when fewer than seven days remain between a bill's presentation and adjournment, "the Governor shall have thirty days from the date of adjournment of the regular or special session in which to act on the bills presented to him." Article IV, Section 13 says laws enacted at a regular session "shall take effect on the first day of July following the adjournment," with the general appropriation law excepted. For the 2027 session the default effective date is Thursday, July 1, 2027.
