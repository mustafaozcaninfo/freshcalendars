The Football Association (The FA) lists the Emirates FA Cup third round proper for Saturday 9 January 2027, 53 days after Nov. 17, and says the draw is on Monday 7 December 2026, live on Turner Network Television (TNT) Sports before the Guiseley v Notts County tie. The 32 winners get £121,500 per club in the 2026-27 prize fund, and The FA has not yet given the exact draw time.

## FA Cup 2026-27 round dates and prize money

The FA's round dates page gives a Saturday for every round. The gap column counts the days since the round before it and is computed by Fresh Calendars. Prize money is per club, from The FA's 2026-27 prize fund page; rounds marked "Not listed here" have prize money not reported here.

| Round | Date | Days after previous round | Prize for winners, per club |
|---|---|---|---|
| Third round qualifying | Sat., Oct. 3, 2026 | n/a | Not listed here |
| Second round proper | Sat., Dec. 5, 2026 | 63 | £79,500 (20 winners) |
| Third round proper | Sat., Jan. 9, 2027 | 35 | £121,500 (32 winners) |
| Fourth round proper | Sat., Feb. 13, 2027 | 35 | Not listed here |
| Fifth round proper | Sat., Mar. 6, 2027 | 21 | Not listed here |
| Quarter-final | Sat., Apr. 3, 2027 | 28 | Not listed here |
| Semi-final | Sat., Apr. 24, 2027 | 21 | Not listed here |
| Final | Sat., May 22, 2027 | 28 | £2,120,000 (1 winner) |

The third round is the first of the 2027 rounds. From there, the 133 days from Saturday 9 January to the final on Saturday 22 May 2027 hold five more dates: February, March, two in April and May. The round dates page does not name the final's venue, so this story leaves it out.

## What the third round pays

For 2026-27 The FA's prize fund lists £121,500 for each of the 32 third round winners and £26,500 for each of the 32 losers, per club. Second round winners (20) receive £79,500 per club, and the one Final winner £2,120,000.

## FA Cup third round draw: Monday 7 December on TNT Sports

The FA's Nov. 12, 2026 broadcast notice says: "The third round draw will take place before the tie between Guiseley and Notts County on Monday 7 December live on TNT Sports, and further details will be announced in due course." Coverage of Guiseley v Notts County starts at 6:30pm GMT (1:30 p.m. ET, computed by Fresh Calendars) and includes the draw, with kick-off at 7:30pm GMT on TNT Sports 1 and HBO Max. The exact minute of the draw is not announced. Kick-off times for the third round are not confirmed either, and neither is the replay rule or a U.S. broadcaster.
