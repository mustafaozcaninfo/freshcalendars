**Beaujolais Nouveau 2026 is released on Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, the third Thursday of November, per Inter Beaujolais (the Beaujolais wine trade body, through its Discover Beaujolais site).** The date is not fixed on the calendar, so it lands on a different day of the month most years. This story lists the release dates through 2030 and the festival dates in Beaujeu, France.

## When is Beaujolais Nouveau released in 2026?

Thursday, Nov. 19. Discover Beaujolais says "the official release begins on the third Thursday of November" and that "the exact date changes every year." It also calls Beaujolais Nouveau "the first wine of the harvest," arriving "every third Thursday of November."

The 2026 date follows from that rule. Nov. 1, 2026, is a Sunday, so the Thursdays in November are the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th, and the third one is Nov. 19.

We computed each year from the third-Thursday rule. The 2024 and 2025 dates are already past.

## Why does the Beaujolais Nouveau date move?

The rule names a weekday and a position in the month, not a day number. The first Thursday of November falls between Nov. 1 and Nov. 7, so the third Thursday always falls between Nov. 15 and Nov. 21. The table shows both ends: Nov. 15 in 2029 and Nov. 21 in 2024 and 2030.

## How does Nov. 19 line up with Thanksgiving?

In 2026 the release falls exactly one week before Thanksgiving, which is Thursday, Nov. 26 (see [when Thanksgiving falls](/when-is/thanksgiving/)). ## What is Beaujolais Nouveau made from?

Discover Beaujolais says it is made predominantly from Gamay grapes grown within the Beaujolais and Beaujolais-Villages appellations.

## When is the Les Sarmentelles de Beaujeu festival in 2026?

The official site of Les Sarmentelles de Beaujeu lists the festival "Du 18 au 22 novembre 2026," which is Wednesday, Nov. 18, to Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, in Beaujeu, France. The release date, Thursday, Nov. 19, sits inside that window. We have not listed a program, prices or times because we did not confirm them on an official page.
