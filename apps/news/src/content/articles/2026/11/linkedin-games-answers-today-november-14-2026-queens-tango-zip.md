## Reading the Crossclimb #928 ladder without the words

Seven words, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word appears in this section.

| Move | Letter position that changes | Kind of letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 2 | consonant |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 2 | vowel |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 3 | consonant |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 1 | consonant |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 4 | vowel |

Position 1 and position 2 each change twice, positions 3 and 4 once each. Only the third and the last moves bring in a vowel. The last move is the only one that touches the final letter, so rungs 1 to 5 all end in the same letter as the top row.

## Starting points for Queens #928, Tango #768, Zip #607 and Mini Sudoku #460

**Queens #928.** Find the smallest color region and test each of its cells: a crown there blocks its whole row, column and touching cells.

**Tango #768.** Scan for a line with three cells still open and two symbols placed; the rule against three in a row often settles the last one.

**Zip #607.** Work backward from the highest number as well as forward from 1, since the path has to meet in the middle.

**Mini Sudoku #460.** Take the row or column with the most digits already filled and list what is missing there.
