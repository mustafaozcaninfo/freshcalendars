## How Quordle works

You have nine guesses to solve four five-letter words. Every guess is entered on all four boards at once, and the tiles color like Wordle's: green for the right letter in the right spot, yellow for a letter in the wrong spot, gray for a letter not in that word.

## Clues for each word

**The word that starts with D**

- Letters: starts with D, ends with E, and has an R and an O inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: the past tense of a verb for steering a car.

**The word that starts with P**

- Letters: starts with P, ends with D, and has an R, an I and an E inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: forced something open with a lever.

**The word that starts with S**

- Letters: starts with S, ends with L, and has an H and an E inside; only one vowel letter, and it is the only word today that repeats a letter.
- Meaning: the hard outer covering of an egg, a nut or a snail.

**The word that starts with B**

- Letters: starts with B, ends with N, and has an E, a G and an I inside; two vowel letters, no repeated letter.
- Meaning: to start something.

## A starter strategy for today

A first guess such as Raise, with R, A, I, S and E, puts at least one letter into all four boards, because E turns up in every word. Solve the board with the most green first, and do not spend a guess on a board that is already solved.
