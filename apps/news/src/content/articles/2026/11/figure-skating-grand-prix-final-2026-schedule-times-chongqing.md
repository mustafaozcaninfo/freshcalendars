**The International Skating Union (ISU) Grand Prix Final 2026 opens Thursday, Dec. 10 in Chongqing, China, and the senior men's short program starts at 6:05 p.m. local time, 5:05 a.m. ET, according to the ISU timetable dated Nov. 3, 2026.** The timetable marks competition on Dec. 10, 11 and 12 and says "Subject to changes!" The ISU has not named the venue.

## Grand Prix Final 2026 dates and place

The ISU's June 16, 2026 announcement lists the Final in "Chongqing, China (December 10–13)", and Skate Canada's June 26 guide gives "December 10-13, 2026". The ISU timetable fills those days this way: Wednesday, Dec. 9 is practice, Thursday to Saturday are competition, and Sunday, Dec. 13 has only off-ice round-table meetings.

At the end of the series, per the ISU, "the top six Women, Men, Pair Skating teams and Ice Dance couples will qualify." The series has two stops left: the Finlandia Trophy in Helsinki, from Nov. 20, and the Nippon Hoso Kyokai (NHK) Trophy in Tokyo, Nov. 27-29. The six per discipline are set after the NHK Trophy, so no skater is confirmed for Chongqing yet. The earlier stops were Angers, France (Oct. 23-25), Kelowna, Canada (Oct. 30-Nov. 1, [our Skate Canada International schedule](/news/skate-canada-international-2026-schedule-kelowna-times-tv/)), Shenzhen, China (Nov. 6-8) and Everett, Washington (Nov. 13-15).

## Early-morning senior skating and other timetable details

- Every senior segment starts between 4:35 a.m. and 8:30 a.m. ET, so the senior skating falls in the early morning in the U.S. East. The senior men's short program (5:05 a.m.) is the first senior event after the opening ceremony.
- The exhibition is Saturday night in Chongqing (Dec. 12), not Sunday. Sunday, Dec. 13 has only off-ice meetings.
- Senior pairs skate the short program on Friday, Dec. 11, not Thursday. Thursday's senior events are the men's short, women's short and rhythm dance.

## How to watch the Grand Prix Final 2026 in the U.S., Canada and the UK

U.S. television for the 2026 Final is not yet announced. NBC Sports' figure skating guide, dated Nov. 14, 2026, mentions the Final on Dec. 10-13 in Chongqing, but its broadcast schedule does not list it yet.

For comparison, in 2025 NBC Sports said the Final in Nagoya, Japan (Dec. 4-7) would stream live on Peacock "starting Thursday, Dec. 4, at 5 a.m. ET". NBC aired the Sunday men's and women's free skates at 4:30 p.m. ET and E! aired earlier shows. That is last year's plan, not the 2026 one.

Canadian and UK broadcasters are not announced. Skate Canada lists the ISU YouTube channel for Grand Prix events in general; it has not said the Final is included.
