**The Great Dickens Christmas Fair opens Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026 at the Cow Palace in Daly City, California, and runs five weekends through Dec. 20, plus the Friday after Thanksgiving, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., according to the fair's own site.** Three other Dickens festivals fall inside that window: Holly, Michigan (Nov. 27 to Dec. 13), Galveston, Texas (Dec. 4-6) and Franklin, Tennessee (Dec. 12-13). The day-by-day hours, prices and costume rules are below, with a one-page planner to print.

The printable planner adds each start time in all four U.S. zones: the Cow Palace's 10 a.m. PT opening is 1 p.m. ET, and Galveston's noon start on Sunday, Dec. 6 is 1 p.m. ET.

The Galveston Historical Foundation says prices rise $5 on Nov. 20, so the $20 and $13 figures are advance prices. The Cow Palace fair says "There are no on-site ticket sales at the Cow Palace," and that daily attendance is limited, so tickets for a given day are bought ahead on its [tickets page](https://dickensfair.com/tickets).

## Great Dickens Christmas Fair at the Cow Palace

The fair is held in the Cow Palace Exhibition Halls at 2600 Geneva Avenue in Daly City, in the San Francisco area, and is produced by Red Barn Productions and the Patterson Family, according to its [general information page](https://dickensfair.com/general-information). It is indoors, with shows on multiple stages included in admission. The fair lists American Sign Language interpretation on Saturday, Nov. 28 and Saturday, Dec. 12.

## Dickens on The Strand in Galveston

The Galveston Historical Foundation calls 2026 the 53rd anniversary of [Dickens on The Strand](https://www.galvestonhistory.org/news/dickens-on-the-strand), held in the historic downtown Strand district. Friday, Dec. 4 is Fezziwig's Friday with free admission from 5 p.m. to 9 p.m.; Saturday, Dec. 5 (10 a.m. to 9 p.m.) and Sunday, Dec. 6 (12 p.m. to 6 p.m.) are paid. The foundation's phone number is 409-765-7834. Parking prices are not listed on the pages we checked.

## Dickens of a Christmas in Franklin, Tennessee

The Heritage Foundation of Williamson County lists the 41st annual [Dickens of a Christmas](https://williamsonheritage.org/event/41st-annual-dickens-of-a-christmas-2026/) from Dec. 12 at 10:00 a.m. to Dec. 13 at 5:00 p.m., on Main Street in downtown Franklin. Its festival page gives Saturday as 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Sunday as 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. The foundation's pages do not list an admission price. There is no street parking within the festival footprint; Franklin Transit shuttles run from the Park at Harlinsdale Farm for $1 per person each way.

## Holly Dickens Festival in Michigan

Admission to the [Holly Dickens Festival](https://www.hollydickensfestival.org) is free for all ages, and the festival calls itself the "longest-running of its kind." It centers on Historic Battle Alley and downtown Holly. The kickoff is a lighted parade and tree lighting on Friday, Nov. 27 at 6 p.m. Saturdays run 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Sundays 12 p.m. to 5 p.m. on Nov. 28-29, Dec. 5-6 and the third weekend, Dec. 12-13.
