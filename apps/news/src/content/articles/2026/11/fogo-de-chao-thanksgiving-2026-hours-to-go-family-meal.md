**Fogo de Chão is open on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 8:30 p.m., with dinner pricing all day, its Thanksgiving to-go and catering page says.** The page reads: "Celebrate Thanksgiving at Fogo on Thursday, November 26, from 11 AM to 8:30 PM. Enjoy dinner pricing all day, plus specialty Thanksgiving offerings like roasted turkey and au jus, sweet potato casserole, Brazilian sausage and apple dressing and cranberry relish."

Fogo de Chão is a Brazilian steakhouse known for its churrasco service. For Thanksgiving it adds two ready-made options to take home: a Holiday Turkey Family Meal for 3 to 4 people and a Holiday Group Package for groups of 10 or more. The page does not list the dinner price in dollars, which varies by location, so the per-person figures below are our arithmetic from the chain's starting prices. To reserve a table, the page says to "Reserve at fogo.com/reservations."

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026; the date is on our [Thanksgiving 2026 page](/when-is/thanksgiving/). "Dinner pricing all day" means the dinner price applies during lunch hours too; the dollar figure is set at each restaurant.

## What is in the Fogo de Chão Holiday Turkey Family Meal to go

The Holiday Turkey Family Meal "Serves 3–4," starts at $100, is "Pre-cooked and ready to eat" and is offered "Thanksgiving weekend only." Fogo de Chão says pickup or delivery runs "all day, Thursday, November 26 – Sunday, November 29," that prices vary by location and that it is available while supplies last. The page lists these items in the meal:

- Roasted Turkey Breast Au Jus
- Sweet Potato Casserole
- Brazilian Sausage & Apple Dressing
- Honey-Roasted Rainbow Carrots
- Cranberry Relish
- Caesar Salad
- Pão de Queijo (Brazilian cheese bread)

The Holiday Group Package is fire-roasted catering for "Groups of 10 or more," "hot and ready to serve," starting at $32 per person. The page says you "Choose two of your favorite fire-roasted meats (yes, turkey made the list, and so did our signature Picanha) and we'll bring the Brazilian holiday sides, au jus and plenty of Pão de Queijo." Sweet potato casserole, Brazilian sausage and apple dressing, and honey-roasted rainbow carrots are also sold by the pan, each serving 8 to 10, for pickup or delivery starting Nov. 20.

## Fogo de Chão Thanksgiving catering dates to order by

To order catering, the page says to "Order at catering.fogo.com at least 24 hours ahead for pickup or delivery, November 20–29. Thanksgiving Day orders are pickup only." A Thanksgiving Day order therefore has to be placed by the same time on Wednesday, Nov. 25, and can only be picked up. The Family Meal's own order deadline is not listed on the page.

| When | Step |
|---|---|
| Fri., Nov. 20 to Sun., Nov. 29 | Catering ordering window at catering.fogo.com, at least 24 hours ahead |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | Side-dish pans (serving 8 to 10 each) available for pickup or delivery |
| By Wed., Nov. 25 | Order catering for Thanksgiving Day pickup, 24 hours ahead; pickup only that day |
| Thu., Nov. 26 to Sun., Nov. 29 | Holiday Turkey Family Meal pickup or delivery window |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Dine in 11 a.m. to 8:30 p.m.; reserve at fogo.com/reservations |
