**The Denver Nuggets play at the Golden State Warriors on Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, at 8:30 p.m. Mountain Time (MT), which is 10:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), on ABC and ESPN, according to the National Basketball Association (NBA) schedule.** The Nuggets have 11 games in December 2026, four at home and seven on the road, and open the month at Philadelphia on Wednesday, Dec. 2.

The NBA's 2026-27 regular season schedule PDF, dated Aug. 13, 2026 and marked "subject to change," prints each game in ET and in the home team's local time. The table converts every ET time to MT (ET minus 2 hours). A printable of the same rows is further down.

The Nuggets' own NBA.com schedule article lists the game as "Christmas Day, December 25th, 8:30 p.m. MT @ Golden State Warriors; ABC." The league's PDF puts it last on a five-game Christmas slate, all on ABC and ESPN, with times in ET:

## No Nuggets games from Dec. 3 to Dec. 11

The NBA's August schedule lists no Nuggets game from Thursday, Dec. 3, through Friday, Dec. 11, and its PDF has no regular-season games for any team from Dec. 4 to Dec. 11. After the Dec. 2 game at Philadelphia, the next listed Nuggets game is Saturday, Dec. 12, at Utah.

That window holds the Emirates NBA Cup knockout rounds. NBA.com's key dates page, updated Nov. 7, 2026, sets the quarterfinals on Dec. 4 and 5, the semifinals on Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9, and the championship on Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Whether the Nuggets play in the knockouts depends on their group results, which this schedule does not show. Our [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) has every round, and the [Nuggets November schedule](/news/denver-nuggets-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-mexico-city-printable/) covers the group games.

## Four home games at Ball Arena, seven on the road

The four home games are Dec. 17 against Atlanta, Dec. 19 against the Lakers, Dec. 27 against Indiana and Dec. 28 against Minnesota. The other seven are away: Philadelphia, Utah and Portland come first, then the Clippers on Dec. 21, the Lakers on Dec. 23, Golden State on Dec. 25 and Minnesota on Dec. 31.

The only back-to-back is Dec. 27 and 28, both at home. The rows show three road games in five days from Dec. 21 to Dec. 25. Two opponents appear twice: the Lakers (home Dec. 19, away Dec. 23) and Minnesota (home Dec. 28, away Dec. 31).

The earliest tip-off of the month is the New Year's Eve game at Minnesota, Thursday, Dec. 31, at 2 p.m. MT (4 p.m. ET), and the latest in MT is the Dec. 15 game at Portland, 9 p.m. MT (11 p.m. ET).
