**The New York Rangers play 13 games in November 2026, and their next is Monday, Nov. 16, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against the Montreal Canadiens at Madison Square Garden (MSG), according to the National Hockey League (NHL) schedule.** They have played seven so far and are 5-2. The full month with start times, TV and results is below, and a one-page printable is further down.

The source is the NHL's club schedule for the Rangers, checked Nov. 14, 2026, including final scores for games already played. We converted start times to ET, which is New York time, and calculated the weekdays. Seven games are at home. Six are on the road. Edmonton opened the month with a win at the Garden on Sunday, Nov. 1, and the Rangers then won two straight at home before the Seattle and Western Canada road trip.

Where a game has no MSG feed listed, it is a national broadcast.

## The Rangers' Seattle and Western Canada road trip, Nov. 7-13

The Rangers played four games in seven nights out West, every one a late start in New York time. They opened at Seattle on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 5 p.m. ET (2 p.m. PT) at Climate Pledge Arena and won 4-3, lost at Edmonton 3-1 on Monday, Nov. 9, at 8 p.m. ET (6 p.m. MT), won at Calgary 3-1 on Wednesday, Nov. 11, at 7:30 p.m. ET (5:30 p.m. MT) and won at Vancouver 5-1 on Friday, Nov. 13, at 9 p.m. ET (6 p.m. PT). The three games in Canada added Sportsnet, Sportsnet+ or Prime Video alongside the MSG feed; the Seattle game was on MSG and MSG 2. For the Oilers' own month, see our [Oilers schedule](/news/edmonton-oilers-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## What channel is Rangers vs. Canadiens on Nov. 16, 2026?

The Rangers host the Montreal Canadiens on Monday, Nov. 16, at 7:30 p.m. ET at Madison Square Garden. The NHL lists it as a national broadcast on TNT, truTV and HBO Max, with no MSG feed. It is one of two Rangers games on TNT this month; the other is at Chicago on Black Friday. For another Original Six schedule, see our [Maple Leafs November 2026 schedule](/news/toronto-maple-leafs-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## Rangers on Thanksgiving weekend 2026

These are our own counts from the NHL's schedule:

- **Thanksgiving week:** the Rangers play at Buffalo on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 7 p.m. ET, then at Chicago on Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27, at 5:30 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. CT) on TNT, truTV and HBO Max. They have no game on [Thanksgiving Day](/when-is/thanksgiving/), Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026.
- **Home and away:** seven of the 13 games are at Madison Square Garden; six are on the road.
- **Month end:** Carolina visits on Monday, Nov. 30, at 7 p.m. ET, the last game of the month, after a Sunday matinee against the same team on Nov. 22.
