**The New York Rangers play 13 games in November 2026, starting Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against the St. Louis Blues at Madison Square Garden (MSG), according to the National Hockey League (NHL) schedule.** The full month with start times and TV is below, and a one-page printable is further down.

The source is the NHL's club schedule for the Rangers, fetched Nov. 2, 2026. We converted its start times to ET, which is New York time, and calculated the weekdays. Seven games are at home. Six are on the road. Edmonton opened the month at the Garden on Sunday, Nov. 1, so the next home date is Tuesday night against St. Louis, the first of three straight home games before the team leaves on a Western Canada and Seattle road trip.

## Rangers game times and TV for every November game

Network names are written as the NHL lists them. MSG Networks (MSG, MSG 2 and MSG Sportsnet) is the Rangers' regional outlet; the NHL Network, Turner Network Television (TNT), truTV and HBO Max are national in the United States; Sportsnet, Sportsnet+ and Prime Video carry the three games played in Canada. Where a game has no MSG feed listed, it is a national broadcast.

## The Rangers' Western Canada and Seattle road trip, Nov. 7-13

The Rangers play four games in seven nights out West, every one a late start in New York time. They open at Seattle on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 5 p.m. ET (2 p.m. PT) at Climate Pledge Arena, then visit Edmonton on Monday, Nov. 9, at 8 p.m. ET (6 p.m. MT), Calgary on Wednesday, Nov. 11, at 7:30 p.m. ET (5:30 p.m. MT) and Vancouver on Friday, Nov. 13, at 9 p.m. ET (6 p.m. PT). The three games in Canada add Sportsnet, Sportsnet+ or Prime Video alongside the MSG feed; the Seattle game stays on MSG and MSG 2. For the Oilers' own month, see our [Oilers schedule](/news/edmonton-oilers-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## What channel is Rangers vs. Canadiens on Nov. 16, 2026?

The Rangers host the Montreal Canadiens on Monday, Nov. 16, at 7:30 p.m. ET at Madison Square Garden. The NHL lists it as a national broadcast on TNT, truTV and HBO Max, with no MSG feed. It is one of two Rangers games on TNT this month; the other is at Chicago on Black Friday. For another Original Six schedule, see our [Maple Leafs November 2026 schedule](/news/toronto-maple-leafs-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## Rangers on Thanksgiving weekend 2026

These are our own counts from the NHL's schedule:

- **Thanksgiving week:** the Rangers play at Buffalo on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 7 p.m. ET, then at Chicago on Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27, at 5:30 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. CT) on TNT, truTV and HBO Max. They have no game on [Thanksgiving Day](/when-is/thanksgiving/), Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026.
- **Home and away:** seven of the 13 games are at Madison Square Garden; six are on the road.
- **Month end:** Carolina visits on Monday, Nov. 30, at 7 p.m. ET, the last game of the month, after a Sunday matinee against the same team on Nov. 22.
