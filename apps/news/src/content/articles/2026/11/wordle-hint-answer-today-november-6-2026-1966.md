## How Wordle works

You get six tries to find a five-letter word. After each guess, a green tile means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means the letter is in the word but in another spot, and gray means it is not in the word at all. A letter that appears twice is only marked twice if you guess it twice. Gray tiles matter too.

## Six clues, gentle to strong

1. **Part of speech:** it is a noun.
2. **Topic:** it means worthless stuff, such as trash, junk or poor-quality material.
3. **Vowels:** one vowel letter, E, in the third spot, and the word ends in K.
4. **Repeated letter:** none. All five letters are different.
5. **First letter:** D.
6. **Strong nudge:** a critic might call a badly made film this: "The sequel was pure ___."

## Guessing tips for today

The word starts with D and ends with K, so a guess with D first and K last can turn two tiles green right away. The last two spots are a pair, C then K. Try it. Apart from the E in the third spot, there are no other vowels to hunt for.
