American Education Week 2026 runs Nov. 16-20, Monday to Friday, according to the National Education Association (NEA), which lists a different theme for each day. The week opens with Kickoff Day on Nov. 16 and ends with Substitute Educators Day on Nov. 20. Below is the day-by-day list and a one-page printable of the five themes.

## American Education Week 2026 daily themes

The NEA's [American Education Week page](https://www.nea.org/AmericanEducationWeek) gives one theme per day. The last column is our own suggestion for each day, not an NEA instruction.

| Date | NEA theme | What the NEA says | A way to take part (our suggestion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday, Nov. 16 | Kickoff Day | Schools will celebrate excellence in education by hosting kickoff events and activities | Check your school's newsletter for its plans |
| Tuesday, Nov. 17 | Family Day | Schools across the nation invite parents into the classroom to experience what the day is like for their child | Ask the teacher whether a visit can be scheduled |
| Wednesday, Nov. 18 | Education Support Professionals Day | First celebrated in 1987 after the NEA's Representative Assembly | Write a thank-you note to a school staff member |
| Thursday, Nov. 19 | Educator for a Day | Community leaders are invited to experience the day as educators and experience the challenges of teaching | Ask the school how community members can sign up |
| Friday, Nov. 20 | Substitute Educators Day | Substitute educators play a vital role in the maintenance and continuity of daily education | Thank a substitute who has covered your child's class |

The printable below puts the five days on one sheet so you can pencil in a school event or a note to send.

## Where does the week fall around Thanksgiving?

Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, Nov. 26, so American Education Week sits in the week before Thanksgiving week. That is our own arithmetic from the two dates, not an NEA rule. Districts set their own break dates; our [Thanksgiving break list for school districts](/news/thanksgiving-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) covers those, and the [November 2026 dates list](/news/november-2026-important-dates-days-until/) shows the rest of the month.

## What is Education Support Professionals Day?

Wednesday, Nov. 18 is Education Support Professionals Day, per the NEA, which says the day was first celebrated in 1987 after the NEA's Representative Assembly. The NEA publishes an [Education Support Professionals (ESP) Day toolkit](https://www.nea.org/resource-library/esp-day-toolkit) and more ideas at [nea.org/espday](https://www.nea.org/espday).

## How did American Education Week start?

The NEA says the first observance took place Dec. 4-10, 1921, with the NEA and the American Legion. A year later, the then U.S. Office of Education joined as a cosponsor, and the Parent Teacher Association (PTA) followed in 1938. Counting from 1921, 2026 is 105 years later (our arithmetic).

## Where are the NEA's day-specific resources?

The NEA has pages for [Educator for a Day](https://www.nea.org/be-an-educator-day) and for [substitute teachers](https://www.nea.org/resource-library/substitute-teachers). Dates for 2027 are not yet announced.
