**Ask Italian's Christmas menu is available in all its UK restaurants "now until 4th January 2027" at £29.95 for three courses or £24.95 for two, according to the Ask Italian Christmas page.** All restaurants are closed on Christmas Day and open from 11:30am on Boxing Day. Our research desk read the page and the set menu PDF on 22 November; the prices, dates and dishes below follow their wording.

## What does the Ask Italian Christmas menu cost in 2026?

The page says: "Choose two courses (£24.95) or three courses (£29.95)." Its banner reads "Enjoy 3 courses for £29.95 with our Christmas Set Menu". The page gives no start date for the menu, so none is shown here.

| Option | Price |
|---|---|
| 2 courses | £24.95 |
| 3 courses | £29.95 |
| Difference | £5.00 (our arithmetic) |

The set menu PDF prints these upgrades and extras exactly as follows:

| Extra | Price on the PDF |
|---|---|
| Slow-cooked beef short rib (main) | + £5.00 upgrade |
| King prawn & saffron risotto (main) | + £2.00 upgrade |
| Garlic king prawn tagliatelle (pasta) | + £2.00 upgrade |
| Pandoro Tree (dessert) | + £2.00 upgrade |
| Welcome drink | +4.95 per person |
| Nibbles | from 3.95 |
| Sides | from 5.75 or 3 for 15 |

The PDF says the welcome drink is "available for your first drink only when dining from our Festive Set Menu".

## How do bookings and deposits work at Ask Italian?

The page says "Our bookings are open now!" and that some spaces are kept for walk-ins. For tables of 8 or more, Ask Italian sends "a link to pre-authorise a £5 'deposit' per head". The page says the link may come from its booking partner Design My Night. "In the event that you do not turn up for your booking or fail to cancel 24 hours before, the full amount of the deposit will be charged." The page gives no refund terms beyond that. Ask Italian also says: "No, we won't be offering a buffet style service over the Christmas."
