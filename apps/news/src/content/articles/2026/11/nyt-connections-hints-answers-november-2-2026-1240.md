## How the game works

Sort the 16 entries into four groups of four. You can make four mistakes. The groups run from yellow to green, blue and purple, with purple the group that depends most on wordplay.

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Things you pack for a day on the trail.
- Hint 2: One shows the way, one carries the rest, one goes on your feet and one holds a drink.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Each entry can be a verb meaning to irritate someone.
- Hint 2: A prickly plant, a scented stick that burns, a sudden flash of resentment, and what you do to a clock spring.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Each one puts a cloud of tiny water drops or vapor into the air.
- Hint 2: Two are machines, one is a frozen solid that gives off cold vapor, and one is found in nature.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Each entry has a mirror in it or on it.
- Hint 2: One spins from a ceiling, one holds toothpaste, one is where you try on clothes and one is a tube you turn to see patterns.

## Solving tips

Start with yellow, because the trail theme is clear. Be careful with Water Bottle, which also looks like it belongs next to Waterfall. Incense is a trap of its own, since it can be a thing you burn rather than a word for anger. Leave purple for last: once the other three groups are placed, the four entries left over are easy to check against the idea of a mirror.
