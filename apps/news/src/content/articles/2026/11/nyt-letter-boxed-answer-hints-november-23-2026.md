**Letter Boxed from The New York Times (NYT) for Monday, Nov. 23, 2026 has the sides I/H/Z, A/D/S, V/O/R and M/T/U, with a par of 6, according to the puzzle data on the Letter Boxed page.** The saved word list lists 802 dictionary words, and just 2 pairs of them use all twelve letters. ## Which sides are linked by the fewest words?

Every move in a word jumps from one side to another. For each pair of sides, the table counts the dictionary words with at least one move between them, then the moves themselves in each direction.

| Sides | Words with a move between them | Moves, first side to second | Moves, second to first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | 427 | 356 | 295 |
| 1 and 3 | 281 | 169 | 180 |
| 1 and 4 | 278 | 146 | 223 |
| 2 and 3 | 379 | 256 | 296 |
| 2 and 4 | 488 | 404 | 447 |
| 3 and 4 | 353 | 257 | 235 |

Side 1 holds the Z and the I, and it sits in the two thinnest pairs: sides 1 and 4 at 278 words, sides 1 and 3 at 281. The busiest pair is sides 2 and 4 with 488. Moves from side 1 to side 4 number only 146, the lowest of the twelve directions.

## Seventeen pairs that never occur

Of the 108 ordered pairs of letters on different sides, 17 appear in no dictionary word: Z then D, S, R, T or U; D then T; S then Z, V or R; V then H, Z, D, M, T or U; M then V; and T then V. Six more occur in exactly one word: H then V, Z then V, D then M, R then Z, M then Z and M then D.

The V is in 9 of the 17 and the Z in 7. After a V nothing from the H, Z, D, M, T or U sides can follow, and the V is the last letter of only 6 words. The Z is rare too: 46 words use it, 4 begin with it and 6 end with it.

## Longest words and what a two-word route allows

Two words reach 11 letters, Arthritisms and Idiomatisms, and six more have 10. Neither of the 11-letter words can be part of a two-word route. Of the 2 routes, the first word never has more than 7 letters and the second never more than 9, so the long words only matter if you aim for three or more words.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, four fewer than the par of 6.
2. The first word has 7 letters and the second has 9 letters, picked from 78 words of the first length and 10 of the second.
3. The first word opens on the M/T/U side and closes on the I/H/Z side. The second opens on that I/H/Z side and closes on the A/D/S side.
4. The Z is in both words, and the V is in the first.
5. The first word starts with M and ends in H. The second starts with H and ends in S.
