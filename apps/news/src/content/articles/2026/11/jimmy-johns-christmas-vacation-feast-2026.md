**Jimmy John's Christmas Vacation Feast starts Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at participating Jimmy John's locations nationwide, according to a Nov. 18 story on the Inspire Brands newsroom.** Inspire Brands is Jimmy John's parent company. Guests order through the Jimmy John's app or jimmyjohns.com. The tables below list what is in each item and the dates Jimmy John's gave, from Chicago's light display on Saturday, Nov. 21 to Saturday, Dec. 26.

Jimmy John's calls the feast a limited-time meal inspired by the legendary holiday film National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation. It has three menu items, film-themed packaging and a collectible turtleneck dickie. From Thursday, Nov. 19, the start is four days away. For how other chains compare, see our [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/).

## What is in the Jimmy John's Christmas Vacation Feast?

| Part | What is in it |
|---|---|
| Jelly of the Month Club Sandwich | Toasted French bread with turkey, bacon, cheddar, cranberry sauce, lettuce, tomato and cranberry mayo |
| Stuffing Flavored Jimmy Chips | Kettle-cooked chips in a classic stuffing flavor |
| Griswold Family Chaos Cookie | Chocolate chips, candies, peppermint, pretzels, marshmallows, sprinkles and a cornflake "Crunch Enhancer" |
| Cousin Eddie-inspired turtleneck dickie | Four colors with movie quotes; one comes with each feast |

Source: Inspire Brands newsroom, Nov. 18, 2026. The dickies are also sold on their own. Jimmy John's gave no price, date or count for them.

## Can you swap items in the Jimmy John's feast?

Yes. Guests can swap the Jelly of the Month Club Sandwich for any Toasted sandwich. The page also allows swaps of the chips and the cookie. It does not say whether a swap changes the price.

## Jimmy John's Christmas Vacation dates to mark

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| Saturday, Nov. 21 | Chicago light display can be viewed |
| Monday, Nov. 23 | Feast starts at participating locations; merch collection goes on sale at the Jimmy Store |
| Saturday, Dec. 26 | Chicago light display viewing ends |

Jimmy John's covers its restaurant at 501 N. LaSalle Dr. in Chicago in 25,000 lights, viewable Nov. 21 through Dec. 26. From Nov. 23, a limited-edition film-inspired collection is also at the Jimmy Store, store.jimmyjohns.com. The feast has no end date, so Dec. 26 applies only to the lights.

## What Jimmy John's has not said about the feast

The Inspire Brands story gives no price for the feast or the dickie, no end date and no limit on quantity. It does not say whether the feast can be ordered in store or through catering, or how many dickies exist. Jimmy John's has not posted a price.
