**Christmas falls on Friday, Dec. 25, 2026, and these 20 trivia questions draw on the Census Bureau's newest holiday edition and four other federal sources.** The Census Bureau published "The 2025 Winter Holiday Season" on Dec. 16, 2025, and its Facts for Features list showed no 2026 holiday edition as of Nov. 15, 2026. So the Census numbers below describe 2023 and 2024, and each answer carries its source and the year it refers to. A free one-page quiz prints with the answers on a cut-off strip.

## Christmas tree trivia: how many are cut and where

| # | Question | Answer | Source | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What day of the week is Christmas in 2026? | Friday, Dec. 25 | Office of Personnel Management (OPM) federal holidays | 2026 |
| 2 | How many Christmas trees did U.S. farms cut? | More than 14.5 million | U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Economic Research Service (ERS), citing the Census of Agriculture | 2022 |
| 3 | About how many U.S. tree farms grow Christmas trees? | About 16,000, in all 50 states | ERS | 2022 |
| 4 | What did growers earn from cut trees? | $553 million, up from $377 million in 2017 | ERS | 2022 |
| 5 | Which two states grew more than half the trees cut? | Oregon and North Carolina | ERS | 2022 |

ERS published these figures in a Chart of Note on Dec. 4, 2024. They come from the 2022 Census of Agriculture, so they are the latest the source gives.

| # | Question | Answer | Source | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | When was the first National Christmas Tree lit? | Dec. 24, 1923, at 5:00 p.m. on the Ellipse, by President Calvin Coolidge | National Park Service | 1923 |
| 7 | What kind of tree was it, and from where? | A balsam fir cut in Vermont, listed at 48 feet; reports about its height vary | National Park Service | 1923 |
| 8 | How was it lit? | With 2,500 electric bulbs in red, white and green | National Park Service | 1923 |
| 9 | Where is the 2026 Capitol Christmas Tree from? | A 76-foot Engelmann spruce from the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests in Idaho | Architect of the Capitol | 2026 |
| 10 | When did Congress pass the first federal holiday law? | June 28, 1870, an act that named New Year's Day, Independence Day and Christmas Day among its holidays | Congressional Research Service (CRS) | 1870 |
