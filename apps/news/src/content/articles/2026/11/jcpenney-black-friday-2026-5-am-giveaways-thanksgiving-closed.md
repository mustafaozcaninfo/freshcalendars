**JCPenney stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26, 2026, and open at 5 a.m. local time on Black Friday, Nov. 27, according to the retailer's Nov. 2 release from Plano, Texas.** The same release lists "5, 9, 1, 5" surprise giveaways in stores that day and a 9 a.m. opening on Christmas Eve. ## Is JCPenney open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. The release says "JCPenney stores will be closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day." Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, and Christmas Day is Friday, Dec. 25. The retailer says it will offer extended shopping hours throughout the season, but the release gives no store-by-store schedule, so look up your own store's page on jcpenney.com.

## JCPenney Black Friday 2026 hours and the 5, 9, 1, 5 giveaways

Stores open at 5 a.m. local time on Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27. JCPenney has not announced a closing time. The "5, 9, 1, 5" giveaways return on the same day, and the release lists four times:

| Giveaway | Time on Fri., Nov. 27 (local time in stores) |
|---|---|
| First | 5 a.m. |
| Second | 9 a.m. |
| Third | 1 p.m. |
| Fourth | 5 p.m. |

The release calls them surprise giveaways and does not say what they are or how many each store holds. The Black Friday Deals themselves run for 10 days, from Friday, Nov. 20, through Sunday, Nov. 29.

## What time does JCPenney open on Christmas Eve 2026?

The release says stores open at 9 a.m. local time on Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, and are closed on Christmas Day. No closing time for Dec. 24 is given. For other chains, see our [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 store hours](/news/christmas-eve-christmas-day-2026-store-hours/) list.

## What has JCPenney not announced for 2026?

- The Black Friday closing time.
- Any Cyber Monday (Nov. 30) event; the Black Friday Deals end Nov. 29.
- Golden Tote dates after the Nov. 5-11 sale.
- What the surprise giveaways are.

The release adds that Rewards Access members can earn $10 back when they spend $50, up to four times, for use at JCPenney or Aéropostale. It gives no dates for that offer.
