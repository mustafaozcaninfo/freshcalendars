**The 2026 BBC Sports Personality of the Year show is on Sunday, 13 December, live on BBC One from 17:45 GMT, which is 12:45 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the BBC's own announcement, reported by Yahoo Sports, The Telegraph and Stylist.** The Sports Personality of the Year (SPOTY) programme takes place at MediaCity in Salford. The table below converts the start and end time for the U.S., Canada, Ireland, Europe and Australia, and a printable watch guide carries the same times.

The BBC's pages did not open to our automated checks, so the facts here come from the BBC's story as syndicated by Yahoo Sports, plus The Telegraph and Stylist, all listed under sources.

## What time is Sports Personality of the Year 2026?

The BBC said the show will be "broadcast live on BBC One, BBC iPlayer and the BBC Sport website and app from 17:45 GMT." Stylist gives the slot as "from 5.45pm to 7.15pm", a run of 1 hour 30 minutes, and The Telegraph describes it as "an earlier slot between 5.45pm and 7.15pm." Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) is the UK's time in December, and the U.S. is on standard time, so Eastern Time is five hours behind.

## Is Sports Personality of the Year on Sunday this year?

Yes. The BBC's announcement says the show "will be held on Sunday, 13 December", and The Telegraph says it returns "to its traditional Sunday timing." Last year it was on a Thursday.

## Who hosts and what awards are given in 2026?

The BBC says Clare Balding, Gabby Logan and Alex Scott will again host. Nine awards are presented on the night, and "five of the nine awards to be presented on the night will be decided by public vote." The Telegraph reports that the BBC has introduced a new footballer category.

Stylist's list of awards includes Helen Rollason, Young Sports Personality, Coach, Team, Lifetime Achievement, Men's Footballer, Women's Footballer (BBC World Service) and World Sport Star. The dates for the other awards are not yet announced.

## How does voting work and when is the shortlist out?

The BBC says "voting - for global audiences - opens on Tuesday, 10 November," and that "full voting details, dates and T&Cs will be announced in due course." The 2026 shortlist, its date, and the UK phone and online voting rules and times are not yet announced.

For scale, last year's six-person shortlist was out by Dec. 11, 2025, Salford Now reported, a week before the Dec. 18 show. The printable lists the open items so you can fill them in.

## Can you watch from the U.S., Canada or Australia?

The BBC has not announced a U.S. broadcast, and no 2026 source we found covers Canada or Australia. BBC iPlayer is the BBC's own streaming service, and the BBC lists it with BBC One for the live show. Whether it works where you live is not announced; check the BBC's pages before Dec. 13.

## Last year's winners

On Thursday, Dec. 18, 2025, Rory McIlroy won the main award for the first time, the Irish Times reported. Sky Sports reported that Young Sports Personality went to Michelle Agyemang and Coach to Sarina Wiegman.
