**The School District of Lee County in Florida has no school for students Nov. 23-27, 2026, for Thanksgiving Break, and Winter Break runs Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027, by its 2026-2027 Instructional Calendar.** Spring Break is Monday-Thursday, March 22-25, 2027, and the last day is Thursday, May 27, 2027. This is Lee County, Florida (Fort Myers and Cape Coral), not one of the other Lee Counties.

The district's calendar PDF is marked "Board Approved 11/04/2025" and is linked from the district's [Instructional Calendar](https://www.leeschools.net/calendars/instructional_calendar) page. The next day with no school for students is Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026.

## Lee County school breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates. The three Professional Duty Days (remote) are included, because students have no school on them.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027, plus Mon., Jan. 4 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon., March 22 to Mon., March 29, 2027 | 6 | Tue., March 30, 2027 |

Return days are inferred from the calendar: it shows no closure on Nov. 30, Jan. 5 or March 30. Winter Break itself ends Jan. 1, New Year's Day, but Monday, Jan. 4 is a remote Professional Duty Day.

Spring Break is four days on the calendar, Monday-Thursday, March 22-25. Good Friday, March 26, and a remote Professional Duty Day on Monday, March 29, extend it, so students are out from March 22 to March 29. ## How long are the Lee County quarters and semesters?

The calendar counts 180 school days. Quarters end on the dates below, and the first semester has 85 days and the second 95.

| Quarter | Ends | School days |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fri., Oct. 16, 2026 | 47 |
| 2 | Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | 38 |
| 3 | Fri., March 19, 2027 | 52 |
| 4 | Thu., May 27, 2027 | 43 |

Dec. 18 and March 19 are early dismissal days as well as quarter ends. The last day, May 27, is both.

## Can severe weather change the Lee County calendar?

It can. The calendar lists Severe Weather Make-up Days for June 1-4, 2027, after the May 27 last day, with this footnote: "In the event of extended Severe Weather Days, the school calendar will shift to make up the appropriate days." The calendar does not say how many lost days would trigger a shift, and it does not say any have been used. The printable lists June 1-4 as unconfirmed.

## When does the 2027-28 Lee County school year start?

The district has also posted a [2027-2028 calendar](https://www.leeschools.net/common/pages/GetFile.ashx?key=OllyDber), with the same board approval date of 11/04/2025. Students start Tuesday, Aug. 10, 2027, and the last day is Tuesday, May 30, 2028, an early dismissal.

Students start Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026 and finish Thursday, May 27, 2027.
