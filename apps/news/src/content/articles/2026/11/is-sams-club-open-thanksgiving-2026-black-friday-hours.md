**Sam's Club is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and closes at 6 p.m. on Christmas Eve, according to the holiday schedule on the Sam's Club Help Center's hours page.** The page does not name a year, so it is Sam's Club's standing schedule rather than a 2026 notice. The Scripps television station in Wichita, Kansas (KAKE) reported on Oct. 27, 2026 that its list of stores closed on Thanksgiving includes Sam's Club.

## Sam's Club holiday hours, 2026-27

The hours page lists "Holiday Hours and Closings" by name. The weekdays are computed for 2026 and early 2027. Hours are local time and, per the page, may vary by location.

| Date | Holiday | Plus members | Club members |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thu., Nov. 26, 2026 | Thanksgiving | Closed | Closed |
| Thu., Dec. 24, 2026 | Christmas Eve | 8 a.m.-6 p.m. | 9 a.m.-6 p.m. |
| Fri., Dec. 25, 2026 | Christmas | Closed | Closed |
| Thu., Dec. 31, 2026 | New Year's Eve | 8 a.m.-8 p.m. | 9 a.m.-8 p.m. |
| Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | New Year's Day | Closed | Closed |

The page also lists Easter as closed, and Memorial Day, the Fourth of July and Labor Day at 8 a.m.-8 p.m. for Plus members and 9 a.m.-8 p.m. for Club members. Those fall outside this season, so they are not in the table.

## Is Sam's Club open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. The page says "Thanksgiving: Closed." KAKE's Oct. 27, 2026 report on stores closing for the holiday says: "The list includes stores like Target, Walmart, Academy Sports, Costco, Lowe's and Sam's Club." Neither source is a 2026 notice from Sam's Club itself.

In 2025, Parade reported on Nov. 26 that Sam's Club would be closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 27, 2025, the same as the 2026 listing.

## What are Sam's Club's Black Friday 2026 hours?

Not posted. The hours page has no Black Friday entry, and Sam's Club has not published special hours for Friday, Nov. 27, 2026. The page's regular hours are 9 a.m.-8 p.m. for all members, with early shopping at 8 a.m.-9 a.m. for Plus members, but the page does not say those apply on Black Friday. Check your club's page in the Sam's Club app.

## What are Sam's Club's regular hours?

| Service | Hours |
|---|---|
| Club, Monday to Sunday | 9 a.m.-8 p.m. (all members) |
| Early shopping | 8 a.m.-9 a.m. (Plus members) |
| Curbside and delivery, Club members | 9 a.m.-8 p.m. |
| Curbside and delivery, Plus members | 7 a.m.-8 p.m. |
| Cafe | 10 a.m.-8 p.m. (all members) |
| Fuel center | 6 a.m.-10 p.m. (all members) |

The page says regular club hours and specialty departments may vary by location, and all times are local. It gives no holiday hours for curbside, delivery, the cafe or the fuel center. The member contact center is closed on Christmas, Easter and New Year's Day.
