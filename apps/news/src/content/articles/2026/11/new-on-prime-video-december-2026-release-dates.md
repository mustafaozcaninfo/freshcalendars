**Beast Games season 3 premieres on Prime Video on Wednesday, Dec. 16, 2026, with three episodes, according to Amazon's Nov. 16 guide.** Amazon had not posted a full December list on Nov. 24, so this page keeps the dates Amazon confirms: The Man with the Bag, Beast Games, One Night in Idaho and five Thursday Night Football games with kickoffs in ET and PT. A free printable checklist follows.

Several movie dates differ between published December lists, so they are left out until Amazon confirms them.

## Which Prime Video premieres and games are dated in December 2026?

| Date | Weekday | What | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dec. 2 | Wednesday | The Man with the Bag (movie) | Premieres globally; no time given |
| Dec. 3 | Thursday | Chiefs at Rams (Week 13) | 8:15 p.m. ET / 5:15 p.m. PT |
| Dec. 10 | Thursday | Vikings at Patriots (Week 14) | 8:15 p.m. ET / 5:15 p.m. PT |
| Dec. 16 | Wednesday | Beast Games season 3 | Three episodes |
| Dec. 17 | Thursday | 49ers at Chargers (Week 15) | 8:15 p.m. ET / 5:15 p.m. PT |
| Dec. 24 | Thursday | Texans at Eagles (Week 16) | 8:15 p.m. ET / 5:15 p.m. PT |
| Dec. 28 | Monday | One Night in Idaho: Catching a Killer (docuseries) | Episode count not announced |
| Dec. 31 | Thursday | Ravens at Bengals (Week 17) | 8:15 p.m. ET / 5:15 p.m. PT |

Amazon's page for The Man with the Bag (updated Nov. 2) says: "The Man with the Bag premieres globally on Prime Video on December 2, 2026." For One Night in Idaho, Amazon's Nov. 18 headline reads: "Watch the trailer for 'One Night in Idaho: Catching a Killer,' coming to Prime Video December 28." ## When do new Beast Games season 3 episodes come out?

Amazon says: "The season premieres Wednesday, December 16, with three episodes." and "Two new episodes follow each week until the one-episode season finale on Wednesday, January 13." Counting those two lines gives this weekly calendar (episode numbers are our tally):

| Date | Weekday | New episodes |
|---|---|---|
| Dec. 16 | Wednesday | Three (episodes 1-3) |
| Dec. 23 | Wednesday | Two (episodes 4-5) |
| Dec. 30 | Wednesday | Two (episodes 6-7) |
| Jan. 6 | Wednesday | Two (episodes 8-9) |
| Jan. 13 | Wednesday | Season finale, one episode (episode 10) |

## What time is Thursday Night Football on Prime Video in December 2026?

Amazon's "Thursday Night Football schedule on Prime Video" (updated May 14, 2026) says: "Games kick off at 8:15 p.m. ET." Pregame starts at 7 p.m. ET. That is 7:15 p.m. CT, 6:15 p.m. MT and 5:15 p.m. PT for every game below. The lead-in is Week 12 on Friday, Nov. 27 (Black Friday Football): Denver Broncos at Pittsburgh Steelers.

| Week | Date | Game | Kickoff (ET / CT / MT / PT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | Thu., Dec. 3 | Kansas City Chiefs at Los Angeles Rams | 8:15 / 7:15 / 6:15 / 5:15 p.m. |
| 14 | Thu., Dec. 10 | Minnesota Vikings at New England Patriots | 8:15 / 7:15 / 6:15 / 5:15 p.m. |
| 15 | Thu., Dec. 17 | San Francisco 49ers at Los Angeles Chargers | 8:15 / 7:15 / 6:15 / 5:15 p.m. |
| 16 | Thu., Dec. 24 | Houston Texans at Philadelphia Eagles | 8:15 / 7:15 / 6:15 / 5:15 p.m. |
| 17 | Thu., Dec. 31 | Baltimore Ravens at Cincinnati Bengals | 8:15 / 7:15 / 6:15 / 5:15 p.m. |

## Is the NBA Cup on Prime Video in December 2026?

NBA.com's "Emirates NBA Cup key dates" (updated Nov. 21) lists the quarterfinals for Dec. 4-5, the semifinals for Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9 and the championship for Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Amazon had not said which knockout games stream on Prime Video, so none are listed here. See the [NBA Cup 2026 schedule](/news/nba-cup-2026-schedule-dates-group-play-knockouts-final-indianapolis/) for the full dates.
