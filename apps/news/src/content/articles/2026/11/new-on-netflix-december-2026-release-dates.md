**Emily in Paris season 6, the final season, premieres on Netflix on Thursday, Dec. 24, 2026, according to TVLine's report of Aug. 26 and both December 2026 lists from TVLine and /Film.** Netflix had not posted its own December list on Nov. 21, so the table below keeps only titles that both newsrooms date the same way. A free printable checklist and the NFL Christmas Day kickoff times in ET and PT follow.

Dates can change before December. Netflix's own Tudum pages are the source for the NFL games, Fury vs. Joshua, Six Kings Slam, Pride and Prejudice and The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.

## Which Netflix premieres and live events are dated in December 2026?

- Thu., Dec. 3: Pride and Prejudice, a series. Netflix Tudum, Oct. 28, 2026: "release date: Dec. 3." Both lists give six episodes.
- Fri., Dec. 11: Tyson Fury vs. Anthony Joshua, live from Principality Stadium in Cardiff. Start time not yet announced. See the [Fury vs. Joshua fight time story](/news/fury-vs-joshua-december-11-2026-fight-time-ring-walk-netflix/).
- Wed., Dec. 23: The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, the David Fincher film with Brad Pitt, arrives on Netflix after a two-week theatrical run that starts Nov. 25 (Tudum, Sept. 21, 2026).
- Thu., Dec. 24: Emily in Paris season 6, the final season.
- Fri., Dec. 25: two NFL games, covered below.
- Sun., Dec. 27 to Wed., Dec. 30: Six Kings Slam. Netflix lists Dec. 27-30; times not yet announced.

For the November titles, see [New on Netflix November 2026](/news/netflix-new-movies-november-2026-release-dates/). The [Pride and Prejudice reading plan](/news/pride-and-prejudice-netflix-release-date-december-3-reading-plan/) covers the Dec. 3 series.

## When are Fury vs. Joshua and Six Kings Slam on Netflix?
