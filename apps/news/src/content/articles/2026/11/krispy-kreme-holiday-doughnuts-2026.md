**Krispy Kreme's 2026 holiday doughnuts and Sugar Cookie Latte are available from Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, through Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, at participating shops, according to the company's holiday page.** The page prints the window as "Tuesday, 11/03 thru Monday, 1/04/27!" and repeats it as "available November 3, 2026 through January 4, 2027 at participating shops."

## Krispy Kreme holiday menu 2026, item by item

The page lists four items. Each carries the same window; the weekdays are computed here.

| Item | What the page says | On sale from | Until |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caramel Toffee Cheesecake Doughnut | Filled with cheesecake Kreme, dipped in caramel icing and topped with a toffee graham flavored crunch | Tue., Nov. 3 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Sugar Cookie Doughnut | An Original Glazed doughnut dipped in smooth vanilla flavored icing, finished with a festive sugar cookie topping | Tue., Nov. 3 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Hot Cocoa Doughnut with Ghirardelli | Finished with a dollop of Ghirardelli hot cocoa frosting, marshmallow bites and a sprinkle of Ghirardelli hot cocoa | Tue., Nov. 3 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Sugar Cookie Latte | "Available Frozen, Hot & Iced"; a holiday latte inspired by fresh-from-the-oven sugar cookies | Tue., Nov. 3 | Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 |

The page also names three bundles: the Holiday Specialty Dozen + Original Glazed Dozen Bundle, the Holiday Specialty Dozens to Share Bundle and The Holiday Seasonal Dozen. It prints no prices, so what they cost at your shop is not confirmed here.

## How long do the holiday doughnuts last?

The window covers 63 days, counting Nov. 3 and Jan. 4. It runs across the whole of [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) from the third, all of [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) and the first four days of [January 2027](/january-calendars.html). Krispy Kreme's promotions page shows the earlier Autumn Seasonal Collection & Pumpkin Spice running "8/11/26 through 11/2/26", so the holiday menu began the next day.

## How does it compare with last year?

In 2025, Fox 5 Atlanta reported on Nov. 29 that the Krispy Kreme x Peanuts Collection was "available now through December 24." That lineup had Snoopy Cookies & Kreme, Charlie Brown Ornament, Christmas Wreath, Santa Belly and Holiday Sprinkle doughnuts. The 2026 window ends on Jan. 4, 2027, 11 days after last year's Dec. 24 end date.

## Which shops carry the holiday menu?

Only participating ones. The page says: "Search for your closest participating shop. If your preferred shop doesn't appear in the list below, it is not participating in this promotion."

## Is there a Day of the Dozens in 2026?

Krispy Kreme has not announced one in the sources checked. In 2025, Fox 5 Atlanta reported, it fell on Dec. 12, with a second Original Glazed dozen for $1 with any dozen at regular price. Treat that as last year's offer until the company posts a 2026 date.
