**Letter Boxed from The New York Times (NYT) for Friday, Nov. 13, 2026 has the sides H/A/R, T/G/E, I/M/O and N/J/V, and a par of 5, according to the puzzle data on the Letter Boxed page.** The puzzle lists 1,207 dictionary words. With par at 5, the [Letter Boxed page](/news/topic/nyt-letter-boxed/#how-it-works) covers the rules. Below you get the sides, a count of the two-word routes, five hints and four warm-up words, and the Times' solution comes last.

## The four sides

| Side | Letters |
|---|---|
| 1 | H, A, R |
| 2 | T, G, E |
| 3 | I, M, O |
| 4 | N, J, V |

Each letter has two side-mates, so 24 ordered letter pairs can never sit next to each other in a word.

## How many two-word routes cover the box?

Seventeen. A script paired every word in the list with every word that starts on its last letter, then kept the pairs that use all twelve letters.

| Check on the saved list | Result |
|---|---|
| Two-word routes covering all 12 letters | 17 |
| Joining letters | T (10 routes), G (5), O (2) |
| Total letters across a route | 13 to 25 |
| Routes with 16 letters in total | 3 |
| One-word solutions | 0 |
| Three-word routes that need all three words | 10,901 |

The shortest route holds 13 letters in all, and one route runs to 25. Par of 5 leaves room: a three-word finish beats it by two words, and a four-word finish by one.

## Which letter pairs cannot touch today?

Of the 108 ordered pairs that sit on different sides, 19 never appear next to each other in any of the 1,207 words. Seventeen of the 19 involve the J or the V.

| Letter | Words containing it | Words starting with it | Words ending with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| J | 48 | 26 | 1 |
| H | 242 | 85 | 24 |
| M | 269 | 133 | 22 |
| V | 293 | 78 | 4 |
| N | 788 | 137 | 170 |

The J ends a single word and the V ends four, so both must sit in the middle of a word or open one. The N is in 788 words, more than any other letter. The two missing pairs without a J or a V are H followed by G, and M followed by T.

Side 2 (T, G, E) closes 583 words, far more than any other side, so most routes hand over to the next word from there.

| Side | Letters | Words that start on it | Words that end on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | H, A, R | 353 | 298 |
| 2 | T, G, E | 347 | 583 |
| 3 | I, M, O | 266 | 151 |
| 4 | N, J, V | 241 | 175 |

The longest word in the list runs 14 letters, and it is the only one that long. The best any single word does is 10 of the 12 letters.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' own solution is two words, three fewer than par.
2. The longer word has 11 letters, one of 17 words with exactly that length in the list.
3. The first word starts and ends on the T/G/E side, and the second begins on that same side.
4. The J and the M sit in the first word. The V and the O sit in the second.
5. The first word starts with T and ends in G. The second starts with G and ends in E.
