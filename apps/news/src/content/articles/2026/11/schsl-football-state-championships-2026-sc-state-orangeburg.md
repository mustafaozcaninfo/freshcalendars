**The South Carolina High School League (SCHSL) plays its six football state championship games on Friday and Saturday, Dec. 4-5, 2026, at Oliver C. Dawson Stadium at South Carolina State University in Orangeburg, according to its 2026 Football State Championships Hub.** Class 1A Division II kicks off the series at noon ET on Dec. 4 and Class 5A closes it at 8 p.m. ET on Dec. 5. No matchup is set yet.

## SCHSL football state championship schedule 2026

SCHSL's hub lists the times in Eastern Time, and Sports Illustrated printed the same times on Nov. 4, 2026. Central Time (CT) and Pacific Time (PT) are one and three hours earlier. The hub gives the venue as South Carolina State University, Oliver C. Dawson Stadium, 300 College Avenue, Orangeburg, SC 29115.

| Day | Kickoff (ET) | CT | PT | Class |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 1A, Division II |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 4 p.m. | 3 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 2A |
| Fri., Dec. 4 | 8 p.m. | 7 p.m. | 5 p.m. | 4A |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 12 p.m. | 11 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 1A, Division I |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 4 p.m. | 3 p.m. | 1 p.m. | 3A |
| Sat., Dec. 5 | 8 p.m. | 7 p.m. | 5 p.m. | 5A |

Matchups are decided in the semifinals. SCHSL says the Lower State representative is the home team for each final. The semifinal date is not in the sources used here.

## How many SCHSL finals are there in 2026?

Six. Class 5A has one final in 2026, with no Division I and Division II split. In 2025, SCHSL's championships page listed two 5A finals, Division 1 and Division 2. Class 1A keeps both divisions. The league's brackets page, updated Nov. 16, 2026, links brackets for 5A, 4A, 3A, 2A, 1A Division I and 1A Division II.

## What happened at the 2025 SCHSL finals?

In 2025 the finals were played Dec. 5-6 at South Carolina State University, per SCHSL's 2025 page, with kickoffs at 12:06, 4:06 and 7:36 p.m. Two results are confirmed by two newsrooms each.

| 2025 class | Result | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| 5A Division I | Dutch Fork 40, Summerville 20 | ABC News 4, Sports Illustrated |
| 4A | South Pointe 35, South Florence 14 | Fox Carolina, High School Sports Report |

## SCHSL state championship football press conferences

SCHSL's football page lists two press conferences on Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026. The one for 1A Division II, 2A and 4A is at 9:30 a.m. and the one for 1A Division I, 3A and 5A is at 11:30 a.m.

## SCHSL championship tickets and how to watch

SCHSL's 2026 hub says ticket prices and the event-specific purchase link have not yet been confirmed. In 2024, SCHSL listed tickets at $15 through GoFan; that is last cycle's price, not 2026's. Parking and the bag policy are not yet announced.

The hub lists the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Network as the broadcaster and says streaming availability and any subscription requirements are determined by the broadcaster. The NFHS Network lists the six games with teams to be announced.
