**Bob Evans Restaurants says to pre-order its Thanksgiving Farmhouse Feast by Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, and that its dining rooms are open from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26.** The Farmhouse Feast page reads "Pre-order your Thanksgiving Farmhouse Feast® by Wednesday November 25th." Bob Evans says each feast is "fully cooked and packed cold, so all you have to do is heat and serve at home."

Its Thanksgiving page also says "The last day to order a Farmhouse Feast is November 25th, 2026" and asks guests to "Check your local restaurant for adjusted hours and availability." Bob Evans, based in New Albany, Ohio, does not list 2026 prices, pickup days or a delivery cutoff on the pages we read on Nov. 18, 2026. Prices and pickup times are set at the restaurant, so the order page for yours is the place to confirm them. This is the restaurant company, not Bob Evans Farms, the maker of grocery-store sides.

| Item | What Bob Evans says |
|---|---|
| Pre-order deadline | "Pre-order your Thanksgiving Farmhouse Feast® by Wednesday November 25th." |

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026; the date is on our [Thanksgiving 2026 page](/when-is/thanksgiving/).

## Which Bob Evans Farmhouse Feast size fits your guests?

The Bob Evans page says the feasts come in "options to serve 4, 8 or 10 people." The page lists four options.

| Option | Serves | Main | Also listed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium | 8 to 10 | Slow-roasted turkey and hickory-smoked ham | Bread & celery dressing, sides, pies, pumpkin bread |
| Slow-roasted Turkey | 4 or 8 | Slow-roasted turkey | Sides, pumpkin pie |
| Hickory-Smoked Ham | Up to 8 | Hickory-smoked ham | Dressing, sides, rolls, pumpkin pie |
| Pot Roast | 8-10 | Slow-roasted pot roast with caramelized onions and carrots | Dressing, sides, rolls, pies |

The homepage also lists a Holiday Hot Celebration Family Meal that serves up to 6 (slow-roasted turkey, hickory-smoked ham and sides) and a Holiday Celebration Premium Platter for one. Bob Evans posts no price for any of them. For a party of 4, the turkey option is the one the page lists at that size.

## Bob Evans Farmhouse Feast order-to-table timeline

The two-hour heating time is Bob Evans'; the steps around it follow from the page.

| When | Step |
|---|---|
| By Wed., Nov. 25 | Pre-order and reserve a pickup time; Bob Evans recommends ordering early |
| At your reserved time | Pick up the cold, fully cooked meal (or take delivery where available) |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Start heating about two hours before you plan to eat |

## Did the Farmhouse Feast cost less in 2025?

Bob Evans' 2025 release, reprinted by RestaurantNews.com on Oct. 22, 2025, said Farmhouse Feasts "starts at $89.99" and serve four or eight to ten. It gave delivery through Tuesday, Nov. 25 (2025) and said all locations would be open 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 27 (2025). The 2026 hours are the same, per the Thanksgiving page; the 2026 pages list no price or delivery cutoff. The page also says the Farmhouse Feast "comes back every year for Easter, Thanksgiving and Christmas"; no Christmas dates are listed.
