**Bass Pro Shops says free studio-quality Santa photos run from Sunday, Nov. 8, through Christmas Eve, Dec. 24, 2026, at Bass Pro Shops and Cabela's stores, according to its Oct. 26 release.** Each family gets a free 4x6 photo and a free shareable video. The dates for crafts, Tuesday story times and the Dec. 5 breakfast are in one table below, with a 2025 comparison and a printable.

## Bass Pro Santa's Wonderland 2026 dates, Nov. 7 to Dec. 24

| Date | Day | What Bass Pro Shops published |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 7, 2026 | Saturday | Santa's arrival at 5 p.m. local time, a free outdoor celebration with a Mini Reindeer Plush giveaway while supplies last (passed) |
| Nov. 8, 2026 | Sunday | Free Santa photos begin |
| Nov. 9 to 27, 2026 | Monday to Friday | Santa Ornament Crafts, weekday visits while supplies last |
| Nov. 10 to Dec. 22, 2026 | Tuesdays | Christmas Tales story time at 5 p.m. |
| Nov. 28, 2026 | Saturday | Merry Morning Breakfast reservations open |
| Nov. 30 to Dec. 18, 2026 | Monday to Friday | Bass Sticker-By-Number Sheet, weekday visits while supplies last |
| Dec. 5, 2026 | Saturday | Merry Morning Breakfast at 9 a.m. |
| Dec. 24, 2026 | Thursday | Christmas Eve, last day of free photos |

The weekdays are our computation. Mark them on the [November 2026](/november-calendars.html) and [December 2026](/december-calendars.html) printable calendars; our [Christmas date page](/when-is/christmas/) has the holiday itself.

## What does the free Bass Pro Santa photo include?

The release says families can visit Santa's Wonderland in-store from Nov. 8 to receive a free 4×6 studio-quality photo with Santa and a free shareable video. Additional photo packages are for sale, and members of its loyalty club get 20% off. Kids can also do crafts and drop a letter in Santa's mailbox; those who include an email address with the letter get a message back from Santa.

Santa's Wonderland has run since 2008 and is in its 18th season, the release says. It applies to Bass Pro Shops and Cabela's locations across the U.S. and Canada.

## Do you need a reservation to see Santa at Bass Pro?

No. The release says free reservations are available up to seven days in advance at basspro.com/santa or cabelas.com/santa, and "walk-ins are always welcome" on a first-come, first-served basis. Basspro.com blocked our automated check, so open your own store's page for its Santa's Wonderland hours: the release lists no daily hours.

## Which crafts and giveaways run on which dates?

The release lists these giveaways for weekday visits while supplies last:

| Giveaway | Dates in 2026 |
|---|---|
| Santa Ornament Crafts | Nov. 9 through Nov. 27 |
| Bass Sticker-By-Number Sheet | Nov. 30 through Dec. 18 |

## Which Tuesdays have Christmas Tales story time?

At 5 p.m. on Tuesdays from Nov. 10 to Dec. 22, Santa and Mrs. Claus read Christmas classics to families, and Santa takes a photo with all participating families afterward. Separate reservations are required and open seven days in advance. The seven Tuesdays, computed from the Nov. 10 and Dec. 22 end dates:

| Story time | Date | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tue., Nov. 10 | 5 p.m. |
| 2 | Tue., Nov. 17 | 5 p.m. |
| 3 | Tue., Nov. 24 | 5 p.m. |
| 4 | Tue., Dec. 1 | 5 p.m. |
| 5 | Tue., Dec. 8 | 5 p.m. |
| 6 | Tue., Dec. 15 | 5 p.m. |
| 7 | Tue., Dec. 22 | 5 p.m. |

The release says local time. Nov. 10 has passed; the next one is Nov. 17.

## How do you book the Merry Morning Breakfast on Dec. 5?

The breakfast is at 9 a.m. on Dec. 5 with Santa and his helpers. Reservations open Nov. 28, are separate from the photo reservations and are capped at 4 people per family. Each family takes home a free Santa hat cup and a 4x6 photo. The release does not state a price.
