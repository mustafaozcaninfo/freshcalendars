Hard has nine greater-than or less-than regions today, and [our Pips page](/news/topic/nyt-pips/#how-it-works) explains those signs. Placements in the answer section come from the Times' data.

## Where each Pips board starts on Nov. 12

R8C1 is row 8, column 1; count down from the top and across from the left.

**Easy.** R3C1 must be under 3 and touches only R3C2, which must add up to 9 with R2C2. Run through the options: a 2 or 1 in R3C2 would ask R2C2 for a 7 or 8. Only the 0-6 works, with 0 in R3C1 and 6 in R3C2. R2C2 is then a 3, which comes from the 3-6, and since R3C2 is taken, its 6 goes in R2C3.

**Medium.** R1C2, R1C3 and R1C4 must total less than 3. R1C3 has no cell below it, so it pairs with R1C2 or R1C4 and its domino lies inside the row. Both halves of that domino must be small, and the 0-0 is the only one that fits; every other domino has a half of 3 or more.

**Hard.** R1C7 must be 0 and touches only R1C6, which must be under 4. Of the three dominoes with a 0 (0-3, 4-0 and 0-5), only the 0-3 fits. R4C5 touches only R3C5, which must be 0, and R4C5 must be under 5, so the 4-0 goes there. The 0-5 is left for R8C1 and its dead-end neighbor R7C1.

## Thursday's Pips hints, Nov. 12, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 0-6, 2-2, 1-1, 2-1, 3-6)**

- Hint 1: R3C5 and R3C6 add up to 4, and R3C6 touches only R3C5. One domino in the set totals 4 on its own.
- Hint 2: R2C3, R2C4, R1C4 and R2C5 add up to 9. Three halves show a 1, two of them on the 1-1.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 1-6, 5-6, 0-3, 0-0, 4-4, 6-6, 5-4)**

- Hint 1: R3C1, R4C1 and R4C2 must match, and R4C4 must be more than 5. The 6 is the only value with four halves.
- Hint 2: R3C3 and R4C3 add up to 9, and so do R2C1, R2C2 and R3C2.

**Hard (sixteen dominoes on a nine-row board)**

- Hint 1: R5C7, R6C7 and R7C7 must match. R7C7 touches only R6C7, so that domino is a double: 1-1, 2-2 or 5-5.
- Hint 2: R3C7, R3C8 and R4C7 add up to 3. The three 0s are needed elsewhere, so look at the 1s.

## Reading the boards before you check

Hard's pairs of dead ends, such as R3C2 with R4C2 and R6C5 with R7C5, are dominoes before you start; only their values are open. R7C1, R8C4 and R9C2 have no condition. On Easy, R1C3 is the blank. Medium carries three doubles, 0-0, 4-4 and 6-6.
