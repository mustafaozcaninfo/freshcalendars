## How Pips works

You place every domino so that each colored region meets its condition. A number means the pips in the region add up to it; = means every half shows the same value; ≠ means all are different; > and < set a total above or below a number; a region with no condition takes anything. Rows are counted from the top and columns from the left, so R2C5 is the second row, fifth column.

## Thursday's Pips hints, Nov. 12, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 0-6, 2-2, 1-1, 2-1, 3-6)**

- Hint 1: R2C2 and R3C2 must add up to 9. With no 4 or 5 on the board, the pair is a 6 and the only 3, which is on the 3-6 domino.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 1-6, 5-6, 0-3, 0-0, 4-4, 6-6, 5-4)**

- Hint 1: R4C4 must be more than 5, so it holds a 6. Four halves show a 6, on the 1-6, 5-6 and 6-6 dominoes.
- Hint 2: R1C2, R1C3 and R1C4 must total less than 3. Only small values fit: the board has three 0s, on the 0-3 domino and the 0-0 double, and one 1.

**Hard (sixteen dominoes on a nine-row board)**

- Hint 1: R1C7, R3C5 and R8C1 must each add up to 0. The board has exactly three 0s, on the 0-3, 4-0 and 0-5 dominoes, so each lands in one of those cells.
- Hint 2: R3C7, R3C8 and R4C7 must add up to 3. With every 0 placed, each of the three cells holds a 1, and the 1-1 double supplies two of them.

## Solving tips

Count values before placing anything. On Thursday's Easy board, a pair that adds to 9 can only be 6 and 3, because there is no 4 or 5. The Medium board needs three matching halves in R3C1, R4C1 and R4C2, and it has four 6s and three 4s to choose from. The Hard board is tall and spread out, nine rows by nine columns, and three of its cells take any value: R7C1, R8C4 and R9C2. Easy has one such cell, R1C3; Medium has none. Easy has two doubles, 2-2 and 1-1; Medium has three, 0-0, 4-4 and 6-6; Hard has three, 1-1, 2-2 and 5-5.
