**Peacock simulcasts NBC's Christmas in Rockefeller Center live on Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026, from 8 to 10 p.m. ET, according to NBC's holiday lineup of Nov. 18.** Peacock had not posted its own December list on Nov. 24, so the dates below come from NBC, NBC Sports and Peacock's NBA schedule, with library titles from ScreenRant and Comic Book Resources (CBR). A free printable checklist covers every NBA game on Peacock with its ET and PT tip, plus four Sunday Night Football (SNF) games.

Dates can change before December, and NBC calls its football schedule tentative from week 11 on.

## Which NBC specials are on Peacock in December 2026?

NBC's lineup of Nov. 18, 2026 names four specials that are on Peacock, live or the next day:

| Date | Special | Peacock |
|---|---|---|
| Wed., Dec. 2 | Christmas in Rockefeller Center, 8-10 p.m. ET on NBC, hosted by Cynthia Erivo | Simulcast on Peacock, and in Spanish on Telemundo |
| Wed., Dec. 2 | Christmas with Snoop, 10-11 p.m. ET on NBC | The next day, Thu., Dec. 3 |
| Tue., Dec. 8 | Alan Jackson: The Last Show, 8-10 p.m. ET on NBC | The next day, Wed., Dec. 9 |
| Thu., Dec. 10 | NBC100: The Live Celebration, 8-11 p.m. ET/PT on NBC | Simulcast on Peacock |

NBC also lists A Saturday Night Live Christmas for Friday, Dec. 11 at 8 p.m. on NBC. It gives no Peacock date for that special or for the Grinch, Frosty and Rudolph broadcasts, so none is printed here. For the tree itself, see the [Rockefeller Christmas tree lighting story](/news/rockefeller-christmas-tree-2026-lighting-december-2-arrival-november-7/).
