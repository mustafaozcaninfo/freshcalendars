**Panera Bread's 2026 holiday menu arrived the week of Nov. 5 with new Peppermint White Mocha and Pistachio White Mocha drinks, the company said in a Nov. 5, 2026 press release.** On Thursday, Dec. 3, 2026 only, the first 12 customers at each participating cafe who make an in-cafe purchase of $25 or more at the cashier can get a free Snowman Cup. The table below lists every item and form, and the checklist covers the free cup rules.

The release, datelined Boston, says the menu is available at bakery-cafes nationwide. It gives no exact start day, no end date and no drink prices. For other chains, see our [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/) and the [Caribou Coffee holiday menu 2026](/news/caribou-coffee-holiday-menu-2026-cookie-butter-white-mocha/) story.

## What is new on the Panera holiday menu in 2026?

Panera's new drinks come in two flavors, Peppermint White Mocha and Pistachio White Mocha. Each is offered as a latte, an iced coffee and a Frozen Java beverage. The Peppermint White Mocha is topped with peppermint bark pieces and the Pistachio White Mocha with pistachio pieces. The release says the latte and Frozen Java versions also get whipped cream.

In the bakery case, the Cocoa Marshmallow Cake Slice is new. The Mitten Cookie is a returning seasonal item. Panera says the menu can be ordered for dine-in, delivery, Rapid Pick-Up and drive-thru.

## How do you get the free Panera Snowman Cup on Dec. 3, 2026?

Panera calls it a one-day chance to receive a free Snowman Cup with a qualifying purchase. The release's footnote sets these rules, and the date is a Thursday.

1. Go on Dec. 3, 2026. The offer is for that day only.
2. Make an in-cafe purchase of $25 or more at the cashier.
3. Be among the first 12 customers. Supplies are limited to 12 cups per participating cafe.
4. Take one cup. The limit is one per person.

The offer is not valid with any other coupon or offer, and it runs while supplies last. The release names no membership requirement for the free cup. The release does not list which cafes take part.

## What do the Panera Snowman Cups cost?

The Snowman Cups are limited-edition glass cups sold in mystery boxes. Each box contains one snowman glass cup. They are sold on The Panera Shop for $25 plus tax and shipping, while supplies last. As of Oct. 27, 2026, Panera had 2,244 bakery-cafes, according to the release's company description.
