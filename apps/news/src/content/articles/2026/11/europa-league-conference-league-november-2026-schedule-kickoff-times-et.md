**The Europa League and Conference League play on Wednesday, Nov. 4 and Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, with Thursday kickoffs at 12:45 p.m. and 3 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the fixture lists the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) updated on Oct. 31.** The next matchday is Thursday, Nov. 26, which is Thanksgiving Day in the United States. Below: every game with its ET kickoff, a time zone table, the English and Scottish clubs in action, how to watch and the dates after November.

UEFA lists kickoffs in Central European Time (CET) and says all kickoffs are 21:00 unless stated. Central Europe has been on CET (UTC+1) since Oct. 25, and U.S. Eastern Standard Time (UTC-5) began when clocks fell back on Nov. 1, so the gap is 6 hours. The ET, Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT), Pacific Time (PT) and UK columns below are our conversion from UEFA's CET times, not an official schedule. Our [Champions League November schedule](/news/champions-league-november-2026-schedule-kickoff-times-et-matchday-4-5/) covers the other UEFA competition.

## How to watch the Europa League and Conference League in the U.S.

CBS Sports reported on Aug. 19, 2022 that UEFA and CBS Sports/Paramount agreed a six-year deal, beginning in 2024-25, to air the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League. Matches are on Paramount+, with select matches on CBS Sports Network and CBS. We have not confirmed which channel carries each Nov. 5 or Nov. 26 game, so check the Paramount+ listings for your match. Our [soccer on TV this weekend story](/news/soccer-on-tv-this-weekend-october-30-november-2-2026-us-channels/) lists channels for the current weekend.

In the UK, Sky Group said on Nov. 20, 2025 that Sky Sports will hold all UEFA Europa League and Conference League rights from the 2027/28 season. For this season's UK coverage, check your broadcaster.

## Europa League and Conference League dates after November

UEFA lists these dates for the rest of the season. The league phase continues in December and January, then the knockout rounds begin in February. UEFA says the dates are subject to change.

| Date | What | Competition |
|---|---|---|
| Thu., Dec. 10, 2026 | Europa League Matchday 6 | Europa League |
| Thu., Dec. 10, 2026 | Matchday 5 | Conference League |
| Thu., Dec. 17, 2026 | Matchday 6, final league phase round | Conference League |
| Thu., Jan. 21 and Jan. 28, 2027 | Matchdays 7 and 8 | Europa League |
| Feb. 18 and 25, 2027 | Knockout play-offs | Both |

Matchday 6 on Dec. 10 includes Milan vs Sunderland, Jagiellonia vs Crystal Palace and Bournemouth vs Viktoria Plzeň, which UEFA lists at 21:00 CET (3 p.m. ET).
