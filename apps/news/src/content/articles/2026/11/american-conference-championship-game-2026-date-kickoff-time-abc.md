**The American Conference Football Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, at 8 p.m. ET on ABC, according to the conference.** Its release of May 27, 2026 says the game "will air on ABC on Saturday, Dec. 5, at 8 p.m. ET." Searchers may know the league as the American Athletic Conference (AAC); the conference now publishes as the American Conference.

Neither team is set. The game is "hosted at the home stadium of the conference's regular-season champion," per a May 12, 2026 release, so the site stays open until that champion is known. Tickets are not announced.

## What time does the American title game start where you live?

| Time zone | Kickoff on Sat., Dec. 5 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | 8 p.m. |
| Central (CT) | 7 p.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 6 p.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | 5 p.m. |
| United Kingdom (GMT) | 1 a.m., Sun., Dec. 6 |

Only the 8 p.m. ET time is official. The other rows are computed from it. The local time in the host city depends on which team hosts.

## Who is in the American Conference title race?

The top two teams in the final single-division regular-season standings meet, the conference said on Feb. 26, 2026. The table copies the conference's standings page as of Nov. 23, 2026, in the order the page lists the teams, starting with the University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA).

UTSA leads by one game, and the teams at 5-2 are one game back. The conference had not posted its 2026 tiebreaker rules in the sources used, so this page does not say who has clinched a spot or been eliminated.

## Which flex games decide the final week?

The conference's May 27 release puts a noon ET game on ESPN on Fri., Nov. 27. It also lists a 3:30 p.m. ET ESPN game, marked "Friday/Saturday Flex": "Florida Atlantic at East Carolina or Temple at Memphis or Tulane at South Florida or Tulsa at UTSA."

| Flex game | Possible day |
|---|---|
| Florida Atlantic at East Carolina | Fri., Nov. 27 or Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Temple at Memphis | Fri., Nov. 27 or Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Tulane at South Florida | Fri., Nov. 27 or Sat., Nov. 28 |
| Tulsa at UTSA | Fri., Nov. 27 or Sat., Nov. 28 |

The Feb. 26 release says the four flex games "will either be played Friday on Black Friday (Nov. 27) or on Saturday, November 28." Which games play on which day, and which one takes the 3:30 p.m. ET ESPN slot, are not in the sources used. UTSA's last conference game is Tulsa at UTSA.

## Who won the 2025 American Championship Game?

In 2025, Tulane beat North Texas 34-21 on Fri., Dec. 5, 2025, in New Orleans, the conference reported. That is last year's result and last year's date; the 2026 game is on a Saturday.
