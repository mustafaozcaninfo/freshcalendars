**The 2026 Pac-12 Championship Game is Friday, Dec. 4, 2026, at 5 p.m. PT (8 p.m. ET) on CBS, hosted by Boise State at Albertsons Stadium in Boise, Idaho, according to the Pac-12 Conference and reports from The Associated Press (AP) and CBS2 Idaho News.** The Pac-12's broadcast release says the game "will air on CBS and be streamed live on Paramount+ with a kick-off at 5 p.m. PT."

Boise State beat Oregon State 46-41 on Saturday, Nov. 14, to "wrap up the host spot in the Pac-12 championship game Dec. 4," AP reported through Oregon Public Broadcasting on Nov. 15. CBS2 Idaho News wrote on Nov. 16 that the win "locked up Albertsons Stadium as the host site." Which team travels to Boise is not decided yet.

## Who will Boise State play in the Pac-12 Championship Game?

The Pac-12 said in February that "the Pac-12's title matchup will be determined upon the conclusion of Week 12 (Nov. 21)." Each of the eight teams plays a seven-game conference round robin, per the same release.

CBS2 Idaho News reported that Boise State was undefeated in Pac-12 play after the Oregon State win and that "a win for San Diego State would send the Aztecs to the title game." Boise State hosts San Diego State on Saturday, Nov. 21, at 7:30 p.m. MT (9:30 p.m. ET) at Albertsons Stadium on USA Sports, CBS2 Idaho News said. Other outcomes and tiebreakers are not covered here because no official tiebreaker page was found. The College Football Playoff (CFP) rules for the Pac-12 champion are also outside the sources used here.

## Why is the Pac-12 title game in Boise?

"For the first season of the new league, the Pac-12's title game will be hosted at the home venue of the highest seed," the Pac-12 said in its May release. Boise State, 9-1 and 6-0 in the Pac-12 at the time of CBS2 Idaho News's Nov. 15 report, took that spot.

The Pac-12's February release lists the eight-team membership: Boise State, Colorado State, Fresno State, San Diego State, Texas State and Utah State joined Oregon State and Washington State. At the Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) level, AP reported on Sept. 2, 2026, Texas State's addition in 2025 "was the final piece, giving the conference an FBS-mandated eight football schools."

## When was the last Pac-12 Championship Game?

Washington beat Oregon 34-31 on Dec. 1, 2023, University of Washington Athletics reported under the headline "No. 3 UW Beats No. 5 UO, 34-31, To Win Pac-12 Championship." That is the earlier result; the 2026 game is the first title game for the league's new membership.

## Does the Pac-12 game clash with other title games?

The Big 12 Championship Game also kicks off at 8 p.m. ET on Friday, Dec. 4, on ABC at AT&T Stadium, per the Big 12's Nov. 9, 2026 release. Both games share the same Friday night start.
