**Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, Thanksgiving Day in the U.S., brings a Spelling Bee from The New York Times (NYT) with A in the middle and C, G, I, N, R and V around it.** The list is long: 72 words worth 402 points, with Genius at 281 and three pangrams in the mix.

Only two vowel letters, A and I, are on the board, and every answer has to use A. Today's Genius bar sits 227 points above Wednesday's, so a find-everything pass pays off; the scoring rules are on our [Spelling Bee page](/news/topic/nyt-spelling-bee/#how-it-works).

## The pangram clues

- **Count:** three pangrams share this board, two of 7 letters and one of 11.
- **The ends:** all three start with C and finish with G.
- **The twins:** the two 7-letter pangrams are anagrams of each other.
- **The long one:** the 11-letter pangram repeats A three times and N three times.

## How many points is each word length worth?

Four-letter words score 1 point, longer words 1 point per letter, and every pangram adds 7. A 7-letter pangram is therefore 7 + 7 = 14 points and the 11-letter pangram is 11 + 7 = 18. The running total adds the rows from the top.

- **Where Genius falls:** the total is 279 after the 7-letter block, 2 short of 281, so one 8-letter word is enough to cross the bar.
- **Six letters carry the most:** 19 words hold 114 points, 28% of the 402.
- **Pangram bonus:** the three pangrams are 46 points together, about 11% of the total.

## The hardest words on Thursday's list

- **Longest answers:** one of 11 letters, two of 10, one of 9 and six of 8.
- **Rare pairs:** AA and IV each appear in a single answer; GN, IC, NC, RN and RV appear in two each.
- **Doubled letters:** 11 answers repeat a letter back to back, 4 with RR, 3 with NN, 3 with GG and 1 with AA.
- **Triple A:** 5 answers hold three A's, and 29 hold at least two.
- **Endings:** 35 answers end in G, 15 in N, 14 in A, 4 in R, 3 in I and 1 in C.

## The gap from four-letter words to Genius

The 17 four-letter answers give 17 points, about 6% of the 281 needed. That leaves 264 points to find among the 55 answers of five letters or more, which hold 385 in all. Cut every pangram and the other 69 words are worth 356, still 75 points over the bar.
