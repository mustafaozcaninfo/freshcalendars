The Honolulu Marathon starts at 5:00 a.m. Hawaii-Aleutian Standard Time (HST) on Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026, which is 10 a.m. ET, and the Honolulu Marathon Association says all start groups go at 5:00 a.m. The start is on Ala Moana Boulevard at the Queen Street Extension and the finish is in Kapiolani Park. There is no cut-off time.

Three facts are still open. The 2026 traffic advisory, any start-line fireworks and a TV or livestream listing had not been announced as of Nov. 21, 2026. Entry fees are not covered here because no official page we checked lists a price.

## What time does the Honolulu Marathon start in each US time zone?

On Dec. 13, 5 a.m. in Honolulu is 10 a.m. Eastern. The race's own pages give only the Hawaii time; the other columns are computed from that.

| Event | Hawaii (HST) | Pacific | Mountain | Central | Eastern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalakaua Merrie Mile, Sat., Dec. 12 | 7 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 10 a.m. | 11 a.m. | noon |
| Marathon, Sun., Dec. 13 | 5 a.m. | 7 a.m. | 8 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 10 a.m. |
| Start to Park 10K, Sun., Dec. 13 | 5 a.m. | 7 a.m. | 8 a.m. | 9 a.m. | 10 a.m. |

The Merrie Mile starts at Monsarrat Avenue and finishes at Queens Beach, at Monsarrat and Kalakaua. Its starts are in waves based on predicted finish time, and runners meet from 6:00 a.m. The Start to Park 10K begins at the marathon start on Ala Moana Boulevard and Queen Street and "finishes 10km into the marathon course in Kapiolani Park." It has no time limit either.

The expo is at the Hawaii Convention Center, 1801 Kalakaua Avenue. Bring valid ID to packet pickup. The race says parking in the Convention Center lot is limited during race week. Shuttles say "Board early."

## Which roads close on race day?

The City and County of Honolulu Department of Transportation Services (DTS) 2026 events page lists the marathon with roads closing at "2:30 AM" and reopening at "4:00 PM" (approximate). The entry says the permit application has been submitted, so the plan can change. It names two stretches: the H1 Freeway at the Waialae Avenue off ramp to Aina Koa Avenue, and Kalanianaole Highway between Aina Koa Avenue and Keahole Street. That is about 13.5 hours of closure on the posted times (our arithmetic).

For the Merrie Mile on Dec. 12, DTS lists lane closures from 4:30 a.m. and reopening about 8:30 a.m.

| | 2026 plan (DTS, not final) | 2025 (race advisory and police notice) |
|---|---|---|
| Closures begin | 2:30 a.m. | 12:30 a.m. (one or more lanes closed) |
| Tow-away zones | Not yet announced | Midnight to 5:00 p.m. Sunday |
| Roads reopen | About 4:00 p.m. | Not listed |
| Start | 5:00 a.m. | 5:00 a.m., fireworks start |

In 2025 the Honolulu Police Department (HPD) said on Dec. 11 that traffic control measures would start at 12:30 a.m., with tow-away from 12 a.m. to 5 p.m. A 2026 notice from HPD or the race had not appeared on the pages we checked. Last year's notice was posted in November and repeated by HPD on Dec. 11, so check both before race morning.

## How many people are entered in 2026?

The race's media center said "More than 40,000 entrants have registered as of October 8, 2026." Its table lists 14,531 from Hawaii and 24,716 visiting, a total of 39,247. Registration for the 2026 weekend was open on Nov. 21, and the race says online registration closes Dec. 3. Transfers at the expo cost $30; the transfer deadline was Nov. 13, 2026.

## Who won in 2025?

On Dec. 14, 2025, Tsegay Weldlibanos of Eritrea won in 2:13:38 and Calli Hauger-Thackery of Great Britain won the women's race in 2:30:43, according to the race's media center. Those are last year's results.

## How can you follow the race?

The association says a live tracking and results app or page will be available on race day, using your bib number. No 2026 TV or livestream was listed as of Nov. 21. Start-line fireworks started the 2025 race, per that year's advisory; none are announced for 2026.
