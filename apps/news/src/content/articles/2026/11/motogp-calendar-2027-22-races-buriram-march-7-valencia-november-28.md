**The 2027 MotoGP season has 22 Grands Prix, opening at Buriram, Thailand, on Sun., March 7, 2027 and ending at Valencia on Sun., Nov. 28, 2027, according to the calendar motogp.com published on Sept. 25, 2026.** Every race day is a Sunday. The table gives each weekend's Friday-to-Sunday dates and how far local time at the venue runs ahead of or behind U.S. Eastern Time (ET) on race day. Session and race start times are not yet announced, and a one-page printable with a winner line for each round is in the downloads box.

## What is the full MotoGP 2027 race calendar?

The race days come from motogp.com. The weekend dates are computed from them (Friday to Sunday) and match the four ranges motogp.com's Nov. 5, 2026 blog prints: Mar. 5-7, Mar. 12-14, Apr. 23-25 and Nov. 26-28. The last column is computed from time zones, not published by MotoGP.

U.S. clocks go forward on Mar. 14, 2027 and back on Nov. 7, 2027, and Europe's change on Mar. 28 and Oct. 31. The Thailand round is measured against Eastern Standard Time, every round from Qatar through Japan, Malaysia and Indonesia against Eastern Daylight Time, and the Australia and Valencia rounds against Eastern Standard Time again. That is why Great Britain and Portugal show 5 hours while the other European rounds show 6, and why Austin, in Central Time, is an hour behind ET.

## Is there a MotoGP race in Austin in 2027?

Yes. The United States round is Round 5, on Sun., Apr. 25, 2027, at the Circuit of the Americas in Austin, with the weekend running Apr. 23-25. It follows Argentina on Apr. 11 and comes two weeks before Jerez on May 9, a 14-day gap on each side. Local time in Austin is one hour behind ET on race day. The [April printable calendar](/april-calendars.html) shows the weekend against the rest of the month.

## What are the back-to-back MotoGP weekends in 2027?

These gaps are computed from the race days above; MotoGP does not publish them. Of the 21 gaps between the 22 races, 7 are exactly 7 days, 12 are 14 days, one is 21 days and one is 28 days. They add up to the 266 days from Mar. 7 to Nov. 28.

| Consecutive weekends | Race days |
|---|---|
| Two in a row | Thailand and Qatar (Mar. 7 and 14) |
| Two in a row | Brazil and Argentina (Apr. 4 and 11) |
| Two in a row | Spain and France (May 9 and 16) |
| Two in a row | Germany and Czechia (July 11 and 18) |
| Two in a row | San Marino and Aragon (Sept. 12 and 19) |
| Three in a row | Japan, Malaysia and Indonesia (Oct. 17, 24 and 31) |

The longest break is 28 days, from Czechia on July 18 to Great Britain on Aug. 15. The next longest is 21 days, from Qatar on Mar. 14 to Brazil on Apr. 4. After Indonesia on Oct. 31 the season has two more two-week gaps, to Australia on Nov. 14 and Valencia on Nov. 28. ## What changes on a 2027 MotoGP weekend?

Each weekend has a Saturday Sprint and a Sunday Grand Prix, according to motogp.com's blog. Under the format announced on Oct. 7, 2026, all riders take part in Grand Prix Qualifying 1 and the top 12 go through to GP Q2, which creates distinct grids for the Sprint and the Grand Prix at every round. For the machines, motogp.com's rules overview says engine size is to be reduced from 1000cc to 850cc, and a motogp.com notice of Mar. 6, 2025 says Pirelli will become the official tyre supplier from 2027.
