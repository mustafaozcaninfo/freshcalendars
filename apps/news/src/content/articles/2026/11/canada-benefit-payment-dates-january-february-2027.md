**The Canada Pension Plan (CPP) pays on Wednesday, Jan. 27 and Wednesday, Feb. 24, 2027**, according to the Service Canada benefits payment calendar. Old Age Security (OAS), the Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS), the Allowance and the Allowance for the Survivor use the same two dates. The table below sets every federal and provincial date for January and February next to the date the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) listed a year earlier.

<p class="callout"><b>Read Nov. 22, 2026.</b> The Service Canada calendar page was last changed Nov. 12, 2026. The CRA payment-dates page, last changed Oct. 14, 2026, has no 2027 rows yet, so the 2026 column comes from its January and February 2026 rows.</p>

## Which benefits pay in January and February 2027?

| 2027 date | Weekday | Payment | Same payment in 2026 (CRA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan. 5 | Tuesday | Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) | Jan. 5, 2026 (Monday), listed as the Goods and Services Tax (GST) and Harmonized Sales Tax (HST) credit |
| Jan. 8 | Friday | Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB) | Jan. 9, 2026 (Friday) |
| Jan. 12 | Tuesday | Advanced Canada workers benefit (ACWB) | Jan. 12, 2026 (Monday) |
| Jan. 20 | Wednesday | Canada Child Benefit (CCB) | Jan. 20, 2026 (Tuesday) |
| Jan. 21 | Thursday | Canada Disability Benefit (CDB) | Not on the CRA page |
| Jan. 25 | Monday | Newfoundland and Labrador Disability Benefit (NLDB) | Not on the CRA page |
| Jan. 27 | Wednesday | CPP, OAS, GIS, Allowance, Allowance for the Survivor | Not on the CRA page |
| Feb. 10 | Wednesday | Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB) | Feb. 10, 2026 (Tuesday) |
| Feb. 18 | Thursday | Canada Disability Benefit (CDB) | Not on the CRA page |
| Feb. 19 | Friday | Canada Child Benefit (CCB) | Feb. 20, 2026 (Friday) |
| Feb. 24 | Wednesday | CPP, OAS, GIS, Allowance, Allowance for the Survivor | Not on the CRA page |

| Feb. 26 | Friday | Alberta Child and Family Benefit (ACFB) | Feb. 27, 2026 (Friday) |

The weekdays were computed from the dates on the Service Canada calendar. Print the one-page sheet in the downloads box to mark each date, or use the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html) and the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html).

## When does the Canada Child Benefit pay in 2027?

The calendar lists the Canada Child Benefit on Wednesday, Jan. 20 and Friday, Feb. 19, 2027. The January date matches the CRA's Jan. 20, 2026 date, and the February date is one day earlier than Feb. 20, 2026. The Canada Disability Benefit comes the next day in January (Thursday, Jan. 21) and the day before in February (Thursday, Feb. 18). CPP and OAS follow a week later in January and five days later in February.

## Which payments have no January or February 2027 date?

| Payment | What the Service Canada calendar shows |
|---|---|
| Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) | Jan. 5, 2027 only. The CRA calls it the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (formerly the GST/HST credit) and says you receive payments 4 times in a year. No April, July or October 2027 date is confirmed here. |
| Advanced Canada workers benefit | Jan. 12, 2027 only; no February entry. |
| Alberta Child and Family Benefit | Feb. 26, 2027 only; no January entry. |
| Veterans disability pension | No 2027 row on the calendar. |
| British Columbia | No 2027 row on the calendar. |

## How much will OAS and CPP pay in 2027?

OAS amounts for January to March 2027 are not yet announced. The last posted amounts are for October to December 2026.

| Benefit | Last posted amount |
|---|---|
| OAS, ages 65 to 74 (Oct.-Dec. 2026) | Up to $762.50 a month |
| OAS, ages 75 and older (Oct.-Dec. 2026) | Up to $838.75 a month |
| GIS, single (Oct.-Dec. 2026) | Up to $1,138.90 a month |
| CPP maximum monthly retirement pension at 65 (2026) | $1,507.65 |

The 2027 CPP maximum monthly pension is not yet announced. The earlier schedule is in the [November and December 2026 payment dates](/news/canada-benefit-payment-dates-november-december-2026-cpp-oas-ccb/).

## What are the 2027 CPP contribution figures?

The CRA's contribution page, last changed Nov. 9, 2026, lists these 2027 figures against the 2026 Year's Maximum Pensionable Earnings (YMPE) of $74,600.

| 2027 CPP figure | Amount |
|---|---|
| Year's Maximum Pensionable Earnings (YMPE) | $76,700 |
| Basic exemption | $3,500 |
| Maximum contributory earnings | $73,200 |
| Employee and employer rate | 5.75% each |
| Maximum employee and employer contribution | $4,209.00 each |
| Self-employed maximum | $8,418.00 |

More detail is in the [2027 YMPE story](/news/cpp-2027-ympe-76700-maximum-pensionable-earnings-yampe/). For tax-season dates, see the [Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP) deadline for 2027](/news/rrsp-deadline-2027-march-1-canada-tax-season-dates/).

## What is the Ontario Trillium Benefit date in January 2027?

The Service Canada calendar lists the Ontario Trillium Benefit on Friday, Jan. 8 and Wednesday, Feb. 10, 2027. The [November 2026 payment date story](/news/ontario-trillium-benefit-november-2026-payment-date-otb/) covers the program in more detail. Days off around these dates are on the [Canada holidays calendar](/canada-holidays/).
