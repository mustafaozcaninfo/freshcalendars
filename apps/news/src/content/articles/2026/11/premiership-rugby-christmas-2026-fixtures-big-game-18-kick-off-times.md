**Premiership Rugby's Big Game 18 is Harlequins v Northampton Saints on Mon., Dec. 28, 2026 at Allianz Stadium in Twickenham, with a 5 p.m. GMT kick-off (noon ET, 9 a.m. PT) listed by Turner Network Television (TNT) Sports.** The league, Premiership Rugby (PREM), plays as Gallagher PREM after its title sponsor, and its Christmas round has five games from Sat., Dec. 26 to Mon., Dec. 28. Below: all 15 December games in GMT, Eastern and Pacific time, where to watch in the UK and U.S., and a printable fixture sheet.

## What time is Big Game 18 on Dec. 28?

TNT Sports, RugbyPass and Rugby World all list Harlequins v Northampton Saints at 5 p.m. GMT at Allianz Stadium. RugbyPass wrote that the game is "being played under the Allianz Stadium lights on Monday, 28 December, kick off 5pm," and Rugby World lists "Mon 28 Dec Harlequins v Northampton Saints (5pm, Allianz Stadium)." That is noon ET and 9 a.m. PT.

Planet Rugby's July fixture list showed 3:05 p.m. for the game. Harlequins had not posted the time on a page we could read, so check the club before you travel. Ticket prices and on-sale status for Big Game 18 are not in the sources we found.

RugbyPass reported that Monday night rugby returns to the league for the first time in five years. Dec. 26 is a Saturday, so the UK Boxing Day bank holiday moves to Monday, Dec. 28, as covered in our [UK Christmas bank holidays 2026 story](/news/uk-christmas-bank-holidays-2026-boxing-day-substitute-december-28/). Big Game 18 is played on that bank holiday.

## Where can you watch Premiership Rugby in the UK and U.S.?

In the UK, the table above lists TNT Sports for 12 of the 15 December games and HBO Max for three: Bristol v Newcastle on Dec. 26, Gloucester v Newcastle on Dec. 5 and Gloucester v Saracens on Dec. 19.

In the U.S., Rugby World's 2026-27 viewing guide says "fans in the United States can watch Gallagher PREM live streams on FloRugby." Sports Video Group reported in September 2025 that FloSports signed an exclusive three-year deal to show every regular-season and play-off match, running to 2027-28. We list no price.

Ten clubs: Bath Rugby, Bristol Bears, Exeter Chiefs, Gloucester Rugby, Harlequins, Leicester Tigers, Newcastle Red Bulls, Northampton Saints, Sale Sharks and Saracens.
