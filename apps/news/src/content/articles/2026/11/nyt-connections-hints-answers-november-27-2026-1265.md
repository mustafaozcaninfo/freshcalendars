## Why the royal tiles split in puzzle #1265

Four tiles read as royalty: Queen, Prince, Royalty and Monarch. They land in three groups, so a player who bundles them together is one tile off at best. A second lure runs through a holiday custom.

| Tile | Group it tempts | What it really does |
|---|---|---|
| Queen | Purple, as a third royal beside Prince and Royalty | Names a female animal, with Leopardess, Peahen and Vixen |
| Monarch | Purple, as a ruler beside Prince and Royalty | Ends in arch, a part of the foot, in the blue group |
| Prince | Female animals, as a title in the same family as Queen | Belongs to purple, next to Alice Walker |
| Mistletoe | Touch lightly, since it invites a kiss | Ends in toe, in the blue group |
| Kiss | Blue, as a holiday word beside Mistletoe | A light touch with Brush, Graze and Skim |

Monarch carries two meanings at once, a ruler and a word ending in a foot part. Pinwheel and Camisole hide theirs in the final letters too, so reading the last few letters of every tile is the quickest test for the blue group.
