## Friday's Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints, Nov. 6, 2026

- **Pinpoint #920, first nudge:** the category is a short phrase that starts with the words "Things that", so think about what the five clues are, not which word they share.
- **Pinpoint #920, stronger nudge:** picture an everyday object you can turn over, and ask what every one of them has in common once you do.
- **Crossclimb #920, the locked ends:** read together, the top and bottom words name what someone does with an axe.
- **Crossclimb #920, the first middle rung:** a place where you buy things, one letter away from the top word.
- **Crossclimb #920, the next two rungs:** an attempt or a photograph comes next, followed by the black powder a chimney leaves behind.
- **Crossclimb #920, the last two rungs:** an owl's call, then a covering that goes over your head or sits at the front of a car.

## Crossclimb #920 ladder map, Nov. 6, 2026

The seven rows make six steps, and each step swaps one letter. Positions run 1 to 4 from left to right, and no word appears in this table.

| Step | Letter position that changes |
|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 1 |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 4 |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 2 |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 1 |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 4 |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 1 |

Position 1 changes three times, position 4 twice and position 2 once; position 3 never moves. If you are stuck, hold the third letter fixed and play with the other three.

## Starting points for Queens, Tango and Zip

**Queens #920.** Look for two regions that are squeezed into the same two rows or columns. Together they claim those lines, so no other color can place a queen there.

**Tango #760.** Spot a pair of equal symbols side by side, then fill both neighbors with the opposite symbol. Rows that already hold half of one symbol need only the other kind for the rest.

**Zip #599.** Work from the highest number and from the corners, where the path has the fewest ways through. Then join the numbered cells in order and fill the gaps.

## Friday's LinkedIn answers, Nov. 6, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you are still solving.

- **Pinpoint #920:** Things that have two sides.
- **Crossclimb #920:** Chop, Shop, Shot, Soot, Hoot, Hood, Wood. Shop, Shot, Soot, Hoot and Hood fill the middle in that order; Chop and Wood are the two ends.

The ladder with the letter that changes at each step:

| Step | Word | Letter swapped in |
|---|---|---|
| Top row | Chop | none |
| Rung 1 | Shop | S for C |
| Rung 2 | Shot | T for P |
| Rung 3 | Soot | O for H |
| Rung 4 | Hoot | H for S |
| Rung 5 | Hood | D for T |
| Bottom row | Wood | W for H |

Checked against the hints: a shop sells things, a shot is an attempt, soot is chimney powder, a hoot is an owl's call and a hood covers the head. Chop and Wood together are what an axe does to a log.
