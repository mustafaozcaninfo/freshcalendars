## How Pips works

You place every domino so that each colored region meets its condition. A number means the pips in the region add up to it; = means every half shows the same value; ≠ means all are different; > and < set a total above or below a number; a region with no condition takes anything. Rows are counted from the top and columns from the left, so R2C5 is the second row, fifth column.

## Sunday's Pips hints, Nov. 15, 2026

**Easy (five dominoes: 0-3, 5-4, 5-0, 4-3, 0-6)**

- Hint 1: R1C2 and R3C1 must each add up to 5. Two halves show a 5, on the 5-4 and 5-0 dominoes.
- Hint 2: R1C3 and R1C4 must be equal. The 0, 3, 4 and 5 are the only values that appear on more than one half.

**Medium (seven dominoes: 3-6, 5-1, 4-1, 1-2, 2-3, 0-5, 2-2)**

- Hint 1: R2C5 must add up to 0, and the only 0 is on the 0-5 domino.
- Hint 2: R2C3 and R2C4 must be equal. The 1, 2, 3 and 5 are the only values that appear on more than one half.

**Hard (fifteen dominoes on a six-row board)**

- Hint 1: R1C5 must add up to 0. Five halves show a 0, on the 2-0, 0-3, 0-0 and 6-0 dominoes.
- Hint 2: R3C6 and R6C4 must each be more than 4, so each holds a 5 or a 6.

## Solving tips

Look for the targets that force a value. On Sunday's Easy board, two single cells must add up to 5 and only two halves show a 5. On Medium, one cell must add up to 0 and R3C2 and R1C5 take any value; Easy and Hard have no cell without a condition. Hard has two doubles, 6-6 and 0-0, and its 6 appears on seven halves, more than any other value. Medium has one double, 2-2.
