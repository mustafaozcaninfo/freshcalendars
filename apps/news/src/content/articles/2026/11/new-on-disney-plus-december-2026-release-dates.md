## When do the Percy Jackson season 3 finale episodes arrive?

Disney's post prints "Percy Jackson and the Olympians (Season 3) - New Episodes" under Friday, Dec. 4 and "Percy Jackson and the Olympians (Season 3) - Finale Episodes" under Friday, Dec. 11, both tagged Disney+ and Hulu. The "Percy Jackson and the Olympians Official Podcast (Season 3)" has a new episode on Dec. 4 and a finale episode on Dec. 11. The post gives no release time for either Friday. For the season's start, see the [Disney+ November 2026 list](/news/new-on-disney-plus-november-2026-release-dates/).

## Which Disney+ podcasts and kids' shows have December 2026 dates?

The post lists 14 Disney+-only podcast rows: Stick to Football (Dec. 1, 10, 15, 17 and 22), Stick to United with Wayne Rooney (Dec. 1, 8, 15 and 22), Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday Official Podcast (Dec. 3 and 10) and Pod Meets World (Dec. 7, 14 and 21). For the film itself, see the [Avengers: Doomsday release date and countdown](/news/avengers-doomsday-release-date-december-18-countdown-tickets-runtime/).

Several kids' series appear in the first table. A Disney+ Press article of Nov. 19, 2026 describes Ozzy Fox as a music-driven preschool short-form series that comes to Disney+ on Dec. 5. Bluey Cookalongs (Season 1) premieres on Wednesday, Dec. 16 with all episodes streaming, and Chibiverse (Season 5) premieres on Saturday, Dec. 19.

## What does Disney+ cost in December 2026?

Disney's sign-up page, read Nov. 20, 2026, lists Disney+ With Ads at $12.49 a month. Disney+ Premium, No Ads is $21.49 a month or $214.99 a year.
