**Dutch Bros announced its 2026 holiday drink lineup on Nov. 1, 2026, with new Merry Magic and Sprinkle Cookie drinks on the menu at all 1,225+ locations while supplies last, according to the company's release.** Hazelnut Truffle and Candy Cane return, and a Holiday Sugar Cookie Cake Pop joins the food case. Below is every item with the forms Dutch Bros lists for it, a 2025 comparison and a printable table.

## Which Dutch Bros holiday drinks are on the 2026 menu?

Dutch Bros published its release on dutchbros.com, dateline Tempe, Arizona, Nov. 1, 2026, a Sunday. It says the flavors are "available now" at all 1,225+ locations, so we cannot give a different launch date. The release gives no end date beyond "while supplies last" and lists no prices.

| Item | New or returning | Ordered as, per Dutch Bros |
|---|---|---|
| Merry Magic | New | Blended or iced Rebel Energy Drink, Myst Energy Refresher or lemonade |
| Sprinkle Cookie | New | Freeze, iced latte or iced chai |
| Holiday Sugar Cookie Cake Pop | New (food) | Tree-shaped cake pop |
| Hazelnut Truffle | Returning | Iced or hot mocha, or mocha Freeze |
| Candy Cane | Returning | Iced or hot mocha, mocha Freeze, mocha cold brew or hot cocoa |

## What is in Merry Magic and Sprinkle Cookie?

Dutch Bros describes Merry Magic as "a fruity blend full of holiday spirit with blackberry, peach and cherry topped with festive holiday candy." Sprinkle Cookie "unwraps the season's magic with sugar cookie inspired flavor finished with Soft Top and holly sprinks."

The two returning flavors are built around chocolate milk. Hazelnut Truffle pairs hazelnut with rich chocolate milk, and Candy Cane pairs peppermint with velvety chocolate milk. The new cake pop is sugar cookie flavored, tree-shaped, coated in white chocolate and decorated with drizzle and sprinks.

## How does the 2026 Dutch Bros lineup compare with 2025?

Dutch Bros announced its 2025 holiday menu on Nov. 1, 2025, saying it was available starting that day at all 1,050+ locations. The 2025 and 2026 releases are the only sources for the table below.

| | 2025 menu | 2026 menu |
|---|---|---|
| Locations named in the release | 1,050+ | 1,225+ |
| New fruity drink | Mistletoe Rebel (blended, iced or lemonade) | Merry Magic (blended or iced Rebel, Myst Energy Refresher or lemonade) |
| New cookie drink | Holiday Cookie Freeze (Freeze, shake or iced latte) | Sprinkle Cookie (Freeze, iced latte or iced chai) |
| Returning | Hazelnut Truffle Mocha, Candy Cane Mocha | Hazelnut Truffle, Candy Cane |
| New food | Not listed in our source | Holiday Sugar Cookie Cake Pop |

Mistletoe Rebel and Holiday Cookie Freeze are not on the 2026 list. The 2026 release names no shake among the Sprinkle Cookie forms, which the 2025 cookie drink had.

## What cups and app features does Dutch Bros mention?

Every drink is served in one of nine seasonal-themed cups, including special editions for kids and pets, according to Dutch Bros. The release does not describe the designs. It also points customers to the Dutch Bros app to order ahead, earn points and collect rewards.
