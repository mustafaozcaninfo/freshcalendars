**Wagamama's festive set menu costs £23 for two courses or £28 for three and is available until 5 January, according to its set menu page on wagamama.com, read on 21 November 2026.** The festive dishes, led by a roast dinner ramen, launched on Wednesday 18 November, wagamama said in its announcement, as reported by Retail Times and Verge Magazine. The official site shows no dish prices; the prices below come from those two reports.

## What is on the Wagamama Christmas menu 2026?

The food menu page marks a "festive limited time only" range and says the dishes are "only here 'til the new year". Prices are as reported by Retail Times (12 November 2026) and Verge Magazine (17 November 2026); the labels are wagamama.com's own.

| Dish | Reported price | wagamama.com says |
|---|---|---|
| Roast dinner ramen | £19.90 | "our festive take on a ramen" |
| Cranberry chicken katsu curry | £14.90 | Named on the menu |
| Cranberry yasai katsu curry | £14.50 | Labelled vg |
| Sweet heat sprouts | £7.10 | Labelled vg; "sweet + spicy cranberry gochujang sauce. onion. ginger" |
| Katsu chicken wrap | £8.90 | Named on the menu |
| Yasai katsu wrap | £8.90 | Labelled v (vegetarian) |
| Chocolate orange bao nut | £6.70 | "crispy panko bao dusted with cinnamon sugar" |
| Spiced berry gin spritz | £9.20 | Labelled vg; "roku gin. spiced red berry syrup. ginger beer. cranberries + orange" |
| Non-alcoholic spiced berry spritz | £7.40 | Reported only; not seen on the menu page |

The reports say the wraps can be made spicy or festive for 80p extra ("Make it spicy or make it festive (+80p)"). Prices may vary by restaurant; wagamama.com shows none, so check yours.

## How much is the Wagamama festive set menu?

The set menu page says: "our festive set menu is here, with 2 courses for £23 or 3 for £28." Restaurant Online gave the same prices on 14 October 2026. It adds that wagamama would take reservations for the first time in its 34-year history, and that groups of 20 or more can book directly through their chosen restaurant.

The page says the set menu has "all our new seasonal dishes plus all your longtime wagamama favourites". It lists these festive items:

| Course | Dishes named on the set menu page |
|---|---|
| Mains | Cranberry chicken katsu curry, cranberry yasai katsu curry, roast dinner ramen |
| Sides | Sweet heat sprouts, chicken gyoza |
| Desserts | Chocolate orange bao nut, miso caramel banana bread, coconut reika ice cream, miso caramel ice cream |

A separate Northern Ireland menu is linked from the page.

## How long does the Wagamama festive menu run?

The festive dishes began on Wednesday 18 November 2026. The set menu page says it is "only available until january 5". It prints no year, so 5 January 2027 is read from context, and it falls on a Tuesday.

| Measure | Result | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| First day | Wednesday 18 November 2026 | Retail Times and Verge Magazine |
| Last day | Tuesday 5 January 2027 | wagamama.com (day only) |
| Days in November | 13 (18 to 30 November) | Day count |
| Days in December | 31 | Day count |
| Days in January | 5 (1 to 5 January) | Day count |
| Total, both ends counted | 49 | Day count |

Mark the window on a [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html), a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and a [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html). The "til the new year" wording on the food menu page is looser than the 5 January date on the set menu page.

Last year, Verge Magazine reported on 11 November 2025 that wagamama's festive range was "launching 17th November in all wagamama restaurants nationwide".

## Is Wagamama open on Christmas Day 2026?

Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year opening hours are not announced on wagamama.com. Check your restaurant before you go. Our [Christmas bank holidays 2026 story](/news/uk-christmas-bank-holidays-2026-boxing-day-substitute-december-28/) lists the public holidays by nation.

## Does Soul Club change what you pay at Wagamama?

The Soul Club page says: "earn a stamp every time you spend £12 at wagamama" and says every three stamps earn food on the house. Retail Times and Verge Magazine reported that new members get a free portion of edamame or prawn crackers on a first spend of at least £12.
