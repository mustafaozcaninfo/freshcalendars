**April 2027 starts on Thursday, Apr. 1 and has 30 days, no federal holiday and 22 working days.** The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists Washington's Birthday on Feb. 15 and Memorial Day on May 31 in 2027, with nothing between them. The free one-page printable marks 8 days from official sources, with every Moon phase and its time in Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), Patriots' Day, Passover and the NFL Draft.

## April 2027 at a glance

The month begins on a Thursday and ends on a Friday. Because 30 days is four full weeks plus two days, those two weekdays each occur five times. The other five weekdays occur four times. A Sunday-start calendar and a Monday-start calendar both need five rows for it.

| | April 2027 |
|---|---|
| First day | Thursday, Apr. 1 |
| Last day | Friday, Apr. 30 |
| Days | 30 (22 weekdays, 8 weekend days) |
| Five-time weekdays | Thursday and Friday |
| Four-time weekdays | Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday |
| Federal holidays | None |
| Working days | 22 |

The 22 working days are the weekdays, since the month holds no federal holiday. ## April 2027 Moon phases in Eastern Daylight Time

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes Moon phases in Universal Time (UT). Eastern Daylight Time is four hours behind UT, so each time below is the UT time minus four hours.

| Phase | UT | Eastern time |
|---|---|---|
| New Moon | Apr. 6, 23:51 | Tue., Apr. 6, 7:51 p.m. EDT |
| First Quarter Moon | Apr. 13, 22:56 | Tue., Apr. 13, 6:56 p.m. EDT |
| Full Moon | Apr. 20, 22:27 | Tue., Apr. 20, 6:27 p.m. EDT |
| Last Quarter Moon | Apr. 28, 20:18 | Wed., Apr. 28, 4:18 p.m. EDT |

Each phase is in the evening or late afternoon on the U.S. East Coast, and all four fall on the same UT date as the Eastern date.

## Passover, Easter and UK bank holidays in April 2027

Passover begins in the Diaspora at sundown on Wednesday, Apr. 21, 2027 and ends at nightfall on Thursday, Apr. 29, according to Hebcal. The first Seder is that Wednesday evening.

Easter is not in April this year. The Church of England lists Easter Day on Mar. 28, 2027 and Ascension Day on May 6. GOV.UK lists Good Friday on Mar. 26 and Easter Monday on Mar. 29, so the UK has no bank holiday in April 2027. Our [Easter 2027 guide](/news/easter-2027-march-28-good-friday-easter-monday-whats-open/) covers that weekend. Ramadan, Eid, Orthodox Easter and Vaisakhi dates are not on the printable because none is confirmed from an official source here.

## Tax Day 2027 and Emancipation Day

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) has not posted the 2027 filing deadline yet. The 2026 deadline was April 15, 2026, according to irs.gov. IRS Publication 509 says a due date that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday moves to the next business day, and it lists April 16 as the District of Columbia's Emancipation Day, a legal holiday. April 15, 2027 is a Thursday and April 16 is a Friday. Check irs.gov for the 2027 date before you mark it.
