**The Boston Bruins play 11 games in November 2026, starting Monday, Nov. 2, at 7 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) against the Vegas Golden Knights at TD Garden, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) schedule.** The full month with start times and TV is below, and a one-page printable is further down.

The source is the NHL's club schedule for the Bruins, fetched Nov. 2, 2026. We converted its start times to ET, which is Boston time, and calculated the weekdays. Nine games are at home. Two are on the road. The month opens with Vegas on Monday night, the first of three home games before the only back-to-back road trip, at Pittsburgh on Nov. 5.

Network names are written as the NHL lists them. New England Sports Network (NESN) is the Bruins' regional outlet; the NHL Network, Turner Network Television (TNT), truTV and HBO Max are national in the United States. Where a game has no NESN feed listed, it is a national broadcast.

## Bruins home stand to open November

The Bruins play their first six games of the month inside a stretch that is nearly all at TD Garden. After hosting Vegas on Monday, Nov. 2, and visiting Pittsburgh on Thursday, Nov. 5, the team returns home for a Sunday matinee against Florida on Nov. 8 at 1 p.m. ET, then hosts Montreal on Thursday, Nov. 12, Minnesota on Saturday, Nov. 14, and Los Angeles on Tuesday, Nov. 17. The one road game at Detroit on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 7:30 p.m. ET is the Bruins' only national broadcast of the month.

## What channel is Bruins vs. Canadiens on Nov. 12, 2026?

The Bruins host the Montreal Canadiens on Thursday, Nov. 12, at 7 p.m. ET at TD Garden. The NHL lists it on NESN, the Bruins' regional outlet. The Original Six rivals meet here once in November; for Montreal's full month, compare our [Maple Leafs November 2026 schedule](/news/toronto-maple-leafs-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-printable/) and the [Rangers November 2026 schedule](/news/rangers-schedule-november-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).

## Bruins on Thanksgiving weekend 2026

These are our own counts from the NHL's schedule:

- **Thanksgiving week:** the Bruins host Winnipeg on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 7 p.m. ET, then host Toronto on Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27, at 1 p.m. ET. They have no game on [Thanksgiving Day](/when-is/thanksgiving/), Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026.
- **Home and away:** nine of the 11 games are at TD Garden; two are on the road, at Pittsburgh and Detroit.
- **Afternoon starts:** two home games begin at 1 p.m. ET, against Florida on Nov. 8 and Toronto on Nov. 27, with a 5 p.m. ET close against Vancouver on Sunday, Nov. 29.

Another NHL date this season is the [2027 Winter Classic](/news/nhl-winter-classic-2027-date-time-avalanche-mammoth-salt-lake-city/).
