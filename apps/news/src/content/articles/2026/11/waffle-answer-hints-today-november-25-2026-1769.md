### Why 10 is the minimum

Fourteen tiles start out of place. A loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops. Here the 14 tiles form 4 loops (one of six tiles, two of three and one of two), which leaves 10 swaps, and no route can use fewer.
