**Forsyth County Schools (FCS) in Cumming, Georgia, has Thanksgiving break from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, a holiday break on Dec. 21-31 and spring break from Monday through Friday, April 5-9, 2027**, according to the "2026-27 Student Calendar" on the Forsyth County School District website. The last day of school is Thursday, May 27, 2027. This is the district in Forsyth County, Georgia, not Winston-Salem/Forsyth County Schools in North Carolina.

The first day of school was Thursday, Aug. 6, 2026, and fall break (Sept. 28 to Oct. 2) is already past. Forsyth County News reported that the Board of Education approved the 2026-27 calendar; the district page gives no approval date. This story adds a table of every student day off, weekdays off by month, the school days between breaks and a side-by-side with Gwinnett County, all computed from the district's dates. A printable school-year calendar is at the bottom of the page.

## Forsyth County Schools breaks at a glance

Weekday counts and return days are our arithmetic from the district's dates. The district does not print return days, so each is the next weekday its calendar does not list as off.

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Break (past) | Mon.-Fri., Sept. 28 to Oct. 2, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Oct. 5, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Holiday Break, New Year's Day and Jan. 4 | Mon., Dec. 21 to Thu., Dec. 31, 2026; Fri., Jan. 1 and Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| February break | Fri., Feb. 12 to Mon., Feb. 15, 2027, plus Tue., Feb. 16 | 3 | Wed., Feb. 17, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., April 5-9, 2027 | 5 | Mon., April 12, 2027 |

The district lists Dec. 21-31 as "Holiday Break – Student/Staff Holiday," New Year's Day on Jan. 1 as a "Student/Staff Holiday" and Jan. 4 as "Professional Development (Student Holiday)." Together, students are out from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Monday, Jan. 4, which is 17 days in a row counting weekends. Friday, Dec. 18 is an all-grades early-release day. The [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the weeks.

## Why is the Forsyth County February break five days long?

The district lists "February 12–15: Student/Staff Holiday" and "February 16: Professional Development (Student Holiday)." Feb. 12 is a Friday and Feb. 15 is a Monday, so the break is a four-day weekend. Adding the Tuesday professional development day, students are out Friday through Tuesday, with only three weekdays missed: Feb. 12, 15 and 16. The page does not name Presidents' Day.

The November student-day count supports the Nov. 3 reading: the district counts 16 student days in November 2026, which is that month's 21 weekdays minus the five Thanksgiving weekdays (our arithmetic).

## Which Forsyth County online learning days are not days off?

The legend marks "OL" as "K-12 Online Learning/Parent Conference." The district lists these OL days:

- Friday, Oct. 16, 2026: K-12 Online Learning/Parent Conference (past).
- Friday, March 12, 2027: K-12 Online Learning/Parent Conference.
- May 10-14, 2027: Online Learning for high schools.
- May 20-21, 2027: Online Learning for graduating high schools.

None is called a holiday. The district's monthly student-day counts include them: October 2026 shows 19 student days, which is 22 weekdays minus three fall break days, so Oct. 16 is counted (our arithmetic). Check your school's own instructions for what students do on those days; the calendar page does not say. The district also lists early release on Sept. 4 and Dec. 18 for all grades and on Dec. 16-17 and May 24-26 for high school (grades 9-12) only, with exams noted; the district counts them in its student days.

Counting every weekday the district lists as a student holiday, there are 33 weekdays off between Aug. 6, 2026 and May 27, 2027. Online learning days and early-release days are school days and are not counted as off. December has the most, nine, and the district's monthly student-day counts are in the last column.

## How does the Forsyth County calendar compare with Gwinnett County?

Gwinnett County Public Schools (GCPS) posts its own calendar; our [Gwinnett County calendar story](/news/gwinnett-county-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/) uses the "2026-27 School Year Calendar" PDF listed in this story's sources. The two districts match on several dates and differ on others.

| Item | Forsyth County Schools | Gwinnett County Public Schools |
|---|---|---|
| First day | Thu., Aug. 6, 2026 | Wed., Aug. 5, 2026 |
| Fall break | Sept. 28 to Oct. 2, 2026 | Oct. 12-16, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving break | Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Nov. 23-27, 2026 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21-31, plus Jan. 1 | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 |
| Jan. 4, 2027 | Professional development (student holiday) | Student holiday |
| February | Feb. 12-16, 2027 | Feb. 12, 15 and 16, 2027 |
| Spring break | April 5-9, 2027 | Apr. 5-9, 2027 |
| Last day | Thu., May 27, 2027 | Wed., May 26, 2027 |
| Weekdays off before the last day | 33 | 31 |

Both districts are off for Thanksgiving, winter break, February and spring break on the same weekdays. The two-day difference in weekdays off comes from two Forsyth professional development days that Gwinnett's calendar does not list as student holidays: Monday, Oct. 19, 2026 and Monday, March 15, 2027 (our arithmetic from both calendars). Gwinnett's fall break falls two weeks after Forsyth's.

## When does the Forsyth County 2027-28 calendar start and end?

The district's 2027-28 page lists the first day of school as August 5, 2027 and the last day as May 25, 2028, with 178 student days and 190 staff days. It lists "December 20–31: Holiday Break – Student/Staff Holiday" and "February 18–21: Student/Staff Holiday." By our calculation, Dec. 20, 2027 is a Monday and Feb. 18-21, 2028 runs Friday through Monday. The district's calendar hub also lists a draft 2028-29 student calendar open for feedback, which we have not used for dates.
