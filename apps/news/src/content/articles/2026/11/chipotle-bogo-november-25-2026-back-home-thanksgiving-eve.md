**Chipotle's Back Home BOGO gives Chipotle Rewards members a second entrée free from 5 to 9 p.m. local time on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, in the restaurant only, according to a Chipotle release dated Nov. 20, 2026.** Members must scan their Rewards card at checkout. Chipotle says the offer is limited to five free entrées per check, and each free entrée needs a purchased entrée of equal or greater value. The terms table, a 2025 comparison and the Cyber Weekend offers that follow are on this page, with a printable card for all three.

## What Chipotle's Nov. 25 BOGO says

The release states: "On Wednesday, November 25, Chipotle Rewards members who purchase an entrée in-restaurant from 5 to 9 p.m. local time and scan their Chipotle Rewards member card at checkout can receive a second entrée free." The time is each restaurant's own local time. The offer is valid only on Nov. 25, 2026, at participating U.S. Chipotle locations and is subject to availability.

Chipotle describes the night as Thanksgiving Eve. Stephanie Perdue, Senior Vice President of Brand Marketing, said: "Thanksgiving Eve is when the hometown group chat comes to life," ... "we're giving friends a place to get together over real food." The release adds that the Back Home BOGO debuted in 2025 and "became Chipotle's largest sales-driving campaign of the year."

## What counts toward the free entrée and what does not

| Rule | What Chipotle's terms say |
|---|---|
| Who | Chipotle Rewards members who scan for Rewards at point of sale at time of purchase |
| When | Nov. 25, 2026, 5 p.m. to 9 p.m. local time |
| Where | In restaurant only, at participating U.S. Chipotle locations |
| Limit | Five free entrée items per check, subject to availability |
| What you buy | Each free entrée requires purchase of an entrée item of equal or greater value |
| Kids' meals | Do not count as an entrée purchase |
| Not valid | Catering, Burritos by the Box, mobile, online or delivery orders, or orders placed via third-party platforms |
| Rewards points | Purchased entrées are eligible for Chipotle Rewards points |
| Other offers | The promotion may not otherwise be combined with other coupons, promotions or special offers |

The release does not name which entrées qualify; it refers to "an entrée" throughout. "Additional restrictions may apply; void where prohibited," the footnote adds.

## How the 2026 BOGO differs from 2025

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Nov. 26, 2025 | Nov. 25, 2026 |
| Hours | Starting 4 p.m. until close, local time | 5 to 9 p.m., local time |
| Limit | Five free menu items per check | Five free entrée items per check |

The window is the change. In 2025 the offer ran from 4 p.m. until close, per Chipotle's Nov. 20, 2025 release; in 2026 it ends at 9 p.m. and starts an hour later. Chipotle's 2025 release says "menu items" and the 2026 release says "entrée items."

## How do you join Chipotle Rewards before Nov. 25?

The BOGO needs a Rewards scan, so a member account comes first. Chipotle's Rewards terms, last updated April 13, 2026, say there are no membership fees. Membership is open to legal residents of the 50 United States and Washington, D.C. Under 13 is not eligible, and members 13 to 17 need parent or guardian permission. Sign up in the Chipotle app or at chipotle.com/rewards. No sign-up deadline is given, but the scan happens at checkout, so join before you go.
