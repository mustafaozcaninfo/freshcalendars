**Cinnabon gives $10 in Rewards for every $25 in gift cards bought Nov. 22-30, 2026, and Auntie Anne's runs its own Black Friday and Cyber Monday gift card bonus for the same dates, according to each brand's offers page, read Nov. 25.** The buyer gets "two (2) $5 Reward Codes" from Cinnabon. Neither brand published a holiday press release that we could find, so the offers pages are the source. The tables compare the two brands and list the other dated offers.

## How do the Cinnabon and Auntie Anne's Black Friday gift card offers compare?

| | Cinnabon | Auntie Anne's |
|---|---|---|
| Black Friday window | 12:00 a.m. PST Nov. 22 to 11:59 p.m. PST Nov. 30, 2026 | 12:00 a.m. EST Nov. 22 to 11:59 p.m. EST Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Spend | $25 in gift cards or eGift Cards online, under one transaction | $25 in gift cards or eGift Cards online, under one transaction and one order number |
| Reward | $10 in Rewards, as two $5 Reward Codes | A bonus Reward; the page does not make the amount clear, so check at checkout |
| Rewards membership | Not given | Reward Code must be unlocked in an Auntie Anne's Rewards account |
| Reward codes valid | Not given | Nov. 4, 2026 to Jan. 31, 2027 |
| Standard holiday offer | $5 Reward per $25, Nov. 1 to Dec. 31, 2026 | One $5 Reward Code per $25, Nov. 4 to Dec. 31, 2026 |

Source: Cinnabon and Auntie Anne's offers pages, Nov. 25, 2026. Cinnabon's pages print the start in Eastern time and the end of the standard offer in Pacific time; the Black Friday dates are both Pacific time. Auntie Anne's homepage shows the standard offer as "11/04/26-01/31/2027," a later end date than the offers page, so confirm at checkout. Cinnabon's page says the Black Friday offer is for gift cards bought online under one transaction; it does not say whether Cinnabon Rewards membership is required.

## What other Cinnabon and Auntie Anne's holiday offers have dates?

| Date | Offer | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Wed., Nov. 4, 2026 to Wed., Dec. 30 | Cinnabon CinnaPack reward | A $5 Reward with a CinnaPack, for Cinnabon Rewards members; one per person, not with other offers or third-party delivery |
| Wed., Nov. 18, 2026 to Tue., Jan. 12, 2027 | Auntie Anne's Holiday Ornament | One ornament "on us" with any Pretzel Item (Bucket excluded), a Medium or Large Drink and a Dip; in store, while supplies last, one per person |
| Thu., Dec. 3, 2026, 12-4 p.m. local time | Cinnabon 41st birthday | $1.25 Classic Roll, in person at participating bakeries while supplies last; Rewards membership required |

Source: Cinnabon and Auntie Anne's offers pages, Nov. 25, 2026. Cinnabon's page lists no Cyber Monday-only offer.

## What is on the Cinnabon and Auntie Anne's holiday menus?

Cinnabon's homepage says "Holiday favorites are back" and its menu lists an Apple Crumble Topped Bon, a new Caramel Cold Brew topped with Cold Foam, shareable CinnaPacks and a gift box of 16 individually boxed Classic Rolls, "Available at participating locations only." Auntie Anne's homepage shows a limited-edition holiday cup, the "Holiday Cup of Cheer," marked "Limited Time Only." Neither site gave prices or launch and end dates for these items.

Last year, Auntie Anne's added a "Sparkleberry Smittens" merchandise drop, which GoTo Foods announced Nov. 17, 2025, with the Smittens at $24.99. A 2026 merchandise item has not appeared on the offers page.
