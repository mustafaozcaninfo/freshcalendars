**Quordle #1762 from Merriam-Webster lands on Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026.** Below: three clues for each of the four words, a scorecard for five opening guesses played against all four boards, and the answers last.

Friday's set is on the [Quordle #1761 page](/news/quordle-hints-answers-november-20-2026-1761/). Today's four words never repeat a letter inside themselves, which makes the opener matter; the scoring rules sit on the [Quordle guide](/news/topic/quordle/#how-it-works).

## Three clues for each Quordle word

The words are numbered in the order the solution pages list them.

**Word 1**

- Hint 1: Opens with the 7th letter of the alphabet and closes with the 5th.
- Hint 2: Two vowels, one in the middle and one at the very end.
- Hint 3: A grumble about something that annoys you, or a sharp pain in the stomach.

**Word 2**

- Hint 1: Opens with the 19th letter and closes with the 4th.
- Hint 2: Its two vowels have a single consonant between them.
- Hint 3: Neither liquid nor gas; firm and hard all the way through.

**Word 3**

- Hint 1: Opens with the 19th letter and closes with the 5th.
- Hint 2: The vowels take the third and fifth places.
- Hint 3: The uncomfortable feeling of guilt about something you did or failed to do.

**Word 4**

- Hint 1: Opens with the 3rd letter and closes with the 12th.
- Hint 2: Its two vowels sit side by side, just right of center.
- Hint 3: Willing to hurt others and feeling nothing about it.

## Five opening guesses scored on all four boards

Across the four words there are 20 slots, but only 14 distinct letters, and eight of the 20 are vowels. G means a green tile, Y a yellow one.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raise | 2G 1Y | 0G 2Y | 1G 2Y | 0G 2Y | 10 |
| Cider | 0G 3Y | 0G 2Y | 0G 1Y | 2G 1Y | 9 |

Raise leads with ten colored tiles and reaches every board. Cider gets nine, but none of its tiles on the first three grids is green. Slate and Adieu tie at eight. Crane colors seven, and Word 2 gets none of them.

Add the columns and Word 4 collects 12 colored tiles, Words 1 and 3 eleven each and Word 2 eight.

## Letter overlap between Gripe, Solid, Shame and Cruel

| Letter | Words | Positions |
|---|---|---|
| E | Gripe, Shame, Cruel | 5, 5 and 4 |
| R | Gripe, Cruel | 2; 2 |
| I | Gripe, Solid | 3; 4 |
| S | Solid, Shame | 1; 1 |
| L | Solid, Cruel | 3; 5 |

Nine more letters appear in one word only: G, P, O, D, H, A, M, C and U. Five shared letters plus nine single ones give the 14 distinct letters. Counted as slots, E adds two repeats and R, I, S and L one each, so six of the 20 slots are repeats.

Each word holds exactly two vowels, so eight vowels and twelve consonants in all. There is no Y, and no double letter in any word.

Three shared letters sit in the same spot twice. S opens both Solid and Shame, R is second in both Gripe and Cruel, and E ends both Gripe and Shame. A guess with S first, R second or E last turns tiles green on two boards at once. Cruel's E is fourth, so it stays yellow there when the guess ends in E.

Pair by pair, Gripe and Cruel share two letters, R and E. Every other pair of words shares one letter at most, so no pair overlaps by more than two.
