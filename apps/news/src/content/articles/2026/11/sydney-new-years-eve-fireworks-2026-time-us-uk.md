The City of Sydney says "fireworks light the harbour at 9pm and midnight" on New Year's Eve, which on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026 is 5 a.m. ET for the 9pm show and 8 a.m. ET for the midnight show (10 a.m. and 1 p.m. Greenwich Mean Time, GMT). The times below are computed from Sydney's offset, not published by the city. For the date itself, see [when is New Year's Day](/when-is/new-years-day/).

## Sydney fireworks times in US time zones and the UK

Daylight saving in New South Wales (NSW) started on Sunday, Oct. 4, 2026 and ends on Sunday, April 4, 2027, per the NSW Government, so Sydney is on Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT), which is UTC+11, on Dec. 31. US clocks have been on standard time since Nov. 1, 2026 (ET is UTC-5) and the UK is on GMT. The midnight show falls on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 in Sydney but on Thursday, Dec. 31 everywhere in the table.

| Show (Sydney time) | GMT | ET | CT | MT | PT | Honolulu |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9pm display | 10 a.m. | 5 a.m. | 4 a.m. | 3 a.m. | 2 a.m. | 12 a.m. |
| Midnight display | 1 p.m. | 8 a.m. | 7 a.m. | 6 a.m. | 5 a.m. | 3 a.m. |

All rows are Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026. Toronto matches ET. Honolulu is on UTC-10. The City of Sydney has not published show lengths, so none are given here.

The schedule page also lists "9.15pm, Sydney Harbour lights: Illuminated boats cross the harbour" (5:15 a.m. ET). That page carries no year, so it is shown as the page lists it. It describes the 9pm show as "a celebration of sky, land and sea, presented by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander artists." The guide recommends the 9pm Calling Country fireworks, produced by We Are Warriors, for "families with young children."

## How to watch Sydney's fireworks from the US or UK

The official accessibility page says: "You can watch the event on the ABC and iview. The broadcast begins at 8:45pm." The Australian Broadcasting Corporation (ABC) start is 4:45 a.m. ET, with live closed captions, and ABC Radio carries a live audio description of the midnight fireworks. The page names no stream for viewers outside Australia, and none has been announced.

For context, the City of Sydney said in its Dec. 29, 2025 release that "The ABC will broadcast live from the Sydney Opera House". That was the 2025 plan; the 2026 pages do not say where the broadcast will be based.

## What happened on the last New Year's Eve in Sydney

In 2025, the City said "more than 40,000 pyrotechnic effects will shimmer across the sky at 9pm and midnight." Its Jan. 1, 2026 release said "nearly a million people are estimated to have come together on the night," and that "nine tonnes of fireworks were shot from the Sydney Harbour Bridge, Sydney Opera House" with "6 aerial pyrotechnic platforms." These are figures for the 2025-26 night, not forecasts.

## Viewing spots and entry rules in Sydney

The guide says "most free vantage points are alcohol-free zones in 2026" and advises: "Plan to stay until after the midnight fireworks to avoid losing your spot." The ticketed vantage points page, which carries no year, lists:

- Sydney Opera House Forecourt: free ticketed, "Tickets available 10am 26 December".
- North Sydney Olympic Pool: "Adults $250" (Australian dollars) and $125 for children aged 3 to 15.
- Cahill Expressway: ballot allocation exhausted.

The vantage points map says Strickland Estate tickets "are free but only valid for entry between 5pm to 6pm," with general access after 6pm if it is not full.
