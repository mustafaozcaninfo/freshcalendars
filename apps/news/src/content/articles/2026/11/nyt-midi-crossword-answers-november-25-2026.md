**Wednesday's New York Times (NYT) Midi Crossword, dated Nov. 25, 2026, is called "Short-Term Growth": 34 clues on a 10-by-9 grid by Aidan Deshong, edited by Ian Livengood.** Every clue has a hint behind a tap, and the answers sit in the marked section at the bottom.

## How does "Short-Term Growth" show up in the entry lengths?

The title names no entry, but the clues do the work. The clue for 1-Across ends with "for short," and its answer has 4 letters. Two later Across clues, 15-Across and 24-Across, end with a question mark and ask for "more 1-Across" and "even more 1-Across." Those two answers have 6 and 10 letters. Read in grid order, the same idea stretches from 4 to 6 to 10 squares, and the 10-letter one fills a whole row from edge to edge. When a long entry stalls, go back to the 4-letter one: both longer answers start from its letters in some form.

## Which clue hides its trick best?

16-Down, *Answer to the riddle "What cheese is made backwards?"*. A riddle clue asks for a name and gives a stunt instead of a definition. The stunt is in the word "made": read it from the last letter to the first and you get a short real word, and the answer is a cheese named by it. The answer has 4 letters. Your crossings at 15-Across and 24-Across will confirm the first and last of them.
