**May 2027 begins on Saturday, May 1 and has one federal holiday, Memorial Day on Monday, May 31, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** Mother's Day is Sunday, May 9, the Preakness Stakes is Sunday, May 23 and the Indianapolis 500 is Sunday, May 30. The countdowns below are counted from Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026.

## May 2027 month layout and the days with a note

May 2027 has 31 days and runs from a Saturday to a Monday, so it holds five Saturdays, five Sundays and five Mondays. A Sunday-start calendar needs six rows: the first holds only Saturday, May 1 and the last holds Sunday, May 30 and Monday, May 31.

To count from another day, add the days left in the current month to the days in each month before your date. From Nov. 21 there are 9 days left in November, 31 in December, 31 in January, 28 in February, 31 in March and 30 in April, so May 31 is 9 + 31 + 31 + 28 + 31 + 30 + 31 = 191 days away.

## Memorial Day 2027: the only federal holiday in May

OPM's federal holiday list shows "Monday, May 31" for Memorial Day. The long weekend runs from Saturday, May 29 to Monday, May 31, computed. In England and Wales, GOV.UK lists the Early May bank holiday on Monday, 3 May and the Spring bank holiday on Monday, 31 May.

## Mother's Day, Armed Forces Day and Peace Officers Memorial Day

Federal law, 36 U.S.C. 117(a), says "The second Sunday in May is Mother's Day," which is Sunday, May 9, 2027. GovInfo describes Armed Forces Day as "Observed the third Saturday in May," set by Presidential Proclamation 3399 in 1961; in 2027 that is Saturday, May 15. The same day is Peace Officers Memorial Day: 36 U.S.C. 136 is the law "designating May 15 as Peace Officers Memorial Day."

## Preakness, Indy 500, NFL Draft and Eurovision in May 2027

The Preakness Stakes is on Sunday, May 23, according to Preakness.com, and marks the race's return to Pimlico Race Course. The Indianapolis Motor Speedway lists "Indianapolis 500 presented by Gainbridge" for May 18-30, 2027, and the race falls on Sunday, May 30. Our [IndyCar 2027 schedule story](/news/indycar-schedule-2027-17-races-indy-500-may-30/) has the season.

The NFL says the 2027 Draft "will take place Thursday, April 29 to Saturday, May 1, 2027, in Washington, D.C.," so its last day is in May.

The Kentucky Derby is held on the first Saturday in May, which is Saturday, May 1 in 2027, and Churchill Downs has run it on that Saturday by tradition. The Derby's own site blocked automated access, so the date is listed as unconfirmed here. See our [Kentucky Derby 2027 story](/news/kentucky-derby-2027-may-1-post-time-triple-crown-dates/). Eurovision 2027 is in Burgas, Bulgaria; the [Eurovision 2027 dates story](/news/eurovision-2027-dates-burgas-semi-finals-grand-final-may-15/) has the show dates.

## AP Exams, Jewish observances and dates not yet set

The College Board says AP Exams are "administered in schools over two weeks in May: May 3–7 and May 10–14." Hebcal lists Pesach Sheni on May 20-21 and Lag BaOmer from sunset on Monday, May 24 to nightfall on Tuesday, May 25. Shavuot begins at sundown on Thursday, June 10, so it is not in May.

Ascension Day (Thursday, May 6) and Pentecost (Sunday, May 16) are computed from Western Easter on Sunday, Mar. 28, 2027, at 39 and 49 days after it. Eid al-Adha is expected around May 16 or 17 and depends on the moon sighting, so no exact date is given. The Met Gala 2027 date is not yet confirmed by the Metropolitan Museum of Art. The Monaco Grand Prix is June 4-6, outside May.
