**Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week 2026 runs November 15-21,** according to the National Coalition for the Homeless and the National Student Campaign Against Hunger and Homelessness, which sponsor it. The organizers' theme is "Volunteer. Donate. Educate." Below are the dates, a day-by-day plan with our suggestions and the latest federal figures on homelessness and food security.

## When is Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week 2026?

The organizers give the dates as "November 15-21, 2026." By our calendar check that is Sunday, Nov. 15 through Saturday, Nov. 21. The organizers say the week is held "during the week prior to Thanksgiving," and Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, Nov. 26. See the [November 2026 important dates](/news/november-2026-important-dates-days-until/) and the [Thanksgiving 2026 planner](/news/thanksgiving-2026-planner-countdown-checklist/) for the rest of the month.

The organizers say the week was first held at Villanova University in 1975. The organizers' site links an event finder at hhweek.org/events and a registration page at hhweek.org/register. Their resources page offers an "H&H Week 2026 Organizing Toolkit" for campuses and communities, plus a printable handout and a poster for 2026. We did not check local events, so times and places come from that finder.

## Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week plan for each day

The organizers' three words are volunteer, donate and educate. The table below is our own suggestion for spreading them over the week, not an official schedule.

| Day | Volunteer | Donate | Educate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun., Nov. 15 | Find an event on the organizers' finder | Check what food or supplies a local group asks for | Read the figures below |
| Mon., Nov. 16 | Ask a local group about shifts | Sort pantry food you will not use | Share one fact with a friend |
| Tue., Nov. 17 | Sign up for a shift | Set aside a money gift | Read the federal housing report |
| Wed., Nov. 18 | Invite a friend or coworker | Gather coats, socks or hygiene items | Read the federal food report |
| Thu., Nov. 19 | Confirm your shift time | Pack the items | Post what you learned |
| Fri., Nov. 20 | Prepare what you need | Drop off food or supplies | Talk with family about a plan |
| Sat., Nov. 21 | Volunteer or join an event | Give if you did not earlier | Note what you will do after the week |

## What do federal figures say about homelessness and hunger?

Two federal reports give the latest national counts. Both come from government agencies, and each figure carries its year.

| Measure | Figure | Source and date |
|---|---|---|
| People homeless on a single night, January 2025 | 745,652 | U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), 2025 Annual Homeless Assessment Report (AHAR), May 29, 2026 |
| People living unsheltered on a single night, January 2025 | 266,320 | HUD, May 29, 2026 |
| Change in the homeless count since 2024 | 3% decrease | HUD, May 29, 2026 |
| Households food insecure at some time in 2024 | 13.7 percent | U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Economic Research Service (ERS), released Dec. 30, 2025 |
| Households with very low food security in 2024 | 5.4 percent | ERS, released Dec. 30, 2025 |

HUD's release said "745,652 individuals were homeless on a single night in January 2025" and that "266,320 individuals were living unsheltered." The USDA report says 13.7 percent of households were food insecure at least some time during 2024, and "5.4 percent of households experienced very low food security."
