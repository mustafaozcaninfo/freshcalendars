**The Boulder Valley School District (BVSD) has no classes Monday to Friday, Nov. 23-27, 2026, and from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, and students return Tuesday, Jan. 5**, according to the district's calendar page and its Academic Calendar 2026 - 2027 PDF. Spring break is March 15-19, 2027, and the last day for students is Thursday, May 20, 2027.

The district's web page and its PDF label the breaks differently, so the table below merges them. Both were read Nov. 13, 2026. The page says the calendar is "created and approved by the Board of Education every two years" and prints no approval date. The PDF says the calendar is for kindergarten through 12th grade (K-12) only.

## BVSD breaks in 2026-27

| Break | District's dates | No classes for students | Back in class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break | Oct. 12-13 (page) | Mon.-Tue., Oct. 12-13, 2026 (past) | Wed., Oct. 14 |
| Thanksgiving | Nov. 25-27 (page), Nov. 23-27 (PDF) | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Dec. 21-31 (page), Dec. 21 - Jan. 4 (PDF) | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring break | March 15-19 (page) | Mon.-Fri., March 15-19, 2027 | Mon., March 22 |

The return days follow from the dates: Nov. 30 and March 22 are the Mondays after the breaks, and the PDF prints "Second Semester Begins Jan. 5." Plan the weeks on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html) and the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html).

## Why do the BVSD page and PDF show different Thanksgiving and winter break dates?

The web page lists "Thanksgiving break – no school" for Nov. 25-27 and "Winter break – no school" for Dec. 21-31. The PDF lists the Thanksgiving break as Nov. 23-27 and the winter break as Dec. 21 - Jan. 4. The extra days are not holidays. They are work days for staff: the PDF marks Nov. 23 and 24 as parent-teacher conference exchange days with no classes, and Jan. 4 as a teacher professional development (PD) day with no class for students.

For students the result is the same: no classes all of Thanksgiving week and none from Dec. 21 through Jan. 4. The PDF's winter break range includes Dec. 24-25, which it lists as the observed Christmas holiday, and Jan. 1, listed as New Year's.

## How long is the Presidents' Day stretch at BVSD?

Monday, Feb. 15 is Presidents' Day, Tuesday, Feb. 16 is a conference exchange day and Wednesday, Feb. 17 is a PD day, so students are out Saturday, Feb. 13 through Wednesday, Feb. 17, five days in a row, and return Thursday, Feb. 18. The web page lists only Feb. 15, so the three weekdays off appear only when the PDF is read with it.

## When does the BVSD school year start and end?

The web page lists Aug. 12, 2026 as the first day for grades 1-5, 6 and 9. The PDF lists Aug. 13 for grades 7, 8 and 10-12, K Welcome Days Aug. 12-14, and a K-5 Assessment Day on Aug. 25. The first semester ends Friday, Dec. 18, and the second semester begins Tuesday, Jan. 5 and ends Thursday, May 20, 2027, the last day for students. Friday, May 21 and Monday, May 24 are staff days with no classes.
