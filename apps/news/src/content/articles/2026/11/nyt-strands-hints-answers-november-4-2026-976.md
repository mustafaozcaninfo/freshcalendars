## How Strands works

The board is six letters wide and eight tall. Theme words are made by linking letters that touch, up, down, sideways or diagonally, and every letter is used exactly once. One special answer, the spangram, names the theme and stretches from one side of the board to the opposite side. Find three non-theme words and you earn a hint that highlights the letters of a theme word.

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: It is the part of a company that hires and supports its workers.
- Hint 2: It is a phrase of two words: one means "of people," and the other means supplies or assets.
- Hint 3: Fourteen letters, and the first letter is H.

## Theme word hints

There are five theme words, all names for workers or for the work they do:

- **5 letters, starts with L:** hard work, or workers as a group.
- **6 letters, starts with P:** individuals, here the ones who work.
- **9 letters, starts with P:** the employees of an organization.
- **5 letters, starts with S:** the employees who work in a place.
- **9 letters, starts with W:** all the workers in a company or a country.

## Solving tips

Start with the spangram. On today's board it begins in the top-left corner and ends in the bottom-right corner. It is fourteen letters long, five more than the two longest theme words. The five theme words use 34 letters, so the spangram and the theme words together fill all 48 tiles. Labor and Staff are the shortest theme words, at five letters each. Personnel holds a double N, Staff a double F, and People two P letters and two E letters. If you get stuck, spell any ordinary words you can see to earn a hint.
