## How Strands works

The board is six letters wide and eight tall. Theme words are made by linking letters that touch, up, down, sideways or diagonally, and every letter is used exactly once. One special answer, the spangram, names the theme and stretches from one side of the board to the opposite side. Find three non-theme words and you earn a hint that highlights the letters of a theme word.

## Spangram hints

- Hint 1: It is everyone a performer plays to.
- Hint 2: It is a single word.
- Hint 3: Eight letters, and the first letter is A.

## Theme word hints

There are five theme words, and each one names a person in the crowd:

- **6 letters, starts with P:** a regular customer, or a supporter of the arts.
- **6 letters, starts with V:** someone watching a screen.
- **8 letters, starts with L:** someone who pays attention to a radio show or podcast.
- **9 letters, starts with S:** someone watching a game or a race from the stands.
- **11 letters, starts with C:** someone at a symphony or a rock show, written as one word.

## Solving tips

Start with the spangram. On today's board it starts at the left edge in the seventh row, drops to the bottom-left corner, runs along the whole bottom row and ends one row up, in the seventh row, sixth column. The longest theme word has eleven letters, three more than the spangram, so do not assume the longest answer spans the board. The five theme words use 40 letters, so the spangram and the theme words together fill all 48 tiles. If you get stuck, spell any ordinary words you can see to earn a hint.
