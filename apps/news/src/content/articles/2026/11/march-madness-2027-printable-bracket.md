**The 2027 men's National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) tournament has 76 teams, Selection Sunday is Sunday, March 14, and the Final Four is Saturday, April 3 at Ford Field in Detroit**, according to NCAA.com. Our free printable bracket draws that format on one US Letter page: the 12 Opening Round games, four 16-line regions and a Final Four box, with the dates of every round printed on it.

## March Madness 2027 printable bracket: what is on the page

- **Opening Round box:** 12 games, 24 lines, in two columns of six, one for UD Arena in Dayton and one for InTrust Bank Arena in Wichita.
- **Four regions:** South, West, Midwest and East, each with 16 first-round lines numbered by seed in the usual order (1, 16, 8, 9 and so on), then lines for the Second Round, Sweet 16 and Elite Eight.
- **Final Four and championship box:** the two semifinal lines, the title game and a line for the champion, with Ford Field printed on it.
- **Round dates** in the headers of each column.

## How does the 76-team bracket work?

NCAA.com says the men's and women's fields "will expand from 68 to 76 teams." The men's Opening Round has 24 teams matched in 12 games, played over two days before the first round. That sends 12 winners to join the other 52 teams, which makes the 64 lines of a standard bracket. The NCAA's May 2026 page does not say which bracket lines the Opening Round winners take, so our sheet keeps the Opening Round as its own box and leaves you to copy each winner over.

## Men's tournament 2027 dates and sites by round

| Round | Dates | Sites |
|---|---|---|
| Selection Sunday | Sun., Mar. 14 | Time and network not announced |
| Opening Round | Tue., Mar. 16 and Wed., Mar. 17 | UD Arena, Dayton, and InTrust Bank Arena, Wichita (six games each) |
| First Round | Thu., Mar. 18 and Fri., Mar. 19 | Charlotte, NC; Fort Worth, TX; Louisville, KY; Minneapolis, MN; Omaha, NE; Pittsburgh, PA; Sacramento, CA; Spokane, WA |
| Second Round | Sat., Mar. 20 and Sun., Mar. 21 | The same eight cities |
| Sweet 16 | Thu., Mar. 25 and Fri., Mar. 26 | South: San Antonio, TX; West: Los Angeles, CA; Midwest: Kansas City, MO; East: New York, NY |
| Elite Eight | Sat., Mar. 27 and Sun., Mar. 28 | The same four regional cities |
| Final Four | Sat., Apr. 3 | Ford Field, Detroit, MI |
| Championship | Mon., Apr. 5 | Ford Field, Detroit, MI |

Source: NCAA.com, May 7, 2026. The NCAA page listed the Opening Round second site as "to be announced later." CBS Sports and Yahoo Sports both reported on July 9, 2026 that Wichita would host, and that is the site on our sheet. The Kansas City regional is at T-Mobile Center, with the Sweet 16 on March 26 and the Elite Eight on March 28, according to the arena's March 25, 2026 announcement. Arenas for the other regionals, the First and Second Round arenas and which round is in which city had not been published in the sources we checked, so the sheet prints cities only.

## Where are the women's Final Four and championship?

The women's Final Four is Friday, April 2 and the championship game is Sunday, April 4, both at Nationwide Arena in Columbus, Ohio, according to NCAA.com. The printable covers the men's bracket only.

## What is not announced yet for March Madness 2027?

NCAA.com said in May 2026 that it did not have specifics on game times and networks. The Selection Sunday broadcast time and the 2027 television and streaming schedule are not yet announced, so neither is on the sheet. We will update this story when the NCAA publishes them.

## How to use the bracket on Selection Sunday

1. Print the sheet on US Letter paper, landscape.
2. After the field is announced on Sunday, March 14, write each Opening Round matchup in the box.
3. Write the other 52 teams on their seed lines in each region.
4. Copy each Opening Round winner onto its line after the games on March 16 and 17.
5. Advance winners column by column, from the First Round on March 18 and 19 to the Final Four on April 3.
