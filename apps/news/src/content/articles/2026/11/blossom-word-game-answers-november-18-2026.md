**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026, centers on G, with A, D, I, L, N and Z around it.** Beebom and Connections Solver print this same flower. A word with a doubled petal is the pangram, and the hints below nudge toward it without saying it.

## Today's petals: G in the center

- **Center:** G (every word needs it)
- **Petals:** A, D, I, L, N, Z

Only A and I are vowel letters here, and Z is the odd one out. G pairs naturally with N and with I, so endings such as -ing are within reach. - Hint 3: The spare slot goes to a petal, not to a letter outside the flower.

- Hint 5: It describes something that shines or stuns, and it ends with a familiar three-letter suffix.

## How a doubled letter helps with the pangram

A pangram must hold every one of the seven letters. Drop any one of them and the word is just a valid word, however long. A pangram of seven letters would use each exactly once, which is rare in English. An eight-letter one has a single spare slot, so exactly one letter repeats.

That narrows the hunt. Count the letters you have placed, and if seven slots hold seven different letters, the eighth must copy one of them. Here the repeat is the petal that looks least likely to double, so a solver who hunts for a word with two of the same common petal will stall.

| D | Petal | 1 |
| I | Petal | 1 |
| L | Petal | 1 |
| N | Petal | 1 |
| Z | Petal | 2 |

Six rows read 1 and one row reads 2, which adds to eight. Counts were computed from the word itself.
