**Pancake Day 2027 is Tuesday, 9 February, and Mothering Sunday in the UK is Sunday, 7 March, according to dates we worked out from the Church of England's calendar.** The church's table of moveable dates gives Ash Wednesday as 10 February 2027 and Easter Day as 28 March 2027. Pancake Day is the day before Ash Wednesday, and Mothering Sunday is 21 days before Easter Day. The table below carries both dates through 2030.

Neither day is a bank holiday. For the 2027 days off that are, see [UK bank holidays 2027](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/). The dates also sit on the [February 2027 printable calendars](/february-calendars.html) and the [March 2027 printable calendars](/march-calendars.html), and the printable below keeps all of them on one page.

## Pancake Day and Mothering Sunday dates, 2027 to 2030

The Church of England's calendar lists Ash Wednesday and Easter Day for each year. Shrove Tuesday, known as Pancake Day, is the day before Ash Wednesday. The church's page on the origins of Mothering Sunday says it falls "on the fourth Sunday of Lent", which is three weeks before Easter Day. We counted the dates from those two rules and checked each weekday.

| Year | Pancake Day | Ash Wednesday | Mothering Sunday | Easter Day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Tue 9 Feb | Wed 10 Feb | Sun 7 Mar | Sun 28 Mar |
| 2028 | Tue 29 Feb | Wed 1 Mar | Sun 26 Mar | Sun 16 Apr |
| 2029 | Tue 13 Feb | Wed 14 Feb | Sun 11 Mar | Sun 1 Apr |
| 2030 | Tue 5 Mar | Wed 6 Mar | Sun 31 Mar | Sun 21 Apr |

Ash Wednesday and Easter Day are the church's own dates. Pancake Day and Mothering Sunday are our arithmetic from them. In 2028, Pancake Day falls on 29 February, a leap day. For the full run of Lent days from Ash Wednesday to Easter, see our [Mardi Gras 2027 and Lent calendar](/news/mardi-gras-2027-february-9-ash-wednesday-february-10-lent-calendar/).

## When is the Olney Pancake Race in 2027?

The Olney Pancake Race organiser says on its registration page: "We're looking forward to the 2027 Race on Shrove Tuesday 9 February." The 2027 start time has not been announced. In 2025, Olney Town Council listed the start as 11:55am and described the event as the "world famous Pancake Race", in which the ladies of Olney compete on Shrove Tuesday.

Check the organiser's page for the 2027 time before you travel.

## Is Pancake Day or Mothering Sunday a bank holiday?

No. GOV.UK's bank holidays list, last updated on 24 November 2025, names these dates for England and Wales in 2027: 1 January, Good Friday on 26 March, Easter Monday on 29 March, 3 May, 31 May, 30 August, 27 December and 28 December. Pancake Day and Mothering Sunday are not on it. Easter weekend 2027 runs from Friday 26 March to Monday 29 March. Mothering Sunday, 7 March, comes three weeks before it. See [when is Easter](/when-is/easter/) for the Easter date on its own.

## How does UK Mothering Sunday differ from US Mother's Day?

The UK date moves with Easter. The US date does not: under 36 U.S.C. § 117, "The second Sunday in May is Mother's Day". That makes US Mother's Day 2027 Sunday, 9 May, 63 days after UK Mothering Sunday on 7 March. For the US date and the Memorial Day weekend after it, see [Mother's Day 2027 in the US](/news/mothers-day-2027-may-9-memorial-day-2027-may-31-long-weekend/).

| Day | 2027 date | How the date is set |
|---|---|---|
| UK Mothering Sunday | Sunday, 7 March | Fourth Sunday of Lent (Church of England) |
| US Mother's Day | Sunday, 9 May | Second Sunday in May (36 U.S.C. § 117) |
