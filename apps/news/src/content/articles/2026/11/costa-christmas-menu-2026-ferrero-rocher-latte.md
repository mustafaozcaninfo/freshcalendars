**Costa Coffee's Christmas menu launched in stores across the UK and Ireland on Thursday, 22 October 2026, while stocks last, according to Retail Times and What's The Jam.** Costa's own Christmas page returned an error when we checked, so every fact here rests on those two newsrooms, which agree on the list. The new hot drink is a Golden Hazel Latte, which Retail Times says is served with a Ferrero Rocher.

## What is new and returning on the Costa Christmas menu?

Both reports sort the range into drinks, savoury food and bakes. The table puts every item in one place, new or returning, as the two reports list it.

| Type | New in 2026 | Returning |
|---|---|---|
| Drinks | Golden Hazel Latte with Ferrero Rocher (hot); Gingerbread Iced Matcha Latte (iced); Ultimate Yule Brownie range (Hot Chocolate or Latte) | Black Forest Hot Chocolate; Terry’s Chocolate Orange Hot Chocolate; Gingerbread Latte; Caramel Nutcracker Latte |
| Savoury | Pigs in Blankets ‘Nduja Mac & Cheese; Festive Flatbread; Festive Feast Toastie | Pigs & Blankets Toastie; Brie, Bacon & Cranberry Panini |
| Bakes | Chocolate Brownie Fudge Cake; Chocolate Brownie; Dubai-Style Speculoos Tiffin; Toffee Apple Crumble; Black Forest Yule Log | All-Butter Mince Pie; Gluten Free Mince Tart; Terry’s Chocolate Orange Muffin; Festive Spice Muffin; Iced Penguin Gingerbread Biscuit (from 5 November) |

Retail Times describes the Caramel Nutcracker Latte as hot or iced; What's The Jam does not say.

## Golden Hazel Latte with a Ferrero Rocher

The Golden Hazel Latte is a hot drink and is new this year. Retail Times says it is "served with a Ferrero Rocher® for an extra special Christmas treat." The reports describe the Ferrero Rocher as part of the serve; they do not describe a partnership or any other tie-in.

The other two new drinks are the Gingerbread Iced Matcha Latte, the one iced drink among the new items, and the Ultimate Yule Brownie range, which comes as a Hot Chocolate or a Latte.

## Costa Christmas menu dates to mark

The menu comes in two stages. Most items went on sale on 22 October; the Iced Penguin Gingerbread Biscuit follows on 5 November. Retail Times writes "launching 5th November" and What's The Jam gives "November 5".

| Date | Day | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 22 October 2026 | Thursday | Christmas menu launches in stores across the UK and Ireland, while stocks last |
| 5 November 2026 | Thursday | Iced Penguin Gingerbread Biscuit launches |

Neither report gives an end date or a launch time. To count the weeks, mark both days on a [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html) or a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## How does the 2026 menu compare with 2025?

Last year Retail Times reported that Costa's Christmas menu "launches from Friday 24th October 2025 in stores nationwide", so the 2026 launch is two days earlier on the calendar. The 2025 headline drink was the new Butter Pecan Cookie Latte.

| | 2025 (Retail Times) | 2026 (Retail Times and What's The Jam) |
|---|---|---|
| Launch date | Friday, 24 October 2025 | Thursday, 22 October 2026 |
| Drink named first | Butter Pecan Cookie Latte (new) | Golden Hazel Latte with Ferrero Rocher (new) |
| Returning drinks listed | Mint Hot Chocolate; Terry's Chocolate Orange Hot Chocolate; Black Forest Hot Chocolate; Caramel Nutcracker range; Gingerbread Latte | Black Forest Hot Chocolate; Terry’s Chocolate Orange Hot Chocolate; Gingerbread Latte; Caramel Nutcracker Latte |

Mint Hot Chocolate was among the returning drinks in 2025 and is not in either 2026 list.

## Costa Christmas menu prices in 2026

Costa has not published prices in its launch information, What's The Jam reported, so we print none. Check the price in your branch or on Costa's app or website. Costa Club offers for 2026 were not part of either report.
