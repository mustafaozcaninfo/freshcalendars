## How far are the four-letter words from Genius?

The 23 four-letter answers give 23 points, about 18% of the 127 needed. The other 104 points must come from the 26 answers of five letters or more, which hold 158 between them. Drop the pangram and the remaining 25 longer words are worth 143, still 39 over the bar once the four-letter words are added (166 against 127).
