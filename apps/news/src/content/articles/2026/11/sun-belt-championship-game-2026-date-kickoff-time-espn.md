**The 2026 Sun Belt Football Championship Game is Friday, Dec. 4, at 7:00 p.m. ET (6:00 p.m. CT) on ESPN, according to the Sun Belt Conference.** Its May 27, 2026 release says the game "will be played at the site of the divisional winner with the best overall conference record" and "will feature the champions of the conference's East and West Divisions."

Louisiana Tech has clinched the West, the conference said on Nov. 16, 2026. The East and the host are decided on Saturday, Nov. 28, when Louisiana Tech visits Georgia Southern.

## What time does the Sun Belt title game start in each time zone?

| Time zone | Kickoff on Fri., Dec. 4 |
|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | 7 p.m. |
| Central (CT) | 6 p.m. |
| Mountain (MT) | 5 p.m. |
| Pacific (PT) | 4 p.m. |

The Sun Belt lists the Eastern and Central times; Mountain and Pacific are computed from them. The Nov. 28 game between Louisiana Tech and Georgia Southern starts at 12:00 p.m. ET (11:00 a.m. CT) on ESPNU, the conference said on Nov. 16.

## Who can win the Sun Belt East and host the game?

The Sun Belt standings page, read on Nov. 22 after the Nov. 21 games, shows Louisiana Tech 7-0 in conference play and Georgia Southern 6-1, with James Madison at 5-2 and App State at 4-3. The scenarios below are our arithmetic from those records, with each team's one remaining conference game.

| Nov. 28 result | East champion | Louisiana Tech record | Title-game host |
|---|---|---|---|
| Louisiana Tech beats Georgia Southern | Georgia Southern (6-2) if James Madison loses; if James Madison wins, both are 6-2 and head-to-head decides | 8-0 | Louisiana Tech, at Joe Aillet Stadium in Ruston, Louisiana |
| Georgia Southern beats Louisiana Tech | Georgia Southern (7-1) | 7-1 | Not given: both finish 7-1 |

The conference's posted tie-breaking procedures list "head-to-head result between the tied teams" as the first step for a two-team tie in a division. The May 27 release gives no rule for a host when the two division winners have the same record, so the site stays unconfirmed if Georgia Southern wins. Louisiana Tech cannot finish below 7-1, and no East team can pass Georgia Southern at 7-1.

## Who won the 2025 Sun Belt Championship Game?

In 2025, the game was Friday, Dec. 5, at 7 p.m. ET, with Troy at James Madison. The conference reported that "James Madison pulled away in the fourth quarter to outlast Troy, 31-14" at Bridgeforth Stadium, with attendance of 19,836. That is last year's result.
