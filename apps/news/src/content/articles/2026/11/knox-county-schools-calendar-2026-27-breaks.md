**Knox County Schools (KCS) in Knoxville, Tennessee, has winter break from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and spring break from Monday through Friday, March 8-12, 2027**, according to the "Calendar - Knox County Schools" page on the district's website. The last day for students is Wednesday, May 26, 2027, a half day. This is Knox County, Tennessee, not the Kentucky district of the same name.

The district page does not give a board approval date, so every date here is "as posted by the district" on Nov. 5, 2026. This story adds a table of every student day off and half day, a count of weekdays off by month, and the number of school days between breaks, all computed from the district's dates. A printable school-year calendar is at the bottom of the page.

## Knox County Schools breaks at a glance

Weekday counts are our arithmetic from the district's dates.

| Break | District's dates | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Break | Mon.-Fri., Oct. 5-9, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Oct. 12, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving Holidays | Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27, 2026 | 3 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | 10 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Mon.-Fri., March 8-12, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 15, 2027 |

Only Jan. 5 is named on the page as a return: "First Day for Students after Winter Break (Full Day)." The other return days are the next weekdays for which the page lists no day off. ## How many weekdays off does Knox County have each month?

Counting every weekday the district lists as closed or a student holiday, including the breaks, there are 30 weekdays off between Aug. 10, 2026 and May 26, 2027. The eight half days are counted separately. December has the most, because winter break takes nine of its weekdays and Jan. 1 is the tenth.

The 177 total, which leaves out the Jan. 4 half day, matches the number in the district's footnote: "177 student instructional days plus three (3) days accumulated under daily 7-hour schedule."

## What is the 180-day rule and how do weather closures work?

The district page lists "180" instructional days, with the footnote above. It also says the calendar allows "up to 8 instructional days due to inclement weather or serious outbreaks of illness before any makeup days will be required." In other words, the page gives eight closures of that kind before makeup days begin. It does not list the makeup dates. The district writes that "once approved, the finalized calendar is published."

## When does Knox County start winter break and spring break?

Winter break starts Monday, Dec. 21, 2026, after a half day for students on Friday, Dec. 18. By our count, students are out for 16 days in a row from Saturday, Dec. 19 through Sunday, Jan. 3, then Jan. 4 is the half-day in-service day and Jan. 5 is the first full day back. The [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the weeks.

Spring break is Monday through Friday, March 8-12, 2027, and the district lists no other days off that week. Two more days follow later: Friday, March 26 and Monday, March 29 are each listed as "Holiday (Knox County Schools Closed)," which makes a four-day weekend. Another Tennessee district, Nashville, has its own dates in our [Metro Nashville Public Schools calendar story](/news/metro-nashville-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/).

## Does the Knox County 2027-28 calendar have dates yet?

The same district page has a "Next Year" section. As listed on the district page, with status not stated, the first day for students in 2027-28 is Tuesday, Aug. 10, 2027, and the last day is Wednesday, May 24, 2028, a half day. The page does not say whether the board has approved those dates, so treat them as subject to change.
