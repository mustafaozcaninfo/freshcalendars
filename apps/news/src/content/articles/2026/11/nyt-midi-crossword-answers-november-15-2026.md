**The New York Times (NYT) Midi Crossword for Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, is titled "Keep It Simple" and has 44 clues on an 11-by-11 grid, credited to Jeffrey Martinovic.** A hint waits behind a tap for every clue, and the answers are in the marked section at the bottom.

## What the title "Keep It Simple" points to

The title is advice, and the puzzle takes it literally: four long entries each hide the same three consecutive letters of the alphabet, the ones a child learns first. The clue for 31-Across, which calls its answer child's play and adds a hopeful aside about this grid, is the key to the other three.

Read that clue first. Once you see the pattern, test it on 12-Across and 22-Across, the two long entries whose clues describe a place and a type of clothing, and on 17-Across, a food.

The four entries sit on rows 3, 5, 7 and 9, every other row from the top. The hidden letters shift sideways from one to the next: they land in columns 4 to 6 on row 3, 6 to 8 on row 5, 2 to 4 on row 7 and 9 to 11 on row 9.

## The least literal clue today

2-Down: *Cruising along?* The question mark tells you that "cruising" is not about speed on a highway. Picture a holiday on a large passenger ship and ask where that traveler is. The answer is one word of four letters, and its first and last letters match.

## How today's grid is built

The grid has 121 squares: 21 black and 100 letters. Of the 44 entries, 16 have 3 letters, 10 have 4, 10 have 5, 4 have 6, 2 have 8 and 2 have 11. The longest ones are the theme entries: the 11-letter pair runs the full width of the grid on rows 3 and 9, and the 8-letter pair starts on column 4 of row 5 and column 1 of row 7.

| Entry length | Entries | Across | Down |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 letters | 16 | 8 | 8 |
| 4 letters | 10 | 2 | 8 |
| 5 letters | 10 | 6 | 4 |
| 6 letters | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| 8 letters | 2 | 2 | 0 |
| 11 letters | 2 | 2 | 0 |

Three 6-letter Downs stand in a stack in the top right, columns 9, 10 and 11, and the fourth runs down column 3 from row 6 to the bottom edge. The two 11-letter entries each cross 11 Down entries, one per column, so a single wrong letter in either of them breaks a lot of crossings. The 8-letter theme entries cross 8 each.

The grid is not mirrored: the black squares of the top left do not have a twin in the bottom right.
