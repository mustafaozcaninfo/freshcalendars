**Harvester lists its Christmas Day 2026 menu from £69.99 per adult and asks for full payment and pre-order by 1 December, according to the pub restaurant chain's own pages read on 22 November 2026.** Christmas Day is Friday 25 December, and 1 December is a Tuesday. Festive dining is from £20.99, and deposits range from £5 to £10 per person depending on the day.

## What are the Harvester Christmas prices and deposits for 2026?

Every 2026 price and rule Harvester has published, by occasion. Boxing Day is Saturday 26 December.

| Occasion | Price | Deposit | Payment deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Festive menu | From £20.99 | £5 per person, parties of 12 guests or more | Not published |
| Christmas Day, Friday 25 December | From £69.99 per adult | £10 per person, whatever the party size | Full payment and pre-order by 1 December |
| Boxing Day, Saturday 26 December | Not published | £5 per person, groups of 12 guests or more ordering from the Boxing Day menu | Not published |

The prices are "from" prices. The Christmas Day figure is the only one the chain labels per adult.

## When do you have to pay for Harvester Christmas Day?

The Christmas deposit policy page says Christmas Day needs "full payment, and pre-order is required by 1st December." That is nine days from this story's date of 22 November 2026, a count computed from the two dates. The same page sets a £10 per person deposit "whatever the party size." The Christmas Day menu page adds that "at the time of booking you may be required to pay a deposit." Mark 1 December on a [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## What is on the Harvester Christmas Day menu 2026?

The page shows examples, and Harvester does not say they are the full menu. They include the starter Bubble & Squeak Bon Bons, and mains of Hand-Carved Turkey with all the trimmings and Festive Pork Tomahawk & Crispy Christmas Hash. A Mushroom Suet Pudding is described as "a meat-free alternative." Desserts shown are Christmas Pudding and Baileys® Salted Caramel Profiterole Sundae. The page does not label dishes vegetarian or vegan.

## What does the Harvester festive menu cost and when does it run?

The Christmas page says "festive dining available from £20.99." It gives no start or end dates, no course counts and no weekday split. A deposit applies only to parties of 12 guests or more, at £5 per person. That is £49.00 less than the Christmas Day starting price, a difference computed from the two figures, though the pages do not say the two menus match in courses. The Christmas page also mentions a Christmas Day Click & Collect service, with no price or details.

## What does Harvester charge for a Boxing Day deposit?

The deposit page sets "a deposit of £5 per person for groups of 12 guests or more ordering from the Boxing Day menu." Boxing Day menu prices and dishes are not published.
