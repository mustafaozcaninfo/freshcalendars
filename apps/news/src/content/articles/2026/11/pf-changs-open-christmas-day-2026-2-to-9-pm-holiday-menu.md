**P.F. Chang's restaurants nationwide will be open on Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25, 2026, from 2 p.m. to 9 p.m. for dine-in, takeout and third-party delivery, according to the company's Nov. 4 release.** The company calls it the first time it has welcomed guests nationwide on Christmas Day. Christmas Eve runs on regular hours. The dated table, the $30 menu course by course, the gift card bonus window and a printable follow.

## P.F. Chang's holiday dates, Dec. 2 to Jan. 26

| Date | Day | What P.F. Chang's published |
|---|---|---|
| Nov. 4, 2026 | Wednesday | Three-course holiday meal and new seasonal items begin |
| Dec. 2, 2026 | Wednesday | Gift card bonus window opens (online purchases) |
| Dec. 24, 2026 | Thursday | Christmas Eve: regular hours |
| Dec. 25, 2026 | Friday | Christmas Day: open 2 p.m. to 9 p.m. |
| Dec. 29, 2026 | Tuesday | Gift card bonus window ends |
| Jan. 6, 2027 | Wednesday | $10 bonus rewards can be redeemed (to Mar. 31, 2027) |
| Jan. 26, 2027 | Tuesday | Holiday meal and seasonal items end |

The U.S. Office of Personnel Management lists Christmas Day 2026 as Friday, December 25; the other weekdays are our computation. ## Is P.F. Chang's open on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day?

Yes to both. The release says: "On Christmas Day, P.F. Chang's restaurants nationwide will be open from 2 p.m. to 9 p.m. for dine-in, takeout and third-party delivery. Restaurants will operate regular hours on Christmas Eve." The trade site fsrmagazine.com repeated the Christmas hours on Nov. 6.

Jim Mazany, the company's chief executive officer, said in the release: "Opening nationwide on Christmas Day for the first time gives us another opportunity to welcome families and friends around our tables." The release does not list Christmas Eve opening or closing times, and it does not address exceptions. Hours may vary by location, so check your restaurant's page before you go.

## What is on the $30 three-course holiday menu?

"Available November 4, 2026, through January 26, 2027, the three-course holiday meal starts at $30 per person," the release says. Guests pick one item per course:

| Course | Choices |
|---|---|
| Starter | Hot & Sour Soup, Egg Drop Soup or House Salad |
| Entrée | Chang's Spicy Chicken, Miso Glazed Salmon, Crispy Honey Shrimp, Mongolian Beef, Ma Po Tofu or Duck Fried Rice |
| Premium entrée upgrade (extra cost) | Dashi Risotto with Hokkaido Scallops, NY Prime Strip Steak Tataki or Roasted Duck with Cherry Chutney |
| Dessert | Key Lime Pie, Red Velvet Cake or Chocolate Peanut Butter Cream Pie |

The release gives no price for the upgrades and does not say whether the meal is dine-in only.

## What new seasonal items are on the P.F. Chang's menu?

The release lists new items from Nov. 4, 2026, to Jan. 26, 2027:

- King Crab Gyoza.
- Dashi Risotto with Seared Hokkaido Scallops.
- Fire-Roasted Crispy Duck, served with warm roti, hoisin and julienned vegetables.
- Honey Sesame Crème Brûlée, torched tableside.
- Fortune & Fire cocktail, made with WhistlePig PiggyBack 6-Year Bourbon, Disaronno, pomegranate, lemon and Fever-Tree ginger beer.
- The Frosted Cranberry Cosmo, which returns, and Belle Glos "Las Alturas" Pinot Noir.

"All new food items are available for dine-in, online ordering and third-party delivery," the release says, except the crème brûlée, which is dine-in only. It gives no item prices. For a party, the Fire-Roasted Crispy Duck Catering Package feeds four to six guests and starts at $195. ## When does the P.F. Chang's gift card bonus run?

The release offers "a $10 bonus reward for every $50 gift card purchase through December 29." The fine print sets the terms: gift cards bought online from Dec. 2, 2026, through Dec. 29, 2026; the bonus reward can be redeemed from Jan. 6, 2027, through Mar. 31, 2027, on food and non-alcoholic beverages. Fortune cookie gift card holders cost $2.99, or are free with a gift card purchase of $100 or more. Compare with other chains in the [restaurant gift card bonus deals for 2026](/news/restaurant-gift-card-deals-2026-bonus-cards-cheesecake-factory-panera-red-robin/).

The holiday campaign, "Bright Nights. Festive Flavors.", stars actor Harry Shum Jr. and draws on Japan's winter illuminations.

## What has P.F. Chang's not announced?

- Thanksgiving Day 2026 status.
- New Year's Eve and New Year's Day hours.
- Prices for the new seasonal items and for the premium entrée upgrades.
- Whether the three-course meal is dine-in only.
