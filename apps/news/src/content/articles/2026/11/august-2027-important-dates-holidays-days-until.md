**August 2027 begins on Sunday, Aug. 1 and has no federal holiday: the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) lists Memorial Day on May 31 and then Labor Day on Monday, Sept. 6.** The month's big date is the total solar eclipse on Monday, Aug. 2. The countdowns below are counted from Monday, Nov. 23, 2026.

## Is there a federal holiday in August 2027?

No. OPM's federal holiday list shows "Monday, May 31" for Memorial Day and then "Monday, September 06" for Labor Day, so nothing falls in between in August. The next federal holiday after the month is on [When is Labor Day?](/when-is/labor-day/). The month before is in [July 2027 important dates](/news/july-2027-important-dates-holidays-days-until/), which covers Independence Day observed.

National Aviation Day on Thursday, Aug. 19 is an observance, not a federal holiday: 36 U.S.C. 118 says "The President may issue each year a proclamation" designating August 19 as National Aviation Day.

## What is the August 2, 2027 total solar eclipse?

NASA's list of eclipses in 2027 shows "2027 Aug 02: Total Solar Eclipse", with greatest eclipse at 10:07 UT, which is 6:07 a.m. EDT. NASA's page for the eclipse gives 6 minutes 23.2 seconds as the greatest duration. Totality is over Spain and North Africa and is not visible from the U.S.; NASA lists only a partial eclipse for northern Maine. City-by-city times are in our guide to [totality times in Spain and Egypt](/news/total-solar-eclipse-august-2-2027-path-times-by-city-travel/), and all five 2027 eclipses are in the [2027 eclipse overview](/news/eclipses-2027-dates-times-total-solar-eclipse-august-2/).

NASA also lists a penumbral lunar eclipse on Aug. 17, with greatest eclipse at 07:14 UT, which is 3:14 a.m. EDT.

## Moon phase times in Eastern Time for August 2027

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) publishes times in Universal Time (UT). Daylight time is in effect all month, so New York is four hours behind UT on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). The month has two new moons and one full moon, so there is no blue moon. Our [2027 Moon phase calendar](/news/moon-phases-2027-full-moon-calendar/) has the other months.

| Phase | UT | Eastern Time |
|---|---|---|
| New moon, Monday, Aug. 2 | 10:05 | 6:05 a.m. EDT |
| First quarter, Monday, Aug. 9 | 04:54 | 12:54 a.m. EDT |
| Full moon, Tuesday, Aug. 17 | 07:29 | 3:29 a.m. EDT |
| Last quarter, Tuesday, Aug. 24 | 02:27 (Aug. 25) | 10:27 p.m. EDT |
| New moon, Tuesday, Aug. 31 | 17:41 | 1:41 p.m. EDT |

The new moon and the total solar eclipse fall on the same day, Aug. 2, and the new moon at 6:05 a.m. EDT comes shortly before NASA's greatest-eclipse time of 6:07 a.m. EDT.

## When do the Perseids peak in 2027?

The American Meteor Society (AMS) lists the Perseids as active from July 17 to August 24, with the peak on Aug. 12-13. No peak hour is given. The Full Moon is on Aug. 17, so the Moon is waxing in the days before it.

## Sports and events in August 2027

The Pro Football Hall of Fame says "The 2027 Pro Football Hall of Fame Game is at 8 p.m. Thursday, Aug. 5, 2027" and that the Class of 2027 will be enshrined Saturday, Aug. 7. Little League lists the Baseball World Series in Williamsport, Pennsylvania for August 18 to 29 and the Softball World Series in Greenville, North Carolina for August 1 to 8. Leagues Cup runs "August 3 through September 5, 2027".

Formula 1's 2027 calendar has the Hungarian Grand Prix on July 30 to Aug. 1 and no other race in August. The Edinburgh Festival Fringe says its 80th edition takes place from "06 – 30 August 2027", and Burning Man lists "August 29 through September 6, 2027".

## UK summer bank holidays in August 2027

GOV.UK lists the summer bank holiday on Monday, Aug. 2 in Scotland and on Monday, Aug. 30 in England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Other UK bank holidays are in our [2027 UK bank holidays list](/news/uk-bank-holidays-2027-dates-england-scotland-northern-ireland/).

Some August events have no confirmed 2027 date here. FedEx Cup Playoffs dates are not confirmed on pgatour.com, the U.S. Open tennis start is not confirmed on usopen.org and the NFL's 2027 preseason is not confirmed.
