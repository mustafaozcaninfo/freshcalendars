National Puzzle Day is Friday, Jan. 29, 2027, according to puzzledayfun.com, the founder's site, which says "Puzzle Day is January 29th every year." This story adds a computed weekday table for 2027 to 2032, a note on what the founder's free kit offers, links to our own daily puzzle pages and a free one-page plan sheet.

## Which weekday is Puzzle Day from 2027 to 2032?

Because the date is fixed, the weekday moves. We computed it from the calendar; the date itself comes from puzzledayfun.com and the founder's page at jodijill.com.

| Year | Jan. 29 falls on |
|---|---|
| 2027 | Friday |
| 2028 | Saturday |
| 2029 | Monday |
| 2030 | Tuesday |
| 2031 | Wednesday |
| 2032 | Thursday |

Mark the day on the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html). Just before it, [National Handwriting Day](/news/national-handwriting-day-2027-saturday-january-23/) falls on Saturday, Jan. 23.

## Who started National Puzzle Day?

The "About Puzzle Day" page says: "Puzzle Day was founded by Jodi Jill, a professional puzzle maker and lifelong puzzle enthusiast." Fort Wayne radio station Star 88.3 also named Jill as the founder in a Jan. 29, 2025 post.

The start year is not settled in the founder's own material. One media page says the day launched in 2002, while the about pages say "over 30 years ago" and "over 32 years ago". We print no start year as fact. Jill's site also says Puzzle Month is an extension of Puzzle Day on January 29th.

## What does the founder's free puzzle kit include?

The site invites visitors to "Download, Copy and Play our official 2026 Puzzle Kit," free for everyone As of Nov. 4, 2026, we saw no 2027 kit posted, so check puzzledayfun.com closer to January. The site also lists a Puzzle Day Fact Sheet, an escape room activity kit and the hashtag #PuzzleDay.

We found no confirmed New York Times (NYT) Games or USA Today activity tied to the day, and we do not claim one.
