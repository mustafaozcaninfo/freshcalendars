**International Volunteer Day (IVD) is Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026,** according to the United Nations (UN), which says the day "is celebrated on 5 December every year." In 2026 it also lands at the close of the UN's International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development. Below are the U.S. volunteering figures, the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) rules on volunteer expenses, where to find a role and a free one-page planning sheet.

## What is International Volunteer Day and what is the 2026 theme?

The UN page says the day "started as an international observance mandated by the United Nations General Assembly in 1985." It names the "International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development" and cites the General Assembly resolution numbered 78/127. The UN Volunteers knowledge portal reported on June 18, 2025, that the General Assembly "has officially designated 2026 as the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development." The global launch of the year was on Dec. 5, 2025, so this Dec. 5 closes it.

The 2026 theme is not yet announced. The 2025 theme was "Every Contribution Matters."

## How many Americans volunteer?

The U.S. Census Bureau and AmeriCorps published their volunteering figures on Nov. 19, 2024. They cover September 2022 to September 2023.

| Measure | Figure | Survey period | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formal volunteers, age 16 and up | 75.7 million (28.3% of the population) | Sept. 2022 to Sept. 2023 | Census Bureau and AmeriCorps, Nov. 19, 2024 |
| Hours served by formal volunteers | 4.99 billion (estimated) | Sept. 2022 to Sept. 2023 | Census Bureau and AmeriCorps, Nov. 19, 2024 |
| Value contributed | Over $167.2 billion | Sept. 2022 to Sept. 2023 | Census Bureau and AmeriCorps, Nov. 19, 2024 |

"Formally volunteered" means through an organization, in the Census Bureau's wording.

## What can you deduct for volunteering?

IRS Publication 526 (2025 edition) covers charitable contributions. It draws a line between your time and your costs:

| Item | Deductible? | What the IRS says (2025 edition) |
|---|---|---|
| Value of your time or services | No | You cannot deduct "the value of your time or services" |
| Unreimbursed expenses directly connected with volunteering | Yes, for a qualified organization | Such expenses may be deducted |
| Car mileage by the standard rate | Yes, under the same rule | "You can use a standard mileage rate of 14 cents a mile" |

The rate above comes from the 2025 edition; check the current publication before you file. Keep a log of hours and miles. Our [GivingTuesday 2026 tax deduction and charity check](/news/giving-tuesday-2026-december-1-tax-deduction-charity-check/) covers giving money rather than time.

## Where can you find volunteer opportunities near you?

VolunteerMatch is now part of Idealist; volunteermatch.org redirects to Idealist's VolunteerMatch page, where you can search by keyword, skill or interest within a radius. Volunteer.gov is a federal volunteer listing site, and AmeriCorps has a volunteer page at americorps.gov. We could not read that page, so we do not describe it here.
