**World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) holds the 40th annual Royal Rumble on Saturday, Feb. 6, 2027, at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona, with general tickets on sale Wednesday, Nov. 18, at noon ET (10 a.m. Arizona time) through SeatGeek, according to WWE's Nov. 2, 2026 announcement.** WWE has not announced a start time or ticket prices. Below: the presale and on-sale schedule by time zone, the venue, how to watch and the WWE dates on the road to the Rumble.

## Royal Rumble 2027 presale and ticket on-sale times

WWE lists all three steps at noon ET, and the Nov. 2 release gives the on-sale as "Wednesday, Nov. 18, at noon ET/10 a.m. MST via SeatGeek.com." Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so in November its clocks are on Mountain Standard Time (MST), two hours behind ET. The Pacific Time (PT) column is our conversion.

| Step | Date | ET | Arizona (MST) | PT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Club WWE presale | Mon., Nov. 16, 2026 | Noon | 10 a.m. | 9 a.m. |
| General presale (register at wwe.com/RoyalRumble27) | Tue., Nov. 17, 2026 | Noon | 10 a.m. | 9 a.m. |
| General on-sale via SeatGeek.com | Wed., Nov. 18, 2026 | Noon | 10 a.m. | 9 a.m. |

WWE has not announced prices or travel packages. Mark the three days on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## Where is the Royal Rumble 2027?

State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona. WWE's Aug. 4, 2026 announcement says it is the first Royal Rumble at the stadium and "Arizona's third time hosting Royal Rumble following previous editions in 2019 and 2013." The same day a WWE corporate release described a seven-event agreement spanning three years, and it names the Royal Rumble for February 2027.

Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale hosts a double event on Friday, Feb. 5: Friday Night SmackDown and Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide (AAA) Road to Rey de Reyes, per WWE's Nov. 2 release. SmackDown is on Friday, not on Rumble Saturday.

## What time does the Royal Rumble start and how do you watch?

WWE has not announced a start time. Feb. 6, 2027 is a Saturday; see the [February 2027 printable calendar](/february-calendars.html).

In the United States the show streams "exclusively on the ESPN App" for ESPN Unlimited subscribers, according to the WWE release as reported by Fightful on Nov. 2, 2026. WWE and ESPN said on Aug. 6, 2025 that ESPN platforms would become the exclusive U.S. home of all WWE Premium Live Events starting in 2026, and that Netflix is the exclusive home of WWE programming around the world, other than select international markets. WWE has not named a Netflix stream for this show.

## What is at stake in the 2027 Royal Rumble matches?

WWE says the event is headlined by the 30-man and 30-woman Royal Rumble matches, with each winner receiving a championship match opportunity at WrestleMania 43. WWE's announcement, as reported by Fightful on Sept. 12, 2025, puts WrestleMania 43 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, in 2027; the date is not officially announced.

The Elimination Chamber 2027 date and city and the WrestleMania 43 date are not announced.
