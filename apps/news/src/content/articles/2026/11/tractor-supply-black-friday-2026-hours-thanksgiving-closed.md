**Tractor Supply Company will run seven days of Black Friday savings from Monday, Nov. 23, to Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026, with Friday-only doorbusters when stores open at 6 a.m. on Nov. 27, according to its Nov. 19 release.** Stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26. The release also lists adjusted hours through New Year's Day, and the full list is in the table below and on a free printable card.

## What are Tractor Supply's Black Friday dates?

The company, based in Brentwood, Tenn., calls itself "the largest rural lifestyle retailer in the United States." Its release says it "will offer seven days of Black Friday savings Nov. 23-29 on Christmas gifts, décor, outdoor gear, pet favorites and winter essentials." The release gives two dated events:

- **Nov. 23-29: Seven Days of Savings.** "Black Friday pricing will be available across gifts, everyday essentials, outdoor gear and more."
- **Nov. 27: Black Friday Doorbusters.** "Tractor Supply stores open at 6 a.m. with Friday-only, in-store doorbuster deals available while supplies last."

Nov. 23 is a Monday and Nov. 29 is a Sunday. The release does not give online sale dates separate from these.

## Is Tractor Supply open on Thanksgiving?

No. The release lists Thursday, Nov. 26, Thanksgiving Day, as closed. It says stores "will operate on adjusted hours around Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's." The list does not say "local time" and does not give regular hours, so each store's own hours may differ. The company's [store locator](https://www.tractorsupply.com/tsc/store-locator) is the place to confirm yours.

## Tractor Supply holiday hours 2026, Nov. 24 to Jan. 1

The release lists each date with its weekday; the labels for Nov. 24 and 25 are ours.

| Date | Day | Tractor Supply hours |
|---|---|---|
| Tue., Nov. 24 | Tuesday before Thanksgiving | 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. |
| Wed., Nov. 25 | Wednesday before Thanksgiving | 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. |
| Thu., Nov. 26 | Thanksgiving Day | Closed |
| Fri., Nov. 27 | Black Friday | 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. |
| Thu., Dec. 24 | Christmas Eve | 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. |
| Fri., Dec. 25 | Christmas Day | Closed |
| Thu., Dec. 31 | New Year's Eve | 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. |
| Fri., Jan. 1, 2027 | New Year's Day | 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. |

Tractor Supply lists Christmas Eve hours as 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. New Year's Eve runs 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., and New Year's Day opens an hour later, at 9 a.m., with the same 6 p.m. close.

## What is discounted during Tractor Supply's seven days of savings?

The release gives a ceiling for each line, not a price for any one item. "Customers can save up to 25% on Christmas décor and toys, up to 50% on apparel, up to $1,500 on recreation vehicles, up to $400 on safes and up to 30% on dog food, treats and toys."

| Product line | Savings, per the release |
|---|---|
| Christmas décor and toys | Up to 25% |
| Apparel | Up to 50% |
| Recreation vehicles | Up to $1,500 |
| Safes | Up to $400 |
| Dog food, treats and toys | Up to 30% |

## Which Tractor Supply doorbusters are Friday-only?

The doorbusters are in stores on Friday, Nov. 27, "while supplies last." The release names these groups:

- Tractor Supply buckets, winter beanies and fleece blankets
- Pet beds
- Heavy-duty jack stands and garage jacks
- Trail cameras, electric dirt bikes, gun safes and select ammunition
- Meat grinders

It adds: "Select styles and product availability may vary by location. Purchase limits and additional restrictions apply." No doorbuster prices are listed in the release.

## What Christmas items does Tractor Supply feature?

The release lists four decor and toy items: a 6-Foot Cowboy Santa Rooster, a 70-Inch Animated Dancing Cowboy Santa, a Dynacraft Ride-On Highland Cow and a 44-Inch Light-Up Cowboy Gingerbread Man, described as a Tractor Supply-exclusive decoration. LEGO is "new to Tractor Supply this Christmas." The release gives no prices for these.

"Christmas at Tractor Supply is a little different," said Seth Estep, executive vice president and chief merchandising officer at Tractor Supply. "You can find a gift for the kids, something for the dog, a new tool for Dad and everything you need to get ready for winter, all in one trip."
