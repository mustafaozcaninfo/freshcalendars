**February 2027 begins on Monday, Feb. 1 and has one federal holiday, Washington's Birthday, commonly called Presidents Day, on Monday, Feb. 15, according to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM).** Super Bowl 61 (LXI) is the day before, on Sunday, Feb. 14, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The month also holds Lunar New Year on Feb. 6, Mardi Gras on Feb. 9 and the NBA All-Star Game on Feb. 21. The countdowns below are counted from Friday, Nov. 20, 2026.

February 2027 has 28 days and runs from a Monday to a Sunday, so it holds exactly four of each weekday. A Sunday-start calendar needs five rows, with Sunday, Feb. 28 alone on the last one. The one federal holiday is in bold.

## Federal holiday in February 2027

OPM lists one federal holiday for February 2027, Washington's Birthday on Monday, Feb. 15. OPM uses the legal name from 5 U.S.C. 6103(a); the day is commonly called Presidents Day. What is open and closed, from federal offices to the stock exchange, is in our story on [Presidents Day 2027](/news/presidents-day-2027-february-15-whats-open-closed/). The holiday follows Valentine's Day by one day, and our [Valentine's Day 2027](/news/valentines-day-2027-sunday-february-14-dates-printables/) story covers that weekend.

## Moon phases in February 2027

## Lunar New Year, Mardi Gras, Ash Wednesday and Ramadan

Hong Kong's Labour Department lists Lunar New Year's Day on 6 February 2027, a Saturday; our [Lunar New Year 2027](/news/lunar-new-year-2027-year-of-the-goat/) story has the Year of the Goat and a zodiac chart. In Louisiana, Mardi Gras is a legal holiday under Revised Statutes 1:55 A(1), and it falls on Tuesday, Feb. 9. The U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops' 2027 calendar puts Ash Wednesday on Feb. 10; the [Mardi Gras and Lent calendar](/news/mardi-gras-2027-february-9-ash-wednesday-february-10-lent-calendar/) and the [Ash Wednesday 2027 fasting rules](/news/ash-wednesday-2027-february-10-fasting-rules-lent-dates/) cover the rest.

Ramadan is expected to begin on Monday, Feb. 8, with the first night prayers on the evening of Sunday, Feb. 7; the start depends on moon sighting. Our [Ramadan 2027 calendar](/news/ramadan-2027-calendar-dates-timetable/) has the dates and the New York timetable.

## Not yet announced for February 2027

The Super Bowl kickoff time and the Royal Rumble start time are not yet announced. The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) said on Nov. 16, 2026 only that the 2027 filing season is "just months away," so no start date is listed here.
