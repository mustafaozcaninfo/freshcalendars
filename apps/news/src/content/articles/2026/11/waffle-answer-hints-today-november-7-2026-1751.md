## How the swap budget works out on today's board

Waffle gives you 15 swaps. Seven green tiles cost nothing, so 14 tiles are left to place. If every swap fixed two tiles at once, seven swaps would do it. Today's route cannot manage that: four of its swaps fix two tiles and the other six fix one, so 4 x 2 + 6 = 14 tiles take 10 swaps. Five swaps stay in the bank.

The doubles arrive late. Swaps 1 and 2 fix one tile each, the first double is swap 3, and the next three doubles are swaps 7, 9 and 10. A route that opened with its doubles would feel quite different, because the early moves here are about setting up squares for the later ones.

One square works as a relay. Row 2, column 3 is touched by swaps 1, 2 and 3 in a row, more than any other square in the route. If you solve by hand, expect to park a tile there and pass it along.

The 21 tiles hold one letter three times, five letters twice each and eight letters once, which makes 14 different letters in the pile.
