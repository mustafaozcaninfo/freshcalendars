Western Easter 2027 falls on Sunday, March 28, according to the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB), and Orthodox Easter falls five weeks later, on Sunday, May 2, according to the American Carpatho-Russian Orthodox Diocese (ACROD). The table below runs both dates through 2036, with the gap in days, so you can see the years they match: 2028, 2031 and 2034. For what is open on Good Friday and Easter Monday 2027, see our [Easter 2027 story](/news/easter-2027-march-28-good-friday-easter-monday-whats-open/).

## What are the Western and Orthodox Easter dates from 2027 to 2036?

Every date is a Sunday. "Western" means the Gregorian rule used in the Church of England and USCCB tables; "Orthodox" is the Julian rule with the date converted to the Gregorian calendar.

| Year | Western Easter | Orthodox Easter | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 | March 28 | May 2 | 35 days |
| 2028 | April 16 | April 16 | Same day |
| 2029 | April 1 | April 8 | 7 days |
| 2030 | April 21 | April 28 | 7 days |
| 2031 | April 13 | April 13 | Same day |
| 2032 | March 28 | May 2 | 35 days |
| 2033 | April 17 | April 24 | 7 days |
| 2034 | April 9 | April 9 | Same day |
| 2035 | March 25 | April 29 | 35 days |
| 2036 | April 13 | April 20 | 7 days |

The table is computed by Fresh Calendars from the rule. The Western dates for 2027 to 2035 match the Church of England's table of Easter dates, and 2036 is not in that table, so it is computed only. The Orthodox dates for 2027, 2028 and 2029 match the ACROD list of movable feasts; the Orthodox dates for 2030 to 2036 are computed only, because we could not reach an official table for those years.

In the ten years shown, the dates match three times, the gap is 7 days four times and 35 days three times. ## How is the date of Easter set?

The U.S. Naval Observatory (USNO) gives the rule in its page "The Date of Easter." The vernal equinox is fixed at March 21, and the ecclesiastical full moon is the 14th day of a tabular lunation (new moon). Easter is the first Sunday following the first ecclesiastical full moon that occurs on or after the vernal equinox. USNO says Easter can never occur before March 22 or later than April 25, and that the rule is only an approximate statement of the actual ecclesiastical rules.

The Orthodox dates in our table go past April 25, up to May 2, because they come from a different calendar, as the next section explains.

## Why does Orthodox Easter fall on a different day?

USNO says the Eastern churches determine Easter with the older Julian calendar method. Here is our explanation from the rule, not a statement from USNO: the Julian calendar's March 21 falls 13 days later than the Gregorian March 21 this century, and the Julian method also uses older lunar tables. The later equinox date and the different moon tables can push the full moon, and so the following Sunday, to another week. That gives the 7-day and 35-day gaps in the table, and the years where the two methods land on the same Sunday.

## Will Western and Orthodox Easter ever be on one date?

Not by an agreed rule yet. Vatican News reported that Pope Leo and Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew, in a joint declaration of Nov. 29, 2025, noted that in 2025 all Christians celebrated Easter on the same day, on the 1,700th anniversary of the Council of Nicaea. No common date has been fixed.
