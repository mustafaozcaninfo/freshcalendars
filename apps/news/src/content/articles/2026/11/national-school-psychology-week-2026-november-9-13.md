National School Psychology Week 2026 ran Nov. 9-13, Monday to Friday, with the theme "Inspire Connection," according to the National Association of School Psychologists (NASP). On its National School Psychology Week (NSPW) page NASP wrote that it "and schools across the United States will celebrate" the week. The 2027 week is Nov. 1-5.

## National School Psychology Week 2026 dates and theme

NASP describes the theme this way: "Inspire Connection" highlights "how school psychologists build meaningful relationships that support students, strengthen collaboration, and transform school communities." The South Carolina Association of School Psychologists (SCASP), a state group, lists the same week, "November 9-13, 2026," and says the theme "celebrates the relationships they build with students, families and educators."

| Item | 2026 detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Dates | Mon., Nov. 9 to Fri., Nov. 13 | NASP, SCASP |
| Theme | Inspire Connection | NASP, SCASP |
| Next year | Nov. 1-5, 2027 | NASP |
| Year after | Nov. 13-17, 2028 | NASP |

The weekdays are our calendar check: Nov. 8, 2026, is a Sunday, so the week starts the next day.

## What was on each day of National School Psychology Week?

<p class="days-live" data-days-until="2026" hidden></p>

SCASP lists one question for each day and says NASP pairs each day with a question. The NASP page itself does not list them, so treat the wording as SCASP's. Wednesday was also Virtual Hill Day, when SCASP asked school psychologists to contact legislators about the workforce.

| Date | Day | Question of the day (SCASP) | Days from today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 9 | Monday | What connections inspire you? | Passed |
| Nov. 10 | Tuesday | Where can connection take you? | Passed |
| Nov. 11 | Wednesday | How will we inspire change? (Virtual Hill Day) | Passed |
| Nov. 12 | Thursday | What connects us? | Passed |
| Nov. 13 | Friday | Let's inspire connection! | Passed |

Neither NASP nor SCASP says anything about school closures during the week. A staff member planning next year's event can check the [printable school calendar](/printable-school-calendar.html) and the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html) against the district's own list.

## Which dates and themes are confirmed?

Three things come from NASP's own page: the 2026 dates, the "Inspire Connection" theme and the future dates. Some secondary sites show a different theme for 2026. NASP's page does not mention them, so the theme here is NASP's.

What the sources do not say:

- Daily prompts on NASP's own page. Only SCASP lists them.
- School closures during the week.

## When is National School Psychology Week in 2027 and 2028?

NASP lists three weeks under "Future NSPW Dates": Nov. 9-13, 2026, Nov. 1-5, 2027, and Nov. 13-17, 2028.
