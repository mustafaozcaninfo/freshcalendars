Two published solutions that print the board layout agree on it, so each clue names its corner. Answers wait in a marked spoiler section at the end.

## Clues for each board

**Top left board**

- Letters: five letters, two of them vowels, no repeated letter; it ends in a vowel.
- Meaning: to give off a bright light, or to polish something until it gleams.

**Top right board**

- Letters: five letters with two vowels, and one consonant that appears twice, once at each end.
- Meaning: a deck of illustrated cards used for fortune-telling.

**Bottom left board**

- Letters: five letters with one vowel that appears twice, in the second and last spots; two consonants sit between them.
- Meaning: the edge of something, or the grassy strip beside a road.

**Bottom right board**

- Letters: five letters with a single vowel and a Y at the end; no repeated letter.
- Meaning: tall and thin, with a loose, awkward build.
