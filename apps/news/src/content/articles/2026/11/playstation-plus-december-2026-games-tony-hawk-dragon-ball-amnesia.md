**The PlayStation Blog said on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026 that Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4, Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 and Amnesia: The Bunker are the PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for December, available from Tuesday, Dec. 1 to Monday, Jan. 4.** The post, by Adam Michel of Sony Interactive Entertainment, says the three games are "available to all PlayStation Plus members" in that window and adds that the lineup "may differ by region." The tables below add the dates and platforms, and a free printable calendar follows.

<p class="callout"><b>Time of day.</b> Sony has not given a time for Dec. 1 or Jan. 4.</p>

## What are the PS Plus December 2026 Monthly Games?

The post describes each game in a few lines. Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 offers "more skaters, new parks, gnarlier tricks," expanded Create-A-Skater and Create-A-Park tools, and custom goals you can share with friends. On PS5 it runs at native 4K and 60 frames per second in Fidelity Mode.

Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 lets players create a character, explore Conton City and train with iconic characters. It also has online multiplayer: player-versus-player battles and Raid Battles against gigantic foes. Amnesia: The Bunker is "a first-person horror game from the makers of Soma and Amnesia," set in a World War I bunker where French soldier Henri Clément has one bullet. The post says "no play-through is the same."

## Which platforms are the December games listed for?

The blog lists these platforms for each game. Amnesia: The Bunker is listed for PS4 only, and nothing beyond the listing is claimed here.

| Game | Platforms as listed | Playable from | Add by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 - Cross-Gen Edition | PS5, PS4 | Tue., Dec. 1 | Mon., Jan. 4 |
| Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 | PS5, PS4 | Tue., Dec. 1 | Mon., Jan. 4 |
| Amnesia: The Bunker | PS4 | Tue., Dec. 1 | Mon., Jan. 4 |

## What dates matter for PS Plus in December 2026?

Weekdays are computed from the dates. Counting both ends, the December window from Dec. 1 to Jan. 4 is 35 days.

| Date | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mon., Nov. 30 | Last day for members to add the November games: Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered, Monster Train 2 | PlayStation Blog, Nov. 25, 2026 |
| Tue., Dec. 1 | December Monthly Games become available | PlayStation Blog, Nov. 25, 2026 |
| Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | Last day of the December window | PlayStation Blog, Nov. 25, 2026 |

For comparison, in 2025 the December games ran "from Tuesday December 2 until Monday January 5," per the PlayStation Blog post of Nov. 26, 2025. They were LEGO Horizon Adventures, Killing Floor 3, The Outlast Trials, Synduality Echo of Ada and Neon White. That is last year's lineup, not this year's.

## What happens to the November PS Plus games?

The post says PlayStation Plus members have until Monday, Nov. 30 to add the November Monthly Games: Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered and Monster Train 2. Our [PS Plus November 2026 games story](/news/playstation-plus-november-2026-games-dead-island-2-tomb-raider-monster-train-2/) has their platforms and the November calendar.

## What does PS Plus cost for the December games?

The post names no tier and says "all PlayStation Plus members." Essential is the lowest tier, and these are the one-month US prices on playstation.com on Nov. 25, 2026.

| Tier | 1 month |
|---|---|
| Essential | $10.99 |
| Extra | $16.99 |
| Premium | $19.99 |

The same page lists a 12-month plan offer that it says ends Nov. 30, 2026.

## Is the December 2026 PS Plus Game Catalog announced?

Not yet. The Game Catalog is the Extra and Premium lineup, and Sony has not announced the December 2026 titles.
