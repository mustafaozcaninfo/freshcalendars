The Marine Corps celebrated its 251st birthday on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, according to a Nov. 6 news release from the Defense Commissary Agency (DeCA). DeCA wrote that the Corps marked "its 251st birthday Nov. 10." Nov. 10 is not on the federal holiday list of the Office of Personnel Management (OPM), and Veterans Day followed one day later, on Wednesday, Nov. 11.

## Date and 251st anniversary

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Birthday | Nov. 10, 2026, a Tuesday | DeCA, Nov. 6, 2026 |
| Anniversary | 251st | DeCA, Nov. 6, 2026 |
| Founding | Established by the Continental Congress in 1775 | DeCA, Nov. 6, 2026 |
| Federal holiday on Nov. 10 | Not on the 2026 list | OPM, Federal Holidays |
| Veterans Day | Wednesday, Nov. 11 (past) | OPM, Federal Holidays |

The DeCA page does not print the year. The year 2026 follows from the arithmetic: 1775 plus 251. The weekday is computed from the calendar. For the date itself, see [when Veterans Day falls](/when-is/veterans-day/) and the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## Why Nov. 10 is the Marine Corps birthday

DeCA's release says the Corps was "Established by the Continental Congress in 1775." The release gives Nov. 10 as the day the Corps marked its birthday. It does not explain why that day was chosen, and this story does not add a reason the source lacks.

## How Nov. 10 differs from Veterans Day on Nov. 11

The two days are separate. OPM's federal holiday list shows "Wednesday, November 11" as Veterans Day in 2026, and its 2026 section does not list Nov. 10. So the Marine Corps birthday is not a federal holiday, and the federal holiday came on Nov. 11. ## What was not confirmed for Nov. 10

- **Ceremony or message time.** The Commandant's birthday page and marines.mil blocked automated access when this story was written, so no message text, speaker or release time is listed.
- **Closures.** DeCA's release gives no commissary hours, closures or sales for Nov. 10. Check your local commissary or installation.
- **Birthday balls.** Local ball dates found in search results conflict with each other, so none is listed. Use the host's own page.
