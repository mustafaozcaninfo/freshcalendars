**Connections: Sports Edition #786, Wednesday's Nov. 18, 2026 board from The Athletic and New York Times (NYT) Games, is full of words that belong in two places at once.** Each color gets two tap-to-open hints, and the answers stay under the spoiler heading.

Sixteen tiles, four colors, and a handful of decoys that look like they belong elsewhere. The rules are on our [Connections page](/news/topic/nyt-connections/#how-it-works).

## Wednesday's hints, #786, Nov. 18, 2026

Read the board once for plain meanings, then again asking which tiles could be a person or a place.

- Hint 1 (yellow): Every tile is something you can do while afloat, and none of them needs a ball.
- Hint 2 (yellow): One of the four reads the same backward as forward.
- Hint 1 (green): Each tile can mean striking something hard, or finishing a job with force.
- Hint 2 (green): Three of the four would also fit in a workshop.
- Hint 1 (blue): The tiles are only the opening word of a longer name, and the full name belongs to a place with a flag on every green.
- Hint 2 (blue): One tile is a small, smooth stone.
- Hint 1 (purple): These are surnames, and the puzzle ties each to the same leadership role in one league.
- Hint 2 (purple): One surname is an everyday word for a piece of rock; the other three are not ordinary English words.

## Why Wednesday's board has so many decoys

One tile does double duty as a rock word and as a name, so a solver who gathers every stony word will end up a tile short and with a tile left over. Hold off on that idea until the other groups are fixed.

A second pull comes from the toolbox: several tiles sound like things in a workshop, though the color asks for an action. A plain verb can also mean a line of seats or a loud argument, which is why the water color takes a moment to see. ## Wednesday's answers, #786, Nov. 18, 2026 (spoilers)

Stop here if you still want to solve it. Beebom and Nerdschalk list the same four groups and sixteen entries.

- **Yellow, "Aquatic Sports Verbs":** Kayak, Row, Sail, Swim
- **Green, "Make a Big Hit":** Drill, Level, Nail, Rock
- **Blue, "First Words of Notable Golf Courses":** Augusta, Bethpage, Pebble, Torrey
- **Purple, "NHL Captains":** Benn, Celebrini, Stone, Suzuki

## Who each entry is

- **Benn, Celebrini, Stone, Suzuki:** the puzzle groups Jamie Benn, Macklin Celebrini, Mark Stone and Nick Suzuki as National Hockey League (NHL) captains.
- **Augusta, Bethpage, Pebble, Torrey:** Augusta National Golf Club, Bethpage Black at Bethpage State Park, Pebble Beach Golf Links and Torrey Pines Golf Course.
- **Drill, Level, Nail, Rock:** four ways to say a hard hit. To drill a ball is to hit it hard and flat, to level an opponent is to knock them down, to nail a shot is to hit it cleanly, and to rock someone is to stagger them with a blow.
- **Kayak, Row, Sail, Swim:** four ways to move across water. A kayaker paddles, a rower pulls oars, a sailor uses wind and a swimmer uses their own body.
