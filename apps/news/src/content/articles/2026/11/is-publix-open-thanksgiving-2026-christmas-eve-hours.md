**Publix stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, according to the Q&A in the Publix Super Markets newsroom, read Nov. 16, 2026.** Publix says its stores are closed three days a year: Thanksgiving, Christmas and Easter.

## Publix holiday hours from Thanksgiving 2026 to Easter 2027

Dates and weekdays are computed. The status column uses Publix's wording. Publix has not posted any early-close times; it says they are posted in its stores before the holidays.

| Date | Publix stores | What Publix says |
|---|---|---|
| Wed., Nov. 25, day before Thanksgiving | Not given | Hours not given in the Q&A |
| Thu., Nov. 26, Thanksgiving Day | Closed | "Publix stores are closed three days a year: Thanksgiving, Christmas and Easter." |
| Thu., Dec. 24, Christmas Eve | Early close | "We also close early on Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day" |
| Fri., Dec. 25, Christmas Day | Closed | Same sentence: closed on Christmas |
| Thu., Dec. 31, New Year's Eve | Early close | Times "are posted in our stores prior to the holidays" |
| Fri., Jan. 1, 2027, New Year's Day | Early close | Listed with Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve |
| Sun., March 28, 2027, Easter | Closed | Easter is one of the three closed days |

Source: Publix Super Markets Q&A, read Nov. 16, 2026. Easter 2027 is computed.

## Is Publix open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. Thursday, Nov. 26 is one of the three closed days. Publix says it has been closed on these three holidays since its founding, "in observance of the holiday and to allow our associates time with family and friends."

Wednesday, Nov. 25 is the last day to shop before the holiday, computed from the closure. Publix's Q&A gives no hours for it, so check your store. Publix's holiday meals page adds that all stores and pharmacies will be closed on Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26; neither page mentions Publix Liquors. [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26. For other chains, see [what is open on Thanksgiving 2026](/news/whats-open-thanksgiving-2026-grocery-stores-walmart-costco/).

## Is Publix open on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve?

Yes, with an early close. Publix says it closes early on Christmas Eve (Thursday, Dec. 24), New Year's Eve (Thursday, Dec. 31) and New Year's Day (Friday, Jan. 1, 2027). The times are posted in stores before the holidays and are not yet announced. Stores are closed on Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25. Other retailers' hours are in the [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2026 store hours](/news/christmas-eve-christmas-day-2026-store-hours/) list, and the dates sit on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## Is Publix open on Easter 2027?

No. Easter is the third closed day. Easter 2027 is Sunday, March 28, a date computed from the Easter rule; see [when is Easter](/when-is/easter/).

## When to order a Publix holiday meal

Publix's holiday meals page says "Order at least 48 hours in advance" and "We need at least 48 hours before pickup." It also says to "try to order at least three days before your holiday feast." Pickup is at the Deli: "Just head to the Deli and ask for your holiday meal." The page posts no dates or prices.

Because stores are closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas, pickup on those days is not possible (computed from the closure). The order-by days below are computed from the 48-hour rule, at the same time of day as pickup.

| Pickup day | Order by (48 hours) | Publix's three-day suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Wed., Nov. 25 | Mon., Nov. 23 | Sun., Nov. 22 |
| Thu., Dec. 24 | Tue., Dec. 22 | Mon., Dec. 21 |

Thanksgiving meal prices from other chains are in the [Thanksgiving meal deals story](/news/thanksgiving-meal-deals-2026-aldi-lidl-target-sams-club-prices-dates/); Publix has not posted prices on its ordering page.

## What Publix has not posted

Not yet announced: the early-close times for Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day, hours for Wednesday, Nov. 25, and meal prices. Whether Publix Liquors follows the same closure is not on either Publix page.
