**LinkedIn's Thursday set for Nov. 19, 2026 carries Pinpoint #933 and Crossclimb #933, numbered by Nerd's Chalk and Try Hard Guides; the answers wait in the spoiler section.** Hints come first.

## Pinpoint and Crossclimb hints for Thursday, Nov. 19

- **Pinpoint #933, first hint:** the five clues are House, Mind, Secret, Mic and Sesame, and Sesame is printed with a note calling it a magical phrase.
- **Pinpoint #933, second hint:** one short word can stand in front of every clue to make a phrase you have heard. Mic is short for microphone.
- **Pinpoint #933, third hint:** the category has five words, and the last has four letters.
- **Crossclimb #933, the ladder:** all seven words have four letters.
- **Crossclimb #933, the locked ends:** the top is how you think of your own identity, and the bottom is to look after someone or something.
- **Crossclimb #933, the middle:** five words sit between the ends, and the first move changes the last letter.

## Testing the Pinpoint #933 clues as phrases

Pinpoint rewards a quick test: guess a short partner word, then say it aloud with each clue in turn. A good guess makes all five clues sound like things people really say. Four of the clues are everyday nouns, but Sesame is the odd one, because on its own it is a seed. The note printed beside it tells you to hear it as a spoken phrase, and that tells you what kind of partner word to look for. If one clue sounds wrong after your guess, change the partner word, not the clue.

## Reading the Crossclimb #933 ladder without the words

Seven words of four letters, six moves, one letter changed per move, positions counted from the left, 1 to 4. No word appears in this section.

| Move | Letter position that changes |
|---|---|
| Top row to rung 1 | 4 |
| Rung 1 to rung 2 | 1 |
| Rung 2 to rung 3 | 2 |
| Rung 3 to rung 4 | 3 |
| Rung 4 to rung 5 | 4 |
| Rung 5 to bottom row | 2 |

Every position is visited. Positions 2 and 4 change twice, positions 1 and 3 once. The first four moves sweep across the word, starting at the last letter and then running 1, 2, 3, so the fourth move closes the first pass, and the last two moves return to positions 4 and 2.

## How far apart the Crossclimb #933 locked ends sit

The two locked words differ in all four positions, so no letter sits in the same place in both. That is why the ladder needs six moves for four differences: two positions are changed a second time on the way.

## Starting points for Queens #933, Tango #773, Zip #612 and Mini Sudoku #465

**Queens #933.** Find two color regions that between them fill only two rows or two columns; those lines can hold no crown from any other region.

**Tango #773.** Each row holds three of each symbol, so a row that already shows one symbol three times takes the other symbol in every open cell.

**Zip #612.** Draw the path backward from the highest number as well as forward from the lowest, then join the two ends.

**Mini Sudoku #465.** Pick a digit and look at where it already sits; it can only go in the boxes and lines that still lack it.
