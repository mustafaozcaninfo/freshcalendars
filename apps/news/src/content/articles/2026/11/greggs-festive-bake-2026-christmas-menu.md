**Greggs says its 2026 Christmas menu is "in shop from 22nd October 2026", a Thursday, according to the bakery chain's "Fill your festive feeds" page.** The page lists six items, led by a Festive Bake with a new recipe. It gives no prices, so this story sticks to what Greggs has said and marks what it has not.

## When is the Greggs Festive Bake back in 2026?

Greggs' page says: "Pop into your nearest Greggs from 22nd October and discover this year's Christmas menu." The page prints no publication date; the year comes from its own wording, "22nd October 2026". Two newsrooms give the same day. Brit Brief reported on 14 October 2026 that the pastry "will land in stores nationwide from Thursday, October 22". The Times and Star in Cumbria wrote that the line-up "arrives in Cumbria's Greggs branches on Thursday, October 22, as well as nationwide."

## What is on the Greggs Christmas menu 2026?

Greggs names six items. The table keeps to what its page says about each one.

| Item | What is in it, per Greggs |
|---|---|
| Festive Bake | Cooked chicken, smoked ham hock and sweetcure bacon with soft cheese |
| Pepperoni Pizza with Hot Honey | Focaccia-style base, jalapeños, red onion, hot honey sauce |
| Pigs in Blankets | A pot of six pork sausages wrapped in smoked streaky bacon |
| Festive Flatbread | Greggs lists no filling |
| Festive Feast Baguette | Greggs lists no filling |
| Pigs Under Blankets Toastie | Greggs lists no filling |

The Pigs in Blankets pot and the Pigs Under Blankets Toastie are two separate items on the page, so a search for the toastie lands on the sixth row, not the side.

## What is new in the Festive Bake recipe?

Greggs describes "our new and improved recipe" as one that "features cooked chicken, smoked ham hock and sweetcure bacon with soft cheese." The page also lists pork, sage and onion stuffing, sage and cranberry sauce, glazed puff pastry and a crumb topping. Greggs does not say what changed from the last recipe, and we do not rate how it tastes.

## How early is the 2026 launch compared with 2025?

The Manc reported on 29 October 2025 that the 2025 menu would be available "from 6 November", and Food Bible, the same day, wrote of "Thursday 6 November". Both days are Thursdays.

| Year | Launch date | Weekday | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 6 November | Thursday | The Manc and Food Bible, 29 October 2025 |
| 2026 | 22 October | Thursday | Greggs; Brit Brief and Times and Star agree |

Counting the calendar, 22 October 2026 is 15 days before 6 November, the 2025 launch day.
