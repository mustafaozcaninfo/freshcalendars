## When could a Texans game time still change?

The NFL's flexible scheduling procedures let it move games into Sunday Night Football and Monday Night Football (MNF) slots late in the season. Per the procedures, Sunday Night Football "Flex may be used up to twice in Weeks 5-10 and in any week of Weeks 11-17." Decisions for Weeks 5-13 come "no later than 12 days in advance of the game," and for Weeks 14-17 the decision "generally comes no later than 6 days before the game." Monday night "Flex may be used in any of Weeks 12-17," with the same 12-day rule. Thursday night "Flex may be used up to twice in Weeks 13-17," with the decision "no later than 21 days prior to the game." Week 11's Thursday game is not flex-eligible.
