**Today's Blossom from Merriam-Webster, for Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, puts S in the center with the petals A, C, E, H, L and T.** Solver sites list at least 16 words that use all seven letters and that they agree on, from 7 to 10 letters long. Below are the rules, words to try and hints for the pangrams, which are revealed in a marked spoiler section at the end.

## Today's letters

- **Center:** S (every word must include it)
- **Petals:** A, C, E, H, L, T

## How Blossom works

You get 12 words to score as many points as you can. Each word must be at least four letters long and include the center letter, and letters can be used more than once. Proper nouns, hyphenated words and offensive words do not count. Longer words score more, a pangram, a word that uses all seven letters, earns 7 extra points, and each use of the highlighted bonus petal earns 5 more, so check which petal is lit before each word.

## Words to try

With S in the middle and A, C, E, H, L and T around it, several short words fall out quickly: Seat, Cast, Last, Least and Heals. Six-letter words such as Castle and Tassel score more, and Tassel uses the center letter twice. Tastes is another that repeats S.

## Pangram hints

- **Pangram 1** (10 letters): the longest of the set, with S used three times.
- **Pangrams 2 to 4** (9 letters): three words, ending in -ates, -eas and -ach.
- **Pangrams 5 to 13** (8 letters): nine words, starting with A, C, C, C, H, H, L, S and S.
- **Pangrams 14 to 16** (7 letters): three words, starting with C, L and S.

- **Alcahest**, **Chattels**, **Chelates**, **Clatches**, **Hatchels**, **Hellcats**, **Latchets**, **Satchels**, **Slatches** (8 letters)

All use S, A, C, E, H, L and T, so each earns the 7-point pangram bonus.
