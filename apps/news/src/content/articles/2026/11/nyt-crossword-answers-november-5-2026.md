## The four rebus squares in Thursday's grid

A rebus square holds several letters instead of one. Here, four squares hold three letters each, so an entry that passes through one has more letters than squares. Every count in the hints below is a count of squares.

| Square | Across entry | Down entry |
|---|---|---|
| Row 3, column 6 | 17-Across, sixth of 11 squares | 5-Down, third of 4 squares |
| Row 5, column 13 | 26-Across, seventh of 9 squares | 11-Down, fifth of 8 squares |
| Row 11, column 5 | 55-Across, fifth of 9 squares | 56-Down, first of 5 squares |
| Row 13, column 10 | 64-Across, sixth of 11 squares | 61-Down, second of 4 squares |

What links the four is a pair of alphabets. According to the clue for 59-Across, the circled letters in the Down answers belong to one alphabet, and the Across answers use another. So each square reads as a three-letter letter name in one direction and as a single letter in the other. The clue for 39-Across says it is a hint for four answers, and these are the four.

To enter a rebus, most crossword apps have a rebus key or a mode that lets one square take several letters. Look in your app's help for the exact control. On paper, write the small letters across the square.

## The five question-mark clues, explained

Five clues end in a question mark. None of the notes below names an answer.

- **46-Across, "Political leader?"** Read "leader" as the part that comes first in a longer word, not as a head of state. Three letters.
- **47-Across, "Gray matter?"** Here "Gray" is a surname, not a color, and the answer is a plural kind of poem. Four letters.
- **68-Across, "Vehicle for taking a trip?"** The trip is not a journey. The vehicle is a three-letter drug abbreviation.
- **37-Down, "Unlikely buyer?"** Picture a shopper who doubts every pitch. The answer is a seven-letter word for that person.
- **66-Down, "Letter indicating ownership?"** The letter is the one added after an apostrophe to show possession, and the answer spells that letter out in full.
