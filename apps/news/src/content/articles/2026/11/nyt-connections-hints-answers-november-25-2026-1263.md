## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Picture a fast road built for cars; each tile is something you would see on it.
- Hint 2: The entries have four, four, six and eight letters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Trace the outline of each tile with a finger: it dips down and curves back up, in all four cases.
- Hint 2: The entries have eight, eight, nine and ten letters.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: Read a product listing for something that lies on a floor; each tile is a line on its spec sheet.
- Hint 2: The entries have four, five, eight and ten letters.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Every tile is short by one letter at the front, and the missing letter completes a familiar kind of pet.
- Hint 2: The entries have five, five, five and seven letters.

## Solving tips

Oberman is not a word most solvers know on its own, which makes it the best tile to examine first. Ask what single letter could stand in front of it.
