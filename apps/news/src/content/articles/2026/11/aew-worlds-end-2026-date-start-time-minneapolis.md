**All Elite Wrestling (AEW) lists Worlds End for Saturday, Dec. 19, 2026, at Target Center in Minneapolis, at 6:00 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. Pacific Time (PT), according to AEW's events page.** AEW has not announced the card, the price, the platforms or a pre-show time. Below: every AEW and Ring of Honor (ROH) show listed from Nov. 21 to Jan. 6, 2027, with Eastern and Pacific conversions, and a printable watch guide.

## When is AEW Worlds End 2026 and what time does it start?

AEW's events page lists "December 19, 2026 6:00pm CT" for "AEW: Worlds End" at Target Center in Minneapolis, with tickets sold through AXS. That is 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT by our conversion; U.S. clocks are on standard time in December. The page gives one time and does not separate a main-card or pre-show start, so the pay-per-view (PPV) start is not yet announced.

The same page says AEW is bringing Worlds End to the Twin Cities for the first time, and calls it the fourth annual pay-per-view. Wrestling Inc. reported on Sept. 27, 2026 that "the fourth edition of Worlds End will take place on December 19, 2026, at the Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota."

## How can you watch Worlds End?

AEW has not announced the platforms or the price for Worlds End. The last AEW pay-per-view gives a reference only. Full Gear took place Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026 at Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, with the main card "kicking off at 8 p.m. ET/5 p.m. PT on HBO Max PPV," according to AEW's preview. The same preview says Full Gear was "available to order on HBO Max in the United States" and "also available on Amazon Prime, MyAEW, YouTube, and other streaming and pay-per-view platforms." AEW's results page lists AEW World Champion Will Ospreay defeating Kyle Fletcher in the main event.
