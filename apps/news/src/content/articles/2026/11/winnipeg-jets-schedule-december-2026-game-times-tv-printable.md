**The Winnipeg Jets play 13 games in December 2026, five of them at Canada Life Centre, according to the National Hockey League (NHL) club schedule.** The month opens Wednesday, Dec. 2, at 8:30 p.m. Central Time (CT), which is 9:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), at the Utah Mammoth, and ends Thursday, Dec. 31, at the Calgary Flames.

The source is the NHL's club schedule feed for Winnipeg, read Nov. 12, 2026. It gives every start in UTC, so we converted the times to CT, which is Winnipeg time, and ET, and worked out the weekdays. Canadian TV is the first channel column and the U.S. listing the second. The feed lists broadcasters as of that date, and they can change. A one-page printable follows the table.

## Jets December 2026 game times and TV channels

Every game lists TSN3 or a Sportsnet channel in Canada, by our count from the feed: TSN3 for eight games, Sportsnet West for Dec. 5, 19 and 26, Sportsnet for Dec. 12 and SN+ for Dec. 28. The Dec. 31 game lists both TSN3 and Sportsnet West. In the U.S. column, the market is the one the feed names, "Regional channel" is a local or team outlet the feed lists under a short code, and "None listed" means the feed shows no U.S. broadcaster.

## How many Jets games are at Canada Life Centre in December 2026?

Five: Dec. 8, 10, 19, 20 and 28. Our own count from the feed: eight away games, so the Jets are on the road for more of the month than at home. The first two home games, against Philadelphia and Chicago, come right after the opening trip.

The Dallas Stars visit twice in two days, Saturday, Dec. 19, at 6 p.m. CT (7 p.m. ET) on Sportsnet West and Sunday, Dec. 20, at 4 p.m. CT (5 p.m. ET) on TSN3. The feed lists Prime Video for both in the U.S. The San Jose Sharks close the home schedule on Monday, Dec. 28, at 6 p.m. CT, listed on SN+.

## Jets road games in December 2026

These are our own counts from the feed:

- **Opening trip, Dec. 2 to Dec. 6:** three games in five days at the Utah Mammoth (Delta Center), the Vegas Golden Knights (T-Mobile Arena) and the Anaheim Ducks (Honda Center), the last two on consecutive days.
- **Twice at Minnesota:** Sat., Dec. 12, at 2:30 p.m. CT (3:30 p.m. ET) and Sat., Dec. 26, at 6 p.m. CT (7 p.m. ET), both at Grand Casino Arena. The Minnesota side is in our [Wild December schedule](/news/minnesota-wild-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/).
- **Two more in the central U.S.:** Dec. 15 at St. Louis (Enterprise Center) and Dec. 22 at Nashville (Bridgestone Arena), each at 7 p.m. CT.
- **No game:** Dec. 23 to Dec. 25.

## Where are the Jets on New Year's Eve 2026?

In Calgary. The Jets play the Flames at Scotiabank Saddledome on Thursday, Dec. 31, at 7 p.m. CT (8 p.m. ET), the month's last game. The feed lists TSN3 and Sportsnet West in Canada and no U.S. broadcaster. The Flames' view of the same night is in their [December schedule](/news/calgary-flames-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/), and the [Oilers December schedule](/news/edmonton-oilers-schedule-december-2026-game-times-tv-printable/) covers the other Canadian team in the Prairies.
