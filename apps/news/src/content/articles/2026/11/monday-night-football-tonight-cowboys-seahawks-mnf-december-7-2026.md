**The Dallas Cowboys play the Seattle Seahawks on Monday Night Football (MNF), Monday, Dec. 7, 2026, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on ESPN, according to the National Football League (NFL) Week 13 schedule on NFL.com.** The Seahawks list the game at Lumen Field in Seattle as "5:15 PM PST", and the Cowboys list it as "7:15 PM CST".

Below are the kickoff in every U.S. zone and in the United Kingdom, how to watch, the flex rule, the standings, the last meeting and the teams' Week 12 games. A one-page watch guide is at the end.

## What time is Cowboys at Seahawks in my time zone?

Clocks are on standard time, so the 8:15 p.m. ET kickoff is 8:15 p.m. Eastern Standard Time. The two teams' own listings match NFL.com: 5:15 p.m. Pacific for Seattle and 7:15 p.m. Central for Dallas. We worked out the other zones from those listings.

|---|---|
| Eastern | 8:15 p.m. |
| Central | 7:15 p.m. |
| Mountain | 6:15 p.m. |
| Pacific | 5:15 p.m. |
| Alaska | 4:15 p.m. |
| Hawaii | 3:15 p.m. |
| United Kingdom (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT) | 1:15 a.m., Tue., Dec. 8 |

## What channel carries Cowboys at Seahawks?

NFL.com, the Seahawks and the Cowboys all list ESPN. The Cowboys' schedule adds radio on 105.3 The Fan. The official schedules do not list anything else for television, so we name only ESPN.

## Could the Dec. 7 game still move from Monday night?

The NFL's flexible scheduling procedures say the tool for Monday Night Football "may be used any week in Weeks 12-17" and that the league "will decide and announce no later than 12 days in advance of the game." Counting back 12 days from Dec. 7 gives Wednesday, Nov. 25. As of Nov. 25, NFL.com, the Seahawks and the Cowboys still list the game on Monday at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN, and no change had been announced.

## Where do the Cowboys and Seahawks stand before the game?

NFL.com's standings, read on Nov. 25 before Week 12, show both teams second in their National Football Conference (NFC) divisions.

| Team | Record | Division place |
|---|---|---|
| Cowboys | 5-6 | Second in NFC East; Giants lead at 6-3-1 |
| Seahawks | 6-4 | Second in NFC West; 49ers lead at 9-1, Rams also 6-4 |

Both teams play in Week 12 first, so these records will change.

The Cowboys host the Eagles on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, at 4:30 p.m. ET on FOX at AT&T Stadium, per NFL.com. The Seahawks visit the 49ers on Sunday, Nov. 29, at 4:25 p.m. ET on FOX at Levi's Stadium. No results were available when we checked. The last regular-season meeting was Thursday, Nov. 30, 2023, when the Cowboys beat the Seahawks 41-35 at AT&T Stadium, per the Seahawks' 2023 schedule.
