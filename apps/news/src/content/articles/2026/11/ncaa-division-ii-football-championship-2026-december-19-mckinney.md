**The National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division II football championship is Saturday, Dec. 19, 2026, at McKinney Independent School District (ISD) Stadium in McKinney, Texas, with kickoff at 3 p.m. Central Time, or 4 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), according to the NCAA and Visit McKinney.** The second round is Saturday, Nov. 28, and the quarterfinals and semifinals on Dec. 5 and Dec. 12 are listed as to be announced.

All eight second-round kickoffs are below in four U.S. zones, with a round-by-round date table, the other small-college title games and a one-page printable.

## Division II football championship 2026: dates by round

The NCAA's 2026 Division II bracket shows a first round that ended Nov. 21 (for example Ferris St. 45, Truman St. 14, and Slippery Rock 59, Charleston (WV) 21). It lists the rest as below.

| Round | Date | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Second round | Sat., Nov. 28 | Eight games, times below |
| Quarterfinals | Sat., Dec. 5 | Matchups and times to be announced |
| Semifinals | Sat., Dec. 12 | Matchups and times to be announced |
| Championship | Sat., Dec. 19 | McKinney ISD Stadium, 4 p.m. ET |

## Where, when and on what channel is the Division II final?

NCAA.org lists the game for December 19, 2026, at McKinney ISD Stadium in McKinney, Texas. Visit McKinney says "Kickoff at 3 p.m." and that tickets go "Through NCAA"; the NCAA bracket's 9:00 PM UTC matches 4 p.m. ET. No ticket price is posted in the sources read.

ESPN's Nov. 10, 2026 release lists Division II football on ESPN+ and ESPNU from Nov. 21 to Dec. 19. The channel for the final itself is not yet announced.

## Who won the 2025 Division II championship?

Ferris State beat Harding 42-21 on Dec. 20, 2025, in McKinney, per Ferris State University and the Associated Press (AP). Both finalists are in the 2026 second round: Ferris St. plays Findlay and Harding plays Northwest Mo. St.

## Three small-college title games on the calendar

| Level | Date | Site | Kickoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCAA Division II | Sat., Dec. 19, 2026 | McKinney ISD Stadium, McKinney, Texas | 4 p.m. ET (3 p.m. CT) |
| National Association of Intercollegiate Athletics (NAIA) | Sat., Dec. 19, 2026 | Crowley ISD Multipurpose Stadium, Fort Worth, Texas | Not yet announced |
| NCAA Division III (Stagg Bowl) | Sun., Jan. 3, 2027 | Salem Stadium, Salem, Va. | Not yet announced |

The NAIA says its game "will air on the NAIA family of broadcast networks at www.naia.org/watch." For Division III, ESPN's release lists "ESPN+/ESPNU*/ESPN (*Tape Delay)" from Nov. 21 to Jan. 3, with no channel yet set for the Stagg Bowl. Dates for the earlier Division III rounds are not confirmed in the sources read.

Last season, UW-River Falls beat North Central (Ill.) 24-14 in the Stagg Bowl on Jan. 4, 2026, at Tom Benson Hall of Fame Stadium in Canton, Ohio, per the AP and North Central College. The 2026 season's game moves to Salem.
