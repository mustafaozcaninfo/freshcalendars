**UFC Fight Night: Procházka vs. Stirling, the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) card in Qatar, is Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, with the main card at 1 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and the prelims at 10 a.m. ET, according to Paramount+.** The card is in Doha, Qatar, and streams live exclusively on Paramount+, which says there is no pay-per-view. We read the pages on Nov. 19; below are the times in nine zones and all 11 bouts ESPN lists.

## When the Doha card starts in your time zone

Paramount+ gives times in ET and Pacific Time (PT). ESPN's fight center shows the same start times, prelims at 15:00 and main card at 18:00 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). The other zones are our conversion for Nov. 21, when US clocks are on standard time and the United Kingdom is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). Qatar is three hours ahead of UTC all year, and in eastern Australia the card falls on Sunday, Nov. 22.

| Zone, Sat., Nov. 21 | Prelims | Main card |
|---|---|---|
| ET | 10:00 a.m. | 1:00 p.m. |
| Central Time (CT) | 9:00 a.m. | 12:00 p.m. |
| Mountain Time (MT) | 8:00 a.m. | 11:00 a.m. |
| PT | 7:00 a.m. | 10:00 a.m. |
| Alaska | 6:00 a.m. | 9:00 a.m. |
| Hawaii | 5:00 a.m. | 8:00 a.m. |
| United Kingdom (GMT) | 3:00 p.m. | 6:00 p.m. |
| Doha | 6:00 p.m. | 9:00 p.m. |
| Sydney (Sun., Nov. 22) | 2:00 a.m. | 5:00 a.m. |

Paramount+ lists "Main Card: 1 PM ET/10 AM PT" and "Prelims: 10 AM ET/7 AM PT". Sydney is on Australian Eastern Daylight Time, 11 hours ahead of UTC. The main event comes last on the main card; no clock time for its walkout has been announced.

## Full Prochazka vs. Stirling fight card

Paramount+ describes the main event as a light heavyweight title bout between No. 2-ranked contender Jiří Procházka (32-6-1) and Navajo Stirling (11-0-0), the No. 5 division contender. The bouts and weight classes below are as ESPN lists them on Nov. 19, and the order can change.

| Segment | Bout | Weight class |
|---|---|---|
| Main card | Jiří Procházka vs. Navajo Stirling (main event) | Light heavyweight |
| Main card | Aljamain Sterling vs. Kevin Vallejos | Featherweight |
| Main card | Dan Hooker vs. Brian Ortega | Lightweight |
| Main card | Kyoji Horiguchi vs. Asu Almabayev | Flyweight |
| Main card | Jared Cannonier vs. Ikram Aliskerov | Middleweight |
| Prelims | Aleksandre Topuria vs. Santiago Luna | Bantamweight |
| Prelims | Shamil Gaziev vs. Tallison Teixeira | Heavyweight |
| Prelims | Amir Albazi vs. Alessandro Costa | Flyweight |
| Prelims | Jake Matthews vs. Tahir Abdullayev | Welterweight |
| Prelims | Nikolas Motta vs. Dom Mar Fan | Lightweight |
| Prelims | Diyar Nurgozhay vs. Alik Lorenz | Light heavyweight |

Fight cards change, so check ESPN and Paramount+ on fight day. This story gives no odds or predictions.

## How do you watch UFC Qatar in the US?

Paramount+ says the event streams live Saturday, Nov. 21, exclusively on Paramount+, with no pay-per-view. A UK broadcaster, an early prelims segment and a CBS simulcast are not listed on the pages we read.
