## Which letter pairs never occur today?

Two letters from one side may not sit side by side, so 108 ordered pairs remain possible. Nine of them never occur in the list: V then D, F or M; T then V, N or D; F then N or D; and L then R. Five more occur in exactly one word: V then T, V then R, D then F, F then V and M then T.

The V is in four of the nine dead pairs, and the T, D and F are in three each. Any route that uses the V must keep a T away from its left and a D, F or M away from its right.

The longest word has 13 letters, and only one word reaches that length. No word covers the box: the widest reach 9 of the 12 letters, and 7 words tie at that figure. Chaining every pair of words that share a join letter and use all twelve letters gives 11 two-word routes.

| Join letter | Two-word routes |
|---|---|
| T | 5 |
| E | 6 |

Those routes total 16 to 19 letters: 5 at 16, 3 at 17, 1 at 18 and 2 at 19. They draw on 6 different first words and 8 different second words, and 48 words in the list have 9 letters.
