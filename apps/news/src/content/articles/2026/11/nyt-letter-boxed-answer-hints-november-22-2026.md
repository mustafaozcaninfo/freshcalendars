## What a par of 5 means next to a two-word route

The Times' route uses two words, three below the par of 5. Counting every pair of dictionary words that chain on a shared letter and use all twelve letters gives 25 routes. Their total lengths are spread like this: 13 letters, 1 route; 14, 5 routes; 15, 6; 16, 7; 17, 4; 18, 1; and 21, 1 route. The Times' route has 13 letters in all, so it is the shortest of the 25 and the only route of that length.

| Join letter | Two-word routes |
|---|---|
| S | 10 |
| L | 8 |
| T | 4 |
| R | 3 |

Four join letters carry all 25 routes. They draw on 14 different first words and 17 different second words, and 25 routes means some words appear in more than one.

## Twelve dead pairs and the letter that causes most of them

Two letters of one side never sit together, which bars 24 ordered pairs. Of the 108 pairs left, 12 never occur in the dictionary: C then V, N or W; R then W; T then V; V then C, T, N or W; S then R; and W then T or V. Three others occur in exactly one word: T then N, I then U and S then N.

The V is in 7 of the 12 dead pairs and the W in 5. A route through the V cannot put a C, T or W before it or a C, T, N or W after it, and the W has only 83 words to draw on.

Three words reach 15 letters, the longest in the dictionary, with 7, 8 and 9 different letters. A two-word route cannot use any of them: its longest first word has 12 letters and its longest second word has 10. Another 12 words run to 13 or 14 letters.

## Five hints, light to strong

1. The Times' route is two words, three fewer than the par of 5.
2. The first word has 8 letters and the second has 5 letters, picked from 110 words of the first length and 308 of the second.
3. The first word opens on the V/U/S side and closes on the T/I/A side. The second opens on that T/I/A side and closes on the C/L/R side.
4. The V is in the first word and the W is in the second.
5. The first word starts with V and ends in T. The second starts with T and ends in L.
