**Quordle #1750 from Merriam-Webster is the Monday, Nov. 9, 2026 puzzle.** Each of the four boards gets a clue below, and none of the words repeats a letter. The answers sit at the end, under a spoiler warning.

Today's set has no doubled tile to plan around; the rules are on our [Quordle page](/news/topic/quordle/#how-it-works). Sunday's set is on the [Quordle #1749 page](/news/quordle-hints-answers-november-8-2026-1749/).

## Clues for the four boards

Boards are named by position, the same order as the answer list: top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right.

**Top left**

- Letters: five letters, and the first one is a vowel.
- Meaning: still not satisfied, as a need or a target that was never reached.

**Top right**

- Letters: five letters with the 19th letter of the alphabet in front and the 23rd at the end.
- Meaning: a tough cord of tissue in the body; it also stands for muscle and strength.

**Bottom left**

- Letters: five letters that begin with the 14th letter and end on the 18th.
- Meaning: the lowest point, the worst moment after a long slide.

**Bottom right**

- Letters: five letters with three vowels, and the last letter is one of them.
- Meaning: the style everyone is following, as in "in ___" for clothes.

## What the four boards share

Twenty tiles hold 14 different letters, because no word repeats one. Counting different letters two boards share, with none named:

| Boards | Letters in common |
|---|---|
| Top left and top right | 2 |
| Top left and bottom left | 1 |
| Top left and bottom right | 2 |
| Top right and bottom left | 2 |
| Top right and bottom right | 1 |
| Bottom left and bottom right | 0 |

Three of the words hold two vowels from A, E, I, O and U, and the bottom right holds three. Two letters appear in three boards, two more appear in exactly two, and ten appear in just one. The top left is the only word that opens with a vowel. The bottom pair share nothing, so a good guess for one of them tells you little about the other. Start with letters that the top two boards have in common.

## Today's Quordle answers (spoilers)

Look away now if you want to keep playing.

- **Top left: Unmet**
- **Top right: Sinew**
- **Bottom left: Nadir**
- **Bottom right: Vogue**
