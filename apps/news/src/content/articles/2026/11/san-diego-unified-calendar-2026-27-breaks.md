**San Diego Unified School District (SDUSD) students have no school from Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, from Monday, Dec. 21 to Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, and from Monday, March 29 to Friday, April 2, 2027**, by the district's "2026-27 Academic Calendar." The grid marks Wednesday, May 26, 2027 as the last day of school, so June has no instruction days.

The calendar is a one-page PDF with a color grid. Its header calls it final and says the Board of Education adopted it June 24, 2025. The key has four entries: "H Holiday," "F/L First/Last Day of School," "N/I Non-Instructional Day (No School)" and "Instruction Day." The grid does not print holiday names, so we name a holiday only as the date of a federal or state holiday. "Winter break" and "spring break" are our words for the no-school blocks the grid marks, not district labels.

## How long is San Diego Unified winter break?

The grid marks every weekday from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 as H or N/I. That is 11 weekdays: Dec. 21-25, Dec. 28 to Jan. 1 and Jan. 4. Students are away from class from Saturday, Dec. 19 to Monday, Jan. 4, 17 days with the weekends, and classes resume Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027. Our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/) compares other districts.

## When is San Diego Unified spring break in 2027?

Monday, March 29 to Friday, April 2, 2027, five weekdays, all marked N/I. Wednesday, March 31 is the date of Cesar Chavez Day, and the grid marks it like the rest of the week, so the holiday adds no extra day. Classes resume Monday, April 5. Other districts' spring weeks are in our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/).

## Is there a four-day weekend in February?

Yes. Friday, Feb. 12, 2027 and Monday, Feb. 15 are both marked H. Feb. 12 is the date of Lincoln's Birthday, a California school holiday, and Feb. 15 is the date of Presidents Day. With the weekend, school is out Friday through Monday.

## When is the last day of school at San Diego Unified in 2027?

Wednesday, May 26, 2027, marked L in the grid. Monday, May 31 is a holiday. The grid's June total is 0 instruction days, and it marks Friday, June 18 as H. The first day was Monday, Aug. 10, 2026.

## Does every San Diego school use this calendar?

This is the district's traditional calendar: the PDF sits in a folder named Traditional on the district's [Academic Calendars page](https://www.sandiegounified.org/academics/academic_calendars). The calendar does not say which schools follow it, so check your school.
