**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, sets P in the center with C, E, I, K, L and S around it.** Three published solution pages agree on four pangrams, all from the same seven letters. Letter checks and graded hints come first.

## Monday's letters and the center P

- **Center:** P
- **Petals:** C, E, I, K, L, S

Only two of the seven letters are vowels, E and I; the other five are consonants. Sunday's flower, centered on B, is on the [Nov. 22 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-22-2026/). How petals, pangrams and scoring work is on our [Blossom page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works); today every find has to include the P.

## Four pangrams from one set of letters

Merriam-Webster shows no official word list, so these counts come from the three solution pages that agree.

| Letters | Pangrams | Letters used twice |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 3 | none |
| 8 | 1 | one |

A script comparing the four words shows that the three 7-letter ones use the same seven letters, in a different order each time. Two of them differ only by two neighbors swapped near the end. The 8-letter word is a 7-letter one with an S added, and the S can be placed at either end to reach a different 7-letter relative.

## Check a find against the seven letters

Tick P first. A word without it never counts, however many petals it covers.

| Tick | Letter | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | P | Center |
| 2 | C | Petal |
| 3 | E | Petal |
| 4 | I | Petal |
| 5 | K | Petal |
| 6 | L | Petal |
| 7 | S | Petal |

Seven ticks make a pangram. A find with six petal ticks and no P is rejected.

## Pangram hints, light to heavy

- **Hint 1:** Four pangrams are listed.
- **Hint 2:** Three of them have seven letters, so each letter appears exactly once.
- **Hint 3:** One pangram has eight letters, and the extra letter is a petal.
- **Hint 4:** Two of the 7-letter pangrams are the same word with two letters swapped.
- **Hint 5:** Take the eight-letter pangram and remove its first or last letter: either way you land on a 7-letter pangram.

## Why the extra S matters

The petal that repeats is the one that turns a 7-letter word into the 8-letter one, and it does so in two ways. Add S in front of one 7-letter pangram, or after another, and both routes give the same 8-letter word. That makes it the only pangram you can reach from two different directions.

### Pangram

The solution pages list these four. Each holds P, C, E, I, K, L and S at least once.

- **Pickels** (7 letters)
- **Pickles** (7 letters)
- **Spickle** (7 letters)
- **Spickles** (8 letters)

Pickels and Pickles are anagrams, differing only by the E and the L trading places. Spickle is Pickles with its S moved from the end to the front. Spickles adds an S to Spickle at the end, which is the same as an S at the front of Pickles. Pickels and Spickle are on the published lists; we do not define them. Spickles uses S twice, and the other three use each letter once.
