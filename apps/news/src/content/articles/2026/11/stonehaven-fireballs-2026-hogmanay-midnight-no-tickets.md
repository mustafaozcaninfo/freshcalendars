**The Stonehaven fireball festival starts at midnight on December 31st and lasts approximately 20 minutes, the organisers say, which is 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026.** There is no entry fee and tickets are not required, according to the [Stonehaven Fireballs site](https://stonehavenfireballs.com/). The table below puts it beside Scotland's other fire festivals through Jan. 26, 2027, and a printable timeline keeps the dates together.

## What time are the Stonehaven Fireballs in 2026?

The organisers' page, headed "Stonehaven Fireballs 2027" for the ceremony that welcomes the new year, says the festival "starts at midnight, and lasts for approximately 20 minutes. It is followed immediately afterwards by a fireworks display." VisitAberdeenshire lists the ceremony on Stonehaven High Street, AB39 2XN, for "Thursday 31 December" at "12:00am". Midnight in Stonehaven is Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), so the ceremony begins at 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT on Dec. 31. Add the date to the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) or see [when is New Year's Eve](/when-is/new-years-eve/).

## Plan your night in Stonehaven

The organisers say "People start to gather in the High Street from 10.00pm onwards on December 31st." They add: "We have to close access to the High Street when we reach capacity." This "can happen any time after 11.00pm."

| Step | Stonehaven time (GMT) | ET | PT |
|---|---|---|---|
| People start to gather on the High Street | 10 p.m. | 5 p.m. | 2 p.m. |
| Access can close at capacity (any time after) | 11 p.m. | 6 p.m. | 3 p.m. |
| Fireballs ceremony starts | Midnight | 7 p.m. | 4 p.m. |
| Ceremony ends (approximately 20 minutes) | About 12:20 a.m. | About 7:20 p.m. | About 4:20 p.m. |
| Fireworks display | Immediately afterwards | Right after the ceremony | Right after the ceremony |

All rows are Dec. 31 in the US; the UK side crosses into Jan. 1 at midnight. The ceremony end is our sum of midnight and the organisers' 20 minutes. The organisers invite donations; entry is free.

## What is the Burning of the Clavie date in 2027?

VisitScotland's Hogmanay page says New Year is celebrated on 11 January, known as "old Hogmanay," in Burghead. Jan. 11, 2027 is a Monday. The page we read gives no start time, so none is given here.

## When is Up Helly Aa 2027?

The Up Helly Aa site says "Up Helly Aa 2027 is on Tuesday 26th January 2027." The festival is held in Lerwick, Shetland, "on the last Tuesday in January every year," its site says. No procession time is confirmed in the pages we read; see the [Up Helly Aa 2027 date story](/news/up-helly-aa-2027-january-26-shetland-fire-festival-dates/), and the day before is [Burns Night](/news/burns-night-2027-january-25-burns-supper-order-of-events/). Mark both on the [January 2027 printable calendar](/january-calendars.html).

## What do Comrie and Biggar publish?

Comrie's programme page is undated, so the details below are the village's published programme, not a 2026-27 schedule. It describes a torchlight parade that sets off from Melville Square "on the usual route round the village," with torches "cast over the Dalginross Bridge into the waters of the River Earn." It lists a children's fancy dress parade at "6.30pm" and fireworks "anytime after 7.00pm," and says the flambeaux are lit as midnight is announced by the bells of Big Ben. The 2026-27 timings are not yet announced.

For Biggar, the 2026 plans are not yet announced. Last year, a South Lanarkshire Council notice for the Biggar Community Council and Biggar Cornets Club Bonfire Committee on 31 December 2025 listed an outward procession at 9:15pm from Biggar Business Park, Station Road, to the Corn Exchange, as a precursor to lighting the bonfire. A Transport Scotland order closed the A702 "from 1900 hours on 31st December 2025 until 0400 hours on 1st January 2026" for the Biggar Hogmanay Bonfire. Those are 2025 details; check for the 2026 notices. For Edinburgh's New Year events, see [Edinburgh's Christmas and Hogmanay 2026 dates](/news/edinburghs-christmas-2026-dates-big-wheel-hogmanay-torchlight/).
