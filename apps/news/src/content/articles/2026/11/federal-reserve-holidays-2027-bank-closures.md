**The Federal Reserve observes 11 holidays in 2027, according to the Federal Reserve Board's "Holidays Observed - K.8" schedule (last updated July 8, 2026), and a Saturday rule decides which Fridays Reserve Banks stay open.** Two holidays, Juneteenth and Christmas Day, fall on Saturdays in 2027, and Independence Day falls on a Sunday. This story lays the Fed's list beside the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) federal holiday table and has a one-page printable below.

## What are the Federal Reserve holidays in 2027?

| Holiday | Date | Reserve Banks | Board of Governors | OPM federal offices observe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day | Fri., Jan. 1 | Closed | Closed | Fri., Jan. 1 |
| Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. | Mon., Jan. 18 | Closed | Closed | Mon., Jan. 18 |
| Washington's Birthday | Mon., Feb. 15 | Closed | Closed | Mon., Feb. 15 |
| Memorial Day | Mon., May 31 | Closed | Closed | Mon., May 31 |
| Juneteenth National Independence Day | Sat., June 19 | Open Fri., June 18 | Closed Fri., June 18 | Fri., June 18 |
| Independence Day | Sun., July 4 | Closed Mon., July 5 | Closed Mon., July 5 | Mon., July 5 |
| Labor Day | Mon., Sept. 6 | Closed | Closed | Mon., Sept. 6 |
| Columbus Day | Mon., Oct. 11 | Closed | Closed | Mon., Oct. 11 |
| Veterans Day | Thu., Nov. 11 | Closed | Closed | Thu., Nov. 11 |
| Thanksgiving Day | Thu., Nov. 25 | Closed | Closed | Thu., Nov. 25 |
| Christmas Day | Sat., Dec. 25 | Open Fri., Dec. 24 | Closed Fri., Dec. 24 | Fri., Dec. 24 |

The first four columns come from the Federal Reserve Board's K.8 page; the last column is the OPM "Federal Holidays" 2027 table. The K.8 page also lists New Year's Day 2028, a Saturday (Jan. 1, 2028): Reserve Banks are open the Friday before, Dec. 31, 2027, and the Board is closed that day.

## What is the Federal Reserve's Saturday rule?

The Board states two rules. "For holidays falling on Saturday, Federal Reserve Banks and Branches will be open the preceding Friday; however, the Board of Governors will be closed." And: "For holidays falling on Sunday, all Federal Reserve offices will be closed the following Monday."

Applied to 2027, that gives three Fridays when Reserve Banks are open and the Board is closed: June 18 (Juneteenth), Dec. 24 (Christmas) and Dec. 31 (New Year's Day 2028). On Monday, July 5, the Sunday rule closes the Reserve Banks and the Board. The K.8 footnotes name these same dates.

By our count, Reserve Banks close on 9 weekdays in 2027: the eight holidays that fall Monday to Friday plus Monday, July 5. Because Juneteenth and Christmas fall on Saturdays, they cost the Reserve Banks no weekday. The Board is closed on 12 weekdays, those 9 plus the three Fridays above. Both numbers are our arithmetic from the K.8 list, not figures the Fed publishes.

## Are Federal Reserve Banks open on Juneteenth 2027?

Juneteenth National Independence Day falls on Saturday, June 19, 2027. Under the Saturday rule, Reserve Banks and Branches are open Friday, June 18, and the Board of Governors is closed. Check your own bank's hours for the weekend itself.

## Which Fridays do federal offices close while Reserve Banks stay open?

Two, according to the two official lists side by side. OPM's 2027 table shows Friday, June 18 (Juneteenth) and Friday, Dec. 24 (Christmas) as federal holidays, and OPM says: "If a holiday falls on a Saturday, for most Federal employees, the preceding Friday will be treated as a holiday for pay and leave purposes." The K.8 schedule says Reserve Banks are open on those same two days.

| Friday | Reserve Banks | Board of Governors | OPM federal holiday |
|---|---|---|---|
| June 18, 2027 | Open | Closed | Yes (Juneteenth) |
| Dec. 24, 2027 | Open | Closed | Yes (Christmas) |
| Dec. 31, 2027 | Open | Closed | Not on the OPM page we read |

For Dec. 31, 2027 we do not state OPM's status; OPM's 2028 table is the place to check. For how OPM's dates play out for federal workers, see our [federal holidays 2027 list](/news/federal-holidays-2027-observed-dates-list/).

## How do Federal Reserve holidays differ from stock market holidays?

The Fed's list does not include Good Friday. The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) closes on Good Friday, March 26, 2027, as covered in our [stock market holidays 2027](/news/stock-market-holidays-2027-nyse-closed-dates/) story. For a day count that sets the federal and NYSE calendars side by side, see [working days in 2027](/news/working-days-2027-business-days-by-month-federal-nyse/).

## Will my bank be closed on these days?

The K.8 list is the Federal Reserve's own schedule. Individual banks set their own branch hours, so check your bank's posted holiday hours. Check with your bank or employer about direct deposit dates near these holidays.
