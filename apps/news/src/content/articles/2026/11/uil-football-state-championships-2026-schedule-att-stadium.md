**The University Interscholastic League (UIL) schedules its 12 Texas high school football state championship games for Wednesday to Saturday, Dec. 16-19, 2026, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, three games a day, according to its state championships page.** Classes 1A to 6A are covered, and no matchup is set yet. The table gives each game with its time in Central Time (CT) and Eastern Time (ET), plus last season's champion in each division.

## UIL football state championship schedule 2026

UIL's page prints the times without a time zone. Arlington is on Central Time and December is Central Standard Time, so the times below are read as local time at the stadium; the ET column is our addition of one hour.

| Day | Class and division | Kickoff (CT) | Kickoff (ET) | 2025 champion | Teams |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wed., Dec. 16 | 1A Six-Man Division 2 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | Jayton | Not yet decided |
| Wed., Dec. 16 | 1A Six-Man Division 1 | 2 p.m. | 3 p.m. | Gordon | Not yet decided |
| Wed., Dec. 16 | 2A Division 2 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Muenster | Not yet decided |
| Thu., Dec. 17 | 3A Division 2 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | Wall | Not yet decided |
| Thu., Dec. 17 | 2A Division 1 | 3 p.m. | 4 p.m. | Hamilton | Not yet decided |
| Thu., Dec. 17 | 3A Division 1 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Yoakum | Not yet decided |
| Fri., Dec. 18 | 5A Division 2 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | Dallas South Oak Cliff | Not yet decided |
| Fri., Dec. 18 | 4A Division 2 | 3 p.m. | 4 p.m. | Carthage | Not yet decided |
| Fri., Dec. 18 | 6A Division 2 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | DeSoto | Not yet decided |
| Sat., Dec. 19 | 5A Division 1 | 11 a.m. | 12 p.m. | Comal Smithson Valley | Not yet decided |
| Sat., Dec. 19 | 4A Division 1 | 3 p.m. | 4 p.m. | Stephenville | Not yet decided |
| Sat., Dec. 19 | 6A Division 1 | 7 p.m. | 8 p.m. | Galena Park North Shore | Not yet decided |

Wednesday's middle game is at 2 p.m. while the other three days use 3 p.m., per the page. The Teams column on UIL's page is blank, so the matchups are not decided.

## When does the UIL playoff run before the finals?

UIL's football page lists the playoff dates by week. It gives no round names, so only the dates are shown here.

| Playoff week | Dates |
|---|---|
| Week One | Nov. 12, 13 and 14 |
| Week Two | Nov. 19, 20 and 21 |
| Week Three | Nov. 26, 27 and 28 |
| Week Four | Dec. 3, 4 and 5 |
| Week Five | Dec. 10, 11 and 12 (2A Division II may play on Dec. 9) |
| State Championships | Dec. 16, 17, 18 and 19 (all conferences) |

District certification is listed for Nov. 7. Week Three overlaps the Thanksgiving weekend; Thanksgiving is Nov. 26, 2026.

## UIL state championship tickets 2026

UIL says tickets are sold through SeatGeek "once available", and the sale start date is not yet announced. Its spectator page lists two prices:

- General public: $20.
- Participating school: $15, which needs a code from a participating school once the team qualifies.

UIL also says tickets will not be sold onsite and no ticket offices will be open on game days.

## How to watch the UIL football state championships

UIL says fans can watch all 12 games free through participating television stations and the digital streaming platforms of Nexstar Media and Mission Broadcasting, and that availability varies by market. The examples on its page are CW33+ in Dallas, CW39+ in Houston and eL+ in Lubbock. The page does not assign a channel to each game.
