**Peet's Coffee holiday menu began Nov. 4, 2026, with Iced Peppermint Bark Matcha and Sparkling Black Cherry Energy as the two drinks Peet's calls new, according to the company's Nov. 4 release on PR Newswire and its holiday page.** The menu is available for a limited time at participating coffeebars nationwide and through the Peetnik Rewards app. Peet's gave no prices, no end date and no launch time.

The menu opened two days earlier than in 2025, when Peet's launched its holiday menu on Nov. 6. For other chains, see the [Caribou Coffee holiday menu 2026](/news/caribou-coffee-holiday-menu-2026-cookie-butter-white-mocha/), the [Starbucks holiday menu 2026](/news/starbucks-holiday-menu-2026-red-cup-day-date/) and our [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/).

## What is on the Peet's holiday menu in 2026?

Peets.com says "Iced Peppermint Bark Matcha and Sparkling Black Cherry Energy are new to the scene, joining a couple of return favorites." The release does not say whether the other items are new or returning, so the table marks them "not given."

| Item | Type | New or returning |
|---|---|---|
| Iced Peppermint Bark Matcha (also named Peppermint Bark Matcha in the release) | Drink | New, per peets.com |
| Sparkling Black Cherry Energy | Drink | New, per peets.com |
| Holiday Spice Latte, hot or iced | Drink | Not given |
| Peppermint Mocha, hot or iced | Drink | Not given |
| Peppermint Matcha Dream Top | Add-on for any iced beverage | Not given |
| Iced Gingerbread Loaf | Food | Not given |
| Mini Apple-Cran Queen Croissant | Food | Not given |
| Holiday Blend 2026 | Coffee | Not given |

Source: Peet's Coffee release, Nov. 4, 2026; peets.com/pages/holiday, read Nov. 23, 2026.

Peets.com describes Holiday Blend 2026 this way: "Holiday Blend 2026 unites Kenya's berry brightness, Guatemala's fudgy chocolate, and Sumatra's warm spice." The release says the Peppermint Matcha Dream Top can be added onto any iced beverage.

## How does the 2026 Peet's menu compare with 2025?

In 2025, Peet's launched its holiday menu on Nov. 6 ("starting today" in that day's release), alongside its first-ever coffee Advent calendar. Four 2025 items do not appear in the 2026 sources we read.

| Item | 2025 (Nov. 6 release) | 2026 (Nov. 4 release, peets.com) |
|---|---|---|
| Launch date | Nov. 6, 2025 | Nov. 4, 2026 |
| Matcha drink | Dubai Style Chocolate Matcha, new | Peppermint Bark Matcha, new |
| Second new drink | Pistachio Cold Brew Oat Latte, new | Sparkling Black Cherry Energy, new |
| Holiday Spice Latte | Topped with salted caramel crunch | Listed |
| Peppermint Mocha | Returning | Listed |
| Ginger Spritz | Listed | Not listed |
| Turkey Cranberry Ciabatta | Listed | Not listed |
| Gingerbread Loaf | Listed | Iced Gingerbread Loaf |
| New 2026 food | Not listed | Mini Apple-Cran Queen Croissant |
| Holiday Blend | Listed | Holiday Blend 2026 |
| Advent calendar | 12 Days of Peet's Advent Calendar, $79.95 | Not mentioned in the 2026 release |

Source: Peet's Coffee releases, Nov. 6, 2025 and Nov. 4, 2026; peets.com/pages/holiday.

The 2026 sources do not say the 2025 items were dropped, only that they are not listed. Peet's did not name a 2026 Advent calendar.

## When are the Peet's Home for the Holidays giveaway draws?

Peet's says winners of its Home for the Holidays giveaway "will be selected weekly from November 23 through December 14." The prize is a Southwest Airlines e-voucher for holiday travel. The giveaway is open to legal U.S. residents age 19 or older, and the release says no purchase is necessary. Entry is by scanning a QR code on holiday packaging.

The entry dates differ between Peet's pages. The release says the giveaway is valid 8/3/26 to 12/31/26, while a banner on peets.com reads "Valid 8/3/26 through 12/13/26." We give no entry end date; read the official rules before you enter. The final weekly draw falls in the window ending Monday, Dec. 14, per the release.

## What Peet's has not announced for 2026

Peet's did not give prices for any 2026 holiday item, a menu end date or a launch time in the sources we read. It has not posted Thanksgiving Day hours there; check your local coffeebar. No Black Friday or Cyber Monday offer appears in the release.
