**Belfast City Council lists the Christmas lights switch-on at Belfast City Hall for Saturday 14 November 2026, 6.30pm to 7.30pm, and Newcastle's Christmas site gives Thursday 12 November for its own switch-on**, while Nation.Cymru reported on 17 September 2026 that Cardiff's lights are due the same Thursday. Manchester's Christmas Markets already opened on Friday 6 November 2026, and no Manchester lights event is listed. The table has each date with the weekday, the place and the Eastern Time (ET) conversion, and a printable follows.

Official times are in Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), which is five hours ahead of ET in November. Four lights and market events fall between 12 and 14 November: Cardiff and Newcastle lights on Thursday, Belfast's market and switch-on on Saturday. For the London streets, see the [London Christmas lights switch-on dates](/news/london-christmas-lights-switch-on-2026-dates-regent-street-carnaby-covent-garden/).

## Belfast City Hall switch-on: time, gates and tickets

Belfast City Council's page gives "Saturday 14 November 2026, 6.30pm to 7.30pm", with gates opening at 6pm, at Belfast City Hall. The event is free, but the council says "All tickets are now allocated for our Christmas Lights switch-on." The council also says the Christmas Market opens at 12 noon on the same day. It has not announced the market's closing date or hours. The photo above is a file photo from 2014.

## Cardiff lights and Winter Wonderland dates

Cardiff Council's own page was blocked to us, so the lights date rests on one report. Nation.Cymru wrote on 17 September 2026 that the city's festive lights were "due to come on on November 12", citing the council, with no time or venue given. Visit Cardiff, the city's tourism site, lists Cardiff's Winter Wonderland "from Thursday 12 November to Sunday 3 January 2027", with an ice rink and ice walk at Cardiff Castle and City Hall. Its first-day hours are 17:00 to 22:00, and it is closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day. Christmas at Bute Park runs 19 November to 31 December 2026, 16:15 to 20:30, and is ticketed.

## Newcastle lights and market dates

Newcastle's Christmas site lists the Christmas Lights Switch On for Thursday 12 November 2026 and gives no time or venue yet. Its Christmas Markets "officially open on Thursday 19th November and will be in place until Wednesday 23rd December", 10am to 8pm Monday to Saturday and 10am to 5pm on Sundays.

## Is there a Manchester Christmas lights switch-on?

Visit Manchester lists no 2026 switch-on event. It says "Manchester Christmas Markets will open on Friday 6 November 2026 and run until Tuesday 22 December 2026", with Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens running to Sunday 3 January 2027. Northern Lights at Heaton Park runs 19 November to 31 December 2026; the page gives no times. Dates and hours for the stalls are in our [Manchester and Birmingham Christmas markets story](/news/manchester-birmingham-christmas-markets-2026-dates-opening-times/).
