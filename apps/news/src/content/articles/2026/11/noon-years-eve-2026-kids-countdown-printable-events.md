## What else is planned at these events?

Toledo Zoo lists crafts, an apple juice toast and a 2027 resolutions activity with its noon ball rise. The Children's Museum of Indianapolis says free festive favors are available while supplies last. Kansas Children's Discovery Center bills its morning as a way to "ring in 2027" and sells tickets for the 9:30 a.m. to noon window. Science Center of Iowa asks families to reserve ahead because tickets are limited.

## What did two venues run for Noon Year's Eve in 2025?

Omaha's Henry Doorly Zoo ran a Noon Year's Eve on Dec. 31, 2025, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., with the countdown at 11:59 a.m. and the event included with admission. Santa Fe Children's Museum's 2025 Noon Year's Eve sold out. Children's Museum of Denver has posted a 2026 event; its page did not load when checked, so check its site.

## What is noon in other time zones?

Each venue counts down at its own noon. For a family joining a livestream or planning around Eastern Time, the conversions are computed: noon ET is 11 a.m. Central, 10 a.m. Mountain and 9 a.m. Pacific on Dec. 31. Midnight is a different event: the Times Square Alliance says its free webcasts begin at 6 p.m. ET on Dec. 31. ## How to run a Noon Year's Eve at home

A home version needs no ticket. These are our suggestions, not venue rules.

1. Make party hats and noisemakers in the morning.
2. Pick a spot for the countdown and hang balloons or keep a bag of confetti ready.
3. Set a clock or phone to 12:00 p.m. and count down from 10 together.
4. Pour apple juice for a toast, as Toledo Zoo does.
5. Have each child write one or two 2027 resolutions on the sheet.
