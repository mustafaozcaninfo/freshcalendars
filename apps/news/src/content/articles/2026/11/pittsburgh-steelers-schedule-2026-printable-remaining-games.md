**The Pittsburgh Steelers play at the Philadelphia Eagles on Sun., Nov. 22, 2026, at 1 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on CBS, then host the Denver Broncos on Black Friday, Fri., Nov. 27, at 3 p.m. ET on Prime Video, according to NFL.com.** Eight games remain, and a one-page printable with a pick column is further down.

The Steelers are 6-3-0, second in the American Football Conference (AFC) North behind the Cincinnati Bengals (7-2), per the [NFL standings](https://www.nfl.com/standings/) checked Nov. 16. They lost 38-18 at Cincinnati on Sun., Nov. 15, and had their bye in Week 9. The tables use the National Football League (NFL) schedule and the [Steelers' own schedule page](https://www.steelers.com/schedule/). Times are in ET; Central (CT), Mountain (MT) and Pacific (PT) are computed by us.

## When could a Steelers game time still change?

The NFL's flexible scheduling procedures let it move games into Sunday Night Football and Monday Night Football (MNF) slots late in the season. Per the procedures, Sunday Night Football decisions for Weeks 5-13 and Monday Night Football decisions for Weeks 12-17 come "no later than 12 days in advance of the game." For Sunday Night Football in Weeks 14-17 the NFL says the decision will "generally be made no later than 6 days prior to the game." Thursday Night Football "may be used up to twice between Weeks 13-17," with notice "generally ... no later than 21 days prior to the game."

We subtracted those windows from each Steelers date. These are the latest notice dates, not dates a change will happen:

| Week | Game date | Latest flex notice | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Sun., Nov. 22 | Tue., Nov. 10 (passed) | 12 days |
| 13 | Sun., Dec. 6 | Tue., Nov. 24 | 12 days |
| 14 | Mon., Dec. 14 | Wed., Dec. 2 | 12 days |
| 15 | Sun., Dec. 20 | Mon., Dec. 14 | 6 days, generally |
| 17 | Sun., Jan. 3, 2027 | Mon., Dec. 28 | 6 days, generally |

Week 11's deadline has passed, so the Eagles game stays at 1 p.m. ET on CBS as posted. The flexible scheduling page does not mention the Black Friday game in Week 12. The schedule above is as posted on Nov. 16, 2026.
