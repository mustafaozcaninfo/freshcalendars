1. **Part of speech:** it works as a noun and as an adjective.
2. **Meaning:** the value of something, in money or in merit. A thing can be this much, or be this to someone.
3. **Vowels:** one vowel letter, the second tile.

5. **First letter:** W. The last letter is H, and the final two letters are TH.

## Tips for guessing today

Nothing repeats, so a yellow tile points to one spot only and never to two.
