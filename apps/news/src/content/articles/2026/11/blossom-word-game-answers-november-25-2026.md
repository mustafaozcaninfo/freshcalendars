**Merriam-Webster's Blossom for Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, centers on U with I, M, O, R, S and T around it, and three published lists agree on five pangrams.** Solver sites disagree on the count only because some print fewer. Hints and counts come first, with no word given away.

## Wednesday's flower: center U

- **Center:** U
- **Petals:** I, M, O, R, S, T

Beebom, Minute Cryptic, Connections Solver and Nytsolvers print this same set of letters. Two of the seven are vowels besides the center, I and O, so U plus those two make three. Tuesday's flower is on the [Nov. 24 Blossom page](/news/blossom-word-game-answers-november-24-2026/). Scoring and the pangram bonus are on the [Blossom rules page](/news/topic/blossom/#how-it-works); today the center U matters most, because every find must hold it.

## One board, three different pangram counts

Merriam-Webster's Blossom page shows no puzzle data, so there is no official list to settle it. Beebom, Minute Cryptic and Word.Tips print the same five pangrams. JJ Apps prints 3, Nytsolvers prints 1, and Connections Solver shows the top 3 and tucks the rest behind a see-all link. Beebom's intro says three, while the list under it holds five.

We read the shorter lists as subsets. Each word they print is on the five-word list, and none adds a sixth, so five is the count we use.

### Pangram

The three agreeing lists give these 5, each holding U, I, M, O, R, S and T at least once.

- **Tourism** (7 letters)
- **Tourisms** (8 letters)
- **Timorous** (8 letters)
- **Tutiorism** (9 letters)
- **Tutiorisms** (10 letters)

Tourisms is Tourism plus a second S, and Tutiorisms is Tutiorism plus the same S, so the list is two singular-and-plural pairs and one word on its own, Timorous, which repeats O. Tutiorism and Tutiorisms each repeat T and I. Word.Tips gives its own point values: Tutiorisms 28, Tutiorism 25, Timorous 22, Tourisms 22 and Tourism 19. Those are Word.Tips's figures, not Merriam-Webster's.
