The Supreme Court of the United States has six cases on its calendar for the session beginning **Monday, Jan. 11, 2027**, spread over five argument days through **Wednesday, Jan. 20**. The court's Monthly Argument Calendar for January 2027, dated Nov. 2, 2026, says the court convenes at 10 a.m. That is 10 a.m. Eastern Time (ET), or 7 a.m. Pacific by our conversion. The session opens 62 days after Nov. 10, 2026 (our count) with *Apple Inc. v. Epic Games, Inc.*

## Which cases does the Supreme Court hear in January 2027?

The calendar's header reads "October Term 2026, For the Session Beginning January 11, 2027." The table gives the docket number, the court that ruled first, the date the court agreed to hear the case and the question in plain words, paraphrased from the court's Questions Presented.

The calendar lists no cases on Monday, Jan. 18. It marks the day as a legal holiday without naming it; Jan. 18, 2027 is Martin Luther King Jr. Day, a federal holiday (see [what is closed on Martin Luther King Jr. Day 2027](/news/martin-luther-king-jr-day-2027-january-18-whats-closed/)).

## What is the Apple v. Epic Games argument about?

The court's order granting review in No. 25-1311 is limited to Question 1. That question asks whether a court may hold a party in civil contempt based on a violation of an injunction's "spirit" where the injunction is silent about the conduct at issue, as the Ninth Circuit holds, or whether a court must ground civil contempt on the violation of an order that clearly and unambiguously bars the precise conduct, as other circuits hold. The 9th Circuit decided the case Dec. 11, 2025 and denied rehearing March 30, 2026. The court also denied a stay application (26A194) on Aug. 13, 2026.

## How to listen to the January 2027 arguments

The court's Oral Arguments page says an audio feed of the argument is live-streamed on the court's website, and the court posts the audio later in the day. On the afternoon of each argument, the court posts transcripts of that day's arguments. We could not confirm the rules for the public seating line.

## What else is on the court's January 2027 calendar?

The court's 2026 Term calendar, a graphic, shows a non-argument session on Friday, Jan. 8, and conferences on Fridays, Jan. 15 and Jan. 22. It shows the February session opening Feb. 22, 2027; the February argument calendar was not posted as of Nov. 10, 2026. Last argument day of the session: Wednesday, Jan. 20. Decision dates are not announced; the court has not said when it will rule on any of the six cases.
