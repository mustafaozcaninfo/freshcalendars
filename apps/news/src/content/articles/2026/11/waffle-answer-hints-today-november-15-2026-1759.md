### A 10-swap route to finish

The minimum is 10 swaps, found by an exhaustive search and replayed on the starting grid to reach the solved one. Play them in order, because each one assumes the earlier ones are done. Swaps 2, 5, 7, 8 and 10 put two tiles home at once; the other five fix one each.

### Why the count is 10

Fifteen tiles are misplaced, and a loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops. Ten swaps means 5 loops.
