Swan, Bandit, Raccoon, City, Hawk, Stage, Robin, Dumpster, Sports, Move, Scuba, Style, Peddle, Opinion, Zorro, Vend

## Hints for each group

**Yellow**

- Hint 1: Picture a person working a market stall, and look for what that person does with the goods.
- Hint 2: The entries have four, four, six and four letters.

**Green**

- Hint 1: Think of the labels you would see across the tabs of a printed daily.
- Hint 2: The entries have four, seven, six and five letters.

**Blue**

- Hint 1: A dark band across the face is the shared look, whether the wearer is real, drawn or in costume.
- Hint 2: The entries have six, seven, five and five letters.

**Purple**

- Hint 1: Each tile can start a phrase about a plunge, and the plunges range from a pool to a crowd to a bin.
- Hint 2: The entries have eight, five, five and four letters.

## Solving tips

Several tiles carry a second meaning, so a first glance can pull you toward the wrong neighbor. Pick the group whose entries have the fewest rivals, lock it, and let the remaining tiles settle the rest.

## What each of the 16 words means here

**Sell**

- **Hawk:** to offer goods by calling out to passers-by, not the bird.
- **Move:** to get stock out of the shop, as when a store tries to move old inventory.
- **Peddle:** to sell small goods, often from place to place, and not the same word as pedal on a bike.
- **Vend:** to sell through a stall or a machine.

**Newspaper Sections**

- **City:** the pages on local news.
- **Opinion:** the pages for editorials and columns.
- **Sports:** the scores and game coverage.
- **Style:** the fashion and lifestyle pages, not a way of doing something.

**They Have Eye Masks**

- **Bandit:** a masked thief as a character, rather than any outlaw.
- **Raccoon:** the animal whose dark fur forms a mask around the eyes.
- **Robin:** the sidekick in a domino mask, not the bird.
- **Zorro:** the fictional masked swordsman.

**___ Dive**

- **Dumpster:** to search through bins for things thrown away.
- **Scuba:** to go underwater with a tank and breathing gear.
- **Stage:** to jump from a stage into the audience.
- **Swan:** a dive with the arms spread wide and the back arched.

The bird idea is the loudest lure, since three tiles are birds and each belongs elsewhere. After that come a few smaller pulls, listed in the table.

A safe route starts with the group that has the fewest rivals:

1. **Newspaper Sections.** City, Opinion, Sports and Style are the only tiles that work as page headings; locking them removes the Style and Move confusion.
2. **They Have Eye Masks.** Bandit, Raccoon, Robin and Zorro all wear a mask over the eyes, which clears Robin from the bird pile.
3. **Sell.** Hawk, Move, Peddle and Vend are what is left that a trader does, so Hawk drops out of the birds.
4. **___ Dive.** Dumpster, Scuba, Stage and Swan finish the set, and Swan is the last bird standing.
