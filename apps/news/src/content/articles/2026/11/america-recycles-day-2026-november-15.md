**America Recycles Day is Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026.** The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) says: "On America Recycles Day (November 15), EPA recognizes the importance and impact of recycling." Below are EPA's rules for common items, three local events from official pages and a free one-page checklist (all checked Nov. 4, 2026).

## When is America Recycles Day in 2026 and after?

The date is Nov. 15 every year, so only the weekday changes. The weekdays below are our calculation.

| Year | Nov. 15 falls on |
|---|---|
| 2026 | Sunday |
| 2027 | Monday |
| 2028 | Wednesday |
| 2029 | Thursday |
| 2030 | Friday |
| 2031 | Saturday |

EPA's page repeats the date on its [Reduce, Reuse, Recycle page](https://www.epa.gov/recycling), updated Sept. 3, 2026. Mark it on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html). If you are planning the rest of the holiday season, see [trash pickup changes for Thanksgiving 2026](/news/thanksgiving-trash-pickup-2026-by-city/).

## What does EPA say about the day and the 2030 goal?

EPA says it "encourages every American to contribute by recycling right, not only on America Recycles Day, but all year long." It asks people to "help us reach our current National Recycling Goal to increase the U.S. recycling rate to 50 percent by 2030."

EPA's most recent national figure is from 2018: "The recycling rate (including composting) was 32.1 percent in 2018, down from 34.7 percent in 2015." Keep America Beautiful (KAB) runs the day's campaign. KAB describes it as "the only nationally recognized day that's all about recycling" and says "This November, America Recycles Day becomes America Recycles Month." It asks people to "complete a daily challenge, share using #AmericaRecycles, and tag @KeepAmericaBeautiful."

## Bin or not: what EPA says about common items

EPA says "most" community programs accept many of these items, so your city's own list decides. Use this as a starting point.

| Item | EPA's rule | EPA page |
|---|---|---|
| Glass | "Most curbside recycling programs accept different glass colors and types mixed together." | How Do I Recycle Common Recyclables |
| Paper | "Most community or office recycling programs accept paper and paper products." | How Do I Recycle Common Recyclables |
| Food containers | "'Spatula-clean' is clean enough to recycle!" | How Do I Recycle Common Recyclables |
| Pizza box | "Make sure to remove any food scraps from the box and flatten it before placing it in the bin." | How Do I Recycle Common Recyclables |
| Plastic bags, wraps, films | "These items are recyclable, but they cannot go in your household recycling bin." | How Do I Recycle Common Recyclables |
| Lithium-ion batteries | Lithium-ion batteries and devices containing them should not go in household garbage or recycling bins. | Used Lithium-Ion Batteries |
| Electronics | "Remove any batteries from your electronics; they may need to be recycled separately." | Electronics Donation and Recycling |

EPA adds that donating or recycling consumer electronics "conserves our natural resources and avoids air and water pollution." The agency's [battery page](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/used-lithium-ion-batteries) covers devices that contain lithium-ion batteries as well.
