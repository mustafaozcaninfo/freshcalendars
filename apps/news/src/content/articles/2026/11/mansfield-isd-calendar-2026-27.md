**Mansfield Independent School District (Mansfield ISD) in Texas has no school for students Nov. 23-27, 2026, and from Friday, Dec. 18, 2026 through Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027**, by its 2026-2027 Student & Teacher Calendar. Classes resume Thursday, Jan. 7, 2027, and the last day of school is Thursday, May 20, 2027.

The dates come from the PDF the district posts as its "2026-2027 School Calendar" on its calendars page. The file is named "Option A" and carries no printed date, so this story does not say when or whether the school board adopted it. The district's page also links a separate "2026-2027 A/B Calendar", which is not used here.

## Mansfield ISD breaks and student holidays in 2026-27

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving Break | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Student Holiday, Winter Holiday and Student Holiday/Professional Learning & Work Days | Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 to Wed., Jan. 6, 2027 | 14 | Thu., Jan. 7, 2027 |
| Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day | Mon., Jan. 18, 2027 | 1 | Tue., Jan. 19 |
| Student and Teacher Holidays | Fri., Feb. 12 and Mon., Feb. 15, 2027 | 2 | Tue., Feb. 16 |
| Spring Break | Mon., March 15 to Fri., March 19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., March 22 |
| Student Holiday | Fri., March 26, 2027 | 1 | Mon., March 29 |

Weekdays off and return days are our count from the district's dates. Plan the week on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html).

## When is Mansfield ISD's winter break in 2026-27?

The district labels Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 as Winter Holiday. That is 10 weekdays. It adds a Student Holiday on Friday, Dec. 18, the day after First Semester ends on Thursday, Dec. 17, and "Student Holiday/Professional Learning & Work Days" on Jan. 4-6. Students are out 14 weekdays in a row, Dec. 18 to Jan. 6, and Second Semester begins Thursday, Jan. 7. Dec. 21 to Jan. 6 is 13 of those weekdays.

Sites that give winter break as "Dec. 18 to Jan. 6" are combining three separate labels on the district's calendar. ## Which Mansfield ISD holidays can turn into school days?

The calendar lists "Feb. 12: Bad Weather Makeup Day #1" and "March 26: Bad Weather Makeup Day #2". Friday, Feb. 12 and Friday, March 26, 2027 are student holidays now, and they become school days if the district needs the makeup days. The calendar does not say how families would be told.

## When does the Mansfield ISD school year start and end?

Teachers report Monday, Aug. 3, 2026, with professional learning and work days through Aug. 11. The first day of school was Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026. The last day is Thursday, May 20, 2027, and Friday, May 21 is a Teacher Workday. Graduation dates are not on the calendar and are not yet announced here.
