**Eight Simon mall hours pages we read on Nov. 16, 2026 list Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, as closed, and seven of them open at 6 a.m. on Black Friday, Nov. 27.** The pages are on simon.com and premiumoutlets.com, run by Simon Property Group. Check your own mall's hours page before you go: this is eight malls, not every mall.

## Are malls open on Thanksgiving 2026?

At the eight Simon pages, no: every one lists Thursday, Nov. 26 as closed. [Thanksgiving](/when-is/thanksgiving/) falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026; mark it on the [November 2026 printable calendar](/november-calendars.html).

We did not find a 2026 Simon corporate statement; the company's investor site refused automated access, so the hours above come only from the individual mall pages. Westfield, Macerich and Brookfield Properties had not posted 2026 Thanksgiving or Black Friday hours on their sites when we checked, and Tanger Outlets showed only that its Tanger Deal Days end Nov. 22. Look up your mall's page before you plan the trip.

## What time do Simon malls open on Black Friday 2026?

Seven of the eight pages show 6 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 27: Penn Square Mall, The Empire Mall, Haywood Mall, Briarwood Mall, Cape Cod Mall, St. Charles Towne Center and Woodbury Common Premium Outlets. Stanford Shopping Center in Palo Alto opens at 8 a.m. and closes at 8 p.m.

Closing times differ: 9 p.m. at six of the malls, 8 p.m. at Stanford and 10 p.m. at Woodbury Common. Chain-store hours for that day are in the [Black Friday 2026 store hours](/news/black-friday-2026-store-hours-walmart-target-best-buy/) list, and the [Small Business Saturday 2026 story](/news/small-business-saturday-2026-participating-stores-events/) covers the next day.

## Do store hours match mall hours?

Not necessarily. Store hours inside a mall can differ from the mall's posted hours, and an individual store can set its own, so check the store's page as well as the mall's. The posted mall hours say when the doors are open, not when every store is.

## Simon mall hours on Christmas Eve 2026

On Thursday, Dec. 24, the eight pages show opening at 8 a.m. (Haywood Mall and Briarwood Mall) or 9 a.m. (the other six), and closing at 6 p.m. everywhere except The Empire Mall, which closes at 5 p.m. Plan the rest of the month on the [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html).
