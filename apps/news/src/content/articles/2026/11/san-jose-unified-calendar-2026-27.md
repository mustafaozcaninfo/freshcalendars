**The San Jose Unified School District (SJUSD) 2026-27 student calendar has New Year recess from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, winter recess on Feb. 15-19, 2027 and spring recess on April 5-9, 2027**, according to the district's "2026-2027 Student Calendar" PDF. School starts Thursday, Aug. 6, 2026 and the last day of the regular school year is Thursday, May 27, 2027. The PDF prints no approval date.

## SJUSD New Year recess 2026: the December break

The district's wording matters here. It calls Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 "New Year recess," and it calls the February week "Winter recess." Families who search for "winter break" in December want the New Year recess: Monday, Dec. 21 to Friday, Jan. 1, ten weekdays. The grid shows Monday, Jan. 4, 2027 as a regular student day, so students return that day. Before the break, the list says "14-17 December Secondary schools out 2 hours early" and "18 December All schools out 2 hours early." The [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html) shows the weeks.

## Every SJUSD no-school and early-release day

Rows marked "list" come from the district's "Important Dates for Students." Rows marked "grid" are read from the calendar grid, where an X means "No student activities at all schools due to holiday"; the PDF has no text layer, so those were read by eye.

Counting weekdays from Aug. 6 to May 27 when every student is out, the total is 31: 1 (Sept. 7) + 5 (fall recess) + 1 (Nov. 11) + 3 (Thanksgiving recess) + 10 (New Year recess) + 1 (Jan. 18) + 5 (Feb. 15-19) + 5 (spring recess). The three single holidays come from the grid, so a reader who counts only the list gets 28. The district does not state a number of instructional days, and this story does not give one. Memorial Day, Monday, May 31, falls after the last day.

## When is SJUSD spring break 2027?

"5-9 April Spring recess" is Monday, April 5 to Friday, April 9, 2027, five weekdays. The calendar does not mark Cesar Chavez Day, Wednesday, March 31, so it is a regular day on this calendar. February has its own week: "15-19 February Winter recess" runs Monday to Friday and includes Presidents Day on Feb. 15. See it on the [February 2027 calendar](/february-calendars.html).

## SJUSD last day of school 2027

"27 May Last day of regular school year" is Thursday, May 27, 2027, and the list adds "27 May All schools out 2 hours early." Secondary schools are also out two hours early on May 24-26, and high school graduation ceremonies are listed for May 25-26. The list also shows "3-30 June Optional extended school year window."

## How does SJUSD compare with Oakland and San Francisco?

Dates for the other two districts come from our stories on the [Oakland Unified calendar](/news/oakland-unified-calendar-2026-27/) and the [San Francisco Unified calendar](/news/san-francisco-unified-calendar-2026-27/), which cite each district's own calendar PDF.

| Break | San Jose Unified | Oakland Unified | San Francisco Unified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving week | Wed.-Fri., Nov. 25-27 | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27 | Mon.-Fri., Nov. 23-27 |
| December break | Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 4 | Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 |
| Spring break | April 5-9 | March 29 to April 2 | March 26 to April 2 |
| Last day | Thu., May 27, 2027 | Thu., May 27, 2027 | Wed., June 2, 2027 |

San Jose is the only one of the three with a week off in September and another in February. Its Thanksgiving recess covers three days, against five in the other two. Oakland and San Francisco also start spring break a week or more earlier. Other districts are in our [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).
