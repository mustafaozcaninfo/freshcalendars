**Waffle (wafflegame.net) puzzle #1765, for Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, opens with 6 of 21 tiles green, 8 yellow and 7 gray.** One clue per word comes first, then what the colors already tell you, and the answers stay in the marked last section.

All four corners start green today, so the work happens in the middle of the grid; the colors and swap rules are on our [Waffle page](/news/topic/waffle/#how-it-works).

## One clue for each word

**Across**

- **Row 1:** starts with S and ends with D. Past tense of holding yourself upright on your feet.
- **Row 3:** starts with A and ends with T. Up in the air, overhead, the way a flag flies or a kite climbs.
- **Row 5:** starts with N and ends with Y. Poor, short of essentials and relying on other people for help.

**Down**

- **Column 1:** starts with S and ends with N. Eggs of a fish or frog laid in a mass; as a verb, to bring forth in large numbers.
- **Column 3:** starts with O and ends with E. The gas layer high above that blocks much of the sun's ultraviolet light.
- **Column 5:** starts with D and ends with Y. A short, light, catchy song or little poem.

## How the starting colors narrow each word

Each green tile is a crossing, and none of the 12 tiles that belong to only one word starts green. That leaves 15 tiles to move: 8 yellow, 7 gray.

| Word | Green | Yellow | Gray |
|---|---|---|---|
| Row 1 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| Row 3 | 2 | 0 | 3 |
| Row 5 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Column 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Column 3 | 1 | 4 | 0 |
| Column 5 | 3 | 1 | 1 |

Column 5 is the closest to done, with three tiles green. Column 3 is the furthest: only its centre is home, but all four of its other tiles are yellow, so none is a dead end. Two of those yellows sit in squares no across word touches, which means both belong somewhere in column 3.

Column 1 offers a shortcut. Its two yellow tiles sit in rows 2 and 4, neither on a crossing, and each belongs in the other's square, so one swap fixes both.

Four of the six words contain a repeated letter, which is a clue in itself when a tile has a twin. In position terms, two of the pairs sit side by side at places 3 and 4, one at places 2 and 3, and one has its twins at places 1 and 3. Row 3 and column 1 are the only words with no repeat.

### The 10 swaps in order

Each swap assumes the ones before it are done. Swaps 2, 4, 7, 8 and 10 send two tiles home together; the other five send one.

| Swap | Squares | Letters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Row 1, column 2 and row 1, column 3 | A and T |
| 2 | Row 1, column 3 and row 3, column 1 | A and O |
| 3 | Row 1, column 4 and row 5, column 3 | L and O |
| 4 | Row 2, column 1 and row 4, column 1 | W and P |
| 5 | Row 2, column 3 and row 3, column 2 | E and Z |
| 6 | Row 2, column 5 and row 4, column 5 | F and I |
| 7 | Row 3, column 2 and row 5, column 3 | E and L |
| 8 | Row 3, column 4 and row 4, column 5 | T and F |
| 9 | Row 4, column 3 and row 5, column 4 | E and N |
| 10 | Row 5, column 2 and row 5, column 4 | D and E |

- **Swap 1:** T moves into the second square of row 1. A takes its place for now.
- **Swap 2:** A leaves for the start of row 3 and O arrives at row 1, column 3; both are right.
- **Swap 3:** O drops into row 1, column 4, which completes the top word. L lands at the bottom crossing as a stopover.
- **Swap 4:** W and P trade places in column 1 and both are home.
- **Swap 5:** Z reaches row 2, column 3. E waits at row 3, column 2.
- **Swap 6:** I climbs to row 2, column 5, and F drops to row 4, column 5 for a turn.
- **Swap 7:** L moves up to row 3, column 2 while E goes down to the bottom crossing; both finish.
- **Swap 8:** F takes row 3, column 4 and T takes row 4, column 5, both in place.
- **Swap 9:** N settles at row 4, column 3. E moves to row 5, column 4, one square short of its home.
- **Swap 10:** D and E trade along the bottom row, and the grid is solved.

### Why 10 is the minimum

Fifteen tiles start out of place. A loop of n tiles costs n minus 1 swaps, so swaps equal misplaced tiles minus loops, and 10 swaps means 5 loops.
