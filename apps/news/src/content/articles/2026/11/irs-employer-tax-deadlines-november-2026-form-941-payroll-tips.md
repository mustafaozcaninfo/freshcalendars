**Monthly depositors owe October's social security, Medicare and withheld income tax on Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, according to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) fourth-quarter tax calendar.** The Form 941 dates for the third quarter have passed: Monday, Nov. 2, and Tuesday, Nov. 10 for employers who timely deposited and paid in full all required payments. The calendar's remaining employer entries are deposit dates and a tip-reporting date, listed below with the IRS's own wording.

## IRS employer tax dates for November and early December 2026

The table follows the IRS page "Fourth quarter – Tax calendar" for 2026, which we re-read on Nov. 14. Weekdays are computed from the dates. The first four rows are past.

| Tue., Nov. 10 | "File Form 941 for the third quarter if you timely deposited and paid in full all required payments" |

| Thu., Nov. 12 | "Deposit payroll tax for payments on November 4-6 if the semiweekly deposit rule applies" |

## Form 941 on Nov. 2 or Nov. 10: which date applies?

The two dates are not interchangeable. The Nov. 2 entry is the plain "File Form 941 for the third quarter" line. The Nov. 10 entry carried a condition: the employer timely deposited and paid in full all required payments. An employer who did not meet that condition was not covered by the later date in the IRS wording, and with both dates behind us, should check its position with the IRS or a tax professional. This story is general information, not tax advice.

## Which payroll tax deposit date applies to your business?

The IRS calendar ties each deposit line to a rule. Semiweekly dates apply "if the semiweekly deposit rule applies" and the Nov. 17 line applies "if the monthly deposit rule applies." Nov. 17 covers social security, Medicare and withheld income tax for payments made in October, and Tuesday, Dec. 15 covers the same taxes for payments made in November. In its Publication 509 (2026), the IRS also says that deposits of "$100,000 or more of taxes on any day during a deposit period" are due by the next business day. Which rule a business follows depends on its own deposit schedule, which this story does not determine.

## When do employees report tips to their employer?

The IRS calendar says employees were required to report to their employer tips of $20 or more earned during October by Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026. Tips earned in November are due Thursday, Dec. 10, 2026. Employers who run payroll for tipped staff can use that date to remind employees ahead of time.

## Other dates on the same IRS calendar

The Nov. 2 line also lists dates for other tax forms and payments, among them Form 720 for the third quarter, Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA) tax owed through September "if more than $500", Form 2290 for vehicles first used during September and Form 730 for wagers accepted during September. Corporations have a Tuesday, Dec. 15 line, which points to the Electronic Federal Tax Payment System (EFTPS): "Deposit the fourth installment of your 2026 estimated tax - see Pub. 542 and EFTPS." Dates for Forms 8027, 940 and 945 are not covered here because they were not confirmed for this story.

## How weekends and holidays change the dates

Publication 509 says that when a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the act is timely if done by the next business day. The IRS adds that "the calendars provided in this publication make the adjustments for Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays." Its legal holiday list for November includes Veterans Day on Nov. 11 and Thanksgiving Day on Nov. 26. Because the IRS calendar already carries those adjustments, the dates in the table above are the ones to use.
