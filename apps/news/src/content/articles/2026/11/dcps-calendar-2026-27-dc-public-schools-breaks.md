**District of Columbia Public Schools (DCPS) students are off Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 through Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, and Monday, March 22 through Friday, March 26, 2027, and the last day for students is Thursday, June 17, 2027**, according to the district's "School Year 2026-27" calendar (English PDF). The first day was Monday, Aug. 24, 2026. The PDF prints 180 student days and 193 teacher days. The next day off is Wednesday, Nov. 11, Veterans Day.

DCPS posted the calendar on its [School Year 2026-2027 Calendar](https://dcps.dc.gov/page/school-year-2026-2027-calendar) page. The PDF file name carries the number 061126, but it prints no approval date, so we say only that the district posted it.

## When is the DCPS winter break 2026?

The calendar has no single winter break label. It marks Monday, Dec. 21 as PD for staff with no school for students, Dec. 22-24 and Dec. 28-31 as no school for students or teachers, Dec. 25 as a holiday and Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 as a holiday. From those rows we compute that students are off ten weekdays, Monday, Dec. 21 through Friday, Jan. 1, and classes resume Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, a date with no marker on the PDF. Plan the weeks on the [December 2026 calendar](/december-calendars.html).

## When is the DCPS spring break 2027?

Spring break is Monday, March 22 through Friday, March 26, 2027, marked no school for students or teachers. That is five weekdays, and classes resume Monday, March 29 by our count. Good Friday, March 26, 2027, is in the PDF's list of religious observances, which it says are dates to avoid for events and are not days off unless coded. Mark the days on the [March 2027 calendar](/march-calendars.html).

## How does DCPS spring break compare with Maryland districts nearby?

DCPS students go back to class on March 29, the Monday before Montgomery County and Prince George's County students return.

| District | Students off in spring 2027 | Back in class |

## When is the last day of school for DCPS in 2027?

Thursday, June 17, 2027 is marked L/ET, "Last Day for Students / End of Term." Friday, June 4 is marked LS, "Last Day for Seniors." Friday, June 18 is a holiday, and Monday, June 21 is a half-day for records and PD with no school for students.

The PDF lists Tuesday and Wednesday, June 22-23, 2027, as "Planned Snow-Day Make-Up Days." They come after the last day. The PDF says nothing more about them, so we do not state what happens if snow days are used.

## When do DCPS terms end?

The PDF marks four end-of-term dates (ET) and prints terms of 46, 44, 44 and 46 days.

| Term | End of term | Weekday | Day after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Term 1 | Oct. 29, 2026 | Thursday | Oct. 30 is a PD day, no school for students |
| Term 2 | Jan. 25, 2027 | Monday | Jan. 26 is a half-day for records and PD |
| Term 3 | April 8, 2027 | Thursday | April 9 is a half-day for records and wellness |
| Term 4 | June 17, 2027 | Thursday | Last day for students |

## What other dates does the DCPS calendar list?

Parent-teacher conference days are Tuesday, Oct. 13, 2026 and Thursday, Feb. 11, 2027. The Pre-K first day (F-ECE) is Thursday, Aug. 27, 2026. Wellness Day is Friday, May 14, 2027. The PDF lists these religious observances, when schools avoid events: Rosh Hashanah (Sept. 12-13, 2026), Yom Kippur (Sept. 21), Diwali (Nov. 8), first day of Ramadan (Feb. 8, 2027), Ash Wednesday (Feb. 10), Eid al-Fitr (March 10), Good Friday (March 26), Passover (April 21-22) and Eid al-Adha (May 17).

## When does DCPS start in 2027-28?

The same PDF shows the next first day as Monday, Aug. 23, 2027, and Pre-K as Thursday, Aug. 26. This story covers the 2026-27 year.
