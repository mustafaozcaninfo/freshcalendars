**Metro Nashville Public Schools (MNPS) students have no school Nov. 23-27, 2026, from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, and from Monday, Mar. 22 to Friday, Mar. 26, 2027**, according to the district's 2026-2027 District Calendar. Students return Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, and the last day for students is Tuesday, May 25, 2027. The PDF is marked "Revised 2/9/26"; the district's calendar page links to it.

The next day the district is closed is Veterans Day, Wed., Nov. 11, 2026. School began Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026, and Labor Day, the Oct. 9-16 fall break and the Nov. 3 parent conference day have passed.

## When are the MNPS breaks in 2026-27?

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates; the return days marked "our reading" are the first school day after each break.

| Break | Students out | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall break (past) | Fri., Oct. 9 to Fri., Oct. 16, 2026 | 6 | Mon., Oct. 19 (second quarter begins) |
| Thanksgiving | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 (our reading) |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (third quarter begins) |
| Spring break | Mon., Mar. 22 to Fri., Mar. 26, 2027 | 5 | Mon., Mar. 29 (our reading) |

Oct. 9 is a stockpiled PD day, which puts a Friday in front of the Oct. 12-16 Fall Break. The district lists Jan. 4 the same way, "Stockpiled PD Day (Students do not report)," and Jan. 5 as the day the third quarter begins. Students return on Tuesday, Jan. 5; that Monday is a PD day for staff only. For the December weeks, see our [December 2026 printable calendar](/december-calendars.html) and the [winter break 2026 roundup](/news/winter-break-2026-school-districts-dates/).

## Which MNPS days are no-school days?

The district separates days when it is closed from days when students stay home but staff work. A stockpiled PD day is the second kind: "Students do not report." For the rest of the break weeks the lines we checked do not say whether staff work, so that column says "not stated."

| Date | Weekday | What the district lists | Staff status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sept. 7, 2026 | Monday | Labor Day observed | District closed |
| Oct. 9, 2026 | Friday | Stockpiled PD day | Students do not report |
| Oct. 12-16, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Fall Break | Not stated |
| Nov. 3, 2026 | Tuesday | Parent conference day | Students do not report |
| Nov. 11, 2026 | Wednesday | Veterans Day observed | District closed |
| Nov. 23-27, 2026 | Monday-Friday | Thanksgiving holiday | District closed Nov. 26-27; Nov. 23-25 not stated |
| Dec. 21-31, 2026 | Monday-Thursday | Winter break holiday | District closed Dec. 23-25; other days not stated |
| Jan. 1, 2027 | Friday | New Year Day holiday | District closed |
| Jan. 4, 2027 | Monday | Stockpiled PD day | Students do not report |
| Jan. 18, 2027 | Monday | Martin Luther King Jr. (MLK) holiday observed | District closed |
| Feb. 15, 2027 | Monday | Stockpiled PD day | Students do not report |
| Mar. 22-25, 2027 | Monday-Thursday | Spring Break | Not stated |
| Mar. 26, 2027 | Friday | Spring Holiday | District closed |

That is 32 weekdays of no school before the last day, by our count, including the 11 weekdays of winter break. The calendar names Mar. 26 the "Spring Holiday"; it does not name it Good Friday.

## Is Presidents Day a day off for MNPS students?

Presidents Day, Mon., Feb. 15, 2027, is not a named holiday on the calendar. The district lists that date as a stockpiled PD day with "Students do not report," so students are out and the day is not a district closure. The district also closes for Martin Luther King Jr. Day on Jan. 18, and Memorial Day, May 31, and Juneteenth, June 18, 2027, fall after the last student day.

## How many PD and weather days does MNPS stockpile?

The calendar's note reads "6 Stockpiled PD days, 7 Stockpiled inclement weather days," 13 in all. Among the dates we checked, Oct. 9, Jan. 4 and Feb. 15 are stockpiled PD days. The district's line is all this story reports; it does not say which dates would be used if snow or storms close schools.

## When do MNPS quarters end?

| Quarter | Dates the district lists |
|---|---|
| First quarter | Ends Thu., Oct. 8, 2026 |
| Second quarter | Begins Mon., Oct. 19; ends Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 (half day, exams for grades 9-12) |
| Third quarter | Begins Tue., Jan. 5; ends Fri., Mar. 5, 2027 |
| Fourth quarter | Begins Mon., Mar. 8, 2027; school ends Tue., May 25 |

Exams run Dec. 15-17 as a half day for grades 9-12, with a full day for Pre-K through grade 8. Mar. 19, 2027, the Friday before spring break, is a half day for all students. May 26 is the Teacher In-Service Day and the last day for teachers.

## How does MNPS compare with Memphis-Shelby County Schools?

Both Tennessee districts return from winter break on Jan. 5, 2027, but spring break and the last day differ. The Memphis dates come from our [Memphis-Shelby County Schools (MSCS) calendar story](/news/memphis-shelby-county-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), which cites that district's 2026-27 Student Calendar.

| Winter break | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027 | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027 |
| Students return | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring break | Mar. 22-26, 2027 | Mar. 15-19, 2027, plus Mar. 26 and 29 |
