**State exchanges set their own 2027 open enrollment deadlines: Idaho's Your Health Idaho ends Dec. 15, Vermont Health Connect ends Dec. 28, and Covered California and New Jersey's exchange run through Jan. 31, 2027**, each according to its own site or press release. HealthCare.gov closes Friday, Jan. 15, 2027, per the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). This page is the state lookup for the Affordable Care Act (ACA) Marketplace; the federal window, and why it was not shortened, is in our [HealthCare.gov dates story](/news/aca-open-enrollment-2027-dates-november-1-january-15/).

## Which dates stand out?

Among the 16 state exchanges in the table:

- **Earliest start:** Your Health Idaho opens Thursday, Oct. 15, and Connecticut's open enrollment began Friday, Oct. 23, according to the Connecticut Insurance Department's July 15, 2026 notice. HealthCare.gov and the other exchanges that state an opening day use Nov. 1.
- **Earliest end:** Idaho closes Tuesday, Dec. 15, the same day HealthCare.gov's Jan. 1 coverage deadline falls. Vermont Health Connect closes Monday, Dec. 28.
- **Latest end:** Covered California's release says enrollment runs "through Jan. 31, 2027," and the New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance says the same date for GetCoveredNJ. Both ask people to enroll by Dec. 31 for Jan. 1 coverage.
- **Jan. 15 closers:** Colorado, Connecticut, Georgia, Illinois, Kentucky, Nevada, New Mexico and Oregon list Jan. 15, 2027, the same as HealthCare.gov. Maryland does too, according to two Maryland newsrooms, the first (WYPR) and the second (WMAR), branded WMAR 2 News.

If you live in one of the states that ends Dec. 15 or Dec. 28, do not assume the Jan. 15 date you see in national coverage applies to you.

## Which states have their own exchange, and what changed in Oregon?

CMS's State-based Exchanges page, updated Aug. 13, 2026, lists 21 state-based exchanges for plan year 2026 and Oregon as transitioning for 2027. Oregon Health Authority (OHA) confirmed the switch in its Nov. 2, 2026 release: Explore Health "replaces the federal platform HealthCare.gov," open enrollment runs Nov. 1, 2026, through Jan. 15, 2027, and people "should enroll by Dec. 31 for coverage that begins Jan. 1, 2027."

## Maryland: what two newsrooms report

We did not confirm the Maryland dates on the Maryland Health Connection site, so the end date rests on two independent reports. WYPR (Nov. 2, 2026) says enrollment "will stay open until Jan. 15, 2027," and WMAR 2 News reports the same Jan. 15, 2027 end. The Dec. 31 deadline for Jan. 1 coverage that WMAR 2 News gives appears in only one report, so it is not confirmed on Maryland Health Connection and is left out of the table.

## Exchanges whose 2027 dates are not confirmed

We could not confirm 2027 dates on an official page for the District of Columbia (DC Health Link), Massachusetts (Massachusetts Health Connector), New York (NY State of Health), Pennsylvania (Pennie), Rhode Island (HealthSource RI) and Virginia (Virginia's Insurance Marketplace). Check the exchange directly. States that have no exchange of their own use HealthCare.gov and its Nov. 1 to Jan. 15 window.
