**St. Lucie Public Schools in Port St. Lucie, Fla. have no school for students Nov. 23-27, 2026, Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027, and March 22-29, 2027**, by the district's 2026-2027 School Year Calendar, Board Approved August 12, 2025. The last day for students is Friday, May 28, 2027. The Thanksgiving holiday is next.

The district's home page links the calendar as "2026-27 Student/Employee Calendar". The dates below come from that two-page PDF. We found no later revision. The calendar prints no hurricane or storm make-up days, and release times for early release days are not on it.

## St. Lucie County school breaks in 2026-27

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates, and return days are our reading of the calendar.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving holiday | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Fri., Jan. 1, 2027, then teacher workday Mon., Jan. 4 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring break | Mon., March 22 to Fri., March 26, then teacher workday Mon., March 29, 2027 | 6 | Tue., March 30 |

Plan the week on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html). For another Florida district, see the [Palm Beach County school calendar](/news/palm-beach-county-school-calendar-2026-27/).

## When do St. Lucie students return after winter break?

Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027. The calendar lists "Jan. 5: Students Return". Winter break is printed as "Dec. 21 - Jan. 4: Winter Break Holiday", and the grid shows Dec. 21 to Jan. 1 as holiday with Monday, Jan. 4 as a teacher workday. Students are out 11 weekdays: Dec. 21-25, Dec. 28-Jan. 1 and Jan. 4. Friday, Dec. 18 is the last school day before the break, an early release day and the end of the second nine weeks. The calendar notes that 12-month employees are off Dec. 24 to Jan. 1.

## Is St. Lucie County spring break one week or six school days?

Six school days. Spring break is Monday, March 22 to Friday, March 26, 2027, and Monday, March 29 is a teacher workday with no students. Students return Tuesday, March 30, the first day of the fourth quarter. The calendar notes that 12-month employees work March 22-26.

## Which St. Lucie County days are early release days?

The calendar lists eight early release days for students. It does not print release times, so check with your school.

| Date | Weekday | Reason the district lists |
|---|---|---|
| Aug. 26, 2026 | Wednesday | Recordkeeping |
| Oct. 28, 2026 | Wednesday | Faculty Council choice |
| Dec. 18, 2026 | Friday | Faculty Council choice, end of second nine weeks |
| Feb. 10, 2027 | Wednesday | Professional learning |
| March 19, 2027 | Friday | Faculty Council choice, end of third nine weeks |
| April 21, 2027 | Wednesday | Faculty Council choice |
| May 27, 2027 | Thursday | Recordkeeping |
| May 28, 2027 | Friday | Recordkeeping, last day for students |

## How long are the St. Lucie County grading quarters?

The calendar totals 180 student days: 85 in the first semester and 95 in the second.

| Quarter | Dates | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Quarter 1 | Aug. 10-Oct. 9, 2026 | 43 |
| Quarter 2 | Oct. 13-Dec. 18, 2026 | 42 |
| Quarter 3 | Jan. 5-March 19, 2027 | 51 |
| Quarter 4 | March 30-May 28, 2027 | 44 |

## What if a storm closes St. Lucie County schools?

The calendar prints no hurricane or storm make-up days. Summer school dates are listed as to be determined. If a storm closes schools, watch district notices before you book travel around a break.

## When does the St. Lucie County school year start and end?

Students start Monday, Aug. 10, 2026, and the last day for students is Friday, May 28, 2027. Monday, May 31 is Memorial Day, and June 1 is a teacher workday and the last day for teachers.
