## Solving tips

If the board feels heavy, count letters before you think about meanings. Several tiles are built on one repeated vowel, and one of them uses it too few times to count.

Five tiles name jobs, and only four of them share a group. Hold the most modern-sounding trade back until the older ones are placed.

## Why the traps worked

Beekeeper is a trade, and an old enough one to sit with Apothecary and Wainwright. Its letters decide where it goes: B, K, P and R, plus E five times and no other vowel.

Oxymoron is the near miss for purple. It also has one vowel, O, but only three of them, so it stays with the writing terms. Hullabaloo has exactly five vowels too, a mix of U, A and O, which is why a quick count can mislead you.
