**Friendly's Snow Days menu is available now through the holiday season, with a free Happy Ending Sundae for anyone who orders a Snow Days entrée, the company said in an Oct. 21, 2026 release carried by RestaurantNews.com.** Nation's Restaurant News reported the same day that the Jubilee Cake, an ice cream roll cake discontinued in 2021, is back this season. The table below puts every deal in one place. Neither source gives an end date, item prices or holiday hours. Participating restaurants and prices vary, so check your local Friendly's.

Friendly's own promotions page could not be read when this story was written, so the details come from the release text as published by RestaurantNews.com and the independent report in Nation's Restaurant News. For other chains, see the [holiday menus 2026 tracker](/news/holiday-menus-2026-every-chain-tracker/).

## Friendly's Snow Days deals, prices and hours

| Deal | Price | What you get | Days and hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free sundae | Free with an entrée | A Happy Ending Sundae with any Snow Days entrée | Not given |
| Weekday lunch | $11.99 | One of five select entrées plus a Happy Ending Sundae | Monday through Friday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. |
| Better Together | From $24.99 | Two entrées and one appetizer | Not given |
| Better Together, premium picks | $29.99 | Two entrées and one appetizer, premium picks | Not given |
| Kids' meals | Part of the kids' meal | A sealed Conehead Crew trading card pack with two surprise cards | Program began Sept. 16, 2026 |

Source: Friendly's release, Oct. 21, 2026 (Franchising.com release of Sept. 17, 2026 for the kids' cards). The premium Better Together price is $5.00 above the starting price ($29.99 minus $24.99).

## What is on the Friendly's Snow Days menu?

| Item | What is in it |
|---|---|
| Snowfall Burger | Big Beef Burger, white American cheese, Alfredo sauce, bacon, Parmesan-dusted pretzel bun, fries |
| Fireside Melt | Grilled sourdough, two cheeses, grilled chicken, bacon, tomatoes, garlic aioli, fries |
| Harvest Stack | Sourdough sandwich with stuffing, mashed potatoes, turkey breast, cranberry sauce, gravy |
| Snowball Fight Sundae | Vanilla ice cream, powdered sugar doughnut holes, hot fudge, mini marshmallows, whipped cream, sprinkles |
| Peppermint Stick Fribble | Peppermint stick ice cream, hot fudge, whipped cream, cherry |
| Jubilee Cake (returning) | Ice cream roll cake, discontinued in 2021 |

Source: Friendly's release and Nation's Restaurant News, Oct. 21, 2026. The release gives no prices.

## Is the Friendly's Jubilee Cake back in 2026?

Yes, per Nation's Restaurant News: the ice cream roll cake, discontinued in 2021, is "back this season." The report does not say what it costs or whether it is sold in restaurants, in stores or both.

## How do you get the free Friendly's sundae?

The release says, "Guests who order any Snow Days entrée receive a free Happy Ending Sundae." Ask your restaurant which items count as Snow Days entrées when you order.

## How does the $11.99 Friendly's lunch deal work?

The weekday lunch is $11.99 for one of five select entrées plus a Happy Ending Sundae, Monday through Friday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. The sources we read do not name the five entrées, and prices vary by restaurant.

## Is Friendly's open on Thanksgiving and Christmas 2026?

Friendly's has not posted holiday hours for Thursday, Nov. 26 or Friday, Dec. 25 that we could find. Call your local restaurant before you go.
