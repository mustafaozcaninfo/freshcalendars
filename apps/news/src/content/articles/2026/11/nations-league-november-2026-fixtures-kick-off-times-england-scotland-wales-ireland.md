**The 2026/27 Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) Nations League league phase ends with Matchday 5 (Nov. 12-14, 2026) and Matchday 6 (Nov. 15-17), opening for England with England v Croatia at Wembley on Thursday, Nov. 12 at 7.45pm UK time (2:45 p.m. ET).** The Scottish FA gives the matchday windows, and UEFA lists the fixtures in Central European Time (CET). Spain v England follows on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 20:45 CET, which is 7.45pm in the UK and 2:45 p.m. Eastern Time (ET).

UEFA's fixture list was last updated on Oct. 6, 2026. The UK and Ireland are on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) for these games, one hour behind CET. U.S. clocks moved to standard time on Nov. 1, so ET is 5 hours behind GMT and Pacific Time (PT) is 3 hours behind ET.

## What happens after Matchday 6 in the Nations League?

UEFA says the league phase concludes on Nov. 17, 2026. The quarter-finals are on March 25-30, 2027 and the Finals on June 9-13, 2027; UEFA says the Finals host "has not been confirmed yet."

In League A, group winners and runners-up advance to the quarter-finals, and UEFA says the "two lowest-ranked fourth-placed teams are relegated to League B." League B group winners are promoted. We do not list standings, points or earlier results here because the sources we used do not carry them.
