**Collier County Public Schools (CCPS) in Florida has no school for students or teachers Nov. 23-27, 2026, which the district calls Fall Break, and no school for students and teachers Dec. 21-31, 2026 for Winter Break**, by its "Approved Academic School Calendar 2026 – 2027." Spring Break is March 22-29, 2027, and the last student day is Thursday, May 27, 2027.

The calendar PDF is headed "Board Approved January 15, 2025" and is linked from the district's [Academic & District Calendars](https://www.collierschools.com/calendar) page. This story adds our arithmetic from the district's dates: every no-school day with its weekday, the early dismissals, the four quarters and how Collier compares with Lee County next door. A printable school-year calendar is at the bottom.

## Collier County school breaks in 2026-27

The district calls Thanksgiving week "Fall Break", so a search for a Thanksgiving break or a fall break leads to the same dates. Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates and include Thanksgiving Day, New Year's Day and the teacher plan days, since students have no school on them.

| Break | No school for students | Weekdays off | Students return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Break | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30, 2026 (our reading) |
| Winter Break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Thu., Dec. 31, plus Fri., Jan. 1 and Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring Break | Fri., March 19 (plan day), then Mon., March 22 to Mon., March 29, 2027 | 7 | Tue., March 30, 2027 (our reading) |

The district prints "5- Students return to school" for Jan. 5. It prints no return day for the other two breaks, so Nov. 30 and March 30 are the next weekdays the calendar does not list as off.

The district lists Spring Break as "22 - 29", Monday to the next Monday, which is six weekdays. Friday, March 19 is a Teacher Plan Day with no school for students, so the full stretch runs from Friday, March 19 through Monday, March 29. Some websites give spring break as March 19-29; the district's own line is March 22-29, and we use that. The [March 2027 printable calendar](/march-calendars.html) shows the run, and our [spring break 2027 roundup](/news/spring-break-2027-dates-school-districts/) compares other districts.

Students started Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026, a week after teachers on Tuesday, Aug. 4, so the dates before today, Nov. 5, are past. Plan the next break on the [November 2026 calendar](/november-calendars.html).

Sept. 21 is listed as a Professional Development Day with "activities allowed after sundown"; the calendar gives no other detail. Report cards are published Monday, Jan. 11, 2027.

### Collier County early dismissal days

Students leave early on these days, and the calendar pairs most with Professional Learning for Teachers. The district's printed text gives no dismissal times, so we list none.

| Date | Weekday | What the district lists |
|---|---|---|
| Aug. 26, 2026 | Wednesday | Early Dismissal/Professional Learning for Teachers |
| Oct. 29, 2026 | Thursday | Early Dismissal Day/ Professional Learning for Teachers |
| Feb. 2, 2027 | Tuesday | Early Dismissal Day/ Professional Learning for Teachers |
| April 21, 2027 | Wednesday | Early Dismissal Day/ Professional Learning for Teachers |
| May 21, 2027 | Friday | Early Dismissal Day, in graduation week (May 17-21) |
| May 27, 2027 | Thursday | End of Q4, Early Dismissal and Last Student Day |

Friday, Dec. 18, 2026 is the end of Quarter 2, marked "end of day on the 18". We could not confirm an early dismissal that day, so ask the school before you plan around it.

## How long are the Collier County quarters?

The calendar prints a day count for each quarter. They add up to 180 student days, our sum of the four counts.

| Quarter | Ends | Student days |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fri., Oct. 9, 2026 | 42 |
| 2 | Fri., Dec. 18, 2026 | 44 |
| 3 | Thu., March 18, 2027 | 51 |
| 4 | Thu., May 27, 2027 | 43 |

Quarter 3 is the longest at 51 days and ends March 18, one day before the plan day that opens the spring break stretch.

## Does the Collier County calendar include hurricane make-up days?

The 2026-27 calendar prints none: no days are set aside after the last student day, May 27, 2027. The district's calendar page is the place to look for any change.

## How does Collier compare with Lee County?

Lee County, Florida, is a Southwest Florida neighbor with its own district. The Lee dates come from our [Lee County calendar story](/news/lee-county-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/), which cites the School District of Lee County's 2026-2027 Instructional Calendar.

| | Collier County | Lee County |
|---|---|---|
| First student day | Tue., Aug. 11, 2026 | Tue., Aug. 11, 2026 |
| Thanksgiving week | Nov. 23-27, called Fall Break | Nov. 23-27, called Thanksgiving Break |
| Winter break | Dec. 21-31 | Dec. 21, 2026 to Jan. 1, 2027 |
| Students return | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 |
| Spring break | March 22-29 | March 22-25, with March 26 and 29 also off |
| Last student day | Thu., May 27, 2027 | Thu., May 27, 2027 |

The two districts share the same start, Thanksgiving week, return day after winter break and last day. They differ in how the March stretch is built: Collier lists Spring Break as March 22-29, and Lee lists March 22-25 as Spring Break with Good Friday and a remote duty day on March 26 and 29. For another Florida county see the [Hillsborough County calendar story](/news/hillsborough-county-public-schools-calendar-2026-27-breaks/).

## Is the 2027-28 Collier County calendar posted?

Yes. The district's [calendar page](https://www.collierschools.com/calendar) links a [2027-2028 calendar PDF](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1769005015/collierschoolscom/o881tuo3iwl9vsa4dqfq/2027-2028AcademicCalendarEnglish.pdf). We have not read it, so we give no 2027-28 dates here.
