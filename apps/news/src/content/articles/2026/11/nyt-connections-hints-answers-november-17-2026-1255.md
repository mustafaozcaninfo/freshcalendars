## Solving tips

Four of these entries come from the same band's catalog, and that is bait. Treat any link between entries from different corners of the board with suspicion until a full set of four is sitting in front of you. Spotting one clean group and removing it shrinks the leftovers fast, and a fresh look at what remains often shows a pattern that was invisible before.

## What each color asks of you today

Yellow asks for vocabulary only. Four verbs or verb phrases share one meaning, and the two-word Come Together is the one that takes a moment to see.

Green asks for outside knowledge. You need to know what the rhyme contains, and the clue is hidden in the fourth entry, which names the coin in the title.

Blue asks you to restore missing punctuation. Say each title with an exclamation point added and you will recall the film.

Purple asks you to stop reading whole words. Kingpin, Masked Ball and Penny Lane end in a separate bowling word, while Valley only contains one at its tail, so you have to look at the last letters rather than the meaning.
