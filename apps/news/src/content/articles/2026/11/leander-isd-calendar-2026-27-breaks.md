**Leander Independent School District (ISD) has no school Nov. 23-27, 2026, from Monday, Dec. 21, 2026 to Monday, Jan. 4, 2027, and Mar. 15-19, 2027**, according to its 2026-2027 Approved Calendar. Students return Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, and the last day of school is Thursday, May 27, 2027, an early release day. The Board of Trustees approved the calendar at its Feb. 19, 2026 meeting, the district said in a Mar. 2, 2026 news post.

Classes began Wednesday, Aug. 12, 2026. The next break is Thanksgiving week. The Oct. 9-13 weekend, Sept. 7 Labor Day and the Nov. 2-3 Continuous Improvement Conference (CIC) days have passed.

## When are the Leander ISD breaks in 2026-27?

Weekdays off are our count from the district's dates. Return days are the first weekday after each break and are our reading, not a date the district prints.

| Break | No school | Weekdays off | Classes resume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thanksgiving | Mon., Nov. 23 to Fri., Nov. 27, 2026 | 5 | Mon., Nov. 30 (our reading) |
| Winter break | Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 to Mon., Jan. 4, 2027 | 11 | Tue., Jan. 5, 2027 (our reading) |
| Spring break | Mon., Mar. 15 to Fri., Mar. 19, 2027 | 5 | Mon., Mar. 22 (our reading) |

The Approved Calendar calls Nov. 23-27 "Thanksgiving Break (No staff/Students)," while the district's Essentials sheet calls the same week "Fall Break." Both are the same dates.

The winter stretch is built from three lines: "Winter Break (No staff/Students)" on Dec. 21-31, New Years' Day on Jan. 1, and "Professional Learning" on Jan. 4. The Essentials legend marks Professional Learning days "Professional Learning/No School," so students are out Jan. 4. ## Which Leander ISD days make long weekends?

These stretches are our count: a Professional Learning day or break day that touches a weekend or another day off.

| Stretch | Days off in a row | Weekdays off |
|---|---|---|
| Fri., Oct. 9 to Tue., Oct. 13, 2026 (past) | 5 | 3 |
| Fri., Feb. 12 to Tue., Feb. 16, 2027 | 5 | 3 |
| Fri., Mar. 26 to Mon., Mar. 29, 2027 | 4 | 2 |

The Feb. 15 Monday is not named Presidents Day on the calendar; it is one of the two "Student & Staff Break" days. Memorial Day falls after the last day of school, and the calendar's statutory notes say "No school on Memorial Day."

## What are the Leander ISD early release times?

Dec. 18, 2026 and May 27, 2027 are early release days. The district's Essentials sheet gives these hours in Central Time (CT), next to the regular schedule.

| Level | Regular day | Early release |
|---|---|---|
| Elementary | 7:30 a.m. to 2:50 p.m. | 7:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. |
| Middle | 9:00 a.m. to 4:20 p.m. | 9:00 a.m. to 1:05 p.m. |
| High | 8:15 a.m. to 3:35 p.m. | 8:15 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. |

The district's calendar page adds that "Child Nutrition Services will offer sack lunches these days."

## When is the last day of school at Leander ISD?

Thursday, May 27, 2027, an early release day. The district's news post says the final calendar "ends on Thursday, May 27, 2027, an early-release day, instead of Friday, May 28 as previously shared."

## What does the calendar say about school days and minutes?

The Approved Calendar prints its "Statutory Guidelines": 187 teacher days and 75,600 minutes, "with enough on top of that for two bad weather days." It does not list make-up dates for weather closures, so none are given here.
