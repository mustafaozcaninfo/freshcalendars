## 2026-11-03 (layout, story page)

- **Printables are below the whole story.** On a story page the order is breadcrumb, H1, lede, byline, hero, ad, key points, key dates, a one-line "free printables below" link, the table of contents, the full body, and only then `DownloadGrid` (`apps/news/src/pages/[slug]/index.astro`, lines 187-196). Printables earn the best click rate on /news/ (November gratitude PDF 29%, pumpkin templates 81 clicks). Suggest: for stories with `downloads`, a compact card right after the key points with the first printable's thumbnail and a "Download PDF" button, the full grid staying where it is. Measure: PDF downloads per page view before and after (Worker logs), and whether PDF URLs keep ranking.
- **PDFs rank on their own.** `/news/downloads/november-2026-calendar/november-2026-gratitude-calendar.pdf` takes 35 clicks from 121 impressions at position 3.5; `chinese-lunar-calendar-2027.pdf` 22 clicks. A searcher who lands on the PDF never sees the story, the other printables or an ad. The build already stamps a top link into each PDF; suggest checking that it is a visible "More free printables at freshcalendars.com/news/<slug>/" line on page 1, and that each PDF response carries `Link: <story URL>; rel="canonical"` so Google credits the story when it chooses.
- **Hero height on phones.** The hero (2400x1350) sits between the byline and the answer box, so on a phone the key points start below the first screen. Suggest testing a lower max height for the hero on narrow screens. Measure: scroll depth or click rate on the printable link.
