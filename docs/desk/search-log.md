# Search desk log

The search desk (`s`, `docs/DESK.md` "Search routine") keeps this file. Writers read "Learned" before they choose a headline or a lede.

## Learned

Patterns that two or more measured changes agree on, newest first. An observation that is not yet measured is marked "seen".

- seen (2026-11-08): every experiment with an "after" window that contains Nov. 8, 2026 measures two changes: the desk's brief and the headline migration of that day (945 stories retitled, seoTitle removed, `updatedAt` unchanged). Do not add a lesson to this list from those results alone. `updatedAt` does not show such a change: check `git log` on the story file before filing a brief.

- seen (2026-11-05): holiday-list queries for next year earn almost nothing even at position 1: "bank holidays 2027" 10,311 impressions at 1.1, 0 clicks; "federal holidays 2027" 3,745 at 1.2, 1 click; "winter solstice 2026" 14,138 at 1.5, 1 click (28 days to Nov. 2). Same pattern as "thanksgiving 2026": Google shows the list or date itself. Do not brief these on the title; their value is the printable and image search.

- seen (2026-11-03): most of the largest /news/ opportunities are event pages whose date has passed or is today (sample ballot, Election Day "what's open", Day of the Dead, Halloween sunset, All Souls' Day, UK Budget). The data is 3 days late, so by the time a page shows up as an opportunity its event is often over: brief event pages at least 10 days before the date, and leave them once it has passed.

- seen (2026-11-03): a query Google answers in its own box (a holiday date, a time) shows huge impressions and almost no clicks even at position 3 ("thanksgiving 2026": 204,000 impressions at position 3.3, 39 clicks in 28 days). A better title does not win those clicks; do not spend a brief on them.
